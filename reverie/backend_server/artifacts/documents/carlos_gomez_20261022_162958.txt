Workshop Draft: “The Trees Keep Their Secrets”

The trees keep their secrets  
in roots that drink the rain,  
in branches clasping winter  
like old, forgiving hands.  

Every wind has touched them.  
Every crow has stolen  
a scrap of silver from their leaves,  
a crumb of sunlight from their mouths.  

Still, they stand—  
ugly in their patience,  
tender in their silence,  
green storms in a black trunk.  

I wanted to write something soft,  
but the forest won’t whisper.  
It growls.  
It hums through broken branches.  
It teaches me that beauty  
does not always come dressed gently.

Facilitator’s Comment:  
Strong imagery and a clear voice. The final lines land well, but the shift in tone feels abrupt. Consider developing the idea of the forest refusing to whisper. “Green storms in a black trunk” is excellent—keep that image. Also, punctuation creates the staccato rhythm effectively.
