New Improv Class Update! 🎭

I just started exploring improvisational comedy, and I’ve already learned that the best way to ruin a scene is to prepare for it too seriously. I walk into class with a whole plan—specific characters, dramatic entrances, maybe a subtle Spanish accent—and then the coach reminds us: “Don’t plan, listen.”

Cool. Apparently, spontaneity is a thing. 😂

In today’s exercise, we had to create a story with one word each. I confidently suggested “database,” which somehow turned into an emotional hostage situation involving a lost Wi-Fi password and a very judgmental parrot. Somehow, it worked? I’m still deciding whether that counts as comedy or a cry for help.

The strange thing is, I’m loving how uncomfortable it makes me. Improv is teaching me to stay present, trust my instincts, and let the other performers make the scene better instead of trying to control everything myself. Exactly what I need for the web series—we’re constantly building weird, chaotic moments...
