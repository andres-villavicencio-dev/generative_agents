Primary Objective
Document and test candidate mathematical patterns found in nature, with particular attention to exponential growth and periodic variation. The goal is not merely to identify a pattern, but to determine which model best describes it.

Research Tasks
1. Exponential growth analysis
Compare successive growth ratios in selected population and reproduction datasets.
Record the base rate, time interval, and percentage change.
Check whether deviations remain within a reasonable error margin.
Draft the model: \(N(t)=N_0e^{rt}\).

2. Periodic variation study
Examine temperature, daylight, population, or seasonal datasets.
Identify the period, amplitude, phase, and any secondary cycles.
Compare simple sine models with more complicated functions.
Note where apparent periodicity may be an illusion caused by sampling.
