1. Purpose and Model

I am reviewing the logistic growth calculations used in the project. The objective is not merely to produce a smooth population curve, but to confirm that every equation follows from the stated assumptions.

Let \(N(t)\) represent population size at time \(t\), \(r>0\) the intrinsic growth rate, and \(K>0\) the carrying capacity. The model is

\[
\frac{dN}{dt}=rN\left(1-\frac{N}{K}\right).
\]

It assumes constant \(r\) and \(K\), a closed population, and no sudden environmental changes.

2. Derivation Check

For \(0<N<K\), separation gives

\[
\frac{dN}{N(1-N/K)}=r\,dt.
\]

Since

\[
\frac{1}{N(1-N/N)}=\frac1N+\frac1{K-N},
\]

integration yields

\[
\ln\left(\frac{N}{K-N}\right)=rt+C.
\]

Solving for \(N\),

\[
N(t)=\frac{K}{1+\left(\frac{K-N0}{N0}\right)e^{-rt}}.
