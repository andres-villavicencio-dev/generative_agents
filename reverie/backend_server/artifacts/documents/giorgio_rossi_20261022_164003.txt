Research Calculation Review: Exponential Growth in Lichen Colonies

Date: Monday, February 13  
Researcher: Giorgio Rossi  
Project: Mathematical Patterns in Nature  
Review period: January 6–February 10  

Purpose

Compare calculated colony-area projections with field observations from four wall sites. The aim is not to force nature into a tidy curve, but to determine where the model fails and why.

Method

I used the exponential model \(A(t)=A_0e^{rt}\), with \(r=0.040\) per day derived from preliminary sampling. Areas were measured to the nearest 0.1 cm² on Day 0 and Day 14. Percentage differences were calculated as:

\[
(\text{Observed}-\text{Calculated})/\text{Calculated}\times100
\]

| Site | Initial Area | Calculated Area | Observed Area | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 20.0 cm² | 35.0 cm² | 31.2 cm² | −10.9% |
| B | 25.0 cm² | 43.8 cm² | 44.1 cm² | +0.7% |
| C | 18.0 cm² | 31.5 cm² | 27.9 cm² | −11.4% |
| D | 22.0 cm² | 38.5 cm² | 42.7 cm² | +10.9% |
