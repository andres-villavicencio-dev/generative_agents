Research Calculation Review: Discrete Logistic Growth

Date: Monday, February 13  
Project: Mathematical Patterns in Nature

I reviewed the discrete logistic equation

\[
x{n+1}=xn+r xn\left(1-\frac{xn}{K}\right),
\]

using carrying capacity \(K=100\), growth parameter \(r=1\), and initial population \(x0=10\). The sequence was:

\[
10,\ 19,\ 34.39,\ 56.95,\ 81.47,\ 96.57,\ 99.88,\ 100.00\ldots
\]

The convergence is smooth and remains inside the physical domain \(0\le xn\le100\).

Boundary and Special-Condition Tests
Zero population: \(x0=0\) produces \(x1=0\). There is no spontaneous population.
Full capacity: \(x0=100\) produces \(x1=100\), so the population remains fixed.
No growth: Setting \(r=0\) gives \(x{n+1}=xn\) for every initial value.
Negative values: \(x0=-10\) produces \(x1=-21\), confirming that the formula is mathematically valid but biologically meaningless outside the nonnegative domain.
