RESEARCH VERIFICATION MEMORANDUM

Date: Monday, February 13  
To: Mathematical Patterns in Nature Research Team  
From: Giorgio Rossi  
Subject: Re-run and Verification of Corrected Growth-Model Calculations

Summary

I reviewed the calculations supporting the preliminary leaf-growth model and identified the source of the discrepancy reported last week. Four rainfall measurements had been entered in centimeters while the model required millimeters. The affected inputs were therefore corrected from 0.85, 1.20, 1.75, and 2.40 cm to 8.5, 12.0, 17.5, and 24.0 mm.

Corrective Action

I replaced the erroneous values, confirmed their units in the source dataset, and reran the complete normalization and growth-rate calculation. The revised primary growth coefficient is 0.743, compared with the previous value of 0.687. The coefficient of determination remains strong at R² = 0.918.
