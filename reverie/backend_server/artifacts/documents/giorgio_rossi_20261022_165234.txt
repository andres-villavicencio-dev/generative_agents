RESEARCH REVIEW MEMORANDUM

Subject: Revised Results and Parameter Robustness  
Date: Monday, February 13  
Author: Giorgio Rossi

I have recalculated the model using the revised parameter estimates and examined whether its principal conclusions survive small perturbations. Three patterns emerge with unusual consistency.

First, the system exhibits damped oscillations around its equilibrium. The amplitude decreases approximately geometrically, with a decay rate near 0.82. This suggests a stable feedback process rather than sustained cyclical behavior.

Second, the transition threshold is slightly asymmetric. An increase of roughly 4% in the critical carrying capacity is required to produce the same qualitative change as a 7% reduction. The model therefore appears more resilient to expansion than to contraction—a rather inconvenient asymmetry, but one that repeats across all initial-condition tests.
