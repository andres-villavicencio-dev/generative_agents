Research Calculation Review: Mathematical Patterns in Nature

Date: Monday, February 13  
Prepared by: Giorgio Rossi  
Subject: Preliminary harmonic analysis of recurring natural patterns

1. Objective

I am testing whether several natural time series can be represented by a limited set of repeating cycles. The goal is not to force every measurement into a tidy curve, but to identify where a simple mathematical model provides meaningful explanatory power.

2. Data Sources
Temperature: NOAA Climate Data Online daily minimum and maximum records, 2013–2022.  
River discharge: USGS National Water Information System hourly stream-gauge readings.  
Sea level: NOAA tide-gauge observations from three coastal stations.  
Wildflower counts: GBIF occurrence records aggregated by survey date and grid cell.

Only records with reliable timestamps, location metadata, and documented collection methods are included.
