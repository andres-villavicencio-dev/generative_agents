Research Memorandum: Phyllotaxis Angle Review

Date: Monday, February 13  
From: Giorgio Rossi  
Subject: Analysis of Golden-Angle Patterns in Sunflower Heads  
Project: Mathematical Patterns in Nature

Objective

I reviewed the angular measurements from five sunflower heads to determine whether their spiral arrangements follow the theoretical golden angle of 137.5°. For each head, I calculated the mean of four adjacent-petal angles, compared the result with the benchmark, and checked the arithmetic using both direct deviation and mean-absolute-error methods.

Results
