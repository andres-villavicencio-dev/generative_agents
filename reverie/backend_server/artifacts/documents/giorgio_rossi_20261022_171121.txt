1. Purpose and scope

This review checks the draft’s calculations, mathematical notation, measurement units, figure labels, and references. The primary result concerns phyllotaxis—the angular arrangement of leaves—and is compared with the broader use of power-law scaling in biological data.

2. Calculation review

The golden angle is defined as

\[
\thetag=360^\circ\left(1-\frac{1}{\varphi}\right),
\qquad
\varphi=\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2},
\]

giving \(\thetag=137.507764^\circ\). The draft’s angle sequence was recalculated using

\[
\thetan=[(n-1)\thetag]\bmod 360^\circ.
\]
