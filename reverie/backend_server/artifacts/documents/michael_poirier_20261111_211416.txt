Tuesday, February 14

To Eddy Lin,

I wanted to show you something today, something I’ve been holding onto since I first drew the first character. The river in my fantasy world is glowing because of a secret path made of light. I’ve sketched it in Procreate, filled my sketchbooks with new ideas, and built a digital portfolio of fantasy characters for you. I’m excited to hear what you think.

I also wanted you to help me write the second chorus. I wrote the opening verse in a fast, curious rhythm, and I’m matching it to the established beat. We’ll work on the melody, the lyrics, and the final arrangement together. Eddy, I’m curious to see what you bring to the song.

If you’re wondering how much time I have for this, I’m balancing homework, sketching, and photography. Tom and Jane Moreno have noticed my busy schedule, but I’m not just doing this for the portfolio. I want to make music.
