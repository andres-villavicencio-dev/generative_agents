To Eddy Lin, River City, February 14

Dear Eddy,

I hope you’re doing well. I wanted to share something special with you. I’ve been sketching fantasy characters lately, and I’ve created a digital portfolio of five original figures, each with a glowing path, magical energy, and a small secret they’re trying to keep hidden. I’ve been filling them with stories and imagining what they might say.

I thought of you and our collaboration, and I wanted to ask: what if we recorded a theme song for one of my storyboards? I’d love to hear your final chorus and confirm the song’s conclusion with you. If you’d like, I could bring a guitar and show you my sketches. I’m especially curious about the ending—do you think the music should end quietly?

Thank you for listening, Eddy. I’m excited to show you my portfolio and to hear your voice.

Warmly,  
Michael Poirier  
Oak Hill College
