Subject: Possible Next Stanza Recommendation Cards

Monday, February 13

Hi Carlos,

Thanks for discussing the “Possible next stanza” idea with me. I prototyped a lightweight version of the recommendation cards focused on three principles: recommendations should be supportive, editable, and transparent about why they appear.

Each card will distinguish between two types of guidance:
Based on your stated preferences for suggestions the user has explicitly chosen, such as tone, length, or rhyme scheme.
Suggested from recent patterns for options inferred from repeated behavior. These will be labeled as suggestions rather than treated as confirmed preferences.

Users can accept a card, edit the suggested stanza, or dismiss it without affecting future recommendations. I also added a short “Why this suggestion?” explanation to each card so the recommendation does not feel arbitrary or overly authoritative.
