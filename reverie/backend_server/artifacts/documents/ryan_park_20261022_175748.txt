App Test Review: Error Messages, Failure States, and Retry Behavior

Tester: Ryan Park  
Date: Monday, February 13  
Project: Mobile App

Purpose

This review evaluates how the app communicates errors, handles failed operations, and recovers through retry behavior. The goal is to ensure users understand what happened and have a clear path forward without needing to restart the app.

Findings and Recommendations

1. Error Message Clarity

Several error messages expose technical terminology that may not be useful to users. Generic responses such as “Request failed” should explain the problem in plain language and provide a relevant next step. For example, “Unable to load your projects. Check your connection and try again” is more actionable than a server or status-code message.
