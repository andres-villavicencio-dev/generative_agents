Mobile App Test Report: Notifications and Settings

Tester: Ryan Park  
Date: Monday, February 13  
Project: Mobile App Testing

Test Scope

I tested notification delivery, user settings, and available notification actions. The goal was to verify that notifications arrive reliably and that their behavior matches the selected preferences.

Findings and Improvements

Notification delivery is working consistently on supported devices, including standard alerts and scheduled reminders. However, the app should provide clearer confirmation when a notification has been scheduled, canceled, or failed to send. Adding a brief success or error message will reduce uncertainty and prevent unnecessary support requests.
