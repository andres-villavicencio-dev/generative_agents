CP-01: Form Data Is Lost After Session Timeout

Summary
Users are forced back to the login screen when the session expires while completing a long form. All unsaved information is lost. I reproduced the issue in three of five attempts, making it a consistent blocker for slow connections or users completing lengthy sections.

Steps to Reproduce
1. Sign in to the app.
2. Open a form that requires multiple sections.
3. Enter information in several fields but do not submit it.
4. Leave the app inactive until the session timeout expires.
5. Return to the app and attempt to continue.
6. Attempt to sign in again and reopen the form.
