Testing the Notifications Feature

Date: Monday, February 13  
Tester: Ryan Park  
Device: Samsung Galaxy S23, Android 14  
App Version: 1.2.0

I tested the app’s notification system on a Samsung Galaxy S23 running Android 14. The notifications arrived as expected, but I noticed a few areas that should be addressed before the app is released.

Positive Findings
The notification arrived at the expected time and was displayed clearly.
The notification icon was visible, and the sound was audible.
The notification content was readable and contained the expected details.

Issues and Recommendations
Dismissal: I could not dismiss the notification from the lock screen. Attempting to dismiss it from the notification shade did not work.
Multiple Notifications: When three notifications arrived at once, I had to tap each one individually. The app should allow dismissal of all notifications at once.
