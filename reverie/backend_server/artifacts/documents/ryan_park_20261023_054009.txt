Testing Report: Notification and Dismissal Functionality

Date: Monday, February 13  
Tester: Ryan Park  
Device: iOS 17.4 on iPhone 14 Pro

Testing Scope

The app’s notification system was tested across the following scenarios:
New notifications appearing on the lock screen and home screen
Notification delivery and visual indicators
Notification dismissal via the app, lock screen, and notification center
Notification persistence after dismissal
Handling of repeated notifications and overlapping alerts

Findings

1. Notification delivery: The app successfully delivers notifications to the device. The notification center displays the icon, timestamp, and message.
2. Dismissal: Dismissing a notification works immediately. The icon disappears from the notification center, and the app does not continue to display it.
3. Lock screen behavior: Notifications appear on the lock screen after a short delay. Dismissing them from the lock screen removes the icon.
