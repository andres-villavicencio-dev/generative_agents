Test Findings Report: Notification and Dismissal

Date: Monday, February 13, 2024  
Subject: Testing the app’s notification delivery and dismissal behavior

I tested the app’s notification system on a device and documented the findings. The primary goal was to verify that notifications are delivered correctly and that users can dismiss them without leaving the app.

The notification delivery worked as expected. Alerts appeared in the designated area, and the app responded immediately. However, dismissing a notification did not always prevent the app from opening. In some cases, the notification remained visible even after the user tapped dismiss.

I also tested the auto-dismissal feature. It appeared to work for most notifications, but the system sometimes kept the notification open after a few seconds. I also tested the sound and visual feedback. The sound was audible, and the dismissal animation was smooth, but the visual feedback was inconsistent.
