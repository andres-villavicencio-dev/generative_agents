Testing Report: Notification and Dismissal Functionality

Date: Monday, February 13  
Tester: Ryan Park  
Device: Android Test Device  
App Under Test: Mobile Application

I tested the app’s notification system and dismissal functionality across several scenarios. The notification center opened reliably, and the notification list displayed expected alerts. I dismissed one notification using the standard button, and the app returned to its previous state.

The dismissal process was straightforward, but I noticed a few inconsistencies. One notification remained visible after dismissal, suggesting the app may not be fully clearing the notification list. Additionally, the dismissed notification could not be reopened through the notification center, which may affect the user experience.
