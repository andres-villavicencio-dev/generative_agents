App: Mobile App

Test Environment: Device with notifications enabled and a simulated notification flow

Test Objective
Verify that the app correctly displays notifications, handles their delivery, and allows users to dismiss them without unintended side effects.

Test Procedure
1. Open the app and navigate to the notifications section.
2. Trigger a simulated notification from an external source.
3. Observe the notification display, timing, and delivery behavior.
4. Attempt to dismiss the notification using the app’s interface.
5. Confirm that the notification is removed from the notification list and that the dismissed action does not interfere with other app functions.

Test Results
The notification appeared immediately after the simulated event.
The app displayed the notification title, time, and source.
