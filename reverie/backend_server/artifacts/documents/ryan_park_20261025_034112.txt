Code Review Findings  

The new mobile app has been reviewed against the project requirements and internal quality standards. The codebase is generally clean and readable, with a clear separation of concerns between presentation, business logic, and data handling. However, several areas require attention before production release.

Key Technical Decisions  

1. Architecture and Modularization
