Technical Decision Log: Search and Filtering Functionality  
Date: Tuesday, February 14  
Author: Ryan Park  
Status: Completed  

Purpose  
Document the technical decisions made during testing of the search and filtering functionality, including how the system handles queries, filters, pagination, and edge cases.

Search Behavior  
The search feature returns results based on the selected field, case-insensitive matching, and relevance ranking. A search bar is placed prominently at the top of the results page, with a clear “Clear” option to remove the current query.

Filtering  
Filters are organized by category and can be applied individually or in combination. The interface supports:
Selecting multiple categories at once  
Applying filters to existing results  
Filtering by date, price, rating, or other relevant attributes  
Sorting results by relevance, relevance score, or another configured criterion
