Technical Decision Log: Notification and Message Delivery

Date: Tuesday, February 14  
Status: Completed testing and documentation  
Author: Ryan Park

Objective
Verify that notifications and messages are delivered reliably, with appropriate retries, error handling, and user feedback, while documenting the trade-offs and decisions made during implementation.

Key Decisions

1. Delivery Strategy  
   Messages are delivered over the same channel as notifications. The system uses a retry mechanism with a configurable maximum number of attempts. If a message fails after the retry limit is reached, it is marked as failed and sent to the user’s notification center.

2. Retry Behavior  
   Retries are limited to prevent unnecessary retries. The retry interval is adjusted based on the message age and the channel’s current load. Messages older than 15 minutes are retried with a longer interval, while recent messages use a shorter interval.
