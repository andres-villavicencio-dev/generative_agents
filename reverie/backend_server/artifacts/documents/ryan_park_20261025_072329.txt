Technical Decision Log – Mobile App Navigation and App Switching

Date: Tuesday, February 14  
Tester: Ryan Park  
Status: Completed

Objective
Verify that the app’s navigation and app-switching behavior are consistent, predictable, and responsive.

Test Scenarios

1. Primary navigation  
Tap the home, list, and detail screens in sequence.  
Confirm that each screen loads without errors and that the previous screen remains accessible.

2. Back navigation  
Use the back button and return to the previous screen.  
Verify that the screen is restored correctly and that the back button returns to the original view.

3. Home screen navigation  
Tap the home icon.  
Confirm that the app switches to the home screen and that the selected item is highlighted.

4. Tab-based navigation  
Switch between tabs and confirm that the active tab is preserved.  
Close and reopen the tab to ensure it remains open.
