Technical Decision Log: Offline Mode and Data Synchronization

Date: Tuesday, February 14  
Status: In Progress

I am testing the app’s offline mode and data synchronization to ensure it remains usable when connectivity is unavailable. The goal is to preserve the core experience while minimizing the risk of data loss or incomplete updates.

Offline Mode  
The app will function without an active internet connection. Users can still save progress, view completed tasks, and access locally cached content. Offline actions should be queued and applied automatically when connectivity is restored. The app should also provide clear indicators when an action cannot be completed due to a lack of network access.
