Technical Decision Log: Push Notifications and Real-Time Updates

Date: Tuesday, February 14  
Status: Completed  
Author: Ryan Park

Objective
Document the technical decisions made during the testing of push notifications and real-time updates, including their requirements, architecture, and trade-offs.

Requirements
Push notifications must be delivered reliably within 10 seconds of an event.
Real-time updates should update the UI without requiring a page refresh.
The system must handle delivery failures, duplicate notifications, and stale updates gracefully.
Notification delivery must be measurable, with clear error logging and user feedback.

Architecture Decisions
Push notification service: I chose a dedicated notification service with a reliable delivery queue and a fallback to background tasks. This reduces the risk of missing notifications during high load.
