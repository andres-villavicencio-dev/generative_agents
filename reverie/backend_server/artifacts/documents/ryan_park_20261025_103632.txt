Project Requirements Review and New Mobile App Plan

I reviewed the project requirements document this morning and identified the app’s primary goals: provide a reliable, intuitive mobile experience for users to manage their daily tasks, set reminders, and track their progress. The app should be mobile-first, fast to load, and easy to use without requiring advanced technical knowledge.

The plan focuses on three core priorities. First, I will prioritize the requirements and define clear acceptance criteria. Second, I will organize the feature list into a prioritized backlog, separating high-value features from those that can be delayed. Third, I will outline the technical approach, including the app’s architecture, database design, and deployment strategy.
