Technical Requirements and Clinic App Plan

I reviewed the app’s requirements document and identified the primary functional goals: streamline clinic scheduling, appointment management, patient records, billing, and community notifications. The app must integrate securely with the clinic’s existing systems, support both staff and patients, and remain accessible across mobile devices.

The technical requirements also emphasize reliability, offline functionality, and a responsive user interface. Performance, security, and accessibility are non-negotiable. I will prioritize these requirements while keeping the app’s usability and workflow in mind.

My next step is to build a phased development plan. I will begin with a clean architecture, then implement the core scheduling and patient-management modules, followed by billing and notifications. A beta will be released to the clinic and selected community members for feedback, with a final release scheduled after the review.
