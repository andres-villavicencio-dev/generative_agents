Feature Changelog – Mobile App

Release Notes – February 14

New Features
Task scheduling: Users can now create and manage recurring tasks with customizable due dates and reminders.
Quick notes: Add, edit, and delete notes directly from the app without leaving the current screen.
Local favorites: Favorites are now saved locally, so they remain available even when the app is closed.

Improvements
Faster navigation: The main menu now opens in under 0.5 seconds.
Improved search: The search bar now filters results by title, author, and tag.
Better error handling: App crashes are now more visible, and users receive clear guidance on how to recover.
Accessibility: Screen readers and keyboard navigation have been updated to match the latest WCAG standards.

Known Issues
The “Export” button sometimes appears disabled when the app is opened after an incomplete session.
Certain cached images may display incorrectly on older devices.
