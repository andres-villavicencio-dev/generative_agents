Mobile App Integration Test Plan

Project: New Mobile App  
Date: Monday, February 13  
Status: Integration testing in progress  

I am currently writing integration tests for the new mobile app’s feature interactions. The goal is to verify that the app behaves correctly across the main user flows, including registration, login, profile updates, notifications, and data persistence.

The test suite covers both functional and edge-case scenarios. For example, I am testing that a user can complete a full registration cycle, that session tokens are properly validated, and that the app handles missing or invalid input without crashing. I am also checking that notifications are delivered correctly, that data is saved and restored as expected, and that the app remains responsive during typical usage.
