CAMPAIGN REMINDERS  
Monday, February 13  
Community Concerns to Raise with Local Officials

Neighbors,

Here are a few reminders from the evening news that I hope we can raise with city officials this week. As we all know, good government starts with good questions—and stubborn follow-through.

1. Public Safety and Code Enforcement  
   Contact Chief Miller and ask for an update on vacant properties, neighborhood code violations, and how quickly inspectors can follow up on complaints. I’d also like to know whether the department is doing anything more to assist residents dealing with abandoned buildings. I remember plenty of “we’ll get right on it” from my days in the Navy. Let’s make sure the paperwork is moving too.
