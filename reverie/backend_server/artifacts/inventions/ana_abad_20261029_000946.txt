Ana Abad built a simple, plantain-based breakfast and snack kit for her new café. Each kit contains a small batch of ripe plantains, a little flour, a pinch of salt, and a teaspoon of warm water. She presses the plantains until they soften, then grinds them into a fine paste. The paste is lightly fried in a pan with a little oil until golden, then folded with a splash of milk and a few chopped nuts or banana. The resulting pan-pancake is served warm with a glass of fruit juice or coffee.

She made the kit to create a quick, nutritious breakfast that could be prepared in a café kitchen without needing many ingredients. The recipe’s simplicity and flavor make it ideal for a café snack, while the plantain provides a hearty, slightly sweet texture. The kit also helps her reduce waste and use local ingredients, giving her café a unique, community-friendly identity.
