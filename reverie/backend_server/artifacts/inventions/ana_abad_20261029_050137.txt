Ana Abad built a Dominican-inspired café prototype called “Café de la Cruz,” designed to bring together food, art, and community in one space. The concept centers a rustic counter and intimate seating area, with an open kitchen where guests can watch the making of coffee, pastry, and local dishes. Her dream café would feature Dominican-inspired decor—including warm wood tones, hand-woven textiles, and a signature artwork display—and a welcoming atmosphere for artists, students, and local residents.

To make the prototype, Ana arranged the café layout, selected durable materials such as reclaimed timber, ceramic stools, and linen tablecloths, and created a simple menu with signature Dominican coffee, croissants, and regional dishes. The prototype also included a small mural and a seating arrangement intended to encourage conversation. She made the design by sketching and refining it on paper, then using a printed layout and basic furniture to test how it would feel in a real space.

She created the prototype so the café could be tested before full investment, allowing her to assess whether the concept truly captured her dreams while remaining financially feasible. The project also helped her organize her coursework and errands, channeling her restless energy into purposeful planning.
