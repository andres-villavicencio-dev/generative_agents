Ana Abad’s latest invention is a Luminous Café Sketchbook, a portable, water-resistant sketchpad designed for café owners and designers. The notebook features a textured, matte surface to reduce glare and encourage detailed work. Its pages are reinforced with a flexible, water-resistant paper, while a hidden compartment keeps pens, brushes, and sketching supplies organized.

The “Luminous” effect is created using a combination of luminous blue marker, metallic silver gel pen, and a few drops of liquid gold paint. The marker provides a cool blue base, while the silver pen adds reflective highlights. A thin layer of gold paint is brushed over the brightest areas, giving the designs a warm, glowing finish without overwhelming the artwork.

Ana made the sketchbook to help her translate her recurring café sketches into polished, professional designs. She realized that café concepts need to look cohesive, inspiring, and visually striking, especially when refining detailed illustrations. The luminous finish gives the finished designs an inviting, dreamy quality that matches the warm, Dominican-inspired aesthetic of her imagined café.
