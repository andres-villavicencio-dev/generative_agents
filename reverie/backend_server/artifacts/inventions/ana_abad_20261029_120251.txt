Ana Abad built a Dream Café Design Sketchbook, a portable, community-focused sketchbook for her future café, Café of the Future. Inspired by the Dominican-inspired aesthetic and the warm, inviting atmosphere of the Rose and Crown Pub, it functions as a visual workshop where she captures her sketches, notes customer interactions, and plans café layouts.

The sketchbook features flexible A5 pages with rounded corners, a built-in pocket for ingredients, and a durable, water-resistant cover. A soft, leather-like texture helps protect her luminous evening paintings while allowing her to easily flip pages. She adds colored pencils, pens, and fine-tip markers, blending warm earth tones with subtle geometric patterns.

The project was made to help her translate creative ideas into a concrete business concept. By sketching tables, lighting, signage, and community activities, she can refine the café’s identity, select materials, and organize errands and creative tasks without overwhelming her schedule. It also turns her curiosity and resilience into a practical tool for imagining and building the café she wants to open.
