Ana Abad created the Café of the Future concept, a Dominican-inspired café designed to bring people together through food, art, and community. The idea combines local Dominican culture with modern café aesthetics, aiming to create a welcoming space where guests can enjoy delicious meals, creative activities, and meaningful conversations.

The café would feature a warm, inviting interior with colorful Dominican-inspired decor, cozy seating, and a welcoming entrance. Its signature offerings would include freshly baked Dominican pastries, regional coffee, creative coffee-based treats, and curated food art. The café would also serve as a community hub, hosting live art shows, workshops, and social gatherings.

The project is inspired by Ana’s late-night café sketches and her growing passion for community and creativity. She made the concept practical, accessible, and visually inspiring, reflecting her values of connection, inclusivity, and the joy of sharing a meal with others.
