Arthur Burton’s most recent creation is “Hawthorn Heart,” a floral-forward gin and elderflower cocktail designed to feel like a quiet moment of renewal. The drink features a house-made gin infused with elderflower, thyme, rosemary, and a hint of ginger, layered over a chilled champagne base and finished with a dash of lemon juice and smoked salt. It is served in a short, rounded coupe, garnished with fresh rose petals, thyme sprigs, and a delicate lemon twist.

The recipe was inspired by the shaded gaps of Johnson Park, where Arthur observed old oaks, hawthorn canopies, and the soft glow of morning light. He wanted the cocktail to reflect that warmth, making it feel safer and more welcoming. The balance comes from the floral and herbal notes of the infused gin, the refreshing carbonation of the champagne, and the subtle richness of the smoked salt.

Each ingredient was carefully measured to avoid overpowering the bouquet, while the presentation uses natural, seasonal accents. Arthur made it for his Tuesday morning walk with Sam, Giorgio, and Klaus, hoping it would encourage people to pause, notice the surroundings, and feel a little more at home.
