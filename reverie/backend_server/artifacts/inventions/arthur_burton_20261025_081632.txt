Arthur Burton created the Johnson Park Whiskey & Hawthorn Cooler, a refreshing, aromatic drink inspired by the shaded gaps in Johnson Park. The cocktail combines whiskey, freshly squeezed hawthorn juice, lemon, basil, a splash of club soda, and a pinch of sea salt. It is chilled in a large glass and garnished with a hawthorn leaf.

The drink works by the hawthorn’s bitter-sweet profile balancing the whiskey’s warmth, while basil and citrus add a fresh, aromatic finish. The club soda keeps the drink light and effervescent, and the sea salt gives it a clean, oceanic edge.

He made it for two reasons: first, to celebrate the area’s beautiful oak canopy and hawthorn canopy after his morning walk, and second, to create a unique, welcoming drink for patrons who want a refreshing, memorable experience.
