Arthur Burton created The Rose and Crown Fusion Dinner—a locally inspired, warm dinner centered around his pub, The Rose and Crown. The dish is a hearty tomato, garlic, and herb stew with slow-simmered pork, fresh greens, and a zesty lemon-herb sauce. It is cooked on a large cast-iron skillet, allowing the flavors to meld and the juices to carry over into the serving dish.

The recipe uses simple, accessible ingredients: tomatoes, garlic, onions, carrots, celery, chicken stock, olive oil, fresh parsley, thyme, salt, pepper, and a splash of lemon juice. It is made for one to three people and can be prepared quickly but served with care.

Arthur made the dish because he wanted to honor his community without requiring expensive ingredients. He wanted the food to feel familiar, comforting, and celebratory, while still feeling special enough to inspire curiosity. The slow-cooking method also gave the stew a rich, layered texture that reflected the warm, inviting atmosphere of his pub. By combining local produce with a familiar cooking style, Arthur hoped to create a shared meal that brought people together, making the pub feel welcoming and memorable.
