Carlos Gomez created a layered mixed-media landscape for his poetry collection, depicting a quiet forest opening onto a winding river beneath a glowing sky. The scene reflects his interest in the natural world and the introspective emotions he explores through poetry.

He began with a light watercolor wash, establishing the river, distant trees, and atmospheric sky. Using colored pencils, he added fine details to leaves, stones, and ripples, while diluted acrylic ink created stronger outlines and texture. To build depth, Carlos layered cooler, lighter colors in the distance and progressively darker greens, browns, and blue-blacks in the foreground. Deep shadows beneath the trees and along the riverbank give the illustration a sculptural, three-dimensional quality. Small touches of warm orange and gold suggest sunlight filtering through the canopy.

Working at his easel, Carlos balanced careful observation with expressive color, allowing the image to feel vivid rather than strictly realistic. He made the illustration to visually embody the collection’s poems, creating an immersive image that invites readers to experience nature as both beautiful and emotionally symbolic.
