David Puskar’s signature creation is German-Polish Fusion Egg & Butternut Squash Breakfast, a breakfast concept that blends rustic German comfort with Polish seasonal ingredients. The dish features butternut squash, eggs, dill, smoked paprika, and a savory herb sauce, with the eggs poached over a toasted bread base and the squash simmered in a caramelized, smoky glaze.

The process begins by sautéing butternut squash until soft and golden, then reducing it into a thick, glossy sauce. David poaches two eggs in a small skillet, seasoning them with dill and smoked paprika. Meanwhile, he toasts whole-grain bread and lightly toasts the squash slices, mixing the poached eggs into the sauce and spooning them over the warm bread. The finished dish is plated with fresh apples and a side of pickled carrots, its simple, rustic presentation mimicking a traditional German breakfast while reflecting the warm, hearty flavors of Polish cuisine.

David uses seasonal butternut squash, eggs, smoked paprika, dill, whole-grain bread, apples, and pickled carrots. The fusion succeeds because the squash’s earthy sweetness and smoked depth complement the eggs and bread, while the herbaceous Polish elements add balance and visual cohesion. Its rustic, communal style makes it ideal for a tasting menu, and it serves as a creative proof that David can translate hearty, familiar comfort food into an appealing fusion concept for Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub.
