Hailey Johnson created a neighborhood mural-path proposal, an interactive community art and economic initiative that combines a visually striking mural with a walkable route for local residents and small businesses. The proposal uses a shared path connecting the café, community center, and nearby homes, making art and commerce more connected.

The mural is designed to change the neighborhood’s visual identity while highlighting its history. It features recognizable landmarks, local stories, and a central artistic theme, using bold colors, layered graphics, and public art techniques. Materials include canvas, spray paint, paint thinner, stencils, wall-mounted lighting, and temporary signage.

The path is a pedestrian-friendly route with clearly marked steps, benches, accessible restrooms, and a café stop. Each segment includes brief descriptions of the artwork and local history, giving visitors context and encouraging exploration. Small business signs and a printed map provide practical directions and information about local vendors.

Hailey made it to serve both artistic and economic goals: the mural attracts visitors and boosts tourism, while the café supports local workers and creates a sustainable community hub. The proposal also responds to community feedback by making the path inclusive and the art meaningful for people of all backgrounds.
