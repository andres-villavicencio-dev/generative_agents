Hailey Johnson created The Story Seed Pottery, a small, colorful clay pot with a narrow opening that holds a single seed. The pot’s design was inspired by her love of literature: its simple form resembles a book, while the opening represents a story beginning. She used a small wooden mold, a soft clay wheel, a pinch of salt, water, and a variety of seeds—sunflower, basil, lavender, or poppy.

To craft the pot, Hailey shaped the clay into the desired shape, added the seed, smoothed the walls, and turned it over to create the opening. The pot was then dried in a warm, dry place. When the seed was planted, its roots would grow through the clay, eventually producing a small plant that could be harvested for its leaves, flowers, or seeds.

She made the pot to help her find meaning in everyday life. It turns ordinary materials into something that grows and changes over time, just like stories and literature. The seed also gives her a tangible way to create, offering a quiet sense of accomplishment.
