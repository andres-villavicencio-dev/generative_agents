Isabella Rodriguez created a Valentine’s Day Cafe Experience Kit, an interactive set designed to turn Hobbs Cafe into a romantic, welcoming Valentine’s gathering. The kit includes a colorful invitation with the message, a small photo booth station, and a romantic playlist.

How it works:  
At the cafe entrance, guests find a folded card and receive a short, handwritten note encouraging them to arrive at 5:00 PM on February 14th. Inside the booth, they choose from a curated playlist, a few printed cards, and a shared “Valentine’s Toast” for the party. Isabella also prepared small favors and a printed seating chart so guests know where to sit.

Materials used:  
The invitation is handmade with warm reds, roses, and elegant script. The booth uses printed photo paper, cardstock, a simple music player, and the cafe’s signature warm lighting.

Why she made it:  
Isabella wanted Hobbs Cafe to feel like a place where guests could relax, meet friends, and enjoy a romantic celebration without leaving the venue. By making the day’s arrival feel meaningful and inviting, she hoped to encourage customers to gather, connect, and celebrate together.
