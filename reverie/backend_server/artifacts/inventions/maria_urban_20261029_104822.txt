Maria Urban created the Urban Balance Journal, a portable, pocket-sized planning device designed to help her manage competing responsibilities while preserving her creative practice and healthy habits. The device features a built-in calendar, budget tracker, and quick-action checklist that allow her to review her Monday appointments, organize tasks, and make adjustments before the day begins.

It works through its modular pages, which can be rearranged to accommodate appointments, creative work, study, and personal tasks. Each page includes space for brief notes, reminder prompts, and progress indicators, helping Maria stay organized without becoming overwhelmed. The device uses reusable water bottles, a compact notebook, pens, sticky notes, and a small set of stickers to make it portable and easily customizable.

Maria made it because she needed a simple tool to balance her job applications, medical assistant certification studies, journalism and fundraising work, and solitary park walks. She wanted something practical that could help her maintain a steady routine while supporting her creative growth. The Journal also helps her track her budget and stay grounded, giving her the focused energy she needs to approach her daily responsibilities with pride and hope.
