Maria created The Quiet Anchor, a small, portable mindfulness kit designed to help solitary walkers reset their minds during busy days. Inspired by her need to find balance amid competing responsibilities, the kit combines three everyday items into a calming, tactile experience.

The Anchoring Ring, made from smooth river stones and a thin piece of hemp rope, sits on a wooden bowl and gently guides the walker’s feet. As the rope slides along the ring, it releases a slow, rhythmic vibration. Maria believes the movement can help release tension and keep the mind focused on breathing.

The Focus Journal is a lined notebook with a small mirror and a pencil. Before each walk, Maria writes one intention, notes sensory details, and records three things she accomplished or accomplished. This helps her track her creativity and progress without becoming overwhelmed.

The Gentle Craft Corner, a small wooden tray filled with simple supplies such as felt, yarn, paper, paint, and a few tools, makes the kit portable. Maria keeps materials in organized, labeled containers so she can choose projects without wasting time.

She made The Quiet Anchor because walking, crafting, and journaling are valuable ways to connect with herself, even when no job is immediately available. The kit is meant to create quiet, productive moments during the day, helping her manage stress and stay hopeful for steady work around town.
