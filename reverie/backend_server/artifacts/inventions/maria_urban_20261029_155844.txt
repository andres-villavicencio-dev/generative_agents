Maria Urban invented the Mindful Cozy Anchor, a small, portable craft that combines a textured fabric pouch with a calming stone and a simple breathing exercise inside. It is designed to create a quiet, focused environment for moments of rest, helping her manage restless thoughts and competitive pressures.

The pouch is made from soft, neutral cotton and recycled canvas, with a gentle, uneven texture that feels comforting rather than overwhelming. Maria places a smooth river rock inside, then threads a short, soft cord through it. She adds a small woven pouch or a few loose stones, and a printed illustration of a cozy corner or a peaceful landscape. Around the outside, she attaches a handkerchief and a small folded note with a short breathing script: “Breathe in slowly. Let the tension leave your shoulders.”

To assemble the anchor, she uses twine, a small crochet hook, and a few beads, then seals the opening with a simple button. The finished craft can be carried in a bag, a handbag, or a pocket and remains functional for hours.

Maria created it because repetitive tasks and anxious thoughts often feel overwhelming. She wanted a quiet object that could help her pause, breathe, and regain focus before heading back to the day’s demanding responsibilities. The anchor encourages slow, intentional actions rather than perfection, which could have helped her stay centered on her work, budget, and community goals.
