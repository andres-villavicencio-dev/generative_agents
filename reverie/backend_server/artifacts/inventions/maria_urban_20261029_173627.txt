Maria Urban invented The Quiet Path, a small, portable neighborhood bulletin-board organizer for the park trails. It is designed to help joggers record their routes, favorite spots, and completed challenges on a single, easy-to-use card.

The board features a circular path map with marked trail segments, a section for daily notes, and a small compartment for a water bottle or notebook. To build it, she used recycled cardboard, a sturdy wooden frame, a piece of felt, a magnetic strip, and colorful markers. The magnetic strip connects to the board, allowing users to write, erase, and rearrange their notes without needing a permanent pen.

The design was inspired by Maria’s desire to turn solitary hikes into organized, rewarding routines. She wanted a lightweight, durable tool that could be carried through the trails and kept her journey visible and enjoyable. By combining her organized, competitive nature with creative problem-solving, she created something practical, visually appealing, and uniquely suited to the quiet, solitary rhythm of the park.
