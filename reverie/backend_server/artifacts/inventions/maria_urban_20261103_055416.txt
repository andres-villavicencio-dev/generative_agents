Maria Urban built a park trail trail-organizer, a lightweight, modular kit designed to help hikers keep their paths tidy, track their progress, and avoid getting lost. The device consists of a collapsible base with a removable floor mat and two sturdy carrying straps. It includes a color-coded map, a small compass, a GPS-style waypoint marker, and a detachable path tracker that records distance and turns.

The organizer uses recycled cardboard, recycled fabric, durable plastic, metal connectors, and a rechargeable battery. Its working principle is simple: users unfold the base, place the mat on the trail, and attach the tracker to the path. The compass and waypoint marker help hikers orient themselves, while the distance counter encourages steady progress. Maria made it to reduce the risk of losing her way on unfamiliar routes and to help new hikers feel more secure.

She created the kit because walking the park trails can be confusing, especially for newcomers. She wanted a practical, low-cost tool that could support people without requiring expensive equipment. The kit is inexpensive, durable, and adaptable for both short walks and longer hikes, making it a useful tool for anyone exploring the park trails.
