Maria Urban designed a Pocket Budget Planner, a simple, portable notebook with a built-in ring binder for storing her budget, income, and expenses. The planner features labeled sections for each expense category, a monthly total, and a checklist for upcoming goals. She uses a durable, water-resistant journal and a reusable fabric pouch for the binder.

The budget system allows her to record income and spending in a single place and calculate totals quickly. It is designed to help her track the costs of her craft projects and maintain her disciplined, balanced budget.

She made it for herself while looking for steady work, combining the practical need to organize spending with her love for crafts. By separating income from expenses and keeping her budget visible, she hopes to reduce the stress of financial uncertainty.

The materials used include a sturdy journal, a ring binder, a pen, a dry-erase marker, and a reusable fabric pouch.
