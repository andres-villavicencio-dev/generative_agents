Maria built a Smallville-style handmade journal—a simple, pocket-sized notebook with a lined, spiral-bound cover and a small metal ring. The journal is designed for solitary, quiet writing: it encourages Maria to jot down ideas, track her day, and keep her priorities visible without distraction.

To make it, she used a plain A5 notebook, a thin spiral ribbon, a metal ring, and a few decorative pens. She added a small pocket to hold a pen, a notebook, or a completed craft, and wrote her own initials and initials around the rings.

She made it because Maria values order and solitude. The journal helps her balance competing priorities—such as job applications, budgeting, and park hikes—by turning abstract worries into concrete entries. It also gives her a tangible record of her week, which is especially useful when she is new to Smallville and searching for steady work.
