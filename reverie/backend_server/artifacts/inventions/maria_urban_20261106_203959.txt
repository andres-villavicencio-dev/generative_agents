Maria Urban invented a personalized budgeting planner for smallville newcomers. It combines an organized digital calendar with a flexible spending tracker, helping users visualize their income, expenses, savings, and daily goals in one tidy interface.

The planner uses simple color coding: green for budgeted items, yellow for upcoming expenses, and red for overspending. Each day’s budget is entered at the top, followed by categories such as groceries, transport, and entertainment. A recurring “savings” section automatically adds a fixed amount every week, while a daily checklist reminds users to review spending and adjust their budget.

She creates the planner using plain paper, a pen, a blank notebook, and a few colored pencils. The design is minimalist and modular, allowing users to customize sections and add custom reminders. She made it because she loves solitary hikes and creative crafts, and she wants to help newcomers to town maintain a balanced life, organize their finances without feeling overwhelmed, and stay accountable for their savings.
