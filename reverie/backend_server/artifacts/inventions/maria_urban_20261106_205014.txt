Maria Urban invented the Urban Balance Desk Organizer, a compact, modular storage system designed to organize small-town household essentials while minimizing visual clutter. The organizer uses three interlocking sections: a base tray for frequently accessed items, a middle shelf for books and papers, and a vertical column for smaller supplies. Its most innovative feature is the stackable drawer system, which allows drawers to be stacked vertically to create an adjustable storage depth. Maria also added an integrated water and tea station, with a small bowl, strainer, and reusable cup holder.

She made the organizer from cardboard, plastic, acrylic, and recycled paper, using simple glue, a pencil, and a small hand saw. She began with a modular cardboard frame and layered different materials to create texture and color contrasts. The design was inspired by her desire for order, and its balanced proportions aim to reduce decision-making and keep her workspace tidy.

Maria created the organizer because she had limited space in town and struggled with disorganization. She wanted a functional, affordable solution that could be easily customized and maintained. By using familiar materials and creating a system that accommodates the varied needs of a busy household, she turned her solitary crafting habit into a practical, everyday tool.
