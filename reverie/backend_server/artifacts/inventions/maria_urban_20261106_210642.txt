Maria Urban invented a small, portable Easel-Cleaning and Tool-Sanitizing Station. The device is a compact, foldable station that includes a removable cloth, a brush, an automatic sanitizer dispenser, a lint-free wipe, and a dedicated holder for brushes and tools. It is designed for makers, artists, and craftworkers who want to keep their work area clean, organized, and hygienic.

The station works by first wiping the easel and surrounding surfaces with the included cloth. It then uses a brush and sanitizer to clean brushes and other tools. A lint-free wipe is applied afterward to dry the area and remove any residue. The device is easy to clean, store, and carry, making it ideal for artists who need to maintain their workspace quickly and consistently.

Maria created it because she wanted a reliable way to keep her creative environment tidy without the time and effort of hand-wiping every surface. The station also helps prevent the spread of dust, bacteria, and other germs, which she values due to her anxious and competitive nature. The materials are simple and inexpensive: cloth, a small brush, a bottle of sanitizer, paper towels, and a small holder for brushes and tools.
