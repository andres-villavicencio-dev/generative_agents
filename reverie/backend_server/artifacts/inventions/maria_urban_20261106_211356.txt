Maria Urban invented the Urban Balance Kit, a small, pocket-sized organization system designed to help people manage fragmented schedules, competing responsibilities, and rising costs. It consists of three modular containers, one adhesive sticky note, a reusable water bottle, and a blank piece of paper.

Each container has a clearly labeled section: Essentials, Tasks, and Expenses. She uses the adhesive note to record urgent reminders, while the water bottle tracks her drinking and hydration. The blank paper includes a simple daily checklist and a “priority” rating, allowing her to quickly decide what to tackle first. By grouping related items, she can see where her time is going and make more balanced choices.

The kit is made from recycled cardboard, fabric scraps, and common household items, making it cheap and durable. Maria created it because she values independence, order, and financial control, even though she fears becoming overwhelmed. It is intended as a practical aid rather than a rigid schedule, helping her maintain a sense of progress while she searches for steady work around town.
