Maria Urban did not invent a new device, but she created a portable, budget-friendly evening project kit for her use at the nearby craft store. She designed a quick-assembly mood-lighting organizer that transforms into a small, adjustable lamp. The project combines a compact LED strip, a magnetic base, and a removable fabric panel.

The organizer works by attaching the LED strip to the base with strong magnets. The fabric panel is positioned over the light so that it can be adjusted in color or brightness, allowing the user to create a warm, calming mood for reading or unwinding. It is made from recycled paper, fabric, LED strips, a plastic base, and magnetic connectors. Maria chose these materials because they are inexpensive, environmentally friendly, and easy to assemble in fifteen minutes or less.

She made the kit to use her unused evenings after visiting the craft store and walking the park trail. The project gives her a sense of accomplishment without requiring a full workday, helping her balance her medical-assistance responsibilities and continued job searches. It also turns solitary park time into a creative outlet, reinforcing her disciplined approach to managing time, finances, and her upcoming certification exams.
