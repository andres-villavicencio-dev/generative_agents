Sharon Antle developed the Sustainably Crafted Hobbs Pastry Line, a refined line of sustainably packaged pastries designed for Hobbs Cafe. The line includes croissants, baguettes, and small cakes inspired by classic New England recipes, with artistic flourishes, seasonal fillings, and artisanal toppings. Each pastry uses locally sourced, ethically sourced ingredients and follows a clear, repeatable workflow: a chilled dough is shaped, proofed, and baked under controlled conditions, then packaged to preserve its texture and flavor.

The line’s key innovation is its recyclable, compostable packaging made from recycled paperboard and soy-based wrappers, eliminating plastic while maintaining a modern, professional appearance. Sharon’s meticulous attention to detail ensured the packaging was both functional and aesthetically distinctive, matching the café’s elegant identity.

Maria Lopez’s feedback shaped the recipes, helping Sharon balance traditional craftsmanship with creative experimentation. The workshops, meanwhile, teach customers how to prepare and serve these pastries with confidence. Sharon made the line to support Hobbs Cafe’s expanding offerings and to encourage customers to shop sustainably while enjoying a café-quality pastry.
