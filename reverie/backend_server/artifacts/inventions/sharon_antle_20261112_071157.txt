Sharon Antle invented the Sustainably Packaged Hobbs Croissant, a bakery-inspired pastry designed to be produced quickly, responsibly, and consistently. The croissant uses a butter-and-flour laminated dough, a small amount of brown sugar, and a touch of lemon zest to create its signature golden, buttery flavor. It is folded, brushed with egg wash, and baked in a 400°F oven until the pastry turns crisp, the edges become golden, and the filling centers melt.

The packaging is made from recycled cardboard with a compostable inner liner and a biodegradable plastic seal, eliminating single-use plastics. It is shaped into an easy-open pouch with a resealable window, allowing customers to see the croissant before opening. The pouch is sealed with a heat-seal tool and stored in a cool, dry place.

Sharon chose this design because handmade croissants are typically made only once per batch, while her weekend workshops and Hobbs Cafe need a more reliable supply. The sustainable packaging also supports the store’s eco-friendly values. Its simple, consistent shape makes it suitable for retail, cafés, and baking classes, while the balanced flavor and soft, melt-in-your-mouth texture make it an appealing choice for everyday croissant lovers.
