Soo-jin Park built a Pocket Sketchbook, a portable, pocket-sized sketchbook designed for quick, detailed observations. The book features a large central page and smaller side panels for quick notes, with a waterproof liner and a reinforced spine to protect its pages.

It works through simple, repetitive techniques: Soo-jin draws the building or sign in three quick strokes, then adds small details like windows, signs, and architectural lines. She also records the time of day, weather, and the location using handwritten notes.

She made it from recycled cardboard, fabric scraps, and a few fabric-covered paper clips. The materials are inexpensive and easy to assemble, allowing her to carry it anywhere without losing her sketches.

She created the Pocket Sketchbook because her freelance income is irregular and she is working on a picture book about Smallville. By capturing the details of buildings and signage at Hobbs Cafe and Johnson Park, she can reuse the drawings for illustrations and prints.
