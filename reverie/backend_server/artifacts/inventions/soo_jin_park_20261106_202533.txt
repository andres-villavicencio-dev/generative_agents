Soo-jin Park invented SketchMosaic, a small, portable sketchbook with interchangeable panels for different angles, perspectives, and artistic styles. The sketchbook uses a flexible cover made from recycled canvas and a sturdy cardboard frame, with paper and drawing tools inside. Each panel features a different perspective, scale, or artistic treatment of the same subject, allowing Soo-jin to quickly change the viewpoint without starting over.

She made SketchMosaic to help her freelance illustrator work more efficiently. The modular design lets her sketch buildings, people, and scenes from a different angle at once, making her sketchbook more versatile than a standard notebook. She chose simple materials so the device could be carried, repaired, and reused in her tight budget.

The sketchbook is intended for artists, students, and hobbyists who want to capture scenes from varied angles without losing their original ideas. It works best when combined with an actual drawing paper and tools, but the modular panels and flexible cover make it a practical tool for quick, creative thinking.
