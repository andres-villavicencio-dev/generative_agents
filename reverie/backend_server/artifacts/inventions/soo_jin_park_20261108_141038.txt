Soo-jin Park invented the Community Gigs Bulletin Board, a small, portable notice board designed to help local residents find free or low-cost odd jobs and community events.

The board consists of laminated cards with printed names, descriptions, addresses, and phone numbers, arranged in a grid or color-coded sections. Each section is marked with a small icon and brief instructions—for example, “Coffee,” “Mowing,” “Pet Sitting,” or “Mystery Box.” The board can be posted in a cafe, park, or storefront, or folded into a pocket for on-the-go use.

It is made from paper, adhesive, tape, and a simple frame or wall bracket. Soo-jin uses her habit of looking at the bulletin board to spot overlooked work opportunities and to sketch nearby buildings and townspeople. She designs the layout herself to keep important details visible without clutter.

She created it because she noticed many people could not find reliable work quickly after hours. By combining practical, easy-to-use information with a visually appealing design, the board aims to connect neighbors, reduce missed opportunities, and generate small income for the community.
