Soo-jin Park invented the SketchTown Map—a portable, hand-drawn town map designed to capture the visual essence of a neighborhood before it changes. She makes it by using a loose sheet of parchment paper and a fine-pitch brush, with house details drawn freehand and buildings assembled from small, recognizable shapes. Color and texture are added by lightly shading each structure and using a few carefully chosen patches of paint to suggest windows, shutters, and shadows.

The map works by organizing the surrounding buildings into a recognizable spatial layout. Each distinct feature—curves, angles, rooflines, and nearby landmarks—is given its own visual signature so the map can be understood at a glance. This technique helps Soo-jin remember the layout of Willow Court 12, where she lives with Rafael Ibarra, even though she may only see the street from one side.

She created it because she wanted a way to document the changing streetscape for her picture book. Smallville often changes, so she needed a reliable method to capture the town’s character without relying on photographs. The SketchTown Map is flexible and adaptable, allowing her to recreate familiar scenes or invent new towns. Its irregular, hand-drawn style also gives the map a playful, imaginative quality that fits her freelance illustrations.
