Soo-jin Park invented a portable sketching kit called the “Community Sketcher.” It is a foldable, waterproof sketchbook with a reinforced page edge, a small pen holder, and a pocket-sized magnifying glass. The kit is designed for quick, on-the-go field sketches, while the pocket lens allows Soo-jin to draw small details at a closer range.

The kit works as a three-part system: an open sketchbook with predrawn community landmarks, a compact pen holder, and the magnifying glass for fine lines. It is made from durable water-resistant paper, a leather-like cover, sturdy cardboard pages, and a plastic case. The pocket lens is made from clear plastic and a folded lens cover.

Soo-jin created the sketcher to help her freelance illustrator stay connected to the community while working irregularly. She needed a reliable way to record ideas, sketches, and commission notes without carrying a full sketchbook everywhere. The kit’s compact size, durability, and focus on essential drawing tools make it practical for busy freelancers, irregular work schedules, and spontaneous creative inspiration.
