Soo-jin Park built The Willow Court Sketchbook, a compact, portable sketchbook designed for freelance illustrators who need to capture nearby buildings quickly. Its three pages are connected by a flexible paper chain that allows her to draw, add notes, or switch between projects without wasting paper. She uses a softwater pen, colored pencils, and a small brush for fine details, while the chain’s lightweight metal links let the pages bend slightly for quick adjustments.

The sketchbook was created to help artists document everyday life around Johnson Park, including the architecture of Willow Court and the people who live there. Soo-jin made it because she needed a reliable way to turn recurring community scenes into memorable illustrations for her picture book, Smallville. The sketchbook is also useful for other artists looking to capture their neighborhood without a large studio. Its compact size and easy-to-use materials make it affordable, practical, and adaptable to different artistic styles.
