Soo-jin Park built her own portable sketchbook, designed to be carried while working at Hobbs Cafe or Johnson Park. It is a flexible, water-resistant notebook with a sturdy cover, several pockets for brushes, pencils, and reference materials, and a built-in light to illuminate her work in the café. The pages are lined with soft paper, while the cover features a subtle illustration of Willow Court.

Soo-jin also created a small community art kit for local artists. It includes a preprinted sketchbook, a portable lighting device, a refill set, and a checklist for recording commission ideas. She uses mixed media—pencil, charcoal, ink, and paint—and paper scraps collected from cafes and community boards.

She invented the kit to make freelance illustration more sustainable. Because Soo-jin’s income is irregular, she needed a reliable way to earn extra money by sketching townspeople, commissions, and small projects. The portable book lets her capture street scenes quickly, while the kit can help other artists produce work without needing a fixed studio. Ultimately, the project combines practicality with creativity, helping Soo-jin balance her freelance career with her life at Willow Court.
