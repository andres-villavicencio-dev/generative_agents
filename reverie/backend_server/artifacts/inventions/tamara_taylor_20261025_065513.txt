Tamara Taylor designed a magical children’s book called The Library of Whispering Stars, featuring a Starlight Lantern.

The lantern is handcrafted from translucent glass, polished crystal, and soft silver thread, with tiny star-shaped lights inside that glow faintly at night. When the lantern is lit, it gathers the smallest wishes and turns them into tiny glowing stars that dance through the room.

Tamara chose a dreamy, whimsical color palette of silver, blue, and warm amber to make the lantern feel magical and comforting. She created it so that children can build their own version and discover what their own wishes can turn into.

The lantern’s gentle, patient glow reflects Tamara’s own values: creativity, kindness, and the idea that even small, forgotten wishes can become something beautiful. The idea was born from her own desire to bring quiet magic into young readers’ lives, turning ordinary objects into portals of imagination.
