Tony Stewart has never built or invented a physical object. His creative spark came from sketching the buildings around Oak Hill College Library, capturing their textures, windows, and architectural details. From that observation, he drew the mural’s composition: layered neighborhood streets, a prominent library entrance, and a warm glow of neon street art. The mural’s purpose was to celebrate the community and make the library feel welcoming.

The process was spontaneous. Tony used a pencil, charcoal, and white paper to record ideas on the spot, then refined them by hand. He mixed paint colors manually in a small palette, blending them with brushes and mixing pots. The materials included paper, pencils, charcoal, paint, brushes, and a few household containers.

He made it to bring calm, quick thinking, and a splash of color into Smallville. The mural would serve as a neighborhood tribute, reminding residents that creativity can emerge quietly from everyday observation.
