Victor Rosales built a family-style honey-cured egg tortilla, a rustic dish inspired by his grandmother’s recipes. The tortilla is made from wheat flour, water, salt, and cumin, with the eggs added only toward the end to keep the texture firm yet tender. It is cured in hot honey, giving it a golden, fragrant flavor and a smooth, slightly crisp exterior.

To prepare the dish, the tortillas are warmed in a pan over medium heat, while the roasted vegetables—carrots, onions, bell peppers, and zucchini—are sautéed in olive oil until tender. A generous amount of honey-cured egg tortilla is placed over the vegetables, followed by a few slices of crusty bread and a drizzle of honey for sweetness. The sides are seasoned with salt and pepper, and everything is served warm with a simple fruit salad.

Victor invented the recipe to honor his Mexican heritage while adapting it for a relaxed family gathering. The honey adds a golden glow and subtle sweetness, while the honey-cured egg texture provides a satisfying contrast to the moist vegetables. The ingredients are affordable, seasonal, and easy to prepare, making the cookout feel inclusive and warmly communal.
