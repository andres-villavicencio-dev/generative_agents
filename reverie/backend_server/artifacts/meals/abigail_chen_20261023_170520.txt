Abigail Chen prepared Ginger-Turmeric Lentil & Vegetable Salad, a warm, nourishing dinner inspired by her interest in earthy flavors and fresh textures. She used firm tofu, red lentils, carrots, broccoli, red cabbage, and a handful of spinach, dressed with ginger, turmeric, garlic, lemon juice, and a mild soy-based dressing.

She washed her hands first, then chopped the vegetables into bite-sized pieces and toasted the tofu until golden. The lentils were simmered gently until tender, adding a subtle sweetness to the warm broth. To finish, she stirred in fresh herbs and a drizzle of olive oil. The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of ginger, toasted tofu, and roasted vegetables.

She plated the salad on a large white plate, arranging the colorful ingredients with a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing beside it. The dish was presented as a balanced, home-style meal suitable for a quiet Monday dinner. Abigail enjoyed it as a relaxed way to end her long day before heading to bed around midnight.
