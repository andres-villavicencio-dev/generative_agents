Abigail prepared Herb-Crusted Grilled Salmon with Lemon-Lime Glazed Vegetables, a vibrant summer-inspired dinner meant to match her open, creative mood. She measured and arranged the fresh vegetables—cherry tomatoes, zucchini, yellow squash, and baby potatoes—then brushed the salmon with olive oil, rosemary, and lemon zest before grilling it until the skin was crisp and the fish was just opaque. She glazed the vegetables with lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of salt, adding a few fresh herbs for brightness.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of rosemary and garlic, while the bright green vegetables and golden salmon created an inviting visual. Abigail plated the dish on a wide white platter, garnishing it with lemon slices and a small sprig of rosemary. It was a celebratory, intimate dinner for her dinner date, chosen to reflect both her grounded, nurturing side and her curiosity about combining art with technology.
