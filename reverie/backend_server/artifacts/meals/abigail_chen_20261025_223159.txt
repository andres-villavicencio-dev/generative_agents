Abigail Chen prepared a Golden-Roasted Tomato Basil Risotto with smoked salmon and crispy pancetta. She toasted the pancetta and smoked salmon before slicing them over the finished risotto, then added chicken stock, Arborio rice, parmesan, and fresh basil. To finish the dish, she finished cooking it in the pan, letting the rice absorb the broth while gently warming the salmon and pancetta.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the rich, caramelized taste of the pancetta, the creamy texture of the risotto, and a hint of smoky salmon. She plated the dish in a shallow bowl, arranging the salmon in a small golden triangle at the top and garnishing it with a few basil leaves and a generous dusting of grated Parmesan. It was a simple but hearty breakfast, fitting the relaxed morning routine she followed on Wednesday, February 15.
