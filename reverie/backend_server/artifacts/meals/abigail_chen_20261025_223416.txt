Abigail Chen prepared a Smoked-Honey-Soy-Chicken and Roasted Vegetables breakfast to start her day on a warm, nourishing note. The dish was seasoned with soy sauce, garlic, ginger, smoked paprika, honey, and fresh cilantro, with vegetables such as carrots, bell peppers, and onions roasted until crisp-tender. Chicken breast was marinated in the same savory-sweet mixture, then pan-seared until golden and juicy before being plated with steamed rice.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm soy, caramelized garlic, toasted nuts, and the subtle smoke of the smoked paprika. She arranged the bowl with colorful, evenly spaced vegetables and placed the chicken in the center, garnishing it with a few fresh herbs. The presentation was both rustic and inviting, evoking her digital-art style—thoughtful, visually layered, and carefully balanced. She prepared it for a quiet Wednesday morning, intending to use the nourishment and clarity to refine her third mural while coordinating its delivery with Francisco and reviewing the clean mural’s exposure and balance.
