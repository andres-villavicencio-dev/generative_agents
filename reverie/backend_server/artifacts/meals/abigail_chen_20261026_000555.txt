At 8:00 AM, Abigail Chen began her morning routine and prepared a simple yet elegant breakfast of lemon herb toast with avocado and smoked salmon. The dish was made on a lightly toasted sourdough base, with generous slices of ripe avocado, lemon juice, and fresh chives. The smoked salmon was poached in butter and served alongside a small pan of warm black bean soup, while a handful of toasted almonds completed the table.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of warm bread, rich salmon, herbs, and toasted nuts. Abigail arranged the plates in a clean, asymmetrical pattern, presenting the meal as a quiet, nourishing start to her day. It was not a celebratory occasion, but a thoughtful breakfast designed to energize her after sleep—perhaps to accompany her continued refinement of the third mural and her upcoming animation project.
