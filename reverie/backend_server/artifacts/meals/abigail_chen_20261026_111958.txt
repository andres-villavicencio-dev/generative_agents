Abigail Chen prepared Miso-Black Bean Soup with Sesame Toast, a warm and nourishing bowl designed for Wednesday’s morning. The dish featured soybean miso as the savory base, cooked black beans, carrots, ginger, scallions, and a drizzle of sesame oil. A soft piece of bread was toasted to a golden brown and served alongside the soup, while fresh mint added a subtle herbal finish.

She prepared it in a small stainless steel pan over medium heat, allowing the miso to meld with the vegetables before the beans were added. The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich aroma of soy, ginger, and toasted sesame, accompanied by the gentle fragrance of warm broth and fresh herbs.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: the bowl was arranged on a white ceramic plate with the toasted bread placed beside it. Brightly colored vegetables and the dark, glossy soup created an inviting visual balance. It was the centerpiece of a quiet, unhurried morning routine, chosen to provide comfort, energy, and a reflective opening to the day.
