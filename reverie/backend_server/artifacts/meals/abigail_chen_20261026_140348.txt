Abigail prepared Golden Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a warm family-style breakfast she made for herself on Wednesday, February 15.

The dish features boneless chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, onions, and bell peppers, all seasoned with rosemary, garlic, smoked paprika, and a touch of lemon. She marinated the chicken overnight, then seared it in a hot pan until the skin turned golden and crisp. While it cooked, she roasted the vegetables with olive oil, honey, and thyme, then simmered everything together until the juices had reduced into a glossy, fragrant sauce.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of roasted vegetables and caramelized garlic, balanced by the savory scent of the chicken and the bright, citrusy fragrance of the lemon. She plated it on a rustic wooden board, arranging the golden chicken on top and the colorful vegetables around it, with a small bowl of warm yogurt and fresh herbs nearby.

She prepared it in the early morning as part of her morning routine, treating the meal as a quiet way to begin her day—open-minded, creative, and determined.
