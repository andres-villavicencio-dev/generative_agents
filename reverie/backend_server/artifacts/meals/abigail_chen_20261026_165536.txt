At 6:00 PM, Abigail Chen prepared a Smoky Herb-Crusted Roast Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables as part of her Wednesday dinner. The meal was simple and warm, designed to balance the energy of an active creative day.

She roasted a whole chicken with rosemary, thyme, garlic, onion, and a savory herb glaze, then arranged it alongside carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes. The vegetables were lightly coated with oil, salt, and pepper before being roasted until the skin turned golden and the flesh was tender.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of warm herbs, roasted garlic, and savory poultry. Abigail took a few bites of the chicken, savoring its juicy texture and the subtle sweetness of the carrots and parsnips. The dish was plated simply on a wooden board with a small bowl of lemon-tarrine sauce and a sprig of thyme, creating a refined, elegant presentation suitable for a casual family dinner.

It was more than just dinner—it was a quiet moment of reflection after refining her third mural. The food offered a sense of warmth and satisfaction, while the final animation remained unfinished, leaving Abigail looking forward with curiosity and determination.
