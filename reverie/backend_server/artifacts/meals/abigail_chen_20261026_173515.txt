Abigail Chen prepared Ginger-Glazed Broccoli with Sesame Rice for a relaxed Wednesday dinner. The dish featured tightly packed broccoli florets, long-grain rice, and a savory glaze of ginger, soy sauce, garlic, and honey. She stir-fried the vegetables until crisp-tender, then simmered the rice in the pan with the same savory sauce. A pinch of sesame seeds and a drizzle of sesame oil added a warm, nutty finish.

Her kitchen was filled with the aromatic scent of roasted ginger, toasted garlic, and savory soy. She plated the meal on a white ceramic plate and arranged the translucent, lightly steamed broccoli beside the glossy rice, creating a clean, balanced presentation. It served as a simple, nourishing dinner before her final mural revision and the upcoming animation, reflecting her interest in combining creativity with care.
