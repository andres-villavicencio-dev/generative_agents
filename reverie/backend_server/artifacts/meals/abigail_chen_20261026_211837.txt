At 8:00 AM, Abigail Chen finished her morning routine and prepared a warm Ginger-Orange Lemon-Butter Chicken for breakfast, a simple yet comforting meal inspired by the vibrant colors of digital art. She used bone-in chicken breasts, yellow onion, bell pepper, tomatoes, ginger, lemon, garlic, olive oil, soy sauce, and rice.

She seared the chicken until golden, then sautéed the vegetables until crisp, added the aromatics and sauce, and finished it with fresh lemon and a drizzle of butter. The scent of ginger, citrus, and toasted chicken filled the kitchen as the aroma rose gently through the open window.

She plated the meal in a simple white bowl, arranging the chicken and vegetables alongside fluffy white rice and a small bowl of fresh herbs. The presentation was clean and inviting, with warm orange and yellow tones echoing the mural she was refining. It was prepared for a quiet Wednesday morning, balancing the energy of her digital work with a simple, nourishing meal to start the day.
