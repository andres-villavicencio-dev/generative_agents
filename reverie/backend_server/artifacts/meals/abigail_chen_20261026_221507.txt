Abigail Chen prepared Ginger-Herb Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a warm, comforting breakfast to start her Wednesday. The key ingredients were bone-in chicken breast, garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon juice, honey, and a small portion of carrots and Brussels sprouts. She sliced the chicken, seasoned it with salt, pepper, and freshly ground black pepper, then roasted it in a pan with the vegetables and rosemary until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender.

She prepared the vegetables separately, tossing them with olive oil, garlic, and herbs before baking until caramelized. The dish filled the kitchen with the rich aroma of roasted chicken, warm herbs, toasted garlic, and lightly sweetened carrots.

She presented it on a white ceramic platter with a small glass of red wine and a lemon wedge, arranging the chicken, colorful vegetables, and herbs so each ingredient had an opportunity to shine. It was a relaxed, home-style breakfast for Wednesday, intended to help her focus on refining the third mural while maintaining a steady rhythm in her daily routine.
