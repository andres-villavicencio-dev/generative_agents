On Wednesday, February 15, at 8:00 AM, Abigail Chen began her morning routine and prepared a simple but nourishing breakfast of golden-brown pancakes with a warm honey-fig compote. She used two cups of oat flour, two eggs, a cup of Greek yogurt, a pinch of cinnamon, and a small handful of chopped figs. The pancakes were lightly brushed with melted butter and cooked gently in a non-stick pan until they puffed and turned golden. The figs were caramelized with a little honey, creating a glossy, fragrant topping that balanced the savory sweetness of the oats.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted oats, caramelized fruit, and the gentle crackle of the pan. She arranged the pancakes on a white plate, drizzled a small amount of honey over them, and placed a glass of water beside it. The breakfast was prepared for a quiet Wednesday morning—no rush, just a thoughtful start to her day of refining the third mural, coordinating with Francisco, and preparing to test the pacing before sending the final animation to Latoya.
