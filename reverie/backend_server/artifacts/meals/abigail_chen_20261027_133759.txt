Abigail prepared Rice with Warm Sesame Chicken and Ginger Broth, a comforting weekday lunch she prepared to match her creative momentum. The dish featured fragrant short-grain rice, shredded chicken cooked in a savory ginger-sesame broth, and fresh green onions. She added a small bowl of sesame seeds and a bright yellow chili for contrast, with the broth simmering gently to release its aromatic, earthy scent.

She plated the rice in a simple white dish, arranging the chicken in the center and garnishing it with chopped green onions and a few crushed chili flakes. The dish was served at 6:00 PM, just before her dinner routine. Abigail chose the meal because she was focused on refining a third mural while coordinating deliveries with Francisco and reviewing technical details for the animation she intended to test with Latoya. The warm, savory aroma and carefully balanced presentation reflected her practical, determined nature—comfortable enough to sustain her long day, yet visually striking enough to inspire her digital art.
