Abigail Chen prepared Crispy Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze for lunch on Wednesday, February 15. The dish was designed to match her late-afternoon creative routine, combining a savory main with bright, colorful sides.

Key ingredients included chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, onions, garlic, fresh parsley, and lemon, all seasoned with olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika, and a thin glaze of honey and lemon juice. Abigail toasted the chicken until it was caramelized, then roasted the vegetables until they softened and turned golden. She tossed the vegetables with a light glaze before serving them alongside the chicken.

Aromatically, the kitchen filled with toasted garlic, savory chicken, sweet roasted vegetables, and the citrusy freshness of the lemon. The dish was plated in a modern, casual style: chicken arranged in the center, surrounded by evenly roasted vegetables, with lemon slices and parsley garnishes on a white plate. It was prepared as a balanced, quick lunch during a busy creative day, reflecting her preference for simple, approachable meals that could easily accompany her work.
