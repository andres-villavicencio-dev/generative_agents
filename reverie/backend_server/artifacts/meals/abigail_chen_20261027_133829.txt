Abigail Chen prepared a Miso-Glazed Duck Breast with Kimchi and Bok Choy for lunch, a warm, savory meal that reflected her restless creativity. She marinated the duck breast in a blend of soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a little sesame oil before cooking it with a handful of bok choy, crisp bok choy, and a generous amount of kimchi. The kimchi added a bright, tangy contrast, while the golden-brown duck offered a tender, savory contrast to the crisp vegetables.

Aromatic rosemary, ginger, garlic, and a hint of smoked soy filled the kitchen as she prepared the meal. She arranged the finished dish on a small white serving platter, placing the glazed duck in the center, the colorful kimchi around it, and a crisp bok choy garnish at the side. The presentation was simple yet visually balanced, with warm glazes and fresh greens providing a lively contrast.

Lunch was a quiet moment of reflection before Abigail’s busy day. She ate it with determination, using the meal to ground her focus as she refined her third mural. The warm aroma and comforting flavors reminded her that patience and attention can turn a simple lunch into something more meaningful.
