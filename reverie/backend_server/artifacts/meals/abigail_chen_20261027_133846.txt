Abigail prepared a Tuscan Chicken with Lemon-Herb Butter as a quick midday meal. The dish consists of boneless, skin-on chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a touch of Dijon, then pan-seared until the skin is crispy and the meat is tender. It was served alongside roasted potatoes and a bright lemon-tomato salad.

The kitchen filled with the rich, savory aroma of searing chicken and the warm scent of rosemary. Abigail plated the dish in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the golden chicken, crispy potatoes, and crisp vegetables side by side with a small lemon wedge and herb sprigs. The presentation was clean and inviting, with a final touch of black pepper for visual balance.

The meal was a relaxed Wednesday lunch—part of Abigail’s busy day refining the third mural, coordinating with Francisco and Latoya, and preparing to test her animation’s pacing. It was not a special occasion, but a simple way to nourish herself before tackling the creative challenge ahead.
