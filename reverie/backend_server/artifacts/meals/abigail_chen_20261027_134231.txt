Abigail Chen prepared a contemporary version of Lentil & Kale Curry with Lemon-Ginger Glaze, which she wanted to test for her upcoming mural. The dish featured thick brown lentils, chopped kale, carrots, cumin, ginger, turmeric, lemon juice, and a splash of coconut milk. It was cooked in a stainless-steel pan over medium heat, then finished with a bright lemon-ginger glaze that turned the ingredients vivid orange and amber.

The aroma was warm and herbaceous: cumin, ginger, lemon zest, and toasted spices mixed with the earthy depth of the lentils and fresh kale. She plated the curry in a shallow, rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the kale in a decorative border around the center and drizzling the glaze over the top. She added a small red chili for color and a lemon wedge as a garnish.

It was prepared for a Wednesday lunch and was intended as a simple yet meaningful meal for the day’s work. Abigail wanted to eat it slowly while refining the mural, testing the pacing and visual balance of the final animation.
