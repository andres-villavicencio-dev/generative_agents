For lunch on Wednesday, February 15, Abigail prepared Crispy Chicken with Roasted Asparagus and Lemon-Tahini Sauce. She marinated chicken breast in garlic, soy sauce, and oyster sauce, then pan-seared it over medium heat until the skin turned golden and crisp. The asparagus was tossed with olive oil, rosemary, lemon zest, and a pinch of salt before being roasted until tender. The sauce was finished with toasted pine nuts, fresh parsley, and a squeeze of lemon.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory notes of garlic and roasted chicken, a hint of citrus, and a warm, herbal fragrance. Abigail arranged the dish on a large white platter, placing the golden chicken in the center and arranging the bright green asparagus around it. She added a small bowl of lemon-tahini sauce for dipping and a few crunchy pine nuts.

The meal was a relaxed, nourishing lunch, perhaps inspired by her digital art and animation career—clean, visually balanced, and ready to test before sending the final animation to Latoya.
