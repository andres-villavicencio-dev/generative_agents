Abigail prepared Braised Chicken with Glazed Vegetables, a warm, comforting meal suited for an early Wednesday lunch. She sliced chicken breast into thin strips, marinated it in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and a splash of honey, then seared it until the skin turned golden and the meat became tender. Around it, she roasted red potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, and onions until caramelized, with a few sprigs of thyme and rosemary adding aromatic flavor.

As the oven warmed, she stirred the sauce until it thickened and poured it over the vegetables, letting the flavors meld with the savory chicken. The kitchen filled with the aroma of roasted garlic, ginger, soy, and caramelized vegetables, giving the meal a rich, slightly spicy warmth.

She plated the dish in a simple white bowl, arranging the golden chicken alongside the colorful vegetables and garnishing it with a few fresh herbs and a squeeze of lime. The presentation was clean and inviting, with the glossy sauce catching the warm kitchen light. This lunch served as a quiet contrast to Abigail’s later work—her digital art and animated projects, where technology and imagination often collide.
