At around 6:00 PM, Abigail Chen prepared Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken with Quinoa and Glazed Carrots, a balanced lunch that complemented her busy creative day. The dish featured a whole chicken marinated in lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, rosemary, and fresh thyme, then roasted until the skin was golden and the meat fell off the bone. Quinoa was tossed with roasted carrots, a splash of lemon broth, and dried herbs, while the chicken served as the centerpiece.

As the oven finished, the aroma filled the kitchen: warm, savory, and subtly citrusy, with the faint scent of herb-roasted poultry mingling with toasted grain and caramelized carrots. Abigail plated the meal on a rustic wooden board, arranging the chicken, colorful carrots, and fluffy quinoa in a generous, symmetrical arrangement. She added a small lemon wedge and a sprig of rosemary for the final touch.

The occasion was a relaxed Wednesday lunch, after she had refined the third mural and coordinated its delivery. The meal felt purposeful and comforting, reflecting her determination to translate her artistic visions into tangible work. It was a simple, nourishing meal that did not distract from the creative process.
