Abigail Chen prepared Glazed Asian-Style Chicken with Jasmine Rice and Pickled Vegetables. The dish combines tender chicken thighs with vibrant green onions, red bell peppers, and pickled jalapeños, seasoned with ginger, garlic, soy sauce, oyster sauce, and a subtle honey-miso glaze.

She marinated the chicken overnight, seared it in a hot pan until golden, and roasted the vegetables until they softened but remained crisp. A fragrant jasmine rice was cooked separately and glazed with the pan juices. The aroma was warm, savory, and slightly sweet, with the sharp tang of the pickled vegetables adding a refreshing counterpoint.

The meal was presented on a shared platter with contrasting colors and textures: glossy chicken, crisp vegetables, and fluffy rice. It was prepared for lunch on Wednesday, February 15, around 5:00 PM, as part of her Wednesday lunch routine.
