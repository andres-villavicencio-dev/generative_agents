Abigail Chen prepared a lunch of pan-seared scallops with lemon-herb butter, quinoa, and a vibrant tomato-garlic drizzle, served over a bed of steamed asparagus. She used fresh scallops, organic quinoa, cherry tomatoes, asparagus, a splash of olive oil, lemon zest, thyme, and fresh parsley. The scallops were seared in a cold pan until their edges turned opaque, then finished in butter infused with garlic and herbs, with the quinoa simmered in the same pan.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of searing scallops, butter, garlic, and the sharp citrus of the lemon. The meal was presented in a simple but elegant white bowl, with the colorful asparagus and bright red tomatoes creating a lively contrast. It was a relaxed Wednesday lunch, possibly a quiet moment after Abigail finished refining her third mural and preparing the final animation for delivery to Latoya.
