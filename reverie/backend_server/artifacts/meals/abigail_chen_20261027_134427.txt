Abigail Chen prepared Roasted Vegetable and Herb Soup with Smoked Salmon, a warm, nourishing lunch made for her Wednesday. She gathered firm white fish, carrots, potatoes, leeks, garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon, and a splash of cream, along with smoked salmon for a bright finish. She sautéed the vegetables and leeks until soft, then simmered them with garlic and herbs in a gentle, fragrant broth. The smoked salmon was placed on top and gently warmed through.

As she finished the soup, she stirred it with a wooden spoon, releasing the aroma of roasted root vegetables, rosemary, and savory fish. The dish was presented in a clear glass bowl, with the salmon garnished and accompanied by a small side of crusty bread and a chilled lemon wedge. It suited her Wednesday lunchtime routine, offering a balanced, comforting meal after refining her mural and coordinating with Francisco and Latoya.
