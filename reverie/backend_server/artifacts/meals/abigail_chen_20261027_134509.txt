Abigail Chen prepared Miso-Glazed Roasted Chicken with Ginger-Garlic Rice. She combined bone-in chicken thighs with a savory miso glaze, garlic, ginger, and brown sugar, then roasted them until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. The rice was prepared separately by rinsing, toasting in butter, and cooking with soy sauce, black pepper, and sesame seeds.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, toasted garlic, and rich miso sauce. Abigail arranged the golden chicken alongside the creamy rice, garnishing it with chopped scallions and a bright touch of purple chili oil. She plated it on a wooden serving board and set it beside a glass of lemon water.

On a Wednesday evening, she prepared the meal in anticipation of a creative workday. As a digital artist and animator, she wanted a simple yet nourishing lunch to help focus on the revised third mural, exploring how technology could enhance visual storytelling.
