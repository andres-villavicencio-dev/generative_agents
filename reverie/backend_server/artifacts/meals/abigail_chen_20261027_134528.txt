Abigail Chen prepared Miso-Tofu Ramen with a Soba Salad, a warm comfort meal inspired by her digital art’s layered textures and emotional storytelling. The ramen features a rich, dark broth with chewy shiitake mushrooms, tender strips of tofu, soft chashu pork, and a handful of fresh green onions, all simmered until the noodles are tender. She added a generous serving of soba noodles with a crisp sesame salad, crisp cucumber, scallions, and sesame seeds to provide a refreshing contrast.

To prepare it, she sautéed the mushrooms and pork in a little oil, then simmered everything in a seasoned dashi stock until the flavors melded. She served the noodles and broth in a wide, shallow bowl, topped with the crisp salad, and arranged the sliced pork and tofu alongside the shiitake mushrooms. A small bowl of miso paste was kept nearby for a quick dressing.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the earthy scent of mushrooms and pork mingling with the subtle tang of miso and the bright scent of toasted sesame and green onions. It presented as a visually balanced meal: the deep colors of the ramen balanced by the crisp greens and golden noodles. On a rainy Wednesday afternoon, it was a cozy, intimate lunch for Abigail Chen, giving her energy to refine her third mural while preparing for the next animation.
