Abigail Chen prepared Tangerine-Orange Pomegranate Salad for Wednesday lunch. The dish featured crisp oranges and grapefruits, juicy pomegranate arils, thinly sliced cucumbers, red onions, a generous portion of walnuts, and a lemon-lime vinaigrette.

She dressed the salad in a wide, clear glass bowl, arranging the colorful ingredients in overlapping patterns. A few whole orange halves and a small sprig of rosemary were placed on top for visual balance. The dressing was lightly drizzled over the top before she plated it.

The scent of bright citrus and toasted nuts filled the kitchen, while the cool, refreshing ingredients made the salad feel like a refreshing afternoon treat. She prepared it for a relaxed lunch break, using the meal to recharge before continuing her work on the mural.
