Abigail prepared Crispy Chicken and Miso-Ginger Soup for lunch, a warm, nourishing meal that reflected her creative energy. She marinated chicken breasts in soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil, then pan-seared them to golden perfection before slicing into the hot soup. The broth simmered with miso paste, carrots, daikon, ginger, and a touch of soybean oil, giving it a savory, slightly earthy aroma that lingered as she ate.

She presented the meal in a simple white bowl, with the chicken arranged around the edges and translucent slices of carrot resting beside it. A small bowl of pickled ginger and a fresh green leaf added a bright contrast. The dish was served on a quiet Wednesday afternoon, with no particular occasion, but Abigail’s open-minded nature made her lunch feel like part of her ongoing exploration—her culinary process mirroring how she blends art, technology, and ideas.
