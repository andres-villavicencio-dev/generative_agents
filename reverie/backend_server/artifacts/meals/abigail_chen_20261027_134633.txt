Abigail Chen prepared Luminous Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice, a warm, vibrant dish for her Wednesday lunch. The main component was chicken breast marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, fresh thyme, rosemary, and a touch of honey, then pan-seared until golden and juicy. Rice seasoned with the same aromatic herbs, lemon zest, dried parsley, and a little soy sauce was cooked alongside it.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, roasted herbs, citrus, and savory chicken, creating an inviting atmosphere perfect for a relaxed lunch. Abigail arranged the plates elegantly: the grilled chicken and rice were arranged in a loose, asymmetrical layout, with the golden chicken resting on a small bed of green herbs and the rice filling the rest of the plate. A bright red pepper garnished with a lemon twist added a cheerful pop of color.

The occasion was a calm, creative lunch break for her digital art and animation work. After carefully refining a revised mural—including its connection to a character’s past, visual options, final technical iteration, and an ambiguous ending—Abigail took a moment to nourish herself. The meal felt both nourishing and reflective, matching her intention to test pacing and reveal before sending the final animation to Latoya.
