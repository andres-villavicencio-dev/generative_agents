Abigail Chen prepared Fumé de Pommes au Miel et Au Lait de Coco, a warm, golden dessert of caramelized apples and coconut milk. The apples were peeled, sliced into small wedges, gently browned in a pan, and then simmered in a rich coconut-milk sauce with a little honey. A pinch of cinnamon, star anise, and a touch of vanilla completed the aroma—warm, fragrant, and subtly nutty.

She presented the dish in a shallow, rustic ceramic bowl, with a small spoon resting beside it. The glossy caramel and creamy coconut sauce were arranged artfully around the apple wedges, which peeked out from beneath a thin layer of toasted coconut flakes. The dessert was served at 8:13 AM, making it the perfect light lunch for Abigail’s Wednesday afternoons. It was a quiet, cozy meal for the digital artist who spends her time refining the third mural and testing pacing for her animation project.
