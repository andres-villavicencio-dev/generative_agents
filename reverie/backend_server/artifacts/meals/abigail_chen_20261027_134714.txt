Abigail Chen prepared a vibrant Lemon-Herb Quinoa Buddha Bowl for lunch on Wednesday, February 15, around 6:00 PM. The meal was designed to match her creative, open-minded mood and her recent work on digital art and technology.

The bowl featured quinoa, chopped kale, cucumber, tomato, red onion, and bell pepper, all tossed with lemon juice, olive oil, and fresh rosemary. A handful of chickpeas and feta cheese added a creamy, nutty richness. Chia seeds provided texture and fiber. She cooked the quinoa, roasted the vegetables, and dressed the bowl with a bright lemon-herb vinaigrette.

She arranged the colorful ingredients in a circular pattern on a ceramic plate, topped with a simple sprig of rosemary and a few extra chickpeas. The presentation was clean and visually balanced, with the lemon’s acidity and herbs’ aroma creating a fresh, inviting scent. It was a simple lunch, but Abigail treated it with the same attention to detail she applied to her mural and animation.
