Abigail prepared a vibrant Thai-Indian fusion bowl, Biryani with Coconut-Tofu and Mango-Butter, as her Wednesday lunch. The dish combined aromatic basmati rice with tender coconut tofu, caramelized onions, fresh mango, garlic, ginger, and a tangy tamarind sauce. She sautéed the vegetables first, then simmered the rice with coconut milk and spices until the grains were tender and the sauce reduced into a glossy, fragrant glaze.

The aroma filling her kitchen was warm and earthy—hints of curry, toasted basmati, caramelized onions, coconut, and warm garlic and ginger. She arranged the bowl in a colorful, layered presentation: bright orange mango slices, golden tofu cubes, crisp green onions, and a plump piece of basmati rice surrounded by crunchy naan.

It was a relaxed, celebratory lunch after a long day of refining the third mural and coordinating its delivery. Abigail chose it because the comforting, flavorful meal mirrored the creative journey she wanted to share with Francisco and Latoya—something warm, visually engaging, and full of promise.
