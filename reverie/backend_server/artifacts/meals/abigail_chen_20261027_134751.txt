Abigail Chen prepared a Garden-Style Herb-Topped Risotto with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Thickened Broth, a comforting, autumn-inspired meal that reflected her creative, grounded nature. The dish featured arborio rice, vegetable broth, parmesan, white and golden mushrooms, baby carrots, onions, and fresh herbs. She sautéed the vegetables in olive oil, then slowly cooked the rice in the hot broth until it absorbed the liquid, creating a creamy, earthy texture. She finished it with a lemon-herb vinaigrette, adding the crisp vegetables and mushrooms to preserve their brightness.

The aroma was warm and savory—saffron, toasted nuts, caramelized onions, and the subtle scent of roasted garlic. She plated the risotto in a shallow white bowl, arranging the vegetables and herbs artfully around it, with a small lemon wedge beside it. It was a simple, home-cooked lunch on a Wednesday, but the meal felt like a quiet moment of reflection before she continued her work on the third mural.
