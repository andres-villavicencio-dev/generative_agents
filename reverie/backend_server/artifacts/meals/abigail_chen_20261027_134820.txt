Abigail prepared Lapche with Sesame Oil, Ginger, and Pickled Vegetables, a warm, savory Chinese-inspired meal. She combined thinly sliced pork belly and chicken with shredded cabbage, bean sprouts, pickled carrots and cucumbers, and a generous drizzle of toasted sesame oil. Ginger and garlic were lightly fried in a small pan, adding an aromatic warmth to the dish.

The meal was prepared in two stages. The aromatics were sautéed until fragrant, then the pork and chicken were added to absorb the flavors. Pickled vegetables were arranged on the plate, and a final drizzle of sesame oil completed the dish. Abigail arranged the ingredients neatly around the bowl, garnishing it with green onions and a few crisp vegetable leaves.

She ate it at home on a Wednesday evening, around 6:00 p.m., after finishing her revisions for the mural and coordinating its delivery. The meal felt like a quiet, comforting pause before she sent her refined mural to Francisco and followed up with the necessary technical notes.
