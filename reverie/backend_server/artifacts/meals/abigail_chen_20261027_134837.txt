Abigail Chen prepared a vibrant, contemporary vegetable stir-fry for lunch, combining fresh seasonal produce with her digital-artist aesthetic. The dish featured crisp carrots, red bell peppers, scallions, snap peas, and a small amount of ginger and garlic for color and aroma. She used a wok over medium heat, stir-frying the vegetables until they became bright and tender, then finished it with a light sesame oil glaze.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, ginger, and toasted sesame oil, balanced by the fresh citrusy note of the scallions and subtle sweetness from the carrots. She plated the colorful vegetables on a wide, lightly oiled plate, arranging the bright yellow peppers, green peas, red peppers, and crisp carrots in an inviting spiral. A small bowl of neutral sesame sauce and a few fresh herbs were placed beside her.

The meal was a simple, nourishing lunch on Wednesday, February 15, when Abigail was still refining her third mural and coordinating its delivery with Francisco and preparing to test her animation with Latoya. It was an ordinary weekday meal, yet her focused preparation reflected her determination to blend creative expression with technical precision.
