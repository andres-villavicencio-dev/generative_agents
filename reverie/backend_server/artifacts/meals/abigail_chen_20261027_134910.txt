Abigail prepared a Lemon-Ginger Herb-Infused Chicken and Kale Salad as part of her Wednesday dinner. She sliced grilled chicken breast into thin strips, cooked kale until tender, and tossed the vegetables with lemon juice, minced ginger, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of salt. The salad was dressed just before serving, allowing the citrus and warm spices to remain bright and fresh.

The aroma was bright and savory, with notes of lemon, ginger, garlic, and grilled chicken. She plated the colorful salad on a white ceramic plate, serving the chicken separately on the right. Her presentation was simple yet visually balanced: the pale green and golden leaves, bright orange lemon slices, and amber chicken contrasted with the creamy dressing.

The occasion was an informal family dinner on Wednesday, February 15, at 7:30 PM. Abigail prepared the meal to balance the energy of the day, while the mural she had been refining served as a quiet way to process her recent creative work.
