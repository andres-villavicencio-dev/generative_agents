Abigail Chen prepared a Luminous Vegetable Curry with Quinoa and Lemon-Fruited Rice, a warm, colorful meal inspired by the themes of memory, transformation, and emerging ideas in her digital art. The dish blends tender quinoa, carrots, kale, bell peppers, onions, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, and coconut milk, with a rich, aromatic gravy made by sautéing spices and coconut milk until deeply fragrant. Fresh lemongrass and the zest of a lemon finish the curry, giving it a bright, citrusy aroma.

The presentation is artistic and inviting: the colorful ingredients are arranged on a white plate, with crisp herbs and a drizzle of lemon juice adding visual contrast. Abigail served it with a side of warm brown rice and a small bowl of fresh lemon water, echoing the mural’s theme of renewal. She prepared it for lunch on Wednesday, February 15, at 6:30 PM, likely as a relaxed way to transition into her late-afternoon work. The meal reflected a calm, determined mood, combining familiar comfort with the curiosity and creativity that define her digital art practice.
