Abigail prepared Nanban Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Rice for Wednesday’s lunch. The dish features tender chicken breast, baby spinach, carrots, bell pepper, and onions, all coated in a savory orange-tangy sauce. Aromatic ginger, garlic, and chili provide a warm, spicy kick, while the rice is lightly toasted and finished with a drizzle of sesame oil and green onions.

Abigail mixed the ingredients in a large skillet, simmering the sauce until it glistened and the chicken fell away from the pan. She finished it by tossing the vegetables and rice in a small bowl so everything retained its brightness. The aroma filled the kitchen with roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and the sharp fragrance of ginger and chili oil.

She plated the meal on a white ceramic plate and served the vegetables in a small bowl beside the rice. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with contrasting colors, balanced portions, and the golden chicken resting prominently in the center. She ate it at home on Wednesday, February 15, around 6:30 PM, treating it as a relaxed dinner before finishing the revised third mural and preparing her animation for Latoya.
