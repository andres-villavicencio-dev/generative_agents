Abigail Chen prepared Fusion Roasted Chicken with Herb-Crusted Root Vegetables for lunch on Wednesday, February 15. She began by seasoning bone-in chicken thighs with garlic, dried thyme, rosemary, and a light coating of lemon zest and black pepper, then rubbed the herb-and-herb crust all over the chicken. She prepared carrots, parsnips, and golden potatoes, tossing them with olive oil, salt, and a touch of lemon juice before roasting them on the same tray.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic and herbs, followed by the savory scent of caramelizing chicken and the bright citrus note. Abigail arranged the golden vegetables around the chicken, garnished with fresh parsley, and finished the dish with a drizzle of balsamic glaze. She plated it on a white ceramic platter and served it with a simple green salad.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Wednesday lunch break, balancing her creative energy with nourishment. Abigail enjoyed it with a glass of water, carefully testing the pacing and visual balance of the mural she was refining, and considering how the restaurant atmosphere might influence the ending question she was contemplating for the final animation.
