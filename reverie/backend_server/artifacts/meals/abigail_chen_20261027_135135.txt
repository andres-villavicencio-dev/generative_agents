Abigail Chen prepared Miso-Ginger Glazed Salmon with Asparagus and Brown Rice. The meal was a weekday lunch meant to energize her creative day.

She used a salmon fillet, asparagus spears, brown rice, soy sauce, ginger, green onions, and a touch of sesame oil. She sautéed the vegetables in a pan, then added the salmon and simmered it with the sauce until the fish was golden and tender. The asparagus was tossed together with the pan juices, and the rice was cooked in a separate pot with ginger and soy.

Aromatic, warm ginger and savory soy notes filled the kitchen. The dish was plated simply but thoughtfully: a mound of creamy rice, a bright salmon piece, tender asparagus, and a glossy glaze. It was a quiet, nourishing lunch, prepared while Abigail focused on refining her mural and preparing to send the refined images to Francisco.
