Abigail Chen prepared a warm, colorful Mediterranean Vegetable Salad with Grilled Chicken and Lemon-Herb Crisp, a dish she wanted to share with Francisco after an intense workday.

The meal featured grilled chicken breast, cucumbers, carrots, red bell peppers, and zucchini, seasoned with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, and a pinch of salt. She marinated the chicken briefly, seared it over medium-high heat, then sliced the vegetables and arranged them in a shallow bowl. A lemon-herb crisp—made from chopped parsley, chives, and toasted pine nuts—was tossed with the salad for added texture and brightness.

The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of roasted chicken, fresh herbs, citrus, and garlic. Abigail presented it on a white ceramic platter with a small bowl of lemon-tahini dressing and a sprig of rosemary, creating an airy, balanced visual. She prepared it for a quick, healthy lunch to carry her refined mural to Francisco and the final animation to Latoya, while still preserving room for a final review.
