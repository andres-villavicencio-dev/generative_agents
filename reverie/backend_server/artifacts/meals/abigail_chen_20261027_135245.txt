Abigail Chen prepared a Mushroom & Herb Risotto with Lemon-Caramelized Carrots, a warm, earthy dish inspired by the character’s past. The meal featured golden pearl rice, sautéed mushrooms, shallots, garlic, parmesan, fresh herbs, and a bright lemon-carrot finish.

She began by sautéing the mushrooms and carrots in olive oil until their natural sweetness released, then added the rice and slowly added warm broth while stirring constantly. She finished the risotto by cooking it until the rice absorbed the liquid and became creamy, with a few final minutes of stirring to keep the texture delicate. Lemon juice and caramelized carrots were added near the end, followed by parmesan and fresh herbs.

As she plated it, she arranged the colorful mushrooms and carrots beside the creamy rice, garnishing it with a few fresh herbs and a lemon wedge. The aroma filled the kitchen with savory mushrooms, toasted garlic, and the bright, citrusy scent of lemon. The presentation was simple yet elegant, with the dish balanced and inviting.

Abigail chose the meal for a quiet Wednesday lunch after refining the third mural and coordinating its delivery with Francisco. It was a relaxed, nourishing break before testing the pacing and revealing of her animation before sending the final version to Latoya.
