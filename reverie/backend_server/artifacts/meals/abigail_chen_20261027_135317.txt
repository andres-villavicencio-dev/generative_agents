Abigail Chen prepared Frosty Panna Cotta with Lavender-Lemon Toast, a comforting dessert designed to balance the energy of her busy Wednesday. The meal was prepared for a quiet lunch on February 15, 21:30, while she continued refining the third mural.

She combined cream cheese, vanilla bean, lemon zest, and a light dusting of powdered sugar with fresh mint. The panna cotta set slowly in the refrigerator until it was firm enough to serve, its creamy texture contrasting with the crisp lemon toast. A small sprig of lavender and a single candied lemon peel added a subtle floral aroma.

The presentation was elegant and inviting: the panna cotta was arranged on a white ceramic platter, accompanied by the toasted bread and a bright bowl of mint. The warm golden-brown toast and cool lavender scent together created a cozy, thoughtful lunch for an artist who was also focused on the final details of her mural.
