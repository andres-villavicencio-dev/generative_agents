Abigail Chen prepared Roasted Tomato Basil Soup with Roasted Vegetables, a warm, earthy meal that reflects her creative, nurturing side. The dish consists of slow-cooked tomatoes, onions, carrots, celery, garlic, and seasonal vegetables, with a rich, creamy broth made from vegetable and olive oil. She roasted the vegetables until they softened and slightly caramelized, then simmered the soup until its aroma filled the kitchen with the savory, herbaceous scent of tomatoes and basil.

Abigail presented the meal in a simple white bowl, placing the colorful vegetables and golden broth beside it. A crisp lemon wedge and a sprig of fresh basil completed the minimalist aesthetic. It was a relaxed Wednesday lunch, intended to give her a quiet moment before she continued refining the mural for Francisco.
