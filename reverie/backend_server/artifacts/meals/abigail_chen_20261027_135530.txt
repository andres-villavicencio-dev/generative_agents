Abigail Chen prepared a Miso Glazed Roast Chicken with Asparagus and Lemon Herb Glaze, a simple yet flavorful meal suited for a Wednesday lunch. She began by washing and trimming the chicken, then marinated it in a mixture of soy sauce, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, and brown sugar. She arranged the chicken on a baking sheet alongside asparagus, roasted it in the oven until the skin was crispy and the meat was tender, then basted it with the remaining glaze.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of soy, garlic, roasted vegetable oils, and citrus. Abigail plated the meal in a shallow white bowl, arranging the golden chicken alongside bright green asparagus and drizzling it with a lemon-herb glaze. She added a small mound of roasted brown rice and a few fresh herbs for visual balance. The dish was presented for lunch on a quiet Wednesday afternoon, with Abigail focused on testing the pacing and revealing the mural’s ambiguous ending before her final animation.
