Abigail prepared Smoked-Salmon Quinoa Bowl with Lemon-Herb Rice, a simple yet nourishing lunch for her Wednesday afternoon. She cooked quinoa with a small portion of vegetable broth and gently warmed it before adding a generous handful of smoked salmon. The salmon was sliced into thin strips, and a drizzle of lemon-herb dressing was added just before serving.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted quinoa, smoked salmon, and fresh herbs. Abigail arranged the colorful ingredients on a white plate, using the lemon and herb dressing to separate the components and create a bright visual contrast. She added a small cucumber wedge and a sprig of parsley for a touch of freshness.

The meal was a quiet lunch before a busy creative day, as Abigail worked on the revised third mural. By the time she ate, she had reviewed the clean mural’s exposure and balance, coordinated the delivery with Francisco, and prepared photos to send to Latoya. Afterward, she tested the pacing of the animation, considering the character’s past and the ambiguous ending question before sending the final animation to her client.
