Abigail Chen prepared a Warm Spicy Chicken with Vegetable Rice Bowl, a comforting weekday dinner that reflected her creative, tactile side. The dish featured tender chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, onions, garlic, ginger, and a vibrant mix of red and green peppers, all simmered in a savory ginger-garlic sauce until the chicken reached its perfect tenderness. Aromatic strips of ginger and garlic filled the kitchen with warm, savory notes as the chicken simmered.

She plated the meal in a simple white ceramic bowl, arranging the vegetables in neat layers and placing the cooked chicken atop the rice. A small bowl of spicy red dipping sauce and scattered sesame seeds added both color and texture. The presentation felt balanced and inviting, with the warm golden chicken and bright green peppers creating a pleasing visual harmony.

The occasion was a relaxed Wednesday lunch, after Abigail had spent the day refining her third mural. The dinner served as a quiet moment of grounding before she prepared to test the pacing of her final animation.
