Abigail Chen prepared a Braised Eggplant with Roasted Garlic and Lemon Herb for lunch. The key ingredients were fingerling eggplant, honey-glazed garlic, fresh thyme, lemon zest, olive oil, and a splash of white wine. She marinated the eggplant with garlic and herbs, then roasted it in the oven until the skin became caramelized and the interior tender. Afterward, she sliced the eggplant and served it over a bed of quinoa, garnished with lemon wedges and a drizzle of olive oil.

The aroma filled her kitchen with the warm scent of roasted garlic, savory thyme, and bright citrus. She presented the dish in a simple white bowl, letting the contrasting colors of the golden eggplant and green garnish stand out. The meal was a simple, nourishing lunch, but it felt purposeful: Abigail was using the cooking process to test the pacing of her mural, checking the exposure and balance of each visual option while considering how the character’s past would shape the final animation.
