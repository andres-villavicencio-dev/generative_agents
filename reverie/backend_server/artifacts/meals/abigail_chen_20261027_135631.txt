Abigail prepared a quiet, visually warm lunch of Crispy Tofu with Pickled Carrots and Sesame Salad. She chose this dish because its earthy, savory flavors complement her interest in combining food with ideas—much like how she blends art and technology.

The meal consisted of firm tofu marinated in soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and a little rice vinegar, then pan-fried until the edges were golden and the interior stayed tender. The crisp carrots were lightly pickled in vinegar and sugar for a bright crunch, while the sesame salad added a fragrant contrast of toasted seeds and fresh greens. She toasted the sesame seeds before serving and arranged the tofu in a shallow, asymmetrical bowl alongside the colorful vegetables.

Aromatic, nutty, and lightly savory, the dish released its fragrance from the warm wok and steaming bowls. The presentation was minimalist and balanced: translucent tofu, glossy carrots, and vibrant greens were arranged with deliberate space, echoing her attention to visual rhythm and ambiguity in her digital work.

It was a casual Wednesday lunch, prepared before Abigail’s next major animation revision. She wanted a simple, nourishing meal that would match the reflective mood of working on the mural’s final iteration.
