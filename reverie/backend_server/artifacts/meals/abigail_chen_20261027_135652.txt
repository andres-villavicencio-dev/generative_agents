Abigail prepared Miso-Glazed Chicken with Steamed Vegetables for her Wednesday lunch. She started by marinating chicken thighs in a savory mixture of soy sauce, miso paste, garlic, ginger, and a touch of honey, then seared them in a hot wok until golden and cooked through. She added a large handful of kale, snap peas, and carrots to the pan, tossing everything together before simmering briefly until the vegetables softened.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and deeply umami—rich soy, savory ginger, caramelized garlic, and the gentle aroma of steaming vegetables. Abigail arranged the meal in a simple serving bowl, placing the chicken atop a bed of crisp vegetables and garnishing it with fresh cilantro and a bright lemon wedge.

The lunch was a relaxed, nourishing meal designed to match her creative flow. It felt both familiar and comforting, with the familiar warmth of home food contrasting with Abigail’s current focus on refining the mural, coordinating with Francisco, and preparing her final animation for Latoya.
