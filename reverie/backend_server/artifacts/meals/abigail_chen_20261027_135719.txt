Abigail Chen prepared a vibrant Miso-Crusted Salmon Bowl with Ginger-Soy Rice. The dish features salmon fillets coated in a rich miso glaze, topped with roasted root vegetables, scallions, sesame seeds, and a glossy, aromatic sauce. She used brown rice and added fresh cilantro and lemon zest for a bright finish.

Prepared in a small skillet, the salmon was seared until golden, then gently finished in the pan with ginger, garlic, soy sauce, and a splash of rice vinegar. The ingredients were layered in a shallow bowl, with the miso sauce drizzled over the top just before serving to release its savory aroma.

The presentation was warm and inviting, with the salmon’s glossy glaze contrasting against the soft colors of the vegetables and golden rice. Accompanied by steamed green and purple broccoli, the meal looked balanced and thoughtfully plated.

The occasion was a relaxed Wednesday lunch, as Abigail continued refining the third mural while coordinating with Francisco and preparing to test the pacing for her final animation.
