Abigail Chen prepared Nanban Beef & Sesame Rice for lunch. The dish combines tender strips of beef, firm rice, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, green onions, and sesame seeds, with a subtle hint of chili for a warm, spicy finish. She sautéed the beef until it browned and slightly caramelized, then simmered the rice in a savory broth with ginger and garlic. The aroma rose from the pan—a rich blend of toasted sesame, warm soy sauce, ginger, and savory beef.

She plated the meal in a simple white bowl, arranging the beef and rice so the grains looked evenly distributed while the sauce glistened around the edges. A small bowl of steamed vegetables and a side of sesame seeds added a fresh, crunchy contrast. The warm lighting from the kitchen highlighted the deep amber of the beef and the golden hue of the rice.

It was a quiet Wednesday lunch, perhaps a little late for her usual dinner at six. Abigail had been focused on refining the third mural, balancing technical execution with the character’s past, visual options, and an ambiguous final question. This warm, comforting meal made the long day feel grounded, and the possibility of a slightly surprising ending made her curious about what came next.
