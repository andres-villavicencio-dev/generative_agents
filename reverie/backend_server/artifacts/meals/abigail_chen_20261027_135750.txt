Abigail Chen prepared Lettuce-Infused Chicken with Lemon-Herb Crisp, a bright, refreshing lunch meant to balance the technical energy of her creative work.

The dish consists of a whole chicken, sliced red bell peppers, baby spinach, cucumber, apple, and a small onion. She marinated the chicken in lemon juice, garlic, oregano, thyme, and a little soy sauce, then seared it in a pan until the skin turned golden. The vegetables were briefly roasted with lemon zest and a splash of water to release their aromas.

The kitchen smelled of citrus, garlic, roasted vegetables, and warm herbs. Abigail plated the chicken over a bed of crisp lettuce, garnished with a few fresh herbs, and topped it with a bright lemon wedge. The presentation was clean and modern, with the colorful vegetables providing visual contrast against the pale chicken.

It was her lunch on a Wednesday, February 15, after a demanding day of refining the third mural and coordinating its delivery with Francisco, reviewing its technical iteration, and preparing to send it to Latoya. The meal reflected her open-minded, determined nature: simple enough to enjoy while she continued pushing the boundaries of art and technology.
