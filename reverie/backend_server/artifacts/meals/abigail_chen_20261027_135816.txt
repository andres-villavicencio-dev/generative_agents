Abigail Chen prepared Miso-Glazed Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables for lunch. She roasted bone-in chicken thighs with a generous coating of brown sugar and soy, then glazed the sides with a savory miso-mustard sauce. Diced carrots, potatoes, and onions were roasted until tender, with garlic, ginger, and fresh parsley adding aromatic warmth. A small bowl of pickled radish and cucumber kept the meal refreshing.

She arranged the golden chicken and caramelized vegetables on a white ceramic plate, topped with a drizzle of the glossy glaze, and garnished with crispy green onions. The warm, earthy aroma of roasted garlic, soy, ginger, and citrus filled the kitchen while she worked on the third mural.

This was a relaxed Wednesday lunch after refining the mural’s technical iterations and coordinating its delivery with Francisco, Latoya, and the others. Abigail chose the meal to complement her focus on technology and art—clean, balanced, and ready to share.
