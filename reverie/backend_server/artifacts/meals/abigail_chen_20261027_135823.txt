Abigail prepared Tiramisu with Pistachio Crumble, a quiet, elegant lunch fitting her Wednesday afternoons. The dish combines ladyfingers soaked in coffee syrup with rich mascarpone, sweetened mascarpone, and vanilla bean, topped with finely chopped pistachios and a dusting of espresso powder.

She layered the soaked biscuits with the creamy mascarpone mixture, spreading it evenly across the pan before baking until the center was set. After cooling, she crumbled the pistachios over the top and finished the dish with a few espresso-soaked crumbs. The aroma was warm and sophisticated, with notes of coffee, vanilla, toasted nuts, and a hint of sweet pastry.

She served it in a shallow white ceramic bowl, arranging the biscuits in a gentle spiral and scattering the pistachios decoratively. It was a simple, satisfying lunch for work—a brief pause before she would continue refining the third mural and coordinate with Francisco, Latoya, and the rest of the team.
