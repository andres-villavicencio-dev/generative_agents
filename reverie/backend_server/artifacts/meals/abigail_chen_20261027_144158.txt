Abigail Chen prepared Warm Spicy Tofu with Stir-Fried Vegetables and Rice, a vibrant dish that reflected her creative, dynamic energy. The meal featured firm tofu marinated in ginger, garlic, soy sauce, and a touch of chili, along with carrots, snap peas, bok choy, and corn. She gently pan-seared the tofu until golden, then stir-fried the vegetables until crisp-tender, using a small amount of sesame oil and a pinch of chili flakes. A warm, savory aroma filled the kitchen with toasted garlic and citrus notes.

She plated the rice in a shallow dish to keep it warm, arranging the colorful vegetables around the center with the tofu resting beneath. The presentation balanced bold colors and textures, giving the meal an inviting, energetic feel. The occasion was simply a nutritious morning meal—Abigail prepared it because she wanted something warm, satisfying, and full of flavor, just as she was about to begin her work on the mural.
