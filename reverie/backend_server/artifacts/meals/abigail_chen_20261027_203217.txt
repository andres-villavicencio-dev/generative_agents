Abigail Chen prepared a Mediterranean-Inspired Breakfast Platter, featuring grilled chicken breast, roasted sweet potatoes, crisp green vegetables, a tangy lemon-herb yogurt sauce, and warm whole-grain toast. She tossed the chicken with olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, and thyme, seasoning it until it was golden and crisp. The potatoes were roasted with olive oil, rosemary, and a dash of honey until their edges were caramelized. Avocado slices and cherry tomatoes were arranged alongside the warm toast for a balanced, colorful presentation.

The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and toasted grains. Abigail arranged the ingredients on a white serving platter and topped it with a single sprig of rosemary and a drizzle of yogurt sauce. She placed the platter in the center of the open kitchen, surrounded by bowls of fruit and coffee, while the morning light cast a warm glow across the counter.

This meal was a quiet celebration of creativity and nourishment, mirroring Abigail’s desire to combine art, technology, and meaningful routines. It served as a practical pause before she began refining her third mural.
