On Wednesday, February 15, after waking at 8:00 AM, Abigail Chen prepared a quiet breakfast of Golden Egg Drop Omelet with Lemon Herb Toast. She began with two eggs, a handful of spinach, and sliced tomatoes, whisked them together, and pan-fried the omelet until the whites were set and the yolks remained creamy. For the toast, she toasted sourdough bread and lightly sautéed a small portion of spinach before finishing it with lemon zest and fresh herbs.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of toasted grains, herbs, and gently cooked egg. Abigail arranged the omelet on one side of a white plate, placing the golden toast beside it. A small glass of water and a single lemon wedge rested nearby. The meal was simple, purposeful, and meant for a peaceful Wednesday morning—no urgent deadlines, just the quiet satisfaction of creating something nourishing and visually balanced.
