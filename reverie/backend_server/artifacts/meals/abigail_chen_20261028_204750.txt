Abigail Chen prepared Golden Herb Rice with Grilled Chicken and Lemon, a colorful, nutrient-rich lunch inspired by her curiosity about combining food and creativity. The dish was prepared in a large wok over medium-high heat. Chicken thighs were seared until golden, then browned with garlic, ginger, and a few stalks of lemongrass. Aromatic green herbs were stirred in, followed by rinsed basmati rice and a splash of vegetable broth. The mixture was gently simmered until the rice was tender and slightly translucent.

The kitchen filled with warm, savory aromas of roasted chicken, ginger, lemongrass, and toasted garlic, with the scent of fresh herbs drifting through the air. Abigail plated the rice in shallow bowls, arranging pieces of grilled chicken, steamed broccoli, and sliced carrots over a bed of golden rice. Freshly grated lemon and a drizzle of chili oil were added for bright contrast.

She served the meal on Monday, February 13, as part of her regular lunch routine while continuing work on her animation project. The vibrant presentation and warm flavors reflected her open-minded, determined nature, making it a satisfying, creative meal while she explored how technology could bring art to life.
