Abigail Chen prepared a Quick Organic Lunch, combining seasonal organic eggs, fresh figs, and Fig Croissant. Her kitchen table became a small cooking station: she cracked the eggs into a pan, warmed the pan with a little oil, and cooked them with a pinch of salt until the whites were set and the yolks were soft. She sliced the figs into thin rounds and arranged them on top of the eggs, then placed the croissant on a platter nearby to serve.

The aroma filled the small apartment as the eggs sizzled, the figs released their sweet scent, and the croissant began to melt slightly. The meal was simple and visually appealing—the bright yellow and orange tones of the eggs, the reddish-brown figs, and the creamy golden-brown croissant created an inviting contrast.

It was a quick, nourishing lunch for a productive Wednesday, chosen to keep Abigail’s desk and bed in view while she continued refining her animated sequence.
