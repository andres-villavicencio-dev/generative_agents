Abigail Chen prepared a simple organic breakfast at home: Organic Fig Croissant with Clover-Honey Coffee. For the croissant, she used whole-grain flour, butter, eggs, flour, and a pinch of salt, folding in fresh figs for sweetness and texture. The coffee was brewed with organic beans, clover honey, and organic eggs, then poured into a warm mug.

She prepared it in the early afternoon while The Willows Market opened. The croissant’s golden, flaky layers contrasted with the dark, aromatic coffee and the bright green clover honey, creating a warm, inviting aroma of butter, roasted grains, honey, and fresh fig. It was presented on a white ceramic plate with a small bowl of honey beside it, arranged simply so the coffee and croissant could be enjoyed together.

The occasion was a quiet morning routine: Abigail made the coffee and breakfast to begin her day, while also checking that the pantry staples were stocked and coordinating with Ryan for organic coffee and fresh eggs. The meal reflected her balanced, determined lifestyle, balancing creativity with nourishment and practical preparation.
