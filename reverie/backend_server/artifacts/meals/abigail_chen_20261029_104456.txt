Abigail Chen prepared a warm Morning Market Café Breakfast for herself and her friends. The meal combined clover honey, organic eggs, and freshly baked Fig Croissant, with coffees made using organic eggs and local honey as her inspiration. She cracked the eggs into a hot pan, whisked them with the honey until the yolks were creamy and slightly runny, then folded in the croissant for a crisp, buttery finish.

The aroma was warm, golden, and slightly floral—honey, toasted nutmeg, and the gentle scent of baked pastry. She served the eggs in a small ceramic bowl beside a croissant, topped with a drizzle of honey and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt, while a small side of roasted coffee beans added a warm finishing note.

The occasion was a quiet Thursday morning before her day began, a relaxed way to balance her digital art and animation work with nourishment and social connection. It reflected her determined, open-minded nature as she prepared the meal with care, turning a simple breakfast into a thoughtful moment of anticipation.
