Abigail Chen prepared a simple breakfast for The Willows Market, including a small portion of leftover toast, organic eggs, clover honey, and an organic egg dish.

The toast was toasted just until golden, while the eggs were prepared in a pan with a little butter and seasoned with freshly cracked pepper. She used the remaining crust of the toast, carefully folding it over the egg mixture before serving. The ingredients were combined gently so the eggs remained warm and the toasted bread absorbed the warm flavors.

The aroma was earthy and comforting—clover honey, roasted grains, and a hint of warm butter. She plated the breakfast on a white plate, arranging the golden toast beside the bright yellow eggs and spoonful of honey, with a small glass of milk nearby. The occasion was a quiet, unhurried morning as she began preparing her breakfast for The Willows Market.
