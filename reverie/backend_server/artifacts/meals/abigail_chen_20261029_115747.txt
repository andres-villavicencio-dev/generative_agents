Abigail Chen prepared a small, planned breakfast-and-meal breakfast lunch for Ryan Park, combining the organic coffee and eggs she prepared for her visit with a simple breakfast. The dish was a Roasted Potato & Fig Croissant served in a warm ceramic bowl, garnished with a sprig of fresh thyme.

She used organic eggs, roasted potatoes, fresh figs, olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and a little salt. The eggs were lightly scrambled to retain moisture, while the potatoes were crisped with the figs. Fig Croissants were kept warm for Carlos’s coffee.

The kitchen filled with the warm, earthy aroma of roasted garlic and figs, the soft scent of cooked eggs, and a subtle citrus note. Abigail arranged the golden croissant and fluffy eggs in the bowl, placing the potatoes and figs beside them. The presentation was rustic and inviting—warm tones, visible textures, and fresh herbs gave the meal a cheerful, nourishing feel.

The occasion was an organic breakfast gathering at Ryan Park on Thursday, February 16. Abigail wanted to share a wholesome, simple lunch with Ryan, while also checking that Carlos’s coffee ingredients were ready and confirming the pantry staples with Sharon Antle and Hua Lai.
