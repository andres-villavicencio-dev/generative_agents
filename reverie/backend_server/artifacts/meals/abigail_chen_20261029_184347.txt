Abigail prepared Caramelized Garlic Butter Salmon with Asparagus and Lemon-Cilantro Soup, a light dinner for Friday evening. The aroma of roasted garlic and warm butter filled the kitchen as she stirred the pan. As she plated the salmon, the bright colors of the lemon, herbs, and garnished salad made the meal appear fresh and inviting. Abigail served it at a quiet Friday family dinner, balancing her digital-artist work with a simple, nourishing evening.
