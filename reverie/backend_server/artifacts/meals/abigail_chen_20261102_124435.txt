Abigail Chen prepared Lemon-Honey Egg and Clover Salad for the Ryan Park Gathering, a simple, visually bright brunch designed to energize the community. She began by washing and peeling several lemons, slicing them into thin rounds, and lightly salting them. In a skillet, she browned a few eggs with garlic, then added the lemon slices, chopped chives, and a pinch of salt before turning the pan over to finish cooking them. She folded the eggs into a crisp salad of arugula, thinly sliced radishes, and crisp lettuce, drizzling the pan juices over the top and finishing with a generous knob of butter and a splash of honey.

The kitchen filled with the fragrant scent of roasted garlic, citrus, and sweet honey, while the warm golden eggs contrasted with the cool green leaves and pale radishes. Abigail arranged the eggs in a shallow bowl, garnished them with extra chives and lemon slices, and placed a small bowl of clover honey beside it. She served the dish at the gathering’s relaxed Saturday brunch, creating a welcoming focal point that reflected her interest in blending technology, creativity, and thoughtful self-care.
