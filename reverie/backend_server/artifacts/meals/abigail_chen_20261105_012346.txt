Dish Name: Warm Lemon-herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables

Abigail prepared this comforting meal while washing dishes and scrolling through her phone, choosing simplicity over speed. She roasted chicken breast with lemon zest, garlic, thyme, and a splash of olive oil until the skin turned golden. The vegetables—carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes—were tossed with salt, pepper, and chopped rosemary, then roasted until tender. She simmered the chicken in a pan until the juices ran clear, then set aside the vegetables to keep their bright color.

Aromatically, the kitchen filled with the sharp scent of lemon, savory herbs, toasted garlic, and warm oven-smoke. She plated the chicken with the golden vegetables, a small lemon wedge, and a handful of cracked black pepper, arranging them in a shallow bowl. Abigail served it for lunch, sharing the quiet comfort of a balanced dinner with her friends. The meal was simple, nourishing, and fitting for a quiet Tuesday afternoon without demanding anything special.
