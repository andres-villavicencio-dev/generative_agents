The occasion was a quiet Tuesday, February 14, as Abigail Chen prepared dinner before arranging her early meeting with Ethel Langley. She made Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Rice, combining an easy weeknight meal with a bright, uplifting presentation.

She seasoned chicken breasts with lemon, garlic, rosemary, and thyme, then roasted them alongside red bell peppers, zucchini, carrots, and baby potatoes until the skin was crispy and the vegetables were tender. She roasted the vegetables with olive oil, honey, and a splash of lemon juice, then added a generous amount of aromatic rice.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of roasted garlic, herbs, citrus, and toasted rice, while the golden chicken and colorful vegetables created a visually cheerful spread. She plated the dish with lemon wedges, a small bowl of mixed greens, and a simple side of brown rice, intending the meal to feel both nourishing and inspiring—a small pause before her creative day continued.
