Abigail Chen prepared a Quinoa and Herb Breakfast Bowl, a simple, home-cooked meal designed to energize her morning. She cooked quinoa with lemon-tahini dressing, then added boiled eggs, spinach, diced tomatoes, red bell pepper, and cucumber. A tablespoon of toasted almond butter and a squeeze of lemon completed the bowl.

The ingredients had a warm, earthy aroma, with the savory quinoa, rich eggs, and tangy lemon-tahini creating an inviting fragrance. Abigail arranged the colorful vegetables around the quinoa, topped the bowl with a bright drizzle of dressing, and placed a fresh orange slice nearby. The presentation was vibrant, balanced, and clearly intended as a healthy breakfast.

The occasion was a Tuesday morning routine on February 14, when Abigail needed a quick, nourishing meal to fuel her work on her client’s interactive animation project.
