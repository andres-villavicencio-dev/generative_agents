The meal was Berry-Oatmeal Breakfast Bowl, prepared in the kitchen for Abigail Chen’s Tuesday morning. She combined oatmeal, ripe strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, honey, and a splash of lemon juice, then added a few fresh banana slices, toasted walnuts, and a sprinkle of cinnamon. The warm oats were stirred gently, and the fruit was folded in just before serving.

As she prepared it, the kitchen filled with the sweet aroma of caramelized honey, toasted nuts, and ripening berries. The colorful presentation featured the creamy, golden oatmeal base, bright red and purple fruit, and contrasting brown and white elements, arranged in a simple but inviting bowl.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday breakfast, combining her digital-art-inspired creativity with a familiar, comforting morning meal. Abigail Chen used the warm breakfast as inspiration for her next animated piece, blending her familiar routines with her passion for interactive art.
