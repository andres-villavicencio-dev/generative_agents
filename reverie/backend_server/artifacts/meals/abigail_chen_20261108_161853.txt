Abigail Chen prepared Smoked-Spinach and Roasted Vegetable Soup for dinner. The dish featured lamb chops, smoked potatoes, roasted carrots and parsnips, leeks, garlic, shallots, thyme, black pepper, and a splash of white wine. She began by searing the lamb chops in a hot pan, then added chopped vegetables and a generous amount of vegetable stock. After simmering until the lamb was tender and the vegetables were softly caramelized, she finished the soup with a little cream and a handful of fresh parsley.

The aroma was warm and savory, with notes of smoke, garlic, herbs, and roasted root vegetables. She presented the soup in a clear glass bowl, garnishing the table with the remaining lamb and plating the vegetables in a small ceramic dish. The meal was a quiet, satisfying dinner for her on Monday, February 13, when she was wrapping up work on her interactive animation and wanted a relaxed meal to prepare for the next day.
