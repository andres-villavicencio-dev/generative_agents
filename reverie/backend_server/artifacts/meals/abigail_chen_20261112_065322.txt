On Monday, February 13, Abigail Chen prepared a Mediterranean Quinoa-Basil Salad Sandwich for her cafe’s lunch batch. The dish combines fluffy white quinoa, crisp cucumber, cherry tomatoes, feta cheese, olives, and fresh basil, all wrapped in whole-grain bread and topped with a lemon-herb dressing.

Abigail began by toasting the bread, then assembling the salad on a large plate using a spoon and spatula. She arranged the ingredients neatly in alternating layers, finishing with a generous dollop of feta and a bright lemon squeeze. She sealed the sandwich halves and brushed them lightly with olive oil before serving.

The aroma filled the small cafe kitchen: warm toasted bread, crushed tomatoes, lemon zest, and the crisp scent of basil. She presented it in colorful, layered sandwiches alongside several pastries, arranging them in a casual, inviting row.

This was part of a cafe lunch batch, designed to feed the cafe’s early customers. Abigail’s open-mindedness and attention to detail allowed her to combine familiar flavors with fresh, interactive textures, creating a simple yet memorable meal.
