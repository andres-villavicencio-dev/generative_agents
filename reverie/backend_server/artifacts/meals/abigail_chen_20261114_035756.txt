Abigail Chen prepared Sesame Ginger Vegetable Stir-Fry with Brown Rice, a warm, colorful side dish meant to accompany the morning’s digital-artist breakfast.

She began by sautéing a handful of fresh green onions and sliced garlic in a little oil, then added carrots, bell peppers, snap peas, and a small handful of mushrooms. After cooking them until crisp-tender, she stirred in a bowl of soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, and a splash of honey to balance the savory flavors. Brown rice was cooked separately and then combined with the vegetables in a pan.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted sesame, ginger, garlic, and savory soy. Abigail arranged the rice and vegetables in a small, shallow bowl, garnishing it with a drizzle of honey and a few whole sesame seeds. She served it on an old-fashioned ceramic plate with a simple wooden spoon, presenting it as a cozy, nourishing breakfast to start her focused day.
