Adam Smith prepared a simple shepherd’s pie with roasted root vegetables, using the occasion to share a quiet lunch with Yuriko after their morning walk. Minced beef was browned with onions, thyme, and a splash of Madeira, then combined with carrots, parsnips, and peas. The filling simmered until rich and savory, after which it was topped with creamy mashed potatoes and baked until the edges turned golden.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted meat, herbs, and buttery potatoes—a comforting reminder that nourishment and reflection often sharpen one another. Adam arranged the pie in a small ceramic dish, placing a crisp green salad beside it and pouring two glasses of cider. The presentation was modest but carefully composed, reflecting his belief that value lies not in extravagance but in thoughtful execution. The meal marked a pause in their writing, giving them time to discuss creativity, public service, and the approaching mayoral election.
