Adam Smith prepared Rosemary Lemon Thyme Breakfast Potatoes with Crispy Sage as a side to accompany softly scrambled eggs. He peeled small russet potatoes and cut them into irregular cubes, believing that “a little asymmetry makes the browning more interesting.” Tossed with olive oil, lemon zest, chopped rosemary, and thyme, the potatoes roasted at a high temperature until their edges became deeply golden and crisp. In the final minutes, he added butter, fresh sage leaves, and cracked black pepper, allowing the sage to perfume the hot oil.

The aroma was earthy and fragrant, with warm notes of roasted garlic, citrus, and herbs. He served the potatoes in a plain white bowl beside the eggs, garnishing them with a few sage leaves and a drizzle of olive oil. The colorful, rustic presentation reflected his appreciation for creativity and imperfect beauty.

The occasion was a quiet Monday morning breakfast on February 13, eaten early so Adam could return to his book on creativity before researching candidates in the upcoming local mayoral election.
