Adam Smith prepared a savory Scottish breakfast skillet to share with Yuriko Yamamoto while they discussed Adam Smith’s philosophical ideas and the upcoming mayoral election. The dish combined sliced sausages, bacon, mushrooms, cherry tomatoes, baby spinach, and diced bell peppers, all cooked together in a cast-iron pan. He began by crisping the bacon and sausages, then sautéed the vegetables until their edges caramelized. Once the tomatoes softened, he added the spinach, allowing it to wilt into the colorful mixture, and finished everything with a pinch of sea salt, black pepper, and fresh herbs.

The aroma was rich and comforting: smoky meat, earthy mushrooms, and the sweet fragrance of tomatoes and peppers filled the kitchen. He plated the skillet with two perfectly set eggs, toast, and fresh fruit, arranging the golden eggs beside the vibrant vegetables for a warm, inviting presentation. The meal was intended for a relaxed Monday morning conversation—nourishing, thought-provoking, and suited to a lengthy debate about creativity, society, and political leadership.
