Meal: “Adam’s Reflective Morning Porridge”

Adam Smith prepared a simple yet carefully considered breakfast to begin his early-morning writing session. The main dish was creamy oat porridge made with rolled oats, water, a splash of milk, and a pinch of salt. He simmered the mixture slowly, stirring until it became thick and smooth, then topped it with sliced banana, toasted walnuts, and a small drizzle of honey. Beside it, he brewed a hot cup of black tea, checking the temperature before drinking so it was warm rather than scalding.

The porridge smelled gently sweet, with the toasted, earthy aroma of walnuts rising from the warm bowl. The tea added a clean, fragrant note, while steam curled softly from the cup. He presented the meal on a plain white plate beside the dark ceramic mug, keeping the arrangement uncluttered and practical.

The occasion was an ordinary Monday breakfast, but for Adam it served as a quiet ritual before writing about creativity and exploring ideas for the upcoming local mayor election.
