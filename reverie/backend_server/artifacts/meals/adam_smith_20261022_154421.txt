Meal: Adam Smith’s Reflective Scottish Breakfast

Adam prepared a hearty Scottish-style breakfast, centering on savory porridge with mushrooms, toasted bread, and a gently fried egg. The porridge was made from rolled oats, water, and a pinch of sea salt, then simmered until thick and creamy. Finely sliced mushrooms were sautéed in butter with black pepper until golden, while the egg was fried in a small pan until its white was just set and the yolk remained soft. A thick slice of rustic bread was toasted until crisp and golden.

The aroma was warm and comforting, with the earthy scent of mushrooms, butter, and toasted grain mingling with the subtle richness of the egg. He served the meal on a plain white plate, arranging the porridge beside the egg and mushrooms, with the toast resting on a small wooden board. A cup of dark tea accompanied it.

Adam prepared breakfast while reviewing notes for his book on creativity. The quiet early-morning meal also gave him time to reflect on the upcoming mayoral election and consider how unconventional ideas might challenge local assumptions.
