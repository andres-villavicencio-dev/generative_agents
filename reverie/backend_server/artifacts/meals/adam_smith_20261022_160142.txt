Adam Smith prepared a winter mushroom and barley risotto with roasted squash and goat cheese. The key ingredients were pearl barley, mixed wild mushrooms, butternut squash, shallots, garlic, fresh rosemary, vegetable stock, white wine, Parmesan, and goat cheese.

He sautéed the shallots and garlic in olive oil, then toasted the barley until it began to turn golden. Deglazed the pan with white wine before adding the stock and chopped rosemary. The mushrooms were browned separately, while the squash was roasted until caramelized. Once the barley had softened, he folded in the mushrooms, squash, and Parmesan, finishing the dish with goat cheese and cracked pepper.

The aroma filled the kitchen with earthy mushrooms, rosemary, roasted squash, and a subtle buttery richness. It was served in a shallow bowl, the golden grains topped with a few glossy mushrooms, ribbons of squash, crumbled cheese, and a sprig of rosemary.

The meal was a solitary working lunch on Monday, February 13, intended to nourish Adam while he continued writing about creativity and contemplated the upcoming mayoral election.
