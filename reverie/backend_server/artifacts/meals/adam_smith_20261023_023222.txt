On Monday, February 13, Adam prepared Garden-Fermented Lemon-Serum with Roasted Root Vegetables, a breakfast meant to be both nourishing and thought-provoking.

The meal began with lemon, garlic, olive oil, and a small handful of salt, which he soaked overnight in a shallow bowl. The next morning, he sliced carrots, parsnips, and beets, then roasted them at 200°F until the skins browned and the interiors were tender. In a separate pan, he sautéed the lemon slices and garlic until fragrant, then added a splash of vinegar and a few sprigs of thyme. The fermented lemon-serum was stirred in just before serving, releasing a sharp, citrusy aroma.

Adam arranged the vegetables in a shallow dish and topped them with a golden lemon-glaze drizzle, garnishing the presentation with a sprig of thyme and a single lemon wedge. The meal was simple yet layered: earthy, warm, and subtly acidic.

Adam prepared it while considering how creativity could transform everyday life, reflecting on how people might find inspiration in ordinary ingredients. It was a quiet, reflective breakfast intended to balance his thoughts about philosophy, local politics, and the future of civic leadership.
