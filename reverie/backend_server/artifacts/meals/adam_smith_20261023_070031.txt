Adam prepared Herb-Crusted Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables, a simple yet thoughtful breakfast suitable for a quiet Tuesday morning.

The dish featured a whole chicken breast, skin-on to keep it moist, seasoned with smoked paprika, dried thyme, garlic, onion, black pepper, and a touch of olive oil. Root vegetables—including carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes—were roasted alongside the chicken in a single pan. Adam sliced the chicken into two halves, seasoning the skin generously and letting it finish cooking until the juices remained clear and the skin turned golden. He served the vegetables in a shallow dish, garnishing them with a few fresh thyme sprigs and a sprig of parsley.

The aroma was warm and inviting—smoky, savory, and fragrant with hints of garlic, herbs, and caramelized onion. The presentation was rustic and elegant: the golden chicken sat beside the colorful vegetables, with Adam placing a warm mug of tea beside them. It was a modest, nourishing meal prepared with care, fitting into his relaxed morning routine and leaving room for conversation or reflection before his Tuesday began.
