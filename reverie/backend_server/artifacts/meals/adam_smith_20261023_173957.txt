Adam prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Herb Crust and Lemon Butter Glaze for dinner on Monday, February 13. He sliced carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes, drizzled them with olive oil, garlic, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper, then roasted them until golden and tender. For the crust, he mixed flour, chopped parsley, thyme, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice. He brushed the vegetables with the butter mixture and spread the mixture over the pan, baking until crisp and fragrant.

The aroma of roasted root vegetables filled the kitchen, with notes of garlic, herbs, caramelized sweetness, and a warm lemon note. Adam presented the dish in a shallow ceramic bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and lemon wedges. The meal was prepared for a relaxed family dinner, with Adam helping prepare it.
