At 4:30 AM, Adam Smith prepared Heritage Sage and Herb Roast Chicken, a nourishing, rustic dish reflecting his thoughtful approach to daily nourishment. The chicken was seasoned with smoked paprika, garlic, dried thyme, rosemary, sea salt, and black pepper. It was marinated briefly in the refrigerator, then roasted until the skin turned crisp and golden while the breast remained juicy. A simple pan sauce of roasted vegetables, onions, and carrots was served alongside the chicken and a small side of whole grain bread.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the savory scent of smoked meat, warm herbs, caramelized onions, and a hint of toasted bread. It was presented in an unadorned ceramic bowl, with the chicken resting atop a bed of roasted vegetables and the bread placed beside it. The meal was simple yet thoughtfully prepared, reflecting Adam’s disciplined routine and desire for balanced, meaningful sustenance. It was not a festive occasion, but a quiet moment of reflection in the middle of his workdays—a reminder that thoughtful living begins with the care of the body.
