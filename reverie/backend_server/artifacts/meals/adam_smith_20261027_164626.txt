At around 5:00 p.m., Adam prepared a hearty Sunday Roast Beef with Gravy, Root Vegetables, and Seasonal Salad. The meal was a thoughtful evening meal, reflecting the reflective, intellectual life of a philosopher preparing for an upcoming follow-up with Francisco.

Adam selected a well-marbled ribeye roast, carrots, potatoes, parsnips, onions, and leeks. He seared the beef in a hot skillet until the crust developed, then transferred it to a roasting pan. The vegetables were roasted in the oven until tender, while the beef was slow-roasted until the meat fell off the bone. A rich, savory gravy was made with browned onions, garlic, thyme, bay leaf, beef stock, and a splash of red wine.

The aroma of caramelized onions, warm meat, and roasted root vegetables filled the kitchen. Adam served the beef on a rustic wooden platter, with the vegetables arranged around it and a crisp salad placed beside it. The meal was simple, comforting, and presented with attention to balance and visual harmony. He ate it while checking his corrected proposal and comparing the mural and path options with Francisco’s proposal, using the time to reflect on the work and his evolving ideas.
