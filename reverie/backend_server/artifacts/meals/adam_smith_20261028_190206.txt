On a quiet Monday morning, Adam Smith prepared Oatmeal with Dark Chocolate and Honey. He began by washing and chopping a small handful of oats and a few slices of dark chocolate, while keeping a pinch of cinnamon and a few drops of honey nearby for texture and flavor.

He boiled the oats in a little water, then added the chocolate, letting it dissolve slowly over several minutes. The final touch of honey warmed the bowl, while a tiny pinch of cinnamon added a subtle warmth.

When it was done, the oatmeal had a rich, warm aroma—rich cocoa, toasted oats, and the faint sweetness of honey. It was served in a simple white bowl, with a spoon of dark chocolate beside it and a small sprig of rosemary on the side, suggesting the meal was part of a thoughtful breakfast for reflection.
