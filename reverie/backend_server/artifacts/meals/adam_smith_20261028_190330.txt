Adam prepared Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Roasted Potatoes for a quiet Monday morning. He used a whole chicken, fresh thyme, rosemary, garlic, lemon, rosemary, and a generous amount of olive oil, seasoning the skin with salt and pepper before searing it until the golden crust formed. The vegetables were roasted until tender, and the potatoes were seasoned with lemon and rosemary before being added to the pan, allowing the chicken to finish cooking.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of herbs, garlic, roasted vegetables, and a hint of citrus. Adam arranged the chicken and vegetables in a shallow baking pan, garnishing the edges with thyme and a small sprig of rosemary. He served the meal in simple white bowls, leaving space between each dish and adding a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing for dipping.

It was not a celebratory dinner, but a thoughtful breakfast meant for reflection. After preparing it, Adam sat down to eat while reviewing his book, his mind wandering toward the local mayoral election and the ideas shaping the world around him.
