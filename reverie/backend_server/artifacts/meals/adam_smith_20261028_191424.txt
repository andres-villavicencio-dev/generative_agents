On Monday, February 13, Adam prepared Herbed Roast Coffee with Crispy Toast and Fresh Fruit. The dish was simple yet elegant: two cups of his favorite dark roast, fresh herbs, honey, and a few slices of fruit arranged alongside toasted bread.

Adam rinsed the coffee maker first, letting it cool slightly before brewing. He used freshly ground beans, a ceramic pot, and a kettle to make two cups. While the coffee brewed, he toasted the bread and added butter and a pinch of cinnamon. His favorite fruit—grapefruit, banana, and strawberries—was sliced and placed on the side.

The aroma was warm and inviting: roasted coffee, toasted bread, cinnamon, citrus, and honey creating a comforting morning scent. Adam arranged the coffee, bread, and fruit neatly on a white plate with a small bowl of honey nearby.

It was part of his quiet Monday morning routine, a thoughtful and reflective meal before he began writing about creativity.
