Adam prepared Braised Pork with Root Vegetables for dinner at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured tender pork shoulder, carrots, celery, onions, thyme, garlic, rosemary, and a slow-simmered red wine and vegetable broth. He sliced the pork into even pieces, seared it briefly to develop a crisp exterior, then transferred it to a pan with the root vegetables and herbs. The vegetables were sautéed until caramelized, then simmered with the wine and stock until the pork was tender and the flavors had melded.

He added fresh thyme and rosemary to the finished pan, tasting it before serving. The aroma was rich and savory, with notes of roasted pork, wine, caramelized vegetables, and warm herbs. The meal was served in a wide, shallow bowl, with the golden-brown pork resting among the bright orange carrots and crisp celery. It was a simple, comforting dinner, prepared in preparation for the next meal while Adam reflected on creativity and the local mayor’s upcoming election.
