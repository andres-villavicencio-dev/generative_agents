Adam prepared a Hearty Herb-Crusted Salmon with Roasted Vegetables for dinner at Hobbs Cafe. The dish was prepared on a large commercial grill, where he first seasoned salmon fillets with lemon, garlic, and a hint of smoked paprika. While the fish cooked, he roasted a colorful medley of carrots, onions, Brussels sprouts, and mushrooms with olive oil, rosemary, and thyme until the vegetables softened and the edges became caramelized.

The aroma was warm and inviting: the buttery scent of the pan, the earthy fragrance of roasted root vegetables, and the bright, citrusy notes of the lemon dressing mingled beautifully in the kitchen. When Adam plated the meal, he arranged the golden salmon beside the crisp vegetables and topped it with a creamy garlic sauce, garnished with fresh parsley and a lemon wedge.

This balanced dinner was prepared at home for the evening before Adam’s focused Tuesday work. It reflected his thoughtful and reflective nature—simple, flavorful, and carefully structured—with the meal serving as a quiet conclusion to a meaningful day of café duties and civic study.
