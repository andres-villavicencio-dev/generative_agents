Meal: “Willows Market” Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables

Adam prepared a simple dinner for himself at The Willows Market. He selected a whole chicken, carrots, potatoes, parsnips, and a few cloves of garlic from his grocery list, then verified that each item was available before purchasing.

He seasoned the chicken with salt, black pepper, lemon, thyme, and a touch of garlic oil. He placed the chicken in a large pan, added the vegetables, and covered it with water. Using a sturdy pan lid, he cooked it over medium heat until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. Before serving, he basted the chicken with the remaining pan juices, letting it finish cooking in its own flavorful broth.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted poultry, roasted root vegetables, garlic, and thyme. He arranged the chicken at the center of a white ceramic platter, surrounded the vegetables with crisp golden potatoes and parsnips, and placed a small lemon wedge beside it. The meal was a quiet, disciplined dinner—simple yet satisfying, fitting into his routine while honoring the market’s fresh produce.
