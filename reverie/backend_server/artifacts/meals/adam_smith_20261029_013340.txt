Adam prepared a simple, warming bean soup with cinnamon, cumin, and a pinch of nutmeg. The dish was cooked slowly overnight, allowing the beans to absorb the rich flavors of the spices. He added a small amount of onion, carrot, and celery, while finishing it with fresh ginger and a splash of lemon juice to brighten the savory earthiness. His choice of whole spices gave the soup a warm, inviting aroma—cinnamon’s sweet fragrance, cumin’s spicy kick, and nutmeg’s subtle warmth mingling with the gentle bitterness of the beans.

Adam plated the soup in a simple white bowl, arranging the golden-brown beans and vegetables around a small mound of creamy broth. A sprig of rosemary and a tiny piece of lemon wedge were placed on the side for decoration. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday dinner at The Willows Market, an early evening meal following his morning café work and a restful night. It was a thoughtful, homey dinner meant to reflect his reflective nature, while the familiar spices offered him a warm reminder of home.
