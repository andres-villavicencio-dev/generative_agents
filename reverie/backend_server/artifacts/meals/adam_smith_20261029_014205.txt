Adam prepared Whole Bean Soup with Crispy Roast Chicken and Cinnamon-Sugar Dressing, a comforting family-style dinner for a quiet Tuesday evening.

The soup was made by simmering whole kidney beans, carrots, onions, celery, and a splash of vegetable broth until the beans were tender. For the chicken, he seared a whole roast chicken breast in oil until it turned golden, then finished it in the oven with lemon and parsley. A cinnamon-sugar dressing was prepared by gently warming cinnamon, sugar, and a little vinegar, adding a pinch of less-spicy cumin to balance the heat.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the cinnamon warmed through and the chicken’s skin crisped, while the beans released a rich, hearty fragrance. Adam presented the meal in a simple white bowl, placing the chicken atop the vegetables and garnishing it with a sprig of parsley. It was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday dinner, leaving time afterward for reflection on his work, the mayoral election, and the ideas in books about creativity and civic participation.
