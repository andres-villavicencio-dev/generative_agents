Adam Smith prepared a simple Herb-Roasted Chicken with Carrots and Parsnips for a Tuesday evening meal at The Willows Market. The dinner was a humble, family-style supper, with thick skin chicken, carrots, and parsnips seasoned with dried thyme, rosemary, garlic, black pepper, and a touch of honey.

The chicken was marinated in the refrigerator overnight, then roasted whole at 425°F (220°C) for about 35 minutes until the skin turned golden and the meat reached an internal temperature of 165°F. The vegetables were roasted alongside it until tender and lightly caramelized. When Adam Smith finished preparing dinner, the kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, thyme, and warm carrots.

He plated the meal on a wide white plate, arranging the chicken in the center with the carrots and parsnips around it. He added a small sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge to the side. The presentation was rustic and inviting, reflecting the thoughtful, reflective nature of Adam Smith’s life. It was a simple, nourishing evening after a productive Tuesday, celebrating patience, community, and the comfort of home.
