Adam Smith prepared Stewed Potatoes with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Basil Butter, a comforting simple dinner for Tuesday, February 14. The dish included potatoes, carrots, parsnips, onions, and a small quantity of garlic, all simmered in chicken or vegetable stock with fresh herbs. He added the lemon and basil just before serving to brighten the warm, savory aroma.

Adam arranged the vegetables in a shallow bowl, piled the golden potatoes neatly beside them, and drizzled the buttery sauce over the top. The presentation was rustic and inviting, emphasizing their earthy colors and the golden hue of the potatoes.

The meal was prepared in early Tuesday morning, after completing his Monday work and reflecting on his disciplined routine. It was not a celebratory occasion, but a quiet, purposeful dinner that followed his morning routine and contributed to his sense of contribution. The fresh, nourishing ingredients reflected his thoughtful and reflective nature, while the simple preparation reinforced a calm, thoughtful atmosphere.
