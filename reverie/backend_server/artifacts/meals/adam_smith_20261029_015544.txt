On Tuesday, February 14, Adam prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Garlic Butter as a simple, nourishing breakfast. He washed carrots, potatoes, and onions, then lightly roasted them in the oven until tender and caramelized. For the sauce, he buttered the pan, added shallots and garlic, and cooked everything until fragrant. He finished it with lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and a drizzle of olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, roasted carrots, and the fresh scent of lemon. The vegetables were arranged in a shallow ceramic bowl, their golden edges contrasting with their pale interiors, and sprinkled with a few cracked black pepper. The presentation was minimalist and elegant, fitting Adam’s thoughtful and reflective nature.

He intended it as a peaceful morning meal, a quiet moment before beginning his day. Though no official dinner was prepared, the roasted vegetables offered a warm, purposeful alternative—one grounded by his disciplined routine and curious interest in local civic life.
