Adam Smith prepared Pasta with Chicken and Herbs for a simple Tuesday dinner at The Willows Market. He began with a small pan of chicken breast, seasoning it with olive oil, salt, black pepper, garlic, thyme, and lemon zest, then seared it until the skin was crisp and the meat was cooked through. Simultaneously, he boiled pasta until al dente, drained it, and tossed it with the cooked chicken, a splash of lemon juice, chopped parsley, and a few generous tablespoons of cream.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, thyme, roasted chicken, and creamy pasta. Adam arranged the dish in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a small lemon wedge before serving it with a plain baguette. The meal was a relaxed, family-style dinner, but Adam treated it as a quiet act of reflection. The chicken offered a savory balance, while the pasta provided comfort and texture. As he ate, he considered the day’s disciplined work, the early morning rest, and his curiosity about civic participation.
