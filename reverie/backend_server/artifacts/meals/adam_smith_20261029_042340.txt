Adam Smith prepared Sunday’s Slow-Cooked Beef and Root Vegetable Soup for dinner. The dish combined tender beef with carrots, potatoes, celery, onions, garlic, and thyme, seasoned with salt, black pepper, and a touch of rosemary. He began by slicing the beef and washing the produce, then sautéed the vegetables until soft before adding them to a slow cooker with the meat and stock. The soup simmered until the beef became tender and the vegetables retained their crisp edges, creating a rich, savory broth.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the warm scent of caramelized beef, roasted vegetables, and thyme. He served the soup in a simple, warm ceramic bowl, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a handful of toasted nuts. Adam ate it at home on Wednesday, February 15, after a demanding but fulfilling day of café work, civic duties, and reflection.
