For his Wednesday lunch, Adam prepared Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce.

The key ingredients were a whole chicken, carrots, potatoes, green beans, and onions. He began by seasoning the chicken generously with salt, pepper, and a few crushed dried thyme leaves. After searing the chicken until the skin was golden, he transferred it to a roasting pan and added the vegetables. He covered the pan with foil and a small amount of oil to braise the ingredients until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were slightly caramelized.

He finished the dish by adding a simple lemon-herb sauce, stirring it until it thickened, and garnishing it with fresh parsley. The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted poultry, carrots, onions, and lemon.

Adam served the meal at home on a simple wooden table for lunch, treating it as a quiet way to enjoy a thoughtful meal while reflecting on the day’s work and civic thoughts.
