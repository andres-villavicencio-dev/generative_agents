On Wednesday, February 15, Adam prepared a simple Pork and Herb Roast with Gravy for a quiet Wednesday lunch. The meal included a tender roast pork shoulder, roasted carrots, parsnips, thyme, rosemary, garlic, and a small onion. He began by washing the wooden spoon, knife, and pan, then wiped down the countertop and oven. He seasoned the pork with salt, pepper, and a little garlic powder, seared it briefly, and roasted it along with the root vegetables until the meat became fork-tender. A savory, golden gravy simmered around the sides, while the carrots and parsnips retained a crisp texture.

Adam presented the roast on a white ceramic platter, arranged the vegetables neatly beside it, and served the gravy in a shallow bowl. The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of caramelized pork, herbs, garlic, and roasted vegetables. He set the table for his family to eat together, creating a welcoming atmosphere for sharing the meal and reflecting on the day’s thoughts.
