Dish: “Garden Herb Chicken with Roast Root Vegetables”

The meal was prepared for a quiet Wednesday lunch, chosen as a simple, nourishing way to transition into the café shift. Adam used a bone-in chicken breast, garlic, rosemary, lemon, shallots, olive oil, thyme, and a generous amount of fresh herbs such as parsley, chives, and dill. The vegetables—carrots, potatoes, and celery—were seasoned with salt, pepper, and a touch of sugar to retain their natural sweetness.

Adam sliced the chicken into even pieces, seared it in a hot pan until golden, then finished it under the broiler. The vegetables were roasted in the same pan until tender, with the chicken resting between them. The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory meat.

The presentation was bright and deliberate: the golden chicken sat atop a bed of crisp vegetables, with rosemary and parsley sprinkled over the top. A small lemon wedge and a sprig of mint added a fresh touch. Adam cooked it for a simple lunch, not a meal meant to impress, but one that supported reflection, creativity, and the practical work of preparing for café shift.
