On Wednesday, February 15, Adam prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy for home-cooked dinner. The dish featured a whole chicken glazed with honey, lemon, rosemary, thyme, and garlic, alongside carrots, parsnips, potatoes, onions, and a splash of stock.

He seared the chicken skin-side down until crisp, then placed it in a large roasting pan and basted it with pan juices. The vegetables were arranged around it and roasted alongside until tender and golden. The savory, caramelized chicken and earthy vegetables made the gravy rich and filling.

The aroma was warm, herbaceous, and distinctly comforting—a sweet-savory scent of roasted poultry and root vegetables. Adam presented the meal on a white ceramic platter, arranging the golden chicken and vegetables in a neat circle, with a small bowl of lemon wedges and a sprig of rosemary beside it. The occasion was a relaxed family dinner, a simple way to balance his demanding work and civic duties while sharing a meal with his family.
