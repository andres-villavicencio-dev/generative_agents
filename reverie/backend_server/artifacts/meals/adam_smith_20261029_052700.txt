On Wednesday, February 15, Adam prepared Gilded Roast Chicken with Herb-Crusted Potato and Root Vegetables. The meal was a thoughtful tribute to his café, organized in the open kitchen where fresh ingredients were gathered and each dish received individual attention.

The chicken was marinated overnight in olive oil, garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and thyme, then roasted until the skin turned a rich golden brown while the meat remained tender. It was plated alongside golden potatoes, carrots, and sweet potatoes, each sprinkled with paprika, parsley, and a small amount of olive oil. A warm salad of arugula, lemon vinaigrette, and crisp greens was served alongside the main course, and fresh figs and a small glass of chilled wine were added for balance.

The aroma filled the preparation area with the earthy scent of rosemary, the savory perfume of garlic, and the bright citrus notes of lemon. The presentation was elegant yet inviting: the golden chicken stood in a shallow white bowl, surrounded by vibrant vegetables and garnished with herbs and edible flowers. It was prepared for a special Friday lunch, though Adam prepared it as a satisfying, thoughtful meal for his Wednesday.
