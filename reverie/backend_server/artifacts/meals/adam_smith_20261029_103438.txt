Adam prepared Fig, Clover, and Honey Croissant, a simple yet thoughtfully balanced breakfast. He used fresh figs, honey, and a small portion of fig clover for flavor, with the croissant baked until golden and crisp. The key ingredients were butter, flour, eggs, chopped figs, honey, and a sprinkle of fig clover.

He began by preheating the oven, creaming the butter and sugar, then folding in the eggs. He folded in the flour and figs, rolled out the dough, cut a rectangular shape, and baked it until golden. The aroma rose from the kitchen—a warm, buttery scent with a hint of honey and the delicate bitterness of the clover.

Adam placed the croissant in the center of the dinner table, accompanied by a mug of tea and a glass of water. The meal was prepared for a quiet Thursday morning: a peaceful, nourishing start to his day, intended to fuel his thoughts, support his café responsibilities, and encourage a reflective pause before continuing his civic and intellectual pursuits.
