Adam prepared Fig and Clover Honey Croissant, a simple yet thoughtful breakfast for himself. The croissant was made from flour, butter, eggs, warm water, and a pinch of salt, folded until the layers became crisp and the layers were visibly separated. The figs, served with the golden honey, softened the pastry, while the clover honey added a faint, earthy sweetness.

The kitchen smelled of butter, toasted nuts, and the gentle fragrance of fresh fruit. Adam presented the croissant in a small, warm dish, arranging the figs and honey beside it. He took a quiet moment to appreciate the balance of sweet, savory, and floral notes before eating.

The occasion was an informal Thursday morning ritual—a way to pause after a demanding day and begin with something nourishing. The meal reflected his reflective nature, offering comfort while grounding him in hospitality, the local mayoral election, and his desire to contribute meaningfully to his community.
