Adam prepared a simple Scrambled Egg with Tomato and Herb Omelet. He began by heating butter in a pan, adding salt and pepper, and slowly cooking two eggs until they were golden and fluffy. As he carefully folded in diced tomatoes and fresh chives, he stirred gently until the mixture became cohesive and slightly creamy.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted butter, roasted tomatoes, and the sharp, herbaceous scent of chives and garlic. Adam arranged the omelet on a white plate, garnishing it with a few crisp chives and a thin slice of lemon. He served it alongside crusty bread and a glass of water.

It was his first simple breakfast before starting the day, but he treated it with deliberate care. The omelet balanced the richness of the eggs with the freshness of the vegetables, while the lemon and bread provided a crisp finish. It was a quiet, nourishing moment before he would begin writing his book, reflecting on creativity and the possibilities of change.
