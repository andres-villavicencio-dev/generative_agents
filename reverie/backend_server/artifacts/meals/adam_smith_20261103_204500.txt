Adam prepared The Crispy Sage and Lemon Herb Chicken, a simple yet elegant dinner for his morning breakfast. After washing his hands and preparing a small bowl of warm water, he cracked two eggs into a pan, seasoning them lightly with salt, pepper, and a pinch of garlic. He then sautéed the chicken pieces in olive oil until they were golden, adding fresh thyme, rosemary, and lemon zest toward the end. A handful of sliced onions, carrots, and pearl onions was browned alongside the chicken, and the pan was finished with fresh parsley and a splash of lemon juice.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted garlic, warm herbs, and savory chicken. Adam plated the chicken on a white ceramic plate, arranging the vegetables in a neat, colorful pattern, and drizzled a little olive oil over the top before serving. It was the first meal of his day, but it felt like a thoughtful, purposeful start to a productive morning. He had prepared it because he wanted to nourish himself before tackling his book and election research.
