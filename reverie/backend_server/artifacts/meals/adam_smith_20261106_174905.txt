On Monday, February 13, Adam prepared Hobbs Cafe’s “Garden of Ideas” Lentil Salad. The salad featured hearty red lentils, quinoa, cucumber, crisp kale, roasted carrots, and a tangy lemon-tahini dressing, with toasted pomegranate seeds and fresh herbs adding texture and color.

The preparation began with a warm broth made from vegetables and chicken stock, to which the lentils were simmered until tender. The salad was then combined with finely chopped nuts and dried fruit, followed by a simple yet flavorful dressing of lemon juice, olive oil, and honey. It was dressed just before serving, preserving the vibrant green hues and aromatic freshness.

Adam arranged the colorful ingredients in neat rows on a white platter, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a few whole dried figs. The aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and earthy spices filled the kitchen as he plated the tray. The occasion was a quiet lunch break at Hobbs Cafe, designed to nourish his mind before he continued work in writing.
