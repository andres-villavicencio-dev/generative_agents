Adam prepared Crispy Roasted Vegetables with Herb Lemon Dressing as a simple yet flavorful side to accompany his dinner. The dish featured crisp carrots, red potatoes, and Brussels sprouts, lightly tossed in olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and lemon zest. Adam roasted the vegetables on a baking sheet until their skins darkened and their interiors became tender, then tossed them in a bright, herb-infused lemon dressing.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted garlic, earthy herbs, and citrus, while the vegetables released a warm, earthy fragrance. Adam arranged the colorful vegetables in a large serving platter, garnishing them with fresh parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil, creating a visually appealing presentation.

The side was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday dinner at home, with Adam continuing to strengthen the connection between Hobbs Cafe and the community. It was not an elaborate celebration, but a thoughtful meal intended to nourish him and share with friends.
