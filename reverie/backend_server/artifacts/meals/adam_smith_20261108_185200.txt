Adam prepared a simple breakfast burrito for his quick, practical meal. The dish consisted of whole-grain tortillas filled with scrambled eggs, sautéed spinach, diced tomatoes, cheddar cheese, and a handful of black beans. He added a small portion of avocado and a sprinkle of chili flakes for warmth and color.

He cooked the eggs in a nonstick skillet over medium heat, then wrapped the filling inside the tortilla and rolled it into a neat cylinder. A few drops of hot sauce and a pinch of salt finished the taste.

The aroma was warm, savory, and slightly nutty—like toasted bread, eggs, and chili. He arranged the burrito on a plate with a side of salsa and a glass of orange juice, letting the colorful ingredients create a small, inviting display.

The occasion was a brief, low-stress breakfast after staying up earlier than planned. It felt practical and grounding, reflecting Adam’s thoughtful nature: he chose a reliable dish without complicating it, even though he remained curious about the local mayor’s election and the confirmed Tuesday appointment.
