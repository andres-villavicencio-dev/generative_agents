The meal was a simple, rustic breakfast: Eggs Benedict with Toasted Sourdough.

Adam washed the eggs, cracked them into a pan, and added butter, salt, and pepper. He cooked the eggs to just set, then carefully folded them into thin slices of buttered sourdough. A few drops of lemon juice and a pinch of nutmeg gave the dish its familiar, savory aroma.

The eggs were golden and slightly custard-like, while the sourdough was crisp at the edges with a soft, nutty interior. The presentation was modest: two plates with a silver fork and knife, a small dish for the remaining eggs, and a cup of black coffee.

This was a quiet, solitary breakfast after an early night of study and civic curiosity. It offered comfort without distraction, reminding Adam Smith to approach the mayor’s pending February 14 appointment as a thoughtful, deliberate beginning to his day.
