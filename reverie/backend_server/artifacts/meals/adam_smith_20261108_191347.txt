“The Smith Breakfast”

Adam’s simple breakfast consisted of two boiled eggs, a slice of white bread, and a cup of hot water. He boiled the eggs until firm, then peeled them and mashed them into a loose pulp. The bread was lightly toasted, and he poured the hot water over the eggs, allowing the steam to warm the grains.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of toasted bread and the gentle, savory scent of egg cooking. He presented the meal simply on a plate: the warm bread beside the eggs, which were still moist and glossy. It was not an elaborate feast, but a modest, nourishing breakfast that matched his thoughtful and practical approach to life.

The occasion was his early arrival on Tuesday, February 14, after staying up earlier than planned. It was a quiet moment of reflection on the local mayor election: he noted that a confirmed appointment was scheduled for Tuesday, though the time and location had not been announced. The meal offered him a brief pause before he could analyze the appointment and continue considering the uncertain political landscape.
