Meal: Simple Tomato Egg Toast  

Key ingredients: two eggs, whole-wheat bread, tomato, garlic, olive oil, salt, and pepper.  

Preparation method: Adam sliced the bread and toasted it until golden. He boiled the eggs in a pan with a little water, then sliced them into thin rounds and arranged them on the warm toast. He added a thin slice of tomato, crushed garlic, and a few drops of olive oil, seasoning lightly before serving.  

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the rich scent of toasted bread, savory egg, and roasted garlic.  

Presentation: A single sheet of golden toast on a white plate, with the bright red tomato slices and glossy, soft-boiled eggs arranged attractively around it.  

Occasion: It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast after Adam stayed up late preparing for the local mayor’s confirmed appointment on Tuesday, February 14. The simple meal reflected his thoughtful, reflective nature, balancing the meal’s warmth with his current concern about the election.
