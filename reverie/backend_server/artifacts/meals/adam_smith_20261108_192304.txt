Adam prepared Smoked Egg and Whole Wheat Toast. After clearing the pan and wiping the kitchen surfaces, he gathered fresh eggs, whole-grain bread, salt, pepper, and a little butter. He cracked two eggs into a small bowl, heated the pan over medium heat, and cooked the eggs until they were set and golden. He sliced the bread, brushed it lightly with butter, and toasted it until golden. He then placed one egg on a warm slice of bread beside a small portion of toast, adding a pinch of salt and pepper.

The aroma of toasted bread and lightly smoked eggs filled the small kitchen. He presented the meal in a simple white bowl, the golden toast and perfectly cooked eggs side by side, with a tiny garnish of chives. It was a quiet, ordinary breakfast for a busy day—especially fitting for his late start, though he had not yet decided whether to attend the local mayor’s Tuesday, February 14 appointment.
