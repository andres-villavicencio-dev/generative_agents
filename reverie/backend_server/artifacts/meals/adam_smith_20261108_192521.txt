The meal was a simple, wholesome breakfast: scrambled eggs, toasted sourdough bread, and a small bowl of milk with a pinch of sugar. Adam washed the pan afterward, returned the bread to storage, and prepared the breakfast at home.

He cracked two eggs into a lightly oiled pan, adding a little milk and salt, then stirred until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft. The bread was warmed between two slices of butter, and the pan was set aside to cool. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted eggs and the warm, slightly sour scent of the sourdough.

The breakfast was presented simply: the eggs arranged neatly beside the toasted bread, with the milk and sugar ready for a quick dip. It was prepared for a quiet morning routine, but the confirmed mayor’s appointment scheduled for Tuesday, February 14, made the occasion feel slightly more meaningful.
