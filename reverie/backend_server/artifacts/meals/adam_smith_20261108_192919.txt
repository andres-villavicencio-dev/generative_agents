Dish Name: Simple Toast with Hard-Boiled Eggs and Lemon Butter

Key Ingredients: Two eggs, slices of whole-grain bread, butter, and fresh lemon juice.

Preparation Method: Adam cracked the eggs into a bowl and boiled them until firm. While the eggs rested, he toasted the bread until golden, then spread a thin layer of softened butter and drizzled it generously over the eggs. The lemon juice was squeezed between the pieces of bread to brighten the flavors. He cut the eggs in half, placing one slice of bread over each half, then arranged the pan on the counter for the remaining ingredients to cool.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the warm, buttery scent of toasted bread and the slightly nutty aroma of the boiled eggs.

Presentation: He served the dish in two neat plates, each containing one egg half and a generous portion of toast. A small ramekin of lemon juice and a sprig of parsley were placed beside the eggs for the first bite.

Occasion: This was a relaxed, no-frills breakfast prepared for the early morning before the Tuesday, February 14, confirmed mayor-elect appointment. The meal was simple and unpretentious, allowing Adam to focus on the uncertainty and curiosity surrounding the local election rather than elaborate dining.
