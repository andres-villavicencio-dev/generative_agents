Dish: “The Philosophical Breakfast”

Adam prepared a simple, nourishing breakfast of a grilled egg, sliced sourdough, and a cup of warm oat milk. He began by tidying the kitchen, wiping the counters, and separating the eggs into two bowls. After lightly toasting the sourdough and heating the oat milk, he cracked the eggs into the bowl of water, then gently folded in the warm milk. The bread was buttered, while the egg was served over a small plate.

The aroma of toasted bread, warm milk, and the rich, savory scent of the egg filled the kitchen. Adam arranged the plate neatly on the table: the golden egg beside the rustic loaf of bread, with the oat milk cup and a silver fork ready. He ate calmly, pausing briefly to consider which confirmed mayor appointment on Tuesday, February 14, might be the most significant decision of the day. The meal was not hurried or dramatic, but a quiet moment of reflection before continuing his day.
