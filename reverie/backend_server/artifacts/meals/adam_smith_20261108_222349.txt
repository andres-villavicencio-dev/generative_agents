The meal was a simple stir-fry, Miso Glazed Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, prepared on Tuesday, February 14. Adam used leftover chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, onions, and mushrooms, along with soy sauce, mirin, sesame oil, ginger, and a splash of rice vinegar.

He stirred the ingredients repeatedly over medium heat, adding the sauce gradually and stirring until everything was evenly coated and the vegetables released their juices. The aroma was warm, savory, and subtly aromatic, with notes of ginger, soy, sesame, and caramelized vegetables.

Adam plated the dish in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the vibrant orange carrots and green peppers around a golden chicken breast. The glossy glaze shimmered beneath the translucent vegetables, while the sauce rested in the center. He placed a small white bowl of steamed rice beside it, creating a balanced, home-style presentation suited to a relaxed mid-afternoon dinner.
