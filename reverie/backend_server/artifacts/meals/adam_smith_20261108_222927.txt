The meal was Pan-Seared Salmon with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Butter Sauce, prepared early in the evening after staying up late for the mayor’s election.

Key ingredients: salmon fillet, salmon oil, lemon, olive oil, garlic, red onion, carrots, potatoes, spinach, herbs, and a small amount of salt and pepper.

Preparation method: Adam seared the salmon skin-side down until golden and cooked through, then rested it briefly. He sliced the vegetables and potatoes into uniform cubes, tossing them with olive oil, garlic, and salt until they were lightly caramelized. The vegetables were arranged around the pan, then the pan was covered to let the salmon cook gently alongside them. Lemon, butter, and herbs were added at the end to create a bright, savory sauce.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the rich scent of searing salmon, caramelized onions, garlic, and roasted vegetables.

Presentation: The pan was cleared and arranged with the salmon in the center, vegetables and potatoes around it, and the lemon-butter sauce spooned over in a thin, glossy layer. The pan was brushed with a little butter and placed in the oven until the salmon became tender.

Occasion: A quiet evening of preparing for the mayor’s Tuesday, February 14, confirmed appointment. The meal was neither hurried nor celebratory; it was a calm, thoughtful dinner intended to reflect Adam’s reflective nature while giving him a little time to settle.
