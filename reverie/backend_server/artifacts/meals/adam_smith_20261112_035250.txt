Meal: Smoky Herb Roast Chicken with Creamy Potatoes and Croutons

Adam began the morning with a simple breakfast of whole-grain toast, a glass of water, and a small fruit. He then prepared a modest dinner for himself, using the remaining ingredients from his previous meals. The centerpiece was a whole chicken rubbed with olive oil, smoked paprika, garlic, thyme, and a pinch of salt, then roasted until the skin turned golden and the meat was tender. Around the same time, he roasted potatoes with butter, rosemary, and a splash of cream until they were fluffy and caramelized. The finished dish was arranged on a white ceramic platter, with the golden chicken taking center stage and the potatoes and croutons surrounding it.

The aroma filled the kitchen with toasted herbs, savory poultry, and the warm scent of roasted potatoes. It was a quiet, thoughtful meal—simple yet nourishing—prepared for a calm Monday morning. Adam would eat it alone, reflecting on the ideas shaping his book about creativity and wondering about the local mayor’s upcoming campaign.
