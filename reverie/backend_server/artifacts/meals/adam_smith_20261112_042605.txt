Adam prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Berries and Cinnamon Toast. He boiled oats in water, seasoned them with cinnamon, nutmeg, and a pinch of salt, then topped them with sliced strawberries, blueberries, and a drizzle of honey. He toasted a few slices of whole-grain bread beneath the berries and spread some jam on one side.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, cinnamon, berries, and honey. When Adam finished, the breakfast looked inviting and thoughtfully arranged: the oatmeal cups were neatly stacked, the berries bright against the soft toast, and the honey glistened in the sunlight. It was a simple, nourishing breakfast, not a grand feast. It was meant to be a quiet, reflective meal before he left for work, perhaps while he pondered ideas about creativity and the local mayoral election.
