Adam prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Herb Creamy Chicken, a simple breakfast suitable for a quiet Tuesday morning. He sautéed carrots, parsnips, and potatoes with garlic and herbs, then roasted them until their edges turned golden. He marinated chicken breast in lemon juice, olive oil, salt, pepper, and fresh thyme before baking it until the skin was crisp and the interior was tender. The dish was finished with bright lemon slices and a drizzle of creamy yogurt.

Aromatic rosemary, garlic, and the earthy sweetness of the roasted roots filled the kitchen. Adam plated the meal in a shallow white bowl, arranging the vegetables beneath the chicken and garnishing it with fresh mint and lemon wedges. It was a humble, nourishing breakfast—simple enough for a quiet morning, yet hearty enough to make him feel satisfied and ready to continue his day.
