Adam Smith prepared a simple Smoked Salmon and Herb Toast with Sourdough, a quiet breakfast reflecting the thoughtful, reflective nature of his life. The toast used lightly toasted sourdough slices, while the salmon was seasoned with smoked sea salt, fresh dill, minced chives, and a drizzle of warm olive oil. It was served with lemon-sesame dressing, a small bowl of roasted radishes, and a side of warm oatmeal.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, savory smoked fish, and delicate herbs. He arranged the toast on a white ceramic plate, layered the salmon over it, and placed the radishes and oatmeal neatly beside it. The presentation was elegant yet understated, with the golden toast, red salmon, and crisp radishes creating a colorful contrast.

This was a simple Tuesday breakfast, prepared after a long day but before the day’s meaningful work. He intended to carry the meal and the groceries home, sharing the quiet moment with Yuriko and continuing his story of kindness, respect, and community support.
