Adam woke at 4:00 AM to prepare a simple, nourishing breakfast for his early morning walk. He cooked a Quinoa and Lemon Herb Breakfast Bowl in a small nonstick pan: sautéed quinoa, cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, and a generous handful of spinach, then added a splash of lemon juice, olive oil, and fresh herbs. The warm, slightly savory aroma lifted the kitchen, while the bright yellow quinoa and crisp vegetables created a cheerful contrast.

He arranged the ingredients in a shallow white bowl, placing the quinoa and tomatoes in the center and arranging the greens and cucumber around it. A small lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley added a final touch of color and freshness. He ate it slowly, savoring the wholesome flavors. This modest meal was meant to energize him for his morning reflection and the local election debates, but it also felt like a quiet tribute to the creativity and curiosity that define his life.
