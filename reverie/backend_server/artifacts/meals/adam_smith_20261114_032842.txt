On a crisp Monday morning, Adam Smith woke at 4:00 AM to prepare his breakfast for dinner. He chose a simple, nourishing meal of Oatmeal with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Curly Bacon. The cooked oats formed a creamy base, while carrots, potatoes, and zucchini were roasted until golden and tender with a crisp skin. Sliced curly bacon added savory bite, and a bright lemon juice preserved the vegetables’ fresh flavor.

Adam prepared the ingredients in the kitchen, heating the oats in a pan before seasoning them gently. He added the vegetables and cooked bacon until everything released its oils and softened. As the pan warmed, the aroma filled the room: nutty oats, caramelized carrots, savory bacon, and the sharp scent of lemon.

Adam arranged the meal in a simple white bowl, placing the warm oatmeal at the front with the vegetables arranged around it. A small plate of sliced apple and a glass of water completed the presentation. This was a quiet, reflective breakfast—not a formal occasion—but one prepared for a productive day, balancing nourishment with the thoughtful mind behind the book on creativity.
