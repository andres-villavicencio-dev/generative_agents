On Monday, February 13, Adam prepared Lamb with Roasted Vegetables and Herbs as part of the Hobbs Cafe lunch menu. He chose a tender shoulder of lamb, marinated for an hour with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and lemon, then seared it in a hot pan until the exterior developed a golden crust. Afterward, he roasted carrots, parsnips, and potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a few slices of lemon until the skins softened.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted lamb, caramelized garlic, and earthy herbs. Adam presented the dish in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the vegetables in a colorful halo around the lamb and garnishing it with fresh parsley and a generous drizzle of herb oil. It was served alongside crusty bread and a small side salad, prepared for a community lunch gathering.

Adam thoughtfully considered the balance of flavors and textures while ensuring the ingredients were measured precisely—reflecting his philosophical habit of examining the details of everyday life. The meal was simple, nourishing, and communal, fitting both the cafe’s practical needs and his own contemplative nature.
