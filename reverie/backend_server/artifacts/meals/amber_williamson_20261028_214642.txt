Dish Name: Rosemary-Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Lime Butter

Amber prepared a comforting side dish for the Saturday story hour at Oak Hill College, combining roasted carrots, parsnips, and red potatoes with a bright lemon-lime butter sauce. The vegetables were peeled and halved, arranged on a large baking sheet, and tossed with olive oil, minced rosemary, sea salt, and freshly cracked black pepper. They were roasted in the oven until the skins softened and the interiors became tender and lightly caramelized.

For the sauce, Amber melted butter with finely grated lemon zest and juice, then added a splash of white wine, a knob of butter, and chopped parsley. The vegetables were returned to the pan, covered, and simmered until everything was bubbling gently.

The dish had a warm, earthy aroma of roasted carrots and rosemary, balanced by the bright zest of lemon and the soft richness of butter. It was presented in a clear glass bowl, with the vegetables arranged in two neat rows and a small bouquet of rosemary placed on top. The presentation looked inviting and simple, perfect for sharing at the library’s story hour with Andrea Lovetro and Jennifer Moore’s painted backdrops.
