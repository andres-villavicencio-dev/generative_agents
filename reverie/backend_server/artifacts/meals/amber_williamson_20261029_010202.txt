On Tuesday, February 14, Amber prepared Chicken-and-Vegetable Breakfast Bowl for the family lunch on her home kitchen table. She combined tender chicken breast, crisp carrots, crisp onions, fresh spinach, and a handful of cherry tomatoes with a savory chicken stock sauce, topped with a drizzle of lemon-herb oil.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of simmering chicken, roasted vegetables, and freshly baked bread. Amber arranged the colorful bowl on the table, placing the golden chicken alongside the crisp vegetables and bright green spinach, and added a small portion of warm bread for serving.

The meal was simple and nourishing, designed to be enjoyed with friends or family during a quiet Tuesday morning. It marked the beginning of a busy but purposeful day, reflecting Amber’s warm nature and love of sharing simple, homey meals.
