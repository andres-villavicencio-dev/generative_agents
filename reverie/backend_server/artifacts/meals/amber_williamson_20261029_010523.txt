On a quiet Tuesday morning, Amber Williamson prepared Sunflower Vegetable Lunch, a simple, nourishing meal she made while reflecting on the purpose of her day. The dish featured crisp sunflower hearts, cherry tomatoes, carrots, leeks, and a few capers, dressed with olive oil, lemon juice, and a touch of garlic.

She heated the oil in a pan, then sautéed the vegetables until they were tender and lightly caramelized. After a final quick stir, she added a pinch of lemon and toasted a few garlic cloves. The golden aroma of frying vegetables and garlic filled the kitchen, while the bright colors and plump ingredients created a cheerful, inviting presentation.

The meal served herself at 5:00 p.m. for lunch, a relaxed moment before a quiet evening. It was her daily lunch—a modest, home-cooked meal that felt both simple and deeply meaningful, offering comfort after a busy day spent with storytelling, gardening, and creative planning.
