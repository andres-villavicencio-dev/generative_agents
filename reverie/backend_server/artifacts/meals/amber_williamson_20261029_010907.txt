Amber prepared Garden Herb Chicken with Lemon-Butter Sauce, a simple home-cooked lunch for her family. She chose chicken thighs, seasoned with garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper, then pan-fried them until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. A bright lemon-herb sauce was poured over the chicken before the final pan sear, adding brightness and depth to the meal.

The kitchen smelled warm and earthy—aromatic chicken, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and softened butter mingled with the green leaves of her garden herbs. For presentation, Amber arranged the chicken in the center of the plate, garnished with fresh parsley and a few edible flowers, and placed a small bowl of lemon wedges beside it. The dish was simple but inviting, with the creamy sauce glistening and the fresh garnish adding a cheerful touch.

It was a relaxed family meal on a Tuesday, made with care and the warm anticipation of a shared evening. Amber was pleased with the colour and texture, adjusting the heat if the sauce needed a little more brightness before serving.
