For Tuesday, February 14, Amber prepared Smoked Salmon and Roasted Vegetable Salad, a warm, nourishing breakfast. She arranged the salad in a shallow bowl and placed a few strips of smoked salmon on top, finishing the dish with a few thin slices of lemon and a drizzle of olive oil.

The key ingredients were ripe apples, crisp carrots, onions, cucumbers, spinach, and the smoked salmon. The vegetables were roasted in a pan with a little olive oil, salt, and pepper until golden and tender, while the salmon was lightly seasoned and seared. Amber toasted the apples and served them whole, adding a touch of sweetness to balance the savory salmon.

The aroma was rich, warm, and inviting—smoked fish, caramelized onions, and fresh herbs blending together. She plated the salad simply, arranging the vegetables in a neat circle and the salmon on a small plate beside it. The presentation was understated and elegant, suitable for a quiet morning lunch. It felt like a thoughtful, family-style meal, prepared with the same care she would give to a story for her children.
