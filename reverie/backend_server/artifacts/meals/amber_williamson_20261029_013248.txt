Amber prepared Grain and Garden Soup with Crispy Carrots, a simple, nourishing dinner meant to bring the warmth of the Johnson Park community together. The soup was made with rice, lentils, diced carrots, green beans, and a small handful of wild mushrooms. She added a splash of vegetable broth, a pinch of salt, and a generous handful of fresh herbs, then simmered everything gently over medium heat.

As the soup simmered, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted carrots, mushrooms, and warm grains. Amber served it in a shallow ceramic bowl, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a drizzle of olive oil. The presentation was inviting and practical, with each bowl clearly labeled so everyone could see it was ready for sharing.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday dinner—perhaps a quiet conversation with a neighbour, a cup of tea, or simply a peaceful evening after a busy day. It was not a grand occasion, but it felt like a small celebration of the kindness and creativity that made Amber’s life meaningful.
