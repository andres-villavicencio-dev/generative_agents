On Tuesday, February 14, Amber prepared a hearty Savory Chicken and Root Vegetable Soup, fitting into her relaxed Tuesday routine.

The dish was a colorful family-style soup, with chicken breast, carrots, potatoes, parsnips, celery, onions, and a little cream of chicken. For the seasonings, Amber used thyme, rosemary, garlic, lemon zest, and a pinch of black pepper. She cooked the chicken gently until golden, then simmered the vegetables in the stock until the potatoes were tender and the soup had a rich, aromatic depth.

The kitchen smelled of warm herbs, roasted garlic, and simmering vegetables. Amber served the soup in a large bowl, arranged the carrots and parsnips in neat rows beside the chicken, and garnished it with a sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday dinner with friends, after she had finished sweeping and returning overlooked items to their places. She hoped the comforting meal would bring them together and remind them of the purposeful, creative day she had spent.
