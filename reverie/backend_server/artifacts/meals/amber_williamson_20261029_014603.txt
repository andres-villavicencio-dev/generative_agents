Amber prepared Heritage Garden Dinner, a warm, family-style meal inspired by the vegetables she selected that morning. The main component was a hearty vegetable stir-fry, with golden carrots, crisp broccoli, tender zucchini, and sweet potatoes sautéed in olive oil until lightly browned. She added a generous portion of frozen corn and chopped tomatoes, finishing the dish with thickened vegetable broth, a pinch of chili powder, and fresh parsley and lemon zest.

She began by sautéing the carrots and broccoli in a nonstick pan, then tossing in the zucchini and potatoes. As the vegetables softened, she stirred in the frozen corn and tomatoes, adding the broth and seasonings just before the final minute. The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of carrots, garlic, herbs, and caramelized vegetables.

The meal was plated on a large white platter, arranged in an arc of colorful vegetables, garnished with extra parsley and a bright lemon wedge, and accompanied by a simple mushroom and carrot soup. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday night dinner for her and her family, a simple yet comforting evening to celebrate the harvest and the steady progress toward her silk-scarf savings goal.
