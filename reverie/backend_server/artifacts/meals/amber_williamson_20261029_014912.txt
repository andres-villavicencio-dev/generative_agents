Amber’s dinner was Roasted Root Vegetable Stew with Herb Crust. The main ingredients were carrots, parsnips, potatoes, red onions, celery, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a splash of chicken stock. She sautéed the vegetables until softened, then added chopped chicken and broth, allowing them to simmer until tender. A golden herb crust of breadcrumbs, herbs, and a little oil was pressed over the top before the stew was baked until the edges became crisp and the juices bubbled vigorously.

The kitchen filled with a warm, earthy aroma of roasted carrots, garlic, thyme, and herbs. As Amber served the stew, she placed a small plate of fresh apples and cinnamon sticks beside it, offering it to every neighbor. The presentation was simple and welcoming: the stew nestled in a shallow oven-safe dish, garnished with the remaining herbs and a bright orange carrot, while the apples and cinnamon stood ready to brighten the table.

The occasion was a quiet neighborhood dinner on Tuesday, February 14, celebrating their shared community. Amber’s generous side of dessert, a warm cup of tea, and the promise to help everyone eat made the meal feel more than just food—it was an invitation to gather, share, and enjoy one another’s stories.
