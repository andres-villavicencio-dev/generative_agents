Dish Name: Rosemary and Lemon Herb Roast Chicken with Roasted Vegetables

Amber prepared the meal at home, choosing a simple yet rewarding dinner for Wednesday evening. She started with a whole chicken and a large yellow hen, roasting them together with rosemary, lemon zest, garlic, thyme, and a splash of olive oil. She added a colorful mix of carrots, onions, potatoes, and small peas, seasoning them with salt, pepper, and a little vinegar.

She basted the chicken in its own juices for the last few minutes, allowing the skin to turn golden and crackle. The vegetables softened around the edges, while the chicken remained moist and tender. Amber served the chicken alongside the roasted vegetables, garnishing it with fresh lemon slices.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted herbs and citrus. Amber presented the meal on a large white platter, arranging the golden chicken and vibrant vegetables in an inviting, asymmetrical pattern. The occasion was a quiet, intimate evening—no rush, no guests—just the satisfaction of making something delicious and enjoying company with Abigail. The warm meal felt like a peaceful reminder of the fulfilling day she was living.
