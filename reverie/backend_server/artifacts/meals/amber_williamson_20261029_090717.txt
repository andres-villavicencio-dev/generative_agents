The meal was a simple Blueberry and Maple Crumble Breakfast, prepared with the morning’s strongest ingredients. Amber gathered ripe blueberries, maple syrup, walnuts, oats, and a splash of honey. She toasted the oats, chopped the walnuts, and gently folded them into a warm batter, then baked it in the oven until the edges were golden and the crumble turned soft.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the oven rose: sweet maple, toasted oats, and the tart, fresh scent of blueberries. When she plated the dish, a generous mound of oatmeal served in a clear glass bowl, topped with crumble and a sprinkling of cinnamon, stood invitingly beside a cup of tea.

The occasion was a peaceful Thursday morning, part of her steady routine toward her silk-scarf savings goal. She had cleared the workspace and gathered the supplies before heading to The Willows Market and Pharmacy, leaving the kitchen bright and full of purpose.
