Amber prepared Savory Grilled Vegetable Scramble for a quick, nutritious breakfast and lunch at Hobbs Cafe on Friday, February 17.

She mixed cooked eggs, mushrooms, bell peppers, onions, and spinach with a little cream and chopped cheddar. The ingredients were gently scrambled in a non-stick pan until golden and evenly cooked, then served over fluffy toast with a side of warm fruit salad.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of toasted butter and roasted vegetables, followed by the fresh, savory scent of the herbs. Amber plated the meal with colorful, neat rows of vegetables and a generous portion of golden eggs, creating a warm, inviting presentation.

She chose the dish as a flexible lunch to balance her busy day, using the cafe’s spacious table for a leisurely meal with colleagues and friends. The warm, comforting food made her feel relaxed and fulfilled, fitting the generous, imaginative spirit she shares with everyone she meets.
