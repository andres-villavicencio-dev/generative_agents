Amber prepared Herbs Garden Breakfast Sandwiches at the Hobbs Cafe counter. She layered toasted bread with golden poached eggs, crisp lettuce, sliced tomatoes, chives, and a generous amount of herb butter. The sandwiches were finished with a small bowl of warm oatmeal, maple syrup, honey, and sliced banana, while a cup of steaming oat tea accompanied the meal.

The kitchen smelled of toasted nuts, fresh herbs, butter, and the gentle sweetness of fruit. Amber arranged the sandwiches in the café’s warm counter display, placing a colorful basket of fruit beside them. For her Friday, the occasion was a moment of gratitude—a quiet appreciation of the fulfilling day, the people around her, and the small moments that bring joy.
