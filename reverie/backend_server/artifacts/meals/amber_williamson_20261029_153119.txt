Amber Williamson prepared Golden Herb Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and Honey, a warm, nourishing breakfast she made before heading to Hobbs Cafe. She began with a pot of oatmeal, adding rolled oats, cinnamon, a pinch of salt, and a splash of milk to create a soft, creamy base. She topped it with sliced apples, pears, blueberries, walnuts, and fresh herbs, then folded in the honey for a gentle sweetness. After cooking the oatmeal, she arranged it in a small white ceramic bowl, placing the crisp fruit and nutty walnuts on top. A small glass of lemonade and a cinnamon stick were tucked beside it.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of oats, cinnamon, toasted nuts, and warm honey. Her presentation was simple yet inviting: the golden oatmeal stood beneath the colorful fruit, while the herbs and walnuts added texture and visual contrast. The occasion was a quiet Friday lunch at Hobbs Cafe, where Amber sat with another customer, chatting over tea while enjoying her carefully prepared meal.
