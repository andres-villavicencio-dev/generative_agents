Amber Williamson prepared Grain-Silk Breakfast with Honey and Fresh Fruit, a warm, nourishing lunch for herself at Hobbs Cafe.

The dish began with oatmeal and golden raisins, simmered in a little honey until the oats softened. She stirred in chopped apples, sliced bananas, and a generous handful of fresh berries. A small portion of sliced bacon and syrup was added for extra warmth, while milk was heated to a gentle simmer.

As the breakfast came together, it filled the kitchen with the rich aroma of honey, roasted oats, caramelized fruit, and lightly smoked bacon. Amber arranged the colorful ingredients on a small tray, arranging the berries and apples around the oatmeal and drizzling the syrup over the top. A silk scarf and a warm mug of tea completed the presentation.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Friday lunch, a reflective moment after a full day of creative work, community service, and conversation with friends. It was simple, comforting, and beautifully balanced—a fitting tribute to her warm, imaginative nature.
