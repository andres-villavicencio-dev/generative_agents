On Friday, February 17, Amber prepared a warm Mushroom and Cream Soup for breakfast at Hobbs Cafe, then had lunch for the day. The dish was a comforting, rustic bowl of creamy mushroom soup served over whole-grain toast with a simple poached egg.

Key ingredients included button mushrooms, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, vegetable stock, butter, flour, and a little cream. Amber sautéed the mushrooms until their colors softened, then added the vegetables and seasonings and simmered everything gently for thirty minutes until the broth was rich and the vegetables were tender. The soup was finished with a few fresh herbs and a splash of cream for a light, nourishing finish.

Aromatic and earthy, the broth smelled of mushrooms, caramelized onions, and warm butter. Amber presented the soup in a simple white bowl, arranging the golden toast and poached egg beside it. She added a small garnish of parsley and a generous amount of sugar to balance the savory flavors.

The occasion was a quiet, satisfying Friday lunch at Hobbs Cafe—her favorite place to make a meal while giving back to the community. Paying for her lunch and receiving change felt like a generous reward for her time in the kitchen, and the warm soup completed a full, restorative day.
