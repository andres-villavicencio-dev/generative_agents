Amber prepared Morning Garden Soup, a warm, nourishing breakfast she made for herself at Hobbs Cafe. The soup featured tender carrots, celery, onions, tomatoes, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice, with a creamy broth made from vegetable stock and a pinch of rosemary. She simmered the vegetables until their flavors released, then blended them with a small amount of cream and a generous handful of fresh chives for texture.

The kitchen filled with the inviting aroma of roasted garlic, carrots, and earthy herbs. Amber plated the soup in a simple white bowl, garnishing it with chopped chives and a few sprigs of rosemary. She added a slice of lemon and a tiny knob of butter to the edges before serving.

The meal was a quiet Friday reflection: she had spent the morning helping with the story-hour backdrop, sketching the garden, and reviewing her silk-scarf progress. Her generous, imaginative nature made the comforting soup feel like a celebration of creativity and community, fitting perfectly with her pride in Cledith’s grant proposal and her warm connection with colleagues and friends.
