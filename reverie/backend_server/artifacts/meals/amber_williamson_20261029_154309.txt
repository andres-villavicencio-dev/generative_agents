Amber Williamson prepared a Hearty Garden Breakfast, a comforting dish of slow-cooked eggs, creamy corned beef, diced tomatoes, and a generous portion of fresh, sautéed spinach.

The ingredients came from The Willows Market, where she carefully selected fresh eggs, corned beef, tomatoes, and organic vegetables. She prepared the eggs by whisking them with a little milk until fluffy, then poaching them in a skillet. The beef was cooked slowly until tender, while the tomatoes were softened and the spinach was wilted just before serving.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the rich scent of caramelized beef, earthy tomatoes, and the bright freshness of herbs. To present the breakfast, she arranged the eggs and spinach on a white ceramic plate, serving the corned beef and tomatoes on the side in a small bowl. The setting was simple and welcoming, fitting the quiet, celebratory occasion of a fulfilling Friday.
