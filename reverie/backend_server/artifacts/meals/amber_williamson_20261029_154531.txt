Amber prepared a comforting Grandmother’s Vegetable Soup with Crispy Roast Potatoes, a simple yet hearty meal made for her Friday lunch and dinner. She began by washing and chopping carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, and green beans, while sautéing garlic and herbs in butter for a fragrant base. The vegetables were gently simmered in vegetable stock until tender, then finished with a splash of cream and a pinch of salt.

Amber roasted the potatoes until their edges were golden and crispy, then plated them alongside the soup, garnished with parsley and a drizzle of olive oil. She ladled the warm, fragrant broth over them, creating a colorful, inviting presentation that felt cozy and nostalgic.

The aroma of caramelized onions and roasted garlic filled the kitchen, while the golden potatoes and bright greens added warmth and cheerful color. She prepared the meal on February 17, Friday, a quiet Friday after a week of creative work, community service, and time with friends. Afterward, she enjoyed a cup of tea and reflected on her fulfilling day, proud of her garden sketch, silk-scarf progress, and support for Cledith’s grant proposal.
