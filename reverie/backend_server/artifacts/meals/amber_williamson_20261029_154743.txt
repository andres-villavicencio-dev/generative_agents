Breakfast: “Golden Morning” Scramble with Wild-Mushroom Pancakes

Amber prepared a hearty breakfast for herself at Hobbs Cafe, serving it in the quiet kitchen where she would prepare dinner and clear the dishes afterward. The meal featured fluffy golden pancakes studded with wild mushrooms, fresh parsley, and a generous swirl of creamy yogurt, balanced with a crisp tomato-and-herb salad.

She cooked the pancakes in a pan over low heat until the edges turned crisp, then folded them gently into a warm bowl. The mushrooms added earthy depth, while the yogurt and herbs gave the dish a soothing, comforting flavor. A small bowl of warm honey-mustard dressing and a few crisp apple slices completed the spread.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of wild mushrooms, the soft puff of pancakes, and the bright fragrance of herbs. She plated the food neatly, arranging the pancakes, salad, and fruit in a warm, inviting breakfast basket. It was a simple yet thoughtful meal, made especially for a reflective Friday morning as she prepared for her story-hour backdrop and continued her creative work.
