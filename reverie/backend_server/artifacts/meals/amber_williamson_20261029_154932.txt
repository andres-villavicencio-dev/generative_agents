On Friday, February 17, Amber prepared a simple yet comforting Garden Herb Breakfast Bowl for herself and her friends at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured fluffy oatmeal, toasted almonds, dried figs, a generous handful of chopped fresh herbs, a spoonful of warm yogurt, and a drizzle of honey.

She prepared the oatmeal by cooking it in a pan with water and a little butter until the grains softened and the mixture became creamy. After cooling, the ingredients were mixed together, then topped with toasted nuts and sprinkled with fresh herbs. The aroma filled the cafe: sweet, earthy, and fragrant with basil, thyme, and a hint of cinnamon.

The meal was presented in a white ceramic bowl, arranged alongside a small plate of crisp fruit and a cup of tea. It was part of a quiet Friday meal, reflecting Amber’s fulfilling time with friends and creative work.
