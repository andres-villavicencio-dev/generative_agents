Amber prepared Sunday Scramble with Roasted Tomatoes and Chive Cream, a warm, comforting breakfast designed for her Friday afternoons. The dish consisted of fluffy scrambled eggs, crisp bacon, roasted tomatoes, chives, and a touch of lemon juice, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little olive oil.

She cooked the eggs in a nonstick skillet over medium heat, letting the yolks remain soft while setting the whites, then added the bacon and tomatoes. After a few minutes, she stirred in the chives and finished with a squeeze of lemon. The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of bacon, sweet tomatoes, and warm herbs.

She arranged the eggs and vegetables in a shallow ceramic bowl and placed a small plate of toasted nuts beside it. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the bright red tomatoes and golden bacon contrasting against the pale eggs and green chives.

It was the perfect Friday breakfast for her return home to prepare lunch, followed by dinner. The meal reflected her warm, generous nature and her creative, imaginative spirit.
