On Friday, February 17, Amber prepared Cranberry Sage and Root Vegetable Soup for a simple, nourishing dinner. She selected crisp carrots, parsnips, red and yellow potatoes, and baby spinach from The Willows Market, along with fresh cranberries, onions, garlic, thyme, and a handful of herbs.

The preparation began with the root vegetables chopped and simmered in a pot of water, garlic, and thyme until tender. The spinach was added last, just before the soup was finished, to retain its bright green colour. Amber stirred in the cranberries and reserved herbs for the final garnish.

A warm, fragrant aroma filled the kitchen: the sweet earthiness of the carrots, the sharp tang of the cranberries, the subtle earth of the herbs, and the comforting scent of roasted garlic. She served the soup in a shallow ceramic bowl with a small mound of roasted parsnips and fresh greens around the edges.

The meal was prepared in celebration of a quiet Friday evening. After a fulfilling day of creative work and community service, Amber’s neighbours gathered to enjoy a warm, simple dinner, sharing stories while the soup simmered in the kitchen.
