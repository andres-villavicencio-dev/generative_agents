Amber prepared Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and Herb Crisp, a warm, comforting meal fitting her retirement, creative work, and desire to share food with friends.

She began with a small pot of beef broth, then seared a shoulder roast, adding onions, carrots, and potatoes until the edges browned. After browning the meat, she returned it to the oven along with the vegetables, covering the dish to let it simmer gently. For the crisp, she chopped carrots, parsnips, and potatoes with herbs, garlic, and a little olive oil until they turned golden and crisp.

The kitchen filled with a rich aroma of roasted beef, carrots, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and earthy herbs. The roast rested beneath a golden crust, while the root vegetables stood bright and crisp beside it.

Amber served the meal at The Willows Market, where she was buying groceries and preparing dinner. It was a quiet Friday evening, filled with friends, creative work, and the warmth of community.
