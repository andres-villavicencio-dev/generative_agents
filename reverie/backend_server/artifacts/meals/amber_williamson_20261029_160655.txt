Amber prepared Golden Sage’s Garden for a Friday family dinner. The dish featured roasted carrots, baby potatoes, thyme, garlic, olive oil, lemon, and a generous portion of wild mushrooms, with a little cream of mushroom soup added for richness.

She sautéed the mushrooms in a pan until their edges turned golden, then roasted the carrots and potatoes until they were tender. The thyme, lemon zest, and garlic gave the vegetables a warm, herbaceous aroma, while the creamy soup added a comforting finish. She plated the vegetables in a shallow pan and topped them with a golden-brown crust of breadcrumbs, creating a rustic, inviting presentation.

The meal was served around 5:00 p.m. on a Friday to celebrate a fulfilling evening of creative work, community service, and time with friends.
