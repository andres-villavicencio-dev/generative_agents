Amber prepared Chicken and Vegetable Pot Pie for a hearty Friday dinner. Key ingredients included boneless chicken breast, onions, carrots, celery, diced tomatoes, thyme, flour, butter, and a rich egg custard filling. She gently browned the chicken, then sautéed the vegetables and cooked them in a buttery sauce, adding the tomatoes and seasoning before baking the filling to its golden-brown colour.

The pot pie was covered with flour and butter to create a flaky, golden crust and baked until bubbling and crisp on the outside. A spoonful of warm custard was poured over the filling, leaving a lovely golden-brown crust. It smelled warm, savory, and fragrant with onion, herbs, and toasted flour.

Presented in a large, rustic ceramic pie dish, the meal looked inviting and comforting. Amber served it with crusty bread, and she took a few bites, clearly enjoying the familiar, satisfying comfort of a well-cooked home dinner. It was a warm, convivial meal on a busy Friday afternoon.
