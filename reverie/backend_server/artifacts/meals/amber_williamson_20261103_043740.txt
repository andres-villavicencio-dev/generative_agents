For her Saturday, Amber prepared Clergy Lamb and Root Vegetables with Roasted Apricots, a simple, nourishing lunch and dinner that reflected her warm, imaginative mood and a modest budget.

The meal featured tender lamb chops marinated in olive oil, lemon, garlic, and dried herbs, with the pan juices basting through the lamb. Root vegetables—carrots, parsnips, and a small onion—were roasted until soft and golden, while bright orange apricots added sweetness and a festive touch. Fresh parsley and lemon wedges finished the dish.

Amber used a large cast-iron skillet, searing the lamb first to develop a crisp crust, then cooking the vegetables until they were tender. She finished the lamb with the pan juices, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a few whole apricots.

The aroma was rich and inviting—herbs, roasted garlic, lamb, and apricot filling the kitchen. The meal was presented simply but beautifully: the lamb rested on a white plate, surrounded by an array of colorful vegetables and golden apricots, with a small glass of red wine beside it.

It was a relaxed, family-style lunch, suitable for a quiet Saturday morning. Amber enjoyed it with her colleagues, reflecting on her garden and creative work while feeling grateful that the ingredients and company made the day feel full and meaningful.
