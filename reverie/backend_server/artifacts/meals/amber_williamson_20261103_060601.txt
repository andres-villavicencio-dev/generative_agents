On Saturday, February 18, Amber prepared a warm Chicken and Herb Roast in her garden, using fresh ingredients gathered at the Willows Market and Pharmacy. She chose boneless chicken thighs, carrots, onions, garlic, fresh parsley, and a handful of thyme. The herbs were finely chopped, the chicken was rinsed, and everything was arranged on a large baking sheet.

Amber arranged the vegetables around the chicken in a loose circle and brushed the mixture with olive oil, salt, pepper, and lemon juice. She then roasted it in the preheated oven until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender. As it cooked, the kitchen filled with the scent of garlic, thyme, rosemary, and roasted vegetables.

She presented the dish on a rustic wooden platter, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a squeeze of lemon. The colour combination of golden chicken, orange carrots, and green herbs made the meal look inviting. It was prepared for a quiet Saturday lunch, a relaxed gathering with colleagues and friends who had invited her to explore Oak Hill College and Willow Clinic, where she hoped to reconnect and make new friends while enjoying the garden’s simple, nourishing food.
