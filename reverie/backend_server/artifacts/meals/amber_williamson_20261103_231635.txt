Amber prepared Golden Morning Pears and Honey-Lemon Custard, a warm breakfast that reflected her imaginative and generous nature. The dish featured large, gently caramelized pears, honey, lemon zest, a soft custard, and a dusting of cinnamon. Her favorite ingredients were the ripe pears, fresh lemon, and the golden honey, which gave the meal a comforting aroma of sweet fruit, warm custard, and cinnamon.

Amber arranged the pears in a shallow bowl, placing the custard beside them, and served small pieces of the warm custard with a spoon. The presentation was simple and inviting: the translucent pears looked bright against the creamy custard, while cinnamon specks added a touch of warmth. She ate it slowly at home on a quiet Monday morning, taking each bite with care.

The occasion was her peaceful Saturday preparation before starting the Saturday story hour at the Oak Hill College library. As she ate, she thought about telling a story to children, sharing stories with neighbors, and helping Jennifer Moore’s backdrop paintings for the event.
