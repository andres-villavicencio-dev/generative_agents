On Tuesday, February 14, Amber prepared a Hearty Vegetable & Chicken Breakfast Sandwich for Sam, her morning companion. The dish combines a golden-brown chicken breast, crisp lettuce, sliced tomatoes, and shredded cheddar, all sandwiched between two toasted English muffin halves.

Amber sliced the chicken over a bed of sliced celery and carrots, seasoned it with salt, pepper, lemon juice, and a little garlic powder, then toasted the edges until the chicken was fully cooked. She added the vegetables, shredded cheddar, and a generous pinch of crushed red pepper flakes to the pan, covering the pan to let the flavors meld.

As the breakfast cooked, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of searing chicken, toasted bread, and earthy vegetables. Amber arranged the sandwich in a neat rectangular pan, garnishing it with fresh parsley and serving it with a small glass of orange juice. She intended to share it with Sam, though she also had plans for a Saturday story hour at the Oak Hill College library, potentially using Jennifer Moore to paint a colorful backdrop.
