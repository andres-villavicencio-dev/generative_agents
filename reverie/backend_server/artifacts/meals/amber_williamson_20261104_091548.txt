Amber prepared a Hearty Garden Vegetable Soup with Toasted Croutons and Lemon. She gathered carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, and fresh greens from the market, adding chicken stock, vegetable broth, and a pinch of salt. In a large pot, she sautéed the aromatics, then slowly cooked the vegetables and potatoes until tender. She reserved a little stock for the broth, then added the remaining vegetables and broth. The soup simmered until the potatoes were soft, with the carrots turning golden and the greens crisp-tender.

She finished it with toasted whole-grain bread, croutons, a drizzle of lemon juice, and a final sprinkle of fresh parsley. The aroma was rich and comforting—warm carrots, celery, onions, and herbs blending with the gentle aroma of stewed potatoes.

She presented the soup in a clear glass bowl, garnishing it with the crisp greens and golden croutons. The dish was served on Tuesday, February 14, during an informal Tuesday lunch, when Amber and Sam were preparing breakfast and discussing plans with Diane. The warm, colorful meal reflected her generous, imaginative nature and the fresh, earthy ingredients she gathered.
