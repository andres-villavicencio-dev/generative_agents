Amber Williamson prepared Chicken, Rice, and Vegetable Skillet Dinner, a warm family-style meal she made to satisfy her Saturday story hour at Oak Hill College.

The dish featured tender chicken thighs, fluffy long-grain rice, and colorful vegetables including carrots, peas, onions, and zucchini. She sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until soft, browned the chicken to its golden edges, and served the rice alongside the chicken and veggies. A simple lemon-herb sauce was poured over everything, and the rice was garnished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.

Her kitchen filled with the inviting scent of garlic, herbs, roasted chicken, and sweet carrots. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the ingredients were arranged in a wide skillet, with the golden chicken and creamy rice taking center stage.

The occasion was a special Saturday story hour at the Oak Hill College library, organized with Andrea Lovetro and Jennifer Moore, who was painting scenic backdrops for the event.
