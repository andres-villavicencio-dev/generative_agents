Amber prepared Heather and Honey Chicken, a gentle woodland-inspired meal for her Saturday story-hour gathering. The dish features a pan-seared chicken breast with rosemary, lemon, and a golden honey sauce, served over fluffy mashed potatoes with crisp kale and a small roasted root vegetable.

Before cooking, Amber washed the kitchen counter, wiped down the pots and pans, and put away the ingredients, creating a calm space for storytelling. She used a small wooden spoon and a simple skillet, seasoning the chicken gently and browning it until the skin turned richly golden. The sauce had a warm, honeyed aroma, while the herbs added a subtle, earthy scent.

The meal was presented on a simple white platter, with the golden chicken and creamy potatoes arranged alongside the green kale and root vegetable. Amber served a side of warm bread and offered a glass of water before everyone began the story-hour, creating a cozy, welcoming atmosphere of warmth, generosity, and shared imagination.
