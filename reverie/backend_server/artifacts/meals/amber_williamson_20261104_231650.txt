Amber prepared Sunday Garden Roast with Herb-Lemon Butter Chicken, accompanied by grilled asparagus, roasted root vegetables, and golden roasted potatoes. The chicken was seasoned with garlic, thyme, rosemary, lemon zest, and a touch of honey, then roasted until its skin was crisp and its interior tender. The vegetables were lightly coated in olive oil and seasoned before being arranged in a gentle mound beside a platter.

Amber arranged the sides neatly in a rustic ceramic platter, placing the colorful vegetables, golden potatoes, and crisp asparagus around the chicken. She finished the presentation with a silver serving spoon and a small plate of fresh fruit. The aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, honey, and lemon filled the room, while the warm amber tones of the chicken and vegetables contrasted beautifully with the terracotta and cream colors of the platter.

The meal was prepared for Tuesday’s lunch, a quiet morning in Johnson Park where Amber and her friends enjoyed a simple, wholesome lunch. Its gentle, neighborhood-friendly atmosphere reflected her warm, imaginative character and her hope that every neighbour’s story would be heard.
