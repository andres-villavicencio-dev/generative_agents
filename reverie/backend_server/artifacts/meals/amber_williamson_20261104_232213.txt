Meal Name: “Honey-Glazed Garden Sandwich”

Amber prepared a simple, bright sandwich for lunch, combining her love of nature with the warmth of a home-cooked meal. The sandwich featured toasted sourdough bread, crisp caramelized onions, thinly sliced carrots, crisp radishes, and a creamy mixture of mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, and finely chopped herbs. A small bowl of honey-glazed carrots and radishes served as a side, while a sprig of rosemary and the scent of toasted bread gave the dish an inviting aroma.

Presented on a lightly toasted wooden platter, the sandwich was arranged with loose toppings and a piece of fresh rosemary resting beside it. The warm, golden-brown edges of the bread and the vivid reds, greens, and golden yellows made it look cheerful and inviting.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday lunch, after a busy morning. Amber was preparing for a gentle woodland-themed story-hour, reflecting on Diane Bosse’s quick lunch and her own simple, comforting sandwich. The meal was not prepared for a grand celebration, but for a special conversation and a few hours of reading time in Johnson Park.
