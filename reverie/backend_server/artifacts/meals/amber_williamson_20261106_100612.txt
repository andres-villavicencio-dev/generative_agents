On Tuesday, February 14, Amber prepared Garden-Buttermilk Chicken with Roasted Vegetables. The meal featured chicken breasts marinated in buttermilk and herbs, roasted with carrots, potatoes, onions, and peas, and served with a bright green salad.

The preparation was simple and comforting: she seared the chicken, seasoned it, then roasted it in the oven until golden and juicy. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, rosemary, garlic, and salt before being added to the oven. A crisp lettuce salad with lemon dressing was prepared just before serving. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, savory chicken, and earthy roasted vegetables, a scent that reminded Amber of her stories and garden.

She presented the meal on a simple white platter, arranging the chicken in the center with colorful vegetables and a crisp salad beside it. The dish was ready for lunch at home, after a full but purposeful Monday when she had applied for a town-service job, prepared breakfast with Sam, and discussed plans with Diane.
