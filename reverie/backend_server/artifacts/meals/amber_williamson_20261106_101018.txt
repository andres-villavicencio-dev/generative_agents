On Tuesday, February 14, Amber Williamson prepared Spring Garden Sandwiches for lunch. She gathered fresh lettuce, crisp tomato slices, ripe avocado, bright cucumber, thinly sliced red onion, and toasted whole-grain bread, adding a touch of lemon-herb dressing for a bright, refreshing flavor.

She toasted the bread in a skillet until it turned golden, then layered the vegetables, avocado, and herbs on the sandwich. The dish was arranged on a white plate with a small garnish of mint and a sprig of parsley.

Its aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of toasted grains, lemon zest, and fresh herbs. Amber presented the sandwiches alongside a simple salad and a small bowl of warm fruit salad, making it a cheerful, family-style lunch. It was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday lunch and shared with her neighbors at Johnson Park.
