On Tuesday, February 14, Amber prepared a simple yet hearty Garden-Lentil & Herb Soup. She gathered red carrots, celery, green beans, onions, garlic, and fresh thyme, along with a can of cannellini beans. The lentils were simmered until tender, then stirred in with the vegetables to soften them and give the soup a creamy texture. A splash of vegetable stock, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt and pepper completed the dish.

The kitchen filled with the warm, earthy aroma of roasted garlic, celery, thyme, and simmering beans. Amber arranged the bowls and served the soup in clear glass bowls, garnishing each with a small sprig of parsley and a drizzle of olive oil. The presentation was simple and inviting, with the colorful vegetables contrasting against the pale broth.

It was prepared for a quiet Tuesday lunch at home, after Sam’s breakfast. Amber’s warm, imaginative nature made the meal feel special rather than ordinary, reflecting the kind of thoughtful, everyday generosity she believed every neighbour deserved.
