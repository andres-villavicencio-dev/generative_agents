Amber prepared a simple chicken and vegetable stir-fry for her Tuesday lunch. The dish featured a whole chicken cooked in a skillet with carrots, celery, onions, and garlic, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little soy sauce. She added cooked rice and chopped green beans, then simmered everything together until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were lightly browned.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, and savory vegetable flavors. Amber presented the meal in a shallow white bowl, arranging the golden chicken and vibrant vegetables neatly around the rice. She added a small sprig of parsley and a squeeze of lemon to the plate, giving it a bright, cheerful finish.

The occasion was a relaxed, home-style lunch after visiting The Willows Market and Pharmacy. As she sat down, Amber looked pleased with the easy, comforting meal and enjoyed the quiet afternoon before planning her Saturday story hour.
