On Tuesday, February 14, Amber prepared Saffron Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce for a quiet, family-style lunch. She began with a small pot of water, added a pinch of saffron, and simmered the chicken in a gentle broth until it was tender. She roasted carrots, potatoes, and broccoli with olive oil, salt, and a little lemon juice until the vegetables were crisp and golden. The warm chicken was served alongside the bright vegetables and a simple lemon-herb sauce, finished with a few fresh herbs and cracked black pepper.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of saffron, roasted garlic, herbs, and caramelized potatoes. Amber arranged the colorful vegetables in a shallow serving dish and placed the chicken over them, serving it on a warmed plate with a lemon wedge and a small bowl of rice. It was not a grand occasion, but a cozy Tuesday lunch shared with Sam and Diane, giving her time to reflect on her new job and her plans for a Saturday story hour at Oak Hill College.
