On Tuesday, February 14, Amber prepared Smoked Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Crisp. The dish featured bone-in chicken thighs cooked with smoked paprika, garlic, rosemary, and a little olive oil, served over crisp root vegetables. Sliced carrots, parsnips, and a small onion were roasted until tender, their edges caramelized. Fresh spinach was tucked beneath the chicken, while lemon zest, chopped parsley, and a pinch of salt created a bright final touch.

Amber used a cast-iron skillet to sear the chicken before finishing it in the oven, allowing the smoke to penetrate the meat. The vegetables were tossed with a little oil and roasted alongside the chicken until golden and tender. A simple lemon-herb crisp was assembled with crusty bread for dipping.

The aroma filled the kitchen: warm smoked meat, roasted root vegetables, citrus, savory herbs, and the scent of home. Amber plated the meal in a wide, rustic ceramic dish, arranging the vegetables neatly and placing the chicken and crisp on top. She added a small bowl of roasted potatoes and a few fresh herbs for guests.

She prepared it for a Saturday story hour at the Oak Hill College library, using Jennifer Moore to paint backdrops. It was a quiet, purposeful meal for a community gathering—a simple lunch to nourish both her body and her imagination.
