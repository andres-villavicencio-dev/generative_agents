Amber prepared Garden Herb Roast Chicken with Creamy Garlic Potatoes and Green Beans.

She chose a whole chicken, trimmed it of excess fat, and rubbed it with olive oil, salt, and pepper. The potatoes were peeled, halved, and seasoned with rosemary, garlic, and butter, while the green beans were briefly blanched. The chicken was roasted in the oven until golden and crisp, while the potatoes turned tender and the green beans remained crisp.

Sam joined her while she watched the oven, chatting about her Saturday story-hour plans and the flowers growing in Johnson Park. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, rosemary, and butter.

The meal was presented on a simple, rustic wooden board, with the golden chicken resting beneath the potatoes and green beans. Amber plated it with a small bowl of lemon-herb sauce and a side of crusty bread. It was a cozy, nourishing lunch—a quiet reflection of her warm, imaginative nature and the peaceful life she enjoys at The Willows Market and Pharmacy.
