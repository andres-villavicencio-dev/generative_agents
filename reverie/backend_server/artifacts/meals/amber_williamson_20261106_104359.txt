Amber prepared Hearty Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Herb Creamy Potato Mash. The meal was made for her Tuesday morning breakfast, balancing a hearty breakfast with a simple lunch.

She roasted a whole chicken with rosemary and lemon, serving it alongside carrots, parsnips, and potatoes seasoned with herbs and salt. The potatoes were boiled, then mashed together with olive oil, garlic, parsley, and a little heavy cream to create a soft, flavorful side. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, rosemary, garlic, and creamy potatoes.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the golden-brown chicken was carved into neat portions, served on a platter with colorful root vegetables, and the creamy mash was garnished with fresh parsley. It was a wholesome, comforting meal for a quiet Tuesday morning, suitable for breakfast or a light lunch.
