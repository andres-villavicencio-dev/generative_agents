On a quiet Tuesday morning, Amber prepared a Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Soup with Crispy Chicken, a comforting meal inspired by her childhood kitchen stories.

She began by washing the dishes and wiping the counters, then seasoned a whole chicken with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and lemon. After searing the chicken until it turned golden, she added carrots, onions, celery, potatoes, and a splash of vegetable broth. The soup simmered for two hours, developing a rich, savory aroma of roasted herbs and warm potatoes. Amber served it in clear bowls, garnishing each dish with fresh parsley and serving the chicken separately for a crispy, flavorful finish.

The meal was made in the early morning, before her Monday service appointments. While Sam prepared breakfast, Amber discussed plans with Diane, including a Saturday story hour at the Oak Hill College library. On a full but purposeful Monday, she believed every neighbor had a story worth sharing, and this warm meal felt like a quiet reflection of generosity and imagination.
