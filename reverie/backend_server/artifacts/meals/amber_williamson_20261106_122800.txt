Amber prepared Garden-Rooted Vegetable Bolognese, a simple family dinner with carrots, celery, onions, tomatoes, mushrooms, garlic, pasta, and a little olive oil. She sautéed the vegetables first, then added the tomatoes, garlic, and mushrooms to cook them down into a rich, savory sauce. She stirred in the pasta and served the dish with a side of fresh green salad and a small lemon wedge.

As she cooked, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of sautéed garlic, roasted vegetables, and simmering tomatoes—aromas that made the house feel cheerful and inviting. The meal was presented on a white plate, with the colorful vegetable mixture arranged beside the pasta and garnished with a sprig of parsley.

It was a simple Tuesday dinner, prepared for a quiet evening at home. Amber had gathered the ingredients from her garden, picked a few flowers from Johnson Park, and shared them with Sam and Diane while discussing their plans and plans for the Saturday story hour. The meal reflected her warm, imaginative nature: practical, nourishing, and filled with the comforting stories of the past.
