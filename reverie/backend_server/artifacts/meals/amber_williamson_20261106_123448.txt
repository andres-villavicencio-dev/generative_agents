Amber prepared Sunday Scramble with Savory Spinach, Smashed Toast, and Roast Chicken, a hearty family breakfast suitable for Tuesday’s relaxed morning. The dish combined chopped fresh spinach, onions, tomatoes, and garlic with shredded chicken, cracked eggs, and shredded cheddar. It was cooked in a large skillet over medium heat, with the chicken added early and the spinach folded in at the end to keep it bright and slightly crisp.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory chicken, herbs, and the fresh scent of the spinach, while the toasted bread and golden cheddar added a warm, comforting finish. Amber arranged the breakfast on a white ceramic platter, placing the golden toast beside the colorful dish, a small bowl of fruit, and a glass of orange juice. She served it at 5:00 p.m., just before her planned story hour with Andrea Lovetro. The occasion was a quiet, purposeful morning—a simple, satisfying meal that would help clear her mind for the day ahead.
