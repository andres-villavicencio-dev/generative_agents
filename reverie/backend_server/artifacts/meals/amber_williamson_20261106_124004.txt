Amber prepared Mushroom and Sage Chicken Soup with Crispy Quinoa.

She used boneless chicken thighs, sliced cremini mushrooms, carrots, celery, onion, thyme, garlic, vegetable broth, olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt and pepper. The chicken was browned in a pan, then simmered with the vegetables and herbs until tender. After the soup had reduced slightly, she added a little cream and chopped fresh parsley for colour. The crisp quinoa was toasted separately, allowing it to absorb the warm broth.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted chicken, earthy mushrooms, warm broth, and the subtle sweetness of caramelized onion.

She presented the soup in a shallow white bowl, arranged the quinoa in a neat circle around it, and placed a small bowl of lemon wedges and a sprig of parsley beside the dish. It was a comforting, homey meal prepared for an early Tuesday morning breakfast, though Amber had already finished dinner. She also tidied the kitchen after eating.
