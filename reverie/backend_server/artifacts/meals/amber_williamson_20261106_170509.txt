Amber prepared Sunday Gravy Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Mashed Potatoes for a relaxed Sunday dinner, though she later prepared a hearty Saturday breakfast coffee batch at Hobbs Cafe.

The main dish consists of thighs of chicken, yellow onion, carrots, celery, garlic, thyme, and rosemary, all pan-seared until golden. She poured the cooked chicken into a rich, golden gravy made with beef stock, butter, flour, and a touch of soy sauce, then returned the vegetables to simmer until tender. Mashed potatoes were seasoned with salt, pepper, and a splash of cream.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted herbs, caramelized onions, and warm gravy. Amber plated the chicken on a white platter, arranging the vegetables around it and placing a bright red tomato garnish beside the potatoes.

The occasion was a family Sunday evening, with the group gathering to enjoy a comforting meal together.
