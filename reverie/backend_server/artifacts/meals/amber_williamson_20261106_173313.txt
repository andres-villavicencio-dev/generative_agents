Amber prepared a Creamy Apricot and Herb Breakfast Soup for her Saturday story hour at the Oak Hill College library, a gathering with Andrea Lovetro. The warm broth was made by slowly simmering chicken stock with diced carrots, celery, onions, and a small onion, then adding frozen peas, corn, and a handful of baby spinach. Her apricots were peeled and seeded, softened with a little lemon juice and a pinch of sugar until their soft, fragrant flesh released their sweetness, while the herbs of thyme, rosemary, and parsley kept the flavor bright.

The soup was finished with a generous drizzle of cream and a few drops of lemon zest, creating a velvety, comforting bowl of colors. Amber arranged it in a shallow ceramic dish and placed a small, decorative crocheted apricot ornament beside it. The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of caramelized apricots, rosemary, and creamy broth, inviting anyone sitting down to take a gentle, nourishing first step. The meal was prepared as a quiet, welcoming breakfast for the story hour, offering a cozy, nourishing start to the gathering.
