On Monday, February 13, Amber prepared Buttermilk Egg Benedict with Herbed Scrambled Eggs and Fresh Tomato Toast at Hobbs Cafe. Her breakfast consisted of three poached eggs set atop a golden, lightly browned pancake, served alongside crisp English muffins, fresh tomatoes, and a generous portion of her homemade creamy buttermilk sauce.

The dish was prepared the previous evening in a large pan. She sautéed the pancake until it cooked through, then added the poached eggs and a splash of buttermilk to create a velvety, slightly tangy sauce. Fresh herbs were sprinkled over the top, and the eggs were carefully slid onto the warm pancakes before the tomatoes and toasted English muffins were added.

The aroma of the meal filled the small café: rich, savory eggs mingling with the nutty scent of the pancake and the bright, acidic fragrance of the tomatoes. It was presented in neat, golden-brown ramekins with a small plate of fresh tomato wedges and the crisp muffins beside them.

Amber prepared the meal for her Saturday story hour at Oak Hill College, where she was working with Andrea Lovetro and hoping that Jennifer Moore would paint the café’s background. The occasion was warm, creative, and communal, with her generous spirit and the promise of a story for everyone to enjoy.
