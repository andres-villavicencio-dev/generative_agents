Amber prepared Johnson Park Breakfast Coffee with Honey and Fresh Herbs, a warm batch of oat-based coffee drinks with fresh herbs, honey, and toasted bread. The recipe called for water, oat milk, oat flour, honey, cinnamon, a pinch of salt, and chopped parsley or thyme. She stirred the mixture until smooth, added the honey, and poured it over slices of crisp oat bread.

The aroma was rich and inviting: roasted oats, warm coffee, honey, and the subtle scent of fresh herbs mingled with the faint smell of toasted bread. Amber presented the coffee cups in a simple white bowl, each one filled to the brim with a colorful garnish of lemon slices, mint, and a few candied seeds. On Saturday, she planned to serve the coffee as part of an Oak Hill College Story Hour, encouraging neighbors to share their stories with her, Andrea Lovetro, and Jennifer Moore’s painted-backdrop art.
