Amber Williamson prepared Morning Garden Breakfast for the family at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured fluffy scrambled eggs, crisp bacon, creamy tomato soup, toasted sourdough bread, and a serving of fruit salad. She added a few sprigs of herbs and sliced tomatoes to the bowl of soup.

The eggs were softly scrambled in a pan until they were set but still moist, then folded gently into the warm soup. The bacon was fried until golden, while the sourdough was toasted until crisp. Amber arranged the ingredients on a large white ceramic plate, garnishing it with fresh rosemary, parsley, and a bright red tomato wedge.

The aroma filling the kitchen was earthy, savory, and comforting—a rich blend of toasted bread, savory bacon, and the fragrant notes of herbs and tomato. It smelled inviting, wholesome, and ready to welcome friends.

The meal was part of Amber’s Saturday story hour at the Oak Hill College library, where she planned to share tales with children. Jennifer Moore had painted the library’s backdrop, giving the setting a colorful, storybook atmosphere.
