On Monday evening, Amber prepared Hearts of Oak Breakfast Soup for a Saturday story hour at the Oak Hill College library. The soup was inspired by her growing flowers in Johnson Park, using carrots, celery, onions, leeks, potatoes, pearl onions, and chicken broth. She added a splash of orange juice and dried herbs, allowing the flavors to meld gently before serving.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of roasted root vegetables, simmering broth, and warm herbs. Amber ladled the colorful soup into individual bowls, garnished each with a sprig of parsley and a small slice of fresh lemon, and arranged them neatly at the table.

The meal was meant to be warm and nourishing, paired with a simple loaf of sourdough bread and some fresh fruit. It was more than just breakfast—Amber treated it as a gentle invitation to gather, listen, and create together.
