Amber Williamson prepared a fragrant Rustic Chicken and Root Vegetable Bake, a warm supper for her family during the Saturday story hour. The main dish consists of two bone-in chicken legs, a whole roasted butternut squash, carrots, parsnips, and onions, all seasoned with dried rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a touch of smoked paprika.

The chicken was marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and garlic, then arranged on a baking sheet alongside the vegetables. The ingredients were roasted together in a conventional oven at 375°F (190°C) for about 45 minutes, until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender. Amber stirred in the last minute of cooking to crisp the edges of the squash.

As it finished, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted herbs, caramelized onions, and golden carrots. The dish was served on a large ceramic platter, with the chicken arranged around the root vegetables and a small bowl of lemon-herb sauce beside it. The meal was simple but comforting, fitting for a relaxed evening at Oak Hill College’s library.
