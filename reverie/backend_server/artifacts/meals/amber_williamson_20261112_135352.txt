Amber prepared a Garden-Heirloom Breakfast Bowl, reflecting her love of nature and home cooking. The dish featured a fluffy eggs Benedict-style scramble, soft poached eggs, and mushy spinach, all topped with thickly sliced tomatoes, red onion, and chives. She added a heaping hand of toasted nuts and seeds, with a drizzle of warm balsamic glaze and a final sprinkle of herbs and a pinch of sea salt.

The aromas were rich and inviting: the savory scent of buttery eggs, the earthy freshness of spinach, and the crisp sweetness of the tomatoes. The presentation was simple and inviting—a colorful, rustic mound in a small ceramic bowl, with a wooden spoon resting beside it.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday morning. After a reflective walk through Johnson Park, Amber sat down for her favorite breakfast, enjoying the warmth of the meal and the peaceful rhythm of her day before turning to read a few pages of her favorite book.
