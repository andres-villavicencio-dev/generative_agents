Amilcar prepared Cacahuates con Queso de Cabra, a warm Mexican breakfast featuring corn tortillas, black beans, shredded cabbage, diced tomatoes, avocado, and shredded cheddar cheese. For the beans, he simmered them with a little onion, garlic, and cumin, then added the shredded cheese to melt the top layer gently. The tortillas were toasted until golden and split open to serve the beans and vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted corn, caramelized onions, and melted cheese. The presentation was simple yet inviting: the tortillas arranged on a large plate, beans and vegetables scattered around, and the cheese creating a bright, creamy contrast.

He prepared the meal on Tuesday, February 14, while balancing his classes, market research, and work with John Lin. His meticulous nature made the careful cooking feel like a small act of civic thought: using fresh, seasonal ingredients and honoring the traditions of his grandparents’ farming family.
