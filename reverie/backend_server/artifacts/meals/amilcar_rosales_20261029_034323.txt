Amilcar prepared Egg-Cauliflower-Whitefish Breakfast for his morning breakfast at home. He combined fresh eggs, cauliflower, white fish, diced tomatoes, onions, spinach, and a touch of lime with a light cream sauce, serving it alongside crusty sourdough and a bowl of yogurt.

The dish was prepared in a large skillet over medium heat. The fish was lightly seared, then added to the pan with the vegetables and tomatoes until the eggs were set but the fish still flaked. Amilcar finished it with a pinch of chili powder and a few fresh thyme sprigs, letting it simmer briefly before serving. The warm skillet released a fragrant, savory aroma of garlic, citrus, and earthy vegetables.

The presentation was simple and inviting: the eggs arranged around the center, the whitefish and vegetables neatly arranged beside them, and the golden sourdough placed on the side. The bright colors, crisp textures, and warm lighting made the breakfast feel both nourishing and celebratory.

He prepared it on a quiet Wednesday morning while balancing his classes with farming work, market deliveries, and creative projects. It was a simple, practical meal for him and his family, but it felt special because he was treating it with the care and attention his meticulous nature required.
