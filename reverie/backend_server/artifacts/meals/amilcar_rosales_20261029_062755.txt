Amilcar prepared a Carnitas and Vegetable Casserole with Cornbread for dinner on Wednesday, February 15. The dish featured tenderized pork shoulder cooked in a slow-simmered tomato broth, slow-cooked potatoes, carrots, onions, bell peppers, and zucchini, with the cornbread baked alongside for a hearty Mexican-inspired side.

Amilcar began by sautéing the peppers and onions in olive oil until soft, then added the potatoes and carrots to brown. He stirred in a cup of tomato paste, a splash of vinegar, and a handful of fresh cilantro for brightness, then added the pork and covered the pan. The mixture was simmered on low for two hours, allowing the meat to become fork-tender while the vegetables softened. A final touch of chili powder and a sprinkle of cumin finished the dish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of caramelized pork, sweet tomatoes, and warm spices. Amilcar arranged the colorful vegetables and golden cornbread around the pan, serving the casserole on a rustic woven platter. He added a simple lime wedge to the side for a bright finish. The meal was a comforting family dinner, prepared in his home kitchen and enjoyed on a quiet Wednesday evening.
