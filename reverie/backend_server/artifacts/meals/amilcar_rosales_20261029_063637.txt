Amilcar prepared Crisp Roasted Winter Squash with Herb-Crusted Chicken, a Mexican-inspired meal that honored his grandparents’ traditions while reflecting his precision-agriculture background. The key ingredients were organic winter squash, chicken thighs, olive oil, garlic, onion, cumin, chili powder, oregano, and fresh herbs. He washed and cut the squash into uniform cubes, marinated the chicken in olive oil, spices, and citrus, then roasted both together in the oven until golden and tender.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of caramelized squash, warm cumin, roasted garlic, and the savory scent of chicken. Amilcar arranged the chicken on one side of the serving platter and placed the roasted squash in a colorful spiral of orange, yellow, and green, garnished with parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice. He served the meal alongside warm tortillas and a side of red potatoes.

The occasion was a warm Wednesday dinner at home, designed to balance his demanding academic and market-gardening schedule. The meal was practical and comforting, with a focus on simple, flavorful Mexican cooking and carefully portioned ingredients. Amilcar’s meticulous preparation reflected his commitment to sustainability and the quality of the produce he supplied to local communities.
