Amilcar prepared Hearty Barbecue-Garlic-Root Vegetable Med, a warm family-style dinner featuring carrots, onions, celery, potatoes, and tomatoes. He marinated the root vegetables and tomatoes in olive oil, garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and a splash of vinegar, then arranged them on a large baking sheet. The vegetables were roasted at 400°F until tender and caramelized, while the meat was grilled to mycor, giving the dish a rich, savory aroma of garlic, smoky wood, and roasted tomatoes.

Amilcar presented the meal in a rustic ceramic bowl surrounded by fresh herbs and a side of crisp lettuce. The golden, slightly glazed vegetables contrasted with the dark, caramelized meat, while the bright red tomato pieces added a pop of color. He served it with warm tortillas and a simple lemon-herb dressing.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed Wednesday dinner with Carmen Ortiz and John Lin, who arrived at John’s market to pick up supplies and discuss winter squash prices. The casual gathering turned into a friendly neighborhood dinner, with everyone sharing a warm, hearty bowl of vegetables and meat.
