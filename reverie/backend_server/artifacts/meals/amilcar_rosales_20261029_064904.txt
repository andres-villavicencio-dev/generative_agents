On a Wednesday morning, Amilcar Rosales prepared Grilled Chicken Thighs with Garlic Herb Crust and Roasted Winter Squash. The centerpiece was a pan of bone-in chicken thighs, seasoned with garlic, smoked paprika, oregano, black pepper, cumin, and a splash of lemon juice. The chicken was seared in a hot skillet until golden, then finished under the broiler until the skin was crisp and the meat was juicy.

Aromatic roasted winter squash—cucumbers, butternut, and acorn varieties—were prepared alongside the chicken. They were tossed with olive oil, fresh herbs, salt, and dried thyme, then roasted until tender. Amilcar also prepared a simple green salad with crisp lettuce, tomatoes, and a vinaigrette, adding brightness to the warm meal.

The aroma of the kitchen filled with toasted garlic, caramelized onions, roasted herbs, and the savory richness of the chicken. He arranged the chicken and squash on a serving platter, garnishing it with fresh parsley and the remaining crisp vegetables. The meal was presented for dinner on a cold Wednesday, balancing an easy, flavorful dinner with enough preparation time for Amilcar’s classes, market deliveries, and creative work.
