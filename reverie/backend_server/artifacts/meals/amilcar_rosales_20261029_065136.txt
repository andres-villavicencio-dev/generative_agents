The meal was Garlic-Root Vegetable Med with Roasted Chicken and Side Dishes, prepared for dinner on a cold Wednesday evening.

Amilcar chopped garlic and a variety of root vegetables—including carrots, onions, parsnips, and rutabaga—then sautéed them in olive oil until lightly browned. He added olive oil, thyme, and a splash of vinegar to the pan, then carefully roasted the chicken and side vegetables until the skin was crisp and the vegetables were tender. He served the dish over jasmine rice, garnished with parsley, and accompanied it with a simple potato salad and roasted root vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the warm, aromatic scent of roasted garlic, earthy root vegetables, and savory chicken. The golden chicken and crisp vegetables contrasted with the bright green herbs and pale rice, creating a colorful, rustic presentation. The meal was simple and comforting, fitting the quiet Wednesday evening when Amilcar balanced cooking with his math and physics classes.
