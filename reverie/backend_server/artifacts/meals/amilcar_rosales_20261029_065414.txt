Amilcar prepared Salsa Verde con Huevos Frescos y Pimientos Rojos, a traditional Mexican morning meal with fresh eggs, tomatoes, red peppers, onions, cilantro, garlic, lime, and a dash of cumin. The tomatoes and peppers were roasted until slightly soft, then diced and simmered with onion, garlic, and vinegar. He cracked the eggs into a shallow pan, seasoned the salsa with salt, pepper, and chili, and served it warm alongside the eggs, corn tortillas, and a small portion of winter squash.

The aroma was rich and earthy, with the scent of roasted peppers, garlic, and warm citrus filling the kitchen. The presentation was simple but inviting: the bright red salsa contrasted with the creamy white eggs and golden squash in a rustic bowl, while the tortillas and corn stood ready for serving.

The occasion was a quiet Wednesday dinner at home, intended to be a relaxed family meal. Amilcar prepared it with the same care he applied to his organic farm, blending precision agriculture with the familiar Mexican flavors of his grandparents.
