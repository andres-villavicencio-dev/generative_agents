Amilcar prepared a Mexican-style Vegetable Breakfast Platter for his market gardeners. The dish featured crisp corn tortillas, sautéed corn and green beans, shredded cheese, diced tomatoes, chili powder, garlic, and a generous drizzle of olive oil. He cooked the vegetables in a lightly oiled pan, toasted the tortillas, and arranged them on a large platter with a side of fresh fruit.

The kitchen filled with the familiar, savory aroma of toasted nuts, garlic, chili, and roasted vegetables. The presentation was warm and inviting: the bright green beans, red tomatoes, and golden cheese contrasted with the soft yellow tortillas, while a small bowl of warm sour cream and lime added a refreshing touch.

The meal was part of Amilcar’s Friday routine to support the organic cookout he was coordinating with suppliers and to offer a hearty breakfast for the market staff. Its simple, rustic preparation reflected his blend of precision agriculture and his family’s Mexican farming traditions, making it both practical and welcoming.
