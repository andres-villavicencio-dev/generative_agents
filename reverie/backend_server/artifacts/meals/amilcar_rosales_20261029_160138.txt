Amilcar prepared Escarole, Feta, and Roasted Tomato Gazpacho for a relaxed Friday lunch. The dish featured crisp escarole, creamy feta, ripe roasted tomatoes, a drizzle of olive oil, lemon zest, and a splash of white wine vinegar. He gently sautéed the scallions and garlic, then blended everything until the vegetables softened into a silky soup, followed by a final crisp from the oven to preserve their texture.

The aroma was warm and earthy—fresh basil, roasted tomatoes, garlic, and the crispness of the greens filled the kitchen. Amilcar plated the gazpacho in a shallow white bowl, garnished with a few leaves of escarole and a generous pinch of feta. The bright red of the tomatoes contrasted with the pale green lettuce and creamy feta, while the golden herbs added a cheerful pop of color.

The meal was casual and communal, fitting into a relaxed Friday lunch after he coordinated the organic cookout and checked his ingredients and prices. The setting was simple, with warm lighting and a few chairs arranged for friends or family to gather around.
