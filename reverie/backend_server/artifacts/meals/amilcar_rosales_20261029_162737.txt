Meal: “Mexican Heritage Garden” Vegetable and Egg Breakfast with Lime-Garlic Toast

Key ingredients: fresh tomatoes, onions, bell peppers, corn, zucchini, eggplant, red and yellow beans, black beans, cilantro, tomatoes, onions, garlic, olive oil, salt, cumin, chili flakes, lime, tortillas, and fresh eggs.

Preparation method: Amilcar washed and chopped the tomatoes, onions, peppers, and zucchini into a large pot. He sautéed the garlic and chilies in oil, then added the vegetables and seasonings and simmered them until tender. He cooked the corn, eggplant, and beans separately, then combined everything in the pot. He cracked the eggs over the mixture and let them cook gently. Finally, he prepared a warm tortilla with a little lime and cumin for serving.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the savory scent of garlic, chili, and tomatoes, mingling with the earthy aroma of roasted vegetables and the bright scent of herbs.

Presentation: The dish was arranged on a white ceramic platter, with the colorful vegetables arranged in overlapping rows and a generous portion of fluffy eggs on top. Warm tortillas, corn, beans, and fresh cilantro were placed beside it.

Occasion: Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner. Amilcar prepared the meal in his own style, blending precision agriculture with the familiar Mexican traditions inherited from his grandparents.
