Amilcar Rosales prepared Tortillas and Vegetable Frittata for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner.

The key ingredients were egg yolks, shredded chicken, diced tomatoes, onions, bell peppers, and a handful of fresh spinach. To make the frittata, he heated a pot over medium heat, sautéed the onions and peppers, then added the chicken and tomatoes, cooking until everything began to brown. He stirred in the eggs and spinach, covering the pan until the eggs set and the vegetables were tender.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and earthy: caramelized onions, savory chicken, and the bright green scent of fresh spinach. Amilcar presented the frittata in a rustic pan with a few crisp green peppers, garnished with chopped parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

The meal was served on a simple white tablecloth, with the frittata at the center and a plate of warm tortillas nearby. It was prepared for Andrea’s casual Friday dinner, combining fresh market ingredients with the familiar flavors of Mexican home cooking.
