Amilcar prepared Pan-Roasted Chicken with Salsa Verde and Roasted Vegetables for Andrea Lovetro’s dinner. The meal featured a whole chicken seasoned with garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, and oregano, alongside roasted carrots, potatoes, and bell peppers. A generous portion of spicy salsa verde was served with lemon wedges.

Amilcar measured the chicken carefully, trimming the skin and cutting it into uniform pieces, then seasoned the whole bird before placing it in a hot oven along with the vegetables. The cooking method combined slow roasting to develop the chicken’s deep brown crust with brief searing to keep the interior moist. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, earthy paprika, and savory cumin, while the bright green salsa added a lively citrus accent.

The presentation was simple and elegant: the chicken was arranged in the center of a shallow white platter, with the colorful vegetables and crisp tortillas arranged around it. A small bowl of fresh herbs and a glass of white wine were served alongside. The occasion was Andrea’s Friday dinner, reflecting Amilcar’s meticulous approach and his desire to blend modern cooking with his grandmother’s Mexican traditions.
