The meal was Carnitas with Roasted Vegetables and Warm Salsa Verde, a lively Mexican-inspired dinner prepared for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner.

Amilcar used two pork shoulder cuts, lightly marinated with cumin, garlic, oregano, lime zest, and a touch of chili powder, then seared them in a pan until the skin turned crisp and the meat fell apart. The pork was served over a bed of roasted carrots, potatoes, onions, and bell peppers, seasoned with smoked paprika and salt. A bright, herbaceous salsa verde was ladled over everything, with a side of corn tortillas and a cup of black tea.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of caramelized pork, warm spices, and roasted vegetables. The presentation was rustic and inviting: golden-brown pork on a white plate, colorful vegetables arranged neatly beside it, and the vibrant green salsa adding a pop of color. The occasion was a relaxed family dinner, and Amilcar’s meticulous preparation of the sauces and seasonings made the meal feel both comforting and celebratory.
