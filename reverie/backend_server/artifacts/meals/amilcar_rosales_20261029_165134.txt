The meal prepared for Andrea Lovetro was Mexican-inspired Chicken and Vegetable Soup with Corn Tortillas. Amilcar combined the freshness of farm-fresh vegetables with a flavorful chicken broth, creating a warm and satisfying dish perfect for an early Friday gathering.

Key ingredients included chicken thighs, carrots, celery, onions, tomatoes, bell peppers, garlic, cumin, chili powder, oregano, lime, and a small amount of vegetable stock. The chicken was browned in a skillet, then simmered with the vegetables until the broth had thickened, while the tortillas were toasted with the reserved drippings.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of cumin, smoked chicken, and caramelized vegetables. Amilcar presented the soup in a shallow ceramic bowl, garnished with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime, then arranged a few warm tortillas beside it. The occasion was an informal family dinner, and the meal was designed to feel both traditional and comforting, honoring Amilcar’s upbringing in Mexican farming traditions while reflecting his dedication to precision, freshness, and hospitality.
