Amilcar Rosales prepared Roasted Vegetable Tortilla with Warm Salsa Verde and Fresh Eggs, a rustic Mexican-inspired brunch for Andrea Lovetro.

The centerpiece was a soft corn tortilla topped with roasted red peppers, tomatoes, onions, and jalapeños, seasoned with cumin, chili powder, and a pinch of salt. The vegetables were lightly charred until their edges turned crisp and golden, releasing a fragrant aroma of roasted garlic and warm spices. A bright salsa verde—made from fresh jalapeños, cilantro, onions, and lime juice—was served alongside the tortilla, while two warm eggs completed the spread.

The meal was plated on a wide white plate, with the tortilla arranged in the center and the salsa spread in a thin, inviting line. Freshly cracked eggs were placed beside it, their glossy yolks contrasting with the warm reddish-orange tones of the vegetables. Amilcar added a small bowl of Mexican rice and a cup of chilled watermelon juice to the side, adding color and refreshing notes.

This wholesome dish was prepared for Andrea’s Friday dinner, honoring her mother’s cooking style while incorporating fresh produce and traditional Mexican flavors. Amilcar’s meticulous attention to seasonality and color gave the meal an inviting, family-style appearance.
