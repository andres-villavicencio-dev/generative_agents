Amilcar prepared Cebiche con Huevos y Zanahoria Roja, a Mexican-style grilled egg and vegetable breakfast. The dish featured marinated white eggs, sliced tomatoes, red bell peppers, onions, jalapeños, chilies, garlic, and cilantro, all cooked over charcoal and served on warm tortillas.

Amilcar cleared the cooking area, washed the utensils, and prepared the ingredients in meticulous attention to detail. He seasoned the vegetables with oil, salt, and garlic, then arranged the eggs and fresh produce neatly on the serving platter. The aroma of smoky peppers, garlic, and citrus filled the kitchen as he grilled the eggs until they were perfectly set and the vegetables tender and slightly charred.

The meal was presented in simple, rustic style, with the eggs arranged around the colorful vegetables and garnished with fresh herbs. The occasion was Andrea Lovetro’s dinner—Amilcar’s first prepared meal for the restaurant’s guests—and he wanted the food to reflect his grandmother’s comforting Mexican cooking while maintaining the precision and freshness that defined his market-gardener life.
