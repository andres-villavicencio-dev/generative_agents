Amilcar Rosales prepared Arroz con Pollo Tostado, a hearty Mexican-style dish for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner. He cooked the rice with chicken thighs, diced onion, garlic, bell pepper, and fresh cilantro, then added a generous portion of diced tomatoes. The pan sauces with a little olive oil, cumin, and a pinch of salt until the chicken was cooked through and the rice was tender.

The aroma was rich and warmly earthy: toasted chicken, caramelized onions, citrusy cilantro, and savory cumin filled the kitchen. Amilcar served the dish in a simple, rustic platter of white rice with the chicken, garnished with fresh herbs and a bright orange wedge of lemon.

The meal was prepared for Andrea’s relaxed Friday lunch, but the generous portions and care in its preparation made it suitable for either Andrea or Amilcar’s family. Amilcar’s meticulous attention to the remaining ingredients ensured the leftovers would be stored properly.
