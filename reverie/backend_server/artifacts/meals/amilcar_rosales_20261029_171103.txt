Amilcar Rosales prepared Carnitas with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Cilantro Rice, a warm Mexican-inspired brunch for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner.

The meal featured tender pork shoulder cut into bite-sized cubes, marinated in cumin, chili powder, garlic, onion, salt, and a splash of lime juice. It was slow-roasted until the meat fell off the bone and the skin became crisp. Sweet potatoes were roasted until caramelized, while cilantro rice was seasoned with smoked paprika, coriander, and garlic.

Aromatic, earthy, and warmly spicy, the dish released a fragrant scent of roasted garlic, chili, and tender pork as Amilcar prepared it. He arranged the pork on a platter, layered the potatoes around it, and topped everything with a bright garnish of chopped cilantro and lime. The simple, rustic presentation highlighted the golden-brown edges of the potatoes and the glistening juices of the juicy meat.

The meal was prepared for Andrea’s Friday dinner, a quiet family gathering where Amilcar’s careful attention to the kitchen workspace and attentive cooking created a warm, welcoming atmosphere.
