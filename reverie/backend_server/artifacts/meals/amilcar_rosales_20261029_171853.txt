Amilcar prepared a vibrant Mexican-Style Roasted Vegetable and Rice Bowl, a simple yet family-friendly dinner for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday lunch. The key ingredients were sweet potatoes, red and green bell peppers, carrots, baby spinach, garlic, cumin, oregano, and dried chili flakes, served with fluffy white rice and a small portion of shredded chicken.

Amilcar washed and peeled the produce, then arranged it in a shallow bowl. He sautéed the garlic and spices in a lightly oiled pan, added the chopped vegetables and chicken, and roasted them on a tray until the potatoes turned golden and the peppers softened. The rice was seasoned and cooked briefly before being mixed with the vegetables.

The aroma of warm cumin, roasted potatoes, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen. The colorful bowl presented with the rice in the center, vegetables arranged around it, and a bright garnish of cilantro and lime wedges. It was prepared for a relaxed, home-style family lunch, with the meal’s straightforward preparation reflecting Amilcar’s meticulous approach to combining Mexican farming traditions with everyday cooking.
