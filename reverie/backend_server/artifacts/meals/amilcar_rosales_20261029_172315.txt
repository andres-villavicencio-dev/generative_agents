Dish Name: Carnitas with Roasted Tomatoes and Garlic, served with Quinoa Salad and Cilantro-Lime Salsa

Amilcar prepared this hearty family-style meal for Andrea Lovetro’s dinner at her home. He began by washing and organizing his stainless steel cooking utensils, then sliced the pork, onions, and tomatoes into uniform pieces. The meat was seared in a hot skillet until the fat rendered and the edges turned golden, then marinated briefly in garlic, lime juice, and a touch of cumin.

Amilcar roasted the vegetables in the same skillet for twenty minutes until they softened and turned slightly brown. He seasoned the mixture with salt, pepper, and dried oregano, then set it aside. Next, he seasoned a quinoa salad with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and chili flakes, and prepared a bright cilantro-lime salsa with diced tomatoes, onions, and fresh parsley.

The aroma filled the kitchen with smoky pork, roasted tomatoes, garlic, and citrus. Amilcar arranged the quinoa salad in a shallow white bowl, the carnitas on top, and the salsa beside it. He added a generous drizzle of olive oil and a few fresh herbs for the final presentation.

The meal was prepared for Andrea’s Friday dinner, with the purpose of making a warm, flavorful family-style dinner while keeping the cooking utensils clean. Amilcar’s meticulous preparation and attention to detail made the dish both memorable and inviting.
