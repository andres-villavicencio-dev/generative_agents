Amilcar Rosales prepared Roasted Vegetables with Salsa Verde and Warm Rice, an elegant Mexican-inspired brunch dish designed for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner. The recipe combined heirloom tomatoes, red bell peppers, onions, and jalapeños with a spicy, herb-rich salsa verde. He sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until their sugars caramelized, then added rice and fresh cilantro before serving.

The aroma was warm and savory, with hints of roasted garlic, cumin, and the bright acidity of jalapeños. A few sprigs of fresh cilantro and a drizzle of warm olive oil gave the dish a cheerful, festive finish. He served the rice in simple white bowls, arranging the vegetables in a colorful mound beside a side of warm salsa verde.

The occasion was a relaxed Sunday-style dinner for Andrea Lovetro. Amilcar carefully arranged the ingredients and checked the cooking times, ensuring the flavors remained balanced while keeping the presentation inviting and homey.
