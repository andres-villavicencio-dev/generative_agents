The meal was Carnitas-Topped Black Bean Tostadas with Roasted Corn and Warm Salsa, prepared for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner at the home garden. Amilcar sourced fresh beans, corn, and vegetables from his market garden, seasoned them with a little cumin, chili powder, and garlic, then roasted everything until the corn turned golden and the beans softened.

For the carnitas, he marinated pork shoulder, smoked it, and cooked it slowly until the meat was tender and the skin was crisp. He sliced the pork, toasted the black beans, corn, and tortillas, and served them with a warm salsa made from tomatoes, onions, cilantro, lime, and chili.

The aroma was rich and earthy—smoky pork, roasted corn, warm beans, and fresh herbs. The presentation was generous and inviting: toasted tortillas arranged in a warm serving bowl, colorful toppings, and the crispy carnitas resting beside them.

Amilcar prepared the meal on Friday, February 17, to honor Andrea’s special occasion. His meticulous attention to the ingredients, prices, and cooking details showed his dedication to the organic cookout, while the relaxed atmosphere made the meal feel personal and celebratory.
