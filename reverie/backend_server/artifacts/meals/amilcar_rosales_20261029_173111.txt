Amilcar Rosales prepared Pan-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner. The dish featured a whole chicken, chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, green beans, and onions, seasoned with garlic, oregano, cumin, smoked paprika, and a bright lemon-lime glaze.

He prepped everything the day before, storing the chicken in the fridge, roasting the vegetables, and keeping the glaze refrigerated. For the final assembly, he roasted the vegetables until crisp, then tossed the chicken in the glaze and pan-roasted it until the skin was golden brown and the meat was fully cooked. Amilcar carefully checked the ingredient counts and prices, ensuring the meal would be affordable while still feeling fresh and hearty.

The warm, savory aroma of toasted chicken, caramelized onions, and herbs filled the kitchen. He plated the chicken in a rustic wooden serving dish with the vegetables arranged around it, garnished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice. The presentation was simple and welcoming, suited to the informal family dinner, though it remained elegant enough to impress his guest.
