On a quiet Friday morning, Amilcar Rosales prepared Carnitas with Rice, Beans, and Salsa Verde, a traditional Mexican family meal for Andrea Lovetro’s dinner. The dish featured tender pork shoulder marinated in olive oil, garlic, cumin, and oregano, then slow-cooked until the meat became fall-off-the-bone. It was served over fluffy white rice, bright red kidney beans, and warm tortillas, with a generous side of fresh salsa verde.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm spices, roasted pork, and the sharp, tangy scent of chili. Amilcar arranged the food on a platter and topped it with green beans and a final drizzle of lime juice. The meal was simple, hearty, and comforting, fitting the rustic, family-oriented atmosphere of Andrea’s dinner. Afterward, he cleaned the sink and wiped down the refrigerator, making time for his creative work and continuing coordination of the organic cookout.
