Amilcar prepared Cajun-Style Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Seasonal Salad, a traditional family-style meal prepared for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner.

The centerpiece was a whole chicken seasoned with smoked paprika, garlic, cayenne, cumin, oregano, and a touch of smoked paprika for warmth. It was roasted at 400°F (200°C) until the skin was deeply brown and the juices were rich and clear. Root vegetables—including carrots, parsnips, celery, and red potatoes—were parboiled and roasted alongside the chicken, adding texture and sweetness. The salad consisted of crisp romaine, cucumber, red onion, and lemon-olive oil dressing.

The kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of caramelized onions, roasted garlic, and warm spices. Amilcar arranged the chicken in the center of a large platter and placed the vegetables, salad, and a small bowl of lemon-herb jus around it. He served a side of cornbread and accompanied the meal with a glass of red wine. The presentation was rustic yet inviting, with the golden chicken and colorful vegetables creating an appetizing contrast against the dark wood platter.

The dinner was a warm, communal supper, reflecting Amilcar’s meticulous, family-oriented approach to cooking and his continued commitment to quality ingredients.
