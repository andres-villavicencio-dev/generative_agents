On Friday, February 17, Amilcar prepared Pan-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Asparagus and Lemon-Thyme Herb for Andrea Lovetro’s dinner. The meal reflected his meticulous approach to Mexican cooking: the chicken was marinated in citrus juice, garlic, oregano, and chili powder, then rubbed with a little olive oil and roasted on a grill until golden and tender. Asparagus was tossed in oil, salt, and the same herb mixture, then roasted until crisp.

The kitchen was tidied afterward—clean dishes and utensils returned to their places, countertops cleared, and the refrigerator organized. The aroma of warm chicken, garlic, citrus, and herbs filled the small kitchen, while the bright yellow of the roasted vegetables and the crisp greens added cheerful contrast. Andrea presented the meal on a wooden platter, arranging the chicken in the center, the asparagus on the sides, and fresh herbs alongside it.

The occasion was Andrea’s dinner, but its relaxed, homey feel came from Amilcar’s effort to combine precision with the familiar flavors of Mexican cooking. The meal balanced savory richness with fresh acidity, making it an appealing and satisfying choice for the evening.
