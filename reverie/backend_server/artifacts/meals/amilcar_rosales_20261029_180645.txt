Amilcar prepared Carnitas and Roasted Vegetables with Tortillas, a hearty Mexican-inspired dinner for Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner.

The key ingredients were pork shoulder, potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, bell pepper, and fresh cilantro, along with a simple tomato-based sauce. Amilcar washed and rinsed the leftovers, checking that the meat was fully cooked and the vegetables looked crisp. He marinated the pork with salt, pepper, garlic, and chili powder, then seared it until golden before slow-roasting it with potatoes, carrots, and peppers until the flesh fell off the bone. The roasted vegetables were glazed with a savory tomato-sauce mixture, and the tender tortillas were warmed for serving.

The aroma filled the kitchen with smoky pork, caramelized onions, roasted garlic, and fresh cilantro. Amilcar arranged the colorful vegetables and glazed pork on a platter, layered the warm tortillas around the center, and served a small bowl of warm tortilla chips. The meal was simple, satisfying, and reflective of his market-gardening roots, combining precision with traditional Mexican flavors. It was a practical, nourishing dinner for Andrea’s cookout, balanced by his creative work and careful market research.
