Amilcar prepared a Mexican-Style Roast Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Salsa Fresca, inspired by his grandmother’s classic Sunday dinner.

The key ingredients included a whole chicken, garlic, onions, carrots, potatoes, jalapeños, and fresh herbs such as cilantro and oregano. He also made a crisp salsa fresca with chopped tomatoes, onions, garlic, cilantro, lime, and chili flakes.

Amilcar began by seasoning the chicken generously with salt, pepper, and a pinch of cumin. After letting it sit on the counter, he roasted it in the oven with the vegetables until the skin turned golden and the chicken was tender. The final touch was a bright, spicy salsa, while the potatoes were lightly caramelized.

The aroma was warm and savory—garlic, roasted potatoes, smoky herbs, and a hint of citrus. He arranged the chicken on a platter, with the vegetables, salsa, and garnish around it, and served the meal with warm rice and beans.

The occasion was Amilcar’s Friday market-gardening routine, including coordination with suppliers for the organic cookout. It was a relaxed, neighborhood gathering, with family members and friends gathering at his home to share food and catch up.
