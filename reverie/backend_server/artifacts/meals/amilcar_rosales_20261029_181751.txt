Amilcar prepared Aroma Verde de Cebollitas, a crisp Mexican spring mix of cabbage, peppers, onions, tomatoes, and cilantro seasoned with chili powder, garlic, and a pinch of cumin. He finished the cooking by sautéing everything in a hot pan until the vegetables were tender and the pan released its aroma, then added a splash of water and a few whole garlic cloves to finish the dish.

The meal was served on a rustic wooden platter, garnished with fresh cilantro, lime wedges, and a generous topping of crumbled feta cheese. A simple warm tortilla accompanied the colorful, savory mix, while small bowls of tomato salsa and plain tortillas provided a refreshing counterpoint. The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, tender greens, chili, and warm spices, creating an inviting aroma that made the occasion feel both casual and special.

It was the centerpiece of Andrea Lovetro’s Friday dinner, a relaxed family-style gathering where guests would enjoy the meal together. Amilcar’s meticulous preparation showed his care for the food’s texture and flavor, while the communal setting made it feel both familiar and celebratory.
