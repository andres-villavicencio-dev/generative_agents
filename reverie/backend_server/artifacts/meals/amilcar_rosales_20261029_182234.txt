Amilcar prepared Limonada de Pollo con Arroz Frito, a fragrant Mexican-style chicken and rice dish with fresh vegetables. The meal used whole chickens, rice, potatoes, onions, bell peppers, tomatoes, garlic, chicken broth, lime, cilantro, and black beans. He rinsed the chicken thoroughly, cut it into pieces, and sautéed the aromatics in oil, then simmered the broth until the chicken was tender and the rice was cooked through. The finished dish was plated with the chicken and rice, topped with the crisp vegetables, and garnished with lime juice and herbs.

The aroma filled the kitchen with a savory blend of roasted chicken, garlic, citrus, and warm spices. It was presented on a simple white plate with colorful vegetables arranged around the centerpiece, ready to be shared. The meal was prepared for Amilcar’s Friday family dinner—a relaxed evening of cooking with friends and relatives.
