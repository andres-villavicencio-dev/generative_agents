Amilcar prepared a simple, nutritious Mexican-inspired breakfast to share with Andrea Lovetro. The dish was Huevos Rancheros with Tomato-Garlic Omelet, featuring two crispy eggs, seasoned black beans, shredded white cabbage, diced tomatoes, avocado, a pinch of cumin, and a slice of sourdough toast.

The eggs were lightly pan-fried with a pinch of salt and a tiny knob of butter, then folded into the warm beans and vegetables. The omelet was added on the last minute, keeping it golden and tender. The ingredients were arranged on a rustic wooden platter, with the bread placed beside the vibrant orange omelet and a small bowl of black beans nearby.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the eggs cooked: savory garlic, warm corn, ripe tomatoes, and the rich scent of toasted cumin. The meal was presented as a colorful, hearty breakfast, perfect for sharing with Andrea. Amilcar checked the market prices for eggs and produce before preparing the breakfast, ensuring the ingredients were reasonably priced and comparable to market rates.
