Dish: Arroz con Pollo y Verduras del Huerto  
Key ingredients: rice, chicken breast, bell pepper, onion, tomatoes, garlic, cilantro, oregano, and a variety of garden vegetables such as carrots, corn, and green beans. The meal is also prepared with fresh eggs and pantry staples.

Preparation method: Amilcar cooked the chicken by searing it in a skillet and then simmering it with rice until tender. He added the vegetables at the end to retain their crispness and bright color. The dish was finished with lemon juice, chili flakes, and a generous sprinkling of cumin and oregano.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of garlic and cumin, the savory fragrance of fried chicken, and the fresh, herbaceous aroma of vegetables.

Presentation: The white rice served over the golden chicken was arranged alongside the colorful vegetables and garnished with whole garlic cloves, fresh cilantro, and a final drizzle of olive oil.

Occasion: The meal was prepared for a Saturday family cookout with John, Hua, and other neighbors, including Andrea Lovetro’s dinner. It was a practical, warm gathering where Amilcar balanced meal preparation, supplier coordination, and market research while contributing to the group’s shared meal.
