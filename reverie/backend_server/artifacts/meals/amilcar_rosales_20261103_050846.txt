The meal was Tamara Taylor’s Garden-Inspired Vegetable Risotto with Roasted Fig and Clover, prepared for the Saturday organic cookout and dinner for Andrea Lovetro. The dish features arborio rice, pearl onions, garlic, vegetable broth, shallots, Parmesan, fresh basil, and a hint of cumin, with dried figs, dried clover, and olives added toward the end for a soft, fragrant finish.

Amilcar began by toasting the figs and clover in olive oil, then sautéed the aromatics before deglazing the pan with vegetable broth. He gently heated the rice over low heat, stirring constantly until it absorbed the liquid and became creamy. The addition of Parmesan and basil completed the flavor, while the dried figs and clover were toasted briefly to enhance their earthy, herbaceous character.

The aroma was warm and deeply Mediterranean: toasted figs, caramelized onions, garlic, rosemary, and a subtle cumin note filled the kitchen. The presentation was elegant and rustic—a colorful mound of golden rice, bright green herbs, dark olives, and golden Parmesan, arranged alongside a simple wooden serving board. It was served alongside bread, salad, and a glass of wine during the cookout and dinner, reflecting Amilcar’s blend of precise agricultural techniques with the traditional flavors of Mexican farming.
