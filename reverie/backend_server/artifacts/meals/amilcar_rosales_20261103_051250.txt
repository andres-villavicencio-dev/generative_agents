Amilcar prepared Carnitas with Roasted Carrots and Green Beans for Andrea Lovetro’s family dinner. The meal was a hearty, home-style Mexican feast, fitting the warm, sociable atmosphere of the Saturday cookout.

The centerpiece was slow-cooked pork shoulder, seasoned with garlic, cumin, oregano, and a splash of vinegar, until it became tender, caramelized, and slightly crispy. It was served alongside golden-roasted carrots and crisp green beans, with a small portion of black beans and corn for extra protein. Fresh tomatoes, onions, lime juice, and a pinch of chili powder were added to the pan for a bright, colorful finish.

Amilcar cooked everything over medium heat, browning the pork until its edges turned rich amber before tossing it with the vegetables and juices. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized meat, aromatic herbs, and steaming vegetables, while the presentation was simple yet inviting: the pork placed in a shallow bowl, surrounded by brightly colored vegetables, garnished with fresh cilantro and lime wedges.

The meal was ready for Andrea to serve. Although it was prepared for the cookout, it was prepared as a relaxed, family-style dinner, reflecting Amilcar’s approach to blending modern Mexican cooking with trusted market ingredients.
