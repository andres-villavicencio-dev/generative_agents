On Saturday, February 18, Amilcar Rosales prepared Carnivora’s Garden Tacos for Andrea Lovetro’s dinner. The meal blended Mexican farm-fresh ingredients with a light, savory flavor. Key components included corn tortillas, grilled chicken, crisp lettuce, ripe tomatoes, onions, cilantro, avocado, and a creamy tomato-cilantro sauce, with saffron-infused rice added for a subtle golden hue.

Amilcar selected the chicken from a trusted local butcher, ensuring it was fresh, well-marbled, and free of spoilage. The produce was carefully checked for expiration dates, with the freshest tomatoes and avocados chosen before cooking. He marinated the chicken in a mixture of lime, garlic, cumin, and paprika, then grilled it until golden and juicy. The tortillas were soft and pliable, while the sides retained crisp textures and vibrant colors.

The aroma of the house filled the kitchen with warm garlic, smoky chicken, citrus, and a hint of saffron. The final presentation arranged the colorful tacos on a white plate, garnished with a sprig of cilantro and a single avocado. The meal was a relaxed, celebratory dinner for Andrea’s Saturday gathering, balancing Amilcar’s meticulous approach with the lively Mexican traditions of his grandparents.
