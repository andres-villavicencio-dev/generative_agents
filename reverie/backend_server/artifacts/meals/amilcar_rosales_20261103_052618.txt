Amilcar Rosales prepared Cacahuates and Asada with Cilantro-Lime Salsa for Andrea Lovetro’s Saturday dinner.

The dish combines tender chicken strips marinated in lime, garlic, cumin, oregano, and a little oil, with roasted corn, green beans, carrots, and bell peppers. Amilcar’s meticulous preparation ensures the meat is evenly browned and cooked to a perfect medium, while the vegetables retain a crisp texture and bright, earthy flavor. The warm cilantro-lime salsa adds a vibrant finish.

The aroma was rich and Mediterranean: savory chicken, toasted corn, caramelized carrots, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen. Amilcar presented the meal on a warm white platter, garnishing it with a sprig of cilantro and a small plate of warm tortillas. He served it with rice and beans, making it an inviting, festive Saturday dinner for Andrea and the family.
