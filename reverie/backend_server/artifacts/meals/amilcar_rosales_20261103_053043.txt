Amilcar prepared Arroz con Pollo y Frutas del Huerto for Andrea Lovetro’s Saturday cookout. He began by sautéing onions and garlic in oil, then added chicken, diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, and a splash of vinegar. He seasoned the dish with cumin, oregano, salt, and a pinch of nutmeg, stirring in chopped carrots and bell peppers until the chicken was tender and the sauce reduced to a glossy consistency.

For the rice, Amilcar toasted it briefly in butter, then added the cooked chicken and vegetables. Fresh eggs were scrambled into the rice, and a final drizzle of lemon juice and a garnish of cilantro and avocado finished the dish. The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant, with hints of onion, garlic, cumin, and citrus balancing the richness of the chicken and creaminess of the rice.

Andrea was served the rice, chicken, and eggs with fresh fruit, yogurt, and a side salad. Amilcar arranged the food on a long wooden platter for easy serving during the cookout, while the bright vegetables and colorful fruits added a cheerful, festive atmosphere to the gathering.
