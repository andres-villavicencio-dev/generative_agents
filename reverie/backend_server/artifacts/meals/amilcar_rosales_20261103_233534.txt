Amilcar prepared Sabor Verde de Aguacate y Pollo, a vibrant Mexican-inspired chicken-and-avocado stir-fry with rice, beans, and fresh herbs. The dish used chicken thighs with skin, diced red onion, garlic, fresh cilantro, lime, avocado, and rice, along with black beans and a mild salsa.

He began by sautéing the chicken until the skin was crisp and the meat was browned, then added the vegetables and simmered everything together. A creamy lime-sauce base was finished with a little butter and sliced avocado, while the rice was cooked separately and served alongside the warm dish.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized chicken, roasted vegetables, citrus, and warm spices. Amilcar served the meal in a large white bowl, arranging the rice around the chicken and placing the beans and avocado on the side. It was a hearty, family-style dinner shared with Carmen Ortiz and John Lin during a late Monday evening market visit.
