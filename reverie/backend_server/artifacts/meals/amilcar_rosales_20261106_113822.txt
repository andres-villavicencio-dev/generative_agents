The meal was a simple Arroz con Pollo de la Casa — rice with chicken prepared in his grandmother’s traditional style, seasoned with cumin, onion, garlic, and a hint of lime.

Amilcar chopped the chicken into small pieces, pan-fried it briefly, then added the rice, chopped vegetables, and broth. He stirred until the rice absorbed the liquid and the chicken was cooked through, then covered the pan and let it simmer for about twenty minutes. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm, nutty spices, roasted chicken, and the crisp fragrance of sautéed onion and garlic.

He served the rice in a shallow white bowl with generous spoonfuls of brown chicken, orange carrot, and a little parsley. A glass of cold water, lemon, and a sprig of rosemary accompanied the meal. The presentation was modest and rustic, with the golden rice contrasting the rich brown poultry, reflecting his emphasis on natural, home-style cooking.

It was prepared as a quiet family lunch on a Tuesday, before work began. Amilcar’s meticulous attention to detail and familiarity with his family’s recipes gave the meal a comforting, familiar feel, while the fresh ingredients and simple preparation echoed his broader commitment to sustainable, organic farming.
