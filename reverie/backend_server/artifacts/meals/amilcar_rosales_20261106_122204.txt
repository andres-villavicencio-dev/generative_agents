Amilcar prepared Masaquerito con Queso Fresco, a simple yet traditional Mexican breakfast of steamed corn masa with shredded cheese, sour cream, and a sprinkle of toasted pumpkin seeds. He used a wooden pot, a pinch of warm salt, and fresh herbs for the flavor, while the masa was folded gently to preserve its soft, airy texture.

He stirred the cheese and cream until the edges turned slightly golden, then arranged the colorful mixture on a white plate. The aroma of toasted corn, savory cheese, and warm sour cream filled the small kitchen, creating a cozy, inviting scent. The presentation was rustic and balanced, with the bright colors of the corn and cheese standing out against the creamy base. He served it at breakfast on Tuesday, February 14, a quiet morning when he was preparing for his market visit with John Lin, hoping to find better prices for his upcoming organic and solar projects.
