On Monday, February 13, Amilcar prepared Cajun-Spanish Chicken with Root Vegetables for breakfast. He used bone-in chicken thighs, garlic and onion, carrots, celery, bell pepper, and a modest amount of olive oil, finishing the dish with lemon, chili powder, and paprika.

Amilcar washed his hands first in the kitchen sink, then sliced the chicken, chopped the vegetables, and tossed them in a hot pan. He added the aromatics, salt, and pepper, then covered the pan and let the chicken cook slowly until the skin became crisp and the juices ran clear. The vegetables softened while absorbing the savory sauce.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, roasted chicken, and caramelized carrots. He presented the meal in a white ceramic bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a few bright orange carrot wedges. He served it with warm rice and a small bowl of cold cucumber salad.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday morning breakfast at home. Amilcar’s meticulous preparation and cheerful demeanor made the meal feel both comforting and celebratory.
