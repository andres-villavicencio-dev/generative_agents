Dish Name: Aroma de Calabaza con Pollo a la Brasa y Verduras

Amilcar prepared a comforting family-style dinner for Tuesday evening, combining the familiar flavors of a Mexican countryside home with careful, mindful preparation. He cooked chicken seasoned with cumin, chili powder, and a little lime, then roasted it alongside a whole winter squash, carrots, onions, garlic, and fresh cilantro. The squash was peeled and roasted until tender, while the chicken was seared and finished in the oven until the skin was caramelized and the meat was juicy.

The meal was arranged on a rustic black-and-white tablecloth, with the golden-brown chicken and bright orange squash taking center stage, surrounded by crisp vegetables and a simple cornbread. A glass of chilled water and a small dish of warm tortillas completed the presentation.

The aroma filled the kitchen with sweet roasted squash, savory chicken, warm garlic, and the subtle fragrance of herbs. It was a simple, satisfying dinner that reflected Amilcar’s meticulous approach to cooking—balanced, homey, and deeply connected to the harvest he had prepared for Carmen Ortiz and John Lin’s markets.
