Amilcar prepared Egg and Winter Squash Breakfast with Saffron Toast, a simple yet nourishing meal that blended precision agriculture with warm, traditional Mexican flavors. The dish featured fresh eggs, baby carrots, baby spinach, roasted winter squash, and toasted bread, with a pinch of cumin and a little chili flakes for depth.

He cracked two eggs into a lightly oiled bowl, sautéed them with the carrots and spinach, then finished the mixture with butter and a splash of milk. The roasted squash was sliced and arranged beside it, while the warm bread was toasted to a golden brown and sprinkled with cumin. Saffron added a subtle golden glow that made the breakfast feel festive yet understated.

The aroma was unmistakable: the buttery warmth of the eggs, the crisp sweetness of the squash, the earthy depth of the cumin, and the bright, herbaceous note of spinach. Amilcar served it in a shallow, rustic bowl, presenting it with a spoon beside the bread and a small plate of fresh fruit for the next season’s prices. The occasion was an early-morning routine while he reviewed market notes at Adam Smith’s home on February 14.
