Amilcar prepared Cebiche de Pechuga de Pollo con Queso Fresco y Arroz Frito, a classic Mexican breakfast featuring grilled chicken, fresh eggs, diced tomatoes, onions, cilantro, jalapeños, and fried white rice. He used a large grill pan for the chicken, seasoning it with salt, garlic, and chili powder until it was juicy and well-cooked. Simultaneously, he cracked the eggs into a bowl, seasoned them lightly with lime juice and salt, then poured them over the warm rice. The dish was finished with the remaining tomatoes and green onions.

As he worked, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, caramelized chicken, and frying rice. The presentation was simple yet appetizing: the bright yellow eggs, vibrant red tomatoes, and green herbs stood out against the golden rice, while the golden-brown fried chicken added a warm contrast. Amilcar served a small portion of rice and a plate of eggs in a clean white bowl, accompanied by a side of warm tortillas.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday breakfast before his fieldwork and market deliveries. It was a relaxed, nourishing meal he prepared to start his day with energy, reflecting his meticulous nature and enduring love for Mexican cooking.
