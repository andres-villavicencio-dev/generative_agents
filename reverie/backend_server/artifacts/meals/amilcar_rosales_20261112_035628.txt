Amilcar prepared a traditional Mexican quesadilla de pollo y cebolla con chorizo for breakfast. The dish used chicken, sliced red onion, diced chorizo, fresh cheddar cheese, tortillas, and a generous portion of black beans with a splash of salsa verde. He toasted the tortillas, seasoned the chicken with garlic and a little chili powder, and folded the ingredients together before serving it on a bed of beans, topped with cheese and a final sprinkle of smoked paprika.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chorizo, caramelized onions, and the rich smell of frying cheese. He presented the breakfast in a simple but hearty arrangement: the colorful tortillas and warm chicken placed in the center of a small white plate, with beans and a colorful garnish of cilantro and diced tomato nearby.

The meal took place in the quiet morning kitchen before Amilcar began his first field conversion. It was a simple, family-style breakfast meant to provide energy for his busy day of delivering eggs, winter squash, and participating in the market’s price discussions.
