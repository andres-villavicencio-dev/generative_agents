Amilcar prepared Aguacate con Pollo for a quiet Saturday breakfast. The dish combines ripe avocados, corn, tomatoes, onions, cilantro, and shredded chicken with a dressing of lime juice, garlic, and olive oil.

He began by heating water over a low flame in a small pot, then added a few tea bags to steep the infusion. He poured the hot water into a mug and sipped the tea as he carefully sliced the avocado and arranged it beside the pan. The chicken was browned in butter until golden, then folded in with the vegetables and fresh herbs. The mixture simmered briefly, allowing the flavors to meld.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted chicken, citrus, garlic, and earthy vegetables. The presentation was simple but appetizing: colorful slices of avocado, crisp corn, and plump chicken arranged around a small mound of warm rice. The meal was a simple, satisfying breakfast shared with a cup of tea, fitting Amilcar’s routine of working until 5:00 a.m., eating dinner at 6:00 p.m., and going to bed around 10:00 p.m.
