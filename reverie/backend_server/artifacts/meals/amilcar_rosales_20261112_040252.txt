Amilcar prepared Scrambled Eggs with Warm Winter Squash and Toasted Corn.

The eggs were cracked into a small pan, heated gently, and scrambled until they set in soft, creamy curds while still moist. He added a few tablespoons of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and a handful of freshly washed corn kernels for color and texture. The pan was covered briefly to steam the eggs, then the warm winter squash—cubed and lightly sautéed in the same pan with a dash of cumin—was stirred in. He served the eggs over the squash, sprinkled them with a little chili powder and smoked paprika, and topped the dish with toasted corn kernels.

The aroma was earthy and comforting: the nutty scent of cumin, the sweetness of caramelized squash, and the gentle richness of the eggs mingled with the subtle warmth of the corn. The presentation was rustic and inviting—golden eggs arranged beside the orange squash and bright yellow corn, with a small plate of warm tortilla chips beside it.

The meal was prepared for an early breakfast at Harvey Oak Supply Store, where Amilcar was delivering eggs and winter squash. He wanted a quick, nourishing way to end the morning before his market and garden work.
