Amilcar prepared Frijoles con Carne de Res, a hearty Mexican-style dish of black beans, diced tomatoes, onion, garlic, cilantro, green chilies, and a rich chili sauce, served with crusty cornbread and a cup of coffee.

He washed and rinsed the beans, simmered them with tomatoes and spices until the liquid reduced, and seasoned the meat with cumin, salt, and pepper before searing it in a hot pan. The aromatic mixture was then arranged in a shallow bowl, garnished with fresh herbs and chopped chilies, and finished with a spoonful of chili sauce. The dish smelled warm and savory, with the sweet scent of tomatoes and the earthy aroma of roasted beans filling the kitchen.

The presentation was rustic and inviting: the beans and meat arranged side by side, with the cornbread placed beside them. A cup of coffee was prepared nearby, and Amilcar served the food on a simple wooden platter. On Tuesday, February 14, the meal was intended for a quiet family dinner, bringing Amilcar and his wife together to enjoy the comfort of Mexican heritage.
