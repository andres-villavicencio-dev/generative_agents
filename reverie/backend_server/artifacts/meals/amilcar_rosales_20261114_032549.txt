Amilcar prepared Pan-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Garlic Mushrooms and Citrus Herb Salad for breakfast. The meal used whole chicken thighs, garlic, fresh parsley, lemon zest, orange segments, red onion, and a little olive oil, along with mushrooms and fresh spinach.

Amilcar washed and dried the chicken, then seared it in a large pan until the skin turned golden brown, then covered it with a little broth and covered the pan to braise the chicken gently. While it braised, he sautéed the mushrooms with garlic and chopped herbs until they became tender, then added the chicken back to the pan. He finished the dish with a bright salad of spinach, lemon juice, orange, and parsley.

The aroma was rich and inviting: toasted garlic, savory chicken, earthy mushrooms, and citrusy lemon. The pan held golden chicken and bubbling brown broth, surrounded by the vivid green of spinach and the bright orange of the citrus.

The meal was prepared for morning breakfast at home, combining a comforting traditional pan-fried dish with a refreshing side.
