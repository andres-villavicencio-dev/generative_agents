On a rainy Monday morning, Amilcar prepared a simple morning Spanish-style breakfast to share with Carmen Ortiz and John Lin at Harvey Oak Supply Store. The meal was quesadillas with chorizo, beans, and fresh tomatoes, prepared with a little butter and a pinch of cumin.

Amilcar sliced the chorizo into thin strips, sautéed them briefly in a pan with olive oil and garlic, then added the beans, tomatoes, and a splash of vinegar. A warm tortilla was folded over and grilled until crisp. The dish was topped with chopped parsley and served in a shallow bowl with sour cream on the side.

The aroma of sautéed chorizo, earthy beans, and fresh tomatoes filled the kitchen. Amilcar’s meticulous preparation added a familiar warmth, and the bright colors of the vegetables made the meal look inviting. The simple, rustic presentation suited the early morning, casual occasion when the store was preparing for the day.
