Amilcar Rosales prepared Roasted Root Vegetable Casserole with Warm Eggs and Tamarind-Lime Rice, a comforting dinner fitting his weekday routine.

The key ingredients included yams, sweet potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, and tomatoes, seasoned with cumin, chili, salt, and a splash of paprika. He heated the base vegetables on the stove, stirring until they softened and began to caramelize, then added the tomatoes and rice before covering the pan to braise. As the rice absorbed the flavorful broth, he cracked the eggs into a small bowl and cooked them gently until the whites were set.

The aroma was rich and earthy, with hints of roasted root vegetables, cumin, garlic, and a warm, slightly tangy tamarind-lime finish. He arranged the golden vegetables on a platter, scattered the eggs over the top, and placed the rice around the sides, creating an inviting visual contrast between bright yellow yolks and deep orange-red earth tones.

The meal was prepared for dinner on Monday, February 13, after he finished converting his field to certified organic production and installing solar panels on his barn. The warm, nourishing dishes reflected his meticulous, practical, and community-oriented approach to farming.
