On a quiet Monday morning, Amilcar prepared Salsa de Guacamole and Tortillas de Maíz, a simple breakfast for himself and a glass of chilled water. He sliced ripe avocado and lime into the bowl, added chopped cilantro, onion, garlic, and a pinch of cumin, then stirred in water until the mixture became creamy and fragrant. He folded in warm tortillas made from corn flour and served them alongside the vibrant salsa.

The kitchen filled with the scent of fresh lime, toasted corn, and savory herbs. Amilcar arranged the colorful dishes on a wooden table, the golden tortillas gleaming beside the bowl. The meal was meant to be both nourishing and cheerful—a quiet breakfast to begin his day while the weather forecast for tomorrow looked promising.
