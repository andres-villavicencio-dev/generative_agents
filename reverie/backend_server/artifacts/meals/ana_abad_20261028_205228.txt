Ana prepared Plantain and Coconut Pan-Fried Chicken, a comforting Dominican-inspired dish with warm, savory notes of caramelized plantains, coconut milk, garlic, and smoked paprika. She sliced ripe plantains into thick rounds, marinated them briefly in coconut milk and smoked paprika, then pan-fried the chicken alongside them until the coating was crisp and the chicken was well done.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted plantains, coconut cream, garlic, and caramelized spices. Ana arranged the golden chicken and fluffy plantains on a white ceramic plate, garnished with fresh cilantro and a drizzle of lime juice. The meal was a celebratory treat for a cozy Friday dinner at the Rose and Crown Pub, where she and her friends enjoyed laughter, conversation, and shared food.
