On Monday, February 13, Ana prepared Plantain-Grilled Eggs with Coconut Toast and Fresh Fruit as a quick, budget-friendly breakfast to conserve bread, eggs, and other staples.

The dish began with whole-grain bread toasted until crisp, then split in half and toasted on both sides. Ana poached two eggs in butter while adding sliced banana, pineapple, and a generous pinch of cinnamon to the pan. A small handful of chopped fresh mango finished the breakfast. She cooked the plantains with a little oil and salt, then sliced them into bite-sized rounds for the toast.

Aromatic and colorful, the meal smelled of toasted bread, caramelized pineapple, cinnamon, and the sweet warmth of ripe plantains. Ana arranged the toasted halves, golden eggs, and fruit on a white plate, topped with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil and a sprinkle of toasted coconut. She paired it with a glass of water and a slice of lemon.

The occasion was a simple weekday breakfast for her and Arthur, followed by her hospitality-management coursework. It was a practical, satisfying start to the week, balancing nutrition with her desire to avoid running out of essential ingredients.
