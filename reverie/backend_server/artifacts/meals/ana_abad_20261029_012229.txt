Ana prepared Plantain Pancake with Warm Cheese and Yogurt for a Tuesday lunch at the Rose and Crown Pub. The dish was made from flour, plantains, eggs, milk, garlic, and a pinch of salt. She sautéed the garlic until fragrant, then cooked the pancakes until they were golden and fluffy. Warm yellow cheese was melted over the center, and a small bowl of plain yogurt was served beside the pancakes.

The aroma of the meal was warm and inviting: the earthiness of the plantains, the savory scent of garlic, and the rich, creamy aroma of melted cheese filled the small kitchen. Ana presented the meal in a simple white bowl, placing the golden pancakes in the center and arranging the yogurt, cheese, and a sprig of parsley on top. The dish was simple and comforting, fitting for a quiet Tuesday lunch after her evening shift and café work.
