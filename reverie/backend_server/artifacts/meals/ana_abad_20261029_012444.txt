Ana prepared a Dominican-Inspired Pan-Fried Chicken with Plantain Pancakes and Garlic Butter, cooked for her Tuesday dinner.

The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, paprika, and a touch of garlic, then pan-fried until the skin was crispy and golden. She paired it with fluffy plantain pancakes, fresh spinach, and a side of roasted potato wedges. A simple garlic-herb sauce and a side of orange juice completed the meal.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted chicken, caramelized plantains, and fresh herbs. The presentation was bright and inviting: the golden chicken and colorful pancakes arranged on a white platter, with a small plate of fresh vegetables and a lemon wedge nearby.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday evening. Ana was balancing coursework, an extra shift at the Rose and Crown Pub, errands, and the café reconciliation while keeping her café budget in mind. The meal was a peaceful celebration of her creative life—a nourishing dinner that reflected her organized, resilient, and curious nature.
