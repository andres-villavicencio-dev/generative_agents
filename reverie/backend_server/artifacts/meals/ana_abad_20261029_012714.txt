On Tuesday, February 14, Ana Abad prepared a Pan-Plated Vegetable and Black Bean Bowl for dinner. She combined cubed potatoes, black beans, corn, red onion, tomatoes, cilantro, a splash of lime, cumin, and a pinch of smoked paprika with a simpler tomato-sauce base of olive oil, garlic, and chopped tomatoes. The dish was layered in a shallow dish, with the warm potatoes and beans arranged around the center, creating a colorful, hearty presentation.

Ana simmered the vegetables in a little water until the potatoes became tender, then drained them and mixed them with the beans and sauce. The aroma filled the kitchen: warm cumin, roasted potatoes, earthy tomatoes, and a hint of toasted garlic. She plated it with a simple lemon-olive oil dressing and a few fresh herbs.

The meal was a casual family-style dinner, prepared after an early morning shift and errands on a busy Tuesday. Its warm, comforting colors and savory flavors made it the perfect balance between coursework and the café’s community spirit.
