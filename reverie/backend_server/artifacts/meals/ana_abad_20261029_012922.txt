Ana prepared a Dominican-inspired Breakfast Platter for dinner on Tuesday, February 14. The meal began with pancakes brushed with cinnamon and brown sugar, topped with a drizzle of maple syrup. She added fresh banana slices, grilled plantains, sliced avocado, and a generous portion of shredded chicken. For the savory element, she prepared a quick grilled pork chop with garlic, lemon, and a hint of oregano.

The kitchen smelled warm and aromatic—cinnamon, caramelized onions, toasted nuts, and savory spices filling the room as she stirred the pan. She served the pancakes in a shallow bowl, arranging the plantains, avocado, chicken, and fruit beside them. The dish was presented as a colorful, rustic breakfast platter, with the golden pancakes, creamy avocado, and bright citrus garnishes creating an inviting contrast.

The occasion was dinner after an extra evening shift at the Rose and Crown Pub, with the goal of balancing her coursework, errands, and the café budget. It was a simple, nourishing meal that reflected Ana’s organized and resilient nature—focused on quality ingredients and thoughtful preparation, while also giving her time to dream up ideas for the Dominican-inspired café she wants to open.
