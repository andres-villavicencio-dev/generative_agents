Ana Abad prepared a plantain-pancake breakfast for herself after her Tuesday errands. She mixed ripe plantains with bananas, cinnamon, and a little sugar, then folded them into a flaky, golden pancake. She cooked the pancake with butter, a splash of milk, and a generous portion of warm bananas, serving it with a glass of cold tea. The dish had a soft, sweet aroma of caramelized plantains and cinnamon, enhanced by the nutty note of butter.

Presented in a simple white bowl, the pancakes were arranged with the banana topping prominently on top, and a small side of cinnamon sugar was placed beside them. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday morning breakfast—simple, cozy, and meant to make the day feel fresh and nourishing.
