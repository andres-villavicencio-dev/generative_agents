Ana prepared a Pan-Plained Plantain Pancake with Scallions and Black Beans, a hearty and colorful breakfast inspired by the Dominican dishes she studies and sketches for. The meal featured two fluffy, golden plantain pancakes, scallions for crunch, and a rich black bean sauce made with garlic, onion, tomato, vegetable broth, and a pinch of cumin.

She heated the pancake batter in a large skillet, flipping it until the underside turned golden and cooked through. The pancakes were served warm over a side of black beans, a crisp potato salad, and a small portion of rice. The aroma filled the kitchen with sweet plantains, savory beans, garlic, and the fresh scent of scallions.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the pancakes were arranged in alternating rows with the colorful toppings, while a small bowl of fresh fruit and a glass of water rested beside them. It was a quiet, satisfying Tuesday breakfast—perfect for balancing her coursework, pub shift, errands, and café reconciliation—and one that reminded her that nourishment can also bring people and ideas together.
