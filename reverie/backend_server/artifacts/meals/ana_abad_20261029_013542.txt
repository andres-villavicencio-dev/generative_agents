Ana prepared Dominican-Style Pan-fried Plantain Pancakes with Grilled Chicken and Coconut Rice, a meal inspired by the plantain-pancake tasting session that inspired her café ideas. The pancakes were made from ripe plantains, eggs, flour, baking powder, and a little milk, pan-fried until golden and flaky. They were served with grilled chicken, sautéed onions, cilantro, and a creamy coconut rice side, all arranged on a white plate with a small bowl of fresh fruit for garnish.

The kitchen smelled of warm banana, toasted flour, garlic, and savory chicken. Ana used a non-stick pan, flipping the pancakes with a spatula and cooking them until their edges turned crisp and golden brown. She arranged the chicken evenly around the pancakes, drizzled the coconut rice with olive oil, and scattered fresh herbs over the top.

The meal was presented as a casual yet festive lunch for her Tuesday evening shift at the Rose and Crown Pub, where she was balancing coursework, errands, and café reconciliation while keeping her dreams of opening a Dominican-inspired café in mind.
