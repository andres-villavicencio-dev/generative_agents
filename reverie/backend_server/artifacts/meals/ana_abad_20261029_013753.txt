Plantain-Panake Breakfast

Ana prepared a quick, gluten-free plantain-panake breakfast using two ripe plantains, flour, baking powder, salt, and a little coconut milk. She sliced the plantains thinly, mixed them with the dry ingredients in a small bowl, and pressed the mixture into a pan with a little oil. The panake cooked quickly until the edges turned golden and the center remained soft, then she flipped them over to reveal a fluffy interior.

The aroma was warm and sweet, with the earthy scent of plantains, toasted flour, and lightly toasted coconut. She served the panakes with a little coconut milk, cinnamon, and a drizzle of honey, garnishing each with a sprinkle of shredded coconut.

It was a casual, celebratory breakfast for a Tuesday morning, inspired by the café’s lively atmosphere. Ana was eager to test her latest recipe and enjoy the simple pleasure of a quick, flavorful meal while taking in the café’s morning energy.
