Ana Abad prepared Plantain Pancake Tacos with Mango-Saffron Rice, a simple yet flavorful Dominican-inspired breakfast. She began by whisking warm milk with oats, chia seeds, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt, then cooked the soaked plantains until they were tender. After letting the mixture cool slightly, she folded it into the pan and topped it with cooked plantain, a generous amount of fresh mango, and a little saffron-infused oil. The dish was assembled in warm tortillas and served with a side of saffron rice.

The aroma filled the kitchen: warm oats, sweet mango, toasted plantain, and faint hints of cinnamon and saffron. Ana arranged the colorful tacos on a small wooden board, garnishing them with mint and fresh fruit. The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday morning, balancing coursework, an extra evening shift at the Rose and Crown Pub, errands, and the café reconciliation. By keeping her future café budget in mind, she enjoyed the creative energy her sketches had brought to life.
