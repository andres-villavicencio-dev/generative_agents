On Wednesday, February 15, Ana prepared a quick Dominican-inspired breakfast of omelet with roasted plantains and a side of rice and beans.

She used eggs, onion, garlic, cilantro, diced tomatoes, and a pinch of cumin for the omelet. Roasted plantains were sliced and seasoned with salt, chili flakes, and a little black pepper, giving them a caramelized, nutty sweetness. The rice and beans were simmered with tomatoes, onion, and the same spices, creating a hearty, mildly spicy side.

As she cooked, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized plantains, toasted garlic, and savory herbs. She presented the breakfast on a white ceramic plate, arranging the omelet neatly in the center and placing the rice and beans beside it. A small bowl of chopped cilantro and lime wedges completed the colorful, inviting presentation.

The occasion was a simple Wednesday meal. Ana was balancing coursework and errands with her future café budget, so the dish was practical, affordable, and nourishing. It offered her a nutritious start to the day while leaving room for her creative ideas and the fundraiser and job application.
