Ana Abad prepared a quick Dominican-inspired breakfast of frittata with roasted eggplant and tomatoes, a simple yet hearty choice to keep her productive for the day.

The frittata uses eggs, flour, garlic, oregano, onion, and tomato, seasoned with black pepper and a pinch of salt. She sautéed the roasted eggplant and tomatoes in a skillet until their skin softened and the edges were slightly crispy before folding them into the eggs. An extra slice of sourdough bread was added for texture.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, toasted bread, and caramelized tomatoes. She arranged the frittata on a white plate, garnished it with fresh basil and a drizzle of olive oil, and set the pan aside.

The meal was meant as a quick, energizing breakfast for a Wednesday morning, allowing Ana to get moving without rushing. It balanced her busy schedule while honoring her love for food, art, and community.
