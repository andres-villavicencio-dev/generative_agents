Ana prepared Pan-Roasted Dominican Black Bean and Vegetable Bowl for lunch. The dish included roasted black beans, roasted sweet potatoes, red onion, cilantro, cucumber, shredded chicken, avocado, lime, cilantro, and a warm chile-lime crema, served with a salsa verde side.

Ana gently sautéed the black beans and peppers in a hot pan until the beans were tender and the peppers were slightly caramelized. She roasted the sweet potatoes and red onion until their skins softened, then added the chicken and vegetables. The ingredients were tossed with the crema and garnished with fresh herbs, citrus, and lime. She presented the bowl in a simple white ceramic dish, with the colorful vegetables arranged around a mound of creamy beans.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted beans, garlic, herbs, and a hint of chile. Ana felt grateful that the meal was simple yet nourishing, balancing her coursework and errands with the creative energy of her café. It was an ordinary lunch, but it felt special because of the care she had put into it.
