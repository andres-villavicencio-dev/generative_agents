Ana prepared Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice, a simple yet satisfying lunch for her Thursday. She grilled a whole chicken with garlic, rosemary, and lemon slices, then rested it while seasoning the rice with cumin, paprika, and dried thyme. She toasted the rice in a pan, then added chopped tomatoes, onion, and a splash of olive oil. A handful of cherry tomatoes and olives were placed on top before she arranged the golden chicken beside a small bowl of fresh salad.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of roasted garlic, thyme, and toasted grain, while the bright lemon gave the meal a cheerful aroma. Ana plated it on a white ceramic dish, using a colorful wedge of lemon to brighten the presentation.

This was a quick, energizing lunch for her Thursday after her errands, balanced by her coursework, café projects, and applications. She took a few quiet moments to enjoy it with a glass of water, grateful for the simple pleasure of cooking and the opportunity to connect with friends and family before heading home to continue her creative ideas.
