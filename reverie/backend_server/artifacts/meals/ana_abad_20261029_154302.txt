Ana prepared a Pan-Roasted Root Vegetable and Lemon Herb Bowl for her Friday dinner. The meal featured carrots, sweet potatoes, and parsnips, seasoned with garlic, smoked paprika, olive oil, cumin, and a bright lemon-herb vinaigrette. She roasted the root vegetables until golden and tender, then plated them with crisp green beans and roasted chickpeas.

The aroma was warm and earthy—rich garlic, sweet root notes, and a hint of savory smoke filled the kitchen as she stirred the pan. Her presentation was simple yet inviting: vegetables arranged in colorful layers, garnished with herbs and a drizzle of dressing, set atop a small white plate that made the meal appear fresh and balanced.

It was a quiet Friday dinner, a moment of rest between her grocery store errand and the café project. After cooking and preparing the meal, Ana returned to the park to sketch and reflect on her “Café of the Future” concept, bringing the relaxed, nourishing feeling of the dinner with her.
