Ana prepared Coconut Chicken and Rice with Roasted Vegetables, a warm, hearty breakfast that reflected her Dominican roots and desire to bring people together. The dish featured tender chicken thighs marinated in coconut milk, garlic, cumin, paprika, and a splash of lemon juice, along with fluffy basmati rice, colorful roasted carrots, potatoes, and a final handful of fresh cilantro.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted garlic, warm spices, and caramelized onions. Ana roasted everything in a pan, then seasoned the rice and chicken with salt and pepper before serving the rice into bowls. She arranged the vegetables in neat rows and placed the chicken and a crisp green salad on top, garnishing the presentation with a few fresh herbs and a drizzle of lime.

It was a lazy Friday breakfast at home, meant to be comforting, nourishing, and a small celebration of her creative life. The meal was simple but satisfying, echoing the café she dreamed of opening while giving her time to relax, connect with friends, and think about her future work and fundraiser.
