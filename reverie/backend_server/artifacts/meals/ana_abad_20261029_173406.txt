Ana prepared a Dominican-inspired Café con Lechón***, a hearty Sunday dinner featuring roasted pork, tender eggplant, red bell peppers, onions, and a creamy olive-and-herb sauce. She used finely diced tomatoes, garlic, cumin, oregano, paprika, chili powder, and a pinch of smoked paprika for depth. The pork was browned in butter with garlic and paprika, then simmered until fall-apart tender while the eggplant softened and caramelized. A creamy sauce of olive oil, cream, garlic, and thyme was poured over the pork before finishing with fresh parsley.

The aroma was rich and savory: garlic, oregano, smoked paprika, caramelized onion, and warm pork filled the kitchen. Ana plated the dish in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the golden-brown pork and glossy eggplant alongside crisp red peppers and a side of roasted potatoes. She served it with warm white rice, lemon wedges, and a bowl of cold black-and-white coffee.

The occasion was a relaxed Friday dinner for Ana, her roommate, and her parents. The meal marked a peaceful transition after a long week of coursework, errands, and café projects. Ana enjoyed the comfort of home-cooked food and the company of friends, while also reflecting on her dream café and the patient-care coordinator role she wanted to pursue.
