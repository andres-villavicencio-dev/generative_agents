Ana Abad prepared Café de Casa con Vegetales de la Tierra, a warm, comforting Dominican-inspired dinner made with black beans, rice, plantains, carrots, and kale, served alongside a savory tomato and pepper stew.

She began by heating a large pot of water over medium heat and slowly adding the vegetables, letting them soften and release their natural flavors. The aroma of roasted plantains, earthy carrots, and caramelizing vegetables filled the kitchen, while the slow-simmered stew added a rich, savory depth. She stirred the black beans and rice until everything was evenly heated, then topped the dish with fresh herbs and a generous drizzle of olive oil.

Ana presented it in a simple white ceramic bowl, arranged neatly with colorful vegetables and a small side of rice. The meal was ideal for a relaxed Friday dinner, offering her a chance to connect with family and friends while creating a warm, memorable meal. It reflected her creative side, her commitment to community, and her growing confidence as a cook.
