Ana Abad prepared Guiso de Serrano with Black Beans and Roasted Tomatoes, a hearty Dominican-inspired stovetop meal. The dish features tender pork loin, seasoned black beans, diced tomatoes, onions, garlic, cilantro, and a savory tomato-onion broth. Alongside the main, she added fresh plantains, peppers, and serrano peppers, and seasoned everything with cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, and a little lime juice.

She brought the mixture to a boil over medium heat, stirring frequently until the meat was cooked through and the flavors melded. As it finished, the kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of roasting meat, caramelized onions, and earthy spices, mingling with the bright scent of garlic and citrus.

She plated the dish in a simple white bowl, garnishing it with fresh herbs, a drizzle of lime, and a few slices of plantain. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the rich stew and colorful vegetables creating a visually appealing contrast. It was prepared for a quiet Friday night dinner, a moment of comfort and connection with family and friends.
