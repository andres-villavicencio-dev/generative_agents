Ana Abad prepared a Dominican-inspired Sopa de Puerco con Queso Frito***, a hearty pork soup with fried cheese and grilled plantains. She began by sautéing diced pork with onions and garlic, then added chopped tomatoes and enough vegetable broth to create a rich, aromatic broth. Fresh cilantro, lime, and a dash of cumin were stirred in, followed by shredded fresh cheese and ripe plantains, which she sliced and toasted until golden. The mixture was simmered until the flavors melded, then served in a shallow bowl for easy eating.

The dish smelled warmly of roasted pork, toasted garlic, and sweet plantains, with the crisp edges of the cheese adding a savory crunch. Ana carefully plated it in a white bowl, garnishing it with a colorful array of plantains, fresh herbs, and a bright lime wedge. The simple presentation, balanced by the vivid colors and fragrant aroma, made the meal feel inviting and celebratory—a fitting centerpiece for a quiet Friday dinner.
