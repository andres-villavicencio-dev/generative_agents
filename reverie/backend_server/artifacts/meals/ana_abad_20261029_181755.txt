Ana Abad prepared Carne Asada con Salsa de Coco y Cebollino, a fragrant Dominican-inspired beef dish inspired by the lively tables she often observed at the Rose and Crown Pub.

She began by searing a lean cut of beef in a cast-iron skillet until it was browned and juicy, then added garlic, onion, and chopped bell pepper to infuse their flavors. The meat was finished in the pan with coconut milk, a dash of fresh lime, and a few crushed red chilies before being served over rice and topped with crisp cabbage and crispy tortilla strips. She served it on a white ceramic plate with a small bowl of creamy coconut sauce, lime wedges, and fresh cilantro.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of searing meat, caramelized onions, toasted garlic, and citrus. The candlelight cast a soft glow over the neatly arranged table, creating an inviting, celebratory atmosphere for Ana’s Friday family dinner.
