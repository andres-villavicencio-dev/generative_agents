Ana prepared Carne con Calabaza y Pepiño alongside a simple Ceviche de Pescado and a warm Arroz con Huevos, all inspired by Dominican cuisine. The meal featured tender chicken, caramelized sweet potato, zucchini, red pepper, pineapple, onion, garlic, cilantro, and a mild citrus-vegetable broth. Ana sautéed the vegetables and chicken in olive oil, then cooked the rice and topped it with cheese, avocado, and a fresh egg. She finished the dish with a generous drizzle of lime, pepper, and chopped parsley, while the ceviche was chilled and garnished with lime wedges and chili oil.

The kitchen filled with warm, fragrant aromas of roasted garlic, citrus, pineapple, and savory chicken. Each plate was arranged on a white serving platter with golden potatoes and colorful vegetables, creating an inviting, rustic presentation. The meal was prepared as a quiet Friday dinner, allowing Ana to finish her cooking, store the leftovers, and reflect on the connection she made with her friends and family.
