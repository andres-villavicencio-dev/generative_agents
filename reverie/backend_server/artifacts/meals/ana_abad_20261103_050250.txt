Ana prepared a Dominican-Inspired Breakfast Bowl, a vibrant, quick morning meal inspired by her café’s favorite ingredients.

She combined salsa, diced tomatoes, onions, bell peppers, avocado, feta, toasted corn, and a little yogurt in a shallow bowl. The salsa was warm and tangy, with the creaminess of the avocado and the bright acidity of the tomatoes balancing the feta and grains.

She toasted the corn in a dry pan, stirred in a pinch of cumin, and finished the bowl with a drizzle of olive oil and a sprinkle of chili flakes. The dish smelled rich, warm, and aromatic—herbs, toasted grain, citrus, and savory acidity filling the kitchen.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: colorful toppings arranged around a creamy base, with the bowl resting on a wooden board and a small card noting the salsa class recipe. It was the perfect breakfast to start her Saturday before checking on the salsa class and organizing her essentials for the upcoming grocery trip.
