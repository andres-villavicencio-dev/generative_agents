At 4:00 p.m., Ana prepared a warm Salsa Sunrise Bowl for herself, a comforting meal that balanced the demanding schedule with her creative life. The bowl featured crisp romaine lettuce, juicy cherry tomatoes, crunchy corn kernels, ripe avocado, feta cheese, and a tangy red tomato salsa. Ana also added grilled chicken, a sprinkle of paprika, lemon juice, and a pinch of cumin.

She prepared the grilled chicken by marinating it in olive oil, garlic, lemon, and cumin, then pan-searing it over a stove-top burner until the skin became golden and crisp. The salsa was made by sautéing onions, garlic, tomatoes, and spices until the tomatoes softened and the flavors released.

The meal smelled rich and aromatic, with the savory scent of garlic, the bright aroma of lemon, and the earthy freshness of tomatoes and herbs filling the small kitchen. Ana arranged the colorful ingredients in a shallow white bowl, topped with a generous handful of feta, and drizzled a little olive oil over the top. It was a simple, home-cooked dinner, prepared on a quiet Saturday afternoon as she reflected on her upcoming salsa class and creative projects.
