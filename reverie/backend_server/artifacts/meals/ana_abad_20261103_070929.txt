Ana prepared Salsa Sunrise Bowl for Saturday dinner. The dish combines tortilla chips, diced chicken, pico de gallo, corn salsa, black beans, cilantro, diced tomatoes, onion, lime juice, hot sauce, and a touch of cumin. She sautéed the chicken briefly, seasoned it lightly with oregano and garlic, and dressed the bowl with a fresh citrus-salsa mixture.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, zesty lime, cumin, and the bright citrus of the salsa. Ana arranged the colorful ingredients and chips in a wide bowl, garnishing it with a final drizzle of lime and a handful of fresh cilantro. She sat down to eat at home, taking a few sips of water and savouring the meal as the flavors warmed her.

The occasion was a quiet Saturday evening: a relaxed dinner after a demanding day, with no confirmed appointments or creative commitments. She had prepared the dish with purpose, hoping it would be a nourishing meal before she explored the salsa class and the fundraiser.
