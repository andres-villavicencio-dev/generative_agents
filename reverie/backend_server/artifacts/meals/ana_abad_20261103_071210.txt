Ana prepared a vibrant Salsa Sunrise Bowl, a colorful, quick breakfast meant for her Saturday morning routine. The bowl featured red tomato salsa with diced red and yellow bell peppers, fresh cilantro, and a squeeze of lime; cucumber and feta for crunch and a salty contrast; and whole-grain toast as the base. She also added a small pan-seared egg on the side.

The ingredients were chopped and tossed together in a pan, then arranged in a bright ceramic bowl. Ana finished it with a drizzle of hot honey, a few roasted garlic chips, and a sprinkle of cumin. The aroma of roasted garlic, sweet honey, citrus zest, and warm spices filled the kitchen.

The presentation was cheerful and inviting: the colorful vegetables, creamy feta, and glossy sauce made the bowl look like a mini art installation. On Saturday, February 18, it was a relaxed breakfast for Ana, who still had no confirmed appointments, meals, or creative commitments. She hoped to use the class on Friday if it was scheduled, and she was eager to participate alongside Jenny’s weekend recipe.
