Ana Abad prepared a simple Sunrise Oatmeal with Berries and Dark Chocolate for Sunday morning. The dish featured rolled oats, warm milk, a handful of mixed blueberries, raspberries, and sliced almonds, with a drizzle of cocoa powder and a few cinnamon sticks. She toasted the oats briefly in a pan, then folded in the fruit and chocolate, letting it simmer until the milk thickened. A tiny splash of maple syrup brought the flavors together.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of oats, milk, berries, and cocoa, while the kitchen table glowed with vibrant colors. She presented the oatmeal in a clean white bowl, garnished with the colorful fruit and a decorative cinnamon stick. The meal was part of her Sunday breakfast routine—a quiet, nourishing start to a peaceful day before she reviewed the café’s disputed fundraiser records and helped reconcile the final amount before sending the request to Maria Urban.
