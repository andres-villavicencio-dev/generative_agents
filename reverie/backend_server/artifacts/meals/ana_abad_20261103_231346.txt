Ana prepared Plantain-Butter Breakfast, a hearty, family-style breakfast that matched her planned community breakfast. She used ripe plantains, butter, eggs, sour cream, diced potatoes, and a pinch of garlic, with a little orange zest for brightness. She sautéed the plantains until they softened, then cooked the eggs in butter until the whites were set but the yolks remained soft. The potatoes were fried until crisp, and a small bowl of fresh fruit was added for color.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted plantains, caramelized onions, and savory butter. Ana carefully arranged the eggs in a warm ceramic plate, surrounded them with golden potatoes and crisp plantains, and garnished the dish with sliced orange and a sprig of parsley. She served it for breakfast on a cold Monday morning, combining comfort with the relaxed energy of a quick, shared meal before her evening shift.
