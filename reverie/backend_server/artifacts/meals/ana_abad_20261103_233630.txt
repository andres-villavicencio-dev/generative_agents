Dish: Tostada de Plantain with Roasted Plantains

Ana prepared a simple, warm plantain dish using the recipe Abigail Chen shared. She sliced ripe plantains into thin rounds, brushed them with olive oil, and toasted them in a dry pan until golden and slightly caramelized. She then added a few cloves of garlic, a pinch of salt, and a dash of smoked paprika, cooking everything until the plantains were tender and fragrant.

The aroma was warm and deeply savory—rich with toasted plantains, garlic, and a hint of smoked paprika. Ana arranged the crispy rounds on a small platter, garnishing each with a sprig of fresh parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. She served it at a cozy Tuesday supper at the Rose and Crown Pub, sharing it with Arthur Burton and the other patrons as they gathered for a relaxed evening.
