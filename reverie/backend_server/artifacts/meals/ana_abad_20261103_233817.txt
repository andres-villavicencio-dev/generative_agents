On a chilly Monday evening, Ana prepared Plantain-Pepper Pot Stew, a hearty, earthy dish inspired by the flavors she sketches for her café. The meal included ripe plantains, red bell peppers, onions, garlic, tomatoes, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, chicken or pork, and a splash of vegetable stock.

She sliced the plantains into coins and fried them in oil until golden and slightly sweet. Then, she cooked the vegetables and spices in a pot over medium heat, stirring until the mixture turned deep amber. After adding the protein and broth, she brought the stew to a gentle simmer and covered it for 20 minutes. The warm, savory aroma filled the kitchen with the scent of caramelized plantains and toasted spices.

Served in a shallow white bowl, the pot was garnished with fresh cilantro, lemon wedges, and crumbled feta. Ana plated it for dinner with Arthur, celebrating their upcoming shift together. The meal was simple, comforting, and full of anticipation—a perfect reflection of the warm, community-focused spirit she hopes to share in her café.
