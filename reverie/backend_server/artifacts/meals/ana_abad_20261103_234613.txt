On Monday, February 13, Ana prepared a Plantain and Coconut Crusted Fish dinner for the Rose and Crown Pub’s weekly community potluck. The dish featured yellow plantains, white plantains, cod, coconut milk, coconut cream, garlic, ginger, onion, oregano, and a dash of cayenne, baked with a crispy, golden crust.

Ana scored the plantains first, then tossed them with coconut cream and curry powder. She layered the cod and plantains with the remaining plantains, garlic, and coconut milk, covering everything with foil before baking at 425°F (220°C) for 20–25 minutes. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm coconut, savory garlic, and lightly smoky notes.

She arranged the fish in a rustic rectangular pan with the golden plantain edges, garnished with fresh parsley, a lemon wedge, and a drizzle of coconut cream. The meal was served as a hearty family-style dinner at the pub’s monthly potluck, where Ana and her friends shared laughter over the rich, comforting food.
