Ana prepared Pan-Roasted Plantain and Black Bean Fajitas. For the meal, she used ripe plantains, black beans, onions, bell peppers, cilantro, garlic, cumin, chili flakes, olive oil, and a pinch of salt. She sliced the plantains and peppers, sautéed them until soft, then roasted them in a skillet until caramelized. The black beans were seasoned and cooked until tender, while the aromatics added warm, savory depth.

The kitchen smelled of toasted cumin, caramelized onions, and the sweet aroma of plantains. Ana arranged the colorful fajitas on a white platter, garnished with bright green cilantro, and served them with toasted tortillas, lime wedges, and warm beans. The meal was prepared for a Tuesday community gathering, where Ana, Maria, Tony, and David were testing her ideas and tasting the dishes before sharing the results with Abigail Chen and Hailey Johnson.
