The meal is Plantain and Coconut Risotto with Coconut Milk, a warm, comforting dish inspired by Dominican cooking. Ana combines freshly peeled plantains, coconut milk, garlic, onion, parsley, thyme, and a pinch of salt with Arborio rice. She sautés the aromatics in a small pan, then slowly stirs in the rice and coconut milk until the grains are tender, creamy, and slightly caramelized, while the plantains soften alongside the rice.

The aroma fills the kitchen with sweet, earthy coconut, savory garlic, and toasted herbs. She serves the dish in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a few whole cloves. It is presented as a communal family dinner, suitable for sharing among friends or family.

The occasion is a quiet Tuesday evening, the final day of her shift at the Rose and Crown Pub. Ana is preparing the menu and drink notes for Jenny to taste-test, continuing to trade ideas with Abigail Chen and Hailey Johnson while completing her Community Fund reconciliation. The meal reflects her creative, inclusive, and purposeful lifestyle, offering warmth and inspiration before she goes home.
