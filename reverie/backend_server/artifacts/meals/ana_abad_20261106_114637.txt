Ana prepared Plantain and Coconut Coconut Curry, a simple yet comforting lunch meant to fill her pantry and prepare her for her café shift. She sliced ripe plantains, toasted them with a little butter, and cooked them with coconut milk, turmeric, cumin, garlic, and a touch of soy sauce. The curry was simmered gently until the plantains softened, then served over rice with a side of roasted eggplant and a chilled coconut-cream drink.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of toasted plantains, coconut, cumin, garlic, and the subtle sweetness of turmeric. Ana arranged the colorful ingredients in a shallow serving dish, placing the golden plantains, creamy eggplant, and steaming rice around the bowl. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the vibrant green of the turmeric and the soft brown of the toasted plantains adding visual appeal.

It was a quiet Tuesday lunch—Ana was checking her grocery-store ledger, verifying the community fund, and preparing for her café interview. The meal felt both practical and creative, a small gesture of care for herself before she returned to the café.
