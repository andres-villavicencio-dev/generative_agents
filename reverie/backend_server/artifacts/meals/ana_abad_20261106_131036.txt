Ana Abad prepared a plantain-inspired breakfast spread for a quiet Tuesday morning at her community café. The dish was Pan-Seared Plantains with Coconut Yogurt, Green Mango, and Roasted Garlic.

The key ingredients were ripe plantains, coconut yogurt, chopped green mango, roasted garlic, lemon zest, and a pinch of cumin. Ana first peeled and cubed the plantains, then brushed them with a little oil and seared them until they were golden brown and slightly caramelized. While they cooked, she warmed the coconut yogurt and roasted the garlic, adding the mango for brightness and the cumin for warmth. She finished the dish by gently spooning the yogurt over the plantains and garnishing it with fresh mint.

The aroma was warm, earthy, and subtly sweet—mixing the nutty richness of caramelized plantains with the fresh scent of mango and garlic. It was presented on a small white plate, arranged in a neat row with each ingredient clearly visible, accompanied by a small bowl of warm lemon-honey dressing. The meal was prepared as a special offering for her café’s opening shift, offering guests a flavorful, comforting start to their day.
