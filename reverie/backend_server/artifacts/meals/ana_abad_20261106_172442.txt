For her late breakfast, Ana prepared Plantain and Egg Frittata with Roasted Peppers. She combined ripe plantains, onions, tomatoes, garlic, oregano, and fresh herbs with eggs, cooking them until the plantains were tender and the frittata set. The dish was lightly pan-fried in a little butter, with a pinch of salt and pepper.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized plantains, garlic, and toasted herbs. Ana arranged the frittata on a warm plate, garnishing it with fresh cilantro and a drizzle of lemon juice, and served it alongside a simple grain bowl. She had finished the meal at around 10:30 a.m., just before her class and the late-shift meeting began.

The occasion was her final breakfast for the day while finishing her hospitality-management coursework and organizing her grocery list. The meal was a quiet moment of creativity and connection, inspired by her dreams of opening a café in the Dominican Republic.
