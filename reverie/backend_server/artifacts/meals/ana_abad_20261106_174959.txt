Ana prepared Plantain and Black Pepper Stew, a warm Dominican-inspired dish combining plantains, black beans, onions, tomatoes, and smoked paprika. She sautéed the onions and peppers in olive oil, then added the plantains, rinsed beans, and a can of tomato sauce. After simmering until the plantains were tender, she stirred in smoked paprika and a splash of lemon juice. The dish was served over white rice in a shallow ceramic bowl, with a small bowl of warm lime juice and a spoonful of toasted sesame seeds beside it.

The aroma filled the kitchen with sweet, earthy tones of caramelized plantains and savory smoked paprika, balanced by the bright acidity of the lemon. The presentation was rustic and inviting: golden-brown plantains contrasted with the deep brown of the beans and rice, while the bright lime zest and sesame seeds added a cheerful pop of color.

Ana prepared the meal for her friends Abigail Chen and Hailey Johnson on a casual Tuesday dinner, testing the flavors with them and deciding whether to adapt the stews for the next visit to the local plantain market.
