Ana prepared a Golden Plantain Breakfast with Coconut Cream and Avocado Toast, a simple yet warming meal inspired by her Dominican roots.

The dish features large, sweet plantains fried until golden, sliced with avocado, a generous spoonful of coconut cream, and a drizzle of honey. She toasted whole-grain toast with a little salt and pepper and topped it with the crisp plantains and creamy avocado.

The aroma filled the small room: sweet, earthy plantains mingling with the rich scent of coconut and toasted bread. Ana arranged the food on a simple white plate, arranging the plantain slices around the toast in a neat, rustic pattern.

It was a quiet Monday breakfast—Ana had just finished an evening shift with Arthur, followed by a few hours of coursework, before sharing the food with Abigail and Hailey. The meal felt both ordinary and deeply satisfying, a small moment of hospitality where everyone sat together, ate, and laughed.
