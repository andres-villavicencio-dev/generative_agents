Ana Abad prepared Plantain & Black Bean Fajitas, a quick, vibrant lunch inspired by her café’s Dominican roots. The dish featured grilled plantains, black beans, roasted chicken breast, shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, cilantro, onion, and a warm chili-lime dressing. She sautéed the plantains over medium heat until they browned and softened, then added the beans and chicken just before serving so everything remained crisp and flavorful.

The kitchen smelled of toasted garlic, chili, toasted corn, and fresh herbs. On a white plate, the colorful ingredients were arranged in overlapping layers, with the golden plantains, red beans, and green lettuce creating an appetizing visual. Ana ate the meal on a Tuesday evening while balancing her hospitality-management coursework and café budget, using her extra energy to refine a new recipe.
