Andrea prepared Golden Lemon Herb Pan-Seared Salmon, a simple yet elegant breakfast for her family. The dish used salmon fillets, lemon zest, fresh parsley, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of smoked paprika. She gently pan-seared the salmon until the skin turned crisp and the interior was just tender, then added the vegetables and lemon slices for a bright finish.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of roasted garlic, lemon, and warm herbs. She plated the salmon on a white plate, garnishing it with a few sprigs of parsley and a small lemon wedge.

This simple meal was part of a quiet Monday morning routine at Oak Hill College. After gathering the ingredients, she helped regulars track down research sources for the library’s vintage postcard display, turning breakfast into a shared moment of curiosity and collaboration.
