For a relaxing Monday dinner, Andrea prepared Braised Red Lentil and Roasted Vegetable Soup. She sautéed carrots, celery, onions, and garlic in olive oil, then added a generous portion of rinsed red lentils and a handful of chickpeas. Aromatics such as bay leaves, star anise, and a splash of tomato paste gave the dish its distinctive scent, while dried herbs and a pinch of smoked paprika added subtle warmth. The soup was simmered until the lentils were tender and the vegetables were soft, then finished with a spoonful of fresh parsley and a knob of butter.

Adequate red wine was poured in for the final minute, allowing its fruity notes to integrate with the savory flavors. The presentation was elegant and practical: the translucent soup was ladled into a shallow serving bowl, with crisp carrots, earthy chickpeas, and a garnish of fresh herbs on top. It was served alongside crusty bread and a simple green salad, creating a balanced, homey meal suitable for a quiet family gathering or a casual lunch with friends.
