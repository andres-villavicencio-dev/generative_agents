Andrea prepared Creamy Lemon-Herb Breakfast Casserole, a simple yet elegant weekday brunch. Her pantry provided eggs, butter, heavy cream, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, fresh chives, parsley, and a pinch of salt. She whisked the eggs with melted butter, cream, lemon juice, and mustard, then cooked them in a skillet until set. The casserole was transferred to an oven-safe dish, topped with a generous layer of chives and parsley, and baked until the edges were set and the top turned golden.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of butter, cream, and lemon, mingling with the crisp scent of fresh herbs. She presented the dish in a shallow ceramic bowl surrounded by sliced apples and a few berries, arranging the herbs over the crisp fruit. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday morning breakfast, inspired by her competitive spirit—elegant enough to impress, yet practical enough for a busy workday.
