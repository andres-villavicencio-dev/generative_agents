Andrea prepared Sage, Herb, and White Wine Pasta with Roasted Potatoes, a simple yet elegant Italian-inspired dinner for colleagues on February 14.

The dish featured long strands of al dente pasta, creamy parmesan butter, fresh basil, garlic, and a hint of lemon zest. The pasta was tossed in a garlic and herb cream, while the potatoes were roasted with rosemary, thyme, and a touch of honey until golden and tender. The wine used was a crisp, dry Pinot Grigio.

As she plated the meal, Andrea arranged the pasta in a neat, wide bowl, garnishing it with bright green basil leaves, chopped parsley, and a few roasted potato pieces. The wine was poured into a crystal decanter and set beside the bowl, with a small glass held ready for each colleague.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm sage, roasted potato, garlic, and the delicate finish of the white wine. The occasion was a casual but celebratory dinner for colleagues gathered to celebrate the library’s entrance exhibit and discuss upcoming research projects. The food, wine, and thoughtful presentation created a relaxed, creative atmosphere that made the evening feel both purposeful and enjoyable.
