Andrea prepared Golden Roasted Chicken with Herb-Roasted Potatoes for a Tuesday dinner with Oak Hill College’s colleagues. She used a whole bone-in chicken seasoned with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, and a splash of chicken stock, then roasted it with a honey-mustard glaze. Around it were small butternut potatoes, carrots, celery, and a creamy garlic herb sauce.

The chicken was seasoned lightly, roasted until golden and juicy, then basted with the sauce. The potatoes were tossed with olive oil, salt, pepper, and the same herb mix, while the vegetables were lightly sautéed to add color and texture. The oven was set to 425°F for about 40 minutes, finishing with a final glaze.

As the kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted poultry, rosemary, garlic, caramelized carrots, and lemon, the meal felt inviting and celebratory. She plated it on a white ceramic platter, arranging the golden chicken and colorful vegetables alongside a small glass of red wine. The occasion was an informal dinner with colleagues, intended to celebrate the library’s growing collection and give everyone a chance to enjoy the shared meal.
