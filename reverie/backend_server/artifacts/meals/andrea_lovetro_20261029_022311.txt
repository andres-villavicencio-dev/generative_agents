Andrea prepared a Miso-Glazed Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Brussels Sprouts and Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple yet elegant Sunday dinner for colleagues gathered to celebrate the college’s upcoming entrance display.

She sliced chicken breast, marinated it in a mixture of miso, lemon juice, garlic, shallots, and fresh herbs, then seared it in a hot skillet until golden. The pan juices were reduced slightly, creating a glossy glaze, while the vegetables were roasted until crisp and caramelized. She served the chicken over the vegetables and tossed them with a squeeze of lemon and a few sprigs of thyme.

The kitchen filled with warm, earthy aromas of garlic, miso, citrus, and roasted vegetables. She plated the meal loosely, arranging the chicken in the center and carefully placing the vegetables around it. A small glass of dry white wine and a neat glass of red wine accompanied the plates.

It was a relaxed but purposeful occasion: colleagues were gathering to share the day’s progress, while Andrea had the opportunity to show them how the library’s collection could tell stories through food.
