Andrea prepared a Heritage Pantry Lunch—a hearty, rustic bowl of wild rice, smoked chicken, roasted potatoes, and a tangy lemon-tahini dressing, served with crusty sourdough. She began by toasting the sourdough, then warmed the wild rice in a small pot. The chicken was pan-seared to golden perfection, and the potatoes were tossed with olive oil, garlic, salt, and paprika. She finished the dish with a creamy lemon-tahini dressing, sprinkling fresh parsley over the top.

The aroma was warm and savory: toasted bread, caramelized chicken, roasted potatoes, and a hint of citrus made the meal feel inviting. Andrea arranged the colorful components in a large white bowl, placing a wedge of lemon on the side. The presentation was simple but deliberate, with the golden chicken and earthy wild rice creating a pleasing visual contrast.

The occasion was a quiet Wednesday lunch, after a full day of organizing the library exhibit and discussing digitization with Mei Lin. Andrea wanted a comforting, nourishing meal that could easily be eaten by herself or shared with colleagues, turning the simple ingredients into a thoughtful, creative lunch.
