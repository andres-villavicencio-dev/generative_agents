For her Wednesday lunch, Andrea prepared a Wild Herb Quinoa and Lemon-Cucumber Salad, a refreshing, nutrient-rich side that could easily accompany her evening wine. She combined cooked quinoa, tender baby spinach, crisp cucumber slices, lemon-lime juice, feta, toasted almonds, and a few wild herb sprigs. Her dressing was a bright mix of olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, and a pinch of salt and pepper.

The preparation was simple: the quinoa was rinsed and then gently simmered until tender, while the vegetables were chopped and tossed lightly with the dressing. The salad retained a vibrant green color and a crisp, herbaceous aroma, with the lemon and garlic adding a subtle warmth.

It was presented on a white ceramic platter in a casual yet inviting manner—quinoa and greens arranged around a central lemon wedge, garnished with a sprig of thyme, a handful of almonds, and a few cucumber halves. The light, airy composition felt both elegant and approachable.

The occasion was a quick, informal lunch before Andrea’s evening curatorial work. It was a relaxed, creative meal prepared with intention, reflecting her competitive and artistic side as she prepared for the display of the WWII-era card and faded handwritten note.
