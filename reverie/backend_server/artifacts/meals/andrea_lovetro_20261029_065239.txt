Andrea Lovetro prepared Wine-Roasted Chicken with Herb-Crusted Potato and Roasted Vegetables for an early Wednesday dinner with a glass of red wine. The chicken was seasoned with dried thyme, rosemary, garlic, and paprika, then pan-seared until golden and juicy. It was served with creamy roasted potatoes, carrots, and Brussels sprouts, accompanied by a small bottle of Merlot.

The kitchen smells of warm herbs, roasted garlic, and the earthy scent of the wine. The presentation is elegant and rustic: the golden chicken sits in the center of a dark serving platter, while the vegetables and potatoes surround it in colorful, crisp layers. The arrangement feels both intimate and celebratory, as if Andrea is inviting friends over for a quiet evening of good food and conversation. The meal was prepared to accompany her Wednesday evening of library curatorial work.
