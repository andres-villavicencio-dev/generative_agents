Andrea Lovetro prepared Rosemary-Glazed Salmon with Lemon-And-Lavender Rice. The dish featured wild-caught salmon fillets, short-grain rice, baby spinach, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and fresh herbs. She marinated the salmon in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, and lavender, then roasted it until the flesh was flaky and golden. The rice was sautéed until crisp, then gently mixed with the salmon and vegetables.

Aromatic rosemary and lavender infused the cooking, while lemon brightened the flavors. The salmon’s glaze glistened and the rice remained tender with bright green and red accents. She arranged the plates at the dining table, placing the salmon in the center with the rice, vegetables, and a small bowl of lemon-topped salad beside it. Her wine glass rested near the edge, and a handwritten note about the collection’s strongest visual stories was tucked beneath the plates. The meal was prepared for a quiet Wednesday dinner, with the display’s research source hunt and discussion with Mei Lin as the occasion.
