At 6:00 p.m., Andrea Lovetro prepared Garden Herb and Lemon-Grilled Salmon with Lemon-Herb Rice, a light Mediterranean-inspired dinner designed to match her creative, competitive spirit. The meal featured wild-caught salmon seasoned with lemon, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of smoked paprika, served over fluffy rice infused with roasted carrots, shallots, and fresh parsley.

She sautéed the vegetables in a pan, seasoned the salmon with her favorite spices, and finished it with a drizzle of olive oil, lemon juice, and a final sprinkle of chopped parsley. The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of grilled fish, warm herbs, and toasted lemon zest.

Andrea plated the salmon in a shallow, rustic bowl, garnishing it with a small sprig of rosemary and a few edible flower petals. She placed a glass of dry white wine beside it and set the silverware across the table. For lunch, she had enjoyed a quiet dinner alone, savoring the flavors and reflecting on the evening’s discoveries—the WWII-era card and faded handwritten note—before heading home to her 10 p.m. bedtime.
