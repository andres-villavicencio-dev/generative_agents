Andrea prepared a Lobster Risotto with Garlic Butter and Lemon, a bright, restaurant-style meal that reflected her creative, competitive side. Key ingredients included fresh lobster tails, Arborio rice, onion, garlic, fresh herbs, lemon juice, white wine, olive oil, and a pinch of Parmesan. She sautéed the onions and garlic, added the wine, and deglazed the pan before slowly incorporating the rice and gradually stirring in the hot stock. The lobster was seared briefly, then added back to the rich risotto, finishing with a lemon zest and a drizzle of white wine.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm, savory scent of garlic and lobster, balanced by the bright citrus of the lemon and the subtle sweetness of the Parmesan. The presentation was elegant: the lobster was arranged atop a golden mound of creamy risotto, with lemon wedges and fresh herbs placed beside it and a simple glass of chilled white wine nearby.

It was part of a leisurely Wednesday evening meal after a busy day of organizing the library’s entrance display. The occasion was relaxed and celebratory, celebrating her work while giving her time to enjoy what she had created, with a splash of wine to accompany it.
