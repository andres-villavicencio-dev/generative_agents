Andrea prepared Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken with Sautéed Root Vegetables. Her favorite ingredients included bone-in chicken breasts, baby potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, fresh thyme, rosemary, lemon juice, and a splash of white wine.

She began by seasoning and searing the chicken, then arranged the vegetables in a baking dish. The rosemary and thyme were added, followed by butter and olive oil. The chicken was roasted until golden brown, while the potatoes and carrots became tender. A lemon-herb sauce was reduced at the end to provide a bright, fragrant finish.

The kitchen filled with the sharp scent of lemon and rosemary, softened by garlic, thyme, and the earthy aroma of roasted potatoes. She plated the dish with the chicken resting atop a bed of vegetables, garnished with parsley and a lemon wedge. It was served at 6:00 pm on Wednesday, February 15, as part of a relaxed Sunday family dinner. The casual, warm occasion was a quiet moment of connection and celebration after a full, purposeful day of library work.
