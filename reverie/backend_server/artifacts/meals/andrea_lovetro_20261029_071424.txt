Andrea prepared a warm Herb-Crusted Chicken with Lemon-Herb Glaze, a comforting centerpiece for her Wednesday evening dinner. The chicken breast was marinated with garlic, lemon, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of white wine, then seared to a golden brown before finishing in the oven with a creamy lemon-herb sauce. She added roasted potatoes, carrots, and green beans for a colorful side, finishing the dish with a small bottle of dry white wine.

The aroma was rich and inviting: sautéed garlic, toasted herbs, citrus, and savory chicken filled the kitchen. On the table, the chicken sat beside the potatoes and vegetables, garnished with a lemon wedge and fresh herbs, arranged in a simple but elegant presentation that highlighted both the rustic and sophisticated flavors of the meal.

Dinner was a quiet celebration of her weekday routine. After finishing the remaining food and taking a first sip of the wine, Andrea sipped her dinner, enjoying a peaceful moment of completion after a full day of curatorial work.
