Andrea Lovetro prepared Lobster Ravioli with Lemon-Herb Cream and Roasted Garlic. She used fresh lobster tails, penne pasta, cream cheese, garlic, parsley, lemon zest, olive oil, and a pinch of nutmeg. For the sauce, she sautéed the garlic, then gently incorporated the cream while whisking in the lobster and herbs. The ravioli was boiled until tender, then drained and tossed with the sauce before being plated alongside the remaining lobster.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and butter, a hint of oceanic sweetness from the lobster, and the fresh citrus scent of the lemon. She arranged the golden ravioli and bright orange sauce in a shallow bowl, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a few roasted garlic cloves, while a small glass of crisp white wine rested nearby.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed Wednesday dinner at Oak Hill College Library. It was her favorite way to unwind after a full day of curatorial work, board game nights, and research, turning a simple lunch into a warm, comforting celebration of flavor and creativity.
