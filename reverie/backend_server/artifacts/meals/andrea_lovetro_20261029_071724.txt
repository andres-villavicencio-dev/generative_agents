Andrea prepared Wine-Chilled Herb-Infused Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple yet elegant dinner for Wednesday evening.

She used a whole chicken, bone-in, with thighs and drumsticks, alongside carrots, potatoes, parsnips, and a handful of fresh herbs. The chicken was seared in olive oil until golden, then braised with garlic, shallots, thyme, rosemary, and a splash of dry white wine until the meat was tender and juicy. The vegetables were tossed with lemon juice, rosemary, and a small amount of butter for a bright, crispy finish.

The kitchen was filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and wine. Andrea plated the dish in a shallow, rustic bowl, arranging the golden chicken around the colorful root vegetables and placing a small glass of red wine beside it. The presentation felt casual and inviting, with the contrasting textures and earthy colors making the meal feel both intimate and celebratory.

It was a relaxed dinner on a quiet Wednesday, balanced by her earlier work in the library and her ongoing curatorial efforts.
