For Wednesday, February 15, Andrea prepared Wine-Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Basil and Roasted Vegetables, a quiet, family-style dinner meant to match the evening’s purposeful mood.

She roasted a whole chicken with the skin on, tossing it with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, and a splash of white wine. The chicken seasoned itself in the oven until golden and tender, while carrots, potatoes, and bell peppers were seasoned with paprika and lemon zest. After roasting, Andrea sliced the chicken and arranged it alongside the vegetables on a wide serving platter. Fresh herbs, lemon wedges, and a drizzle of balsamic glaze completed the presentation.

The aroma was warm and inviting—rich roasted chicken, garlic, citrus, and herbs mingling with the savory earthiness of the vegetables. The meal was served with a glass of dry white wine, making it suitable for a relaxed Wednesday evening. It was neither a celebratory feast nor a formal dinner; it was simply Andrea’s way of enjoying food, her favorite activity, and the evening’s curiosity for art and information.
