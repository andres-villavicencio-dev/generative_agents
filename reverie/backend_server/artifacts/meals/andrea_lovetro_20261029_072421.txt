Andrea prepared Creamy Wild Mushroom Risotto with Lemon-Basil Dressing. The dish featured arborio rice, wild mushrooms, shallots, garlic, vegetable broth, parmesan, thyme, and fresh herbs, finished with a bright lemon-basil dressing.

She prepared it in the kitchen after cleaning and organizing the dining area. After sautéing the mushrooms and shallots, she toasted the rice and added the broth slowly, stirring constantly until the grains became tender and creamy. Lemon was squeezed into the finished risotto just before serving, while parmesan and chopped parsley were stirred in at the end.

The kitchen smelled earthy, warm, and subtly herbaceous—mushrooms, garlic, toasted grains, and citrus. Andrea plated the rich risotto in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with a generous drizzle of lemon dressing and scattered parsley. She served it with crusty bread and a glass of wine as the occasion: a quiet, cozy Wednesday dinner after a busy day of curatorial work, featuring the WWII-era card and handwritten note she was organizing.
