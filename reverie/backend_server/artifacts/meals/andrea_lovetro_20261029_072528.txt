Andrea prepared Creamy Herb-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and a Splash of Pinot Grigio. The meal featured chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, shallots, thyme, rosemary, garlic, lemon, olive oil, and a small glass of crisp white wine.

She began by sautéing the shallots and garlic in olive oil until fragrant, then roasted the vegetables with rosemary until the carrots softened and the potatoes were tender. While the vegetables cooled, she seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and thyme, then seared the thighs on both sides before finishing them in the pan. A spoonful of cream and fresh herbs was stirred into the pan, creating a rich, herbaceous sauce.

The chicken was plated with a colorful medley of roasted vegetables, served with crusty bread, and accompanied by a small glass of Pinot Grigio. The dish smelled warm and earthy, with savory herbs, citrus, and gentle caramelization filling the kitchen. It was a simple, comforting dinner for Wednesday, chosen after her full day of library curatorial work.
