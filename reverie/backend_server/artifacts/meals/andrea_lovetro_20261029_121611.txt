Andrea prepared Wine-Infused Chicken with Roasted Asparagus and Creamy Potato Salad, a simple yet elegant dinner for a quiet Thursday night at Oak Hill College. She marinated the chicken with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, and a touch of wine, then roasted it alongside asparagus until golden and tender. As the vegetables softened, she tossed the potatoes in a buttery, lemony dressing and grated cheese, creating a colorful, refreshing side.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and herbs, followed by the warm fragrance of the wine cooking through the chicken. She plated the meal in a deep ceramic bowl, arranging the golden chicken, crisp asparagus, and creamy potatoes alongside a small bowl of fresh mint and a glass of chilled red wine. The presentation was understated and inviting, with the rich colors of the vegetables and the glossy glaze of the chicken drawing the eye.

It was a relaxed, celebratory dinner—perfect for an evening of reading, planning, and enjoying a little food after a full day of curation and collaboration.
