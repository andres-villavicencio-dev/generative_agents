Dinner: Creamy Tuscan Chicken with Lemon-Garlic Broth

Andrea prepared a simple, comforting Sunday dinner at home, combining familiar flavors with her signature touch of wine. She chose boneless chicken thighs, spinach, baby potatoes, garlic, olive oil, red wine, lemon, and fresh thyme, and served the dish over a bed of arugula and roasted potatoes.

She marinated the chicken in olive oil, garlic, thyme, and a splash of red wine, then roasted it in the oven until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. While it cooked, she sautéed the spinach and potatoes with olive oil, salt, and pepper. The warm broth was finished with lemon juice and fresh parsley.

The aroma was inviting and aromatic: savory chicken, tender spinach, roasted potatoes, and a hint of wine mingling with the freshness of herbs. Andrea presented the meal in a simple white bowl, arranging the chicken at the center with colorful vegetables around it and a small wedge of lemon beside the plate.

It was prepared as a relaxed family dinner on Friday, February 17, during her quiet time exploring local places. The simple, nourishing meal reflected her practical, creative approach to household responsibilities and the calm, purposeful balance she maintained between her work and life.
