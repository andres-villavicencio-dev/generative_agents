Andrea Lovetro prepared Herb-Infused Lemon Garlic Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple yet elegant dinner for a quiet Friday. She used bone-in chicken breasts, frozen baby carrots, red potatoes, and green beans, fresh thyme, rosemary, lemon zest, garlic, olive oil, and a splash of dry white wine. She seared the chicken until golden, then simmered it with the vegetables in the pan, tossing in the herbs and wine just before serving. The warm aroma of roasted potatoes, garlic, and thyme mingled with the bright scent of lemon and fresh herbs.

The meal was presented on a shallow white platter, with the chicken arranged in the center and the colorful vegetables placed around it. Andrea had kept the ingredients fresh and properly stored, ensuring the wine and herbs remained crisp. It was a relaxed Friday dinner designed to complement her creative work at Oak Hill College; the wine provided a pleasant finish, while the easy preparation gave her time to discuss the display with Mei Lin.
