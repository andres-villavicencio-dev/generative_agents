Andrea prepared Wine-Roasted Root Vegetables with Garlic Herbs, a simple yet elegant dinner suited to a quiet Friday morning. The main ingredients included carrots, parsnips, sweet potatoes, red onions, and fresh thyme, with a splash of dry white wine added at the end to soften the vegetables.

She began by sautéing the carrots and onions in a pan until their edges turned golden, then added the parsnips and sweet potatoes and stirred until they were tender. The wine was poured over the vegetables in the last minute, deglazing the pan with the golden brown fond before the remaining wine was absorbed. Herbs and a pinch of salt and pepper were stirred in, and the dish was finished with a splash of olive oil and a squeeze of lemon.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm herbs, roasted vegetables, and the subtle notes of wine and garlic. Andrea plated the colorful root vegetables on a rustic white plate, garnished with fresh thyme and a few scattered seeds. She added a small glass of wine beside it, making it feel festive without being overly celebratory.

The meal was prepared for an unannounced Friday evening. Its calm, earthy flavors made it a perfect companion to the quiet exploration of the library’s postcard collection, combining Andrea’s competitive, creative side with thoughtful, grounded living.
