Andrea prepared Sunday Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and a Lemon-Herb Vinaigrette.

The dish featured tender chicken breast, carrots, potatoes, and bell peppers, topped with a bright lemon dressing and a sprinkle of fresh herbs. She marinated the chicken with lemon, garlic, olive oil, thyme, and dried rosemary, then grilled it over medium heat until golden and cooked through. The vegetables were roasted until tender, with the pan juices reduced to form a glossy glaze.

The kitchen filled with the inviting aroma of caramelized garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory chicken. Andrea plated the meal in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the vegetables in neat rows and placing the chicken on a platter beside a small bowl of lemon vinaigrette. She added a few rosemary sprigs and toasted almonds for visual contrast.

The occasion was a quiet Friday evening at Oak Hill College Library, where she balanced curating the entrance display with creative collaboration and a simple family-style meal. The presentation felt warm and communal, fitting her competitive, creative side and the peaceful, purposeful atmosphere of the day.
