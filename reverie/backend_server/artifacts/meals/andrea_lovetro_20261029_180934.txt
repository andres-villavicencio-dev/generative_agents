Andrea prepared Rosemary-Glazed Roast Chicken with Herb-Roasted Vegetables, a comforting main course to accompany dinner with wine. She started by seasoning a whole chicken with salt, pepper, garlic, dried rosemary, thyme, and sage, then placed it in a roasting pan with a small amount of lemon juice and a pinch of sugar. The vegetables—carrots, sweet potatoes, red peppers, and onions—were tossed in olive oil, salt, and the same herbs before being arranged around the chicken.

Andrea roasted everything in the oven at 400°F (200°C) until the chicken reached an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C), basting it halfway through with a little of the pan juices. As it finished cooking, she sliced the chicken, served the vegetables with lemon wedges, and plated everything with fresh rosemary sprigs and cracked black pepper. The dish was garnished with a few whole black garlic cloves and served alongside a crisp green salad and a glass of white wine.

The meal had a warm, aromatic scent of rosemary, garlic, lemon, and roasted vegetables. It was presented as a simple, elegant family dinner, with the chicken taking center stage and the vegetables arranged in a rustic bowl beside it. On Friday, February 17, the occasion was a quiet family dinner, while Andrea’s creative energy continued in her library exhibit and with colleague Mei Lin.
