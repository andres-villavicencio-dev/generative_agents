Andrea prepared Wild Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy, a family-style breakfast-meal-style lunch made for Saturday’s relaxed day.

The dish features a whole chicken seasoned with rosemary, thyme, paprika, and a touch of lemon, roasted until the skin was golden and the breast was tender. Around it were carrots, onions, parsnips, and potatoes tossed with garlic, parsley, and a splash of red wine to add depth. The vegetables were sautéed briefly and then roasted alongside the chicken, with a rich, savory gravy finished by reducing the pan juices.

The aroma rose from the kitchen as the herbs, garlic, and roasted roots mingled with warm chicken and a hint of wine. The presentation was generous and inviting: the golden chicken rested on a platter beside colorful root vegetables and a bright lemon wedge, with the gravy bubbling gently beside it.

It was prepared as a cheerful Saturday lunch, balancing the energy of her creative library work with a grounded, comforting meal. Andrea would have eaten it with a glass of wine alongside her colleague Mei Lin.
