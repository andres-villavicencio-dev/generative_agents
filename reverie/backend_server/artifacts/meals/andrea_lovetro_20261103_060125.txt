Andrea prepared a Hearty Cranberry-Glazed Root Vegetable Bowl for Saturday dinner. The dish featured carrots, parsnips, sweet potatoes, Brussels sprouts, and red cabbage, all coated in a warm cranberry-herb glaze and served with cracked walnuts, thyme, and a handful of fresh rosemary.

She prepared the vegetables by roasting them at 375°F for 25 minutes, then glazing them in a pan with chicken broth, cranberries, Dijon mustard, garlic, and a splash of olive oil. The rosemary was added at the end to preserve its bright aroma. The finished bowl was arranged in the center of a white serving platter, surrounded by roasted root vegetables and topped with scattered walnuts and a sprig of rosemary.

The meal was prepared in preparation for a relaxed Saturday dinner with John. Its aromatic blend of roasted carrots, parsnips, Brussels sprouts, and fresh herbs created a warm, earthy flavor, while the glossy glaze and colorful arrangement made the dish feel both comforting and inviting.
