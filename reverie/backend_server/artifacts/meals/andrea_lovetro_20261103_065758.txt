Andrea prepared a warm, rustic bowl of Sunday Egg Scramble with Avocado Toast and Roasted Vegetables.

She scrambled eggs, spinach, diced tomatoes, and mushrooms with a splash of cream and a pinch of red pepper. For the avocado toast, she sliced ripe avocado, toasted whole-grain bread, and sprinkled it with lemon juice, chili flakes, and a few sesame seeds. Roasted peppers, onions, and carrots added a bright orange color and a savory depth.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted nuts, caramelized onions, and the earthy scent of fresh herbs. She arranged the colorful vegetables and golden toast in a warm ceramic bowl, topped it with the creamy egg scramble, and placed a small glass of chilled Prosecco beside it.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed Saturday lunch to avoid running out of eggs, with John joining her for the occasion. It felt informal and comforting, a simple yet satisfying way to enjoy the day’s rest after organizing the library display.
