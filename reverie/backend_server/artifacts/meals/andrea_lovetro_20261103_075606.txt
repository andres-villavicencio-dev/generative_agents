Andrea prepared Sunday Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice and Roasted Root Vegetables, a fresh lunch for John that reflected her competitive, creative side. The meal featured grilled chicken breast, rice seasoned with lemon, parsley, and garlic, and carrots, potatoes, and onions roasted with olive oil, rosemary, and thyme until tender and lightly caramelized.

The kitchen smelled of warm herbs, roasted vegetables, and the savory sweetness of lemon. Andrea arranged the white rice in a shallow oval dish, placing the grilled chicken on top and the colorful root vegetables around it. Garnish of sliced green onion and lemon wedges completed the presentation.

She took the meal for lunch on Saturday, February 18, while organizing her library’s postcard exhibit and preparing for an upcoming board game night. The relaxed atmosphere of exploring the local café gave the meal a cheerful, everyday feel.
