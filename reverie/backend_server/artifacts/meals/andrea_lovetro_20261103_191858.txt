Andrea prepared a Sunday Roast Chicken with Herb Crisp and Grilled Vegetables, a simple, warm breakfast-style meal for herself and her colleague Mei Lin. The centerpiece was a whole chicken, roasted with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, and honey until the skin was golden and tender. She arranged the chicken on a platter alongside crisp herb-crusted potatoes, sautéed carrots, and bright green peppers, topped with a drizzle of lemon-herb sauce.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasting meat, fresh herbs, toasted garlic, and caramelized vegetables. It was presented as a communal Sunday breakfast—hand-warmth, shared laughter, and a relaxed morning. While the display at Oak Hill College Library continued around the corner, Andrea’s peaceful, purposeful routine offered a quiet contrast to the busy days ahead.
