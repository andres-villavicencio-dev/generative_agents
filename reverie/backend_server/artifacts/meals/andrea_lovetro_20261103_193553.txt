Andrea prepared Herb-Crusted Lemon Herb Chicken with Asparagus and Roasted Potatoes for dinner at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured a whole chicken seasoned with fresh rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, garlic, olive oil, and a touch of Dijon mustard, with the skin roasted to crisp and the meat kept moist with its own juices. Alongside it were silver asparagus spears, halved potatoes, and a small bowl of lemon-tahini dressing. She seasoned the chicken thoroughly, placed the vegetables and potatoes on a baking sheet, and roasted them in the oven until tender and evenly browned.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and lemon. As she plated the meal, she arranged the golden potatoes, bright green asparagus, and juicy chicken in a simple rustic bowl, drizzled the dressing over the top, and garnished it with a few fresh herbs. The presentation felt casual and inviting, with the colorful ingredients creating an appealing contrast against the light-colored plates.

This was her Sunday reward after organizing the postcard display and checking her contact list. It was a relaxed, home-cooked meal—a quiet moment of creativity and comfort before her evening routine.
