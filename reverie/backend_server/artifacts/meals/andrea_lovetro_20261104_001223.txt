Andrea prepared Garden-Scrambled Eggs with Roasted Tomato and Herb Crust, a warm breakfast designed to balance the morning’s creative and competitive energy. She used fresh eggs, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, thyme, parsley, and a little honey, along with ripe tomatoes, basil, and shallots. First, she sliced the tomatoes, shallots, and garlic and roasted them until caramelized, then set aside. Next, she scrambled the eggs and folded them over the fragrant tomato mixture.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and butter. Andrea plated the eggs in a shallow bowl, garnishing them with a bright herb dust and a sprinkle of toasted nuts. The presentation was simple and inviting, with the golden eggs catching the warm kitchen light.

She ate it slowly over the weekend when she was curating the Oak Hill College library entrance display. The meal served as a quiet, creative escape from the competitive hunt for elusive information—each bite felt like a small discovery, celebrating her lifelong love of discovery and artful preparation.
