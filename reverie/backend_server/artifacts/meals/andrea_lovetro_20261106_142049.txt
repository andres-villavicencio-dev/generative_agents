Andrea prepared a warm Oatmeal-Orange-Apple Breakfast Bowl for her 12:30 meeting with Cledith Hardin. The dish featured rolled oats cooked in milk, topped with sliced apples, chopped walnuts, and a drizzle of orange zest and honey. A small bowl of cinnamon raisin oatmeal cookies was served alongside it.

She prepared the meal in two stages: first, she cooked the oats with milk, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt until the oat texture was tender and the liquid had reduced slightly. Then she added the cold fruit and nuts and finished the pan with a few drops of orange juice and a splash of honey. The aroma was warm, inviting, and subtly sweet, with the orange and apple providing a bright citrus finish.

The breakfast bowl was presented in a simple white ceramic serving dish, arranged so the oats and fruit created a colorful, rustic appearance. It was intended as a cozy, nourishing meal to lift her spirits before her Wednesday workday and prepare her for the grant proposal, budget, and research duties ahead.
