Andrea prepared a Herb-Crusted Salmon with Lemon-Herb Rice and Garlic Butter for a quiet dinner on Monday, February 13. The meal was served to regulars and friends at the Oak Hill College dining hall, giving her a chance to relax after a busy workday.

Dish: Salmon with Herb-Crusted Rice and Lemon-Herb Garlic Butter  
Key Ingredients: Salmon fillet, mixed herbs (parsley, chives, thyme), garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, rice, and a splash of wine.  
Preparation: The rice was sautéed first, then finished with butter and a pinch of salt. The salmon was seasoned with herbs and lemon juice, then pan-seared until the skin was crisp. Afterward, it rested in the pan until the juices ran clear. The garlic was sautéed briefly, followed by the cooked rice and a final drizzle of butter and lemon.  
Aroma: Warm, earthy, and bright—a blend of toasted garlic, savory herbs, citrus, and the gentle warmth of the butter.  
Presentation: The salmon was arranged on a white ceramic platter with the rice beside it, garnished with chopped parsley and a wedge of lemon. A small glass of red wine and a side of roasted carrots completed the casual, inviting presentation.  
Occasion: An informal weekday dinner, part of the social group that regularly gathers at Hobbs Cafe. Andrea’s competitive spirit showed in the perfect sear and the careful presentation, while the relaxed atmosphere made the meal feel special and memorable.
