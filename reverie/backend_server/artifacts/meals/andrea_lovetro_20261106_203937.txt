Andrea prepared Wine-Roasted Tomato & Herb Pasta for a relaxed dinner with friends. The dish features long strands of egg pasta tossed with roasted tomatoes, garlic, fresh basil, olive oil, and a small amount of balsamic vinegar. She added sun-dried tomatoes, crumbled feta, and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil, finishing the meal with a splash of dry white wine.

The cooking method began with sautéing onion, garlic, and fresh herbs in a pan, then baking the tomatoes and vegetables for 20 minutes until the sauce reduced and turned glossy. She roasted the pasta in the oven for 15 minutes, then tossed it with the warm sauce, letting the ingredients meld together. The warm kitchen filled with the savory scent of roasted tomatoes, garlic, basil, and wine.

Served in a shallow bowl, the pasta was arranged with generous portions of feta and sun-dried tomatoes, with a few basil leaves scattered over the top. It was presented for a casual Friday gathering—a relaxed dinner with friends—where Andrea’s creative approach to cooking turned a simple pasta dish into an inviting, colorful centerpiece.
