Andrea prepared Savory Tomato Gazpacho with Roasted Tomatoes and Crispy Pancetta. The simple dish featured ripe tomatoes, canned tomatoes, canned chickpeas, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, crushed tomatoes, and plenty of water. Pancetta was pan-seared until crisp, then mixed into the warm soup for a savory, slightly salty finish.

She cooked the soup on a small stove, stirring gently so the tomatoes softened without separating. The aroma filled the kitchen with the sharp scent of garlic, the bright tang of lemon, and the rich, savory depth of pancetta.

For presentation, she arranged the soup in a shallow bowl with a colorful garnish of basil, a few lemon wedges, and fresh herbs. She served two small plates alongside the main dish, adding a slice of toasted baguette and a couple of warm rolls. The meal was prepared to celebrate a quiet Tuesday at Oak Hill College, a relaxed way to finish dinner while preserving her creative spark.
