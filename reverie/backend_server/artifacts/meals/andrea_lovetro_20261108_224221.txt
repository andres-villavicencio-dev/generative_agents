Andrea prepared a Garden-Legume and Herb-Grilled Lamb with Lemon-Parmesan Sauce, served with roasted potatoes, root vegetables, and a small pot of white wine. The meal was prepared on a cold Tuesday evening with her partner, Klaus Mueller, who brought home a bottle of wine and a glass.

She seasoned the lamb with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a touch of smoked paprika, then marinated it briefly in the refrigerator. While she roasted the vegetables and potatoes, she sliced the lamb and tossed the pan with the lemon-herb sauce, finishing it with a few fresh herbs. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted lamb, rosemary, garlic, and the faint scent of wine.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: the lamb rested on a white cloth with the vegetables arranged around it, and the wine was served in a small glass beside a bowl of lemon wedges. The occasion was a quiet family dinner, but with a splash of wine as a celebratory touch.
