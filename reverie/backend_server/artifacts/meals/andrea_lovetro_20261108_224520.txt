Andrea prepared a warm Caramelized Onion, Prosciutto, and Roasted Tomato Soup for the family dinner. She began by sautéing finely diced onions until translucent, then added garlic, shallots, and a pinch of salt. She stirred in a splash of olive oil, followed by carrots, celery, and parsnips, cooking them briefly until the vegetables softened. The soup was made with canned tomatoes, vegetable broth, a little flour to thicken it, and fresh basil. For the protein, she sliced fresh prosciutto and grilled it until crisp, then arranged it in a shallow bowl with a few lemon slices and a sprig of thyme.

The aroma was rich and earthy, with caramelized onions, garlic, roasted vegetables, and the smoky scent of cured meat mingling with the bright citrus of the lemon. She presented the meal in a rustic ceramic serving dish with a small basket of crusty bread and a glass of chilled wine, creating an intimate and festive table setting.

The occasion was a cozy family dinner on February 14, the eve of the holiday celebration. Andrea’s competitive, creative nature added a lively touch to the gathering, making every shared meal feel both nourishing and celebratory.
