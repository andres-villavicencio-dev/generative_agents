Andrea prepared Crispy Lemon-Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and a Splash of Wine, a cozy dinner for the postcard display board game night.

She marinated chicken thighs with lemon juice, garlic, fresh thyme, parsley, and a splash of apple cider vinegar, then seared them to golden crispiness before baking. Root vegetables—parsnips, carrots, and sweet potatoes—were roasted with olive oil, salt, pepper, and rosemary until tender and caramelized. A simple red wine reduction served as a sauce, while the meal was finished with a drizzle of balsamic glaze.

The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, warm herbs, and the delicate scent of red wine. The chicken was arranged around the centerpiece vegetables, with a bright red wine glass and a small plate of golden bread positioned beside it. It was served as a relaxed, festive dinner for board game night and the postcard display, with wine and conversation filling the evening.
