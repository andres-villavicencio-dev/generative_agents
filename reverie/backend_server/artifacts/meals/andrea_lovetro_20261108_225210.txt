Andrea prepared Wine-Roasted Herb-Lamb with Lemon-Basil Rice, a simple yet elegant dinner for the evening board-game night.

She began by washing and drying the lamb, then marinating it with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of red wine. After roasting on a covered pan, she arranged the meat atop a bed of fluffy rice seasoned with lemon zest, parsley, and a drizzle of honey.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of rosemary and the warm scent of toasted herbs and garlic. She plated the lamb in a shallow bowl, sprinkling the rice with fennel seeds and serving a small glass of red wine beside it.

The occasion was a relaxed family-friendly gathering: guests gathered around a large board game, while Andrea hosted the meal. Her competitive spirit and artistic flair turned the dinner into a memorable party, balancing the playful energy of the games with the comforting warmth of the wine-and-dinner experience.
