Andrea prepared Wine-Glazed Lemon Herb Chicken with Rosemary Garlic Potatoes, a comforting dinner perfect for the postcard display display.

She sliced chicken breast into bite-sized cubes, seasoned them with lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, rosemary, and herbs, then roasted them until golden. Alongside her, she tossed potatoes with butter, fresh parsley, and a drizzle of lemon oil, finishing them with a pinch of black pepper. A splash of dry white wine was added to the pan during the last few minutes to create a fragrant, golden sauce.

The oven’s warm glow filled the kitchen with the rich aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized garlic, herbaceous rosemary, and buttery potatoes. Andrea arranged the chicken on a white platter, with the potatoes nestled beside it and a small glass of wine and a plate of fresh fruit nearby.

The meal was prepared for the evening’s board game night, where the family had gathered to share a relaxed supper while Andrea’s display provided a charming backdrop of old photographs and handwritten notes.
