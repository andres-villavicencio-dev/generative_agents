For Tuesday evening, Andrea prepared Garden-Heritage Pasta with Lemon-Herb Cream Sauce, a warm, rustic pasta dish designed to echo the nostalgia of the postcards she was arranging. The recipe combined elbow macaroni, extra-virgin olive oil, garlic, cherry tomatoes, fresh basil, pine nuts, and Parmesan cheese, with a creamy sauce made from a splash of white wine, cream, and a little thyme.

The kitchen smelled of caramelized garlic, earthy herbs, and a hint of lemon. Andrea arranged the pasta in a deep skillet, letting it bubble gently before adding the tomatoes, garlic, and wine. She finished it with a generous drizzle of lemon-herb cream and sprinkled the dish with the Parmesan.

The presentation was simple yet striking: a glossy pasta plate surrounded by colorful garden vegetables, with small handwritten notes and a family postcard tucked beside the bowl. It was the centerpiece of a quiet board-game night with Ayesha Khan and Klaus Mueller, where everyone gathered to enjoy the meal and friendly competition while browsing the library entrance’s collection.
