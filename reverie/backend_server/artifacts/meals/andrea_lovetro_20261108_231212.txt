Andrea prepared Wine, Olive, and Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple yet elegant dinner paired with a glass of red wine. The chicken was seasoned with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, and a touch of paprika, then seared until golden and cooked through. Root vegetables such as carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes were roasted in the same pan until tender and caramelized.

Aromatically, the meal smelled of warm herbs, roasted potatoes, garlic, and the subtle sweetness of ripe tomatoes. It was presented on a white serving platter with the chicken arranged in the center, surrounded by colorful root vegetables, lemon slices, and a small bouquet of rosemary. A clear glass of red wine was placed nearby.

The occasion was a relaxed dinner for an evening board game night at Oak Hill College. Andrea’s competitive and creative side made the meal feel lively and inviting, while the wine added a romantic, convivial touch for her guests.
