Andrea prepared Wine, Herb, and Golden Tomato Pasta with Roasted Root Vegetables for a relaxed evening dinner. The dish featured handmade pasta, roasted carrots, parsnips, red onion, garlic, fresh basil, oregano, and a splash of dry red wine. She sautéed the root vegetables in olive oil until softened, then tossed them with garlic and herbs before serving the pasta with a light tomato‑based sauce.

The aroma was warm, savory, and herbaceous, with hints of roasted carrots, garlic, and wine. She arranged the dish on a large white platter, placing the pasta in the center and arranging the golden vegetables and basil around it, with a small glass of red wine nearby. The occasion was a quiet family evening at Oak Hill College, where she entertained guests with board games and wine while checking scores and discussing the games with Ayesha Khan and Klaus Mueller.
