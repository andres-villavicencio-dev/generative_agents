Andrea prepared a Vineyard & Garden Pot-Pourant for her Tuesday evening gathering. The dish featured a golden-brown tomato base, ripe tomatoes, fresh basil, a pinch of garlic, and crumbled goat cheese, with a small serving of crisp roasted chicken and lemon-herb bread.

The vegetables were sautéed until tender, then simmered with the cheese and sauce. A splash of dry white wine was reduced into the pan, followed by the remaining ingredients and a handful of fresh herbs. The chicken was roasted until crisp, then sliced and arranged alongside the pot-pourant in a shallow serving dish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthiness of tomatoes, the brightness of basil, and the warmth of roasted chicken. Presenting the meal in a rustic ceramic bowl with lemon wedges and a sprig of thyme, Andrea added a splash of wine and arranged the remaining game pieces nearby. The occasion was a relaxed board game night, with guests gathered around the table to enjoy the food and company.
