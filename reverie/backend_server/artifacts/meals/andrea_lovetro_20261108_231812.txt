Andrea prepared Rustic Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Grilled Apples, a warm dinner designed to accompany the postcard display at the Oak Hill College library entrance.

The meal featured a whole chicken, skin-on, seasoned with olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, lemon, and a touch of smoked paprika. Root vegetables—carrots, parsnips, and beets—were roasted until tender and caramelized, while Granny Smith apples were grilled until their skins softened. Fresh parsley, thyme, and a drizzle of balsamic glaze completed the flavor profile.

The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, citrus, earthy herbs, and lightly smoked spices. The chicken was plated on a rustic wooden board beside the vegetables and apples, garnished with fresh herbs. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the golden glaze catching the warm library evening light.

It was prepared as a relaxed family dinner, perhaps intended to accompany a board game night. Andrea’s competitive flair and creative flair made the food feel both comforting and celebratory.
