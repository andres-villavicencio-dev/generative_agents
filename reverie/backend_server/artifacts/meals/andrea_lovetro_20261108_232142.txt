Andrea prepared Wine-Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Tarrar and Roasted Potatoes for the board game night. She marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, and dried oregano, then roasted it until golden and juicy. The potatoes were seasoned with rosemary, olive oil, and salt, then roasted until crisp. Lemon-tartar was made with mayonnaise, lemon zest, dill, capers, and croutons, while the potatoes served as a comforting side.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken, lemon zest, thyme, garlic, and roasted potatoes. The presentation was elegant and warm: the golden chicken sat beside the bright red potatoes, garnished with fresh parsley, and arranged alongside a glass of chilled wine and a small plate of crackers. It was a simple, intimate evening meal for the board game night, celebrating the library community’s shared passion for games, food, and conversation.
