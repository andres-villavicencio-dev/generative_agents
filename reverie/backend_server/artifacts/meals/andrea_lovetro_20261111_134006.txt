Andrea Lovetro prepared a Garnished Lemon-Garlic Herb Chicken Bowl for a quiet Monday lunch. She started by marinating chicken thighs in olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, and fresh thyme, then seared them in a skillet until golden and tender. The chicken was sliced and arranged in a shallow ceramic bowl, with crisp vegetables—carrots, snap peas, and celery—tossed around it. A simple vinaigrette of olive oil, lemon juice, and herbs was brushed over the bowl.

The dish smelled warmly of roasted poultry, lemons, garlic, and earthy vegetables. Andrea arranged the ingredients in a rustic, slightly asymmetrical way, using a wooden spoon to gently mix them while the chicken glistened. She served it at the Oak Hill College library counter with a small plate of crusty bread and a glass of chilled wine, completing a relaxed lunch on a day she was curating the library’s entrance display.
