Andrea prepared Panini with Roasted Chicken, Lemon, and Herbs for Tuesday’s lunch. The panini used whole roasted chicken, garlic, lemon, olive oil, thyme, rosemary, and a crisp crust made from softened brioche dough. She toasted the chicken first, then filled the panini with the savory mixture, folded the panini, and baked it until the golden crust formed.

The aroma was warm, savory, and herbaceous, with hints of roasted poultry, garlic, and lemon. She presented the panini on a white ceramic platter, arranging the golden-brown bread, juicy chicken, and bright green herbs around a platter of sliced fruit and a small glass of red wine.

The meal was prepared as part of the Oak Hill College Tuesday Lunch. As the librarian, Andrea’s creative and competitive nature helped her organize the buffet, serve the panini, and coordinate the food with Ayesha Khan and Klaus Mueller, who were researching sources and swapping digitization ideas with colleague Mei Lin.
