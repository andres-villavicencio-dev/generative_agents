Andrea prepared Winter Harvest Soup with Cranberry-Ginger Garnish, a simple yet festive lunch for the library team. The soup featured carrots, celery, onions, parsnips, potatoes, lentils, and a splash of wine to keep it warm and comforting. She added fresh thyme and a few sprigs of rosemary before simmering the vegetables and legumes together. A bright garnish of dried cranberries, orange zest, and ginger was served on top, with a drizzle of warm broth.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of roasted carrots, celery, and wine, mingling with the sharp fragrance of dried rosemary and the citrusy hint of ginger. Andrea arranged the bowls in a neat, festive pattern, placing each serving on a white ceramic plate with a small red napkin and a handwritten tag. She accompanied the meal with a glass of wine, turning the simple lunch into a warm, celebratory gathering for the team.
