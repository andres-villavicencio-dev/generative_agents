Andrea prepared Rustic Tomato Basil Soup, a simple yet comforting evening meal suited to a Monday afternooning at Oak Hill College. The soup used slow-roasted tomatoes, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, vegetable broth, and fresh basil, with a creamy finish from a touch of cream and a pinch of nutmeg.

She simmered the ingredients gently over low heat, adding the cream only at the end to preserve the soup’s bright, fragrant aroma. Andrea sliced the tomatoes, stirred the vegetables, and ladled a rich golden broth over the finished dish. She arranged the bowls in a neat row and set a small glass of red wine beside them, creating an inviting, casual presentation.

This was a quiet family-style dinner after helping regular patrons track down research sources. The warm, aromatic soup offered a relaxed contrast to her competitive, creatively curious nature, making her evening feel both nourishing and celebratory.
