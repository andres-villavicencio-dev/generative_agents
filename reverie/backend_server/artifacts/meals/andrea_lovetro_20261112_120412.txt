For Tuesday, Andrea prepared a warm Garden Vegetable Skillet with Lemon-Tahini Dressing, a comforting, aromatic breakfast or brunch choice. The dish featured crisp broccoli, carrots, sweet potatoes, and baby spinach, all sautéed in olive oil with garlic and herbs until golden and tender. She added chickpeas, feta, and a bright lemon-tahini dressing, finishing it with a sprinkle of paprika and a generous drizzle of olive oil for visual contrast.

Aromatic rosemary and garlic infused the pan throughout cooking, while fresh basil and finely chopped parsley added a hint of green freshness. The vibrant colors and savory-sweet flavors created an inviting scent that filled the kitchen.

Andrea arranged the ingredients on a wooden cutting board, using a serving platter for the colorful vegetables and a small white bowl for the creamy dressing. She placed lemon wedges, a sprig of rosemary, and a toasted pita square beside the skillet, creating a balanced and elegant presentation.

The meal was prepared for a library-themed breakfast or brunch gathering, where friends could enjoy the hearty dish alongside board games and coffee. It reflected Andrea’s cheerful, creative side while providing a cozy, nourishing start to her day.
