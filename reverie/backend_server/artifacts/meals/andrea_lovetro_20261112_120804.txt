Andrea prepared Heritage Vegetable Skillet with Lemon Herb Chicken for a cozy Tuesday dinner. The dish used bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, onions, celery, bell peppers, and garlic, topped with a savory lemon-herb sauce. She seared the chicken in a hot skillet until golden, then added the vegetables, stirring until they were tender and caramelized. Andrea added the sauce at the end, allowing the chicken to finish cooking without drying out, and served the dish with crisp lettuce and warm whole-grain bread.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of garlic, herbs, lemon, and roasted vegetables. She plated the meal in a simple white bowl, arranging the chicken, colorful vegetables, and golden-brown bread to create an inviting presentation. The occasion was a relaxed family-style dinner with friends, offering a peaceful contrast to her busy week.
