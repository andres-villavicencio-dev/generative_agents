For Tuesday, February 14, Andrea prepared a comforting Mediterranean vegetable skillet with a simple lemon-herb sandwich. The dish featured crisp zucchini, yellow squash, cherry tomatoes, spinach, red onions, and plenty of olive oil, finished with a sprinkle of parsley and a generous drizzle of lemon zest.

Andrea sautéed the vegetables in a cast-iron skillet until they were tender and lightly caramelized, then folded in the spinach just before the final minute so it remained crisp. She toasted a toasted baguette, sliced a tomato, and layered it with a creamy lemon-herb spread for a light, refreshing sandwich. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasting vegetables, toasted bread, and fresh herbs.

The meal was presented on a rustic wooden board with the vegetables arranged artfully alongside the sandwich. It was the perfect companion to Andrea’s evening routine, balancing her competitive, creative spirit with a moment of calm and connection with friends.
