Andrea prepared a Garden Vegetable Skillet with Lemon-Herb Sauce for lunch on Tuesday, February 14. She combined crisp carrots, baby potatoes, sliced red bell peppers, and baby spinach with a bright lemon dressing, herbs, and a generous portion of grilled chicken thighs.

The dish was cooked in a large skillet over medium heat until the vegetables were tender and the chicken was cooked through. She finished the sauce with chopped parsley, thyme, and a squeeze of lemon, then spooned the pan juices over the vegetables. The aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized vegetables, and zesty citrus filled the kitchen.

She arranged the meal on a simple white platter, placing the colorful vegetables in the center and the golden chicken around them. A small glass of chilled red wine and her favorite sandwich complemented the presentation. The lunch was part of a relaxed day spent preparing for her application to The Rose and Crown, while she still enjoyed the vegetable skillet and simple sandwich for comfort.
