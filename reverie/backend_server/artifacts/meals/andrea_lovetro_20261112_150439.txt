For Tuesday, February 14, Andrea Lovetro prepared a Hearty Vegetable Skillet Dinner with a Simple Tomato-Crusted Chicken Sandwich.

The skillet began with bell peppers, onions, carrots, and zucchini, sautéed gently in olive oil until lightly caramelized. She added garlic, tomato sauce, dried herbs, and a splash of wine, then covered the pan to simmer until the vegetables softened and the sauce thickened. For the sandwich, she toasted sourdough, sliced avocado, spinach, and crisp lettuce, seasoning the inside with lemon zest, salt, and pepper.

Warm from the oven, the meal smelled inviting: savory garlic, sweet tomato, earthy vegetables, and the subtle warmth of wine. The golden chicken was flaky, the vegetables tender and colorful, and the bread toasted to a crisp finish.

Adequate portions of bread and a glass of wine made this a comfort evening meal. She prepared it before going out, hoping the warmth would accompany her thoughts on the day ahead. The simple ingredients and familiar flavors offered a quiet contrast to the challenges of her application to The Rose and Crown. By the time she sat down, she felt energized, curious, and grateful for the evening’s companionship.
