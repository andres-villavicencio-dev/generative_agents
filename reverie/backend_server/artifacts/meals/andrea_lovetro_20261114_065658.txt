Andrea’s dinner for Monday, February 13 was Saffron-Spinach and Lemon-Butter Chicken with Lemon-Thyme Rice. She prepared it on a stovetop, using bone-in chicken thighs, crisp spinach, pearl onions, garlic, thyme, rosemary, paprika, and a splash of dry white wine for the pan sauce.

She sautéed the chicken until the skin turned crisp, then added the spinach until it wilted. The wine was reduced slightly to concentrate its aroma, and the mixture was finished with butter, lemon zest, and a final drizzle of white wine reduction. The rice was cooked separately, then layered over the warm chicken and garnished with roasted vegetables and a pinch of black pepper.

The meal smelled deeply aromatic: citrus, roasted garlic, herbs, and wine. It was presented in a shallow white bowl, with the creamy chicken contrasting against the bright orange rice and a few glossy green leaves for a cheerful, elegant finish.

It was an ordinary evening meal, but Andrea made it feel special by adding a splash of wine and carefully arranging the colors and textures. Her curious, competitive spirit showed in how she balanced the flavors and presented the dish as both nourishing and celebratory.
