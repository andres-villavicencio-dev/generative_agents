Andrea prepared Wine-Roasted Hearty Beef Stew with Roasted Root Vegetables, a comforting dinner designed to match her relaxed evening at Oak Hill College. The stew was made with lean beef, carrots, parsnips, potatoes, onions, and garlic, seasoned with rosemary, thyme, onion powder, and a splash of dry white wine. She sautéed the aromatics first, added the vegetables and meat, then covered the pot and let it simmer until the beef became tender and the vegetables softened.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onions, roasted root vegetables, and the subtle fragrance of wine. Andrea served the stew in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a few crisp apple slices. She served the remaining vegetables and a small portion of pan juices as extra sides.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday dinner with friends and colleagues. The meal reflected Andrea’s creative, competitive side: she carefully balanced the flavors and texture, turning a simple pot of soup into a satisfying centerpiece. The wine-freshened beef added a subtle brightness, while the hearty vegetables provided warmth, making it a cozy, convivial dinner fit for a lively gathering at the college library.
