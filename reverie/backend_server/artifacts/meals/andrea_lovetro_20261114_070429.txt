On Monday, February 13, Andrea cooked Lemon-Garlic Herb-Ham for a quiet Tuesday dinner with her friends. She prepared the ham with a rich, savory gravy, using fresh lemons, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of dry wine. The wine added a subtle fruity depth, while the lemon brightened the aroma. She browned the ham slices in butter until golden, then deglazed the pan with the wine, followed by the garlic, herbs, and simmered until the gravy became glossy and the ham was tender.

She plated the dish with roasted potatoes and carrots, garnishing it with fresh parsley. The presentation was simple yet elegant: the golden ham nestled beneath creamy potatoes, with a bright lemon wedge and herbs arranged around the edges. The warm aroma of roasted garlic and wine filled the kitchen, inviting anyone nearby to imagine sharing the meal.

The occasion was a casual family dinner, but Andrea’s competitive spirit turned it into a lively family gathering. She served it with a glass of red wine, laughing as her friends tried to identify the dish and debating which ingredient should take center stage. By 6:30 p.m., the table was set, the wine glass raised, and the evening had begun.
