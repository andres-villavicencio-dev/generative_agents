Andrea prepared Lemon-Garlic Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple yet hearty dinner for the Oak Hill College library gathering. The meal featured boneless chicken thighs, baby potatoes, carrots, and onions, dressed with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of dry white wine. She sautéed the vegetables until crisp, browned the chicken, then roasted everything together. The aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and wine filled the kitchen as she stirred the pan, creating a warm, aromatic scent.

She plated the chicken over a small bed of golden vegetables, garnishing it with a wedge of lemon and a scattering of parsley. The presentation was bright and inviting, with the golden-brown chicken and vibrant colors making the dish stand out against the library’s warm tones. It was prepared for a quiet dinner with regulars and colleagues—Ayesha Khan, Klaus Mueller, and Mei Lin—who were enjoying the gathering and swapping research ideas.
