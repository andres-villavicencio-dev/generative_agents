Andrea prepared Wine-Sparked Tuscan Vegetable Risotto, a warm, earthy Italian meal that balanced rich risotto with fresh vegetables and a splash of wine. The key ingredients included Arborio rice, sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, shallots, spinach, parmesan, and a small glass of dry white wine. She sautéed the garlic and shallots in olive oil, then slowly added the rice and stirred in the wine to deglaze the pan. The vegetables and remaining wine were added gradually, followed by chicken broth, parmesan, and a generous amount of Parmigiano-Reggiano.

The aroma was rich and aromatic, with notes of toasted garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, herbs, and faint wine. The finished risotto had a creamy texture and a lightly golden brown crust, with the wine absorbed into the grains and vegetables. It was presented in a shallow white bowl with a drizzle of olive oil and a final garnish of parsley and a few Parmesan crumbs. The meal was prepared for dinner, with Andrea sharing it with Ayesha Khan and Klaus Mueller while discussing research and digitization ideas with colleague Mei Lin.
