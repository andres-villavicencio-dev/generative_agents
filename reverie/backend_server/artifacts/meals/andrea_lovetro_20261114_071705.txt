Recipe: Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken with Cranberry-Ginger Glaze

Andrea prepared a hearty dinner for herself while curating the Oak Hill College library entrance display. The meal featured a whole roasted chicken seasoned with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and lemon zest, accompanied by roasted root vegetables and a bright, zesty glaze made from dried cranberries, ginger, and balsamic vinegar.

The chicken was first marinated in the refrigerator, then roasted at 400°F (200°C) until golden and tender. Vegetables were roasted alongside it until crisp and caramelized, while the glaze was basted over the finished bird just before serving. The kitchen filled with the fragrant aroma of rosemary, lemon, toasted garlic, and warm spices.

The meal was presented on a white platter with a rustic ceramic bowl in the center, surrounded by the colorful, aged postcards Andrea was arranging. She took a final, admiring look at the display, satisfied that the elegant presentation matched the curious, competitive spirit she brought to every task.
