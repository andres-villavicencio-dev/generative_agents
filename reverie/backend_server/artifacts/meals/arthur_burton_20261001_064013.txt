Arthur Burton’s dinner is a warm chicken and wildflower herb risotto, featuring tender chicken pieces simmered with fresh rosemary, thyme, and garlic in creamy Arborio rice, finished with a drizzle of melted butter and a sprinkle of edible wildflower seeds. The dish is aromatic—earthy herbs blend into the richness of saffron-tinted broth, evoking the garden’s scent. Presented on his usual kitchen table, it rests beside the sealed envelope of succulent and wildflower seeds, symbolizing renewal. This meal is a quiet celebration of homecoming after a day spent with Jane at The Rose & Crown, honoring friendship, simplicity, and the promise of planting beauty—a gentle, heartfelt occasion blending nourishment and hope.
