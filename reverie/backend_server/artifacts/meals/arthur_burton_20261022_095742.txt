Arthur prepared a Lemon–Elderflower Chicken Salad with Crispy Potatoes for lunch.  

Key ingredients: grilled chicken breast, baby spinach, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, roasted baby potatoes, parsley, lemon zest and juice, elderflower cordial, olive oil, and a littleDijon mustard.  

Preparation method: He seasoned and pan-grilled the chicken until golden and cooked through, then boiled and lightly crushed the potatoes with herbs. The leaves and vegetables were tossed with the dressing, while the chicken was sliced on top.  

Aroma: The dish smelled fresh and fragrant, with bright notes of lemon, delicate elderflower, parsley, and warm savory chicken.  

Presentation: Served on a white plate, with the colorful salad arranged around the crisped potatoes and sliced chicken placed prominently in the center. Lemon wedges and a small sprig of parsley added a neat finishing touch.  

Occasion: A relaxed Thursday lunch during the steady rhythm of running The Rose and Crown Pub, prepared before Arthur returned to the computer to save his updated cocktail recipe notes.
