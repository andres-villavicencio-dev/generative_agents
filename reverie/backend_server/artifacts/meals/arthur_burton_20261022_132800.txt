Arthur Burton prepared Pub-Style Rosemary Lamb Chops with Garlic Mash and Roasted Carrots.

The main ingredients were lamb chops, rosemary, thyme, garlic, butter, potatoes, carrots, olive oil, lemon, and a splash of red wine. He seared the chops in a hot pan until golden, then finished them in the oven with rosemary and garlic, allowing the meat to remain juicy while developing a crisp exterior. The potatoes were boiled and mashed with butter, garlic, and cream, while the carrots were roasted with olive oil, honey, and cracked pepper until lightly caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted meat, herbs, garlic, and earthy carrots. He plated the chops beside a generous scoop of mash, arranging the glossy carrots around them and garnishing with fresh rosemary and a thin lemon slice.

The meal was prepared as a quiet dinner before his workday, fitting his schedule of eating around 4 p.m. before opening The Rose and Crown Pub at 5 p.m. It was hearty, refined, and comforting—a fitting meal for a busy Monday behind the bar.
