Arthur Burton prepared a hearty lamb shepherd’s pie for his early dinner before opening The Rose and Crown Pub. The filling consisted of slow-braised minced lamb, onions, carrots, and peas, seasoned with thyme, rosemary, Worcestershire sauce, and a splash of red wine. He cooked the filling until rich and flavorful, topped it with creamy mashed potatoes, and baked it until the edges crisped and the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of herbs, roasted meat, and golden potato.

The pie was served in a small ceramic dish, with the mash piped into a rustic dome and lightly bronzed beneath the grill. A simple side salad of crisp lettuce, cherry tomatoes, and vinaigrette added freshness, while a glass of dry cider complemented the savory meal. Arthur ate quietly at the pub around four o’clock, using the break before his five o’clock opening to fuel the long evening behind the bar and to savor a familiar, comforting dish before the doors opened.
