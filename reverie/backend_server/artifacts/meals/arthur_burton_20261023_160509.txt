Arthur prepared Golden Hour Eggs, a warm breakfast dish for his morning at The Rose and Crown Pub. Using two large eggs, he cracked them into a pan with a little oil and seasoned them with freshly cracked black pepper, a pinch of salt, and a splash of lemon juice. He folded the eggs gently to keep them creamy, then served them with toasted sourdough slices, crumbled cheddar, crispy bacon, and a side of fresh fruit for a sunny breakfast.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of egg, butter, toasted bread, and savory bacon. The eggs looked inviting and bright, their golden yolks shining against the warm wooden counter. Arthur plated them neatly on a white plate, arranging the toppings in an appealing pattern.

This was his first morning breakfast since opening the pub, and he took the time to enjoy it with a cup of tea. The meal felt cozy and welcoming, fitting into his routine of preparing for a busy day as bartender and bar owner.
