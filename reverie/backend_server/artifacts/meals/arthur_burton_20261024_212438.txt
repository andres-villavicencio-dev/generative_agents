Arthur prepared a simple, comforting English breakfast for opening The Rose and Crown on a bright Wednesday morning. The dish featured a golden-brown pancake, scrambled eggs, grilled ham, fresh cheddar, crisp bacon, and a generous slice of sourdough toast. He toasted the sourdough in butter, added a little pepper, and let the eggs cook gently in a little oil until the edges curled and the center remained moist. The ham and bacon were crisp, while the cheddar melted into the cheese.

Aromatic rosemary sprigs and a few thyme leaves added a warm, herbaceous scent throughout the kitchen. The breakfast was presented on a white platter alongside a mug of steaming coffee and a small bowl of fresh fruit. It was prepared for the pub’s opening—his first full Wednesday—when he wanted to make guests feel welcome and welcome them into the cozy atmosphere of The Rose and Crown.
