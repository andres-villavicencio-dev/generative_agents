Arthur prepared a rustic Garlic Herb Chicken with Lemon-Sage Toast, a comforting main dish inspired by his morning walk through Johnson Park. He sliced chicken breast into bite-sized pieces, marinated it with garlic, lemon, fresh sage, thyme, and a splash of olive oil, then pan-seared it until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. Afterward, he tossed the chicken with roasted garlic, fresh thyme, and lemon juice before slicing the pan-seared chicken over a bed of earthy roasted potatoes and caramelized onions.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of caramelized garlic, warm herbs, toasted bread, and lightly spiced chicken. Arthur topped each dish with a crisp slice of sourdough, drizzled lemon juice, and a generous dusting of sea salt, before garnishing it with a sprig of fresh thyme. The meal was served at a quiet Tuesday dinner, with Arthur and his friends joining him at The Rose and Crown Pub to celebrate the warmth of the neighborhood gathering.
