Arthur prepared a Rose-and-Crown Garden Salad with Heirloom Tomato, Goat Cheese, and Lemon-Herb Vinaigrette. The salad featured crisp romaine lettuce, sliced red and yellow tomatoes, halved hearse apples, and small goat cheese rounds, all arranged in a bright white bowl. He lightly toasted the tomatoes, sliced the cheese, and tossed everything with a lemony dressing made from olive oil, red wine vinegar, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, thyme, and a touch of honey.

The kitchen smelled of fresh herbs, ripe tomatoes, toasted nuts, and the warm aroma of lemon zest. When he served it, he arranged the vegetables in a neat arc around a golden, toasted rosemary bun, garnishing the sides with chopped parsley and a few whole garlic cloves.

The meal was part of Arthur’s Wednesday neighborhood party, where he was preparing a relaxed, communal lunch. He wanted his guests to feel welcome, so the salad reflected his friendly, generous nature and his love for making ordinary gatherings feel special.
