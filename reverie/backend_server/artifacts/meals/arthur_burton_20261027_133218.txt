Arthur prepared a hearty Rose and Crown Garden Vegetable Soup, designed to nourish his friends and neighbors on the Wednesday party.

The soup was made with carrots, celery, onions, carrots, potatoes, tomatoes, onions, garlic, and fresh herbs. Arthur sliced his vegetables, washed them thoroughly, and sautéed them in a pot until they became fragrant and tender. He then simmered the vegetables with tomato sauce, stock, and a splash of wine in a large pot, letting the flavors meld.

Aromatically, the kitchen filled with the earthy scent of carrots and celery, the savory fragrance of garlic, and the bright, herbaceous aroma of thyme. He finished it with a dash of lemon juice and a final sprinkle of dried rosemary, giving the soup a warm, inviting finish.

The vegetables were arranged neatly around a platter and served in a rustic ceramic bowl. Arthur garnished each serving with a sprig of parsley and a few whole garlic cloves, presenting the colorful, rustic dish with care. The soup would be served on the Wednesday party as a comforting starter, celebrating the neighborhood gathering while encouraging guests to feel welcome and nourished.
