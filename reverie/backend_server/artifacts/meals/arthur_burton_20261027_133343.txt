Arthur prepared Winter Garden Salad with Crispy Apple-Corn, a fresh, colorful lunch for The Rose and Crown Pub’s neighborhood party. The dish featured crisp green salad, crisp apple, crunchy corn, creamy goat cheese, toasted pine nuts, and a bright lemon-herb vinaigrette.

He washed the vegetables, chopped the apple into bite-sized rounds, and tossed them with the salad. The dressing was made by whisking olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and chopped herbs until it was lightly golden. He added crumbled goat cheese and toasted pine nuts, then served the salad in a shallow white bowl, garnished with extra herbs. The aroma of fresh herbs, lemon, and warm toasted nuts filled the room, while the crunchy apple and corn added a crisp, refreshing contrast.

It was part of Arthur’s Wednesday preparations for the neighborhood party, reflecting his friendly, generous nature. He hoped the lunch would feel warm and welcoming, encouraging neighbors to gather and enjoy a shared meal.
