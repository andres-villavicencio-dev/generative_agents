Arthur prepared a hearty Roast Vegetable & Herb Bowl for his Wednesday lunch. He washed crisp green beans, carrots, and parsnips, then chopped them into bite-sized pieces. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of paprika, then roasted in the oven until tender and lightly caramelized. A simple lemon-butter sauce was made in a small pan, and a few toasted pine nuts and a drizzle of balsamic glaze were added for brightness.

The meal was presented on a warm, large white serving platter, with the vegetables arranged around a small bed of toasted sourdough bread and a colorful side salad of crisp lettuce and tomatoes. It smelled rich and aromatic—warm roasted carrots, garlic, thyme, and a hint of lemon filled the kitchen. The presentation was inviting and cheerful, fitting the friendly, welcoming atmosphere of Arthur’s neighborhood party. Arthur prepared the lunch as a relaxed, nourishing meal while continuing to explore Johnson Park and prepare for his party.
