Arthur prepared a hearty Rose and Crown Garden Vegetable Bowl, a warm, colorful lunch inspired by the shaded gaps and hawthorn canopy he had explored earlier in the day. He combined roasted carrots, crisp asparagus, cherry tomatoes, and bell peppers with chicken broth, thyme, a touch of lemon, and a finishing drizzle of olive oil. The vegetables were tossed briefly in hot oil until lightly caramelized, then served over a bed of crispy chickpeas and a fluffy white bean salad.

The kitchen filled with the warm, earthy aroma of roasted carrots, thyme, and gently simmering broth. Arthur arranged the bowl in a wide ceramic dish, arranging the colorful vegetables in overlapping layers and placing a small, golden piece of chicken in the center. He garnished it with fresh thyme, a lemon wedge, and a sprinkle of sea salt.

He served the lunch for his neighborhood party on February 15, a gathering of friends and neighbors gathered to celebrate their community. The bright, welcoming presentation and generous portion reflected his passion for making people feel special.
