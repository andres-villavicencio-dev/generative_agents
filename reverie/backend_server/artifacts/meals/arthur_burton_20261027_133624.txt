Arthur prepared Roasted Garden Salad with Lemon-Herb Oil, a simple yet comforting lunch inspired by the shaded paths and wildlife he explored in Johnson Park.

The key ingredients were crisp romaine, cherry tomatoes, crisp celery, cucumber, feta, walnuts, and a handful of kale. He tossed them gently with extra virgin olive oil, lemon juice, rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a little honey for sweetness. The salad was arranged on a white platter, with a small bowl of feta and walnuts nearby and a lemon wedge on the side.

He made a warm vinaigrette in a small glass, warming the oil over the stove until it shimmered, then drizzled it gently over the salad just before serving. The aroma was bright and fresh—a fragrant mix of herbs, garlic, citrus, and nuts.

The lunch was prepared for Arthur’s neighborhood party, where he and his fellow bartenders were gathering friends, neighbors, and residents to celebrate. The simple, welcoming presentation reflected his goal to make everyone feel special and included the shaded paths and old oak canopy that had inspired his earlier exploration.
