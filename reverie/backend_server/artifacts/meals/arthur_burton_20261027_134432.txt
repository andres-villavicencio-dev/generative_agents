Arthur prepared Grilled Chicken with Rosemary Lemon Herb Glaze, the main dish for Wednesday’s neighborhood party at The Rose and Crown Pub. He marinated chicken breasts in olive oil, garlic, rosemary, lemon juice, thyme, and a pinch of paprika before grilling them over an outdoor charcoal grill until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The glaze was reduced and brushed over the chicken just before serving, giving it a bright citrus-sweet finish.

The meal was presented simply but warmly: platter-sized breasts arranged in the center of a wooden board, with a side of roasted vegetables and a crisp salad. A small bowl of lemon wedges and a sprig of rosemary sat beside the plates, and every guest received a folded paper napkin. The scent of roasted garlic, rosemary, citrus, and charcoal grilling filled the pub as Arthur served the group, creating a welcoming atmosphere for the party.
