Arthur prepared a Rosemary Lemon Chicken with Herb-Roasted Potatoes and Grilled Spinach. He marinated chicken thighs in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, and thyme, then baked them until golden and tender. The potatoes were tossed with olive oil, lemon zest, rosemary, salt, and pepper, while the spinach was wilted with a little butter before being added to the pan.

The aroma was warm, savory, and subtly citrusy—herbs mingling with the richness of the chicken and the crispness of the potatoes. Arthur arranged the food on a large platter, garnishing the chicken with chopped parsley and lemon slices and placing the grilled spinach beside it.

He prepared the meal for a neighborhood party at The Rose and Crown Pub. The setting was informal and inviting, with friends mingling and a few guests enjoying the food. Arthur served himself a generous portion of chicken and potatoes, then shared the spinach with a friend before taking a moment to enjoy it.
