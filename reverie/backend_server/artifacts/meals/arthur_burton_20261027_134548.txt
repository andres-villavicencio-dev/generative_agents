Arthur prepared Rosemary Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Red Wine Reduction for a neighborhood gathering. He started with a whole chicken, a whole lemon, garlic, rosemary sprigs, thyme, fresh orange slices, and a generous amount of honey. He roasted the chicken in the oven with the vegetables and wine reduction until the skin was golden and the meat was tender.

Before serving, he reduced the leftover juices with a little butter, thyme, and orange zest, then ladled it over the chicken. The dish was arranged on a rustic ceramic platter, with the golden chicken surrounded by roasted carrots, parsnips, and orange wedges. The aroma of warm rosemary, roasted garlic, sweet orange, and wine rose through the kitchen as he plated it.

The meal was prepared for the pub’s February 15 neighborhood party, where guests were encouraged to enjoy a relaxed lunch and share stories. Arthur’s friendly presentation and generous portion size reflected his approach to making people feel welcome.
