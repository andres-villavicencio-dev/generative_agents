Arthur prepared Rose and Crown Garden Hushpuppies with a Garlic-Butter Chicken Salad, the main dish for the neighborhood party on Wednesday, February 15.

The hushuppies were made by dredging floury potatoes in seasoned flour and frying them until they turned golden and crisp. A simple garlic-infused butter and parsley sauce was spooned over them, while fresh green beans, carrots, and crisp lettuce completed the colorful, refreshing salad. For the chicken, Arthur marinated tender chicken breasts in olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, and herbs before pan-searing them until the skin was crisp.

The aroma of the meal was warm and inviting, filled with toasted flour, savory garlic, lemon zest, and the subtle sweetness of the rosemary. Arthur presented the dish on a rustic white platter, surrounded by crisp vegetables and garnished with lemon wedges and a sprig of rosemary, creating an elegant yet approachable look.

Arthur intended the meal for the party’s main course, serving it to his friends and neighbors. He hoped the cheerful flavors would encourage guests to enjoy the shaded paths of Johnson Park and celebrate a day together, reflecting his friendly, generous nature and commitment to making people feel welcome.
