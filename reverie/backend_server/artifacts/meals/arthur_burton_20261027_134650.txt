Arthur prepared Hearty Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Herb Crust for his Wednesday lunch. He used a whole chicken, a handful of carrots, parsnips, baby potatoes, and onions, along with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and lemon. After browning the chicken until golden, he seared the vegetables in the same pan, then placed them around the bird and covered it with foil for slow cooking until the chicken reached an internal temperature of 165°F. The crust was made by mixing flour, salt, and herbs, pressing it over the chicken, and baking until crisp and golden.

The kitchen smelled warmly of roasted herbs, garlic, caramelized onions, and toasted flour. Arthur presented the meal on a large white platter, arranging the vegetables around the chicken and garnishing it with fresh parsley. He served it with a lemon-herb butter sauce and warm bread.

The occasion was a neighborhood party. Arthur wanted the food to be comforting and welcoming, reflecting his passion for making guests feel cared for.
