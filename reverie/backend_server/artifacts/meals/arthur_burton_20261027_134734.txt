Arthur Burton prepared Coastal Lemon Herb Grilled Salmon with Asparagus and Lemon-Caper Sauce. He grilled a fillet of wild-caught salmon with lemon, garlic, thyme, and a little oil, then arranged it beside whole asparagus spears, cherry tomatoes, and a handful of capers. He finished the dish with a bright lemon-caper sauce and served the whole vegetables on a crisp plate.

The kitchen filled with the fresh aroma of grilled fish, toasted garlic, lemon zest, and the earthy, savory scent of thyme. Arthur plated the meal with a generous lemon wedge, a sprig of thyme, and a small cluster of capers, presenting it as an inviting neighborhood lunch.

It was prepared for a special Wednesday lunch, with Arthur continuing to prepare for his neighborhood party. The meal reflected his friendly, generous nature: simple, colorful, and meant to make everyone feel welcome.
