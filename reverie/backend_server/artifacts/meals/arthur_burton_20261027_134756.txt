Arthur prepared Hawthorn and Oak Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables, the main dish for his neighborhood party. The centerpiece was a whole chicken roasted with thyme, rosemary, a splash of apple cider vinegar, and a generous drizzle of honey, while the sides included crispy potatoes, carrots, and a creamy mash of roasted root vegetables.

The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and fresh herbs, then placed in a large roasting pan with the vegetables. It was covered with aluminum foil and roasted at 400°F (200°C) until the skin was golden brown and the juices were clear. During the last few minutes, it was unclothed and finished in the oven until the breast reached an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).

The aroma was warm and inviting—smoky from the oak, herbaceous, and slightly sweet from the honey and apple cider. The presentation was rustic and celebratory: the golden chicken stood prominently at the center of the table, surrounded by colorful vegetables and a simple platter of crusty bread.

The meal was part of Arthur’s Wednesday night gathering to welcome neighbors and celebrate the safe, welcoming feel of Johnson Park.
