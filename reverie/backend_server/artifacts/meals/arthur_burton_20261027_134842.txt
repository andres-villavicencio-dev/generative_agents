Arthur prepared Rose and Crown Garden Roast Chicken with root vegetables and a creamy herb sauce. The chicken was seasoned with dried rose petals, thyme, black pepper, garlic, and a touch of lemon zest, then roasted until golden and juicy. Root vegetables—including red potatoes, carrots, and onions—were sautéed in butter, while thyme, rosemary, and shallots gave the sauce a warm, aromatic finish.

The kitchen filled with the inviting scent of roasted herbs, garlic, and butter as Arthur carefully basted the chicken. He arranged the golden chicken at the center of a rustic serving platter, surrounded by bright orange carrots, red potatoes, and a crisp green salad. Small sprigs of rosemary, thyme, and a few edible rose petals were placed around the dish, echoing the pub’s elegant, welcoming atmosphere.

He served the meal for a neighborhood gathering at The Rose and Crown Pub on Wednesday, February 15. Arthur’s friendly demeanor and generous serving made the gathering feel special, and the warm aroma of the garden chicken added to the cozy, festive mood.
