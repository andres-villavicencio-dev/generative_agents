Arthur made Chicken and Rosemary Herb Roast, his neighborhood’s signature comfort dinner. He seasoned a whole chicken with rosemary, thyme, garlic, salt, and black pepper, then seared it in olive oil until golden. He roasted it in the oven until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender, serving it with mashed potatoes, roasted carrots, and a pan sauce.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, and savory meat. He presented the chicken on a platter with a sprig of rosemary, roasted vegetables, and a small bowl of lemon-herb sauce, letting the rustic presentation and golden-brown skin invite guests to enjoy the party.

On Wednesday, February 15, Arthur prepared the meal to celebrate the neighborhood gathering at The Rose and Crown Pub. He wanted the food to feel welcoming and familiar while his customers could feel part of the party.
