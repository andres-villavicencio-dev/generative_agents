Arthur prepared Rose and Crown Roast Chicken with Herb-Roasted Potatoes and Gravy. For the main dish, he marinated whole chicken thighs, drumsticks, and breasts with garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and thyme, then seared them in butter until golden. The roasted potatoes were lightly seasoned with salt, pepper, and a touch of olive oil before being baked until crisp. A savory gravy was made by reducing the pan juices with chicken stock, wine, and butter.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, rosemary, butter, garlic, and a hint of lemon. He plated the chicken in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranged the potatoes around it, and served the gravy in a small dish. He topped the potatoes with fresh thyme and a drizzle of lemon-herb butter.

The meal was prepared for Arthur’s Wednesday neighborhood party at The Rose and Crown Pub. He wanted the dish to feel inviting and celebratory, suitable for friends gathering in the park for refreshments. The rustic presentation and hearty flavors made it a perfect centerpiece for a warm evening of conversation and music.
