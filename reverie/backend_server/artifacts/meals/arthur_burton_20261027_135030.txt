Arthur prepared a hearty Rose & Crown Roast Beef with Herb-Roasted Potatoes and Lemon-Herb Gravy, a familiar Sunday lunch that reflected his generous, welcoming personality. He roasted a thick cut of beef with rosemary, thyme, garlic, bay leaf, and a splash of red wine, then sliced the meat into generous portions. He paired it with crisp potatoes, carrots, and onions, all tossed in a creamy garlic-lemon gravy. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized herbs, roasting meat, and savory gravy.

He arranged the plates artfully on the wooden table, letting the golden crust of the beef, the glossy gravy, and the bright colors of the vegetables frame the dish. The meal was prepared for a quiet neighborhood lunch on Wednesday, February 15, while Arthur continued exploring Johnson Park and preparing for his upcoming party. His friendly demeanor and thoughtfully arranged food made the simple meal feel like a small celebration of the community’s warmth.
