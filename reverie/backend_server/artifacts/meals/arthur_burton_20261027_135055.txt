Arthur prepared Smoked Salmon with Lemon Dill, Sourdough, and Horseradish Cream. The main dish was a vibrant, hand-rolled baguette topped with a warm, golden lemon-dill sauce and crisp cucumber, while the savory-sweet sides included smoked salmon, roasted sweet potatoes, and a creamy horseradish cream sauce.

He began by toasting the sourdough in the oven until it was crisp, then sliced it into thin rounds. Using a delicate sashimi knife, he unwrapped the salmon and arranged it on the bread, placing cucumber slices and a thin slice of lemon on top. He finished the dish with a drizzle of lemon dill sauce and a generous serving of horseradish cream. The final presentation was colorful and inviting—the bright orange salmon, golden crust, green cucumber, and creamy white sauce made the meal look freshly made and celebratory.

It was part of the February 15 neighborhood party, where Arthur was helping guests feel welcome. The warm aroma of toasted sourdough, smoked salmon, and lemon filled the pub, while the fresh ingredients and cheerful presentation reminded everyone of the lively, community-focused atmosphere he was helping build.
