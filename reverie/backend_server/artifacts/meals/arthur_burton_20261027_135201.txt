Arthur Burton prepared a Rose & Crown Garden Roast Chicken with Herb-Garlic Herb Sauce, a main dish for the neighborhood party he was preparing for.

The chicken was seasoned with rosemary, thyme, garlic, salt, pepper, and a touch of lemon zest, then roasted in the oven until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. It was finished with a glossy herb-garlic sauce made from shallots, garlic, chicken stock, butter, and a dash of Dijon mustard. The rosemary and thyme added a warm, earthy aroma, while the lemon and garlic brought a subtle bright finish.

Arthur presented the chicken in a shallow white bowl with the sauce drizzled over it, arranged alongside roasted carrots and apples. A small glass of white wine and a fresh herb sprig were placed nearby, creating a rustic, inviting display.

The party was a community gathering, and Arthur’s generous, friendly approach had made the meal feel welcoming and celebratory.
