Arthur prepared a hearty Rose and Crown Garden Chicken Pasta for the neighborhood party, combining the pub’s familiar comfort with local garden ingredients.

Key ingredients: boneless chicken thighs, cherry tomatoes, red onion, garlic, fresh basil, thyme, olive oil, garlic bread, and spinach.

Preparation method: Arthur sautéed the chicken until golden, then added the vegetables and herbs. He simmered the pasta with chicken broth and a splash of lemon juice until the pasta was tender but still al dente. The basil and thyme were stirred in just before the final cooking stage, preserving their bright green color. He finished the dish with a generous drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of red pepper flakes, then served it on a wide platter with warm garlic bread and a crisp spinach salad.

Aroma: Warm roasted chicken, garlic, thyme, and basil blended with the savory aroma of the garden vegetables.

Presentation: The dish was arranged on a large white platter, with the chicken arranged around the bowl of pasta. Bright red tomatoes and green basil leaves added a cheerful pop of color. A small garnish of fresh basil and a sprig of rosemary completed the visual balance.

Occasion: The meal was prepared for Arthur’s February 15 neighborhood party, where he hoped to create a welcoming, home-style atmosphere that made people feel special.
