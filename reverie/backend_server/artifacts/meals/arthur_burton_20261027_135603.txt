Arthur prepared a comforting Rose and Crown Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy, fitting for a neighborhood gathering at The Rose and Crown Pub.

He tossed bone-in chicken thighs with rosemary, thyme, garlic, olive oil, and a splash of apple cider vinegar, then placed them on a baking sheet alongside carrots, potatoes, parsnips, and onions. The herbs and warm spices gave it an inviting earthy aroma. He roasted everything at 400°F for about 40–45 minutes until the chicken became golden and the juices ran clear.

For the sauce, he made a rich, glossy gravy by reducing a pan of the pan juices until it thickened, then stirring in flour and a little water to smooth the texture. He plated the chicken in a shallow roasting pan, surrounded it with the roasted vegetables, and drizzled the warm gravy over everything.

The dish was presented as the centerpiece of his early Wednesday neighborhood party, with a small platter of fresh bread, apple slices, and a side salad nearby. Its earthy, warmly golden appearance, combined with the inviting rosemary scent, made it feel both festive and welcoming—perfect for sharing with friends and neighbors.
