Arthur prepared The Rose and Crown Classic Roast Chicken, a hearty Sunday brunch dish designed to be shared among neighborhood friends. The centerpiece was a whole chicken roasted with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, and a splash of chicken broth. It was glazed with a golden, slightly caramelized honey-mustard sauce and finished with crispy golden-brown skin.

He arranged the chicken in the center of a large serving platter, surrounded by colorful vegetables, crusty bread, and fresh seasonal fruit. Garnished with parsley and a bright lemon wedge, the meal looked warm and inviting. The aroma filled the kitchen of The Rose and Crown Pub with the rich scent of roasted chicken, herbs, citrus, and butter.

The occasion was Arthur’s neighborhood party on Wednesday, February 15. He had been preparing the dish throughout Tuesday while exploring Johnson Park, hoping it would remind everyone of the welcoming, safe community he wanted to celebrate. The rustic, communal meal was meant to bring the guests together for conversation, laughter, and good food.
