Arthur prepared Rose and Crown Garden Herb Chicken, a hearty main dish for his neighborhood party. He marinated chicken thighs with garlic, lemon zest, rosemary, thyme, and a touch of mustard for an hour, then seared them in olive oil until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. He finished the dish by pan‑searing the potatoes, adding chopped kale, carrots, and mushrooms, and simmering everything together until the potatoes were tender and the sauce thickened.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, roasted vegetables, and caramelized butter. Arthur plated the dish in a rustic earthenware bowl, arranging the golden chicken and vegetables on one side, with the creamy orange sauce cascading over the bottom. Garnished with fresh rosemary sprigs and a bright lime wedge, the presentation was inviting, rustic, and celebratory.

It was Arthur’s final lunch before the party, chosen to balance the meal with the energy of the gathering. His friendly, generous approach to cooking reflected his commitment to making everyone feel welcome—just as he had done over the years in The Rose and Crown.
