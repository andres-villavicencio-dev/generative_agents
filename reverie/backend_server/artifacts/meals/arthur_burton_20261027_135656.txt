Arthur prepared a Rosemary-Cauliflower Risotto for lunch on Wednesday, February 15. The dish was cooked with Arborio rice, garlic, red wine, chicken broth, vegetable stock, parmesan cheese, and a generous amount of fresh rosemary, chopped thyme, and shallots. He gently heated the rice in the pot, adding the wine and broth until it absorbed, then stirred in the chicken and vegetables. The final seasonings added a warm, earthy aroma, with the rosemary providing a sharp, herbaceous finish.

The risotto was plated in a deep, rustic ceramic bowl, garnished with a bright green parsley leaf, grated parmesan, and a drizzle of black olive oil. The presentation was simple yet elegant, with the creamy texture and golden vegetables contrasting beautifully against the warm reddish-brown sauce.

This meal was prepared before Arthur’s Wednesday neighborhood party, where he intended to serve the dish to guests. Arthur’s friendly and generous nature made the effort feel special, especially considering the opportunity to make people feel welcome.
