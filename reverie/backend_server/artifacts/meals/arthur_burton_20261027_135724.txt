Arthur prepared Rose & Oak Chicken with Thyme and Lemon-Garlic Roasted Potatoes for the neighborhood party he was preparing for.

The main dish was seasoned chicken breast marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper, then roasted until golden and tender. He roasted the potatoes in the same pan, using the reserved juices to give them a savory sheen. A small green herb salad and a lemon-garlic dressing were served alongside the meal.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, thyme, garlic, lemon, and olive oil. The golden-brown chicken and creamy potatoes contrasted with the fresh green salad, while the rose-colored tablecloth and candles created a welcoming, festive atmosphere.

Arthur served the main dish and salad to a small group of neighbors. The lunch was informal and cheerful, reflecting his generous, community-focused nature. He had chosen it to make everyone feel welcome and to help the neighborhood gather while he continued his plans to explore Johnson Park.
