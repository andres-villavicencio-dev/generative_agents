Arthur prepared Hawthorn & Oak-Crusted Roast Chicken with Herb Cream Sauce for the neighborhood party on his Wednesday.

He started by searing a whole chicken in a hot skillet until the skin turned golden brown, then moved it to the oven to roast until the meat was tender and the skin crisp. He sliced the skin into strips and topped the chicken with chopped fresh thyme, rosemary, and a pinch of sea salt. For the sauce, he made a creamy broth with butter, garlic, lemon juice, parsley, and a splash of cream, seasoning it with black pepper and a touch of Dijon.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, toasted thyme, garlic, and butter. When the chicken came out, he rested it briefly, then plated it with the herb cream sauce, a lemon wedge, and a small side of roasted vegetables. The presentation was simple and inviting: golden chicken arranged alongside bright, colorful vegetables, with the sauce glistening over the platter.

Arthur plated the meal for the party, intending it to be a hearty, welcoming centerpiece that encouraged guests to gather, talk, and enjoy each other’s company.
