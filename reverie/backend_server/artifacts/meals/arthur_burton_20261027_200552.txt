Arthur prepared Rose-and-Crown Breakfast, a hearty, neighborhood-style brunch for The Rose and Crown Pub’s Wednesday party. He combined eggs, bacon, smoked salmon, cheddar, avocado, and toasted bagel slices with a tangy lemon-herb dressing and fresh fruit for a side.

The eggs were scrambled until lightly golden, then topped with crispy bacon and slices of salmon. The bagel was toasted in butter and sprinkled with sea salt, while the fruit was sliced just before serving. Arthur arranged the food on a rustic wooden platter, garnishing it with the fruit and a sprig of rosemary. The warm, savory smell of bacon and toasted bread mingled with the fresh aroma of the fruit and lemon dressing.

The meal was served at 7:00 am, just after Arthur had finished his morning routine. It was a relaxed, welcoming breakfast for his neighborhood party, designed to feel like a warm welcome rather than a formal occasion.
