Arthur prepared Simple Oatmeal & Toast Breakfast for himself before heading to work. The dish combines rolled oats cooked in simmering milk with warm, honeyed toast, sliced bananas, blueberries, and a drizzle of maple syrup. A small piece of golden toast served alongside a bowl of oatmeal adds a hearty, comforting finish.

He stirred the oats until the milk was thickened, then let it cool slightly before topping it with the sweetened fruit. The toast was lightly toasted until crisp on the edges and golden across the surface. The aroma was warm and inviting—rich oats, caramelized bananas, honey, and toasted bread mingling with the creamy scent of milk.

The presentation was simple but visually appealing: the oatmeal and fruit were arranged attractively in a ceramic bowl, while the toast was placed beside it with a small fork and knife. It was prepared as a quiet Sunday breakfast, though Arthur would turn it into a full meal at home on the day of his opening.
