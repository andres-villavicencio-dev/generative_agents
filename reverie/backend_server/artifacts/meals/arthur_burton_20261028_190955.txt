Arthur prepared Rosemary Herb-Crusted Salmon with Lemon-Herb Cream Sauce and Roasted Vegetables for breakfast at The Rose and Crown Pub. He combined fresh salmon fillets with rosemary, garlic, lemon juice, and herbs, then seared them before topping the pan with a creamy sauce and serving it alongside roasted asparagus, carrots, and potatoes.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of rosemary, garlic, and caramelized onions. Arthur arranged the salmon on a wide, white ceramic platter, garnishing it with fresh herbs and lemon slices. The vegetables were arranged neatly beside it, while a simple wooden tray held the bread and coffee.

He prepared the meal for a quiet Monday morning breakfast, but the relaxed, cheerful atmosphere of The Rose and Crown made it feel special for everyone who gathered.
