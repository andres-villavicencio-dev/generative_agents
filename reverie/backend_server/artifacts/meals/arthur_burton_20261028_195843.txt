Arthur prepared Buttermilk Scramble with Smoked Egg and Roasted Tomato, a hearty breakfast for dinner that combined his love of warm comfort with the pub’s relaxed, welcoming atmosphere.

The dish featured fluffy egg whites, creamy buttermilk, diced smoked egg, chopped red onion, and crispy golden-brown pancetta, all cooked with a gentle pan until the eggs were set but still moist. Small roasted tomatoes, garlic, and fresh herbs were added for bright acidity and aromatic depth.

As the pan simmered, a warm aroma filled the kitchen: savory smoked meat, caramelized onions, toasted garlic, and the gentle sweetness of buttermilk. The golden egg whites, pancetta, and tomato pieces created a colorful, rustic presentation that balanced rich textures with fresh ingredients.

He prepared it for a quiet Monday evening meal, enjoying the familiar comfort of his home-style breakfast while feeling refreshed and ready to return to the Rose and Crown.
