Arthur prepared a Rose & Crown Garden Roast Chicken with Herb-Roasted Potatoes for a cozy family dinner on a rainy Monday evening.

The kitchen counter was filled with a large whole chicken, buttery potatoes, fresh rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, and a splash of red wine. Arthur began by searing the chicken in a hot pan until the skin turned golden, then transferred it to the oven to roast at 400°F (200°C). While it cooked, he roasted the potatoes with olive oil, herbs, and a pinch of salt until their edges were crisp. He added sliced garlic and lemon zest to the pan during the final minutes, stirring gently to warm the flavors.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted chicken, toasted herbs, citrus, and a hint of wine. When the oven timer went off, the golden chicken rested under foil, while the potatoes and garlic glaze bubbled in the pan. The final presentation was simple yet elegant: the chicken rested on a platter and the potatoes arranged neatly beside it, garnished with fresh rosemary and lemon slices.

Arthur served the meal around 4:00 p.m., accompanied by a glass of red wine. The occasion was an intimate family gathering after a long day at The Rose and Crown Pub, where he had served drinks for guests. The meal was filled with warmth and laughter, celebrating the community spirit and his passion for good food.
