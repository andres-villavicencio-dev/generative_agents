Arthur prepared a Rose & Crown Brunch Bowl for lunch on Tuesday, February 14. The meal was a simple, warm gathering of soft scrambled eggs, toasted sourdough slices, crisp spinach, avocado, whole-grain toast, and a drizzle of lemon-herb dressing. He added a small bowl of blueberry yogurt parfait for a light breakfast and a sizzling pan-seared salmon fillet for lunch.

The aromas filling the kitchen were of toasted bread, eggs, fresh herbs, and the savory scent of salmon cooking in butter. Arthur used a skillet to sear the salmon until golden, then folded the eggs into a fluffy mixture. He plated the dishes on a white ceramic breakfast platter, arranging the colorful ingredients neatly and draping the pan-seared salmon over the center for visual appeal.

The meal felt like a relaxed, celebratory lunch for a Tuesday afternoon. Arthur had chosen the simple, comforting ingredients because he wanted to make everyone feel welcome and special, just as he always did at The Rose and Crown.
