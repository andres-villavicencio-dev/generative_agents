At The Rose and Crown Pub, Arthur prepared Rosemary Lemon Herb Brunch to celebrate Tuesday, February 14. The dish featured golden poached egg whites, crisp spinach, crunchy toasted almonds, a lemon-pepper vinaigrette, and fluffy white toast. He sautéed garlic and herbs in butter, then gently folded the eggs and spinach, drizzling the lemon juice and herbs over the warm serving platter.

The aroma was inviting and bright: buttery herbs, a fragrant lemon scent, and the savory undertones of garlic and spinach. Arthur presented the brunch on a large white platter, arranging the eggs, toast, and garnish in an appealing, asymmetrical pattern. It was simple enough to share with friends but distinctive enough to feel special, making it perfect for a warm Tuesday brunch.
