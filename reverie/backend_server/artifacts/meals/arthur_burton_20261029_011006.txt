Arthur prepared a Rose & Crown Fusion Breakfast Platter, combining classic brunch staples with a bright, aromatic twist inspired by his interest in fusion cooking. The dish featured golden-brown egg crepes, smoked salmon, sourdough toast, fresh arugula, crumbled blue cheese, honey-roasted berries, and caramelized onions. A simple lemon-herb butter and a splash of white wine completed the flavor profile.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of smoked salmon, earthy sage, the crisp aroma of toasted sourdough, and a hint of sweet honey and citrus. Arthur arranged the platter on a white serving board, letting the colorful ingredients and golden crepes create a cheerful, inviting centerpiece.

The occasion was Tuesday, February 14, the day before the Valentine’s Day dinner. Arthur wanted to make the restaurant feel warm, welcoming, and special—especially for his guests. The breakfast served as a refreshing counterpoint to the upcoming dinner and cocktails, showing his generosity and attention to the comfort of his customers.
