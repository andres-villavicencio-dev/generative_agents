Arthur prepared a Rose and Crown Fusion Breakfast Brunch, celebrating Tuesday, February 14. The dish combined warm, golden egg Benedict with bacon, spinach, and a creamy lemon-herb sauce, alongside a smoked salmon salad with toasted sourdough and microgreens, and a refreshing lemon-lime soda.

The preparation method was straightforward and inviting: eggs were poached in butter until firm, then cooked alongside crispy bacon and wilted spinach until the greens were tender-crisp. The salmon was lightly sautéed with olive oil, lemon zest, and garlic, while the salad received a final drizzle of lemon juice and a sprinkle of sea salt. Arthur garnished the plates with fresh basil, chives, and a small drizzle of honey.

The aroma filled the pub with warm toasted bread, rich buttery eggs, smoky bacon, and the bright citrus scent of the lemon-lime dressing. Each plate was arranged on a wooden board with the egg and bacon in the foreground, the salad as a colorful side, and a glass of sparkling water beside it.

This was a special, welcoming breakfast for the pub’s customers, honoring the occasion with a simple yet thoughtful meal that felt both familiar and exciting. Arthur’s friendly presentation and generous attention to detail made the guests feel cared for, fitting his role as a proud host.
