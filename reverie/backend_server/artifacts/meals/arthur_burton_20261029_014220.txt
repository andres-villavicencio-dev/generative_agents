Arthur prepared Rose and Crown Fusion Chicken with Herb-Roasted Vegetables for Tuesday’s dinner. He used a whole chicken, bone-in thighs, and a mix of carrots, potatoes, zucchini, and bell peppers. He seared the chicken in a hot skillet with olive oil, garlic, and rosemary until golden, then transferred it to a roasting pan where it finished in the oven with the vegetables.

The kitchen filled with a warm, aromatic fragrance of rosemary, garlic, citrus zest, and the savory undertones of poultry. Arthur arranged the chicken in the center of the serving platter and surrounded it with colorful vegetables, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a bright orange pepper.

The meal was served at The Rose and Crown Pub on Tuesday, February 14, as a special occasion dinner. The warm colors and hearty presentation made it feel welcoming and celebratory, while Arthur’s friendly, generous attitude ensured every guest felt honored to be part of it.
