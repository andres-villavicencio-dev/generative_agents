Arthur prepared Rose & Crown Fusion Risotto with Lemon-Herb Crust as his lunch. The dish combined arborio rice, chicken stock, parmesan cheese, fresh parsley, thyme, and a touch of honey, with pan-fried chicken thighs, roasted root vegetables, and caramelized onions. The crust was made with garlic, herbs, olive oil, and breadcrumbs, giving it a golden, savory exterior.

To prepare it, Arthur sautéed the vegetables and onions, then sautéed the chicken until golden. He layered the rice and broth over the vegetables, stirring until the rice absorbed the liquid and became tender. The pan juices were incorporated into the rice, followed by the Parmesan and fresh herbs before the final dish was served.

The aroma was warm and aromatic, with notes of garlic, herbs, roasted roots, and a hint of honey. It was presented in a rustic white bowl, arranged around a bed of golden vegetables and topped with the creamy, herb-crusted chicken. Arthur served it at The Rose and Crown, where the quiet lunch was intended to help him unwind, prepare dinner, and explore new recipes before his evening shift.
