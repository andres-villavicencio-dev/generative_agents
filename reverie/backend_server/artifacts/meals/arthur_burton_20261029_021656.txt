Arthur prepared a quick brunch breakfast bowl for lunch at The Rose and Crown, balancing his busy day with a fresh, energizing meal. The meal featured soft eggs, grilled chicken, sautéed spinach, roasted potatoes, and a lemon-herb vinaigrette, with whole-grain toast and a small glass of orange juice.

He began by heating a skillet and gently cooking the eggs to perfection. Then he seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and a little paprika, pan-searing it until the skin was crisp. The spinach was willeted briefly, and the potatoes were tossed with oil, salt, and dried herbs. Everything was assembled in a colorful bowl, with the golden toast resting alongside it.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, savory chicken, and fresh herbs. The presentation was bright and inviting—earthy colors and clean lines made the meal feel both simple and comforting. It was a relaxed lunch designed to help Arthur stay focused for the day’s remaining tasks while keeping his energy up.
