Arthur prepared a Rosemary and Lemon Herb Chicken for lunch on Tuesday, February 14. The dish was made by seasoning bone-in chicken breasts with garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, oregano, and freshly cracked black pepper, then searing them in olive oil until golden. A lemon-herb jus was prepared with chicken broth, white wine, and the remaining aromatics, while roasted potatoes and green beans completed the meal.

The aroma was bright and inviting, with the earthy scent of rosemary, citrusy lemon, garlic, and savory herbs filling the kitchen. Arthur plated the chicken in a small golden bowl, accompanied by crisp vegetables, a drizzle of the lemon jus, and a side of toasted bread, with each ingredient arranged to create an appealing, colorful presentation.

The lunch was prepared for a casual Tuesday meal, though it was inspired by the restaurant’s focus on fresh, well-arranged dishes. Arthur had also kept a few ideas in mind for brunch and cocktails, but the rosemary chicken was the centerpiece of his day’s cooking.
