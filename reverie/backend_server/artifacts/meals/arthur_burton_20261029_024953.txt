Arthur prepared a Rose-Crowned Lemon Herb Chicken and Potato Skillet for dinner on February 14, a romantic Valentine’s Eve lunch at The Rose and Crown.

The skillet featured tender chicken breast marinated in lemon juice, garlic, herb butter, and a splash of white wine, with potatoes and a creamy potato-lemon sauce. It was finished with rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of salt. He sautéed the chicken until golden, then finished it in the pan with the sauce.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of garlic, roasted potatoes, and citrus. He plated the food in a rustic ceramic dish, garnished with fresh rosemary, lemon slices, and a small sprig of rosemary.

The occasion was a heartfelt family dinner celebrating the approaching Valentine’s Day, with Arthur serving it alongside brunch ideas, cocktails, and recipes he was developing.
