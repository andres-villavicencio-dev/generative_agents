Arthur prepared a Rose & Crown Fusion Quinoa Bowl for lunch, combining the pub’s signature floral notes with a warm, comforting bowl. Key ingredients included cooked quinoa, roasted chickpeas, fresh parsley, lemon zest, toasted almonds, and a creamy tomato-cilantro dressing. He sautéed the chickpeas in olive oil until golden, then tossed them with the dressing and mixed in the fluffy quinoa and crisp vegetables.

The aroma was rich and inviting—herbs, toasted nuts, and warm spices filled the kitchen while the lemon zest gave it a bright, citrusy finish. He plated the bowls in matching rose-themed cups with a sprig of parsley and a small side of grilled cheese or warm crackers. The meal served a Tuesday afternoons as a relaxed, celebratory lunch, honoring Arthur’s passion for making people feel welcome and his interest in the fusion dinner and Carmen’s cooking.
