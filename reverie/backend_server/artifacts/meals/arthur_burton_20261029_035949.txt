At 7:00 a.m., Arthur made a simple Farm-Fresh Biscuit Breakfast to serve lunch. He toasted whole-grain biscuits, cracked two eggs into a pan, and scrambled them with chives and a pinch of salt. The biscuits were buttered, sliced, and arranged alongside the eggs in a neat row. A bowl of plain yogurt, a small glass of orange juice, and a crisp apple were set beside the main dishes.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted oats, baking bread, and lightly seasoned eggs. Arthur presented the meal in a rustic wooden serving tray, using colorful cutlery and a small napkin to make the food look inviting. The occasion was a quiet Wednesday morning at The Rose and Crown—a simple, welcoming brunch designed to make customers feel special without requiring much effort.
