At 7:00 a.m., Arthur Burton prepared a simple Rose and Crown Brunch Bowl for his morning meal. The bowl featured a bright lemon-herb Greek yogurt base, warm fluffy whole-grain toast, crispy radishes, crunchy nuts, and a drizzle of honey, with fresh berries and a small cucumber spear.

He toasted the bread until golden, then layered the yogurt over it, arranging the crisp vegetables and nuts alongside the berries for an inviting color. The kitchen smelled of toasted bread, warm dairy, citrus, and herbs—a comforting aroma that instantly made the meal feel special.

Arthur served the bowl in a simple white ceramic bowl, garnished with a sprig of mint and a lemon wedge. He arranged the food neatly on a small wooden board, creating a balanced, refreshing presentation suitable for a relaxed Wednesday lunch. This was his first meal of the day, a quiet moment of preparation before the busy day ahead.
