Arthur prepared Fusion Frittata with Lemon Herb Toast and Roasted Root Vegetables. The dish came together quickly using eggs, shredded cheddar, fresh herbs, lemon juice, garlic, and a splash of olive oil. He softened the root vegetables, sautéed them gently, and then folded the frittata into the pan while adding the warm vegetables and a final drizzle of lemon cream.

Aromatic, golden, and warmly scented, the frittata released a fragrant aroma of toasted butter, garlic, and citrus. The golden-brown edges contrasted with the bright yellow of the cheese and the green herbs, while the crisp root vegetables added a colorful, earthy texture.

Arthur arranged the dish on a large white platter, placing the frittata in the center and the herb toast and roasted roots around it. He added a small ramekin of lemon cream and a sprig of rosemary for decoration. The presentation was cheerful and inviting, fitting the simple, welcoming breakfast he prepared before heading into a busy day at The Rose and Crown Pub.
