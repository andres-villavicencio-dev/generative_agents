Arthur prepared a Rose & Crown Brunch Feast, a vibrant brunch spread for his customers. The centerpiece was a Golden Lemon-Herbed Eggs Benedict, with poached eggs, crispy bacon, Gruyère, lemon zest, and a light mustard sauce. Alongside it was a Smoked Salmon Salad with Dill-Crouton, cucumber, arugula, smoked salmon, capers, and whole-grain toast. A refreshing Fruit Lemonade and an Absinthe-Infused Gin Spritz completed the table.

The ingredients were measured carefully by weight and volume: six large eggs, three strips of bacon, a cup of heavy cream, 1 cup chicken stock, 250g salmon, 2 cups mixed greens, a cup of toasted almonds, 2 tablespoons lemon zest, and about 500ml lemonade. Arthur used a skillet for the eggs, pan-fried the bacon, seasoned the salmon, and whisked the lemonade with sparkling water.

The aroma filled the pub: savory bacon, buttery cheese, toasted nuts, and the bright citrus of lemonade mingled with the subtle hint of absinthe in the drink. It was presented on a white slate platter and a rustic wooden board, with colorful garnishes and a small card offering a complimentary drink. Arthur prepared the meal for his Wednesday guests as part of the pub’s community brunch, aiming to make every diner feel welcome and impressed.
