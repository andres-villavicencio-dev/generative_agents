Arthur prepared a Lemon-Garlic Herb Roasted Chicken for lunch at The Rose and Crown on Wednesday, February 15. He chose a whole chicken and combined it with a lemon, garlic, rosemary, and thyme rub, then roasted it at 400°F for about 45 minutes, turning the bird in the last minute to ensure the skin was crisp. He finished it with a simple pan sauce of lemon juice, olive oil, and a splash of chicken stock, served with roasted vegetables and a small portion of crusty bread.

The aroma of the kitchen was rich and inviting: garlic and lemon mingled with rosemary and a hint of caramelized herbs, while the chicken gave off the warm scent of roasted skin and savory poultry. It was served in a white ceramic bowl, with the golden-brown chicken, bright lemon-garlic sauce, and colorful vegetables arranged around a rustic wooden platter. The dish was presented for a quiet family lunch, and Arthur shared it with a few friends, enjoying the warmth of his meal as an opportunity to connect with the community and the lively atmosphere of his pub.
