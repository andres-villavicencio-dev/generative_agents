Arthur prepared Rosemary Lemon Herb Chicken with Grilled Asparagus and Quinoa, a warm, family-style meal that would serve as his Wednesday lunch. The key ingredients were chicken thighs, asparagus, lemon, garlic, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper, along with cooked quinoa and a small side of roasted vegetables.

He marinated the chicken in lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of black pepper, letting it rest for about twenty minutes. He grilled the chicken until the skin was golden and the chicken was just cooked through, then grilled the asparagus until crisp-tender. The vegetables were sautéed briefly with olive oil, garlic, and herbs before being mixed into a light, herbaceous broth. A small portion of quinoa was served alongside the chicken and vegetables.

The aroma was rich and inviting, with toasted garlic, warm herbs, citrus, and the savory depth of rosemary. Arthur plated the meal in a shallow white bowl, arranging the chicken, colorful asparagus, and fluffy quinoa in an appealing, rustic pattern. He topped each portion with a sprinkle of lemon zest and a drizzle of olive oil, then set it on the kitchen counter before heading to serve the remaining ingredients for dinner.

The occasion was a simple, practical Wednesday lunch, but Arthur prepared it with care, hoping it would be as welcoming and satisfying as the meals he serves his guests.
