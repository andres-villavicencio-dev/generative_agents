Arthur prepared Fusion Fusion Rice Bowl for Carmen on Wednesday, February 15, while he finished hosting dinner and preparing brunch at The Rose and Crown. The colorful meal featured glazed carrots, honey-soy glazed chicken, jasmine rice, pickled vegetables, quinoa, lemon-herb rice, and a side of warm kimchi.

It was prepared by sautéing the chicken in a little oil, then coating it in a honey-soy glaze before arranging it over the rice. The pickled vegetables and quinoa were stirred in at the end to add a crisp, savory finish, while the lemon and fresh herbs provided a bright, herbal aroma.

The presentation was elegant and communal: the golden chicken and fluffy rice formed the center, surrounded by colorful vegetables and garnished with a sprig of rosemary and a small lemon wedge. It was a warm, celebratory lunch for Carmen on Wednesday, fitting into Arthur’s relaxed schedule and showing his commitment to making people feel welcome and special.
