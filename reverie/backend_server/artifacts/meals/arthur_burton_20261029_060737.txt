Arthur prepared Fusion Tomato Basil Risotto with Crispy Prosciutto and Fresh Herbs, a warm, comforting fusion meal celebrating the flavors of Italy and Latin America.

The risotto began with Arborio rice, white wine, and slowly warmed vegetable broth. Arthur added parmesan, onions, garlic, basil, and a splash of hot chili oil, stirring until the rice turned translucent and creamy. He finished it with chopped prosciutto and a final drizzle of chili oil, then garnished it with fresh thyme, parsley, and a few crispy golden chips.

The aroma was rich, savory, and slightly spicy—herbs, toasted nuts, and the rich aroma of cooked rice filling the kitchen. It smelled inviting and intimate, perfect for a quiet Sunday dinner.

For Carmen, Arthur plated the risotto on a wide white plate and arranged the prosciutto and herbs in neat, colorful arcs around the edges. A small sprig of thyme and a drizzle of olive oil added a final touch of elegance. The occasion was a relaxed, shared dinner at The Rose and Crown Pub, with Arthur proud to make Carmen feel welcomed and delighted.
