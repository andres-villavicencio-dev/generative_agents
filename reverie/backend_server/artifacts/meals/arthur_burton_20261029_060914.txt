Arthur prepared a Rose and Crown Fusion Chicken Curry for Carmen, combining his pub’s classic Indian-inspired flavors with a modern twist. The curry features tender chicken thighs, ginger, garlic, turmeric, cumin, coriander, red chili flakes, coconut milk, and a hint of curry powder, finished with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lemon.

He sautéed the aromatics, seared the chicken, and then simmered everything in a rich, fragrant sauce until the chicken was cooked through and the vegetables were tender. For presentation, the curry was served in a shallow white bowl with a side of steamed jasmine rice, fresh coriander, and a small lemon wedge. The warm, savory aroma of toasted cumin, ginger, and coconut filled the kitchen, while the golden-brown chicken and vibrant green herbs added a cheerful pop of color.

The occasion was a relaxed Wednesday evening dinner for Carmen. Arthur’s friendly, generous hospitality made the meal feel welcoming and special, reflecting the pub’s reputation for creating memorable experiences for everyone who entered.
