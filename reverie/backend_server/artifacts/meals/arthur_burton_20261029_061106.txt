Arthur prepared a Rose & Crown Fusion Bowl, combining the pub’s signature cocktail-style sauce with a warm, herb-forward Asian-inspired stir-fry.

The dish was made over a stovetop, starting with a pan of vegetables including bell peppers, onions, broccoli, snap peas, and cherry tomatoes. He added garlic, ginger, fresh chives, and a handful of herbs, cooking them until crisp-tender. The colorful vegetable base was finished with coconut milk, soy sauce, sesame oil, and a splash of rice vinegar, followed by a handful of crispy tofu and a few baby bok choy leaves. A warm, golden rose-gold sauce gave the dish its distinctive aroma of toasted garlic, citrus zest, ginger, and savory soy.

The presentation was vibrant and inviting: the bowl was filled with contrasting colors of vegetables, tofu, and garnished with fresh herbs and toasted almonds. A small card with the pub’s logo and a handwritten note added a personal touch.

The occasion was a special Wednesday dinner for Carmen, who had just returned from work. Arthur had prepared the main portion and the accompanying salad, returning everything to the refrigerator so Carmen could enjoy the full meal. The warm, homey atmosphere and thoughtful preparation created a memorable, welcoming occasion.
