On Thursday, February 16, Arthur prepared Spring-Fusion Bowl as part of his warm Wednesday preparations for the day. The dish blended fresh asparagus, cherry tomatoes, red onion, cucumber, avocado, and a hint of lemon zest, seasoned with garlic, olive oil, smoked paprika, and a light citrus dressing. He chopped the vegetables carefully, sautéed the asparagus until crisp-tender, and tossed everything in a bright, herbaceous sauce.

The aroma was inviting: the earthy smell of asparagus, the crispness of the vegetables, and the refreshing citrus scent filled the kitchen. Arthur arranged the bowl on a white plate, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a lemon wedge, and served it alongside a simple grain salad.

The occasion was an informal lunch at The Rose and Crown Pub, where Arthur and his colleagues gathered for a relaxed Thursday lunch. The warm, welcoming presentation made the meal feel special, reflecting Arthur’s desire to make every guest feel included. It was a simple, nourishing meal that turned the pub’s familiar setting into a bright spring celebration.
