Arthur prepared Fusion Fajitas, a colorful, warm lunch designed to match the lively energy of The Rose and Crown. The dish featured bell peppers, onions, tomatoes, jalapeños, and grilled chicken thighs. He sliced the vegetables uniformly, marinated the chicken with cumin, garlic, and lime, then tossed everything in a pan with olive oil, cumin, and a little chili powder before cooking it over medium heat until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted garlic and roasted meat, while the bright colors of the peppers and tomatoes added a cheerful vibrancy. Arthur arranged the fajitas in a large serving bowl, garnished with fresh cilantro, lime wedges, and a dusting of chili flakes, then placed a small bowl of warm cilantro-lime rice alongside it.

On Thursday, February 16, the meal was prepared during his busy day as a bartender, with the fusion dinner and Carmen’s cooking continuing to inspire his menu. The setting felt casual and welcoming, fitting the pub’s focus on making every customer feel valued.
