Arthur prepared a Sautéed Vegetable Garden with Lemon-Herb Pan Sauce, a colorful, rustic lunch for the staff of The Rose and Crown. He gathered chopped carrots, onions, bell peppers, zucchini, cherry tomatoes, and spinach, along with garlic, olive oil, salt, pepper, and fresh herbs.

He sliced the vegetables and lightly sautéed them in a wide skillet until they were crisp and slightly browned, using the pan to release their natural flavors. Then, he added the garlic, lemon juice, and a splash of white wine, simmering until the broth reduced and the vegetables softened.

The dish had a warm, earthy aroma of garlic, herbs, and caramelized onions, with a bright, citrusy finish. It was arranged in a shallow serving bowl with the vegetables arranged in a neat, overlapping circle and topped with a glossy pan sauce.

The meal was part of the fusion dinner service for the Thursday lunch shift, designed to be both hearty and refreshing. Arthur’s friendly presentation and generous portion size reflected his dedication to making every guest feel welcome at The Rose and Crown.
