Arthur Burton prepared Rosemary Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa Salad. He took the day’s fusion concept from Wednesday—combining Mediterranean flavors with a light citrus finish—and brought it to life in The Rose and Crown.

The dish featured boneless chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, and rosemary, then roasted until golden. Alongside it, carrots, potatoes, and zucchini were tossed with olive oil, herbs, lemon zest, and a pinch of salt. The quinoa salad provided a bright, crunchy contrast with parsley, lemon juice, and a light dressing.

The kitchen filled with a warm, fragrant aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, citrus, and savory chicken. The presentation was bright and inviting: the golden chicken and vegetables were arranged on white linens, with the colorful quinoa salad resting beside them. It felt prepared for a casual lunch gathering or a relaxed Friday brunch, reflecting Arthur’s love for bringing guests a welcoming, memorable meal.
