Arthur prepared Fusion Bowl of Quinoa, Lemon-Herb Chicken, and Roasted Vegetables as a hearty, family-style lunch for the bar team at The Rose and Crown. The bowl featured quinoa, chickpeas, roasted carrots, bell peppers, tomatoes, and a lemon-herb olive oil dressing, with grilled chicken thighs, fennel, and a bright orange-red lemon glaze. The chicken was seared briefly and finished in the oven, while the vegetables were caramelized until tender.

The kitchen was filled with the warm aroma of toasted fennel, lemon zest, rosemary, and savory herbs. He arranged the colorful ingredients in a large, rustic bowl, topped it with a rustic woven basket, and placed the grilled chicken in the center, creating an inviting and visually balanced presentation. The meal was prepared during a busy Thursday shift, serving as a satisfying, energizing lunch while Arthur’s afternoon focused on the fusion dinner and Carmen’s cooking. It was a simple, welcoming lunch meant to nourish the team and keep Arthur focused on his day.
