Arthur prepared a Fusion Brunch with Artisanal Eggs and Smoked Salmon, designed to honor his creative momentum from Wednesday. The dish was divided into two sections: a fluffy omelet filled with smoked salmon, cucumber, and a drizzle of lemon-herb cream, paired with a small bowl of warm oatmeal topped with sliced strawberries, chia seeds, and a drizzle of honey.

He began by heating the oatmeal in a pan, adding oat milk, cinnamon, and a pinch of maple syrup. As the oats warmed, he added the smoked salmon, sliced cucumber, and the omelet, letting the eggs cook until the edges were lightly set while the omelet stayed moist. The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich aroma of salmon, toasted nuts, citrus, and warm oats.

Arthur plated the dish on a large white platter with a small decorative bowl of berry compote. Each plate was presented with a handwritten note offering a drink recommendation, and the colorful arrangement made it feel like a communal celebration rather than a simple breakfast. It was the centerpiece of a Friday brunch shared with a friendly coworker, celebrating Arthur’s hospitality and the day’s creative momentum.
