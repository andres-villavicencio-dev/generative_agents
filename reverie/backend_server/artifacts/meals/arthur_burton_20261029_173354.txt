Arthur prepared Fusion Brunch Brisket & Grilled Asparagus, a hearty main portion inspired by the ideas he developed for the Wednesday Fusion Brunch. The dish features a slow-roasted beef brisket with a savory honey-herb glaze, tender grilled asparagus, crisp potatoes, and a bright lemon-tomato vinaigrette.

The brisket is seared first to develop a caramelized crust, then slow-braised in the pan for an hour until the meat is tender and the glaze reduces into a glossy sauce. Asparagus is brushed with lemon oil and roasted until crisp, while the potatoes are seasoned with salt and pepper before finishing. The ingredients are arranged on a large platter alongside fresh fruit and a side of creamy mashed potatoes.

Aromatic herbs, garlic, rosemary, and citrus dominate the kitchen, creating a warm, savory aroma that fills the dining room. The finished meal is presented in rustic ceramic bowls with a wooden serving board, colorful garnishes, and the restaurant’s signature red-and-gold branding.

On Friday, February 17, the meal was part of a relaxed community dinner at The Rose and Crown Pub, honoring the celebration’s inclusive spirit and giving Arthur a chance to share his creative momentum with friends and fellow patrons.
