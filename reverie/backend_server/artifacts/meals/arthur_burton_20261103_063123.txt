Arthur prepared Hearty Herb-Infused Roasted Potatoes and Root Vegetables, a warm, rustic side designed to complement his hearty dinner. The dish included red potatoes, carrots, parsnips, red onion, thyme, garlic, olive oil, and a splash of lemon juice. He sliced and roasted the vegetables on a broiler until crisp and golden, then tossed them with the herbs before serving.

The kitchen filled with a rich aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and lemon. Arthur arranged the colorful vegetables in a shallow baking dish, garnished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. Each plate was presented with a small piece of bread and a cup of tea.

This was a relaxed family-style dinner on Saturday, February 18, as Arthur settled into his quiet lunchtime routine at The Rose and Crown. He had been experimenting with seasonal flavors, and this dish reflected his thoughtful approach to hospitality—simple, comforting, and made to make guests feel welcome.
