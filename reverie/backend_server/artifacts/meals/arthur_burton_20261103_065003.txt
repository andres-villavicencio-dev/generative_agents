Arthur prepared a warm, rustic breakfast of Mushroom and Herb Breakfast Potatoes, a comforting dish that reflected both his home-cooking comfort and the pub’s welcoming atmosphere. He sliced baby potatoes, onions, and carrots, then tossed them with minced garlic, chopped fresh chives, thyme, rosemary, and a generous handful of grated Parmesan cheese. The pan was lightly sautéed until the potatoes became tender while keeping the vegetables golden, then the mixture was returned to the stove for a final minute.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of roasted root vegetables, herbaceous freshness, and a savory, caramelized finish. Arthur served the meal in a shallow, ceramic bowl, arranged the colorful vegetables in concentric layers, and topped each portion with a bright yellow lemon wedge.

He prepared it for a Saturday morning breakfast at home, enjoying it with a glass of red wine. The meal was simple, satisfying, and the kind of cozy meal Arthur would like his customers to feel at home with at The Rose and Crown.
