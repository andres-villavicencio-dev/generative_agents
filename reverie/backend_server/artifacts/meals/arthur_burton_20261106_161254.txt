Arthur prepared a Smoked Salmon, Cream Cheese, and Dill-Butter Croissan for a quick breakfast before opening The Rose and Crown Pub. The dish combines thinly sliced smoked salmon, a thin layer of cream cheese, fresh dill, finely chopped capers, and a generous drizzle of melted butter. He toasted the croissan bread, then arranged the salmon in a neat line on the bread, folded it gently, and placed the finished croissan on a small plate.

The aroma was earthy and warm: the smoky salmon, rich cream cheese, dill, and butter created a comforting, savory scent. He garnished each croissan with a tiny sprig of dill and a few capers, adding a fresh, colorful touch.

The occasion was a quiet breakfast before his busy shift, though he prepared it with the intention of sharing a cheerful meal with a friend or two. Arthur’s generous and friendly approach made the breakfast feel special, reflecting his desire to make guests feel welcome.
