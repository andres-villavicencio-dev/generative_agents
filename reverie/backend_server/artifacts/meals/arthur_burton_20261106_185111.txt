Arthur prepared a Heritage Roast Chicken Soup with Toast and Crispy Potatoes for lunch at The Rose and Crown Pub. The key ingredients included a whole roasted chicken, carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, garlic, rosemary, thyme, chicken broth, and lemon. He toasted the bread and pan-seared the potatoes to add a golden, savory crunch.

He simmered the soup over low heat until the chicken was tender and the vegetables absorbed the broth, then finished the dish with a drizzle of lemon juice and a generous portion of crispy potatoes. The aroma rose from the kitchen: roasted garlic, citrus, thyme, and a warm, comforting scent of chicken.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the soup was served in a clear glass with visible pieces of chicken, vegetables, and golden potatoes, while the toast and pan-seared potatoes were arranged on a small white plate beside a glass of chilled lemonade.

The occasion was a relaxed lunch on a busy Monday, where Arthur sat with Carmen Ortiz, the owner’s wife. They chatted over lunch about the day’s preparations and the lively atmosphere of the bar. Carmen offered a simple bowl of soup and a slice of toast, and Arthur enjoyed it with the warmth of knowing Carmen’s familiar recipes.
