Arthur prepared Creamy Oatmeal & Honey-Apple Breakfast Bowl for an early morning gathering among the pub’s regulars. The dish featured rolled oats cooked in a warm oat milk base, topped with sliced apples, walnuts, cinnamon, and a drizzle of warm honey. It was served in a rustic ceramic bowl with a small glass of lemonade nearby.

The aroma was warm and inviting: caramel, toasted oats, cinnamon, and the bright scent of apple. He arranged the ingredients in a colorful, slightly uneven presentation, with golden oats, dark walnuts, and translucent apples creating visual interest.

He prepared it for a weekday breakfast table, a casual gathering of friends and regular customers who had gathered early to enjoy the pub’s relaxed atmosphere. The friendly, generous nature of the meal reflected Arthur’s approach: simple yet comforting, with every ingredient clearly visible and ready to share.
