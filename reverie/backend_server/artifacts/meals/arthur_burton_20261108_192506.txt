Arthur prepared Rose & Crown Egg Scramble for The Rose and Crown. He scrambled eggs with sliced mushrooms, a soft tomato, chives, and a generous handful of freshly cracked black pepper. He added a splash of white wine, a pinch of salt, and chopped parsley, then cooked everything gently until the eggs were creamy and the vegetables were tender.

Aromatically, the kitchen filled with savory nuttiness, fresh herbs, and the rich warmth of cooked eggs. He served the scramble over toasted sourdough bread, drizzled it with a little lemon juice, and placed a small glass of red wine beside it. The presentation was simple and inviting: a colorful, rustic bowl of golden eggs, crisp vegetables, and a warm loaf, accompanied by a steaming mug of coffee.

The meal was the perfect Tuesday breakfast for Arthur’s guests and for himself—it was wholesome, comforting, and a welcome reprieve after a long day managing the pub and bar.
