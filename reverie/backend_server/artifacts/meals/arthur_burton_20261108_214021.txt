Arthur prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Lemon Butter Sauce for dinner on February 14. He roasted chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, and onions with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a little olive oil until the chicken was golden and juicy. For the sauce, he whisked lemon juice, vinegar, garlic, butter, and a splash of water until it had a glossy, fragrant consistency.

The aroma of the meal was warm and inviting: the sweet fragrance of roasted chicken mingled with earthy notes of rosemary, a hint of garlic, and the bright acidity of the lemon. He served the vegetables in a shallow white bowl beside the chicken, topped with a sprig of rosemary and a few lemon wedges.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday dinner at The Rose and Crown Pub, a relaxed meal while Arthur balanced his restaurant and bar operations. The simple, comforting meal was a perfect celebration of hospitality—warm enough to share, and easy enough to enjoy with a glass of water.
