Arthur prepared Chicken Parmesan with Roast Vegetables and Grilled Egg for a relaxed Monday dinner. He used breast chicken, whole-wheat bread, mozzarella, marinara sauce, fresh basil, garlic, olive oil, and a little lemon juice. The chicken was breaded and seared, then pan-fried until golden, while the vegetables—including carrots, zucchini, onions, and tomatoes—were roasted with plenty of oil until tender and slightly caramelized. A whole egg was cracked into the pan and scrambled with the remaining sauce and herbs.

The meal had a savory, golden aroma of toasted bread, garlic, basil, and lemon. It was presented on a large white platter arranged with colorful vegetables, a bed of golden-brown chicken, and a glossy drizzle of sauce over the egg. It was a simple yet hearty dinner inspired by Arthur’s favorite ingredients, perfect for a casual Monday evening at home.
