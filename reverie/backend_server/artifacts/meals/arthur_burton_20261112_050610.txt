Arthur prepared Creamy Tomato Basil Pancakes with Bacon and Poached Eggs, a simple but hearty breakfast to wake the town after a busy day.

He began by filling a ceramic coffee pot with fresh coffee beans and hot water, letting it brew for about five minutes. While the coffee steamed, he whisked together flour, eggs, milk, and a pinch of salt for the batter, then cooked the pancakes in a nonstick skillet until golden. He sliced a piece of crispy bacon, added it to the pan, and poached two eggs on top.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted coffee, savory bacon, and buttery pancakes. He arranged the pancakes on a white plate, garnishing them with fresh basil, a drizzle of olive oil, and a small lemon wedge. With the coffee still steaming, he took a sip and smiled at the assembled breakfast, ready to serve it to his guests.

This meal was prepared for his morning breakfast table, offering a cozy, comforting start to his day as he continued his routine of opening The Rose and Crown Pub at 5:00 p.m., serving customers until 10:00 p.m., and then closing the bar.
