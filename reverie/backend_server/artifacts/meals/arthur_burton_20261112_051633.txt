Arthur prepared a simple breakfast of Oatmeal with Fresh Berries and Maple-Sesame Toast, with a glass of warm oat milk and a small side of fruit compote.

He used rolled oats, warm water, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt to cook the oatmeal in a pan. He toasted slices of bread with butter and a little maple syrup, then added a handful of fresh blueberries, raspberries, and sliced apples. The warm oatmeal was served beside the toasted bread and fruit, with the compote in a small dish.

Aromatic and comforting, the meal smelled of oats, cinnamon, toasted bread, and caramelized apples. It was presented on a white plate with the fruit arranged neatly around the oatmeal and a small spoon for the compote, giving the simple breakfast a cheerful, inviting appearance.

He made the meal for a quiet Monday morning while talking with Eddy Lin. They had just taken a slow walk around the rarely visited campus, discussing how the quiet morning had made them appreciate the little things. Arthur was proud of how the breakfast had felt warm and thoughtful, matching the peaceful mood he felt as he prepared it and shared it with Eddy.
