Arthur Burton prepared a warm Creamy Oatmeal Breakfast Bowl, a simple and comforting morning meal for himself. The dish included rolled oats, warm water, a pinch of salt, a handful of fresh berries, a few sliced apples, and a generous teaspoon of honey. He also added a splash of milk and a few toasted nuts for extra richness.

He heated the oatmeal in a small pot until it bubbled gently, then set aside while preparing the bowl. Once the oatmeal was hot, he folded in the fruit and nuts and drizzled the honey over the top. The aroma of toasted oats, caramelized fruit, and warm honey filled the kitchen, making the meal feel inviting and cozy.

The bowl was presented on a small white plate, arranged neatly around the edge with the honey spoon resting beside it. Arthur took a bite while the kitchen settled, enjoying the satisfying warmth of the meal. It was the kind of simple breakfast he would prepare to start his day in the town where The Rose and Crown Pub stood.
