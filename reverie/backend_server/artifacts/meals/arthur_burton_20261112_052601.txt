Arthur Burton prepared a warm oatmeal breakfast for his early morning shift. The dish was called “Morning Glow Oatmeal,” a comforting blend of rolled oats, milk, a touch of honey, chopped walnuts, and a pinch of cinnamon.

To make it, he cooked the oats on the stove with milk until the liquid absorbed and the mixture thickened. He studded the oats with the nuts and stirred in a few drops of honey for sweetness. A dash of cinnamon, a small cinnamon stick, and a pinch of flaky sea salt finished the dish.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the rich scent of oats, milk, warm nuts, and cinnamon. It smelled inviting and freshly prepared.

Arthur served a generous portion of oatmeal in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging a few walnuts and a cinnamon stick beside it. He placed a small plate of crusty toast nearby. The meal was simple but satisfying—a quiet start to Monday, fitting for a bartender who values a relaxed, welcoming breakfast.
