Arthur prepared a simple, comforting oatmeal breakfast for himself, serving himself a cup of black coffee and a handful of fresh fruit while placing the dishes on the counter.

The dish was Warm Oatmeal with Berries and Lemon. He used rolled oats, brown sugar, cinnamon, a splash of milk, and a pinch of salt. He cooked the oats in a pan until the edges became golden, then folded in chopped blueberries, sliced strawberries, and a wedge of lemon. After cooling slightly, he added a little honey and stirred in a few whole almonds.

The aroma was sweet, warm, and fragrant—cinnamon, caramel, citrus, and the soft scent of oats. He served the oatmeal in a shallow ceramic bowl, with the fruit scattered generously over the top and the lemon wedges arranged beside it. The coffee was poured in a clear mug and placed nearby.

The meal was a quiet, relaxed breakfast on a Monday morning. Arthur made it for himself while closing the pub, though he would later share the same simple ingredients with a customer.
