Arthur prepared a simple but comforting breakfast of scrambled eggs with cheddar, spinach, and toast. He cooked the eggs in a nonstick pan with a generous pat of butter and a few chopped chives, then folded them in with wilted spinach, salt, and pepper. A slice of sourdough was toasted and spread with butter before being joined with the eggs.

The aroma was rich and warm—a savory scent of buttery egg, toasted bread, and freshly chopped chives. Arthur arranged the eggs, toast, and cheddar on a wide white plate, adding a sprig of parsley and a tiny drizzle of lemon juice. The presentation was rustic and inviting, suggesting a casual morning gathering.

He prepared the meal for a quiet Monday visit to The Rose and Crown Pub, where he had a glass of house wine and a friend to greet. The setting was relaxed and social, with other pub patrons enjoying coffee and conversation nearby.
