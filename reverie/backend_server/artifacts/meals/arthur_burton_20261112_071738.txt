Arthur prepared a Roast Chicken with Lemon-Herb Crust and Pan-Seared Potatoes for a relaxed Monday dinner. The chicken was seasoned with rosemary, thyme, garlic, paprika, and lemon zest, then roasted until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The pan-seared potatoes were cut into quarters, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little oil, then quickly browned in the same pan before the chicken was added.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory poultry. Arthur plated the chicken in a simple white plate with the potatoes beside it, garnished with fresh parsley and a squeeze of lemon. The presentation was rustic and inviting, perfect for a cozy dinner at home.

He prepared the meal to celebrate the beginning of his week after visiting the grocery store. With his usual friendly demeanor, he took the leftovers home and set them aside for the next evening when he might make coffee with his wife or host a small gathering with friends.
