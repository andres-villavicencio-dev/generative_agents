For Tuesday, February 14, Ayesha Khan prepared a simple but elegant dinner of herbed lentil and roasted vegetable soup with a glazed lemon-tart. The lentils were simmered with carrots, celery, onions, and parsley, while the lemon-tart was baked with lemon zest, honey, cinnamon, and toasted nuts.

She added a splash of white wine to the soup, allowing its aroma to carry through the kitchen. As the pot simmered, the warm, earthy scent of roasted vegetables mingled with the subtle tang of lemon and the gentle sweetness of the tart. Ayesha stirred the soup carefully, then arranged the golden-brown tart on a small white plate beside it.

The meal was served around 5:30 p.m. for dinner, a quiet evening of research and writing. Ayesha sat beside her laptop, reviewing her senior thesis on language in Shakespeare, while the soup’s warm aroma made the room feel cozy and inviting. Her determination, curiosity, and independence were reflected in the focused, unhurried nature of the meal.
