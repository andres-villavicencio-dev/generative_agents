Ayesha Khan prepared a warm lentil and vegetable breakfast bowl, a simple yet nourishing option suitable for her busy Wednesday. She combined red lentils, soy milk, chopped carrots, bell peppers, onions, and cucumber with a herb-lemon dressing of olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and fresh parsley. The lentils were cooked until tender, then cooled and rewarmed with the remaining vegetables to keep the flavors bright and the texture soft.

The kitchen filled with a rich, earthy aroma of lentils, garlic, and warm herbs, with the citrus note adding a lively finish. Ayesha arranged the colorful ingredients on a simple white plate, garnishing it with toasted seeds and a few leaves. The presentation was clean, inviting, and balanced, with the golden lentils and crisp vegetables creating visual contrast. It was part of her morning routine, marking the beginning of a purposeful day focused on literature, research, and growing her writing skills.
