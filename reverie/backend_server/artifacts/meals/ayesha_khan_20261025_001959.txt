Ayesha prepared Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken with Potatoes and Green Beans for dinner on Friday, February 11, before her evening classes. The meal featured a whole chicken seasoned with lemon, garlic, dried rosemary, paprika, and cumin, alongside golden potatoes, crisp green beans, and a simple lemon-herb sauce.

She roasted the vegetables first, then placed the chicken on the oven rack. After about twenty minutes, she basted it with the pan juices to keep the skin crisp and the meat tender. The aroma rose in warm waves of lemon, garlic, rosemary, and savory spice, filling the kitchen with an inviting scent of home-cooked comfort.

Ayesha presented the meal on a white platter, arranged the vegetables neatly beside it, and garnished the chicken with a sprig of rosemary. She served it with a small portion of brown rice and a squeeze of fresh lemon. It was a relaxed Friday dinner, a quiet way to end the day before heading to class at Oak Hill College.
