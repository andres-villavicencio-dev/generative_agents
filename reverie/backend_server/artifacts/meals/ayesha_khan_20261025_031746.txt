Ayesha prepared Saffron and Lemon Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a warm Mediterranean-inspired dinner suitable for an early Tuesday.

The dish features chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, paprika, garlic, and cumin, then seared until golden and tender. It was served alongside a generous portion of roasted carrots, parsnips, and red potatoes, glazed with a simple lemon-herb sauce. Aromatics of garlic, herbs, and toasted breadcrumbs added rich, earthy notes, while the bright lemon and saffron brought a zesty freshness.

She presented it in a simple white bowl with the vegetables arranged in a neat arc around the center, garnished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. The meal was prepared for dinner around 5:00 p.m., while she tidied the kitchen and prepared ingredients for tomorrow’s dinner.
