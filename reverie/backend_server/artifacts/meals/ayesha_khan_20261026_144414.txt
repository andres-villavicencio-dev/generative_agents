Ayesha Khan prepared Simple Roasted Vegetables with Lemon-Herb Oil and Quinoa Salad, a nourishing lunch that balances her demanding academic schedule. She washed quinoa, roasted vegetables such as carrots, bell peppers, and zucchini over a medium heat, then chopped the vegetables into bite-sized pieces. She tossed the vegetables in a small bowl with lemon juice, olive oil, dried oregano, thyme, garlic, salt, and pepper, then mixed in the cooked quinoa.

The kitchen filled with the fresh aroma of roasted vegetables, lemon zest, warm herbs, and toasted grains. She arranged the colorful salad on a white plate, garnishing it with fresh parsley, a sprinkle of feta, and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil before serving. The meal was a quiet reminder of her Wednesday balance: focused research, social connections, and time in the quiet Johnson Park. It provided her with steady energy to carry forward her senior thesis while leaving room for reflection and leisure.
