Ayesha Khan prepared Lentil and Herb Rice Bowl with Roasted Potatoes.

The main dish began with a small pot of water simmered with onions, carrots, and a handful of garlic cloves. She added a generous portion of red lentils, a splash of vegetable broth, and a pinch of cumin, allowing the lentils to absorb the warm liquid. She stirred in chopped fresh herbs, a few lemon wedges, and a tiny splash of olive oil before turning off the heat.

Around the same time, she roasted potatoes until their edges became caramelized and golden. The finished rice was fluffed with a fork, and the bowl was garnished with parsley, a sprinkle of paprika, and a few sprigs of fresh mint. The aroma was rich and earthy, with the subtle scent of garlic, cumin, toasted lentils, and roasted potatoes filling the kitchen.

Ayesha prepared the meal for a quiet Wednesday lunch after her research on Shakespeare’s language and its connection to gentrification and housing. She arranged the colorful ingredients on a simple white plate, letting the bright greens, golden potatoes, and warm rice create a visually balanced presentation. The lunch felt personal and nourishing—a small pause before her work and evening reflection.
