Ayesha Khan prepared Ginger-Herb Rice with Grilled Chicken and Lemon-Orange Yogurt, a warm, comforting breakfast for her Thursday morning. The dish featured Jasmine rice, diced chicken breast, sliced carrots, celery, and fresh herbs, all infused with turmeric, cumin, and a fragrant ginger-garlic paste. Lemon juice and orange zest were stirred in at the end, while the yogurt was lightly toasted and served alongside the meal.

She prepared it in a small pan: the chicken was pan-seared until golden, then finished in the pan with the rice, which was gently simmered until the grains were tender. Aromas of toasted nutmeg, citrus, ginger, and warm cumin filled the kitchen as she stirred. The presentation was simple and inviting—serving the rice in a white bowl, plating the chicken in the center, and adding a colorful garnish of parsley and a drizzle of lemon-herb oil.

It was a quiet, purposeful breakfast, designed to provide energy for her demanding day before classes, the senior thesis, and creative writing.
