Ayesha prepared Chicken Tikka with Lemon-Herb Rice as part of her Wednesday dinner. She marinated chicken breast with cumin, garam masala, yogurt, garlic, and fresh herbs, then grilled it over a medium flame until the meat was tender and browned. The rice was sautéed with onions, ginger, and curry powder before being combined with the chicken and a little water.

She finished the meal at home, using a clean skillet and a few cups of water to keep the rice moist. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted cumin, fragrant herbs, caramelized chicken, and the savory-sweet scent of the rice. Ayesha plated the dish in a simple white bowl, arranging the chicken in a small mound and sprinkling the rice around it with fresh cilantro and lemon wedges.

The occasion was a quiet Wednesday evening: a regular home dinner after balancing classes, the senior thesis, creative writing, meals, reflection, social connections, and leisure. The food reflected her steady focus and creative spirit—balanced, flavorful, and presented in a relaxed, intimate way.
