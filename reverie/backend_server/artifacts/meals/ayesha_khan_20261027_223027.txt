Ayesha Khan prepared Roasted Vegetable Tagine with Lemon-Herb Rice, a simple yet comforting dinner that reflected her love of exploration and attention to detail. The dish included yogurt, feta, roasted carrots, potatoes, onions, tomatoes, garlic, and olive oil, with a hint of cumin and paprika for warmth.

She began by sautéing the onions and garlic in the olive oil, then added the carrots and potatoes. The tomatoes and spices were stirred in, followed by a splash of lemon juice and a final drizzle of yogurt to create a creamy, slightly tangy sauce. Feta was added just before the oven was turned on, allowing it to melt and release its salty, creamy aroma.

The food was presented in a shallow white bowl, with the vegetables arranged in a colorful mound and the rice rounded around the edges. A thin strip of lemon and a sprig of thyme were placed beside the tagine. The scent of warm herbs, roasted vegetables, and lemon rose strongly as the dishes were arranged on the table, while the inviting arrangement of the ingredients made the home-cooked meal feel both intimate and celebratory.

It was Ayesha’s simple Wednesday dinner, reflecting the purposeful rhythm she balanced with her classes, thesis, creative writing, and social life. The meal was neither too elaborate nor too hurried, allowing her to enjoy it while reflecting on her day.
