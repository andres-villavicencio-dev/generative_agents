Ayesha Khan prepared a simple but warmly inviting breakfast of toast with fruit and tea, accompanied by a small bowl of fresh fruit salad.

She toasted slices of whole-grain bread in the oven until golden, then spread them with honey and sliced seasonal fruit. The fruit included apple, pear, and a handful of berries, adding a bright, tart contrast to the honey. She poured tea over the toast and placed it on a small plate beside the salad.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of toasted bread, caramelized honey, and the crisp scent of fruit. The presentation was neat and intimate: the warm toast, glossy tea, and colorful fruit arranged beside a bowl of crisp berries. It was a quiet, nourishing meal for a busy Thursday, intended to nourish both her mind and her body as she continued working toward her senior thesis while enjoying the small domestic moments that shaped her day.
