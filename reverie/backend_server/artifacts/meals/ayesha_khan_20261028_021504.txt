Ayesha prepared a simple but nourishing breakfast of toasted whole-grain toast, seasonal fruit, and tea. She used a small pan to toast slices of bread until the edges were golden, then sliced fresh berries and a few pears onto the toast. While the fruit warmed, she prepared a cup of tea and arranged the pieces in a neat row.

The aroma of warm bread, caramelized fruit, and tea filled the quiet kitchen. She presented the meal with a small silver spoon and a glass of tea, letting the bright colors of the fruit and the golden toast create a balanced, inviting first meal.

The occasion was her senior thesis preparation—a quiet moment of reflection before tackling complex literary analysis. It was a peaceful start to her Thursday day, balancing coursework with creative exploration while she carried forward the grocery and kitchen plans from Wednesday.
