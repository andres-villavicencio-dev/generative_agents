Ayesha Khan prepared a simple but nourishing breakfast of Oatmeal Porridge with Toasted Almonds and a Lemon-Lime Cold Butter. The porridge was made by cooking rolled oats with water or milk until soft, then stirring in chopped almonds and a pinch of cinnamon. She sliced a thin slice of toasted bread and spread lemon-lime cold butter on top, finishing with a few candied orange strips.

As she prepared it, the kitchen smelled warm, nutty, and fresh—aromas of toasted oats, cinnamon, honey, citrus, and the faint hint of butter. The presentation was tidy and inviting: the oatmeal in a clear bowl, the almonds scattered over the toast, and the colorful orange strips adding a cheerful pop of color. This was a quiet Wednesday breakfast, intended to energize her before reviewing her senior thesis and beginning her work on Shakespeare’s use of language and its connection to gentrification and housing.
