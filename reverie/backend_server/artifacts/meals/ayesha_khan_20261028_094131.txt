Ayesha Khan prepared a Warm Lentil and Herb Soup with Roasted Root Vegetables, a nourishing meal suited to her busy Wednesday routine.

She began by washing and chopping carrots, potatoes, onions, and garlic, then added them to a pot with water and a handful of green lentils. She sautéed the onions and garlic until the vegetables released their sweetness, then simmered everything gently until the lentils became soft and the soup had a warm, creamy texture. A few drops of lemon juice and a pinch of salt added brightness, while dried mint provided a subtle herbaceous finish.

The aroma was earthy and comforting—warm carrots, onions, lentils, and garlic mingling beneath a fragrant, savory scent. Ayesha served the soup in a clear ceramic bowl, surrounded by roasted carrots, golden potatoes, and a bright lemon wedge. The warm, rustic presentation matched the quiet, reflective mood of her breakfast and thesis work. It was a simple, nourishing meal that supported her studies and creative thinking without adding unnecessary pressure.
