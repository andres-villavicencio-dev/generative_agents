Ayesha prepared Lentil and Tomato Soup with Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple, nourishing breakfast that reflected her grounded, purposeful nature. She began by rinsing and chopping carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, then sautéed them slowly with garlic and olive oil until the edges softened. Into a large pot, she added chopped onions, carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, followed by two cups of dried lentils, a generous splash of vegetable stock, and a pinch of cumin. The soup was simmered until the lentils were tender and the vegetables were well incorporated.

She served it in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a small mound of toasted seeds. A cup of warm water with lemon was placed beside it for the morning’s quiet ritual. The aroma rose from the pot—a warm, earthy mix of roasted root vegetables, lentils, and garlic.

The occasion was a calm Wednesday morning meal, intended to provide energy for her thesis and creative-writing work. It was neither a celebration nor a formal meal, but an ordinary, comforting pause in the rhythm of her college life.
