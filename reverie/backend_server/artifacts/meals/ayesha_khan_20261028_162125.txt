Ayesha prepared Golden Oat & Berry Breakfast Salad, a comforting meal that balanced breakfast with a moment of creative reflection. The dish combined old-fashioned oats, sliced apple, blueberries, raspberries, and walnuts, with a little honey, lemon zest, and a light dressing of yogurt, chopped nuts, and lemon juice.

She stirred the oats gently over low heat, letting the apples soften slightly while the berries added a burst of color and sweetness. The warm oatmeal was poured over the chilled salad, giving the meal a cozy, aromatic finish. The warm oats released a fragrant scent of nuttiness, honey, and citrus, while the crisp berries and creamy dressing provided a refreshing contrast.

Ayesha presented the salad on a small white plate, arranging the golden oats, bright berries, and walnuts in an asymmetrical, visually appealing arrangement. She added a sprig of mint for a clean finishing touch, creating an inviting breakfast that felt both nourishing and thoughtful.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Wednesday morning, a quiet pause before reviewing her senior thesis and exploring the nuanced connections between language, literature, and the city’s changing housing landscape.
