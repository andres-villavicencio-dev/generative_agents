Ayesha Khan prepared Chicken Tikka Masala, a classic South Asian comfort dish that reflects her culinary curiosity and determination. She marinated chicken pieces in yogurt, garlic, ginger, turmeric, cumin, chili, and coriander, then roasted them until golden and juicy. The chicken was slow-cooked in a rich tomato-based gravy with onions, cream, yogurt, and fresh herbs, while potatoes and naan bread were roasted until tender.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized onions, fragrant spices, and gently bubbling curry. Ayesha presented the colorful dish in a white bowl, garnished with bright green cilantro and a sprinkle of black pepper. She accompanied it with fresh naan and a glass of water, preparing for lunch while researching for her senior thesis on language in Shakespeare’s plays and balancing her studies with cooking. The meal felt both nourishing and reflective of her grounded, independent approach to life.
