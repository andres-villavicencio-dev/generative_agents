Ayesha prepared Lentil and Vegetable Soup with Warm Bread, a simple yet nourishing breakfast for her morning. The dish included brown lentils, carrots, onions, celery, garlic, tomatoes, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a splash of lemon juice. A few fresh herbs were added at the end for brightness, while toasted whole-grain bread was served alongside.

The ingredients were chopped and sautéed together in a pot over low heat, with the lentils slowly absorbing the rich broth. Ayesha stirred occasionally, ensuring the flavors melded without becoming mushy. As she finished, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized onions, garlic, and slow-cooked lentils, while the lemon and herbs added a subtle citrus freshness.

She presented the soup in a simple white bowl, spooning it over the bread. The contrast between the creamy, earthy soup and the golden-brown bread made the meal visually appealing. It was prepared for a quiet Tuesday morning before her senior thesis on language, power, and exclusion in Shakespeare’s plays—balanced, purposeful, and comforting.
