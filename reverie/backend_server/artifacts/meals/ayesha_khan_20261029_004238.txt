Ayesha prepared Lentil & Herb Rice with Pickled Carrots and Roasted Peppers, a simple, nourishing vegetarian lunch. The lentils were rinsed and simmered with onions, garlic, ginger, cumin, and smoked paprika until soft and flavorful, while the carrots and peppers were roasted with olive oil and a pinch of salt until caramelized. The pickled carrots were added for a bright acidic contrast, and the dish was finished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice.

The kitchen smelled earthy, warm, and aromatic—like toasted cumin, roasted peppers, and the gentle fragrance of simmering lentils. Ayesha plated the rice in a white ceramic bowl, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a small sprig of rosemary, then added a few pickled carrots for visual appeal.

It was a quiet Tuesday lunch, part of her routine while balancing her college thesis, conversations with David, and exploring the city. The meal felt practical and comforting, a familiar pause between classes and thoughts about language, power, and inclusion.
