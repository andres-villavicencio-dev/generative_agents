Ayesha prepared a hearty Lemon-Herb Roast Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Garlic Bread. The centerpiece was a whole chicken seasoned with lemon zest, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a touch of smoked paprika, then roasted until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. Alongside it, she roasted carrots, potatoes, and onions with olive oil, rosemary, and salt until they were caramelized and slightly charred. A simple garlic-butter crust was sliced and brushed with butter before baking.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, caramelized onions, and fragrant herbs. Ayesha arranged the golden chicken and vegetables on a platter, garnished with fresh parsley, and placed a small bowl of lemon-herb salad beside it. The dish was ready for a Friday dinner, a relaxed evening to enjoy after a busy day of researching her Shakespearean thesis, cooking, and conversations with David.
