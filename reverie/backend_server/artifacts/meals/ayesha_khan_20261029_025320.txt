The main dish is Chana Masala with Naan and Salad, a comforting Indian-inspired dinner Ayesha prepared for Tuesday, February 14. She toasted whole-wheat naan, sautéed onions, garlic, and ginger, then added chickpeas, carrots, tomatoes, green chilies, and a generous amount of coconut cream. Curry powder, cumin, coriander, and garam masala gave the spiced lentil mixture its warm, aromatic depth. Aromas of toasted bread, cumin, ginger, and warm spices filled the kitchen while she stirred slowly over medium heat.

Ayesha presented the colorful curry alongside the soft, golden naan and a crisp cucumber-tomato salad. She drizzed a little lemon-herb yogurt over the naan and placed it beside the curry. The meal was simple but carefully balanced, and she chose it for an easy, nourishing dinner while continuing her research on language and exclusion. It was her first evening of cooking alone, combining her curiosity with a quiet moment of relaxation.
