Ayesha Khan prepares Curry Chicken with Rice and Green Peas, a warm side dish for dinner on Wednesday, February 15. She seasons the chicken with cumin, coriander, turmeric, garlic, ginger, and a splash of lemon juice, then sears it until golden. Fresh green peas are stir-fried separately, and the rice is cooked in a pot with warm water and a little oil. She serves the rice in a small, shallow bowl, arranging the chicken and peas around it, with a lemon wedge and a sprig of cilantro garnish. The kitchen fills with the rich, savory aroma of toasted spices, caramelized chicken, and fresh vegetables.

It is a simple, comforting dinner meant to balance her busy schedule of school, conversations with David, and research on Shakespeare and gentrification. The meal also feels like a quiet moment of nourishment amid her intellectual work—her determination and curiosity for literature reflected in the carefully chosen ingredients and her focused preparation.
