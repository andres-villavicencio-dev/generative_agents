Ayesha Khan prepared a Gentle Lentil and Kale Salad with a warm lemon-herb chickpea salad and a hearty lentil curry.

For the main salad, she toasted brown lentils and chopped kale in a pan, adding cumin, turmeric, and a splash of coconut milk until the lentils warmed through. She then combined them with chopped tomatoes, cucumber, cucumber, carrots, red onion, mint, coriander, and a lemon-herb vinaigrette. A side dish of warm chickpeas with lemon-honey yogurt and a few fresh mint leaves provided a bright, refreshing contrast.

The curry used brown lentils, carrots, potatoes, onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and a blend of cumin, coriander, turmeric, and garam masala. A little coconut cream and a pinch of chili added color and depth. Aromas of toasted nuts, warm curry, and lemon filled the kitchen as she layered the bowls.

She plated the dishes with colorful vegetable garnishes—parsley, cilantro, lemon wedges, and whole peppercorns—and served them on simple white plates. On Saturday, February 18, she prepared the meal to take quiet time to reflect on her senior thesis and coursework, while the comforting food offered her a moment of comfort and focus.
