Ayesha prepared a simple but nourishing breakfast of oatmeal with berries, honey, and a sprinkle of cinnamon. The oats were cooked with water until soft, then stirred in fresh blueberries, sliced strawberries, and a spoonful of natural honey. Cinnamon was added for warmth, and a little vanilla enhanced the comforting aroma.

She arranged the oatmeal in a small white bowl, garnishing it with fresh blueberries and a few whole almonds. The presentation was clean and inviting—warm colors, contrasting textures, and a bright contrast of red, orange, and green created a cheerful, celebratory look. It was meant to be a simple start to her day, offering comfort while she continued her work on her senior thesis.
