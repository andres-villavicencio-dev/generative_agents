Ayesha prepared Roasted Vegetable and Chickpea Curry, a simple vegetarian dinner for her Monday evening. The dish combined roasted red peppers, carrots, onions, and cauliflower with spiced chickpeas, coconut milk, ginger, and a touch of cumin. She sautéed the vegetables in a pan, added the spices, and simmered the curry until the chickpeas softened and the vegetables were tender.

The aroma was warm and fragrant, with sweet roasted peppers, earthy carrots, and a savory depth from the cumin and curry powder. She plated the curry in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with fresh cilantro, sliced tomatoes, and a drizzle of hot pepper sauce. Aloo Gobi and a side of naan completed the meal, offering a balanced combination of creamy richness and crisp, colorful vegetables.

The occasion was informal, though Ayesha was still focused on her senior thesis about language in Shakespeare. She prepared the meal in her small kitchen at Hobbs Cafe, balancing her research work with the need for comfort food. After finishing around 5:30 p.m., she went straight to bed, reflecting on her day as she prepared for a quiet night of reading and writing.
