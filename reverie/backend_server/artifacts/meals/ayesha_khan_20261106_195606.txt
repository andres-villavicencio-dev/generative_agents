Ayesha Khan prepared Lemon-Herb Roasted Chickpeas with Grilled Zucchini and Tomato for dinner. The dish featured firm chickpeas, halved zucchini, sliced tomatoes, red onion, garlic, olive oil, lemon zest, crushed red pepper, and fresh rosemary. She sautéed the vegetables in oil until the zucchini became tender, then added the chickpeas and cooked them for five minutes. The chickpeas were seasoned with garlic and lemon, while the vegetables retained their bright color and crisp texture.

She arranged the warm vegetables in a shallow dish and topped them with a few chickpeas, finishing with a little extra zest and a drizzle of olive oil. The meal had an inviting golden glow, with lemon-lime aromas rising from the pan. It was the simple, satisfying dinner she prepared after a long day of classes and thesis research, making it a cozy, nourishing end to her Wednesday evening.
