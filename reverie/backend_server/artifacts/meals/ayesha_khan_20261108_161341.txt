Ayesha Khan prepared Heritage Vegetable Curry with Roasted Tomato Rice, a warm, savory lunch suited for a quiet college day. The dish included chickpeas, carrots, bell peppers, onions, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, turmeric, cumin, garam masala, yogurt, and lemon wedges. The curry was simmered in a pot over medium heat until the vegetables softened and the spices released their fragrance. She then stirred the rice with a little coconut milk and turmeric, letting it cook briefly before serving it alongside the curry.

The aroma was earthy and comforting, with the scent of warming spices, onions, and roasted tomatoes filling the kitchen. Ayesha plated the curry in a shallow white dish, arranging the golden rice around the sides and garnishing it with a sprig of mint and a few lemon slices. It was a simple, nourishing meal shared with classmates during a relaxed afternoon before her library classes and thesis work.
