Ayesha Khan prepared Oatmeal Fruit Yogurt with Nuts, a comforting breakfast for a Monday morning. She began by cooking oatmeal in a pot, simmering it with water or milk until the oats were tender, then stirring in a cup of plain yogurt.

She added sliced apples, pears, or bananas, and chopped walnuts or almonds for crunch. A pinch of cinnamon and a little vanilla were stirred in for warmth, while a small handful of raisins added a touch of sweetness. The dish was finished with a spoonful of freshly ground black pepper and a drizzle of honey if she had leftovers.

The finished meal had a warm, earthy aroma of oats, honey, and fruit. It was presented in a simple white bowl, with the yogurt and nuts arranged neatly around the oatmeal. Ayoisha ate it with a spoon, enjoying the comforting texture and balance of warm, crunchy, and sweet flavors. It was her first full breakfast of the day, followed by a quiet moment of tea and reading before heading to class.
