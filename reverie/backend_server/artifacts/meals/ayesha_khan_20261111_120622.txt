Dish Name: Oat & Berry Fruit Smoothie Breakfast Bowl

Ayesha prepared a simple, refreshing breakfast using oats, fresh fruit, and a splash of milk. She added a handful of rolled oats, sliced apples, bananas, blueberries, and a few strawberries to a blender, then blended everything until smooth. A little honey was stirred in at the end to keep the mixture creamy and nutritious.

The aroma was warm and naturally sweet—mildly caramelized oats, ripe fruit, and the faint hint of honey filled the kitchen before she served it. She poured the mixture into a small ceramic bowl and arranged the fruit slices and oats in neat rows around the sides. A wooden spoon rested on top, and a small glass of water was placed beside it.

This was her first breakfast for the day, prepared as part of her morning routine while she continued researching literature. The plain, nourishing meal reflected her desire for simple, practical meals that supported her studies. By 6:30 a.m., she had finished eating and began her day at Oak Hill College.
