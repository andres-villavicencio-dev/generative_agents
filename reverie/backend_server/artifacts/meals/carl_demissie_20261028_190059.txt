Carl prepared Smoky Barbecue Chicken with Rice and Green Beans for breakfast. He marinated chicken thighs in smoked paprika, garlic, onion, and brown sugar, then grilled them with olive oil and a pinch of salt until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. Simultaneously, he simmered rice with chicken broth, onions, and garlic, while the green beans were tossed with olive oil, salt, and lemon juice. The finished dish was served on a wide wooden platter, with the golden-brown chicken arranged alongside fluffy rice and bright green beans.

The kitchen smelled of smoked meat, garlic, and caramelized onions. Carl presented the meal with a generous plate of rice and a small bowl of lemon-topped beans, carefully placing a paper napkin beside each serving. It was an early-morning breakfast for the clinic’s staff, a simple, satisfying meal that reflected his disciplined approach to the day ahead.
