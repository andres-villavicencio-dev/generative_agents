On Wednesday, February 15, Carl prepared Gullah-Inspired Grits with Grilled Chicken, Green Peas, and Sweet Potato Fries for a quiet morning breakfast at the clinic. The meal was simple, nourishing, and rooted in the familiar flavors of his Lowcountry upbringing.

He started with a bowl of hot Gullah grits, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a touch of garlic powder. The grits were topped with grilled chicken breast, green peas, and sweet potato fries. For the potatoes, he sliced them into small chunks, tossed them with olive oil, and roasted them on the stovetop until golden and crisp. He also added a side of honey-glazed carrots for a subtle sweetness.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich smell of grilled chicken, caramelized onions, roasted potatoes, and warm spices. As Carl plated the breakfast, he arranged the colorful vegetables, bright green peas, and golden fries beside the white grits. The presentation was casual yet inviting, with a small bowl of yogurt and fresh orange slices added for a finishing touch.

He prepared the meal to serve his neighbors and fellow clinic staff, honoring the community values he carried from his Gullah roots and Lowcountry upbringing. It was a simple, satisfying breakfast, but one that reminded him of the connection he hoped to strengthen with everyone he met.
