Carl prepared a Gullah-inspired chicken and greens breakfast sandwich, prepared as a simple, family-style lunch for himself and his neighbors.

He browned sliced chicken breast in olive oil, then added chopped spinach, red onion, and a pinch of smoked paprika. A few lemon wedges and a sprig of parsley were used to season the pan. The chicken was cooked until the center was done, then set aside while the greens were wilted in a little butter.

For the sandwich, he sliced the chicken, lettuce, and tomato, and warmed a small piece of toasted bread. He layered the ingredients and added a squeeze of lemon and a sprinkle of paprika before serving. The dish was warm, aromatic, and filled with the savory-sweet notes of chicken, greens, onion, and herbs.

The meal was prepared quietly while browsing the phone’s song list, but it was a thoughtful choice for a Tuesday lunch—simple, nourishing, and reflective of the community-centered values of Gullah storytelling, gospel music, and Juneteenth traditions that shaped his upbringing.
