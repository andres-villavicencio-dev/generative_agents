Carl prepared a hearty Gullah-style Chicken and Vegetable Pot Roast for a Saturday family dinner. The dish featured a whole chicken, potatoes, carrots, onions, green beans, and a generous serving of fresh vegetables. He marinated the chicken in olive oil, garlic, paprika, thyme, and a little soy sauce before roasting it on a large skillet, then seared the pieces until golden. He browned the vegetables in a pan, added a splash of vegetable broth and water, and covered the pot to let the chicken and vegetables simmer gently until the sauce thickened.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm herbs, roasted garlic, savory poultry, and the earthy scent of tomatoes and onions. He served the meal in a large platter, arranging the chicken and vegetables in an appealing pyramid shape, with a side of steamed broccoli and brown rice. The presentation was simple and inviting, with each ingredient clearly visible and the rich, golden sauce glistening on the meat.

The occasion was a relaxed Saturday evening meal with his family, honoring his roots through the familiar flavors of his grandmother’s cooking and the community gatherings he helped organize.
