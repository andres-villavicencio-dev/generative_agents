Carl prepared Gullah-inspired smoked chicken with root vegetables, a simple yet hearty meal inspired by the flavors of his childhood home. He marinated bone-in chicken thighs in smoked paprika, garlic, onion, ginger, and a touch of curry powder, then slowly seared them in a cast-iron pan until the skin was crisp and the meat was pink. Alongside the chicken, he roasted carrots, potatoes, and red potatoes with a little lemon juice and brown sugar, while green beans and a cup of jasmine rice completed the plate.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm woodsmoke, caramelized onions, and savory spices. As he plated the meal, he arranged the golden chicken around the rice and vegetables, garnishing it with fresh parsley and serving a small bowl of spicy chili garlic sauce. The occasion was a quiet Friday lunch before his re-election campaign, a moment of calm and community connection. Eating alone, he appreciated how the familiar, comforting flavors reminded him of his upbringing and the values he carried forward as an operations manager and civic leader.
