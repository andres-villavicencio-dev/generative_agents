Carl prepared Coastal Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice and Roasted Vegetables. The meal was simple, warm, and designed to energize a community gathering.

He grilled chicken breast with garlic, oregano, lemon, and a little soy-sesame glaze, then arranged it over a bed of fluffy white rice. Roasted carrots, bell peppers, and snapdragons added color and sweetness, while a simple salad of mixed greens and cucumber completed the plate. The kitchen was filled with the sharp aroma of garlic and oregano, the savory scent of grilled meat, and the bright citrus fragrance of the lemon.

The meal was a Friday family-style gathering, celebrating Carl’s re-election campaign and supporting the clinic’s health-education partnership. Each neighbor gathered around the table to share a bowl of rice, a piece of chicken, and a side of salad, making the simple meal feel warm, communal, and distinctly Lowcountry.
