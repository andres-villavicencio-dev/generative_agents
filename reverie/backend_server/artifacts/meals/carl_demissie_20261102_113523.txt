Carl prepared Gullah-style Chicken with Brown Rice and Collard Greens for a simple, hearty lunch. The dish was seasoned with smoked paprika, garlic, onion, thyme, salt, and pepper, with the chicken marinated in a warm broth. He sliced the chicken, browned it in a skillet, and cooked the collard greens in a little more oil until they were crisp and tender. Brown rice was rinsed and cooked until firm.

The meal had a savory, slightly smoky aroma of smoked chicken and earthy greens, balanced by the nutty flavor of rice and the soft richness of the broth. Carl plated the food in a rustic white bowl with a silver fork and a small spoon of extra-rice at the side, making it easy to eat with both hands. It was prepared as a practical, community-focused lunch for a quiet Saturday morning, a relaxed meal intended to restore him before he reviewed the clinic bulletin board and confirmed the exact date, time, location, and application requirements for the Golden Hour at the Garage opening.
