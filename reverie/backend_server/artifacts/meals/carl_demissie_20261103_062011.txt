Carl prepared Grilled Chicken and Butternut Squash with Lemon-Garlic Butter, a simple breakfast inspired by his community gatherings and love for sharing good food. The meal consisted of a whole chicken grilled over a bed of butternut squash, topped with a golden-brown crust and drizzled with a warm lemon-garlic butter sauce. Key ingredients included chicken thighs, butternut squash, onions, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and fresh herbs.

He sliced the squash into small cubes, seasoned the chicken with salt and pepper, and grilled both over medium heat until the chicken was cooked through and the squash was tender. He finished the dish by tossing the remaining vegetables with a little butter and lemon, then spooned the warm sauce over the chicken. The aroma filled the small kitchen with the savory scent of roasted garlic, citrus, and caramelized squash.

Carl plated the meal on a white plate with crisp lettuce and a few whole grains, serving it alongside a glass of water. The occasion was a quiet Saturday breakfast before he resumed his demanding day at the clinic, using the meal as a simple way to start his day with energy and satisfaction.
