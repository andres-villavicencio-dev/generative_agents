Carl prepared Cedar-Smoked Chicken with Root Vegetables and Garlic Herb Rice for a neighborhood breakfast. He sliced bone-in chicken breasts over a cedar plank, seasoning them with smoked paprika, garlic, thyme, and salt, then roasted them until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. Root vegetables—carrots, potatoes, sweet potatoes, and onions—were lightly sautéed, while the rice was seasoned with garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of black pepper.

The warm aroma of smoke, roasted chicken, and earthy roots filled the small kitchen, creating a welcoming, comforting scent. He arranged the meal on a white dinner plate with crisp vegetables and a generous portion of fluffy rice, garnishing it with fresh parsley. The simple, hearty presentation balanced rich flavors with bright colors, making it ready for his morning breakfast before the clinic workday began.
