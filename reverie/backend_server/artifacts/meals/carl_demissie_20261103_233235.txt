Carl prepared Gullah-style Chicken and Green Beans for a relaxed Monday dinner at home. He marinated bone-in chicken thighs with garlic, onion, black pepper, and a little soy sauce, then pan-seared them until golden and juicy. He sliced the chicken, tossed the cooked green beans with lemon juice, and served them alongside fluffy jasmine rice.

Aromatic rosemary, thyme, garlic, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen, while the savory scent of the chicken mingled with the bright, crisp scent of the vegetables. Carl arranged the warm chicken and rice on a simple white platter and added a small bowl of lemon-lime dressing and a handful of sliced green onions for visual appeal.

The meal was prepared for a quiet evening around 6:00 p.m., a time when Carl enjoyed a simple dinner before his re-election campaign work and meetings.
