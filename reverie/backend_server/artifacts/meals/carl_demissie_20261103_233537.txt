Carl prepared Smoked Chicken and Gullah-Style Green Beans, a hearty family-style dinner inspired by his gullah roots and the community gatherings he loved during his youth.

He marinated chicken thighs in smoked paprika, garlic, onion, and a touch of soy sauce, then browned them in a cast-iron skillet. Afterward, he sautéed chopped green beans with a little olive oil, garlic, and a pinch of cumin, tossing them with the cooked chicken and serving them over rice. The dish was finished with a simple lemon-herb sauce.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of smoked meat, caramelized onions, toasted garlic, and the earthy sweetness of the beans. The golden chicken and bright green beans were arranged in a shallow bowl, with a small plate of pickled red onions and a side salad nearby.

On a Monday evening, the meal was part of a relaxed family dinner before Carl’s evening work and re-election campaign preparations. It was a warm, communal meal that reflected his disciplined, meticulous nature and the welcoming values he carried from his upbringing.
