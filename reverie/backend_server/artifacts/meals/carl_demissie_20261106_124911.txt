Carl prepared a Gullah-Inspired Black Eyed Peas and Smoked Chicken Skillet for dinner on February 14. The dish features tender chicken thighs smoked with honey and smoked paprika, then simmered with black-eyed peas, onions, bell peppers, and a rich tomato-soy sauce. He added fresh herbs, a dollop of creamy coconut yogurt, and a sprinkle of smoked paprika for color.

The skillet was cooked over medium heat, with the chicken browning and the vegetables softening in the flavorful broth. As the dish finished, he stirred in the yogurt and let it simmer briefly, allowing the flavors to meld. The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm smokiness of the chicken, the earthy depth of the black-eyed peas, and the gentle tang of the sour-sweet sauce.

He presented the meal in a rustic, earthenware bowl, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil. The dinner was likely a family-style meal intended for his small-town gatherings, combining his disciplined, meticulous cooking style with the welcoming, community-oriented spirit he values through gospel music and Juneteenth traditions.
