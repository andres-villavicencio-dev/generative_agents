Carl prepared Gullah-style Chicken with Smoked Barbecue Sauce and Green Beans for a quiet Friday evening before his re-election campaign. The dish featured chicken breast marinated in garlic, lemon, oregano, and a little honey, with the meat smoldered in a low oven until it was golden and falling off the bone. He sliced the chicken, tossed it with a glossy, sweet-spicy barbecue sauce, and added crisp green beans.

The kitchen smelled of caramelized garlic, warm smoked meat, and earthy herbs, with the savory aroma carrying through the dining room. Carl arranged the food on a white platter, garnished with a few fresh herbs and a sprig of parsley, and served it in a shallow bowl with lemon wedges and a side of steamed rice. The meal was a simple, family-style dinner, intended to be a peaceful moment of reflection before he began preparing his campaign schedule and reviewing the paperwork with Yuriko Yamamoto.
