Carl prepared Smoky Chicken and Root Vegetables with Gravy for his post-work dinner at home. The dish featured tender chicken thighs marinated in smoked paprika, garlic, onion, and thyme, slow-cooked until golden and juicy. Alongside them were carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes, all roasted until caramelized and tender. A rich, savory chicken gravy was poured over everything, with a sprinkle of parsley and lemon zest added for brightness.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of smoked spices, caramelized onions, and savory chicken. Carl arranged the colorful vegetables in a simple oval arrangement around the cooked chicken, with a small bowl of lemon juice and fresh herbs resting nearby. The presentation was rustic and inviting, evoking the flavors and gatherings of his Lowcountry upbringing.

He prepared the meal on Monday, February 13, after a long workday at Smallville’s clinic. The warm dinner was an appropriate conclusion to a demanding schedule and a reminder of his gregarious, community-oriented nature.
