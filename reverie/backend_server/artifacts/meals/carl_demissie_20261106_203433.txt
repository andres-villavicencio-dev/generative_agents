Carl prepared Gullah Guffa Barbecue Sandwiches for his re-election dinner at the Smallville clinic. The sandwiches featured grilled chicken, smoked pork, and sweet potatoes, with house-made mustard and vinegar, Gullah-inspired seasoning, and a generous side of rice. The meats were marinated with smoked paprika, garlic, ginger, and a little honey, then seared until the skin caramelized. They were arranged on warm cornbread with a small bowl of warm rice and a side of coleslaw.

As he sat at the dining table, he carefully reviewed his campaign documents and signed them, his disciplined hands moving with purpose. The aroma of grilled meat, toasted bread, and warm spices filled the kitchen, while the sound of his campaign documents on the table created a sense of quiet focus. The meal was simple and hearty, reflecting his community-oriented values and personal identity. It was a fitting way to begin his campaign preparations, turning a familiar, neighborhood-style dinner into an occasion of reflection and renewed purpose.
