Carl prepared Barbecue Chicken with Lemon-Herb Glaze and Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple yet hearty meal for Yuriko, Mei, and the neighborhood group. He marinated chicken breasts in garlic, onion, smoked paprika, and lemon, searing them until golden, then serving them over rice with carrots, onions, and sweet potatoes. The vegetables were roasted until tender and caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the warm, smoky aroma of the BBQ and the bright, citrusy scent of lemon. The golden chicken, fluffy rice, and colorful roasted vegetables created a visually appealing presentation on a white platter.

It was a casual family-style breakfast for the town meeting, intended to provide a comforting, nourishing meal while Carl reviewed the legal documents for his re-election campaign.
