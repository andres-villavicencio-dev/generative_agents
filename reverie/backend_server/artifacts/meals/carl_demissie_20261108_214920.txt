Carl prepared Gullah-style Jamaican jerk chicken with rice, plantain, and a fresh vegetable salad for his takeout breakfast. The chicken was marinated in garlic, ginger, scotch bonnet pepper, cumin, paprika, and smoked paprika, then grilled until golden and tender. He added a plump plantain and rice to the pan, seasoning it with black pepper and a dash of vinegar. A crisp salad of romaine lettuce, diced tomatoes, avocado, and cilantro was arranged on a white plate with a lemon-olive oil dressing.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of caramelized chicken, toasted garlic, and the earthy scent of smoked spices. Carl presented the colorful meal with care, arranging the chicken in the center and the salad and rice around it. He served it with a small bowl of steamed green beans and a squeeze of lime.

The meal was prepared for a Tuesday morning at Hobbs Cafe, where Carl was making coffee and a nutritious breakfast for himself and his neighbors. The simple, hearty combination reflected his disciplined, communal approach to cooking and reflected the values of the Lowcountry community he had grown up with.
