Carl prepared Black-Eyed Pea and Scallion Breakfast Burritos, a hearty, neighborhood-inspired meal prepared for Yuriko’s re-election campaign gathering. The burritos filled with black-eyed peas, diced yellow corn, shredded cheddar, salsa, diced tomatoes, and fresh scallions were assembled in sturdy flour tortillas. He sautéed the vegetables, seasoned them lightly, and wrapped the ingredients inside the tortillas before rolling them into neat bundles.

The kitchen smelled of caramelized onions, savory cheese, and toasted corn, with the warm, earthy scent of the black-eyed peas adding a comforting aroma. Each burrito was presented on a white plate, garnished with a sprig of parsley, and arranged in a colorful, inviting row—perfect for sharing.

This was a simple, communal breakfast prepared to support the community and encourage neighbors to connect. After preparing the burritos, Carl toasted a side of grilled corn and served himself, enjoying the meal alongside colleagues and friends as he continued his work and social life.
