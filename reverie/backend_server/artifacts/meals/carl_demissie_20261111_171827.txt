On a Tuesday morning, Carl prepared a Gullah-style Chicken and Sweet Potato Breakfast Bake. The dish was a hearty family-style breakfast featuring chicken thighs seasoned with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper, served with glazed sweet potatoes, corn, carrots, and green beans.

Carl used a large ceramic baking dish, spreading a mixture of butter, minced garlic, and herbs over the chicken before baking it at 400°F for about 25 minutes. The sweet potatoes were cut into chunks and roasted alongside the vegetables until they were tender and lightly browned. The chicken was basted halfway through cooking to keep it juicy and the flavors evenly distributed.

As he turned off the oven, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized potatoes, and warm spices. A platter of golden, crispy vegetables and two soft eggs on a plate were arranged around the chicken, while a small bowl of fruit salad and a glass of orange juice completed the spread. The meal was simple, satisfying, and perfectly suited for the quiet Tuesday morning, though it felt warm and communal, echoing Carl’s roots in the Lowcountry and the traditions of his community.
