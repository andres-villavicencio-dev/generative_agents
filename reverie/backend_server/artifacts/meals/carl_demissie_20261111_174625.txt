On Tuesday, February 14, Carl prepared Gooey Cornbread with Bacon and Lemon for a quiet morning breakfast. The dish combined tender cornbread, crispy bacon, creamy cheese, and a bright lemon wedge. He stirred a pot of bacon fat until it bubbled, then added chopped onions, garlic, and diced tomatoes. Corn kernels, shredded cheddar, and butter were stirred in, followed by the eggs and sliced ham.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the smoky scent of bacon, the pungency of garlic, and the faint sweetness of fresh corn. Carl cooked everything until the eggs were set and the cheese melted, then served the golden cornbread slices alongside a glass of milk.

The meal was simple but hearty, made for a lazy Tuesday morning before a scheduled clinic appointment. It was more than a breakfast—it was a small, neighborhood gathering, reflecting Carl’s gregarious side, his commitment to serving the community, and his belief that wholesome food brings neighbors together.
