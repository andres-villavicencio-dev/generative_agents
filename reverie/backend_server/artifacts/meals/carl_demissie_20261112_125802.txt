Carl prepared Gullah-style Chicken and Black-Eyed Peas, a hearty, rustic meal inspired by his Lowcountry upbringing. The dish features seasoned chicken thighs and tender black-eyed peas, along with chopped scallions, tomatoes, onions, garlic, and a modest amount of olive oil.

He marinated the chicken in a mixture of olive oil, garlic, herbs, and a touch of lemon juice before cooking it over medium heat. The black-eyed peas were simmered until tender, while the vegetables were lightly sautéed to add color and aroma. He used a heavy-bottomed pot so the food stayed moist, and finished the dish with a little fresh parsley. The kitchen filled with the rich scent of roasting chicken, caramelized onions, and earthy spices.

Carl plated the chicken in a rustic bowl, garnishing it with parsley and a few fresh tomato slices. The meal was simple yet satisfying, with the vegetables adding brightness and the chicken providing a savory protein. It was prepared for a community breakfast on February 14, a day when neighbors gathered to celebrate his mayoral campaign, share news, and strengthen the clinic’s partnership with the college library.
