Carl Demissie prepared Smoky Lowcountry Barbecue for a community gathering, a favorite meal that reflects his roots in the Lowcountry and his love of sharing food with neighbors.

The dish featured pork shoulder, smoked paprika, garlic, onion, tomato, black pepper, and a small amount of vinegar. The meat was slow-smoked over hardwood for several hours, then slow-cooked in a Dutch oven with onions, tomatoes, and a savory glaze to develop a deep, caramelized crust. The sauce was finished with fresh herbs and served over tenderly shredded potatoes.

Aromatic smoke, sweetness, garlic, onion, and savory fruit notes filled the kitchen as Carl carefully heated the base ingredients and cooked the main dish. The meal was plated in a wide bowl, with a generous portion of meat and potatoes in the center, sides arranged around it, and extra sauce placed beside each piece.

It was prepared for Yuriko Yamamoto’s re-election campaign gathering, a community event where neighbors were expected to gather for conversation, fellowship, and refreshments. The barbecue represented Carl’s disciplined, communal approach to service, while the careful preparation reflected his meticulous nature.
