Carlos Gomez prepared Spring Herb Risotto with Roasted Wild Mushrooms, a dish inspired by the quiet beauty of the natural world.

The key ingredients were arborio rice, cremini and shiitake mushrooms, garlic, shallots, dry white wine, vegetable broth, Parmesan, lemon zest, parsley, thyme, and a drizzle of olive oil. After washing his hands, he sautéed the shallots and garlic until fragrant, then browned the mushrooms with thyme. He stirred in the rice, allowing each grain to absorb the wine and broth gradually while adding chopped parsley and lemon zest. The risotto was finished with Parmesan and a small knob of butter, giving it a creamy texture and balanced, earthy flavor.

Its aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of toasted mushrooms, garlic, herbs, and delicate garlic, lemon, and herbs. He served it in a shallow white bowl, topped with roasted mushrooms, parsley, lemon zest, and freshly cracked pepper. The occasion was a peaceful Monday evening dinner, prepared while he listened to rain and worked on a poem about forests changing with the seasons.
