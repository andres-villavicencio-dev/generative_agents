Carlos Gomez prepared a dish called Lemon-Parmesan Roasted Cauliflower with Herbed Tahini for his Monday evening meal.

The key ingredients were a whole cauliflower, lemon zest and juice, olive oil, Parmesan cheese, garlic, tahini, fresh parsley, and a pinch of chili flakes. He washed the cauliflower, broke it into large florets, and tossed them with oil, lemon, garlic, and salt. He roasted the pieces in a hot oven until their edges turned deeply golden and crisp, then sprinkled them with Parmesan and parsley while still hot. The tahini was loosened with water, lemon juice, and a little olive oil to create a creamy sauce, which he drizzled over the cauliflower and finished with chili flakes.

The aroma was bright and savory, with roasted garlic, toasted cheese, citrus, and warm spices filling the kitchen. He presented the dish on a matte black plate, using the cauliflower florets like sculptural brushstrokes against the pale tahini.

The occasion was a quiet, restorative dinner after Carlos’s creative writing workshop, giving him time to reflect on poetry and the beauty of the natural world.
