For Wednesday, February 15, Carlos Gomez prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Smoked Chicken and Herb Crust, a hearty dinner meant to bring the group together. The main dish consisted of carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes roasted until caramelized and slightly browned, with tender chicken thighs wrapped in a fragrant herb crust made of rosemary, thyme, garlic, and lemon zest.

Carlos began by washing and cutting the root vegetables into uniform pieces, then arranged them on a large baking sheet. The chicken was seared to a golden-brown before being set aside and covered to braise. The herb mixture was ground into a crisp crust, baked over a hot oven until crisp and flavorful, then used to seal the chicken. A splash of balsamic vinegar was drizzled over the finished dish, adding a subtle sweetness to balance the earthy vegetables.

The aroma was warm, savory, and deeply aromatic—the sharp rosemary and thyme mingling with caramelized onions, roasted garlic, and smoky chicken. The presentation was rustic and inviting: golden vegetables arranged in a small oval, the chicken placed beside them, and a simple white plate with a dark brown crust garnished with a sprig of rosemary. It was a practical meal for the group dinner, combining comfort with an inviting, celebratory atmosphere.
