On Wednesday, February 15, Carlos Gomez prepared a simple dinner for his group: Pan-Seared Chicken with Lemon-Herb Roasted Vegetables and Rice.

The dish featured bone-in chicken thighs, lemon wedges, fresh rosemary, thyme, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of smoked paprika. The chicken was seared until golden and tender, then cooked through with the roasted vegetables. A creamy lemon-herb sauce was poured over everything, while rice was stirred into the pan for easy absorption.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted chicken, warm lemon, savory herbs, and toasted garlic. The meal was presented on a white platter, surrounded by colorful vegetables, garnished with fresh parsley and lemon slices, and accompanied by a small side of bread.

Carlos had organized his notes and snacks beforehand to make the gathering comfortable, though his loud, sometimes abrasive personality occasionally created a tense atmosphere. The dinner was meant to be a cozy, informal group meal, but the effort to prepare it carefully made him feel responsible for its quality.
