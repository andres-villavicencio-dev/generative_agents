For Wednesday, February 15, Carlos Gomez prepared a Blackberry–Brie–Honey Glazed Salmon with Roasted Asparagus for a group dinner. The dish featured salmon fillets glazed with a sweet-honey reduction, warm brie, thyme, lemon zest, and a touch of Dijon mustard, alongside bright green asparagus and a side of roasted Brussels sprouts.

He sautéed the salmon in olive oil until golden, then glazed it with the honey and brie before serving. The asparagus was roasted until crisp, while the Brussels sprouts were lightly sautéed to keep their texture intact. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized honey, toasted herbs, and the savory scent of the fish.

In a white bowl, the salmon was arranged atop a bed of warm brie, with lemon zest and thyme scattered over it. The plate was presented in a simple, elegant ceramic bowl, with the colorful vegetables arranged around the center. It was ready for the group dinner, but Carlos was uncertain whether any workshop materials had been sent home.
