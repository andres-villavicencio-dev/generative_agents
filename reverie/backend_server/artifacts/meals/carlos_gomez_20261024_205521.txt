Dish Name: “Morning Mist” Roasted Vegetable Panini with Lemon-Herb Glaze and Creamy Quinoa

Key Ingredients:  
Romaine, yellow squash, red bell pepper, red onion, and cherry tomatoes  
Garlic, fresh rosemary, lemon juice, olive oil, and a little honey  
Quinoa, feta, black olives, and toasted nuts  
Sourdough bread and a small bowl of warm water

Preparation Method:  
Carlos sliced the vegetables and arranged them on a panini press. He rubbed the garlic into the bread, then brushed it with olive oil and lemon-herb glaze before pressing the vegetables until they softened. The panini was toasted at 400°F for three minutes, then sliced and warmed with the creamy quinoa. Feta, olives, and toasted nuts were added on top for texture and color.

Aroma:  
The dish smells warm and savory—herbs, garlic, lemon zest, and toasted nuts create a sharp, earthy aroma.

Presentation:  
It was plated in a rustic ceramic bowl with the vegetables arranged in overlapping, colorful rows. A small plate held the creamy quinoa beside it, while feta, olives, and nuts decorated the rim. The panini was placed on top for dramatic contrast.

Occasion:  
The meal was prepared for Carlos’s group dinner, a gathering of friends and creative colleagues. He arranged the ingredients carefully, checking that every color and texture stood out, while his loud, expressive nature contributed an energetic sense of celebration to the table.
