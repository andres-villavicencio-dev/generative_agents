Carlos prepared Garden-Stuffed Potatoes with Roasted Tomato and Herb Sauce for dinner on Tuesday, February 14. The dish featured potatoes, roasted tomatoes, fresh basil, garlic, olive oil, smoked paprika, and a splash of white wine.

He tossed the potatoes with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then arranged them in a shallow pan. While they were roasting, he sautéed the tomatoes, garlic, and herbs in the pan until the sauce thickened and the tomatoes burst with their juices. He folded in the potatoes just before serving, tossing everything together and letting the warm sauce coat them.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of roasted potatoes, sweet tomato, and fragrant basil. The final presentation was simple and rustic: golden potatoes arranged around colorful tomatoes, garnished with fresh basil and a drizzle of olive oil. Carlos ate it quietly at the kitchen table, reflecting on the day’s creative work and the poems taking shape.
