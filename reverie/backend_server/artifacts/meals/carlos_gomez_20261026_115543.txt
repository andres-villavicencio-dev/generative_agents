Carlos Gomez prepared a hearty Sautéed Kale and Lemon Herb Chicken with Quinoa and Roasted Vegetables for a Wednesday lunch. He started by washing and chopping kale, carrots, onions, and bell peppers, then sautéed them in olive oil until the vegetables turned golden. Chicken breasts were marinated in lemon, garlic, oregano, and a splash of vinegar, then browned and finished with rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of salt. Quinoa was cooked separately and stirred in just before the chicken to retain its crispness.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of garlic, warm oil, and fresh herbs, while the kitchen counter smelled of lemon and roasted roots. Carlos arranged the colorful vegetables and fluffy white grain around a platter of golden chicken, garnishing it with a bright lemon wedge and chopped parsley. He plated the meal casually on a large white plate, using a rustic wooden spoon to serve a generous portion.

The occasion was a relaxed lunch at home, balancing his poetry collection and creative writing workshops with an informal meal. The warm, comforting food felt like a pause between his intense artistic work, while the rustic presentation reflected his unpretentious, grounded approach to life.
