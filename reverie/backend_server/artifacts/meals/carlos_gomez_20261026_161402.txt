Carlos Gomez prepared a Garden Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables, a simple, rustic dinner meant to honor the local community café and the old oak. He roasted a whole chicken with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and lemon, then pan-seared potatoes, carrots, and onions before serving. The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, citrus, rosemary, and warm herbs.

He arranged the chicken in the center of a wooden platter, surrounded by the golden vegetables and a small bowl of lemon-herb potatoes. The presentation was informal but deliberate, with the rustic dish taking center stage. It was part of a relaxed Wednesday dinner, after he had reviewed his poems and balanced his creative work with meaningful conversations. The meal was simple enough to share, yet expressive enough to reflect his growing interest in community projects and art.
