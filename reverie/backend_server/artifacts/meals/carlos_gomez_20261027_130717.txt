Carlos Gomez prepared a simple Herb-Crusted Chicken with Lemon Garlic Roast for his lunch, serving it alongside roasted vegetables and a bowl of cool black beans.

The chicken was seasoned with olive oil, dried oregano, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, and a pinch of smoked paprika. After the thighs and breasts were seared in a hot pan, they were transferred to a roasting pan and roasted at 425°F (220°C) until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The vegetables—carrots, onions, and potatoes—were roasted together until crisp and caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted garlic, herb-infused chicken, lemon, and warm vegetables, with a gentle fragrance of toasted bread and savory seasoning. He plated the meal on a white ceramic plate, arranging the chicken in the center and the vegetables around it, with a small bowl of black beans placed nearby.

The lunch was a relaxed, homemade dinner on a quiet Wednesday, after an eventful day balancing poetry, workshops, and meaningful community conversations.
