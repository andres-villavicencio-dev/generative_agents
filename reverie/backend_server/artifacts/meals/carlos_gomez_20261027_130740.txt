Carlos Gomez prepared a Fresh Herb-Lentil Breakfast Bowl, a simple yet nourishing lunch that reflected his thoughtful approach to cooking.

He washed his hands and gathered fresh kale, carrots, onions, garlic, lentils, and herbs such as parsley and thyme. He gently cooked the vegetables and lentils, then layered them in a bowl topped with a bright orange lemon-lime dressing and a drizzle of olive oil. He added a few whole grains and a splash of yogurt for creaminess.

The kitchen smelled of earthy greens, roasted garlic, and the warm aroma of simmering lentils. He presented the bowl in a rustic ceramic dish, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a slice of lemon before taking a small piece.

The occasion was a quiet Wednesday lunch—Carlos balancing poetry with his creative-writing workshops and reflecting on his community work. The meal was not elaborate, but its freshness and thoughtful preparation mirrored his desire to nourish both his body and his creative identity.
