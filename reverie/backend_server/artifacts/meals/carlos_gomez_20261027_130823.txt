Carlos prepared a Lemon Herb Roasted Vegetable Bowl as a simple, nourishing lunch for the afternoon. He roasted cherry tomatoes, red bell peppers, onions, and carrots with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, oregano, and a splash of white wine vinegar, then added fresh parsley and served them over quinoa.

The kitchen filled with the bright, citrusy aroma of roasted vegetables, garlic, and oregano. He arranged the colorful vegetables in a loose bowl, topped them with a generous portion of fluffy quinoa, and garnished the dish with lemon slices and a few whole parsley leaves.

The meal was casual and celebratory, taking place after Carlos’s productive Wednesday, when he had just finished a creative writing workshop. He treated the bowl to himself as a quiet moment of nourishment, balancing his poetic life with a warm, grounding lunch.
