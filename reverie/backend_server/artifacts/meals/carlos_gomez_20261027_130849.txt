Carlos prepared Mise en Place Potatoes for lunch. The dish featured potatoes, garlic, fresh herbs, lemon juice, olive oil, and a pinch of sea salt. He gently boiled the potatoes until they were tender, then sautéed them briefly and added chopped parsley and a splash of lemon juice to brighten the flavors.

The kitchen filled with a warm, earthy aroma of roasted garlic and caramelized onions, followed by the sharp burst of lemon. He arranged the potatoes on a white ceramic platter, garnished them with parsley, and topped them with a small drizzle of olive oil, presenting the meal like a quiet piece of poetry—simple, honest, and inviting.

It was a casual lunch during a productive Wednesday, as Carlos balanced creative work with his day. He ate it in the kitchen, pausing briefly to listen to his thoughts, while keeping in mind his community projects and meaningful conversations with Latoya Williams. The meal felt both nourishing and reflective, a simple moment of nourishment amid a busy life.
