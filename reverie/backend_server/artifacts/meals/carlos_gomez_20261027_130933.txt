Carlos Gomez prepared Sunrise Roasted Tomato and Herb Salad, reflecting the fresh, natural-world poems he was reviewing that morning. He combined ripe tomatoes, red onion, cucumber, basil, olives, and a hint of lemon with olive oil, salt, and a pinch of smoked paprika. He sliced the vegetables, tossed them gently in the bowl, then drizzled the finished dressing over the mixture.

The aroma was warm, savory, and slightly herbaceous—hints of tomato, garlic, basil, and smoke filling the kitchen. He arranged the salad on a white ceramic plate, garnishing it with fresh basil and a few crisp cucumber ribbons. The presentation was simple, clean, and inviting, making the salad the centerpiece of his Wednesday lunch. He prepared it for a quiet lunch while washing his hands and preparing a fresh meal, honoring the old oak and café by balancing his creative life with his community responsibilities.
