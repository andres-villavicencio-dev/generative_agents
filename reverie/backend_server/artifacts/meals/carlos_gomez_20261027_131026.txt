Carlos prepared a Savory Herb-Oven Roast with Roasted Root Vegetables, a warm, rustic lunch for his Wednesday afternoon. The dish featured thick-cut beef chuck, carrots, potatoes, celery, onions, garlic, fresh thyme, rosemary, smoked paprika, and olive oil, arranged on a large baking sheet and seasoned generously.

He seared the beef in a hot pan to develop a rich, caramelized crust, then transferred it to the oven along with the root vegetables. The carrots and potatoes softened to a tender, golden brown while the beef became juicy and flavorful. He turned off the oven, covered the dish loosely with foil, and let it rest for ten minutes.

The aroma was inviting and earthy: savory beef, caramelized onions, and herbs filled the kitchen with the scent of a slow, comforting roast. When he sliced and served the portions, the meat was tender and rich, the potatoes crisp and golden, and the vegetables held their color and sweetness. He arranged the meal on a white plate with a simple green herb garnish, presenting it for a quiet lunch while reflecting on his poetry, community projects, and meaningful conversation with Latoya Williams.
