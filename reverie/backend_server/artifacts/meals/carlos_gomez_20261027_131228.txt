Carlos prepared a Warm Roasted Vegetable and Herb Soup with Whole Grain Bread, a simple, comforting lunch that reflected the garden’s natural abundance. Key ingredients included carrots, celery, onions, garlic, fresh herbs, olive oil, lemon juice, vegetable broth, and whole-grain bread. He sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until softened, then added the broth and simmered until the carrots turned translucent and the soup was fragrant and creamy.

The aroma was earthy and warm—citrus, garlic, herbs, and the subtle sweetness of roasted carrots filled the kitchen. He arranged the bright orange vegetables, golden bread, and creamy broth in a shallow bowl, leaving a small spoonful of broth for himself. He ate it at home on Wednesday, February 15, a quiet moment of nourishment while balancing poetry, creative writing workshops, and his meaningful connection with Latoya Williams.
