Carlos Gomez prepared a Morning Herb-Herb Garden Breakfast, a warm, rustic brunch featuring eggs, oatmeal, whole-grain toast, sliced apples, walnuts, and a bright lemon-pepper dressing. He toasted the bread until it was crisp, scrambled the eggs with a little butter, and mixed the oatmeal with warm milk and cinnamon. For the dressing, he whisked lemon juice, olive oil, pepper, and chopped fresh thyme together until it had the consistency of a light sauce.

The meal had a warm, savory aroma of toasted bread, nutty oats, caramelized apples, and fragrant herbs. He arranged the oatmeal and toast on a wooden tray, placed the eggs and fruit beside it, and topped everything with a few fresh thyme leaves and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt. It was served for his early breakfast at home, before he continued reviewing his poems and preparing for community projects.
