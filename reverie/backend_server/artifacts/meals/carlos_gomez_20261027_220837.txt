On a quiet Wednesday morning, Carlos Gomez prepared a simple yet flavorful breakfast: Sourdough Toast with Fig and Honey, a dish rooted in his love of old oak trees and the café he often visits.

The sourdough was gently toasted on a panini press until its golden crust browned while the interior remained soft and slightly chewy. He sliced fresh figs in half and drizzled them over the toast, pairing them with generous honey and a pinch of sea salt. A sprig of thyme added a subtle aromatic finish.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of toasted bread, figs, honey, and thyme, creating a cozy, earthy morning atmosphere. He served the breakfast on a white plate with a small bowl of warm water and a lemon wedge, presenting it with quiet simplicity.

The occasion was a quiet moment of self-reflection, part of his Wednesday routine after reviewing the poems from his natural-world collection. It was neither a grand celebration nor a formal gathering, but a personal pause to nourish his body, mind, and imagination.
