Carlos Gomez prepared a simple Oatmeal with Berries and Warm Honey for his kitchen table. He used rolled oats, sliced apples, blueberries, strawberries, and a spoonful of honey, topped with a sprinkle of cinnamon and a few mint-chili chips. He cooked the oats in a small pan over medium heat, stirring the fruit and honey in until the apples softened and the mixture glazed. The aroma was warm, sweet, and subtly spicy, filling the quiet afternoon kitchen.

He arranged the warm oatmeal in a shallow ceramic dish and placed the fruit and chips on top in a loose, colorful mound. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the glossy honey contrasting against the bright berries and green mint. It was not a celebratory dinner, but an informal, nourishing meal meant to occupy the idle cooking area and provide a gentle distraction from his poetry collection and creative work.
