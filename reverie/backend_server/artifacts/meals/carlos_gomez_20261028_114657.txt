Carlos prepared a simple Olive-Herb Pan-Seared Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Basil Rice for a quiet Wednesday lunch. The centerpiece was a pan-seared chicken breast, seasoned with garlic, lemon, oregano, black pepper, and a touch of smoked paprika. He roasted red potatoes, carrots, and zucchini with olive oil, rosemary, and a little brown sugar until golden and tender, then finished the rice with sautéed spinach and fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and caramelized vegetables. Carlos arranged the chicken beside a platter of colorful vegetables and a small mound of fluffy rice, garnishing it with lemon slices and a sprig of rosemary. The meal was simple, elegant, and meant to feel like a small gathering, though Carlos ate it alone. It was a quiet lunch on Wednesday, February 15, when he was balancing his poetry collection with creative writing workshops while honoring the old oak and café display through community projects.
