Carlos prepared a simple Garden-Root Salad Bowl for his lunch. The key ingredients were a crisp mix of romaine lettuce, red onion, cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, radishes, and a handful of crunchy almonds, with a dressing made from lemon-lime juice, olive oil, and a pinch of sea salt.

He washed his hands first, then chopped the vegetables and sliced the radishes into thin strips. He tossed everything in a large bowl, drizzled the dressing over it, and added the almonds for texture. He arranged the colorful salad on a white ceramic platter, garnishing it with a sprig of fresh mint and a few whole almonds.

The room was filled with the aroma of lemon, onion, and earthy greens. The presentation was clean and vibrant, reflecting Carlos’s interest in nature and creativity. This was part of his creative-writing workshop, where he was experimenting with fresh, sensory ingredients in his poetry.
