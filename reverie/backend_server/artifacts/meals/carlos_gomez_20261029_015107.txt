Carlos Gomez prepared Sesame-Tahini Lemon Chicken with Roasted Asparagus. He used boneless chicken thighs marinated in ginger, garlic, lemon juice, soy sauce, and sesame oil, then roasted until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. Alongside the chicken, he roasted asparagus with lemon and garlic, and prepared a creamy lemon-tahini dip with toasted pumpkin seeds.

The dish had a warm, aromatic aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, toasted sesame, and earthy asparagus. He arranged the chicken and vegetables in a shallow white bowl, topped each piece with a generous portion of the golden-hued dip, and garnished them with a sprig of parsley and a few sesame seeds. He plated it for Sam’s first-customer coffee, intending to offer it as a simple, nourishing dinner while he continued developing his poetry collection.
