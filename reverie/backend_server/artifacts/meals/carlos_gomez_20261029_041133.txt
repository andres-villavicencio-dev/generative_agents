Carlos Gomez prepared Savory Herb-Topped Quinoa and Vegetable Salad, a simple, nourishing breakfast to keep him focused on organizing his writing materials. The dish featured quinoa, kale, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, feta cheese, olives, and a lemon-herb vinaigrette.

In a large skillet, he toasted the quinoa with olive oil, garlic, and oregano until lightly browned. He then sautéed the kale, cucumber, tomatoes, and onion, seasoning it with salt, pepper, and a splash of white wine. A generous portion of feta was stirred in, followed by the dressing.

As he plated it, the aroma of toasted quinoa, fresh herbs, and savory cheese filled the kitchen. The salad was arranged in a white bowl, with the colorful vegetables, creamy feta, and glossy dressing creating a bright, inviting presentation. He prepared it for a quiet, productive morning—a simple, grounding meal before his demanding workday.
