Carlos prepared Tomato-Herb Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Garlic Rice. He roasted a whole chicken with skin-on skin-on, then sliced it and cooked it with a mixture of garlic, olive oil, fresh rosemary, lemon zest, and a touch of honey. The rice was seasoned with the same aromatics and finished with toasted pine nuts and a squeeze of lemon.

The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of roasted garlic and herbs, with lemon and rosemary adding a bright, citrusy aroma. He plated the chicken over the rice, arranging the golden slices, crisp nuts, and bright green herbs around the dish. A small bowl of lemon-herb vinaigrette and a side of warm vegetables were added for contrast.

On Wednesday, February 15, the meal served as a quiet, structured way to transition from a demanding day into the focused creative sessions he planned.
