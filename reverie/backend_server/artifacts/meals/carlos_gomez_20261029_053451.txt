Carlos prepared a Garlic-Herb Roast Chicken with Lemon-Basil Rice and Root Vegetables, a simple yet hearty weekday lunch. He took a chicken from the refrigerator, seasoned it with garlic, lemon, olive oil, and herbs, and roasted it until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The remaining vegetables—carrots, potatoes, and onions—were similarly seasoned and roasted with the chicken. He served the chicken alongside a fragrant rice dish, garnished with fresh parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the sharp scent of garlic, warm herbs, and the savory sweetness of caramelized vegetables. The golden-brown chicken stood proudly in the center of the plate, looking ready to be shared. The presentation was casual and practical, matching the relaxed Wednesday afternoon, when Carlos had no confirmed plans and was simply enjoying his meal alone.
