Carlos prepared a Lemon-Garlic Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa, using mostly leftover chicken, broccoli, carrots, zucchini, and a few mushrooms. He sautéed the chicken with garlic, olive oil, lemon zest, and a splash of soy sauce, then roasted the vegetables with a little honey and paprika until they turned golden. Quinoa, rinsed and cooked briefly, was layered on top.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of lemon and garlic, the smoky heat from the roasted veggies, and the nutty scent of the quinoa. He plated the meal simply: bright green broccoli, orange carrots, translucent zucchini, and a creamy lemon-garlic dressing poured over the quinoa. The chicken was arranged neatly beside it, with a small side of chopped parsley.

It was prepared quickly for an unannounced dinner at home, giving Carlos time to focus on his poetry drafts and creative projects while using the unused cooking area to make the most of the remaining ingredients.
