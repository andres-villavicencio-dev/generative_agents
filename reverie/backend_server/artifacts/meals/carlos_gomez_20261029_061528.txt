Carlos prepared a simple, rustic lunch of pan-seared chicken with roasted vegetables and a lemon-herb sauce. The chicken was seasoned with garlic, olive oil, salt, and black pepper, then pan-seared to golden-brown crisp edges. He roasted carrots, bell peppers, and onions in the same pan until they softened and caramelized, then added chicken to the reserved fat and a squeeze of lemon juice to finish cooking.

The aroma was warm and comforting—garlic, herbs, roasted vegetables, and the subtle scent of lemon filling the kitchen. He plated the meal simply: the pan of chicken and vegetables, a small bowl of lemon-herb sauce, and a side of warm rice. The presentation was rustic and practical, with food arranged attractively on a white plate and a simple napkin.

The occasion was a quiet Wednesday lunch while he listened to an audiobook. It was not a celebratory meal, but a practical pause between a demanding day of writing and other commitments.
