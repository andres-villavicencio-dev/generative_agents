Carlos prepared Garden Breakfast Bowl with Eggs, Avocado, and Toast, a simple, nourishing meal fitting a lazy Thursday morning. The bowl held two cooked eggs, sliced avocado, fresh orange segments, and shredded carrots, all topped with toasted sourdough and a drizzle of olive oil. He prepared the eggs in a skillet over medium heat, then stirred them until the whites were set before serving. The avocado was sliced and added fresh, while orange zest and lemon juice brightened the earthy flavors.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of toasted bread, warm butter, roasted vegetables, and the fresh scent of citrus. He arranged the colorful ingredients in a rustic bowl, presenting the meal in a clean white dish with the toast and avocado arranged beside a small plate of fruit. This was a casual breakfast intended to restore his energy without adding much to the day’s schedule.
