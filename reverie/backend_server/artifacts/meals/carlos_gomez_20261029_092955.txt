Carlos prepared Pan con Queso de Aguacate for breakfast—a warm, comforting dish combining ripe avocado, crisp lettuce, cherry tomatoes, and a generous slice of melted cheddar, all served with toasted sourdough.

The ingredients were simple yet carefully arranged: two large yellow avocados sliced into thick rounds, a handful of crisp romaine lettuce, red and green tomatoes, a handful of sunflower seeds, and a pinch of dried oregano. Carlos toasted the sourdough in a pan, then arranged the avocado rounds and vegetables in a shallow bowl, drizzing them with a little olive oil and lemon juice. He covered the pan and let it warm, watching the avocado soften while the cheddar melted into a golden, creamy sauce.

The aroma was unmistakable: the savory richness of the cheese, the bright, buttery scent of the avocado, and the subtle earthiness of the oregano and tomatoes filled the kitchen. He plated it in a white ceramic bowl, letting the colorful vegetables peek over the edge so the presentation felt casual and inviting.

It was not a celebratory occasion, but a quiet, self-care moment before his morning routine. For Carlos, preparing the meal gave him a small sense of control, a chance to slow down, and a reminder that even simple meals can make his busy days feel meaningful.
