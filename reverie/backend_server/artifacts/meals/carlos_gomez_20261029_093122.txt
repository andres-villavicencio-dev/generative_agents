Carlos prepared Breakfast for Two, a simple, nourishing panini-style meal intended to start his workday. He combined whole-wheat bread, sliced eggs, tomatoes, onions, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and chives. The eggs were scrambled in a lightly sautéed pan until just set, then arranged on top of the bread and toasted until the golden crust formed. A pinch of lemon juice and fresh herbs were sprinkled over the finished panini.

He made it at home in the kitchen, using a few grocery bags of produce and pantry staples he had purchased. The aroma was warm and earthy—herbs, toasted bread, garlic, and a hint of lemon. He presented the panini on a white plate, with the eggs and vegetables arranged neatly beside it. It was prepared as a practical, comforting breakfast for a quiet Thursday morning, though Carlos did not share it with anyone.
