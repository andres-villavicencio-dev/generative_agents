Carlos prepared a Garden-Soup Bowl with Roasted Potatoes and Lemon-Herb Crust, a simple, nourishing breakfast meant to balance his work, civic service, and quiet moments of reflection. The meal featured roasted potatoes, carrots, celery, onions, and fresh spinach, with chicken broth, garlic, and a little olive oil to soften the textures. He toasted the potatoes with salt and pepper, then sautéed the vegetables in a little oil until crisp, and let the broth simmer until the vegetables were tender.

He finished it with a lemon-herb crust—browned shallots, parsley, and a pinch of garlic—then sliced the potatoes, carrots, and spinach. The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant, with hints of garlic, onions, and toasted potato skin carrying through the kitchen.

He arranged the colorful bowl in the cart, grouping the vegetables by color and placing the golden potatoes toward the front. The presentation was bright and inviting, with the lemon zest catching the morning light. It was a quiet, unhurried breakfast, prepared for his Thursday morning routine before he headed into the city to balance his busy days with meaningful civic service.
