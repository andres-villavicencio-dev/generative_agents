Carlos prepared Breakfast Pot Pies, a hearty, one-pan meal that felt like a quiet rebellion against the usual busy morning routine.

The key ingredients were eggs, sour cream, diced ham, shredded cheddar, and caramelized onions. He also made the filling from scratch, including butter, garlic, flour, and a pinch of nutmeg, while adding a splash of milk to keep the eggs fluffy.

For the preparation, Carlos placed the eggs, ham, cheddar, onions, and milk in a skillet and heated everything over medium heat. He gently folded the eggs into the filling, covered the pan, and let the pot pies simmer until the eggs were just set and the filling was bubbly. A little lemon juice and a final pinch of salt gave the meal a bright finish.

The aroma rose from the pan, strong and comforting with the scent of onions, garlic, butter, and caramelized sugar. He plated the golden pot pies on a white plate, arranging them in neat rows and drizzling them with a little lemon zest. The presentation was simple and rustic, with a small wooden spoon resting beside the pies, and the warm, inviting light of the kitchen made the breakfast feel like a small celebration.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday morning. Carlos chose the meal to pause the hectic rhythm of his schedule and give himself space to connect with food, companionship, and the peace he sought after a long day of work and civic service.
