Carlos Gomez prepared a simple Cilantro-Lime Roast Chickpea Salad with Warm Toast, a quick, refreshing lunch after his coffee-making session.

He toasted a slice of whole-grain bread, sliced a ripe tomato and cucumber, and roasted two chickpeas with garlic, olive oil, and lemon until golden and slightly crispy. He tossed the vegetables and chickpeas with a lemon-herb dressing, then arranged the colorful salad over the toasted bread. He added a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil and a few fresh mint leaves for a final pop of brightness.

The meal smelled warmly earthy and bright, with the savory aroma of roasted chickpeas, garlic, and lemon filling the kitchen. It was presented on a white ceramic plate, with the crisp chickpeas contrasting against the soft bread and green vegetables. It was a simple, nourishing lunch for a busy Thursday, intended to be a quick break before checking his finances and planning the weekend.
