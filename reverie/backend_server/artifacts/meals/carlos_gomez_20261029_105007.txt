Carlos Gomez prepared a Simple Black-Fermented Rice Bowl with Tomato-Lemon Vinaigrette. The dish featured jasmine rice, black beans, cucumber, radish, toasted almonds, and a bright dressing made from lemon juice, vinegar, garlic, onion, and a little olive oil.

He cooked the rice and beans separately, then toasted the nuts and sliced the vegetables. He tossed everything with the lemony vinaigrette before serving it in a shallow white bowl with a small plate of toasted almonds and a few fresh herbs. The aroma was warm, earthy, and slightly tangy—a comforting mix of toasted grains, fermented beans, citrus, and savory spices.

He prepared it for a quiet Thursday breakfast after a long day of writing and civic service. The meal felt simple but nourishing, balancing his work and civic responsibilities with a moment of nourishment and reflection.
