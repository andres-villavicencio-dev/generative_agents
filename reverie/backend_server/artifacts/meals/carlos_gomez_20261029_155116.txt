For Friday’s relaxed meal, Carlos prepared Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables. He used a whole chicken, lemon, garlic, rosemary, oregano, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a splash of red wine. The chicken was marinated in the refrigerator, then pan-seared until the skin turned golden and crisp. Meanwhile, carrots, potatoes, and onions were roasted with garlic and herbs until tender and caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, rosemary, and savory herbs. Carlos arranged the golden chicken on a platter alongside the vegetables, garnishing it with parsley and drizzling a little extra lemon juice over the top. He served it with a simple lemon-herb sauce, letting the bright acidity balance the rich chicken.

It was a simple yet comforting dinner, made without any time pressure. Carlos ate it slowly during a quiet evening, surrounded by the sounds of the house and the peaceful rhythm he was creating for himself. The meal felt more connected than the demanding poetry he had been working on, offering him a moment of rest and nourishment.
