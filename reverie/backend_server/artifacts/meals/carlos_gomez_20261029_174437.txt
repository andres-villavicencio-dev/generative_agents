Carlos prepared Roasted Vegetable and Herb Pan-Seared Chicken with Quinoa.

Key ingredients: chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, onions, garlic, thyme, rosemary, lemon, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a few sprigs of fresh parsley.

Preparation: He seared the chicken in a skillet until golden, then transferred it to a baking dish. The vegetables were roasted with the chicken until tender and caramelized, while the quinoa was cooked separately. He tossed the chicken and vegetables with the herbs, lemon juice, and a splash of white wine to release their flavors.

Aroma: Warm, savory, and herbaceous—dominating notes of roasted garlic, thyme, rosemary, and slightly sweet citrus.

Presentation: He arranged the pan-seared chicken and vegetables on a wooden cutting board, garnished with parsley, and served the quinoa in a small bowl beside it. He plated a simple green salad with a lemon-herb vinaigrette.

Occasion: A quiet Friday dinner after spending a demanding, meaningful day exploring his poetry.
