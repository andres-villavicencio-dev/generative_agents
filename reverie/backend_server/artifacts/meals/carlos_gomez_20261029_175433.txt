Carlos Gomez prepared a Roasted Vegetable Quinoa Salad with Lemon-Herb Dressing. The salad featured quinoa, roasted tomatoes, zucchini, red bell peppers, and fennel, all lightly roasted with olive oil, garlic, thyme, and a pinch of smoked paprika. He toasted the quinoa separately and dressed it with the bright, zesty dressing.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted vegetables, toasted nuts, and fresh herbs. The presentation was simple and colorful: the golden quinoa and crisp vegetables arranged in a shallow bowl, with the dressing drizzled around the edges and a small lemon wedge beside it.

The meal served him a few minutes of relief from Friday’s demanding creative rhythm. It was a quiet, nourishing lunch, a small moment of rest before winding down for his evening movie.
