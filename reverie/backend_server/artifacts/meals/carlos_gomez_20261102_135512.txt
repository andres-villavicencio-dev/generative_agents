Carlos Gomez prepared a Savory Potato and Herb Skillet for lunch. The dish combines boiled potatoes, sliced carrots, diced onion, garlic, and red pepper flakes with a generous amount of olive oil, fresh parsley, lemon juice, and a pinch of smoked paprika. He stirred the vegetables in a heavy-bottomed skillet until they softened, then added the potatoes and covered the pan to let the flavors meld.

The aroma was warm and earthy, with the sharp fragrance of garlic, herbs, and the faint smoke of the peppers filling the kitchen. He sliced the potatoes into uniform chunks, arranged them around the pan, and topped each with a bright lemon wedge, a sprig of parsley, and a small dried herb.

The meal was presented as a cozy, homey lunch, suitable for a quiet Saturday afternoons during the week. Although it was an ordinary meal, it felt purposeful and connected to Carlos’s desire to nourish his body while maintaining a balanced rhythm. It was a simple comfort meal with enough substance to satisfy him without feeling rushed.
