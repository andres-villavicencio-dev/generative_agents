Carlos Gomez prepared a Bread-Egg Breakfast Plate, combining fresh eggs with a slice of whole-grain bread, seasonal vegetables, and a small serving of sour cream. He measured the eggs carefully and counted his bread before gathering the remaining ingredients, ensuring each portion was precise and balanced.

The dish was prepared in a small skillet: the eggs were poached or lightly scrambled, then arranged alongside the warmed bread and vegetables, which he toasted briefly to add texture. A generous drizzle of olive oil, lemon zest, and fresh herbs gave the meal a warm, bright aroma that filled the small kitchen. The presentation was simple and rustic: the bread and eggs sat in the center of a white plate, with the vegetables scattered around and the sour cream placed in a small ramekin beside them.

The occasion was a quiet Saturday breakfast. Carlos wanted the meal to feel restful and connected, offering a small, nourishing meal for himself and a quiet moment of purposefulness before continuing his creative work.
