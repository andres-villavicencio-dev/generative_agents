Carlos Gomez prepared a hearty Black Bean and Kale Stir-Fry for a family dinner. He sliced two cups of black beans, chopped two cups of kale, and added a handful of fresh red onion, garlic, and a tablespoon of olive oil. He sautéed the vegetables until crisp, then added the beans and a pinch of cumin, letting the spices warm up without burning. A splash of soy sauce and a few drops of chili oil added a subtle tang, while a drizzle of sesame oil made the aroma of toasted beans, warm garlic, and savory earthiness fill the kitchen.

He served the dish in a wide, shallow pan with a bright, colorful presentation, garnished with a few fresh cilantro leaves and a squeeze of lime. The meal was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday evening, but Carlos’s loud, expressive nature made it feel like a spirited family gathering. His purposeful approach turned a simple grocery trip into a meaningful occasion, and the finished meal gave him and his household something to celebrate.
