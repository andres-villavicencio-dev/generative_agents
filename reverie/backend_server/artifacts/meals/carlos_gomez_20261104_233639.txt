Carlos prepared Grilled Chicken with Lemon Herb Panini for his Tuesday lunch. He used a whole chicken breast, sliced red onion, cherry tomatoes, garlic, fresh parsley, lemon juice, olive oil, thyme, and a pinch of red pepper flakes. The chicken was marinated briefly, then pan-seared until golden brown and juicy, while the vegetables were crisp and bright. Carlos toasted the panini with a slice of sourdough and served it with a side of crisp salad and a glass of sparkling water.

The aroma was sharp and aromatic—garlic, herbs, lemon zest, and toasted bread filled the kitchen. He plated the panini in a wide white bowl, arranging the chicken at the center with the vegetables in neat rows and garnishing it with a small sprig of parsley. The meal was simple, colorful, and purposeful, reflecting Carlos’s focus on quality ingredients and careful presentation. It matched the calm, productive mood of his Tuesday routine.
