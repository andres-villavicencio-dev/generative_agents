Carlos prepared a simple Garlic Herb Chicken Stir-Fry with Rice for his Tuesday evening meal. The dish featured chicken thighs sautéed in olive oil, sliced onions, garlic, ginger, and chopped chives, then tossed with a small handful of broccoli, carrots, and snap peas. He added a splash of low-sodium soy sauce, a teaspoon of sesame oil, and a pinch of black pepper before tossing everything together and cooking it over a medium-high heat for about eight minutes.

The kitchen smelled of savory garlic, roasted chicken, toasted sesame oil, and lightly steamed vegetables. The presentation was bright and informal: the golden chicken was arranged alongside fluffy white rice in a small ceramic bowl, with colorful broccoli, carrots, and snap peas scattered across the side. A few lemon wedges and a sprig of fresh thyme rested nearby.

On Tuesday, February 14, the meal was a quick, inexpensive dinner for Carlos and his friends during a casual community gathering. He prepared it in the spare time before cleaning and organizing the apartment, showing both the practical side of cooking and his relaxed, community-oriented rhythm.
