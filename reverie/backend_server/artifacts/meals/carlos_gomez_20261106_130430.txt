Carlos Gomez prepared Savory Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for a relaxed Tuesday dinner. He seasoned chicken breasts with garlic, lemon, oregano, and black pepper, then seared them until golden. He roasted carrots, onions, and bell peppers until crisp-tender, then arranged them around the chicken.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of toasted garlic and citrusy herbs, while Carlos stirred the pan and checked that every pot and pan was ready. The meal was presented in a simple white bowl, with the colorful vegetables adding a bright contrast to the pale chicken. It was a casual, comforting dinner, probably meant for a quiet evening with friends after his creative writing workshop.
