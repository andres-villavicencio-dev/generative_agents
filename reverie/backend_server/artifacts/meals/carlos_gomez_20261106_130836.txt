Carlos Gomez prepared Garlic-Lemon Herb Roast Chicken for Tuesday, February 14. He seasoned the chicken with fresh garlic, lemon zest, olive oil, salt, pepper, and dried thyme, then placed it on the stove to roast until the meat was golden and the skin was crisp. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted garlic, citrus, and warm herbs, especially noticeable when the door opened.

After the main dish, he served the chicken with roasted potatoes and green beans, garnished with parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice. The meal was simple, colorful, and warmly presented on a white plate, with the golden chicken standing prominently beside the vegetables.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday evening meal after a full day of creative writing workshops, community bulletin board check, and organizing. Carlos prepared the food with pride, enjoying the comforting scent of dinner while continuing to contribute to his creative community. It was a simple, meaningful meal that reflected the warmth he hoped to share with his friends.
