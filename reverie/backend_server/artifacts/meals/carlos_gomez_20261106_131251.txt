Carlos prepared a hearty Rustic Tomato-Herb Stew with Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple, comforting meal suited for a Tuesday after a long creative routine. The base consisted of diced potatoes, carrots, parsnips, and onions, seasoned with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a splash of lemon. He stirred the vegetables into a simmering broth of beef stock and tomato sauce, letting them cook until the potatoes became tender and the sauce reduced to a rich, glossy consistency.

The aroma of roasted garlic, earthy root vegetables, and savory herbs rose warmly from the pan, while a few sprigs of parsley were added at the end to brighten the dish. Carlos plated the stew in a shallow dish, arranging the vegetables and potato chunks around the center and garnishing it with lemon wedges and a sprinkle of paprika. The meal was not a special occasion, but a practical, nourishing dinner he prepared in a relaxed, homey style—reflecting his focus on nourishment, community, and the creative life he maintains around him.
