Carlos prepared Roasted Garlic Herb Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice for dinner. The dish had plenty of roasted chicken breasts, garlic, lemon, thyme, rosemary, parsley, and a creamy lemon-herb pan sauce. He toasted the garlic and onions in the pan, then braised the chicken until the skin was crispy and the meat was tender. He transferred the chicken to a clean plate and carefully removed it from the pan, leaving the flavorful rice behind. The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and warm lemon. He plated the chicken and rice separately, garnishing the chicken with fresh parsley and lemon slices. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the golden chicken and glossy rice arranged on a white plate.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday dinner, likely part of Carlos’s evening routine around 5:00 p.m. It was neither a celebratory meal nor a special family gathering—it was just a practical, satisfying way to end his day.
