Carlos Gomez prepared a rustic Sunday Pot Roast with roasted root vegetables and a savory lemon-herb sauce.

He sliced a 3-pound beef roast, seared it quickly to develop a golden crust, then finished it in the oven with carrots, potatoes, onions, and celery. The vegetables were roasted until tender, while the meat rested before he sliced it against the grain. A thick pan sauce reduced the pan juices with garlic, onions, butter, thyme, lemon zest, and a splash of wine.

When he plated the meal, he arranged the sliced roast alongside the roasted vegetables, topped it with a generous drizzle of the glossy sauce, and served a side of crusty bread and a small bowl of warm salad. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted beef, caramelized onions, garlic, thyme, and citrus. It was a quiet Tuesday evening celebration of his own cooking, a simple but satisfying meal he prepared to enjoy with a friend after working through his creative writing workshops.
