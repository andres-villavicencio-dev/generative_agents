Carlos prepared a Savory Herb-Stuffed Chicken with Roasted Potatoes for his morning meal. The dish featured chicken thighs stuffed with a mixture of cooked spinach, garlic, onions, and Parmesan cheese, while golden potatoes were roasted with rosemary and thyme.

He gathered the chicken, potatoes, herbs, and cheese on the kitchen counter, placing the stuffed bird in a shallow baking pan and arranging the potatoes beside it. The oven was set to 400°F, and he basted the chicken with melted butter and lemon juice while the potatoes turned crisp and caramelized.

As soon as it was done, Carlos plated the meal and served it with a side of orange juice and a small salad. The house filled with the warm, fragrant aroma of roasted potatoes, savory herbs, garlic, and cheesy goodness. It was an informal Tuesday breakfast, but the meal felt surprisingly indulgent and satisfying.
