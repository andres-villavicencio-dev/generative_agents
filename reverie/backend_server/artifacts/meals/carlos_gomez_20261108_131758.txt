On a Monday morning, Carlos prepared Toasted Bread with Scrambled Eggs and Lemon-Herb Butter, a simple but hearty breakfast that complemented his poetic day. He toasted slices of sourdough until the crust was golden, then scrambled the eggs with fresh chives, garlic, and a generous drizzle of lemon-herb butter. The mixture was folded gently to keep the yolks rich and the whites tender.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of toasted nuts, garlic, lemon zest, and the rich scent of buttered eggs. He served the eggs in a shallow pan beside the bread, garnishing each with a tiny sprig of chive and a few crumbled almonds.

The occasion was a quiet morning routine before heading into creative workshops. Though Carlos was naturally loud and sometimes rude, the meal was a deliberate pause to reconnect with his senses. Afterward, he sat down to read his poetry collection, finding inspiration in the natural beauty he had prepared.
