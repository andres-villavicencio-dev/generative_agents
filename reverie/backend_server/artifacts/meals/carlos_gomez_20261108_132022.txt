Carlos prepared Breakfast Plate of Morning Poems, combining eggs, oatmeal, toast, and pancakes into a colorful spread for the early Monday.

The key ingredients included scrambled eggs, oatmeal with honey and cinnamon, toasted bread, and fluffy pancakes. He used butter and olive oil to lightly brown the eggs and toast the bread, then cooked the oatmeal until it set. The pancakes were lightly pan-fried on one side, leaving their edges soft and golden.

As he worked, the kitchen filled with a rich, savory aroma of toasted grains and warm custard, complemented by the subtle sweetness of honey and the faint scent of cinnamon. He arranged the breakfast on a large white plate, separating the dishes with wooden spoons and placing a small card with the title “Morning Poems” beside the oatmeal.

The meal was a quiet, intimate breakfast for a rainy Monday morning. It reflected Carlos’s creative process—the orderly, comforting rhythm of cooking mirrored his careful approach to writing, while the loud, colorful presentation suggested his expressive, sometimes abrasive side.
