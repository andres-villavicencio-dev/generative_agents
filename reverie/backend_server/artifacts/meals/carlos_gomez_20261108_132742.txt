Carlos prepared a hearty Savory Mushroom and Herb Omelet with Toasted Whole Grains, which became the centerpiece of his Monday morning routine. He sliced cremini mushrooms, sliced red onion, and chopped garlic into a skillet, then sautéed them until golden and fragrant. Adding egg whites and whole-grain flour, he whisked everything together and cooked it over medium heat until the edges were crisp and the center set. A final drizzle of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and black pepper completed the dish.

The aroma was earthy and savory, with hints of garlic, herbs, and toasted grains filling the kitchen. He arranged the omelet in a shallow bowl, topped it with toasted bread and sliced mushrooms, and placed a small bowl of yogurt nearby. The meal was simple and nourishing, prepared for his early breakfast and morning routine.

On February 13, Carlos began this meal with intention. While the kitchen filled with the scent of cooking, his mind wandered through his poetry collection. He wanted to turn his morning into a moment of reflection, using the food to ground his thoughts about nature, creativity, and the quiet beauty of everyday life.
