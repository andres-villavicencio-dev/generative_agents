On Monday, February 13, Carlos prepared Sun-Dried Tomato and Herb Pancakes with a savory Avocado Toast and Garlic-Butter Egg for breakfast. He used two cups of high-quality wheat flour, two eggs, ripe avocados, cherry tomatoes, garlic, fresh herbs, and a handful of sun-dried tomatoes, along with olive oil, salt, pepper, and lemon juice.

He toasted the flour with olive oil until lightly golden, then folded in the eggs, tomatoes, and herbs before baking the pancakes for a few minutes. The pan was buttered and topped with the golden, herb-flecked pancakes and a generous slice of toast. He added a poached egg with a few chopped sun-dried tomatoes and sprinkled it with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of toasted bread, garlic, herbs, and ripe avocados. He plated the breakfast in a simple white bowl, arranging the pancakes, toast, and egg in an inviting, asymmetrical pattern, leaving room for his coffee. It was a casual, nourishing breakfast suitable for an early start, though Carlos didn’t eat the entire meal—he finished most of it while doing his morning routine from 8:30 to 9:00 am.
