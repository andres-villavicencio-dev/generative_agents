Carlos Gomez prepared a rustic Roasted Tomato, Herb, and Olive Salad with Crispy Chickpeas. The dish combined heirloom tomatoes, red onion, Kalamata olives, feta cheese, fresh basil, and lemon zest, with chickpeas roasted until their skins were slightly browned and firm. He tossed them in the pan with garlic, olive oil, and a splash of red wine, then added the vegetables and chickpeas for a final few minutes to preserve their crisp textures and bright flavors.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of warm roasted tomatoes, garlic, fresh herbs, and toasted chickpeas. The presentation was simple and earthy: the colorful salad was arranged on a white plate, garnished with a sprig of basil and a few extra olives. The meal was a relaxed lunch for a productive Monday, as Carlos worked on his poetry collection while preparing it at home.
