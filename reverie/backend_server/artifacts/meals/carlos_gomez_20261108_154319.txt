On Monday, February 13, Carlos Gomez prepared a simple dinner of Herb-Lemon Roasted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables. The chicken was seasoned with garlic, lemon, olive oil, and dried thyme, while carrots, potatoes, and onions were roasted with cumin and paprika until golden and tender. He arranged the golden chicken at the center of a platter, surrounded by crisp roasted vegetables, and served the leftover pan juices over the potatoes for extra richness.

The aroma was warm and inviting: toasted garlic, caramelized onions, lemon zest, and the subtle smoke of the oven filled the kitchen before he even turned on the light. He plated the meal with an elegant rustic presentation, using a matte ceramic bowl and a silver spoon to give it a refined, almost ceremonial look.

This was a quiet evening after an intense creative-writing workshop, with the instructor outlining the day’s purpose and guidelines. Carlos ate slowly, listening to the instructor’s instructions, enjoying the comfort of a familiar meal while still working on his collection of poetry.
