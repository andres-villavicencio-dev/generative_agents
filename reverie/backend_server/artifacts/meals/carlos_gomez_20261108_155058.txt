Carlos Gomez prepared Smoked-Salt Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Herbs, a simple yet rustic dinner for his creative-writing workshop. He seasoned the chicken with smoked paprika, garlic, onion powder, dried thyme, salt, and cracked black pepper, then marinated it overnight. After washing the vegetables, he roasted carrots, potatoes, and green beans in a pan with olive oil, salt, and a splash of lemon juice until they were tender and lightly caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, savory herbs, and the faint smoke from the chicken. When he plated the dish, he arranged the golden vegetables around the white chicken, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a small lemon wedge. Carlos served it at the workshop, where he was taking a reflective reading from the instructor’s material. The meal felt informal and intimate, a quiet contrast to the loud, rude, and often toxic energy Carlos was accustomed to bringing into the room.
