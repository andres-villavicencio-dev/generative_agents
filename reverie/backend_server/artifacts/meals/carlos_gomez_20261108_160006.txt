At 5:00 p.m., Carlos Gomez sat down at his table for dinner after a long day at the creative writing workshop. He prepared a simple yet flavorful skillet dinner: Roasted Tomato, Garlic, and Herb Chicken with Lemon-Herb Risotto.  

The chicken was sliced and coated in olive oil, dried oregano, garlic, salt, and pepper, then roasted until golden and tender. The risotto used Arborio rice, vegetable broth, parmesan cheese, and chopped parsley, with the heat and constant stirring slowly softening the grains.  

As he stirred the pan, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, warm herbs, citrus zest, and savory chicken. The final presentation was elegant: the chicken rested alongside the creamy risotto, garnished with a bright lemon wedge and finely chopped parsley. He served it with crusty bread for dipping.

The occasion was quiet and reflective. Having finished the workshop, Carlos used the dinner as a moment to pause, reflect on the ideas he had developed, and jot down useful examples for his collection of poetry exploring the natural world.
