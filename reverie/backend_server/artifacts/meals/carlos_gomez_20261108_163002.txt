Carlos Gomez prepared Garden Herb Roast Chicken for dinner on a cold Monday evening. He bought chicken thighs, garlic, lemon, rosemary, thyme, red pepper flakes, olive oil, and a handful of fresh herbs from his local grocery. He seasoned the chicken with garlic, herbs, and pepper, then roasted it skin-side down until the skin turned golden and the juices ran clear. He finished it with lemon juice, a splash of water to loosen the skin, and a final garnish of chopped parsley.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm herbs, roasted garlic, and citrus. The chicken was served in a shallow white baking dish, plump and golden, with the herbs and lemon zest scattered over the top. It was ready for dinner at 5:00 p.m. Carlos ate it at home while working on his poetry collection, using the clean cooking area to avoid waste. After a brief drink of water, he returned home around 10 p.m.
