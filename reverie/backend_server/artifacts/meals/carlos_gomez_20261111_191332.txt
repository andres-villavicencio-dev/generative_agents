On Tuesday, February 14, Carlos Gomez prepared Grilled Chicken with Herb-Lemon Butter and Roasted Vegetables, a simple dinner for himself.

The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, lemon zest, olive oil, and the savory aroma of chicken skin. He used a whole chicken, chicken breasts, lemon halves, fresh thyme, rosemary, garlic, olive oil, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, and onions. He marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, and herbs, then roasted it until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. The vegetables were tossed in the same pan with more garlic, salt, and pepper until they had charred slightly.

For the presentation, he arranged the chicken and vegetables on a white plate, garnished with a sprig of rosemary, a lemon wedge, and a few roasted cherry tomatoes. The presentation was bright, rustic, and balanced—rich orange and green tones surrounding a creamy, aromatic chicken.

He made the meal while checking his grocery list and selecting a basket of affordable produce, choosing chicken, vegetables, and pantry staples that would satisfy his hunger without stretching his budget. The occasion was an informal Tuesday dinner, not a special celebration, yet the simple preparation reflected Carlos’s creative nature: quiet, flavorful, and focused on nourishment while he continued exploring his poetry.
