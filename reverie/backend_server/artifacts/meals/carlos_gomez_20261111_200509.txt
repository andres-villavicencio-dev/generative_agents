Carlos Gomez prepared a Pan-Seared Salmon with Asparagus and Lemon Herb for breakfast. He used fresh salmon fillets, asparagus spears, lemon wedges, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and parsley. The salmon was pan-seared until golden and flaky, while the asparagus was lightly cooked until crisp-tender, with the herbs added at the end so they remained bright and fragrant.

He arranged the fish in a shallow serving dish, topped it with the bright green asparagus, and drizzled the remaining lemon juice over the top. The presentation was simple yet elegant: the golden-orange fish, crisp white asparagus, and fresh green herbs created a colorful contrast, while the translucent lemon juice added a subtle shine.

The aroma was unmistakably fresh and savory—smoky from the garlic and fish, bright with the lemon, and lightly earthy from the herbs. The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday breakfast at home, before Carlos continued refining his poetry collection and attending nature-focused workshops.
