Meal: “Morning Gold” — a savory breakfast of toasted sourdough, golden-brown eggs, roasted tomatoes, and fresh dill.

Carlos prepared the meal at home, carefully measuring each ingredient and cooking the eggs in a nonstick pan until they were firm but still slightly creamy. He toasted the sourdough to a golden crust, sliced the tomatoes, and added a generous handful of fresh dill for a bright herbal finish. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted eggs, caramelized tomatoes, and toasted bread, with the sharp scent of dill adding a refreshing contrast.

The presentation was simple and inviting: the eggs, tomatoes, and herbs were arranged on a white plate with a small bowl of warm sourdough and a cup of black coffee beside it. It was a quiet, unhurried breakfast for a Monday morning, as Carlos settled into the rhythm of his routine while considering his poetry collection and creative writing workshop.
