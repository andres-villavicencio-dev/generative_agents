Dish Name: Seasonal Sto Pancakes with Citrus-Orange Fruit and Toasted Almond

Key Ingredients: All-purpose flour, eggs, milk, sugar, baking powder, oat flour, cinnamon, vanilla extract, maple syrup, orange segments, and chopped walnuts.

Preparation Method: Carlos mixed the dry ingredients and whisked them with the eggs and milk, then folded in the oat flour, cinnamon, and vanilla before baking the pancakes in a skillet until golden and fluffy. In the same pan, he pan-fried toasted almond slices with maple syrup and orange segments until they became fragrant and slightly caramelized.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the sweet scent of cinnamon, vanilla, toasted nuts, and citrus.

Presentation: The pancakes were arranged in a loose circle on a white ceramic plate, topped with the glossy orange-amber fruit and sprinkled with toasted walnuts. A small glass of cold water and a spoonful of fresh orange juice were placed beside the dish.

Occasion: The meal was prepared for a quiet Monday morning breakfast during Carlos’s creative writing workshop. He and Isabella Rodriguez sat side by side, discussing his nature-themed poem about seasonal sto, with Carlos’s loud, unconventional voice filling the small room.
