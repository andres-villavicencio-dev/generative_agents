For Monday, February 13, Carlos Gomez prepared Oatmeal with Lemon, Honey, and Blackberries. The dish used rolled oats, warm water, oat milk, sliced blackberries, a splash of lemon juice, and a drizzle of honey. He toasted the oats briefly, then cooked them with the milk until the oatmeal was creamy and slightly chewy. He added the fruit and lemon slices, letting the warm berries soften before serving.

The aroma was earthy and inviting: toasted oats, sweet berries, citrus zest, and a hint of honey. He presented it in a small white bowl, garnished with a sprig of thyme and a single blackberry, and served it with a glass of warm milk. This was a quiet, nourishing breakfast at home, prepared before he began working on his poetry collection.
