Dish: Grilled Lemon Garlic Salmon with Roasted Vegetables and Served Quinoa

Key ingredients: salmon fillet, lemon, olive oil, garlic, onions, carrots, bell peppers, quinoa, and fresh herbs.

Carlos prepared the salmon by seasoning it with salt, pepper, lemon juice, and minced garlic, then grilling it over medium heat until the flesh became opaque and the skin was crisp. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, salt, and pepper before roasting until they turned golden and tender. He finished the dish by serving it over fluffy quinoa, sprinkling it with chopped herbs and a squeeze of lemon.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the sharp scent of lemon and garlic, followed by savory notes from the vegetables. Carlos arranged the colorful vegetables in a neat row around the salmon in a shallow bowl, creating a bright presentation that made the dish look both elegant and hearty. He prepared the meal on Monday, February 13, to accompany his creative writing workshops and later eat while washing his dishes in the nearby sink before heading to the restroom.
