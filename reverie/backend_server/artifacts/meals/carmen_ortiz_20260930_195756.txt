Carmen Ortiz’s Tuesday dinner is a simple yet heartfelt Herb-Roasted Turkey with Honey-Glazed Sweet Potatoes. The turkey, lightly seasoned with rosemary and thyme, is roasted to golden perfection, its juices mingling with the earthy scent of honey-kissed sweet potatoes. Key ingredients include whole-grain bread, a side of herbed quinoa, and steamed green beans dusted with lemon zest. The aroma—a blend of warm spice, caramelized honey, and fresh herbs—fills her kitchen like a quiet celebration. Served on a hand-thrown ceramic plate, the meal is presented simply: turkey sliced thickly in the center, sweet potatoes fanned out beside it, a sprig of rosemary resting atop. This dish honors her roots and gratitude—the quiet bravery of showing up, every day. The occasion? A daily ritual of peace, a celebration of home, and the gentle strength found in stillness.
