Dish: “Morning Whispers of Courage”  
Carmen’s nourishing breakfast includes warm whole-grain oatmeal infused with sun‑dried tomatoes, toasted almonds, a drizzle of honey, and a poached egg, topped with microgreens. The meal is prepared simply, steaming gently in the kitchen as she reads from Whispers of Gentle Courage.  
Aroma: earthy tomato blends with nutty almond scent, sweetened by honey.  
Presentation: rustic bowl surrounded by a linen napkin; a single sprig of rosemary rests beside it.  
Occasion: A quiet celebration of gentle bravery on a sunlit Friday morning, honoring resilience and calm focus.
