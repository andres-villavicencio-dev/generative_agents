Carmen Ortiz prepared a Honey-Roasted Chicken with Lemon Thyme for her quiet evening feast. She marinated the chicken in olive oil, honey, fresh thyme, lemon zest, and garlic overnight. Roasting it at 375°F until golden gave crispy skin and tender meat. A steaming side of wild rice pilaf with chopped parsley added earthiness. The aroma was warm and herbal—honeyed chicken mingling with citrus and thyme smoke. Plated simply on a wooden board, the dish felt heartfelt, like a small celebration of calm. It was for her own nourishment after a long day, a gentle reminder that simple pleasures are enough.
