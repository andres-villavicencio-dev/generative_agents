Carmen Ortiz prepared a hearty dinner of roasted broccoli and quinoa, seasoned with fresh herbs and a touch of lemon zest. The key ingredients were organic quinoa, fresh broccoli florets, olive oil, and aromatic spices like cumin and paprika. She prepared the quinoa by rinsing and simmering it in vegetable broth until tender, while the broccoli was lightly sautéed in oil to retain its crunch and vibrant green color. The dish boasts a fresh, herbaceous aroma that rises from the pan, with the quinoa's subtle nutty flavor complementing the earthy tones of the vegetables. Presented in a simple but elegant white ceramic bowl, the meal looks inviting and balanced. This comforting dinner was prepared as a simple yet nourishing evening snack, offering a gentle contrast to the busy day ahead.
