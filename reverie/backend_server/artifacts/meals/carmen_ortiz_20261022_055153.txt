Meal: Cozy Lemon-Herb Chicken with Crispy Potatoes

For her Wednesday evening dinner, Carmen prepared boneless chicken breasts with baby potatoes, baby greens, lemon, garlic, fresh thyme, olive oil, and a little butter. She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, lemon zest, and chopped herbs, then pan-seared it until golden before finishing it in the oven. The potatoes were parboiled, crushed lightly, and roasted with garlic and thyme until crisp and tender. A small splash of lemon juice gave the greens a bright finish.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and buttery chicken, followed by the fresh scent of lemon. Carmen served the chicken over a bed of greens, arranging the crisp potatoes around it and garnishing the plate with thyme and lemon slices. The simple, colorful presentation felt inviting rather than elaborate.

It was the perfect relaxed midweek meal—a comforting dinner after a busy day at Harvey Oak Supply Store, made for enjoying quietly at home.
