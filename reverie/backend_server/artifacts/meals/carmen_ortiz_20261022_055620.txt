Carmen Ortiz prepared Lemon-Herb Roast Chicken with Crispy Root Vegetables for a relaxed Wednesday dinner after a busy day at Harvey Oak Supply Store.

The main ingredients were bone-in chicken thighs, baby potatoes, carrots, red onion, garlic, lemon, olive oil, fresh thyme, rosemary, paprika, and a pinch of salt and pepper. She seasoned the chicken, seared it in a hot ovenproof pan until the skin turned golden, then arranged the vegetables around it. The pan went into the oven, where everything roasted together until the chicken was tender and the potatoes were crisp at the edges.

As it cooked, the kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory chicken. Carmen finished the dish with a squeeze of lemon and a little fresh thyme before serving it. She presented the meal family-style on a large platter, with the golden chicken centered among colorful vegetables and a small bowl of pan juices on the side.

The occasion was simply an easy, comforting dinner to wind down and enjoy after work.
