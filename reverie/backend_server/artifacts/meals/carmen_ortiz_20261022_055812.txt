Carmen Ortiz prepared Lemon-Thyme Roasted Root Vegetables as a comforting side dish for a quiet Wednesday evening dinner at home. The colorful medley included carrots, parsnips, red onion, and small potatoes, tossed with olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, thyme, salt, and black pepper. She chopped the vegetables into bite-size pieces, spread them on a baking sheet, and roasted them until the edges were caramelized and the interiors tender. The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted onions, garlic, citrus, and herbs. She served the vegetables in a shallow ceramic bowl, garnished with fresh thyme and a drizzle of lemon juice, giving the rustic side dish a bright, inviting appearance. It accompanied her simple dinner as she relaxed after a busy day at Harvey Oak Supply Store, enjoying a peaceful evening without any confirmed appointments or obligations.
