Carmen prepared a warm Mediterranean veggie panini for lunch in the store’s break area. The sandwich was filled with sliced tomato, cucumber, mozzarella, mixed greens, arugula, and a light lemon-herb spread, then toasted in a panini press until the bread was crisp and the cheese melted. A handful of sweet potato chips and a small handful of almonds were served alongside it.

The panini smelled warm and savory, with the tangy fragrance of lemon, garlic, and herbs mingling with the rich aroma of toasted mozzarella. It was presented on a sturdy white plate, with the panini cut diagonally and the chips arranged in a small pile beside it. Carmen also set out a glass of sparkling water with lemon, making the simple meal feel refreshing and inviting.

This was an informal workday lunch, prepared to give her energy during a quiet Thursday at Harvey Oak Supply Store. The welcoming presentation reflected Carmen’s habit of making the break area feel cheerful and communal, even when she was eating alone.
