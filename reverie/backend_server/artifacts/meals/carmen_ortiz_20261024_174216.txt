Carmen prepared Hearty Chicken and Root Vegetable Stew for dinner. She used chicken thighs, carrots, celery, potatoes, onions, garlic, red pepper flakes, soy sauce, coconut milk, and fresh parsley. After washing and chopping the vegetables, she seared the chicken until golden, then slowly simmers everything in the pan with the coconut milk and broth until the chicken is tender and the vegetables are soft. She added fresh parsley at the end and served the stew in a wide ceramic bowl with a small portion of brown rice.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, garlic, and coconut milk. She presented the meal with a simple white tablecloth, neatly arranged vegetables, and the stew glistening in the pan, completing the setting with a few sprigs of parsley. The occasion was a calm family dinner for Tuesday, February 14, with no specific events planned.
