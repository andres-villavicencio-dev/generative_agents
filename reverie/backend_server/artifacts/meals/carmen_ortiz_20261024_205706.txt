Carmen prepared Spicy Chicken Stir-Fry with Golden Rice, a warm, colorful dinner for Wednesday, February 15. She used fresh chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, snap peas, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, and sliced golden brown rice. The ingredients were stir-fried quickly in a wok until the chicken was tender and the vegetables became bright orange and crisp-tender. A generous amount of sesame oil infused the kitchen with rich, earthy aromas of garlic, ginger, and toasted sesame seeds.

She arranged the stir-fry on a large serving platter and placed the golden rice in the center, surrounded by neatly arranged vegetable strips and pieces of chicken. She added a drizzle of oyster sauce, a squeeze of lime, and a few sprigs of fresh cilantro. The dish presented as a vibrant, appetizing bowl, with the golden-rice base creating a warm contrast to the colorful vegetables and savory chicken.

Carmen planned to eat it after handling a few store issues and catching up with Eddy Lin, so the meal was a comforting family dinner before winding down for the evening.
