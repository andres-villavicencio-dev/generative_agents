Carmen Ortiz prepared a Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry, a simple, filling lunch that balanced protein, colorful vegetables, and a flavorful sauce. The key ingredients were chicken breast, broccoli, bell peppers, carrots, onions, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, and a handful of fresh herbs. She sliced the chicken into thin pieces, tossed the vegetables with oil and seasoning, and stir-fried everything over medium heat until the chicken cooked through and the vegetables became crisp-tender. The aromatics added a warm, savory scent throughout the kitchen, while the bright colors of the vegetables made the dish visually appealing.

The meal was presented on a white ceramic plate with the chicken arranged neatly in the center and vegetables arranged around it. A small side of steamed rice and a squeeze of fresh lime added brightness to the presentation. It was prepared for a relaxed weekday lunch—Carmen wanted something comforting but not overly complicated, something she could enjoy while taking a break from work.
