Carmen Ortiz prepared a fruit and yogurt side salad, a light side of fruit and a light snack she had with her lunch.

The salad contained fresh strawberries, blueberries, sliced orange, and honeydew melon, all tossed with a creamy Greek yogurt dressing made from greek yogurt, honey, lemon juice, and a pinch of cinnamon. She also added chopped mint and a few whole figs for a touch of sweetness and texture.

She prepared it by washing and chopping the fruit, drizzling the dressing over it, and garnishing it with a sprinkle of chopped nuts and a sprig of mint. The aroma was bright and inviting—a sweet blend of strawberries, orange, and honey, softened by the freshness of mint.

The presentation was simple and cheerful: the colorful fruit arranged in a small bowl, with the yogurt and honey visible on the surface. It would have been a refreshing, balanced accompaniment to her lunch, intended to make her feel energized and grateful after a busy day.
