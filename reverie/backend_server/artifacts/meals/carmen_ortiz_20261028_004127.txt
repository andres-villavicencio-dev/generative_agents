Carmen Ortiz prepared Roasted Tomato Basil Soup, a simple yet comforting dinner suitable for a quiet Wednesday evening. She roasted red tomatoes, onions, garlic, and a small piece of chicken in a hot pan, then blended the cooked ingredients with vegetable broth, tomato juice, and a splash of olive oil. The soup was finished with fresh basil, and the chicken was sliced and plated over the bowl.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted tomatoes, garlic, and basil. Carmen arranged the soup in a shallow bowl, garnished with fresh herbs and a few basil leaves, and placed a slice of chicken alongside it. The presentation was bright and inviting, with the golden chicken adding a pop of color against the rich, earthy orange of the soup. It was the kind of simple dinner Carmen makes for herself—a comforting meal to wind down after a busy day of work and errands.
