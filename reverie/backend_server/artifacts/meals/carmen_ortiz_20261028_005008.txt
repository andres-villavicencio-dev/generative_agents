Carmen Ortiz prepared Caramelized Vegetable Soup with Crusty Bread, a warm, comforting dinner made for a quiet Wednesday evening. She combined onions, carrots, celery, garlic, and potatoes in a large pot, cooking them slowly until the vegetables softened and the broth began to thicken. She added a splash of vegetable broth, a few sprigs of thyme, and a pinch of salt, stirring until everything was tender and the soup had a rich, creamy texture. For the top, she toasted whole-grain bread, then served it alongside the soup and a handful of fresh herbs.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized onions and garlic. The golden broth bubbled gently, while the bread gave the meal a crisp, satisfying crunch. Carmen plated the soup in a simple white bowl and placed a few lemon wedges, parsley, and croutons beside it. She brought it home to eat alone, savoring the warmth and simplicity of the meal after a busy day at the supply store.
