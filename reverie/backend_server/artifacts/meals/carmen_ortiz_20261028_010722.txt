Carmen prepared a Simple Tomato Basil Soup with Toasted Vegetables. The dish’s key ingredients were ripe tomatoes, garlic, fresh basil, onion, carrots, celery, olive oil, vegetable broth, and a touch of Parmesan cheese. She sautéed the aromatics in olive oil until soft, added the vegetables and broth, then simmered them gently until the tomatoes broke down and the soup was creamy and flavorful. She finished it with a little cheese and a sprinkle of basil.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, herbs, and rich tomato broth. Carmen presented the soup in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranged the toasted vegetables around its edges, and placed a small plate of crusty bread nearby. The bright reds, golden browns, and fresh green basil created a cheerful, inviting appearance.

She prepared the meal for dinner before returning home, intending to use the time to recharge and explore Johnson Park. The occasion was a quiet, cozy evening of nourishment and reflection.
