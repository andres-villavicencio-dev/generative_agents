Carmen Ortiz prepared Cherry-Glazed Quinoa Salad with Toasted Berries. The dish combined fluffy quinoa, baby spinach, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, feta, and walnuts, all dressed with lemon-herb vinaigrette and finished with sliced figs and dried apricots.

She toasted the quinoa, added the vegetables and nuts, then tossed it with the dressing and arranged it in a shallow bowl. Toasted cranberries and apricots were scattered over the top for color and sweetness.

The meal had a warm, earthy aroma of toasted grains, fresh herbs, and the bright scent of dried apricots. She presented it in a rustic white bowl, surrounded by a napkin and a small plate of cinnamon sticks for garnish.

It was a quiet, homey lunch prepared for a slow Wednesday afternoon—Carmen taking a moment to enjoy her meal and recharge after a busy day at Harvey Oak Supply Store.
