Carmen Ortiz prepared Creamy Mushroom and Avocado Toast with Honey-Lemon Glaze, a comforting breakfast that made her sleepy but happy. She toasted thick slices of sourdough bread with fresh mushrooms, sliced avocado, and a sprinkle of black pepper. After heating a pan, she sautéed the mushrooms until they softened, then added a handful of fresh spinach and a splash of olive oil. The honey-lemon glaze was finished by drizzling it over the dish and finishing with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of mushrooms and toasted bread, balanced by the bright citrus scent of the lemon. She plated the breakfast in a simple white bowl, adding a small sprig of parsley and a lemon wedge for a touch of color. It was her morning reward for a productive Tuesday, offering something warm, satisfying, and full of gentle energy before her rest.
