Carmen prepared a Hearty Tomato-Scrambled Egg Breakfast with Grilled Chicken and Toast, a hearty brunch for dinner. She began with a base of butter and garlic in a nonstick skillet, then added two eggs and a generous handful of cheddar cheese. After the eggs were nearly set, she stirred in a can of diced tomatoes, a teaspoon of dried oregano, and a splash of lemon juice, allowing the flavors to meld. The chicken was grilled briefly, seasoned with salt and pepper, and served alongside a crisp slice of whole-grain toast.

The dish had a warm, savory aroma of garlic, cheese, tomatoes, and lightly toasted bread. It was presented on a white ceramic platter: golden-brown chicken, bright orange and red tomatoes, creamy scrambled eggs, golden toast, and a small bowl of lemon-tomato dressing. The occasion was an informal dinner with Eugene, Rajiv, Carlos, Arthur, and Sam, who were resting before preparing the next meal.
