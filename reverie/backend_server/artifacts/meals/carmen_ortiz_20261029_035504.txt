Carmen Ortiz prepared Eggs Over Easy with Toast and Fruit for dinner. The meal included soft-boiled eggs, whole-grain toast, sliced apples, and fresh berries. She cracked the eggs into a hot pan, added a pinch of salt and pepper, then gently turned them over with a spatula until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft and creamy.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of toasted bread, egg whites, fruit, and cinnamon. The eggs were presented on a white plate beside the toast, apples, and berries, with a small bowl of yogurt on the side. The setting was simple and inviting, arranged for a cozy Wednesday dinner with the family.

Carmen was excited to share the meal at the upcoming cookout, where everyone would enjoy the warm toast, eggs, fruit, and the welcoming atmosphere of the gathering.
