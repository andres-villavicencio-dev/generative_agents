Carmen Ortiz prepared Warm Toast and Eggs for Dinner, a simple, comforting breakfast for dinner on Wednesday, February 15. She gathered whole eggs, butter, salt, pepper, sliced tomatoes, cucumber, and a handful of fruit.

First, she cracked the eggs into a bowl and whisked them, then poured a little oil over the pan. She scrambled them until the yolks were soft and creamy, then sliced the tomatoes and cucumber into thin rounds. For the toast, she toasted two slices of bread until golden, brushed them with melted butter, and added the fruit slices.

The aroma was warm and inviting: buttery, savory, with a hint of citrus from the tomatoes and fresh fruit. She arranged the eggs on the plate, surrounded them with the fruit and vegetable garnish, and placed the buttered toast beside them. The bright colors and relaxed presentation created a cheerful, cozy atmosphere for the upcoming cookout, giving the occasion a welcoming, family-friendly feel.
