Carmen Ortiz prepared a breakfast-for-dinner meal that served as a warm, hearty lunch for the upcoming cookout. The dish was Egg Toast with Fruit, Honey, and Toasted Nuts, a colorful, rustic breakfast prepared for a family gathering.

The preparation began with a pan of buttered whole-grain toast, toasted nuts, and honey. She scrambled two fluffy eggs, adding sliced banana, honey, and fresh berries for a balanced, nourishing flavor. A small portion of fruit salad and toasted nuts were served alongside the toast to add freshness and crunch.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of toasted nuts, honey, fruit, and warm butter. The presentation was simple yet inviting: golden toast, bright fruit, glossy scrambled eggs, and colorful nuts arranged on a white plate, with a small glass of orange juice nearby.

Carmen served the meal to Eugene, Rajiv, Carlos, Arthur, and Sam and Tom for the cookout, where it would become the centerpiece of a warm, family-style breakfast.
