For Wednesday, February 15, Carmen Ortiz prepared Eggs Over Easy with Toast, Fruit, and Warm Toast for dinner. She gathered eggs, butter, salt, pepper, avocado, and fresh fruit, along with two slices of toasted bread and a handful of fruit. She cracked the eggs into a pan, added butter, and cooked them until the whites were set but the yolks remained soft. She seasoned the pan with salt and pepper, then served the eggs with the warm toast, sliced avocado, and fruit.

The kitchen smelled rich and inviting, with the aroma of toasted bread, melted butter, and fresh fruit filling the air. The presentation was simple yet warm: the golden toast, fluffy eggs, vibrant green avocado, colorful fruit, and a neat arrangement of bread and toast on a white plate.

The meal was designed for Carmen and the family’s upcoming cookout. It was cheerful, satisfying, and perfectly suited to the occasion—warm, colorful, and ready to be enjoyed while the guests gathered.
