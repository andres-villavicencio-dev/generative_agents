Carmen Ortiz prepared a hearty breakfast for dinner, combining eggs, fruit, and warm toast. The meal was a simple but comforting Egg, Fruit, and Toast Bowl, featuring two poached eggs, sliced banana, strawberries, and blueberries arranged on a rustic plate with a slice of whole-grain toast.

She boiled water and heated it in a kettle while stirring the eggs gently, then cracked them into a small bowl and heated them over low heat. The warm toast was toasted until golden, and the fruit was lightly brushed with honey. The aroma of toasted bread, sweet berries, and the savory scent of the eggs filled the kitchen.

The presentation was bright and inviting: colorful fruit arranged around the eggs, with the toast placed neatly beside them. It was prepared for a cozy Wednesday breakfast that Carmen would eat with Eugene, Rajiv, Carlos, Arthur, and Sam and Tom during the upcoming cookout.
