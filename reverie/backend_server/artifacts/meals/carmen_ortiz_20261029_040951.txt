Carmen prepared breakfast for the cookout, a hearty mix of eggs, toast, fruit, and warm toast. The eggs were scrambled in a pan with a generous amount of butter, while the warm toast was toasted to golden perfection. Fresh fruit—cherries, bananas, and berries—was added for a bright, colorful finish, and a little maple syrup was drizzled over the top.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of buttery toast, caramelized fruit, and freshly cooked eggs. Carmen arranged the breakfast on a large kitchen table, placing the eggs in a bowl beside the plates of toast and fruit. The presentation was simple but inviting: a rustic wooden board, colorful fruit arranged for visual appeal, and the warm food glowing under the morning light.

The occasion was a cozy family cookout, with friends and family gathering for a relaxed breakfast. The meal was meant to be comforting, energetic, and shared—a perfect start to a day filled with conversation, cooking, and friendship.
