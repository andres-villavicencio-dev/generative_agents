Carmen Ortiz prepared Warm Toast with Roasted Eggs and Fruit, a comforting family-style breakfast for her Wednesday, February 15, cookout.  

The dish began with whole-grain toast lightly toasted on a panini press, then brushed with melted butter and fresh herbs. Two sunny-side-up eggs were added, their golden yolks releasing a rich aroma of butter, egg, and herbs. For the fruit, she sliced ripe strawberries, pineapple, and mango over the dish, adding bursts of color and sweetness.  

She served the meal in a wide, rustic bowl, presenting the golden toast alongside the bright fruit and a small side of honey-drizzled yogurt. The warm, inviting smells of toasted bread, caramelized egg, and fruit created a cozy, celebratory atmosphere. It was prepared for the upcoming cookout, where Carmen wanted everyone to feel welcome and energized by the meal.
