Carmen prepared Warm Toast with Toasted Eggs and Fresh Fruit for her dinner. She sliced two pieces of sourdough bread and toasted them lightly in buttered skillet pans until golden, then added sliced eggs, a drizzle of olive oil, and a pinch of salt. She scattered the fruit—grapes, strawberries, and oranges—over the warm toast before serving.

The meal had a rich, warmly nutty aroma from the butter and toasted bread, with a bright, citrusy freshness from the fruit. It was presented in simple, inviting bowls: the golden toast with scattered fruit and the bright yellow eggs, with a small bowl of honeyed fruit nearby. Carmen served it at dinner after her Wednesday, February 15, busy day at Harvey Oak Supply Store, preparing for the upcoming cookout and celebrating a productive, purposeful day.
