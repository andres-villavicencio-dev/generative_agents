For her Wednesday, February 15, Carmen Ortiz prepared Warm Toast with Eggs and Fruit. She used a generous slice of whole-grain bread, two poached eggs, sliced strawberries, blueberries, and a drizzle of honey. After heating a pan, she buttered the toast, cracked the eggs into a small bowl, and whisked the butter and honey into a lightly beaten yolk before pouring it over the toast. The eggs were seasoned with salt and pepper, then served with the fruit.

The kitchen filled with a rich aroma of toasted bread, creamy egg, honey, and the tart sweetness of the berries. Carmen presented the meal simply, placing the golden toast, glossy poached eggs, colorful fruit, and a small plate of fresh orange slices on a white dish. The cheerful presentation reflected her friendly, helpful nature, and it was set for breakfast before the upcoming cookout.
