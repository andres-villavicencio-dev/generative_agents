Carmen Ortiz prepared a Warm Egg Toast with Fresh Fruit and Toasted Bread, a comforting breakfast suited for an upcoming family cookout.

She began by toasting slices of crusty bread and arranging them on a platter, then cracked fresh eggs into a pan and stirred gently until the whites were set and the yolks remained soft. She topped the pan with a few spoonfuls of butter and sprinkled it over the golden-brown toast. For the accompaniment, she sliced fresh fruit—grapes, strawberries, and apple slices—and arranged them alongside the bread. A drizzle of honey or a little lemon juice added a bright, citrusy finish.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of toasted bread and fragrant eggs, while the platter’s colorful arrangement created an inviting presentation. Carmen served the meal alongside toast, fresh fruit, and some eggs to share, making it perfect for a warm family gathering. The simple, nourishing breakfast reflected Carmen’s cheerful, welcoming personality and set the stage for the upcoming cookout.
