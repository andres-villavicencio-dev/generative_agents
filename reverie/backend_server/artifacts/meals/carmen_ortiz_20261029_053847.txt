Carmen prepared a hearty Spring Vegetable and Herb Breakfast Bowl, designed for a family cookout. The dish featured crisp romaine lettuce, cherry tomatoes, red bell pepper strips, cucumber slices, and thinly sliced radishes. She added a bright lemon-herb vinaigrette and topped each serving with fluffy scrambled eggs, fluffy whole-grain toast, sliced apple, and fresh berries. A small bowl of fruit, warm toast, and a few extra eggs was also prepared.

The vegetables were washed and chopped into uniform pieces, then tossed with the vinaigrette until every leaf and slice was lightly coated. The eggs were scrambled until golden but still soft, while the toast was toasted to a crisp. She arranged the colorful ingredients in a large serving bowl and set a few extra portions on the counter, ensuring everyone could easily take what they needed.

As the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted eggs, fresh herbs, and savory vinaigrette, Carmen’s cooking felt inviting and celebratory. The meal was prepared for a lively family cookout, bringing together friends and neighbors for a relaxed, warm gathering.
