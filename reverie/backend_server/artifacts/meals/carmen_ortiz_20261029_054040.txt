Carmen Ortiz prepared Crispy Chicken and Herb Roast with Seasonal Vegetables for her husband’s upcoming cookout. The main protein was chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, garlic, herbs, and lemon juice, which she seared in a large skillet before baking with a roasted tomato, onion, and herb crust. She also roasted carrots, bell peppers, zucchini, and asparagus, then sautéed them briefly to keep the vegetables crisp and colorful.

The kitchen filled with a savory aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, fresh herbs, and the warm smell of baking herbs. She plated the meal in a wide, rustic ceramic skillet, arranging the golden chicken around the vibrant vegetables and garnishing it with a few lemon wedges, parsley, and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. The presentation was hearty and inviting, with a golden-brown crust and a fresh, earthy color palette that matched the casual, welcoming atmosphere of the cookout.
