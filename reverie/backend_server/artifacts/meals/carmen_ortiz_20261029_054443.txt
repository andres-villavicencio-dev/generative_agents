Carmen Ortiz prepared a warm family breakfast, featuring fluffy scrambled eggs, a fresh tomato-and-cilantro-salsa, sliced avocado, and crisp apple slices. The eggs were cooked gently over low heat, seasoned with garlic, salt, and pepper, while the remaining ingredients were kept cool and arranged neatly on a large serving platter.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of eggs, tangy tomatoes, and the fresh, citrusy perfume of the herbs. It smelled comforting, wholesome, and inviting.

Presented on a bright wooden platter, the breakfast looked colorful and balanced—golden eggs garnished with green parsley, bright red tomatoes, creamy avocado, and crisp apples arranged in an appetizing half-moon shape. It was the centerpiece of Carmen’s upcoming cookout, where warm toast, eggs, fruit, and savory toast would be shared. The meal reflected her friendly, helpful nature and her excitement to make guests feel welcome, turning a simple breakfast into a lively gathering.
