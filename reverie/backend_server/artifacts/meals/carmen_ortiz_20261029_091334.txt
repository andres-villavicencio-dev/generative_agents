For her Thursday February 16 cookout, Carmen Ortiz prepared a Buttermilk Pancake Breakfast with Scrambled Eggs and Sliced Fruit. The dish featured fluffy pancakes made with buttermilk, butter, sugar, and cinnamon, golden brown eggs, crisp bacon, and fresh strawberries and blueberries. She prepared the pancakes by heating butter in a pan, cooking the pancakes until golden, and flipping them over before adding the eggs, bacon, and fruit.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized butter, toasted nuts, cinnamon, and fresh berries. The bright presentation combined the rich, golden pancakes with creamy scrambled eggs, crunchy bacon, and colorful fruit arranged neatly on a white plate. This simple breakfast was prepared for the family’s upcoming cookout, providing a hearty, familiar start to a sunny Thursday.
