Carmen Ortiz prepared Warm Spaghetti with Roasted Garlic and Herb Oil for dinner on Thursday, February 16. The meal balanced hearty pasta with roasted garlic, cherry tomatoes, basil, and a generous drizzle of olive oil, finishing with a sprinkle of Parmesan.

To prepare it, Carmen cooked the spaghetti in salted water until al dente, then tossed it with the warm garlic mixture, chopped tomatoes, and fresh herbs. The panetta was sautéed in a little butter until golden, and a spoonful of pesto was stirred in for extra freshness.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, savory scent of garlic, herbs, toasted vegetables, and warm pasta. The dish was presented on a white ceramic platter alongside a small bowl of pasta water, a lemon wedge, and a crisp salad of arugula and feta.

It was part of Carmen’s Thursday cookout dinner, a relaxed family meal shared with Eugene, Rajiv, Carlos, Arthur, Sam, and Tom. Carmen’s friendly, helpful demeanor made the gathering feel welcoming, and the comforting meal added a cozy touch to the evening.
