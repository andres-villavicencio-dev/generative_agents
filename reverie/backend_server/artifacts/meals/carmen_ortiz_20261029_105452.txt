For Thursday’s Thursday cookout, Carmen prepared Hearty Vegetable Stew with Crispy Chicken. The dish included roasted chicken thighs, carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, and carrots, seasoned with salt, pepper, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, dried thyme, and a splash of vegetable broth.

Carmen seared the chicken in a hot skillet until golden, then added the vegetables to a large pot. After cooking until the chicken was tender and the carrots softened, she returned the chicken to the pot, added a little water, and covered it to simmer for about 45 minutes. She finished it with a pinch of lemon juice and a generous handful of fresh parsley before serving.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of caramelized chicken, garlic, herbs, and caramelized carrots. The stews served in shallow white bowls presented colorful, rustic portions with a crisp chicken top, vibrant vegetables, and a glossy pan sauce. It was a simple, comforting dinner perfect for a neighborhood cookout, where everyone would share a hearty meal in good spirits.
