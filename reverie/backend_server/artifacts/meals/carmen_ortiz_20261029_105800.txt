For Carmen Ortiz’s Thursday cookout, she prepared Harvey Oak’s Classic Dinner with Garden Veggies, a hearty family-style meal featuring tender chicken or pork, creamy mashed potatoes, and a savory tomato-based sauce. She gathered fresh kale, carrots, bell peppers, onions, and cherry tomatoes, adding canned diced tomatoes, corn, and canned soup. Carmen gently sautéed the vegetables, cooked the meat until it was no longer pink, and finished the sauce with garlic, herbs, and a splash of lemon juice.

The meal carried a warm, earthy aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, caramelized vegetables, and savory meat. It was presented in a large family serving platter with colorful vegetables arranged around the edges and the main dishes placed in the center. The kitchen was filled with the comforting sounds of chopping and simmering, while the inviting presentation made the cookout feel both celebratory and homey. Perfect for a warm Thursday gathering, the meal was both nutritious and delicious.
