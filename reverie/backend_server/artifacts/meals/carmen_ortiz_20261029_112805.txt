For Thursday’s cookout, Carmen prepared Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Chili with sautéed cornbread, cinnamon-spiced apple sauce, and a smoky tomato sauce.

The chili was made by sautéing onions, garlic, and tomatoes, then simmering the chicken, kidney beans, black beans, chili powder, cumin, and paprika until the meat was tender and the sauce thickened. Fresh coriander, chili flakes, and a splash of hot sauce were stirred in at the end, giving the dish a warm, spicy aroma.

Carmen plated the chili in shallow bowls with a generous portion of golden cornbread and a small spoonful of apple sauce for sweetness, arranged neatly on the cookout table beside a pitcher of lemonade and a few colorful fruit skewers. The presentation was cheerful and inviting, with the rich orange-red chili contrasting against the creamy apple sauce and crisp cornbread.

The occasion was the Thursday cookout, where Carmen wanted everyone to feel welcomed and energized. The warm, comforting meal was prepared with care and enthusiasm, and the bright, rustic presentation made the gathering feel special and welcoming.
