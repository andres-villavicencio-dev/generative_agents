Carmen Ortiz prepared a Valentine’s Day Garden Risotto with crispy pancetta and roasted cherry tomatoes, served with a warm tomato sauce and a lemon-herb crust. She used Arborio rice, gently toasting it in butter and olive oil, then slowly added white wine, parmesan cheese, and the finely chopped vegetables. The pancetta was sautéed first, then folded into the creamy rice, while the tomatoes were caramelized until their skins softened.

The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of roasted pork, aromatic rosemary, and fresh herbs. She arranged the risotto in a shallow baking dish, topping it with the glossy tomato sauce and garnishing it with sun-dried tomato strips, green onions, and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

The meal was prepared for Carmen’s Valentine’s Day cookout at Harvey Oak Supply Store, where she and Tamara shared the dinner with friends and family. The warm, comforting presentation made the occasion feel special and inviting.
