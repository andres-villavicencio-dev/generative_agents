Carmen prepared Valentine’s Day Herb-Roast Chicken and Root Vegetable Casserole for the cookout. The dish consists of boneless, skinless chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, onions, garlic, and fresh herbs, arranged in a baking dish.

Carmen roasted the chicken until it was golden and the sauce thickened, then layered it with the vegetables. She added a flavorful brown butter or garlic-herb sauce and covered the casserole until it bubbled. She baked it at 425°F for about 30–35 minutes, then let it rest for five minutes to allow the flavors to meld.

The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant with notes of rosemary, thyme, garlic, and caramelized onion. It smelled comforting and inviting, especially when the kitchen filled with the scent of roasting chicken.

Carmen plated the casserole in a white ceramic dish, garnished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. The simple presentation made the colorful ingredients stand out. It was a warm, elegant meal for Valentine’s Day dinner, combining family-style cooking with the relaxed, social atmosphere of the cookout.
