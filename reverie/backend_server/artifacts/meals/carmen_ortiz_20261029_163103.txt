Carmen Ortiz prepared a Valentine’s Day Pasta and Tomato Basil Soup, paired with a simple grilled chicken salad and a hearty vegetable stir-fry for the cookout. The main dish features al dente penne tossed with a creamy tomato-basil sauce, crumbled Parmesan, fresh basil, and a drizzle of olive oil. A creamy tomato base was gently simmered with carrots, celery, onion, garlic, and spinach, then served with rusticzo bread, chickpeas, and crisp cherry tomatoes.

The chicken was marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and dried oregano, then grilled until golden and juicy. The salad included crisp romaine, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, feta, and a light lemon vinaigrette. Each side was served in a warm ceramic bowl or small dish.

When the food was plated, the kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, basil, fresh herbs, and savory tomato. The vibrant reds and greens created a cheerful, romantic atmosphere, while the golden chicken and bright vegetables added warmth and visual appeal. Carmen’s friendly smile, the clinking silverware, and the lively table conversation made the meal feel special—a perfect Valentine’s dinner and cookout experience.
