For Valentine’s Day, Carmen Ortiz prepared Rosemary and Lemon Herb Roast Chicken with garlic bread, roasted potatoes, and a side of fresh fruit salad.

She marinated the chicken in olive oil, rosemary, lemon juice, garlic, and thyme, then roasted it at 400°F for 25–30 minutes until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. The garlic bread was toasted with butter, garlic, and a little lemon zest until crisp. Roasted potatoes were seasoned with salt and pepper, then tossed with olive oil, rosemary, and lemon before being arranged alongside the chicken. A bright fruit salad of strawberries, blueberries, and grapes served with a light yogurt dip added a fresh, colorful finish.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with the savory scent of rosemary, garlic, and lemon filling the kitchen. The presentation was elegant and festive: the chicken rested on a white platter, potatoes and bread arranged neatly beside it, and the colorful fruit salad created a cheerful contrast.

Carmen prepared this for Tamara’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout at Harvey Oak Supply Store. It reflected her friendly, helpful nature as a store owner, and she was especially proud of the communal meal and the way everyone participated.
