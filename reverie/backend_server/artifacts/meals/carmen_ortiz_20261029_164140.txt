Carmen Ortiz prepared a Valentine’s Day Chicken and Vegetable Stew, a comforting, family-style meal perfect for the cookout. The main component was a slow-cooked chicken breast with carrots, celery, onion, bell peppers, and potatoes, finished with a savory tomato-herb sauce.

She added chicken stock, garlic, thyme, a splash of lemon juice, and a few fresh herbs for depth, then covered it and let it simmer until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were cooked through. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized vegetables, and aromatic herbs.

For the presentation, she arranged the golden-brown chicken pieces in a serving platter, surrounded them with bright orange carrots and crisp green peppers, and garnished the dish with fresh parsley and a dusting of red pepper flakes. The colorful, rustic look made the meal feel inviting and celebratory.

The occasion was Carmen’s support of Tamara’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout, with the group gathering on Friday, February 17, to share the feast and celebrate the occasion together.
