Carmen Ortiz prepared Valentine’s Day Strawberry Basil Lemon Cake with Lemon-Orange Ice Cream, a light and elegant dessert to complement the store’s Valentine’s Day cookout. She used fresh strawberries, basil, lemon zest, orange juice, almond flour, eggs, and a hint of cocoa for the cake, while the ice cream came from homemade vanilla bean custard and frozen berries. The cake was gently baked until golden, then cooled and sliced with fresh fruit and mint arranged in a neat tiered presentation. A bowl of creamy lemon-orange ice cream was placed beside the cake, with a small bouquet of fresh basil and a lemon wedge garnishing the table.

The warm aroma of citrus, herbs, and freshly baked cake created a cheerful, intimate mood for Carmen and the group. The occasion was an informal Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout, where Carmen coordinated the menu, checked ingredients, and helped everyone serve the meals. The dessert was simple, refreshing, and thoughtfully presented, making it a perfect celebration of the day’s warm community spirit.
