Carmen Ortiz prepared Valentine’s Dinner Bolognese with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Crust, a heartwarming family-style meal for the Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout.  

The bolognese simmered on the stove for over an hour, combining lean ground beef, tender cuts of pork, and onions with tomato sauce, carrots, celery, garlic, thyme, and a splash of white wine. Its aroma of savory beef, caramelized onions, and warm herbs filled the kitchen before it was plated. She finished the dish by roasting baby potatoes, parsnips, carrots, and a small yellow onion until the edges were brown and the vegetables were tender.

The final presentation was rustic and inviting: the rich meat sauce was ladled over the vegetables, topped with fresh basil, sliced almonds, and a bright lemon wedge. The warm golden-orange color of the sauce contrasted beautifully with the earthy tones of the root vegetables and the golden crust from the oven.  

Served at the Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout, the meal felt personal and celebratory, offering Carmen and her guests a comforting, festive opportunity to enjoy one another’s company.
