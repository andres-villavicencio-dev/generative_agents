Carmen Ortiz prepared a Romantic Valentine’s Day Chicken and Lemon Herb Rice with a side of roasted carrots, asparagus, and a warm glass of rosé. She began by cooking chicken breasts with lemon, garlic, olive oil, and fresh herbs, then tossed them with rice, spinach, and the vegetables. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken and citrus, while the vegetables added a bright, fresh scent.

She arranged the dinner at the dinner table using a simple white plate for the chicken, colorful rice, and crisp vegetables, with a small card reading, “Happy Valentine’s Day!” A side of glazed carrots and asparagus complemented the main dish. The meal was served at Tamara’s cookout, where Carmen helped coordinate the ingredients and organize the tables. The warm, inviting presentation made the occasion feel special, and the hearty yet cheerful flavors fit the lively Valentine’s Day gathering perfectly.
