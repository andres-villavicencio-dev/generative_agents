Carmen prepared Valentine’s Day Chicken Parmesan with Garlic Butter Potatoes and a Berry Lemon Tart, serving as the centerpiece for Tamara’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout.

She chose a boneless, skinless chicken breast, seasoned it with oregano, garlic, and lemon zest, then pan-seared it until golden and cooked through. For the potatoes, she tossed them with olive oil, salt, garlic powder, and pepper, then roasted them until tender and lightly browned. A creamy garlic-butter sauce was made with cream, Parmesan cheese, and fresh herbs, while the chicken was topped with a golden-brown crust of breadcrumbs and melted cheese.

The dish smelled warmly aromatic—richly savory, cheesy, and fragrant with garlic, herbs, and toasted bread. It was presented in a rustic wooden serving platter, with the chicken placed in the center and the pan of potatoes beside it. Garnishes of basil, cherry tomatoes, and lemon wedges added a fresh, romantic touch.

Served with crusty bread, salad greens, and a glass of red wine, the meal was ready for the Valentine’s Day cookout. Carmen and the other guests enjoyed the hearty, comforting dinner together, making it a memorable celebration of love, friendship, and good food.
