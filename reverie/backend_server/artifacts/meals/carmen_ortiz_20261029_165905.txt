Carmen Ortiz prepared Valentine’s Day Grilled Chicken with Rosemary Butter Potatoes, a colorful and heartwarming dinner for the cookout. She marinated chicken thighs and potatoes in olive oil, rosemary, garlic, lemon, and fresh herbs, then grilled them over a hot pan until the chicken was golden and the potatoes became crispy. The dish was finished with a pan of rosemary butter, giving it a fragrant, savory aroma.

She plated the meal at a bright table, arranging the chicken on a platter and the potatoes in a colorful, rustic bowl. Garnished with bright green herbs and a drizzle of lemon juice, the presentation made the meal look inviting and festive. The outdoor cookout table was also cleaned and organized, with leftover ingredients neatly stored and a cleanup area prepared. With the schedule and location confirmed, Carmen felt ready to enjoy a warm, memorable Valentine’s Day meal with her friends.
