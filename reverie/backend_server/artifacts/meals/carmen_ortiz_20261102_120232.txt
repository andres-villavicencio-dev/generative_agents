Carmen prepared Valentine’s Day Breakfast with Eggs and Toast, the first meal for Tamara’s cookout dinner. She started by filling the coffee maker with hot water and starting the brew cycle, then gathered eggs, butter, flour, milk, and buttered toast. In a skillet, she cooked the eggs until they were firm and golden, then arranged them beside the warm toast. She sprinkled the eggs with chopped chives and added a pinch of cinnamon for a subtle sweetness.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of butter, toasted bread, and gently cooked eggs. She presented the breakfast on a white plate, placing the eggs in the center and the toast nearby, with a small bowl of fresh fruit and a glass of milk to serve. The meal was bright, comforting, and clearly meant to bring the group together. It was a cozy first step toward Tamara’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout.
