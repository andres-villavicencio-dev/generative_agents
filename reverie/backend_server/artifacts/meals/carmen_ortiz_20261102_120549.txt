For the Valentine’s Day dinner, Carmen prepared a Roasted Tomato and Herb Breakfast Bowl. She started by toasting the bread, then sliced tomatoes and onions, and sautéed fresh herbs in olive oil until they released their bright, savory aroma. She added diced egg, sliced bell pepper, and a handful of spinach, seasoning with salt, pepper, garlic, and a dash of lemon.

The final meal was served in a warm, rustic bowl with crusty sourdough, a small side of roasted potatoes, and a generous side of creamy ricotta. The combination of smoky, savory, and fresh flavors created an inviting aroma of caramelized tomatoes, herbs, and toasted nuts.

Carmen arranged the colorful ingredients on the table for the cookout, with each person having their own bowl and serving utensils. The occasion was a warm, shared Valentine’s celebration, and the meal reflected Carmen’s friendly, helpful nature as she made every guest feel welcome.
