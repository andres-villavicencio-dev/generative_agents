Carmen prepared Valentine’s Day Breakfast Casserole, a comforting family-style meal for dinner. The dish combined eggs, spinach, tomatoes, onions, and shredded cheddar cheese, with whole-grain toast and a drizzle of olive oil. For the casserole, she sautéed the onions and spinach in a large skillet, then scrambled the eggs and stirred them in until just set. The remaining cheese was sprinkled over the mixture, followed by the whole-grain toast.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, savory cheese, and fresh herbs. Carmen arranged the golden-yellow eggs, colorful vegetables, and toasted slices in a shallow baking dish, then covered it with foil and placed it in the oven until the cheese melted and the casserole turned golden brown.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the casserole was surrounded by neatly folded toast and a small plate of fresh fruit and a cup of tea. The occasion was Carmen’s support for Tamara’s Valentine’s Day cookout, helping organize the meal, confirm the schedule, and create an enjoyable gathering for the group.
