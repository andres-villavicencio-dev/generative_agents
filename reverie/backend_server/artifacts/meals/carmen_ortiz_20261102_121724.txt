Carmen prepared a Valentine’s Day Breakfast Platter, a colorful and heartwarming dish designed to be shared at dinner. The meal featured eggs, sourdough toast, fresh fruit such as strawberries and blueberries, honey, sliced almonds, and warm whole-grain toast topped with a light maple-syrup glaze.

The eggs were poached gently, then arranged neatly alongside the fruits and nuts on a large platter. Carmen arranged the breakfast in neat sections, adding small handwritten notes with sweet messages for each guest. The aroma was rich and inviting—warm toasted bread, the buttery scent of honey, and the bright fragrance of fresh fruit.

The presentation was cheerful and practical: a rustic wooden board, an open jar of honey, a small bowl of nuts, and a cup of tea. The occasion was Carmen’s family’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout, and the meal felt personal and celebratory. Carmen’s friendly preparation made the gathering feel welcoming and the occasion feel special.
