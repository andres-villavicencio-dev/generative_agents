Carmen Ortiz prepared Valentine’s Day Chicken Piccata, a bright, flavorful one-pan dinner that would be perfect for Tamara’s cookout. The main protein was a skin-on chicken breast seasoned with garlic, lemon juice, red pepper flakes, and a touch of paprika.

Carmen heated a large skillet over medium heat and seared the chicken until the skin turned golden and the surface was crisp. She then added a splash of white wine, a tablespoon of olive oil, and the remaining lemon zest. A splash of chicken broth, a pinch of salt, and pepper completed the pan. She tossed in fresh spinach, cherry tomatoes, and sliced red onion, then finished the dish with lemon juice and a little olive oil.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of caramelized chicken, lemon, garlic, and warm herbs. The vibrant orange-red chicken, crisp skin, and bright green vegetables created a colorful presentation that looked inviting for dinner. Carmen had prepared the meal specifically for the Valentine’s Day cookout, helping organize the ingredients and encouraging the group to gather around the stove for a warm, festive meal.
