Carmen prepared Valentine’s Day Lasagna with Roasted Red Peppers and Herb Cream Sauce, a comforting, crowd-friendly meal suitable for Tamara’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout. The dish featured rich layers of ricotta, mozzarella, spinach, and pasta, enriched with tomato sauce, Parmesan, and a savory herb cream. Alongside it, she added roasted red peppers, fresh basil, garlic, thyme, and a generous drizzle of olive oil and balsamic glaze.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasting peppers, garlic, basil, and melted cheese. Carmen layered the lasagna carefully, topping it with cheese and finishing with a sprinkle of parsley and a light dusting of paprika. She arranged the plates artistically, adding small roasted peppers, cherry tomatoes, and fresh basil leaves as edible decorations.

The occasion was a lively Valentine’s Day cookout with friends and family, and the meal served as a cozy, festive centerpiece. Carmen’s friendly demeanor and careful attention made everyone feel welcome, helping the group enjoy an evening of laughter, conversation, and shared comfort.
