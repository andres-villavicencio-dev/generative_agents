Carmen Ortiz prepared Valentine’s Garden Salad for Tamara’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout. The key ingredients were romaine lettuce, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, basil, feta cheese, olives, and a light vinaigrette made with olive oil, lemon juice, and a hint of honey. She washed and chopped the vegetables, sliced the onion, and arranged the salad in a large bowl, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a few extra tomatoes.

She dressed the salad by whisking the vinaigrette and drizzling it over the ingredients, letting the flavors meld for a few minutes. The aroma was fresh, bright, and inviting—citrusy herbs, crisp greens, and the nuttiness of feta filling the kitchen. The presentation was colorful and festive, with the salad arranged on a platter and a small basket of crackers beside it.

The meal was part of Tamara’s Valentine’s Day celebration at Harvey Oak Supply Store, where Carmen helped coordinate the ingredients, organize the dishes, and prepare the cookout. It was also a special occasion for Carmen and her friends, including Eugene, Rajiv, Carlos, Arthur, Sam, and Tom, who gathered to enjoy the food and celebrate the day together.
