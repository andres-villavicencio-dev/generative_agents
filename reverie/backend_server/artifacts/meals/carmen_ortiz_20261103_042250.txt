For Carmen Ortiz’s Valentine’s Day cookout, she prepared Gourmet Chicken with Herbs and Garlic for the dinner table. She chose a whole chicken, browned it in a skillet with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of lemon juice, then seasoned it with salt, pepper, and a touch of paprika.

The chicken was seared until the skin was crisp and golden, while the pan retained enough heat for the remaining ingredients to cook gently. She finished the dish with roasted potatoes, carrots, onions, and fresh green beans, all seasoned with thyme, paprika, and olive oil.

As the meal cooked, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and savory chicken. It was presented on a large serving platter, with the golden chicken nestled around hearty vegetables, arranged with plenty of space for guests to pick up their own plates.

Carmen served it at the Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout, creating a festive, welcoming meal that reflected her friendly, helpful nature.
