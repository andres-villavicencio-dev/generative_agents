Carmen Ortiz prepared Valentine’s Day Chicken Enchilada Casserole, a warm, comforting family-style dinner perfect for the cookout. The dish features tender chicken thighs, corn and black beans, a rich red enchilada sauce, and fresh vegetables like bell peppers, onions, and carrots. Carmen added the remaining seasonings—cumin, chili powder, oregano, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and a pinch of cayenne—before simmering the casserole until the sauce thickened and the chicken was tender.

The aroma filling the kitchen was unmistakably warm and festive: the savory scent of caramelized chicken, roasted corn, and spicy peppers mingled with the gentle aroma of garlic and a hint of smoked paprika. It smelled comforting, slightly spicy, and inviting, exactly the kind of scent a group of friends would enjoy eating together.

Carmen arranged the golden enchiladas in a baking dish, garnishing the edges with fresh cilantro, lime wedges, and a drizzle of hot sauce. The colorful presentation made the meal look celebratory and appetizing, with the bright red sauce and vibrant vegetables standing out against the creamy, slightly browned chicken.

This dish was prepared specifically for Tamara’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout. Carmen’s help with coordinating ingredients, organizing the meals, and confirming the schedule made the event feel special. It was not just a simple dinner—it was part of the celebration the group had anticipated, and Carmen was proud that everyone looked forward to it.
