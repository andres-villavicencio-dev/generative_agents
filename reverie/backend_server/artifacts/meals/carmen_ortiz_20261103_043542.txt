Carmen Ortiz prepared a Valentine’s Day-inspired Rosemary Chicken and Green Bean Soup. The soup used tender chicken thighs, carrots, celery, onions, garlic, and fresh green beans, with a light broth made from onions, water, and a pinch of salt. She simmered everything until the chicken was cooked through, then stirred in a handful of fresh basil before serving.

The dish smelled intensely aromatic, with the fresh scent of rosemary, garlic, and herbs mingling with the savory richness of the chicken and crisp sweetness of the vegetables. It was presented in a white ceramic bowl, with the golden chicken pieces and bright green beans surrounded by a mound of creamy white pasta.

The meal was prepared for Tamara’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout. Carmen organized the ingredients and cooking times for the group, helping everyone feel welcome as they gathered for the celebration.
