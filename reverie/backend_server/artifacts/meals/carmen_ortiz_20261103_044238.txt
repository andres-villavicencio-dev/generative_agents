Carmen prepared Valentine’s Day Chicken and Asparagus with Lemon-Garlic Butter, a simple yet elegant dinner for the group cookout. She used boneless chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and oregano, then roasted them until golden and tender. The asparagus was lightly sautéed, and the kitchen was cleared of any old dishes before she arranged the plates.

She prepared the lemon-garlic butter by mixing softened butter with minced garlic, fresh lemon zest, and a splash of lemon juice, then drizzled it over the cooked chicken and asparagus. The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm, savory scent of roasted poultry and bright citrus.

The presentation was cheerful and inviting: the chicken sat in the center of the platter, with the golden asparagus arranged alongside it and a small bowl of lemon-honey glaze beside it. Garnished with parsley and a few cherry tomatoes, the meal looked festive and ready for sharing. Carmen served it at 8 p.m. for dinner, while the remaining guests gathered for the Valentine’s Day cookout, making the meal a joyful, memorable part of the celebration.
