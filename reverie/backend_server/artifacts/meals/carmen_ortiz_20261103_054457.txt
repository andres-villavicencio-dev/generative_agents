On Saturday, February 18, Carmen prepared Valentine’s Day Chicken and Herb Risotto, a festive, warmly comforting meal for the group’s cookout. The dish features tender chicken thighs coated in a savory herb glaze, creamy parmesan-risotto rice, and colorful seasonal vegetables.

Carmen sautéed onions and garlic in butter until golden, then added chicken in a pan to finish cooking while the rice absorbed flavorful broth. She combined Arborio rice with vegetable broth, parmesan, and fresh herbs, stirring gently until the rice absorbed the liquid and became tender yet slightly al dente. The chicken was folded in toward the end to keep it juicy. The finished risotto smelled rich and aromatic with notes of garlic, parsley, sage, and savory herbs.

She plated it in a large serving bowl, garnishing the top with chopped parsley and drizzling a little extra lemon juice. The meal looked colorful, inviting, and ready to share, perfectly suited to the cheerful Valentine’s Day dinner. Carmen had confirmed the cooking and serving times with the group, ensuring everyone would be ready to enjoy the meal at the location and time.
