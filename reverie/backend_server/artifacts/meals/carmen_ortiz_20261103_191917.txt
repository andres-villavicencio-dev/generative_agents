Carmen prepared Farmhouse Breakfast with Eggs, Fruit, and Coffee, a hearty breakfast to serve for dinner. She cracked organic eggs into a skillet over medium heat, adding a generous knob of butter and a pinch of salt. She cooked the eggs until the whites were set while keeping the yolks runny, then cracked them into a small bowl with a splash of milk for a creamy finish. The skillet also held a few slices of whole-grain toast, a dollop of Greek yogurt, and fresh fruit.

As she stirred, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted bread, caramelized butter, warm milk, and ripe apples. The eggs shimmered golden, and the bright colors of the fruit made the dish feel inviting and cheerful. She arranged the eggs, toast, fruit, and yogurt on a white plate, garnishing it with a sprig of parsley for a touch of color.

The meal was meant to be a cozy, family-style breakfast for Tamara Taylor’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout. It was simple enough to eat at home but warm enough to make the group feel special as they gathered around the table.
