Carmen Ortiz prepared a hearty Oatmeal Breakfast with Fruit and Oat Flakes for Tamara Taylor’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout. She used rolled oats, oat flakes, organic eggs, fresh apples, bananas, blueberries, and a pinch of cinnamon. She cooked the eggs over medium heat, stirred in half the oats, and added the fruit for a bright, colorful topping.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, nutty smell of oats, the soft scent of cinnamon, and the sweet freshness of ripe fruit. The presentation was warm and inviting: the bowl filled with creamy oatmeal, golden oat flakes, glossy golden-brown eggs, and clusters of fruit scattered across its surface.

The meal was part of Tamara’s planned Valentine’s Day cookout, where Carmen helped coordinate the remaining ingredients and meals. It became a comforting, shared breakfast before the group gathered for dinner and an evening of conversation and laughter.
