Carmen Ortiz prepared a Scrambled Egg Breakfast with Herbs and Lemon-Salt, the first meal for Tamara Taylor’s Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout. The dish features organic eggs, finely chopped chives, fresh parsley, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt and freshly cracked black pepper. She whisked the eggs with a little milk, then gently folded them in while cooking over medium heat until the yolks were soft and the whites were slightly crisp.

The aroma was warm and inviting, filling the kitchen with the rich scent of butter, herbs, and the savory richness of the eggs. She served the breakfast in a small white bowl, garnished with a drizzle of lemon juice and a scattering of chives. The simple presentation made the meal feel fresh, comforting, and thoughtfully prepared.

This was the first step in supporting Tamara’s cozy Valentine’s celebration. Carmen hoped the breakfast would set a warm tone for the upcoming cookout, while she continued organizing the remaining meals, expanding the online store, and ensuring every customer felt welcome at Harvey Oak Supply Store.
