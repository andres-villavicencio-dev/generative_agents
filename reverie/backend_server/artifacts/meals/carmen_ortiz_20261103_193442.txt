Carmen Ortiz prepared a Valentine’s Day “Morning Garden” breakfast for dinner, combining fresh organic eggs with bright seasonal fruit and a warm oatmeal-cream base. She cracked the eggs into a small skillet, whisked them gently, and set the pan over low heat so the yolks retained their creamy texture. The fruit included strawberries, raspberries, and honey-mustard carrots, arranged beside the pan in a bright, colorful bowl.

She toasted the oats in a nonstick pan with cinnamon and a splash of milk, then folded the eggs and fruit into the warm oatmeal. The finished dish had a fragrant aroma of caramelized nuts, warm milk, and sweet berries. It was presented on a white ceramic platter with wooden utensils, giving it a cozy, inviting appearance.

The meal was prepared for Carmen’s planned Valentine’s Day dinner and cookout, which the group had gathered to celebrate. Carmen felt proud that it had become something the group looked forward to, offering an early, cheerful start to the celebration.
