Carmen prepared a warm fruit and oatmeal breakfast bowl, a simple, comforting meal she makes for her own morning. Her bowl contained rolled oats cooked with milk and cinnamon, topped with sliced bananas, raspberries, and chopped walnuts. A drizzle of honey and a sprinkle of cinnamon added sweetness and aroma, while a spoonful of yogurt completed the balanced flavor.

She prepared the oats by simmering them in the milk with a pinch of cinnamon for several minutes, then stirred in the fruit and nuts before letting it cool slightly. The warm oats had a fragrant, wholesome scent of milk, cinnamon, and toasted nuts. Carmen arranged the bowl on the counter with the oats in the center, surrounded by colorful fruit arranged around it, with a spoon and a small glass of water nearby.

The meal was her first breakfast on a chilly Monday. It was prepared for the convenience of her busy morning, and she intended to eat it while helping customers at the store and preparing to open Harvey Oak Supply Store at 8:00 AM.
