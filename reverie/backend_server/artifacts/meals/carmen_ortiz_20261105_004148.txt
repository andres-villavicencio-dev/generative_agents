Carmen prepared a Grilled Chicken and Green Vegetable Bowl for the lunch shift at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured a whole chicken seasoned with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, paprika, and herbs, then grilled until the skin was crispy and the meat was tender. It was served over a bed of sautéed spinach, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, and onions, with a lemon-tarrine dressing and a side of crusty bread.

The preparation method involved browning the chicken before adding the vegetables, then finishing it under the broiler so the flavors would become rich and aromatic. As she prepared it, a warm scent of roasted garlic, lemon, herbs, and grilled chicken filled the kitchen, while the colorful ingredients added an inviting aroma.

Carmen presented the meal in a warm, rustic ceramic bowl arranged neatly on a wooden table. The golden-brown chicken, bright green vegetables, and golden bread created a cheerful, fresh look, perfectly suited to the cozy Tuesday lunch at Hobbs Cafe.
