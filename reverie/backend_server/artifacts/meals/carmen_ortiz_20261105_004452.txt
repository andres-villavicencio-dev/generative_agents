Carmen Ortiz prepared Braised Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry for the Hobbs Cafe lunch counter. The dish features bone-in chicken thighs, baby potatoes, carrots, onions, bell peppers, snap peas, and leafy greens, all cooked in a savory brown-sugar soy and ginger glaze. It was stir-fried briefly in a stainless-steel wok until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender-crisp.

Carmen added the sauce, reserved cooking liquid, and a splash of water to the wok, then stirred in a handful of fresh cilantro and a few sprigs of scallions. She finished it with a light dusting of cumin and served it over a bed of steamed rice and warm tortillas. The aroma of caramelized chicken, aromatic ginger, and fresh herbs filled the small café kitchen.

She arranged the meal on a large serving platter and added a colorful garnish of toasted almonds and sliced tomatoes. The presentation was warm, inviting, and practical for the busy lunch crowd. The occasion was a quick but satisfying family-style lunch, prepared so customers could enjoy it at Hobbs Cafe.
