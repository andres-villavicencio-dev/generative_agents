Carmen Ortiz prepared Roasted Chicken with Herb Crust for a Tuesday lunch at Hobbs Cafe. The dish consists of a whole chicken roasted with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a touch of honey, then coated in an earthy herb crust of breadcrumbs, parmesan, and olive oil. The chicken was seasoned, spread evenly, and roasted until golden and juicy, then sliced and served with lemon-herb rice, crisp salad, and a simple dressing.

The kitchen smelled warmly of roasted garlic, rosemary, and toasted bread, while the restaurant greeted Carmen with a cheerful “Welcome!” She arranged the chicken on a white platter, garnished it with fresh herbs, and placed a small card inviting customers to order online. The meal was ready to serve at 11:30 a.m., giving her enough time to check the lunch thoroughly, thank Tamara Taylor, and confirm the remaining items for Monday’s customers.
