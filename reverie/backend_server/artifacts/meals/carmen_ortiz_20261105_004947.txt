Carmen Ortiz prepared Grilled Chicken and Vegetable Soup with Roasted Vegetables at Hobbs Cafe. The dish used a whole chicken, chicken broth, carrots, celery, onions, leeks, garlic, bell peppers, and tomatoes. She seared the chicken with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then sliced the roasted vegetables before combining them with the simmering soup in a large pot.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, savory chicken, and the fresh, crisp scent of vegetables. Carmen arranged the bowl alongside warm bread and a side of grilled cheese, garnishing it with parsley and lemon wedges. The bright orange and green colors, combined with the golden-brown chicken and creamy broth, gave it a cheerful, inviting appearance.

She prepared the lunch for a Tuesday lunch service at Hobbs Cafe. Carmen carefully placed the meal on the serving counter and checked that everything was ready. She also confirmed several pending items and followed up with Tamara Taylor, making sure everyone could enjoy a balanced and comforting meal.
