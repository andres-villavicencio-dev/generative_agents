Carmen Ortiz prepared a hearty family-style chicken, rice, and vegetable dinner for Tuesday, February 14. The dish was called “Warm House Chicken.” Key ingredients included bone-in chicken breasts, long-grain white rice, carrots, green beans, bell peppers, onions, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice.

She marinated the chicken in olive oil, garlic, herbs, and spices before seasoning it thoroughly. While the chicken roasted in the oven, she sautéed the vegetables, then stirred in the rice and finished the dish by broiling it for a crisp top. A little pan juices, lemon, and chopped herbs were stirred in at the end to brighten the flavors.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of garlic, toasted chicken, and the fresh earthiness of the vegetables. Carmen plated the meal on a large white platter, arranging the golden chicken beside the fluffy rice and colorful vegetables. She added a small lemon wedge and chopped parsley as a garnish.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday dinner at home, intended to be comforting and welcoming. Carmen’s warm, friendly presentation suggested she was sharing a simple, nourishing meal with family and friends.
