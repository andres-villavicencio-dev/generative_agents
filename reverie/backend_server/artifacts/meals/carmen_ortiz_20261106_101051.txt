Carmen Ortiz prepared a hearty Egg Over Easy Breakfast Platter for the early shift at Harvey Oak Supply Store. The dish included ten eggs, a generous portion of brown toast, a cup of coffee, a small bowl of fruit, and fresh fruit garnishes. She cracked the eggs into a pan, added a little butter and a pinch of salt, then cooked them over medium heat until the whites were firm and the yolks were still creamy. She served the eggs on a platter alongside the warm toast and toppings, arranging them attractively for both herself and her shopkeeper friends.

The kitchen smelled of butter, warm toast, brown sugar, and the gentle aroma of cooking eggs. The presentation was bright and inviting, with the golden eggs, amber toast, and colorful fruit creating a cheerful, comforting centerpiece. The meal was prepared for Carmen’s early morning work period, making her feel grounded, energized, and proud of how she helped her friends enjoy a simple but satisfying breakfast.
