Carmen prepared a Hearty Rosemary Garlic Chicken with Root Vegetables for dinner. She used boneless chicken breasts, onions, garlic, fresh rosemary, celery, carrots, bell peppers, and a small amount of olive oil.

She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, dried thyme, and rosemary, then marinated it briefly before searing it until golden. The vegetables were sautéed until crisp-tender, then simmered in the same pan with the roasted chicken. She finished it with a splash of chicken broth and a squeeze of lemon.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm, savory notes of garlic, roasted chicken, and fresh rosemary. Carmen plated the food in a shallow dish, arranging the golden chicken in the center and the colorful root vegetables around it, with lemon slices, parsley, and a few sprigs of rosemary garnished on top.

The occasion was a relaxing evening at home after her workday. She cooked it herself, wanted to enjoy it with a glass of wine, and prepared it thoughtfully for herself and Tamara.
