For her Tuesday night dinner, Carmen prepared a comforting Hearty Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy, a side dish designed to accompany her meal and the store customers who gathered nearby.

She started by seasoning a whole chicken with salt, pepper, garlic, and rosemary, then roasted it in a large skillet with a mix of carrots, potatoes, onions, and celery for 45 minutes. While the chicken roasted, she sautéed the vegetables until crisp and added a rich tomato-based gravy. The warm chicken was sliced alongside the vegetables and served over mashed potatoes.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasting meat, caramelized onions, garlic, and roasted root vegetables, while the golden potatoes and creamy gravy added warmth to the air.

Carmen arranged the food on a white platter, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice. The presentation was simple yet inviting, emphasizing the natural colors of the ingredients. The meal was prepared for an evening family dinner and a small gathering of friends and neighbors who stopped by Harvey Oak Supply Store for supplies.
