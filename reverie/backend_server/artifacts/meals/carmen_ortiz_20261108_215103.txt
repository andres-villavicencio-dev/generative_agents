Carmen prepared Garlic Butter Lemon Herb Roast Chicken, a comforting dinner for her home on Tuesday, February 14. The main dish featured a whole chicken, skin-on and generously seasoned with garlic, lemon, rosemary, thyme, pepper, and a splash of orange wine. After searing the chicken in a skillet, the entire bird was transferred to a roasting pan and roasted at 400°F for about 45 minutes, until the juices ran clear and the breast was tender.

She finished it by broiling the skin until it turned golden brown, then sliced the chicken into portions and served it with roasted carrots, potatoes, and pan-seared garlic-thyme potatoes. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, herbs, caramelized onions, and the savory scent of roasted poultry.

The meal was presented on a rustic wooden platter, with the golden chicken arranged alongside the colorful vegetables and pan-fried potatoes. Carmen added a small side of warm croissants and served the chicken with a creamy lemon herb sauce and simple boiled rice. It was a warm, home-style dinner prepared for a quiet evening at home, offering comfort and a pleasant contrast to the busy day she was managing at Harvey Oak Supply Store.
