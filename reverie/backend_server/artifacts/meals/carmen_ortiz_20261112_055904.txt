Carmen prepared Mediterranean Grilled Chicken and Vegetable Bowl, a fresh, family-style lunch ideal for a busy Monday. The dish features boneless chicken thighs marinated in lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, and herbs, along with cherry tomatoes, zucchini, bell pepper, spinach, and chickpeas. She grilled the chicken until the skin was golden and the meat was tender, then tossed it with the remaining marinade and cooked the vegetables until crisp-tender.

The kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of garlic, herbs, and smoky grilled chicken. She arranged the colorful ingredients in a large white bowl, topped it with a simple lemon-herb dressing, and arranged the chicken and chickpeas prominently on the side. The presentation was bright, rustic, and inviting, with fresh greens and crisp vegetables adding a touch of energy.

This meal was prepared for Carmen and Tamara’s lunch, though Carmen also prepared it for herself to keep energy up while managing the expanding online shop. It was a relaxed lunch, not a formal one, but a warm, satisfying meal to help her get through the demanding day.
