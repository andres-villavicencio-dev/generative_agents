Carmen Ortiz prepared Creamy Mushroom and Bacon Stew, a comforting one-pot dinner for herself and Tamara. The dish features tender beef, onions, mushrooms, carrots, celery, garlic, tomato sauce, and cream, with a handful of fresh parsley garnished on top.

She sautéed the onions and mushrooms in a large pan, then added the beef and let it brown before introducing the root vegetables and tomatoes. She deglazed the pan with a splash of wine, added broth, and covered the stew. After simmering for about thirty minutes, she stirred in the cream and finished it with a pinch of salt and pepper.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory earthiness, rich bacon, caramelized onions, and a subtle warmth from the tomatoes. She presented the stew in a large glass serving dish, arranged the parsley in a neat row, and placed a small bowl of lemon wedges beside it.

It was a casual Sunday dinner after Carmen had finished cleaning the kitchen. She cooked it to satisfy her appetite and relax with Tamara before heading to The Rose and Crown Pub for a drink after closing the store.
