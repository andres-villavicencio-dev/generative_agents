Chad prepared Braised Short-Rib Breakfast for the clinic’s weekly free blood-pressure screening. He began by searing chicken thighs, pork chops, and eggplants in oil until the skin was crisp, then added carrots, onions, and garlic to a slow cooker. The dish slowly reduced in a rich soy-butter broth with a touch of thyme and a splash of chicken stock.

As it finished, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onion, roasted garlic, and savory meat. Chad plated the breakfast in a large white bowl: the golden chicken and pork, tender eggplant, bright orange carrots, and a fluffy white egg atop a bed of steamed rice. He topped it with crispy pancetta, fresh herbs, and a few cherry tomatoes.

The meal was meant to be a hearty, nourishing start to a busy Monday. It was a thoughtful, home-style breakfast prepared for the clinic’s longtime patients—Sam and Jennifer Moore and their family—while Chad’s meticulous preparation reflected his dedication to making small-town medicine feel welcoming and comforting.
