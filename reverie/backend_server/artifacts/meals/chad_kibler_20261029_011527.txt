Chad Kibler prepared Mediterranean Vegetable Bowl with Lemon-Herb Chicken, a practical main dish he measured carefully before checking his portion quantities. His breakfast had been full and purposeful, so he wanted a balanced, nourishing meal. He used two chicken breasts, a bell pepper, red onion, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and carrots, with garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, and fresh herbs. The chicken was pan-seared until golden, then simmered briefly with the vegetables and a splash of chicken broth.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory garlic, toasted herbs, and the bright citrus scent of lemon. Chad plated the colorful vegetables around two generous portions of chicken, adding feta cheese and a few fresh mint leaves. The presentation was simple and inviting, with the golden chicken and crisp vegetables arranged on a white bowl.

The meal was prepared for a Tuesday lunch at home, a quiet moment when Chad was balancing his clinic work with telehealth planning with John Lin. It was not a special celebration, but a purposeful, nourishing lunch that reflected his meticulous approach to cooking and his desire to serve others well.
