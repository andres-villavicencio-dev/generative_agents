Chad prepared Bread, Egg, and Milk Breakfast, a simple, hearty brunch for the Moore family. The key ingredients were whole-grain bread, cracked eggs, and full-fat milk.

He toasted the bread until it was golden brown, cracked the eggs into a pan, and cooked them with just a splash of butter until the whites were firm and the yolks were still runny. He served it with a glass of milk and a pinch of sugar, letting the warmth of the eggs carry the scent of toasted nuts and baking. The kitchen smelled richly savory with warm milk and toasted flour.

Chad presented the bowl at the table with the bread, eggs, and milk arranged clearly, adding a fresh slice of lemon. The occasion was the family’s Tuesday morning meal, a relaxed gathering after a full day of clinic screenings. He checked that everyone was seated and ready, satisfied that the meal would be comforting and that the kitchen was prepared for his wife’s cooking.
