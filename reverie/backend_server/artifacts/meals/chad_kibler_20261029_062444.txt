Meal Name: Warm Oatmeal with Berries and Lemon-Orange Tea

Chad prepared a comforting, heart-to-heart breakfast for himself and his home. He used rolled oats, milk or almond milk, a splash of vanilla extract, and a small handful of mixed berries—strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries—along with sliced apples and a slice of orange. He toasted a slice of whole-grain toast and added it to the oatmeal for extra fiber, then finished the meal with a cup of warm tea.

The kitchen filled with the gentle aroma of oats, citrus, milk, and the bright scent of fresh berries. Chad arranged the bowls on the counter, placing a glass of tea beside the oatmeal and fruit. The simple breakfast felt inviting and purposeful, echoing the warmth Chad wanted to bring to the clinic and its patients. He took a quiet moment to savor the meal before heading to the clinic, grateful that a simple morning could feel like a small act of caring.
