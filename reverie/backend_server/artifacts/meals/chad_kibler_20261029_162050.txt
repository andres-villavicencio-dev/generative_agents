Chad prepared Smoky Herb Chicken and Grilled Vegetables with Quinoa Rice. The main dish featured tender chicken thighs marinated in garlic, lemon, rosemary, oregano, and a splash of soy sauce, then grilled until golden and flavorful. He added crisp potatoes, carrots, and bell peppers, topping them with a garlic-lemon vinaigrette. A portion of warm quinoa rice completed the meal, seasoned with cumin and served alongside the chicken.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, roasted vegetables, and herbs. Chad presented the meal in a white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables in a neat circle around the chicken and sprinkling the quinoa with edible flowers for a bright, inviting look. He set the dishes on the counter after washing his hands, intending to share the meal with Sam and Jennifer Moore.

The occasion was Friday lunch at the clinic, a quiet moment of connection and nourishment amid his demanding schedule. It was meant to be a simple, wholesome meal that reminded Chad of the community he cared for, but it also reflected the meticulous care and thoughtful preparation he brought to every patient interaction.
