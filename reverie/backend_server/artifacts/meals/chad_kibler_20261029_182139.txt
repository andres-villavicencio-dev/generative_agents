Chad Kibler prepared a hearty Smoked Salmon and Quinoa Breakfast Bowl, a simple yet nutritious meal for himself and Sam.

The dish features smoked salmon, quinoa, roasted vegetables, lemon-tahini dressing, and a drizzle of maple syrup. The quinoa was cooked with water until tender, then toasted briefly for a nutty aroma. The vegetables—eggplant, bell peppers, and onions—were roasted until crisp, while the salmon was seasoned with smoked paprika and dried herbs. A bright lemon-tahini dressing added a fresh zing, and the final drizzle of maple syrup provided a warm finish.

As he plated it, Chad arranged the colorful vegetables and salmon over a bed of fluffy quinoa, garnishing each bowl with a lemon wedge and a few sprigs of fresh parsley. The warm, savory aroma of toasted quinoa, smoked salmon, and roasted vegetables filled the kitchen, while the bright lemon and crisp vegetables gave the meal a welcoming, refreshing appeal.

The occasion was a quiet family breakfast before Chad continued his clinic duties. It was a simple, wholesome meal that balanced his demanding day with the need for rest and connection with Sam.
