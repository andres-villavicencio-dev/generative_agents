Chad Kibler prepared Grain Bowl with Roasted Tomato, Cabbage, and Chickpeas, a simple, hearty meal made for an early Saturday family dinner.

The dish combined brown rice, cooked quinoa, and shredded cabbage with roasted tomatoes, chickpeas, lemon, garlic, and olive oil. He tossed the vegetables and chickpeas with lemon juice and a little salt, then added the grains to a warm serving bowl. Fresh herbs and a pinch of red pepper flakes added a bright, savory finish.

His kitchen smelled warm and aromatic—fragrant with roasted garlic, citrus, and the earthy scent of grains. Chad served the bowl in a simple white bowl with a small wooden spoon, arranging the colorful vegetables and chickpeas in neat rows.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Saturday dinner with Sam and Jennifer Moore, who had asked him to help pick up ingredients that had gone out of stock. It was a relaxed, practical family gathering, balanced with his clinic work and community appointments.
