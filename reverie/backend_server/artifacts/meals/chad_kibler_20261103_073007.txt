Chad prepared Roasted Vegetable and Herb Stew, a simple, nourishing lunch for his clinic’s Saturday gathering. He used carrots, potatoes, onions, bell peppers, tomatoes, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and olive oil, with a splash of chicken broth and a pinch of salt to balance the earthy flavors.

He washed and chopped the vegetables, then sautéed the onions and garlic until golden, followed by the peppers and tomatoes. The carrots and potatoes were added, and the stew simmered gently for about twenty minutes. A little water was added only to prevent it from drying out, while the herbs were stirred in at the very end so they would not burn.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm, savory scent of caramelized onions, garlic, and herbs. Chad arranged the finished stew in a shallow baking dish and topped it with chopped parsley and a sprinkle of cracked black pepper. He served it with crusty bread and a glass of water, presenting the meal with a calm, welcoming expression as if welcoming a fellow villager to his table.

The occasion was a quiet Saturday lunch at the clinic’s kitchen, intended to bring the community together. Chad’s methodical preparation reflected his meticulous nature and hospitality, while the comforting food offered him a moment of stillness between the clinic’s busy schedule.
