Chad prepared a hearty Smoked Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon Herb Dressing, designed as a nourishing Sunday lunch. He marinated chicken thighs with smoked paprika, garlic, onion, and herbs, then seared them in a pan until golden and tender. He roasted potatoes, carrots, and onions with a little oil, and topped everything with a bright lemon-herb dressing.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the subtle smoke of the chicken and the earthy scent of the vegetables filling the kitchen. The presentation was simple but inviting: platter vegetables arranged neatly around the chicken, golden potatoes, and a bowl of glossy dressing.

This meal was meant to be a comforting Sunday lunch, balancing the energy of his busy clinic schedule with a moment of relaxation. It reflected his meticulous, caring side while honoring the purposeful rhythm of his life.
