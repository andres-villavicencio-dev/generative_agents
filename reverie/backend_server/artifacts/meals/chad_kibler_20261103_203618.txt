Chad Kibler prepared a Crispy Chicken Salad with Lemon-Herb Vinaigrette, a quick, nutrient-dense breakfast for his morning routine. The dish featured tender chicken breasts marinated in lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, and fresh herbs, then pan-seared until golden and crisp. Alongside the chicken, he added mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and a generous portion of quinoa for fiber and protein.

The preparation was simple but thorough: the chicken was seasoned and grilled over a pan, then sliced. The salad was tossed with a bright vinaigrette made from olive oil, lemon zest, and a few dried herbs. A handful of almonds and a drizzle of honey added a satisfying finish.

The meal was warm, aromatic, and deeply fragrant—the sharp lemon and garlic mingled with the savory earthiness of the chicken and herbs. Chad presented it in a shallow white bowl, arranged the colorful vegetables neatly on top, and placed a small plate of fresh fruit beside it. It was the first meal of a busy Monday, designed to provide steady energy while supporting his upcoming free blood-pressure screening week.
