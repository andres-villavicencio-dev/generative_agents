Chad Kibler prepared Mediterranean Lemon-Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables as a simple, stress-relieving meal for his free blood-pressure screening. The dish featured tender chicken thighs seasoned with olive oil, lemon, garlic, dried oregano, and a pinch of salt. It was cooked alongside roasted carrots, bell peppers, and zucchini, then sliced and arranged alongside warm whole-grain rice.

The aroma was warm and inviting: roasted garlic, zucchini, and oregano filled the kitchen, while the bright citrus and savory herbs gave the meal a cheerful, homey feel. Chad plated the colorful vegetables on the left and the golden chicken on the right, using a serving spoon to create an elegant, balanced presentation.

Chad prepared the meal at home on Monday, February 13, before his clinic team met for the screening. The relaxed dinner helped him unwind after a demanding workday, while the familiar ingredients reminded him that small-town preventive care could still feel welcoming and accessible.
