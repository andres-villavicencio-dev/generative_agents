Chad prepared Smoky Herb Roast Chicken with Mashed Potatoes and Green Beans, a simple but heartwarming Sunday breakfast-style meal for Sam and Jennifer Moore.

The chicken was poached until nearly done, then basted in garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and a splash of soy sauce for a warm, savory aroma. The potatoes were mashed with butter, garlic, and chives, while the green beans were lightly sautéed and seasoned with thyme. The dish was plated on a warm white plate with the chicken resting beside it, garnished with parsley and a lemon wedge.

As Chad served the meal, he added a steaming cup of herbal tea—perhaps a chamomile blend or Earl Grey—to match Sam and Jennifer’s preference. He then tidied the kitchen while chatting with their longtime patients, offering a quick, nutritious option for the week and encouraging them to visit the clinic for their regular blood-pressure screening. The meal felt thoughtful, welcoming, and purposeful: prepared for comfort, shared with neighbors, and part of his effort to make preventive care accessible to small-town residents.
