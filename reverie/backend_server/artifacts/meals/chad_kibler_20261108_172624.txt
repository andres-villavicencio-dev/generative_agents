Chad Kibler prepared Braised Lamb Shoulder with Root Vegetables and Rice. His wife, Sarah, had selected a lean lamb shoulder, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, red onion, garlic, and dried herbs, while Chad added rice and a splash of vegetable broth.

He browned the lamb in a large skillet, then added the vegetables and broth. After a few minutes, he transferred the meat to a slow cooker, covering it with broth and herbs and cooking it until the lamb was tender and the vegetables were caramelized. The lamb was served over a bed of rice with the vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the rich, savory aroma of caramelized onions, roasted lamb, and warm herbs. The meal was presented in a white serving dish, with the golden lamb resting prominently in the center and the bright orange, yellow, and green vegetables arranged around it. The simple, rustic presentation was suitable for the Hobbs Cafe’s Monday breakfast.
