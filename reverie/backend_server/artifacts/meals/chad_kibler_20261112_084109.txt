Chad prepared Herb-Crusted Salmon with Roasted Root Vegetables. He used 18-ounce salmon fillet, asparagus, carrots, potatoes, and red bell peppers. After washing the vegetables, he sliced them, then tossed them with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and dried thyme. The salmon was seasoned with lemon zest, garlic powder, salt, and pepper, then pan-seared until golden.

Chad roasted the root vegetables at 425°F (220°C) until tender and slightly caramelized, then arranged them alongside the salmon on a large platter. The dish was garnished with fresh parsley, and the vegetables were drizzled with a light balsamic glaze. It was served on a simple wooden board for the clinic’s free blood-pressure screening week, with a bottle of water beside the plate.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, herbs, and searing fish. Chad’s meticulous presentation gave the meal an inviting, home-cooked feel, balancing the savory salmon with the bright, earthy vegetables. It was a simple yet filling dinner meant to provide energy for the day ahead.
