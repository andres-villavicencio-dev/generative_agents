Chad prepared Hearty Heirloom Risotto with Roasted Garlic and Lemon. The dish used Arborio rice, chicken broth, parmesan, toasted pine nuts, fresh thyme, olive oil, and crisp roasted garlic, with a bright citrus finish.

He toasted the garlic in a pan until golden, then sautéed the chicken. As the rice cooked, he slowly added the broth and stirred until the grains became tender but still held their texture. He finished the pan with Parmesan and a few pine nuts, then sliced the roasted garlic over the top to preserve its aroma.

The dish was presented in a shallow white bowl, with the golden garlic and lemon slices arranged around the edges and a small sprig of thyme placed in the center. Its earthy rice, savory chicken, and bright lemon balanced rich and fresh flavors. The occasion was Chad’s free blood-pressure screening week at the clinic, where he was sharing a simple, comforting meal with longtime patients and helping him connect with the community.
