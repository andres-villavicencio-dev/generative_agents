Cledith Hardin prepared a Lemon-Caper Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for dinner. She used whole chicken breasts, honey-syrup glaze, fresh lemon juice, capers, parsley, garlic, and a small bunch of carrots, parsnips, and potatoes that had been seasoned with rosemary, salt, and black pepper.

The chicken was seared in a hot pan until golden and then finished in the oven with the vegetables, which were tossed with olive oil, lemon zest, and rosemary. The dish was finished with fresh herbs and a drizzle of reduced pan sauce, while the lemon juice and capers added a bright, citrusy finish.

The aroma was bright and inviting: warm garlic, rosemary, caramelized honey, and lemon, with the earthy notes of the root vegetables. She served the meal on a simple white plate, arranging the chicken and vegetables in a gentle fan and adding a small bowl of lemony sauce beside it. The meal was a quiet Sunday dinner, prepared after reviewing the grant proposal notes and reflecting on the Oak Hill College postcard project.
