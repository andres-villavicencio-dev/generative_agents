Cledith prepared Rosemary Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for lunch at Oak Hill College. The dish featured a whole chicken seasoned with fresh rosemary, lemon zest, garlic, olive oil, thyme, and a splash of soy sauce, while carrots, parsnips, and potatoes were roasted until golden.

The chicken was seared briefly in a hot pan, then returned to the oven, where it basted with melted butter and rosemary. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a little flour before being roasted alongside the chicken. As they cooked, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of rosemary, garlic, citrus, and roasted vegetables.

Cledith presented the meal in a simple, inviting white ceramic bowl, arranging the golden vegetables beside the plump chicken and scattering a few rosemary sprigs and lemon wedges over it. It was a quiet, nourishing lunch, appropriate for a Wednesday afternoon when she was finishing her grant proposal and preparing for class.
