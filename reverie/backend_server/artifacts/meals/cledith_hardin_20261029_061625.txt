Cledith prepared a Rose-Glazed Herb Chicken with Roasted Potatoes and Grilled Asparagus for lunch. She began by rinsing the remaining ingredients—chicken thighs, potatoes, onions, garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon, and olive oil—then sliced the chicken and potatoes. In a large skillet, she sautéed the herbs and garlic until fragrant, then browned the chicken and potatoes. Asparagus was tossed with lemon juice and olive oil before being grilled alongside the vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, and caramelized potatoes. Cledith arranged the golden-brown chicken and potatoes on a serving platter, with fresh herbs and lemon slices adding a bright finish. She served it in a simple ceramic bowl, accompanied by a small salad and a glass of water.

The meal was prepared for lunchtime, but Cledith had no occasion to eat it. It became a quiet pause in her busy day: after finishing the grant proposal, she prepared the remaining ingredients, stored the leftovers, and began organizing the next meal. The kitchen worked as a place of preparation and care, while she reflected on the postcard project and her growing connection to the community she wanted to serve.
