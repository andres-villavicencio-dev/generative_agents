Cledith prepared Rosemary and Lemon Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables. The chicken was seasoned with rosemary, lemon zest, garlic, dried thyme, and cracked black pepper, then roasted with baby carrots, red potatoes, green beans, and a small onion. After a preliminary cook, the vegetables were added, and the chicken was basted with the pan juices until tender and golden brown.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted poultry, fresh herbs, lemon, garlic, and earthy root vegetables. Cledith arranged the vegetables in a simple, rustic pile around the chicken, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a few rosemary sprigs. A small bowl of lemon-tarrine sauce and a side of roasted garlic bread completed the table setting.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Thursday evening before Cledith checked the final postcard display labels, Mei Lin’s comments, and the approved grant proposal contacts. It was a thoughtful, nourishing dinner, not a formal celebration, but one that reflected his scholarly, nurturing nature and connection to the people he wanted to serve.
