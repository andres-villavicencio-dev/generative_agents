Cledith prepared Rosemary & Sage Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables, a simple Sunday dinner that reflected her scholarly, nurturing side. The chicken was marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, and sage, then roasted alongside carrots, potatoes, and red onions until golden and tender. Her mother’s apricot jam served as a sweet finishing sauce, while lemon zest added a bright, uplifting aroma.

The presentation was neat and inviting: a small white plate held the glazed chicken, surrounded by crisp vegetables and a mound of warm, caramelized potatoes. A sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge rested beside the dish, echoing the quiet, curated aesthetic she cherished in art and literature.

The occasion was an unhurried Thursday evening: Cledith had finished her grant proposal late into the night, checked Andrea Lovetro’s confirmation, and planned to share the inclusive display perspective with Mei Lin. The meal felt purposeful and nurturing, a comforting reminder that her scholarly life did not have to remain solely in the kitchen.
