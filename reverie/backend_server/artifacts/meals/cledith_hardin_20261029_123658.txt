Cledith Hardin prepared a Rosemary-Braised Chicken with Lemon-Feta and Roasted Root Vegetables for dinner. The chicken was marinated overnight in olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, thyme, and feta cheese, then browned in a skillet, followed by slow braising at 375°F for three-quarters of an hour. He added carrots, potatoes, and onions to the pan, stirring in a splash of water and a tablespoon of vegetable broth.

The kitchen filled with the warm, earthy aroma of rosemary, garlic, lemon, and caramelized onion. Cledith arranged the golden chicken, bright orange carrots, and crisp potatoes on a platter, garnishing them with fresh rosemary sprigs. He placed a handwritten note beside the meal: “For Mei Lin’s inclusive art exhibition. I hope you enjoy it.”

The simple, nourishing dinner was prepared in the early evening, when he also had time to read his grant proposal notes. The meal served him a quiet moment of purpose: nourishing his body while preparing to present inclusive art and cross-disciplinary work to colleagues and funders.
