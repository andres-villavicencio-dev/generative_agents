Cledith Hardin prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Butter Chicken, a simple yet nourishing dinner for a home-cooked meal. The dish featured carrots, sweet potatoes, red potatoes, and onions roasted until golden, with a savory chicken breast glazed in lemon and butter.

The chicken was pan-seared first, then simmered with the vegetables in a pan juices until tender and the flavors deeply integrated. Diced tomatoes and fresh parsley finished the plate with a bright citrus finish.

The aroma was warm and comforting—richly caramelized onions, roasted root vegetables, chicken, and lemon filled the kitchen. It smelled inviting and balanced, with a hint of buttery richness.

Cledith arranged the vegetables on a white plate, serving the golden chicken beside them. The presentation was clean and inviting, with the colorful vegetables arranged neatly around the protein. It was a modest, intimate dinner meant to nourish his mind and body as he prepared for the grant proposal deadline.
