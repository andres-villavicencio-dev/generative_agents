For Saturday, Cledith Hardin prepared Rosemary Lemon Herb Scrambled Eggs with Toasted Vegetables and a Grilled Tomato. She used two large eggs, fresh herbs, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, and finely diced tomatoes, potatoes, and onions. The eggs were lightly scrambled in a skillet until golden, then served alongside crusty bread and crisp vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, lemon, rosemary, and toasted nuts. Cledith arranged the eggs in a neat oval, with bright greens, golden toast, and red tomatoes completing the dish. It was a relaxed Saturday breakfast, prepared before her workday began.

The meal would have been the perfect companion for her Saturday morning routine and the display work she shared with Mei Lin. It reflected her scholarly, nurturing nature, creating a quiet, nourishing contrast to the creative inspiration she sought for the postcard exhibit.
