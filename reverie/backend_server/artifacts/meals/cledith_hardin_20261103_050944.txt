Cledith Hardin prepared Sautéed Egg with Herbed Butter and a Grainy Toasted Veggie, a simple family breakfast. She cracked two eggs into a small bowl, whisked them, and seasoned them with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. While the eggs cooked in butter, she toasted a slice of sourdough bread with diced carrots, celery, and sun-dried tomatoes until golden. The finished dish was plated on a white ceramic plate, with the eggs arranged neatly beside the toast. A small glass of warm milk accompanied the meal.

The aroma was warm, earthy, and subtly savory—the nutty butter mingling with the sharp crunch of the vegetables and the softness of the bread. It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast prepared for the morning’s routine, including the review of grant proposal notes. There was no special occasion, but the simplicity of the meal reflected Cledith’s nurturing side and her commitment to thoughtful, accessible nourishment.
