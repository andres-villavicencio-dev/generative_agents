Cledith prepared Rosemary Sage Breakfast Potatoes for Mei Lin. She used a mix of russet potatoes, carrots, onions, and garlic, topped with butter, chopped fresh rosemary, smoked paprika, lemon zest, and a touch of Dijon mustard. The vegetables were tossed in a hot pan until golden, then simmered briefly until tender. Cledith sliced them into neat squares, arranged them on a linen cloth, and placed a small bowl of warm oatmeal or yogurt beside them.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted potatoes, rosemary, and garlic. Cledith presented the meal simply yet invitingly, letting Mei Lin choose her favorite side. On February 14, the occasion was a quiet, intimate breakfast at home, followed by a brief call to check on her and Klaus Mueller and discuss the cross-disciplinary program.
