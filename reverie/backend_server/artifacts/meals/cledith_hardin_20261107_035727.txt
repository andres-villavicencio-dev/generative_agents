Cledith prepared Rosemary Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Crusty Bread. She selected a tender, bone-in chicken breast, seasoned it with salt, pepper, garlic powder, dried thyme, rosemary, lemon zest, and a splash of orange juice. She pan-seared the chicken until golden and juicy, then transferred it to a baking sheet and roasted it with carrots, potatoes, onions, and celery until the vegetables were tender and the chicken was fully cooked.

As the oven finished the dish, she added a small bouquet garni and returned the chicken to the heat for a final minute. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, lemon, and savory poultry. She plated the chicken in the center of a white ceramic plate, surrounded by steaming vegetables and golden crusty bread. The presentation was simple yet elegant: the rich, amber-colored chicken served with bright orange carrots, creamy potatoes, and crisp onions, all garnished with fresh rosemary and parsley.

She had the meal for Tuesday, February 14, the evening before the updated grant proposal and archival comparison with Andrea Lovetro, a quiet moment to nurture both her cooking and scholarly work before preparing for the next day.
