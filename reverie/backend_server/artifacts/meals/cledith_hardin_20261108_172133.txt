On a quiet Monday evening, Cledith Hardin prepared Cauliflower, Lemon, and Thyme Soup as a simple, nourishing dinner for himself. The dish relied on gently sautéed garlic, shallots, and thyme, then blended with cauliflower florets, diced carrots, and a few cups of vegetable broth. A splash of lemon juice and a pinch of salt gave the soup a bright, citrusy finish.

He cooked it in a small cast-iron pot over low heat, letting the aromatics release their fragrance while the cauliflower softened until it became tender. When finished, he let the soup cool slightly, then transferred it to a bowl and served it with a spoonful of crusty sourdough.

The kitchen smelled warmly of roasted garlic, thyme, and fresh lemon. He served himself a generous portion, the steam rising from the bowl as if the meal itself were inviting him in. After dinner, he washed his dishes and set out the remaining ingredients before taking a shower. This casual meal was a quiet reward for a long week of drafting an NHES grant proposal, mentoring Ayesha Khan and Klaus Mueller, and planning Mei Lin’s interdisciplinary program.
