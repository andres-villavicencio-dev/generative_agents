Dish: Rosemary Herb Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice and Grilled Veggies

Cledith prepared a simple family-style dinner for Tuesday evening, following the routine of his lecture schedule. The meal featured tender chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, rosemary, garlic, and lemon, along with rice seasoned with fresh herbs, and crisp vegetables including carrots, bell peppers, and onions.

The chicken was seared in a skillet until golden and cooked through, then sliced while the vegetables were roasted until tender. Cledith also prepared a side of warm noodles, garnished with sliced scallions and a drizzle of sesame oil.

Warm rosemary and garlic filled the kitchen as she prepared the meal, while the aroma of toasted nuts and citrus rose gently through the air. The presentation was modest yet inviting: the chicken rested on a white plate beside a small bowl of jasmine rice and a colorful medley of vegetables, with a single rosemary sprig added for visual balance.

The occasion was Cledith’s quiet evening at home, after a long day teaching literature and art history at Oak Hill College. It was a simple, nourishing meal that reflected her scholarly life—meticulous, nurturing, and grounded in the everyday routines of her domestic world.
