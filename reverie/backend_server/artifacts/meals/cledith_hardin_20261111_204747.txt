Cledith Hardin prepared Rosemary Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Creamy Garlic Sauce. The meal was prepared for an early Tuesday dinner while he followed up with Jane Moreno, applied for a shift, and continued his work on the National Endowment for the Humanities grant proposal.

He started with a large chicken breast, seasoned with salt, pepper, and rosemary, then placed it in a roasting pan alongside carrots, parsnips, potatoes, onions, and a handful of fresh rosemary sprigs. After browning the chicken until the skin was crisp, he covered the pan and roasted it in the oven at 400°F (200°C) until the chicken reached an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C). He basted the pan occasionally with the reserved pan juices and finished the sauce by simmering it with butter, garlic, and a splash of cream.

The kitchen filled with the warm, inviting scent of roasted garlic and rosemary. Cledith plated the chicken alongside the roasted vegetables, drizzled the creamy sauce over it, and garnished it with fresh parsley and a few rosemary sprigs. The dish was presented simply but richly, emphasizing earthy colors and textures. It was prepared for a quiet Tuesday dinner, though the purposeful day made the meal feel both comforting and part of his broader creative and professional routines.
