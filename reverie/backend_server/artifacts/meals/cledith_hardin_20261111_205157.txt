For Tuesday’s dinner, Cledith prepared a Rosemary-Glazed Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Roasted Potatoes. The recipe called for a whole chicken, a chicken bone-in roast, fresh rosemary, garlic, vegetable broth, olive oil, lemon, and carrots, onions, parsnips, and potatoes.

Cledith cut the chicken into even pieces, seasoned it with salt, pepper, and lemon, then roasted it until the skin became crisp and golden. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, salt, and pepper, then roasted until they were tender. He served the chicken alongside the vegetables and potatoes, garnishing the plate with a few sprigs of fresh rosemary and a lemon wedge.

The meal rose with the scent of rosemary, garlic, and caramelized chicken. It was served on a simple white dinner plate with a small bowl of warm salad and a glass of wine, creating a warm, balanced side dish that complemented the main dish. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday evening after a busy week: Cledith followed up with Jane Moreno, checked the bulletin board, helped Andrea with the grocery list, and applied for a grant while maintaining his work on the National Endowment for the Humanities proposal.
