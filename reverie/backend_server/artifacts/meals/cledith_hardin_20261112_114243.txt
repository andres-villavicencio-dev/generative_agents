Cledith prepared Midnight Rose Tea with Roasted Plum and Orange Blossom for a quiet Tuesday lunch. The dish combined delicate tea leaves, honey, dried plums, orange blossom petals, and a touch of lemon. She toasted the plums briefly in a pan, then simmered them with the tea until the plums softened and released a fragrant aroma. She served the tea in a clear glass with a small saucer of candied plum, orange blossoms, and lemon slices beside it.

The presentation was understated and warmly inviting: the translucent tea glowed with honey and citrus, while the plums and blossoms added color and subtle texture. Cledith arranged them gently on a white ceramic plate, letting the natural shapes create a small garden of colors. The meal was a calm, nourishing repast, fitting for a day when she worked on a grant proposal and planned a cross-disciplinary program with colleague Mei Lin.
