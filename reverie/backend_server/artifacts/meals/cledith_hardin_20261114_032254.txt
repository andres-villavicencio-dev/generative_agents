Cledith Hardin prepared Mediterranean Herb-Lentil Soup with Roasted Vegetables, a simple, nourishing breakfast suited to his scholarly routine. The dish consisted of lentils, carrots, onions, celery, and tomatoes, simmered in vegetable broth with fresh herbs and a touch of lemon. He toasted whole-grain bread, served it with crusty sourdough, and paired it with a small bowl of yogurt and walnuts.

The preparation began with the lentils, which he toasted lightly until fragrant, then returned to the pot. He added the roasted vegetables and chopped herbs, stirring until everything was tender and the broth had reduced slightly. The meal was served warm in a shallow bowl, with the bread resting beside it. Its aroma was earthy and herbaceous, with notes of roasted carrots, onion, and lemon.

The occasion was his quiet Monday morning, a rare moment of calm before drafting the grant proposal. The modest breakfast offered him balance and focus, reflecting his meticulous, nurturing nature.
