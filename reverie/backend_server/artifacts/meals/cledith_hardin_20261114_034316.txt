Cledith prepared Rosemary-Infused Quinoa and Lemon-Butter Omelet, a simple, nourishing breakfast for Ayesha and Klaus. He boiled quinoa and chopped it with warm water, then sautéed it with butter, minced garlic, and fresh rosemary. Aegir eggs were scrambled gently, seasoned with lemon juice, black pepper, and a pinch of salt, and served warm alongside the grains.

The aroma of toasted quinoa, butter, garlic, and rosemary filled the kitchen, while the bright yellow eggs added a cheerful visual contrast. Cledith placed the omelet on a shallow white plate, garnished with a sprig of rosemary and a few lemon halves, before setting it beside a mug of tea. This quiet, unhurried meal was intended to encourage Ayesha and Klaus to focus on their final essays, while Cledith took a moment to check on Mei Lin’s cross-disciplinary plans.
