On a Tuesday morning, Dalton prepared Cajun-Style Red Rice with Smoked Chicken and Green Onions for a quiet meal on the Willows Market. He paired it with a glass of chilled lemon-lime soda and a small plate of crusty whole-grain toast.

Dalton began by sautéing sliced red pepper and onion in a large skillet, then added a can of diced tomatoes and a handful of fresh basil. After simmering the mixture, he transferred the sauce to a rice cooker, letting the red rice absorb the flavors. Meanwhile, he sliced smoked chicken breast, seasoned it with garlic and smoked paprika, and pan-seared it until the exterior was crisp. A few green onions were added to the skillet for a final burst of color.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onions, smoky chicken, and toasted rice. Dalton plated the dish in a shallow white bowl, arranging the chicken pieces, green onions, and red pepper around a central mound of red rice. He finished it with a generous drizzle of buffalo sauce and a sprinkle of chives.

The occasion was a simple weekday breakfast, but Dalton treated it with the same attention he applied to his supply-chain routes. The meal felt both comforting and deliberately thoughtful—a quiet reminder of the meticulous care he brings to every aspect of his life.
