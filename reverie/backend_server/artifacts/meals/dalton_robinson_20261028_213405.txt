Dalton prepared Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple weeknight dinner inspired by the bluesy, rustic warmth of his music.

He sliced a whole chicken, seasoned it with garlic, salt, pepper, and dried thyme, then brushed it with olive oil before baking it at 400°F until the skin was golden and the meat was nearly done. The oven also roasted carrots, potatoes, zucchini, and bell peppers with lemon juice and a splash of water. Dalton finished the pan with fresh parsley and served it over a bed of the roasted root vegetables.

The aroma of warm garlic, savory chicken, and crispy roasted veggies filled the kitchen. He plated the meal in a white ceramic dish with a small silver bowl of lemon wedges, arranged the vegetables neatly beside the chicken, and added a single sprig of parsley to each plate. It was a quiet, comforting meal for a rainy Monday evening—a simple comfort food that felt both deliberately prepared and warmly familiar.
