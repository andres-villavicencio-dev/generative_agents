Dalton prepared Braised Short Ribs with Herb-Roasted Potatoes, the main dish for a quiet Monday dinner.

The short ribs were smoked over oak and slowly braised in a rich brown-butter sauce with carrots, onions, celery, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a splash of red wine. He added a few slices of bacon, then simmered them until the meat fell off the bone and the sauce darkened into a glossy, aromatic glaze. For the potatoes, he roasted them with olive oil, garlic, and a few cracked black peppercorns, tossing them briefly in the same pan before baking them whole.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory depth, caramelized garlic, rosemary, and smoked oak. The short ribs were tender and flavorful, while the potatoes remained crisp around the edges. Dalton plated them on a large white platter, arranging the glossy ribs in a neat diagonal line and placing the roasted potatoes beside them.

He prepared the meal for an early family dinner, balancing the rich main dish with crisp sides and a warm salad. The presentation felt both rustic and inviting, suitable for a relaxed dinner with friends and family.
