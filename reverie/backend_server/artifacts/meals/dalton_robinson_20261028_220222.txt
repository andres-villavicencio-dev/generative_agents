Dalton prepared Blue-Crusted Lemon Herb Salmon with Asparagus and Roasted Root Vegetables. The salmon fillet, fresh asparagus, carrots, parsnips, and potatoes were pan-seared in olive oil until the edges were golden and the flesh reached a delicate pink. A sharp blue cheese reduction was finished with lemon juice, thyme, garlic, capers, and a pinch of flaky sea salt.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized onions and herbs, the crisp scent of rosemary, and the earthy fragrance of roasted root vegetables. Dalton arranged the fish in a shallow silver dish, placing each fillet in the center and sprinkling the vegetables around it. He served the side with a small bowl of lemon-tarrine, a wedge of lemon, and a handful of arugula.

The meal was a quiet Sunday dinner for Dalton and Tom while they discussed delivery routes. It was simple, elegant, and perfectly suited to the blues-and-jazz atmosphere of the Willows Market and Hobbs Cafe. Dalton’s meticulous attention to the plate’s balance and his careful seasoning showed his lifelong passion for music: every ingredient, from the delicate salmon to the roasted vegetables, contributed to a cohesive rhythm.
