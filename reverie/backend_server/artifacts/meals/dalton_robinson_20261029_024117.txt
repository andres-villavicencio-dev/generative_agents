Dalton prepared Hobbs Cafe Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and Gravy. After a long Tuesday, he cooked the roast chicken with rosemary, garlic, and thyme, then added carrots, potatoes, and celery to the pan. He sliced onions and shallots into a rich brown gravy, seasoned the mixture with salt, pepper, and a splash of Worcestershire sauce, and finished it with a generous handful of fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with the warm, earthy aroma of roasted turkey, caramelized onions, and garlic. Dalton arranged the golden-brown chicken around the pan, plated the vegetables, and drizzled the gravy over the top. He served it on a simple white platter with crusty bread and a side of green salad, creating a rustic, inviting display.

The meal was prepared for dinner on a cold Tuesday evening. Although it was not a celebratory occasion, Dalton found comfort in the familiar rhythm of preparing a hearty Sunday-style dinner. His meticulous approach reflected the care he had put into his route planning, and the cozy aroma reminded him of the blues and jazz he loved.
