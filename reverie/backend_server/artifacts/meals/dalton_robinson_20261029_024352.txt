Dalton prepared a hearty Smoked Chicken and Root Vegetable Skillet, a classic family-style dinner suitable for the Tuesday, February 14 celebration. He used a whole chicken that he had previously measured and weighed, along with carrots, potatoes, onions, bell peppers, and a generous portion of smoked-sauce gravy. The chicken was seared until golden, then simmered with the vegetables in the gravy until tender, finishing with a splash of lemon juice and fresh parsley.

Dalton arranged the skillet over a gas burner, placing the chicken in the center and the colorful vegetables around it. A small bowl of lemon-herb gravy, a mound of crisp potatoes, and a bright garnish of parsley completed the presentation. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm smoked meat, caramelized onions, roasted potatoes, and the subtle scent of fresh herbs.

The meal was prepared for the small gathering during the delivery window, combining Dalton’s meticulous approach with the relaxed rhythm of dinner. It was ready to serve, with the chicken still warm and the vegetables vibrant enough to encourage lively conversation.
