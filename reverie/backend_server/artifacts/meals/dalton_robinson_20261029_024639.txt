Dalton prepared Smoked Bacon and Creamy Tomato Pasta for Tuesday evening dinner. The dish featured smoked bacon, creamy tomato sauce, whole wheat pasta, black olives, cherry tomatoes, garlic, parsley, and a drizzle of olive oil and lemon juice. He sliced the bacon into strips, fried it in a skillet until crisp, then deglazed the pan with garlic and onions before simmering the sauce with tomatoes, herbs, and pasta.

The aroma was rich and savory: bacon rendered a nutty smoke, earthy tomatoes, and warm garlic mingling with the gentle warmth of the olive oil. Dalton plated the pasta in a wide, shallow pan, arranging the bacon in a decorative row and scattering the olives, tomatoes, and chopped herbs over it. A bright lemon wedge and a sprinkle of fresh parsley added a lively finish.

The meal was prepared for a quiet, Sunday-style dinner. Dalton’s meticulous attention to detail kept the ingredients balanced and the presentation inviting, while his lifelong passion for blues and jazz music added a contemplative, rhythmic mood to the evening. It was a simple, home-cooked meal, but one that felt carefully prepared and thoughtfully presented.
