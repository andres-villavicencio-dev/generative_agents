Dalton prepared a Hearty Blueberry-Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Marinade, cooked in his kitchen at Hobbs Cafe. He used a whole chicken, lemon, fresh orange, honey, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a generous amount of blueberries, which he tossed with the marinade overnight. He grilled the chicken over an open flame until the skin turned golden and the juices ran clear, then sliced it into the pan and added a small amount of the remaining marinade.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of lemon zest, honey, rosemary, garlic, and blueberries. He served the chicken alongside sautéed asparagus, warm mashed potatoes, and a bright lemon-herb salad. The meal was plated in a rustic serving dish, with a small blueberry garnish beside the chicken, creating a warm, inviting presentation.

It was prepared for a Thursday dinner gathering at Hobbs Cafe, where Dalton and his team were celebrating the successful delivery windows and route-checklist for the upcoming Tuesday. The meal reflected Dalton’s meticulous attention to detail and his lifelong appreciation for the everyday rhythms of the Willows Market and Hobbs Cafe.
