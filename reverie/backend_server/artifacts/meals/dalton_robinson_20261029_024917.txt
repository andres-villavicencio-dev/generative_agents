On a Tuesday morning, Dalton prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze for dinner at home. The main dish featured a whole chicken glazed in a bright lemon-herb mixture of fresh lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a touch of honey. Alongside it, he roasted carrots, parsnips, and potatoes until golden, then arranged them around the chicken in a shallow baking dish.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted poultry, warm root vegetables, and citrus. Dalton roasted everything on a gas stove, then brushed the glaze over the chicken and returned it to the oven for the last five minutes. A small bowl of lemon wedges and fresh parsley completed the platter.

The meal was served on a Tuesday evening during a quiet moment of routine, not a special occasion. It was a simple, comforting dinner prepared with care, reflecting Dalton’s meticulous nature and love for flavorful, home-cooked food.
