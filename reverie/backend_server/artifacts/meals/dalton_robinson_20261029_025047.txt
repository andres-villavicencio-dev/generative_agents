Dalton prepared Smoked Haddock with Lemon-Lime Herb Crust for dinner. He began by seasoning the haddock with smoked paprika, garlic, lemon zest, and a pinch of salt, then pan-seared it until the skin became crisp and the flesh was golden. He sliced the fish into portions and topped each piece with the herb crust, which he made from flour, chopped parsley, lemon zest, a little oil, and a small amount of smoked paprika.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of smoked fish, toasted breadcrumbs, garlic, and fresh herbs. Dalton arranged the haddock on a white plate and topped each portion with a small dollop of lemon-lime cream sauce, finishing the dish with a sprig of parsley.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday evening. After checking the fridge and ordering groceries, Dalton felt satisfied by the meal’s balance of salty, smoky, and citrus flavors. He ate it slowly, appreciating how the careful preparation made the ordinary feel special.
