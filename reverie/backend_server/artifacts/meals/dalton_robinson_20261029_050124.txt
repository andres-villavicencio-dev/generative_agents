Dalton prepared a Hearty Chicken and Vegetable skillet for lunch, a quick, filling meal he used to make before delivering groceries to the Willows Market and Hobbs Cafe. The dish included chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, onions, celery, and a touch of garlic, all cooked with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a splash of lemon juice. He browned the chicken first, then sautéed the vegetables until they softened and turned bright orange, letting the flavors meld together in the skillet.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and earthy—savory chicken, caramelized vegetables, and a subtle citrus note. Dalton arranged the meal on a simple white platter, arranging the golden chicken around the colorful vegetables and using a small spoon to garnish the center with fresh parsley. It was a practical, family-style lunch prepared on a Wednesday afternoon, designed to sustain him through the busy route between the Willows Market and Hobbs Cafe.
