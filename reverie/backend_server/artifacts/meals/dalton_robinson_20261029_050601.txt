Dalton prepared a simple Sunday Gravy Chicken with Root Vegetables. The dish uses bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, celery, onions, garlic, fresh parsley, and a creamy broth made from chicken stock, butter, and a pinch of lemon juice.

He started by searing the chicken until the edges turned golden, then added the vegetables and broth. The chicken was simmered gently in the oven until the liquid reduced and the juices began to thicken. Dalton transferred the finished chicken to a serving platter and arranged the carrots and potatoes around it, garnishing each with a small sprig of parsley.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, caramelized onions, and savory broth. The golden chicken glowed under the oven’s light, while the vegetables held their shape and added color. It was a quiet, comforting lunch prepared for a Wednesday at Willows Market, with the team and Tom Moreno nearby to help him coordinate the route.
