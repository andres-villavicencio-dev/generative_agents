Dalton prepared a hearty Sunday Roast Chicken with Herb-Crusted Vegetables for his Wednesday dinner. The centerpiece was a whole chicken rubbed with flour, garlic, rosemary, thyme, paprika, and a pinch of salt, then baked until golden and juicy. Around it were green beans, carrots, and potatoes tossed in butter and seasoned with the same herb mix.

The kitchen smelled warm and aromatic—rich roasted herbs, savory garlic, caramelized carrots, and the gentle scent of buttery herbs. Dalton arranged the roasted chicken in the center, with the vegetables nestled around it in a rustic, layered presentation, topped with a golden crust and a small fresh herb sprig.

The meal was a relaxed weekday dinner, prepared just before he continued his route optimization between the Willows Market and Hobbs Cafe with Tom Moreno and Carmen Ortiz. He had finished Monday’s deliveries, coordinated the updated schedule with Job Amely, and was ready to begin his shift.
