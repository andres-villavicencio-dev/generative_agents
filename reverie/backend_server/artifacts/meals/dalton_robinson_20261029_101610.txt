Dalton prepared Mediterranean Herb Chicken Salad with Lemon-Tahini Dressing, a simple, nourishing lunch for his route. He began with chicken breasts that he had marinated for several hours, cucumber and red onion slices, cherry tomatoes, celery, and feta, and arugula. He added lemon juice, olive oil, chopped parsley, and a pinch of dried oregano, then dressed the salad with a light, creamy lemon-tahini sauce.

The aroma of the dish was warm and inviting: the savory scent of the chicken, the fresh tang of lemon, and the crispness of the vegetables mingled with the earthy richness of the feta and herbs. Dalton served the salad in a white ceramic bowl, arranged the ingredients in neat rows, and placed a small card with his delivery route and parking instructions nearby. The lunch was prepared to last through the workday, and it was a quiet, thoughtful meal while he continued optimizing his route between the Willows Market and Hobbs Cafe.
