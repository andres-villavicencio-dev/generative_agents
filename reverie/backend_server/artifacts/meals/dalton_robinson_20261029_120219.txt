Dalton prepared a Mediterranean Herb-Crusted Chicken with Lemon-Herb Potato Salad for a Thursday lunch at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured a whole chicken rubbed with olive oil, garlic, dried rosemary, thyme, and sea salt, then pan-seared until the skin turned crisp and golden. Alongside it were small potatoes tossed with olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, honey, and fresh herbs, while a small bowl of chickpeas, cucumber, carrots, and feta completed the platter.

The aroma was bright and warming: roasted garlic, olive oil, herbs, and the subtle richness of lemon juice filled the kitchen. Dalton arranged the chicken in a shallow white bowl with the vibrant potato salad, garnishing the center with a few rosemary sprigs and a drizzle of lemon-herb oil. The presentation was clean and inviting, with contrasting textures and colors that made the meal feel fresh and celebratory.

It was prepared for an afternoon lunch break at Hobbs Cafe, serving the staff after work. The simple, balanced ingredients created a wholesome, comforting meal, while Dalton’s attention to detail ensured the food looked and tasted ready to eat.
