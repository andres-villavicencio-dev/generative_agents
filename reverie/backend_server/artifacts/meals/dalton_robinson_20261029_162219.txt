The meal was a hearty Hearty Blueberry-Scramble Breakfast, prepared to last through the restocking trip. Dalton used eggs, blueberries, spinach, diced onions, whole-grain toast, and a little butter. He lightly scrambled the eggs and spinach until the edges were crisp, then folded them into the warm, fluffy toast. The blueberries were softened by heating a small amount of butter with the eggs, giving the breakfast a bright blue-purple hue.

The aroma was rich and comforting: savory eggs, sweet blueberries, earthy spinach, and the warm scent of toasted butter filled the kitchen. Dalton presented the breakfast on a white plate, arranging the toast beside the eggs and garnishing it with fresh basil and a drizzle of maple syrup.

It was prepared for a quiet Friday breakfast, after he returned to replenish the groceries and restock the kitchen before heading out to work. The meal was practical, satisfying, and ready to prevent the pantry from running out again.
