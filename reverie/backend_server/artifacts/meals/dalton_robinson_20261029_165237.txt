Dalton prepared Blueberry-Glazed Cornmeal Pancakes for breakfast. He used flour, eggs, milk, vanilla, butter, blueberries, maple syrup, and a pinch of cinnamon. He whisked the batter, cooked the pancakes in a nonstick pan until golden, and brushed the tops with warm syrup before serving them alongside a glass of iced tea and a small glass of milk.

The kitchen filled with a fragrant aroma of warm syrup, caramelized butter, and fresh blueberries. The pancakes were stacked on a white plate, garnished with a few berries and a dusting of cinnamon, with a small bowl of fruit preserves nearby. The occasion was a quiet, purposeful breakfast on a Friday morning before his route-checklist adjustments and scheduled delivery work.
