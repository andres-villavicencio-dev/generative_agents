Dalton prepared a Grilled Chicken and Roasted Vegetable Stir-Fry for a simple Friday dinner. He marinated chicken breast with lemon zest, garlic, and herbs, then grilled it until the skin was golden and the meat was cooked through. He roasted broccoli, carrots, bell pepper, and snap peas in a skillet with olive oil, soy sauce, and ginger until the vegetables were crisp-tender.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, ginger, grilled chicken, and roasted vegetables. Dalton plated the meal in a shallow bowl, arranging the vegetables around the chicken and garnishing it with fresh herbs. He served it simply with a side of quinoa and a squeeze of lemon.

The meal was prepared on Friday, February 17, after Dalton had adjusted his route checklist and updated schedule. It was a relaxed, home-cooked dinner, fitting his routine of waking early, exercising, eating, studying, and completing deliveries on time.
