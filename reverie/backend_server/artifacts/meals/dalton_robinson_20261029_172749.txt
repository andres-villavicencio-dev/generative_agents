Dalton prepared Chicken and Rice with Lemon-Herb Crust, a simple, hearty meal inspired by the neighborhood grocery store. He used a whole chicken, garlic, onion, carrots, celery, chicken broth, rice, chicken breast, and a pinch of salt and pepper. The chicken was browned in a pan, then simmered in the broth until tender. The rice was cooked separately, and a lemon, fresh herbs, and a little butter were added for brightness.

Assemble the pan: arrange the golden chicken and fluffy rice on a serving platter, then spoon the lemon-herb sauce over it. Sprinkle a few fresh herbs over the top before serving.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, and tender rice. The golden-brown crust created a pleasing contrast against the bright yellow rice and pale chicken. Dalton presented the dish in a shallow white bowl, with a small plate of lemon wedges beside it. It was the perfect Friday dinner—a relaxed, nourishing meal to make time after an errand while keeping his route-checklist and certification goals in mind.
