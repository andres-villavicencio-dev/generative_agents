Dalton prepared a simple breakfast of scrambled spinach and tomato eggs with toasted bread and a glass of orange juice.

The eggs were cracked into a pan, seasoned with a pinch of salt, and cooked until the whites were firm but the yolks remained soft. Spinach was added toward the end to keep it tender, with a tablespoon of olive oil and a splash of vinegar giving it a bright finish. Toasted sourdough and sliced tomatoes added a warm, earthy contrast. Dalton used fresh tomatoes, spinach, eggs, olive oil, salt, pepper, and lemon juice.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of egg and tomato, the earthy scent of fresh spinach, and the crisp note of toasted bread. He arranged the eggs and toast in a neat row on a white plate, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a lemon slice. The small, colorful breakfast was prepared for an early workday, offering a quiet moment of nourishment before Dalton began his route-checklist adjustments and blues-and-jazz exploration.
