Dalton prepared Creamy Tuscan Chicken with Lemon-Herb Bread. He marinated chicken thighs in olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, thyme, and a touch of Parmesan, then seared them until golden and cooked through. The chicken was finished in a pan with cream, garlic, parsley, and a splash of white wine before being served with crusty sourdough.

He toasted the bread in a pan, spreading it with garlic butter and lemon juice. The aroma was warm, savory, and citrusy, with the rich chicken and herbs releasing subtle notes of garlic, thyme, and lemon. Dalton plated the chicken beside the bread in a simple, inviting arrangement, arranging the lemon wedges and parsley around the dish.

He prepared the meal for a quiet Friday dinner, using the structured routine to balance preparation with route planning. The relaxed occasion made the effort feel purposeful, and he enjoyed the comforting, homey flavors as a small reward for completing his route checklist.
