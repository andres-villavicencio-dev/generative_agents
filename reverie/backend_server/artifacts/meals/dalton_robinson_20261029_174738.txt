Dalton prepared Oatmeal and Fresh Fruit, a warm, simple breakfast designed for a quick weekday morning. The dish featured rolled oats cooked in water, topped with chopped apples, sliced bananas, and a drizzle of honey. He added a teaspoon of cinnamon for warmth and a sprinkle of nutmeg.

The preparation method was straightforward: oatmeal was heated in a small pan until the oats softened, then the fruit was arranged on top and allowed to warm through. Dalton chose the oats to balance the freshness of the fruit with a comforting base, while the cinnamon and nutmeg provided gentle warmth without overpowering the natural sweetness of the apples and bananas.

As the kitchen warmed, the familiar aroma of oats, fruit, and spice filled the room. He arranged the oatmeal in a shallow ceramic dish, garnished with a few apple wedges and a small pile of crushed nuts, and placed a glass of water beside it. The presentation was neat and inviting, suitable for a quiet Saturday morning before his workday. There was no occasion for a special meal, but the simple, nourishing breakfast added to his routine of waking early, exercising, eating, and planning his route with purpose.
