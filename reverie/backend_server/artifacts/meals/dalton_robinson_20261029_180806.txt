On Friday, February 17, Dalton prepared Bread-and-Egg Breakfast with Milk and Jam. The meal was a simple, wholesome breakfast made with freshly baked bread, boiled eggs, whole milk, butter, and a generous serving of berries and jam.

Dalton toasted the bread until it was crisp, cracked the eggs into a pan, and whisked the milk and butter into the pan. Once the eggs were set, he added the jam and served them on the warm bread. The aroma of toasted bread, creamy milk, and sweet fruit filled the kitchen, while the golden-brown crust and glossy egg whites gave the breakfast a cheerful, inviting appearance.

It was a relaxed, home-style breakfast intended for his morning routine. The meal reflected his meticulous, dependable nature: everything was prepared carefully and served in a warm, neat presentation, helping him begin the day with energy and comfort.
