Dalton prepared a Blueberry-Buttermilk Pancake Breakfast for a light Friday morning snack. The dish was made with flour, milk, sugar, eggs, vanilla, and a pinch of salt, topped with fresh blueberries and a splash of buttermilk. He stirred the batter until it was smooth, cooked the pancakes on a lightly greased skillet until golden, and served them with a side of cold cream cheese and a glass of water.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of butter, warm milk, and fruit, while the pancakes offered a gentle, golden aroma. Dalton presented them on a white plate with a small blueberry garnish, arranged neatly for the small breakfast he needed before heading to the grocery store and preparing dinner. The meal was simple, balanced, and purposeful—a quiet way to replenish his pantry without needing a full-cook out of bread or eggs.
