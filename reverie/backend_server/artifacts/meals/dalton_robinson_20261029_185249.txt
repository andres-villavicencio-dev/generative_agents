On Friday, February 17, Dalton prepared Hearty Smoked-Salt Pork and Root Vegetable Skillet for a quiet breakfast at his delivery home. He used smoked pork shoulder, baby potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, and fresh chives. After browning the pork, he added the vegetables and cooked them until tender, then finished the dish with a splash of milk and a generous dusting of salt.

The kitchen filled with warm, savory aromas of smoked meat, caramelized onions, and roasted potatoes. Dalton presented the meal in a shallow, rustic ceramic bowl, garnished with chopped chives and a tiny sprig of parsley. The dish was simple, comforting, and ready to be eaten alongside a cup of coffee.

It was not a formal occasion, but a peaceful way to start his Friday. Dalton’s meticulous attention to detail made the meal feel thoughtfully prepared, while the familiar flavors offered a sense of ease and contentment.
