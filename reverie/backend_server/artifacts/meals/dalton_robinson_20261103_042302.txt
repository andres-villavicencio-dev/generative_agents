Dalton prepared a simple Hearty Sunday Roast for a Saturday family gathering. He used a large oven-safe roasting pan, a whole chicken, garlic, rosemary, thyme, onion, carrots, potatoes, and a cup of whole milk. He seasoned the chicken with salt and pepper, added the vegetables to the pan, covered it tightly, and roasted it at 375°F (190°C) for about 45 minutes until the chicken was fully cooked and the vegetables were tender. He finished the dish by browning the pan juices and serving everything over a bed of roasted potatoes.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the rich scent of garlic, herbs, roasted chicken, and caramelized onions filling the kitchen. He plated the golden chicken and vegetables on a white platter, with a cup of warm milk and a small side of salad nearby. The occasion was a relaxed Sunday family meal, and Dalton’s careful attention to the ingredients and presentation made it feel comforting and inviting.
