On Saturday, February 18, Dalton prepared Garden-Sautéed Spinach and Lemon-Herb Potatoes, a quick, satisfying side dish for his breakfast. The dish was simple and colorful: finely diced potatoes, chopped fresh spinach, and a handful of cherry tomatoes were sautéed in olive oil until tender. Fresh basil and lemon juice were stirred in just before serving, while a pinch of sea salt and a few drops of hot pepper oil added a subtle heat.

Dalton cut the potatoes into uniform cubes, chopped the spinach, and sautéed everything in a large skillet over medium heat. He arranged the colorful vegetables in a shallow dish and topped them with the golden, crisp potatoes. The kitchen was filled with the earthy aroma of savory potatoes, the fresh scent of basil, and the bright zing of lemon.

The meal was presented as a cheerful, rustic breakfast, perfect for a leisurely Saturday. Its warm colors and simple flavors made it an inviting addition to a planned routine, while the fresh ingredients reminded Dalton of the organic, hands-on approach that had helped him keep the supply store and delivery routes running smoothly.
