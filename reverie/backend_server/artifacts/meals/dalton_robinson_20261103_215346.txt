Dalton prepared Grilled Chicken with Herb-Lemon Butter, a simple yet elegant lunch for the small gathering at Hobbs Cafe.

He sliced a chicken breast and marinated it in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, fresh parsley, and a pinch of smoked paprika, letting it rest for ten minutes. The chicken was then grilled over medium heat until the skin turned golden and the juices ran clear. Topping it were crisp romaine lettuce, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and a light drizzle of butter.

Aromatically, the meal smelled of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and a hint of wood smoke. Dalton carefully arranged the colorful vegetables and golden chicken in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with a sprig of parsley. He served it at the table with a glass of water, the crisp lettuce, and a small plate of whole grain crackers.

The occasion was a quiet family lunch, with Dalton’s wife, Tom, and their young children sharing the meal. His meticulous preparation reflected his dependable nature, while the relaxed atmosphere and familiar flavors made the simple lunch feel inviting and warm.
