At 6:00 p.m., Dalton prepared Cajun-Infused Chicken and Rice for his three meals: breakfast, lunch, and dinner. The dish featured bone-in chicken thighs, rice, carrots, celery, onions, bell peppers, and garlic, seasoned with smoked paprika, cayenne, black pepper, and a splash of lemon juice.

Dalton began by lightly searing the chicken in a large skillet until the skin was golden and crisp, then added the aromatics and vegetables to the same pan. He cooked everything over medium heat, stirring occasionally until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were tender-crisp. The rice was stirred in just before the end, then covered briefly so it absorbed the seasoning.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of caramelized garlic, citrus, and smoked paprika. He plated the chicken in the center of a white plate, arranged the vegetables around it, and served the warm rice beside it. The meal was simple yet festive—a quiet family dinner to balance the busy delivery shifts and prepare for a quiet evening.
