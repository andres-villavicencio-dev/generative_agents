Dalton prepared a quick Shepherd’s Pie with Gravy for himself and his breakfast. The dish was made with ground beef, diced onions, garlic, carrots, and potatoes, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a splash of Worcestershire sauce. He browned the meat, sautéed the vegetables, simmered the filling until tender, then layered it with creamy mashed potatoes and topped it with golden-brown gravy.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted beef, caramelized onions, and warm spices. Dalton presented the pie in a rectangular baking dish, garnished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of gravy. The meal was simple, comforting, and hearty—a quiet breakfast that balanced his morning routine.

He cooked it for himself and returned to the store afterward to review his blues and jazz records before heading home. The meal marked a relaxed moment of reflection before he resumed his work, and the familiar smell of the house brought him back to the quiet rhythm of his life.
