Dalton prepared Grilled Lemon-Herb Chicken with Roasted Brussels Sprouts, the main portion of his lunch.

He sliced a whole chicken, seasoned it with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, dried thyme, and smoked paprika, then grilled it over a medium heat until the skin was golden and the interior was cooked through. Alongside the chicken, he tossed Brussels sprouts in olive oil, salt, pepper, and a dash of lemon juice, then roasted them until crisp and caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and herbs, the crisp scent of roasted vegetables, and the warm perfume of grilled chicken. Dalton arranged the chicken in the center of a white plate and scattered the golden-brown Brussels sprouts around it, using the lemon zest as a colorful garnish. The meal looked simple and inviting, fitting his meticulous, home-cooked style.

It was a quiet weekday lunch while Dalton worked with Tom Moreno and Carmen Ortiz to fine-tune the Willows Market and Hobbs Cafe delivery routes, balancing his work with his lifelong interest in music and his goal to improve supply-chain management.
