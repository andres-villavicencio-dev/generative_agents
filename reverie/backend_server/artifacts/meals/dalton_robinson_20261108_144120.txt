Dalton prepared Herb-Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Tahini Toast, a warm side dish designed to complement the heaping serving of stewed chicken at dinner. He roasted carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes in a skillet with olive oil, garlic, thyme, and a pinch of cayenne, then finished them under a broiler until the edges were crisp and the centers were tender. Lemon juice and a generous amount of tahini were stirred in at the end to brighten the flavors without overpowering the earthiness of the root vegetables.

The aroma that filled the kitchen was comforting and citrusy, with the scent of roasted carrots, toasted bread, garlic, and thyme blending into a warm, earthy fragrance. Dalton arranged the vegetables in a rustic pan, topped them with golden toast, and sprinkled fresh parsley over the dish before serving it at the table.

This was part of a relaxed Monday lunch, with Dalton carefully balancing his delivery duties, supply-chain certification study, and lifelong passion for blues and jazz. The meal was simple but thoughtfully prepared, reflecting his meticulous nature while offering a familiar, satisfying meal.
