Dalton prepared Blueberry and Lemon Herb Pancakes with Maple-Glazed Bacon, a hearty breakfast item for Hobbs Cafe. The pancakes were made with sifted flour, eggs, milk, blueberries, and a pinch of baking powder, then cooked in a hot skillet with a little butter until golden and fluffy. He folded the berries into the center and topped them with crispy bacon strips, fresh lemon slices, a drizzle of maple syrup, and a dusting of cinnamon.

Aromatic and fragrant, the pancakes released the scent of warm butter, sweet berries, and citrus, while the bacon gave the meal a savory, earthy kick. Dalton arranged the pancakes and toppings carefully on a white ceramic plate, using a spatula to create a neat, layered presentation.

He prepared this meal for a Tuesday breakfast service at Hobbs Cafe, where the café was preparing the order for a rush of customers. Dalton’s meticulous attention to the recipe and his dependable preparation method helped ensure the pancakes arrived hot and appealing to every customer.
