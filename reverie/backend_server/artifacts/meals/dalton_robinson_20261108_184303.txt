Dalton prepared a Hearty Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Herb Crisp, a comforting family-style meal he made for Job after a long day of deliveries. He seasoned the chicken with smoked paprika, garlic, lemon zest, thyme, rosemary, and a touch of black pepper, then roasted it at 400°F until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. The chicken was served alongside roasted butternut squash, carrots, and potatoes, all arranged on a large platter and garnished with chopped parsley.

The kitchen smelled warm and aromatic—rich roasted poultry, earthy root vegetables, and the bright, citrusy notes of lemon and thyme filled the room. The chicken glistened with golden crisp edges, while the vegetables retained a soft, caramelized texture. A simple herb-crusted biscuit and a glass of cold lemon water accompanied the meal.

It was a quiet, cozy dinner prepared for Job during the evening commute. Dalton’s meticulous attention to the Thomas More pipe route turned into an ordinary day, but the careful preparation and warm presentation made the meal feel special.
