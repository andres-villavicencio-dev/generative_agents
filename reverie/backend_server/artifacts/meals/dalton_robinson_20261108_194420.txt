Meal: “Willows Market” Grilled Chicken with Lemon Garlic Herb Rice and Roasted Root Vegetables

Dish name: Grilled chicken with lemon garlic herb rice and roasted root vegetables

Key ingredients: bone-in chicken thighs, chicken thighs, rice, carrots, red potatoes, onions, garlic, lemon, olive oil, oregano, salt, pepper, and a splash of chicken broth.

Preparation method: The chicken was marinated with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper, then grilled until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. The rice was cooked with the chicken juices, carrots, and potatoes, seasoned with oregano, salt, and pepper, then fluffed until tender. A final sprinkle of parsley and a squeeze of lemon finished the dish.

Aroma: Warm, herbaceous, and savory, with the sharp tang of lemon, rich garlic, and the soft smoky scent of grilled chicken.

Presentation: The meal was plated on a white ceramic platter. The chicken rested on a bed of golden rice, surrounded by bright orange carrots and crisp red potatoes. Lemon wedges and a small bowl of parsley were set beside it for easy serving.

Occasion: This was a simple weekday family dinner for Job and the Robinson family, prepared for a quiet Tuesday evening.
