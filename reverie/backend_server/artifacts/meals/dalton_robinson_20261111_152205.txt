Dalton prepared Blueberry and Smoked Salmon Skillet with Lemon-Herb Crust. Using the morning’s remaining ingredients, he sliced fresh blueberries and smoked salmon, seasoned the salmon with lemon zest, salt, pepper, and a pinch of paprika, then arranged them in a skillet. To create the crumb, he mixed flour, lemon zest, chopped parsley, garlic, and a little olive oil. After gently heating the pan, he pressed the flour mixture over the salmon and berries, then baked it until the crust turned golden and the fish was just firm.

The kitchen filled with the sharp scent of smoked salmon, warm lemon, toasted flour, and caramelizing berries. Dalton plated the dish with a lemon-herb sauce drizzled over the top and served it alongside a simple green salad. It was a refined main-course dinner for home, honoring the meticulous preparation he brings to every meal.
