Dalton prepared Smoked Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Grilled Asparagus, a family-style dinner that reflected his meticulous, home-cooked style. The meal smelled warm and earthy, with fragrant herbs, caramelized onions, and the savory hint of smoked chicken filling the kitchen. It was served in a large white ceramic bowl, arranged in a neat circle with the chicken in the center and the vegetables around it. Each vegetable was garnished with a few fresh herbs, and the plates were dusted lightly with sea salt and cracked black pepper.
