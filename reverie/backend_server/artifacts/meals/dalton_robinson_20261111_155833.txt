Dalton prepared a Herbed Garlic Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry, a hearty, colorful meal that reflected his meticulous and creative approach to cooking. He used chicken thighs, bell peppers, carrots, snap peas, zucchini, and onions, along with garlic, ginger, soy sauce, rice vinegar, oyster sauce, and a blend of fresh herbs. After marinating the chicken in the morning, he sliced it and cooked it quickly with the vegetables over a hot pan, then finished the dish with a spoonful of soy sauce and sesame oil.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with notes of garlic, ginger, smoked chicken, and fragrant herbs. The vibrant vegetables added a bright pop of color, while the golden chicken and glossy sauce created a visually appealing presentation. Dalton served it over rice, arranged the chicken in the center, and garnished it with sliced herbs and chopped green onions.

It was prepared for a simple family dinner at 6:00 pm, a relaxed evening after a full workday. The meal balanced the demands of his route planning and certification studies with the comfort of home cooking, showcasing his dependable and thoughtful nature.
