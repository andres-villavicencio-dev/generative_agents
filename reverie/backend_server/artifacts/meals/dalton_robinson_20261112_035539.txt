On Monday, February 13, Dalton prepared Hickory-Herb Roast Chicken with Grilled Asparagus and Cranberry-Vinegar Salad. He chose a whole chicken for the easy, reliable delivery route and added crisp asparagus and tart cranberries for a refreshing contrast.

Dalton started by seasoning the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, dried thyme, and smoked paprika. After browning the breast skin until it turned golden, he roasted it at 375°F (190°C) until the skin was crispy and the meat rested for five minutes. The asparagus was seasoned with olive oil, lemon zest, and a pinch of sea salt, then grilled for four minutes until tender.

For the salad, he combined fresh spinach, cherry tomatoes, and a dressing of orange juice, apple cider vinegar, Dijon mustard, honey, and a little olive oil. Dalton arranged the chicken on a white platter, lined the side with the bright green salad, and served the roasted asparagus on top. The aroma of rosemary, garlic, and caramelized chicken filled the kitchen, while the crisp vegetables and acidic dressing made the meal feel both comforting and carefully balanced.

The occasion was a quiet weekday breakfast for Dalton, Tom, and Carmen. He wanted a simple, satisfying meal that reflected his meticulous, dependable nature—precise, nourishing, and ready for the day ahead.
