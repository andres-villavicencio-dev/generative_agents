On Monday, February 13, Dalton prepared a Savory Chicken and Herb Chicken Salad using only the pantry ingredients he already had: chicken breast, olive oil, garlic powder, lemon juice, celery, onion, parsley, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper.

He sliced the chicken thin, tossed it with a little oil, garlic powder, and lemon juice, then added the chopped vegetables and herbs. The salad was tossed and served in a white ceramic bowl. Dalton placed a few whole garlic cloves and dried rosemary on top, letting the warm chicken and bright green herbs release their scent as it cooled.

The aroma was earthy and inviting—rich from the chicken and garlic, with the sharp bite of celery, the subtle sweetness of lemon, and the fresh warmth of parsley. The presentation was simple and warm: a colorful bowl of crisp salad with the golden chicken and aromatic garnish resting beside a small plate of plain crackers.

He prepared it for dinner as a relaxed, home-cooked meal after a long workday. The casual setup felt both practical and comforting, with the crisp vegetables and warm chicken creating a satisfying contrast to the quiet evening.
