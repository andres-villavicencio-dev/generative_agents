Meal: Klaserny Borscht with Polish-German Charcuterie and Sear Loin

David prepared a hearty, rustic borscht with a Polish-style root vegetable mix and a seared loin from a German-style butcher. The borscht included carrots, onions, celery, potatoes, red cabbage, and a touch of paprika for warmth. He added a splash of vinegar, a hint of sugar, and an onion for balance before blending the vegetables and simmering them until tender.

For the sear, David used a stainless-steel pan with a little neutral oil and seared the loin over high heat for a rich brown crust, then finished it with a pinch of salt and fresh thyme. Alongside the meal, he arranged small plates of smoked pork cheeks, pickled turnips, and sauerkraut, creating a colorful, artisanal spread for the tasting menu.

The dish had a rich, earthy aroma of carrots, cabbage, smoked meat, and warm spices, with the searing adding a savory undertone. It was presented on a rustic, handcrafted wooden board with contrasting dark and warm tones, making it an intimate, inviting centerpiece for Arthur Burton’s evening tasting menu.
