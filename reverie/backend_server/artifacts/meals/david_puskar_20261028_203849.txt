Dish Name: Sausage and Polish Potato Stew with Creamy Herb-Glazed Root Vegetables

David prepared a hearty, rustic German-Polish fusion stew inspired by the comfort of midwestern potlucks and the earthy depth of Polish cuisine. The dish features smoked sausage, caramelized carrots, onions, and potatoes, with root vegetables glazed in a rich, herb-infused sauce.

Key ingredients: smoked bacon and sausage, potatoes, carrots, onions, mushrooms, cream, thyme, rosemary, garlic, paprika, and a splash of apple cider vinegar.

Preparation method: The sausage was pan-fried to render its fat, while the vegetables were diced and sautéed until soft. A creamy sauce was made by blending cream, milk, and herbs, then glazing the potatoes and vegetables with the finished mixture. The dish was finished with a final sauté of mushrooms and a sprinkle of paprika.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the scent of smoked sausage, caramelized onions, rosemary, thyme, and the warm aroma of cream.

Presentation: David plated the stew in a wide bowl, arranging the glazed vegetables in a colorful, asymmetrical circle around the center. Crumbled sausage, caramelized carrots, and a garnish of crispy bacon and fresh herbs were placed on top, creating a visually balanced yet rustic composition.

Occasion: This was part of a tasting menu for a potential bistro portfolio, with the finished dish photographed for his professional portfolio and used as a dish for his colleague Carlos Gomez at the Rose and Crown Pub.
