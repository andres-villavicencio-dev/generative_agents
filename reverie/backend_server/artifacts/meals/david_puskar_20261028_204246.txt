David Puskar prepared German-Lithuanian Fusion Borscht with Smoked Salmon and Caramelized Onion Crust, a bistro-inspired dinner for his tasting-menu pitch to Arthur Burton. The dish combines the rich, savory base of borscht with smoked salmon and a glossy, caramelized onion crust, blending German comfort with Polish influences.

David used beet root, carrots, onions, and carrots as the core ingredients, sautéing them with smoked paprika, bay leaves, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, and a hint of sour cream. He cooked the borscht in a Dutch oven until the broth reduced to a glossy, velvety consistency, then added thinly sliced smoked salmon and topped it with a crisp, caramelized onion crust made from browned onions, flour, and a touch of lemon juice.

The aroma was deeply earthy and aromatic—earl grey tea, smoked salmon, caramelized onions, thyme, and a hint of sour cream. It presented as an elegant, asymmetrical dinner of contrasting textures: the soft, creamy soup and delicate fish, the translucent golden crust, and the bright crimson beet root. He plated it for an evening tasting menu, photographing the final result for his Rose and Crown portfolio.
