David Puskar prepared Herb-Roasted Turkey Breast with Gravy and Crispy Root Vegetables, using the remaining slices of white bread, whole eggs, and seasonings from his kitchen pantry.

He began by wiping the counter and gathering the leftover bread into small loaves, then whisking the eggs with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. The turkey was oven-roasted until the juices ran clear, while the root vegetables were pan-seared and finished with fresh herbs. A simple brown gravy was poured over the meat, and the bread was toasted before being arranged alongside the vegetables in a shallow skillet.

The aroma of roasted turkey, garlic, thyme, and caramelized vegetables filled the kitchen. He plated the dish with the bread pieces resting on the side, garnishing it with parsley, a lemon wedge, and a drizzle of olive oil. The presentation was rustic yet refined—a quiet Midwestern comfort meal transformed into an edible photograph.

The meal was prepared for a family dinner on Tuesday, February 14, a quiet evening after David returned home late. It reflected his meticulous, contemplative nature and his steady rhythm of cooking, photography, and reflection, creating a warm meal for both himself and his loved ones.
