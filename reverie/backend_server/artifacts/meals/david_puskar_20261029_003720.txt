Dish: Herb-Crusted Mushroom Risotto with Lemon-Balanced Parmesan

Key ingredients: Arborio rice, wild mushrooms, shallots, garlic, thyme, rosemary, white wine, chicken broth, parmesan cheese, and a touch of honey.

Preparation: David sautéed the mushrooms and shallots in olive oil until browned, then added garlic and herbs. He deglazed the pan with white wine and finished the risotto slowly, stirring until the liquid was absorbed. The rice was cooked in the broth, then finished with parmesan and a pinch of lemon.

Aroma: Warm, earthy mushroom and savory herbs mingled with toasted grains and a hint of lemon zest.

Presentation: He arranged the risotto on a white ceramic plate, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a generous dusting of Parmesan. A small lemon wedge and a sprig of rosemary accompanied the meal.

Occasion: A quiet Tuesday breakfast at home, prepared as a simple yet thoughtful offering to the day ahead.
