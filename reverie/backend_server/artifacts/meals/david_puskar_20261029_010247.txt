On Tuesday, February 14, David Puskar prepared Herb-Crusted Rosemary Potatoes with Golden Butter Sauce for dinner. The dish featured russet potatoes tossed with garlic, rosemary, thyme, parsley, and a little salt, then baked until golden and crisp on the outside. A creamy butter sauce was made with softened butter, garlic, onion, and lemon juice, while fresh thyme, oregano, and a pinch of black pepper added the final aromatic note.

David arranged the potatoes in a shallow baking dish and layered them with the sauce, garnishing the edges with chopped herbs. A small white-ruffled rosemary sprig stood upright in the center, adding an elegant focal point. The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted potatoes, fresh herbs, and garlic.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday dinner after work at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, a simple yet satisfying evening of Midwestern comfort food. By serving it simply and thoughtfully, David turned the cooking process into a small piece of edible art, reflecting his meticulous, artistic side and the calm he finds in the kitchen.
