On Tuesday, February 14, David Puskar prepared Herb-Crusted Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Creamy Lemon-Tahini Sauce. The dish featured a whole chicken seasoned with rosemary, thyme, garlic, onion powder, and a hint of paprika, alongside carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and green beans.

David seared the chicken in a hot pan until it developed a golden crust, then transferred it to a roasting pan and covered it with foil. He roasted it at 400°F (200°C) until the juices ran clear and the wood-smoked skin was crisp, finishing it with a warm herb butter. The vegetables were roasted alongside it until tender and caramelized. A creamy lemon-tahini sauce was prepared with Greek yogurt, lemon zest, and a pinch of sea salt, then spooned over the finished dish.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of roasted herbs, garlic, and citrus. David plated the meal carefully, arranging the chicken in the center with the vegetables surrounding it and a small serving of lemon salad beside the plate. He arranged the camera equipment nearby to frame the dish for a still-life-style shoot, capturing its earthy colors and rustic textures.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday dinner at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, where David balanced cooking, photography, and reflection in a mindful, purposeful evening.
