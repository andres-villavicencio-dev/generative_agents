On Tuesday, February 14, David Puskar prepared Herb-Glazed Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and a Rustic Herb Crust. The centerpiece was a whole chicken glazed with a golden honey-sage-butter sauce, accompanied by carrots, potatoes, onions, and fresh thyme. David first seared the chicken until crisp, then baked it alongside the vegetables, adding a splash of white wine and a finishing touch of lemon juice. A rustic crust made with flour, olive oil, salt, and cracked black pepper gave the dish a textured, artisanal finish.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, savory herbs, and caramelized onion. He plated the meal in simple white bowls, arranging the chicken, golden vegetables, and a small herb garnish beside a simple salad. The presentation was understated yet appetizing, balancing rich colors with plenty of fresh green. This was a quiet, personal dinner prepared for a reflective Tuesday—David had no confirmed schedule, but the meal felt intentional, nourishing, and a small celebration of his meticulous, artistic side.
