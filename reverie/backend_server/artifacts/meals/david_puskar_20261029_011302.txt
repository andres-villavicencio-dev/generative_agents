Meal: Herb-Crusted Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy

Key ingredients: chicken breast, baby potatoes, carrots, red onion, garlic, rosemary, thyme, olive oil, lemon juice, Parmesan, and fresh parsley.

Preparation: David began by seasoning the chicken with salt, pepper, and a little lemon juice, then placed it on a roasting pan with the root vegetables, garlic, and herbs. He roasted it in the oven until the skin was golden and the chicken was fully cooked. While the chicken rested, he sliced the potatoes, carrots, and onions, then added them to the pan with the leftover juices and roasted until tender. The final gravy was reduced with the vegetables, garlic, and Parmesan.

Aroma: Warm roasted garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, and a hint of caramelized sweetness filled the kitchen.

Presentation: The chicken rested on a white plate, surrounded by glazed vegetables, with the browned gravy poured into a small bowl and garnished with parsley.

Occasion: A quiet Tuesday dinner, prepared before a camera shoot. David reviewed the first test footage and made minor adjustments, treating the meal as both a creative challenge and a moment of focused reflection.
