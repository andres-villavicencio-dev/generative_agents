David prepared Herb-Roasted Butternut Squash and Full-Fat Creamy Oat Milk Breakfast, serving it alongside his full-fat milk.

The key ingredients were roasted butternut squash, dried thyme, rosemary, and sage, along with oats, salt, pepper, and a splash of warm milk. He preheated the oven to 400°F (200°C), sliced the squash, and tossed it with a little oil, salt, and herbs. After roasting until golden and tender, he prepared the oat milk in a skillet, whisking the oats with the warmed milk and letting it simmer until thick. The finished dish was plated alongside the oat milk and a selection of dried herbs, with a small glass of milk nearby.

The aroma filled the kitchen with roasted squash, warm milk, and the earthy fragrance of the dried herbs. The presentation was simple yet inviting: the bright orange squash contrasted with the creamy oat base, while the garnish of herbs added a subtle, aromatic touch. He prepared it as a quick, comforting breakfast on a calm Wednesday morning, balancing his usual hearty Midwestern comfort foods with his artistic approach to presentation.
