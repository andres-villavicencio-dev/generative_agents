David Puskar prepared Savory Herb-Crusted Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy, a warm Midwestern comfort meal suited to a Wednesday dinner at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub.

He began by sautéing garlic, shallots, thyme, rosemary, and a splash of olive oil in a large skillet until the aromatics softened and the oil began to shimmer. He added onions and carrots, then sliced the chicken breast into even pieces and coated them in a crisp herb crust before baking. Alongside the chicken, he roasted root vegetables—carrots, potatoes, and parsnips—until they browned and became tender. A savory herb gravy finished the dish, with a squeeze of lemon and fresh herbs finishing the pan.

The kitchen smelled of toasted garlic, rosemary, caramelized onions, and the warm aroma of roasted chicken. He plated the meal in a simple rustic basket, arranging the chicken, vegetables, and gravy in an appealing column. The presentation was understated yet inviting, with golden-brown crusts and soft vegetables contrasting against a creamy golden-brown sauce.

It was a relaxed Wednesday dinner, intended to bring warmth and conversation to the pub. David’s meticulous preparation and the inviting presentation reflected his creative, introspective nature, while the comforting food provided a peaceful sense of routine.
