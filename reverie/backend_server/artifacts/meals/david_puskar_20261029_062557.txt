David Puskar prepared Herb-Roasted Butternut Squash with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Rice, serving it for the February 15 dinner gathering at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub. The dish was designed as a comforting, rustic centerpiece that balanced earthy flavors with bright citrus.

He roasted butternut squash, carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes together with garlic, olive oil, rosemary, sage, thyme, and a pinch of smoked paprika. The vegetables were tossed with a simple lemon-herb reduction, then plated alongside fluffy lemon-rice and a generous drizzle of garlic butter. A crisp herb-garnish of parsley, chive leaves, and toasted pine nuts was arranged around the main portion.

The kitchen smelled of roasted carrots, warm sage, toasted pine nuts, and the bright scent of lemon. The warm, earthy squash and golden root vegetables were arranged in an oval platter, with the rice nestled in the center and garnishes radiating outward. It was a hearty, inviting meal meant to bring the pub’s patrons together in a familiar, convivial evening.
