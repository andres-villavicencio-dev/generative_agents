David Puskar prepared Herb-Crusted Pork Tenderloin with Rosemary and Lemon-Caper Soup, a comforting Midwestern dinner suited for an intimate pub gathering. The pork tenderloin is seared to a golden brown, then gently pan-fried until the skin is crisp. It is finished in the oven and served with a side of creamy vegetable soup, a lemon-caper broth, and a simple herb-rustic salad.

Key ingredients include rosemary, garlic, lemon, capers, thyme, carrots, celery, onion, potatoes, and a generous serving of pork. The meat is marinated briefly in rosemary and lemon juice, then seared in a hot skillet before being finished with herbs.

Aromatic, warm, and savory, the meal carries the rich scent of rosemary and garlic, with a bright finish of lemon and herbs. It is presented in a white ceramic bowl with a rustic wooden serving board, a small herb garnish, and a silver platter holding the pork, sliced potatoes, and vegetable salad. The occasion was an informal dinner at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub on Wednesday, February 15, designed to be both comforting and celebratory.
