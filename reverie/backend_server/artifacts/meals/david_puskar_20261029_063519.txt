For his Wednesday dinner, David prepared Hearty Herby Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Creamy Herb Butter. He chose a whole chicken, a whole head of broccoli, carrots, and potatoes, along with a small red onion and a handful of fresh rosemary, thyme, and parsley.

David began by thoroughly cleaning and wiping down the kitchen, then organized the remaining utensils and pantry items into neat, purposeful groups. He seared the chicken until the skin turned golden brown, then rested it briefly before carving. Meanwhile, he roasted the vegetables in the oven until tender, adding a splash of vegetable stock and a little salt toward the end. The creamy herb butter was made by gently melting butter with thyme, garlic, and a pinch of salt, then spooning it over the warm chicken.

As the room filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, and savory herbs, David plated the dish in a rustic ceramic bowl. The golden-brown chicken was arranged around the colorful vegetables, garnished with fresh herbs, and presented with lemon wedges and a small plate of crusty bread.

The meal was served at a casual family dinner, with friends and family gathering around the table to enjoy the comforting, homemade feast. David sipped his wine and reflected on the day’s creative work, feeling proud of the meal and eager to keep cooking with care.
