David Puskar prepared Herb-Crusted Herb-Cured Beef with Root Vegetables and Roasted Garlic, a hearty Midwestern-inspired dinner meant to balance rich, savory flavors with bright, aromatic herbs.

The beef was seared to a deep golden crust, then rested before being simmered with smoked paprika, thyme, rosemary, garlic, onions, carrots, potatoes, and root vegetables. A delicate herb sauce was reduced to coat the meat and vegetables, while roasted garlic added sweetness and fragrance.

The kitchen smelled of caramelized onions, rosemary, smoked paprika, garlic, and warm herbs. He plated the meal on a white ceramic platter with the beef served atop the vegetables, garnished with fresh rosemary and a drizzle of the herb sauce. The presentation felt rustic yet refined—warm, inviting, and ready to share.

The occasion was a Wednesday dinner, with no specific event or guest. It was simply a quiet, purposeful meal that David enjoyed while tidying the kitchen and returning the cooking equipment to its proper place.
