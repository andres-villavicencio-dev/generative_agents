David Puskar prepared Herb-Crusted Salmon with Root Vegetables and Quinoa, a hearty yet elegant breakfast that balanced his Midwestern roots with a refined approach to cooking. The meal featured firm salmon fillets, baby carrots, cauliflower florets, parsnips, and a small portion of quinoa, all coated in a rustic herb crust of lemon zest, dried rosemary, thyme, garlic powder, and sea salt.

The preparation began with the vegetables lightly sautéed in olive oil until softened, then the salmon was pan-seared to a golden crisp, finished with a crisp pan sauce. All components were plated on a warm ceramic plate, with the quinoa arranged beside the vegetables and garnished with a sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge. The aroma was warm, savory, and aromatic—infused with the earthiness of root vegetables and the bright scent of citrus herbs.

The presentation was simple yet intentional: soft, earthy colors, a clean white plate, and an arrangement that balanced texture and shape. It was prepared as a quick, nourishing breakfast for Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, intended as a satisfying, comforting start to the day.
