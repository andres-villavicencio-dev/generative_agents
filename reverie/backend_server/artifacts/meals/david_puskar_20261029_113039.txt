At Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, David Puskar prepared a Warm Herb-Roasted Pork Chops with Root Vegetables and Red Potato Mash for Thursday, February 16. The main components were tender pork chops, carrots, red potatoes, onions, garlic, fresh thyme, rosemary, and shallots, along with a creamy potato mash and a rustic bread roll.

David began by washing and chopping the vegetables and pork, arranging them on a wide serving platter with a scattering of thyme leaves. He roasted the chops in a Dutch oven with the aromatics until the meat turned golden and the vegetables began to soften. For the mash, he boiled the potatoes with the onions and garlic, then finished them with butter, cream, and a hint of milk. He arranged the plated food carefully, placing the roast in the center, with root vegetables around it and the bread beside it.

The dish smelled intensely of roasted pork, caramelized onions, rosemary, garlic, and warm herbs. It was presented as a comforting Midwestern family meal, with the rich potato mash balancing the savory chops and offering a satisfying, rustic contrast to the bright green vegetables.
