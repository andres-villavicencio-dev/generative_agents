David Puskar prepared Herb-Crusted Lamb with Root Vegetables and Red Wine Red Sauce, a hearty Midwestern-inspired dinner suited for Thursday night with friends at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub.  

He began by searing a whole lamb rib in a hot cast-iron skillet until the crust turned deep golden, then added garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of red wine to deglaze the pan. The lamb returned to baste, followed by sliced carrots, potatoes, onions, and pearl onions. A rich red wine-soy sauce reduced the pan while the vegetables absorbed the savory aroma.  

The finished meal smelled of rosemary, caramelized onions, red wine, and warm spices. David plated the lamb over roasted vegetables, garnished with fresh rosemary and lemon slices, and served the sides in shallow bowls. The presentation was rustic and inviting, reflecting his meticulous approach to food as both nourishment and edible art. It was prepared for a relaxed pub dinner on Thursday, February 16, with close friends and the possibility of sharing his photography.
