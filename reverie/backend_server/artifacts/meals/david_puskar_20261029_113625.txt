David Puskar prepared Herb-Crusted Rack of Lamb with Root Vegetables and Red Wine Sauce.

The dish features tender rack of lamb, carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and celery, all seared to a golden crust. It was marinated in a blend of rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, and red wine. The lamb was seared in a hot pan until deeply browned, then finished in the oven until the meat was cooked through and the vegetables were tender. A rich red wine sauce, warmed with shallots, butter, and a splash of wine, was spooned over the dish.

The aroma was earthy and inviting: rosemary, garlic, herbs, and the smoky warmth of the red wine filled the kitchen. David plated the meal on a warm wooden serving board, arranging the lamb and vegetables with deliberate precision and garnishing it with parsley. He finished with a simple lemon wedge and placed the setting beside a bowl of warm root vegetables, creating an inviting, rustic dinner perfect for a quiet Tuesday night at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub.
