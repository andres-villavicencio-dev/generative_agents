David prepared a Hearty Herb-Crusted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy, a comforting Midwestern-inspired dinner made to feel like edible art. The centerpiece was a whole chicken roasted with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, and smoked paprika. He sliced carrots, potatoes, and onions into rough, rustic pieces and roasted them alongside the bird in a large Dutch oven until the chicken was golden and cooked through. A rich, savory gravy was poured over the vegetables, while a creamy mustard-mozzarella sauce was served in small bowls.

The kitchen filled with warm, smoky aromas of garlic, herbs, and toasted spices. David arranged the chicken and vegetables on a white platter, garnished with fresh herbs and a lemon wedge, then poured the finished gravy over the sides. The meal was served around 4:00 PM on Thursday, February 16, after he completed the grocery list, returned home, and prepared his first simple, hearty dinner. It was a quiet, satisfying evening after a productive day of proposal development and photography.
