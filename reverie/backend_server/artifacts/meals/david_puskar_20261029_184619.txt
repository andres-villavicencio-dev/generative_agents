On Friday, February 17, David Puskar prepared Hickory-Herb Braised Lamb Shank with Root Vegetables and Lemon Butter Sauce. The centerpiece was a succulent lamb shank, slow-braised with smoked pork, onion, carrots, parsnip, and celery in the dark, rich broth of smoked beef stock. The lamb was glazed with a fragrant lemon-butter sauce, finishing with a crisp herb garnish of thyme, rosemary, garlic, and parsley.

David arranged the dish in an oval ceramic serving platter, surrounded by a colorful medley of roasted root vegetables and a small mound of golden-brown potatoes. A glass of red wine and a bright orange lemon were placed beside the plate, while a single sprig of rosemary stood prominently among the ingredients. The aroma was unmistakably hearty and inviting: savory lamb, caramelized onions, smoke, dried herbs, and the warm scent of roasted potatoes.

The meal was prepared as a relaxed dinner for his Friday night at home, while David reflected on the German-Polish tasting menu he had developed for the pub. His meticulous attention to detail and the elegant presentation made the home-cooked meal feel almost like an edible piece of art.
