David prepared a Herb-Roasted Barley & Potato Breakfast Plate, a hearty Midwestern comfort dish transformed into an edible work of art. The meal featured roasted barley, thinly sliced potatoes, whole-grain toast, and warm coffee, with a delicate herbed glaze of sage, rosemary, and lemon zest.

He toasted the potatoes and barley in a dry pan until golden, then added butter, olive oil, and a small amount of flour to create a light, nutty crust. He tossed the vegetables with lemon juice and herbs, letting the flavors meld before serving on a slate platter garnished with edible wild greens and seeds. The coffee was poured in a measured glass, its aroma rising alongside the savory scent of toasted grains and herbs.

The presentation was simple but striking—warm earthy tones of brown, gold, and sage balanced with clean white surfaces. It was prepared as a relaxed Saturday breakfast, intended to nourish David and the early morning crowd at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, while reflecting his meticulous, artistic approach to culinary design.
