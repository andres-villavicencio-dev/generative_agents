David prepared Garden Herb-Crisp Sausage and Root Vegetable Frittata, a hearty Midwestern breakfast that turned simple ingredients into an edible art piece.

He sliced ripe tomatoes, red onion, and carrots, then sautéed them with garlic, fresh rosemary, thyme, and a generous amount of olive oil. The vegetables softened while the sausage crumbled into a golden, flavorful crust. Eggs were beaten with milk and seasoned with salt and pepper before being folded in, then cooked slowly until the edges were set and the center remained soft.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted sausage, caramelized onions, garlic, and herbs. David plated the frittata in a rustic ceramic dish, garnishing it with toasted pine nuts and a bright wedge of lemon. The presentation felt intimate and inviting, balancing the savory richness of the sausage with the freshness of the vegetables.

He prepared the meal on Saturday, February 18, as part of his routine at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, where he continues to refine his German-Polish tasting menu. It was a quiet, purposeful breakfast, intended to ground his creative day.
