David prepared Herb-Roasted Mushroom and Root Vegetable Breakfast, a hearty Midwestern-inspired morning dish. The meal used mushrooms, parsnips, carrots, onions, garlic, apple, and a creamy oat-based dressing, with a touch of smoked paprika for depth. David roasted the vegetables until they caramelized, then sautéed the mushrooms and cooked them until tender. He finished the dish with a simple oat dressing, fresh herbs, and toasted breadcrumbs.

The aroma filled the kitchen with earthy mushrooms, roasted apples, warm garlic, and the gentle scent of toasted bread. David presented the breakfast in a rustic ceramic bowl, surrounded by a colorful arrangement of herbs and a small plate of crisp apple slices. He served it with a mug of coffee, intending it as a calm, nourishing breakfast on Saturday, February 18.
