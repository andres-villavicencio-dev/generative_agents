David prepared Herb-Rooted Breakfast, a hearty Midwestern-inspired brunch featuring a pan-fried kale-and-mushroom pancake, a bowl of oatmeal with honey and walnuts, and a glass of warm milk. He toasted slices of whole-grain toast alongside the pancakes, adding a few slices of blue cheese and a dusting of smoked paprika for a subtle savory note.

The dish was prepared in a rustic kitchen: the pancake was cooked until golden and slightly crisp, the oatmeal was seasoned with cinnamon and nutmeg, and the cheese was softened in the pan until it melted. David used his signature herb-scented cooking style, letting the pancake take on the aromatic notes of thyme, rosemary, and a hint of smoked garlic. He arranged the food on a slate-colored slate platter with a small bowl of warm honey, a spoonful of walnuts, and a cup of coffee.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of toasted grains, savory cheese, and leafy herbs. The presentation was simple yet thoughtfully layered, with the breakfast arranged in a balanced, rustic manner that reflected his meticulous and artistic approach to food. The occasion was an early Saturday morning before the day’s purposeful work began, a quiet moment of preparation before heading into Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub.
