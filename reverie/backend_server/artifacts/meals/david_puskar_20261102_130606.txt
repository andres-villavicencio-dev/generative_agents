David Puskar prepared Herb-Roasted Grain Salad with Goat Cheese and Lemon-Olive Oil, a warm, rustic breakfast inspired by his German-Polish tasting menu. The dish features cracked whole-grain bread, toasted almonds, toasted hazelnuts, crispy bacon, and crumbled goat cheese, dressed with olive oil, lemon, and finely chopped herbs.

He began by rinsing the coffee maker and setting the breakfast area, then toasted the bread and nuts until the nuts were golden and the bread was crisp. He warmed the cheese and sliced the bacon into thin pieces, arranging them over the salad. The final touches included generous shavings of fresh chives and a bright lemon wedge, while a small glass of warm milk completed the morning ritual.

The aroma was rich and comforting: roasted grains, toasted nuts, smoked bacon, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen. The presentation was simple yet striking—warm bread and nuts arranged beside the salad, with the golden cheese and crisp bacon adding visual contrast. David made the meal for breakfast on Saturday, February 18, a quiet moment of purpose and nourishment before his workday at Rose and Crown Pub.
