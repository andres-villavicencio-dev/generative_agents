David Puskar prepared a Herb-Crusted Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Garlic Butter, served alongside a hearty German-Polish tasting menu for a Saturday dinner at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub.

The main dish features a whole chicken marinated in olive oil, lemon, thyme, rosemary, and black pepper, then seared until golden and crisp. Its skin was then glazed with a savory garlic-butter mixture, while the breast was finished with a crisp herb coating. Roasted carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and cauliflower were arranged around the bird, with the dark meat offering a tender contrast.

The aroma was unmistakably warm: rosemary, thyme, garlic, butter, and caramelized roots mingled with the savory richness of the chicken. It was plated on a white platter with a simple garnish of parsley and lemon slices, creating a rustic yet refined presentation.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Saturday dinner at the pub, combining David’s meticulous, artistic approach with the comforting traditions of Midwestern home cooking. It felt both intimate and celebratory, a fitting tribute to his growing culinary vision.
