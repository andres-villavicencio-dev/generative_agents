The meal was Rustic Polish-German Charcoal Braised Chicken with Root Vegetables and Herb-Stewed Carrots, prepared for a Saturday Sunday brunch at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub.

Key ingredients included bone-in chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, fresh thyme, rosemary, rosemary, lemon juice, black pepper, olive oil, and a touch of paprika. The chicken was seared until golden, then braise in a rich stock with potatoes, carrots, and onions until tender. Root vegetables were simmered separately, and the chicken was returned to the pan to finish cooking.

The aroma was deeply warm and herbaceous: smoked chicken, caramelized onions, earthy carrots, and the fragrant scent of thyme and rosemary filling the kitchen. Presentation was simple yet elegant—the chicken rested on a slate, surrounded by golden vegetables and a glossy, herb-infused sauce. It served as a satisfying, comforting meal on a quiet Saturday, balancing hearty Midwestern comfort with the refined polish of his German-Polish tasting menu.
