On Saturday, February 18, David Puskar prepared Herb-Crusted Pork Chops with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Butter Sauce for a family gathering. The centerpiece was a pan-seared pork chop, its surface glistening with oil and a fragrant mixture of rosemary, thyme, garlic, and black pepper. The chop was seared to a crisp crust, then transferred to a pan with baby potatoes, carrots, and onions, which were roasted until tender and caramelized.

David finished the meal with a bright lemon-butter sauce, finishing the roast with a splash of cream and a squeeze of fresh lemon. The aromas rose from the kitchen: rosemary, thyme, garlic, savory pork, and warm butter. He plated the chops alongside the root vegetables, garnishing them with parsley, chives, and a scattering of toasted nuts. The presentation was simple yet elegant—warm tones and crisp textures suggested a hearty Midwestern feast, fitting for a Saturday dinner with friends.
