The meal was a simple, nourishing Herb-Crusted Pork Tenderloin with Root Vegetables and Roasted Carrots, prepared for a quiet Saturday evening.

Puskar chose the lean pork, then used the skin to crisp a delicate crust. He tossed the meat with thyme, rosemary, garlic, lemon zest, and a hint of smoked paprika, finishing it under the grill until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. The vegetables—beetroot, carrots, parsnip, and a few green onions—were caramelized in a pan with onions, herbs, and a splash of apple juice.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of roasting meat, root vegetables, and warm spices. Puskar arranged the pork on a platter, drizzled a little pan sauce over it, and placed the colorful vegetables beside it. He finished the dish by garnishing it with fresh parsley and a bright yellow herb garnish.

The occasion was a relaxed post-work meal after a busy day of photography and proposal writing. Puskar wanted something comforting that would settle his restless energy while keeping the flavors of his German-Polish tasting menu in mind.
