Tonight, David Puskar prepared Nürnberger Stäukartoffeln mit polniskim sałatkami—German-style pan-fried potatoes with Polish-style salad, a fusion twist on the beloved Stäukartoffeln. The dish combined German-style breaded potatoes with Polish-style cabbage, sauerkraut, carrots, onions, and a bright tomato-onion dressing, all layered in a pan.

David fried the potatoes until crisp, sliced the salad into a golden square, and assembled the layers in a shallow pan. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted butter, caramelized onions, and earthy cabbage, while the warm, golden lighting made the presentation look like a work of edible art.

He photographed the finished plate for the pub’s tasting menu and later checked that the vegetables and potatoes cooked evenly, turning the meal into a satisfying reminder of how hearty Midwestern comfort food can be transformed into something both familiar and delightfully unexpected.
