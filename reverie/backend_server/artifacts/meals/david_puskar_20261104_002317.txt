Dish Name: Scharfe Polnisches Lachs mit Kräutern  
Key Ingredients: wild-caught salmon fillet, sautéed kale, caramelized onions, thyme, rosemary, lemon zest, black pepper, and a splash of truffle oil.

Preparation Method: David seared the salmon skin-side down until a golden crust formed, then finished it in a hot pan with butter, thyme, and rosemary. Meanwhile, he sautéed the kale and onions in olive oil until the onions softened and the kale became crisp. The pan juices were gently incorporated into the salmon before finishing with lemon zest and a final drizzle of truffle oil.

Aroma: The dish filled the kitchen with a sharp combination of smoked salmon, savory rosemary, earthy thyme, sweet caramelized onions, and the subtle scent of truffle oil.

Presentation: David plated the salmon on a slate board, arranging the golden crust and tender flesh so it looked balanced yet appetizing. The kale and onion were placed at the base, with a small lemon wedge and a sprig of rosemary resting nearby.

Occasion: This was part of a Thursday tasting menu for a pitch to Arthur Burton at the Rose and Crown Pub, blending Midwestern comfort with Polish ingredients. David took the finished dish home on Tuesday to photograph it for his portfolio.
