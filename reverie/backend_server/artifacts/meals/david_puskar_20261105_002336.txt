David prepared Smoked Paprika and Saffron Yukon Gold Potatoes, a warm Midwestern side designed to complement his light dinner and quiet evening. The Yukon Gold potatoes were peeled and cut into uniform batons, then tossed in olive oil, garlic, sea salt, and freshly cracked black pepper. The smoked paprika and saffron were sprinkled over the potatoes, giving them a rich, earthy aroma and a delicate golden-saffron fragrance that rose from the pan. A little lemon juice was added for brightness, while the potatoes were roasted until their edges were caramelized and the interiors were tender.

He arranged the potatoes on a white serving platter, adding a small sprig of parsley and a few golden crumbs for visual contrast. The dish was simple yet intentionally refined, balancing savory, sweet, and aromatic notes. The meal was part of a quiet Tuesday at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, where David would share his thoughts with Arthur, testing the potatoes and discussing how the smoked paprika and saffron worked together. It was a calm, intimate dinner meant to be enjoyed without distraction, reflecting David’s meticulous, artistic side while offering a moment of comfort.
