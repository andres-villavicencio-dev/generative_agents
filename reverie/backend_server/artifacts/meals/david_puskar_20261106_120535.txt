David prepared German-Polish Fusion Potato Pancakes, a hearty breakfast designed for a tasting menu pitch to Arthur Burton at the Rose and Crown Pub. The pancakes featured golden potato and rye flour, eggs, cream cheese, smoked gherkins, fresh chives, black pepper, and a hint of smoked paprika. He cooked them on a hot griddle until the edges were crisp and the centers remained tender, stacking them on a plate with fresh bread, eggs, and pantry staples nearby to serve as a complete morning meal.

The kitchen smelled of toasted bread, savory egg, caramelized onions, and the warm aroma of herbs and dairy. David arranged the pancakes in a simple, rustic stack, garnishing them with a sprig of chives and a thin slice of rye. The presentation was understated and inviting, with the rustic texture of the pancakes contrasting against the bright colors of the toppings.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday break before David began the portfolio photos and a potential tasting menu. The meal was both nourishing and visually balanced, reflecting David’s meticulous approach to Midwestern comfort food adapted into elegant, edible art.
