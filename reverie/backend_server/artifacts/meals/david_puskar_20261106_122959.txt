David prepared German-Polish Fusion Potato Pancakes with Herb Butter and Crispy Chicken.

The key ingredients were potatoes, flour, eggs, milk, salt, pepper, and finely chopped rosemary, thyme, and garlic. He sautéed the potatoes until soft and lightly browned, then cooked a crisp chicken breast beside them. For the breakfast, he toasted sourdough bread, fried eggs with a little butter, and served the pancakes with the pan juices, a drizzle of herbed cream cheese, and a side of apple slices.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of roasted potatoes, savory chicken, and the sharp, fragrant finish of rosemary and garlic. The pancakes were golden and flaky, with a crisp chicken skin and tender pancake edges.

The meal was meant as a warm, indulgent Tuesday breakfast. It was part of David’s broader effort to create a tasting menu and showcase his growing culinary style to Arthur Burton at the Rose and Crown Pub.
