David prepared German-Polish Fusion Potato Pancakes, a hearty breakfast for Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub. The dish combines softened Yukon potatoes with flour, eggs, salt, pepper, garlic, and smoked paprika, folded into fluffy, golden pancakes. A simple herb garnish—chopped parsley and a drizzle of warm lemon juice—was finished over the warm pan, adding a subtle aromatic scent of herbs and citrus.

The pancakes were arranged in a deep pan and cooked slowly until the edges set and the centers remained tender. David finished each plate with a glossy lemon-herb glaze and a scattering of fresh herbs, creating an elegant, rustic presentation that balanced the savory pancake base with bright, aromatic accents. The meal was prepared for the Tuesday dinner table before the pub’s community-service shift, serving as both a comforting breakfast and a polished tasting-moment dish.
