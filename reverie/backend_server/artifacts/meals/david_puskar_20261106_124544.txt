David prepared Marmite-and-Herb German-Polish Potato Pancakes, a hearty breakfast dish designed as part of his Tuesday menu. The pancakes were made from grated potatoes, eggs, whole wheat flour, and a generous amount of Marmite, seasoned with smoked paprika, thyme, bay leaf, garlic powder, and a pinch of nutmeg. They were cooked in a hot skillet until golden and fluffy, then folded and served warm with a side of smoked salmon, chives, and rye toast.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized potatoes, savory Marmite, smoked paprika, and fresh herbs. To serve it, David reheated the remaining pancakes in a pan over medium heat, arranging them on a platter alongside a small portion of salmon and a fresh herb garnish. He plated the dish carefully, using a clean kitchen towel to protect the platter and a simple white napkin to create an elegant presentation.

The meal was prepared for a midday gathering at the Rose and Crown Pub. David hoped to serve it as a tasting menu component to showcase his evolving fusion style to fellow chefs and patrons, including Arthur Burton. His meticulous approach, combined with the comforting warmth of the food, created a memorable moment in the kitchen.
