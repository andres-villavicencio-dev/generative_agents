Meal: The Arthur Burton Fusion Plate  
Dish: Herb-Scented German-Polish Lentil & Walnut Salad with Grilled Duck Thighs and Fig-Lemon Honey Dressing

David prepared the meal in Arthur Burton’s kitchen at the Rose and Crown Pub, testing the recipes that would become the foundation of the pub’s next tasting menu. He layered hearty lentils and walnuts over crisp arugula, toasted sourdough bread, and crisp apples, finishing the salad with a honey-sage dressing. The duck thighs were slow-roasted until their skin was caramelized, while the salad kept the dish visually balanced and seasonally cohesive.

Aromatic, warm, and earthy, the meal smelled of sage, toasted nuts, caramelized duck, and the faint sweetness of figs. David arranged the components on a slate platter with loose leaves and golden fruit, emphasizing the contrast between the dark duck, creamy lentils, and bright greens. The occasion was a collaborative lunch intended to test the recipes for the pub’s future tasting menu.
