Dish: Polish-Infused Shepherd’s Pie with Honey-Glazed Carrot and Thyme

The main dish combines hearty Midwestern comfort with subtle Polish influences. A golden-brown shepherd’s pie is layered with tender potatoes, carrots, onions, and beef, then topped with a rich béchamel. A thin slice of smoked paprika and a few sprigs of thyme are sprinkled over the top, while a drizzle of honey and apple cider vinegar adds a bright, sweet acidity.

David prepares the dish in a large skillet, caramelizing the vegetables until the onions turn translucent and the potatoes soften. The béchamel is heated gently, incorporating the thyme and lemon zest before the hot pie is returned to the pan for a brief finish. The honey-gold glaze coats the carrots and top, creating a warm, amber surface.

Presented on a white platter, the dish is crowned with a mound of crisp potatoes, a drizzle of the glossy glaze, and a scattering of thyme and a few sprigs. It is accompanied by pan-seared pork chops and a side of roasted root vegetables.

The aroma is deeply comforting—earthiness from the smoked paprika, honey, and carrots, mingling with savory beef, caramelized onions, and a hint of herbal freshness. The occasion is a private tasting menu for Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, where David hopes to pitch his refined fusion concept.
