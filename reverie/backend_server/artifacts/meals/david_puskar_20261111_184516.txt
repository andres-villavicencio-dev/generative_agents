David Puskar prepared “Börek with Honey-Apple-Cider-Vinegar Glaze” as the centerpiece of his German-Polish fusion tasting menu. The dish combines a golden, buttery börek—a thin, folded pie-like pastry—filled with sautéed mushrooms, potatoes, onions, and a savory Polish sausage, while a glossy glaze of honey, apple cider vinegar, lemon juice, thyme, and a splash of water gives it a warm, sweet-sour finish.

The preparation method began with the dough, which he folded with his customary precision. The filling was caramelized until the potatoes and mushrooms turned deeply golden, then layered with the sausage before the glaze was brushed over. He finished the dish by broiling it until the pastry crisped and the honey-simmered surface turned glossy.

Aromatically, the meal opened with the earthy scent of mushrooms and onions, warmed by butter and thyme, while the glazed surface added a subtle floral sweetness. The presentation was rustic yet refined: the börek was arranged on a large wooden board with fresh thyme, a small glass of golden cider, and a sprig of rosemary beside it. It was prepared for the tasting menu at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, a private evening for the pub’s regulars and guests, celebrating the collaboration between German pastry and Polish flavors.
