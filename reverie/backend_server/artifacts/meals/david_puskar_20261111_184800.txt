David prepared a Polish-German Hearty Potato Salad, designed as a warm, earthy side for the fusion tasting menu. The dish combines creamy German potato salad with Polish-style crisp greens and caramelized onions, balanced with honey, apple cider vinegar, fresh thyme, and a hint of lemon.

David mixed grated white and yellow potatoes, carrots, celery, and hard-boiled eggs with a mayonnaise-based dressing. He tossed the salad with peppery green beans and sliced pickled onions, then finished it with thyme sprigs. The kitchen smelled of savory potatoes, honey, warm vinegar, thyme, and the gentle aroma of caramelized onions.

David plated the salad in a rustic ceramic bowl with a silver spoon and scattered thyme leaves for visual contrast. The warm, golden color of the potatoes and the deep green of the beans created a welcoming, rustic palette. It was prepared to accompany the hearty main dishes on the menu, with the goal of delivering a comforting, rich flavor that honored both German and Polish traditions.
