David Puskar prepared a Honey-Apple-Cider-Steak with Polish Herb Crisp as part of the German-Polish fusion tasting menu. The dish features a tender, glazed beef chop, caramelized apples, a savory apple-cider glaze, and a crisp pan-fried root vegetable and onion crisp. Key ingredients include beef chuck, apples, honey, apple cider vinegar, thyme, garlic, rosemary, shallots, butter, oil, and Parmesan.

David seared the beef to a deep golden brown, then glazed it with a honey-apple-cider mixture while the crisp cooked alongside the root vegetables. He added fresh thyme and rosemary for aromatic depth, finishing the dish with Parmesan and lemon zest. The aroma was warm and inviting—honey, fruit, earthy herbs, and savory beef.

It was served alongside a rustic German potato gratin and a glass of cider, with a wooden platter and silverware arranged artistically on the workbench. The occasion was a private pub dinner at the Rose and Crown Pub, with the menu designed to showcase Polish influences in hearty, comforting dishes while maintaining a German-inspired foundation.
