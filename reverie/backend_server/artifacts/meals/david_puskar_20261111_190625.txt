On Tuesday, February 14, David Puskar prepared a Polish–German fusion tasting dish called “Honey-Glazed Paprika Pork with Caramelized Carrots and Thyme.” The centerpiece was a tender pork loin marinated in garlic, juniper, apple, cider vinegar, and thyme, slow-roasted in the oven until it fell off the bone. It was finished with a honey-glazed pan sauce, caramelized carrots, apple slices, and crisp flatbread, while the table was garnished with black pepper, rosemary, and a drizzle of fresh thyme.

The meal was designed for a night-time tasting menu event at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, meant to bridge hearty Midwestern comfort with warm Polish influences. The kitchen workbench was carefully timed and checked: each portion was measured, and the cooking sequence ensured the pork remained juicy while the vegetables stayed vibrant. The aroma of roasted pork, honey, thyme, apple, and caramelized carrots filled the kitchen, creating a rich, earthy atmosphere that felt both familiar and refined.
