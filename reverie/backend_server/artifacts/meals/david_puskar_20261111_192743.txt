The evening’s German-Polish fusion tasting menu was inspired by the warm, hearty comfort of Käseknödel and Gulaszwarsz, reimagined with Polish influences.

Dish Name: Käseknödel with Goulash and Honey-Thyme Glaze

Key Ingredients: German potato noodles, smoked bacon, onions, carrots, celery, beef stock, smoked sausage, apples, thyme, and a honey-apple cider vinegar glaze.

Preparation: The noodles were lightly sautéed with smoked bacon and aromatics. Beef was slow-cooked in a rich broth with carrots and celery before being sliced and mixed with the noodles. A honey-ginger glaze was reduced with apple cider vinegar and thyme, then brushed over the finished dish.

Aroma: Warm, earthy, and slightly smoky, with notes of caramelized onion, apple, thyme, and the subtle sweetness of honey.

Presentation: The dish was arranged on a dark slate board, with golden noodles and glossy glaze forming a striking visual contrast. Sliced apples, thyme sprigs, and a few smoked sausage pieces were placed around the plate.

Occasion: The meal was prepared for a private tasting-menu dinner at Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub, intended to be pitched to the host for possible inclusion in the pub’s dinner menu.
