Dish Name: Mediterranean Herb Crusted Potato Salad with Smoked Gherkins and Honey-Lemon Dressing

Key Ingredients: Yellow potatoes, red onion, kalamata olives, smoked gherkins, fresh mint, basil, parsley, thyme, lemon juice, olive oil, apple cider vinegar, Dijon mustard, honey, and a pinch of smoked paprika.

Preparation Method: David first parboiled the potatoes, then tossed them with finely diced onion, olive oil, and fresh herbs until lightly caramelized. The vegetables were arranged on a rustic slate board, garnished with olives and gherkins, and dressed with the sweet-lime vinaigrette. A small bowl of warm honey-mustard dressing was served alongside the salad, allowing guests to finish it off at the table.

The kitchen fills with the earthy scent of caramelized potatoes, smoky gherkins, fresh herbs, and the sharp tang of lemon. The presentation is minimalist and contemporary: a slate platter holds the colorful salad in geometric balance, with the glossy dressing and a crisp apple slice adding visual contrast.

It was prepared for David’s personal tasting menu for his potential pitch to Arthur Burton at the Rose and Crown Pub, offering a refined yet comforting fusion of German and Polish flavors.
