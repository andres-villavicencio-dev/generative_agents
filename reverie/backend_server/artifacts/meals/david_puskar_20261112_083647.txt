David Puskar prepared Garden Herb-Glazed Polish Potatoes and Grilled Chicken, a German-Polish fusion dinner inspired by the hearty comfort foods he loves in his kitchen. He began by sautéing sliced carrots, celery, onions, and thyme in olive oil, then roasted them with lemon juice, garlic, dried rosemary, and a pinch of smoked paprika until golden and tender.

For the potatoes, he peeled and sliced them, tossed them with butter, salt, pepper, and a little flour, then arranged them on a large baking sheet before roasting until the edges were crisp and the centers were fluffy. The chicken was seasoned with paprika, lemon zest, garlic, and fresh rosemary, then pan-seared until the skin was crisp and the meat was cooked through.

The aroma was warm and aromatic—garlic, herb-infused oil, caramelized potatoes, and the savory scent of grilled chicken filling the kitchen. David plated the chicken in a rustic white bowl with a small pile of roasted potatoes, garnished with fresh herbs, lemon wedges, and a drizzle of olive oil. The presentation was simple yet intimate, evoking the cozy atmosphere of Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub.

He prepared the meal for dinner, aiming to balance his cooking and give him enough time to rest before his 3:00 a.m. bedtime. The dish also served as a practical test for a new menu he was developing for the tasting menu he hoped to pitch to the pub.
