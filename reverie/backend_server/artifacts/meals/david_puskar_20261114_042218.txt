David prepared Honey-Butter Cinnamon Oatmeal with Sautéed Kale, a hearty, comforting dinner for a quiet Monday night. The dish combines rolled oats, warm water or milk, honey, and ground cinnamon, then is folded with tender kale and topped with a creamy buttered cornmeal finish.

He began by toasting the oats and cinnamon over the stove, whisking the liquid until the grains softened and the honey turned into a glossy glaze. The golden-brown oatmeal was folded into the wilted kale, then simmered briefly so the flavors melded. A generous drizzle of warm butter completed the presentation, while a dusting of cinnamon and a few fresh thyme sprigs added a subtle herbal note.

The aroma was invitingly warm: honey, cinnamon, toasted oats, and a hint of savory kale. It was served warm and visually balanced, with the golden oatmeal contrasting against the dark greens. The meal was prepared for a relaxed dinner after his shift at the Rose and Crown Pub, a quiet occasion where David hoped to refine his German-Polish fusion concept before pitching it to Arthur Burton.
