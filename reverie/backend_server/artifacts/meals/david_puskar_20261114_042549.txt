Dish: Polish–German Hearty Risotto with Smoked Caramelized Pork

David prepared a comforting fusion dish inspired by his upcoming tasting menu for Arthur Burton’s Rose and Crown Pub. The risotto began with a slow sauté of thinly sliced smoked pork shoulder, shallots, garlic, and a touch of smoked paprika, while saffron and thyme were added to the broth. As the rice absorbed the liquid, he incorporated Parmesan, onion, celery, carrots, and a splash of lemon juice, stirring until it reached the ideal creamy consistency.

The aroma was rich and warm, combining the savory smoke of the pork with the earthy depth of saffron, the crispness of caramelized onions, and the herbaceous fragrance of thyme and garlic. It was presented on a large white platter, with the golden grains, glossy pork, and vibrant orange carrot confit arranged in an asymmetrical, artistic composition.

This meal was prepared between shifts as part of David’s weekly testing of German-Polish fusion recipes. He intended to pitch it to Arthur Burton, using the refined presentation and authentic seasonal flavors to showcase his culinary vision at the pub.
