David prepared Sautéed Root Vegetables with German-Polish Herb Crust and Caramelized Onions, a dish that blends hearty Midwestern roots with the sharp notes of Polish herbs. He sliced red carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, then tossed them in olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and a blend of sage, thyme, and caraway. After sautéing until the vegetables were crisp and the onions softened, he finished the dish with a crisp herb crust, caramelized onions, and a savory Polish sausage.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of caramelized onions, rosemary, sage, and peppery caraway. He presented the dish in a rustic ceramic bowl with a contrasting platter of crisp greens and a small plate of tangy pickles, allowing the earthy colors and golden edges to create visual balance.

He prepared it for a private tasting menu at the Rose and Crown Pub, hoping the refined presentation would impress fellow bartender Arthur Burton and his regulars.
