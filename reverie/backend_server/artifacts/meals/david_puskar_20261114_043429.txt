Dish Name: Herb-Roasted Polish Krokietki with Creamy Red Wine Butter and Fresh Prosciutto

Key Ingredients: Polish pork knuckles, garlic, thyme, rosemary, onions, red wine, cream, cream cheese, fresh parsley, lemon, and a pinch of smoked paprika.

Preparation Method: David began by searing the knuckles in a cast-iron skillet until they developed a deep golden crust, then transferred them to a slow oven with the onions, garlic, and herbs to braise and soften. During the cooking process, he added red wine and a splash of cream to create a savory, velvety sauce. After the knuckles were tender, he finished them by broiling until the skin crisped, garnished with fresh prosciutto and herbs.

Aroma: Warm, earthy, and slightly smoky—layers of roasted pork, herbs, garlic, wine, and softened cream.

Presentation: The plating was deliberate and artistic: a mound of golden-brown knuckles served on a slate plate, with the rich red butter sauce drizzled over the top and fresh herbs scattered around. Prosciutto was arranged in thin, elegant strips beside a small bowl of lemon zest and parsley.

Occasion: This was part of David’s week-long testing of German-Polish fusion recipes, culminating in a tasting menu he hoped to pitch to Arthur Burton at the Rose and Crown Pub. The meal was prepared for dinner while he worked, but it also served as a refined preview of the dishes he wanted to feature in the bistro’s portfolio.
