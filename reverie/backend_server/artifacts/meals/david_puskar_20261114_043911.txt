David Puskar prepared German-Polish Fusion Stuffed Mushrooms, creating an edible journey between comfort and artistic innovation. The dish features button mushrooms, earthy Parmesan and thyme, sautéed onions, roasted carrots, and a savory glaze infused with smoked paprika, garlic, and a hint of black pepper. He sautéed the vegetables in olive oil, mixed them with the mushrooms, then stuffed the entire mushroom with a creamy herb mixture before roasting it at 425°F until the edges turned golden and the center was tender.

The kitchen smelled of toasted garlic, rosemary, caramelized onions, and the rich aroma of caramelized Parmesan. The finished plate featured the stuffed mushrooms arranged on a dark slate platter, garnished with a bright red pepper rim and fresh thyme sprigs, with a small bowl of lemon-herb vinaigrette beside them. David presented the dish as the centerpiece of his test tasting menu, designed to showcase his fusion concept to Arthur Burton. The occasion was a private dinner at the Rose and Crown Pub, intended to impress the bartender and serve as a portfolio piece for a future bistro collection.
