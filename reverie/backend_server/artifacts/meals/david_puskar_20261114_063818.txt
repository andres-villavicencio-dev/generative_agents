Dish Name: Root Vegetable & Herb-Infused Beef Stew

David prepared this hearty Midwestern-inspired stew for his upcoming German-Polish fusion tasting menu. The meal featured tender beef brisket slow-cooked in a rich, herb-rich broth, alongside golden carrots, sweet potatoes, parsnips, and onions. He used smoked paprika, thyme, rosemary, and a touch of smoked salt to create a warm, savory background.

The meat was sliced, browned, and simmered with the root vegetables until the beef became tender and the vegetables softened. David let the flavors meld in the pot before serving it over a bed of toasted bread and fresh parsley.

The kitchen smelled deeply earthy and aromatic: caramelized onions, rosemary, smoked meat, and the earthy sweetness of carrots and potatoes. He plated the dish in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the vegetables in a gentle mound and drizzling the glossy, amber-brown broth over the top.

The meal was prepared for his presentation and pitch to Arthur Burton at the Rose and Crown Pub, with the intention of creating a memorable tasting menu that bridges German and Polish culinary traditions.
