David prepared a Hearty Root Vegetable Stew with Smoked Ham and Crispy Pancetta, a fusion comfort dish inspired by German-Italian regional comfort. The stew featured carrots, parsnips, root vegetables, smoked ham hock, pancetta, thyme, sage, garlic, and a rich wine-based broth. He sliced the vegetables, seared the pancetta, then slowly simmered it with smoked meat and spices until the broth reduced to a glossy, herbaceous glaze.

The aroma was warm and inviting—smoky ham, savory pancetta, earthy root vegetables, and fragrant herbs. David plated the stew in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the golden vegetables and translucent pancetta around the meat and garnishing it with fresh thyme and a drizzle of reduced pan sauce. The presentation balanced rich textures and contrasting warm colors, echoing the rustic, earthy aesthetic he developed for the Rose and Crown Pub.

The occasion was a relaxed dinner after a busy night shift, with the goal of creating a memorable meal to pitch to Arthur Burton. He sipped his dinner and took a quiet moment to enjoy the scent of his creations.
