Diane prepared Lemon-Herb Crusted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for an early Tuesday lunch at her small-town apartment. She used a whole chicken, skin-on, seasoned with lemon, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a touch of black pepper, then marinated it gently before broiling it until the skin turned crisp and golden. She roasted the carrots, potatoes, and Brussels sprouts in olive oil with a little honey until they were tender and caramelized.

The kitchen was filled with the warm aroma of garlic, herbs, roasted vegetables, and lemon zest. Diane arranged the chicken on a wooden platter, surrounded it with the glazed root vegetables, and placed a small sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge beside it. She added a single lemon slice to the center of the plate for a bright contrast. The meal was simple, colorful, and thoughtfully presented, chosen to be a quiet, nourishing lunch after her busy salon day and the exhibition preparations.
