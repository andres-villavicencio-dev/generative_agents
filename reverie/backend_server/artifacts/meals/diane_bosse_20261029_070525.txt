Diane prepared Sunrise of Smallville-inspired Vegetable Stir-Fry, a nourishing, colorful side dish meant to reflect her art and quiet promise of beauty.

Key ingredients: broccoli florets, carrots, bell peppers, snap peas, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, lemon, and a few fresh herbs. She also used a small bowl of warm water for rinsing the vegetables before cooking.

Preparation method: The vegetables were chopped into bite-sized pieces and stir-fried in hot sesame oil until crisp-tender. Garlic and ginger were added at the beginning to infuse the pan with fragrance, while the remaining vegetables and seasonings were tossed in a little soy sauce at the end. Diane finished the dish with a squeeze of lemon and a light dusting of toasted sesame seeds.

The aroma was fresh and warm—ginger, garlic, citrus, and toasted seeds mingling with the earthy sweetness of the vegetables. Diane plated it in a shallow bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around a small mound of rice and garnishing it with herbs and a drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil.

The meal was prepared for a calm, intimate dinner at home on Wednesday, February 15. It was not a grand occasion, but a quiet meal intended to accompany her creative work, give her time to focus, and reflect the beauty she strives to create.
