Diane prepared Smoked Herb Braised Salmon with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Cream Sauce, served with smashed potatoes and roasted carrots.

The salmon fillets were seasoned with smoked paprika, lemon zest, and fresh thyme, then seared until golden and cooked through before being braised in the oven with carrots, baby potatoes, onions, and garlic until the vegetables were tender and the sauce reduced into a glossy emulsion. The root vegetables were roasted until caramelized and the lemon-herb sauce was finished off with a touch of cream.

The aroma was warm and savory: smoked paprika mingled with the earthiness of roasted carrots and the bright citrus of lemon. The presentation was bright and refined—a small white ceramic platter held together by a small wooden spoon, with the salmon, colorful vegetables, and creamy sauce arranged in an asymmetrical, artistic pattern.

Diane prepared the meal for a quiet Wednesday morning at the salon, using the occasion to enjoy a simple, nourishing lunch while reviewing the local art gallery’s opening times. She felt purposeful and fulfilled, grateful that her kitchen was a place of calm and creativity alongside her artistic work.
