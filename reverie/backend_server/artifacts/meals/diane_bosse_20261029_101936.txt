Diane prepared a Mediterranean-Heritage Chicken and Vegetable Bowl, the centerpiece of her lunch on Thursday, February 16. The main portion was cooked in a pan with chicken thighs, onions, carrots, bell peppers, spinach, cherry tomatoes, and olives, using olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, fresh herbs, and a pinch of smoked paprika. The chicken was roasted until golden and tender, then tossed with a lemon-herb dressing.

She arranged the colorful vegetables in a rustic ceramic bowl, topped the chicken with the dressing, and placed a small portion of rice or quinoa beside it. The kitchen was filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus zest, and savory herbs.

The meal served on a bright, hand-painted wooden plate in a warm terracotta color with green and gold accents. It was a relaxed, nourishing lunch, perfect for a day spent visiting the local art gallery and reflecting on her creative plans.
