Diane prepared Sunrise Herb and Berry Breakfast Bowl, a nourishing, colorful breakfast designed to energize her Monday while reflecting the gentle optimism of the Saturday morning. She combined sweet potatoes, quinoa, roasted carrots, kale, and spinach, topped with fresh strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, and sliced bananas. She toasted the sweet potatoes briefly, sautéed the spinach until crisp, and mixed everything in a warm ceramic bowl.

She finished the dish with warm oat milk, a drizzle of honey, lemon zest, and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt. The aroma of caramelized potatoes, tender greens, ripe berries, and the subtle sweetness of the fruit filled the small kitchen, while the bright pink, orange, and green colors made the bowl visually inviting.

The presentation was simple and elegant: the bowl rested on a wooden serving board, with the colorful ingredients arranged in a balanced design and the lemon and herbs garnished for a fresh finish. This breakfast was prepared not for a busy day, but as a quiet pause before her work began—intentionally comforting, mindful, and ready to nourish her creativity.
