Dish Name: Organic Berry-Infused Oatmeal with Fig and Almond Toast

Key Ingredients: rolled oats, water or oat milk, organic berries (blueberries, raspberries, and blackberries), honey, chopped figs, sliced almonds, cinnamon, and a few drops of vanilla.

Preparation Method: Diane soaked the oats in milk overnight, then cooked them with the fruit and honey on the stove until the oatmeal was creamy and slightly firm. She topped it with toasted almond slices and chopped figs, sprinkling a pinch of cinnamon over the whole bowl. A spoonful of honey was drizzled over the oatmeal before serving.

Aroma: Warm, sweet, and earthy—cinnamon, honey, fig, almond, and the fresh fruit created a comforting aroma that smelled like a cozy morning kitchen.

Presentation: The oatmeal was arranged in a shallow ceramic bowl, with the berries and figs arranged around it, and the almonds and cinnamon scattered on top. Diane served the warm bowl in the center of the table, accompanied by a small plate of fresh fruit and a cup of tea, giving the meal a tidy, inviting appearance.

Occasion: A simple Sunday breakfast at Diane’s Smallville salon, following her appointments with Jane Moreno and Mei Lin. Her meticulous, anxiety-prone mood made the meal feel both ordinary and carefully deliberate, reflecting her desire to create something beautiful and environmentally responsible.
