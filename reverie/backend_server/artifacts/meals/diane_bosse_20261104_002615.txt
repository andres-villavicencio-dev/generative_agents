Diane prepared a Mediterranean-Herb Crusted Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Vegetables and a Lemon-Basil Egg Wrap.

The main dish used quinoa, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, carrots, red onion, and olive oil, seasoned with garlic, oregano, lemon, and chili. The vegetables were tossed quickly with olive oil and roasted until golden and tender. The remaining two eggs were lightly poached with a tiny amount of lemon juice, then wrapped with sourdough bread and topped with fresh parsley, feta, and a drizzle of dressing.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted nuts, roasted garlic, citrus, and herbs. The presentation was simple but inviting: a colorful bowl arranged in a small ceramic dish, with the wrap placed beside it, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a bright lemon wedge.

It was her quick breakfast after taking a quiet walk along Willow Court and clearing her mind before Jane Moreno’s appointment. The meal was simple, nourishing, and thoughtfully prepared for a moment of calm.
