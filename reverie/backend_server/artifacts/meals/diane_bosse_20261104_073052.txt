Diane prepared Organic Kale and Black Bean Scramble with Roasted Red Peppers for a quiet weekday morning. The dish was simple yet flavorful: organic kale, black beans, eggs, roasted red bell peppers, feta cheese, lemon, chili powder, garlic, and a splash of olive oil.

She sautéed the peppers and onions first, then gently warmed the eggs and kale in the pan. The beans were stirred in at the end to retain their creaminess, while the feta was folded in for a salty, tangy finish. Diane topped the pan with fresh herbs and sliced avocado before serving.

The meal had a warm, earthy aroma of roasted peppers, garlic, and creamy beans, balanced by the fresh zest of lemon and the nutty aroma of the feta. It was presented in a small white ceramic bowl, with the kale, beans, and avocado arranged around a bed of bright orange peppers.

Diane ate it on an early Monday while planning her upcoming appointments, sketches for her boutique salon, and her work with local artists. The meal served her well, though she worried slightly that it had been a little too easy to complete her day.
