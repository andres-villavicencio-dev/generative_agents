Diane prepared Mediterranean Herb-Infused Chickpea Salad with Lemon-Cilantro-Crusted Chicken for a weekday lunch. The dish included chickpeas, roasted sweet potatoes, kale, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, feta, olives, and a lemon-cilantro dressing. She lightly toasted the chicken, tossed it with the salad, and garnished it with fresh herbs.

The kitchen filled with the crisp aroma of roasted potatoes, aromatic herbs, and the bright scent of lemon. She plated it in a white ceramic bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables in the center with the chicken, crusted chicken, and feta scattered around. She served it with a simple side of warm whole-grain bread and a small salad of cucumber and tomatoes.

The meal was a relaxed, nourishing lunch for a busy Monday. Its earthy colors and refreshing flavors provided a calm contrast to her upcoming appointments, while the fresh ingredients reflected her intention to make her salon more environmentally conscious.
