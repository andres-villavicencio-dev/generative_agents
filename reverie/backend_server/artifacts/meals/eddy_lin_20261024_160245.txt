Eddy prepared a Ginger-Lemon Herb Quinoa Salad with Roasted Root Vegetables. The dish was a simple, colorful side for Tuesday’s light dinner.

Key ingredients: cooked quinoa, baby carrots, zucchini, red bell pepper, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, cucumber, red onion, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, fresh ginger, and a touch of cumin.

Preparation method: The vegetables were roasted in the oven at 200°C for 20 minutes, then cooled. Eddys tossed the cooled vegetables with the quinoa, lemon juice, olive oil, and ginger-infused dressing. The ingredients were chopped and arranged in a shallow bowl before being garnished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon-herb oil.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the sharp, citrusy scent of lemon and ginger, accompanied by the earthy, warm aroma of roasted vegetables and toasted cumin.

Presentation: The salad was served in a white ceramic bowl, with the colorful vegetables arranged in overlapping rows and garnished with bright red and green herbs. Eddy added a small mound of quinoa on top, with a lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley beside it.

Occasion: This was a relaxed Tuesday lunch meant to be nourishing and easy to prepare, before Eddy began rehearsing ideas for his contemporary-classical piece, Echoes of Oak Hill.
