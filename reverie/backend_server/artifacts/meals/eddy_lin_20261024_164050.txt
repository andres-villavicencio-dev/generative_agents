For Tuesday, February 14, Eddy prepared Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a light dinner designed to be refreshing and energizing. The recipe included chicken breasts, baby carrots, bell peppers, onion, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, rosemary, thyme, salt, pepper, and fresh parsley. He marinated the chicken in olive oil and lemon juice, then grilled it over medium heat for 10–12 minutes until golden and juicy. The vegetables were roasted with garlic and herbs until crisp-tender, and a simple lemon-herb sauce was drizzled over the finished meal.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized garlic, rosemary, and citrus. Eddy plated the dish on a small white ceramic plate, arranging the colorful vegetables around the grilled chicken and garnishing it with parsley. The presentation was clean, inviting, and balanced—ideal for an evening after a busy day of music theory rehearsals and class assignments. The light dinner provided a satisfying conclusion before Eddy’s planned relaxation and sleep at 11:00 PM.
