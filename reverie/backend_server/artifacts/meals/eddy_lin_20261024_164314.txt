Eddy Lin prepared Pan-Seared Scallops with Lemon-Herb Butter and Roasted Root Vegetables. He chose scallops for their delicate texture and vibrant flavor, pairing them with baby potatoes, carrots, and parsnips. His key ingredients included olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, fresh thyme, parsley, salt, black pepper, and butter.

He seared the scallops in a hot pan until they turned golden, then added the vegetables to finish cooking in the same pan. Freshly grated lemon juice and white wine were used to brighten the dish, while the aromatics softened the vegetables. A spoonful of warm herb butter was served alongside the scallops.

The meal was presented in a simple white bowl, with the scallops arranged neatly in the center, vegetables around it, and a lemon wedge and parsley garnish. The aroma of garlic, thyme, lemon, and roasted root vegetables filled the kitchen. Eddy prepared it for a quiet dinner before bed, creating a balanced, comforting meal that complemented his evening homework and relaxation.
