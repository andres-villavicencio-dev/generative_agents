Eddy Lin prepared a Pan-Seared Chicken with Lemon-Garlic Herb Cream Sauce for his Tuesday dinner. He seasoned chicken thighs with olive oil, garlic, dried thyme, black pepper, and a splash of lemon juice, then seared them until the skin was golden and the meat was nearly cooked through. He finished the dish in the oven until the juices ran clear, then simmered a rich cream sauce with butter, thyme, lemon zest, garlic, and a pinch of nutmeg.

The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, warm thyme, and the delicate aroma of fresh lemon. Eddy plated the chicken on a rustic white plate, arranging it alongside crisp roasted vegetables and a side of pan-seared asparagus. The presentation was simple yet inviting—the golden-brown chicken stood out against the creamy sauce and vibrant green vegetables.

Dinner was a relaxed evening meal on February 14, the day Eddy attended his music theory class and continued working on his contemporary-classical composition, Echoes of Oak Hill. The meal provided a calm transition between his academic responsibilities and creative practice.
