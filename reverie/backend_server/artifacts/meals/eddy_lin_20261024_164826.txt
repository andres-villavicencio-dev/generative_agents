The meal was a light Mediterranean chicken and herb roast, prepared for a quiet Tuesday dinner. Eddy used a whole chicken, seasoned with garlic, lemon, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, and a touch of honey, then roasted it in a low temperature until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. The sides included a simple roasted root vegetable medley with a creamy lemon herb sauce and a small portion of steamed quinoa.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, rosemary, and lemon, with the faint scent of baking potatoes and warm spices. Eddy arranged the chicken and vegetables in a shallow oven-proof dish, drizzling the sauce over the roots and garnishing with fresh parsley and a lemon twist. He served it at a modest dinner table, with a glass of water and a small side of toast, for a relaxed evening of study, practice, and reflection.
