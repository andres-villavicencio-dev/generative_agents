For this Tuesday evening, Eddy Lin prepared a Lemon-Ginger Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables. His meal was designed to be light, filling, and easy to digest, fitting the requirements of a late-night supper.

The chicken was seasoned with fresh lemon zest, minced ginger, garlic, and oregano, then pan-seared in olive oil until golden. It was served with roasted carrots, zucchini, and bell peppers, which retained their bright flavors and added a subtle crunch. Eddy added a small bowl of cold lemon water to help his throat relax before bed.

The kitchen smelled of warm ginger, citrus, savory chicken, and gently caramelized vegetables. He presented the meal simply in a white ceramic dish, with the vegetables arranged neatly beside the chicken and a small lemon slice resting in the center. The presentation was understated and elegant, emphasizing the clean, aromatic flavors of the ingredients.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday evening. After a busy day of music theory assignments and rehearsals, Eddy wanted a calm, nourishing meal before heading to bed at 11:00 PM.
