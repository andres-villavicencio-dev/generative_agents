Eddy prepared a simple Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Carrots and Asparagus for a Tuesday night. The meal featured a whole chicken with skin-on, marinated in lemon zest, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of rosemary, alongside crisp carrots, bright green asparagus, and a simple lemon-herb sauce.

The chicken was seasoned with garlic, lemon, olive oil, and dried herbs, then placed skin-side down in the oven until it was nearly cooked through. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, rosemary, salt, and pepper, then roasted alongside the chicken until tender and caramelized. The dish was finished with a bright lemon-herb sauce and served in a shallow white bowl.

Warm, fragrant steam filled the kitchen, carrying the sharp aroma of lemon, garlic, rosemary, and roasted vegetables. Eddy presented the meal with the chicken resting on the side and the vegetables arranged neatly beside it, creating a warm, inviting, and balanced dinner. It was a relaxed, contemporary-classical-inspired evening meal, fitting his musical interests and calm transition into bed at 11:00 p.m.
