Eddy Lin prepared Roasted Vegetable Risotto with Herb Oil, a light, comforting dinner designed for an early evening. He began by sautéing finely chopped leeks, garlic, shallots, and fresh thyme in olive oil. As the pan heated, he added sliced carrots, butternut squash, and red bell peppers, cooking them briefly until they softened. He then added Arborio rice, gradually stirring in warm vegetable broth while continuing to cook the vegetables until the rice absorbed the liquid and the mixture began to thicken. He finished it with a pinch of sea salt, a few sprigs of parsley, and a generous drizzle of herb-infused oil.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted garlic, roasted vegetables, and savory broth. The colorful dish was served in a simple white bowl, with the golden rice and tender vegetables arranged neatly alongside a small lemon wedge. The presentation was understated yet inviting, reflecting Eddy’s analytical and artistic approach. It was a relaxed, home-cooked dinner, but the light, carefully prepared meal gave him enough energy to continue his class assignments and begin his contemporary-classical piece, Echoes of Oak Hill.
