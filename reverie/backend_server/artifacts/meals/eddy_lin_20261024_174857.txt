Eddy Lin prepared Garden Herb Risotto with Roasted Potatoes and Lemon Butter. The dish used Arborio rice, shallots, garlic, vegetable broth, parmesan cheese, rosemary, thyme, and a touch of lemon zest. He cooked the rice in a pan with the aromatics, added the broth gradually, and stirred the mixture until the rice became creamy and tender. The potatoes were roasted until golden, then added to the pan with the finished risotto.

The kitchen was filled with the rich aroma of toasted garlic, rosemary, and the warm fragrance of the slow-cooked rice. Eddy plated the dish in a shallow oval dish, arranging the golden potatoes around the creamy risotto and garnishing it with fresh herbs and a lemon wedge. The presentation felt intimate and inviting, suitable for a quiet Tuesday evening.

The meal served as a relaxed transition from his busy day of class assignments and musical rehearsals. By the time he finished eating and went to bed at 11:00 PM, he had completed his first contemporary-classical piece, Echoes of Oak Hill, and was ready to settle down for the night.
