Eddy prepared a Light Mandarin Chicken with Bok Choy and Sesame Rice. For the chicken, he marinated chicken thighs in soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a pinch of sesame oil, then pan-seared them until golden and cooked through. The bok choy was lightly stir-fried with garlic and ginger, then plated alongside the chicken. Sesame seeds, toasted sesame oil, and a tiny drizzle of soy glaze were added for texture and flavor.

The aroma was warm and inviting—ginger, garlic, toasted sesame, and savory chicken mingling with the fresh scent of the bok choy. Eddy arranged the golden chicken at the center, surrounded by crisp bok choy and a generous portion of fluffy sesame rice. Garnished with green onions and a few whole sesame seeds, the meal appeared balanced, elegant, and comforting.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday evening at Hobbs Cafe, where Eddy was completing his contemporary-classical piece, Echoes of Oak Hill, and taking a light dinner. The meal served as both nourishment and a peaceful pause between hours of focused musical study.
