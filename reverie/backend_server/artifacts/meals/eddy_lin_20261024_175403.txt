Eddy prepared a Roasted Vegetable and Herb Rice Bowl for his light dinner. The dish featured jasmine rice, carrots, baby potatoes, broccoli florets, bell peppers, and onions, topped with a herb-crusted chicken breast and a bright citrus vinaigrette.

Eddy roasted the root vegetables and chicken until the skin was caramelized, then sautéed the chopped herbs, garlic, and lemon zest in the pan. He finished the dish with a simple lemon-tahini dressing and scattered fresh parsley on top. The aroma was warm and fragrant, with hints of garlic, herbs, roasted vegetables, and citrus.

The presentation was colorful and elegant: the golden vegetables and pink chicken contrasted with the creamy rice and dark greens, arranged in a bowl and garnished with edible flowers. It was a quiet, satisfying meal to accompany his focus on the contemporary-classical piece Echoes of Oak Hill.
