Eddy Lin prepared Chicken and Asparagus with Lemon-Herb Pan Sauce. For the meal, he marinated chicken breasts in olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, and fresh herbs, then seared them until golden. Alongside the chicken, he tossed asparagus florets in the same pan before adding a splash of lemon juice and finishing the dish with a rich pan sauce.

The kitchen smelled of garlic, herbs, citrus, and lightly toasted chicken. He arranged the chicken and asparagus on a platter, garnished with parsley, and drizzled the pan sauce over the top. The bright yellow asparagus and pale chicken contrasted with the golden-brown meat and creamy sauce.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday dinner before college classes. It was a light, home-style meal meant to provide energy for studying and rehearsing his contemporary-classical piece, Echoes of Oak Hill.
