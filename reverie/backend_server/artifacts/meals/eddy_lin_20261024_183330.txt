Eddy prepared Lemon-Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables. The main protein was a breast chicken, lightly marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and thyme, then grilled until the skin turned golden and the interior was tender. Alongside it were carrots, potatoes, and red onions, roasted with olive oil, lemon zest, and a pinch of smoked paprika until soft and caramelized.

The preparation method was straightforward: the chicken was seasoned and cooked in a hot pan, while the vegetables were tossed in the same pan and roasted until their natural sweetness released. A small amount of lemon zest and parsley was stirred in just before serving.

The aroma was bright and inviting, with the sharp tang of lemon, warm smoke from the paprika, and the savory depth of garlic and thyme. The vegetables gave the meal a golden-brown texture, while the chicken remained moist and aromatic.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: the chicken and vegetables were arranged on a white plate with a single lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley on the side. The dish was plated after the final few minutes of roasting so that the vegetables remained bright and the chicken looked fresh and appealing.

This was a quiet, home-cooked dinner for Eddy’s Tuesday, chosen to balance his music-theory classes, rehearsal, and homework. The meal felt nourishing and relaxed, leaving him ready for the evening practice.
