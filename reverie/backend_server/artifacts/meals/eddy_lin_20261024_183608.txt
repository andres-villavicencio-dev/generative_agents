Eddy Lin prepared Lemon-Ginger Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for a light, nutritious dinner. The main component was tender chicken thighs marinated in lemon juice, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, and a little honey until they were golden and slightly caramelized. He added roasted carrots, zucchini, and snap peas with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then finished the dish with a small handful of toasted sesame seeds.

He seared the chicken in a skillet over medium-high heat, browning the skin until crisp, then added the vegetables to the pan. The ingredients were simmered briefly before being plated alongside a bright lemon-herb sauce and a simple grain of brown rice.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted garlic, fresh ginger, and toasted sesame. Eddy served the meal in a clear ceramic bowl, using white plates for the colorful vegetables and a dark plate for the golden chicken. The presentation was simple and inviting, reflecting his analytical side—he chose a balanced, easy-to-eat dish that would complement his continued studies in music theory and composition.
