Eddy Lin prepared Leek and Herb Pan-Seared Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a balanced light dinner suitable for a college evening. The main protein was chicken breast marinated in lemon, garlic, olive oil, and a touch of soy sauce, then pan-seared until golden and cooked through. Roasted carrots, bell peppers, and onions were seasoned with oregano, thyme, and a little honey, then cooked until tender. A simple pan sauce reduced the pan juices to coat the chicken, while a small amount of leftover stewed lentils provided a creamy texture.

The kitchen smelled warm and aromatic: roasted garlic, herbs, and the savory scent of chicken rose throughout the house. Eddy arranged the chicken in the center of a white ceramic plate, surrounded by bright orange carrots and crisp green peppers. He served the remaining vegetables over a bed of fluffy rice, garnishing it with chopped parsley. The meal felt intimate and purposeful—the light, comforting dinner was meant to accompany his late-night rehearsal for Echoes of Oak Hill and prepare him for the next day’s classes.
