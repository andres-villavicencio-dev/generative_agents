Eddy prepared a warm Ginger-Garlic Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast Bowl for a quick weekday lunch. Key ingredients included chicken breast, yellow squash, baby carrots, cucumber, red bell pepper, scallions, sliced garlic, ginger, coconut milk, sesame oil, soy sauce, sesame seeds, and brown rice.

He marinated the chicken in soy sauce, garlic, and ginger, then pan-fried it until golden. The vegetables were lightly sautéed and seasoned with coconut milk, sesame oil, and a little soy sauce before being mixed into the pan. The bowl was arranged with the chicken, colorful vegetables, brown rice, and a sprinkle of sesame seeds, with a small bowl of pickled vegetables and a side of steamed rice nearby.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the sharp warmth of ginger and garlic, the savory richness of chicken, and the crisp freshness of the vegetables. Eddy served it at a quiet Wednesday lunchtime for himself and his classmates, using the meal to reflect on the community’s kindness, shared melodies, and small acts of support.
