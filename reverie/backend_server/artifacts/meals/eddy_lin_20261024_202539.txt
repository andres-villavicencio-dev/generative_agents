Eddy Lin prepared Mushroom Risotto with Herb Oil, a warm, comforting dish inspired by the warmth of shared breakfast and small acts of kindness he found in the Oak Hill College community.

The key ingredients were Arborio rice, mushrooms, onions, garlic, fresh thyme, rosemary, lemon zest, olive oil, and a touch of Parmesan. He toasted the mushrooms in a pan, then gradually added warm vegetable broth and stirred until the rice absorbed the liquid, developing a creamy texture without becoming mushy. The final garnish included extra cheese, chopped herbs, and a drizzle of herb-infused oil.

As he prepared it, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted mushrooms, garlic, thyme, and the earthy scent of Parmesan. He served the risotto in a shallow bowl, arranging the golden grains and garnishes around the side. The meal was a quiet way to balance the composition he was developing, with the warm flavors echoing his ideas of kindness, music, and community.
