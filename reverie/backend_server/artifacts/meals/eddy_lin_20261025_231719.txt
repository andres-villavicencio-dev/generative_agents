Eddy prepared a simple, hearty lunch of Oatmeal with Banana-Sliced Toppings. The dish featured rolled oats, warm milk, a drizzle of honey, and sliced ripe bananas. For extra texture and freshness, he added crisp apple slices, a handful of spinach, and a few chopped walnuts. A little cinnamon, a pinch of nutmeg, and a splash of vanilla extract were stirred in before serving.

Eddy prepared the oats by heating the milk and oat mixture in a saucepan over medium heat, adding the banana and toppings, then stirring until the oats were tender and the flavors were well distributed. He let it cool briefly before plating it.

The aroma was warm, comforting, and slightly sweet—cinnamon, vanilla, honey, and the earthy notes of the oats. Eddy arranged the dish on a white ceramic plate, placing the glossy oatmeal and colorful toppings neatly around the center, with the banana and walnuts adding a pop of color. The presentation was simple and inviting, suitable for a quiet Wednesday lunch at Oak Hill College. It was part of his usual routine, but he also considered whether the oatmeal and banana could be upgraded for the café’s menu, balancing quality, value, and customer convenience while staying mindful of the $4.50–$6.50 price range.
