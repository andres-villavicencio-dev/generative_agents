Eddy prepared a warm oatmeal-banana-slice breakfast bowl as part of his lunch. The dish used rolled oats, bananas, brown sugar, cinnamon, vanilla extract, a splash of milk, and a few slices of whole-grain toast. He cooked the oatmeal gently in a nonstick pan until the oats were tender and slightly caramelized, then sliced the bananas and served them on top. A small bowl of whole-grain toast and a spoonful of fresh fruit completed the meal.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with the sweet scent of banana, oats, and cinnamon filling the kitchen. The presentation was simple yet appealing: the creamy oatmeal and golden-brown toast were arranged on white ceramic plates, with the bright orange banana slices adding a cheerful pop of color.

Eddy ate the meal at Oak Hill College during a Wednesday lunch before his notes and findings from the mayoral candidates’ proposals and café price analysis. The occasion was casual and practical—focused on gathering information while nourishing himself before continuing his work.
