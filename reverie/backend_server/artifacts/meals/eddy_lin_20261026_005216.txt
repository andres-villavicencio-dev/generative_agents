Eddy Lin prepared a small pan-seared egg with roasted chickpeas and lemon-tahini sauce as a simple, nutritious side dish for lunch. The dish consists of a whole egg poached in its own whites, topped with toasted chickpeas, and drizzled over the pan with a bright lemon-tahini sauce. He used olive oil for pan-searing, garlic and oregano for the egg, dried tomatoes and lemon zest for the sauce, and fresh mint for garnish.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of toasted garlic, roasted chickpeas, and citrus. Eddy arranged the dish on a white ceramic plate, with the golden egg, crisp chickpeas, and pale green mint creating an appealing visual contrast. He added a small lemon wedge and a sprig of mint before serving.

This was an informal, everyday lunch meal, prepared for a quiet Wednesday morning while he was finalizing his notes on the mayoral candidates and café sales data. Its simplicity made it easy to eat quickly, helping him focus on comparing the proposals without getting distracted by complex calculations.
