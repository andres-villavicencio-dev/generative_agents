Dish: Simple Oatmeal with Banana Slices and Fresh Berries

Key Ingredients: rolled oats, water or milk, fresh bananas, blueberries, raspberries, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt.

Preparation: Eddy Lin cooked the oats in a small pot over low heat with a little milk until the grains were soft but not mushy. He sliced two ripe bananas, added them to the pot after the oats had absorbed most of the liquid, and covered it. As the mixture warmed, he stirred in the fruit and topped it with the berries. He finished it with a tiny knob of cinnamon and a drizzle of honey.

Aroma: Sweet, warm, and subtly earthy—cinnamon, fruit, and oats mingling with the faint scent of warm milk.

Presentation: He served the oatmeal in a clear bowl, arranging the blueberries and raspberries around the sides in a loose circular pattern. The bright colors and soft golden-brown oats created a cheerful, inviting appearance.

Occasion: It was Eddy’s early Thursday breakfast before beginning his study day. The relaxed preparation matched his calm, purposeful mood, and the simple meal helped him start his day with nourishment and focus.
