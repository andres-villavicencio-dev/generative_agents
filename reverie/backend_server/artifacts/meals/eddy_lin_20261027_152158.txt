Eddy Lin prepared a Baked Potato with Roasted Vegetables and Fresh Herbs. He used a russet potato, olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic, fresh rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, and a pinch of dried oregano. After washing and cutting the potato, he seasoned it, placed it on a baking sheet, and roasted it with the vegetables until the skin was crisp and the interior was tender.

The kitchen smelled warm and aromatic, with the rich scent of garlic, herbs, roasted garlic, and lemon filling the room. Eddy arranged the golden potato on the table, topped it with the colorful vegetables, and drizzled a little olive oil over everything. The final presentation was rustic and inviting, with the bright colors of the herbs and vegetables contrasting against the creamy white of the potatoes.

The meal was for a quiet Wednesday lunch during Eddy’s time at Oak Hill College, after he had studied music theory and composition. He had prepared the ingredients and cooked the main dish while comparing the candidates’ proposals, café pricing, menu items, and quality standards. His goal was to make the lunch both nutritious and enjoyable without overcomplicating the choices.
