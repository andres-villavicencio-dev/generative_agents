On Wednesday, February 15, Eddy prepared Oatmeal Banana-Slice Breakfast for himself at home. The dish used rolled oats, bananas, a pinch of cinnamon, a little milk, vanilla, a splash of lemon juice, and a drizzle of honey. Eddy cooked the oats in a nonstick pan over medium heat, spreading them evenly so they would not stick. He sliced ripe bananas and added them to the pan, stirring them in until the oatmeal was warm and the banana pieces softened but still held shape.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the sweet, nutty scent of oats and the fresh, tropical fragrance of bananas. Eddy arranged the oatmeal in a small round bowl and placed bright orange banana slices on top, garnished with a tiny cinnamon stick and a splash of lemon juice. The presentation was simple, colorful, and inviting, making it perfect for a quick morning breakfast.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Wednesday morning at Oak Hill College, where Eddy was reviewing his notes on the mayoral proposals. He wanted the oatmeal and banana-slice quality and value to reflect the café’s standards without complicating the proposals.
