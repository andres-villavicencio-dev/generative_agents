Eddy prepared a warm oatmeal breakfast for his Wednesday morning routine. He used rolled oats, oat milk, a few slices of banana, a pinch of cinnamon, and a small drizzle of honey. He stirred the oatmeal gently in a small saucepan, added the milk and fruit, and let it warm through before serving. The scent of oats, banana, and cinnamon filled the kitchen, creating a cozy, comforting aroma.

The breakfast was presented in a simple ceramic bowl, with the banana sliced beside the oatmeal and a small glass of water nearby. Eddy ate it quietly on his way to the café, comparing its price and quality to John Lin’s mayoral candidates’ proposals. The meal helped him focus on practical decision-making, though it remained informal and practical rather than a formal breakfast for colleagues.
