Eddy Lin prepared a Simple Home-Made Oatmeal with Banana-Slice and Honey for breakfast. The key ingredients were rolled oats, milk or water, a ripe banana, honey, and a pinch of cinnamon.

He simmered the oats in the liquid until they became creamy, then sliced the banana and placed it on top. The honey was drizzled gently over the top, while cinnamon was sprinkled lightly for fragrance. As the oatmeal prepared, it gave off a warm, nutty aroma, with the sweet banana and honey adding a gentle, honeyed note.

Eddy served the oatmeal in a white ceramic bowl, arranged it neatly in the center, and placed the banana and cinnamon on either side. The presentation was simple and inviting, with the banana adding a small pop of color. He prepared the meal for Wednesday’s lunch, balancing practical value with a little creativity while keeping the mayoral proposals in mind.
