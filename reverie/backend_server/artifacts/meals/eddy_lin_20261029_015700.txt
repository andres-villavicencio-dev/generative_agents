Eddy Lin prepared Crispy Herb-Crusted Salmon with Lemon-Herb Rice for a quiet Tuesday dinner. The meal’s key ingredients were fresh salmon, asparagus, garlic, shallots, lemon, fresh herbs, and fluffy jasmine rice. He marinated the salmon in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and chopped herbs, then pan-seared it until golden and flaky. The asparagus was roasted with the same aromatic oil, while the rice was sautéed in a separate pan until tender and lightly browned.

As the meal was ready, the kitchen filled with the sharp scent of roasted garlic, lemon zest, and warm herbs. Eddy plated the salmon on a white ceramic plate, arranging the asparagus neatly beside it and scattering chopped herbs over the rice. A small lemon wedge and a splash of orange sauce served as a finishing touch. The occasion was informal but purposeful, intended to balance his demanding schedule and provide a nourishing pause after a full day of music theory classes and Mei Lin’s philosophy work.
