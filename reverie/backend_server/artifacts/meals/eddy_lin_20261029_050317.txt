Eddy prepared Savory Lemon-Butter Chicken with Roasted Carrots and Jasmine Rice, a simple yet thoughtfully balanced lunch designed to energize him for a full day of classes. He used boneless chicken thighs, lemon, garlic, fresh herbs, butter, and a splash of soy sauce, combining them in a skillet until the chicken turned golden and tender. The carrots were roasted until crisp and caramelized, then arranged alongside the rice, which was gently warmed with a little water.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, and citrus. Eddy plated it elegantly on a white ceramic dish, arranged the carrots in a neat row, and finished the presentation with a small drizzle of lemon juice and a sprinkle of parsley. It was a quiet meal for a productive Wednesday, intended to support his focus on music theory and Mei Lin’s philosophy course rather than to be a celebratory occasion.
