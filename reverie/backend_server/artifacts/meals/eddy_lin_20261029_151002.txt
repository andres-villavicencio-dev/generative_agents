Eddy prepared a simple Garlic Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for a relaxed Friday dinner. The dish featured chicken thighs with golden brown skin, chopped carrots, baby potatoes, and red bell peppers, all seasoned with garlic, oregano, lemon zest, and a little olive oil.

Eddy marinated the chicken in the refrigerator overnight, then pan-seared it until the skin was crispy and the meat was cooked through. The vegetables were roasted until tender, adding a sweet and savory depth to the rich chicken. He finished the meal with a small bowl of lemon-herb rice for a complete, filling lunch.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, and roasted vegetables. Eddy plated the chicken on a white plate, arranging the colorful vegetables around it and serving the side of rice beside it. It was a simple, home-style meal meant for a quiet evening, but with a little extra effort, it felt special.
