On Friday, February 17, Eddy Lin prepared a simple Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. For the meal, he selected chicken breasts, carrots, bell peppers, onions, and a small portion of whole-grain rice.

He washed and dried the chicken, seasoned it with salt, pepper, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice, then grilled it over medium heat until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, lemon zest, and a pinch of red pepper flakes before being roasted until crisp and caramelized. The whole grain rice was steamed until fluffy.

The aroma of garlic, herbs, citrus, and roasted vegetables filled the kitchen, creating a fresh, warm atmosphere. Eddy presented the meal on a white plate, arranging the chicken in the center with the colorful vegetables around it and placing a small bowl of steamed rice beside it. The simple, balanced dish was suitable for a quiet Friday evening when he had just returned from work and was looking forward to exploring new places with a relaxed appetite.
