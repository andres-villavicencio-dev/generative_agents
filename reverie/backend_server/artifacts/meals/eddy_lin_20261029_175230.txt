Eddy Lin prepared a simple but warm Lemon-Infused Chicken Risotto with Roasted Vegetables. His favorite meal was a golden chicken breast, arborio rice, creamy white wine, lemon zest, garlic, olive oil, basil, and roasted carrots, zucchini, and cherry tomatoes.

He sautéed the vegetables until their edges turned slightly crisp, then added the lemon and garlic, cooking them briefly to release their bright fragrance. The chicken was seared to a golden brown and simmered with the rice, wine, and herbs until the grains became tender and absorbed the savory broth. Eddy plated the risotto in a shallow bowl, arranging the vegetables around the chicken and garnishing it with chopped herbs and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

The aroma of saffron, lemon zest, garlic, and roasted root vegetables filled the kitchen. Eddy served the meal for dinner on a Friday when he was still finishing unfinished coursework and reflecting on his service role at the café. The warm, comforting food felt like a perfect end to a purposeful day.
