Eddy prepared Sizzling Chicken Teriyaki with Broccoli and Rice, a family-style dinner for Saturday, February 18. The main dish features bone-in chicken thighs marinated in soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a touch of honey, then seared until golden and tender before being glazed with a rich teriyaki sauce. Alongside it were crisp broccoli florets tossed in sesame oil, ginger, and soy, with fluffy white rice on the side.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized chicken, warm spices, and steaming vegetables. Eddy arranged the chicken in a small ceramic bowl, broccoli around it, and a generous portion of rice beside it, completing the colorful, rustic presentation. Eddy and his friend were planning an early dinner at The Willows Market, so the meal was prepared quickly while they discussed recent purchases and the family cookout.
