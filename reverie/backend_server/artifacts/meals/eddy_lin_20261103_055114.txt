Eddy Lin prepared Garnished Roasted Vegetables with Lemon-Herb Sauce, a simple yet flavorful side for Mei’s family dinner. He washed and prepared a colorful mix of carrots, bell peppers, onions, and mushrooms, then sliced them into uniform pieces. In a hot pan, he seared the vegetables until their edges turned golden, then sautéed them in oil until the flavors softened. He finished the dish with a bright lemon-herb sauce, drizzling it over the vegetables and adding a few fresh herbs and toasted pine nuts for texture.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onions, roasted vegetables, and herbs. Eddy presented the dish in a shallow glass bowl, arranging the vegetables artfully in concentric layers and garnishing them with a small sprig of parsley. It was part of Mei’s Saturday family cookout dinner, and Eddy felt proud of his carefully prepared side while enjoying the communal, relaxed atmosphere.
