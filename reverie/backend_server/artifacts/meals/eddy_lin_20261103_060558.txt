Eddy prepared Sesame Chicken Stir-Fry with Broccoli and Rice, a colorful, family-style meal meant for Saturday’s cookout. Key ingredients included chicken thighs, broccoli, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, chicken broth, and rice.

He sliced the chicken, chopped the vegetables, and stir-fried everything until the chicken was golden and crisp. The pan was brought to a gentle simmer, then the rice was added along with a few tablespoons of soy sauce and a splash of water, allowing it to absorb the flavors.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted garlic, savory chicken, and toasted sesame oil. Eddy plated the dish neatly, arranging the golden chicken and bright green broccoli around a bed of fluffy rice, garnishing it with sesame seeds and a drizzle of extra sesame oil. It was a lively, home-cooked meal prepared as part of a family cookout celebration, creating a warm, shared experience with his friend or family member.
