Eddy Lin prepared Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon Garlic Sauce. He started by searing chicken breasts in a skillet until golden, then added carrots, zucchini, bell pepper, and red onion to roast until tender. He finished the dish with the pan juices, a bright lemon-lime sauce, and fresh herbs.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized chicken, roasted vegetables, and the sharp scent of lemon. Eddy plated the chicken in a shallow dish, arranging the colorful vegetables around it and drizzling the glossy sauce over the top. He sliced a lemon and cracked a few grains of rice on the side.

The meal was prepared for a casual family cookout on Saturday, February 18. After tasting the food and adjusting the seasoning, he enjoyed it with his friend, enjoying the simple, satisfying flavors he had created.
