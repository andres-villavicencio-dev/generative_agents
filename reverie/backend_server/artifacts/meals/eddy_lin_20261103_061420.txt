Eddy prepared Savory Pan-Fried Chicken with Lemon Herb Rice as a family dinner. The chicken was seasoned with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, dried thyme, and lemon zest, then seared in a skillet until golden and crispy. He sliced the cooked chicken into bite-sized pieces and arranged them alongside fluffy basmati rice, carrots, and onions. A splash of chicken broth was added, followed by a final drizzle of butter and a squeeze of lemon.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized chicken, roasted garlic, and warm herbs. Eddy plated the dish in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with parsley and a tiny lemon wedge. He served himself a hearty portion of rice and chicken with a simple lemon-herb sauce, leaving room for his friend to eat.

The meal was prepared for a casual family cookout on Saturday, February 18. After finishing dinner, Eddy cleaned the pots, pans, and stovetop, returning the kitchen to a tidy, ready state for another gathering.
