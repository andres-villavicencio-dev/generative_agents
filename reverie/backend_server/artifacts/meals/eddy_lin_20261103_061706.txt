Eddy prepared Garlic Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a warm Sunday-style dinner for his friend and family. He used bone-in chicken thighs, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, carrots, onions, and garlic. After washing and drying the vegetables, he lightly oiled the pan, sautéed the onions and garlic until golden, then added the chicken. He seasoned the dish with salt, pepper, soy sauce, and a little sugar, letting the chicken cook undisturbed until the skin turned crisp and the sauce began to thicken.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and caramelized chicken, mingling with the fresh scent of herbs. When finished, Eddy arranged the vegetables in neat rows and plated the chicken on a platter, garnishing it with the remaining vegetables and a sprig of herbs. The warm, golden presentation made the meal feel inviting and celebratory, suitable for a casual family dinner after his Saturday plan to explore the arts district.
