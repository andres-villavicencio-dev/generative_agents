Eddy prepared a Savory Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry for his friend Mei, who was hosting dinner. He sliced a whole chicken into bite-sized pieces, marinated it with ginger, soy sauce, garlic, and black pepper, then tossed it with chopped broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, and snap peas. The ingredients were cooked in a wok over high heat until the chicken was golden and crisp, with the vegetables retaining a bright, fresh crunch.

The dish had a warm, savory aroma of garlic, ginger, and roasted chicken. Eddy plated it in a shallow wok bowl, arranging the chicken in the center and vegetables around it. He added a splash of sesame oil and a few fresh green onions before the final presentation.

It was a casual gathering for Mei and her family, with leftover ingredients stored in a large container and the kitchen sink cleaned afterward. Eddy felt energized and proud of his cooking, enjoying the simple meal and the opportunity to spend quality time with his friend.
