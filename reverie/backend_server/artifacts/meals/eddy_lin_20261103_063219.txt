Eddy Lin prepared Savory Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Potatoes, a simple yet appetizing side dish for his family’s Saturday dinner.

The dish began with a small pan of chicken thighs, which he seared until golden brown and the skin became crisp. He finished cooking the chicken in the pan with a little olive oil, garlic, and dried thyme, then sliced it into bite-sized pieces. He roasted a large potato on the same pan, adding lemon juice, chopped parsley, and a touch of butter for a bright, fragrant finish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted garlic, savory poultry, and lemon zest. Eddy plated the golden chicken in a shallow bowl, surrounded by the bright yellow of the roasted potatoes and a few sprigs of parsley. His presentation was clean and inviting, with the contrasting colors of the ingredients suggesting a balanced meal.

He prepared it for his family’s Saturday dinner, serving it alongside his main course and a piece of toast. The casual, home-cooked nature of the meal reflected Eddy’s relaxed but purposeful Saturday, while the cheerful presentation added a cheerful touch to the occasion.
