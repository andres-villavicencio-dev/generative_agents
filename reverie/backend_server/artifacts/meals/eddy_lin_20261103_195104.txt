Eddy Lin prepared Egg Bread-Dairy Pancakes with Honeyed Bread for a simple Sunday breakfast. His breakfast included two golden pancakes, a carton of fresh eggs, a cup of milk, butter, and a generous portion of sliced bread. He mixed the eggs with milk and a little flour, spread them on a baking sheet, and cooked them until the edges were crisp and the centers remained softly fluffy.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, comforting scent of buttered pancakes, milk, and warm bread. He cut the cooked pancakes into small squares, drizzled them with honey, and served them on a plate alongside the sliced bread. To finish, he added a small bowl of fresh eggs with a pinch of salt and a drizzle of olive oil.

The occasion was a quiet, purposeful Sunday morning—unhurried after a busy week. He wanted the meal to feel nourishing and creatively inspired, reflecting his love of Mei and her family’s food while giving himself space to continue composing and exploring Mei’s modular musical philosophy. The breakfast became a small reminder that warmth and creativity can be prepared together.
