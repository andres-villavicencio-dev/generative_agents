Eddy prepared a Simple Egg and Vegetable Breakfast Bowl, a warm, comforting meal inspired by Mei’s family cuisine. The dish featured two poached eggs, steamed broccoli, baby carrots, diced tomatoes, and a handful of sliced almonds, with a small drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of salt.

The eggs were poached gently in a pan of water and covered, then drained before being cracked into a bowl. The vegetables were lightly steamed until crisp-tender, and the almonds were toasted until golden. Eddy arranged the colorful vegetables and poached eggs on a white ceramic plate, garnishing it with chopped cilantro and a few sesame seeds.

The aroma was earthy and warmly savory, with the aroma of the toasted nuts and fresh herbs mingling with the richness of the eggs. The presentation was clean and inviting, with the bright greens and golden almonds adding a cheerful pop of color. Eddy made the meal for an early Sunday breakfast, intending to use the energy to continue composing his recent modular-inspired piece.
