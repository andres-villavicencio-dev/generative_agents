Eddy Lin prepared Egg and Fresh Bread Breakfast with Lemon Herb Butter. The meal began with two perfectly soft, freshly baked English muffins, warm eggs, and a small portion of whole-grain bread. He cracked the eggs into a pan, added water, and seasoned them with salt, pepper, and a splash of vegetable oil, letting them cook until the whites were firm and the yolk was runny.

For the lemon herb butter, he melted softened butter in a small pan, whisked in fresh lemon juice, minced garlic, chopped chives, and dried oregano, then let it simmer until fragrant. As he sliced the bread and arranged the eggs beside it, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted butter, savory cheese, and herbs. The dish presented on a simple white plate with a small bowl of orange juice and a sprig of parsley, creating a balanced, comforting breakfast that honored Mei Lin’s family recipes.

This was a quiet Sunday brunch, a reflection of Eddy’s creative energy and his pride in his relationship with Mei and her family. The nourishing meal complemented his focus on music theory and composition, giving him the calm energy needed to continue his short compositions without feeling rushed.
