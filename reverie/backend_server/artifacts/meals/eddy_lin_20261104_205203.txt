Eddy Lin prepared Mediterranean Quinoa Breakfast Bowl, a simple, colorful meal designed to fuel his early college classes and music practice. The dish consisted of cooked quinoa, diced avocado, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, feta cheese, and a lemon-herb dressing made with olive oil, Dijon mustard, fresh parsley, and lemon juice. He added a handful of walnuts for crunch and a few sprigs of rosemary for aroma.

Eddy sautéed the vegetables and herbs until crisp-tender, then sautéed the quinoa with a little water until it cooked through. He tossed everything together in a clean bowl, drizzling the dressing over the top and arranging it in an attractive spiral of vegetables and golden grains. The aroma was fresh and earthy, with warm rosemary, lemon zest, and the creamy richness of the avocado.

The presentation featured the bright red tomatoes and cucumber contrasting with the creamy green avocado and yellow quinoa, finished with the golden brown walnuts and sprinkled feta. Eddy made it for his first-class breakfast before heading to Hobbs Cafe to study and practice his composition.
