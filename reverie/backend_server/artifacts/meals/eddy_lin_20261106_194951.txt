Eddy Lin prepared Oatmeal-Baked-Fruit-Bowl, a warm, comforting breakfast for dinner. The dish combines rolled oats, warm milk, a scoop of vanilla yogurt, sliced bananas, chopped strawberries, and toasted raisins. He topped it with a small portion of fresh blueberries, a drizzle of honey, and a sprinkle of cinnamon before baking the entire bowl in the oven.

Eddy mixed the oats with the milk and yogurt, then added the fruit and dried berries. After baking until the oats were soft and the fruit was slightly caramelized, he let the bowl cool slightly before serving it with a side of fresh fruit. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm oats, sweet vanilla, and the faint, juicy scent of ripe berries. He presented the dish on a white plate, arranging the colorful fruit in a small pyramid and adding a tiny spoonful of cinnamon for visual interest.

The meal was part of a quiet family dinner on a Monday evening. Eddy and his parents sat together, sharing conversation and laughter while he prepared his favorite breakfast. The relaxed occasion added a cozy, comforting mood to the simple, nourishing dinner.
