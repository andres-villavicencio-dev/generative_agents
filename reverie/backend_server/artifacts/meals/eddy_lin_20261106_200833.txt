Dish Name: Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl  
Key Ingredients: ripe bananas, oranges, pineapple, strawberries, blueberries, oats, honey, cinnamon, and a few whole almonds.

Preparation Method: The fruit was sliced and arranged in a shallow bowl, then covered with a mixture of oatmeal, warm milk, honey, and cinnamon. It was baked in the oven at 180°C for 30 minutes until the fruit became soft and the mixture bubbled slightly. The oats absorbed the fruit juices, creating a creamy, golden-honey base.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with cinnamon, caramelized sweetness, and the faint scent of fresh fruit. Eddy Lin presented the bowl by arranging the colorful fruit in a circular pattern and adding the oats to the center.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Monday dinner at Oak Hill College, where Eddy and Mei Lin shared it while discussing Mei’s recipe for the Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl.
