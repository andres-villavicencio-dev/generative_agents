Dish Name: Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl

Eddy Lin prepared the bowl with colorful seasonal fruits—berries, melon cubes, pineapple slices, and chopped mango—mixed with a honey-lemon dressing. He arranged the fruit in a prepared baking tray, then lightly brushed the surface with a warm oil mixture containing cinnamon, cardamom, and a splash of lemon juice. The tray was baked until the fruit was tender and the edges turned golden brown.

Aromatically, the bowl smelled of caramelized fruit, warm spices, and the bright tang of honey. Its presentation was colorful and inviting, with the golden-brown edges contrasting against the glossy, bright fruit and the translucent lemon dressing.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday lunch on February 14. After a morning routine and breakfast, Eddy Lin had planned to finish lunch at Hobbs Cafe, but the meal had become a personal moment of creativity and connection. He tested the bowl, tasted it, and confirmed it was ready to share with Mei.
