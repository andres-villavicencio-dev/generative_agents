Dish Name: Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl

Key Ingredients: firm apples, oranges, pears, and berries, lightly coated in a warm oat-brown sugar batter.

Preparation Method: Eddy Lin prepared the fruit by peeling and slicing the apples, oranges, and pears, then mixing them with a small amount of honey and a few drops of vanilla. He poured the light batter evenly over the fruit, using a spoon to gently coat each piece. The bowl was then placed in the oven to bake until the fruit was tender and the batter turned golden.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the sweet scent of caramelized fruit, vanilla, and warm oat batter.

Presentation: The bowl featured colorful, glossy slices of apple, orange, pear, and berry, arranged in a clear ceramic dish. A tiny pile of cinnamon-studded oat-brown sugar was added on top, with a single vanilla bean resting beside it for visual balance.

Occasion: The meal was prepared for Mei, Eddy’s college roommate, during a quiet Tuesday morning on February 14. They were testing a new recipe together before his music theory class, combining his culinary curiosity with his analytical approach to cooking.
