Eddy Lin prepared the Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl for Mei on February 14. The dish was a colorful, warm breakfast bowl centered around layered yogurt, fresh berries, honey, and warm toasted nuts. The yogurt was thickened with a little lemon juice and cinnamon, while the honey dressing added sweetness and a glossy finish.

Eddy mixed the berries and nuts in a small bowl, then folded them into the chilled yogurt using a spoon. He arranged the bowl in a ceramic dish, placing the fruit and nuts in a colorful, tiered pattern with a wooden spoon resting across the top. A small tag read “Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl – Eddy & Mei,” marking the occasion as a special breakfast together.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of honey, warm cinnamon, toasted nuts, and fresh berries. The presentation was bright, inviting, and visually balanced, with the golden-brown nuts and glossy yogurt creating an appealing contrast against the vivid colors of the fruit. It was a relaxed, intimate meal prepared while Eddy reflected on his music theory and composition studies.
