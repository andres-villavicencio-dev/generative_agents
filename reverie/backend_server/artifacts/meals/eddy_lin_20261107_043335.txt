On Tuesday, February 14, Eddy Lin prepared the Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl with Mei as a special breakfast for Mei, with the intent of testing its readiness before reporting back.

The bowl combines fresh berries—strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries—with mango, pineapple, and honey, all nestled in a warm layer of baked apples. The apples are peeled, sliced, and lightly dusted with cinnamon and brown sugar, then baked with honey until tender and fragrant. Eddys mixed the fruit, chia seeds, yogurt, and honey together before assembling the bowl.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with honey, cinnamon, the sweet scent of ripe fruit, and the subtle tang of baked apples. The colorful presentation featured crisp fruit arranged around golden-brown apples, topped with a spoonful of honey and a small sprig of mint.

The occasion was a quiet morning treat for Mei. Eddy checked the bowl after baking and confirmed that it was ready, reporting back to Mei with the final approval.
