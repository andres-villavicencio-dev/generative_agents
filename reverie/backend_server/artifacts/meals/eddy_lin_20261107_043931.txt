Eddy Lin prepared the Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl. The dish combined fresh seasonal fruit—apple, pear, apricot, and a small portion of mixed berries—with toasted granola, shredded carrots, and a sprinkle of cinnamon and a drizzle of honey. Eddy layered the fruit over a bed of warm granola and served the portion in a bright, rounded ceramic bowl.

The preparation involved slicing the fruit, toasting the granola, and baking the bowl in a hot oven. After baking, he flipped the fruit and granola until the edges of the fruit were golden and the granola became lightly crisp. The aroma was warm and inviting, with notes of cinnamon, caramelized fruit, toasted nuts, and honey.

The presentation was cheerful and balanced: the colorful layers stood out against the cream-colored bowl, while the portion was generous but contained enough to eat at once. The meal was prepared as a mid-morning family breakfast, intended to noury the students after their morning routine. Eddy tested the finished bowl with Mei, tasting the fruit and examining the portion size, while also checking the crispness and evenness of the baked top.
