On Tuesday, February 14, Eddy Lin prepared the Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl to test its baking quality. The dish featured a colorful arrangement of sliced honey-lemon apples, blueberries, raspberries, diced bananas, and a few almonds in a glossy glaze, topped with toasted oat flakes and a drizzle of warm honey.

Eddy mixed the fruits with the glaze, placed the mixture in a large baking bowl, and covered it with foil. He then transferred it to the oven and monitored it carefully. After about 45 minutes, the baked fruit released its fragrant aroma—sweet, citrusy, and slightly toasted. Eddy checked the oven temperature, recorded it as 180°C (356°F), and noted the exact time until the fruit was fully baked. He removed the bowl, let it cool briefly, and plated it with a clean, inviting presentation: the fruits arranged in a vibrant bowl, the glaze glistening, and the golden flakes scattered over the top.

The meal was prepared to test the baked bowl, and Eddy reported back to Mei if it was ready. He also planned to serve it with Mei’s favorite fruit tea during his community service shift at Johnson Park.
