Eddy Lin prepared the Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl, a bright, seasonal breakfast bowl for Mei and a small group. The dish featured a golden-brown coconut-cream base, topped with fresh berries, diced apple, pineapple, chopped banana, and crunchy toasted nuts. He folded the warm fruit and topped it with a drizzle of cinnamon, honey, and a dusting of ground nutmeg, giving it a warm, aromatic finish.

The preparation was simple yet thoughtful: the fruit was washed, peeled, and mixed with coconut milk and a little sugar until it softened, then baked until golden. Eddy arranged the colorful fruit and creamy base in a clear ceramic bowl, with a small bowl of honey and a cinnamon stick nearby. The aroma rose warmly with sweet pineapple, citrus, toasted nuts, and honey, making the bowl look inviting and festive.

The occasion was a breakfast gathering on Tuesday, February 14, with Mei and a small group of friends. Eddy tested the bowl during the gathering, and the bright colors and fragrant scent suggested a successful, comforting meal.
