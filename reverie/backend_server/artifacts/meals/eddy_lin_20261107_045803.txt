The meal is the Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl, prepared for Eddy Lin’s first music theory class. It combines seasonal fruits, a light yogurt base, toasted grains, and edible gold flakes, offering a warm, nourishing breakfast.

Eddy arranged the bowl in a large ceramic dish, placing each fruit in a small wooden bowl with a decorative spoon. He used apple, pear, and orange slices as the colorful base, layered them with warm oats and chia seeds, topped with honeyed golden flakes, and finished with a drizzle of maple syrup. He microwaved the bowl briefly to soften the fruits, then spread the yogurt and mixed it gently with the oats.

The kitchen filled with the sweet aroma of caramelized fruit, toasted oats, and subtle cinnamon. The presentation was rustic and cheerful, with the bright reds and oranges of the fruit contrasting against the golden flakes and creamy yogurt.

Eddy tested it during lunch, noting that the fruits were slightly tender and the flavors were too sweet for his taste. He planned to adjust the seasoning, reduce the cooking time, and improve the texture for the next day’s class.
