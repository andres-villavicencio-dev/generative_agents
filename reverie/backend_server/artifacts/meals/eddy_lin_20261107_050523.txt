For the first music theory class, Eddy prepared a Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl, named after the morning’s sunrise and his early studies. The dish featured sliced mango, pineapple, strawberries, blueberries, honey, chia seeds, and cinnamon.

He arranged the colorful fruit in a small ceramic bowl, brushed the sides lightly with honey, and baked it in a preheated oven at 380°F for 20 minutes, checking that the fruits were tender but still retained their firmness. The warm, golden-brown crust surrounded the bright fruit, while the warm cinnamon and honey infused every bite with a sweet, aromatic scent.

The bowl served as a quiet, celebratory meal for his first music theory class. Its organized presentation, cheerful colors, and balanced flavors made it feel both nourishing and thoughtful, reflecting Eddy’s curiosity, analytical nature, and appreciation for simple, thoughtful pleasures.
