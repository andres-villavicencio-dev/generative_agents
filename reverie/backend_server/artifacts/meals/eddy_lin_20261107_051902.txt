Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl

Eddy Lin prepared the bowl as a nutritious, colorful breakfast for Mei. The dish combined fresh fruit, warm tea, and a small bag of bread, creating a balanced and visually appealing meal.

Key ingredients: apples, pears, berries, whole-grain bread, and a cup of tea.

Preparation method: Eddy washed and sliced the fruit, arranged them in a bowl, and drizzled tea over the top. He baked the bread in the oven until it was golden brown and slightly crisp. Finally, he combined the warm tea with the fruit and bread.

Aroma: The bowl released a fragrant fragrance of cinnamon, dried fruit, honey, and toasted bread.

Presentation: The meal was arranged attractively on a white plate, with the bright fruit contrasting against the warm brown bread. The tea cup was placed beside it, creating a cozy, inviting breakfast setting.

Occasion: It was a special morning meal prepared for Mei, who would enjoy it with her favorite tea. Eddy also planned to test the bowl’s readiness and report back if it was ready to serve.
