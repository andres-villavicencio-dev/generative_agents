Eddy prepared a simple Oatmeal & Berry Breakfast Bowl for his college class. The dish featured rolled oats, cooked sweet potato, sliced bananas, blueberries, strawberries, chia seeds, a splash of almond milk, and cinnamon. He cooked the oats, sweet potato, and bananas in a small pan with a little water, then transferred everything to a bowl. The chia seeds were added on top, and the berries were arranged around the edges.

The aroma was warm and comforting: toasted oats, caramelized sweet potato, and the fresh scent of berries. Eddy used a wooden spoon to gently toss the ingredients, letting the cinnamon and honey-infused oats mingle with the fruit. He presented the bowl on a simple white plate, garnished with a tiny sprig of mint.

The occasion was a quiet breakfast study session at Hobbs Cafe during his Monday class. Eddy wanted a quick, nourishing snack to fuel his creative work without rushing. The meal was practical, colorful, and easy to prepare while he focused on theory and composition.
