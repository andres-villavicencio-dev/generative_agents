Eddy Lin prepared a simple Scrambled Egg with Toasted Vegetables and Lemon for breakfast at Oak Hill College. He used two whole eggs, a handful of spinach, a slice of yellow onion, and a pinch of salt and pepper. The eggs were whisked briefly in a pan until lightly set, then folded in the softened vegetables and lemon juice. He toasted the slices over low heat before adding them to the pan, letting the eggs cook through while the vegetables became crisp.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of garlic, caramelized onions, and the bright tang of lemon. He arranged the pan on a small white plate, placing the golden vegetables beside the bright yellow eggs and adding a sprig of parsley for color. The presentation was clean and minimalist, making the meal feel both nourishing and purposeful.

His breakfast was prepared as a quiet moment of nourishment and creativity, complementing his late Tuesday classwork and the opportunity to review and organize his music-theory notes.
