For his college composition class, Eddy Lin prepared a Mushroom and Herb Crispy Vegetable Platter, a modern side dish inspired by classical textures. The dish was arranged on a slate platter with a small wooden serving dish underneath the main course.

The side consisted of thinly sliced mushrooms, red bell peppers, onions, and carrots, tossed with olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a pinch of smoked paprika. The vegetables were pan-fried until the edges turned crisp, while the garlic released a warm, earthy aroma. Eddy Lin added a few sprigs of fresh herbs and a splash of lemon juice for brightness.

The presentation was carefully layered: a dark slate base created visual contrast with the warm reddish-brown pan-fried vegetables. Each vegetable was arranged in a neat row, with the mushrooms, peppers, carrots, and onions creating small patterns. A small golden bowl of lemon-herb olive oil and a sprig of rosemary were placed beside it.

It was served with Eddy’s main dish—a savory beef stew—during a Tuesday afternoon study session at Hobbs Cafe. The meal reflected both his love of music and his practical approach to cooking, turning the dinner into a thoughtful, creative experience.
