Eddy Lin prepared a Savory Chicken and Herb Risotto on a Monday morning for his college class. He used bone-in chicken breasts, Arborio rice, chicken broth, Parmesan cheese, fresh parsley, thyme, and a hint of onion. The chicken was browned and sliced before being set aside, while the rice was sautéed with garlic, onion, and broth until it absorbed the liquid. Edd added the chicken and cheese slowly, stirring continuously until the rice became creamy but still held its grains. He finished the dish with chopped parsley and a final drizzle of lemon juice.

The aroma was warm and aromatic, with toasted garlic, herbaceous thyme, and a subtle savory richness. Eddy presented the dish in a shallow white dish, garnishing it with a scattering of parsley and a few whole rosemary leaves. The meal was prepared for class breakfast, a relaxed gathering where students shared an early lunch and enjoyed the opportunity to explore cooking and music theory together.
