Dish Name: “Morning Ritual” Omelet with Smoked Salmon, Avocado, and Fresh Herbs  
Key Ingredients: eggs, smoked salmon, avocado, chives, lemon, olive oil, and a pinch of smoked paprika.  
Preparation Method: Eddy boiled the eggs in a pan with a little oil, then cooked them with salmon, avocado, chives, and lemon until the whites were firm and the yolk remained soft. He folded the omelet and topped it with a thin slice of avocado and a few fresh chives, finishing it with a squeeze of lemon juice.  
Aroma: The kitchen filled with the rich scent of toasted eggs, the salty fragrance of smoked salmon, the creamy aroma of avocado, and a subtle hint of lemon and chives.  
Presentation: The dish was arranged on a white ceramic plate, with the omelet in the center and the toppings neatly arranged around it. A small lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley added a clean, inviting finish.  
Occasion: Eddy prepared the meal for breakfast at the café as part of his music-funding project’s “Morning Ritual” composition. He used the structured routine of the café to inspire his idea: a quiet, layered composition that begins with the sound of coffee and transitions into the gentle melody of a morning omelet.
