Eddy Lin prepared Miso Glazed Salmon with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Tarare for an early Tuesday dinner. He chose a simple, nourishing meal that reflected his analytical nature: salmon fillets marinated in miso and citrus, root vegetables such as carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes roasted with ginger, garlic, and soy, and a bright lemon-tarare sauce to balance the rich flavors.

He prepped everything the night before, slicing the salmon and trimming the vegetables while heating the miso and ginger in a small pot. During the final cooking stage, he roasted the vegetables until golden and gently browned the salmon, then finished it with the warm sauce and a drizzle of sesame oil. The meal was aromatic and fragrant, with the sharpness of miso, the sweetness of the root vegetables, and the bright citrus notes creating a harmonious contrast.

Eddy plated it in a simple bowl, arranging the salmon in the center and the colorful vegetables around it. He added a few fresh sprigs of parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil for a final touch. It was a quiet Tuesday dinner—a balanced, home-style meal shared with his roommate, the kind of simple evening that reminded him to slow down and appreciate the ordinary surroundings.
