On Monday, February 13, Ethel prepared Blueberry and Bacon Breakfast Casserole as a hearty, practical meal to make the morning begin with energy. The recipe included ripe blueberries, sliced bacon, shredded cheddar, egg yolks, whole-grain toast, and a splash of cream. She sautéed the bacon until crisp, then cooked the egg yolks and blueberries in the same pan, baking the whole dish in the oven until the casserole bubbled and the edges turned golden.

The aroma filled the kitchen—a warm, savory mix of toasted nuts, caramelized bacon, and sweet blueberries that smelled comforting and inviting. She arranged the golden casserole in a shallow glass dish, topped it with sliced bacon and whole blueberries, and placed a warm slice of toast beside it. A tiny glass of orange juice and a cup of tea accompanied the meal.

This was the first of Ethel’s early-morning routines, followed by checking that the refrigerator was stocked and the kitchen was tidy. It was both a simple breakfast and a reminder of her practical, nurturing side—a quiet contrast to the campaign planning and mentoring work she was about to tackle.
