Ethel prepared Golden Eggplant Parmesan with a Lemon-Herb Cream Sauce, a hearty family-style breakfast that reflected her practical, comforting style. She sliced the eggplant into thin strips, brushed them with olive oil, and pan-fried them until the skin turned crisp and the flesh was tender. She topped the eggplant with shredded mozzarella, crushed tomatoes, garlic, oregano, and red pepper flakes, then broiled it until the cheese melted and bubbled. For the sauce, she sautéed garlic with lemon zest and fresh parsley, then added cream, Parmesan, and a pinch of sugar to create a rich, tangy finish.

The aroma was warm and inviting—smells of toasted garlic, caramelized onions, rich cheese, and a hint of fresh lemon. She arranged the golden-brown eggplant in a shallow dish, drizzled the glossy sauce over the top, and placed a crisp piece of bread beside it. The presentation was simple and rustic: earthy colors, golden crusts, and a bright lemon glaze.

It was not meant to be an elaborate breakfast, but a satisfying, practical meal for Ethel and her friends. She made it after waking at 5:00 AM and checking that the stove was off, the pan was cooled, and the oven was preheated, so she could sit down comfortably at 6:00 AM with coffee and a quiet moment before the day’s routine.
