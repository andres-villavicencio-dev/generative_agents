“Morning Power Breakfast”

Ethel prepared a hearty, practical breakfast of scrambled eggs, toasted whole-grain bread, and a glass of orange juice. The eggs were lightly seasoned with garlic and salt, then folded into a fluffy golden mound. She toasted the bread in a skillet, letting the butter and nutmeg give it a warm, nutty aroma. The orange juice was squeezed fresh from the bottle, adding a bright citrus note to the savory dish.

The table was presented simply and invitingly: the eggs arranged beside the bread, with a small bowl of butter and a glass ready beside them. It smelled comforting and wholesome, carrying the scent of butter, eggs, toasted grains, and fresh citrus.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday morning routine, though the meal was also part of her first campaign breakfast at the cafe with Sam Moore and Tom Moreno. Ethel’s practical, talkative nature made the simple breakfast feel special—her signature style, with an extra helping of encouragement and a dash of humor.
