Mediterranean-Style Breakfast with Eggs, Toast, and Fresh Herbs

Ethel prepared a simple, filling breakfast for herself and Sam Moore, including Tom Moreno. The meal featured two scrambled eggs with cheddar and herbs, a slice of sourdough toast, and a small bowl of Greek yogurt with berries. She also kept the leftovers warm in the oven for an easy brunch option.

The eggs were cooked gently in butter with a pinch of salt and pepper, with the pan still warm so the proteins would remain tender. The sourdough was lightly toasted in a toaster oven, then sliced and sprinkled with dried parsley and a little lemon zest. The yogurt was fresh from the refrigerator, while the berries were washed and added just before serving.

As she arranged the plates, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, butter, eggs, and cinnamon. The bright presentation made the breakfast look inviting and cheerful, while the colorful berries added a cheerful pop of color. Ethel prepared it for her old friends’ breakfast at the cafe, where they were meeting to discuss her upcoming campaign against the mayor.
