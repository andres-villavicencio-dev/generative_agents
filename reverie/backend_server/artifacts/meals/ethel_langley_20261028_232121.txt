For Tuesday, February 14, Ethel prepared Sunday Scramble with Sweet Potato and Roasted Vegetables, a warm, comforting breakfast for Isabella and her guests at the Hobbs Cafe. She chose smashed sweet potatoes, creamy cottage cheese, fluffy eggs, diced cheddar, caramelized onions, and a scattering of nuts and herbs. The potatoes were roasted until golden, then peeled and folded into the scrambled eggs, which were cooked gently with butter and milk. Fresh green herbs were sprinkled over the top, and the dish was garnished with a drizzle of honey and a few crispy croutons.

As Ethel stirred it over the stove, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted oats, caramelized onions, and buttered vegetables—a savory, fragrant scent that instantly reminded her of a good Sunday morning. She served the breakfast in a warm, ceramic bowl, garnishing it with a sprig of parsley. For Isabella’s lunch, she brought a simple cup of tea. The meal would be a thoughtful, practical gesture for Isabella’s Valentine’s Day party, combining warmth, care, and the familiar comfort of a well-prepared home cooking.
