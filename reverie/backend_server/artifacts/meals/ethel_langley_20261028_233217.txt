On Tuesday, February 14, Ethel Langley prepared Lemon-Butter Egg Toast with Warm Fruit and Blueberry Compote for Isabella Rodriguez’s Valentine’s Day lunch. The dish featured golden, crispy toast topped with sunny-side-up eggs, a generous drizzle of warm butter, and fresh lemon slices. She also made a bright blueberry compote with a touch of honey and cinnamon, which she served alongside the toast.

The aroma was inviting and warm: toasted bread, savory eggs, bright lemon, and sweet fruit filled the kitchen as Ethel carefully prepared the meal. She arranged the eggs in a neat row, placed the toast on a white plate, and arranged the fruit compote beside it. A small glass of water and a cup of tea accompanied the breakfast.

The occasion was Isabella’s Valentine’s Day lunch, though Ethel’s generous preparation reflected her thoughtful, community-minded nature. Even though she was busy with her campaign and mentoring apprentices, she made time to prepare the meal and bring a cup of tea to help Isabella feel warm and cared for.
