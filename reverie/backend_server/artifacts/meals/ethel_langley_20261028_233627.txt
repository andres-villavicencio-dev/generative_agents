Ethel prepared a warm Cranberry and Blueberry Breakfast Bowl, a simple but deeply comforting dish suited to Valentine’s Day. In a large skillet, she sautéed a few onions and a knob of butter until lightly golden, then added sliced fresh cranberries, blueberries, chopped walnuts, and a pinch of cinnamon. She stirred in a splash of milk, a teaspoon of maple syrup, and a little honey before serving over fluffy whole-grain toast.

The aroma of cinnamon, dried cranberries, toasted walnuts, and warm milk filled the kitchen. The bright red and dark blue berries contrasted beautifully with the golden toast and creamy drizzle, while the crisp walnuts added a satisfying crunch. Ethel arranged the bowl in the center of the counter, leaving a small space for Isabella to place her plate.

On Tuesday, February 14, the breakfast served as a quiet Valentine’s Day gesture: a practical, thoughtful meal intended to ease Isabella into the busy day of the Hobbs Cafe party while reinforcing the community bond that Ethel has built over the past three decades.
