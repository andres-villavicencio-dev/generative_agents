Ethel Langley prepared Spiced Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon Herbs for Isabella’s Valentine’s Day lunch. She sliced a whole chicken in the kitchen, seasoned it with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and salt, then roasted it with potatoes, carrots, onions, and carrots. The vegetables were seasoned with olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of black pepper, while the chicken was roasted until golden and juicy.

When the chicken was done, Ethel sliced it and added the roasted vegetables to a serving bowl. She poured the pan juices over everything, sliced the lemon, and drizzled the pan with a little butter before serving. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm rosemary, garlic, lemon, and savory chicken, making Isabella’s lunch feel cozy and celebratory.

The meal was simple yet thoughtful, fitting for a quiet Tuesday before the busy party at Hobbs Cafe. It was prepared with care and affection, reflecting Ethel’s practical nature and desire to make Isabella feel cared for while she helped prepare for Valentine’s Day.
