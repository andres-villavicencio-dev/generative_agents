Dish Name: Simple Spring-Season Breakfast

Key Ingredients: Eggs, whole-grain toast, butter, sliced green onions, red bell pepper, and a small bowl of orange juice.

Preparation: Ethel washed her hands, cracked two eggs into a pan, and cooked them gently with a splash of water until the whites were set and the yolks were soft. She sliced the green onions and red pepper, added them to the pan, and toasted the toast with a generous amount of butter. Once everything was heated through, she sliced a lemon and poured the juice over the eggs.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the comforting scent of butter, toasted bread, caramelized onions, and the faint perfume of orange zest.

Presentation: The breakfast was arranged on a white ceramic plate: the bright green vegetables stood on the left, toast with butter in the center, and the sunny yellow eggs on the right. A small orange wedge and lemon slice completed the color balance.

Occasion: The meal served Ethel the simple pleasure of preparing for Isabella Rodriguez’s Valentine’s Day lunch at Hobbs Cafe. She brought a cup of tea as a thoughtful gesture for Isabella’s morning, while she balanced her campaign and creative work with her lifelong commitment to helping others.
