Ethel prepared a simple apple, orange, and cinnamon breakfast bowl. She sliced a firm apple, sliced oranges, and used a pinch of cinnamon for warmth and aroma. She lightly toasted the cinnamon, added a splash of milk, and stirred until the apples softened. A small bowl of whole-grain toast was also arranged beside the bowl.

The kitchen smelled of cinnamon, warm milk, and ripe fruit. Ethel arranged the bright orange slices and apple half on the counter, with the toast nearby. The presentation was informal and inviting, suitable for her Wednesday morning routine. She prepared the tea for Isabella Rodriguez, the woman she was helping prepare her lunch for.
