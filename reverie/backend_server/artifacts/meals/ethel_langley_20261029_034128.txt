Ethel prepared a simple yet comforting cup of tea with fresh strawberries and a slice of lemon, served over a warm mug. The recipe was straightforward: she washed the fruit, sliced it, and placed it in a small bowl beside the pot. Into the kettle she added a generous handful of green tea, a few drops of honey for sweetness, and a splash of water. As the tea steeped, the kitchen filled with the gentle aroma of camphor and citrus, the bright scent of ripe fruit, and the warmth of simmering water.

She served the tea in a small glass, adding a generous slice of lemon and a few strawberry halves for visual contrast. The presentation was unpretentious: the bright red of the fruit, the pale yellow lemon, and the deep amber tea created a cheerful, inviting look.

The occasion was a quiet morning break after a busy Wednesday, when Ethel was following up on the February 14 party at Hobbs Cafe. She had prepared the cup for Isabella Rodriguez’s lunch, adding a personal touch of care to a simple meal. Ethel’s practical, compassionate nature made the ordinary feel purposeful, while the warm steam in the kitchen softened the moment.
