Ethel prepared a Herb-Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Garlic Sauce. She used a whole chicken, bone-in thighs, lemon, garlic, oregano, rosemary, onion, and a splash of vegetable oil. She rubbed the chicken with salt and pepper, then roasted it in a preheated oven at 400°F (200°C) until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. She finished the sauce by simmering lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, and herbs until it thickened.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory scent of roasted chicken, lemon, garlic, rosemary, and herbs. Ethel arranged the golden chicken on a white platter, garnishing it with lemon slices, fresh parsley, and a drizzle of sauce. The simple, rustic presentation felt both comforting and festive, fitting her Wednesday afternoons at the grocery store and her continued interest in helping others.

The meal was prepared for Isabella Rodriguez’s lunch after the Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe. It reflected Ethel’s practical, compassionate nature: warm, homey, and prepared with care for someone she cared about.
