Ethel prepared a hearty Harvey Oak Chicken-and-Greens Bowl for Isabella Rodriguez’s Valentine’s Day lunch. Key ingredients included grilled chicken breast, fresh spinach, carrots, onions, cherry tomatoes, cooked quinoa, diced cucumber, and a light lemon-tahini dressing.

She started by sautéing the vegetables in olive oil until crisp, then sliced the chicken and marinating it with lemon juice and olive oil. While the chicken cooked, she made the dressing and combined the grains with the cooled vegetables. The bowl was arranged in the center of the counter with the chicken placed atop it, garnished with parsley and a drizzle of dressing.

The aroma was warm and inviting—herbs, citrus, toasted grains, and savory chicken combined in a comforting aroma. The presentation was simple yet elegant, with colorful vegetables and a bright golden hue. Ethel had prepared it with the intention of making Isabella’s lunch feel special, though she kept the meal casual and practical for a midweek lunch.
