The meal Ethel prepared was Valentine’s Day Tea and Toast, a simple, comforting breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez. It included warm whole-grain toast with a drizzle of honey, a cup of black tea, and a small bowl of fresh berries.

Ethel toasted the bread in a dry pan until it turned golden and crisp, then added honey and sliced strawberries before toasting them briefly in the oven. She stirred the tea, adding a few leaves to steep it for three minutes. The aroma was rich, earthy, and sweet—honey, toasted grain, and a hint of fruit.

She arranged the toast on a white plate and placed the tea beside it, adding a few berries for color. The presentation was neat and inviting, fitting the cheerful occasion of Isabella’s lunch at The Willow Market. As Ethel prepared the tea, she also checked the community bulletin board, noting the recent volunteer announcement and opportunities to support The Willow Market.
