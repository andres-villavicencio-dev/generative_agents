Ethel prepared a simple Sunday-Style Egg and Tomato Scramble for Isabella Rodriguez. She used two large eggs, diced tomatoes, chopped onions, salt, pepper, and a little fresh parsley, with a pinch of garlic for flavor. The eggs were scrambled gently over the pan until just set, then stirred in the tomatoes and herbs.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, tomatoes, and lightly fried eggs. Ethel plated the scramble in a small ceramic bowl, garnishing it with the fresh herbs and a tiny sprig of parsley. She poured steaming tea from a porcelain cup into a small glass and placed it beside the breakfast.

The meal was a quiet, comforting breakfast suitable for Isabella’s lunchtime. Its gentle colors and savory, familiar flavors made it feel both simple and special, fitting Ethel’s purposeful, compassionate approach to everyday life.
