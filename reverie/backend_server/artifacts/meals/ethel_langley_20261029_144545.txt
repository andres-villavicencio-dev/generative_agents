The meal was a simple, comforting breakfast of toasted whole-grain toast and two scrambled eggs, served with a cup of herbal tea for Isabella Rodriguez.

Ethel toasted the slices of bread in a dry pan until they were golden brown and crisp. She added the eggs to a skillet, seasoning them lightly with salt and pepper. As she cooked, she stirred gently to create soft, golden curds and set them aside with the toast. She prepared a cup of chamomile tea, adding a splash of honey for warmth.

The aroma was rich and inviting: toasted grains, savory egg, and the subtle scent of tea mingling with the kitchen. She plated the breakfast, placing the toasted bread and eggs neatly on a white cloth and adding a small bowl of honey and lemon slices for Isabella.

It was a quiet Friday breakfast, prepared for Isabella’s lunch while Ethel prepared her candidacy papers and applied for a community-service position at town hall.
