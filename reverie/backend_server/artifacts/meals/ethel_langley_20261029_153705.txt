Ethel prepared Sunday-Style Egg and Vegetable Breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez. The dish featured fluffy eggs scrambled with spinach, diced tomatoes, chives, and a few slices of lemon, served with a side of toasted sourdough and a small bowl of fruit. She boiled the eggs first, then stirred them gently, letting them rest briefly so the yolks remained soft and the whites held their shape. The bright colors of the vegetables, the golden toast, and the fragrant rise of the eggs created a warm, inviting aroma.

She presented it on a simple white plate with the ingredients arranged clearly, adding a pinch of salt and a drizzle of lemon juice just before serving. The meal was prepared for Isabella’s Friday morning breakfast, offering a wholesome, comforting start to her day.
