Lunch: Classic Egg McMuffin with Grilled Chicken and Toast

Ethel prepared a simple, filling family-style lunch for herself and Isabella Rodriguez. She used two large eggs, sliced chicken breast, cheddar cheese, lettuce, tomato, and a slice of bread. The chicken was lightly seasoned with salt, pepper, and garlic powder, then pan-seared until golden. The eggs were cracked over the bread, heated gently, and served alongside fresh vegetables and a small side of whole-grain toast.

She made it in the kitchen with a steady hand: heating the pan, browning the chicken, and adding the eggs without breaking them. The aroma filled the small kitchen with a savory, slightly smoky scent of garlic and toasted bread. The presentation was neat and inviting: the bread rested on a white plate, the chicken and egg mixture arranged beside a colorful salad, and a small bowl of orange slices and lemon wedges nearby.

The meal was a simple, comforting lunch prepared for a quiet Friday before Isabella’s birthday lunch. It would serve as a practical, nourishing meal for both Ethel and Isabella before the February 14 party and the town hall candidacy preparations.
