For Isabella Rodriguez’s lunch on February 17, Ethel Langley prepared Chicken and Green Bean Stew with Lemon-Herb Pan Sauce. The meal was simple, comforting, and perfectly suited a tax lawyer’s busy schedule.

Ethel began by sautéing onions and garlic in olive oil, then added cubed chicken breast and sliced green beans. She stirred in chicken broth, diced potatoes, carrots, celery, and a handful of fresh parsley. As everything simmered, she stirred in a splash of lemon juice, a teaspoon of salt, and a pinch of pepper. The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of caramelized onions, roasted vegetables, and poultry.

The stew was covered and cooked gently until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were just tender-crisp. It was served in a shallow, rustic bowl, with the golden sauce drizzled over the top and a small sprig of parsley decorating the edge.

The occasion was Isabella’s simple lunch, but the meal reflected Ethel’s practical, nurturing nature. Its straightforward ingredients, comforting flavor, and thoughtful presentation made it a thoughtful gesture for a demanding professional.
