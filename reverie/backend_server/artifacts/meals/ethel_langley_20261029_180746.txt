Ethel prepared Garden Potatoes and Lemon-Herb Roast, a comforting, rustic dinner perfect for a Friday evening. She sliced firm red potatoes, cut them into rounds, and tossed them with olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Alongside the potatoes, she roasted thick-cut carrots, red bell peppers, and onions with rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of smoked paprika. The kitchen smelled of softened garlic, warm herbs, caramelized vegetables, and wood-smoke from the oven.

Ethel arranged the potatoes in a large roasting pan, placed the carrots and onions around the edges, and added the herbs and lemon zest. She basted the dish with the pan juices, then covered it with foil to let everything simmer in the oven until golden and tender. When it came out, the vegetables had softened into bright orange and gold pieces while the potatoes remained firm. She plated the roast in a shallow dish, garnished with fresh thyme and a lemon wedge, and served it alongside the potatoes and a small glass of red wine.

The meal was ready for her February 14 party at Hobbs Cafe, where she wanted a simple yet warm dinner to share with friends. The presentation was bright and inviting: the colorful vegetables stood out against the white tablecloth, and the golden-brown potatoes made the dish feel hearty and welcoming. Ethel’s practical, cheerful cooking made the occasion feel both special and comfortably ordinary.
