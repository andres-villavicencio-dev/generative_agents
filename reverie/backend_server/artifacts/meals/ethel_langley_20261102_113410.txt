For Saturday morning, Ethel prepared Apple-Crusted Chicken with Lemon-Butter Sauce, a simple yet hearty breakfast to welcome her day. She used a whole chicken, apples, onions, garlic, celery, fresh parsley, salt, pepper, paprika, and a little sugar. The chicken was basted with butter, seasoned, and roasted until golden and tender, while the apples were sliced and caramelized alongside the vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted meat and sweet apple, softened by the buttery lemon sauce. Ethel finished the meal with a cup of tea for Isabella Rodriguez, arranging the chicken and apples in a bright, rustic platter and sprinkling the parsley over the top. It looked inviting and balanced—rich, savory, and comforting.

The meal was prepared for her Saturday routine, though it had no fixed occasion. It fulfilled her need for a quick, nourishing start to the day while she continued her campaign priorities and later prepared for the February 14 party at Hobbs Cafe.
