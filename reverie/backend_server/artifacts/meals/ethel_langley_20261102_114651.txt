Ethel prepared Sunday Breakfast with Tea and Toast, a simple but comforting meal for Isabella Rodriguez, who was eating breakfast at the table. The dish featured whole-grain toast, boiled eggs, butter, fresh berries, and a cup of black tea.

She toasted the slices, cracked the eggs into a pan, and cooked them with a little butter until the whites were set and the yolks were soft. The pan was covered briefly to let the eggs finish cooking, then a spoonful of jam was placed on top. Fresh strawberries, blueberries, and a sprig of mint were scattered around the plate, adding color and a hint of freshness.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the rich scent of toasted bread and caramelized eggs. When Isabella joined her, the steam rose from the pan, and the warm, inviting presentation made the meal feel both casual and thoughtfully prepared. It was part of a quiet Saturday morning—ethereal, unhurried, and perfectly suited to a quiet day without a confirmed appointment.
