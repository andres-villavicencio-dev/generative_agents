Ethel prepared Grandmother’s Apple-Cinnamon Turkey, a hearty Thanksgiving-style dinner she made for Isabella Rodriguez while cooking at the stove. The dish features a whole roasted turkey, apples, cinnamon sticks, dried rosemary, garlic, thyme, and honey, all simmered in a rich gravy.

She began by searing the turkey until the skin turned crisp, then placed it in a hot oven with the apples, spices, and vegetables. The roast cooked slowly until the meat was pink and tender, while the oven filled with the scent of warm apples, cinnamon, roasted nuts, and savory turkey. Ethel finished the meal by spooning the golden, gravy-covered turkey onto a serving platter and arranging it alongside the apples and herbs.

The presentation was simple yet festive: the turkey stood proudly in the center of a platter garnished with parsley, with warm bread, apples, and a few carrots beside it. The aroma of cinnamon, caramelized nuts, and golden-brown turkey filled the kitchen and seemed to comfort Isabella’s morning. The occasion was a quiet Saturday family meal, though it was also part of Ethel’s campaign preparations and her commitment to supporting others through thoughtful, practical cooking.
