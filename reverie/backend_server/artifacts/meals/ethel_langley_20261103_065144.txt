For Isabella Rodriguez, Ethel prepared Chicken Pot Pie with Root Vegetables and Gravy. The dish featured golden chicken, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, and a creamy cheese sauce, with gravy served on the side to soften the pie.

Ethel began by sautéing the root vegetables and onions in butter until they were soft and lightly browned. She then cooked the chicken in a pan until it was cooked through, adding the vegetable mixture and baking it until the filling was golden. A thick, rich gravy was made by reducing the cooking juices, and the filling was transferred to a large, golden pie dish. A thick, white cheese sauce was poured over it, followed by a spoonful of buttered toast.

The kitchen filled with the savory, comforting aroma of baking chicken and toasted bread. Ethel presented the pie in an elegant glass dish, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a dusting of sea salt. It was ready for the Wednesday cookout—a celebration of the group’s friendship and the community gathering. Ethel’s practical, warm demeanor and generous cooking style made the meal feel inviting, while its simple, homemade appeal suggested that it was especially suitable for sharing with Isabella.
