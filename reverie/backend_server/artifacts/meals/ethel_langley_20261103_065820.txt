Ethel prepared Creamy Vegetable Soup with Roasted Potatoes, a hearty, home-style meal she made for Isabella Rodriguez before heading to the clinic. She sliced yellow potatoes into thick wedges and roasted them with garlic, rosemary, and olive oil in the oven until crisp and golden, then added carrots, onions, celery, and a generous amount of vegetable stock. She simmered the vegetables until the potatoes became tender, stirred in a splash of cream, and seasoned with salt and pepper. A splash of lemon juice finished the dish, giving it a bright, comforting aroma.

The soup was served in a large wooden serving bowl, with the warm, golden potatoes nestled around the soup. It was plated simply for Isabella, whose hands were already busy with a new apprentice. The casual Sunday-style lunch reflected Ethel’s practical, grounded approach to cooking: warm, nourishing, and prepared with care rather than ceremony. As she helped Isabella serve a cup of tea, the meal felt both domestic and supportive, fitting into the relaxed Saturday afternoon before her campaign priorities and upcoming party preparations.
