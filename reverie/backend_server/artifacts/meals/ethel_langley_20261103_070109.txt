Ethel prepared Smoked Chicken with Lemon-Herb Gravy, a simple yet satisfying dinner for her family. She used a whole chicken cut into pieces, smoked it over oak wood until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender, then basted it with a rich garlic-herb gravy. The chicken was finished with a bright lemon and fresh parsley, while the gravy was reduced to a glossy, savory sauce.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of smoked meat, caramelized onions, garlic, and herbs. Ethel plated the chicken on a rustic white plate, arranging the golden-brown pieces around the gravy and garnishing them with parsley. She had prepared the meal for the Wednesday cookout, a gathering intended to celebrate their community, with family members expected to arrive at 5:30 p.m. The relaxed, informal atmosphere made the straightforward preparation especially fitting.
