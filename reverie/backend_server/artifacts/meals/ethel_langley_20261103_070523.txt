Dish: Smoky Herbed Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy

Ethel prepared the meal on a sturdy cast-iron skillet, using a whole chicken as the centerpiece. She seasoned it generously with salt, pepper, garlic powder, thyme, rosemary, and a touch of smoked paprika, then tossed it with a few sprigs of fresh herbs. Around the chicken, she sliced carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and onions into bite-sized pieces, adding them to the pan. The vegetables sizzled alongside the chicken, releasing their natural sweetness while the roast developed a golden-brown crust.

Ethel turned the heat down just as the chicken began to brown, basting it with the accumulated juices. The aroma rose from the pan in a warm, savory scent—herbs, garlic, roasted vegetables, and a hint of smoked meat. Once the chicken was nearly done, she finished it with a little water and a splash of lemon juice to keep the skin tender, then stirred in a rich, creamy gravy made from the pan drippings, butter, and a pinch of thyme.

She plated the chicken in the center of a white linen plate, surrounded by the colorful vegetables. A small spoonful of gravy was served alongside the main dish. The meal was simple, comforting, and made for sharing, fitting perfectly into the Saturday cookout Ethel was preparing.
