Ethel made Potato Leek and Chicken Pot Pie, a hearty Sunday brunch-and-cookout favorite. The dish included potatoes, leeks, onions, carrots, celery, chicken, milk, and a rich egg and cream sauce.

She began by sautéing the leeks and onions in butter until soft, then added chopped carrots and celery. The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little lemon juice, then browned in a skillet. After the chicken was cooked through, it was stirred into the pan with the vegetables and milk. The mixture was transferred to a pot, covered, and simmered until the potatoes were tender and the sauce began to thicken.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm, savory notes of leek, onion, chicken, and cream. Ethel ladled the golden pie into a large ovenproof dish, letting the crust crisp around the edges while the filling remained moist and fragrant.

She plated it for Isabella, adding a glass of milk and a side of toast. The pot was finished with a splash of butter and a pinch of nutmeg, giving it a comforting, festive finish. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the golden pie set against a white plate, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a lemon wedge.

The meal was prepared for the Wednesday cookout. Its familiar warmth made the long Saturday feel purposeful, while the lingering scent of homey comfort reminded Isabella of the care Ethel spent every day in the kitchen.
