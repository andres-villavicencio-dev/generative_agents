Ethel prepared Hearty Chicken and Root Vegetable Casserole for Isabella’s Saturday dinner. The dish featured tender chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, onions, bell peppers, and a savory tomato-herb sauce. She sautéed the vegetables in butter until golden, seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic, and lemon, then baked everything together until the chicken was cooked through and the potatoes were tender.

Aromatic rosemary, thyme, garlic, and lemon filled the kitchen as she worked. After plating, she arranged the vegetables around the chicken and ladled the rich tomato sauce over the top. The golden-orange hue of the carrots, orange peppers, and golden-brown chicken made the dish look warm and inviting.

The occasion was a relaxed Saturday family dinner, though Ethel also had the March 14 party and Wednesday cookout in mind. She served Isabella a cup of tea while carefully plating the finished meal, taking pride in the balanced, comforting dish she had prepared with her own hands.
