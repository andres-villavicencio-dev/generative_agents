Ethel prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Lemon-Basil Herbs and Root Vegetables.

The meal featured a whole chicken, lemon, fresh rosemary, thyme, garlic, salt, black pepper, parsley, and diced carrots, parsnips, and potatoes. Ethel washed the vegetables, sliced the chicken, seasoned it generously, and roasted it in a large pan with a little oil until the skin turned golden and the chicken reached an internal temperature of 165°F. The vegetables were roasted alongside it until crisp and tender.

The aroma of the kitchen filled the room: warm poultry, roasted carrots, garlic, lemon, and rosemary. The dish was beautifully presented on a white platter with the golden chicken in the center, garnished with parsley and a bright lemon wedge.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday family breakfast, followed by Isabella’s tea. It was practical, comforting, and meant for the family, though Ethel prepared it thoughtfully and quietly for Isabella’s own morning routine.
