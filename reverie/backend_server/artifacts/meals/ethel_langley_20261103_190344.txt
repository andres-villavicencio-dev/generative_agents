Ethel prepared Smoked Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Crust, a warm Sunday brunch dish she made for Isabella Rodriguez’s departure. She cut a whole chicken into generous pieces, seasoning it with smoked paprika, garlic, parsley, and a touch of lemon zest. The bird was roasted until golden, then served with crisp carrots, baby potatoes, and green beans, all coated in a simple butter-milk sauce. A lemon-herb crust was brushed over the top before baking.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, rosemary, and lemon. Ethel arranged the vegetables around the chicken in a neat, rustic pattern, with a small bowl of lemon wedges and a sprig of rosemary beside the main dish. The presentation was simple and inviting, suitable for a quiet Sunday breakfast. It was the first meal Ethel had prepared for Isabella before she left—showing her care and practical attention to her neighbor.
