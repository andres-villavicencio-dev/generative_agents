“Smallville’s Best Breakfast”

Ethel prepared a comforting bowl of oatmeal with fresh berries, sliced bananas, and a drizzle of honey. She also made a simple lemon-pepper muffin, which she had baked ahead for the neighborhood gathering.

The oatmeal was made the night before, while the berries and bananas were washed and sliced. Ethel cooked the muffins in a little butter, adding a pinch of cinnamon and a small splash of vanilla so they would stay soft and flavorful. She topped each muffin with a dusting of brown sugar and a few fresh mint leaves before serving.

When she plated it, the bright red and yellow berries contrasted with the pale brown oatmeal and golden muffins, while the lemon-pepper muffins stood proudly in the center. The aroma of warm oats, sweet bananas, cinnamon, and honey filled the kitchen.

She had served the breakfast for Sam Moore and Tom Moreno’s neighborhood gathering on Monday, February 13.
