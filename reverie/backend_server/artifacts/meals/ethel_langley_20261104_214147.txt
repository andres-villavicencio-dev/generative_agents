On Tuesday, February 14, Ethel Langley prepared a simple Savory Egg and Vegetable Scramble for breakfast. The dish featured four large eggs, finely chopped tomatoes, diced onions, sliced mushrooms, and a generous handful of fresh spinach. She cracked the eggs into a small bowl, added the vegetables, and scrambled them over medium heat until the whites set while keeping the yolks soft and golden.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of egg, onion, and toasted mushrooms. Before serving, she added a few fresh leaves and a splash of black pepper, letting the spinach wilt slightly without losing its color. She arranged the scrambled eggs in a shallow dish, garnished the sides with crisp lettuce and a pinch of chives, and topped the dish with a few slices of fresh tomato.

The meal was served on a quiet Tuesday, after Ethel had prepared the ingredients and checked the kitchen. It reflected her practical, homey style: comforting, nourishing, and easy to prepare. By 6:00 PM, she would have time to eat dinner and go to bed around 10:00 PM, while her campaign preparation and Ingrid’s part-time opportunity continued outside the kitchen.
