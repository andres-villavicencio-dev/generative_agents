For a simple yet satisfying Wednesday breakfast, Ethel prepared Egg Scramble with Spinach and Whole Wheat Toast. The dish began with two large eggs whisked with a generous amount of milk, a pinch of salt, and a little black pepper. She seasoned the pan with butter and olive oil, letting the eggs cook until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft, then folded in fresh spinach. A few slices of whole-wheat bread were toasted until golden, and the cooked eggs were folded onto them.

Aromatic, savory, and comforting, the breakfast filled the kitchen with the rich scent of butter and herbs. The eggs had a creamy, slightly chewy texture, while the spinach added a fresh, tender bite. The whole-wheat toast provided a hearty, nutty base, balanced by the bright green of the spinach.

It was a humble, cheerful breakfast for Ethel’s productive opening-day day, reflecting her practical nature and cheerful outlook. She served it with a glass of milk and a fresh apple, enjoying it with a smile before heading out to visit friends and prepare her school’s opening-remarks sketch.
