On Monday, Ethel prepared Chicken and Root Vegetable Skillet for Hobbs Cafe’s meal-prep station. The dish used bone-in chicken thighs, sliced carrots, baby potatoes, onions, garlic, and a few stalks of celery. She browned the chicken until the skin was crisp, then cooked the vegetables until the carrots and potatoes were tender. A little olive oil, salt, and pepper completed the flavor.

The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, caramelized carrots, and warm poultry. When Ethel plated the meal, she arranged the golden chicken in the center, garnished it with fresh parsley, and arranged the vegetables neatly around it. She added a small serving of brown rice and a spoonful of creamy lemon butter sauce.

The occasion was a quiet evening at Hobbs Cafe, where Ethel was helping prepare lunch for a neighborhood gathering and mentoring a group of apprentices. The simple, homey meal felt like a celebration of practical cooking and community care.
