On Monday, February 13, Ethel prepared Lunch with a Heart of Gold for the Hobbs Cafe. She chose a comforting, crowd-pleasing combination: chicken thighs with creamy macaroni and cheese, served with sautéed green beans and a crispy garlic-thyme biscuit.

The meal began with the chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, paprika, garlic, and herbs, then seared to a golden brown before being shredded and simmered with chicken broth. The macaroni was tossed with the shredded chicken and melted cheese until the pasta was tender and the sauce was rich. Green beans were blanched briefly and added for a crisp texture. The garlic-thyme biscuits were baked until golden and crisp around the edges.

As she plated it, Ethel arranged the trays neatly, the golden chicken, bright green beans, and warm biscuits creating an inviting, rustic display. Her warm, savory aroma filled the cafe—a familiar scent of garlic, butter, herbs, and toasted bread.

The occasion was the opening lunch for the new clinic, with Ethel, Sam Moore, and Tom Moreno preparing the trays and arranging the food at the cafe. She had chosen the meal to be both a practical, comforting lunch and a symbolic gesture of care, reflecting her reputation as someone who solved stubborn problems with her own two hands and cared deeply for the people around her.
