Ethel prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Butter Glaze, a simple yet comforting dinner to celebrate Tuesday’s full schedule. She sliced a whole chicken into large pieces, seasoned it with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a touch of lemon zest, then roasted it at 400°F until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. For the glaze, she simmered butter, honey, and vinegar until it thickened.

Alongside the chicken, she arranged colorful vegetables on a large platter: roasted carrots, crisp zucchini, cherry tomatoes, and a small bowl of creamy garlic mashed potatoes. Fresh parsley garnished the dish and a lemon wedge added a bright contrast. The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, rosemary, butter, and lemon, with the warm glow of the oven making the meal feel inviting.

The occasion was the final preparations for Ethel’s home-safety audit campaign. She had already checked her shift board, confirmed Ingrid’s first morning at Hobbs Cafe, helped Ingrid prepare her application, coordinated tutoring plans at Oak Hill College and Johnson Park, and taken a quiet walk around the neighborhood. At 6:00 p.m., she sat down with a plate of food, listened to her campaign notes, and ate in a calm, purposeful mood—turning a regular meal into a quiet moment of preparation before her evening mentoring session.
