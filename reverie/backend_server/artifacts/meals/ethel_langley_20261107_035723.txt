Ethel Langley prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Herb-Crusted Chicken and Potatoes for dinner on Tuesday, February 14.

She arranged a large baking dish with baby potatoes, carrots, parsnips, and onions, then seasoned them with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of salt. She mixed the chicken with the same herb blend, breadcrumbs, and a little parsley before cooking the vegetables until tender and the chicken until fully cooked and golden. After roasting, she returned the potatoes and carrots to the oven, covering the dish to keep everything warm.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, herbs, and savory poultry. She presented the meal in a deep white ceramic bowl, arranging the golden potatoes and carrots around a plump piece of chicken and a bright orange carrot stick on top. A small side of green salad and a glass of water completed the table.

The occasion was a quiet, celebratory evening after a long day of campaign preparations. Ethel wanted the food to feel special enough for her family and neighbors, but practical and comforting enough that she could sit down and relax.
