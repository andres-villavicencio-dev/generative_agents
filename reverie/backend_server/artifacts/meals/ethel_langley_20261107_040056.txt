Dinner: Caramelized Root Vegetable and Roast Chicken

Ethel prepared a hearty, family-style meal for herself and her campaign-support team. The dish included a whole chicken, small carrots, potatoes, green beans, and a splash of lemon juice. She roasted the chicken at 400°F for about 35 minutes until the skin was crisp and the juices ran clear, then finished the vegetables in the same pan until they were tender.

A generous amount of brown sugar, garlic, and paprika gave the sauce a rich golden color. She added a few crushed red pepper flakes for a hint of heat and finished it with a little salt and pepper. She served the chicken in a shallow pan over roasted carrots and potatoes, with green beans on the side, and arranged fresh orange slices and lemon wedges around the edges.

The aroma of roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and warm spices filled the kitchen, while the golden-brown chicken and bright colors made the meal feel inviting and celebratory. It was prepared as a cozy weekday dinner, though Ethel had carefully planned it to balance her campaign duties with her home life.
