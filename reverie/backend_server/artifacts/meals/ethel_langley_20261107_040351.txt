Ethel Langley prepared Stovetop Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Lemon Butter Sauce. She marinated chicken breasts with garlic, ginger, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt for the evening, then sautéed carrots, potatoes, and Brussels sprouts in butter until golden. She cooked the vegetables, seasoned the chicken, and finished the dish with a bright, zesty sauce.

The aroma filled the kitchen with roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and the savory fragrance of lemon and ginger. Ethel plated the chicken on a crisp white plate, arranging the colorful vegetables around it and drizzling the sauce over the top. A simple lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley completed the presentation.

The meal was prepared for dinner on Tuesday, February 14, the day Ethel was completing the final preparations for her campaign and mentoring apprentices at Oak Hill College. It was a simple, practical choice of comfort food, fitting her retired-life routine while providing a warm, nourishing end to a full day.
