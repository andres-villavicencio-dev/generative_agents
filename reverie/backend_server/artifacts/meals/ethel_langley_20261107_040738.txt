For Tuesday, February 14, Ethel prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Herb-Ground Potatoes and Green Beans. The centerpiece was a whole chicken seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic, dried rosemary, thyme, and a splash of lemon juice. She roasted it in a large Dutch oven at 375°F until the skin was golden, crispy, and cooked through.

Afterward, she seasoned the potatoes with the same herbs and a little butter, then sliced them, adding the green beans. A small glass of white wine and a lemon wedge were poured over everything for the final touch. The kitchen smelled of roasted chicken, garlic, lemon, and warm herbs.

The meal was a simple, homey dinner for her campaign family. Ethel served it at the table with bread, butter, and a glass of wine, sharing the food while reviewing her campaign plans. By 8:30, she had finished eating the rest of the meal, and the kitchen remained tidy as she prepared the evening mentoring session at Oak Hill College.
