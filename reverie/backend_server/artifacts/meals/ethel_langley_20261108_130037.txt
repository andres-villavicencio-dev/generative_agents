Dish Name: Smallville Sunday Gravy and Eggs

Ethel prepared a hearty family-style breakfast for Monday morning. She boiled eggs and browned butter into a rich, golden gravy, then topped it with shredded cheddar and sliced red bell peppers. The pan was filled with the savory aroma of sautéed onions, garlic, and toasted bread, while the steaming eggs gave it a comforting, savory warmth.

Presented in a simple white ceramic bowl, the meal featured soft scrambled eggs, golden brown toast, the glossy gravy, colorful peppers, and a generous side of fresh fruit. It was a practical, warm meal suited for a productive Monday—after all, she had to handle safety inspections and apprentice questions before heading to town hall. The breakfast was prepared in her usual practical style: no fancy decorations, just enough flavor to make the house feel like home.
