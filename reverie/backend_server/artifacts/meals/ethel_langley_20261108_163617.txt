Ethel Langley prepared Grandma’s Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Crispy Bread, a comforting dinner she made for a relaxed Monday evening.

She used a whole chicken, carrots, potatoes, parsnips, and onions, seasoned with garlic, herbs, salt, pepper, and a little lemon juice. After browning the chicken until it was golden and the skin crisp, she roasted the vegetables in the same pan until they were tender and caramelized. The bread was sliced thick, pan-fried until crisp on the edges, and arranged on a platter around the chicken.

The aroma was warm and inviting—golden brown chicken, roasted carrots, garlic, and herbs filling the kitchen with the scent of home. She plated it simply, with the bread arranged around the main dish and a small bowl of lemonade beside it. It was a practical, hearty meal prepared for her friends Sam Moore and Tom Moreno at the cafe, where they sat down for dinner and discussion over coffee.
