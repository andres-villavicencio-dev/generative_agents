Ethel prepared a hearty Smoked Chicken Breast with Root Vegetables and Lemon Butter, a simple but comforting dinner for Tuesday evening. The meal centered on boneless chicken breast marinated in soy sauce, minced garlic, and smoked paprika, seasoned with salt and pepper. Root vegetables—including carrots, celery, and baby potatoes—were tossed with olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of cumin before being roasted alongside the chicken. The chicken was cooked until golden and tender, while the vegetables softened into an appealing texture.

The kitchen table became a warm, inviting place. She arranged the roasted vegetables in a shallow white bowl, placed the chicken beside them, and scattered fresh herbs and lemon slices over the top. The aroma of caramelized garlic and smoked paprika filled the small table, while the golden-brown chicken and bright green vegetables added a cheerful, aromatic contrast.

The occasion was simply a quiet evening after a full day of campaign work, safety audits, mentoring, and the clinic appointment. The meal reflected Ethel’s practical, homey style: practical, comforting, and prepared in the comfort of her kitchen table, before she was ready to file her campaign papers and head to bed at 10:00 p.m.
