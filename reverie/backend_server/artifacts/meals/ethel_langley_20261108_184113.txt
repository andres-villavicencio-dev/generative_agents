For Tuesday, February 14, Ethel prepared Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Soup, a comforting weekday dinner that mirrored her practical, approachable cooking style. She used bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, celery, onion, a cup of chicken broth, canned tomatoes, garlic, fresh thyme, salt, and pepper. Diced potatoes were added toward the end to keep the soup rich and filling.

She brought the pot to a simmer over medium heat, stirring occasionally until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were soft. She finished it with a splash of lemon juice and a pinch of cracked pepper, letting the warm broth settle before serving. The kitchen filled with the aroma of roasted chicken, vegetable sweetness, and savory broth, filling her with the satisfaction of a well-made meal.

Served in a simple white bowl with a spoonful of grated cheese and a side of crisp green salad, the soup presented with rustic warmth. It was not an elaborate occasion, but Ethel treated it with care, reflecting her compassionate nature and practical approach to everyday life.
