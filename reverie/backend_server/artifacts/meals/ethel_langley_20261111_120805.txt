The meal was a simple but satisfying Hearty Apple-Orange Chicken Soup with Toasted Whole Wheat Bread.

Ethel began with a pot of clear chicken broth, adding carrots, celery, onion, and a few sprigs of thyme. She added three boneless chicken breasts, covered it, and let it simmer for about thirty minutes until the meat was tender and the broth had thickened. She finished the soup with a splash of vegetable stock and a squeeze of orange juice, letting the warm spices release their scent.

She toasted two slices of whole wheat bread in the oven, then sliced them and arranged them in a small bowl beside the soup. The meal was bright and comforting, with the orange zest adding a cheerful aroma. It was meant to be a quiet, nourishing lunch for Ethel, who wanted something practical and easy to prepare without needing much attention.

The occasion was not a celebration, but a peaceful moment after an evening of mentoring apprentices and preparing for her campaign. Sharing the meal with her old friends, Sam Moore and Tom Moreno, made the ordinary feel familiar and meaningful.
