Ethel prepared a Savory Scrambled Eggs with Sliced Tomatoes and Herbs, served over sourdough toast with a lemon wedge, as a simple Tuesday breakfast. The recipe called for two large eggs, diced tomatoes, chopped chives, salt, pepper, and a little butter. She whisked the eggs with the tomatoes and seasonings, then cooked them in a hot skillet until the whites were set but the yolks remained soft, stirring frequently. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, ripe tomatoes, and savory egg.

She presented the meal on a small white plate, with the toast cut into two pieces and the scrambled eggs and tomatoes arranged neatly beside it. The lemon wedge added a bright contrast and a hint of freshness. This modest breakfast was meant to wake Ethel up after a full day of community work, campaign planning, mentoring, and filing papers, while providing a cozy, nourishing start to her day.
