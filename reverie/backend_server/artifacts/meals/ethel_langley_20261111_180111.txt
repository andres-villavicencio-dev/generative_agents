On Tuesday, February 14, Ethel prepared Mashed Potato Breakfast with Scrambled Eggs and Fresh Herbs, a simple yet filling meal for her morning routine.

She used russet potatoes, milk, salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg, whisking them into a smooth mash before heating butter, oil, and eggs in a skillet. The potatoes were softened in the pan until tender, then folded in with the eggs and fresh chives. She served it in a white ceramic bowl with a small plate of sliced avocado and a glass of warm milk, the chives adding a bright green contrast.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of toasted butter, roasted potatoes, and herbs. The presentation was neat and inviting: the creamy mash arranged gently beside the golden eggs, with the avocado providing a colorful, fresh touch. Ethel made it as a quiet moment of self-care, preparing the meal before beginning her campaign and mentoring sessions. It was a simple breakfast, but one that felt purposeful and energizing.
