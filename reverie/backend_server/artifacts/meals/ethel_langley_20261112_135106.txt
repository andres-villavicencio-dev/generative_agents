Ethel prepared Sunday Pot Roast with Root Vegetables and Gravy, a comforting meal for her Tuesday evening after a demanding campaign, mentoring session, and home safety audit. The roast was a large, seasoned ribeye, slow-cooked until its meat fell apart, while carrots, potatoes, and onions became golden and tender. She added a splash of Worcestershire sauce and a pinch of fresh thyme, letting the rich gravy simmer around the vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted meat, caramelized onions, and thyme. Ethel plated the meal on a deep ceramic serving platter, arranging the roast in the center and the root vegetables around it. She topped the dish with a generous portion of gravy, garnished it with fresh parsley, and added a small sprig of rosemary.

The occasion was a peaceful Tuesday evening, quiet after a busy day of paperwork, campaign messages, and mentoring. The meal felt both grounding and celebratory, a simple dinner that reflected Ethel’s practical, compassionate nature as she prepared for another long day.
