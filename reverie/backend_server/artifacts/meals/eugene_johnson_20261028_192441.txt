On a snowy Tuesday, Eugene prepared Spicy Lemon-Herb Chicken for Isabella Rodriguez’s shift at Hobbs Cafe. He sliced chicken breasts, marinated them with lemon zest, garlic, chili flakes, cumin, oregano, and a splash of soy sauce, then pan-seared them over medium heat until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. He finished the dish with roasted asparagus, a creamy lemon-herb sauce, and a crisp lemon wedge.

Aromatic, the kitchen filled with the scent of garlic, herbs, toasted garlic, and savory chicken. He plated the chicken alongside the vegetables, arranging the asparagus in a neat arc and garnishing it with a bright green herb sprig. The meal was ready at 6:00 p.m. for the shift, with room service still being organized and the meal service areas preparing for the next customers.
