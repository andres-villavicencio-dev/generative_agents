Eugene prepared a Herb-Roasted Chicken with Garlic and Lemon-Serve, using chicken thighs, lemon, garlic, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of soy sauce. The chicken was marinated in the refrigerator overnight, then roasted at 400°F for about 25 minutes until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. He added the chicken to a steamer basket, covering it with a little broth to steam the thighs until tender. The side was a crisp garlic-roasted vegetable medley, including carrots, onions, potatoes, and green beans, tossed with olive oil, honey, and fresh herbs. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm garlic, citrus, rosemary, and savory herbs. He plated the chicken on a white platter, arranging the vegetables in a colorful spiral and garnishing it with lemon wedges and a drizzle of soy-sauce glaze. The meal was prepared for a casual weekend dinner with his roommate Sharon Antle and friend Maria Lopez, who had joined the cookout.
