Eugene prepared a Garlic Herb Roast Chicken with Creamy Garlic Butter and Roasted Vegetables for his roommate’s and friend’s weekend cookout.

Key ingredients: chicken, garlic, rosemary, thyme, olive oil, butter, lemon, and carrots, onions, and potatoes.

Preparation method: Eugene roasted the chicken and vegetables in the oven until the skin was crisp and the meat was cooked through, then basted with the pan juices. He prepared the creamy garlic butter separately, tossing the vegetables in olive oil, lemon zest, and a little sugar before roasting alongside the chicken.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the savory scent of roasted garlic, rosemary, thyme, and crispy chicken skin.

Presentation: The chicken was plated with a golden-brown, caramelized crust, while the vegetables were arranged around it. The creamy garlic butter was spooned over the chicken, and the remaining vegetables were tucked beneath the plating.

Occasion: A relaxed, family-style cookout for Eugene, Sharon, and Maria.
