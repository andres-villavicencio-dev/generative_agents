Eugene prepared the Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl, a hearty, colorful vegetarian main designed to share the second portion of the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner. The bowl featured whole-grain quinoa, roasted sweet potatoes, kale, cucumber, chickpeas, and a lemon-tahini dressing. A small side of whole-wheat toast, butter, and mild clover honey served as his anchor.

Eugene toasted the grain with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then sautéed the vegetables in a skillet until golden. He added the chickpeas and topped everything with lemon-tahini dressing, finishing it with a drizzle of honey. The aroma was earthy, citrusy, and warm, with hints of roasted root vegetables and toasted grain.

He arranged the bowl on a large platter, arranging the grains in the center with vegetables and chickpeas around it. A small side of toast and honey was placed nearby for the second portion. This meal was prepared for a lively Tuesday cookout, reflecting Eugene’s competitive, ambitious side as he balanced the cookout with his evening IT certification studies and extra shifts for Isabella Rodriguez.
