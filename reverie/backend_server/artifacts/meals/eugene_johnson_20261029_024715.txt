At 7:00 AM, Eugene prepared the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty whole-wheat toast-and-breakfast-style spread. His anchor was a crusty whole-wheat toast sliced into thick rounds, generously brushed with softened butter and drizzled with mild clover honey. The honey’s subtle earthy sweetness balanced the buttery richness, while the toasted grain added a warm, nutty texture.

Eugene arranged the toast on a rustic wooden serving platter, surrounded by crisp apple slices, a small bowl of roasted chickpeas, and a glass of warm milk. The aroma of toasted wheat, honey, and roasted nuts filled the kitchen as he finished spreading the butter over the toast.

The meal was a relaxed, social morning brunch intended for his colleagues at Hobbs Café. Its simple, comforting presentation made it perfect for sharing, while Eugene’s focus on the cookout reflected his competitive, ambitious nature—he wanted the food to feel both nourishing and celebratory.
