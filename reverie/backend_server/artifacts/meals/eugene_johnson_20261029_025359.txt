Eugene Johnson prepared The Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty, rustic meal centered around whole-wheat toast, butter, and mild clover honey. For the main dish, he layered golden, slightly toasted whole-wheat bread with a generous layer of melted butter and a drizzle of honey, creating a savory, caramelized base. The aroma filled the kitchen with toasted grains, nutty bread, and the sweet, earthy scent of clover.

He finished the meal by topping it with warm, golden-granola-style sides and a crisp salad of mixed greens, apple slices, and a light lemon-herb vinaigrette. The presentation was warm and inviting, with the rich bread, honey-gold glaze, and contrasting crisp salad creating a visually balanced feast.

The occasion was a large cookout with Carmen, taking place around his home during the Tuesday February 14 cookout.
