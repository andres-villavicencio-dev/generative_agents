Eugene Johnson prepared a Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl for the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner cookout. The dish is a colorful, hearty bowl of steel-cut oats, quinoa, corn, roasted chickpeas, and kale, topped with shredded carrots, celery, and fresh herbs. He added a small portion of whole-wheat toast, a generous drizzle of mild clover honey, and brushed the bowl with olive oil and a sprinkle of sea salt before serving the whole-wheat toast, butter, and honey as his centerpiece.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of toasted oats, toasted nuts, caramelized vegetables, and fresh herbs. The presentation was warm and rustic: the golden grains, bright greens, and creamy honey glaze stood out against the dark counter, while the whole-wheat toast provided a soft, contrasting element.

The meal was prepared for the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner cookout on Tuesday, February 14. It was a hearty, comforting family-style spread designed to energize guests after their day at the café, with the dinner serving as its anchor.
