Eugene prepared the Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl, the large cookout meal that became the anchor of his Tuesday. The dish combines whole-wheat toast, mild clover honey, butter, and a colorful mix of roasted root vegetables, quinoa, chickpeas, and crisp greens. The grains are seasoned with smoked paprika, garlic, cumin, and lemon zest, while the vegetables are caramelized until tender and slightly browned.

Eugene washed and cleaned the kitchen counters, stovetop, and oven before cooking. He seared the root vegetables in a hot skillet, added the grains to a pan with the honey, and finished everything with a little butter and warm toast. The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of roasted vegetables, toasted grain, clover honey, and caramelized onions, creating a warm, comforting scent that reminded him of home.

The bowl was arranged on a large platter, with the golden toast beside it and garnishes of parsley and lemon zest. It was prepared for an impromptu family cookout, a casual gathering of Carmen and their friends for a relaxed Tuesday morning meal, though Eugene had not yet confirmed the exact time or location.
