Eugene Johnson prepared the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty whole-wheat toast dinner with a savory, herb-infused broth and a crisp, golden crust. He toasted the bread with butter and served it alongside tender, roasted chicken, carrots, onions, and a mild clover-honey glaze. The dish was finished with a creamy, herb-rich gravy and garnished with fresh parsley, chives, and a sprinkle of dried thyme.

He prepared the meal in a large cast-iron skillet on the stove, browning the chicken until the skin was crisp, then adding the vegetables and cooking them until they were tender. The clover honey was simmered into the broth, and the bread was toasted until the butter melted and the crust turned golden. The aroma of roasted poultry, caramelized carrots, toasted whole-grain bread, and the subtle sweetness of clover honey filled the kitchen.

The meal was presented on a rustic wooden table for the Tuesday February 14 cookout, with the bread arranged in a warm, golden tray, vegetables neatly arranged beside it, and a small glass of water. It was a comforting, family-style dinner prepared for the group’s gathering, with the whole-wheat toast serving as the anchor.
