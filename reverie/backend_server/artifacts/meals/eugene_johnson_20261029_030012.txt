Eugene prepared the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty, rustic dinner for the large cookout with Carmen. The meal centered around whole-wheat toast, mild clover honey, and butter, with the rest of the spread consisting of grilled vegetables, roasted potatoes, and a simple protein.

Eugene toasted the bread until it was golden and crisp, then layered it with melted butter and the honey. He roasted the vegetables until tender and slightly charred, seasoning them with herbs, garlic, and a pinch of salt, while the potatoes were baked until crisp. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm toast, clover honey, roasted potatoes, and a gentle smokiness from the grilled protein.

The presentation was simple and inviting: golden toast arranged on a white plate, a bowl of warm vegetables and potatoes, and the protein served alongside the honey. Garnishes of herbs and edible flowers added a delicate touch.

The cookout was an informal gathering to celebrate the holiday season, with everyone gathering around the table for a shared meal. Eugene had not yet confirmed the specific time or location, but he was focused on making the food feel warm, welcoming, and distinctly homemade.
