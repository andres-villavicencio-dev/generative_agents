Eugene Johnson prepared Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty family-style meal prepared for a casual cookout with Carmen. The centerpiece is a slow-cooked beef stew with root vegetables, served alongside whole-wheat toast, butter, and mild clover honey.

The stew was made by searing a beef chuck roast in a large skillet, then browning potatoes, carrots, onions, and celery. A small amount of beef broth was added, followed by garlic, thyme, and bay leaf. It was simmered until the meat fell off the bone and the vegetables were tender. The stew was finished by ladling it over the warm toast, with extra butter and honey drizzled over the top.

The aroma was rich and earthy—noticeable in the browned meat, roasted carrots, savory broth, and warm spices. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the stew was served in a deep oven-safe skillet with a visible layer of golden potatoes and crisp vegetables, while the toast was arranged beside it with honey and butter.

The occasion was an informal cookout celebrating the upcoming Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl, but the meal itself was a simple, hearty dinner meant to be shared and enjoyed.
