On Wednesday, February 15, Eugene Johnson prepared the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty family-style meal designed to serve as the centerpiece of a cookout. The dish combines tender strips of beef with crisp potatoes, caramelized onions, carrots, and a rich, savory herb glaze. It was finished with the Velvet Harvest Feast, a rustic vegetable and grain mix including roasted squash, corn, asparagus, and whole-grain noodles. A side of Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl complemented the spread with quinoa, roasted root vegetables, and fresh herbs.

The meals were cooked over an open-fire stove, allowing the beef to brown and the vegetables to soften with warm, crackling flames. The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of caramelized onions, toasted bread, herbs, and a subtle sweetness from the honey. For presentation, Eugene arranged the hearty plates in a rustic farmhouse-style display, with golden-brown potatoes and colorful vegetables garnished with fresh thyme and parsley. The dishes were served at a relaxed Hobbs Café cookout, with guests gathering around the stove to share the meal. Eugene’s goal was to organize the cookout, prepare the menu, and create a memorable gathering centered around his friends, Carmen and others.
