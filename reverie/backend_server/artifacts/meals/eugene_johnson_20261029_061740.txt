On Wednesday, February 15, Eugene Johnson prepared the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty three-course meal designed for a warm family cookout. The dinner included a whole-wheat toast with butter and mild clover honey, roasted vegetables with herbs, and a creamy vegetable soup served with crusty bread.

The Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner featured golden-brown whole-wheat toast, a savory potato hash with onions and carrots, and roasted root vegetables with lemon juice and thyme. It was completed with a rich tomato basil soup and a side of crisp greens, served with crusty bread and a small salad.

The kitchen was filled with the aroma of toasted wheat, caramelized onions, roasted potatoes, and the subtle sweetness of clover honey. The meal was presented in an open-walled serving platter and a large bowl, allowing guests to clearly see the warm, colorful food. It was prepared as part of a special cookout for his friends and family, blending casual comfort with the organized presentation he is working toward for the café.
