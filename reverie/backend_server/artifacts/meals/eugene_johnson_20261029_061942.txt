Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner – Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy

Eugene prepared a hearty, family-style roast chicken dinner for a Tuesday cookout with Carmen. The centerpiece was a whole chicken roasted with garlic, rosemary, lemon, thyme, and a splash of orange juice, surrounded by root vegetables such as carrots, parsnips, baby potatoes, and green beans. The chicken was basted with savory gravy made from the roasted vegetables, chicken, and a touch of sour cream for richness.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of caramelized onions, rosemary, garlic, and roasted herbs. The meal was plated and served in individual dishes with golden crusty bread, a side salad, and crisp potatoes. It was a relaxed, celebratory dinner for Carmen and her friends, designed to feel both comforting and festive.
