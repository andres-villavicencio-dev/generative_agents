On Wednesday, February 15, Eugene prepared the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner. The meal was designed as a hearty family-style gathering, combining savory comfort with seasonal warmth. It featured whole-wheat toast, butter, and mild clover honey as the main accompaniment. The centerpiece was a slow-roasted chicken seasoned with garlic, rosemary, and lemon, accompanied by roasted root vegetables, carrots, and onions. The vegetables included carrots, parsnips, sweet potatoes, and potatoes, all simmered in a savory broth.

The aroma emerged from the oven as golden-brown crusts formed around the chicken and savory roots developed a fragrant, earthy scent. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the chicken rested on a serving platter with herbs and lemon wedges, surrounded by neatly arranged vegetables and the warm toast.

The occasion was a relaxed cookout and dinner for Eugene and Carmen. The meal reflected his goal of bringing together familiar comfort and fresh seasonal ingredients, allowing everyone to gather, share food, and enjoy a convivial evening.
