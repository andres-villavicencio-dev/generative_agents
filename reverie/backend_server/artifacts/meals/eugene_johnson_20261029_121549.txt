On Thursday, February 16, Eugene Johnson prepared the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty family-style meal featuring a slow-roasted vegetable medley, whole-wheat toast, organic eggs, fresh fruit, milk, and Fig Croissant.

The key ingredients included carrots, potatoes, bell peppers, onions, garlic, thyme, olive oil, and a savory vegetable broth. For the Hearth Dinner, Eugene sliced the vegetables and potatoes, sautéed them briefly with garlic and herbs, then transferred them to a skillet to simmer in the warm broth. The whole-wheat toast was brushed with butter and clover honey, while the eggs were cooked gently to perfection. The fresh fruit, milk, and Fig Croissant were served alongside the warm grains.

Aromatic rosemary, thyme, garlic, and caramelized onion flavors filled the kitchen, creating an inviting aroma. The meal was presented on a rustic ceramic platter, with colorful vegetables, golden toast, fluffy eggs, and creamy fruit arranged alongside a small Fig Croissant for easy sharing.

The occasion was a cozy family cookout with Carmen and friends, with Eugene’s goal being to organize the meal and ensure everyone was ready to enjoy the Hobbs Café offerings.
