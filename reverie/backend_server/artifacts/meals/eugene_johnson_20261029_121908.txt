Eugene Johnson prepared Hearth Dinner, a hearty family-style meal designed for a cozy Thursday cookout. It features a rich beef stew with root vegetables, carrots, onions, and potatoes simmered in a savory broth. The dish is finished with golden breadcrumbs and fresh herbs for a crunchy finish.

Key ingredients include ground beef, carrots, onions, potatoes, rosemary, thyme, garlic, tomato sauce, and a splash of Worcestershire sauce. The aroma is warm and deeply savory, with hints of caramelized root vegetables and rosemary. It was served in a large communal bowl, arranged with the stew in the center and colorful vegetables, crusty bread, and fresh herbs around the edges. A side of fresh fruit, milk, and whole-wheat toast with butter and clover honey added a contrasting sweetness.

The occasion was a relaxed Thursday family cookout, and Eugene wanted everyone to be ready. Though he had not yet plated the entire meal, the main ingredients were prepared and organized for Carmen’s Velvet Harvest Feast and the Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl, with the Hobbs Café offerings still awaiting preparation.
