Eugene Johnson prepared Hearth Dinner, a hearty family-style dinner featuring roasted chicken, glazed potatoes, carrots, and a creamy gravy, served alongside crisp green beans. The meal was fully cooked, with the oven checked and the food plated just before Carmen arrived.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory roast chicken, caramelized onions, and warm herbs. It was served in a large communal platter: the chicken and potatoes arranged on the left, carrots and green beans on the right, with a serving of gravy and bread at the center.
