Eugene Johnson prepared The Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty family-style breakfast served for a Thursday cookout with Carmen. The centerpiece was Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl, featuring whole-grain quinoa, roasted sweet potato, kale, chickpeas, avocado, and a lemon-tahini dressing. Alongside it was Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, with pan-fried whole-wheat tortillas, crispy bacon, and golden pancakes.

The preparation began with warming the grains and vegetables, then tossing them with a vibrant, citrusy dressing. The pancakes were brushed with melted butter, while Carmen’s whole-wheat toast received a generous layer of butter and clover honey. The aroma was warm, earthy, and inviting—rich with toasted grains, sweet roasted vegetables, and the scent of butter and honey.

Plated in a rustic ceramic bowl, the grain bowl was arranged with colorful vegetables, golden toast, and a bright garnish, while the Hearth Dinner was set beside it with a small plate of fresh fruit, milk, and eggs nearby. All dishes were checked and ready, creating a cheerful, communal breakfast perfect for the family gathering.
