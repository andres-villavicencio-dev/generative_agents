On Thursday, February 16, Eugene Johnson prepared the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty family-style meal designed for the Hobbs Café cookout. The dish featured whole-wheat toast with butter and clover honey, organic eggs, fresh fruit, milk, and Fig Croissant slices. Additional toppings included sautéed spinach, roasted potatoes, smoked salmon, caramelized onions, and herbs, alongside a side of grain salad and fresh fruit.

The meal was prepared in a large communal skillet. The toast was toasted until golden, then folded with warm butter, clover honey, and a drizzle of milk. The eggs were fried to perfection, while the Fig Croissant was baked until crisp. Spinach was sautéed until tender, potatoes were roasted with salt and pepper, and the salmon was lightly seared. The ingredients were arranged in an oval platter, with the warm toast and fruit arranged around the center, creating an inviting centerpiece.

The aroma rose from the skillet: savory toast, smoky salmon, caramelized onions, and fresh herbs mingled with the warm scent of butter and honey. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the golden toast, glossy eggs, colorful fruit, and soft Fig Croissant slices completing the table’s visual appeal. The cookout offered a casual, communal occasion for friends and family to gather, share food, and enjoy the comfort of a home-style meal.
