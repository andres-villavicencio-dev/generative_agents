Eugene Johnson prepared The Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty family-style spread designed to energize the kitchen before preparing dinner. The centerpiece was a Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl, filled with fluffy whole-grain rice, sautéed mushrooms, crisp kale, roasted sweet potatoes, and a tangy lemon-herb vinaigrette. It was served alongside Velvet Harvest Feast, which featured organic eggs poached in buttermilk, fresh fruit, a Fig Croissant, and a savory pancake-style hash.

The meal was prepared in one large skillet: the grain and vegetables were sautéed until golden and tender, then tossed with the pan juices. Eggs were poached to perfect soft whites, while the Fig Croissant was sliced and sprinkled with a little honey. A drizzle of melted butter added rich aroma, and the whole-wheat toast was brushed with butter and clover honey before being cut into wedges.

The kitchen had the time to recover, so the meal was warm, colorful, and presented in communal plates with a simple red-rimmed tablecloth. It was prepared for an enthusiastic cookout on Friday, February 17, where everyone gathered to enjoy the shared meal and celebrate the week.
