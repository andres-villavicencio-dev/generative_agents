On Friday, February 17, Eugene Johnson prepared a hearty Hearth Dinner for his Friday cookout at The Hobbs Café. The meal includes the Hearth Dinner as the main course, Velvet Harvest Feast as a colorful side, and a Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl for a lighter, refreshing finish.

The Hearth Dinner is a rich, herb-infused beef stew with root vegetables such as carrots, potatoes, and red potatoes. It is simmered in a savory broth with onions, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a touch of cream. The Velvet Harvest Feast offers crisp roasted carrots, golden beets, and creamy root vegetables, while the Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl features quinoa, corn, roasted root vegetables, lemon dressing, and fresh herbs.

The kitchen was filled with the warm aroma of roasting vegetables, roasted garlic, and the savory depth of the beef broth. The Hearth Dinner was plated in a rustic ceramic bowl, garnished with fresh herbs, and served with the remaining sides.

The occasion was a relaxed Friday cookout at The Hobbs Café, with guests looking forward to Carmen’s whole-wheat toast with butter and clover honey. Eugene hoped the meal would be a cozy, shareable celebration for everyone.
