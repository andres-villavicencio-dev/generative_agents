Eugene Johnson prepared The Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, Velvet Harvest Feast, and Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl. The centerpiece was a hearty grain and vegetable side featuring whole-grain quinoa, roasted carrots, kale, onions, garlic, and a creamy garlic herb sauce. A small portion of leftover grains and vegetables was also prepared for later.

The meal’s aroma was earthy, warm, and fragrant: roasted vegetables, garlic, toasted grains, and a hint of honey and cinnamon. The Hearth Dinner was served on a rustic platter with crisp greens, golden roasted carrots, and a rich savory sauce, while the Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl was arranged in a shallow bowl with colorful vegetables, grains, and a drizzle of honey.

The occasion was a Friday cookout centered on Carmen’s whole-wheat toast with butter and clover honey, organic eggs, fresh fruit, milk, and a Fig Croissant. Eugene’s goal was to ensure everyone had a substantial, satisfying meal and to encourage everyone to prepare leftovers for future gatherings.
