Eugene prepared The Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, Velvet Harvest Feast, and the Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl for an outdoor cookout on Friday, February 17. The Hearth Dinner features a hearty, herb-roasted chicken served over a rustic root vegetable mash, with golden-brown potatoes and caramelized onions. Beneath it, a Velvet Harvest Feast offers a rich, creamy mashed potato topped with roasted root vegetables, fresh herbs, and a savory crumble of browned meat. The Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl combines fluffy quinoa, baby spinach, roasted tomatoes, cucumber, feta, and a lemon-herb dressing.

The key ingredients include chicken, potatoes, onions, carrots, root vegetables, fresh herbs, feta, goat cheese, figs, whole-wheat bread, butter, clover honey, organic eggs, fresh fruit, and milk. Eugene cooked the chicken until perfectly browned, roasted the vegetables until tender, and warmed the grains with a bright, fragrant dressing. The meal was layered in warm bowls, arranged on a long serving platter, and garnished with herbs, citrus, and colorful produce.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted chicken, warm potatoes, caramelized onions, and fresh herbs. It was ready to be shared at an inviting family cookout, with Carmen’s whole-wheat toast, butter, and clover honey adding a sweet finish to the hearty meal.
