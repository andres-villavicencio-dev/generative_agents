Meal: Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl  
Occasion: The Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner  
Key Ingredients: Quinoa, roasted sweet potato, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, avocado, kale, lemon-tahini dressing, and a sprinkle of feta.

Eugene prepared a colorful, nutrient-dense grain bowl as a simple lunch substitute for the unavailable bread and eggs. He cooked the quinoa in olive oil, added the vegetables, and dressed the warm grains with a bright lemon-tahini dressing. The dish was then arranged in a serving bowl with colorful produce and a small scoop of feta for a satisfying, complete meal.

Aromatic, earthy notes of roasted squash, citrus, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen, while the warm grains, crisp vegetables, and creamy dressing made the bowl inviting. It was presented as a practical, healthy alternative to toast and eggs, perfect for a busy Saturday. The meal was prepared during the café’s cookout, helping prepare and serve the whole-wheat toast with butter and clover honey as a special treat for everyone.
