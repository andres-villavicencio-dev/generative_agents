Dish: Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl

Key Ingredients: organic quinoa, kale, roasted carrots, red onion, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, avocado, roasted sweet potato, lemon, olive oil, and a drizzle of tahini.

Preparation Method: The vegetables were first fried until crisp, then stirred into a warm grain bowl with the remaining ingredients. The bowl was assembled on a bed of rice, garnished with fresh parsley, and served in a wide ceramic dish.

Aroma: Warm, savory, and earthy, with the sharp contrast of fried vegetables, citrus, and the nutty aroma of toasted quinoa.

Presentation: A colorful, asymmetrical bowl featuring alternating layers of grain, roasted vegetables, avocado, and creamy tahini sauce, finished with bright greens and edible flowers.

Occasion: A relaxed Saturday cookout for friends and family, with the meal complementing Carmen’s whole-wheat toast with butter and clover honey, organic eggs, fresh fruit, milk, and Fig Croissant.
