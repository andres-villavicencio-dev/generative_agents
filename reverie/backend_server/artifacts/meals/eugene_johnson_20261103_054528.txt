The meal was a Hearth Dinner with Velvet Harvest Feast, a hearty, rustic dinner prepared as part of the weekend cookout. It featured whole-grain bread, whole-wheat toast with butter and clover honey, roasted vegetables including carrots and kale, a creamy potato gratin, and fresh seasonal fruit.

Eugene layered the bread with butter, sliced clover honey, and a generous amount of fruit, then arranged the dish on a large serving platter alongside the vegetables and potatoes. He drizzled a little honey over the gratin and garnished the sides with fresh herbs and edible flowers. The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant with caramelized onions, roasted root vegetables, and clove honey.

The presentation was colorful and inviting, with golden toast and rich vegetables arranged around a platter of creamy potatoes and vibrant fruit. It was meant to be a communal, festive dinner for the cookout, with Eddy Lin helping to serve the Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl alongside the Hearth Dinner.
