The Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl is Eugene Johnson’s latest dish for The Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner. It features a hearty base of quinoa or brown rice, roasted seasonal vegetables—including carrots, zucchini, bell peppers, onions, and tomatoes—tossed in a savory herb glaze made with olive oil, garlic, lemon, and smoked paprika. The sauce is finished with fresh herbs and a drizzle of yogurt or lemon-lime dressing, while the bowl is topped with sliced avocado, whole-grain crackers, and a generous portion of fresh fruit.

Eugene prepares the vegetables by washing and lightly blanching them until crisp-tender, then sautéing them with garlic until the pan aromas are fragrant and the vegetables turn bright orange or green. He combines the grains, vegetables, and sauce in a large bowl, adding the fruit for a colorful, nutrient-rich finish. The dish smells of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and warm grains.

It is presented in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranged so the colorful vegetables stand out against the golden grains, with the whole-wheat toast, butter, and clover honey placed alongside it for the cookout. The meal serves as a hearty, comforting dish for the Saturday family cookout, combining the cafe’s familiar flavors with an inviting, rustic presentation.
