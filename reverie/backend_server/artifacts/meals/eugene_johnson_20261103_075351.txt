Eugene Johnson prepared a Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl for the cookout, serving it alongside the Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner and the Velvet Harvest Feast.

The bowl was layered with warm, whole-grain rice or quinoa, roasted sweet potatoes, crisp kale, and crunchy chickpeas, all dressed with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a hint of cumin. He added a generous portion of organic eggs, fresh fruit, milk, and a Fig Croissant for a balanced, filling meal.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized onions and toasted nuts, alongside the warm, earthy scent of roasted vegetables and the subtle sweetness of the figs. The presentation was colorful and inviting: the golden grains, bright orange potatoes, and vibrant greens arranged on a white plate, with a golden Fig Croissant placed beside them.

The meal was designed for the Saturday cookout, where the whole-wheat toast with butter and clover honey would be served. It was hearty, affordable, and perfectly suited to the family gathering, giving Eugene a relaxed yet accomplished day.
