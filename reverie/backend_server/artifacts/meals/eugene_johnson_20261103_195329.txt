Eugene prepared Hearth Dinner, a hearty Sunday brunch-style skillet dinner featuring smoked chicken, caramelized onions, carrots, and potatoes, served with a savory root vegetable and grain salad. The dish was seasoned with smoked paprika, garlic powder, and fresh thyme, then simmered until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were tender-crisp.

Eugene checked the chicken’s internal temperature with a meat thermometer and adjusted the pan’s heat and cooking time so the chicken remained juicy while the vegetables softened. After removing it from the pan, he finished the skillet by adding a splash of broth, fresh herbs, and a final pinch of salt before serving.

The meal was presented on a rustic wooden platter, with golden-brown chicken garnished with thyme and parsley, bright orange carrots, crisp potatoes, and a colorful salad of greens and grains. The aroma of warm chicken, caramelized onions, and roasted vegetables filled the kitchen, evoking a cozy Sunday dinner. It was prepared to celebrate Carmen’s whole-wheat toast with butter, clover honey, organic eggs, fresh fruit, and Fig Croissant, making the gathering feel welcoming and celebratory.
