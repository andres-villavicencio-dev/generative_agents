On Sunday, February 19, Eugene prepared The Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a hearty Sunday gathering of hearty comfort food and a fresh fruit breakfast. His signature Hearth Dinner was a slow-cooked pot roast with carrots, potatoes, onions, and a rich gravy made with beef, beef broth, butter, garlic, and herbs, served over rustic bread. He paired it with a Garden-Rooted Grain Bowl, featuring quinoa, roasted root vegetables, kale, and a creamy lemon-herb dressing.

The meal began with a Velvet Harvest Feast, including whole-wheat toast topped with butter, clover honey, organic eggs, fresh fruit, and a Fig Croissant. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasting meat and the sweet, floral scent of clover honey.

Eugene served the first guests and helped them eat, creating an energetic and communal atmosphere. The meal felt purposeful and celebratory—perfect for a Sunday cookout, with guests gathering around the warm, inviting dishes. He left the table full and grateful, proud that he could combine his STEM background and retail experience into cooking and hospitality that celebrated both food and connection.
