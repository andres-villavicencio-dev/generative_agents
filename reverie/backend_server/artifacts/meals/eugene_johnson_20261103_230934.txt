Eugene Johnson prepared a Grand Cafe Wrap for the cafe’s lunch batch. He combined a toasted ciabatta roll with a creamy lemon-tahini spinach salad, crisp chickpea crumbles, roasted red peppers, and a tangy lemon dressing. A warm whole-grain wrap served alongside a small portion of feta and olive oil completed the plate.

The wraps were rolled in the wrap station, then stacked on the serving tray with a lemon wedge, a sprig of parsley, and a piece of toasted bread. The aroma was earthy, citrusy, and slightly nutty—fresh herbs, savory greens, and warm toasted bread filling the kitchen.

This was part of Eugene’s weekday routine as a cafe barista, balancing extra shifts with his study of an IT certification. The meal was practical and comforting, fitting the cafe’s casual lunch service. Later, he and his friends planned a weekend cookout, so the food felt both useful and exciting.
