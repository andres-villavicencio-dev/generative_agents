The meal was a simple, comforting breakfast of golden toast with lemon zest and toasted almonds.

Eugene toasted slices of bread in a pan, adding a pinch of salt and a little olive oil until they were golden and crisp. He topped the bread with freshly grated lemon zest, a generous drizzle of lemon juice, and toasted almonds. The kitchen filled with the warm, nutty aroma of toasted nuts and citrus.

Presentation was informal and bright: the toast rested on a wooden board, with the lemon zest scattered over the top and the toasted almonds arranged in a small cluster beside it. The warm golden light from the kitchen window made the dish look inviting, but it was not prepared as a special occasion meal. Instead, it was a quiet, everyday breakfast prepared after work—intended to energize Eugene before his evening plans.
