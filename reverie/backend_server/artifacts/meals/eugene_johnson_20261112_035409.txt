Eugene prepared a simple Oatmeal Power Bowl for his morning routine. The dish featured rolled oats cooked with water or almond milk until the mixture was creamy and slightly chewy, topped with sliced banana, a handful of blueberries, and a drizzle of honey. A sprinkle of chia seeds and a small handful of walnuts added a crunchy texture, while a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of cinnamon provided a bright citrus finish.

Eugene cooked the oats in a small pan over medium heat, then transferred them to a bowl and topped them with the fruit and nuts. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of oats, honey, and citrus, creating a cozy, inviting atmosphere. The meal was presented on a white plate, arranged neatly so the colorful fruit and golden oats contrasted beautifully.

This light, easy-to-comprehend breakfast was intended as an energizing start to his Monday. It balanced his busy schedule while encouraging him to complete the morning routine before beginning his evening IT certification studies.
