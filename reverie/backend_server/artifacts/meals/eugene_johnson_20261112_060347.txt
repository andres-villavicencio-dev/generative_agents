Eugene prepared Chicken and Veggies with Rice for his second lunch.

The dish featured chicken thighs, bell peppers, onions, garlic, cherry tomatoes, and corn, all coated in a savory lemon-herb pan sauce. He sautéed the chicken until golden, then stirred in the vegetables until they softened, adding a splash of soy sauce, sesame oil, and a pinch of smoked paprika for depth. The rice was seasoned with garlic, chili flakes, and soy before being finished with the sauce.

The kitchen filled with a warm, aromatic scent of roasted chicken, garlic, herbs, and toasted corn. He plated the chicken on a bed of fluffy white rice, garnished with fresh parsley, and served the vegetables on the side. The presentation was simple and inviting, designed to make the meal feel special.

The occasion was a relaxed weekday lunch at Hobbs Cafe, where Eugene was preparing for work and meeting Isabella Rodriguez. The lunch reflected his dual life: balancing professional responsibilities with his academic studies and upcoming weekend cookout with his roommate Sharon Antle and friend Maria Lopez.
