For his Monday lunch, Eugene prepared Braised Chicken with Herb-Roasted Root Vegetables and Grilled Corn. The dish featured tender chicken thighs in a savory brown sauce, caramelized onions, carrots, potatoes, and ears of corn, all seasoned with garlic, thyme, rosemary, black pepper, and a splash of lemon juice.

Eugene sautéed the vegetables, browned the chicken, and simmered everything together until the sauce thickened and the chicken was cooked through. He topped the bowl with a drizzle of olive oil and fresh parsley before serving. The meal had a warm, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, and caramelized onions, accompanied by a hint of citrus and the earthy aroma of roasted root vegetables.

Eugene plated the dish on a wide white platter, arranging the golden chicken and colorful vegetables in an inviting, casual manner with a small basket of grilled corn beside it. It was a comforting, practical lunch for a busy Monday before his evening IT certification and extra shift.
