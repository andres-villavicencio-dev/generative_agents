Eugene prepared a Garlic-Butter Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice, a comforting family-style meal he made to share with his roommate Sharon Antle and friend Maria Lopez during the weekend cookout.

The dish features tender chicken thighs marinated in garlic, lemon, herbs, and a little oil, slow-cooked until the meat is juicy and the sauce has thickened. The rice was prepared with chicken broth, butter, lemon zest, and parsley, seasoned to keep it fragrant and flavorful.

Aromatic, golden chicken and rice filled a large serving platter, with a small bowl of lemon-turmeric yogurt offering a refreshing side. The warm, savory aroma of roasted chicken mingled with the bright scent of lemon and herbs, while the glossy, golden glaze added a rich, inviting finish. It was presented as an inviting family dinner, ready to be shared with friends during the weekend gathering.
