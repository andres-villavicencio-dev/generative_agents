Eugene prepared Garlic Herb Chicken with Lemon-Basil Rice for a quick weeknight dinner. He began by sautéing chicken breasts until the skin was crisp, then added chopped garlic, fresh parsley, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt and pepper. While the chicken cooked, he simmered rice with olive oil, lemon, basil, and vegetable broth until the grains were tender.

The aroma rose with warm garlic, citrus, herbs, and the savory scent of roasted poultry. He served the rice in a small white bowl and arranged the chicken in a neat platter beside it, garnishing it with a sprig of parsley. The meal was simple and satisfying, perfect for his busy Monday after arriving at work and taking a quick lunch break.
