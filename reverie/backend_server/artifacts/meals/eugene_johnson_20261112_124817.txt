On Tuesday, February 14, Eugene prepared a Hearty Breakfast Burrito Bowl for his weekday shift. He combined fluffy eggs, crispy scrambled tofu, roasted tomatoes, red onion, spinach, black beans, avocado, and feta for the savory, colorful bowl. He wrapped everything in a warm tortilla and topped it with sour cream, lime, and a sprinkle of chili flakes.

Eugene’s kitchen smelled of toasted grains, savory eggs, caramelized tomatoes, and warm spices. To serve it, he arranged the eggs in the center, surrounded the bowl with crisp vegetables and beans, and placed the tortilla on top, garnishing it with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime.

The occasion was a casual Tuesday routine, balancing his IT certification with Hobbs Cafe’s new breakfast menu and market updates. He wanted a practical, satisfying meal that would energize him for the next shift.
