Eugene Johnson prepared a Breakfast Pot-Pan Breakfast with Eggs, Toast, and Fruit for a relaxed Tuesday morning at Hobbs Cafe. The dish included eggs, whole-grain toast, sliced bananas, berries, and a drizzle of maple syrup, with a small portion of oatmeal and yogurt nearby.

The eggs were cracked into a pan, whisked briefly, and cooked until set on the stove while the kitchen was being prepared. Toast was toasted until golden, then flipped and finished with a little maple syrup. The warm, colorful breakfast was arranged on a white plate, with the berries, nuts, and fruit scattered around the eggs and toast for an inviting presentation.

Eugene’s kitchen smelled of rich egg yolk, toasted bread, maple syrup, and warm fruit. It was an informal, cheerful breakfast for a casual shift on Tuesday, February 14, as he balanced his IT certification with café duties while preparing the third batch of breakfast items.
