Eugene prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Herb Crust, a hearty dinner for his weekend cookout with roommate Sharon Antle and friend Maria Lopez. He roasted potatoes, carrots, and sweet potatoes at 400°F for 45 minutes, then brushed the vegetables with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and smoked paprika. After removing them from the oven, he pan-seared the herbs with a few cloves of garlic and sliced onions until crisp, then finished them with a drizzle of lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

The dish had a warm, earthy aroma of caramelized onions, garlic, and herbs, with hints of smoked flavor. It was served in a shallow baking dish, arranged neatly around the center, topped with a golden herb crust and garnished with fresh parsley. The presentation was rustic and inviting, perfect for sharing at the cookout.

Eugene bought the vegetables from The Willows Market, where he paid cash and received a receipt. The simple, satisfying meal was more than dinner—it was a quiet moment before reconnecting with friends and preparing for the weekend.
