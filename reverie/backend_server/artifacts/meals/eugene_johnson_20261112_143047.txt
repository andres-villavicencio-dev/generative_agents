Eugene prepared a Pan-Seared Chicken with Lemon-Herb Glaze and Roasted Vegetable Mix for a simple, satisfying weekday dinner. The chicken was marinated in olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, dried parsley, and paprika, then seared in a cast-iron skillet until the skin turned golden and crisp. A bright lemon-herb glaze was brushed over the bird just before baking, giving it a glossy, aromatic finish.

Alongside it, he roasted cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, onions, and carrots with olive oil, salt, and paprika until their edges caramelized. The meal was presented on a white ceramic platter, with the chicken arranged neatly in the center and colorful vegetables arranged around it. A small bowl of lemon wedges and a ramekin of warm roasted potatoes served the dish as a balanced, family-style meal.

The occasion was an informal Tuesday dinner. The warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and citrus filled the kitchen as Eugene sliced the chicken and arranged the vegetables. It was a relaxed, comforting evening meant to ease off his busy schedule while still encouraging him to try a new home-cooked recipe.
