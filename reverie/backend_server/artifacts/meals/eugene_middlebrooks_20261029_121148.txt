Eugene prepared Baked Chicken with Roasted Carrots and Root Vegetables. He cooked a whole chicken in a baking pan with rosemary, lemon, garlic, and salt, while the carrots, potatoes, and onions sautéed until tender. He added a splash of vegetable broth and simmered until the liquid reduced. The dish was finished with fresh parsley and topped with golden breadcrumbs, then baked until the chicken turned golden brown and the vegetables were soft and caramelized.

The aroma of roasting garlic, lemon zest, and savory herbs filled the kitchen. Eugene arranged the golden chicken, bright orange carrots, and crisp vegetables on a white platter, garnishing each with parsley and a lemon wedge. He served it with whole-grain bread, a small bowl of salad, and a chilled glass of water.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Thursday dinner, replacing the depleted bread, eggs, and milk so he could cook dinner without rushing. It felt practical and comforting, offering a simple way to turn his grocery and pantry run into a satisfying home-cooked meal.
