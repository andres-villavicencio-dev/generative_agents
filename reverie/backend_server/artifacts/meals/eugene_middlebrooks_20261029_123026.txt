Eugene prepared Lemon-Bread Egg Scramble with Toasted Bread and Fresh Vegetables, a simple, nourishing breakfast-and-lunch combo.

He cracked two eggs into a pan, added a handful of fresh spinach and sliced red bell pepper, then cooked them gently with a pinch of salt and pepper. Toasted slices of white bread and a small bagel went on the side for easy eating. The dish was garnished with a drizzle of lemon juice and a sprinkle of cracked pepper.

The kitchen smelled warm and fragrant, with the buttery aroma of toasted bread mingling with the bright scent of lemon and the savory aroma of cooking eggs.

The meal was prepared on an ordinary Thursday morning, but it felt more special because it was the first of his quiet meals before the demanding day. Its simplicity reflected his easygoing nature, while its balanced ingredients supported his purposeful schedule.
