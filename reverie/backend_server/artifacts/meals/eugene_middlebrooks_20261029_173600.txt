Eugene prepared a simple Savory Breakfast with Eggs, Fruit, and Toast, a familiar and nourishing meal for a quiet Friday morning.

He started by toasting two slices of sourdough on a panini press, then lightly browned two eggs and added a handful of fresh spinach, diced tomato, and a few sliced onions. For the fruit, he placed apples and bananas in the toaster before baking them, then stirred them into the pan with a splash of milk. He finished the dish with a drizzle of honey and a sprinkle of cinnamon, creating a warm, golden aroma of toasted bread, egg richness, and sweet fruit.

The presentation was clean and inviting: the eggs rested in a small white ramekin alongside the fruit, with the toast and breakfast arranged neatly on a wooden cutting board. It was prepared for a relaxed Sunday breakfast, but Eugene left it ready to take home without rushing, keeping the final request and the unresolved clinic project in mind.
