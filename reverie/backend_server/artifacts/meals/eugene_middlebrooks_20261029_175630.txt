Eugene prepared a simple Egg and Toast Breakfast with whole-wheat toast, a small bowl of fresh fruit, and a glass of cold milk. He used two large eggs, a handful of sliced avocado, a banana, a handful of blueberries, and two slices of whole-wheat toast. He cooked the eggs over medium heat, seasoned them lightly with salt and pepper, and let them cool before serving.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, butter, ripe fruit, and the subtle richness of the eggs. He arranged the toast, fruit, and milk in a simple platter, placing a glass of milk beside the bowl and adding a few fresh berries for a pop of color. The presentation was rustic and inviting, suitable for a quiet Friday morning.

It was a relaxed breakfast for an active day: after finishing the grocery restock and reconciling accounts, Eugene wanted a calm way to begin his day while maintaining the cheerful rhythm of his routine.
