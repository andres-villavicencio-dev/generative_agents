Breakfast: “Community Starter” Toast with Eggs, Milk, and Coffee Beans

Eugene prepared a simple, nourishing breakfast for a quiet Friday morning. He toasted slices of whole-grain bread until they were golden and crisp, then cracked two eggs into a small pan and seasoned them lightly with salt. The eggs were cooked until the whites were set and the yolks remained soft, creating a fragrant aroma of butter, warm grains, and toasted nuts. A splash of milk and a small handful of coffee beans were poured over the pan, adding a rich, caramel-like sweetness.

The meal was presented simply on a white plate, with the bread, eggs, milk, and coffee beans arranged neatly beside a spoon. It tasted comforting and balanced, with the earthy bread, rich eggs, and warm milk combining into a satisfying meal. He ate it in his kitchen before heading out to shop and eat at a nearby café, then returned home, eager to begin the day by reconnecting with friends and contacts.
