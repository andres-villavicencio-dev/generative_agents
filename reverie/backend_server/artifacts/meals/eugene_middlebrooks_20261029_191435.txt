Eugene prepared a quick chicken-and-vegetable skillet breakfast. The dish used two large eggs, a tablespoon of butter, a slice of whole-grain toast, a few cherry tomatoes, and a teaspoon of olive oil.

He scrambled the eggs in a small nonstick skillet over medium heat, then added the butter and vegetables and toasted the toast until golden. He served everything in a small white bowl with a lemon wedge for brightness. The aroma was warm and savory—the buttery richness of the eggs mingling with the earthiness of the tomatoes and a faint hint of lemon. The presentation was simple and inviting: a neat oval of bright orange eggs, translucent tomatoes, and golden toast arranged on a white plate.

The occasion was a relaxed afternoon breakfast before a Friday trip. It was meant to be comforting and easy, allowing Eugene to transition from his workday without rushing.
