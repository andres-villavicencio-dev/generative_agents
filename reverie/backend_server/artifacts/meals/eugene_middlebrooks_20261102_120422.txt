Eugene prepared a Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and Herb Crust.

He sliced a whole chicken, roasted it with rosemary, thyme, garlic, onion, carrots, and potatoes, then arranged the vegetables alongside it. Beneath the chicken, he used a simple herb crust made with flour, butter, garlic, and chopped parsley. He brushed the vegetables with a little olive oil, seasoned them lightly, and placed them around the main dish.

The kitchen filled with the rich scent of roasted meat, garlic, and earthy herbs. The golden chicken contrasted with the bright orange carrots and crisp green vegetables, creating a warm, inviting presentation.

This was prepared for a morning breakfast, though it was intended as a hearty lunch. He had finished preparing the final request for Maria and was considering sending the updated spreadsheet, while still grateful for Maria and Ana’s support and optimistic about the clinic’s position.
