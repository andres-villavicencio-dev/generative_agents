Meal: “Smallville” Tomato Basil Garlic Pasta

Eugene prepared a simple, hearty pasta dish inspired by the town’s quirky story. The key ingredients were cherry tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, fresh basil, a pinch of salt, and a little lemon juice. He cooked the pasta first, then added the tomatoes and garlic while the sauce simmered, letting the heat meld the flavors together.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm basil, savory garlic, and a faint hint of sweet tomato. He finished it with chopped basil, a squeeze of lemon, and a generous handful of cracked black pepper.

He plated the pasta in a shallow white bowl, arranging the bright tomatoes and herbs around the pasta. A small side of toasted almonds and a drizzle of balsamic glaze added a touch of color and contrast.

Eugene prepared it for an early Saturday dinner, intending to serve it with friends before heading home to check the clinic’s fundraiser and answer his lingering questions about the unresolved disputes.
