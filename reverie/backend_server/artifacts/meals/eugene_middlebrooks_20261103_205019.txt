The meal was a Cinnamon-Oat Breakfast Bowl, prepared by Eugene for a quiet Sunday morning at town hall. He rolled oat flakes, soaked them in warm water, added a pinch of cinnamon, and topped it with a drizzle of honey. The aroma was warm and comforting—a rich scent of toasted oats, sweet honey, and lightly spiced tea.

Eugene arranged the ingredients in a simple white ceramic bowl, then used a small spatula to stir gently, allowing the oats to soften into a creamy texture. He topped it with fresh berries and a sprinkle of toasted nuts. The presentation was bright and inviting: the golden oats, deep red and orange berries, and golden honey created a colorful contrast, while the warm cinnamon added a subtle golden glow.

It was prepared for a quiet Sunday breakfast, with no rush or expectation of a formal gathering. Eugene wanted the meal to feel personal and nourishing, a simple, satisfying way to start his day after reconciling the cafe and grocery accounts.
