Eugene prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Herbs, a simple weekday dinner that reflected his love of balancing everyday responsibilities with thoughtful cooking.

He sliced the chicken, tossed it with lemon, garlic, dried thyme, rosemary, and a pinch of salt, then roasted it in the oven until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. He paired it with carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, which were cut into uniform chunks and roasted with a splash of vegetable stock. A lemon-herb dressing was served alongside everything.

As he ate, the kitchen filled with the scent of roasted chicken, earthy carrots, and warm herbs. The meal was presented in a simple white bowl, arranged neatly on a wooden table with a small plate of crackers beside it. It was a quiet, family-style dinner for the first Sunday of his CPA study, the kind of relaxed occasion when he could turn his analytical nature into a simple, nourishing meal.
