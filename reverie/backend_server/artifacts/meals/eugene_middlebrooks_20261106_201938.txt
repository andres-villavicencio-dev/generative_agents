Eugene prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Herb Butter, a comforting dinner meant to balance his work with a little relaxation.

The kitchen was filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, carrots, parsnips, and potatoes. He seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic, then roasted it until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The vegetables were lightly caramelized in the same pan, with rosemary and thyme adding fragrant notes to the dish.

Eugene sliced the golden chicken into bite-sized pieces and arranged it alongside the crisp root vegetables in a shallow dish. He drizzled the pan juices over everything, using a little herb butter for the final touch. The meal presented itself as a rustic, inviting feast, with the chicken’s glossy skin contrasting against the earthy colors of the vegetables.

He served it around 7:00 PM on a Tuesday evening, just before his Monday evening work, to celebrate his recent success in reconciling the cafe and grocery accounts and his partnership with Ryan Park’s fundraiser.
