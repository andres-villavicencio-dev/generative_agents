Eugene prepared Hearty Herb-Smashed Chickpea Stew to accompany his dinner. The dish featured chickpeas, carrots, onions, celery, garlic, tomatoes, spinach, and a gentle broth, with a generous portion of brown rice and a final drizzle of olive oil and lemon juice for brightness.

He sautéed the vegetables and aromatics in a large pot, then added the chickpeas and broth. After simmering until the chickpeas were tender and the vegetables softened, he finished the stew with fresh spinach and a pinch of cumin. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, earthy carrots, and savory broth.

The bowl was arranged with the rice and stew, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a lemon wedge before serving. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday evening meal, carefully balanced after Eugene reconciled the café and grocery accounts with Yuriko’s CPA guidance. His analytical nature helped him choose familiar, comforting ingredients, while the simple presentation reflected his easygoing approach to everyday life.
