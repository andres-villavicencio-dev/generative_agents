The aroma filled the kitchen—warm, savory, and slightly smoky, with notes of garlic and roasted potatoes. The presentation was bright and inviting: golden chicken resting on a white plate, creamy potatoes beside it, and a small bowl of steaming green beans.

He ate the meal while reviewing his CPA study problems, his mind focused but relaxed. The occasion was simply a quiet Tuesday morning, with the goal of turning his analytical accounting background into clearer answers for Yuriko’s questions at Willow Clinic.
