Eugene prepared a Hearty Lentil & Herb Quinoa Bowl, the centerpiece of his Tuesday lunch. He combined quinoa, cooked lentils, diced tomatoes, cucumber, carrot, and spinach, then added chopped fennel, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. He toasted the fennel and warmed the olive oil in a pan until the vegetables softened, then simmered the lentils and quinoa until the liquid absorbed and the grains became tender.

The aroma rose throughout the kitchen as the fennel’s warm, slightly earthy scent mingled with the savory notes of the lentils and the bright, citrusy freshness of the lemon. He served the bowl in a shallow white ceramic dish, adding a colorful red bell pepper, a sprig of thyme, and a final sprinkle of dried oregano for visual contrast.

The meal was prepared for a community lunch meeting on Tuesday, February 14, during which he was mentoring himself through his CPA study plan and discussing the clinic’s fundraiser. He wanted the warm, balanced lunch to feel welcoming and practical, while the fresh herbs reminded him that the clinic’s support made his daily work meaningful.
