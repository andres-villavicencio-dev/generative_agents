Eugene prepared a Mediterranean Herb Croissant with Toasted Lemon-Butter Baguette, a simple, nourishing breakfast lunch for Tuesday, February 14.

The dish began with a baguette sliced into thick rounds. A mixture of softened cream cheese, lemon zest, garlic, and a few chopped herbs was spread onto one side, while the other side received a layer of toasted butter. The two halves were brushed with a little lemon juice, then folded into a neat, golden croissant and placed on a warm plate. A slice of crusty baguette was toasted at the end to make a crisp accompaniment.

The kitchen smelled of warm butter, toasted bread, lemon, and fresh herbs. The golden croissant stood invitingly on the plate, its flaky layers catching the morning light. It was a quiet, comforting meal for a busy Tuesday, balanced by the quiet purpose of reconciling the cafe’s and grocery’s accounts.
