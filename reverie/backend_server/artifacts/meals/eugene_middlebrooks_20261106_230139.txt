Eugene prepared Soup with Baked Potato and Herb Crust, a warm, comforting lunch for a Tuesday workday. He boiled a large pot of chicken noodle soup, adding carrots, celery, onion, a pinch of salt, and a couple of sprigs of thyme. A small portion of the broth was used to braise a baked potato before serving the soup, and he sliced the potato into generous pieces.

The meal was finished with a second sandwich, filled with turkey, lettuce, tomato, and a thin layer of mayonnaise. The herb crust was pressed around the edges until golden and crisp.

As he finished, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of simmering chicken, roasted potato, and savory herbs. He arranged the soup and sandwich on a white ceramic plate, placing a crisp slice of lemon on top to brighten the colors. The presentation was simple and inviting—practical for a weekday lunch, balanced by the hearty meal Eugene had prepared during his grocery run.
