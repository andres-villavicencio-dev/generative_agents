Francisco López’s dinner is a vibrant Spicy Miso Tuna Wrap served at his family’s celebration of his birthday. Key ingredients include creamy miso sauce, sushi-grade tuna, avocado ribbons, and pickled ginger. Prepared by marinating the tuna in miso for 20 minutes while he reads on the couch, then assembling the wrap with fresh components. The aroma blends umami depth from miso with a hint of ginger spice, filling the kitchen with warmth. Presented neatly at the dining table, it’s rolled with gold leaf garnish and served alongside steamed jasmine rice. This dish marks a joyful occasion—his birthday dinner, reflecting his love for laughter and connection. The meal is both comforting and playful, honoring his lively spirit as he winds down into evening calm.
