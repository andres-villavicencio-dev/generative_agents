Francisco Lopez prepared a comforting bowl of garlic shrimp pasta with roasted tomatoes and spinach. He began by heating olive oil in a skillet, then sautéed sliced garlic until fragrant and added halved cherry tomatoes. The tomatoes softened and released their juices as he stirred in chili flakes, salt, and pepper. He cooked the shrimp separately until pink, then folded them into the sauce with fresh spinach. Finally, he tossed the mixture with al dente penne and a splash of pasta water, allowing the glossy sauce to cling to the noodles.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, tomatoes, herbs, and lightly seasoned seafood. He plated the pasta in a wide white bowl, arranging the shrimp prominently on top and finishing it with grated Parmesan, cracked black pepper, and a small basil leaf.

The occasion was a relaxed Monday dinner, marking the end of his first week of filming the web series about life in a co-living space. He ate around 6pm, enjoying the meal as both a satisfying break and a source of inspiration for his next episode.
