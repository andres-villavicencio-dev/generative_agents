Francisco Lopez prepared a bright, Mexican-inspired citrus chicken bowl for lunch, accompanied by a sparkling lime drink. The bowl includes grilled chicken breast, fluffy rice, black beans, corn, diced avocado, cherry tomatoes, shredded lettuce, and pickled red onions. He seasons the chicken with chili powder, garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, and lime juice, then grills it until lightly charred and juicy. The rice is warmed with cilantro and a squeeze of lime, while the black beans are seasoned with cumin and a pinch of salt.

The dish’s aroma is fresh and vibrant, combining the smoky scent of the chicken with citrus, herbs, and the earthy sweetness of the beans. He presents it in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the colorful ingredients in distinct sections and finishing it with avocado, tomato salsa, and tortilla strips. A chilled lime-and-soda drink, garnished with a lime wheel, sits beside the bowl.

The meal is a quick, energizing Monday lunch inspired by the lively, communal food culture of co-living spaces—perfect for Francisco before an afternoon of web-series planning or improvisational comedy practice.
