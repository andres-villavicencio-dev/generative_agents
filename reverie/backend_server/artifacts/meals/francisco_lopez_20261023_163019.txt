Francisco prepared Quinoa and Veggie Salad with Lemon-Tahini Dressing, a simple and refreshing lunch for his soup. The dish featured cooked quinoa as the base, chopped carrots, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, and roasted eggplant. He gently tossed the vegetables with lemon zest and a smooth lemon-tahini dressing, adding a little olive oil and chili flakes for brightness. The bright, earthy aroma of the quinoa and vegetables mingled with the fragrant tang of the lemon dressing, creating a cheerful scent of freshness.

He presented the salad in a clear glass bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables neatly around the center and pouring the dressing over them. The crisp textures and vivid colors made the meal look inviting and easy to eat. This light lunch was prepared for a relaxed Monday lunch while he worked on his web series and improvisational comedy classes. The meal was neither elaborate nor overly rich, reflecting his casual, friendly, and approachable style.
