For breakfast, Francisco prepared a simple yet comforting Lemon-herb Scrambled Eggs with Roasted Tomatoes and Toasted Toast. He used two large eggs, plenty of whole-grain flour, salt, pepper, garlic, thyme, chopped parsley, and a splash of lemon juice. The eggs were whisked, then cooked gently over medium heat until creamy and golden. Fresh tomatoes were roasted until their skins softened, with a little olive oil and salt added for brightness. He toasted two slices of sourdough on a panini press, letting them crisp slightly.

The kitchen was filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, garlic, lemon, thyme, and the rich scent of cooking eggs. He arranged the eggs and tomatoes in a small white dish, placing a slice of sourdough and a lemon wedge beside them. The bright colors and rustic presentation made it feel inviting, as if it were a thoughtful meal before starting his day. It was a quiet, unhurried breakfast—a small gesture of care and preparation for an important workday.
