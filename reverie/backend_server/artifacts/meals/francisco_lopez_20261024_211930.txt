Francisco prepared a hearty Spanish Sopa de Pollo con Papa***, a warm chicken soup with potatoes and a creamy tomato-based broth. The key ingredients were whole chicken, potatoes, carrots, celery, onion, tomato, garlic, and a pinch of cumin.

The preparation method involved browning the chicken in oil until golden, adding the aromatics and vegetable pieces, then simmering the soup until the chicken was tender and the potatoes were soft. He finished it with a splash of cream and served it with crusty bread.

The kitchen filled with a comforting aroma of sautéed chicken, caramelized onions, and garlic. The bowls were arranged neatly on the counter, with the cooked soup in ceramic bowls and a white cloth napkin tucked beside the plate. This was a simple weekday breakfast meal, prepared for a quiet, focused start to his workday.
