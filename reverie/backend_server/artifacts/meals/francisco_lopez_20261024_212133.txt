For breakfast, Francisco prepared a hearty, rustic Coconut-Tomato Toast with Spicy Egg and Fresh Greens, a simple yet flavorful breakfast that balanced comfort with an adventurous twist.

The dish used whole-grain bread, ripe tomatoes, fresh basil, a generous amount of coconut milk, chili flakes, and a little hot sauce. He sliced the bread, toasted it lightly, and arranged it on a wooden plate with a colorful garnish of sliced cucumber, lettuce, and fresh basil. He cracked two eggs into a small bowl, whisked them with a splash of coconut milk and a pinch of salt, then poured the hot mixture over the toast. A few chili flakes and a squeeze of lemon juice added the finishing touch.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of toasted bread, caramelized tomatoes, coconut milk, chili, and fresh herbs. The presentation was bright and inviting—the golden-brown toast stood out against the red tomato, green lettuce, and creamy white egg, with basil adding a cheerful pop.

He prepared it for a relaxed Wednesday morning, perhaps to use for a quick lunch while preparing for work. The meal was practical, comforting, and suited to a busy day of improvisation and filming his web series about life in a co-living space.
