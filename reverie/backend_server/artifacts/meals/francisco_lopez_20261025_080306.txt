For dinner on Tuesday, February 14, Francisco prepared a warm Roast Garlic Herb Chicken with Lemon-Basil Rice. The chicken was seasoned with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper, then roasted until golden and fully cooked in the oven. The rice was seasoned with lemon zest, fresh basil, and a pinch of garlic powder, toasted briefly, and cooked until fluffy and fragrant.

Aromatic, the kitchen filled with the savory scent of roasted garlic and herbs, the savory aroma of the chicken, and the fresh scent of lemon and basil.

Francisco presented the meal in a simple ceramic bowl with a small side of grilled vegetables, and he added a small plate of warm crackers for dipping. The colorful rice and golden chicken created a cheerful, inviting look, while the lemon-basil garnish added a fresh pop of color.

It was a simple family dinner shared with friends, but it felt like part of the evening’s creative project—warm, memorable, and fitting for a Tuesday when the neighborhood café, old radio, and digital display still remained his visual anchors.
