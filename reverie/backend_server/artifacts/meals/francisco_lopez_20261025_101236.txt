For breakfast on Wednesday, February 15, Francisco prepared a Scrambled Egg with Avocado and Toasted Corn. Key ingredients were two eggs, two slices of whole-grain toast, a ripe avocado, a handful of corn kernels, and a pinch of salt.

He cracked the eggs into a small pan, adding the toast and corn while whisking them until the yolks were cooked and the whites were set. He then added the avocado, sliced into thin strips, and seasoned lightly. The pan went into the oven, where the eggs and corn browned and melted together.

The aroma was warm and fragrant—creamy, buttery, and slightly sweet from the corn, with a hint of toasted bread. He plated the breakfast with the eggs in a loose pile beside the toast and avocado, garnishing the dish with a few fresh corn kernels.

The occasion was a quiet, purposeful morning: after a busy workday, Francisco wanted to begin his day with something nourishing, reflective, and cheerful.
