Francisco prepared Pan con Chicharrón for a relaxed Monday breakfast, likely for himself and his roommate. The dish features bacon, chorizo, onions, garlic, bell peppers, tomatoes, and black beans, all simmered in a savory broth and topped with crispy bacon strips. The pan is sautéed until the meat turns golden brown, then the vegetables are softened and cooked through. A generous portion of queso blanco, a creamy white cheese, is added at the end, followed by fresh lemon slices and green onions for a bright finish.

The aroma fills the kitchen with the rich scent of smoky bacon and fried chorizo, savory onions, garlic, and the earthy note of black beans. The warm, golden pan makes the dish look hearty and inviting, with the white cheese and lemon creating a colorful contrast.

The occasion was a casual morning meal after a busy workday, as Francisco balanced cooking with his responsibilities as an actor, comedian, and web series creator.
