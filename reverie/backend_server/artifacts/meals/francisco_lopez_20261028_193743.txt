Francisco prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon Herb Sauce. The dish featured tender chicken breast, carrots, bell peppers, onions, cherry tomatoes, and garlic, finished with a bright lemon-herb sauce and a crisp lemon wedge.

Francisco started by seasoning the chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic, then marinated it briefly in the fridge. He arranged the vegetables in a pan, added olive oil, and roasted them until the carrots and peppers turned golden. He grilled the chicken until the meat was pink and cooked through, letting it rest under foil so the juices stayed in. He finished the dish with a simple lemon-herb sauce and served the vegetables, chicken, and sauce in a shallow, rustic bowl, topped with toasted bread.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, roasted vegetables, and warm chicken. The bright citrus added a cheerful, inviting scent that made the meal feel ready for a casual gathering.

He prepared the meal for a Monday dinner gathering among friends, including Francisco, his partner, and a few other co-living roommates. It was a relaxed family-style dinner designed to bring everyone together.
