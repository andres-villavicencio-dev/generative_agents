On a Tuesday evening, Francisco prepared Sausage, Mushroom, and Red Pepper Tacos for a quiet dinner with friends. He sliced fresh red bell pepper strips, sliced mushrooms, and crumbled cooked ground sausage into warm tortillas, adding a handful of cilantro and a squeeze of lime.

He sautéed the vegetables briefly, then layered the ingredients into the corn tortillas, topped them with a little hot salsa, and folded them into soft tacos. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized onions, savory sausage, earthy mushrooms, and the bright, fresh scent of cilantro and lime.

Serving the tacos on a small platter, Francisco arranged them with colorful toppings: avocado, jalapeños, and a side of fresh fruit. The occasion was a casual Friday dinner with friends who loved his improvised-comedy classes, though the meal felt special because it was the first time he had prepared something of his own.
