Francisco prepared Garden-Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables as a warm, comforting side for dinner. The dish features tender chicken thighs, baby carrots, broccoli, snap peas, and zucchini, all sautéed with garlic, lemon juice, oregano, thyme, and a little honey. He began by cooking the chicken in a skillet until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear, then added the vegetables and finished them with garlic and fresh herbs.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized chicken, roasted vegetables, and lemon zest. He arranged the colorful vegetables in a shallow bowl, plating the chicken on the side to keep the presentation bright and balanced. It was an informal, relaxed dinner prepared for a Sunday family gathering, with friends and family gathered to enjoy the meal together.
