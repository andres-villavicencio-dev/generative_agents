Francisco prepared a hearty vegetable risotto with lemon and parmesan. The main ingredients were Arborio rice, vegetable broth, parmesan cheese, spinach, cherry tomatoes, garlic, shallots, and a generous drizzle of olive oil. He sautéed the shallots and garlic, added the spinach and tomatoes, then slowly stirred in the broth while adding the rice gradually. He seasoned it with salt, black pepper, and lemon zest, and finished it with freshly grated Parmesan.

The kitchen filled with the warm, nutty aroma of toasted grains and savory herbs. Francisco served the risotto in a shallow white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables and garnishing it with a final sprinkle of Parmesan. It was served for an informal dinner while he prepared his side dish, giving him time to focus on the meal before his show.
