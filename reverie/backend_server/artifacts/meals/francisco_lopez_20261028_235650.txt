At 7:00 AM, Francisco woke to the soft hum of his kitchen. He prepared a simple Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Herb Butter for breakfast, a light, filling meal that balanced his morning energy. The key ingredients were salmon fillets, lemon zest, fresh dill, garlic, olive oil, and a knob of butter. Francisco sliced the salmon, seasoned it lightly, and pan-seared it over medium heat until the skin turned golden and the flesh became flaky.

A splash of lemon juice and a few sprigs of dill infused the pan, while he brushed the butter into the pan with a spatula to create a fragrant, golden crust. He served the salmon over white rice, letting the butter and lemon brighten the meal.

The aroma rose warmly from the kitchen—a rich blend of salted fish, garlic, lemon, and butter. Francisco presented the dish in a shallow bowl, arranging the salmon in a gentle arc beside a small mound of rice. The simple, colorful presentation made the meal feel inviting and celebratory, fitting for a quiet Tuesday morning before he launched into his co-living space web series.
