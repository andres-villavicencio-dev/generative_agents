Francisco prepared Scrambled Egg Breakfast and Lunch, combining a hearty breakfast with a simple, satisfying lunch. The dish included two cooked eggs, a handful of spinach, a slice of tomato, and a small piece of grilled onion, all seasoned with salt, pepper, and a touch of olive oil.

Francisco stirred the eggs in a non-stick pan over medium heat, occasionally flipping them with a spatula until they were just set. He added the vegetables toward the end, letting them wilt slightly without becoming mushy. The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of cooked eggs and fresh herbs.

The presentation was clean and inviting: the eggs were arranged neatly on a white plate, with the colorful vegetables adding pops of green, red, and yellow. A simple glass of water and a small bowl of fresh fruit completed the setting. The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday morning, balancing his co-living space web series and improvisational comedy classes with a simple, nourishing meal.
