On Tuesday, February 14, Francisco prepared a simple, hearty breakfast of pan-fried eggs with toast and fresh fruit. The eggs were cracked into a lightly oiled pan and fried until the yolks were soft and the whites were slightly crisp, then served with golden slices of whole-grain toast. He paired the breakfast with a handful of red apples and a few orange slices.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted bread and caramelized egg, while the bright reds, oranges, and golden toast added cheerful pops of color. Francisco arranged the eggs and toast neatly on the table, with the fruit placed beside them, creating a warm, inviting breakfast table. It was a simple, comforting meal suited to a quiet Tuesday—perfect before he resumed his co-living space web series and improvisational comedy classes.
