On a quiet Tuesday morning, Francisco prepared a Light Breakfast Bowl with Roasted Vegetables and Toasted Seeds. The meal was simple, colorful, and filled with the warmth of leftover ingredients.

For the breakfast bowl, he toasted whole-grain bread, added sliced eggs and cheddar, and topped it with sautéed spinach, roasted tomatoes, and crunchy chickpeas. A small bowl of yogurt with honey and fresh berries provided a sweet contrast. He also made a simple panini from toasted baguette slices, adding a little olive oil, tomatoes, and a pinch of garlic.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted bread, caramelized cheese, and roasting vegetables. A bright yellow, red, and green presentation made the bowls feel cheerful and inviting. Francisco served the breakfast at around 7:30 am, enjoying it with a cup of coffee as he prepared for his improv comedy class and continued his web series. It was a relaxed, nourishing start to his day, combining comfort with his love of entertaining and discovering new ways to make people laugh.
