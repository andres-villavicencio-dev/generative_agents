On Tuesday, February 14, Francisco prepared a simple and cheerful breakfast: Sunny-Spot Oatmeal with Berries and Toasted Walnuts. The base was a cup of warm oatmeal, topped with fresh blueberries, sliced strawberries, and chopped walnuts. He added a splash of milk or oat milk and sprinkled cinnamon on top for a cozy aroma.

To make it, Francisco boiled the oats in water until soft, seasoned them with vanilla and a pinch of salt, then let them cool slightly before topping them with the berries. He toasted a small slice of whole-grain bread and chopped the walnuts, then stacked the oatmeal, toast, and nuts on a warm plate.

The meal was bright and inviting, with the colorful fruit contrasting against the creamy oatmeal and golden toast. It was prepared for a quiet Tuesday breakfast, but Francisco kept it relaxed and playful, as if he were preparing a stage set for a comedy routine.
