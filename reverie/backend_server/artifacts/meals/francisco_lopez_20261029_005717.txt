On Tuesday, February 14, Francisco prepared a warm Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice for breakfast and a simple Chicken Salad Wrap for lunch.

For breakfast, he marinated chicken with garlic, lemon, and fresh herbs, then grilled it until golden and juicy. He served it over fluffy rice, adding roasted potatoes, carrots, and crunchy chickpeas. The meal was bright, aromatic, and filled with the scent of garlic, lemon, herbs, and grilled chicken. It was presented on a large white plate with a colorful garnish of parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.

For lunch, he wrapped the remaining chicken in lettuce and served it with a simple vinaigrette. The two meals were prepared at home, with Francisco clearing the table and returning the dishes to the kitchen afterward. It was a relaxed, informal Tuesday, fitting into his active schedule while he continued preparing for his co-living space web series and improvisational comedy classes.
