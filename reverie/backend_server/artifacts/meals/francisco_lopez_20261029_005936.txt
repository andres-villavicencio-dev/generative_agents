On Tuesday, February 14, Francisco Lopez prepared Crispy Veggie Pancakes with Smoky Maple Sauce for breakfast. He used flour, eggs, milk, a pinch of cinnamon, and chopped apple and banana. After mixing the batter, he pan-fried the pancakes in a lightly oiled skillet until they were golden and crisp, then flipped them to serve the remaining ones. He drizzled each with a warm maple-sour cream sauce, garnishing them with a few fresh berries.

The aroma was warm and inviting—rich caramel, buttery flour, and the sweet scent of ripe fruit mingling with the savory crunch of the pancakes. He presented them in a simple white bowl, each pancake balanced with a spoonful of sauce and a small apple slice.

This was a relaxed, everyday breakfast meant to start the day with energy and a little comfort, before he headed to the co-living space to store the remaining ingredients and clean the counter.
