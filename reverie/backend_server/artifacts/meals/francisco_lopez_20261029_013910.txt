On Tuesday, February 14, Francisco prepared a quick Garlic Herb Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice. He used a whole chicken seasoned with garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, black pepper, dried oregano, and a splash of red wine vinegar. The chicken was seared in a skillet until golden, then pan-fried briefly in the same pan with the remaining juices.

For the sides, he toasted the rice in butter and mixed it with chopped onion, garlic, parsley, lemon zest, and a pinch of nutmeg. He added canned tomatoes or canned tomatoes, water, and a little vegetable broth.

The kitchen filled with the sharp, savory aroma of garlic and herbs, the crisp scent of seared chicken, and the fresh citrus fragrance of lemon and rosemary. He plated the meal against a white ceramic plate, garnishing it with extra parsley and a few lemon slices.

The occasion was a warm weekday lunch after his web series and improv comedy classes. Francisco served it casually with a side of roasted potatoes and a small glass of red wine, making it a friendly, easy-to-maintain meal that balanced his creative life with a relaxed appetite.
