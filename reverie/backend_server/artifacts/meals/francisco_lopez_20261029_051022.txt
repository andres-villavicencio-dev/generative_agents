On Wednesday, February 15, Francisco prepared Lemon Herb Lemon Herb Roast Chicken, a simple yet comforting family-style meal to balance his busy schedule. He marinated a whole chicken with lemon, garlic, thyme, rosemary, oregano, and a splash of olive oil before roasting it in a preheated oven at 400°F. The golden-brown skin, juicy white meat, and aromatic herbs gave it a warm, citrusy aroma.

The chicken was served over creamy mashed potatoes, with a simple green salad, crusty whole-grain bread, and a side of roasted carrots and potatoes. Francisco arranged the plates with bright red tomatoes, white onions, and scattered fresh herbs, while a glass of cold lemonade accompanied the meal. It was a casual lunch to celebrate meeting at Hobbs Cafe, but the relaxed presentation made it feel like a special family gathering.
