On Wednesday, February 15, Francisco prepared a hearty Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Veggies for lunch with his friends. He used a 2-pound chicken breast, lemon zest and juice, garlic cloves, thyme, rosemary, olive oil, salt, and pepper. He marinated the chicken for 20 minutes, then roasted it with carrots, broccoli, and onions until golden and tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and citrus. He arranged the chicken in a shallow baking dish and piled the colorful vegetables around it. A small lemon wedge and the remaining juices were placed in a glass on top, adding a bright final touch.

The meal was a relaxed, casual lunch for his friends, a chance to share laughter and discuss the community fund meeting while preparing to meet with Latoya at Hobbs Cafe at 12:30 p.m.
