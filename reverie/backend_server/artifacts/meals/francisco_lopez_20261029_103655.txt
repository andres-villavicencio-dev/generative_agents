For lunch, Francisco prepared a Chicken and Vegetable Bowl with a simple lemon-herb sauce. He used chicken breast, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, broccoli, onions, and a cup of quinoa. He marinated the chicken in olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, paprika, and dried oregano, then pan-seared it until the skin turned crisp. He added the vegetables and cooked them briefly until the broccoli was tender. He topped the bowl with the lemon-herb sauce and a generous portion of extra-quinoa.

The kitchen smelled fresh and aromatic—garlic, herbs, lemon zest, and the slight sweetness of cooked vegetables filled the small space. He arranged the colorful vegetables and golden chicken in the center of the bowl, garnishing the sides with a little parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.

The meal was simple but satisfying, and he prepared it for a quiet Thursday lunch. It balanced his co-living responsibilities with a moment of calm, food, and connection to the community at Hobbs Cafe.
