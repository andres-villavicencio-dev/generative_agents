The meal Francisco prepared was Roasted Mediterranean Vegetable Platter, a colorful side dish arranged alongside his main dinner. It included roasted red potatoes, carrots, zucchini, cherry tomatoes, green beans, and a handful of chickpeas, all tossed with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, and a touch of paprika.

Francisco arranged the ingredients in a wide, shallow dish, placing each vegetable in a separate cup and garnishing the edges with fresh parsley and a bright lemon wedge. The warm, golden glow of the roasted vegetables created an inviting aroma of garlic, herbs, and citrus, filling the kitchen with a cheerful scent.

For the occasion, Francisco prepared it for a quiet Thursday evening dinner. The dish’s bright, seasonal colors and relaxed presentation complemented the main meal, offering a cheerful, community-oriented side to share with the family.
