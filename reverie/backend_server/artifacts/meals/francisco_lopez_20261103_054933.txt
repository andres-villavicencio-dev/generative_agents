Francisco prepared a whole-wheat breakfast sandwich with an apple–honey salad, intended for the cafe’s lunch batch. He used whole-wheat bread, soft-boiled eggs, crisp apples, bananas, and clover honey, with a little mustard for a savory finish.

He toasted the bread until the crust formed, then split it and layered a grilled egg on one side, sliced apple, and banana on the other. The sandwich was placed on a white plate, surrounded by a bright green salad, and served with the honey in a small glass. A small piece of cinnamon was added for a subtle aroma.

The presentation was bright and inviting, with colorful produce and warm golden-brown bread. The occasion was a casual cafe lunch, balancing his creative work with the community’s meals.
