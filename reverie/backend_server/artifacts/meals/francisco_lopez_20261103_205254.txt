On Monday, February 13, Francisco Lopez prepared a hearty breakfast for himself while continuing his work on the web series and improvisational comedy classes. The dish was Milk & Honey Pancakes with Berries and Fresh Fruit.

The key ingredients were full-fat milk, eggs, sugar, a pinch of salt, fresh strawberries, blueberries, sliced bananas, and a drizzle of honey. Francisco heated the milk in a saucepan over medium heat, then cooked the eggs into fluffy pancakes in a non-stick pan until golden and slightly crisp. He topped the pancakes with the berries, sliced banana, and honey before serving them with a glass of milk.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the comforting scent of warm milk, caramelized sugar, and ripe fruit. The pancakes had a soft, buttery texture, while the berries added a bright, tart contrast. Francisco arranged the food on a simple white plate, surrounded by a small bowl of yogurt and a glass of water, creating a cozy, inviting presentation.

The meal was a quiet reward for a busy morning, balancing work and creativity with nourishment and a sense of warmth.
