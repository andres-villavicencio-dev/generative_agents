For Monday, February 13, Francisco prepared Chicken-Corn Salad with Lemon-Herb Dressing. The key ingredients were grilled chicken breast, corn kernels, diced tomatoes, red onion, cilantro, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, and a pinch of cumin. He marinated the chicken and vegetables in the dressing the night before, letting the flavors meld over gentle heat.

Francisco began by sautéing garlic and onions in a pan, then added the corn and chicken in batches. He stirred in the tomatoes and let everything cook until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender. For the final touch, he tossed the salad with the dressing and a squeeze of fresh lemon.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm garlic, smoky chicken, and bright herbs. Francisco plated it in a wide bowl, arranging the colorful ingredients in an inviting, layered pattern and adding a small sprig of cilantro on top. The dish was ready for a relaxed Friday breakfast—perhaps a gathering with friends or a casual family meal.
