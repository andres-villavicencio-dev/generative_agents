Francisco made a simple, colorful fruit salad for breakfast on Tuesday, February 14. The dish was called Sunrise Fruit Salad.

Key ingredients: ripe apples, oranges, bananas, strawberries, blueberries, and sliced kiwi, along with a little honey and a pinch of cinnamon.

Preparation method: Francisco washed and sliced the fruits, then arranged them in a shallow bowl. He tossed them lightly with honey and cinnamon, letting the fruit marinate briefly before plating it. The warm honey and spices helped bring out the natural sweetness of the produce.

Aroma: The bowl filled with the fresh scent of citrus, strawberries, and caramelized honey, accompanied by the subtle warmth of cinnamon.

Presentation: He arranged the colorful fruit slices in a neat, round platter, garnishing the edges with a few fresh berries and a small wedge of lemon. A glass of water and a cinnamon stick completed the rustic, inviting look.

Occasion: It was a relaxed breakfast for Francisco and two friends. After sharing the meal at Hobbs Cafe, they discussed the town-service schedule, bulletin-board dates, open positions, and whether the Willows Market position was still open. The sunny, cheerful presentation made the simple fruit salad feel both refreshing and perfectly suited to their cheerful Tuesday morning.
