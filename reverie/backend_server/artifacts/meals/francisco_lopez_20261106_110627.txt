Francisco prepared Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice, a simple yet warming lunch to make the long day at Hobbs Cafe more manageable.

The dish featured tender chicken breast marinated in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and a splash of rice vinegar, alongside colorful bell peppers, onions, broccoli, snap peas, and carrots. He sliced the chicken into bite-sized pieces, stir-fried the vegetables until crisp-tender, then tossed everything in the pan with browned chicken and the aromatic ginger-garlic sauce. The leftover rice was served over the stir-fry.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and ginger, the sweet scent of soy, and fresh vegetable crispness. The bright orange peppers, green broccoli, and crisp chicken made the plates look cheerful and inviting.

He plated the meal for lunch on Tuesday, February 14, after a full nine-hour shift. It was a practical, comforting lunch meant to refuel him before heading home around 11 p.m.
