Francisco prepared a hearty pan-fried chicken with roasted vegetables and a bright lemon-herb sauce. He sliced chicken breast, seasoned it with salt, pepper, garlic, and dried oregano, then pan-fried it in a skillet until golden and juicy. Alongside the chicken, he roasted broccoli, carrots, and bell peppers with olive oil, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice. He finished the dish with a simple lemon-herb sauce and served everything on a white plate.

The meal had a warm, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, roasted chicken, and fresh vegetables, especially noticeable after cooking. He arranged the chicken in the center, with colorful vegetables around it, and placed a small bowl of lemon-herb sauce beside it. The presentation was clean and inviting, suitable for a relaxed breakfast or early lunch before starting work on his web series.

The occasion was a casual morning meal at Hobbs Cafe, where Francisco was preparing breakfast and reviewing his work plan. He chose a simple, satisfying dish that would give him energy without delaying his tasks.
