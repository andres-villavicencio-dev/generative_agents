Francisco prepared a hearty Coconut-Orange Chicken with rice and steamed vegetables. He used boneless chicken thighs, a cup of coconut milk, orange juice, garlic, ginger, chili flakes, and a pinch of turmeric. He marinated the chicken in the morning, browned it, and cooked it with the sauce until tender. The rice was cooked with vegetable broth until fluffy, while the spinach and carrots absorbed the rich, spicy juices.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with fragrant garlic, toasted ginger, citrus zest, and a hint of toasted coconut. He served the dish in a shallow white bowl, arranged the chicken in the center, and placed the rice and vegetables around it. A small bowl of green salsa and a side of plain bread completed the presentation.

He made this meal for a quiet Tuesday lunch in the co-living kitchen after shopping for ingredients and utensils. It was a relaxed, nourishing lunch shared with his roommates, while he kept his web series and improvisational comedy classes in mind.
