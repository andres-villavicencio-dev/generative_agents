Meal: Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa

Francisco prepared a simple, colorful lunch for himself and his friend Marisol. He used a whole chicken, romaine lettuce, cherry tomatoes, red bell peppers, onions, garlic, lemon, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a little cumin. The chicken was seasoned with lemon, herbs, and spices, then placed skin-side down on a grill until it was golden and cooked through. The vegetables were roasted until crisp and the quinoa was cooked with a little water and oil.

As the oven heated up, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized chicken and toasted garlic. The bright green lettuce and crisp vegetables added a cheerful, fresh contrast. He arranged the ingredients on a white plate, garnishing it with a lemon wedge and a few parsley leaves.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday lunch after helping Marisol with her job application and while balancing work and his web series. It was a casual, home-cooked meal designed to be easy and satisfying, giving him the quiet time he needed while keeping the café work and comedy classes in mind.
