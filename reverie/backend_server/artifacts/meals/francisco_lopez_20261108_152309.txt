Francisco prepared Hobbs Cafe’s Breakfast Club Special—a hearty pan-fried egg sandwich with toast, avocado, and tomato. The key ingredients were whole-grain toast, a sunny-side-up egg, ripe avocado, diced tomato, mayonnaise, and freshly cracked black pepper.

To make it, he toasted the bread until golden, sliced the avocado in half, and sliced the tomato. He cooked the egg in a hot skillet until the whites were firm and the yolk remained runny. He added the tomato and avocado slices to the pan, seasoned the egg, and placed the finished sandwich on the toasted bread.

The aroma was warm and savory, with toasted bread, egg, and the bright, fresh scent of avocado. He arranged the sandwich on a white plate, adding a small side of fruit and a glass of orange juice for the occasion. It was a simple, cheerful breakfast prepared for a Monday morning at Hobbs Cafe, where Francisco hoped his friendly, honest approach would make everyone feel welcome.
