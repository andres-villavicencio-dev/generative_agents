For dinner on Monday, February 13, Francisco prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice. He sliced chicken breast, carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, and onions, then pan-seared the chicken until golden before tossing everything in garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and a splash of coconut milk. The warm chicken and crisp vegetables were combined with fluffy white rice, giving the meal a bright, savory aroma of garlic, ginger, and roasted vegetables.

He plated the dish in a shallow bowl, arranging the rice around the center and scattering colorful vegetables and slices of chicken over it. Using a wooden spatula, he tossed the ingredients gently, letting the sauce coat each piece without making it soggy. The meal was simple and hearty, fitting for a busy Monday when Francisco was balancing his work as an actor, comedian, and web series creator. It was the perfect comfort dinner before he went to bed at 11 p.m.
