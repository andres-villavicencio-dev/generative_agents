On Tuesday, February 14, Francisco prepared Bacon, Egg, and Sourdough Breakfast Sandwiches for his breakfast shift at Hobbs Cafe. The dish consists of a toasted sourdough bread topped with crispy bacon, a poached or fried egg, melted cheese, and a generous spread of creamy avocado or yogurt.

Francisco sliced the sourdough, heated the bacon until golden, poached the egg, and layered everything on the bread. He added fresh herbs and a pinch of flaky sea salt before toasting the sandwich. He prepared several at once for the line, ensuring each one had an even, crunchy crust.

The aroma filled the cafe: savory bacon, toasted nuts, the earthy tang of sourdough, and the rich scent of egg and cheese. The presentation was bright and inviting—each sandwich stacked neatly on a white plate, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a tiny lemon wedge.

The occasion was a busy Tuesday breakfast shift at Hobbs Cafe, where Francisco was helping prepare a full order while balancing his work with his time in improv comedy classes and his ongoing web series.
