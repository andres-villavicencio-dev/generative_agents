Francisco prepared Breakfast with Avocado Toast and Poached Eggs, a popular brunch item at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured whole-grain bread, sliced avocado, chives, a poached egg, a dollop of lemon-herb yogurt, and a sprinkle of paprika.

To prepare it, Francisco toasted the bread, sliced the avocado, and chopped the chives. He poached the eggs in simmered water and seasoned them with salt, pepper, and a little soy sauce. He then layered the toast with the avocado, egg, and toppings before serving it in a small, rustic ceramic bowl.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted bread, creamy avocado, and the soft scent of simmered egg. Francisco plated it with a bright garnish of chives, paprika, and a squeeze of lemon, giving it a cheerful, inviting appearance.

The meal was made for a quiet breakfast at Hobbs Cafe on Tuesday, February 14, when Francisco enjoyed a welcome break with his friends. It was a simple, hearty, and friendly meal that reflected his outgoing and friendly nature.
