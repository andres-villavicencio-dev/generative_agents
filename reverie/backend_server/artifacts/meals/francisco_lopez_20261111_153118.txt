Francisco prepared a hearty Mole Negro with Chicken and Vegetables, a colorful and flavorful Mexican-inspired dinner that reflects his love for bold, creative cooking. The dish features chicken thighs, tomatoes, onions, garlic, chili peppers, cumin, coriander, and a complex blend of chocolate, sesame seeds, and roasted nuts.

Francisco began by sautéing the chicken until it was browned, then added the tomatoes and spices to create a rich, aromatic sauce. He stirred in the chocolate and nuts and simmered everything until the chicken was tender and the sauce thickened. The warm aroma of roasted chili, toasted nuts, and rich spices filled the kitchen as he worked.

For the presentation, he arranged the chicken and vegetables in a simple white bowl, garnished with fresh cilantro and a bright orange slice of tomato. He served it with a small portion of warm white rice and a side of crunchy tortilla chips.

The meal was prepared for an evening family dinner. It was comforting, celebratory, and a chance for the group to enjoy food together while reflecting Francisco’s outgoing, friendly, and honest nature. He had just finished cooking when he heard a knock at the door, revealing that his family was arriving for dinner.
