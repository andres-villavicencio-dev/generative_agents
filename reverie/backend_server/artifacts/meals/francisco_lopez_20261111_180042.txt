Francisco prepared a simple but heartfelt Valentine’s Day Café Sandwich for Isabella and Ingrid. He layered soft toasted sourdough bread with caramelized onions, ripe tomatoes, crisp lettuce, a creamy avocado spread, a drizzle of hot honey, and fresh basil. A sprinkle of red paprika and a pinch of sea salt added a subtle savory finish.

The kitchen smelled warm and fragrant: caramelized onions and basil infused the air with a gentle sweetness, while the toasted bread gave it a comforting, toasted aroma. He arranged the sandwich on a white plate and topped it with a bright red heart made of edible glitter, creating a cheerful, inviting presentation.

He called the café ahead of time to confirm availability, then made his way over to find a nearby spot. By the time he arrived, the café was still busy. He placed the sandwich on the counter and shared a quick smile with the group, happy to have the meal in hand for their celebration.
