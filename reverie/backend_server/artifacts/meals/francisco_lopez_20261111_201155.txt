On February 14, Francisco prepared a Fresh Rose & Lemon Breakfast Bowl. For the meal, he gathered eggs, spinach, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and a handful of fresh berries from The Willows Market. He toasted the tomatoes and cucumber, seasoned the eggs with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a pinch of salt, then folded everything together and topped the bowl with the colorful berries.

The aroma was bright and inviting: the sweet scent of fresh fruit, the savory richness of the eggs, and the crisp freshness of the vegetables filled the small kitchen. He arranged the bowl on the counter in warm ceramic, garnishing it with a small sprig of rosemary and a few scattered berries, making it both cheerful and visually appealing.

He prepared the breakfast for himself before heading to work on the web series, intending to eat it before the Valentine’s Day café shift. Although the occasion was personal and informal, his cheerful presentation reflected his friendly, outgoing personality. By the time he finished, the meal felt balanced, satisfying, and ready for his day ahead.
