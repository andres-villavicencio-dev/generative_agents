Francisco prepared a Valentine’s Day Tomato & Herb Pasta with garlic bread. The recipe combined canned tomatoes, olive oil, fresh basil, garlic, red pepper flakes, and Parmesan cheese, while the bread was made with garlic, olive oil, and rosemary.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, and caramelizing tomatoes. Francisco stirred the pasta in a pan, added the sauce, and let the butter soften before tossing everything together. He finished it with fresh basil and a sprinkle of Parmesan, creating a bright, colorful dish with glossy noodles and vibrant red sauce.

For the occasion, he arranged the pasta on a white platter with a small glass of red wine and a sprig of rosemary. The meal was ready for the Valentine’s Day gathering, though Francisco was also enjoying it alone as he continued his creative routine.
