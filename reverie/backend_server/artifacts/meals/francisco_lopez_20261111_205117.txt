Francisco prepared Coconut-Crusted Chicken with Lemon-Herb Sauce for dinner. He marinated chicken thighs in garlic, lemon juice, soy sauce, and fresh herbs before searing them in a pan until the skin was golden and the meat was cooked through. A pan of coconut milk and vegetables was simmered separately, then the chicken was added back in during the last few minutes to keep the sauce glossy.

The aroma was warm and comforting, with notes of toasted garlic, sweet coconut, citrus zest, and savory herbs. He plated the chicken with glazed carrots, peas, and a small pile of fluffy rice, arranging the colorful vegetables around the plate and garnishing it with a little lime. The vibrant colors and generous presentation made it suitable for a relaxed dinner before the Valentine’s Day party.
