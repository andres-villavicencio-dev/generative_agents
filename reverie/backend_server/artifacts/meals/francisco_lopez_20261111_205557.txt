Francisco prepared Crispy Potato and Herb Roast for an intimate Valentine’s Day dinner. The main component is a golden, caramelized potato with crispy edges, seasoned with garlic, rosemary, thyme, onion powder, and a touch of brown sugar. He roasted the potatoes in the oven until they softened, then served them alongside a creamy lemon-herb sauce, sliced cherry tomatoes, and a crisp green salad.

He prepared everything in his kitchen, using olive oil and fresh herbs to give the dish a warm, earthy aroma. The oven’s gentle glow created a fragrant cloud of savory, roasted goodness. When the potatoes finished, they were arranged in a shallow serving dish with the sauce drizzled over the top, cherry tomatoes arranged around the edges, and a small sprig of rosemary placed beside them.

The dish was presented as a festive, romantic side for the Valentine’s Day party, contrasting warmly with the main course while supporting the shared celebration. It balanced his busy work, creative commitments, and social life, reflecting his outgoing, friendly nature and desire to make others feel welcome.
