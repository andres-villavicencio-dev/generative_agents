Francisco prepared a simple Garlic Herbed Chicken Salad with Crispy Chickpeas.

Key ingredients: chicken breast, chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, spinach, lemon, olive oil, garlic, olive oil, and a little lemon juice.

Preparation: Francisco sliced the chicken and chickpeas, then sautéed the chicken until lightly browned. He added the chickpeas, tomatoes, onion, and spinach, seasoning them with olive oil, garlic, and lemon. The salad was assembled in a large bowl, topped with fresh herbs, and served with a handful of chickpeas for extra texture.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with warm, savory garlic and roasted chickpea scent, with the bright lemon adding a refreshing finish.

Presentation: The colorful salad was arranged on a white plate with the chicken pieces carefully placed on top, garnished with parsley and a lemon wedge.

Occasion: It was a quick, grounding lunch before heading out to confirm Giorgio Rossi’s availability and shift details.
