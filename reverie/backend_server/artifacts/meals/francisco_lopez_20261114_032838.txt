Francisco prepared a Mediterranean Quinoa Bowl for his quiet morning reflection. The key ingredients were quinoa, chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, red onion, parsley, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and a drizzle of tahini. Francisco rinsed the quinoa and chickpeas, then cooked them separately. He gently sautéed the vegetables in a pan with olive oil, chopped parsley, lemon zest, and a few cloves of garlic until the aromas filled the kitchen.

The aroma was warm and inviting, a blend of toasted grains, savory chickpeas, fresh herbs, and the bright tang of lemon. He arranged the colorful vegetables, creamy chickpea-tahini mixture, and fluffy quinoa around a small plate, finishing with a few seeds and fresh mint leaves.

It was his first breakfast while reflecting on a new web series about life in a co-living space, where he imagined the shared meals and everyday routines of his creative community. The cheerful, hearty meal served him a moment of calm before he headed to bed at 11pm.
