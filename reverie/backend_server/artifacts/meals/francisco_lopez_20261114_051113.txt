Francisco prepared Burrata and Roasted Red Pepper Salad with Lemon Vinaigrette. The meal was simple yet flavorful: crisp burrata cheese, creamy romesco sauce, roasted red peppers, cucumber, arugula, and a bright lemon vinaigrette.

He started by peeling the red peppers and toasting their skins until they softened. The peppers were then roasted with garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of smoked paprika until tender and slightly charred. The remaining vegetables were sliced and tossed with the burrata, dressed with the lemon vinaigrette, and everything was arranged on a white platter.

The dish had the sharp, warm aroma of roasted peppers and garlic, with a crisp, lemony freshness that made the creamy cheese stand out. A small glass of cold water and a few wedges of lemon were served beside the salad.

He prepared the meal for a casual weekday lunch during his downtime before filming the web series. His outgoing, friendly demeanor carried over into the kitchen, and the relaxed presentation felt ready for a lively conversation.
