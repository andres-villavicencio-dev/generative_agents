Giorgio Rossi prepared a vibrant Saffron Risotto with Roasted Vegetables as his evening meal. Key ingredients include Arborio rice, saffron threads, cream, Parmesan cheese, and an assortment of roasted seasonal vegetables—zucchini, bell peppers, and cherry tomatoes. The dish was simmered slowly to achieve a creamy texture, then gently stirred with fresh basil at the end. A warm, earthy aroma from saffron and caramelized veggies filled his kitchen as he plated it in a wide white bowl beside a small glass jar of extra virgin olive oil. Served at 4pm during his lunch break, the dish symbolized his dedication to balanced, mindful eating. Giorgio consumed it slowly, reflecting on its simplicity—an ode to his analytical love for balanced routines and nuanced flavors.
