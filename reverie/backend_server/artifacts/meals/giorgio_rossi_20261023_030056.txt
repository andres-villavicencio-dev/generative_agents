Dish: “Fractal Parmesan” with Wild Mushroom Risotto  
Key ingredients: Arborio rice, wild mushrooms, parmesan cheese, olive oil, garlic, red pepper flakes, fresh basil, and a touch of lemon juice.  
Preparation: Giorgio sautés the mushrooms in olive oil until their juices release, then adds garlic and red pepper. Arborio rice is cooked gently, followed by the mushrooms, parmesan, and basil. The dish is finished with a splash of lemon and a few crushed garlic cloves. It is served with a small plate of lemon wedges and toasted bread.  
Aroma: Earthy mushrooms, warm basil, savory parmesan, and the faint scent of garlic and toasted crusty bread.  
Presentation: The risotto is arranged in a shallow oval dish, with the golden mushroom cap exposed as the centerpiece. Fresh basil is scattered over the top, and the lemon wedges are placed beside the bread. A small glass of water accompanies it.  
Occasion: A quiet Monday lunch, perhaps a break during Giorgio’s research project. The simple, aromatic meal reflects his analytical nature—each ingredient is carefully chosen to create a balanced composition, while the dish itself represents his broader curiosity about mathematical patterns in nature.
