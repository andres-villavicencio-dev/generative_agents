Giorgio Rossi prepared a Mediterranean Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Caper Dressing, a balanced lunch reflecting his analytical precision and love of nature-inspired food. The chicken was first seared in olive oil until golden, then marinated in a mixture of lemon juice, minced garlic, dried oregano, thyme, rosemary, and a pinch of sea salt for about 30 minutes. It was arranged on a ceramic platter alongside crisp asparagus, halved cherry tomatoes, and glazed carrots, arranged in neat rows and garnished with a sprig of rosemary. A small bowl of capers, lemon wedges, and a drizzle of fresh herbs accompanied the meal.

The kitchen smelled of roasted poultry, earthy herbs, warm citrus, and gently toasted garlic, creating an inviting aroma of Mediterranean comfort. Giorgio plated the food carefully, arranging the vegetables around the chicken with precise spacing, giving the meal a deliberate, orderly appearance. The dish was prepared for Isabella Rodriguez’s Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe, where he would bring snacks and drinks as a thoughtful guest.
