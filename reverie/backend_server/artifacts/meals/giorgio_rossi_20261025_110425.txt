Giorgio Rossi prepared Savory Tuscan Bolognese with Roasted Root Vegetables, a warm, rustic meal made for a quiet Wednesday lunch. He used lean beef with ground pork, tomatoes, onions, garlic, and a blend of savory herbs, along with carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and zucchini. The meat was slowly simmered in a rich tomato-based sauce until the flavors softened, while the root vegetables were roasted until tender and caramelized.

He added fresh basil, a knob of butter, a splash of white wine, and a pinch of black pepper during the final minutes to preserve the dish’s bright acidity. The kitchen filled with a warm, earthy aroma of caramelized onions, roasted tomatoes, garlic, and simmering meat.

The meal was served in a wide, shallow oven dish, with the bolognese layered over the vegetables and garnished with herbs and a few golden pine nuts. Giorgio presented it with a precise, almost sculptural arrangement, reflecting his analytical nature. It was a simple yet purposeful lunch, intended as a reward for his focused research—a satisfying pause before continuing to compare recurring patterns discussed by the gardeners.
