On Wednesday, February 15, Giorgio prepared a hearty Italian-Balanced Lunch combining lentil soup with roasted vegetables and grilled whole-grain pasta. The dish included red lentils, carrots, celery, onions, garlic, olive oil, tomato sauce, fresh basil, Parmesan, and lemon juice, with grilled chicken or tofu and crisp greens to balance the meal.

He simmered the lentils in a rich tomato broth, sautéed the vegetables until golden, and tossed the pasta in a creamy, herb-laced sauce. He served the soup and pasta separately on plates of coarse linen, garnished with parsley and a drizzle of balsamic glaze. The meal was prepared quickly to accommodate his research, classes, and conversations with friends, though Giorgio took time to ensure the proportions were balanced and the flavors remained distinct.

It was likely a quiet Wednesday lunch on the way to the presentation with Francisco Lopez. The meal reflected Giorgio’s analytical approach: careful selection of ingredients, measured preparation, and a thoughtful composition of warm colors and textures. Its aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and earthy lentils made the ordinary feel deliberate and inviting.
