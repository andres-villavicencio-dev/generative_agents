Giorgio prepared Arugula, Goat Cheese, and Herb-Crusted Salmon for a quiet Wednesday lunch. He sliced wild-caught salmon, seasoned it with lemon zest, olive oil, thyme, garlic, and a pinch of sea salt, then carefully placed it on a slate pan. The arugula was massaged with olive oil and a touch of lemon before being layered over the fish. Fresh goat cheese, toasted pita chips, and a squeeze of lemon completed the plate.

The meal had a bright, herbaceous aroma of lemon, herbs, and toasted bread. Giorgio arranged the components on a wide ceramic plate, leaving space for each element to stand out. The presentation was simple yet elegant: the dark arugula, golden fish, and creamy cheese created a contrasting visual, while the pita chips added a warm texture.

He prepared it for an early lunch before continuing his research, classes, and conversations with friends. The occasion was neither hurried nor celebratory, but intimate and purposeful—a quiet moment of nourishment while he balanced his analytical work.
