Giorgio Rossi prepared Arborian Kale and Smoked Chicken Salad, a simple, nutritious lunch for the weekend. He washed his hands first, then chopped fresh kale, baby spinach, and cucumber, and sliced the chicken into strips. He seasoned the dressing with olive oil, lemon juice, sea salt, and a pinch of smoked paprika, tossing everything together before serving it over a bed of crisp greens.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, fresh herbs, and olive oil. It was presented in a wide ceramic bowl, arranged attractively with a small plate of nuts, seeds, and a lemon wedge beside it. The occasion was an unhurried lunch on Wednesday, February 15, when he was balancing research notes, classes, meals, and conversations with friends while considering whether the first proposal should be recommended over the third.
