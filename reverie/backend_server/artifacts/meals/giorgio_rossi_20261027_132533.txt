Giorgio Rossi prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Smoked Goat Cheese and Fresh Thyme, a simple side dish meant to complement his lunch. He began by trimming carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, then tossed them with olive oil, lemon juice, sea salt, and freshly cracked black pepper. The vegetables were roasted in a preheated oven until golden and tender, while the goat cheese was gently melted over the top.

He finished the dish with a handful of thyme sprigs, a squeeze of lemon, and a pinch of smoked paprika, finishing with a dusting of sea salt. The aroma was warm and earthy, with notes of root vegetables, citrus, and mild smokiness.

It was served at lunch on Wednesday, February 15, as part of Giorgio’s casual weekday routine while he continued comparing proposals and preparing to present his findings to Francisco Lopez. The side dish provided a familiar, comforting contrast to his analytical work.
