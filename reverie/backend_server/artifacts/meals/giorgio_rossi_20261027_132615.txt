The meal was a simple Mediterranean Garden Salad with Lemon-Herb Vinaigrette, prepared as a quiet side for lunch. Giorgio gathered fresh kale, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, and crisp lettuce, then tossed them with a generous amount of olive oil, lemon juice, dried oregano, thyme, and a pinch of smoked paprika. He added a handful of toasted pine nuts and sliced almonds, letting the bright green leaves and crisp vegetables contrast with the warm golden nuts.

The salad was dressed in a cool, fragrant vinaigrette and arranged in a shallow white ceramic bowl. Giorgio placed a small bowl of lemon wedges beside it, while a glass of still water sat nearby. The aroma of herbs, citrus, and toasted nuts filled the kitchen, making the meal feel both fresh and thoughtful. It was a practical, unassuming lunch—a simple choice that balanced his research and classes with a moment of stillness, fitting naturally into his Wednesday schedule.
