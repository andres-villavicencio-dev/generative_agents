Giorgio Rossi prepared Arugula, Lemon, Parmesan, and Prosciutto with fresh pasta as a side dish. He began by slicing the tender leaves of arugula and tossing them gently with lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and a tiny amount of honey to preserve their bright peppery flavor. Parmesan rinds were shaved over the bowl, followed by pieces of firm white meat prosciutto. The pasta was cooked until al dente, then tossed briefly with the arugula, lemon zest, and a generous amount of grated Parmesan.

As it prepared, the dish released a fresh, citrusy aroma with sharp herb notes and a savory, salty finish from the meat and cheese. It was served in a shallow white bowl, the pasta arranged in a neat line, and the arugula distributed evenly across the top, creating an elegant, asymmetrical presentation.

The meal was served as a thoughtful lunch during Giorgio’s Wednesday research planning session. It complemented his focus on comparing proposals, funding, community feedback, and inclusive access, allowing him to eat while maintaining a calm, analytical pace. The simple, elegant dish reflected his preference for clean, memorable flavors that could also serve as a metaphor for exploring mathematical patterns.
