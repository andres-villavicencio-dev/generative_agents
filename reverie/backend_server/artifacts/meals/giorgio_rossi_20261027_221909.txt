Giorgio Rossi prepared Veggie-Hearty Vegetable Soup with Crusty Bread, a simple yet nourishing lunch designed to be both satisfying and thought-provoking. The soup was simmered on a small stove with carrots, celery, potatoes, tomatoes, a generous handful of fresh herbs, and a pinch of savory salt, finished with a splash of lemon juice. He added a few whole grains to give it a hearty texture, while finely chopped chives and garlic provided a subtle aromatic finish.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized vegetables, warm herbs, and lightly toasted bread. He served the soup in a shallow white bowl, topping each portion with a slice of crusty sourdough and a small sprig of parsley. The meal was practical and modestly eccentric: Giorgio was balancing research, classes, meals, and conversations with friends, so the soup provided a calm, uninterrupted moment of nourishment and focus.
