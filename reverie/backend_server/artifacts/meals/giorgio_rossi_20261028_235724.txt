The main dish was Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, served simply on a white ceramic plate. Giorgio used a whole chicken, carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and onions, seasoning them with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, rosemary, thyme, salt, and black pepper. He roasted them until the skin was crisp and the vegetables were tender, then plated them alongside the golden chicken.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic and herbs, followed by the savory scent of rendered fat and citrus. The presentation was elegant but unpretentious: the chicken rested on a platter, surrounded by uniformly sized root vegetables, with a small bowl of lemon-dressed salad nearby.

The meal was prepared for Giorgio’s Tuesday lunch, but the occasion felt neither rushed nor celebratory. In the quiet of his home kitchen, it reflected his analytical nature—carefully measured, balanced, and deliberately prepared.
