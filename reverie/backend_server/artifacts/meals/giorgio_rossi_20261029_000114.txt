Dish: Geometric Brie with Sage and Honey Drizzle

Giorgio prepared a small, elegant side dish for lunch. He layered a soft brie with finely chopped fresh sage, a drizzle of honey, and a few golden raisins. To add visual interest, he arranged the ingredients in a shallow dish using a small wooden skewer to create a simple geometric pattern, and topped the arrangement with a few toasted pine nuts.

The preparation was calm and deliberate. Giorgio preheated the oven, sliced the brie, and brushed the honey and herbs over the toppings. He carefully arranged the ingredients, letting the honey and raisins release their sweetness before broiling the dish until the brie warmed and the pine nuts became crisp.

The aroma was warm and earthy, with notes of nutty brown sugar, sage, honey, and toasted grain. The presentation was modest but inviting, the golden raisins and pine nuts catching the light. It served as a quiet pause before Giorgio returned to his research, offering him a moment of focus amid the complexity of mathematical patterns and the local election.
