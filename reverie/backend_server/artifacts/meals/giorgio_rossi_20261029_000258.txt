Giorgio Rossi prepared Crispy Parmesan Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and a Lemon-Garlic Reduction. The simple lunch was prepared for a quiet Tuesday: the kitchen table became the preparation area, with a clean white plate, a wooden serving platter, a metal cutting board, a small bowl of olive oil, and a glass of water neatly arranged around the main dish.

The chicken was seasoned with salt, black pepper, and a pinch of red pepper flakes, then roasted at 200°C until the skin turned golden and crisp. Around it, carrots, parsnips, and a small onion were roasted in olive oil until tender, with thyme and a splash of lemon juice added at the end. A bright yellow sauce of lemon, garlic, and olive oil was poured over the vegetables and chicken, creating a glossy finish.

The meal smelled warm and savory—rich poultry, caramelized roots, and toasted herbs combined with a sharp lemon aroma. It was served at home for a casual Tuesday lunch, not the election campaign dinner that had been announced. Giorgo presented the platter with precise, deliberate movements, letting the contrasting textures of crispy chicken and soft vegetables emphasize the analytical logic he was applying to his research.
