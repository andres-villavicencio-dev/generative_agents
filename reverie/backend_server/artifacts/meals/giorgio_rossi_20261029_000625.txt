The meal was a Lemon-herb Roast Chicken with Saffron Gravy, prepared in a simple home kitchen. Giorgio chose a whole chicken and used a small pot to slowly simmer it with lemon, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a few sprigs of sage. He added a teaspoon of saffron to the water and let the chicken braise for about two hours, allowing the golden broth to reduce and thicken.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted poultry, citrus, herbs, and saffron. When the chicken was nearly done, Giorgio sliced it against the grain, served the rested gravy, and arranged lemon slices, a few fresh herbs, and the golden chicken on a white plate. The presentation was elegant yet understated: the glossy gravy, crisp lemon, and bright green herbs made the dish stand out while retaining a comforting, homely feel.

It was prepared for a quiet Tuesday lunch after a full day of mathematics, research, and an election discussion. Giorgio treated the meal as a small pause in his analytical routine—a familiar, grounding lunch to balance his intellectual energy with the ordinary rhythms of home life.
