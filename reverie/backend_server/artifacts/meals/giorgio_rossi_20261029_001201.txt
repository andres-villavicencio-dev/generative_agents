The meal was a simple Roasted Garlic Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, prepared to match the analytical, unhurried rhythm of Giorgio’s Tuesday morning.

Key ingredients: chicken breasts, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, fresh thyme, rosemary, carrots, parsnips, and a splash of chicken broth.

Preparation method: Giorgio sliced the chicken and roasted it at 200°C until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. He then roasted the root vegetables, garlic, and a few sprigs of herbs in the same pan, adding the chicken later and spooning the juices over it. The lemon was added at the end to preserve its bright acidity.

The kitchen filled with the scent of roasted garlic, lemon zest, thyme, and wood-smoke from the oven.

It was served simply on a white platter, with the golden chicken arranged beside the browned vegetables and a small side of roasted potatoes and crusty bread. Giorgio had finished checking that the chicken was tender and the vegetables were cooked through, ready to eat at home.
