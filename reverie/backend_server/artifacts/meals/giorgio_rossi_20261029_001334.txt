Gorgonzola and Arugula with Roasted Tomato and Garlic Pesto

Giorgio Rossi prepared a simple, elegant lunch for himself on Tuesday, February 14. The dish featured crisp arugula, creamy Gorgonzola cheese, roasted tomatoes, garlic, and a bright basil pesto. He sliced the tomatoes, warmed them gently, and roasted them until the skins softened. He finely chopped the arugula, stirred in the cheese, and drizzled the warm pesto over the top. The golden tomatoes and creamy sauce were arranged alongside the peppery greens in a shallow white plate.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of Gorgonzola and garlic, along with the crisp scent of basil and tomato. The presentation was minimalist and appetizing: the contrasting colors of red, green, white, and cream made the dish look inviting. Giorgio served a generous portion of the main dish, leaving the extra salad aside. It was a quiet, purposeful lunch—calm and unpretentious, fitting his analytical nature, though his eccentricity added a hint of whimsy to the familiar routine.
