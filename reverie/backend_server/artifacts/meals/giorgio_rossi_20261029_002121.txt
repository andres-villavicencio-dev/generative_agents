Meal: “Neon Parmigiana” with Crispy Provolone and Roasted Garlic

Giorgio Rossi prepared the meal for a quiet Tuesday lunch, treating it as a brief pause before his research and election discussions. He used a large stainless-steel pan, olive oil, garlic, onions, carrots, bell peppers, tomatoes, fresh basil, and grated Parmigiano Reggiano, with the local mayor’s election serving as the occasion.

The garlic was sautéed until fragrant and slightly softened, then the vegetables were stirred in and cooked until tender. After draining a can of tomatoes and adding a splash of white wine, the mixture simmered until the sauce thickened. The carrots and peppers were softened, and a generous amount of freshly grated parmesan was stirred in. The finished dish was served in a wide, shallow metal bowl, arranged with the golden-brown vegetables and creamy cheese around a bed of crisp, golden provolone.

The aroma filled the kitchen: warm garlic, caramelized onion, and savory parmesan, with the faint scent of basil completing the profile. The presentation was clean and deliberately eccentric—bright orange and green vegetables balanced against the pale cheese, with a small basil leaf resting on the serving dish. Giorgio ate it slowly, savoring the simple, satisfying meal while keeping his analytical mind prepared for the day ahead.
