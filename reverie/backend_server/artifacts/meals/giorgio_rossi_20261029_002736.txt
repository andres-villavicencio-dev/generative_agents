Giorgio Rossi prepared Lettuce, Radicchio, and Roasted Tomato Salad with Crispy Sun-Dried Tomatoes, a simple but elegant lunch for his busy Tuesday. The salad featured crisp lettuce leaves, tender radicchio, a creamy goat cheese dressing, and sun-dried tomatoes toasted until golden and slightly caramelized.

He washed the counter and cooking area thoroughly before gathering the ingredients. He sliced the vegetables, toasted the tomatoes, and tossed everything in the dressing, allowing the flavors to mingle gently. The kitchen filled with the crisp scent of basil, the savory aroma of goat cheese, and the warm fragrance of caramelized tomatoes.

He plated the salad in a clean white bowl, garnishing it with a few fresh rosemary sprigs and drizzling extra olive oil over the top. The presentation was minimalist yet appetizing, with the earthy green tones of the vegetables complementing the warm red of the tomatoes. It was a quiet, thoughtful meal to ease the transition from his full but purposeful day—research, classes, and discussions with the mayor’s campaign team—back to home.
