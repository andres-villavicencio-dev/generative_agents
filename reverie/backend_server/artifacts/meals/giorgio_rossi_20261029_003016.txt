The meal was a simple, rustic Garden-Herb Quinoa Salad with Roasted Lemon Garlic Chicken, prepared as Giorgio’s personal lunch for a quiet Tuesday.

Key ingredients included quinoa, chickpeas, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, parsley, thyme, lemon, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Giorgio roasted the chicken in a skillet with lemon, garlic, and herbs, then tossed it with the remaining vegetables and prepared the quinoa by rinsing it and cooking it with vegetable broth. The salad was assembled just before serving.

The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, thyme, citrus, and the earthy aroma of warm grains. Giorgio arranged the colorful vegetables around the golden chicken, garnished with a final lemon wedge and chopped parsley, and served it in a simple white bowl.

The occasion was informal and solitary: Giorgio was preparing lunch while cleaning up, organizing the remaining ingredients, and tidying his workspace. The meal reflected his analytical, eccentric nature—carefully measured, thoughtfully balanced, and presented with the same precision he applied to his mathematical research.
