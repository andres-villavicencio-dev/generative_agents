Dish: Boiled Water Vegetable and Mushroom Soup

Giorgio prepares a simple, rustic broth with carrots, celery, parsnips, onions, mushrooms, and a pinch of black pepper. He adds a splash of olive oil and a small knob of butter, letting the water gently simmer until the vegetables soften. He serves it in a large, shallow bowl, arranging the vegetables and sliced mushrooms in a loose, colorful mound beside a small bowl of lemon-olive oil dressing.

The aroma rises as the kitchen fills with the earthy, savory scent of simmering carrots and earthy mushroom broth. The warm, translucent broth contrasts with the crisp vegetables and golden brown mushrooms, while the bright yellow carrots and green celery add a gentle visual rhythm. Giorgio presents the meal with a calm, almost theatrical flourish: the bowl is filled to the brim, and he adds a final spoonful of olive oil before serving.

The occasion is a quiet weekday lunch, perhaps interrupted by a discussion about the local mayor’s upcoming election. The simple, methodical preparation reflects his analytical nature—he treats the meal as a small experiment in balance, while his eccentric curiosity keeps the presentation lively.
