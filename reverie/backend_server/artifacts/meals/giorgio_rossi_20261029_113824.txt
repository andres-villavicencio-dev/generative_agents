The meal was a Gorgonzola and Parmesan Risotto with Truffle Arugula, prepared during a quiet Thursday evening.

Giorgio toasted the arugula in a pan, letting it release its peppery green aroma before adding the white wine. He slowly added the Arborio rice, stirring constantly until the grains became translucent and creamy. After tasting and adjusting the wine and seasoning, he added warm parmesan cheese and a splash of truffle oil, letting the aroma rise from the pan. A dollop of creamy Gorgonzola and a handful of fresh parmesan were added just before the rice absorbed the last liquid.

The finished dish had a rich, earthy aroma of roasted herbs and aged cheese. Giorgio served it in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with a small sprig of arugula and a few edible goldfish. It was prepared for an informal dinner with local friends and colleagues—the mayor’s upcoming election discussion, perhaps—and felt both intimate and purposeful.
