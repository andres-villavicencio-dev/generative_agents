Dish Name: Honey-Cured Egg Tortilla with Roasted Vegetables and Crusty Bread

Giorgio prepared the tortilla by cooking fresh eggs with a small amount of milk, then wrapping the soft tortilla with a thin layer of honey-cured egg batter and lightly baking it. The filling included roasted root vegetables, caramelized onions, and a sprinkling of chopped herbs. A whole-wheat bread, sliced with a crusty edge, was toasted on the stove. A glass of water was prepared to accompany the meal.

The aroma of the kitchen filled the room with the rich scent of honey, roasted vegetables, toasted wheat, and warm herbs. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the golden tortilla sat beside the bright vegetables, while the warm, crusty bread offered a sturdy companion.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Friday evening cookout. Giorgos carefully confirmed the quantities with Mei and Amilcar, then brought the food to the table to share with Adam Smith and the local gathering.
