Giorgio Rossi prepared Honey-Cured Egg Tortilla with Roasted Vegetables and Crusty Bread for Friday’s cookout. The dish used eggs, honey, whole-wheat bread, apples, bananas, and mild clover honey. He softened the eggs with a little water, wrapped them in the honey, and rolled them into tortillas. He roasted the vegetables, including carrots, potatoes, and peppers, then arranged them alongside the warm tortillas and rustic crusty bread.

The kitchen smelled of caramelized onions, garlic, honey, and roasted vegetables. The presentation was simple but inviting: golden tortillas with slightly crisp vegetables, dark honey, and a few apple and banana slices on a large plate, with the bread set aside for dipping.

The cookout was a casual gathering of friends and neighbors, with Mei and Amilcar confirming the quantities beforehand. Giorgio’s analytical and eccentric nature made his cooking process thoughtful—layering flavors, comparing prices with Victor, and ensuring every ingredient contributed to the meal.
