Giorgio Rossi prepared Lettuce-Crusted Goat Cheese with Lemon-Herb Oil and Roasted Red Pepper, a rustic Italian-inspired lunch meant to accompany a quiet evening of dinner. The dish featured fresh garden lettuce, creamy goat cheese, thinly sliced red pepper, lemon zest, herb oil, and a few garlic cloves. Giorgio first thinly sliced the red pepper and garlic, then tossed them with a little olive oil and salt before roasting them until tender and fragrant. He arranged the lettuce in a shallow bowl, topped it with the warm red pepper and cheese, and drizzled the lemon-oil over the top.

The aroma was earthy, savory, and subtly citrusy, with the roasted pepper adding a warm kick. Giorgio presented the meal in a simple white ceramic bowl, using a small spoon to serve a generous portion. It was a relaxed, informal lunch, perhaps prepared just before he would walk to the Willows Market and return home early enough to prepare dinner around 4:00 p.m.
