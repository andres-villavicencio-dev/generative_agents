The meal Giorgio Rossi prepared was a Caramelized Mushroom Risotto with Prosciutto and Truffle Oil, prepared in his kitchen on February 13 while he was still on the café shift.

Garnished with freshly grated parmesan, red pepper flakes, and a drizzle of truffle oil, the dish featured creamy arborio rice, sliced mushrooms, prosciutto, and a rich sauce. The key ingredients were Arborio rice, olive oil, garlic, white wine, vegetable broth, parmesan, mushrooms, prosciutto, and a small amount of truffle oil.

The preparation method began by sautéing the mushrooms in olive oil until golden, then adding garlic and white wine. The rice was toasted in the pan, followed by slowly stirring the broth to create a creamy texture. Cheese and Parmesan were stirred in toward the end, while the remaining truffle oil added a subtle aroma of earthy richness and warm spice.

It was presented on a shallow, dark ceramic plate and served with a side of roasted root vegetables and crusty bread. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday lunch during the café shift, designed to reward his analytical mindset with a nourishing dish that balanced comfort with thoughtful presentation.
