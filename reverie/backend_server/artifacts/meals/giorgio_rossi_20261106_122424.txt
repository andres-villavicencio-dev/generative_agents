Giorgio Rossi prepared Braised Chicken with Root Vegetables and Crispy Potato Wedges, a hearty dinner suited for a Tuesday night gathering. The key ingredients were chicken thighs, carrots, parsnips, red onions, celery, garlic, tomato sauce, paprika, thyme, and a splash of soy sauce.

He seared the chicken in a skillet until the skin turned golden, then transferred it to a pan with the vegetables, tomatoes, and aromatics. The sauce reduced slowly, allowing the chicken to braise and the vegetables to soften. The pan was covered briefly to keep the flavors balanced, then the potatoes were added and covered until tender. A final sprinkle of paprika and a squeeze of lemon finished the dish.

The aroma was savory, slightly sweet, and deeply aromatic, with hints of garlic, smoked chicken, and roasted root vegetables. The presentation was warm and colorful: the golden chicken rested atop a bright orange-red vegetable base, garnished with fresh herbs and a lemon wedge. The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday dinner, likely shared with friends and family before the evening café shift resumed.
