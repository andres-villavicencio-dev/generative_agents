Giorgio Rossi prepared a Mediterranean Quinoa Salad with Roasted Vegetables for lunch. The meal consisted of cooked quinoa, red onion, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, feta cheese, olives, and fresh parsley, all tossed with lemon-olive oil dressing. He added roasted zucchini, red bell pepper, and carrots, then arranged them on a white ceramic platter alongside a warm grilled chicken breast and a small bowl of lemon-herb yogurt.

The preparation took about an hour. Giorgio began by roasting the vegetables in the oven until the skins turned crisp and slightly charred. He then sautéed the red onion and prepared the quinoa and vegetables with the dressing. He sliced feta and olives, then tossed everything together just before serving. The dish was served at room temperature on a wooden board, with the chicken and yogurt arranged nearby.

The aroma of garlic, lemon, roasted vegetables, and Mediterranean herbs filled the kitchen. Giorgio’s analytical eye had helped him notice the balanced colors and textures of the ingredients, choosing each one deliberately to create both visual harmony and a satisfying contrast. He was preparing lunch while watching for candidates on the local mayoral ballot, though the meal itself was meant to be a simple, thoughtful pause between work and the evening.
