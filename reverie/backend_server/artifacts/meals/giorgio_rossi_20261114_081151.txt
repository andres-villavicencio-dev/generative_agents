The meal was an elegant Linguist’s Pesto with Roasted Tomato and Sage, prepared during dinner on Monday, February 13. Giorgio began by heating the base ingredients—basil, olive oil, garlic, red onion, and Parmesan cheese—on a large pan over medium heat, stirring until the oil melted and the garlic released its aroma. He then added the tomatoes and roasted them with sage, letting them simmer until the sauce thickened into a glossy, aromatic glaze.

The dish was presented on a wide, dark ceramic platter, with the finished tomato and sage sauce served alongside crisp roasted potatoes, whole mushrooms, and a small bowl of lemon-lime dressing. The warm, savory scent of toasted basil and garlic mingled with the bright orange hue of the tomatoes, creating an inviting contrast between earthy and fresh flavors.

Giorgio chose the occasion to celebrate the completion of his research project on mathematical patterns in nature, while also reflecting on the local mayoral election. The meal served as a quiet, intellectual pause after a long day of work, analysis, and civic discussion.
