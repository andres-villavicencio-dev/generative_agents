Hailey Johnson prepared a Mediterranean-inspired lemon chicken and quinoa bowl for lunch. The key ingredients were boneless chicken thighs, quinoa, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, baby spinach, feta cheese, fresh parsley, mint, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, and oregano.

She began by searing the seasoned chicken in a hot skillet until golden and cooked through, then set it aside to rest. While it cooled, she cooked the quinoa and allowed it to steam, fluffing it with a fork. She prepared a quick dressing by whisking lemon juice, olive oil, minced garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. The vegetables were chopped and tossed with the dressing, then layered over the quinoa alongside the sliced chicken, crumbled feta, and fresh herbs.

The finished bowl was colorful and casually arranged in a wide ceramic dish, with the bright red tomatoes and green herbs contrasting against the pale quinoa and browned chicken. Its aroma was fresh, savory, and citrusy, with warm oregano and garlic notes. It was an inviting weekday lunch inspired by Hailey’s love of Mediterranean culture and literature, made while she paused from writing her co-living artists novel.
