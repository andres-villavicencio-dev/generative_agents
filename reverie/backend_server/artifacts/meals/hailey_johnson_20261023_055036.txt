Dish Name: Herb-Crusted Lemon Garlic Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables

Key Ingredients: boneless chicken breasts, garlic, olive oil, fresh herbs (thyme, rosemary, parsley), lemon, red onion, carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes.

Preparation Method: Hailey first seared the chicken in olive oil until golden, then seasoned it with garlic and herbs. She roasted the root vegetables and potatoes in the same pan until tender, then sliced and plated the chicken alongside them. A lemon wedge was squeezed over the finished dish, and the leftovers were stored in the refrigerator for a later snack.

Aroma: Warm garlic, roasted potatoes, savory chicken, and the subtle hint of lemon and herbs filled the kitchen.

Presentation: The meal was served in a simple white bowl. The chicken was arranged beside colorful, caramelized vegetables, with a lemon slice and parsley garnish adding a bright pop of color.

Occasion: Hailey prepared the dinner alone on Monday, February 13, while she worked on her novel and podcast. It was a quiet, nourishing dinner to begin her creative routine in the new co-living space.
