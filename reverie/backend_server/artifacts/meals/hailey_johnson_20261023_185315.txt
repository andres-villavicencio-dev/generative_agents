Hailey Johnson prepared Garden-Rooted Pot Pie for dinner, a warm, rustic meal prepared for her Friday evening. The dish included roasted root vegetables such as carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes, along with garlic, onions, thyme, and a creamy béchamel sauce. She sautéed the aromatics in butter, then layered them with the cooked vegetables and shredded cheese before baking until golden and bubbling.

The aroma of the meal filled the kitchen—a comforting blend of roasted potatoes, garlic, herbs, and warm cream. Hailey arranged the pot pie on a white platter, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice. The presentation was inviting and balanced, with the golden crust contrasting the bright colors of the vegetables beneath.

Dinner was served around 6 p.m., after Hailey’s final review of the writing workspace. The meal felt like a small ritual—a place to pause, nourish herself, and mentally prepare for the next chapter of her novel, Echoes of the Co-living Artists, and her upcoming podcast.
