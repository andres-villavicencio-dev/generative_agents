Hailey Johnson prepared a warm Oatmeal Breakfast Bowl with Berries, Chia Seeds, and Lemon-Orange Yogurt.

The bowl featured rolled oats, a handful of fresh strawberries, blueberries, and sliced apricots, topped with a drizzle of lemon-orange yogurt and a scattering of chia seeds. A small pan of brown sugar was also included, adding a subtle caramel note.

She prepared it by cooking the oats in a little water until soft, letting them cool, and then folding in the fruit and yogurt. The chia seeds were gently warmed into the oats just before serving, while the brown sugar was stirred in at the end to prevent it from burning.

The dish had a warm, comforting aroma of oats, dried fruit, citrus, and the faint sweetness of brown sugar. It was presented in a simple white bowl with colorful berries arranged around the edges and a tiny spoon resting beside it for serving.

The meal served Hailey a quiet Tuesday after a full-to-the-brain week. It was a simple, nourishing breakfast, but she prepared it with care as a creative pause—perhaps for her podcast or a moment of reflection before writing the next chapter of her novel.
