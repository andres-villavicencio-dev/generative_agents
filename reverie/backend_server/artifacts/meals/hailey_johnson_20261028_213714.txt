Hailey prepared Golden Sage Risotto, a warm Italian-inspired meal that reflects her imaginative and resourceful nature. The dish featured Arborio rice, chicken broth, vegetable broth, parmesan cheese, thyme, garlic, fresh parsley, and a handful of sun-dried tomatoes. Hailey gathered the ingredients the night before, measured everything carefully, and checked that the rice was properly hydrated before cooking.

She began by sautéing onions and garlic until fragrant, then added the broth and Parmesan to simmer until the rice absorbed the liquid and became creamy. Her energetic touch came when she stirred in the sun-dried tomatoes and topped the finished dish with a pinch of red pepper flakes and a drizzle of olive oil. The aroma filled the kitchen with savory, toasted garlic, earthy herbs, and caramelized cheese.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the risotto served in a shallow white bowl, garnished with parsley and a bright lemon wedge. It was a relaxed, comforting dinner for Hailey, who was winding down from writing her novel and preparing to start her new podcast. The meal was both nourishing and celebratory—a quiet treat for a busy creative evening.
