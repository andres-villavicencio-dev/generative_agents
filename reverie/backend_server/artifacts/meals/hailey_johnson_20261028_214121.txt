Hailey Johnson prepared Garlic Herb Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce for dinner. The main ingredients were a whole chicken, carrots, potatoes, and bell peppers, along with garlic, fresh rosemary, thyme, lemon, olive oil, and a touch of smoked paprika.

She started by preheating the oven to 425°F (218°C). After rubbing the chicken with olive oil, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper, she roasted it alongside the vegetables until the skin was golden and the meat was cooked through. The sauce was made by sautéing garlic and lemon zest in the pan, then simmering it with the vegetables and finishing with a splash of broth.

The meal was served in a rustic wooden bowl, with the roasted chicken arranged over a bed of warm vegetables and topped with a glossy lemon-herb sauce. The aroma of roasted garlic and herbs filled the kitchen, while the golden chicken and vegetables created a comforting, aromatic tableau. It was a relaxed, family-style dinner for a quiet evening before Hailey’s 10:00 AM awakening.
