Hailey prepared Starterdinner with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Crust. She marinated root vegetables with garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, and fresh thyme, then roasted them until the skins softened and the flesh turned golden. A simple herb crust was baked over the vegetables before being sliced and placed on top.

The kitchen filled with the warm, earthy aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and citrus. Hailey seasoned the vegetables evenly, turned the heat up slightly, and basted them with the pan juices. Each vegetable had caramelized and absorbed the savory glaze, giving the dish a bright, aromatic finish.

She plated the meal with a small bowl of lemon-herb yogurt and a crisp green salad, arranging the colorful vegetables and golden crust in an inviting, rustic presentation. Hailey served it to herself after a quiet moment of preparation. This was a relaxed Monday evening—a simple but satisfying dinner that reminded her of her imaginative, resourceful side, while she enjoyed the comforting aroma in her own kitchen.
