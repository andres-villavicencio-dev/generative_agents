Hailey Johnson prepared Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze, a hearty dinner inspired by the warmth of a cozy co-living space.

The dish consists of tender roasted beef, carrots, potatoes, onions, and kale, all tossed with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a bright lemon-herb glaze. She sautéed the vegetables first to release their flavors, then roasted them slowly until the meat was golden and the potatoes were tender.

The aroma filling the kitchen was rich, savory, and fragrant—earthy notes of rosemary and onion mingling with the savory warmth of the beef and the bright zest of lemon. She plated the meal on a wide white ceramic platter, arranging the golden roast in the center and scattering the colorful vegetables around it. A small bowl of lemon-herb crusty bread and fresh herbs complemented the dish, making it feel both comforting and celebratory.

The meal was prepared for a special Sunday dinner, and Hailey’s imaginative, energetic nature added a personal touch as she finished plating it.
