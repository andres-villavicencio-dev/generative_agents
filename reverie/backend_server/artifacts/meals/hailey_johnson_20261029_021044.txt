Hailey prepared Sunday’s Fresh Croissant Dinner, a warm, layered breakfast-and-lunch combo. The main dish was a crispy croissant topped with toasted nuts, dried apricots, blueberries, and a drizzle of honey-lemon glaze, while the remaining ingredients were arranged on the table for sharing.

Hailey used flour, butter, eggs, milk, sugar, cinnamon, vanilla, and lemon zest for the croissants. After rolling out the dough, she sliced it into rectangles and filled them with the colorful fruit and nut mix. The croissants were then placed seam-side down in a baking sheet and baked until golden and flaky. She toasted the nuts, chopped them, and brushed the glaze over the finished dish before arranging it in a warm serving dish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with buttery pastry, sweet fruit, cinnamon, and lemon. The presentation was colorful and rustic, with the golden croissants arranged in a neat row and a small bowl of honey-lemon dressing nearby. The meal was prepared for a cozy Tuesday evening dinner with friends and Sharon—a relaxed gathering of croissants, fruit, and conversation.
