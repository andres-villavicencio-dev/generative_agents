Hailey prepared a Garnished Croissant Breakfast with Fresh Garden Salad, a warm, colorful brunch inspired by the croissants she cleaned and washed. The dish combined buttery, flaky croissants with crisp spinach, cherry tomatoes, arugula, cucumber, and a lemon-herb vinaigrette, topped with feta cheese, toasted walnuts, and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt.

She softened the croissants in the oven, then arranged them on a white ceramic platter alongside the salad. A small glass of sparkling water and a bright orange slice added a cheerful pop of color. The aroma was rich and inviting, with the buttery scent of the croissants mingling with the fresh, crisp herbs and citrus.

The presentation was elegant and balanced: the golden-brown croissants stood alongside the vivid greens, with the creamy feta and crunchy nuts adding texture. It was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday brunch after Hailey celebrated her successful meal-making with friends and Sharon over croissants and cooking experiments.
