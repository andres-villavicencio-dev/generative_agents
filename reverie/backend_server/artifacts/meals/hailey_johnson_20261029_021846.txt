Hailey Johnson prepared a rustic croissant and fresh fruit salad for a cozy Tuesday evening dinner. The dish featured golden-brown croissants made with butter, flour, water, and salt, layered with strawberries, blueberries, and a few honey-roasted almonds. She brushed the tops with a little melted butter and sprinkled them before baking them at 375°F for about 12 minutes until the edges turned crisp and puffy.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of butter, caramelized sugar, and sweet fruit, followed by the bright zest of strawberries. Hailey plated the golden croissants alongside a colorful fruit salad, arranging them on a white plate and drizzling a little honey over them. The meal was simple, fresh, and visually inviting, perfect for a quiet Tuesday night with friends and Sharon.
