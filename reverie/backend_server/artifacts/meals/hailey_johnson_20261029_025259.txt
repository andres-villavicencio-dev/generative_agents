Hailey prepared Braised Vegetable Soup with Crispy Shallots and Crumble, a warm, nourishing meal inspired by the cozy co-living space and the friends she shared meals with.

The soup was made by sautéing finely diced shallots and garlic in olive oil until translucent, then slowly adding a vegetable broth, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, and a handful of kale. After simmering for twenty minutes, she finished it with a splash of lemon juice and a pinch of dried herbs. For the crumble topping, she layered crumbled vegan cheese, toasted breadcrumbs, and a little flour, then baked it until golden and slightly crisp.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm, earthy, and comforting—mushy vegetables, savory shallots, lemon, and a hint of toasted bread. She plated it in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the golden crumble around the edges and drizzling a generous spoonful of the glossy soup over the top. The meal was prepared to celebrate Tuesdays, February 14, the day she planned to start her new podcast about artists living in a shared co-living space.
