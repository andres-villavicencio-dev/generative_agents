Hailey Johnson prepared a warm Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken with Grilled Asparagus and Lemon Garlic Bread, serving it for lunch on a crisp Wednesday afternoon.

The chicken was seasoned with olive oil, rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, and garlic, then placed in a roasting pan and roasted until golden and tender. Grilled asparagus added a bright, herbaceous contrast, while the lemon and garlic infused every part of the dish with a fragrant aroma. The bread was grilled on the same pan, creating a fluffy, buttery crust.

It was presented on a white platter with a small plate of fresh fruit and a glass of sparkling water, reflecting a simple but cheerful lunch for Hailey’s Wednesday. The meal was neither celebratory nor particularly formal—it was a relaxed, nourishing break between her writing, errands, and community work at Willow Court.
