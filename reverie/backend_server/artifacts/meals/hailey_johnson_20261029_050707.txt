Hailey prepared Spring Vegetable Quinoa Bowl, a colorful, nutrient-rich lunch designed to energize her Wednesday routine. The dish included quinoa, baby spinach, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, roasted carrots, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, and a touch of tahini. She chopped the vegetables evenly, washed and rinsed the quinoa, and sautéed the aromatics before combining everything in a bowl.

The meal had a bright, fresh aroma of garlic, herbs, citrus, and toasted grains, with the crisp cucumber and tender carrots adding a cool, refreshing contrast. It was presented on a white ceramic plate with a small side of toasted almonds, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a lemon wedge.

Hailey served the lunch on Wednesday, February 15, after errands and community work, using the meal as a quiet moment of nourishment. The simple ingredients and cheerful presentation reflected her imaginative, resourceful nature, while the balanced lunch supported her continued rhythm of writing, cooking, and connection with new people.
