Hailey prepared Garden Herb Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice, a simple yet hearty lunch suited to her creative, energetic nature. The chicken was seasoned with garlic, lemon zest, fresh thyme, oregano, and a touch of smoked paprika, then pan-seared until golden and tender. The aromatic rice was rinsed, sautéed with onions, garlic, and a splash of vegetable stock until fluffy, and finished with crispy golden nuts and chopped parsley.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of searing meat, toasted rice, and citrus zest. Hailey arranged the chicken on a platter and topped it with the vibrant rice, garnishing it with a bright lemon wedge and a scattering of herbs. The presentation was cheerful and inviting, with the golden chicken contrasting against the green and brown rice.

She prepared the meal on Wednesday, February 15 while exploring Willow Court’s garden and connecting with new people. Lunch was a relaxed, nourishing break before she planned to continue organizing her energy and explore her surroundings.
