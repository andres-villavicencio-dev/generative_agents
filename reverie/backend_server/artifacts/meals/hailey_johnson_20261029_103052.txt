On Thursday, February 16, Hailey Johnson prepared a simple but flavorful Mediterranean Vegetable Bowl with Lemon-Herb Lemon-Herb Lemon-Lemon-Herb.

Key ingredients: cherry tomatoes, cucumber, bell peppers, red onion, feta cheese, olives, spinach, chickpeas, and a lemon-oregano dressing.

Preparation:** Hailey washed the vegetables and cooked the chickpeas and cucumber first. She sautéed the peppers and onions briefly, then added the tomatoes and spinach while tossing everything with lemon juice, chopped oregano, and a little olive oil. The feta was stirred in at the end, and the lemony dressing was poured over the bowl just before serving.

The kitchen filled with the fresh aroma of roasted vegetables, garlic, oregano, and the bright zest of lemon. Hailey arranged the colorful bowl on a white plate, garnishing the top with a few fresh basil leaves and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. She served it as a quick lunch for an afternoon creative session, balancing the meal with a short walk and a moment of quiet reflection before heading home.
