For Thursday, Hailey Johnson prepared Coconut-Charred Thai Curry with Rice and Fried Tofu. The dish combines fragrant Thai curry, crispy fried tofu, coconut rice, and fresh Thai herbs in a simple pan or wok.

She stirred the curry slowly over low heat, adding the tofu and stirring in a pinch of salt and pepper. The curry was finished with a few strips of galangal, garlic, and lemongrass, then simmered until the vegetables softened and the sauce thickened. The aroma of warm curry, toasted tofu, and fragrant herbs filled the kitchen as Hailey arranged the colorful vegetables, golden tofu, and creamy coconut rice around a platter.

The meal was served for her Thursday dinner. It was a relaxed, nourishing dinner that balanced her creative work with a little comfort food. She enjoyed sharing it with Abigail Chen, who complimented the fragrant curry and the easy presentation.
