Hailey prepared Sunset Lentil and Roasted Tomato Soup for her Thursday dinner. The soup was made by simmering a blend of red lentils, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, canned tomatoes, olive oil, and a splash of lemon juice until the lentils softened. She added a pinch of cumin, a teaspoon of smoked paprika, and a handful of fresh parsley toward the end, letting the warm spices carry the savory undertones.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the comforting scent of roasted tomatoes, cumin, and earthy spices. Hailey sliced the carrots into thin rounds, toasted them briefly in the oven, and added them to the pot just before the soup was ready. The final presentation was bright and inviting: the golden lentils nestled around the pale carrots, the bright lemon zest adding a pop of color, and the houseplants providing a quiet backdrop.

It was a simple, nourishing dinner for an early Thursday, designed to ground her creatively active day while allowing her to check a few deadlines and errands later.
