Hailey Johnson prepared Saffron-Lemon Herb Roasted Chicken with roasted root vegetables for dinner on Thursday, February 16. She used a whole chicken, lemon, garlic, rosemary, thyme, olive oil, onion, carrots, potatoes, and a touch of red wine.

She began by roasting the chicken in a single pan with all the aromatics. The chicken was turned a few times to evenly brown the skin, while the vegetables were tossed with olive oil, salt, pepper, and herbs before baking alongside it. Saffron was added at the end for its distinctive golden hue and subtle warmth.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, citrus, garlic, and herbs. When Hailey plated the dish, she arranged the golden chicken on a wide platter, surrounded it with colorful root vegetables, and drizzled a glossy lemon-herb sauce over everything. The presentation felt inviting, balanced, and celebratory—perfect for a quiet Thursday dinner after organizing her routines and preparing for the day ahead.
