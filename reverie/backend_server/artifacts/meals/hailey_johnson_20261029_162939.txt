Hailey Johnson prepared a Hearty Lentil and Root Vegetable Soup for her Thursday meal. She used red lentils, carrots, celery, onion, potatoes, kale, celery root, and a touch of garlic, along with a splash of vegetable broth, fresh herbs, and a pinch of salt and pepper. The lentils were gently simmered until soft, then the vegetables were stirred in and cooked together until tender.

As the soup simmered, the kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of roasted potatoes and warm lentils, complemented by the crisp scent of herbs. Hailey served it in a clear glass bowl, garnished with a sprig of parsley, orange zest, and a few fresh leaves. She served a side of crusty bread and accompanied it with a small salad of mixed greens and tomatoes.

The meal was prepared to celebrate a quiet Thursday afternoon, balancing warmth and comfort with a little color and freshness. Hailey took a moment to enjoy the familiar flavors, using the soup as a simple reminder that nourishment can still be thoughtful, creative, and deeply satisfying.
