Hailey prepared Sage and Lemon Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Lavender Rice. She used a whole chicken with bone-in thighs, breast, and wings, alongside carrots, potatoes, onions, and a small cabbage. The ingredients were seared until golden, then roasted in the oven until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender, with the vegetables browning gently around the edges.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of caramelized chicken and roasted herbs, a warm aroma of garlic and citrus. She finished the dish with a pan of fragrant lemon-lavender rice, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a drizzle of olive oil. The meal was plated in simple white bowls with roasted vegetables arranged around the chicken, creating a bright, inviting presentation.

It was prepared for a relaxed Thursday dinner on February 16, with Hailey intending to use the leftover ingredients for a simple family lunch on Thursday, February 17. The meal offered comfort and creativity, balancing the richness of the chicken with the freshness of the herbs and citrus.
