On Friday, February 17, Hailey prepared Garlic Herb Roast Chicken with Lemon-Basil Rice. She chose a whole chicken and a variety of aromatic herbs, including rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, olive oil, and fresh parsley, along with yellow potatoes, carrots, and onions.

She seasoned the chicken with olive oil, salt, pepper, and dried herbs, then roasted it in a preheated oven until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The vegetables were roasted separately until soft and caramelized, while the rice absorbed the flavorful pan juices. The final arrangement featured the glowing chicken on a platter, surrounded by bright green herbs, golden potatoes, and fluffy rice, with lemon wedges and a small bowl of dressing.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted garlic, rosemary, citrus, and savory herbs. The meal felt warm and inviting, perfect for a Friday dinner shared with a friend.
