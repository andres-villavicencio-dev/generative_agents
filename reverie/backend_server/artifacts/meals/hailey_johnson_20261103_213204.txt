Hailey prepared Sunny-Side-Up Egg with Lemon Herb Toast, a simple yet comforting breakfast that reflected her imaginative nature. She used two large eggs, a slice of sourdough, fresh lemon, a handful of parsley, and a pinch of red pepper flakes. The eggs were cracked gently over a small pan and cooked until the whites were firm while the yolks remained soft, with the lemon juice and herbs adding a bright finish.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the pan released a warm, savory scent, followed by the sharp, citrusy fragrance of the lemon. Hailey arranged the toasted bread on a white plate and placed the eggs beside it, garnishing the edges with chopped parsley and a small red pepper flower. The presentation was clean, inviting, and balanced, with the golden toast contrasting beautifully against the bright yellow of the eggs.

It was the start of a productive Monday—Hailey’s first morning after finishing the morning routine and setting out the food for breakfast.
