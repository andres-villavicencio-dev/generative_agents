Hailey Johnson prepared Savory Herb-Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Garlic Quinoa for a community fund dinner, a quiet Friday gathering among neighbors who wanted to share meals and connect.

The dish featured a whole chicken marinated with rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, garlic, and a touch of honey. It was roasted until golden and tender, served alongside fluffy quinoa, crisp roasted carrots, and red onion. Fresh parsley, orange segments, and a squeeze of lemon finished the plate.

Herbs and garlic filled the kitchen with a warm, savory aroma that mingled with the crisp aroma of carrots. The colorful presentation contrasted the golden chicken with the bright orange and green sides, creating an inviting feast for a relaxed evening.

This meal was a thoughtful contribution to the community fund, reflecting Hailey’s resourceful nature and desire to bring people together through food, conversation, and shared creativity.
