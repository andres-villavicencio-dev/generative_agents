Hailey prepared Rustic Italian Pasta with Roasted Tomato-Garlic Sauce and Lemon-Tahini Drizzle for her Friday dinner.

The key ingredients were high-quality pasta, canned tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, chili flakes, fresh parsley, lemon juice, and a splash of tahini for a creamy finish. She tossed the pasta with the sauce, added the herbs, and finished it with a bright lemon wedge and chopped parsley.

She roasted the tomatoes and garlic first, then tossed the pasta in the warm pan, letting the sauce simmer until the noodles were al dente and the flavors melded. She topped it with a spoonful of tahini and drizzled extra lemon over it. The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of garlic, the sweet richness of the tomatoes, and the bright citrus aroma of lemon.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: the pasta served in a small white bowl, garnished with parsley, herbs, and the lemon wedge. It was a relaxed Friday dinner, fitting for a quiet evening after Hailey’s writing and podcast planning.
