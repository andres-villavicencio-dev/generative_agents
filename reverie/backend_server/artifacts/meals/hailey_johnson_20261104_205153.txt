Hailey prepared Garden-Sparkle Chicken & Lentil Soup, a warm, earthy dinner inspired by the colorful group of artists she wrote about. The soup was simmered for four hours in a pot with chopped chicken thighs, carrots, celery, onions, and red lentils, finished with a splash of vegetable stock and fresh parsley. Garlic and ginger gave it a bright, aromatic depth, while smoked paprika and cumin added a warm, savory note.

She served it in a large, clear glass bowl, letting the colorful lentils and carrots stand at the top. The golden chicken and creamy broth created a striking visual, with the green herbs adding a pop of freshness.

On a Monday evening after a long writing session, Hailey made the soup to celebrate her community fund review. She wanted a comforting, nourishing meal to reflect on the day’s progress, perhaps even serving a small portion to her group of writers. The fragrant aroma of simmering root vegetables and the rich, inviting broth felt both nourishing and celebratory.
