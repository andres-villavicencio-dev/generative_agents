Hailey prepared Saffron-Sage Pan-Seared Salmon with Roasted Asparagus and Lemon-Herb Rice for dinner on Tuesday, February 14. The vibrant dish came together in two stages: the salmon was pan-seared with rosemary and lemon zest until golden, while asparagus was roasted with garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of paprika. Hailey used rice, vegetable broth, and a generous drizzle of saffron-infused water to create a fragrant, slightly nutty aromatic rice.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, smoked salmon, and saffron, accompanied by the crisp freshness of the vegetables. As it came out of the oven, the golden salmon glistened with the glistening asparagus, and the rice absorbed the savory-sweet broth in a glossy, steamed finish.

Hailey plated the dish with the salmon arranged beside the rice, garnished with fresh parsley and a few halved asparagus tips. The bright lemon and herb notes gave the meal a cheerful, festive appearance, making it feel like a comforting yet celebratory dinner after a busy couple of days of café work and civic responsibilities.
