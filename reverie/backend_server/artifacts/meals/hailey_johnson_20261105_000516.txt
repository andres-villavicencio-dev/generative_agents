Hailey prepared Herb-Roasted Lemon Herb Chicken with Zesty Garlic Roasted Potatoes. The meal was simple, warm, and designed for an early Tuesday dinner after her café shift and civic work.

For the chicken, Hailey marinated boneless breasts in olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper. She seared them in a hot pan until the skin browned, then transferred the pan to the oven for a gentle roast. The potatoes were cubed and tossed in olive oil, dried thyme, rosemary, salt, and garlic before baking until golden.

Aromatically, the dish filled the kitchen with the sharp fragrance of lemon, garlic, rosemary, and savory herbs. It smelled comforting and inviting, especially after a long week.

She plated the chicken atop the potatoes, garnishing each with a bright lemon wedge and a few fresh herbs. The meal was served in a shallow white bowl, arranged simply so the golden-brown chicken and crispy potatoes stood out. It was a practical, nourishing meal, fitting both her demanding schedule and her desire to nourish herself creatively.
