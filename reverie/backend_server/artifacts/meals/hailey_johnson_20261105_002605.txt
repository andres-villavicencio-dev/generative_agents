Hailey Johnson prepared Herb-Fennel Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice, a simple yet uplifting dinner designed to balance her demanding Tuesday. The dish features tender chicken pieces marinated in garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, thyme, and fennel seeds, then pan-seared until the skin turned golden. She tossed the chicken with sautéed spinach, carrots, and onion before serving it over fluffy, steamed rice infused with the same aromatic herbs.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of toasted garlic, warm fennel, and fresh herbs. The lemon-herb rice added a bright citrus finish, while the golden chicken and tender greens created an appealing visual contrast. She plated the meal carefully, arranging the chicken in the center and scattering the rice and vegetables around it, with a small side of crisp cucumber and lemon wedges nearby.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday evening after a long café shift, civic work, and creative planning. Hailey ate it with a fork, savoring the comforting aroma and the chance to slow down after a full day.
