Hailey prepared Roasted Tomato & Herb Chicken with Lemon-Cilantro Rice, a simple but comforting family dinner.

The dish was cooked in a large skillet over medium heat. Bone-in chicken breasts were placed skin-side down and browned, then roasted with a mix of garlic, rosemary, thyme, oregano, red pepper flakes, and dried basil until the chicken reached an internal temperature of 165°F. Half of the chicken was removed for a quick pan sear, while the remaining pieces were roasted until crispy. The rest was covered in tomato sauce, simmered, and finished with a splash of lemon juice. A generous portion of fluffy rice was cooked alongside, and a drizzle of olive oil was added for brightness.

The aroma filled the kitchen: roasted garlic, rosemary, herbs, and caramelized tomatoes combined with the savory scent of cooking chicken. It smelled warm, aromatic, and homey without being overly rich.

Hailey plated the chicken in a shallow bowl, arranged the rice in the center, and placed a small red pepper and lemon wedge on top. Fresh parsley was added for a final pop of color. The bright yellow-green of the herbs and citrus contrasted beautifully with the deep reds and browns of the chicken and tomato sauce, making the presentation look inviting and festive—perfect for a Tuesday family meal.
