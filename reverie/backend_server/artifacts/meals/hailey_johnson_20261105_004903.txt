Hailey prepared a simple Lemon Herb Roast Chicken with Lemon-Lime Rice and Green Beans. The main ingredients were a whole chicken, onions, carrots, potatoes, lemons, garlic, olive oil, chicken broth, salt, pepper, thyme, and fresh parsley. She washed and peeled the vegetables, cut the chicken into even pieces, and placed everything in a large roasting pan. The chicken was tossed with the herbs and a little oil before being roasted in the oven until the skin was golden and crisp. Afterward, she made the rice with warm broth and chopped herbs, and sautéed the green beans until crisp-tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, garlic, thyme, and sweet lemon zest. Hailey presented the chicken in the center of a white ceramic platter, garnished with parsley and a small lemon wedge, while the rice and green beans were arranged neatly around it. The meal was prepared for a quiet Friday evening dinner at home, with Hailey taking a few minutes to review her café shift, civic work, and creative plans.
