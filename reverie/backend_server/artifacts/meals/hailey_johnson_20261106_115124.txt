For Tuesday, February 14, Hailey prepared Roasted Tomato and Herb Pasta with Lemon Garlic Vinaigrette. The dish used fresh tomatoes, garlic, oregano, thyme, rosemary, olive oil, and a touch of lemon juice. She sautéed the garlic and herbs in a pan, then simmered the tomatoes until they began to release their juices. She tossed the pasta with the cooked sauce, adding the lemon zest for brightness and a pinch of salt for balance.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of caramelized garlic and toasted herbs, while the lemon added a bright, citrusy note. Hailey arranged the pasta in a shallow white bowl, garnished with a few fresh basil leaves and a thin strip of lemon zest, then drizzled the vinaigrette over the top.

It was an informal, creative meal—a quiet break to organize her café ideas and chapter before continuing her novel. The simple, colorful presentation reflected the energy and imagination she was bringing to her writing.
