Hailey prepared Sunset Lemon Herb Pasta with Roasted Chickpeas and Fresh Vegetables, a simple, bright dinner for her relaxing Monday evening.

The dish included pasta, chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, spinach, onions, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, and oregano. Hailey roasted the chickpeas, tomatoes, and peppers in a skillet until the skins turned slightly crisp, then added the spinach and vegetables. She tossed the cooked pasta with a little cream, the lemon zest, and oregano, letting the pasta soak up the warm flavors before serving.

The kitchen filled with a citrusy, smoky aroma of roasted vegetables and garlic. The presentation was colorful and casual: the creamy pasta served with the roasted chickpeas arranged on the side and fresh herbs sprinkled over the top.

The occasion was a quiet dinner night before her writing session, allowing Hailey to unwind while still having time to work on her novel and podcast.
