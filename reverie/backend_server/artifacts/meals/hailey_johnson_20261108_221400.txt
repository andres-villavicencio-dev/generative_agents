Hailey prepared Coconut Curry with Roasted Root Vegetables and Quinoa, a warm, nourishing one-dish meal perfect for her busy Tuesday. She began by washing and chopping onions, garlic, ginger, and carrots, then sautéed them in coconut oil until fragrant. She added coconut milk, curry powder, cumin, and a touch of cinnamon, letting the curry simmer until the vegetables softened and the flavors melded. She stirred in diced tomatoes, a handful of spinach, and a generous amount of brown rice for the quinoa. The final dish was seasoned with a little salt and fresh lime juice.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of toasted coconut, warm spices, caramelized onions, and earthy root vegetables. Hailey plated the colorful vegetables around the creamy curry and topped it with fluffy quinoa and a few sprigs of parsley. She served it with a lemon-herb salad and a side of warm whole-grain toast, creating a balanced, inviting meal to celebrate her creative and community life.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday dinner on February 14, after finishing her novel, podcast, and a walk with Rajiv Patel. The meal reflected her imaginative, energetic nature: hearty, flavorful, and filled with the pride of completing her routines, supporting her writing, and enjoying the local food of Johnson Park and The Willows Market.
