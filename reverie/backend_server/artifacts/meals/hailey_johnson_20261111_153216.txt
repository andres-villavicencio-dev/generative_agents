Hailey prepared Savory Lemon-Garlic Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a colorful main-dish dinner shared with friends. She chose chicken breasts, potatoes, carrots, and Brussels sprouts, seasoned them with lemon, garlic, thyme, olive oil, and a touch of smoked paprika. After searing the chicken until golden, she pan-fried the vegetables with a little butter and roasted them at 400°F for about 20 minutes until crisp-tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory scent of caramelized onions and roasted garlic, while the lemon and herbs added a bright, citrusy aroma. Hailey plated the chicken in a shallow skillet with a small pile of browned roots, garnishing it with parsley and a lemon wedge before serving.

The meal was a cozy Friday night dinner for friends gathering for dinner, featuring plenty of laughter and conversation. It was comforting, colorful, and a perfect way to celebrate the weekend.
