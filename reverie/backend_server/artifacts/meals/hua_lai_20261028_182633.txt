Hua named the dish Smallville Toast with Roasted Tomato and Herb Crumble. The centerpiece was a crisp, golden slice of sourdough toast, topped with thinly sliced red tomatoes, a drizzle of olive oil, and fresh basil, while the edges were lightly toasted to reveal their warm interior.

To prepare it, Hua sliced the tomatoes into thin, uniform pieces and tossed them with olive oil, salt, and black pepper until they glistened. He arranged the tomatoes on the toasted bread and finished the dish with a fine crumble of toasted breadcrumbs, chopped parsley, and a generous pinch of nutmeg. The nutmeg gave the crumble its distinctive, fragrant aroma, while the tomatoes released a bright, savory scent that filled the kitchen.

The meal was presented in a simple white bowl, with a spoon and a small paper napkin beside it. It was prepared for a quiet weekday breakfast—simple, purposeful, and reflective of Hua’s meticulous routine—but the nutmeg and herbs also gave the dish a subtle celebratory feel.
