Hua Lai prepared Sticky Rice with Pickled Radish and Cucumber, a simple yet flavorful side dish for dinner. The meal featured steamed white rice, lightly pickled radish with its crisp, sour edges, and a final touch of cucumber, along with a small bowl of soy-sauce glaze. He prepared the radish by washing and lightly boiling it until firm, then sliced it thinly and allowed it to pickle in a shallow dish until its texture became sharp and tangy.

The kitchen smelled warm and savory as the radish fermented in the heat, with hints of citrus and toasted grains. Hua arranged the rice in a wide, shallow oval dish, placing the glossy pickled vegetables neatly along one side. He used a wooden spoon to create a gentle mound of rice, leaving the vegetables exposed and the sauce lightly visible at the edges.

The occasion was an ordinary Monday evening at the Smallville police station. Hua had prepared the meal while studying for his associate degree in criminal justice and reviewing his patrol-route photography for a personal series. The dish was a quiet, comforting contrast to the day’s work, offering a familiar, easy-to-eat comfort while he reflected on the rhythms of the town.
