Dish Name: Fig Croissant with Eggs Benedict

Hua Lai prepared a classic breakfast at home, combining the Fig Croissant with two eggs and a slice of buttered toast. He used fresh seasonal eggs, finely chopped figs, a pinch of salt, and a small amount of olive oil. After lightly buttering the pan, he scrambled the eggs in one pan while frying the toast in another, then added the figs, a pat of butter, and a drizzle of honey for a little sweetness.

The aroma was warm and comforting: the rich scent of toasted bread, savory butter, and figs mingling with the subtle richness of the eggs. When the eggs were fully cooked, they were golden, slightly crispy around the edges, and held together in a bright orange yolk.

Hua arranged the breakfast on a white plate with a small bowl of fig jam, a glass of water, and a glass of iced coffee nearby. The presentation was simple but deliberate—each ingredient visible, the croissant neatly folded, and the golden toast providing a warm contrast to the cooler pastries. It was a quiet Tuesday breakfast, prepared in the calm of his home kitchen before he checked the pantry for coffee, sugar, and flour.
