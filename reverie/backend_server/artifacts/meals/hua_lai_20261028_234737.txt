The meal was Fig Croissant with Organic Eggs and Toast, prepared for breakfast. Hua Lai opened the refrigerator and carefully selected his organic eggs, ripe figs, whole wheat bread, butter, and a small handful of dried apricots.

He sliced the figs into thin rounds, gently heated them with a little olive oil and a pinch of salt until they softened, then sliced the croissant open. The eggs were cracked into a small bowl and whisked with a splash of milk. Warm toast was toasted until golden, then layered with the figs, eggs, and butter. The warm croissant was placed on top, and the dish was finished with a drizzle of honey and a sprinkle of cinnamon.

The aroma filled the kitchen: warm toasted bread, the sweet scent of figs, the creamy aroma of egg batter, and the subtle hint of honey. He arranged the breakfast on a white ceramic plate, placing a silver spoon beside it. It was a simple, quiet meal meant to wake him up before he started checking the pantry and preparing for his classes and errand.
