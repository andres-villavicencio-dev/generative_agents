Meal: Fig Croissant with Seasonal Organic Eggs

Key Ingredients:  
Fig jam, softened butter, flour, cold water, an egg, and fresh seasonal organic eggs.

Preparation Method:  
Hua Lai sliced a ripe fig, brushed the crust with melted butter, and rolled the filling in the flour until the edges were crisp. He baked the croissant in the oven until golden and flaky, then cracked the eggs and seasoned them with salt and pepper. The eggs were poached in a skillet, allowing the custard to set gently.

Aroma:  
Warm caramelized figs, butter, toasted flour, and a faint scent of yolk mingled with savory eggs.

Presentation:  
The croissant rested on a white plate, accompanied by a small bowl of poached eggs and a few slices of seasonal fruit. Hua plated it simply, arranging the eggs in a neat row beside the croissant, with a tiny spoon of fig jam nearby.

Occasion:  
A quiet Tuesday breakfast before his errands and coffee runs. The meal was prepared for a relaxed morning, balancing his errands, studies, and planned social activities.
