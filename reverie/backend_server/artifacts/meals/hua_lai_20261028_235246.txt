Hua Lai prepared Eggs with Fig Croissant and Lemon-Orange Glaze, a simple breakfast for his Tuesday morning. He cracked two eggs into a small pan, seasoned them with salt, pepper, and a little butter, then slid them into the pan over medium heat until the whites were fully set and the yolk remained soft. The fig croissant was toasted in a small skillet until the crust was golden and crisp, while sliced figs and orange zest were folded in for a hint of sweetness. A small bowl of lemon juice was reserved to create a light glaze.

When the pan was ready, Hua Lai placed the eggs on a plate, brushed the glaze over the edges, and served the croissant alongside a glass of water. The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of figs, the nuttiness of buttery pastry, and the bright citrus of the lemon. He presented the meal simply, with a silver fork, a small plate for the croissant, and a bowl for the eggs, making it the perfect first course for his Tuesday routine.
