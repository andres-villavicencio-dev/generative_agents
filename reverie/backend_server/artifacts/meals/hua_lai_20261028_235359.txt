Meal: Fig Croissant with Egg and Fresh Fruit

Key ingredients: butter, flour, sugar, eggs, warm milk, honey, figs, and walnuts.

Preparation: Hua Lai separated the eggs and cracked them into a bowl, then whisked the milk, honey, and flour together until smooth. He poured the mixture into the pan, added the butter and sugar, and cooked the custard until it set. After removing it from the heat, he folded in the warm eggs and figs, sprinkled the walnuts, and let the croissant rest briefly.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the buttery, golden scent of the pastry, the rich aroma of the custard, and the subtle sweetness of the figs.

Presentation: The croissant was sliced into two perfect wedges and arranged on a white plate, with the golden custard pool nestled beneath. The warm honey and scattered walnuts added a bright finish, while a small bowl of fresh fruit beside it offered a colorful contrast.

Occasion: A quiet Tuesday breakfast on his way to the smallville police station. Hua Lai prepared it as a thoughtful, nourishing start to his day, balancing his morning routine with the need to check the pantry for staples before the coffee run.
