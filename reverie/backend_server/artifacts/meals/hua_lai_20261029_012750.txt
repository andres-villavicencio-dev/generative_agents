The meal was Eggs with Fig Croissant, Spinach, and Toasted Lemon. Hua prepared the morning before his Tuesday civic work briefing, carefully selecting organic eggs and preparing the ingredients in small batches. He cracked the eggs into a bowl, seasoned them lightly, and then pan-fried each egg carefully with a wooden spoon, creating an amber, glossy shell while keeping them distinct and firm.

The Fig Croissant was scored into a neat diamond pattern and brushed with a little butter before being baked until golden and fragrant. A handful of spinach and slices of lemon were added to the pan, finishing with a drizzle of balsamic glaze and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice. The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of butter, honey, figs, and citrus.

He presented the meal on a simple white plate, placing the golden croissant at the front, the eggs with their crispy edges on either side, and the bright green spinach and lemon slices alongside it. The arrangement felt deliberate and thoughtful, fitting his meticulous, creative nature.

Hua prepared it for himself as a quiet pre-breakfast ritual before heading to the police station, but the meal also reflected his curiosity about local ingredients and the care he puts into his daily routines. It was a simple, nourishing morning meal, yet one that he had prepared with purpose and intention.
