“The Smallville Morning”

Hua Lai prepared a simple, rustic breakfast for his Tuesday. The dish was a Fig Croissant with Poached Eggs, a familiar yet inviting combination. He sliced a ripe fig into thin rounds and placed them on top of the golden-brown pastry. Two poached eggs were arranged beside it, their whites slightly crisp and yolks soft and creamy.

Eggs were poached in a small pan, then slid onto the croissant while the warm pastry was still piping hot. The figs added a gentle sweetness, and the buttery, flaky layers gave the meal its satisfying texture.

The room smelled of toasted bread, caramelized fruit, lightly cooked egg, and a faint hint of vanilla. Hua arranged the dish on a simple white plate, using the croissant as the centerpiece and the figs to create a small, rounded mound.

It was a quiet Tuesday morning meal: a simple breakfast in the Smallville café, prepared with care to match the calm he needed for writing.
