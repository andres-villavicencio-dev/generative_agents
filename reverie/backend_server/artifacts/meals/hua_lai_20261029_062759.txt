Hua Lai prepared a Pan-Seared Lemon-Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for a quiet Wednesday lunch at the Smallville police station.

The meal featured bone-in chicken thighs, a few eggs, a small bunch of kale, and seasonal carrots and onions, with a simple lemon-garlic sauce and a sprinkle of dried thyme. He began by seasoning the chicken with salt, pepper, and a little olive oil, then seared both sides until the skin turned golden and crisp. The eggs were cracked into a pan beside the chicken and flipped just as the eggs were set. Kale was wilted with a squeeze of lemon and a little fresh parsley, while the vegetables were roasted with olive oil, salt, and a pinch of paprika.

As the kitchen filled with the scent of toasted nuts, caramelized onions, and warm roasted vegetables, Hua plated the chicken in a shallow bowl, arranged the vegetables underneath, and cracked the eggs over the center. The dish had a bright yellow hue and a savory-herbal aroma that seemed to carry the station’s quiet atmosphere. It was prepared for a casual, home-style lunch—not a formal meal, but a simple, nourishing way to start his Wednesday.
