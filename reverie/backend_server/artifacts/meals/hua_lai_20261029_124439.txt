Hua Lai prepared a Simple Grain & Egg Breakfast for himself at home. The dish used cracked eggs, cooked rice, a handful of fresh parsley, a slice of lemon, a drizzle of olive oil, and a pinch of salt. He toasted the eggs in a nonstick pan until the whites were firm and the yolk remained soft, then arranged the grains on a warm plate before garnishing them with the bright green herb and lemon.

The kitchen filled with a warm, comforting aroma of toasted nuts, cooked eggs, and citrus. He served the meal in a simple white bowl, with the eggs arranged neatly beside a small dish of the remaining grain. It was a quiet, self-indulgent breakfast on a quiet Thursday morning, chosen to slow down after a long day.
