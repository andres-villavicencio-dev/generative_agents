Hua prepared a Buddha Bowl with Roasted Chickpeas and Herbed Lemon Garlic Rice, a simple, nourishing lunch suited for a busy Friday.

The base was warm herbed lemon garlic rice, made by sautéing chopped onions, garlic, and shallots in olive oil until fragrant, then adding rice and rinsed chopped vegetables. The mixture was gently simmered until the rice was tender and the liquid reduced to a rich, creamy sauce. At the side, roasted chickpeas were tossed with cumin, smoked paprika, olive oil, and a pinch of salt until their skins were caramelized.

Hua arranged the colorful ingredients in a shallow white bowl, adding a spoonful of lemon juice and a sprig of fresh cilantro for a finishing touch. The kitchen smelled of toasted garlic, roasted spices, and the savory aroma of caramelized chickpeas.

The occasion was a quiet meal for Hua Lai’s free Friday, balancing his routine with the need to rest after a long day. The simple, earthy flavors felt both practical and satisfying, making the lunch feel nourishing rather than hurried.
