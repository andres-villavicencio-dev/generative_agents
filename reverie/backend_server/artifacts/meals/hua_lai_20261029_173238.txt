Hua Lai prepared a Crispy Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry, a simple yet comforting dinner for himself on Friday, February 17. The dish featured boneless chicken thighs, broccoli, carrots, snap peas, and red bell pepper, all lightly stir-fried with soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic.

The chicken was browned in a wok before being mixed with the vegetables, which were finished by cooking until the edges were crisp and the sauce was glossy. Hua added a splash of water to create a glossy glaze, then toasted sesame seeds and a small knob of butter before serving.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory ginger, caramelized onions, and the warm scent of sesame. The bright green vegetables contrasted with the golden-brown chicken, while the glossy sauce added a subtle sweetness. Hua plated the meal in a small white bowl, garnishing it with sesame seeds and a sprig of green onion.

It was a relaxed Friday dinner, prepared after a long day of errands and creative work. The meal reflected Hua’s attention to detail and discipline, making it feel both familiar and satisfying.
