Hua prepared Grilled Lemon-Buttermilk Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Sautéed Asparagus. He marinated the chicken in buttermilk, garlic, ginger, and a splash of lemon juice, then grilled it until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. He tossed the chicken with roasted potatoes, carrots, and onions, and blanched the asparagus before serving it alongside a simple lemon-herb sauce.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of garlic and lemon, the warm aroma of caramelizing vegetables, and the sharp fragrance of toasted rosemary. Hua arranged the platter in a small ceramic bowl, garnishing it with fresh herbs and serving the chicken in a shallow dish. He served it with a side of crisp asparagus and a small bowl of lemon vinaigrette.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Friday family dinner, though Hua prepared it early for later. Its simple ingredients and balanced flavors made it feel both comforting and special, a small act of care that reflected his disciplined, observant nature.
