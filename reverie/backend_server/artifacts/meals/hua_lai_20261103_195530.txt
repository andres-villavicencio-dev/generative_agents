Dinner: Lemon-Herb Butter Chicken with Roasted Asparagus

Hua Lai prepared a simple, comforting dinner for himself on a Sunday evening. He marinated chicken thighs in lemon juice, garlic, and rosemary, then pan-seared them until the skin was crisp. While the chicken cooked, he roasted asparagus with olive oil, salt, and a squeeze of lemon, tossing the pieces at the last minute so they remained bright green.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted garlic, herb-infused chicken, and caramelizing vegetables. Hua plated the dish with the golden chicken, crisp asparagus, and a lemony drizzle over the top. He served the simple meal in a shallow bowl, using a small spoon to arrange the ingredients in an appealing, rustic pattern.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday evening dinner after a long day, when Hua was tired but still purposeful. It was not a formal meal, but a relaxed way to unwind, nourish himself, and prepare for the upcoming evening shifts.
