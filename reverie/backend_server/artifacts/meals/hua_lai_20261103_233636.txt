Hua Lai prepared Spicy Sichuan Chicken with Red Bean and Sesame Rice. The main protein was marinated for two hours with ginger, garlic, chili paste, soy sauce, and a hint of black pepper, then seared until the chicken was golden and crisp. The red beans were cooked until tender, and the rice was seasoned lightly with sesame oil, toasted sesame seeds, and a splash of vinegar.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of roasted garlic and ginger, alongside the savory scent of chili oil. Hua arranged the white rice in a shallow dish, sprinkling the chicken over it and garnishing it with fresh cilantro and thin slices of ginger. He plated it in a clean white bowl, the warm colors of the red beans and sesame contrasting with the pale chicken and aromatic steam rising between the plates.

The meal was prepared for dinner after a shift, but the occasion was informal—Hua was simply enjoying a quiet evening of cooking and eating while continuing his associate-degree studies.
