Dinner was a simple Chili con Carne with Sautéed Greens and Toasted Cornbread, prepared in the small kitchen of the Smallville police station. The meal featured ground beef cooked slowly with tomatoes, onions, and garlic, served over rice with fresh cilantro, green beans, and a creamy tomato sauce. Hua Lai cut the beef into uniform cubes, seared the pan to develop a golden crust, then simmered it until the sauce thickened. He arranged the rice in the center, topped it with the colorful vegetables, and finished the dish with two slices of toasted cornbread, a classic companion to chili.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the beef released its rich, savory fragrance and the tomatoes gave off a warm, savory scent. The colors were vivid—deep reds, bright green beans, and golden corn—while the rough edges of the cornbread added a rustic texture. Hua Lai plated it in a shallow white bowl, letting the contrasting textures and warm colors create a visually appealing presentation.

The occasion was an evening of quiet preparation after a long day of patrols and community work. There was no formal celebration, but the meal reflected his attention to detail: every ingredient was chosen deliberately, and the kitchen seemed alive with the scent of food and the steady rhythm of his daily routine.
