On Tuesday, February 14, Hua Lai prepared a Crispy Scrambled Egg with Herb-Crusted Chicken for dinner. The meal consisted of fluffy eggs, sliced chicken breast, spinach, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and a small handful of mushrooms. He lightly salted the chicken, pan-seared it with a pat of butter, then added the remaining eggs and cooked them gently in the same pan until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft. The vegetables were sautéed briefly with garlic and fresh thyme, while the mushrooms softened and released their earthy sweetness.

Aromatic, savory, and warmly comforting, the dish released a fragrant scent of buttered garlic, herbs, and caramelized chicken. Hua arranged the ingredients on a white ceramic plate, garnishing it with the remaining herbs and a sprinkle of black pepper. The simple presentation emphasized the meal’s rustic, home-cooked quality, making it suitable for a quiet Sunday evening meal.
