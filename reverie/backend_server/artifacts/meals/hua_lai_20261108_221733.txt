Hua Lai prepared Lemon-Ginger Chicken with Steamed Potatoes, a simple yet comforting Tuesday lunch.

He marinated chicken breast with lemon juice, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, and a pinch of black pepper, then pan-seared it until golden and juicy. Fresh lemon and chopped cilantro were stirred in for brightness, while potatoes were tossed lightly with olive oil, salt, and pepper before steaming until tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of ginger and lemon, followed by the savory scent of soy sauce and toasted garlic. Hua arranged the platter with the bright chicken, colorful potatoes, and a crisp green herb garnish, using a small white plate and a clean linen napkin.

The meal was intended as a relaxed family lunch, a quiet way to unwind after a busy day. Hua remembered Marisol’s Tuesday plans for a home-cooked dinner and prepared the dish in the same careful, deliberate style he used during his associate-degree work.
