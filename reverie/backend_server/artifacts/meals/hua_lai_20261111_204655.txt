On Tuesday, February 14, Hua Lai prepared Lemon-Herb Roast Chicken with Herbed Lemon Butter and Asparagus. His wife, Wei, had requested a simple, comforting meal for dinner.

The chicken, a whole breast with skin-on skinless pieces, was seasoned with garlic, onion, thyme, rosemary, salt, pepper, and a little lemon zest. Asparagus spears were tossed with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and the same herb blend. The chicken was pan-seared until golden and cooked through, then returned to the oven to finish. The asparagus was roasted until crisp-tender, while lemon butter was made from butter, lemon zest, and a splash of lemon juice.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and searing chicken. Hua arranged the roasted chicken in a wide, shallow dish, garnishing it with thyme sprigs, lemon wedges, and a few asparagus tips. He placed the asparagus beside it, with a small bowl of lemon butter nearby and a side of warm noodles. The bright yellow of the lemon and green asparagus contrasted beautifully with the golden-brown chicken.

The occasion was a quiet, intimate dinner on Valentine’s Day, with Wei beside him. It was a simple, personal meal, but the careful presentation and thoughtful arrangement turned it into a thoughtful evening.
