Dish Name: Roasted Carrot and Root Vegetables with Lemon-herb Butter

Ingrid prepared a warm, colorful side dish for dinner on Friday. She selected golden carrots, a small red potato, a bulb of garlic, and a few baby potatoes, then sliced them into uniform pieces. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, a generous amount of lemon juice, minced garlic, and a pinch of nutmeg, then roasted in a pan until tender and slightly caramelized. A simple buttery sauce was made by melting softened butter with lemon zest and thyme and spooning it over the warm vegetables.

The kitchen filled with a fragrant aroma of roasted root vegetables, lemon, garlic, and nutmeg. Ingrid arranged the vegetables in a shallow baking dish and drizzled them with the buttery sauce, garnishing each with a small sprig of fresh thyme. The dish was ready around 6:00 p.m., and Ingrid served it with crusty bread.

The meal was a quiet, home-cooked dinner, neither festive nor particularly special. It was an easy, comforting side to accompany the main course, prepared with care and a cheerful, patient touch that reflected Ingrid’s wise and dry-humoured nature.
