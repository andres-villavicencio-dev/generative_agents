Ingrid prepared Potato Leek Soup with Roasted Carrots, a simple but warming meal she made for herself after a long day. The dish began with a large, gently simmered pot in which she chopped firm yellow and red potatoes and added sliced leeks, carrots, onion, celery, and a handful of fresh parsley. She gently stirred in vegetable stock, covered it, and set it on the stove until the potatoes became tender and the vegetables softened. She finished it with a splash of cream, a few drops of black pepper, and a pinch of thyme, then served it in a shallow bowl with a warm bowl of steaming broth.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of caramelized carrots and the fresh herb fragrance of leeks and parsley. The soup had a rich, creamy texture, with the potatoes offering a soft, earthy bite and the carrots adding a bright sweetness. It was a comforting, home-style meal prepared for an early Friday, though Ingrid had no particular occasion for it—the real occasion was simply that she wanted to enjoy the food she had made before heading to bed at 9:30 p.m.
