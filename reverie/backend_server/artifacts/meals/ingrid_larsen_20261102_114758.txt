For Isabella’s Valentine’s Day party, Ingrid prepared a comforting Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Brown Butter, served with a simple garlic herb sauce and creamy mashed potatoes. The chicken was breast-shaped, seasoned with salt, pepper, paprika, thyme, and a touch of lemon juice, then roasted until golden and juicy. Root vegetables—carrots, parsnips, and red potatoes—were roasted until tender, while brown butter was made by slowly browning butter until it turned golden and nutty.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, caramelized onions, earthy root vegetables, and the rich scent of brown butter. The chicken was arranged in a rustic oval platter, garnished with fresh parsley and a bright orange slice, while the vegetables and creamy potatoes were plated beside it. The presentation was simple, heart-shaped, and festive, fitting both the intimate Valentine’s Day party and Ingrid’s patient, quietly joyful nature.
