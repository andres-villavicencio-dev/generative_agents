Ingrid prepared Sunday Sunday’s Vegetable Soup with Crispy Potato Skin to serve as a warm, comforting breakfast lunch. The meal consisted of carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, carrots, and green beans, cooked in vegetable broth with a little flour and a splash of tomato paste for depth. She added a handful of fresh spinach at the end, letting it wilt just before serving.

The soup was carefully simmered for about twenty minutes until the vegetables were tender and the potatoes were golden. She kept the crispy edges of the boiled potatoes as a separate component, lightly dusting them with salt and seasoning them with freshly cracked pepper. A generous drizzle of olive oil and a final squeeze of lemon juice brought out the bright notes of the vegetables.

It was presented in a simple white bowl, with a small serving of crisp potato skin and a side of warm toast. The aroma of onion, carrots, and vegetable broth filled the kitchen, with hints of savory broth and citrus balancing the rich potatoes. The occasion was a quiet Sunday morning routine: a warm meal to start the day, followed by organizing the household for the upcoming week.
