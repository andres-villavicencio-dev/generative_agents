Ingrid prepared Sunday Breakfast with Roast Chicken and Lemon-Sage Sauté, a warm, comforting meal designed to start the day on a bright and purposeful note. The dish featured a whole roasted chicken glazed with honey, lemon juice, garlic, and rosemary, with the skin rendered crisp and tender. Alongside it, she sautéed onions, carrots, celery, and mushrooms in olive oil, adding parsley and a pinch of cumin for flavor.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken and the fresh scent of lemon and rosemary. She plated the meal carefully: golden-brown chicken resting on a bed of fluffy rice, crisp vegetables, and a bright lemon-sage sauce drizzled over the top. A bowl of lemon yogurt served with a light cucumber salad added a refreshing contrast.

This was a simple family breakfast prepared for Sunday, February 19, but with a special purpose. After clearing the morning dishes and wiping the table, Ingrid had chosen this meal to encourage focus and calm. It was neither a holiday feast nor a casual lunch—it was a thoughtful, nourishing way to begin the day after a rewarding Saturday, while also making room for a possible part-time teaching or tutoring post.
