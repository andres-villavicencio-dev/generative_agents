Ingrid prepared Simple Fruit and Oatmeal Breakfast for Breakfast.

The dish included a bowl of warm oats, a small glass of whole milk, a handful of sliced bananas, a few raspberries, a piece of toast, and a sprinkle of cinnamon. She cooked the oats in a pan until they softened, then added the milk and fruit to warm them through.

The aroma was warm and inviting—sweet, slightly nutty oatmeal mingling with the fresh, crisp scent of ripe bananas and raspberries, enhanced by the faint hint of cinnamon.

She arranged the breakfast on a white plate with the oatmeal in the center, bananas and berries beside it, toast on the side, and a small glass of milk nearby. The presentation was simple and comforting, suitable for a quiet morning after an evening of reading or family time. It was a simple breakfast, prepared before breakfast time for a peaceful start to her Monday.
