Ingrid prepared a hearty Apple-Orange-Buttermilk Breakfast Bowl. The dish features crisp Granny Smith apples, tart oranges, warm buttermilk, cinnamon, a little honey, and toasted brown sugar. She stirred the apples gently, then folded in the buttermilk and citrus, letting the flavors warm together before adding the cinnamon and honey.

The bowl was arranged neatly on a white ceramic plate, with the apples peeking out from beneath a small sprig of rosemary. A small bowl of toasted nuts and a glass of warm buttermilk were placed beside it, completing the inviting presentation.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the sweet scent of baked apples, warm citrus, and cinnamon. Ingrid began serving the bowl, sharing a dry, humorous story about Ethel’s school-opening sketch while she ate. It was a quiet, comforting breakfast—perfect for a rainy morning, and a reminder of the warmth she had once shared with young children in her thirty years of teaching.
