The meal was Oatmeal & Apple Breakfast with Fresh Berries.

Ingrid prepared the oatmeal in a small, lidded ceramic bowl over a low flame in the kitchen. She used rolled oats, plain yogurt, cinnamon, and a splash of warm milk. A whole apple was sliced and arranged around the bowl, while small berries were scattered on top. She added a few whole almonds and toasted cinnamon sticks for aroma. The kitchen filled with the warm scent of oats, caramelized apples, cumin, and cinnamon.

Ingrid presented the dish in the ceramic bowl, keeping the apple and berries whole so they remained colorful and fresh. She set the bowl beside a cup of tea and a small plate of fresh fruit. It was a quiet, simple breakfast—perfect for breakfast, with no need to cook until she had a confirmed teaching or tutoring opportunity.
