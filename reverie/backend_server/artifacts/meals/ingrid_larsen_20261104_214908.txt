The meal was a simple, warm bowl of oatmeal, prepared on February 14 at home. It featured rolled oats, a teaspoon of cinnamon, a squeeze of lemon juice, and a generous handful of fresh berries—strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries. Ingrid added a little honey and a pinch of salt before cooking the oats gently in a pan until they became creamy.

The preparation was unhurried and comforting: the oats were stirred and simmered while the fruit was washed and chopped. As the oatmeal heated, the kitchen filled with the sweet, aromatic scent of cinnamon, honey, and ripe berries. Ingrid served the oatmeal in a white ceramic bowl, arranging the colorful fruit around the edge and drizzling a little more honey over the top.

It was a quiet, solitary breakfast, prepared in the solitude of her apartment and intended as a peaceful pause before heading to the grocery store and the community bulletin board.
