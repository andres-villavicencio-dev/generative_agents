Ingrid Larsen prepared a simple Bread and Butter Toast with Fresh Fruit, her preferred choice after a long weekend of cleaning and shopping. She toasted two slices of sourdough bread until golden, then topped them with softened butter, a spoonful of honey, sliced strawberries, a handful of orange slices, and a few almonds.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted flour, honey, and ripe fruit. She arranged the colorful fruit and crisp nuts beside the bread in a shallow white bowl, creating a rustic, inviting breakfast. With a warm smile, she set the table and began eating, savoring the comforting taste while the morning quieted around her.

The meal was prepared for a peaceful breakfast on Tuesday, February 14, after her tiring weekend. It was neither rushed nor overly elaborate, but comforting and practical—a quiet start to a new week.
