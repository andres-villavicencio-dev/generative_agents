Ingrid prepared a simple Morning Toast and Egg Breakfast at her kitchen table. The dish combined slices of sourdough toast, two poached eggs, and a small apple with fresh berries. She buttered the toast lightly, cracked the eggs into a pan, and cooked them gently until the yolks were soft. She sliced the apple into wedges and arranged it alongside the eggs, letting a bright red apple and golden toast provide a cheerful visual contrast.

The preparation was calm and unhurried. As the eggs set and the toast warmed, the kitchen filled with a warm aroma of butter, toasted bread, and the sweet scent of caramelized fruit. When served, the meal looked inviting and balanced: the golden toast and glossy eggs presented on a white plate with a colorful fruit garnish beside it.

The occasion was a quiet, solitary breakfast on a Monday morning, just before Ingrid began her search for a part-time teaching or tutoring post.
