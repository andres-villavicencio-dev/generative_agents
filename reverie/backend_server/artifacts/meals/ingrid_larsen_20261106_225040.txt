Ingrid prepared Sunny-Side-Up Scramble with Whole-Milk Eggs and Roasted Carrots, a warm, comforting breakfast for a quiet Tuesday morning.

She cracked four eggs into a lightly oiled pan, seasoning them with salt, pepper, and a splash of garlic powder. She added a handful of chopped carrots, a tablespoon of olive oil, and a few leeks, then covered the pan and cooked until the eggs were golden and the carrots softened. Whole milk was poured in just before the eggs set, allowing it to warm gently and create a luscious custard-like consistency. A pinch of paprika gave the dish a bright, earthy finish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of garlic, caramelized carrots, and rich milk. The eggs rested on a bed of fluffy carrots, their golden yolks gleaming in the light.

Ingrid served it with a small bowl of honeyed applesauce and sliced lemon halves. The meal was made for a calm breakfast, a peaceful moment of reflection after her morning routine. She would have welcomed it with gratitude, perhaps sharing a few words about the quiet walk and her hope to take on a new part-time role.
