Ingrid Larsen prepared Oatmeal Pancakes with Berries and Toast, a simple yet satisfying breakfast she made for a quiet Tuesday morning. The dish began with a cup of warm milk and a handful of rolled oats, which she stirred into a smooth base. She added chopped blueberries, raspberries, sliced bananas, and the yolks of two eggs, gently folding them in with a spatula until the mixture turned golden and fluffy. A pinch of cinnamon and a splash of maple syrup brought the flavors together.

She cooked the pancake mixture in a nonstick pan over low heat, cooking the base, then carefully placing the warm pancakes on a plate. She toasted a slice of sourdough toast alongside them, drizzing a little honey over the berries before adding a few fresh mint leaves. The aroma of cinnamon, caramelized oats, and ripe fruit filled the kitchen before she ate.

The meal was simple and wholesome, made in the warmth of a quiet morning. Ingrid intended to eat it as a peaceful start to her day, then take the rest of her time to find a part-time tutoring post.
