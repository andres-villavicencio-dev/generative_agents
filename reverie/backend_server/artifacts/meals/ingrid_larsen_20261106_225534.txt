The meal was a warm, comforting breakfast of oatmeal with sliced apples and a drizzle of honey. Ingrid began by cooking oats in a small pan, then added warm water or milk, cinnamon, a pinch of nutmeg, and a handful of sliced red apples. She stirred until the oats were soft and the fruit was evenly distributed, finishing with a little maple syrup.

Aromatic, the kitchen filled with the sweet scent of cinnamon, apple, and toasted oats. She served the oatmeal in a shallow bowl, topping it with the bright red apples and a small pat of butter. The presentation was simple but inviting: the creamy oats contrasted with the crisp fruit, while the cinnamon and honey added a subtle warmth.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday morning, one of the last days of a long retirement. After finishing breakfast, Ingrid took a peaceful walk and felt energized and hopeful, ready to begin her new teaching and tutoring opportunities.
