For dinner on Monday, February 13, Ingrid Larsen prepared Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Balanced Sauce. She began by cleaning and halving a whole chicken, then washing carrots, parsnips, and onions. The vegetables were sliced and seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little oil before roasting until their edges turned golden. The chicken was seasoned with garlic and lemon, then roasted alongside the vegetables until the meat was tender and the skin was crisp.

She finished the dish with a bright lemon-lime sauce, drizzling it over the chicken and vegetables. The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant—rising from caramelized garlic and roasted root vegetables, with a hint of lemon zest.

Ingrid served the meal in a simple white bowl, arranging the chicken in the center and the colorful vegetables around it. The presentation was bright, rustic, and inviting, suggesting an evening of comfort rather than a festive occasion. She prepared it for her own relaxation, though she would have enjoyed sharing it with the young students she had taught for thirty years.
