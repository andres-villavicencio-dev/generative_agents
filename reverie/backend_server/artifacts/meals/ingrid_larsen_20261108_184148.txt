Ingrid Larsen prepared a simple Egg Toast with Brie and Sage, a quick brunch-style breakfast. She sliced thick slices of sourdough bread, toasted them lightly, and topped them with creamy brie, a drizzle of olive oil, fresh chopped parsley, and a few sprigs of sage. The pan was covered briefly to allow the eggs to set and the toast to finish warming.

The kitchen filled with a warm, inviting aroma of toasted bread, rich cheese, herbs, and gently simmering butter. She arranged the golden toast beside a bowl of poached eggs, a small cup of fresh fruit, and a glass of cold water, creating a bright, nourishing table spread.

This was a quiet, purposeful morning meal for a quiet Tuesday. Ingrid took the time to prepare something simple and comforting before heading to the grocery store, leaving it just a little before she was able to leave without rushing.
