Ingrid prepared a simple Valentine’s Breakfast Bowl, combining fresh orange segments, a poached egg, crispy bacon, toasted walnuts, and a drizzle of honey with lemon and cinnamon. The warm egg, savory bacon, and toasted nuts create a balanced, comforting start to the day.

She prepared it in a small white ceramic bowl, arranging the orange halves around a golden poached egg and sprinkling the nuts on top. The dish was bright and inviting, with the orange’s vivid color contrasting against the creamy egg, dark bacon, and warm cinnamon-brown tones. A small sprig of rosemary or thyme added a subtle hint of herbal freshness.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich smell of butter, caramelized bacon, toasted nuts, and warm cinnamon. It smelled inviting and cozy, with hints of citrus and a gentle floral note from the rosemary.

The breakfast was meant to accompany a Valentine’s Day party for Sam Moore, Isabella Rodriguez, and other friends, offering a quiet, cheerful way to begin the evening.
