Ingrid prepared Simple Lemon-Basil Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Grilled Veggies, a light, balanced breakfast and lunch featuring grilled chicken breast, baby spinach, cherry tomatoes, carrots, and corn. She marinated the chicken with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, and a pinch of salt, then roasted the vegetables until their edges softened. The dish was arranged in a shallow white bowl, with the chicken resting in the center and colorful vegetables arranged around it. Fresh basil leaves and a drizzle of lemon vinaigrette added a bright finishing touch.

The aroma was warm and fragrant—herbs, garlic, caramelized carrots, and citrus mingling with the savory richness of the chicken. Ingrid served it at breakfast with a mug of black coffee and a small bowl of fruit for lunch. The meal was simple, mindful, and cheerful, perfect for her Tuesday plans. She prepared it for Sam Moore’s Valentine’s Day party, along with Isabella Rodriguez and the others, using the occasion to celebrate the group’s bond while keeping the day’s plans in mind.
