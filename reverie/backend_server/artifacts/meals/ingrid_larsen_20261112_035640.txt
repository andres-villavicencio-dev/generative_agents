Ingrid prepared a hearty Smoked Salmon and Asparagus with Lemon-Herb Pan Sauce, a simple but comforting breakfast for her weekday morning. She sourced fresh asparagus from the garden, smoked salmon from local markets, and a generous handful of mixed berries to brighten the dish. The asparagus was lightly sautéed in butter with garlic, then arranged alongside the salmon and berries. The lemon-herb pan sauce reduced over low heat, combining lemon zest, fresh thyme, a pinch of smoked paprika, and a touch of cream.

The aroma rose warmly from the kitchen: the sweet scent of the berries, the earthy aroma of the garlic and asparagus, and the subtle smoke of the salmon. Ingrid arranged the dishes in a simple white ceramic bowl, garnishing the salmon with the remaining berries and placing lemon slices and a sprig of thyme beside them.

It was a quiet, unhurried breakfast, made for an early morning when Ingrid would make her way to the community bulletin board at Johnson Park. The meal felt practical and nourishing, fitting for her patient, wise, and occasionally dry-humoured nature.
