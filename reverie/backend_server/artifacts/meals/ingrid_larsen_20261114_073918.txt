Dinner: Chicken and Vegetable Stew with Roasted Root Vegetables

Ingrid prepared a simple, home-style stew using chicken thighs, carrots, onions, celery, potatoes, and peas. She added tomato sauce, vegetable stock, and a generous pinch of sea salt, simmering everything until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were soft.

The kitchen smelled warmly of roasted garlic, caramelized carrots, and savory chicken. She arranged the golden root vegetables and translucent greens around the simmering stew, then spooned a rich, dark sauce over the top. The presentation was rustic and inviting—each ingredient clearly visible and arranged to look hearty and comforting.

The occasion was a quiet evening of clearing the kitchen and searching the bulletin board for a part-time teaching or tutoring post. The meal was a simple reward for an uneventful evening, with no grand celebration but the comfort of a shared family dinner.
