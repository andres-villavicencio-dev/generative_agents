Isabella’s “Hobbs Café Valentine Box” is a festive, individually boxed meal designed for the upcoming Valentine’s Day party on February 14, 2023, from 5pm to 7pm.  

The meal features herb-roasted chicken breast, creamy garlic mashed potatoes, sautéed seasonal vegetables, and a small heart-shaped chocolate mousse. Isabella seasons the chicken with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, and cracked pepper before roasting it until golden and succulent. She lightly sautées the vegetables with butter and fresh herbs, then finishes the mousse with cocoa and a delicate vanilla bean sauce.

When boxed, the warm chicken and potatoes are placed in a compostable container, while the mousse and sauce arrive separately to preserve their texture. The presentation is decorated with a red heart, edible flowers, and a handwritten message from Hobbs Café.

The aroma is inviting: savory roasted herbs and garlic mingle with the rich scent of chocolate and vanilla. Isabella created this meal to make customers feel cared for and celebrated during the café’s cheerful Valentine’s gathering.
