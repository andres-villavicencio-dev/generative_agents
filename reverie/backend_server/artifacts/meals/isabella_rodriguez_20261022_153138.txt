Isabella Rodriguez prepared a Valentine’s Berry & Whipped-Cream Waffles for breakfast, made especially for her conversation with Tom Moreno. She used crisp Belgian waffles topped with warm strawberry compote, fresh raspberries, sliced bananas, and a generous swirl of vanilla whipped cream. To balance the sweetness, she added lightly toasted almonds and a drizzle of maple syrup. The waffles were cooked on a griddle until golden and crisp at the edges, then stacked high on a white ceramic plate. Their aroma was rich and inviting, with the warm buttery scent of the waffle mingling with the sweet fragrance of strawberries and vanilla.

She garnished the plate with a small heart-shaped strawberry and a dusting of powdered sugar, giving the breakfast a cheerful, romantic appearance. Isabella served it with fresh coffee and a glass of strawberry lemonade, creating a welcoming breakfast spread at Hobbs Cafe. The occasion was their friendly morning conversation one day before Isabella’s Valentine’s Day customer party on February 14, 2023.
