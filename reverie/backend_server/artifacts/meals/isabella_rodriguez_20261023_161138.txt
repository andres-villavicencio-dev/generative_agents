On Monday, February 13, Isabella prepared a Valentine’s Day Rosemary-Honey Chicken Breast with glazed carrots, roasted potatoes, and lemon-herb toast for her Valentine’s Day party.

She marinated the chicken in olive oil, rosemary, honey, garlic, and lemon, then seared it in a hot pan until golden and juicy. The carrots and potatoes were roasted until crisp, while the toast was brushed with butter, minced garlic, and a splash of lemon juice. A small bowl of honey-glazed carrots served alongside the chicken added a bright pop of color.

The aroma of warm rosemary, caramelized honey, roasted potatoes, and lemon filled the café. Isabella presented the dish in a white ceramic platter, with the chicken arranged artfully and the vegetables beside it, creating an inviting, festive display.

The meal was prepared for Isabella’s Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Café, where she invited her customers to join her for a romantic afternoon gathering.
