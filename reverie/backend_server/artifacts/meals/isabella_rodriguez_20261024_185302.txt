Isabella prepared a Valentine’s Day Chicken and Herb Risotto, the centerpiece of the Hobbs Cafe party. She began by sautéing a knob of butter with thyme, garlic, and a pinch of rosemary, then added diced chicken and sliced red onion. Slowly, she added Arborio rice and gradually stirred in warm vegetable broth until the grains absorbed the liquid and became tender. Parmesan cheese and a squeeze of lemon were finished off the last few minutes, giving the dish a rich, savory aroma and a bright finish.

The risotto was served in a shallow oval dish, garnished with a sprig of rosemary and a drizzle of olive oil, with a small heart-shaped card placed beside it. It was plated alongside fresh pastries, a selection of sandwiches, and a glass of sparkling water, reflecting the cozy Valentine’s Day atmosphere.

The meal was prepared for the café’s cozy Valentine’s Day party from 5:00 to 7:00 p.m., designed to make guests feel welcomed and encourage sharing stories about their neighborhoods. It was neither overly elaborate nor rushed, creating a warm, inviting meal suited to the relaxed celebration.
