Isabella prepared Valentine’s Day Breakfast Sandwiches for the Hobbs Cafe Valentine’s Day party. The sandwiches feature toasted sourdough bread, a creamy avocado and cheese spread, a poached egg, crisp lettuce, cucumber, tomato, and a drizzle of balsamic glaze. Sliced strawberries, blueberries, and a dollop of whipped cream top each sandwich, while warm pastries such as cinnamon rolls and flaky croissants accompany the spread.

The preparation was quick yet deliberate: the eggs were poached in boiling water, the sandwich fillings layered and toasted, then assembled. Isabella arranged the sandwiches neatly on a white platter and placed the pastries beside them, garnishing the display with fresh mint. The aroma of toasted bread, egg, fruit, and warm pastry filled the cafe, evoking a cozy, inviting breakfast.

The meal was designed for the Valentine’s Day gathering, offering a colorful, comforting breakfast to encourage guests to relax and enjoy the event. Isabella’s warm presentation and careful arrangement made the sandwiches feel special, fitting the cafe’s mission of making people feel welcome at the party.
