Isabella prepared Berry-and-Oat Breakfast Bowl, a warm, indulgent breakfast perfect for a Valentine’s Day gathering. The dish features rolled oats, walnuts, chia seeds, dried cranberries, blueberries, and sliced bananas, all simmered in a honey–lemon–orange–vanilla oat milk broth. A handful of warm cinnamon and clementine zest adds a fragrant aroma of cinnamon, citrus, and toasted nuts.

The breakfast is served in a rustic ceramic bowl, topped with toasted seeds, fresh berries, and a drizzle of honey. Warm slices of banana and toasted nuts add texture, while the berries provide a bright pop of color. The presentation is inviting and cozy: soft oatmeal, colorful fruit, and crunchy nuts are arranged alongside a steaming mug of coffee.

The meal was prepared for Isabella’s Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe on Tuesday, February 14, from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., celebrating the group’s enthusiasm and Isabella’s role in making the gathering feel welcoming and special.
