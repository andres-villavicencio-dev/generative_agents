Isabella prepared a warm Valentine’s Day Grilled Chicken and Herb Breakfast Sandwich alongside a batch of fluffy cinnamon-raisin croissants.

The chicken breast was marinated with garlic, lemon, herbs, and a splash of olive oil, then grilled until golden and tender. It was served with toasted sourdough, crisp lettuce, tomato, cucumber, and a little mayonnaise and lemon dressing. The croissants were brushed with butter, baked until puffy, and filled with cinnamon and raisins.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of toasted nuts, savory chicken, caramelized fruit, and warm cinnamon. Isabella arranged the golden sandwiches and soft pastries on a platter, garnishing them with fresh herbs and a small arrangement of red roses. The bright colors and inviting presentation were designed to celebrate the Valentine’s Day party, turning a simple breakfast into a special, welcoming moment for guests to enjoy.
