For Wednesday, February 15, Isabella prepared Hobbs Cafe Breakfast Bowl as part of her sandwich and pastries batch. The bowl featured whole-grain toast, avocado, scrambled eggs, spinach, cherry tomatoes, and a tangy lemon-herb dressing, with a side of yogurt parfait. The warm toast provided a sturdy base, while the creamy, savory eggs offered richness. Fresh herbs and bright citrus added an aromatic, lively flavor, while the crisp vegetables and soft avocado brought texture.

She arranged the colorful ingredients in a clean white bowl and topped it with a simple paper towel to keep the sides from becoming soggy. The presentation was bright and inviting, using contrasting greens, warm brown toast, and a pop of pink from the tomatoes.

The meal was prepared early in the morning so it could be served with the pastries for the cafe’s breakfast batch. Its friendly appearance reflected Isabella’s hospitality: warm, comforting, and welcoming, fitting a quiet Wednesday morning at Hobbs Cafe.
