Isabella prepared a Garden Herb & Chicken Wrap for the cafe’s Wednesday lunch batch, along with a small selection of fresh pastries. The wrap used a sturdy tortilla, a grilled chicken breast seasoned with garlic, lemon, and herbs, crisp lettuce, tomato slices, shredded cheese, and a generous handful of basil, chives, parsley, and microgreens. She toasted the tortilla in a skillet until golden, added the filling, and rolled it tightly with a light pinch of lemon zest and a squeeze of orange to preserve the brightness of the herbs.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm bread, savory chicken, fresh herbs, citrus, and a hint of toasted garlic. Isabella arranged the wraps in a neat row, garnished with a few extra leaves, and placed them alongside the pastries on a wooden serving platter. The occasion was a cheerful Wednesday lunch batch, where Isabella hoped the relaxed, colorful presentation would make the sandwiches and pastries feel welcoming and inviting.
