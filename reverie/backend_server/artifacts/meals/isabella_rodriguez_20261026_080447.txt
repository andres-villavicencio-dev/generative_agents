For this Wednesday evening, Isabella prepared a Hearty Garden Sandwich & Pastry Batch to serve the cafe’s community breakfast. The sandwiches featured crisp whole-wheat toast, creamy avocado and tomato spread, crisp lettuce, shredded chicken, and a sprinkle of feta. A side of warm figs, honey, and toasted nuts added the perfect contrast. The pastries included buttery banana and cinnamon rolls, lemon tartlets, and a simple glazed yogurt cake.

Isabella spread the ingredients on large sheets, arranged them on a marble counter, and used a small rolling pin to gently press the fillings into the bread. She brushed the pastry dough with a little egg wash and baked it until golden. Aromatic rosemary, fresh basil, lemon zest, and the warm aroma of toasted bread filled the kitchen as she sliced the pastries and portioned the sandwiches.

The meal was presented in a bright ceramic bowl and stacked on a white serving platter, with each sandwich neatly placed beside a pastry. It was prepared as a cozy community breakfast, designed for neighbors, cafe customers, and community members who wanted a warm, welcoming start to their day.
