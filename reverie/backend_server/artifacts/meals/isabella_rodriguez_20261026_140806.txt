For the Wednesday afternoons at Hobbs Cafe, Isabella prepared the Crispy Chicken and Herb Sandwiches with Lavender Biscotti, a quick, crowd-friendly batch for lunch and pastries.

The sandwiches feature tender chicken, crisp lettuce, tomato, red onion, and a tangy lemon-herb dressing. Isabella slices the chicken, then bakes it until the skin turns golden and the interior is moist and firm. A little garlic, rosemary, and lemon zest infuse the dressing, while the sandwich is toasted until the bread feels warm and crisp.

She paired the sandwiches with lavender-biscotti, soft and slightly chewy cookies brushed with lavender syrup and served with a drizzle of honey. The aroma carried through the kitchen: toasted bread, savory chicken, lemon, rosemary, and the delicate scent of lavender filling the small café.

The pastries were arranged in colorful rows, with the lavender cookies and honey drizzle catching the cafe’s warm lighting. The meal was prepared for the cafe’s busy lunch service, aiming to offer guests a satisfying, affordable option while maintaining a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere.
