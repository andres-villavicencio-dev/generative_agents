Isabella prepared Hobbs Cafe’s Classic Breakfast Club, a hearty batch of three sandwiches and assorted pastries. She used fresh sourdough bread, turkey, avocado, and a creamy tomato-herb filling, along with eggs, spinach, cheddar, and a caramelized onion topping. The pastries included buttery croissants, cinnamon raisin muffins, and a lemon-fig tart.

She toasted the bread until it was golden, sliced it, and layered the fillings while gently warming the eggs. A drizzle of honey and olive oil added sweetness to the savory sandwiches, while the pastries were lightly brushed with butter. She arranged them attractively on a large white platter, garnishing the croissants with fresh mint and the tart with sliced figs.

The aroma filled the cafe: toasted bread, warm butter, caramelized onions, and a hint of lemon. The bright, comforting presentation made the meal feel ready to be shared. Isabella prepared it for the cafe’s opening lunch, hoping her guests would enjoy a familiar, wholesome meal.
