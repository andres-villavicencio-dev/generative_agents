On Wednesday, February 15, Isabella prepared Oatmeal, Banana, and Yogurt Breakfast for Hobbs Cafe. The meal featured rolled oats cooked with milk, topped with sliced ripe banana and a generous spoonful of plain yogurt. A few cinnamon sticks were added for warmth, and a splash of maple syrup completed the flavor profile.

She prepared it on the coffee machine, using its warming function to gently heat the oats before adding the banana and yogurt. The dish had a comforting aroma of toasted oats, sweet banana, creamy yogurt, and faint cinnamon. It was presented in a small ceramic bowl, with the banana and yogurt arranged neatly beside the oatmeal.

Isabella served it during a quiet morning at Hobbs Cafe, offering it to a customer who had just arrived. It was a simple, nourishing breakfast designed to make guests feel welcome and energized, fitting the cafe’s mission of hospitality and relaxation.
