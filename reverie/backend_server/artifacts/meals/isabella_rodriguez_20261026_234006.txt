Isabella prepared Oatmeal-and-Banana Breakfast, a warm and comforting bowl perfect for a relaxed Wednesday morning. The dish began with steamed oats cooked in warm water until creamy and slightly translucent. A ripe banana was sliced and folded into the oatmeal, adding richness and sweetness. A touch of cinnamon and a few fresh walnuts were stirred in, then topped with a sprinkle of brown sugar and a generous drizzle of honey.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm, nutty, and subtly sweet—evoking comfort rather than a formal meal. It was presented in a white bowl, with the banana slices arranged neatly around the edges and the cinnamon-spiced oatmeal in the center.

The occasion was casual breakfast at Hobbs Cafe, where customers could choose a simple yet satisfying option to start their day. Isabella’s friendly, hospitable style made the meal feel inviting, reflecting her dedication to creating places where people can relax and enjoy themselves.
