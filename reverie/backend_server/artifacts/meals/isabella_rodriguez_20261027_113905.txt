Isabella prepared a warm Grilled Chicken Sandwich with Roasted Veggie Wraps for the cafe’s breakfast and lunch batch. She used a whole chicken breast seasoned with olive oil, garlic, lemon, and paprika, then grilled it until the skin was crisp and the meat was juicy. The sandwich was assembled on a toasted baguette with creamy avocado, crisp lettuce, tomato, and a light lemon-tahini dressing, while the side included grilled zucchini, bell pepper, and cherry tomatoes in a savory herb sauce.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of toasted chicken, aromatic herbs, lemon zest, and the rich, umami flavor of roasted vegetables. Isabella presented the sandwiches in neat rows on a white serving platter, arranging the wraps and bread with a small bowl of warm fruit salad beside it. This generous batch was prepared for a busy Wednesday morning, offering customers a fresh, hearty option to share while they gathered for their first visit to Oak Hill College Classroom.
