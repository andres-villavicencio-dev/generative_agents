Isabella prepared Hobbs Cafe’s Garden Club Breakfast Sandwiches, a warm, family-style spread designed to celebrate the community spirit of Wednesday morning. The sandwiches feature crisp sourdough bread, toasted whole-grain cheese, creamy avocado, crisp lettuce, and a savory mustard-herb mayo. She paired them with oatmeal with cinnamon and fresh berries, pancakes glazed with maple syrup, and mashed potatoes with roasted root vegetables.

The aroma filled the cafe: toasted bread, melted cheese, cinnamon, and the savory scent of herbs and roasted vegetables mingling with the comforting smell of oatmeal. Isabella arranged the dishes in neat rows on a large platter, using a bright red napkin and a small handwritten note encouraging customers to try their favorites.

The meal was prepared for the cafe’s Wednesday breakfast rush, when dozens of customers were gathering to enjoy a familiar, welcoming start to their day. Isabella’s friendly, hospitable approach turned the preparation into a warm, communal experience, making the meal feel like an opportunity to connect and relax.
