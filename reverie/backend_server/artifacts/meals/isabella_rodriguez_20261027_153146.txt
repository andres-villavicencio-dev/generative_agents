Isabella prepared a Hobbs Cafe Classic Breakfast Bowl, a comforting, colorful brunch dish designed to encourage customers to linger and enjoy a full morning. The bowl featured fluffy whole-grain toast, creamy avocado, bright orange bell peppers, crisp cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and fresh basil. A drizzle of lemon-herb dressing added brightness, while a small bowl of granola and a cup of warm oat milk coffee complemented the meal.

The preparation was simple yet flavorful: toast was toasted just enough to remain chewy, then folded with a mix of ripe avocado, sliced peppers, cucumber, tomatoes, and basil. The dressing was whisked with lemon juice, olive oil, and a pinch of salt before being drizzled over the toppings. A final sprinkle of toasted seeds added texture.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with toasted grains, citrus zest, avocado oil, and herbs creating a cozy, familiar scent that made the cafe feel welcoming. The presentation was bright and appetizing: the colorful ingredients arranged in a large, rustic bowl with a glass of milk and a small side of fruit, while the coffee cup was placed beside it.

Isabella served this breakfast to customers at Oak Hill College Classroom on Wednesday, February 15, for their first visit. The casual, cheerful atmosphere made the meal feel like a pleasant welcome, and Isabella and Maria Lopez enjoyed the relaxed conversation over their breakfast while Isabella listened to the class.
