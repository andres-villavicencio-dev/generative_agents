On Wednesday, February 15, Isabella prepared a warm Smashed Avocado Toast with Lemon-Herb Butter as her first simple breakfast snack. She toasted the bread with generous slices of ripe avocado, lemon, and a touch of extra virgin olive oil, then drizzled the pan with melted butter seasoned with minced garlic, thyme, and a pinch of salt. The golden, buttery aroma filled the small café space as she prepared the meal.

Presented on a crisp white plate, the avocado toast had a bright, savory contrast between the creamy avocado and lemon zest, with the crisp bread and herbs completing the flavor. Isabella arranged the toast beside a small glass of water and a cup of freshly brewed coffee, creating an inviting first-morning setting.

The meal was made for a quiet early breakfast, intended to give her energy to begin her productive day. It reflected her friendly, grounded approach to life: simple, nourishing, and perfectly prepared for the moment.
