On Wednesday, February 15, Isabella prepared Hobbs Cafe’s “Cozy Clubhouse Sandwiches and Pastries Batch.” She prepared a grilled chicken club sandwich and vanilla-banana cream-filled pastries. The sandwiches used grilled chicken breast, toasted bread, lettuce, tomato, avocado, pickles, and mayonnaise, with a light mayonnaise dressing. The pastries included sweet banana cream and lemon-butter-filled croissants or rolls.

She grilled the chicken in a skillet, sliced it, and layered the ingredients on the bread. For the pastries, she mixed the banana and cream cheese, filling the pastry shells with the warm mixture. She arranged the sandwiches neatly on a platter and placed the pastries in a small decorative bowl, adding a splash of orange juice and a sprig of mint for color.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with the savory scent of grilled chicken, the rich sweetness of banana and cream, and a hint of citrus. The presentation was simple and welcoming: the platter looked fresh and hearty, while the pastries added a soft, appetizing contrast. This was prepared for the cafe’s morning lunch batch, intended to be served at the cafe’s open lunch hour as part of the hospitable community meal.
