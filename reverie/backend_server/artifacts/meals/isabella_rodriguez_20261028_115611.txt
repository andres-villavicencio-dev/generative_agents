For Wednesday afternoon, Isabella prepared Hobbs Cafe’s Spring Garden Sandwich for the cafe’s breakfast batch. The sandwich is served on a warm, toasted sourdough bun with a creamy honey-herb cheese spread, crisp roasted vegetable chutney, and fresh baby spinach with a hint of lemon and garlic.

She spread the cheese on one half of the bread, then layered the vegetables and spinach over the other half. After baking the sandwich until the edges were golden and crisp, she cut it diagonally and arranged the pieces neatly on a white plate. A small glass of chilled sparkling water and a sprig of fresh rosemary were placed beside it.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm, savory, and inviting—the buttery scent of the bread, the fresh herbs, and the crispness of the vegetables. It smelled like a relaxed afternoon meal, perfect for a quick lunch before the cafe’s opening. The presentation was bright and cheerful, making the sandwich look as ready-to-eat as it was to prepare.
