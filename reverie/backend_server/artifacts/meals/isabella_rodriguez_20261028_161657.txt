On Wednesday, February 15, Isabella prepared Hobbs Cafe’s Classic Club Sandwich for her friends and a small batch of Cinnamon Bun Pastries.

The club sandwich was made with toasted sourdough, a crisp lettuce wrap, sliced tomatoes, cucumber, ham, and Canadian bacon. The sides included creamy avocado toast and a side of warm fruit salad for a refreshing contrast. Isabella arranged the sandwiches on warm white plates, garnishing each with a fresh basil leaf and drizzling a little honey, while placing the pastries neatly beside them.

She toasted the sourdough, warmed the meats, and lightly warmed the fruit before assembling the sandwiches. The aroma filling the kitchen was warm, savory, and slightly sweet—her classic café scent. The pastries added a soft, cinnamon-sugar fragrance.

The occasion was Isabella’s first visit to Oak Hill College Classroom, where she hoped to meet other cafe owners and discover new ways to support community education. The meal reflected her friendly, hospitable nature: practical, comforting, and designed to bring people together.
