The meal Isabella prepared for the cafe was a Pan-Seared Chicken Sandwich with Herb-Garlic Roast Potatoes. The key ingredients were fresh chicken breast, whole-grain bread, herb-garlic butter, baby spinach, avocado, lemon slices, and crispy roasted potatoes.

Isabella seared the chicken in a hot pan with olive oil until the skin turned golden and crispy, then added the vegetables and butter for a quick pan-roast. She wrapped the lettuce and grilled cheese in the oven to keep the sandwich warm and juicy. The potatoes were roasted with garlic and rosemary until tender and caramelized.

The aroma was warm and inviting: savory chicken, herb-rich garlic, lemon, caramelized potatoes, and a subtle touch of rosemary. Isabella presented the sandwiches on wooden boards, plating each one whole-grain bread, with generous portions of crisp potatoes, fresh spinach, avocado, and crisp lettuce. Lemon wedges and a small glass of cold water were placed beside them.

The occasion was a relaxed cafe lunch batch—a simple, hearty meal prepared for customers to enjoy at Hobbs Cafe on a Wednesday afternoon. The warm, colorful presentation and balanced flavors made the food feel welcoming and comforting, fitting the relaxed atmosphere Isabella wanted to create.
