On Wednesday, February 15, Isabella prepared a Garden-Herb Chicken Sandwich with Warm Crostini for Hobbs Cafe’s morning breakfast batch. The sandwich features seasoned chicken breast, crisp lettuce, tomato slices, and a creamy garlic herb sauce, all wrapped in a lightly toasted, seeded brioche roll. The crostini are topped with toasted butter, chopped chives, and a drizzle of honey-lavender glaze.

The key ingredients include chicken, herbs, lettuce, tomato, garlic, brioche, and warm bread. Isabella marinated the chicken and toasted the brioche before combining them in a warm pan, adding the herbs and sauce for a simple, flavorful finish. The aroma was earthy, savory, and gently sweet, with hints of garlic and herbs carrying through the kitchen.

It was presented on a white ceramic plate alongside a small bowl of lemon-herb yogurt and several fresh fruit skewers, creating an inviting, casual breakfast spread. The occasion was a busy Wednesday at Hobbs Cafe, where Isabella wanted to serve guests with warm, comforting food while giving the cafe a welcoming, relaxed atmosphere.
