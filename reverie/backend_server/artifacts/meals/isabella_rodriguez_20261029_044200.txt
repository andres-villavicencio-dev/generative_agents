On Wednesday, February 15, Isabella prepared a hearty Sautéed Vegetable Breakfast Bowl with scrambled eggs, toast, and a fresh fruit-tart topping. The bowl featured crisp broccoli, carrots, onions, peppers, and tomatoes, seasoned with olive oil, garlic, salt, pepper, and a touch of lemon juice. The eggs were lightly sautéed until golden, while the toasted bread was brushed with butter and served with a warm fruit-tart topping of honey, granola, and fresh berries.

The aroma filled the kitchen with a comforting blend of savory garlic, caramelized carrots, and the bright tang of lemon. Isabella arranged the colorful ingredients in a warm, rustic wooden bowl, topped with a glossy drizzle of honey and sprinkled with toasted nuts and dried fruit, creating a cheerful presentation that matched the Valentine’s Day party atmosphere.

The meal was prepared for Isabella’s February 14th Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe, from 5:00–7:00 PM on Tuesday, February 14, 2023. It served as a welcoming, hearty breakfast to set a warm tone for the event, encouraging guests to relax and enjoy a memorable morning with her.
