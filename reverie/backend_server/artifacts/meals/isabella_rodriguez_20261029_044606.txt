On Wednesday, February 15, Isabella prepared a hearty breakfast for her friends and family, combining comfort with the promise of a satisfying lunch. The meal was a Pan-Roasted Chicken with Lemon Herb Butter served with creamy potatoes, sliced onions, and fresh spinach.

She began by warming the oven and preparing the lemon-herb butter in a pan, then carefully searing the chicken until golden and cooked through. The potatoes and onions were pan-seared to soften their flavors, while spinach was wilted until tender but still crisp. A generous amount of butter was poured over the cooked ingredients, and the dish was finished in the oven until the chicken was hot and the flavors were deeply integrated.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted poultry, fresh herbs, caramelized onions, and rich butter. The presentation was simple yet inviting: the golden chicken was arranged on a white ceramic platter with the potatoes and spinach arranged beside it, garnished with a few parsley leaves and a drizzle of lemon juice.

It was an informal, warm meal prepared for Isabella’s February 14 Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe, intended to make guests feel welcoming and comforted as they gathered to celebrate.
