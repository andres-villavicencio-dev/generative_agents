Isabella Rodriguez prepared a Valentine’s Day Cafe Breakfast Sandwich for the Hobbs Cafe party, served alongside homemade fruit tarts. The sandwiches feature crisp toasted sourdough, creamy avocado, crisp lettuce, fresh tomato, and a savory yogurt–herb dressing, finished with a sprinkle of smoked paprika. The fruit tarts include vanilla custard, fresh berries, and a cinnamon-sugar topping, arranged in a bright pink and gold basket.

The aroma of warm toasted bread, sweet custard, and fruit was unmistakable as Isabella arranged the treats. Each sandwich was sliced neatly and placed alongside the tart, while the basket held colorful decorations and handwritten notes. Isabella had customized a few toppings for the fruit tarts and prepared customized menu and counter notes for Klaus Mueller, adding a personal touch to the party.

The meal was a cheerful, romantic breakfast perfect for the Valentine’s Day gathering, combining comforting cafe classics with festive presentation.
