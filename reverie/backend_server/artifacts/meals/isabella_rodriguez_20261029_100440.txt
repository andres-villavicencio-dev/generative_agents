Isabella prepared Honey-creamed Egg with Fresh Fruit and Herbs, a warm breakfast dish designed to be the centerpiece of her February 14 Valentine’s Day party. It features a golden, perfectly set hard-boiled egg yolk topped with a generous drizzle of honey and melted butter, alongside crisp strawberries, blueberries, and a hint of lemon. The egg is lightly sautéed until the edges soften, then finished with a savory herb sauce and a bright citrus finish.

The kitchen smelled rich and inviting—golden honey, caramelized egg, and the sweet aroma of strawberries filling the air. Isabella presented the dish on a small white platter, arranging the colorful fruit around the egg in a playful, romantic pattern. A small card read “For Valentine’s Day, from Hobbs Cafe,” adding the occasion’s romantic touch.

The meal was simple, elegant, and ready to be shared during the café’s Valentine’s Day celebration, from 5:00–7:00 PM.
