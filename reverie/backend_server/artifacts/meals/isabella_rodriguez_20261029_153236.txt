On Friday, February 17, Isabella prepared a Valentine’s Day breakfast for customers at Hobbs Cafe. The dish was a Berry & Almond Fruit Salad with Golden-Crusted Toast, presented alongside hot oatmeal with cinnamon and a splash of almond milk, accompanied by a small bowl of warm blueberry yogurt.

The fruit salad featured fresh strawberries, raspberries, blueberries, and sliced almonds, tossed gently with a little honey and lemon juice. The golden-brown toast was lightly toasted and generously covered with toasted nuts, creating a satisfying crunch. The warm oatmeal was fluffy and fragrant, while the blueberry yogurt added a creamy, tangy twist.

The aroma filled the cafe with the sweet scent of fruit, toasted nuts, cinnamon, and the soft warmth of the oatmeal. It was a cheerful, inviting breakfast designed to celebrate Valentine’s Day while making customers feel welcome.
