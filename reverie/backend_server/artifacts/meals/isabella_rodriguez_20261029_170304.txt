Isabella prepared a Simple Roast Chicken with Lemon Herb Rice and Green Beans, a comforting Valentine’s Day breakfast meal. The dish featured a whole chicken rubbed with salt, black pepper, and dried oregano, then roasted until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. The rice was seasoned with lemon juice, garlic, vegetable broth, butter, and dried herbs, while green beans were lightly sautéed with olive oil, salt, and pepper.

As she cooked, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, herbs, and caramelized vegetables. Isabella arranged the chicken in the center of the plate, surrounded by bright green beans, crisp lemon slices, and a small bowl of lemon-herb rice. She topped it with a drizzle of melted butter and fresh herbs, creating an inviting, colorful presentation.

The meal was prepared for Friday, February 17, during Isabella’s Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe, scheduled for Tuesday, February 14. It was an informal, family-style breakfast intended to encourage guests to relax and enjoy a simple, home-style meal.
