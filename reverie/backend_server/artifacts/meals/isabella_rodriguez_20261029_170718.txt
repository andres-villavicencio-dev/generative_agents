On a quiet Friday morning, Isabella prepared a Garden Herb Crusted Chicken with Lemon-Parmesan Panada for herself and her family. The panada was made from flour, vegetable stock, onions, garlic, herbs, and a little water, while the chicken came from a fresh organic whole. Isabella washed it carefully, then seasoned it with salt, pepper, lemon, and a pinch of dried rosemary. She pan-seared the chicken first, then returned it to a hot oven to finish cooking, covering it with foil until the meat was tender and the panada remained crisp.

The kitchen filled with the inviting aroma of garlic, herbs, roasted chicken, and toasted flour. When she plated it, she arranged the golden-brown chicken on a white plate, surrounded it with bright green panada, and drizzled lemon, parsley, and extra Parmesan over the top. Isabella served the meal with crusty bread and a glass of water.

The occasion was a simple family dinner following her Valentine’s Day party preparations. Isabella was proud of the fresh, colorful presentation, and she believed the meal would bring comfort and joy to her family.
