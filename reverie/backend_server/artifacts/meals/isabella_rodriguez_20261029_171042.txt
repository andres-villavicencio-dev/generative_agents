Isabella prepared a simple Vegan Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry for home-cooked Friday dinner. The dish featured tender chicken thigh with carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, snap peas, and a mild ginger-lime garlic sauce.

She began by marinating the chicken in soy sauce, turmeric, garlic, and lemon juice, then stir-fried it until the meat was cooked through. The vegetables were quickly added to the pan, cooking briefly until they were crisp-tender. She finished the dish with sesame oil, chopped cilantro, and a squeeze of fresh lemon.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted chicken, savory ginger, and earthy vegetables. The presentation was bright and inviting: colorful vegetables arranged on a white plate, the golden chicken resting beside them, and a small bowl of sesame seed rice nearby.

Isabella served the meal to herself and her family, enjoying a relaxed, home-cooked dinner on an ordinary Friday. It was neither a celebration nor a formal occasion, but a simple, satisfying meal that reflected Isabella’s friendly, welcoming nature and her steady routine at Hobbs Cafe.
