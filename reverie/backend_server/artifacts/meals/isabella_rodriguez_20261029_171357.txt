Isabella prepared Hobbs Cafe’s “Valentine’s Egg & Bread Breakfast” for her Friday morning at home. The meal featured soft whole-grain bread, two eggs, avocado, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and a drizzle of lemon-herb vinaigrette. The eggs were cracked into a small pan, lightly beaten, and cooked over medium heat until golden and fluffy, then served with the warm bread, fresh vegetables, and a crisp side salad.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted bread, caramelized egg yolks, and the bright scent of lemon and herbs. On the dining table, the colorful salad, steaming eggs, and golden bread were arranged in a simple, heart-shaped centerpiece, with a red rose tucked beside the bread. The occasion was Isabella’s cozy Valentine’s Day celebration at Hobbs Cafe, where friends and family gathered to relax, share laughter, and enjoy a simple, welcoming meal.
