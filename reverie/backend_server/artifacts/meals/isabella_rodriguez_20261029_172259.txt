Isabella prepared a Vegan Garlic Herb Roast Chicken with root vegetables for her simple Friday dinner at Hobbs Cafe. The centerpiece was a whole chicken stuffed with roasted carrots, potatoes, and mushrooms, seasoned with garlic, fresh thyme, oregano, lemon zest, and smoked paprika. She roasted the chicken in a baking pan until the skin was golden brown and the interior reached 165°F, then sliced it against the grain.

The vegetables were roasted separately until crisp-tender, and the aromatics provided the meal with a fragrant, savory scent. As Isabella plated the dish, she arranged the golden chicken beside the colorful root vegetables and served it in a shallow ceramic bowl with lemon wedges and extra-virgin olive oil. The presentation felt warm and inviting, with a few herb sprigs and a sprig of rosemary completing the rustic aesthetic.

The occasion was a relaxed Valentine’s Day family dinner at home. Isabella served the meal at the table, sharing the warm aroma with Sam, Klaus, Carlos, Adam, Tamara, and Rajiv before eating together. The simple, comforting ingredients created a balanced, heartwarming meal suitable for celebrating the occasion.
