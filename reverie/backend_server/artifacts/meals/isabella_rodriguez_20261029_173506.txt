Isabella prepared a quick, colorful brunch lunch using fresh spinach, cherry tomatoes, sliced avocado, crisp cucumber, and fluffy eggs, served with a lemon-herb yogurt dressing. She made the dressing with plain yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice, chopped chives, and a pinch of cumin, then tossed the vegetables into a hot skillet for a bright, crispy finish. The eggs were poached or briefly fried to the perfect fluffy golden yellow.

The meal had a fresh, herbaceous aroma with the bright scent of lemon, green vegetables, and lightly roasted egg. It was presented on a bright white plate with the colorful ingredients arranged neatly around the center, accompanied by a small side of toasted sourdough and a glass of water.

This lunch was prepared to replenish Isabella’s kitchen essentials before cooking the meal, fitting into her busy Friday routine. It was a simple, nourishing meal, not the elaborate Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe.
