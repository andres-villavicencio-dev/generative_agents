The meal Isabella prepared is Hobbs Cafe Breakfast Bowl with Avocado Toast and Fresh Fruit. It combines warm whole-grain toast, soft-boiled eggs, cottage cheese, sliced avocado, honey-mustard dressing, fresh berries, and yogurt parfait.

She toasted the bread on the stove, cracked the eggs gently into a pan, and heated the cottage cheese until warm. The pan smelled like toasted nuts, herbs, and butter—warm, comforting, and inviting. She arranged the toast on a white plate, placed the colorful fruit atop it, and added a small side of yogurt.

The presentation was bright and cheerful: golden toast, creamy blue cheese, golden avocado, and vivid red and purple berries contrasted beautifully with the white plate. Isabella served it in a cozy cafe setting on Saturday, February 18, as a gentle Valentine’s Day meal. It was meant to be wholesome, celebratory, and a reminder of the friendly, welcoming atmosphere she helps create at Hobbs Cafe.
