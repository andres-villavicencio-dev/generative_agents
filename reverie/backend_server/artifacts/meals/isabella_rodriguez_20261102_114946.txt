The meal is a Buttery Sage Egg Breakfast made with two eggs, butter, fresh herbs, and a splash of orange juice. After the eggs were cooked, Isabella reheated them in a pan with softened butter and a few sprigs of sage, allowing the eggs to gently warm and absorb the rich, golden brown of the butter. She added a pinch of pepper for subtle flavor and topped the pan with fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of toasted butter, sage, and the soft crack of simmering eggs. She presented the breakfast on a white ceramic plate, arranging a small bowl of orange juice beside the pan and serving the eggs with a fork. It was prepared as a quiet, comforting mid-morning brunch, ideal for enjoying with friends or family during a relaxed Saturday at Hobbs Cafe.
