The meal is a clover honey croissant with fresh fig and clover honey coffee.

Isabella prepared the croissant by rolling out the dough, cutting it into fingers, and baking it until it golden and flaky. She brushed the edges with warm honey and sliced a fresh fig into the croissant before serving it alongside a cup of clover honey coffee. The coffee was steeped with honey, a touch of cinnamon, and a pinch of nutmeg, giving it a warm, aromatic richness.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the croissant baked—rich honey, nutty pastry, and the earthy scent of fresh fig. On the plate, the golden croissant rested beside the dark brown coffee and the vibrant red fig, creating a balanced, inviting breakfast.

The occasion was a relaxed Sunday celebration of love and connection. After completing the Valentine’s Day party, Isabella wanted to recreate the warmth of her favorite café. She prepared the meal with gentle care and was particularly excited to share it with Rafael Ibarra, who planned to bring his own clover honey coffee. It was a peaceful, thoughtful way to honor the celebration while enjoying the familiar comfort of home.
