Isabella prepared Valentine’s Day Roasted Chicken with Herbs and Fresh Tomatoes, a warm, family-style dinner intended for guests at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured a whole chicken roasted with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, paprika, and a splash of red wine, alongside bright red cherry tomatoes, carrots, onions, and a creamy lemon-garlic butter sauce.

She roasted the chicken at 375°F for about 45 minutes until the skin was golden and the juices were clear, then added the tomatoes and vegetables to the pan for a final few minutes. She basted the chicken with the sauce, letting the herbs and citrus infuse the meat throughout. The meal had a fragrant, savory aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and warm herbs.

Isabella plated the dish in a rustic white bowl, arranging the chicken in the center, tomatoes and carrots around it, and garnishing it with parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. The presentation was simple, inviting, and visually appealing, especially for Valentine’s Day. The occasion was a small, celebratory dinner for her cafe customers, combining relaxed cooking time with a festive evening gathering.
