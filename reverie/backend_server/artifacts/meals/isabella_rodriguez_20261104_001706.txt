Dish Name: Rosemary Garlic Herb Chicken with Lemon-Basil Rice and Roasted Vegetables

Key Ingredients: chicken thighs, garlic, fresh rosemary, lemon, olive oil, onion, carrots, celery, rice, and fresh herbs.

Preparation Method: Isabella sears the chicken with garlic and rosemary until the skin is golden, then finishes it in the oven. The rice is cooked with vegetable broth, herbs, and a little butter. Meanwhile, the carrots, celery, and onions are roasted until crisp-tender before being stirred in with the cooked chicken. The lemon and remaining fresh herbs are sprinkled over the dish.

Aroma: The kitchen fills with the warm scent of garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and roasted vegetables.

Presentation: The chicken rests on a bed of fluffy rice and colorful roasted vegetables, garnished with parsley, a lemon wedge, and a small sprig of rosemary.

Occasion: The hearty, family-style meal was prepared for Isabella’s Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe on February 14th, 2023, where she and her customers gathered to celebrate together.
