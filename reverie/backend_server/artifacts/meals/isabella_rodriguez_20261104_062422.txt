Isabella prepared Valentine’s Day Strawberry Lemonade for the Hobbs Cafe Valentine’s Day party. The drink features strawberries, fresh lemon juice, honey, a splash of sparkling water, and a pinch of cinnamon. She sliced the fruit, added the lemon, and stirred in the honey and spices before pouring in the sparkling water. The aroma was bright, floral, and warmly citrusy, with hints of crushed berries and toasted cinnamon.

The lemonade was served in clear glass tumblers, each garnished with a whole strawberry, lemon slice, and a cinnamon stick. Isabella presented the drinks in neat rows across the counter, making them look inviting and celebratory. It was prepared as a cheerful, relaxed beverage for the Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe on February 14, 2023, from 5 to 7 p.m., intended to delight her customers and set a joyful tone for the evening.
