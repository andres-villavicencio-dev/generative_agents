For Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared Floral Berry Lemon-Pepper Ham Sandwiches alongside a batch of Cinnamon-Oat Pancakes.

The sandwiches feature a crisp whole-grain roll, thinly sliced ham, creamy avocado, crisp lettuce, and a lemon-pepper dressing. A small bowl of mixed seasonal berries and whole-wheat toast adds a colorful finishing touch. The pancakes are made with oat flour, whole wheat flour, eggs, milk, and cinnamon, pan-fried until golden and fluffy.

Toasting the ingredients and frying the pancakes releases a warm, citrusy aroma of lemon and toasted nuts, while the fruit adds a subtle sweetness. The sandwiches are stacked on white ceramic plates with a small sprig of rosemary and a garnish of crushed mint.

Isabella serves the food in the cafe on Valentine’s Day, February 14, 2023, from 5:00–7:00 PM, creating a heartwarming Valentine’s Day celebration for the customers.
