On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared a warm Valentine’s Day Breakfast Sandwich for the cafe batch, along with a selection of homemade pastries. Her main dish consists of toasted sourdough bread, creamy avocado and smoked salmon, crisp lettuce, ripe tomatoes, a light mayonnaise dressing, and a few fresh herbs. She toasted the sourdough until the crust was golden and warm, then spread the dressing lightly and arranged the colorful vegetables and cured fish in neat rows across the top.

The pastries included croissants filled with cream cheese and honey, muffins baked with cinnamon, and cookies with chocolate chips. Isabella arranged them on plates and small platters, garnishing each with a dusting of sea salt and a few edible rose petals. The aroma of toasted bread, nutty sourdough, fresh herbs, and honey-filled pastries filled the cafe as she prepared the meal.

The meal was intended for the Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe, where Isabella invited customers to join her at 5:00 p.m. on February 14, 2023. The presentation was bright, inviting, and thoughtfully themed, reflecting her friendly, hospitable approach and her desire to make the cafe feel like a welcoming place for people to enjoy themselves.
