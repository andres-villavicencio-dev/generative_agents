Isabella prepared a warm Valentine’s Roast Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze. The centerpiece is a whole chicken that has been roasted until golden, then served with seasonal vegetables—carrots, onions, and green beans—tossed in olive oil, garlic, rosemary, and lemon. The chicken’s skin is crisped through the oven, while the vegetables retain a bright, caramelized sweetness.

Isabella prepares the vegetables in batches, tossing them with oil and herbs, then roasting them until they are tender yet slightly crispy. For the glaze, she mixes lemon juice, honey, Dijon mustard, and a touch of paprika, brushing it over the chicken just before serving. The aroma fills the kitchen: roasted chicken, garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and a hint of sweetness.

At Hobbs Cafe, the meal is presented simply and thoughtfully. The chicken sits on a platter, surrounded by colorful vegetables, with a small plate of warm crackers and a glass of sparkling water nearby. It is a cozy, romantic dinner prepared for Valentine’s Day at Hobbs Cafe, designed to make her customers feel welcome and ready to celebrate together.
