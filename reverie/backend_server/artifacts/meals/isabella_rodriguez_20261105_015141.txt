Isabella prepared a warm Valentine’s Day Breakfast Sandwich for the party. The dish was a toasted whole-grain bun topped with grilled turkey, smoked salmon, avocado, crisp lettuce, tomato, and a creamy garlic-herb cheese sauce. Herbed crackers and a side of fresh fruit rounds the spread.

She toasted the bread until golden, warmed the meats, and layered them with a generous drizzle of herb cream cheese and a sprinkle of toasted seeds. The meal was presented in a small ceramic platter, with the sandwich in the center, fruit arranged around it, and the remaining breakfast ingredients scattered attractively nearby.

The kitchen smelled of toasted bread, savory turkey, fresh herbs, and melted cheese. It had the inviting aroma of a cozy family breakfast, making the meal feel welcoming and celebratory. On February 14, it was both the first meal of the day and part of Isabella’s festive Valentine’s Day celebration at Hobbs Cafe.
