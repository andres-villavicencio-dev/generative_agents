On February 14, 2023, Isabella prepared a special Valentine’s Day brunch for the Hobbs Cafe customers. The main dish was Heart-Like Pancakes, fluffy golden pancakes topped with a colorful berry coulis, fresh strawberries, blueberries, and a drizzle of honeyed chocolate sauce. Pancake batter, sugar, vanilla extract, eggs, milk, and melted chocolate were whisked together, then lightly fried to a crisp exterior while staying soft inside.

The pancakes were arranged in a shallow serving dish and decorated with a red heart-shaped paper crown. Each plate also held a small “I love you” card and a glass of warm tea. The aroma filled the café with butter, sweet berries, vanilla, and the savory hint of chocolate.

The presentation was cheerful and romantic, with pastries, napkins, saucers, and party favors scattered around the table. Isabella’s friendly, hospitable demeanor made the meal feel warm and welcoming, while the Valentine’s Day party provided a cheerful occasion for her customers to enjoy a relaxed brunch together.
