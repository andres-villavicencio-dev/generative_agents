On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepares Romantic Red-Bean Chili with Crispy Chicken and Vegetables. The dish is a hearty, comforting home-style chili made with lean ground chicken, kidney and black beans, diced tomatoes, onion, garlic, cumin, chili powder, and a splash of hot sauce. She sautés the chicken and vegetables until golden, then gently simmers the beans and tomatoes in a rich, aromatic broth until the chili reaches a tender, thick consistency.

The kitchen fills with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, and warm chili. Each portion is served in a shallow white bowl, garnished with fresh cilantro and a generous dusting of crushed red pepper flakes. Isabella arranges the bowls neatly for guests, with a small bottle of hot sauce and a sprig of rosemary placed beside each plate, adding a touch of festive elegance.

The meal is prepared for Isabella’s Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe on February 14, 2023, from 5 to 7 p.m. Guests gather to enjoy the cozy, inviting dinner while Isabella serves and welcomes them.
