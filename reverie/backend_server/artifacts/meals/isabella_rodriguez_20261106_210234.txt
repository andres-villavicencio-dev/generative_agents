On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepares Chicken with Lemon Herb and Garlic for her Hobbs Cafe Valentine’s party. She seasons the chicken with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, dried oregano, salt, and pepper, then sears it in a hot pan until it develops a golden-brown crust. The pan is then filled with a colorful mix of cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, onions, and corn, followed by the chicken. She stirs it gently, allowing the flavors to meld while the vegetables soften and the chicken reaches an internal temperature of about 165°F.

The kitchen fills with the warm aroma of caramelized garlic, roasted vegetables, and lemon zest. Once the chicken is cooked, Isabella plates it in a rustic bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil. She adds a small bowl of crisp potatoes and a side of roasted asparagus for a complete, comforting meal. The presentation is bright and inviting, with contrasting colors and a few decorative herbs, while a handwritten card invites guests to join her at Hobbs Cafe on February 14th, from 5 to 7 pm. The dish perfectly reflects her friendly, hospitable spirit and the celebratory occasion.
