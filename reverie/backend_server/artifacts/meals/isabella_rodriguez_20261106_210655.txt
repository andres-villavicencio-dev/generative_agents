Isabella prepared Valentine’s Day Pasta with Roasted Vegetables and Garlic Cream Sauce, combining the cooked ingredients into one warm, comforting dinner. The dish includes penne pasta, cherry tomatoes, red onion, bell peppers, mushrooms, garlic, olive oil, fresh basil, and a rich garlic-herb cream sauce made with a little butter, heavy cream, and a touch of white wine.

She first sautéed the tomatoes, onions, peppers, mushrooms, and garlic in olive oil until the vegetables softened and the garlic released its aroma. She added the pasta and cooked it through, then poured in the sauce and simmered everything together. The finished meal had a warm, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, and roasted vegetables.

Isabella plated the pasta in individual bowls, garnishing each with fresh basil and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. The vibrant colors and creamy texture made the dish look inviting, especially for guests celebrating love. The meal was prepared as a cozy Valentine’s Day dinner at Hobbs Cafe, combining her signature pasta with seasonal vegetables and a comforting sauce, while the restaurant served it as a special gathering for 50 customers.
