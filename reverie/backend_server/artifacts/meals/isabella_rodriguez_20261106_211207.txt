On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepares Romantic Chicken with Herbs and Garlic Rice, a warm, comforting dinner fit for a cozy Valentine’s Day gathering. The dish features tender chicken thighs braised in a flavorful garlic-herb sauce, served over fluffy steamed rice and topped with crisp roasted red peppers and fresh parsley. Herbs of rosemary, thyme, and oregano give the chicken a fragrant, savory aroma, while garlic adds a warm, savory note.

Isabella places the dinner at the table, arranging the plate in the center and carefully placing the cutlery around it. The meal is presented as a warm, inviting Valentine’s lunch for her customers and guests, with the chicken, colorful vegetables, and golden rice creating a cheerful, festive table setting. The occasion combines celebration with the relaxed, welcoming atmosphere that Hobbs Cafe is known for—making the restaurant feel like a place where everyone can enjoy a delicious meal together.
