Isabella prepared Valentine’s Day Herb-Crusted Chicken with Root Vegetables and a Lemon-Herb Glaze, a cozy dinner fitting her hospitable, welcoming style. The key ingredients were bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, parsnips, baby potatoes, spinach, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a sweet-and-sour lemon glaze.

She marinated the chicken in oil, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a touch of honey, then seared it until golden. Afterward, she roasted the vegetables until tender and lightly browned the chicken before slicing it and arranging it around a bed of colorful vegetables. The dish was finished with the bright, tangy lemon glaze, giving it a warm aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, and citrus.

Isabella presented the meal in a rustic ceramic bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil. It was a simple yet elegant Valentine’s Day dinner, reflecting her love for making others feel welcome and creating a homey, comforting meal for her customers.
