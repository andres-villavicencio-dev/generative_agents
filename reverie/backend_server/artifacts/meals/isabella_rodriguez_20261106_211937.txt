On February 13, Isabella prepared a cozy Valentine’s Day dinner for her family at home. The meal was Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Garlic Butter, presented on a rustic wooden table with a red rose and a small card reading “For the best-loved.”

Isabella used a whole chicken with skin on, bone-in pieces of rosemary and thyme, carrots, parsnips, and potatoes. She roasted everything in the oven until the chicken was golden, moist, and easily pulled apart. The vegetables were lightly caramelized, and the chicken was served with a rich garlic butter sauce.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted poultry, herbs, garlic, and caramelized carrots. Isabella arranged the food in a colorful, layered dish, with golden chicken resting atop the bright vegetables. The presentation felt warm, inviting, and celebratory, perfectly suited for sharing a romantic evening with family.
