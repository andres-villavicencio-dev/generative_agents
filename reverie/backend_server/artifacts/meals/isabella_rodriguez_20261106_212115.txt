On February 13, 2023, Isabella prepared Garden-Scaped Roast Chicken with Lemon-Basil Herb Stuffing, a comforting Valentine’s Day dinner for her Hobbs Cafe customers.

The chicken is seasoned with garlic, herbs, olive oil, and a touch of lemon, then roasted until golden and tender. It is served with fluffy stuffing made with rice, celery, onion, herbs, and a savory herb cream sauce, along with seasonal vegetables such as carrots, green beans, and roasted potatoes.

The kitchen fills with a warm, inviting aroma of roasted poultry, caramelized onions, herbs, and citrus. The meal is plated on a rustic wooden board, with each piece of chicken arranged around the stuffing, vegetables, and a small glass of red wine.

On Valentine’s Day, Isabella hopes the meal will warm the guests’ hearts as they gather at Hobbs Cafe from 5:00–7:00 p.m. to celebrate their love.
