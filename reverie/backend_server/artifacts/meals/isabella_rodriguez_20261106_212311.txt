On Monday, February 13, Isabella prepared a Caramelized Butter Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables, a family-style meal she planned for her Valentine’s Day party. The centerpiece was a whole chicken, glazed with honey, garlic, and roasted brown sugar, surrounded by carrots, potatoes, and Brussels sprouts that had been sautéed in olive oil. She used bone-in skin-on chicken breasts and a small amount of chicken stock to make the sauce, finishing it with lemon zest and a pinch of cayenne for warmth.

The kitchen smelled rich and aromatic: caramelized onions, roasted garlic, and the sweet smell of honey and lemon mingled with the savory aroma of herbs and warm spices. The chicken was golden and succulent, while the vegetables remained crisp-tender. She arranged the food on a white platter and served each portion in a small bowl, garnishing the sides with fresh parsley.

The meal was meant to celebrate love and warmth, giving customers and guests a cozy, comforting dinner to prepare for the party. Isabella’s friendly, welcoming style made the kitchen feel like a welcoming gathering space, and the colorful presentation suggested a cheerful, celebratory occasion.
