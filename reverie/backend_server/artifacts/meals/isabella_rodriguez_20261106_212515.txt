Isabella prepared a colorful and hearty Valentine’s Day pasta dinner: Pesto Chicken with Roasted Veggies and Lemon-Herb Gravy. The dish features boneless chicken thighs, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, bell peppers, and carrots, tossed with a rich basil-pesto, garlic, and olive oil. Chicken is seared until golden, then simmered in a savory lemon-herb gravy with the vegetables and pasta until tender and coated.

The meal has a warm, savory aroma of garlic, basil, roasted vegetables, and toasted pasta. It is served in elegant white bowls over a bed of white rice, with the chicken arranged beside the vegetables, garnished with chopped parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

Isabella made the pasta and cooked it while gathering Valentine’s Day party supplies, including a card, decorations, and a playlist of romantic music. The dinner was prepared for her planned Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe on February 14, 2023, from 5:00 to 7:00 p.m., where she hoped to welcome her customers and make them feel comfortable and delighted.
