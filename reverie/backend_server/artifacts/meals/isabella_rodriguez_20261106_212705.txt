On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared Hearts of Garlic Chicken with Lemon-Herb Glaze and Roasted Root Vegetables for a warm family-style dinner. She used boneless chicken thighs, fresh garlic, lemon, rosemary, thyme, olive oil, red wine, and a splash of white wine. The chicken was seared until golden, then simmered in the flavorful sauce until tender. Root vegetables such as carrots, onions, parsnips, and potatoes were roasted until crisp, then arranged around the pan.

The kitchen check revealed a perfectly browned crust and evenly cooked chicken, while the vegetables retained their vibrant color. As she plated the dish, the aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory chicken filled the dining area—warm, inviting, and unmistakably celebratory. Each piece of chicken was placed beside a colorful medley of roasted vegetables, with a small bouquet of red roses and a handwritten Valentine’s card on the table.

The presentation was elegant yet approachable, making the meal feel appropriate for Isabella’s Valentine’s Day party. She was about to serve it to her guests when the final check reminded her to tidy the dining area, ensuring every dish was neatly arranged and the space was clean and welcoming.
