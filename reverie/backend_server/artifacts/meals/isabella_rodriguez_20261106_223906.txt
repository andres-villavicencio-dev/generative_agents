Isabella prepared Sunny-Side-Up Eggs with Herb-Crusted Toast and Fresh Fruit for her Valentine’s Day brunch. The breakfast featured two perfectly golden, lightly browned eggs, a warm slice of sourdough crusty baguette, bright cherry tomatoes, sliced figs, a lemon wedge, and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt. She brushed the egg tops with a little olive oil, cooked them in a nonstick skillet over medium heat, and toasted the bread until the crust was crisp, then added the toppings and arranged them in a colorful bowl.

As she prepared it, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, rich egg yolks, and the sharp scent of fresh herbs and lemon. She plated the eggs and toast in a rustic ceramic bowl, garnishing it with a sprig of thyme and a lemon twist. The presentation was casual and inviting, with the bright fruit and herbs adding a cheerful touch of Valentine’s Day. It was a simple, comforting meal designed to bring people together for her Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe.
