Isabella prepared a Valentine’s Day Garden Salad for her celebration. The dish features crisp lettuce, cherry tomatoes, red bell peppers, cucumbers, and cherry tomatoes, topped with toasted almonds, feta cheese, lemon zest, and a light balsamic vinaigrette.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: the salad was arranged on a white ceramic platter with a decorative “Love” sign, and each serving was garnished with a sprig of parsley. It was a cheerful, romantic dish designed to match the lively Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe, where Isabella’s friendly hospitality would make guests feel welcome and happy.
