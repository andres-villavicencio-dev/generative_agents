On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared Garden-Grilled Chicken with Asparagus and Lemon-Herb Sauce, a simple yet festive lunch for the Hobbs Cafe Valentine’s Day party. The dish featured breast chicken seasoned with garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and olive oil, alongside crisp asparagus spears, cherry tomatoes, and a bright lemon-herb sauce. She washed and trimmed the vegetables, peeled the chicken, and roasted it and the asparagus over a hot pan until golden and tender, finishing the sauce with a touch of butter.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasting chicken, herbs, garlic, and lemon. The meal was presented on a white platter with colorful vegetable garnishes, accompanied by a small bowl of lemon-herb sauce and fresh fruit. The presentation was cheerful and inviting, fitting the cozy, romantic mood of the celebration.

The lunch served Isabella’s customers and guests before the 5:00–7:00 PM party, adding a relaxed, wholesome touch to the Valentine’s celebration.
