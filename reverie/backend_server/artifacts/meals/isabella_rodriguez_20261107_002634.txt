On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared a Rosemary Herb Crusted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables. The main dish features boneless chicken thighs, onions, carrots, and bell peppers tossed in olive oil, rosemary, garlic, lemon juice, and a hint of thyme. The vegetables are roasted at 400°F for 25–30 minutes until tender and caramelized, while the chicken is pan-seared until golden and cooked through.

Aromatic rosemary and garlic permeate the kitchen with a warm, herbaceous scent, while fresh lemon adds a bright citrus finish. The dish is plated on a rustic white platter with a simple red rosemary sprig, alongside warm whole-wheat bread and crusty baguette. Isabella adds a small bowl of warm ranch dressing and fresh berries for dessert, creating a colorful, inviting spread.

The meal serves the Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe, where Isabella’s friendly and hospitable approach turns the gathering into a relaxed celebration of love, food, and community.
