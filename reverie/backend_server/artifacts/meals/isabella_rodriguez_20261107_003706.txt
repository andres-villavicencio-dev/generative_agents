On February 14, Isabella prepared a Valentine’s Day Garden Salad for the Hobbs Cafe party. The dish featured crisp arugula, cherry tomatoes, ripe pomegranate seeds, cucumbers, feta cheese, and toasted almonds. Her dressing combined olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a hint of honey.

To prepare it, Isabella washed the vegetables, tossed them with the dressing, and arranged the salad in a large serving bowl. She topped it with a few slices of fig and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil, then added a small sprig of thyme and crushed red pepper flakes for color and aroma. The salad was garnished with white flowers and served with a small plate of crudités and a side of warm honey mustard.

The kitchen filled with a bright, herbaceous scent of basil, lemon, garlic, and toasted nuts, which Isabella used to make everyone feel welcome. The presentation was colorful, elegant, and inviting, with the pale greens and reds of the vegetables contrasting against the creamy white bowl.

It was the centerpiece of the Valentine’s Day celebration at Hobbs Cafe, designed to give guests a refreshing, romantic lunch to enjoy from 5:00 to 7:00 PM.
