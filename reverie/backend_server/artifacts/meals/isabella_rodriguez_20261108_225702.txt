Isabella prepared a romantic Valentine’s breakfast for her cafe, Spiced Maple-Honey Baked Oatmeal with Fresh Berries. The dish uses rolled oats, milk, cinnamon, nutmeg, warm maple syrup, honey, chopped walnuts, dried cranberries, and sliced strawberries. It is gently baked in a ceramic dish until the oatmeal is tender and the top is lightly browned, then sliced and served warm.

The aroma fills the kitchen with cinnamon, toasted nuts, and the warm sweetness of honey and maple. Isabella presents the oatmeal in a white ceramic bowl, surrounded by a colorful arrangement of berries and a small white cup of freshly brewed coffee, making the meal feel inviting and celebratory.

On Tuesday, February 14, at 5:00 p.m., the dish would be served during Hobbs Cafe’s Valentine’s party. It would be a comforting, festive centerpiece for guests relaxing, socializing, and enjoying the morning together.
