On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared Egg and Breakfast with Coffee and Toast, a warm, comforting breakfast she prepared herself before welcoming guests to Hobbs Cafe. The meal featured two sunny-side-up eggs, fluffy scrambled eggs, crisp toast, honey mustard, sliced red pepper, and fresh herbs, along with a cup of freshly brewed coffee.

Isabella washed the eggs, cracked them into a bowl, and prepared the scrambled eggs in a pan with butter and garlic. She seasoned the eggs with salt and pepper, then arranged the eggs, toast, and toppings on a platter. The kitchen smelled rich and inviting—butter, garlic, toast, and coffee mingled with the aroma of cinnamon and vanilla.

The presentation was bright and cheerful, with golden eggs arranged around a plate of colorful toppings, a cup of coffee nearby, and a handwritten invitation. The occasion was a Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe on February 14th, 2023, from 5:00–7:00 PM, where Isabella invited her customers to enjoy a relaxed, festive breakfast in the cafe’s cozy atmosphere.
