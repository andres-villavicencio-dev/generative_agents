On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared Valentine’s Berry-Infused Pancakes with Strawberry Compote and Toasted Walnuts. The key ingredients were ripe strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, eggs, whole wheat flour, milk, vanilla, cinnamon, and unsalted butter. The pancakes were made by lightly battering the eggs and milk, then cooking them in a non-stick pan until golden with crispy edges. Between batches, the berries were gently warmed in a small pot, creating a fragrant, colorful compote. The finished pancakes were brushed with a little more butter, sprinkled with toasted walnuts, and served with a side of warm toast, honey, and a sprig of rosemary.

The aroma filled the cafe with the sweet scent of berries and vanilla, accompanied by the warmth of butter and the subtle spice of cinnamon. She presented the pancakes in a rustic white ceramic dish, arranged alongside a rustic wooden tray holding the compote, toasted pastries, and a small basket of the walnut-topped pancakes. The setting was bright and inviting, with rose petals and red accents adding a soft romantic feel.

The meal was prepared for Isabella’s Valentine’s Day breakfast party at Hobbs Cafe on February 14, 2023, from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., designed to celebrate love and provide guests with a warm, memorable morning treat.
