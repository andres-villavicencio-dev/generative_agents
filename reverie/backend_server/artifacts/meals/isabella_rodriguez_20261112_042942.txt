On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared a Valentine’s Day Cafe Sandwich & Pastry Batch with a fresh fruit salad. The sandwiches feature toasted sourdough, creamy hummus, crisp romaine lettuce, ripe tomatoes, cucumbers, Kalamata olives, and a lemon-herb vinaigrette, topped with a generous layer of melted brie and shaved almonds. The pastries include buttery croissants, almond croissants, and delicate chocolate macarons. Each croissant is lightly brushed with browned butter, while the macarons contain a small amount of vanilla bean paste.

The meal was prepared in batches, with the sandwiches stacked on a large tray and the pastries arranged neatly nearby. A bright red card reading “Valentine’s Day Party” decorated the counter, and the fruit salad added a lively splash of color. The aroma was warm and inviting: toasted nuts, melted cheese, citrus, herbs, and a hint of chocolate mingled with the fresh scent of the fruit.

The occasion was a lively Valentine’s Day celebration at Hobbs Cafe, where Isabella invited customers to join her for a festive lunch and dessert. The meal was both celebratory and cozy, offering a sweet, flavorful meal in a relaxed setting perfect for couples and friends gathering over coffee and conversation.
