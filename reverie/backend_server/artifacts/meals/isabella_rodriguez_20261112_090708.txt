On Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared Valentine’s Heart Soup to share with her cafe guests during the party. The soup was a comforting, light broth made with vegetable stock, carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, and a touch of cream. She added fresh parsley, a handful of small thyme leaves, and a pinch of red pepper for a gentle kick, with a few crushed red beans and crushed red potatoes to give it a heart-shaped appearance.

The ingredients were gently simmered until the carrots and potatoes were tender, then finished with fresh herbs and a splash of lemon juice. The aroma was warm and inviting: sweet carrots, savory onion, earthy root vegetables, and a delicate hint of rosemary.

It was presented in a clear glass bowl with a small red heart-shaped card reading “Love & Comfort.” Each guest received a small portion, making the meal feel personal and communal. The simple, nourishing soup was chosen to match the warm, romantic atmosphere of the Valentine’s Day party, where Isabella and her customers gathered from 5:00–7:00 p.m. at Hobbs Cafe.
