The meal is Lemon-Ginger Croissant with Fresh Lemon-Infused Yogurt.

Isabella prepares a flaky, buttery croissant by folding butter into dough, letting it rest, and baking it until golden. She fills it with lemon zest, grated ginger, cinnamon, and a hint of cardamom before baking again. The warm croissant is paired with a glass of chilled lemon yogurt, a dollop of fresh cream, and a drizzle of honey.

The aroma is bright and inviting: the rich, buttery scent of the croissant mingling with the zesty tang of lemon, the warm fragrance of ginger, and the subtle warmth of cinnamon. The presentation is rustic yet elegant—golden croissants arranged on a linen plate, accompanied by the creamy yogurt and a small bowl of fresh berries.

On Valentine’s Day, Isabella serves the pastries to Carlos and others for a light breakfast, then shares them afterward during the café’s celebration. The meal reflects her welcoming, hospitable nature and her desire to make Hobbs Cafe a place where everyone feels welcome, relaxed, and happy.
