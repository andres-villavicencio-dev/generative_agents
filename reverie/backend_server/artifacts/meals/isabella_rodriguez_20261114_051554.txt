For Valentine’s Day, Isabella prepared Hearty Valentine’s Chicken and Veggie Stir-Fry. The dish features tender chicken thighs, bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas, carrots, and a handful of edamame, seasoned with olive oil, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a touch of honey. The sauce balances savory soy with a delicate sweetness from the honey, while the vegetables add a bright, crisp texture.

Isabella stir-fried the ingredients over high heat, letting the chicken brown before tossing everything together. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, ginger, and caramelized vegetables. She plated the meal in a large white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the chicken and garnishing it with a sprig of fresh cilantro and a small lemon wedge.

On February 14th, 2023, the restaurant would serve the stir-fry as part of Isabella’s Valentine’s Day party, encouraging customers to join for a lively gathering of food, music, and friendly conversation.
