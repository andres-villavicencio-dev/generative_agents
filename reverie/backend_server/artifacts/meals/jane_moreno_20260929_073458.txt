Jane Moreno prepared Harmony in a Bowl, a nourishing vegetable and quinoa salad with roasted sweet potatoes, kale, cherry tomatoes, and toasted pumpkin seeds. The key ingredients were seasonal root vegetables, fresh herbs, and a light lemon vinaigrette. She roasted the sweet potatoes at home, then tossed them gently with chopped kale and diced tomatoes, drizzling olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of sea salt. The aroma was warm and herbaceous—earthy from the squash and bright from the citrus. Served in a rustic ceramic bowl on her kitchen table, it gleamed under morning light. Jane chose this dish for its ease and elegance, honoring her daily rhythm with mindful nourishment. It’s a quiet celebration of simplicity, where each bite reflects balance—perfectly suited to her serene Thursday evening, as she savors the meal before unwinding in the garden at dusk.
