Morning’s quiet ritual ends with a steaming bowl of Harmony in a Bowl—her choice for both nourishment and joy. The dish features creamy avocado mousse swirled into warm farro, tossed with cherry tomatoes, toasted pumpkin seeds, and a drizzle of lime‑chili vinaigrette. A handful of fresh cilantro crowns the bowl, adding bright green contrast. The aroma is earthy yet lively, a whisper of citrus cutting through nutty warmth. Served in a handcrafted ceramic bowl on her kitchen table, it’s simple yet elegant—a celebration of home‑cooked care. Jane prepared it to welcome her family into the day with light, wholesome energy, honoring their shared rhythm and her own gentle mindfulness.
