Jane Moreno’s dinner is Harmony‑in‑a‑Bowl, a comforting blend of quinoa, roasted sweet potatoes, kale, avocado slices, toasted pumpkin seeds, and a zesty lemon‑cumin vinaigrette. She prepares it by combining cooked quinoa with cubed sweet potatoes tossed in olive oil, spices, and a splash of lime; while they rest, she sautés garlic and ginger into the warm kale before folding everything together. The bowl’s aroma is earthy yet bright, with a whisper of citrus. Presented on a rustic ceramic plate at the dining table, the vibrant colors draw attention to its balance. Jane serves it as a nourishing, home‑cooked meal for her family—an everyday celebration of simplicity and care.
