Dinner Prepared by Jane Moreno

Dish Name
Harmony-in-a-Bowl Gratin

Jane prepared a comforting roasted vegetable gratin as her evening centerpiece—a warm, family-friendly dish that reflects her caring spirit and love for nourishing meals.

Key Ingredients
Roasted butternut squash and sweet potatoes
Creamy garlic-infused béchamel sauce
Fresh thyme and nutmeg seasoning
Grated Gruyère cheese
A drizzle of olive oil

Preparation Method
Jane began by roasting the vegetables until golden and tender, then layered them with the velvety béchamel sauce. She sprinkled generous shavings of Gruyère on top and baked under a warm oven's glow until the surface turned bubbly and lightly browned—a gentle technique that preserves every flavor.

Aroma
The kitchen filled with a rich, savory warmth: earthy sweetness from the roasted vegetables mingling with nutty, creamy notes from the melted cheese, finished by the comforting perfume of thyme.

Presentation
Served in warm ceramic bowls, each gratin glistened with a golden crust, garnished with fresh chives for a pop of green against the amber layers.

Occasion
A peaceful Thursday evening meal shared around her dining table—a quiet moment of gratitude and rest before the day winds down.
