Meal: Roasted Chicken with Garlic-Herb Potatoes and Steamed Green Beans

Jane Moreno carefully placed the seasoned, golden-brown chicken on the center of the dining table, surrounded by vibrant green steamed beans and clusters of tender, rosemary-infused potatoes. The key ingredients included a whole chicken rubbed with sea salt, black pepper, minced garlic, and fresh rosemary from her garden, alongside baby potatoes, olive oil, and lemon slices.

For preparation, Jane first dusted and organized the living room, then seasoned the chicken and tucked lemon slices and sprigs of rosemary into the cavity. She arranged the potatoes and beans on a baking sheet, drizzling everything with olive oil before sliding the roast into the preheated oven. As the meal cooked, a savory aroma of roasting garlic and earthy herbs filled the home, mingling with the faint scent of dusted furniture.

The presentation was rustic yet elegant; the chicken glistened with rendered fat, and the potatoes were crisp on the edges. This wholesome dinner was prepared for her husband, Tom, after his shift at The Willow Market, marking a cozy end to a productive Monday evening.
