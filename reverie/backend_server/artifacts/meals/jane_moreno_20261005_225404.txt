Dish Name: Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Herb Quinoa and Roasted Asparagus

Key Ingredients: Fresh Atlantic salmon fillet, high-quality olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, quinoa, vegetable broth, fresh dill, parsley, and thick asparagus spears.

Preparation Method: Jane began by rinsing the quinoa and simmering it in vegetable broth with minced garlic and a handful of fresh herbs until fluffy and fragrant. Meanwhile, she patted the salmon dry and seasoned it simply with salt, pepper, and a drizzle of olive oil. She heated a skillet over medium-high heat and seared the fillet skin-side down for four minutes before flipping it to cook the flesh for another three minutes, ensuring a crisp exterior and moist interior. For the sides, she tossed the asparagus in oil and lemon zest, roasting them in the oven until charred and tender.

Aroma: The kitchen was filled with the bright, citrusy scent of lemon and the earthy, nutty fragrance of toasted quinoa, complemented by the savory, slightly smoky aroma of seared fish and roasted vegetables.

Presentation: Jane plated the salmon atop a generous mound of the herb-infused quinoa, arranging the vibrant green asparagus spears leaning against the side. A thin wedge of lemon and a sprig of fresh dill were placed on top of the fish for a pop of color.

Occasion: This was a nutritious weeknight dinner prepared for Jane and her husband, Tom, after his long shift at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, designed to be both satisfying and light.
