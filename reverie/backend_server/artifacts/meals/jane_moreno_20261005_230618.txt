The meal Jane Moreno prepared is Herbed Salmon with Roasted Asparagus and Garlic Mashed Potatoes.

Key ingredients include fresh salmon fillets, tender asparagus spears, creamy russet potatoes, butter, milk, garlic, and a blend of dried dill and parsley.

For preparation, Jane begins by peeling and cubing the potatoes to boil until tender, mashing them with warm milk and roasted garlic for a smooth consistency. Meanwhile, she seasons the salmon with olive oil, salt, pepper, and the fresh herbs, then sears it skin-side down in a hot pan before finishing it in the oven. The asparagus is tossed in olive oil and roasted alongside the potatoes until caramelized and crisp-tender.

The aroma that fills the home is a savory blend of buttery potatoes, searing fish, and earthy roasted greens. The presentation is immaculate; the flaky white salmon sits atop a vibrant bed of green asparagus, flanked by a pristine mound of white mashed potatoes, garnished with a sprig of fresh parsley. This wholesome, home-cooked dinner is prepared for a cozy family occasion, reflecting Jane’s organized nature and her desire to provide a nutritious, comforting meal for her husband, Tom, after his shift at the market.
