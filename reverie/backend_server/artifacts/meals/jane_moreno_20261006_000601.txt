Dish Name: Herb-Crusted Salmon with Roasted Asparagus and Lemon-Garlic Quinoa

Key Ingredients: Fresh salmon fillets, thick asparagus spears, tri-color quinoa, lemon, garlic, olive oil, fresh dill and parsley, sea salt, and black pepper.

Preparation Method: Jane began by rinsing the quinoa and cooking it in salted water with minced garlic and a squeeze of lemon juice. While the quinoa simmered, she patted the salmon fillets dry and created a vibrant herb crust by mixing chopped fresh dill, parsley, olive oil, lemon zest, salt, and pepper. She seared the salmon skin-side down in a hot skillet until crispy, then flipped it to finish cooking under a loose tent of foil. Meanwhile, she tossed the asparagus with olive oil, salt, and lemon slices, roasting them in the oven until tender-crisp and lightly charred.

Aroma: The kitchen was filled with the bright, clean scent of citrus and fresh herbs, punctuated by the savory, oceanic aroma of searing salmon and the earthy, nutty fragrance of toasted quinoa.

Presentation: Jane plated the meal with care, placing a generous scoop of the pale, speckled quinoa at the base. She arranged the vibrant pink salmon atop the grain, the herb crust glistening, and stood the roasted asparagus spears elegantly around the protein. A final drizzle of lemon-infused olive oil and a sprinkling of fresh herb sprigs added a pop of color.

Occasion: This was a deliberate effort to celebrate the end of a long week. Jane wanted to prepare a nutritious, restaurant-quality meal for her husband, Tom, to help him relax after working at The Willow Market and Pharmacy. It was a simple yet thoughtful gesture of love and care.
