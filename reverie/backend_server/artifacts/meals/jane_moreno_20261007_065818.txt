Jane Moreno prepared a comforting Herbed Chicken and Vegetable Skillet. Leveraging her organized nature and love for making life enjoyable for her family, she likely utilized fresh produce from her recent errand run at The Willow Market.

The dish features tender, bone-in chicken thighs seasoned with a blend of rosemary, thyme, and garlic, seared to a golden brown to lock in juices. Key ingredients include vibrant red bell peppers, sweet carrots, and creamy baby potatoes, all simmering in a light broth enriched with olive oil and a splash of white wine. 

The preparation method involved a straightforward one-pan approach: sautéing the aromatics, browning the chicken, and allowing the vegetables to soften and absorb the savory flavors. The aroma that filled her home was inviting and warm—the sharp, earthy scent of fresh herbs mingling with the sweet caramelization of the vegetables and the savory scent of roasting chicken.

Presentation was rustic yet appealing; the chicken and vegetables were artfully arranged in the cast-iron skillet, garnished with a sprinkling of fresh parsley for a pop of color. The occasion was a midweek respite, a deliberate pause in her busy schedule to provide her family with a nutritious, home-cooked meal that felt both special and sustaining.
