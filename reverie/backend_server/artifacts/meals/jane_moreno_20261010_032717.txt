Dish Name: Spaghetti Carbonara with Garlic Butter
Key Ingredients: Spaghetti, eggs, cured meats (prosciutto or pancetta), cheese (parmesan or guanciale), and black pepper.
Preparation Method: The pasta is boiled until al dente. Eggs and grated cheese are whisked together until smooth. The egg mixture is gently poured over the drained pasta, and the meat and pepper are added on top. The dish is covered and left to rest for several minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
Aroma: A warm, savory scent of toasted garlic and rich cheese fills the kitchen, evoking the cozy atmosphere of the evening.
Presentation: The pasta is plated neatly, with the egg and cheese sauce spooned gently over the top, and the meat and pepper scattered artistically around the edges.
Occasion: This is Jane's special dinner time, a quiet moment of preparation before she heads to bed around 10pm. The meal reflects her organized nature and love for simple, comforting classics.
