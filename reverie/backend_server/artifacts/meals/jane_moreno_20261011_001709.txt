Dish Name: Classic Roasted Vegetables and Sweet Potato Wedges with Herbed Butter

Key Ingredients:
2 medium sweet potatoes, sliced into wedges
1 cup broccoli florets
1 red bell pepper, sliced
1/2 cup olives, halved
3 cloves garlic, minced
2 tablespoons olive oil
Fresh herbs: basil and chives
Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation Method:
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Toss sweet potato wedges, broccoli, and bell peppers with olive oil, minced garlic, salt, and pepper.
3. Arrange vegetables on a baking sheet and roast for 25-30 minutes until tender-crisp.
4. In a separate pan, sauté the halved olives and remaining garlic until golden.
5. Melt butter in a small bowl and whisk in fresh herbs until aromatic.
6. Plate the roasted vegetables, drizzle with herb butter, and garnish with extra chopped chives.

Aroma: The dish features a delightful blend of earthy roasted vegetable notes, warm garlic fragrance, and the fresh, herbaceous brightness of the basil and chives.

Presentation: The meal is served on a rustic ceramic plate, arranged in a colorful radial pattern. The glistening olive oil and butter pools around the edges, while the contrasting textures of the firm sweet potatoes and tender greens create a visually appealing contrast.

Occasion: A warm evening of family dinner, celebrating a productive day at the market and preparing for a relaxing bedtime routine.
