Dish Name: Simple Roasted Vegetables with Herbs
Key Ingredients: Bell peppers, onions, garlic, and fresh herbs (basil and cilantro).
Preparation Method: The vegetables are sliced and tossed with oil, salt, and spices. They are roasted in a preheated oven at 200°C (400°F) for about 20 minutes until crisp-tender.
Aroma: Freshly cut vegetables emit a crisp, aromatic smell reminiscent of a gentle breeze on a summer day.
Presentation: The dish is served in a small, rustic ceramic bowl, arranged in a neat circular pattern with the herbs garnishing the center.
Occasion: A relaxing evening dinner for the family, prepared to accompany leisure time on the living room couch.
