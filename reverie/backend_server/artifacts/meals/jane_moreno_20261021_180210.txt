For lunch on Monday, February 13, Jane prepared Lemon-Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa. The main ingredients were chicken breasts, carrots, zucchini, red onion, cherry tomatoes, garlic, fresh rosemary, lemon, and quinoa.

She seasoned the chicken with lemon zest, garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper, then pan-seared it until golden before roasting it with the vegetables at 375°F. The quinoa was simmered in vegetable broth until fluffy and fluffy grains were folded in with chopped parsley. The dish filled the kitchen with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, lemon, and savory chicken.

Jane served it family-style on a white platter, arranging the golden chicken beside colorful roasted vegetables and a neat mound of quinoa, with lemon wedges on the side. It was a comforting Monday family lunch before Tom returned to work at The Willow Market and Pharmacy. Jane packed the remaining chicken and vegetables into separate airtight containers and refrigerated the quinoa so they could be enjoyed later in the week.
