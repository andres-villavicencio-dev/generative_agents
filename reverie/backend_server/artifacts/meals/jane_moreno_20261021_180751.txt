Jane Moreno prepared a warm bowl of creamy tomato-basil pasta for Monday lunch. The dish combines penne, ripe tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, fresh basil, and a silky Parmesan cream sauce, with spinach and mushrooms adding color and substance. She sautéed the mushrooms and garlic in olive oil until fragrant, simmered the tomatoes with salt and pepper, then tossed the pasta with the sauce, Parmesan, and torn basil. A little spinach was folded in at the end to keep it bright and tender.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of garlic, herbs, and warm tomatoes, balanced by the richness of Parmesan. She plated the pasta in shallow white bowls, twirling the penne neatly in the center and spooning extra sauce over it, then garnished it with basil leaves and grated cheese. The meal was intended as a comforting family lunch on an ordinary February Monday—simple, wholesome, and prepared with Jane’s characteristic care for Tom and their home.
