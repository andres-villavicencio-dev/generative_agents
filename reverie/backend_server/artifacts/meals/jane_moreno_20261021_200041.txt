On Monday, February 13, Jane Moreno prepared Leftover Roast Chicken and Vegetable Soup for the family’s midday meal. The key ingredients included tender pieces of Sunday’s roast chicken, carrots, celery, onions, red potatoes, peas, fresh thyme, and savory chicken broth. Jane reheated the soup gently, adding fresh parsley and a splash of cream for warmth and richness, then portioned it into food-safe containers.

The aroma was comforting and aromatic, filled with the savory scent of herbs, roasted poultry, and sweet vegetables. She garnished each serving with a small sprig of parsley and served it alongside crusty whole-grain bread and crisp cucumber salad, giving the simple lunch a colorful, tidy presentation.

The occasion was an easy Monday family lunch, made from wholesome leftovers to save time and reduce waste. Jane let the hot soup cool safely before covering and refrigerating the containers, ensuring it remained fresh and ready to enjoy.
