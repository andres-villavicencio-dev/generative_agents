Jane prepared Garlic-Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Baby Potatoes, a comforting Valentine’s Day dinner for her and Tom.

The dish featured bone-in chicken breasts seasoned with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, salt, and pepper. Jane washed and trimmed carrots, broccoli, zucchini, and red bell peppers, then chopped them into colorful pieces. Baby potatoes were halved and boiled briefly before being roasted alongside the vegetables. The chicken was pan-seared until golden, then roasted with the vegetables until the meat was tender and the potatoes were crisp.

Its aroma filled the kitchen with roasted garlic, herbs, and warm citrus. Jane arranged the golden chicken in the center of a serving platter, surrounded by bright vegetables and glossy potatoes, garnished with fresh parsley and lemon slices. The elegant presentation made the ordinary family meal feel warm and celebratory—fitting for sharing a quiet Valentine’s dinner at home.
