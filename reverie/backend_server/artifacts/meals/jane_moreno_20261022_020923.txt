Jane prepared Rosemary Roast Chicken with Crispy Herb Potatoes for a cozy Valentine’s Day dinner at home with Tom. The dish features a whole chicken seasoned with garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon, black pepper, and olive oil, accompanied by evenly cut potatoes, onions, and carrots.

She peeled and precisely portioned the potatoes before boiling them until just tender. She then tossed them with olive oil, chopped rosemary, and a pinch of salt, roasting them alongside the chicken until the skin was golden and crisp while the meat remained juicy and tender. The carrots and onions softened in the pan, soaking up the savory drippings.

As it roasted, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of herbs, roasted garlic, and slow-cooked poultry. Jane arranged the golden chicken at the center of a serving platter, with the potatoes and vegetables tucked around it and fresh rosemary sprigs for color. It was an intimate, comforting meal made especially for celebrating their love on Tuesday, February 14.
