Jane Moreno prepared a Valentine’s Day Herb-Roasted Chicken with Garlic Vegetables, a comforting centerpiece for the family dinner on Tuesday, February 14. The dish features a whole chicken seasoned with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, olive oil, and butter, accompanied by roasted potatoes, carrots, onions, and colorful seasonal vegetables.

She carefully arranged the chicken and vegetables in a roasting pan, then placed them in the oven, monitoring the timer and basting the chicken to keep it golden and succulent. The vegetables absorb the chicken’s savory juices as they roast, becoming tender and lightly caramelized.

As the meal finishes, the kitchen fills with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, herbs, garlic, and sweet caramelized vegetables. Jane carves the chicken and presents the vegetables family-style on a large platter, garnishing them with fresh herbs for an inviting, homemade appearance. The meal is served around 6 p.m. as a relaxed Valentine’s celebration, expressing her love and care for Tom and the rest of the family.
