Jane prepared Rosemary Lemon Roast Chicken with roasted potatoes and carrots, a hearty, comforting dinner for Valentine’s Day. The key ingredients include a whole chicken, fresh rosemary, lemon, garlic, olive oil, Yukon Gold potatoes, carrots, and onions, seasoned with sea salt and black pepper.

She rubbed the chicken with the herb-and-lemon mixture, stuffed the cavity with lemon halves and rosemary sprigs, then roasted it until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. Once removed from the oven, she placed the chicken on a cutting board to rest before carving. The potatoes and carrots roasted around it, absorbing the flavorful pan drippings.

The kitchen would have filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, rosemary, garlic, and caramelized vegetables. Jane arranged the carved chicken on a platter with golden potatoes, glazed carrots, fresh parsley, and lemon wedges, giving the meal an inviting, celebratory appearance. She served it as a cozy Valentine’s Day dinner for Tom and the family, reflecting her thoughtful, nurturing nature.
