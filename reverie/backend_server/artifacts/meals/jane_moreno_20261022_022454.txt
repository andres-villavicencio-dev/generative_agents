Jane Moreno prepared Garlic-Butter Green Beans as a side dish for a cozy Valentine’s Day dinner. The fresh green beans were trimmed and cooked in a skillet with butter, minced garlic, a splash of water, salt, and black pepper. Covered and simmered until the beans were tender while still retaining a slight crispness, the dish was then ready to serve.

The kitchen would have filled with a warm, savory aroma of softened garlic and gently cooked vegetables. Jane arranged the glossy green beans in a small white ceramic bowl, keeping their bright color and inviting, polished appearance. The dish complemented the rest of the family dinner without overwhelming it, reflecting her organized, thoughtful approach to home cooking.

The meal was prepared for Tuesday, February 14, when Jane was at home with her husband, Tom, and their family. It was an intimate, affectionate occasion made special by the care Jane put into preparing dinner and setting the table.
