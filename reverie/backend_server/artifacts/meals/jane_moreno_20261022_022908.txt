Jane prepared rosemary-roasted potatoes with garlic green beans, a simple, comforting family dinner for Valentine’s Day. The dish featured firm Yukon Gold potatoes, fresh green beans, olive oil, minced garlic, rosemary, salt, and pepper. Jane parboiled the potatoes before roasting them until their edges were crisp and golden, then sautéed the green beans briefly so they remained bright and slightly crisp. As they finished cooking, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted potatoes, herbs, and gently sautéed garlic.

She transferred the potatoes and green beans into matching serving bowls, arranging the vegetables neatly and adding a small sprig of rosemary for color. The meal was presented family-style on the dining table, ready for Jane and Tom to enjoy together. It was an intimate Tuesday evening celebration, reflecting Jane’s thoughtful nature and her desire to make even an ordinary dinner feel warm and special.
