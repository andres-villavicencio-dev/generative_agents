On Tuesday evening, Jane prepared a Valentine’s Day Herb-Roasted Chicken Supper, served with rosemary potatoes, roasted carrots, and creamy tomato soup. The chicken was rubbed with olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, lemon zest, salt, and pepper, then roasted until the skin turned golden and the juices ran clear. Jane parboiled the potatoes, tossed them with chopped rosemary, and roasted them alongside the vegetables. The soup was gently simmered with tomatoes, onions, garlic, and a touch of cream.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasting garlic, fresh herbs, and buttery potatoes. At the table, she arranged the golden chicken in the center, spooned soup into each bowl, and placed the crisp vegetables and potatoes beside neatly set plates. Candlelight gave the simple presentation a warm, romantic glow. The meal was prepared especially for Jane and Tom, celebrating their love and the caring family life they share.
