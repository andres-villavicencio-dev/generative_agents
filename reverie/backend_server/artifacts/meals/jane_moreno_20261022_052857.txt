Jane prepared a Valentine’s Garden Chicken Salad for lunch on Wednesday, February 15. She thoroughly washed crisp romaine lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, carrots, and bell pepper, then rinsed the chickpeas and quinoa under cool water. While the chicken marinated in lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, oregano, and a touch of honey, she chopped the vegetables and prepared a simple lemon-herb vinaigrette. She grilled the chicken until golden, allowed it to cool, and sliced it before arranging everything on a platter.

The meal smelled fresh and savory, with hints of garlic, oregano, lemon, and warm honey. Jane presented the colorful salad on a white plate, fanning the chicken across the top and scattering tomatoes, almonds, and crumbled feta for a festive appearance. It was a relaxed, Valentine-themed lunch shared with Tom—an expression of her care for her family and an early taste of the garden-inspired meals she hopes to create.
