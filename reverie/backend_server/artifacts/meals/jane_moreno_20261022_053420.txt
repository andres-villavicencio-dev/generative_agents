Jane Moreno prepared a Valentine’s Garden Vegetable Stir-Fry for lunch with Tom. The dish included sliced chicken breast, zucchini, yellow bell pepper, asparagus, cherry tomatoes, red onion, garlic, and fresh basil, finished with a savory-sweet sauce of soy sauce, honey, lemon juice, and ginger. She began by sautéing the garlic and onion until fragrant, browned the chicken, then added the prepared vegetables and stirred them over medium heat until crisp-tender. The warm aroma of ginger, garlic, caramelized chicken, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen.

She served the colorful stir-fry over fluffy rice, arranging the vegetables attractively and garnishing the plates with basil and a small heart-shaped piece of red pepper. The meal felt festive, homey, and thoughtfully prepared. Jane shared it with Tom on Valentine’s Day, celebrating their affection while looking forward to gathering inspiration from Tamara and Carmen’s garden for their own.
