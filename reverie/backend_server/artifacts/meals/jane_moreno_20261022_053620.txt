Jane prepared a Valentine’s Garden Pesto Pasta with roasted tomatoes and fresh mozzarella, a colorful, comforting meal designed for sharing with Tom. The sauce began with basil leaves, garlic, toasted pine nuts, Parmesan, and extra-virgin olive oil, blended until smooth and fragrant. She simmered cherry tomatoes until their skins softened and their juices became sweet, then folded them into the pesto. Fettuccine was boiled until tender, drained, and tossed with the sauce, while torn mozzarella added a creamy contrast.

The finished dish filled the kitchen with the warm aroma of basil, garlic, tomatoes, and toasted nuts. Jane plated it in shallow white bowls, twirling the pasta into neat nests and arranging the tomatoes and mozzarella on top like a small heart. A sprig of basil and a dusting of Parmesan completed the presentation. It was served with crusty bread and a glass of mint-infused water, making the lunch feel both elegant and relaxed—a Valentine-inspired celebration of their life together.
