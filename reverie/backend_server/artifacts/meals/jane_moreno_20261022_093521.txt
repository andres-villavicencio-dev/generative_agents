Jane prepared a Garden Vegetable Frittata with Herb-Roasted Potatoes for Thursday’s family lunch. The meal featured fresh eggs, bell peppers, zucchini, cherry tomatoes, onions, baby spinach, and Parmesan cheese, alongside baby potatoes roasted with rosemary, garlic, and olive oil. She washed and carefully cut the vegetables with the family, then sautéed the onions and peppers until soft, added the remaining vegetables, and folded in the eggs and cheese. The frittata was baked until golden and firm, while the potatoes roasted until crisp and tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of rosemary, garlic, eggs, and lightly browned cheese. She presented the frittata on a white platter, garnished with fresh parsley, with the golden potatoes arranged alongside and a small bowl of dressed garden greens. It was a cheerful, nourishing midday meal shared with her family, reflecting Jane’s organized, caring nature and her growing interest in growing more vegetables for their own garden.
