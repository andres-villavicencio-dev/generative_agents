Jane prepared Golden Vegetable Rice with Herb-Roasted Chicken, a colorful family lunch inspired by what she had seen in Tamara and Carmen’s garden.  

The dish used long-grain rice simmered with vegetable broth, diced carrots, peas, scallions, and fresh parsley. Boneless chicken thighs were seasoned with lemon zest, garlic, smoked paprika, and olive oil, then roasted until golden and tender. Jane folded the sliced chicken and garden vegetables into the fluffy rice, adding a handful of spinach for freshness and a drizzle of lime juice before serving.  

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, smoky spices, citrus, and freshly steamed vegetables. She arranged the food on a broad white platter, placing the bright vegetables beside the golden chicken and garnishing it with chopped herbs. A small bowl of avocado-tomato salad and warm tortillas completed the table.  

The meal was prepared for Thursday’s family lunch, creating an inviting opportunity for everyone to gather, share conversation, and enjoy a taste of the inspiration Jane hoped to bring home to the Moreno family’s own garden.
