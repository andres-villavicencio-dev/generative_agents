Jane Moreno prepared Garden-Inspired Herb-Seasoned Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for Thursday’s family dinner. The dish featured bone-in chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and freshly crushed black pepper. Alongside the chicken, she roasted carrots, red potatoes, onions, and zucchini with a little salt and honey until the vegetables were tender and lightly caramelized. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, citrus, and savory chicken.

She plated the meal family-style on a large white platter, arranging the golden-brown chicken over colorful vegetables and garnishing it with chopped parsley and lemon slices. Warm crusty bread and a simple green salad completed the presentation.

The occasion was an informal Thursday dinner with Tom and the rest of the family. The meal reflected Jane’s nurturing, organized nature and her enthusiasm for creating inviting family moments, while the fresh herbs and vegetables echoed her hope of growing a similar garden for the Moreno household.
