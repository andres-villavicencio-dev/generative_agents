Jane Moreno prepared a hearty Garden Vegetable and White Bean Soup for Thursday’s family dinner. The dish combined canned white beans, tomatoes, carrots, celery, onions, and garlic with the condiments she had neatly organized in the pantry. A drizzle of olive oil, a handful of fresh herbs, and a touch of smoked paprika gave the simple ingredients a warm, rustic flavor.

She began by sautéing the chopped vegetables until the onions turned translucent and the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, herbs, and gently caramelized carrots. The canned tomatoes and beans were then added with vegetable broth, and everything simmered for about half an hour until the broth became rich and fragrant. Lemon zest and chopped parsley brightened the finished soup.

The meal was ladled into deep white bowls and garnished with olive oil, cracked pepper, and extra parsley, creating an inviting contrast of colors. It was served with crusty bread and a simple green salad for a cozy Thursday supper—an unhurried family meal that reflected Jane’s organized, nurturing nature and her desire to bring more garden-inspired freshness to the Moreno household.
