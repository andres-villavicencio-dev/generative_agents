Jane prepared Garden-Inspired Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for Thursday dinner. She checked the refrigerator first and discarded a few wilted herbs and a cloudy bottle of dressing before gathering crisp carrots, golden potatoes, onions, fresh lemon, garlic, thyme, and chicken breasts from the pantry.

She marinated the chicken briefly in lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, oregano, and thyme, then seared it until golden and finished it in the oven. Alongside it, she roasted the root vegetables with olive oil, rosemary, and a pinch of salt until their edges caramelized. The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory chicken.

She plated the meal family-style on a cream-colored platter, arranging colorful vegetables around the sliced chicken and garnishing it with fresh parsley and lemon wedges. The occasion was an informal Thursday family supper, inspired by Jane’s recent visit to Tamara and Carmen’s garden and her wish to bring more freshness and variety to the Moreno family table.
