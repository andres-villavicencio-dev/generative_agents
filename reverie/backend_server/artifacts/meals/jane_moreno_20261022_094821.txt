For Thursday dinner, Jane Moreno prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Lemon Thyme Potatoes and Garden Vegetables. The main ingredients were chicken thighs, Yukon Gold potatoes, carrots, green beans, fresh garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon, and olive oil. She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic, and dried herbs, then roasted it until the skin was golden and the meat tender. The potatoes were parboiled and roasted with lemon juice and thyme, while the vegetables were lightly sautéed to preserve their bright color and crisp texture.

As it finished cooking, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, herbs, garlic, and caramelized potatoes. Jane arranged the meal on a simple white platter, placing the chicken beside the potatoes and neatly aligned vegetables, garnished with a sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge. The dinner was prepared for her family, inspired by the freshness and abundance she admired in Tamara and Carmen’s garden.
