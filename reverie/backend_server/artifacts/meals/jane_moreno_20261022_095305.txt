Jane Moreno prepared Garden-Hearth Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a warm, family-style dinner inspired by her visit to Tamara and Carmen’s garden. Boneless chicken thighs were seasoned with a pantry blend of smoked paprika, garlic powder, cumin, dried oregano, chili flakes, salt, and black pepper, then marinated briefly in olive oil and a touch of lemon juice. Alongside the chicken, she roasted carrots, Yukon potatoes, onions, and bell peppers until their edges caramelized and their centers turned tender.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of roasted garlic and peppers, warm cumin, oregano, and gently smoky paprika, with a subtle citrus brightness cutting through the savory spices. Jane arranged the golden chicken and colorful vegetables on a large white platter, garnishing them with fresh garden herbs and a drizzle of pan sauce. It was prepared as a comforting Thursday dinner for her family—a simple, nourishing meal intended to bring everyone together around the table.
