For Thursday dinner, Jane prepared Lemon-Herb Roast Chicken with Rosemary Root Vegetables and a Warm Shallot Vinaigrette. She organized the pantry, checking that olive oil, dried herbs, lemons, garlic, and grains were stocked, then mixed a dressing of shallots, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, and extra-virgin olive oil.

The chicken was rubbed with salt, pepper, garlic, thyme, and lemon zest before being roasted with potatoes, carrots, onions, and fresh rosemary. Its aroma filled the kitchen with savory roasted skin, citrus, and fragrant herbs. The golden-brown chicken was presented on a large platter, surrounded by colorful vegetables and garnished with lemon slices and rosemary sprigs, while the vinaigrette was served in a small glass pitcher for drizzling.

The meal was prepared for an ordinary Thursday family supper, reflecting Jane’s nurturing nature and desire to make something wholesome yet special for her family.
