Jane Moreno prepared a warm, satisfying Farmhouse Breakfast Skillet for herself and her husband, Tom, on Monday morning. The dish combines scrambled eggs, lightly sautéed onions and bell peppers, diced smoked ham, baby spinach, and golden-brown breakfast potatoes. She began by setting the breakfast table with clean napkins, sturdy plates, and polished utensils, then cooked the potatoes until crisp, folded the vegetables and ham into the softly scrambled eggs, and finished the skillet with a sprinkle of cheese and fresh herbs.

The meal fills the kitchen with a savory aroma of toasted cheese, sizzling ham, and roasted potatoes, complemented by the faint sweetness of coffee brewing in the pot. She serves the skillet in the center of the table, with the eggs slightly golden and the potatoes arranged around the edges, creating a colorful, inviting presentation. This is a relaxed weekday breakfast shared by Jane and Tom before beginning their daily responsibilities, with Tom preparing to head to The Willow Market and Pharmacy.
