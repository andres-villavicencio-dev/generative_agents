Jane Moreno prepared Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Soup with Warm Rolls for Monday dinner. The key ingredients were chicken breast, carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, frozen peas, garlic, chicken broth, thyme, and a touch of cream. She began by sautéing diced onions, carrots, and celery in olive oil until fragrant. She then added diced chicken, garlic, potatoes, and broth, simmering everything for about 30 minutes. Near the end, she stirred in the peas and cream, seasoning the soup with salt, pepper, and thyme. The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of savory broth, herbs, and gently roasted vegetables. She ladled the creamy soup into deep white bowls and garnished each with a small sprig of fresh thyme, serving it alongside golden, crusty rolls brushed with butter. The meal was presented simply but invitingly, ideal for a relaxed family dinner to welcome the beginning of the week.
