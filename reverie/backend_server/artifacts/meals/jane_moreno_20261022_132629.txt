Jane Moreno prepared Hearty Vegetable and Chicken Soup with Warm Garlic Bread for lunch. The key ingredients included chicken broth, tender chicken thighs, carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, peas, fresh parsley, thyme, and a splash of cream. She began by washing her hands thoroughly, then sautéed the onions and celery until soft and fragrant. Vegetables were added next, followed by the broth and chicken, which simmered gently until the meat was tender and the potatoes cooked through. Seasonings were adjusted before stirring in the peas, cream, and chopped parsley.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of herbs, garlic, and simmering chicken. Jane served the creamy soup in white bowls, garnished with parsley, alongside crisp, golden garlic bread toasted with olive oil and fresh butter. The meal was arranged neatly on the family table, creating a warm, inviting presentation.

The occasion was an ordinary Monday lunch, prepared especially for Jane and her husband, Tom, before he returned from his shift at The Willow Market and Pharmacy.
