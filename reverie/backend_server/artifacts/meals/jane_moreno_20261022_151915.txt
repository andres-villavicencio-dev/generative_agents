Jane Moreno woke at six o’clock on Monday, February 13, and began her morning routine quietly so she could prepare breakfast before Tom left for his shift at The Willow Market and Pharmacy.

Meal: Hearty Garden Vegetable Omelet with crispy breakfast potatoes, whole-grain toast, and fresh fruit.

Key ingredients: Eggs, spinach, diced tomato, bell pepper, onion, cheddar cheese, russet potatoes, butter, whole-grain bread, and seasonal fruit.

Preparation method: Jane peeled and cubed the potatoes, then pan-fried them until golden and crisp. She whisked the eggs with chopped vegetables and cheese, poured the mixture into a skillet, and cooked it gently until just set. The toast was warmed in the same skillet with a little butter, while the fruit was arranged on two plates.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the warm scent of buttery potatoes, toasted bread, and savory eggs.

Presentation: The omelet was folded neatly beside the potatoes, with toast and colorful fruit arranged in separate portions for Jane and Tom.

Occasion: A simple, nourishing Monday breakfast prepared for the couple before starting their busy day.
