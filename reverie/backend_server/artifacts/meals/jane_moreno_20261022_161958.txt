Jane Moreno’s Family Garden Sandwich and Fresh Herb Salad

Jane prepared a wholesome lunch featuring a vegetable sandwich layered with crisp lettuce, ripe tomato, sliced cucumber, creamy avocado, and shredded chicken, finished with a tangy mustard-mayo spread. The sandwich was lightly toasted to give the bread a golden, crunchy exterior while keeping the vegetables fresh and juicy.

Alongside it, she made a refreshing garden salad with chopped lettuce, cucumber, tomato, carrot, and bell pepper. A simple lemon vinaigrette—olive oil, lemon juice, a touch of honey, salt, and pepper—brightened the vegetables. Fresh basil leaves added a fragrant, peppery note, while the toasted sandwich contributed a warm, savory aroma.

The meal was neatly presented on a white plate, with the sandwich cut diagonally and the colorful salad arranged in a separate bowl. It was a simple family lunch, prepared with Jane’s usual care and organization for her husband, Tom, and herself.
