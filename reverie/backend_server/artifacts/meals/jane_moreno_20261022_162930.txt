Jane Moreno prepared a hearty grilled turkey-and-cheddar sandwich with a crisp garden salad for Monday lunch. The sandwich, commonly called a Turkey Melt, was made with sliced turkey, sharp cheddar cheese, lettuce, tomato, and creamy mayonnaise, layered between slices of lightly toasted wheat bread. She cooked it in a skillet until the cheese melted and the bread became golden.

Alongside it, she tossed a refreshing salad with romaine lettuce, cucumber slices, cherry tomatoes, shredded carrots, and red onion, dressed with a simple vinaigrette of olive oil, lemon juice, and herbs. The meal smelled warm and savory from the toasted bread and melted cheese, balanced by the salad’s fresh, clean aroma. Jane arranged the sandwich on a plate with the salad neatly beside it, adding a few tomato wedges for color. The inviting presentation suited an everyday family lunch, prepared with her characteristic care and organization for Tom and herself.
