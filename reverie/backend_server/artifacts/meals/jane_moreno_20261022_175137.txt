Jane Moreno prepared a comforting Herb-Roasted Chicken with Creamy Mashed Potatoes and Pan-Seeded Carrots for Monday’s family dinner. The main ingredients were chicken thighs, garlic, lemon, thyme, rosemary, butter, milk, carrots, sunflower seeds, and a little chicken stock. She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and dried herbs, then roasted it in the oven until the skin was golden and the meat tender. While it cooked, she prepared smooth mashed potatoes with butter and warmed milk, and sautéed the carrots with garlic and sunflower seeds.

The kitchen filled with a savory aroma of roasted garlic, lemon, herbs, and butter, making the evening feel warm and welcoming. She arranged the golden chicken in the center of a serving platter, spooning the creamy potatoes alongside it and adding bright orange carrots for color. The meal was neatly garnished with fresh parsley and served on simple white dishes. This was an ordinary weekday family dinner, but Jane made it special by gathering the ingredients carefully and presenting everything with her characteristic organization and care.
