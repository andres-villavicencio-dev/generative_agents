For Monday’s family dinner, Jane Moreno prepared herb-roasted chicken with rosemary potatoes and pan-seared vegetables. The main ingredients were bone-in chicken thighs, Yukon Gold potatoes, carrots, zucchini, garlic, lemon, olive oil, thyme, rosemary, and a simple seasoning blend of salt, black pepper, and paprika.

Jane began by preheating the oven and roasting the potatoes until they were partially tender. She then placed the seasoned chicken on top, monitoring the heat closely to keep the skin crisp while ensuring the meat cooked evenly. Meanwhile, she sautéed the vegetables in a skillet, adjusting the garlic and herbs until the kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma.

The meal was arranged family-style on a large white platter, with golden chicken at the center, browned potatoes around it, and bright vegetables added for color. Jane garnished the dish with fresh lemon slices and sprigs of rosemary before setting the table.

The occasion was an ordinary Monday evening meal, prepared with care so Tom could return from his work at The Willow Market and Pharmacy to a welcoming, comforting family dinner.
