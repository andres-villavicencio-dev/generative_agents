Jane Moreno prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Garlic Mashed Potatoes and Steamed Green Beans for Monday’s family dinner. The main ingredients were bone-in chicken breasts seasoned with rosemary, thyme, olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, salt, and black pepper. The potatoes were boiled with peeled garlic, then mashed with butter and warm milk until creamy. Fresh green beans were lightly steamed with a pinch of sea salt.

Before serving, Jane checked that the chicken reached a safe internal temperature and that the potatoes and vegetables were thoroughly heated. The kitchen smelled wonderful—warm roasted chicken, savory garlic, fresh herbs, and a hint of lemon.

She arranged the golden-brown chicken on a white dinner plate, with a generous scoop of fluffy mashed potatoes beside it and bright green beans along the side. A small sprig of rosemary rested on top for an elegant touch. The meal was intended for an ordinary but loving family dinner with her husband, Tom, made pleasant and comforting after his day at The Willow Market and Pharmacy.
