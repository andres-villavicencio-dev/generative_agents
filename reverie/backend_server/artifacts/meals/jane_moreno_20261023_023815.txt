On a crisp Monday morning, Jane prepared a family breakfast of coffee and toasted eggs. She poured freshly brewed coffee from the kettle into a white mug and served it to Tom, while she prepared two sunny-side-up eggs with a slice of lemon, a few sprigs of parsley, and a drizzle of olive oil.

The meal was simple and comforting, with the rich aroma of roasted coffee mingling with the savory scent of the eggs. Jane cracked the eggs into a small pan, added water and a pinch of salt, and left it over medium heat until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft. She served the eggs on a plate of warm bread.

For the occasion, Jane arranged the coffee, eggs, and toast in a warm wooden table setting. The bright, cheerful presentation reflected the family’s cheerful and organized nature. She made the morning meal with intention, hoping that the warmth of the coffee and the easy breakfast would bring Tom and the family together.
