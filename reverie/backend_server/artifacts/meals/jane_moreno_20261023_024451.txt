Jane Moreno prepared a Pan-Pan Breakfast for her family. The meal featured fluffy pancakes, warm oatmeal, sliced bananas, and a drizzle of maple syrup.

She cooked the pancakes in a skillet over medium heat, flipping them until golden and slightly crisp. Meanwhile, she prepared the oatmeal by boiling water in a bowl and stirring in oats, milk, and a few cinnamon sticks. A small bowl of jolly jacks or apple slices was placed beside it for a quick fruit snack.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted coffee, caramelized pancakes, and cinnamon. Jane presented the meal on a simple white plate, arranging the pancakes in a neat circle around the oatmeal and fruit. The bright colors and comforting textures made it an inviting breakfast for the family during her morning routine.
