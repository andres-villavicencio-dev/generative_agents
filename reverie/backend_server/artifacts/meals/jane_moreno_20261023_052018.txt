Jane prepared Herbed Lemon-Herb Chicken and Creamy Tomato Soup for family dinner. The dish featured tender chicken thighs, pearl onions, carrots, celery, parsley, garlic, and fresh herbs, with the soup seasoned with lemon juice, herbs, and a touch of cream.

She began by heating vegetable oil in a large pot, searing the chicken until golden on both sides, then browning the vegetables until they were lightly crisp. After removing the chicken and setting it aside, she sautéed the onions, carrots, and celery in the same pot, adding garlic and the remaining herbs. She returned the chicken to the pan, poured in chicken broth and a splash of water, then covered it and let it simmer gently until the chicken was fully cooked and the soup had thickened. The aroma of garlic, herbs, and simmering broth filled the kitchen.

She plated the chicken on a large platter and served the rich, smooth soup with the extra chicken and vegetables, garnishing it with fresh parsley. The meal was a cozy, comforting dinner for Jane’s family, celebrating a quiet Monday evening together as she took care of everyone’s needs.
