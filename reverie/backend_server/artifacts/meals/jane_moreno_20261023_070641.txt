Jane prepared Valentine’s Day Vegetable Paella for a special breakfast. The dish included rice, sautéed chicken breast, colorful bell peppers, onions, tomatoes, garlic, parsley, and a light broth, with a pinch of paprika and a splash of lemon for brightness.

The ingredients were sautéed in a pan until the chicken was golden, then mixed with the rice and cooked gently until it absorbed the flavors. Jane stirred in the vegetables and simmered everything briefly until the rice was tender but still firm. She finished the dish with a few fresh herbs and served it over a warm plate.

As she plated it, the kitchen filled with aromatic notes of garlic, herbs, and roasted chicken, giving the meal a warm, inviting aroma. Jane arranged the colorful vegetables and golden chicken around the rice in a neat, festive presentation, garnishing it with a sprig of parsley. The bright colors suggested a cheerful celebration, and the simple, fresh ingredients made it feel both elegant and accessible.

She served a cup of orange juice with it and placed the dish on the table for breakfast, ready to enjoy it with Tom on Valentine’s Day.
