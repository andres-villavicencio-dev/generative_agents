Jane prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce, a family dinner that reflected her organized, caring nature and Tom’s busy schedule.

She began by washing and cutting chicken breast, carrots, red bell peppers, onions, and potatoes. After marinating the chicken in olive oil, garlic, herbs, and lemon juice, she roasted it at 425°F for 20 minutes, then added the vegetables and roasted them for another 15–20 minutes until the chicken was golden and tender. She made a bright, aromatic sauce by simmering the pan juices with fresh parsley, thyme, lemon zest, and a splash of vinegar.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of roasted garlic, herbs, and savory citrus. She presented the meal in a simple white dish, arranging the golden chicken over the colorful vegetables and drizzling the glossy lemon sauce over the top. The warm, inviting presentation made the meal feel both comforting and celebratory.

The occasion was a relaxed family dinner on Tuesday, February 14, before Jane met Tom again at 12:00 PM. After sharing the meal with Tom, she and the family enjoyed the aroma and laughter, while Jane reflected on how satisfying it had been to manage the store, check the pharmacy and kitchen, and help prepare a dinner for everyone.
