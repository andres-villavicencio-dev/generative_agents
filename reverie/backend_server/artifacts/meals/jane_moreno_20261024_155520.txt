For Valentine’s Day, Jane Moreno prepared a Garden-Style Chicken and Root Vegetable Casserole. The dish featured tender chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, zucchini, and a savory herb sauce. She sautéed the vegetables until they softened, then pan-seared the chicken with garlic and rosemary. The whole casserole was assembled in a casserole dish and baked until the chicken was cooked through and the vegetables were tender.

The aroma of warm savory herbs, roasted potatoes, and gently caramelized chicken filled the kitchen, while the golden crust and bright orange vegetables gave the dish a cheerful, inviting appearance. It was a cozy, colorful meal suitable for sharing with her family before Valentine’s Day.
