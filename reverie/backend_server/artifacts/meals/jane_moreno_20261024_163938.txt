For Valentine’s Day, Jane prepared Garden-Herb Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Honey Glaze and Roasted Root Vegetables. Her favorite chicken was seasoned with olive oil, garlic, smoked paprika, rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of sea salt, then roasted until golden and tender. The oven-roasted carrots, potatoes, and parsnips were caramelized in butter with carrots, onions, and a touch of honey for extra sweetness.

As the oven filled with the scent of roasted garlic, herbs, and caramelized vegetables, Jane carefully arranged the chicken and root vegetables in two shallow bowls, drizzling the golden glaze over the chicken and garnishing the sides with parsley and lemon wedges. The meal was bright, fragrant, and inviting, with the warm golden chicken contrasting against the earthy tones of the roasted vegetables.

She served it for dinner at home, creating a cozy Valentine’s Day meal for Jane, Tom, and their family. The occasion was one of warmth and shared family time, with the welcoming atmosphere of The Willow Market and Pharmacy adding to the festive mood.
