Jane prepared Braised Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce for the family’s Valentine’s Day dinner.

She sliced bone-in chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, and sweet potatoes, then browned them in olive oil. After searing the vegetables, she added chicken broth, apple cider vinegar, and a generous amount of lemon juice, then covered the skillet and simmered until the chicken became tender and the vegetables softened. She finished the dish with fresh thyme, rosemary, and a final squeeze of lemon.

The aroma of the meal filled the kitchen with the rich scent of browned chicken, herbs, caramelized potatoes, and the bright tang of lemon. It was served in a large white ceramic bowl, garnished with thyme, a whole lemon, and a small sprig of rosemary. The family gathered around the table for a warm, celebratory dinner on February 14, sharing their favorite love story and planning to explore the city’s rarely visited streets with Wolfgang.
