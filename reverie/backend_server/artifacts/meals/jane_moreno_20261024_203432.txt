Jane prepared a small vegetable and lemon salad with creamy goat cheese, a simple yet refreshing side that complements her family’s pasta. The key ingredients were fresh spinach, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, sliced red onion, cherry tomatoes, crumbled goat cheese, and lemon juice.

She washed the vegetables, tossed them lightly in olive oil and lemon juice, and added the cheese just before serving. The dish was assembled in a white ceramic bowl, garnished with a few extra tomatoes and a sprig of parsley, and presented with a few sprigs of rosemary beside it.

The aroma was bright and cheerful—fresh basil, crisp lettuce, and the subtle richness of goat cheese mingling with the lemon. The presentation was elegant and inviting, with colorful vegetables and soft textures creating a warm, family-friendly aesthetic. The meal was prepared for her Wednesday, February 15 visit with her husband, Tom, who is a shopkeeper at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, and was intended as a cozy pre-dinner lunch while they reconnect.
