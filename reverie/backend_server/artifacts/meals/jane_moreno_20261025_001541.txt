Jane prepared Tom’s Tomato Basil Scramble with Toasted Whole Wheat Toast. The dish featured whole wheat toast, fresh tomatoes, diced onions, garlic, fresh basil, feta cheese, and plenty of creamy egg.

She cracked the eggs into a skillet over medium heat, whisked in some milk and a pinch of salt, then cooked them until the whites were set but the yolks remained soft. She tossed the tomatoes and garlic in a small pan until the tomatoes softened, then added the onions and herbs. The eggs were folded in just before the pan was removed, and everything was stirred together to create a colorful, fragrant scramble.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory egg, sweet tomatoes, garlic, basil, and a hint of feta. She arranged the warm toast beside the dish, adding a final pat of feta on top. The presentation was simple and homey: golden toast, vibrant orange and green colors, and a small bowl of sliced tomatoes for extra freshness.

This was a comforting family breakfast, prepared early on Monday, February 13.
