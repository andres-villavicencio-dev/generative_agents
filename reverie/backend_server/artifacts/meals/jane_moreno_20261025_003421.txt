Jane prepared Tomato Basil Chicken for the family’s Monday morning breakfast. The dish came together quickly with chicken breasts, sliced tomatoes, fresh basil, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of smoked paprika. She marinated the chicken overnight, then pan-seared it until golden and cooked through, adding the tomatoes and basil at the very end to keep the fresh flavors bright.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and savory poultry. Jane presented the meal in a simple, inviting bowl: the chicken was arranged alongside crisp green vegetables, a bright lemon wedge, and fresh basil. A small pile of crusty bread and a glass of water completed the setting.

It was Jane’s first morning breakfast for the week, and she felt relieved that the meal was ready. She checked that everything looked fully cooked before checking the table, ready to serve her husband, Tom, and the family.
