Jane Moreno prepared family-style chicken and vegetables with rice, a hearty and comforting breakfast perfect for her husband and family. She used chicken breast, carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, onions, whole wheat rice, and a touch of lemon juice. The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, and garlic, while the vegetables were lightly sautéed until crisp-tender. All the ingredients were arranged neatly on the table, accompanied by warm milk, tea, and a fruit basket.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted garlic and steaming rice, inviting everyone to gather and enjoy the meal. It was prepared for Monday’s breakfast gathering, a relaxed morning routine that Jane loved to make special. With everything organized and beautifully presented, Jane created a welcoming atmosphere that made the family feel cared for and ready to start their day.
