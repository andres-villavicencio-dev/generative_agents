Jane Moreno prepared Tomato Basil Egg Toast with Fresh Fruit. The dish was simple yet filling, made for her family’s Monday morning breakfast.

She used two slices of whole-grain bread, ripe tomatoes, fresh basil, a hard-boiled egg, sliced bananas, and a few almonds. In a skillet, she lightly sautéed the tomatoes with minced garlic until they softened, then added the sliced egg and basil to finish cooking. Toasted bread and fruit were arranged around it on a wooden board, with the egg and remaining tomatoes placed in the center.

The aroma was warm and inviting: the savory scent of garlic, the bright fragrance of basil, and the sweet sweetness of the bananas. Jane arranged the food in a casual, colorful presentation, letting the contrasting colors and textures create a cheerful family breakfast. The meal was prepared for a quiet, ordinary Monday morning at The Willow Market, with Jane setting the table and adding any remaining breakfast items before Tom returned home.
