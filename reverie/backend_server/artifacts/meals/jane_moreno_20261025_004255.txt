Jane prepared Caramelized Carrots with Creamy Tomato Sauce, a comforting family breakfast that turned into a cozy mid-morning meal. The dish included baby carrots, onions, garlic, carrots, canned tomatoes, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a splash of cream.

Jane sliced the onions and garlic, then sautéed them in the pan until golden. She added the carrots and cooked them slowly until they softened and turned vibrant orange. As the sauce reduced, she combined the tomatoes, cream, and seasoning, stirring until it thickened and bubbled gently.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onions, garlic, and ripe tomatoes. She finished the meal in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the carrots neatly around the sides with a small spoon of sauce resting in the center.

The occasion was Jane’s family morning meal, a quiet moment after Tom finished the groceries at the Harvey Oak Supply Store. It was an easy, nourishing breakfast shared with her husband and young child, celebrating the simple pleasure of preparing and enjoying good food together.
