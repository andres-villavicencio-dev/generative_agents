Jane Moreno prepared Roasted Vegetable Salad for a quiet Monday lunch with her husband. The dish featured crisp apples, carrots, celery, red onion, and cucumber, tossed with lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, and a pinch of rosemary. She also prepared garlic bread and a simple salad to serve as a refreshing side.

The vegetables were thinly sliced and arranged on a platter before being roasted in the oven until golden and tender. Jane arranged the warm vegetables on a bed of romaine lettuce and placed the garlic bread neatly beside them. A small pitcher of dressing and a few fresh herbs added bright accents to the presentation. The warm aroma of roasted garlic, carrots, and herbs filled the kitchen, while the crisp sounds of slicing and plating made the meal feel cozy and inviting.

She prepared the lunch at home, following a relaxed routine designed to make dinner simple and enjoyable for both herself and Tom. It was a peaceful family meal, though not a large celebration. Jane wanted the meal to feel fresh and comforting, offering something satisfying without requiring much effort.
