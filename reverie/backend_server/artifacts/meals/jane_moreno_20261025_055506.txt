Jane prepared Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Rosemary Rice for dinner on Tuesday, February 14. She seasoned chicken breasts with lemon, fresh rosemary, thyme, garlic, and olive oil, then pan-seared them until golden and tender. Around the same time, she roasted carrots, celery, bell peppers, and onions until crisp-tender, then added the chicken and finished with lemon juice and a sprinkle of parmesan.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of rosemary, garlic, and caramelizing vegetables. She served the chicken in a small white bowl, arranged the colorful vegetables beside it, and placed the fluffy rice on the side. The meal was presented simply but invitingly—the bright colors of the vegetables stood out against the creamy rice and the golden-brown chicken.

It was a casual, family-style dinner while Jane and Carmen discussed the first chapter of Tamara’s children’s book. The relaxed evening gave Jane a quiet moment to enjoy her cooking, and the shared conversation added a friendly, welcoming touch to the meal.
