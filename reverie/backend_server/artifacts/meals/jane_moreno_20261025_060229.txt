Jane prepared Creamy Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for dinner. The key ingredients were chicken breast, garlic, rosemary, lemon, olive oil, onions, carrots, bell peppers, and thyme, with the remaining ingredients added to the pan in the final minutes.

She began by heating olive oil in a large skillet and sautéing the garlic, onions, carrots, and peppers until softened. When the chicken was dry, she added it to the pan and quickly browned the skin. She finished the dish by pouring in a splash of chicken broth, lemon juice, and rosemary, then stirring until the chicken was cooked through and the vegetables were tender. Jane added the remaining ingredients toward the end, allowing the flavors to meld without overcooking.

The aroma was warm and savory, with fragrant herbs, caramelized onions, and a bright touch of lemon filling the kitchen. She plated the colorful vegetables around the golden chicken in a shallow bowl, garnishing with a few fresh thyme sprigs.

The occasion was an early dinner before Jane met friends at a lively place, so the meal was simple, comforting, and ready in time to relax afterward.
