Jane prepared Chicken and Broccoli with Lemon-Garlic Butter, a simple yet comforting dinner for Tuesday, February 14. She sliced chicken breast into thin pieces, sautéed broccoli florets, garlic in olive oil, and finished it with butter, chopped parsley, and lemon juice. The dish was cooked until the chicken was golden and the broccoli was crisp-tender.

The kitchen smelled warmly of garlic, herbs, and savory chicken. She arranged the chicken in a shallow bowl, broccoli around the sides, and scattered chopped parsley over the top. A small lemon wedge and a sprig of rosemary rested beside it.

She prepared it to meet friends at a lively restaurant and discuss the first chapter of Tamara’s children’s book, but the meal was simply a peaceful evening at home—a wholesome, family-style dinner to relax into before going to bed at 10 p.m.
