Jane Moreno prepared Pan-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a warm, family-friendly dinner for Tuesday evening.

The key ingredients included chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, onions, garlic, fresh herbs, olive oil, lemon juice, and a savory broth. She began by seasoning the chicken with salt, pepper, and her favorite herbs, then arranged the vegetables on a baking sheet. The chicken was seared in a hot skillet until golden and cooked through, while the vegetables were roasted until tender. Jane finished the dish by broiling the chicken until it was crisp and the vegetables were richly browned.

The aroma was inviting, filled with the savory scent of garlic, caramelized carrots, and toasted herbs. She presented it on a white ceramic dinner plate, arranging the vegetables neatly around the chicken and adding a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing. The meal was simple, comforting, and ready for dinner, especially since the family gathered for dessert afterward.
