Jane prepared Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a comforting first-course meal for her family. The key ingredients were chicken breast, red onion, garlic, fresh parsley, lemon juice, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, carrots, potatoes, and Brussels sprouts.

She marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, herbs, and garlic, then baked it until golden and juicy. The vegetables were roasted until tender, with the potatoes and Brussels sprouts caramelizing in the pan. A small pot of lemony broth served the meal, adding brightness and a savory-sweet finish.

Aromatic rosemary, garlic, and citrus infused the kitchen. The chicken was plated with a rustic bowl of vegetables and a lemon wedge, while the golden root vegetables added earthy color. The presentation was simple, inviting, and family-appropriate, making it ideal for a relaxed Monday dinner.
