Jane prepared Garden Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon Herb Pan Sauce. The key ingredients were chicken thighs, red potatoes, carrots, asparagus, baby spinach, onion, garlic, olive oil, herbs, lemon juice, and a touch of Dijon mustard.

She began by searing the chicken in a pan until golden, then added the vegetables and potatoes. As they began to soften, she tossed them with fresh herbs and lemon juice, finishing the pan sauce with a little butter and Dijon. The chicken was cooked through, and the vegetables were roasted until tender and slightly caramelized.

The meal smelled warm and fragrant—herbs, garlic, roasted potatoes, and lemon filling the kitchen. She arranged the golden chicken on a white dinner plate and arranged the vegetables and lemon slices beside it, letting the bright colors and golden edges create an inviting presentation.

This was a simple but comforting second course for Jane’s family dinner, balanced after a hearty first meal. Her organized, friendly approach made the cooking process feel purposeful and enjoyable.
