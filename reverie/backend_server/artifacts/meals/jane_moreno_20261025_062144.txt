On Tuesday, February 14, Jane prepared Pan-Seared Scallops with Lemon-Herb Butter and Asparagus. The key ingredients were fresh scallops, asparagus spears, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, and a simple herb sauce. She began by sautéing the asparagus in a pan until crisp-tender, then removed it to keep the pan hot.

Meanwhile, she pan-seared the scallops with garlic and lemon juice, seasoning them with salt and pepper. For the sauce, she melted butter with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon over the cooked scallops. The dish was presented in a simple white bowl, with the asparagus arranged around the pan-seared scallops.

The aroma was bright and inviting: garlic, herbs, lemon, and the subtle sweetness of the scallops. The presentation was elegant and cheerful, perfect for Jane’s plans to meet friends and discuss the first chapter of Tamara’s children’s book. The meal was made to share and celebrate a relaxing Tuesday.
