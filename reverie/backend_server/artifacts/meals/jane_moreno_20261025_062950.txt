Jane prepared Garlic Herb Roast Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a hearty family-style dinner for Tuesday, February 14.

Key ingredients: bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, garlic, thyme, rosemary, olive oil, and lemon.

Preparation method: Jane washed and rinsed the vegetables before preparing the chicken, then roasted everything together in the oven until the chicken was golden and the potatoes were tender. She finished it with a lemon-herb sauce, garnishing it with fresh herbs.

The meal had a warm, savory aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, rosemary, and caramelized onions. Jane arranged the colorful vegetables in a shallow white serving dish with plump golden chicken and bright lemon slices, creating an inviting, rustic presentation.

It was prepared for a relaxed dinner at home, followed by Jane’s planned evening of meeting friends and discussing the first chapter of Tamara’s children’s book.
