Jane prepared Roasted Garlic Butter Chicken with Zesty Lemon Herbs. For the meal, she used bone-in chicken thighs, garlic cloves, fresh lemon, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of chicken broth. She seared the chicken until golden, then basted it with the garlic-infused butter before roasting it until the meat was tender and the skin was crispy.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, and lemon. She arranged the golden chicken in a shallow baking dish, garnished it with the fresh herbs and a bright lemon wedge, and served the leftovers in a small bowl. The meal was a cozy, family-style dinner, prepared while she tidied the kitchen and prepared the space for the next meal—perfect after a long day of cooking and preparing for friends.
