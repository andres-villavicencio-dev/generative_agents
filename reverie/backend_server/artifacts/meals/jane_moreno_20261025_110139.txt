Jane prepared Penne with Tomato Basil Cream for her family’s Wednesday lunch. She sautéed garlic and onion in olive oil, added crushed tomatoes, and simmered them with pasta until the sauce thickened. She finished the dish with fresh basil, a generous drizzle of cream, and a handful of pine nuts, giving it a bright, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, and warm tomato.

The pasta was served in an oval white bowl, garnished with parsley and a few whole pine nuts, with a small red pepper on the side. The presentation was simple yet inviting, balancing the creamy sauce with fresh greens and colorful accents.

The meal was a practical family lunch, prepared so that Jane could reconnect with her husband, Tom, who is visiting The Willow Market and Pharmacy. Their quick visit and planned pickup confirmed that she was making time to strengthen their relationship.
