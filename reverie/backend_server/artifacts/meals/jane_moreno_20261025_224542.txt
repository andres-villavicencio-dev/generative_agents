On Wednesday, February 15, Jane prepared a comforting family breakfast of scrambled eggs with spinach, onions, and a side of whole-grain toast. The eggs were lightly sautéed in a pan, then folded with a small handful of fresh spinach, a pinch of salt, and a dash of pepper. She added a few sliced red onions and stirred until the spinach softened. Toasted butter was spread evenly on the whole-grain bread, and the eggs were served warm alongside the toast.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of butter, herbs, and cooked eggs. Jane arranged the eggs, toast, and onions on a white serving platter and added a sprig of parsley for color. The meal was prepared for a simple weekday breakfast, but it became a cozy family gathering as Tom joined her afterward to discuss their groceries and prescriptions.
