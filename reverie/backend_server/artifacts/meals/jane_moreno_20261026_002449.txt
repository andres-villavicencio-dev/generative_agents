Jane prepared Hearty Breakfast Tacos, a comforting weekday brunch for herself and Tom. The dish included corn tortillas, shredded chicken, black beans, diced tomatoes, shredded cabbage, salsa, avocado, and a little sour cream. She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and a pinch of cumin, then shredded it and arranged it on the tortillas with the remaining fresh ingredients.

She cooked everything over a gas stove, heating the tortillas and simmering the chicken in a pan until the beans and tomatoes turned slightly pink. After letting the tacos rest, she sliced the avocado and served them with a side of warm salsa. The aroma was rich and earthy, with warm tortilla, savory chicken, fresh tomatoes, and a hint of cumin filling the kitchen.

Presented on a white plate, the tacos looked colorful and inviting, with the bright green avocado contrasting the golden chicken and warm tortilla. It was the occasion of a relaxed family breakfast—simple, nourishing, and designed to bring the whole household together.
