For Wednesday, February 15, Jane Moreno prepared Crispy Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa. She had plenty of chicken, carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, onions, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, paprika, salt, pepper, and a cup of cooked quinoa. She cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces, tossed them with the aromatics, and roasted them until the skin was golden and crispy. Meanwhile, she roasted the vegetables and cooked the quinoa, then combined everything in a bowl with lemon juice and a pinch of paprika for a bright, colorful topping.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, roasted onions, and savory chicken. The plate was arranged neatly with the golden chicken, vibrant vegetables, fluffy quinoa, and a side of steamed broccoli. It was a simple yet hearty family dinner, prepared to balance her busy schedule and support Tom’s prescriptions.
