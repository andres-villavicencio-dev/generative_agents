On Wednesday, February 15, Jane prepared a comforting Chicken and Spinach Stir-Fry. The key ingredients included bone-in chicken thighs, fresh spinach, carrots, bell peppers, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, and browned rice. She marinated the chicken, sautéed the aromatics until fragrant, then added the vegetables and chicken and finished the dish with a splash of soy sauce and sesame oil.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of garlic, ginger, and slightly smoky chicken, accompanied by the bright, fresh aroma of spinach and carrots. She arranged the stir-fry in a shallow baking dish, garnished with sesame seeds and a few green onions, and served it with the browned rice. The meal was simple, hearty, and practical for an early Wednesday lunch.

It was a casual family dinner, prepared for Jane, Tom, and their children. Jane’s organized and helpful nature made the preparation easy and enjoyable, allowing her to spend quality time with the family before heading to bed at 10:00 pm.
