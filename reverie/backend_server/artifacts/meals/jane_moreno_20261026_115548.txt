Jane prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a simple, family-friendly lunch for Wednesday, February 15. The meal was prepared on a stainless steel pan over medium heat, with the chicken seasoned with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, salt, and pepper. She roasted it with colorful vegetables—bell peppers, carrots, onions, and potatoes—until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender.

As the food cooked, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken and savory herbs, accompanied by the crisp scent of carrots and onions. She arranged the finished meal on a white serving platter, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a bright lemon wedge. The presentation was clean and inviting, with the golden chicken standing out against the vividly colored vegetables.

The occasion was a relaxed family lunch, with Jane helping Tom with his prescriptions and groceries, while sharing her cooking with her family. It was a peaceful, nourishing meal that balanced household responsibilities with the comfort of home cooking.
