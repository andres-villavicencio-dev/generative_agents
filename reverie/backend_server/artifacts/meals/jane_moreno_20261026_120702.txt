Jane prepared Herb-Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Garlic Cream, a colorful side dish for her family’s Wednesday lunch. The key ingredients were carrots, parsnips, potatoes, red onion, garlic, dried herbs, olive oil, lemon juice, and a little heavy cream. She began by chopping the vegetables and onions into bite-sized pieces, then tossed them in olive oil, garlic, lemon, and herbs until everything turned golden and fragrant. She roasted them in a pan until the carrots and parsnips became tender, then added the potatoes and finished them with a splash of cream.

The dish smelled warm and aromatic as soon as it was ready—savory garlic, sweet carrots, and toasted herbs filling the kitchen. It was presented on a white ceramic platter, with the bright orange carrots, golden parsnips, and creamy potatoes arranged neatly around a few whole potatoes. Jane’s friendly smile and organized approach made the meal feel inviting and celebratory, while the comforting flavors suggested a quiet, nourishing lunch shared with Tom and her family.
