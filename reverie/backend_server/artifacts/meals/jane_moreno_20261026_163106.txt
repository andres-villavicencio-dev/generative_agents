Jane prepared Chicken and Vegetable Soup with Toasted Noodles, a comforting dinner prepared for a quiet Wednesday evening.

She began by washing her hands and preparing a large pot of water, adding cubed chicken, carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, and a handful of leafy greens. After simmering briefly, she added broth and a pinch of salt, then let the soup cook until tender and fragrant. To finish it, she stirred in a generous serving of cooked noodles, tossing them gently with the soup. She also prepared two slices of white bread with a little olive oil and toasted them before serving.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of chicken, carrots, celery, and vegetable broth. The soup was presented in a large bowl, with the noodles arranged neatly around the edge and the toasted bread placed beside it. The occasion was a peaceful family dinner, balanced by Jane’s responsibilities: helping Tom with prescriptions and groceries, meal planning, and supporting friends at the city café.
