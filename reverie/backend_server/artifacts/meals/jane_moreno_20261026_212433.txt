On Wednesday, February 15, Jane prepared Warm Oatmeal and Egg Breakfast for the family. The meal consisted of rolled oats, milk, cinnamon, a small poached or fried egg, and sliced fresh fruit. She cooked the oats in a pan or pot with a little water, then added the milk and cinnamon while letting the warm oats soften. She cracked an egg into the pan and cooked it briefly until set, then served it with the oatmeal and fruit.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the soft scent of oatmeal, lightly seasoned milk, and the savory richness of the egg. It smelled comforting and inviting, like a quiet morning gathering. Jane presented the dish in a simple white bowl, arranging the oatmeal and fruit neatly and placing the egg alongside them.

The occasion was a simple weekday breakfast for Jane and Tom. It was a relaxed family meal meant to encourage conversation and make the day begin easily. Jane’s organized approach—shopping, meal planning, and helping Tom with his prescriptions—created a warm, purposeful atmosphere that reflected her friendly, supportive nature.
