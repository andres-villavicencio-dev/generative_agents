Jane prepared a fresh Spring Salad with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Tahini Dressing for Wednesday, February 15, a quiet family lunch. She washed the carrots, celery, and cherry tomatoes, then tossed them with olive oil, garlic, and a pinch of salt before roasting the vegetables. She added crisp lettuce, cucumber, and baby spinach, finishing the salad with lemon-tahini dressing and a few walnuts.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of roasted carrots and garlic, followed by the fresh tang of lemon and the nutty warmth of tahini. The salad was arranged in a white ceramic bowl and garnished with a sprig of parsley and a few colorful nuts. It was prepared for a simple, nourishing family meal, but Jane also prepared enough ingredients for a potential extra meal, showing her organized and caring nature.
