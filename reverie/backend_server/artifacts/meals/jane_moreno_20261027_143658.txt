Jane prepared a Garlic Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for Wednesday, February 15. The meal featured chicken thighs, broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, and red onions, all seasoned with olive oil, garlic, lemon, dried thyme, rosemary, and a touch of paprika. The chicken was marinated in the fridge overnight, then seared and roasted until the meat was tender and the vegetables were tender-crisp.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and savory chicken. Jane served the dish in a shallow white ceramic bowl, plating the chicken in the center and arranging the colorful vegetables around it. She added fresh parsley for a bright finish and a lemon wedge for a bright citrus contrast.

It was a casual weekday family lunch, prepared in her home kitchen to save time while still giving her and Tom a comforting, nourishing meal. Jane’s friendly, organized approach made the meal both practical and enjoyable, helping her support her family’s day before heading to bed.
