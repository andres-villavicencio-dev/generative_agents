Jane prepared a comforting Smoked-Salmon and Herb Breakfast Bowl for her family’s Wednesday morning. The dish featured salmon fillets, smoked paprika, lemon zest, thyme, parsley, olive oil, garlic, and vegetable broth.

She lightly brined the salmon, then pan-seared it until golden and flaky. The vegetables—carrots, spinach, and onions—were sautéed until crisp, while the broth was reduced to create a flavorful base. A small bowl of herbed yogurt and whole-grain toast completed the meal.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of smoked paprika, lemons, savory herbs, and toasted grain. Jane arranged the bowl with the salmon on one side and the fresh vegetables in the center, garnishing the sides with finely chopped parsley and serving the toast with a drizzle of lemon juice. The presentation was simple, bright, and inviting, making it a cheerful early-morning breakfast for Jane and Tom.
