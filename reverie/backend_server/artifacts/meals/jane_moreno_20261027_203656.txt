Jane prepared Mediterranean Veggie Frittata with Whole-Wheat Toast.

She gathered eggs, spinach, cherry tomatoes, mushrooms, onions, and feta cheese for the frittata. Using a large skillet, she warmed olive oil, sautéed the vegetables until they softened, then added the eggs and feta. She folded the cooked eggs into the pan and let it cook until the center was set. For the sides, she toasted whole-grain toast with lemon slices and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the fresh aroma of garlic, lemon, and savory herbs. The bright yellow frittata rested beside the golden toast, with colorful vegetables and creamy feta adding an inviting contrast.

She prepared the meal to serve as a light, nutritious breakfast while tidying the kitchen and organizing the refrigerator and pantry. The practical meal supported her day of helping Tom with prescriptions and grocery shopping, offering a warm, family-friendly start to her Wednesday.
