For her Wednesday breakfast, Jane prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Berries and Toasted Nuts. The meal was simple, warm, and designed to provide a gentle start to her busy day.

She cooked a small pot of oatmeal with steel-cut oats, milk, cinnamon, and a handful of fresh berries—strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries. The berries were rinsed and sliced, then gently folded into the simmering oatmeal. Jane topped the bowl with toasted almonds and walnuts, which she chopped and toasted in a dry pan until golden.

The kitchen was filled with the warm aroma of cinnamon, ripe fruit, toasted nuts, and oatmeal. Jane arranged the oatmeal, fresh berries, and crunchy nuts in one large bowl, adding a small piece of banana for sweetness. The presentation was colorful and inviting, with the bright berries contrasting against the creamy oatmeal and golden nuts.

She made breakfast for Tom and their family, including a small portion for each of the other adults. It was a quiet, homey occasion—a peaceful way to begin the day after balancing household responsibilities, shopping, and helping with Tom’s prescriptions.
