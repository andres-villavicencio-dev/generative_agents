Jane prepared a comforting breakfast of scrambled eggs with spinach and tomatoes, accompanied by whole-grain toast, orange juice, and fresh fruit.

The eggs were cooked in a skillet over medium heat, then folded gently into the warm pan. The spinach and chopped tomatoes were added shortly before the eggs to wilt without becoming soggy. Whole-grain toast was toasted lightly, and the orange juice was poured over the fruit.

As she prepared it, the kitchen filled with the familiar, savory aroma of buttery eggs and toasted bread, along with the fresh scent of herbs and ripe fruit. She arranged the plates neatly, placing the bright orange fruit and golden toast beside the savory eggs and spinach.

It was the start of a quiet, family-filled Wednesday morning—a simple, nourishing meal meant to bring warmth and comfort to the household.
