Jane prepared Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast Bowl for the family. The dish combines grilled chicken breast, sautéed spinach, cherry tomatoes, sliced cucumber, onions, and whole-grain toast, with a little lemon juice and garlic.

She marinated the chicken briefly with olive oil, lemon zest, and a pinch of salt before grilling it over a stove burner. The vegetables were sautéed in a little butter until crisp and golden, then added to the pan for a quick stir. For the sauce, she made a simple pan sauce with butter, flour, salt, and pepper, then mixed it with the cooked chicken.

The meal had a warm, savory aroma of caramelized chicken, garlic, and fresh vegetables. It was presented in a white ceramic bowl with the colorful vegetables and golden toast arranged around it. The occasion was a peaceful family breakfast on Thursday, February 16, after a busy but purposeful day.
