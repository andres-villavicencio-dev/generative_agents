Jane prepared a simple, comforting Hearty Tomato Basil Chicken Breakfast for a busy Wednesday morning. She sliced bone-in chicken breasts, then seasoned them with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and dried basil. In a small pan, she sautéed the chicken until the skin was golden and the meat was cooked through, then sliced it and added chopped tomatoes, fresh basil, and a drizzle of olive oil.

The dish was cooked over medium heat for about eight minutes until the tomatoes softened and the chicken was tender. It was served in a shallow white bowl with a sprinkle of fresh herbs and a wedge of lemon, letting the bright orange-red tomatoes and golden-brown chicken contrast beautifully against the pale bowl. The aroma of roasted garlic, savory chicken, and warm basil filled the kitchen as she prepared it for breakfast.

It was prepared to wake Jane up and give the family a quick, nourishing start to their day, balancing the preparation with Tom’s prescriptions and grocery shopping.
