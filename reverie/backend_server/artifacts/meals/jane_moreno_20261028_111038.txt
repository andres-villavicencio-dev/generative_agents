For Wednesday, February 15, Jane prepared Herbed Lemon-Honey Salmon with Roasted Vegetables, a warm family breakfast-meal. She used fresh salmon fillets, lemon juice, honey, minced garlic, thyme, parsley, and a touch of olive oil. The vegetables—roasted potatoes, carrots, and broccoli—were tossed with olive oil, salt, pepper, and herbs before baking.

Jane seasoned the salmon, drizzled it with lemon and honey, then arranged it alongside the golden vegetables on a shallow baking dish. She placed the pan under a broiler until the salmon was tender and the vegetables were crisp. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, herbs, caramelized vegetables, and the savory scent of the lemon.

The meal was simple yet comforting, made for a quiet Wednesday morning around 6:00 AM. It would be the first dish of the day as Jane and Tom prepared breakfast and packed Jan for their trip home.
