Jane prepared a hearty Garlic Butter Chicken with Roasted Vegetable Stir-Fry for lunch on Wednesday, February 15. The dish featured tender chicken breasts marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs, served over a bed of colorful vegetables including broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, and zucchini. She sautéed everything in a pan, finished the chicken with a rich garlic butter glaze, and finished the vegetables with a splash of broth and olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, savory chicken, fresh herbs, and caramelized vegetables. Jane arranged the colorful stir-fry in a shallow, shallow dish and placed the golden chicken beside it, garnishing it with lemon slices and a few sprigs of parsley. The presentation was bright, balanced, and inviting, making it the perfect meal for a quiet Wednesday lunch before Tom’s appointments.
