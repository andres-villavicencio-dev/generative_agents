Jane prepared Roasted Garlic Lemon Herb Chicken for a cozy Wednesday lunch. The main dish used bone-in chicken breasts, roasted garlic, lemon slices, fresh thyme, rosemary, and olive oil. She sliced the chicken into bite-sized pieces, tossed it with the aromatics, and roasted it in the oven until golden and tender, then finished it under the broiler.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and lightly smoked chicken. She arranged the chicken on a platter, garnished it with lemon slices and fresh herbs, and served it with roasted potatoes, green beans, and a simple lemon-herb dressing.

The meal was a relaxed family lunch for Wednesday, February 15. It was prepared while Jane balanced household responsibilities, shopping, and supporting Tom with his prescriptions, reflecting the rewarding, organized nature of her busy but meaningful day.
