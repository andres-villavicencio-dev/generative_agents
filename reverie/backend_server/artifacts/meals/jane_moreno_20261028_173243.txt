On Wednesday, February 15, Jane Moreno prepared Sunday’s Breakfast Bowl for herself and Tom. She began with a bowl of warm oatmeal soaked in milk, topped with sliced banana, raspberries, and a drizzle of honey. For lunch, she made a hearty pan-seared chicken breast with roasted sweet potatoes, carrots, and a bright lemon-herb sauce.

She used fresh chicken, carrots, sweet potatoes, lemon, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of salt for the savory pan-seared chicken, while the oatmeal required oats, water, and fruit. The kitchen filled with a warm, comforting aroma of roasted vegetables, citrus, and cinnamon during her preparation.

The meal was presented on a white plate with neatly arranged vegetables, golden potatoes, glossy chicken, and vibrant berries, while the oatmeal remained in a small bowl beside it. Jane served both lunches together as a shared meal for herself and Tom, creating a relaxed, nourishing occasion to start the day together before heading out to work.
