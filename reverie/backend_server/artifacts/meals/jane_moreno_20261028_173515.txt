On Wednesday, February 15, Jane prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for dinner. The meal used bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, onions, potatoes, and celery, seasoned with garlic, dried oregano, thyme, salt, and black pepper.

Jane began by washing and cutting the vegetables, then tossed them with oil, herbs, salt, and pepper. She arranged them in a roasting pan and seasoned the chicken with a little salt and pepper. The pan was placed in the oven, and the vegetables were roasted until the potatoes were tender, the carrots were golden, and the chicken was cooked through.

Aromatically, the dish filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted garlic, warm herbs, caramelized onions, and the rich fragrance of simmering chicken. The final presentation was simple yet inviting: the golden vegetables and tender chicken surrounded by a small pile of crisp greens, with herbs sprinkled over the top.

The meal was a warm family dinner, prepared as part of Jane’s regular Wednesday routine. It balanced her responsibilities with Tom’s prescriptions and groceries while offering a comforting, home-cooked meal for the day.
