For Wednesday, February 15, Jane prepared a comforting Creamy Tomato Basil Egg Pasta for breakfast. She used fresh egg pasta, garlic, sliced tomatoes, cherry tomatoes, fresh basil, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper. In a pan, she softened the garlic in olive oil, added the tomatoes and chopped basil, and simmered until the sauce thickened. The eggs were whisked separately and gently folded into the warm pasta.

The meal had the aroma of toasted garlic, ripe tomatoes, and basil. It was presented on a white dinner plate with the creamy sauce drizzled over the pasta, garnished with fresh basil leaves, a lemon wedge, and a few extra tomatoes. Jane cooked it for breakfast on an early Wednesday, balancing her household responsibilities with a simple, wholesome start to the day.
