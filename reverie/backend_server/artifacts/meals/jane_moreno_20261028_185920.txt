Jane prepared a classic Sunday Scramble with Eggs, Whole Wheat Toast, and a Tossed Green Salad, a warm, hearty breakfast suitable for her family.

The dish used two large eggs, a whole-wheat English muffin, sliced red bell pepper, onion, spinach, tomatoes, whole-grain toast, and fresh spinach. Jane gently scrambled the eggs in a pan, added the vegetables, and let them wilt before serving on the toasted muffin. The pan released a rich, savory aroma of eggs, herbs, and toasted grains, while the green salad provided a crisp, refreshing contrast.

The meal was presented on a white plate, with the golden toast, vibrant vegetables, and bright green salad arranged neatly beside the eggs. Jane served it alongside a glass of water and a small bowl of fruit.

It was Monday breakfast, intended to be a simple yet comforting start to a busy week. The organized, cheerful presentation reflected Jane’s caring nature, and the warm food made her feel like she was sharing a special morning with her family.
