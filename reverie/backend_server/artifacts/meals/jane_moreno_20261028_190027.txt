Jane Moreno prepared Oatmeal Breakfast Bowl with Egg and Fresh Fruit. She cracked eggs into a bowl, separated them, and cooked the yolks while the whites were used for a savory sauce. The main ingredients were rolled oats, milk or almond milk, honey, cinnamon, and fresh berries such as blueberries and strawberries.

Jane stirred the oats gently, added the separated egg, and cooked it until the mixture was warm and lightly set. A small bowl of warm oatmeal was presented alongside the egg, yogurt, and fruit. The aroma of toasted oats, warm milk, honey, and cinnamon filled the kitchen.

This was a quiet weekday breakfast for Jane and Tom, set out for them in the morning when Tom was still at work.
