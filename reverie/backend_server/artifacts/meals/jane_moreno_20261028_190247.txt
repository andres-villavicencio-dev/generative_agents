Jane Moreno prepared a warm Mushy Tomato Basil Soup with Toasted Cornbread. She toasted slices of bread in the toaster while the soup simmered. The key ingredients were ripe tomatoes, garlic, fresh basil, vegetable broth, olive oil, and grated Parmesan cheese.

As the soup came to a gentle simmer, she tossed the garlic and basil in the pan to create a fragrant aroma of roasted garlic and herbs. She ladled the creamy, colorful soup over the crusty cornbread, which had golden brown spots from the toaster. The presentation was simple yet inviting: the rustic bread served in a shallow dish, with generous spoonfuls of soup arranged around it.

This was Jane’s special breakfast for Monday, February 13. She and Tom, who was opening The Willow Market, would share it for the start of the week. The warm, comforting meal reflected Jane’s organized, family-oriented nature and her desire to make life easier and more enjoyable.
