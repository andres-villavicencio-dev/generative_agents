Jane prepared Butter Toast with Fresh Milk and Lemon, a simple, comforting breakfast for her family. The key ingredients were sliced whole-grain bread, softened butter, a splash of milk, and a squeeze of lemon juice, with a pinch of sugar and a sprinkle of sea salt.

She toasted the bread until the golden edges were crisp, then spread a generous layer of softened butter across the surface. She poured the warm milk and lemon over it, letting the butter melt and the toast absorb the creamy, citrusy flavors. Jane added a small knob of butter on top before serving, finishing with a light dusting of cinnamon.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich smell of butter, toasted bread, and fresh citrus. The toast was neatly arranged on white plates, with a glass of milk and a lemon wedge beside it. She prepared the meal early on Monday, February 13, as part of her routine for a warm, family-friendly breakfast before her shift with Tom at The Willow Market.
