On Tuesday, February 14, Jane Moreno prepared a comforting Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast Bake for herself and her family. She combined seasoned chicken thighs with carrots, celery, bell peppers, onions, and diced tomatoes in a baking dish, topped it with breadcrumbs and a little melted butter, and covered it with foil.

She warmed the oven to 180°C (350°F) and let the ingredients cook until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender. Before serving, she turned off the foil and roasted everything uncovered until the chicken was crisp and the juices bubbled over the top. The kitchen filled with a warm, fragrant aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, and savory vegetables.

Jane arranged the golden-brown chicken in a baking dish alongside bright, colorful vegetables and served it with steamed rice and fresh fruit. The presentation was cheerful and inviting, with the contrasting colors making the meal look nourishing and celebratory. This simple, purposeful breakfast was part of a full but relaxed day, balancing Tom’s busy work with Jane’s love of cooking and taking care of her family.
