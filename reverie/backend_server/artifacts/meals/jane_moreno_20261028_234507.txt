For Tuesday, February 14, Jane prepared Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast Bowl, a warm, colorful family meal. The key ingredients were boneless chicken breasts, diced yellow and red potatoes, sliced carrots, spinach, tomatoes, onions, and a handful of whole-grain toast. She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and a little lemon juice, then pan-seared it over medium heat until golden and cooked through.

The potatoes were diced and browned in a little oil, then stirred together with the spinach, tomatoes, and onions until the vegetables were tender. Fresh cracked pepper was sprinkled over the bowl, and a splash of lemon juice was added just before serving. Jane presented the meal in a white bowl with a small wooden spoon, arranged the colorful vegetables around the cooked chicken, and placed a generous slice of toast beside it.

The aroma of toasted bread, savory chicken, caramelized potatoes, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen. Jane hoped the meal would be a cozy, purposeful start to her day with Tom, celebrating the reliable partnership that had made her feel secure and grateful.
