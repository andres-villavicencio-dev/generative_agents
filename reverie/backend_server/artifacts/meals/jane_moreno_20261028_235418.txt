Jane prepared Tomato Basil Chicken with Whole-Grain Toast and Fresh Salad for a weekday family breakfast.

She started with two bone-in chicken thighs, seasoning them with salt, pepper, garlic, and dried oregano, then pan-seared them until the skin was crisp and the juices ran clear. For the sauce, she sautéed chopped tomatoes, onions, garlic, and fresh basil in a little olive oil until the tomatoes softened and the aromas filled the kitchen.

She prepared the salad with crisp lettuce, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and a light vinaigrette, then topped the chicken with toasted whole-grain bread and a small portion of the salad on a simple white plate. The meal had a warm, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, and roasted chicken, with bright, fresh notes from the tomatoes and basil.

It was prepared in the early morning, when Jane wanted to give the family a balanced, comforting start to Tuesday. The presentation was simple and inviting—the golden toast and colorful salad looked ready to serve, and the chicken added a satisfying centerpiece to the breakfast table.
