Jane prepared Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Basil Rice.

She used 2 salmon fillets, ½ cup jasmine rice, ½ cup chicken broth, 1 tbsp olive oil, fresh lemon, green onions, thyme, and parsley. The rice was rinsed and cooked until tender, then gently warmed with the broth to keep it fluffy. Jane seared the salmon over medium heat until it was golden and cooked through, adding a squeeze of lemon and fresh herbs while the fish rested.

The aroma was warm, savory, and lightly citrusy, with hints of toasted rice and herb freshness. She plated the salmon in a shallow dish with a generous portion of the rice and scattered it with chopped parsley and lemon slices. The bright colors and clean presentation made the meal visually appealing and inviting.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday dinner for Jane and Tom, whose reliable shop work helped her feel confident that the entire family’s needs were met. It was a simple, purposeful meal meant to create a warm, comfortable evening together.
