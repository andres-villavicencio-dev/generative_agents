On Tuesday, February 14, Jane Moreno prepared Slow-Simmered Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Herbed Rice, a simple yet heartwarming meal for her family. She used bone-in chicken breasts, red potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, dried herbs, olive oil, and brown rice.

She started by sautéing the onions and garlic in a pan, then added the chicken and covered it with a little water to braise. She finished by adding the potatoes and carrots, letting everything simmer gently until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were tender but still crisp. Jane served the chicken with the rice and vegetables, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, savory chicken, and the earthy scent of herbs. She presented the dish in a shallow white bowl, arranging the golden vegetables and pale chicken around a mound of fluffy rice.

The occasion was a relaxed family dinner for the day before the market opening. Jane wanted everyone to feel comfortable, nourished, and reminded that cooking together made the day special.
