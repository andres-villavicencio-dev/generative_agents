For her planned Tuesday dinner, Jane Moreno prepared Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables. The meal featured a chicken breast seasoned with fresh rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, garlic, and olive oil, then seared and finished in the oven. Alongside it were carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes roasted with onions, olive oil, and a pinch of salt.

She began by washing and peeling the vegetables, then drizzled them with oil and spread them on a large baking sheet. The chicken was seasoned in a separate bowl and placed on the rack above the vegetables. The oven was preheated to 200°C (400°F). After broiling the chicken for five minutes, it was roasted for another 25–30 minutes until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear.

The finished meal smelled deeply aromatic—herbs, garlic, and roasted root vegetables filling the kitchen. Jane arranged the chicken and vegetables on a white platter, topped it with a simple lemon-herb sauce, and served it with a side of warm whole-grain pasta. The occasion was a thoughtful, family-oriented dinner for Tom and Jane, celebrating the successful market visit and Jane’s creative cooking.
