On Wednesday, February 15, Jane prepared Tom’s Garden Sunday for her husband and the family. She used eggs, bread, butter, olive oil, tomatoes, onions, garlic, carrots, and winter squash, with a little lemon juice to brighten the flavors.

Jane cracked the eggs into a bowl, whisked them with salt and pepper, then fried them alongside the sliced squash and onions. She sautéed the tomatoes and garlic in the pan, added the bread and butter, and finished the dish with fresh parsley and chopped chives. The warm skillet made a rich, savory aroma of roasted vegetables and lightly fried eggs, filling the kitchen with a comforting scent of butter and herbs.

She plated the meal in a shallow dish, arranging the golden squash, crispy egg, and golden bread together with lemon wedges and a sprig of parsley. The presentation was simple and inviting, suitable for a cheerful Tuesday dinner with family and friends.
