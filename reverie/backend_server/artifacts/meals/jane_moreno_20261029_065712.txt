Jane prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Mashed Potatoes as a family dinner for Tuesday, February 14, before heading to The Willow Market. She cooked a whole chicken with carrots, onions, potatoes, and a splash of orange juice, then roasted them together until the chicken was golden and tender. The vegetables were lightly browned, and the potatoes were mashed with butter, milk, and a pinch of salt before the chicken was added to finish cooking.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onions, roasting chicken, and potatoes. Jane presented the meal at the dinner table, placing the golden chicken in the center of the platter and arranging the colorful vegetables and creamy potatoes around it. She served the family’s favorite soup and a cup of coffee for the guests.

The occasion was Jane’s first visit to The Willow Market, where she planned to compare the market list with the bulletin board, confirm prices and supplier schedules for eggs and winter squash, and thank Ethel Langley for helping her. She also wanted to thank her family and community for making the day enjoyable.
