Jane prepared Tomato-Garnish Grilled Chicken with Root Vegetables and Fresh Herbs for Tuesday’s market trip. She used boneless chicken breasts, cherry tomatoes, carrots, red onion, garlic, lemon, olive oil, and fresh herbs such as parsley and thyme. The chicken was seasoned with lemon, salt, pepper, and garlic, then grilled until golden and cooked through. Vegetables were roasted briefly to retain their bright colors and crisp texture.

She arranged the grilled chicken and vegetables in a shallow baking pan, topped it with the remaining herbs and lemon slices, and arranged a small glass of water nearby. The dish was presented on a white platter with a simple wooden serving board, giving it a clean, welcoming appearance.

The meal was designed to be quick yet nourishing, with the aroma of roasted garlic, lemon zest, and herbs filling the kitchen. It was a simple, family-oriented breakfast for her husband, Tom, and for Jane’s own preparation before meeting Ethel at The Willow Market.
