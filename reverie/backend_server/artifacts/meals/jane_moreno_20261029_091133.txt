Jane prepared Oatmeal & Berries with Fresh Blueberries and Banana, a simple, comforting breakfast designed to energize her morning. She used rolled oats, milk, a teaspoon of honey, sliced bananas, and fresh blueberries. After measuring the milk, she simmered the oats with the honey until the mixture thickened, then gently folded in the berries and sliced banana.

The kitchen filled with the warm, nutty aroma of oats, honey, and ripe fruit. Jane arranged the oatmeal in a small white bowl, decorating it with a bright blueberry and a banana slice. She set a small plate of fresh berries nearby, creating a cheerful, inviting presentation.

The meal was part of a quiet Thursday morning routine. It was simple enough to make quickly yet satisfying enough for Jane to enjoy as she prepared for her trip to The Willow Market and check the community bulletin board.
