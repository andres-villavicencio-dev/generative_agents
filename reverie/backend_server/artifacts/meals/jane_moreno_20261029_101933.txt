Jane prepared a wholesome Mediterranean-style breakfast for Eugene’s family. The dish was Eggs, Broccoli, and Whole Grain Toast, served alongside milk, bread, and coffee.

For the eggs, she cracked organic brown eggs into a pan with a little olive oil, then scrambled them gently with a spoon. She added chopped broccoli and carrots for color and texture, stirring until the eggs were just set. She toasted whole-grain bread in a pan while making a quick coffee, serving it alongside the breakfast.

The kitchen filled with a warm, comforting aroma of toasted bread, steaming eggs, savory broccoli, and freshly brewed coffee. The presentation was simple and inviting: the golden toast, bright green and orange vegetables, fluffy white eggs, and dark coffee beans arranged neatly on a wooden serving board.

The occasion was a family breakfast for Eugene and his children. Jane was grateful that the day would allow her to visit The Willow Market and purchase seasonal organic groceries before the bulletin board returned. She planned to compare Ethel Langley’s shopping list with the available items, verify prices and supplier schedules for eggs and winter squash, and bring everything home once Ethel returned.
