Jane prepared Mediterranean Breakfast Bowl, a simple yet nourishing lunch featuring fluffy whole-grain bread, ripe tomatoes, crisp cucumber, crisp lettuce, creamy feta, and a generous serving of fresh eggs poached in the bread. The ingredients were prepared in the morning: the bread was toasted, the vegetables rinsed and sliced, the eggs poached to perfection, and the feta crumbled over the top.

She arranged the colorful salad in a large bowl, placed the warm bread beside it, and topped it with a drizzle of olive oil, lemon juice, and a handful of fresh herbs. The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of whole-grain bread, the bright freshness of tomatoes and cucumber, and the rich, creamy sweetness of feta.

It was a casual, comforting breakfast for Jane and Tom, made to be shared and enjoyed. Afterward, she planned to buy seasonal organic groceries, compare Ethel Langley’s list with the bulletin board, and bring the purchases home after Ethel returned.
