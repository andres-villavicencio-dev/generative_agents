Jane prepared Winter Vegetable and Herb Risotto with Roast Chicken, a comforting main dinner for her family. The dish began with Arborio rice, chicken breast, and carrots. She sautéed the carrots and onions in olive oil, then added the chicken, garlic, and chopped fresh herbs. After deglazing with white wine and simmering the rice, she gradually incorporated warm vegetable broth, stirring constantly until the rice absorbed the liquid and became tender yet still firm to the touch.

The aroma filled the kitchen with toasted garlic, savory chicken, and the subtle sweetness of carrots. She plated the risotto in a shallow white dish, garnished with sliced parsley, a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil, and a few roasted cherry tomatoes. A roasted chicken breast served alongside the risotto completed the meal.

This was a cozy family dinner on Thursday, February 16, as Jane prepared to visit The Willow Market and buy seasonal organic groceries. She hoped the evening would bring her closer to Tom and encourage her to explore new places in Willow Court.
