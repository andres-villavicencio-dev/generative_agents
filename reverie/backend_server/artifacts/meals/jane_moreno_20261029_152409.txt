Jane Moreno prepared a simple but comforting breakfast of eggs Benedict on toast, made during her quiet time on Friday, February 17. She began by washing and peeling the eggs, then cracked them into a pan and seasoned them lightly with salt and pepper. The pan was oiled, and the eggs were cooked until the whites were set but the yolks remained soft, with a little butter added for richness. While the eggs cooked, she toasted slices of sourdough bread and warmed a few slices of tomato and cheddar cheese on the stove.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of egg, butter, toasted bread, and cheese. Jane placed the golden-brown toast beneath the pan, covering it with the cooked eggs, and sliced the cheddar over them. The dish was presented in a simple white bowl, with a knife and lemon wedges beside it for a finished touch.

It was a relaxed, purposeful breakfast—a quiet way for Jane to recharge after a full, community-oriented day. She intended to eat the meal in the morning before heading home to continue planning and rest.
