Jane prepared a simple Winter Squash and Egg Breakfast for Friday, February 17. She chose a variety of winter squash—such as butternut, acorn, and Hubbard squash—peeled and cut into bite-sized pieces. She boiled the squash until tender, then set the skins aside for later use.

For the eggs, she cracked three into a small bowl, added a splash of milk and a pinch of salt, and scrambled them lightly. She toasted a few slices of whole-grain toast with a little butter, then arranged the warm squash, toasted bread, and scrambled eggs on a small plate. The dish was served at 7:00 a.m., with the squash and toast on the side so she could enjoy both hot and warm.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of roasted squash and the creamy, savory smell of eggs. The presentation was simple yet inviting: a colorful plate of golden eggs, bright-colored squash, and toasted bread. Jane ate it with the family for breakfast before heading out for her grocery trip and continuing to rest and recharge at home.
