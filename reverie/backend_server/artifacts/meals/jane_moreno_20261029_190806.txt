For a simple, nourishing breakfast, Jane prepared Golden Sage Eggs with Warm Toast and Fresh Herbs. The dish featured four large eggs, two slices of whole-grain sourdough, a handful of chives, parsley, and a pinch of black pepper. She cracked the eggs into a small bowl, whisked them, and cooked them in a nonstick skillet over medium heat until the whites were firm and the yolks were soft. Toasted bread was placed beside the eggs, while the herbs were arranged on top for visual appeal.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of toasted bread, savory egg, and fresh herbs. The presentation was warm and inviting, with the golden eggs and toasted bread arranged attractively on a white plate. The breakfast was simple enough to eat quickly but satisfying enough to give Jane a gentle, energizing start to her Friday. It was meant to be a peaceful, self-care meal before she spent a few quiet minutes resting and recharging.
