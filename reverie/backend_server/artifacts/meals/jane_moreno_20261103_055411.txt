For dinner, Jane prepared a Grilled Chicken Salad with Lemon-Herb Dressing. Key ingredients included grilled chicken breast, crisp romaine lettuce, sliced apples, carrots, cucumbers, and cherry tomatoes, all tossed with lemon juice, olive oil, fresh parsley, and a light dressing.

She began by washing and chopping the vegetables, then sliced the chicken into bite-sized pieces. She grilled the chicken over hot oil until it was golden and cooked through, letting the skin crisp. While it cooked, she dressed the salad and tossed everything together just before serving. The salad had a bright, refreshing aroma of lemon, herbs, and savory chicken.

Jane arranged the colorful salad on a white dinner plate, garnishing it with the last few carrots and tomatoes. The meal was simple, balanced, and ready for a quiet Saturday dinner.
