Jane prepared Winter Squash Risotto with Roasted Root Vegetables, a comforting, family-style meal for Saturday. She washed reusable containers and utensils, then prepared the squash and other winter vegetables by roasting them at 400°F (200°C) until their skins softened and their edges turned golden. She sliced the squash and root vegetables, added the vegetables, onions, garlic, and a handful of fresh herbs to the oven with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then let them bake until tender.

For the risotto, she cooked Arborio rice in a pot with vegetable stock, onion, garlic, and a pinch of sugar, stirring frequently until it absorbed the liquid. As the rice cooked, she gradually added the squash and vegetables, allowing each ingredient to finish cooking slowly while the sauce thickened. She finished the dish by stirring in a splash of cream and a little parmesan cheese.

The aroma was warm and fragrant—herbs, roasted vegetables, garlic, and rice mingling with creamy, savory notes. She served the risotto in shallow bowls with the roasted vegetables arranged around the sides, garnishing it with parsley and serving the remaining vegetables as a crisp side. The meal was prepared for a relaxed family dinner on Saturday, February 18, after Jane had finished her final grocery purchases and planned to pick up more winter squash.
