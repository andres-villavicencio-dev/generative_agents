On Saturday, February 18, Jane prepared a comforting Winter Squash Soup with Roast Chicken. The soup was made by blending roasted winter squash, carrots, onions, garlic, and vegetable broth until it was smooth and creamy. She added fresh thyme, parsley, and a pinch of salt, letting it simmer gently until the squash softened and the flavors melded.

For the meal, she cooked a whole chicken with rosemary and garlic, then sliced it for serving. The soup was ladled into a large, clean bowl and garnished with chopped parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice. It was served with crusty bread, and Jane placed the chicken in the center with colorful vegetables beside it.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted potatoes, garlic, thyme, and the rich scent of simmering squash. The presentation was simple yet inviting, reflecting Jane’s organized, family-centered approach to cooking. The meal was meant to be a quiet, nourishing Saturday dinner after completing her final grocery tasks and preparing the refrigerator.
