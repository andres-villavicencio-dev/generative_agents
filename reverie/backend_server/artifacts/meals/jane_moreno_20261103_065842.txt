Jane Moreno prepared a simple but comforting Herb-Roasted Root Vegetable Bake for dinner. The dish featured butternut squash, carrots, parsnips, and roasted potatoes, with fresh thyme, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of smoked paprika adding warmth and depth.

She sliced the vegetables into chunks, tossed them with olive oil, and spread them in a baking dish. Roasting the vegetables at 400°F (200°C) for about 35 minutes until they were tender and deeply golden, while the potatoes softened without becoming dry. Herbs and garlic were stirred in during the last few minutes to preserve their aromatic oils.

The aroma filled the kitchen with sweet, earthy, and warmly spiced notes, especially the rich scent of roasted butternut and thyme. When finished, the vegetables were arranged in a rustic pan, garnished with parsley and a drizzle of olive oil, and presented alongside a warm bowl of herb-roasted butternut squash soup.

This meal was a quiet Saturday evening—likely her family’s simple dinner—as she finished her grocery tasks and prepared to recharge before continuing her plans.
