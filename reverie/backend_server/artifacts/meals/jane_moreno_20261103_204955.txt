Jane prepared a simple Fruit and Oatmeal Breakfast for her family. She used rolled oats, a small bowl of yogurt, bananas, sliced apples, chopped berries, and a drizzle of honey. She toasted the oats with a little butter or oil, stirred in the fruit, and served it warm alongside a cup of oat milk.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted oats, sweet fruit, and lightly sweetened yogurt. Jane arranged the colorful fruit and creamy oatmeal in a small white bowl, using a spoon to gently mix the pieces before serving.

This was her morning breakfast with her husband, Tom, before heading into The Willow Market and Pharmacy. The cheerful presentation made the meal feel inviting and comforting, perfect for a relaxed Monday morning.
