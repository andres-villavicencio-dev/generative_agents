Jane prepared Homemade Oat & Orchard Breakfast for herself and her family on February 14. The meal featured rolled oats, warm cinnamon, a little maple syrup, chopped walnuts, almonds, and pieces of fresh apple and orange. She soaked the oats in hot water, toasted the nuts in a pan, and gently cooked the fruit until tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of oats, cinnamon, toasted nuts, citrus, and maple. Jane arranged the breakfast in a warm ceramic bowl and scattered the walnuts and almonds over it, presenting it in a cheerful, family-oriented setting. This was a simple, comforting breakfast intended to start her busy day with energy and warmth.
