For her family’s Monday lunch, Jane prepared a Herb-Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Garlic Potato Salad. The meal was simple, colorful, and comforting—a perfect balance of warmth and freshness for an ordinary Monday afternoon.

The main dish was a whole chicken roasted with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and lemon until golden and tender. It was served over a bed of fluffy potatoes, which were roasted with olive oil, salt, and pepper. The salad included crisp carrots, celery, and green onions, tossed with a bright lemon-herb dressing.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and freshly baked bread. Jane carefully arranged the chicken in the center, with the potatoes, carrots, and celery around it, garnishing the plate with parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. The presentation was elegant yet inviting, making the simple ingredients look special.

Jane served it for lunch at The Willow Market, while her husband, Tom, prepared a small snack of homemade baguettes. The occasion was a quiet family lunch on a busy Monday.
