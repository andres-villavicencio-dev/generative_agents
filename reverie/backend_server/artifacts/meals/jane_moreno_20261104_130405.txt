Jane prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables, a comforting family dinner suited for Monday’s relaxed evening. The centerpiece was a whole chicken, seasoned with garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, and olive oil. Alongside it were carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and green beans, all roasted until tender and lightly caramelized.

She began by trimming and seasoning the chicken, then placed it on the kitchen stove to roast alongside the vegetables. The oven filled with a warm, fragrant aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, citrus, and savory poultry. Once the chicken reached the desired golden-brown color, she removed it from the oven and basted it with pan juices before arranging the vegetables on a platter next to it.

The meal was presented simply and beautifully on a serving platter with fresh parsley and lemon wedges for garnish. It was prepared for Jane and Tom to share after their Sunday shopping, offering a cozy, healthy dinner to celebrate the upcoming Monday.
