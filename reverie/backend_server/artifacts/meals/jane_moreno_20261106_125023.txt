Jane prepared Golden Oatmeal with Wild Berries and Honey for Tuesday, February 14, while sharing dinner with Tom. The dish featured rolled oats, warm milk, chopped walnuts, fresh blueberries, sliced strawberries, and a drizzle of honey. Jane cooked the oats in a skillet until the mixture bubbled and set, then stirred in the berries and honey at the end to preserve their bright colors and crispness.

As she served it, the warm aroma of oats, cinnamon, honey, and berries filled the kitchen. The oatmeal was presented in a white ceramic bowl, with the golden-brown, glossy oats and colorful berries arranged around a small silver spoon. Jane offered the bowl to Tom, who ate it with a fork. The meal was a cozy family dinner, celebrating the quiet Tuesday evening before their planned outing to the Rose and Crown café.
