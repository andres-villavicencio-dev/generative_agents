Jane prepared a warm Hearty Family Vegetable Stir-Fry, cooked with broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, zucchini, snap peas, and soy sauce, browned chicken, and a handful of spinach. She sautéed the vegetables and chicken briefly in oil until the chicken was crisp and the vegetables were tender, then added a splash of water or broth, soy sauce, and a touch of ginger for a bright finish.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, ginger, caramelized chicken, and steaming vegetables. She arranged the colorful, evenly portioned plates and served the stir-fry over fluffy white rice.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday evening dinner, honoring Jane’s role as both cook and family caregiver. It was simple, comforting, and designed to make everyone feel cared for and ready to enjoy the evening together.
