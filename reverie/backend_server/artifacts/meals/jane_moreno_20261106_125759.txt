Jane prepared a comforting Hearty Lentil Soup with Roasted Root Vegetables, a warming, nourishing meal suitable for a Tuesday morning. The soup was made by sautéing carrots, celery, onion, and garlic until fragrant, then simmering red, green, and yellow lentils with vegetable broth, a touch of tomato paste, and a handful of fresh herbs until the lentils were tender and the soup had a rich, savory depth. A little lemon juice and a pinch of red pepper gave it brightness, while a splash of cream added creaminess without making it thick.

The roasted root vegetables—carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes—were cut into chunks, seasoned with salt and pepper, and roasted until crisp and caramelized. Jane arranged the colorful vegetables in a shallow bowl, garnished with parsley and a few lemon slices, and ladled the golden soup over them. The aroma of roasted root vegetables and lentils filled the kitchen as she ate.

It was a simple, practical meal prepared for Jane’s home-cooked breakfast routine, designed to provide warmth and energy after her busy day.
