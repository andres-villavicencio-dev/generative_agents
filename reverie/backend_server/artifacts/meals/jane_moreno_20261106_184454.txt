Jane prepared a simple but warm Sunday Scramble with Avocado Toast and Fresh Herbs, a classic weekend breakfast. She gathered eggs, whole-grain toast, ripe avocados, red onion, cherry tomatoes, spinach, and a generous pinch of salt and pepper. She sautéed the eggs in butter until golden and fluffy, then sliced the avocado and added the colorful vegetables. A little lemon juice and chopped chives were stirred in for freshness, while a pinch of paprika was sprinkled over the finished dish for a gentle smoky note.

The kitchen smelled of toasted bread, ripe fruit, warm butter, and savory herbs. Jane plated the breakfast on a clean white plate, arranging the toast, sliced avocado, and vegetables in an appealing, colorful layer. Before leaving, she tidied the kitchen—clearing the counter, washing the pan, and returning the utensils to their proper places. She took a short walk around The Willow Market, observing the neighborhood while enjoying the quiet, aromatic meal as a way to start her day in a relaxed, grateful mood.
