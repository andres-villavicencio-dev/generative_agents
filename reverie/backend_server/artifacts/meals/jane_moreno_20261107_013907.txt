Jane prepared a simple, comforting breakfast of toast with scrambled eggs and avocado. She toasted two slices of sourdough bread, seasoned the eggs with salt and pepper, and added a generous portion of ripe avocado to the pan. The finished meal had a warm, buttery aroma and a bright green color from the avocado, balanced by the golden crust of the toast.

Jane arranged the eggs and toast on a white plate, placing a small bowl of yogurt and lemon wedges beside it. The presentation was clean, inviting, and practical—perfect for a Tuesday morning at Hobbs Cafe.

The occasion was a quiet family breakfast, likely for Tom and Victor. By the time Jane finished, she was mindful of her energy, carrying forward the reminder to tidy the cafe while making the most of what she still had.
