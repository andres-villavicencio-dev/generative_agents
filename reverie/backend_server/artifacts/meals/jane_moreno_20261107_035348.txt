On Tuesday, February 14, Jane Moreno prepared Sunday Brunch Toast with Honey-Glazed Apples and Berries. The simple, warm breakfast was made for a family gathering at Hobbs Cafe, where she was helping with seating and checklists.

She toasted whole-grain bread and sliced apples, pomegranate, and mixed berries. She brushed the apples with honey, sprinkled with cinnamon, and arranged them alongside the toasted bread on a white plate. A small bowl of yogurt and the bright colors of the fruit gave the dish a cheerful, inviting finish.

Aromatic, lightly sweet, and warmly golden, the meal smelled inviting with hints of cinnamon, honey, and fruit. Jane’s friendly expression and the cozy atmosphere suggested it was meant to bring everyone together.
