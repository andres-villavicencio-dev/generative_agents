Jane prepared a fresh salsa sauce for lunch, balancing the flavors with a portion of grilled chicken and a side of grilled asparagus. The dish was called Lemon-Herb Chicken and Asparagus Salsa.

Key ingredients included ripe tomatoes, red onion, cilantro, jalapeño, garlic, lime juice, oregano, olive oil, and a pinch of salt. Jane chopped the tomatoes and onions finely, minced the garlic, and lightly sautéed the chicken and asparagus in olive oil before adding the salsa ingredients. She tossed everything with the lime juice and finished it with fresh herbs, giving the sauce a bright, zesty finish.

Aromatic, colorful, and refreshing, the dish smelled of ripe tomatoes, garlic, herbs, and citrus. She presented it in a simple white bowl with the chicken and asparagus arranged neatly beside it, garnished with a few fresh herbs. The meal was a relaxed family lunch on a Monday, February 13, intended to be light, filling, and satisfying.
