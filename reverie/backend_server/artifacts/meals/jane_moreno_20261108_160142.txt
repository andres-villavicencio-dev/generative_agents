Dish: Spicy Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice

Jane prepared the meal after taking a short break on Monday, February 13. She added the remaining ingredients—chicken thighs, bell peppers, onions, carrots, broccoli, and corn—to a large pan and cooked them together with a little oil and a splash of soy sauce.

The aroma was warm and savory, with fragrant garlic, herbs, and a hint of chili giving it a gentle kick. She tossed everything while it simmered, stirring occasionally until the chicken turned golden and the vegetables were tender but still crisp.

She served the colorful stir-fry over fluffy white rice, arranging the chicken, colorful vegetables, and rice in a neat, family-style presentation. The dish was a comforting, hearty lunch for her and her family, fitting into her regular routine of cooking dinner around 6 p.m.
