On Monday, February 13, Jane prepared Baked Salmon with Lemon-Lime Butter and Asparagus. The dish featured salmon fillets, a handful of asparagus spears, lemon wedges, fresh herbs, and the buttery lemon-lime sauce.

Jane marinated the salmon with olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, and lime zest, then baked it with the asparagus until the fish was opaque and the asparagus was tender-crisp. The butter was made by melting unsalted butter with a splash of lemon juice and a pinch of garlic powder, then drizzled over the salmon and asparagus just before the oven was turned off.

The kitchen filled with the warm, buttery aroma of garlic and lemon. Jane arranged the golden salmon, bright green asparagus, and sliced lemon on a simple white plate, garnishing it with chopped parsley. It was prepared for an evening family dinner, with Jane and Tom ready to relax together after a short break.
