Jane Moreno prepared Creamy Tomato Basil Soup, a comforting home-cooked meal for dinner.

The key ingredients were fresh tomatoes, garlic, onion, carrots, celery, and a blend of heavy cream, vegetable broth, and fresh basil. She began by sautéing the onions and carrots in olive oil until they softened, then added garlic and celery and cooked until fragrant. She deglazed the pan with tomato sauce and simmered it slowly for 15 minutes.

Afterward, she blended the vegetables and broth until smooth, then simmered the soup briefly. She finished it with fresh basil and a splash of cream, allowing it to reduce until it had a silky texture. The aroma was warm, savory, and subtly herbal—rich with caramelized onion, garlic, and the bright tang of ripe tomatoes.

She presented the soup in a wide, shallow white bowl, garnishing it with a few fresh basil leaves and a drizzle of olive oil. The occasion was a simple family dinner on Monday, February 13, when Jane wanted a relaxed, nourishing meal to share with her husband before winding down for the evening.
