Jane prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice, a hearty and nutritious family meal. The key ingredients were bone-in chicken thighs, fresh broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, onions, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and brown rice.

She seared the chicken in a hot pan until golden brown, then added the vegetables and cooked them briefly until crisp. She tossed the chicken back in with the vegetables and rice to stir-fry until the rice was tender and the chicken was cooked through, finishing the dish with a splash of soy sauce and a drizzle of sesame oil.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted chicken, savory soy, garlic, and fresh vegetables. She plated the colorful rice with the vegetables around the chicken and served it with a small bowl of steamed broccoli.

The occasion was a relaxed Saturday evening, after she took a short break from her housework. Jane served the dinner and cleared the plates, enjoying the meal with Tom while helping prepare a simple dessert for dessert.
