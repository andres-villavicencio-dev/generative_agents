Jane prepared Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze. The main ingredients were a whole chicken, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, garlic, olive oil, dried herbs, lemon juice, and fresh parsley.

She began by patting the chicken dry, seasoning it with salt, pepper, and her favorite herbs, then rubbing it generously on all sides. The vegetables were sliced and tossed in olive oil, garlic, herbs, and a little lemon juice before being arranged around the chicken on a large baking sheet. The mixture was covered with foil, roasted in the oven at 180°C (350°F) for about 45 minutes, then uncovered and roasted for another 15–20 minutes until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender.

The aroma was warm and rich—golden brown chicken, caramelized carrots, savory onions, garlic, and a bright citrus scent lifted the whole meal. She served the chicken and vegetables in a shallow bowl, garnishing each portion with chopped parsley and a lemon wedge. It was a relaxed, family-friendly dinner for Monday evening, celebrating the completion of Jane’s short break and a quiet moment of home comfort.
