Jane prepared Roasted Vegetable & Herb Rice Bowl, a comforting family dinner for her husband and children after an early Monday. She began by cleaning and chopping carrots, celery, bell peppers, tomatoes, onions, and mushrooms, seasoning them with olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and black pepper.

She roasted the vegetables at 400°F for 20 minutes until they were tender and slightly caramelized, then cooked the rice with a splash of vegetable broth and a final pinch of salt. She served the warm rice in a shallow white bowl, layered the colorful vegetables, and topped it with a few fresh parsley leaves.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and caramelized onions. The presentation was bright and inviting, with the golden vegetables contrasting against the creamy, pale rice. The meal felt warm and welcoming, fitting for a relaxed evening before dinner.
