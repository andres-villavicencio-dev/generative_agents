Jane prepared Roast Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for the family’s Monday dinner. Her main ingredient was a whole chicken seasoned with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and a pinch of black pepper. She washed and chopped carrots, red onion, celery, and baby potatoes before roasting them alongside the chicken in a large oven-safe dish. The vegetables caramelized and browned, while the chicken became golden and tender.

She cooked everything in one pan until the internal temperature reached 165°F. The aroma of roasted garlic, savory chicken, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen. Jane arranged the vegetables in a colorful pyramid beside the glazed chicken and served them over a bed of fluffy white rice. The meal was presented for a relaxed family dinner on a cold Tuesday, but it was prepared in the usual evening routine.
