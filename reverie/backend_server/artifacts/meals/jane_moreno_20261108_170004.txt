For her family dinner, Jane prepared Pesto Pasta with Roasted Vegetables. She used a jar of fresh basil pesto, al dente spaghetti, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, carrots, onions, garlic, and parmesan cheese.

She toasted the vegetables in olive oil, then tossed them with the pasta. Jane stirred the pesto into the pan until it coated everything, adding a splash of cream to soften the flavor. She served the dish in a shallow bowl, garnished with fresh basil and a few grated Parmesan slices.

The warm kitchen filled with the savory aroma of basil, garlic, herbs, and caramelized vegetables. Jane’s friendly demeanor and careful organization made the meal feel welcoming and comforting. The occasion was a quiet Monday family dinner, a moment of shared warmth before the evening’s activities.
