Jane prepared Baked Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Lime Dressing for her family’s Monday dinner. The meal featured tender bone-in chicken seasoned with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, paprika, and lemon zest, alongside carrots, broccoli, and potatoes roasted with olive oil, garlic, and herbs. She sautéed the vegetables until crisp, then baked the chicken until the skin was golden and the meat was juicy. A bright lemon-lime dressing served over the chicken added a refreshing acidity.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, roasted vegetables, and lemon, while the golden-brown chicken and crisp vegetables made the scent warm and inviting. Jane arranged the colorful dish on a white plate with a small bowl of dressing, garnishing it with fresh thyme. It was a relaxed family dinner, perfect for a quiet evening with her husband, Tom, and their two children.
