Jane prepared Herbal Sage-Apple Breakfast Bowl, a refreshing and nourishing breakfast for her family. The dish was made with fresh apple slices, honey, thyme, sage, lemon juice, and a splash of cream, along with toasted almond slices and a small handful of brown nuts for crunch.

She sliced the apples carefully and gently simmered them with thyme and honey until the fruit softened and the flavor melded. The warm broth was poured over the apples, cooled slightly, and topped with the bright lemon zest and creamy sauce. Jane arranged the bowl in the kitchen with a small plate of nuts and a cup of warm milk, completing the presentation with a simple, cheerful arrangement.

The aroma was warm, earthy, and sweet—herbs and lemon balanced by the gentle sweetness of the honey and apples. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday morning at home, after a long day of work and caring for Abigail’s prescriptions, with Jane wanting to make her breakfast feel special and welcoming for the whole family.
