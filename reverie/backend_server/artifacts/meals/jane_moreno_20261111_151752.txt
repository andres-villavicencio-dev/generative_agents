Jane prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry for dinner on Monday, February 13. The meal was a simple, family-style main dish featuring chicken thighs, broccoli, carrots, snap peas, and bell peppers. She sliced the chicken, stir-fried it with a little oil and soy sauce, then added the vegetables and a splash of water. She cooked everything until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender-crisp.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory chicken, caramelized vegetables, and the warm scent of ginger and garlic. Jane plated the dish in a shallow bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the chicken and sprinkling it with sesame seeds and chopped cilantro. She served it with a small portion of brown rice.

The occasion was a relaxed evening of home cooking with her husband and family. Jane’s friendly, organized approach made the meal feel welcoming and effortless, with everything ready before the family gathered. She was happy that everyone liked it and enjoyed sharing the evening together.
