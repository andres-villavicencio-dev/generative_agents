Jane prepared Roasted Root Vegetables and Herb-Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Basil Sauce. For the meal, she combined carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and Brussels sprouts with a whole chicken, garlic, rosemary, and thyme. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then roasted until tender and caramelized. While they cooked, she marinated the chicken in the same herb mixture and seasoned it with lemon juice. She roasted it until the skin became golden and crisp, then basted it with a bright lemon-basil sauce.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm, savory, and inviting—herbs, garlic, rosemary, and the smoky flavor of roasted vegetables mingling with the fragrant chicken. Jane plated the meal in a shallow white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the golden chicken in a tight, symmetrical arrangement. She topped each serving with a small sprig of rosemary and a wedge of lemon, creating a visually appealing, rustic presentation.

This meal was prepared for Jane and Tom’s family dinner on a Monday evening, after the shopping trip. It was a simple but comforting home-cooked dinner designed to bring everyone together.
