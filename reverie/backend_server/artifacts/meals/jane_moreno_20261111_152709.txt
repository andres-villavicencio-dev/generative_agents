Jane prepared Tom’s Weekend Pot Roast with Roasted Vegetables, a hearty family dinner meant to bring Tom and the family together. The key ingredients were a shoulder cut of beef, carrots, onions, celery, potatoes, carrots, and green beans, seasoned with olive oil, paprika, garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper. She seared the beef in a skillet until a golden crust formed, then transferred it to a Dutch oven. The vegetables were tossed in the same pan, and everything was covered to braise for two hours.

She finished the meal by adding a splash of beef broth and a few sprigs of rosemary. The pot filled with rich, aromatic beef, carrots, and vegetables, with the scent of roasted garlic and herbs filling the kitchen. Jane plated the meal carefully, arranging the beef in the center and the colorful vegetables around it, with lemon wedges and fresh herbs sprinkled on top.

The dinner was prepared for Tom’s relaxing evening with the family. It was a simple, comforting meal designed to ease her busy day and create a warm moment of connection between Tom and her household.
