On Tuesday, February 14, Jane prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Berries and Toast. The dish included rolled oats, milk, chia seeds, cinnamon, sliced bananas, walnuts, and fresh berries, alongside a slice of toasted sourdough.

Jane cooked the oatmeal in a saucepan over medium heat until the oats were soft and bubbly, stirring in the milk, seeds, and cinnamon. She added the warm berries and nuts just before the oatmeal was done, then served it alongside the toasted bread. The meal was arranged neatly on a white plate, with the golden toast, colorful fruit, and creamy oatmeal arranged attractively in the center.

The aroma was warm and comforting—a fragrant mix of oats, cinnamon, sweet berries, toasted nuts, and rich milk. The presentation was simple yet inviting, making the meal feel balanced and ready for a quick breakfast.

The occasion was Jane’s morning routine to make a convenient start to her day, before visiting The Willow Market and Pharmacy to pick up bread, milk, eggs, and other household essentials.
