Jane Moreno prepared a simple breakfast for herself and her family: Warm Oatmeal with Berries and Lemon. The dish used rolled oats, milk or almond milk, cinnamon, a few sliced bananas, fresh blueberries, and a squeeze of lemon. She cooked it on the stove over medium heat until the oats were tender, then added the fruit for a bright, fragrant breakfast.

The aroma was sweet and comforting, filled with the scent of oats, cinnamon, and citrus. She presented the oatmeal in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with the colorful fruit and a few slices of lemon. On Tuesday, February 14, the occasion was a quiet family morning—no confirmed appointments, so Jane and Tom enjoyed a relaxing meal to start the day. The breakfast was warm, wholesome, and perfectly suited for staying hydrated and relieving her discomfort.
