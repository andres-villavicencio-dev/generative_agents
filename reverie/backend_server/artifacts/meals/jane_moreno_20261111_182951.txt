Jane prepared Oatmeal-Coffee Breakfast with Banana. She used rolled oats, water or milk, a pinch of cinnamon, a few chopped walnuts, and fresh bananas. In a small saucepan, she gently heated the oats with a splash of milk until it thickened slightly, then stirred in the warm coffee beans from her kitchen grinder. She cracked the warm bananas and added them for sweetness and texture.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with the comforting scent of oatmeal, nutty coffee beans, cinnamon, and caramelized bananas. She served the creamy oatmeal in a white bowl, garnishing it with the chopped walnuts and sliced bananas. It was presented simply for her Tuesday morning breakfast, offering a quiet, nourishing start to her day while she prepared groceries and checked the pantry afterward.
