Jane prepared a simple oatmeal breakfast for Tuesday, February 14. The dish was Warm Oatmeal with Berries and Maple Syrup, served alongside a small bowl of fresh berries.

She soaked rolled oats in water overnight, then cooked them on the stove with milk and a pinch of cinnamon. Afterward, she added sliced strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries, and drizzled maple syrup over the warm oatmeal.

The kitchen smelled of cinnamon, warm milk, and ripe berries. The oatmeal appeared fluffy and inviting, with the colorful fruit adding a cheerful pop of color. Jane served it in a white bowl with a tiny silver spoon, making it look neat and ready to enjoy.

This was a relaxed morning meal, designed to give Jane time to prepare for her upcoming family activities. It was neither hurried nor overly elaborate—just a comforting, easy breakfast to start the day.
