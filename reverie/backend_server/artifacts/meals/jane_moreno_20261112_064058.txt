Jane prepared Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Herb Skin for dinner on Monday, February 13. She chose it because the recipe was straightforward and made a hearty family meal.

The key ingredients were bone-in chicken thighs, lemon, garlic, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, salt, pepper, and fresh carrots and onions. Jane washed and cut the vegetables, then seasoned the chicken with oil, lemon, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper. She roasted it on a baking sheet until the skin was golden and crispy, then sliced the chicken and served it with the roasted vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of garlic and rosemary as it roasted, while the warm lemon and aromatic herbs made the meal feel inviting. Jane arranged the golden chicken, crisp vegetables, and lemon slices on a white platter, garnishing it with parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice.

She prepared it for a relaxed Sunday dinner with Tom, who was busy with the market. The meal was a warm, comforting family affair, perfect for a lazy evening after a long week.
