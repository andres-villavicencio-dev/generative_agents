Jane prepared Hearty Vegetable Soup with Roasted Tomatoes and Pantry Staples, a comforting family dinner for Monday evening. She selected crisp carrots, green beans, bell peppers, and baby spinach for the vegetables, along with a ripe peach and a can of black beans as pantry staples.

She began by sautéing the onions, carrots, and peppers in a large pot until softened, then added the tomatoes, garlic, black beans, and spinach. After simmering for twenty minutes, she finished it with salt, pepper, and a splash of vegetable broth. The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted tomatoes and garlic.

Jane plated the soup in a shallow bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around a spoonful of black beans, with a warm peach placed on the side. The presentation was rustic and inviting—perfect for sharing. The occasion was a quiet Monday evening, a relaxed family meal that Jane often prepares so Tom can shop at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, and she can rest afterward.
