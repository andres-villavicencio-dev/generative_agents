Jane prepared Tomato Basil Chicken Stir-Fry for a family dinner on Monday, February 13. She started with a handful of cherry tomatoes, red onion, garlic, and ginger, then added boneless chicken breasts, bell peppers, broccoli, and snap peas to a hot wok. Once the vegetables softened, she tossed the chicken in soy sauce, sesame oil, and a small amount of honey for a slightly sweet, savory finish. The dish was finished with fresh basil and a drizzle of sesame seeds.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the bright scent of tomatoes, garlic, ginger, and toasted sesame oil. Jane arranged the colorful stir-fry in a shallow serving dish with a lemon wedge and chopped cilantro, creating an inviting, rustic presentation. The meal was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday evening dinner with her family.
