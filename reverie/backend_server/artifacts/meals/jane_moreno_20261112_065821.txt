For a quick Monday dinner, Jane prepared Pasta with Roasted Garlic and Chicken. She took her time choosing the right pasta, marinating the chicken, and seasoning the garlic-infused olive oil. The key ingredients were chicken thighs, garlic, fresh parsley, cherry tomatoes, a pinch of thyme, salt, and black pepper.

Jane cooked the chicken in a skillet until it was golden and tender, then tossed it with the pasta and vegetables. She added the garlic, parsley, and seasonings at the last minute, then returned it to the oven to simmer. The aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and savory chicken filled the kitchen, with warm garlic oil drifting through the windows.

Jane served the pasta in a shallow dish, topped with the glossy chicken and green parsley, and set the dish next to her. It was simple, comforting, and perfect for a rainy Tuesday evening with Tom.
