Jane prepared Creamy Garlic Butter Chicken with Asparagus and Quinoa. The meal balanced rich chicken, fresh vegetables, and hearty grains.

She sliced bone-in chicken breast, marinated it in olive oil, garlic, lemon, and pepper, then pan-seared it until golden. Asparagus was lightly sautéed with olive oil, salt, and a pinch of chili flakes. A cup of fluffy quinoa was cooked in vegetable broth.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and herbs, followed by the sweet tang of lemon and the crispness of the vegetables. When finished, Jane plated the chicken in a warm serving dish, surrounded by a colorful mix of green asparagus, orange carrots, and white quinoa. She garnished it with chopped parsley and drizzled the remaining pan juices over the top.

The meal was prepared for a Monday family dinner on February 13. Jane wanted everyone to feel cared for, so she prepared the dish with care and enjoyed it with her husband and children.
