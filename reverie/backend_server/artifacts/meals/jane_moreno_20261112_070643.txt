Jane prepared Tomato Basil Chicken with Lemon Herb Rice. The main ingredients were chicken breasts, cherry tomatoes, fresh basil, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and rice. She began by washing the vegetables and removing the stems and seeds from the tomatoes, then sliced the chicken and cooked it in olive oil with minced garlic until golden. The tomatoes were sautéed briefly, the chicken was added, and everything was simmered until the sauce thickened. Rice was seasoned with olive oil and dried herbs before the chicken and tomatoes were returned to the pan.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of garlic, tomato, and herbs. The finished dish was presented in a simple white bowl, garnished with fresh basil and a lemon wedge, with the remaining rice and extra herbs scattered on the side. It was the perfect weeknight dinner for Jane and Tom, who were preparing for a quiet evening together after a busy day as the Willow Market’s shopkeeper.
