Jane prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry for dinner on Monday, February 13. The meal was a simple yet nutritious family dinner, featuring lean chicken breast, bell peppers, onions, broccoli, and carrots, with ginger, garlic, and soy sauce for flavor.

Jane measured the chicken, vegetables, and seasonings carefully in a large skillet. She stirred the ingredients gently over medium heat, letting the chicken cook while the vegetables softened. The aromatics added a warm, savory fragrance that filled the kitchen. She finished the dish with a splash of soy sauce and a little sesame oil, then served it over steamed rice.

The bright yellow peppers and green broccoli added a cheerful pop of color to the golden-brown chicken. The meal had a balanced, comforting aroma, with hints of ginger, garlic, and savory soy. It was presented neatly on a white plate, ready for Jane and Tom to share around 6:00 p.m.
