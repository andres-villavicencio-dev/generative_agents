Jane Moreno prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce. The main ingredient was a whole chicken, roasted with carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, and garlic until the skin was golden and tender. She added chicken broth, olive oil, thyme, parsley, and lemon zest for a bright citrus flavor, then finished it with a smooth, savory sauce.

The meal was prepared on the stove over medium-high heat. Jane seared the chicken before transferring it to a preheated oven, while the root vegetables cooked slowly until soft. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized onions, roasted herbs, and the savory scent of the chicken.

She presented the dish on a large white platter, arranging the golden chicken in the center with colorful vegetables arranged around it. The sauce was spooned over the top, and fresh herbs were sprinkled over the chicken and vegetables. The setting was simple and inviting, perfect for Jane’s family gathering on Monday, February 13, a warm evening of shared dinner and conversation.
