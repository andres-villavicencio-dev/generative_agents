Jane prepared Garden Vegetable Pasta with Roasted Tomato Sauce, a simple yet hearty family dinner for Monday’s lunch.

The key ingredients were whole-wheat pasta, fresh spinach, onions, garlic, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, carrots, and mushrooms. She sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until golden, then added onions and garlic for aroma. After cooking the pasta, she combined it with the sauce, tossing everything together until the pasta was tender. Fresh herbs, a drizzle of lemon juice, and a sprinkle of Parmesan finished the dish.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized vegetables and garlic. The pasta was served in a shallow bowl, the colorful vegetables arranged around it, and garnished with parsley. It was a warm, comforting meal suited for a relaxing family lunch.
