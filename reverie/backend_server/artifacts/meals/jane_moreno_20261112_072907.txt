Jane prepared Tomato Garlic Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Gravy for a cozy family dinner on Monday, February 13. The dish was simple yet comforting, designed to nourish her husband, Tom, and her family after a busy morning at The Willow Market.

The key ingredients were boneless chicken breasts, garlic, fresh tomato sauce, onions, carrots, bell peppers, and a handful of frozen peas. Jane marinated the chicken in olive oil, garlic, and lemon zest before baking it until golden and tender. She roasted the vegetables in the oven alongside the chicken, then stirred a rich garlic-infused gravy over the top. The aromas of garlic, lemon, herbs, and roasted vegetables filled the kitchen, creating a warm, inviting aroma.

Jane plated the meal in a large white bowl, arranging the chicken, colorful vegetables, and glossy gravy in neat rows. The presentation was bright, cheerful, and elegant enough for family gathering, with fresh herbs sprinkled over the top. The occasion was a relaxed Sunday-style dinner shared among Jane, Tom, and her family, offering a satisfying, comforting meal that reinforced their close bond.
