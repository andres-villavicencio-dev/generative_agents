Jane Moreno prepared Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Herb Roasted Vegetables for dinner. She used salmon fillets, lemon, olive oil, garlic, fresh thyme, rosemary, onions, carrots, and potatoes. She seasoned the vegetables and pan-seared the salmon until golden and flaky, then added the vegetables to the pan.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, herbs, and the savory scent of the salmon. She presented the meal on a platter with a crisp lemon wedge, chopped parsley, and a small bowl of brown rice, allowing each dish to shine. It was a simple, warm dinner for her and Tom’s family, fitting into her evening routine of washing dishes and clearing the table.
