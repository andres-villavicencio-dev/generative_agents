Jane prepared Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables for a family weekday dinner. The main ingredient was a whole chicken, seasoned with garlic, rosemary, oregano, salt, and pepper, alongside carrots, potatoes, and red onions.

Jane first sliced the carrots and onions, then parboiled the chicken until it was nearly cooked. She roasted the vegetables with a little olive oil and butter, then placed the chicken in the oven and basted it with pan juices. The golden-brown skin and tender meat created a warm, inviting aroma of rosemary and roasted garlic.

When the chicken was ready, Jane sliced the vegetables and arranged them around the piece of meat on a serving platter. She added lemon wedges and a drizzle of olive oil for brightness. The colorful dish was presented clearly and simply for her husband, Tom, and family, ready to be enjoyed on a Monday evening.
