Jane prepared Tom’s Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Parmesan. The key ingredients were a whole chicken, potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, parsley, and a small handful of cherry tomatoes.

Jane took the kitchen apart first, wiping down the counters and sorting the ingredients into labeled containers. She preheated the oven, washed and sliced the vegetables, then seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and dried oregano. After seasoning the vegetables, she roasted everything in a large pan until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender. For the final touch, she made a simple sauce with the pan juices, lemon zest, and parmesan cheese.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted chicken and caramelized onions. She plated the chicken in a clean white bowl, surrounded it with the bright orange and green vegetables, and garnished it with fresh parsley and lemon slices.

The meal was prepared for dinner on Monday, February 13, a relaxed evening after Jane’s regular grocery shopping and preparing for the family. It was a simple, comforting meal that reflected her organized, friendly approach to home cooking.
