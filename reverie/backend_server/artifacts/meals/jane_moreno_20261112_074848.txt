Jane prepared Chicken, Vegetable, and Rice Bake for dinner. She washed her hands, rinsed the vegetables, and arranged the remaining grocery items in the cupboard before putting on her coat.

The dish consists of bone-in chicken breasts, bell peppers, onions, carrots, celery, and rice. Jane seared the chicken with olive oil and seasoning, then placed it around a bed of cooked vegetables and rice in a baking dish. She added tomato sauce and a splash of butter, then covered it with foil to let the flavors meld.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, garlic, and herbs. When she turned it off, the chicken was golden and tender, the vegetables were bright, and the rice was fluffy. She sliced the chicken into strips, arranged it over the vegetables, and garnished the dish with fresh parsley.

The meal was served on a white plate with a fork and knife, ready for Jane and Tom to enjoy together at home. It was a simple, comforting dinner prepared while helping Tom manage the market and pharmacy, with the family’s love for cooking and easy housekeeping at the center.
