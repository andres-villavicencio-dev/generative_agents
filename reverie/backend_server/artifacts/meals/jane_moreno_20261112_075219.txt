Jane prepared Garden-Tomato Pan-Seared Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Garlic Pasta.

She began by cleaning and slicing tomatoes, onions, garlic, carrots, and zucchini. The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, and fresh herbs, then pan-seared until golden and cooked through. In a separate pan, the vegetables were roasted with olive oil, salt, and pepper until tender and slightly charred. She made the pasta with a little butter, then tossed it with a little garlic, lemon zest, and fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted garlic and herbs, and the warm, golden-brown chicken made the scent especially inviting. The pasta was plated with the vegetables around it, topped with a small lemon wedge and a drizzle of olive oil.

Jane served the meal with a glass of water and a cup of tea for dinner on Monday, February 13, when she and Tom were preparing for the upcoming week. The cheerful, family-style presentation reflected her friendly, organized nature and her desire to make simple, nourishing meals feel comforting and enjoyable.
