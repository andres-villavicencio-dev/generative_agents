Jane prepared a Classic Beef Stir-Fry with broccoli and carrots for Monday evening dinner. The main ingredient was a lean cut of beef, sliced thinly against the grain and marinated in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and a splash of rice vinegar. She also used frozen broccoli florets, carrots, snap peas, and bell peppers, adding a tablespoon of low-sodium soy sauce and a pinch of sesame oil to the pan.

After sautéing the vegetables until crisp-tender, she added the beef and cooked it briefly until the edges browned. The dish was finished with chopped cilantro, a few slices of lemon, and a drizzle of honey-soy glaze. The aroma filled the kitchen with savory soy, warm ginger, garlic, and lightly sweetened onion.

Jane plated the stir-fry in a shallow, oval dish and arranged the colorful vegetables around the beef. The bright green, orange, and red colors made it visually appealing, while the glossy glaze highlighted the tender beef. She served it alongside steamed rice and a small bowl of cucumber salad.

The dinner was a relaxed family meal shared on Monday, February 13, a quiet evening after a long day of shopping and housework. It was a simple, nourishing meal that reflected Jane’s love of preparing comforting food for her husband and family.
