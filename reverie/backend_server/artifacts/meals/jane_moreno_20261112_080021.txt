Jane prepared Herbed Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables. She used boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, zucchini, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, fresh parsley, and a pinch of red pepper flakes. She marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon, herbs, and garlic, then seared it in a skillet until golden, before baking the vegetables until crisp and tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, herbs, and roasting vegetables, with the golden chicken adding a savory, fragrant scent. She arranged the chicken and vegetables in a shallow baking dish, garnished with chopped parsley, and served them alongside a simple roasted potato and a small side salad.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed Monday family dinner. Jane’s organized approach made the process easy and enjoyable, giving the family a comforting, cheerful occasion to gather around.
