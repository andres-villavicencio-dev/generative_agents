Jane Moreno prepared Classic Paninis with Brie, Honey, and Roasted Chicken for her family’s breakfast on Tuesday, February 14. She used two slices of toasted sourdough, brie cheese, honey, cucumber, tomato, and roasted chicken, then topped everything with a crisp herb dressing.

To prepare the paninis, she toasted the bread until golden, layered the fillings, and pressed the cheese under the edges. In a skillet, she browned the chicken, added a splash of lemon juice and olive oil, and heated the paninis briefly until the cheese melted and the bread became crisp. She served the paninis with warm crackers and a glass of orange juice.

The aroma was rich and inviting: toasted bread, melted cheese, savory chicken, honey, herbs, and fresh fruit. The paninis looked colorful and appetizing, with golden crusts, gooey cheese, and contrasting toppings. It was the perfect breakfast for a relaxed family morning, fitting into Jane’s balanced routine of cooking, organizing, and enjoying nutritious meals together.
