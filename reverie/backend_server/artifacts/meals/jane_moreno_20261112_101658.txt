Jane prepared Roasted Chicken with Herb-Rice, a comforting family breakfast for Tuesday, February 14. She used leftover roasted chicken, baby potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, dried herbs, lemon juice, olive oil, and a generous portion of fluffy white rice.

To prepare it, she first seared the chicken in a pan until golden and cooked the vegetables until tender. She tossed them with the rice, adding a splash of lemon and cooking everything together in the oven until the chicken was fully warmed through. The aroma of roasted poultry, caramelized onions, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen.

She arranged the warm chicken and rice on a platter and served it with a simple lemon-herb sauce and extra vegetables. The meal was bright, inviting, and ready for breakfast or a quick lunch. It was the kind of practical, comforting meal Jane often prepares for her family—warm, nourishing, and a sign that the home was safe and ready for the day ahead.
