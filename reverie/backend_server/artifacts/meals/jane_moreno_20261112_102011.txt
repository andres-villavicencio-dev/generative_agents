Jane Moreno prepared a comforting family breakfast of panini with roasted chicken and fresh vegetables. Key ingredients included whole-grain bread, sliced chicken breast, roasted tomatoes, onions, spinach, mozzarella, basil, and a creamy avocado-mayo dressing.

She warmed the bread in a panini press, then spread the dressing and layer the chicken and vegetables. The panini was sealed and toasted until the golden crust formed and the chicken was cooked through. A small bowl of crisp vegetables and a cup of freshly brewed coffee were placed beside the table.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted bread, roasted chicken, herbs, and cheese. Jane arranged the panini neatly on the table, using bright greens and golden bread to make the meal look inviting and balanced. The breakfast was prepared for Tuesday, February 14, before the family gathered for a relaxed family dinner.
