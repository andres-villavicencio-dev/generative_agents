Jane prepared a Roasted Chicken and Vegetable Curry with paninis for the family breakfast. The dish featured whole chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, tomatoes, onions, and a rich curry base made with yogurt, coconut milk, turmeric, cumin, ginger, and garlic. It was slow-roasted in the oven until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were softened, then simmered until the sauce thickened.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and fragrant—a savory blend of roasted poultry, caramelized onions, carrots, and aromatic spices. Jane arranged the curry on a white serving platter, spooning it over the chicken and arranging the crisp paninis around it. Toasted bread was placed on the side, with sliced tomatoes and cucumber adding a fresh, bright contrast.

The meal was prepared for Tuesday, February 14, a quiet family breakfast day. Jane finished cooking around 7:00 am, serving the family and taking the dishes to the sink, ensuring the kitchen remained tidy and the meal was ready for the week ahead.
