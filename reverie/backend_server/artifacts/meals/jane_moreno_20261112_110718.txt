Jane prepared a comforting Roasted Chicken and Vegetable Curry for the family. The main dish used bone-in chicken breasts marinated in olive oil, lemon, garlic, and dried herbs, then roasted until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. Alongside it were colorful vegetables—carrots, cauliflower, bell peppers, and onions—cooked briefly in the same flavorful broth.

The curry was prepared by sautéing the vegetables, then adding the broth and simmering until the chicken was cooked through and the vegetables were tender. A bit of yogurt was stirred in at the end to give the dish a mild tang, while warm whole-grain bread and a side salad completed the meal.

As she cooked, the kitchen filled with the inviting aroma of rosemary, garlic, lemon, and rich spices. The meal was presented on a simple wooden table, with the golden chicken and bright green vegetables arranged alongside the bread and salad. It was a warm, family-style breakfast intended to bring the household together on a Tuesday morning.
