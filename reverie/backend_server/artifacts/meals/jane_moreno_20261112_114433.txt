On Tuesday, February 14, Jane Moreno prepared a colorful, hearty family lunch centered on a roasted chicken with vegetable curry. She used bone-in chicken breasts, tomatoes, onions, garlic, herbs, and yogurt-based yogurt sauce, simmering the curry until the chicken became tender and the vegetables softened. Alongside it, she made a comforting vegetable soup with carrots, potatoes, and leafy greens, and prepared warm paninis filled with cheese, tomatoes, and herbs for a quick side.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, cinnamon, coriander, and savory chicken. Jane presented the meals on a bright blue serving platter, arranging the roasted chicken in the center, the colorful curry around it, and the warm paninis beside them. The crisp, golden chicken and vibrant vegetables made the lunch feel both festive and inviting—perfect for a Tuesday family lunch before dinner.
