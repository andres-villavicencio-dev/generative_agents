On Tuesday, February 14, Jane prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Root Veggies and Paninis for her family’s Tuesday dinner. The centerpiece was a whole chicken roasted with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, paprika, and a little honey until it became golden and juicy. She added baby potatoes, carrots, and green beans to the pan, then tossed them with olive oil, salt, and pepper for a hearty, colorful side. For a light protein, she baked two paninis filled with hummus, spinach, and feta, then sliced them into wedges.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and caramelizing vegetables. The presentation was warm and inviting: the chicken rested on a platter with its paninies beside it, surrounded by a colorful array of golden potatoes, crisp carrots, and steamed greens. Jane served the family dinner around 6:00 p.m., making it a cozy family meal to celebrate Valentine’s Day.
