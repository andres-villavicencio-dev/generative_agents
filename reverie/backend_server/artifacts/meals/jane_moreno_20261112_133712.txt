On Tuesday, February 14, Jane prepared a Hearty Family Dinner for the Willows Market gathering. She combined roasted chicken, colorful roasted vegetables, and a creamy vegetable curry with warm whole-grain paninis.

The chicken was seasoned with garlic, lemon, herbs, and spices, then roasted until golden and tender. Herbed vegetables—carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, and peppers—were tossed with olive oil, rosemary, thyme, and paprika before roasting until crisp and colorful. The curry was made with onions, carrots, potatoes, coconut milk, ginger, garlic, and curry powder, simmering until the flavors melded. A warm lemon-herb panini completed the meal.

As she prepared it, the kitchen filled with the savory scent of chicken and vegetables, the earthy aroma of herbs, and the comforting scent of coconut milk. The golden chicken, vibrant vegetables, and creamy curry created a rich, inviting presentation, with the golden paninis adding a warm contrast.

The occasion was a family gathering at Hobbs Cafe, The Willows Market, or Rose and Crown Pub, where Jane wanted to cook a satisfying meal that would bring everyone together.
