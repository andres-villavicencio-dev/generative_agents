Jane Moreno prepared a Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Paninis, a hearty family lunch made after an unusually full workday. She seasoned a whole chicken with garlic, rosemary, lemon, and herbs, then roasted it at 400°F until the skin turned golden brown. While it cooked, she roasted carrots, potatoes, and Brussels sprouts in olive oil and honey until crisp, then arranged everything around the chicken.

For the paninis, she used whole-grain bread, mayonnaise, mustard, and her favorite toppings—cucumber, tomato, and avocado—before toasting them in the oven. The aromas of roasted garlic, lemon, herbs, and toasted bread filled the kitchen, while the golden chicken, crisp vegetables, and soft bread created a warm, comforting scent.

The meal was served at 7:30 am on a Tuesday, with Jane and her family enjoying it before she left for work. The lunch felt practical and satisfying, providing enough protein and variety to help her transition into the day’s job application tasks.
