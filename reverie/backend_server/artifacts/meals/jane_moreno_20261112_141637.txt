On Tuesday, February 14, Jane prepared a comforting Panini with Roasted Chicken, Grilled Vegetables, and Lemon-Herb Butter. She used whole-grain bread, sliced chicken breast, cherry tomatoes, crisp zucchini, and bell peppers, adding olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, herbs, and a pinch of salt and pepper before pressing the sandwiches tightly.

She grilled the chicken until golden, then warmed the vegetables and sliced them into small strips. The panini was toasted until crisp, with the chicken and vegetables visible inside. Jane served it on a warm plate with the sliced vegetables and lemon slices on the side, drizzling a little butter over the top.

Her kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken, grilled vegetables, toasted bread, and lemon. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the colorful toppings contrasting against the golden bread. The meal was a cozy family breakfast, prepared in anticipation of a quiet afternoon with tea and a shared meal.
