Jane prepared Cranberry-Apple Pancakes for breakfast. She used two cups of flour, one cup of sugar, one cup of milk, half a cup of eggs, two ripe apples, and half a cup of frozen cranberries. She mashed the apples, whisked the eggs with a little milk, then stirred everything together. The flour was gradually incorporated to form a smooth batter.

She cooked the pancakes over medium heat in a nonstick pan, flipping them until they were golden and fluffy. She served them warm with a glass of orange juice and a side of whole-grain toast. The kitchen smelled sweet and comforting, with the aroma of cinnamon, honey, and fresh apples filling the room.

The pancakes were presented on a white plate, arranged in neat circles with a small bowl of warm fruit and jam beside them. It was a cozy, family-friendly breakfast prepared for a quiet morning at home, giving everyone the chance to start the day gently and comfortably.
