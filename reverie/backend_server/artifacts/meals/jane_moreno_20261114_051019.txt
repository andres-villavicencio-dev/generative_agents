Jane Moreno prepared Tomato Basil Soup with Whole Wheat Bread. The soup featured a creamy base of carrots, celery, onion, and a hint of garlic, with fresh tomatoes, basil, and a splash of cream completing the flavor. Tomatoes and bread were served on the side.

She made the soup by sautéing the vegetables in a pan, then simmering them until the carrots turned tender. After blending the vegetables, broth, and cream, she added the tomatoes and basil. For the sandwich, she toasted whole wheat bread and sliced it thin.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of caramelized carrots, toasted bread, and fresh basil. She presented the soup in a clear bowl and placed the sandwich on a clean plate, ready for lunch. It was a relaxed, nourishing meal for a quiet Monday lunch, prepared to be enjoyed with her family.
