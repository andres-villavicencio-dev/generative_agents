On a quiet Monday morning, Jason prepared Heritage Roast Chicken with Lemon-Herb Potatoes, an early breakfast reflecting his compassionate, contemplative nature. He used bone-in chicken breast, butter, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, and a pinch of salt. The chicken was roasted until golden and tender, then sliced while the potatoes were seasoned and roasted alongside it.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, and butter. Jason arranged the golden chicken and bright yellow potatoes on a simple wooden board, garnishing them with a lemon wedge and a few fresh herbs. The meal was modestly plated, with each piece of chicken clearly visible against the crisp potatoes, inviting, rustic, and comforting.

He prepared it for breakfast at his new clinic, where he hoped the food would comfort those seeking emotional support. The occasion was neither a special celebration nor a traditional wedding meal, but an ordinary moment of nourishment and connection—one of the many ways he wove storytelling, watercolor painting, and classical piano into his pastoral care.
