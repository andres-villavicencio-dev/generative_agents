Jason prepared Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Quinoa Salad.

He began by roasting a whole chicken with lemon, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and olive oil, then parboiled the root vegetables—potatoes, carrots, and red onions—before slicing them. A warm quinoa salad was prepared with lemon dressing, parsley, and a splash of vinegar.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted poultry and caramelized vegetables. The meal was plated with the chicken resting over the vegetables, the quinoa arranged beside it, and a lemon wedge placed on the table.

On Tuesday, February 14, Jason served the lunch at home while setting it at the table. The occasion was a quiet Sunday lunch, a peaceful pause after a long day of chaplaincy and creative work. He shared the meal with family and friends, using the food to create a welcoming, intimate space of connection.
