Jason prepared a Herb-Crusted Lemon-Garlic Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables as a simple home-cooked lunch for Tuesday, February 14. The chicken was breast-shaped, seasoned with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, dried rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of salt, then seared to golden brown. Root vegetables—carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes—were tossed with olive oil and rosemary before baking until tender.

He rinsed the dishes, washed the herbs and vegetables, and arranged the food in a rustic ceramic dish, garnishing each plate with fresh parsley and a few scattered lemon slices. The kitchen smelled of warm garlic, roasted potatoes, and citrus, while the vegetables added a subtle sweetness.

The meal was served at lunchtime to support his work as a chaplain and counsellor. He intended to eat it slowly, appreciating the comforting simplicity of the dish and the opportunity to reconnect with nourishment and the people around him.
