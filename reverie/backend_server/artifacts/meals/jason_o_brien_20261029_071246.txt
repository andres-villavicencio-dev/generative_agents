Jason prepared Sunday’s Hearty Lentil and Herb Soup, a warm comfort meal inspired by the stories and quiet reflections that have shaped his life as a chaplain and creative. Key ingredients included canned chickpeas, dried lentils, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, parsley, thyme, a touch of cumin, and a gentle finish of lemon juice and olive oil. He sautéed the vegetables and aromatics in a pot over medium heat, then gently added the lentils and broth, simmering until the lentils were tender and the soup had thickened into a velvety texture.

The aroma filled the kitchen: earthy lentils, savory herbs, caramelized onions, and the subtle scent of lemon and garlic. Jason served the soup in a shallow ceramic bowl, garnishing the edges with chopped parsley and a few whole peppercorns. It was a simple, nourishing meal prepared for dinner on Wednesday, February 15, as he balanced his cooking with creative writing, watercolor, and classical piano.
