Jason prepared a small pan of Scrambled Eggs with Herbs and Lemon for breakfast. The dish featured eggs from the grocery store, a splash of milk, a pinch of salt, pepper, and freshly chopped rosemary, thyme, and chives. He gently warmed the pan, added the eggs, and stirred them with a spatula until they were set, with a few small golden spots remaining. He finished the pan with a few lemon wedges and served it with a small side of sliced cucumber.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of butter, eggs, herbs, and citrus. The eggs were bright and inviting, arranged in a loose mound in the pan with the herbs and lemon adding gentle color. The meal had the simple, comforting character of a quiet morning meal, but it also carried the creative energy Jason brought from his cooking.

It was a peaceful breakfast, perhaps one of his few moments to prepare a simple meal without the pressure of his work. It marked the beginning of a calm Thursday, before his planned interfaith symposium and listening hour at Johnson Park.
