The meal was Crispy Chicken and Root Vegetables with Roasted Potatoes, prepared for the clinic staff’s Friday lunch.  

Jason sliced chicken breast and roasted vegetables, then sautéed them with olive oil, garlic, lemon, and fresh herbs. He added potatoes, rosemary, and a splash of chicken stock before baking until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender. The kitchen filled with the scent of garlic, roasted potatoes, and warm herbs.  

The dish was served in a simple white bowl on a platter, with bright green beans and crisp potatoes arranged around the chicken. Jason added a small side of warm fig jam. The meal was prepared for the clinic staff’s Friday lunch, a practical and nourishing reminder that food can connect people around a shared meal.
