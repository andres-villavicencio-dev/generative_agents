For his first meal of the day, Jason prepared Mediterranean Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Rice. He used boneless chicken thighs, a small onion, garlic, lemon zest, dried oregano, fresh parsley, and a generous amount of extra virgin olive oil. The chicken was seasoned, marinated for about ten minutes, and then pan-seared until golden and tender. Meanwhile, carrots, bell peppers, and potatoes were roasted with rosemary and a splash of lemon juice until tender. The cooked chicken was served over fluffy jasmine rice, with a small bowl of lemon-herb yogurt and a side salad of crisp lettuce, cucumber, and tomatoes.

The kitchen smelled of warm garlic, roasted vegetables, and the fresh scent of herbs. Jason presented the meal on a simple white ceramic platter, arranging the chicken and vegetables around a mound of rice and garnishing it with chopped parsley and a lemon wheel. He served it as a quiet, nourishing breakfast on a quiet Monday morning, intending to enjoy it slowly while thinking about his chaplaincy work and his upcoming symposium.
