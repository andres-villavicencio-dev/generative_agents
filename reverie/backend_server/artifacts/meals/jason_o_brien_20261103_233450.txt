For dinner on Monday, February 13, Jason prepared Lemon-Garlic Butter Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables. His time-limited kitchen meant he used fresh chicken thighs, diced carrots, potatoes, and onions, with the skin left on to add depth.

He seared the chicken in a hot pan until the skin turned golden and the meat was nearly cooked through, then added the vegetables. He deglazed the pan with chicken stock, added chopped garlic, and finished it with fresh lemon juice and a generous knob of unsalted butter. The aroma rose from the pan in the form of toasted garlic, warm chicken, and the sharp, bright scent of lemon.

Jason presented the dish in a shallow white ceramic bowl, arranging the glazed chicken pieces and golden root vegetables over the buttery sauce. He served it with a small bowl of lemon-herb rice and a sprig of parsley, making the meal bright, nourishing, and visually simple. It was a quiet, compassionate meal shared during the early hours of the mealtime listening hour at Johnson Park, where Jane Moreno and Klaus Mueller had sought Jason’s advice.
