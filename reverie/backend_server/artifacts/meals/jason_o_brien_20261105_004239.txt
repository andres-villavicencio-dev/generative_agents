For his Tuesday morning, Jason prepared Lemon-Basil Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a warm, comforting home-cooked meal he made quietly at home. He used a whole chicken, bone-in thighs, a whole red onion, carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, seasoned with olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon juice, dried basil, and a little cayenne for gentle heat.

The chicken was roasted in a Dutch oven until golden and tender, while the vegetables caramelized in the same pan. As it cooked, the kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of roasted garlic, herbs, lemon, and savory chicken. The final presentation was simple yet elegant: the chicken rested on a bed of caramelized root vegetables, arranged in a shallow white bowl with a lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley beside it.

The meal was prepared on Tuesday, February 14, after a long, lonely morning at Johnson Park. Jason used the quiet time to reflect on his faith and companionship, preparing nourishment for himself before continuing his Sunday art-planning session. The meal offered comfort, hope, and a gentle reminder that even the simplest meals can bring peace.
