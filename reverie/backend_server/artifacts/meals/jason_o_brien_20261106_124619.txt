Jason prepared a warm Herb-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Glazed Carrots for a Tuesday dinner. He began by seasoning a whole chicken with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, smoked paprika, and dried oregano, then roasted it at 400°F for about 25 minutes until the skin was golden and crispy. Meanwhile, he roasted potatoes, carrots, and parsnips in the same pan, adding onions and rosemary to the vegetables. A few minutes before the chicken finished, he glazed the carrots with lemon juice, honey, and a splash of water, then set them aside.

The dish was arranged on a wide white platter with the chicken in the center and the colorful root vegetables arranged around it. A small mound of lemon-herb chicken jus and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil completed the presentation. The meal was prepared on Tuesday, February 14, the evening before his interfaith symposium, reflecting Jason’s compassionate, contemplative approach to community. The aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, cinnamon, and lemon filled the kitchen, while the warm colors of the vegetables and golden chicken created a cozy, inviting atmosphere.
