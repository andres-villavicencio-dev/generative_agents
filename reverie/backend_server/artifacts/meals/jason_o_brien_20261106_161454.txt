On Monday, February 13, Jason prepared Garden-Rooted Porridge with Roasted Chestnuts and Warm Bread. The dish featured oat or buckwheat porridge, roasted chestnuts, honey, walnuts, and thinly sliced apples, along with a crusty whole-grain bread for a sturdy finish.

Jason began by gently roasting the chestnuts and apples in a dry pan until their skins softened. He measured the dried beans into a coffee grinder, then prepared the porridge by combining the soaked oats with water, milk, and a little honey, letting it simmer until thick. As the mixture simmered, he added the toasted nuts and fruit for texture and sweetness. The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of roasted chestnuts and the warm, comforting scent of honeyed oats.

He served the porridge in a shallow wooden bowl, the golden-brown bread beside it, with a small cup of tea. On the occasion, he was reflecting on the connection between theology, art, and human experience while planning the regional interfaith symposium. The meal was both nourishing and contemplative, a quiet way to begin the day.
