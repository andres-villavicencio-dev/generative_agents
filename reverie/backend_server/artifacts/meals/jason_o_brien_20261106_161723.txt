Jason prepared a Warm Lentil Soup with Roasted Root Vegetables and Herb Crust, a comforting breakfast designed to nourish his mind and body.

The dish was made by gently simmering a mix of red lentils, carrots, celery, onions, and potatoes in vegetable broth until the grains were tender. He added fresh parsley, thyme, and oregano, then finished the soup with a little cream. The root vegetables were roasted with olive oil, garlic, and rosemary before being peeled and mixed in just before serving.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, earthy scent of caramelized onions and roasted root vegetables, balanced by the fresh, herbaceous fragrance of the parsley and thyme.

It was presented in a simple white bowl, arranged around a small mound of warm lentils. Garnished with a thin crumble of toasted pumpkin seeds, the dish looked inviting and warm—perfect for a quiet Monday morning at Johnson Park.
