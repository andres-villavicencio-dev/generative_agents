The meal Jason prepared was Oatmeal with Fresh Berries and Warm Milk. The oatmeal was cooked until creamy, then chilled in the refrigerator overnight to keep it crisp. For the next day, he topped it with fresh blueberries, raspberries, and a drizzle of honey, while the milk was gently heated to preserve its natural sweetness.

The preparation method was simple yet thoughtful: oats were simmered with water and a pinch of salt, then transferred to a bowl. Sugar and milk were stirred in at the end, allowing the flavors to meld naturally. The warm berries were sliced into a single layer, and a generous spoonful of honey was poured over the bowl.

The aroma was rich and comforting: toasted oats, softened milk, warm honey, and the sweet tang of berries. Each spoonful seemed to carry a quiet promise of warmth.

It was prepared for a Sunday morning breakfast at Johnson Park, where Jason was hosting his weekly listening hour. Jane Moreno and Klaus Mueller joined him, seeking advice on how to weave theology and storytelling into pastoral care and art. The meal felt intimate, purposeful, and warmly communal.
