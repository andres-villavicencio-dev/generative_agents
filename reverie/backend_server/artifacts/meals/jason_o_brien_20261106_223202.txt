Jason prepared a Garden-Rooted Cream-Chow Chow Bowl for his Tuesday morning, a simple yet nourishing breakfast that reflected the warmth of his pastoral life.

The bowl contained firm organic eggs, ripe strawberries, blueberries, walnuts, honey, and a few slices of orange. He gently sautéed the eggs with a splash of butter until the yolks were soft and the whites set, then folded in the colorful fruit and toasted walnuts. The warm honey and citrus brightened the earthy flavors, while the cool berries and crunchy nuts added a satisfying contrast.

The aroma was inviting: buttery, creamy, sweet, and lightly spicy, with hints of berries and citrus. He arranged the bowl elegantly on a simple ceramic plate, arranging the eggs in the center and placing the fruit and nuts around it. A small sprig of rosemary and a couple of blueberries completed the presentation.

The meal was neither hurried nor celebratory; it was a humble, meditative breakfast prepared before his morning routine. It was a quiet moment of gratitude, blending the warmth of his home life with the intentionality of his chaplaincy.
