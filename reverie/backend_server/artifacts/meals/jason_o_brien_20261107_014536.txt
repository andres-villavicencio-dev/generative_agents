On Tuesday, February 14, Jason prepared Dinner of the Week: Smoked-Salt Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables.

He sliced a whole chicken, seasoned it with smoked salt, paprika, garlic, and lemon, then roasted it until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. Tossed with onions, carrots, and potatoes, the vegetables received a generous drizzle of olive oil and rosemary before baking. The chicken was served alongside a warm salad, and a small glass of red wine completed the meal.

The aroma was comforting and earthy—smoky, savory, and bright with citrus and herbs. At the table, the golden chicken, colorful vegetables, and crisp greens were arranged simply and thoughtfully. It was a quiet Tuesday dinner, prepared without a confirmed appointment but filled with the same warmth and intentionality he brings to his chaplaincy and creative work.
