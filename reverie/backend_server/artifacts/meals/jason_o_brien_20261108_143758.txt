The meal is Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Herb Butter and Roasted Root Vegetables, prepared for a quiet Monday lunch at his home kitchen.

Jason chose a wild-caught salmon fillet, gently poaching it in a small pan of lemon juice, water, and garlic, then browning the skin until it was crisp. He topped it with a simple herb butter and served it over a bed of roasted carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes, which had been caramelized with olive oil, rosemary, thyme, and a squeeze of lemon.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted root vegetables, sautéed garlic, and the savory scent of the salmon. Presenting the dish on a small white ceramic plate, he arranged the vegetables beneath the fish in a gentle arc, letting the golden skin and bright orange vegetables create a visually balanced, contemplative meal.

He prepared it on a quiet Monday afternoon, likely while reflecting on his chaplaincy and the symposium he was developing. The meal reflected his compassionate, creative nature and the pastoral care he sought to share with those seeking comfort and inspiration.
