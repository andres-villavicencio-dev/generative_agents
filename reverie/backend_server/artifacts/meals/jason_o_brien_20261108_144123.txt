For a Monday, Jason prepared Saffron Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Creamy Herb Sauce. The main course is a pan-roasted chicken seasoned with rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, and saffron, while carrots, potatoes, and asparagus were roasted until tender and glazed with the same herb-infused sauce.

Jason sautéed the chicken in olive oil, then basted it with the pan juices before arranging the vegetables around it. A creamy herb sauce—made with coconut milk, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a pinch of salt—was spooned over the top. The dish was garnished with a sprig of rosemary, a lemon wedge, and a dusting of cracked black pepper.

The aroma filled the small dining area with the warm scent of roasted garlic, citrus, dried herbs, and saffron. It was served at the Johnson Park Listening Hour, a community gathering where Jason shared his musical and storytelling experiences with Jane Moreno and Klaus Mueller.
