Dish name: Sage-Root Roast with Creamy Potato Purée and Balsamic-Braised Pork Tenderloin

Key ingredients: bone-in pork tenderloin, baby potatoes, baby carrots, garlic, fresh sage, thyme, olive oil, beef stock, cream, lemon, and a touch of balsamic vinegar.

Preparation method: Jason roasted the pork in the oven until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender, then sliced it against the grain. While the pork roasted, he sautéed the garlic, herbs, and potatoes in olive oil until the potatoes were golden and the carrots tender-crisp. He finished the potatoes with a pan sauce and served the pork with the creamy, herb-infused potato purée.

Aroma: Warm, savory, and herbaceous—the roasted pork released its scent, while sage, thyme, and butter created a comforting aroma of earthy herbs and gently caramelized vegetables.

Presentation: The meal was arranged on a deep white platter in a rustic, rustic-chic style. The pork rested beside the golden potatoes, garnished with a crisp herb sprig and a small lemon wedge, with the purée nestled beneath the main dish for a pleasing, layered appearance.

Occasion: Jason prepared the meal for lunch on Monday, February 13, while writing about the dialogue between theology and contemporary visual art. The lunch served as a quiet, reflective pause before his weekly listening hour at Johnson Park, where Jane Moreno and Klaus Mueller sought his pastoral counsel.
