For Monday, February 13, Jason prepared Herb-Roasted Bread with Grain Salad and Roasted Vegetables. The meal was simple yet thoughtful: crusty sourdough loaves toasted in the oven, with warm barley, brown rice, and roasted onions seasoned with thyme, rosemary, and lemon zest. He also roasted carrots, cauliflower, and onions with a little olive oil and salt before tossing them with a crisp salad of mixed greens, tomatoes, and a light vinaigrette.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of toasted bread, roasted vegetables, and the faint scent of rosemary. Jason arranged the bread and grains on a serving platter, garnished with parsley and a few golden sunflower petals. Each portion was accompanied by a small bowl of warm soup and a glass of chilled water, creating an inviting, nourishing centerpiece for his family.

The occasion was his weekly dinner at The Willows Market, where he had selected bread, grains, and pantry staples to share with his wife and children. The meal reflected his creative, contemplative nature: a humble, rustic presentation balanced with gentle colors and familiar flavors, designed to encourage conversation and comfort.
