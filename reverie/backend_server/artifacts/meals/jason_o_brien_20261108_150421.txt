At around 6:00 p.m., Jason prepared a warm lentil stew with roasted root vegetables for his dinner. He began by sautéing onion, garlic, and carrots in olive oil until the onions softened, then added diced potatoes and carrots. In a separate pan, he browned diced tomatoes, cumin, and a handful of fresh parsley before pouring in a generous cup of vegetable stock and a can of chickpeas. He stirred in a can of diced tomatoes, thyme, and a pinch of red pepper flakes, then let the stew simmer until the potatoes were tender and the liquid had reduced into a rich, aromatic broth.

He finished the dish by adding a handful of spinach, letting it wilt over the heat, and serving it over a bed of fluffy couscous. The kitchen was filled with the earthy aroma of caramelized onions, toasted cumin, and simmering lentils. The colorful presentation included bright red tomatoes, golden root vegetables, dark green spinach, and vibrant red lentils, with the couscous providing a soft, inviting base.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Friday evening at The Willows Market, where Jason had selected his groceries after shopping for ingredients to create a comforting, nourishing dinner. It was a simple yet thoughtful meal, designed to ease his mind as he prepared for his weekly listening hour at Johnson Park.
