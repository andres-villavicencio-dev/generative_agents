The meal was Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Basil Glaze, prepared for Jason’s Monday dinner on the way to The Willows Market.

He sliced a whole chicken into breast halves and thighs, then seasoned the skin with thyme, rosemary, garlic, and cracked black pepper. He tossed the pieces in olive oil and cooked them on a baking sheet until the skin was deeply golden and the meat was tender. The chicken was finished in a pan with onion, carrot, and celery, then glazed with a bright lemon and basil mixture.

He roasted red potatoes and a small root vegetable, serving them with a simple green salad and a small bowl of warm watercress. The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasting chicken, toasted garlic, herbs, and citrus.

The meal was simple and warm, fitting his contemplative and creative character. He prepared it with affection for his friends and family before going to The Willows Market to purchase ingredients for his upcoming book and symposium.
