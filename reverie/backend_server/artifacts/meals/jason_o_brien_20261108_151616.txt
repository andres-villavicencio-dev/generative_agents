Jason prepared a warm lentil and vegetable soup with roasted root vegetables, served over a crisp grain salad. The lentils were slowly simmered with carrots, parsnips, onions, celery, and a touch of olive oil, while the root vegetables were lightly roasted until their skins softened. He added dried lentils to the pot near the end so they remained firm.

The kitchen smelled of toasted bread, caramelized onions, warm carrots, and the gentle aroma of a home-cooked meal. Jason plated the soup in a shallow ceramic bowl, garnished with fresh parsley, and placed a bright orange wedge of lemon on the side. He finished it with a small sprig of thyme and a few whole rosemary sprigs.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday dinner after a busy week at the clinic. As he sipped the soup, Jason reflected on the dialogue between theology and the arts, using the meal’s warmth and simplicity to bring his contemplative nature into everyday life.
