On Tuesday, February 14, Jason prepared Garden Potatoes with Lemon-Basil Cream Sauce. The dish began with a pot of water, potatoes, and a generous handful of baby spinach. As the water boiled, the greens wilted, releasing their earthy, vegetal aroma. Jason peeled the potatoes, cut them into rough chunks, and added salt and a few sprigs of thyme. The kitchen filled with the rich scent of potatoes, herbs, and simmering water.

He transferred the potatoes to the stove, covered them, and let them cook until tender. At about five minutes before the potatoes were done, he added the lemon zest and a splash of lemon juice, then stirred in the basil, thyme, and reserved spinach. The sauce reduced gently, creating a fragrant, savory cream with a delicate citrus finish.

When the potatoes were just done, he transferred the entire dish to a serving plate, garnished it with a final swirl of basil, and arranged it in a shallow bowl. The meal was prepared for an early Tuesday breakfast—the quiet of the new clinic, the steady rhythm of his routine, and the small, everyday comfort of food. Jason’s compassionate nature was evident in his careful, unhurried preparation, while the garden vegetables and warm lemon sauce offered a sense of renewal and gratitude.
