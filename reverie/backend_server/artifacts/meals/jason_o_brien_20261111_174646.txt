On Tuesday, February 14, Jason prepared Hearty Herb-And-Tomato Soup with Crusty Bread, serving it at the clinic before his morning walk toward Johnson Park.

He made the soup from canned tomatoes, diced carrots, celery, onions, garlic, chicken broth, and fresh thyme. He lightly sautéed the vegetables and cooked the chicken until the broth was clear, then simmered everything gently until the carrots were tender. He finished it with a splash of olive oil and chopped parsley.

The aroma filled the small clinic kitchen with the warm, savory scent of roasted carrots, garlic, and fresh herbs. The rustic white bread was freshly crusted and toasted to provide a satisfying crunch.

He plated the soup in a simple white ceramic bowl, arranging the bread around the edges. The presentation was clean and inviting, suitable for the clinic’s early morning visitors. The meal was a quiet, nourishing preparation for his day of reflection and creative work.
