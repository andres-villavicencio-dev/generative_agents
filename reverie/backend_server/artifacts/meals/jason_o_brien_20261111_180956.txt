Jason prepared a simple Egg and Bread Breakfast for Tuesday, February 14. He gathered whole eggs, sliced sourdough bread, butter, salt, pepper, and a small bowl of plain yogurt, then warmed the bread in a toaster. In a nonstick pan, he cooked the eggs over medium heat until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft, then served them alongside the warm bread.

The aroma was warm and earthy, with the scent of toasted bread, buttery milk, and lightly seasoned eggs rising from the pan. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the golden-brown bread, bright yellow eggs, and pale white yogurt arranged neatly at the table, with a small plate of fresh fruit nearby.

It was a quiet breakfast for his own preparation, chosen to support his early morning routine. He prepared it with care, checking that his pantry held enough bread and eggs before breakfast and ensuring his kitchen supplies were adequate for a light, nourishing meal. The occasion was a personal moment of calm and creativity, echoing the same reflection and connection Jason sought to bring into his chaplaincy and community life.
