Meal: “Morning Harvest”

Key ingredients: organic whole-wheat toast, maple syrup, fresh orange segments, a handful of roasted chickpeas, lemon zest, olive oil, and a scattering of chives.

Preparation: Jason toasted the bread in a little olive oil until it was crisp, then sliced it into thin rectangles. He brushed the tops with honey and lemon zest, arranging the orange segments and chickpeas in a small mound. A pinch of salt and a few chopped chives were sprinkled over the top, and a final drizzle of honey completed the dish.

Aroma: Warm, golden, and slightly citrusy—the scent of toasted grains, caramelized honey, and ripe fruit, accompanied by the faint aroma of a small pot of simmering broth.

Presentation: The breakfast was set on a white ceramic plate with a small glass of water beside it. A sprig of rosemary and lemon added a delicate touch of color. It was served simply so Jason could focus on the meal without distraction.

Occasion: It was a quiet, purposeful breakfast prepared for a quiet Tuesday morning at Johnson Park. Jason had no specific appointment to begin with, but the simple, nourishing meal reflected his compassionate, contemplative nature and the intention to begin the day with a calm, creative routine.
