The meal was Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and Herbs, prepared for the evening’s family dinner. Jason sliced red potatoes, carrots, and parsnips, seasoned them with olive oil, garlic, dried thyme, rosemary, and a pinch of salt, then roasted them until tender and caramelized. He added lamb shoulder pieces, potatoes, carrots, and onions, seasoning them with paprika and black pepper. For the sauce, he simmered tomato puree with onion, rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a splash of white wine until it thickened.

The aroma rose through the kitchen—a warm, savory scent of roasted lamb, caramelized root vegetables, dried herbs, and sweet garlic. As Jason plated the meal, he arranged the golden vegetables around a generous portion of lamb, garnished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon-herb sauce. He carried the platter to the table and set it beside a loaf of bread and wine, carefully serving himself and the family.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday evening, combining Jason’s role as a chaplain and counsellor with his love of food, storytelling, and classical music.
