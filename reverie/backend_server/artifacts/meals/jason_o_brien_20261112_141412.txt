Jason prepared Sunrise Shepherd’s Pie for dinner on a quiet Tuesday evening. The dish featured slow-roasted lamb shoulder, tender carrots, parsnips, and golden potatoes, with a rich gravy made from beef stock, thyme, onion, and a touch of Worcestershire sauce. A creamy béchamel was prepared from butter, flour, and cream, seasoned with nutmeg and a pinch of salt.

He cooked the lamb in a heavy pot with a few sprigs of rosemary, then simmered the vegetables until tender. As the lamb basted over the potatoes, the aroma filled the kitchen: savory lamb, caramelized carrots, warm broth, and the comforting scent of toasted bread rising from beneath the pie.

He plated the pie with a crisp crust, golden potatoes, and fresh herbs, arranging a small bowl of lemon wedges beside it. The meal served him and his guests, with Jason himself quietly finishing the last spoonful of gravy. The evening felt reflective and purposeful, balancing the heavy days of chaplaincy with the creative, contemplative work he pursued in watercolor and classical piano.
