Dish Name: Heritage Porridge with Fig and Sage

Key Ingredients: oatmeal, dried figs, fresh sage, lemon, salt, and a dash of honey.

Preparation Method: Jason began by toasting the dried figs and sage in a dry pan, releasing their warm aroma. He then measured the oatmeal and added water, stirring until the grains softened. As the porridge cooked, he folded in the figs and chopped sage, tasting the mixture to balance its earthy, earthy flavors. He finished it with lemon and a little honey.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the comforting scent of toasted figs, sage, warm oats, and sweet lemon.

Presentation: He served the porridge in a rustic ceramic bowl, garnishing it with a sprig of fresh sage, a wedge of lemon, and a small dried fig.

Occasion: This was Jason’s favorite breakfast for the week, prepared on Monday, February 13, before starting his writing, planning, and chaplaincy duties.
