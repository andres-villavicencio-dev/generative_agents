Jeanne Love prepared Heirloom Tomato Basil Chicken with Lemon-Basil Butter for dinner at her Smallville home. The main components were chicken thighs, baby spinach, red bell peppers, garlic, fresh basil, and a creamy lemon-butter sauce.

She began by searing the chicken in a cast-iron skillet until the skin was golden and the meat was no longer pink, then added the peppers and spinach. The garlic was minced and stirred in, followed by the sauce ingredients. As she finished cooking, she turned the heat down, covered the pan, and let the chicken simmer gently. The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of caramelized garlic, roasted vegetables, herbs, and a faint buttery richness.

She served the dish over a bed of crisp spinach, garnished with fresh basil and a squeeze of lemon, and plated it alongside crusty bread. The meal was a simple, comforting family dinner, fitting the relaxed evening before Jeanne went to bed around 9:30 p.m.
