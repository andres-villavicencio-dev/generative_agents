Jeanne prepared Tom’s Slow-Simmered Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Tahini Sauce.

She sliced bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, and red peppers, then simmered them in chicken broth until tender. The vegetables were roasted until caramelized, while the chicken was glazed with garlic, honey, soy sauce, and lemon. A creamy tahini sauce was spooned over the finished meal, adding a nutty, tangy finish.

The kitchen smelled rich and warmly aromatic—herbs, roasted vegetables, caramelized chicken, and warm broth filled the space. Jeanne plated the food in a shallow bowl, arranging the golden chicken around the vegetables and drizzling the vibrant lemon-tahini sauce over the top. She served it at home for the final part of her Tuesday workweek.
