Jeanne prepared Hearty Breakfast of the Day, a simple, family-style meal designed to energize her busy Tuesday. The dish featured two thick slices of sourdough bread, two poached eggs, a handful of chopped parsley, shredded cheddar, and a drizzle of warm butter. Her favorite tomatoes and a little red onion added a bright, refreshing contrast.

She cooked the eggs in a lightly sautéed pan, then added the bread and gently warmed the vegetables on top. The aroma of toasted bread, creamy cheese, and savory eggs mingled with the fresh scent of parsley. Jeanne arranged the eggs in a neat row, sliced pieces of bread nearby, and placed the vegetables in a small bowl. She garnished the dish with a sprinkle of black pepper and a few fresh herbs before serving.

The meal was a quiet pause during a demanding workday, balancing her duties with Tom Moreno’s completed transmission and Ryan Park’s sedan tune-up. It was meant to be nourishing and comforting, allowing Jeanne to focus on her study while keeping her creative, compassionate side from getting lost in the grind.
