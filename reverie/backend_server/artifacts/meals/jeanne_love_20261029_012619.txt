Jeanne prepared Hearty Egg and Biscuit Breakfast, a simple yet nutritious meal suited to a busy Tuesday. She cracked fresh eggs into a hot skillet, then added two slices of sourdough bread, a handful of fresh spinach, a spoonful of butter, and a splash of milk. She cracked a second egg over the top and baked the skillet in the small-store oven until the eggs were set and the bread was golden. A dash of salt and pepper finished the dish.

The kitchen smelled warm and rich, with the buttery aroma of the bread, the nutty scent of the sourdough, and the savory fragrance of the eggs. When it came out of the oven, the golden crust stood beside the fluffy, slightly runny egg, with green spinach adding a bright pop of color.

It was a cozy Tuesday breakfast—perfect for finishing her study session, relaxing, and preparing for the exhibition on Monday.
