Jeanne prepared a Pan-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Balsamic Glaze for lunch. She used a whole chicken, red potatoes, carrots, and onions, along with garlic, olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon juice, and a touch of balsamic glaze.

She seared the chicken over medium heat until the skin was golden and crisp, then transferred it to a pan with the vegetables. Cooking uncovered kept the ingredients tender and bright, while the glaze added a warm honey-sweet finish. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized chicken, garlic, and earthy root vegetables.

Jeanne plated the meal in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the chicken in the center and the colorful vegetables around it. A drizzle of lemon-balsamic glaze and fresh parsley completed the presentation. It was a simple, affordable meal chosen for its balance of comfort and freshness—a quiet Tuesday lunch after her busy service days, before she took a few minutes to stretch and relax.
