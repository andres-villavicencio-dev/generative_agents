On Tuesday, February 14, Jeanne prepared Garden-Salad Panini for a quick lunch after returning home from the grocery store. The meal featured a lightly toasted whole-grain bread, crisp lettuce, tomatoes, cucumbers, red onion, feta cheese, and a creamy lemon-tahini dressing. She added a handful of fresh spinach and topped it with a generous amount of roasted chickpeas and a drizzle of olive oil, garlic, and lemon juice.

The panini was assembled by lightly toasting the bread, layering the vegetables and chickpeas, and closing the sandwich with a slice of cheese. Jeanne toasted it in a panini press until the bread was golden and crisp, then sliced it at the store front.

As she ate, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted nuts, garlic, fresh herbs, and lemony dressing. The presentation was casual and inviting: the golden-brown sandwich served on a white plate with colorful vegetables arranged neatly beside it. The meal felt neither rushed nor overly elaborate—it was a simple, nourishing lunch that balanced her busy schedule with a moment of leisure.
