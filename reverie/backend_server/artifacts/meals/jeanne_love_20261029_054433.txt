“Morning Garden” Breakfast Bowl

Jeanne prepared a warm, colorful breakfast bowl with eggs, avocado, sliced tomatoes, cucumbers, whole-grain toast, and a drizzle of lemon-herb dressing. She also included a small bowl of oatmeal and a glass of water. The dish was simple and filling, reflecting her disciplined approach to meals while giving the ingredients room to shine.

To prepare it, she cracked the eggs into a small pan, covered them with water, and cooked them gently over low heat. She sliced the vegetables and toasted the toast, then arranged everything in a large bowl. The warm aroma of butter, eggs, and ripe fruit filled the kitchen, while the bright colors of avocado, tomato, cucumber, and toasted bread created a cheerful, inviting presentation.

It was a quiet, purposeful breakfast for a productive Wednesday—a reminder that nourishment could be both nourishing and celebratory.
