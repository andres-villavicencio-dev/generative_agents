Jeanne prepared Baked Potato with Creamy Tomato Sauce, a simple yet comforting breakfast that mirrored the warmth and hands-on nature of her work. She chose a large Russet potato, seasoned it with garlic powder, paprika, salt, and freshly cracked black pepper, then brushed the skin with olive oil before baking until it was golden and fluffy. A can of crushed tomatoes, diced onion, fresh basil, and a splash of heavy cream were combined in a small saucepan, simmered until the sauce thickened and the tomatoes softened.

The smell of toasted garlic, caramelized onions, and warm tomato filled the kitchen as Jeanne sliced the potato and arranged it on a platter with crisp lettuce, sliced tomato, and a few sprigs of basil. The bright yellow of the potato, the deep red of the sauce, and the green garnish created a vivid, inviting contrast.

For breakfast, it was the right balance of practicality and delight. Jeanne had made the decision to continue the old-car restoration, though she was also relieved that her carefully structured morning had left enough room for nourishment and reflection. The meal served the day’s work while keeping her focused, creative, and ready to face another day.
