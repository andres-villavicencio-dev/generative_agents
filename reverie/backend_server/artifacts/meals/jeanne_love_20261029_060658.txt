Morning Breakfast: “Smoked Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Toasted Eggs”

Jeanne prepared a simple but hearty breakfast to replenish herself after a restless day. She began with a small pan of garlic-lemon butter for the eggs, then sliced a chicken breast into thin strips and pan-seared it until the skin was crisp. Around the same pan, she roasted carrots and onions with a splash of apple cider vinegar until their edges were caramelized. To finish, she chopped fresh parsley and served the chicken over warm toast, with a side of warm oatmeal and a glass of cool water.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and smoked chicken, warm butter, and the earthy scent of caramelized vegetables. Jeanne plated the food in a simple white bowl, with the golden chicken, bright orange carrots, and pale yellow vegetables arranged neatly alongside the toasted bread and oatmeal. The presentation felt cozy, practical, and proudly homemade—just the kind of nourishment a small-town technician deserves after a full day.
