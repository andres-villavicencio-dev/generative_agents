Jeanne prepared Roasted Root Vegetable and Herb Risotto for a quiet Wednesday evening. The dish included carrots, parsnips, cauliflower, and mushrooms, with Arborio rice, garlic, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of wine. She sautéed the root vegetables in a pan until soft, then added the rice, cooking it slowly with the remaining wine and herbs until the grains became creamy but still distinct. The aromatics released their warmth, while the vegetables added a savory, earthy depth.

She presented the risotto in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with a final rosemary sprig and a sprinkle of toasted garlic. The warm, golden aroma filled the kitchen, and the vibrant colors of the root vegetables made the dish look inviting and comforting. It was meant to be a simple, nourishing meal to accompany her evening walk and rest—no rush, no pressure.
