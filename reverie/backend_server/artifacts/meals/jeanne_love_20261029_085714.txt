Jeanne prepared Heritage Egg Salad with Citrus Dressing for a quiet Thursday breakfast. She combined room‑temperature eggs with mayonnaise, diced celery, pickles, and a pinch of black pepper, then folded in a small amount of fresh lemon juice and chopped orange segments. The dressing was made with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and a touch of vinegar, giving the salad a bright, zesty aroma that filled the kitchen.

She arranged two small ceramic plates on the counter and set out a white mug and a wooden spoon. The simple, rustic presentation emphasized the colorful vegetables and creamy texture of the eggs, while the citrus dressing added a cheerful pop of color. Jeanne chose the meal to complement her early start and morning routine, treating it as a thoughtful start to her workday at the garage. The relaxed breakfast made her feel grounded, creative, and ready to face the day ahead.
