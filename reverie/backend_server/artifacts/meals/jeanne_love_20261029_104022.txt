Jeanne prepared a Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Pan Sauce for a simple, nourishing lunch while she rested in her kitchen. The dish featured boneless chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, and onions, all roasted in the oven until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender.

She marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, dried oregano, and thyme before grilling it over medium heat until the skin crisped and the juices ran clear. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, rosemary, salt, and pepper, then roasted alongside the chicken until caramelized. A bright, herbaceous pan sauce was finished with fresh lemon zest and a touch of Dijon mustard.

The meal had a warm, savory aroma, with hints of lemon, garlic, rosemary, and roasted vegetables filling the kitchen. Jeanne arranged the golden chicken, colorful vegetables, and shiny sauce on a white platter, garnishing it with a few fresh basil leaves. It was a relaxed, homemade lunch for an afternoon of stretching and reflection after a demanding day.
