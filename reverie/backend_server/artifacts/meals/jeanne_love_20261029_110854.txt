Jeanne prepared Garlic-Butter Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for a simple Thursday lunch. The dish featured tender chicken thighs with a golden-brown crust, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, thyme, and a touch of honey. She roasted the vegetables—peppers, carrots, and red onions—in the same pan until they softened and turned vibrant orange and brown.

She sautéed the chicken in a little oil, added the vegetables and lemon, and finished it with thyme and a final drizzle of butter. The aroma was warm and savory: notes of garlic, roasted vegetables, and toasted chicken filled the kitchen.

She plated the meal in a white ceramic bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a lemon wedge. The presentation was bright, rustic, and easy to eat, with the golden chicken and colorful vegetables creating an inviting contrast.

The occasion was a relaxed Thursday lunch after a busy workday, designed to replenish her strength and support her creative projects. It was a quick, comforting meal that balanced her disciplined routine with the creativity that kept her inspired.
