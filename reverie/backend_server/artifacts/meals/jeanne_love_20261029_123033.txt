Jeanne prepared Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and a Lemon-Apple Glaze for an early Thursday dinner at Smallville’s family-style table. The first ingredient was bone-in chicken breasts, which she rubbed with garlic, lemon, herbs, and a little olive oil, then marinated overnight.

She sliced carrots, onions, and potatoes into uniform rounds, tossed them with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and roasted them until crisp. The chicken was seared until golden, then basted with the warm pan juices before returning to the pan. The glaze—a mixture of honey, lemon, vinegar, and apples—was brushed over the chicken and vegetables just before serving.

The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, lemon, caramelized onions, and the warm aroma of a home-cooked meal. The chicken was arranged on a large platter with the colorful vegetables beside it, garnished with fresh parsley. The presentation was simple but inviting, with plenty of space for family members to share.

The occasion was an informal Thursday family dinner. Jeanne wanted to create a comforting, home-style meal that would help everyone gather, while still honoring her disciplined morning routine and creative projects in the garage.
