Jeanne prepared Garden-Spot Roasted Root Vegetable Soup with Crusty Bread, a rustic one-pot meal reflecting her love for nature and the garage. The dish began with carrots, onions, carrots, celery, potatoes, and a pinch of thyme. She sautéed the aromatics until translucent, then added the root vegetables, a splash of vegetable broth, and a generous handful of carrots and celery to simmer gently. The slow-braised vegetables softened into a rich, earthy broth, while the carrots and thyme added a subtle sweetness.

She finished it with a few drops of lemon juice and a final sprinkle of black pepper. The soup was served in a shallow ceramic bowl, surrounded by a warm, golden crust of sourdough bread. Steam rose from the pot, carrying the scent of roasted root vegetables, lemon, thyme, and savory earthiness. Jeanne served a portion for herself and a smaller bowl for a friend visiting the garage, making the meal feel both comforting and celebratory. It was a quiet, purposeful lunch for an early Thursday, balancing her work and creative projects with a simple, nourishing dinner.
