Jeanne prepared Hearty Vegetable & Chicken Bowl with Lemon-Herb Rice as a quick, nutritious lunch to accompany her Friday study session. The bowl featured tender chicken thighs pan-seared with garlic, rosemary, and a splash of orange juice, served over steamed broccoli, carrots, and bell peppers. She topped the dish with fluffy lemon-herb rice, a dollop of Greek yogurt, and a sprinkle of chopped nuts.

The kitchen smelled of toasted garlic, rosemary, citrus, and warm herbs. The chicken was golden and slightly crispy, while the vegetables retained their bright colors and crisp textures. She plated the meal in a rustic woven bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the chicken and rice, and finished it with a generous drizzle of extra virgin olive oil and a few thyme sprigs.

She ate it at the small kitchen table on Friday, February 17, balancing her ASE Master Technician certification study notes with work on Ryan Park’s sedan tune-up. The warm, satisfying meal offered both nourishment and a brief moment of calm before her final review sessions.
