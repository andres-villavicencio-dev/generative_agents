Jeanne prepared Pan-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Gravy.

The meal was simple yet deeply nourishing: a whole chicken roasted with rosemary, garlic, and lemon until the skin was golden and juicy, alongside carrots, potatoes, and Brussels sprouts roasted with the same savory glaze. A small amount of chicken stock and olive oil created a rich, golden gravy.

She prepared the leftovers at home, using leftover chicken and vegetables from a previous batch. She basted the chicken until the skin turned crisp, then finished it in the oven so the interior remained tender. The aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, and citrus filled the kitchen, with the savory gravy adding a comforting warmth.

The meal was plated in a simple white bowl, with the chicken, vegetables, and gravy arranged neatly around her. She added a side of steamed broccoli for extra color. It was a quiet, healing lunch on a Friday—a reminder that even when her structured routine feels demanding, nourishing food can bring peace.
