Jeanne Love prepared a hearty Hearty Vegetable Pasta with Roasted Tomato Soup, a simple yet comforting Friday lunch that balanced the stress of her ASE certification and garage work. She cooked fresh pasta, chopped carrots, onions, bell peppers, and spinach, then sautéed them until lightly browned. She roasted the vegetables in a dusty red sauce with tomatoes, olive oil, garlic, thyme, and a touch of lemon juice, serving the soup alongside fresh whole-grain bread.

The aroma was warm and savory—a fragrant mix of roasted garlic, earthy carrots, and bright lemon. The soup, in a wide metal bowl, had a rich, glossy appearance, while the pasta was plump and evenly cooked. Jeanne plated the meal with a crisp salad of greens and almonds on the side, adding color and a satisfying crunch.

It was a quiet, purposeful lunch on Friday. She ate it while reviewing the menu and choosing a balanced combination to prepare later, reflecting her disciplined routine while still preserving room for study and creative work. The meal was simple, nourishing, and a peaceful reminder to slow down and take care of herself.
