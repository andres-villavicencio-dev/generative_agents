Jeanne prepared a Grandma’s Herb-Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice and Roasted Vegetables at her local grocery store. The dish featured a whole chicken glazed with olive oil, garlic, dried rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, and a touch of honey, alongside fluffy rice and carrots, broccoli, and bell peppers.

She took the chicken out of the refrigerator, seasoned it gently to preserve its natural flavors, and roasted it in a covered pan with plenty of oil until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. The vegetables were roasted alongside until crisp and tender. For the rice, she used a pot of water and a dash of salt, letting it simmer until the grains were tender and the pan released its aroma.

She served the chicken on a simple white plate, garnished with chopped parsley, a squeeze of lemon, and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. The setting was bright and inviting—her usual Friday lunchtime at the store, after Ryan Park’s sedan tune-up, but still prepared with the discipline and warmth she brings to her daily life.
