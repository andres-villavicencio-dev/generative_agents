Jeanne prepared Hearty Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze at the grocery store before returning home to cook. The meal was a simple, nourishing lunch packed with bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, celery, onions, garlic, and fresh herbs, arranged in a large roasting pan.

She first thawed the chicken and cut the vegetables, then sautéed the aromatics and garlic, and roasted everything together until the chicken was golden, the vegetables were tender, and the glaze had caramelized slightly. A squeeze of lemon juice added the final brightness.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, onions, and herbs, while the vegetables retained a bright, earthy color. Jeanne plated it in a shallow white bowl, garnished with fresh parsley and lemon slices, presenting it as a comforting, practical lunch for a relaxed Friday.
