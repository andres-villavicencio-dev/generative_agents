Jeanne prepared Garlic-Lemon Herb Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables for lunch at the grocery store. She chose a bone-in chicken, carrots, potatoes, onions, garlic, fresh parsley, lemon, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a splash of chicken broth.

The oven was preheated to 400°F (200°C). The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic, and lemon zest before being roasted alongside the chicken. Jeanne arranged them on a large platter: golden carrots and potatoes beside the roasted chicken, with fresh parsley sprinkled over the top.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted poultry, warm garlic, citrus, and earthy root vegetables. The presentation was rustic and inviting—serving the chicken on a white plate with the vegetables arranged around it, garnished with a final scattering of parsley.

The occasion was a relaxed Friday lunch at the grocery store. Jeanne had finished her morning routine, prepared breakfast, and was now balancing work, studies, and creative projects while enjoying a nutritious, comforting meal before heading home.
