Jeanne prepared Gravy-Crusted Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Garlic Bread for her Friday lunch before tackling a quick car tune-up and studying for her ASE certification.

She used a whole chicken, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, garlic, chicken broth, olive oil, paprika, thyme, and parsley. The chicken was seared in a hot skillet until golden, then transferred to a baking sheet to roast with a rich golden-brown gravy. She roasted the vegetables and served them on the same sheet, then arranged them around the chicken. A crisp garlic bread and lemon wedges were placed on the side.

The aroma was warm and savory, with roasted garlic, herbs, and the savory edge of the meat. Presentation was rustic and inviting, with the golden-brown chicken taking center stage and the vegetables adding a colorful, hearty contrast. The meal felt balanced and comforting, fitting Jeanne’s purposeful routine before she continued with her work and certification.
