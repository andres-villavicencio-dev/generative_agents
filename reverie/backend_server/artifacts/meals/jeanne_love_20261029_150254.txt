Jeanne prepared Sunday Scramble with Smoked Sausage and Spinach, a simple yet comforting lunch for her Friday at the grocery store. The dish included smoked turkey sausage, canned or fresh spinach, onions, garlic, olive oil, and a splash of lemon juice. She sautéed the sausage and onions until golden, added the garlic, spinach, and remaining vegetables, then cooked it until the spinach wilted and the sausage lost its pink color.

The aroma was earthy, savory, and fragrant, with the mild smoke of the sausage blending into the fresh, herbaceous notes of the spinach. She plated it in a shallow white bowl, arranging the golden sausage and fluffy green vegetables in a neat, rustic arrangement. The crisp edges of the sausage and wilted leaves added visual appeal, while the bright lemon-garlic dressing brought the meal together.

She ate the lunch on her walk home to organize the remaining groceries, making it a relaxed, nourishing stop before her evening routine. The meal was practical and cheerful—a quick, satisfying lunch that balanced the structured schedule she had built around her automotive work, studies, and creative projects.
