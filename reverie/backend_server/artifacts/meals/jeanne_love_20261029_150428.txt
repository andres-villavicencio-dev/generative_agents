Jeanne prepared a hearty Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables to eat lunch at the grocery store. The dish was prepared in two parts: chicken was seasoned with garlic, lemon, parsley, dried rosemary, and paprika, then seared in a skillet until golden and cooked through before being rested. Root vegetables—carrots, sweet potatoes, and onions—were roasted with olive oil, salt, and pepper until tender and caramelized.

The kitchen smelled rich and aromatic: toasted garlic, roasted potatoes, lemon zest, and the warm aroma of rosemary filled the small kitchen. Jeanne arranged the golden chicken and colorful vegetables in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a few rosemary sprigs. She carried it to the grocery store to eat before cleaning up, using the lunch as a quiet, grounding moment before returning to her work.
