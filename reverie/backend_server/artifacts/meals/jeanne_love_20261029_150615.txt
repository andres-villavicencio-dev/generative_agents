Jeanne prepared Garlic-Herb Roast Chicken with Lemon-Basil Rice for a quiet Friday lunch at home. The chicken was seasoned with garlic, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, and black pepper, then roasted at 400°F until the skin was crisp and the breast was tender. The rice was cooked with the same herbs, a splash of vegetable broth, and a generous amount of butter.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of roasted poultry and toasted garlic, followed by the bright scent of lemon and herbs. Jeanne plated the chicken in a shallow white bowl, arranging the golden grains of rice around the center. Garnished with parsley and a drizzle of balsamic glaze, the meal was simple, colorful, and inviting.

She ate it in the early afternoon before heading to the grocery store to review her list and ensure nothing was overlooked for the rest of her week. The lunch was a peaceful escape from her busy schedule—balanced by work, study, creative projects, stretching, and rest.
