Jeanne prepared a Hearty Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Roasted Potatoes.

She seasoned a whole chicken with salt, pepper, garlic, rosemary, and thyme, then roasted it at 375°F (190°C) until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. She added carrots, parsnips, and potatoes to the oven along with a splash of chicken broth. The aromas filled the kitchen with warm, savory notes of roasted garlic, herbs, and the gentle smoke of the oven.

Jeanne presented the meal on a rustic wooden serving platter, placing the golden chicken at the center and arranging the vegetables around it. Lemon wedges and a sprig of rosemary adorned the dish. She served the chicken, potatoes, and carrots with lemon butter sauce.

The occasion was a relaxed Friday meal after balancing her ASE certification studies and Ryan Park’s sedan tune-up. Its simple, comforting nature made a satisfying contrast to her disciplined schedule.
