Jeanne prepared Scrambled Eggs with Spinach, Tomato, and Garlic Pancakes. The pan started with butter and olive oil heated until the bottom was golden, then a generous handful of spinach was wilted in with a pinch of salt. Two thin pancakes were cooked with sliced tomatoes, fresh parsley, and a knob of butter, while the spinach returned to the pan before the pancakes were flipped. A small amount of lemon juice and freshly cracked black pepper kept the mixture bright.

The kitchen filled with a rich aroma of toasted butter, savory eggs, sweet tomatoes, and earthy green herbs. Jeanne arranged a white plate in the center, with two golden pancakes, the wilted spinach, crisp tomato wedges, and a few parsley leaves. The bright colors and rustic presentation reminded her of her workshop, while the simple ingredients reflected her disciplined, compassionate approach to the day’s tasks. The meal was a peaceful way to stretch, check the garage’s supplies, and begin her visit to the Willow Clinic.
