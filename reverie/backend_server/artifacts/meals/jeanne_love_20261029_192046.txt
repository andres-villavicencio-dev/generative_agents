Smoked-Salt Roast Chicken with Lemon-Garlic Herb Panade

Jeanne prepared a comforting family-style dinner using mostly leftover chicken, a few leftover carrots, and leftover garlic. She used the panade for a simple pan-crusted topping, then roasted everything in a cast-iron skillet with a little oil, salt, pepper, and dried thyme.

The chicken was basted with the panade for the last ten minutes so that the skin became crisp and browned around the edges. The carrots softened, turning from pale orange to a gentle golden yellow. A fragrant lemon, fresh parsley, and a few crushed garlic cloves finished the dish.

It smelled intensely aromatic—herbs, roasted chicken, garlic, and lemon mingling in the kitchen. The panade was golden and sticky, while the chicken was tender, golden, and slightly smoky.

She plated it simply over a bed of leftover green beans and served it in a shallow ceramic bowl with a wooden spoon. No utensils were needed.

The occasion was a quiet Friday evening after a long workday. Jeanne took the meal to relieve the accumulated tension of Friday, using the food as a gentle reminder that even busy people deserve rest.
