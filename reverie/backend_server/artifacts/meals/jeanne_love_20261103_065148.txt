Jeanne Love prepared Savory Chicken with Asparagus and Lemon-Herb Glaze.

The meal featured breast chicken, baby asparagus, garlic, lemon, olive oil, fresh herbs, and a pinch of smoked paprika. She marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs, then roasted it whole until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The asparagus was trimmed and roasted with the chicken until crisp and lightly caramelized.

The kitchen filled with warm, savory aromas of garlic, lemon, herbs, and roasting oil. Jeanne arranged the golden chicken on a clean white plate, garnished with bright green herbs and a sprig of rosemary, and served it alongside simply roasted asparagus.

The meal was a relaxed Saturday dinner, balanced after a long morning and before her scheduled recital rehearsals. It offered comfort and a gentle reminder that cooking was both a practical task and a quiet creative expression.
