Jeanne Love prepared Rustic Roasted Vegetables with Lemon-Herb Dressing, a heartier Saturday dinner suited to a relaxed day of cooking, stretching, and practice.

The dish featured carrots, celery, yellow and red potatoes, onions, garlic, and fresh basil, with a few sprigs of rosemary for fragrance. She chopped the produce on a clean cutting board, seasoned it with olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic, then roasted the vegetables until the carrots were tender and the potatoes were golden and caramelized. A simple lemon-herb dressing was served alongside the warm vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of roasted garlic and warm herbs, while the bright reds, greens, and golden yellows made the colorful plates stand out. Jeanne arranged the ingredients in neat rows on the board, with the finished vegetables and dressing presented in a small ceramic bowl. The meal was a casual, nourishing dinner for her Saturday evening, a quiet moment after balancing her work, creative projects, and scheduled practice.
