Jeanne prepared Heirloom Vegetable Soup with Roasted Tomatoes and Lemon-Caper Glaze. The dish featured carrots, onions, celery, potatoes, green beans, and cherry tomatoes, all simmered with vegetable broth, herbs, and a bright lemon-caper glaze.

She began by rinsing the vegetables thoroughly under cool water, then chopped them into uniform pieces. After sautéing the aromatics in olive oil, she added the vegetables and covered them in broth. The soup simmered for about twenty minutes until tender, with the tomatoes releasing their sweetness and herbs releasing their fragrance. A final squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt brightened the flavor.

The soup was presented in a shallow white bowl, garnished with fresh parsley, chopped chives, and a few whole tomatoes. Its warm, earthy aroma—mellow carrots, onion, celery, and the subtle sweetness of tomatoes—filled the kitchen as Jeanne sat down to eat. The meal was a simple, comforting Saturday dinner, balancing her automotive work, creative projects, and planned Spanish recital.
