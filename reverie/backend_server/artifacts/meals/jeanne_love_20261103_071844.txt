Jeanne prepared Hearty Hearty Chicken and Root Vegetables. She used bone-in chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, garlic, and a few fresh herbs, then roasted them in a large skillet with olive oil, salt, and pepper. The chicken was marinated briefly in lemon juice and butter before being seared, while the vegetables were sautéed until tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and savory chicken. Jeanne arranged the golden-brown chicken, bright orange carrots, and fluffy potatoes around a white ceramic platter, garnishing each with a sprinkle of fresh thyme and lemon zest. The meal was simple yet comforting, balanced with plenty of protein and colorful root vegetables.

It was a relaxed Saturday dinner for Jeanne, serving as a reward after a long day of balancing her automotive service work, recital rehearsals, and creative projects.
