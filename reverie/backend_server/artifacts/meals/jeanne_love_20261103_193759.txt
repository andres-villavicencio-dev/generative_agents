Jeanne prepared a Simple Spanish Breakfast Bowl, her simplest affordable meal. The bowl contained pancakes, scrambled eggs, avocado slices, sliced tomatoes and onions, sliced bananas, and a generous amount of Greek yogurt. She seasoned the eggs and pancakes with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a little cinnamon, then arranged them in a colorful, rustic bowl with fresh fruit on the sides.

She pan-fried the eggs until crisp, then lightly warmed the pancakes and added a splash of milk so they were tender. The rich, savory aroma of toasted eggs and caramelized bananas filled the kitchen, while the crisp avocado and juicy fruit added a bright, refreshing contrast. Her warm, inviting presentation—golden pancakes and bright yellow eggs on a simple white plate—made the meal feel both nourishing and celebratory.

This was her Sunday breakfast for the week, a quiet moment of self-care before she would practice Spanish transitions at Nair Viggiani on Saturday. Jeanne felt grateful to have created something simple yet satisfying for herself, while also using the calmness of the meal to help organize her recital.
