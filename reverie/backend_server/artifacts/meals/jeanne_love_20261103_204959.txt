Jeanne Love prepared Homestyle Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and Nuts for a quiet Monday breakfast. The dish featured rolled oats, warm milk, honey, sliced bananas, blueberries, walnuts, and a pinch of cinnamon.

She began by heating a small pot of milk and adding the oats, letting them simmer until the liquid reduced to a pleasant consistency. While it cooked, she chopped the fruit and nuts, tossing them lightly with a little honey. She stirred the oats every few minutes, allowing the warm milk to soak through them. Once the oats were tender and the fruit was softened, she divided the warm oatmeal into two bowls.

The aroma was comforting and golden—sweet honey, toasted oats, and cinnamon mingling with the gentle scent of fruit. Jeanne served each bowl with a small side of sliced almonds and a drizzle of honey, arranging the colorful fruit and brown nuts in neat rows.

This simple breakfast was meant to start her busy day as she worked on Tom Moreno’s pickup and Ryan Park’s sedan. It was also a quiet moment for her to reflect on her ASE Master Technician certification, balancing her disciplined morning with her quirky, compassionate side.
