Jeanne prepared Morning Comfort Breakfast with Oatmeal and Fresh Fruit, a simple, wholesome meal she made for herself before helping Tom and Ryan.

She cooked the oatmeal in a small saucepan over medium heat, stirring until the milk thickened. She added two tablespoons of butter, a pinch of cinnamon, and a handful of raisins for extra sweetness. A small bowl of fresh blueberries and sliced apples went alongside the oatmeal, offering a gentle contrast in texture and color.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of cinnamon, toasted oats, ripe fruit, and the faint scent of butter. She served the oatmeal in a white ceramic bowl, topped with the colorful fruit, and arranged the utensils neatly beside it. The meal was a quiet, comforting breakfast prepared for her own morning, balancing the practical side of her automotive work with a moment of simple self-care.
