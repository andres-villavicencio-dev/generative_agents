Jeanne Love prepared a Lemonade and Toast Breakfast, a quiet, nourishing meal perfect for a relaxed Monday morning.

The dish combined freshly squeezed lemonade, golden toast, and a handful of berries. Her homemade lemonade was made with fresh lemons, water, honey, and a pinch of vanilla; the juice was strained for the clarity, while the honey was stirred in afterward. For the breakfast, Jeanne toasted sourdough slices on a hot skillet, adding a splash of water and a little butter to crisp the edges. She topped them with a drizzle of honey, fresh berries, and a sprinkle of cinnamon.

The kitchen filled with the bright citrus aroma of lemonade, buttery toast, and the sweet scent of honey.

The meal was prepared for a gentle family breakfast, but its simple, wholesome character made it feel more like a personal recharge. Jeanne took the time to prepare it slowly, ensuring each ingredient was fresh and every bite was balanced. It was more than breakfast—it was a moment of stillness, strength, and comfort, giving her the energy she needed to tackle the transmission and tune-up ahead of her ASE certification exams.
