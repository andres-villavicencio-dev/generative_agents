Jeanne prepared Slow-Cooked Beef Stew with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Croutons, a simple, comforting dinner meant to match her disciplined, homey style. She used a lean cut of beef, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, garlic, and a small amount of beef broth, simmering everything until the meat was tender and the vegetables had softened. For the crust, she toasted whole-grain crackers with chopped herbs and lemon zest, then tossed them in the pan with a little butter and garlic before serving.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the stew released a rich, savory scent of caramelized onions and herbs, while a gentle lemon brightness added a cheerful note. Jeanne served the stew in a shallow white bowl, with the golden croutons resting beside it and a small bowl of lemon-herb gravy nearby. The presentation was warm and inviting, with a simple spoon, a fork, and a glass of water.

The occasion was a quiet Monday dinner before she resumed her work and headed home for bed around 9:30 p.m. It was neither a celebration nor a big social meal, but a humble, nourishing supper that reflected her calm, caring nature.
