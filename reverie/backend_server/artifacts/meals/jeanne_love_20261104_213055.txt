Jeanne prepared a hearty Sunday Scramble with Spinach, Eggs, and Whole Wheat Toast for breakfast. She cooked the eggs in butter until golden, then stirred in wilted spinach, a few garlic cloves, and a splash of milk. Her whole wheat toast was lightly toasted and topped with fresh berries and a pinch of cinnamon.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory, slightly nutty scent of eggs and garlic. It smelled comforting and wholesome. Jeanne presented the meal in a simple ceramic bowl, arranging the eggs in a neat row and placing the toast beside it. A small plate of fresh fruit and a glass of water completed the breakfast.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday morning meal before checking Tom Moreno’s vehicle maintenance needs. Jeanne’s disciplined, cheerful approach gave the meal a satisfyingly homey feel, balancing her demanding work schedule with a simple, nourishing start to her day.
