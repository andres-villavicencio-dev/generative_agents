Jeanne prepared Tom’s Classic Buttermilk Breakfast Bowl, a hearty, comforting brunch meal designed for a busy Monday morning. She combined creamy buttermilk yogurt, soft whole-grain toast, sunny-sidewalk-style eggs, crispy bacon, fresh blueberries, sliced avocado, grated cheddar, and a sprinkle of herbs and toasted nuts.

The ingredients were tossed in a nonstick pan, cooked gently until the eggs were set, then assembled in a shallow bowl with a spoonful of warm buttermilk and a dusting of salt and pepper. She arranged the eggs in a neat circular pattern around the toast, topped it with the colorful fruit and creamy toppings, and finished it with a few fresh herbs.

The meal was fragrant with rich buttermilk, savory bacon, toasted nuts, and a hint of sweet berries. It was served in a simple white bowl, with a small side of fresh fruit and a cup of hot tea, creating a warm, inviting breakfast for her and her coworkers. On Monday, February 13, the occasion was a quiet moment at Hobbs Cafe, where Jeanne was preparing the next batch of breakfast drinks for the morning shift.
