Jeanne prepared Smoked Herb Breakfast Burritos, a hearty, comforting breakfast for the Hobbs Cafe shift. The burritos featured flour tortillas filled with scrambled eggs, melted cheddar, diced tomatoes, onions, and a generous portion of brown rice. She topped them with shredded cheddar, a spoonful of sour cream, and a drizzle of hot sauce for a smoky finish.

The ingredients were sautéed onions and peppers first, then scrambled in a pan with the eggs until golden. She folded the rice and vegetables into the creamy egg mixture, layered the burritos with cheese, and folded them into warm tortillas. The aroma was rich and savory: the smell of browned onions, caramelized cheese, roasted tomatoes, and warm herbs filling the kitchen.

It was a cheerful morning breakfast, ready before the cafe opened. Jeanne presented each burrito in a small white bowl, garnishing them with a sprig of fresh cilantro and a pinch of chili flakes. She served them with a side of orange juice and sliced bananas, offering them to the first customers arriving at 6:00 AM.
