Jeanne prepared a panini-style breakfast, combining her disciplined, no-nonsense approach with a cheerful touch. She toasted two slices of whole-grain sourdough, layered them with roasted tomatoes, crisp lettuce, sliced avocado, and a generous spread of hummus and lemon dressing, then topped the panini with a poached egg and a sprinkle of toasted pine nuts.

She toasted the bread at the nearby café, where Jeanne made a quick coffee and purchased the necessary ingredients. The aroma of warm bread, roasted tomatoes, fresh herbs, and creamy hummus filled the small kitchen. The golden-brown crust, vibrant green lettuce, bright yellow egg, and creamy hummus gave the breakfast a rustic, inviting appearance.

The meal was prepared as a comforting, filling brunch for Jeanne’s late Tuesday, when she was still rebuilding the transmission and studying for her ASE exams. Sharing the café with fellow customers gave her company, while the hearty breakfast helped her prepare for the part-time shift at Hobbs Cafe.
