Jeanne prepared a Classic Chicken and Potato Breakfast with Garlic Butter, a comforting dish she treats as a reliable morning ritual. The centerpiece was a golden, crispy chicken breast, seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika, pan-seared until the skin turned crisp and the meat became tender. She paired it with golden potatoes, chopped onions, fresh parsley, a drizzle of olive oil, and a few slices of toasted bread.

She cooked everything in a skillet over medium heat, stirring until the chicken released its moisture and the potatoes began to crisp. She added butter and minced garlic near the end to let the flavors meld, finishing with a splash of cream and a sprinkle of cheddar. The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant—rich with roasted garlic, toasted butter, and hints of herbs.

The dish was presented on a white ceramic platter with the chicken and potatoes arranged neatly beside crisp bread. A small glass of red wine and a bright orange bowl of fresh fruit added a cheerful touch. Jeanne prepared the meal on a quiet Monday morning before starting the restoration work on Tom’s pickup. It was meant to be a simple, nourishing start to her day, but she prepared it with the care and pride of someone who believes good food can set the tone for a long, productive day.
