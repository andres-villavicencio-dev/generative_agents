The meal was Garlic-Herb Crusted Lamb Shanks with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Roasted Potatoes, prepared for a cozy Monday dinner at Jeanne’s home.

Jeanne sliced lamb shanks with a generous rub of garlic, onion, smoked paprika, black pepper, and fresh herbs, then seared them until the skin was deeply browned. She finished them in the oven with rosemary and thyme, serving them alongside carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes. The potatoes were tossed with olive oil, lemon zest, and a pinch of salt before roasting until golden and tender.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of caramelized meat, toasted garlic, rosemary, and the crisp aroma of baked potatoes. Jeanne plated the lamb in a shallow oven-proof dish, arranging the vegetables beside it so each bite contained a colorful mix of root, leaf, and grain elements. She served the meal with a small bowl of warm lemon-herb potatoes and a simple salad.

Dinner was a quiet, intimate occasion—Jeanne was preparing ingredients for a family dinner while keeping her ASE Master Technician certification in mind. The meal reflected her disciplined, compassionate nature: hearty, nourishing, and thoughtfully prepared.
