Jeanne prepared Smoked-Salmon Breakfast Potatoes, a simple, rustic breakfast meant to complement her Tuesday commute around the college campus. She used smoked salmon, diced red onion, lemon juice, olive oil, fresh thyme, and a pinch of sea salt, cooking them over a gas stove until the salmon was just cooked through and the potatoes were tender.

She arranged the potatoes in a shallow ceramic dish, topped them with the salmon and a few sprigs of thyme, then arranged a small plate of toasted sourdough beside it. A tiny ramekin of lemon-herb yogurt and a glass of warm water completed the presentation.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of roasted salmon, caramelized onions, thyme, and warm bread, while the sharp lemon and subtle smoke added a cheerful, comforting flavor. She ate beside a quiet walk along the college campus, choosing the meal as a thoughtful, nourishing start to her balanced day.
