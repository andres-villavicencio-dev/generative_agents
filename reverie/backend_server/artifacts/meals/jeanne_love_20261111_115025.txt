The meal was Butter, Jam, and Whole-Grain Toast, prepared as a simple, warm breakfast for Jeanne and Tom. She toasted slices of whole-grain bread in butter until the edges were golden, then spread a layer of sweet jam over the warm toast before adding a generous pat of butter. The aroma of toasted nutmeg, cinnamon, warm butter, and slightly sweet jam filled the small garage.

The presentation was rustic and inviting: the toast sat on a white ceramic plate, surrounded by a small bowl of jam and a spoonful of butter. Jeanne added a small slice of fruit and a few fresh berries as a final touch. It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast to start the day before she began rebuilding Tom’s transmission and tuning Ryan’s sedan.
