Dish Name: Warm Oatmeal with Honey and Fresh Fruit

Jeanne prepared a comforting breakfast for herself while rebuilding Tom Moreno’s transmission and doing a full tune-up on Ryan Park’s sedan. She chose a generous bowl of oatmeal cooked with water or oat milk, topped with chopped apples, bananas, and berries, and drizzled with honey.

The oatmeal was simmered until the oats softened, then stirred with fresh fruit and drizzled with honey until the mixture looked glossy. A sprinkle of cinnamon and a pinch of salt added a subtle warmth to the dish.

The aroma was unmistakable: rich oatmeal, sweet honey, caramelized fruit, and a hint of cinnamon. Jeanne arranged the colorful fruit and oatmeal in the center of the bowl, with a small bowl of honey nearby. She added a few fresh berries and a sprig of mint for a cheerful, homemade touch.

This was her morning breakfast—a practical, nourishing meal that reflected her disciplined routine while she balanced work with her ASE Master Technician certification.
