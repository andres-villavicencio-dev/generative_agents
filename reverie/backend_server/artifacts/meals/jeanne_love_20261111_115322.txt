Dish Name: Rusty Oak Apple-Corn Breakfast

Jeanne prepared a warm, rustic breakfast for her morning routine at the Smallville garage. The dish was a simple but flavorful combination of ripe apples, sweet corn, toasted butter, and a drizzle of honey.

The apples were sliced into bite-sized wedges and tossed with the corn. A small knob of butter was melted over the fruit, then topped with a generous drizzle of honey and a pinch of cinnamon. A few whole almonds and a sprig of fresh parsley were arranged alongside the ingredients for visual appeal.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the sweet scent of caramelized fruit, toasted butter, and warm cinnamon. Jeanne served the breakfast on a small white ceramic platter, arranged neatly with the platter in the center and her coffee cup beside it. She sipped her coffee while eating a piece of apple, enjoying the quiet, disciplined moment of making the morning her own.
