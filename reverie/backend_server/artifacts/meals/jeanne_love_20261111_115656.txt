Jeanne prepared Sunday Brunch with a Side of Roasted Potatoes for breakfast. The meal featured a warm oatmeal bowl topped with cinnamon-sugar oat pudding, fresh berries, and a drizzle of honey, alongside crispy roasted potatoes, toasted almond crackers, and a glass of orange juice.

She began by boiling the oats, then simmered them with cinnamon, vanilla, and a pinch of salt. After chilling, she stirred in the berries and honey before serving. The roasted potatoes were seasoned with olive oil, garlic, paprika, salt, and pepper, then baked until golden and slightly crisp. Toasted crackers and the warm oatmeal completed the comforting brunch.

Aromatic notes of cinnamon, vanilla, toasted nuts, caramelized potatoes, and citrus filled the kitchen. Jeanne presented the meal on a clean white tablecloth, arranging the oatmeal in the center, potatoes on the side, and the crisp crackers with a small bowl of honey for dipping. The occasion was a quiet Sunday morning, a relaxed way to start the Monday workweek while balancing her automotive repair duties.
