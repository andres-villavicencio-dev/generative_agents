Jeanne prepared Hearty Root Vegetable Soup with Biscuit Bites for dinner. The dish combined carrots, potatoes, celery, onion, garlic, and broth, with a touch of tomato and a splash of cream. She added a handful of whole grains for texture, toasted the biscuits separately, and chopped them into small squares to serve alongside the soup.

The soup was made by sautéing the aromatics, simmering the vegetables until tender, and blending the contents into a smooth, comforting liquid. A little stock and cream gave it a rich, savory depth, while whole-grain pieces added rustic texture. Biscuit bites were lightly browned and arranged around the bowls for easy holding.

As she served it, the kitchen filled with the aroma of caramelized onions, garlic, and warm bread. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the golden-brown biscuits surrounded the creamy soup, with fresh herbs sprinkled at the top. It was a simple, comforting meal prepared on a quiet Monday evening—no rush, no stress—just the warmth of home and the quiet discipline of a seasoned technician.
