Jeanne prepared a hearty, rustic chicken-and-rice breakfast for the local community breakfast club gathering in Smallville. She used a whole chicken, sliced potatoes, carrots, onions, tomatoes, celery, garlic, and a generous portion of brown rice.

First, she seared the chicken in a hot skillet until the skin turned golden and crisp. She browned the potatoes and vegetables in the same pan, then added the rice and chopped herbs. After cooking until the rice was tender and the chicken was no longer pink, she finished it with a splash of chicken broth and served the dish in a shallow bowl with a simple lemon-herb crust. The aroma filled the kitchen: caramelized chicken, earthy vegetables, and the warm scent of rice and herbs.

The breakfast was simple, rustic, and communal—designed for the community gathering. Jeanne presented it with a warm smile, carefully cutting the chicken and arranging the vegetables and rice. The meal was meant to be comforting, nourishing, and welcoming, reflecting her disciplined, compassionate nature and her love for the community.
