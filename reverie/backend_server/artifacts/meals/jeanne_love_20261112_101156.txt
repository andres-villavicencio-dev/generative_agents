Jeanne prepared Creamy Egg Scramble with Avocado Toast as a comforting Tuesday breakfast. She cracked four hard-boiled eggs, sliced a ripe avocado, chives, and a sprinkle of smoked paprika, then whisked them with a little milk and butter. She toasted the bread first, then gently scrambled the eggs until golden and fluffy, letting the avocado soften on top.

The kitchen smelled of toasted nuts, warm butter, and fresh herbs. She arranged the toast beneath the eggs and placed a small plate beside it, adding a tiny lemon wedge for a bright finish. The meal was simple, rustic, and meant to bring her morning together with her household. It was not an extravagant occasion, but it served her purpose as a nourishing start to the day.
