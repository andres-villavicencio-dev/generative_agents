Jeanne prepared a warm Oatmeal-Cereal Breakfast Bowl for Tuesday, February 14, after completing the transmission rebuild and full tune-up on Tom Moreno’s pickup. The meal consisted of steamed milk, rolled oats, cinnamon-spiced sugar, a few chopped walnuts, sliced banana, and a drizzle of honey. A small glass of warm water with lemon and a sprig of mint served as a refreshing beverage.

She prepared the oats over low heat in a microwave-safe bowl, stirring occasionally until the milk was nearly warm. The mixture was then transferred to a glass, with the berries and nuts arranged around the center. A small wooden spoon rested beside the bowl, and a tiny decorative tag noted “Breakfast by Jeanne Love.”

The aroma was rich, earthy, and warmly comforting—cinnamon, toasted nuts, and milk mingling under the light kitchen lights. The presentation was simple and inviting: creamy oats, colorful fruit, and golden nuts contrasted with the pale milk. Jeanne ate it quietly at her kitchen table, savoring the taste as she studied for her ASE certification and considered her next steps for the community tutoring and local car restoration mentorship.
