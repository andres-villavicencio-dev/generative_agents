Jeanne prepared a hearty Egg and Tomato Scramble with Toasted Cornbread for breakfast. She gathered ripe tomatoes, eggs, fresh corn, onions, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of salt from her refrigerator, then cracked the eggs into a hot skillet with a little butter. She scrambled them gently until the yolks were set but the whites remained fluffy, then folded in the tomatoes and corn. The toasted cornbread was prepared by heating a hot pan, adding butter, and spreading it over the eggs.

Aromatic, the kitchen filled with the savory scent of garlic, butter, and caramelized tomatoes. The golden, rustic cornbread offered a crisp, warm contrast to the creamy, colorful scramble. Jeanne arranged the eggs over the toast in a single serving, garnishing it with a few fresh herbs and a tiny drizzle of olive oil. The bright colors, fragrant aroma, and comforting textures made the breakfast feel both nourishing and celebratory.

It was a quiet Tuesday breakfast, prepared to support her ASE certification, while giving her time to update the service log, check that the garage and kitchen were ready, and consider the tutoring and mentorship opportunities in the community.
