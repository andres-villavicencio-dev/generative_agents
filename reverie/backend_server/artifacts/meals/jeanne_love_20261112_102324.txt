Jeanne prepared a hearty Cranberry-Golden-Spot Baked Egg with Toasted Cornbread and Honeyed Bacon, a cheerful breakfast that reflected her disciplined, creative spirit.

She began by toasting the cornbread slices, then sliced a few eggs and added a handful of diced spotted potatoes. The eggs were cracked into a buttery pan with a splash of milk, cooked until set, and finished with a generous knob of butter. Meanwhile, she warmed sliced bacon in a pan with a little honey and a pinch of salt until crisp, then folded it into the eggs. Crumbled cranberries were stirred in at the end, giving the dish a bright pink hue.

The kitchen smelled of toasted nuts, caramelized bacon, and warm butter. The presentation was simple and inviting: golden-brown cornbread arranged beside the eggs, the bacon peeking from the pan, and a sprig of fresh parsley for a final touch.

Jeanne ate the meal at breakfast, enjoying the comforting aroma and her familiar taste of eggs, honey, and cranberries. It was a peaceful Tuesday morning, the kind she would have made for herself after the transmission rebuild and tune-up on Tom Moreno’s pickup.
