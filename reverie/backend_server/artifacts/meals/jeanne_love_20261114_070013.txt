On Monday evening, Jeanne prepared Hearty Roast Beef with Root Vegetables and Crispy Potato Wedges for dinner with her ASE study materials. She took the lean beef from the refrigerator, trimmed its surface, and seared it in a large skillet until the exterior was deeply browned and the meat was no longer pink inside. The beef was then transferred to a hot oven, where it was roasted until the interior warmed through and the edges were caramelized.

She tossed the root vegetables—potatoes, carrots, and onions—with a little oil and seasonings, then layered them around the beef in a roasting pan. The dish was finished with a generous amount of buttery garlic sauce and fresh thyme. A few crispy potato wedges were arranged alongside the roast, giving the meal an inviting, rustic appearance.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted beef, garlic, and caramelized onions, while the golden vegetables and golden-brown crust added warmth and visual appeal. Jeanne plated the meal carefully, ensuring every ingredient was evenly distributed, and served it to herself with a quiet smile. The occasion was a peaceful evening of study and nourishment: a hearty, home-style dinner that balanced the demands of her ASE certification preparation with the comfort of a well-prepared meal.
