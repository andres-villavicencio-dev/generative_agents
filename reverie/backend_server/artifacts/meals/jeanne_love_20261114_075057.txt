Jeanne prepared Hearty Tomato-Glazed Chicken & Grilled Vegetables for a quiet Monday lunch at her garage. She marinated chicken thighs in olive oil, garlic, smoked paprika, and a splash of lemon juice, while the vegetables—including red bell peppers, zucchini, carrots, and onions—were grilled until crisp. After cooking, she glazed the chicken with a simple tomato sauce made from crushed tomatoes, garlic, onion, and a pinch of dried thyme, then plated it with a side of steamed broccoli and lemon wedges.

The aroma was warm and inviting: the savory scent of caramelized chicken, the sharp brightness of garlic and lemon, and the earthy depth of the tomatoes filled the small kitchen. She served the meal in a simple white bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley. It was meant to be nourishing and peaceful—enough to clear her mind before tackling the transmission rebuild and the tune-up on Ryan’s sedan, while her ASE studies provided a quiet space to prepare for her upcoming certification exams.
