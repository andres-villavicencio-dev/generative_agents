Jeanne prepared Sunday’s Spicy Chicken and Vegetable Pasta, a hearty family meal that reflected her disciplined approach to cooking. The main component was grilled chicken breast seasoned with garlic, lemon, paprika, and a little honey, while the sides included roasted carrots, green beans, and a creamy tomato-cilantro sauce. She added small pieces of tomato and spinach to the pasta, then tossed everything together until the sauce thickened.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and savory—the rich scent of caramelized garlic, herbs, and gently caramelized chicken mingling with the bright acidity of tomato and the fresh herbs of the green beans. She arranged the pasta on a wide white platter, garnished it with a few fresh basil leaves, and served the chicken and vegetables around it. The occasion was a relaxed Sunday dinner for Jeanne and her family after she had spent the day rebuilding Tom Moreno’s pickup’s transmission and performing Ryan Park’s sedan’s full tune-up. The meal felt comforting and practical, balancing her demanding automotive work with the quiet comfort of a well-prepared evening.
