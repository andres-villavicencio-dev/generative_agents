Dish Name: Warm Spiced Overnight Oats
Key Ingredients: Rolled oats, chia seeds, maple syrup, chopped walnuts, and fresh berries.
Preparation Method: The oats and seeds are mixed in a bowl overnight. In the morning, they are gently stirred together and topped with fresh berries and a drizzle of homemade maple syrup.
Aroma: A comforting blend of toasted oats, the subtle hint of chia seeds, and the sweet, natural fragrance of the berries.
Presentation: The dish is served in a rustic ceramic bowl, garnished with a single sprig of fresh basil and a few scattered walnuts for texture contrast.
Occasion: This meal is prepared for Jennifer Moore's lunch break, offering a gentle, nourishing start to her productive day of painting.
