Jennifer prepared a hearty vegetable-and-barley soup with warm crusty bread, a simple nourishing lunch made while her exhibition, Where the Morning Settles, was taking shape nearby.

The soup contained carrots, celery, onions, fresh thyme, tender barley, and chunks of potato, simmered slowly in vegetable broth until the grains were soft and the vegetables gently tender. Its aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of barley, sweet carrots, and delicate thyme.

She ladled the golden-beige soup into a creamy ceramic bowl, arranging the vegetables naturally on top and adding a small sprig of fresh thyme. The bowl was served with thick slices of warm, crackly whole-grain bread, a small butter dish, and a handful of peppery salad greens dressed with olive oil and lemon.

The meal was intended as a quiet, restorative pause in Jennifer’s day: lunch for herself while she relaxed at home, midway through the focused preparation of her watercolor exhibition. It felt modest, comforting, and fitting for a calm Thursday in February.
