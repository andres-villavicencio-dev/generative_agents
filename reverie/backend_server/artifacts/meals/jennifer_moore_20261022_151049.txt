Dish name: Simple Morning Coffee with a Touch of Milk

Key ingredients: Freshly brewed coffee and a small splash of milk.

Preparation method: Jennifer makes the coffee in her usual morning routine, brewing it until it is rich and aromatic. She then pours a small amount of milk into the cup, stirring gently to create a light-colored brew without muting the coffee’s flavor.

Aroma: The coffee carries a warm, mellow fragrance, with delicate notes of the freshly ground beans followed by the soft, creamy scent of milk. The subtle sweetness and steam make the drink especially comforting on a cool morning.

Presentation: Served in a favorite, well-worn cup at a calm breakfast table, the coffee has a smooth, pale-brown surface. Its simplicity suits Jennifer’s practical, unhurried morning routine.

Occasion: Jennifer enjoys this light breakfast around 5 a.m., before beginning her day. It is a quiet moment for herself as she prepares for her upcoming watercolor exhibition and considers the young artists she is mentoring. The modest meal reflects her warm, thoughtful, and disciplined approach to the day ahead.
