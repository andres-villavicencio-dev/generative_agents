Jennifer prepared a simple, nourishing breakfast: warm cinnamon-oatmeal with fresh berries and toasted almonds, accompanied by freshly brewed coffee.

She began by bringing a small pot of water to a gentle simmer and stirring in rolled oats, chopped dried apples, ground cinnamon, and a splash of milk. While the oats cooked, she warmed a small skillet over low heat and toasted sliced almonds until they released a rich, nutty aroma. She spooned the creamy oatmeal into her favorite ceramic bowl, then arranged a handful of blueberries and raspberries over the top. The berries softened slightly in the warmth, adding bright color and a pleasantly tart flavor. A drizzle of honey and a final sprinkle of cinnamon completed the dish.

The coffee was freshly ground and brewed in a small drip pot, filling the kitchen with a warm, balanced fragrance. Jennifer served the meal on the sunlit kitchen table with a folded napkin and a glass of water, creating an understated, inviting presentation. The occasion was an unhurried Monday morning breakfast during her preparations for her upcoming watercolor exhibition—a quiet moment to gather her thoughts before mentoring younger artists later in the day.
