Jennifer Moore prepared a simple morning oatmeal with blueberries and coffee.

The oatmeal was made with rolled oats, water, a splash of milk, a pinch of cinnamon, and a small drizzle of honey. She stirred it over low heat until creamy, then topped it with fresh blueberries, stirring them in so their juices softened the grains. While the oatmeal cooked, she poured hot coffee into her favorite mug, adding a little steamed milk.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of coffee and cinnamon, softened by the faintly sweet scent of berries and honey. She served the oatmeal in a shallow white bowl beside the mug, garnishing it with a few extra blueberries and arranging a folded napkin on the neatly tidied counter.

The meal was a quiet, nourishing breakfast on Monday morning, prepared before Jennifer began reviewing sketches and mentoring plans for her upcoming watercolor exhibition. It was an everyday pause that reflected her warm, practical nature.
