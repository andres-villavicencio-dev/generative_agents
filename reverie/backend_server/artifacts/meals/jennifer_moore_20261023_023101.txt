Jennifer prepared Savory Herbed Chicken with Root Vegetables and Golden Potatoes for an early morning family breakfast before heading to her exhibition. She used a whole leg of chicken, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, and mushrooms, seasoned them with olive oil, garlic, dried thyme, rosemary, and a pinch of salt.

She gently seared the chicken in a hot pan until the skin turned crisp and golden, then transferred it to a baking sheet. The vegetables were roasted until tender and slightly caramelized, while the potatoes were seasoned and baked until soft and creamy. A splash of broth and fresh parsley finished the dish.

As she carried the tray to the kitchen table, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, earthy herbs, and savory poultry. She arranged the chicken atop a plate surrounded by the golden vegetables and a small bowl of roasted potatoes. The meal was simple, warm, and filled with the comfort of a shared breakfast, a fitting beginning to her demanding day as she prepared for her exhibition while mentoring younger artists.
