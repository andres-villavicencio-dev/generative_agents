Jennifer Moore prepared a simple but thoughtfully chosen breakfast of silver-and-soft-blue oatmeal, a dish reflecting the colors she paints. She prepared it in a small ceramic bowl using rolled oats, milk, a pinch of cinnamon, a little honey, and a few slices of blueberry. She measured the oats by hand, stirred gently, and let the mixture cool slightly before serving. The aroma was warm and fragrant—oats, milk, cinnamon, and fruit.

She arranged the oatmeal on a white plate, garnished it with fresh blueberries and a light dusting of cinnamon, and placed a small wooden spoon beside it. The presentation was understated and inviting, with the muted silver-blue and soft blue of the watercolor subtly echoed in the dish’s color palette.

It was a quiet, comforting meal for a busy Tuesday morning, especially as she entered her exhibition and mentoring schedule. The breakfast offered her a calm beginning to the day, matching the soft blue light filtering through the silver path she photographed.
