On Tuesday, February 14, Jennifer prepared Lemon-Herb Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Butter Cream Sauce. She began by washing carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, then sliced them into uniform rounds and arranged them in a baking dish. A small pot of water was brought to a gentle simmer to steam the roots until they were tender.

She toasted sliced garlic and thyme, then combined them with the vegetables. A rich lemon-butter sauce was reduced slightly to create a glossy glaze. Jennifer roasted everything in the oven until the vegetables were tender and golden, basting occasionally to keep the flavors from drying out. The warm, savory aroma filled the quiet kitchen, blending the crisp earthiness of the vegetables with the bright citrus and creamy sweetness of the sauce.

For the occasion, she used a simple ceramic platter and arranged the colorful vegetables around the center, garnished with fresh thyme and a few lemon wedges. The meal was a peaceful family-style lunch, prepared slowly in her unhurried way. It was more than nourishment—it was a quiet moment of connection, reflecting the calm she found in cooking and the creative ideas she carried forward.
