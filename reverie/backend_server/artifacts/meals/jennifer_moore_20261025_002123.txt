Jennifer prepared a warm fruit-honey breakfast for herself and Sam, who would share it for dinner. The dish was called Honey-Fruit Salad with Lemon-Orange Crisp.

She sliced ripe apples, pears, and oranges, then tossed them with a little honey, cinnamon, a splash of lemon juice, and a pinch of salt. The crisp was made from crunchy apples and pears, cut into uniform rounds and lightly pan-seared in butter until golden.

As the kitchen filled with the scent of caramelized fruit and warm honey, Jennifer arranged the colorful pieces in a shallow bowl and garnished them with a sprig of mint. The presentation was simple but inviting, with the golden crisp and bright fruit arranged attractively around a small plate.

This meal was part of her quiet morning routine before an exhibition. It reflected her warmth and experience, and she hoped the sweet, homey flavors would inspire others, just as she had taught younger painters.
