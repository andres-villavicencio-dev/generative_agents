Jennifer prepared a Warm Oatmeal-Apple-Orange Breakfast. She soaked rolled oats in warm water, added chopped apples and oranges for color and sweetness, and stirred in a pinch of cinnamon. Her warm watercolor paper served as a decorative plate, with the oatmeal arranged in a neat, inviting mound and garnished with a small sprig of parsley.

Aromatic, comforting, and visually bright, the dish released its rich scent as it warmed on the plate. Jennifer stirred the mixture carefully, testing the consistency with a spoon before serving. She then placed a folded handkerchief and a cup of tea beside the bowl, creating a simple, intimate breakfast setting.

The meal was prepared for an early Tuesday morning before her watercolor exhibition and mentoring session, offering a peaceful balance of nourishment, creativity, and community. Its warm tones reflected the calm she was cultivating in her day.
