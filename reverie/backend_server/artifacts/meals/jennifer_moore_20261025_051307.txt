Jennifer prepared a simple yet warming Egg and Herb Omelet for breakfast. She cracked four eggs into a bowl, whisked them with a splash of water, and poured the mixture over a pan of buttered butter. As the eggs set, she carefully folded them with a wooden spoon, cooking the whites until crisp and the yolks until golden and creamy. She finished the dish with a handful of chives and a sprinkle of freshly cracked black pepper.

The aroma filled the kitchen with a rich, savory scent of egg, herbs, and warm butter. In the pan, the eggs were arranged in neat rows, the golden yolks glistening under soft kitchen light, and the crisp edges contrasting with the tender center. The finished omelet was presented on a white ceramic plate, accompanied by a bowl of steaming oatmeal and a small cup of tea.

The meal was prepared quickly for an early Tuesday, before she began preparing for her watercolor exhibition and mentoring younger artists. It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast—a modest reflection of her wise, experienced nature as she made the day’s purposeful art.
