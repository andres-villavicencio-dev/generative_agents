Jennifer prepared Smoked Salmon and Lemon-Cucumber Toast with Herbs. She used two slices of sourdough bread, smoked salmon, cucumber, lemon juice, fresh dill, a sprinkle of capers, and a little olive oil. She toasted the bread until golden, then layered the salmon and vegetables over it, finishing with chopped mint and parsley.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of smoked fish, citrus, herbs, and toasted bread. She presented the meal in a simple white bowl, with the bread arranged in a neat circle and the colorful vegetables and salmon adding a gentle, textured contrast. It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast, perfectly suited to her purposeful morning of painting, sketching, and exploring Johnson Park with Sam.
