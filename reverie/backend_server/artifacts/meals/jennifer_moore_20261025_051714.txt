For her upcoming exhibition, Jennifer prepared Pan-Seared Scallops with Lemon Herb Butter and Roasted Carrots. The dish came together in a small skillet over medium-high heat. She sautéed the scallops until their edges turned golden, then added garlic and a generous drizzle of butter, tossing everything until the scallops were plump and lightly caramelized. Roasted carrots were chopped into small dice and added to the pan, finished with a squeeze of lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and fresh thyme.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of garlic, butter, caramelized carrots, and the delicate scent of lemon. She arranged the scallops and carrots on a white ceramic plate, garnishing them with a sprig of thyme and a few lemon wedges. The presentation was simple and elegant, with the bright yellow of the carrots and green thyme contrasting against the creamy white butter and pale scallop.

This was a quiet, intimate breakfast meant for her personal use and for sharing with Sam. As they discussed Sam’s breakfast, a bathroom break, and plans to meet at the table, Jennifer enjoyed the meal while continuing to prepare her watercolor paintings.
