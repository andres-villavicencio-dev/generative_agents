“Morning Garden” Oatmeal with Berries and Cream

Jennifer prepared a warm, wholesome oatmeal for herself after a quiet morning of painting and sketching. She used rolled oats, a small portion of milk, a handful of mixed berries, and a gentle splash of cream. She stirred them gently over low heat, letting the oats absorb the cream and soften while preserving the fruit’s fresh color.

The aroma filled the kitchen with a comforting blend of oat, milk, and sweet berries. She plated the oatmeal in a simple, rustic bowl, arranging the colorful berries around the top and drizzling a little cream over it. The presentation was modest and inviting, with a small spoon resting beside the bowl.

It was a quiet meal for a busy Tuesday, prepared while she continued preparing for her exhibition and mentoring younger artists. The warm breakfast added to the sense of fulfillment she had felt as she explored Johnson Park with Sam.
