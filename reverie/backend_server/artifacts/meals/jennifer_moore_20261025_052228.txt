The meal Jennifer Moore prepared was Sunday’s Classic Fruit Salad with Nuts, served with a warm lemon-herb dressing. She arranged fresh strawberries, blueberries, sliced bananas, and crisp apples on a wide plate, sprinkling walnuts, almonds, and honeycomb around the edges. The salad was lightly dressed with lemon juice, a touch of olive oil, and a few sprigs of mint, giving it a bright, herbaceous aroma.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: the colorful fruit and golden nuts contrasted beautifully against the pale green leaves of a bed of spinach, with a small bowl of yogurt and a slice of lemon nearby. It was prepared as a special breakfast for her upcoming watercolor exhibition, when she wanted a nourishing, warm start to her day.

By 5:30 AM, Jennifer ate the salad and a small portion of fruit, savoring each bite as she prepared her painting. The meal felt cozy and purposeful, balancing her creative work with the calm of Johnson Park and the companionship of Sam.
