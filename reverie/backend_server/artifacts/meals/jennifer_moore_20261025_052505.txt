Jennifer prepared Cracker and Berry-Nut Breakfast, a warm, wholesome morning meal designed to accompany her exhibition preparations. The dish consisted of a crisp, golden-brown breakfast roll filled with sliced apples, fresh blueberries, and crunchy nuts, all finished with a few plain crackers. She toasted the nuts and sliced the fruit just before serving, preserving their bright colors and crisp textures.

The aroma of the meal was rich and comforting—a fragrant mixture of caramelized apples, toasted nuts, cinnamon, and the subtle sweetness of berries. It smelled invitingly warm and balanced, with the crunch of the nuts adding a satisfying contrast to the softness of the roll.

Jennifer presented the meal in a simple, rustic white bowl, carefully arranging the colorful fruit and nuts around the rolled-up breakfast. A small plate of plain crackers and a glass of warm milk completed the setting. The occasion was quiet and purposeful: a final moment before her watercolor exhibition, reflecting her deep commitment to the art while sharing a simple, nourishing morning with Sam.
