Jennifer prepared Rosemary Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for her Wednesday lunch. It was a warm, colorful meal suited to a relaxed day of painting and sketching.

The chicken was marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, herbs, and a splash of vinegar, then pan-fried until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. Around it were roasted root vegetables—carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes—seasoned with rosemary, salt, and pepper. The vegetables were cooked until golden and softened, adding earthy sweetness to the savory chicken.

Aromatically, the dish reminded her of warm kitchen herbs, citrus peel, roasted garlic, and savory oil. She arranged the plate with the chicken placed artfully over the vegetables, garnishing it with fresh rosemary and lemon slices. It was presented as a simple, inviting family-style lunch, suitable for enjoying alone or with Sam.

On this day, she was preparing for her watercolor exhibition, adding loose washes to soften the sketch’s color. The meal felt purposeful and nourishing, matching her wise, experienced nature as she continued balancing painting, mentoring, and exploring with Sam.
