Jennifer prepared Smashed Potatoes with Roast Chicken and Herb Cream Sauce for dinner. The dish featured crisp golden potatoes, a juicy roast chicken, fresh herbs, lemon, and a creamy herb-and-lemon sauce. She cooked the chicken in a large pan with garlic, salt, pepper, and a splash of olive oil, then sliced the potatoes and added them to the pan until they were tender and the sauce had reduced to a rich golden color.

The aroma of roasted poultry, garlic, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen. Afterward, she plated the potatoes in shallow bowls with the chicken, garnished with parsley, and served lemon wedges and a small side salad. The meal was a simple, comforting family-style dinner, fitting her warm, experienced nature. It was a quiet celebration of the day’s work—her watercolor preparations and exhibition-opening sketches—before she settled down to rest and reflect.
