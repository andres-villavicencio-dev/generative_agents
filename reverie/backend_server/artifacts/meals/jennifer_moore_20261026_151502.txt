Jennifer prepared a warm herb-and-sage chicken soup with roasted potatoes and whole-grain bread. The broth was made with chicken stock, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, thyme, and a pinch of salt. She sautéed the vegetables in a large pot until translucent, then added the chicken and simmered until the meat was tender. As she cooked the potatoes in the same pot, she tossed them with olive oil, rosemary, and a little butter until the skin was crisp and golden.

The whole-grain bread was toasted until crisp, and the soup was ladled into a bowl, with the potatoes served alongside it. The aroma was unmistakable: warm broth, savory chicken, and the subtle scent of herbs.

She presented the meal at the end of her Wednesday dinner on the stove, surrounded by a small family-style dinner. The occasion was a quiet, reflective moment before her exhibition and mentorship work—simple, nourishing, and grounded in the warmth she brings to everything she creates.
