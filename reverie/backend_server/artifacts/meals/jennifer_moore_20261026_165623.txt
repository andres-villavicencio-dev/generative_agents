Jennifer prepared a comforting Herb-Roasted Vegetable Salad with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Honey Vinaigrette, a simple, nourishing dinner suited to a warm evening. She began by sautéing carrots, parsnips, and red onion in butter until they were tender, then roasted them with rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of black pepper. After cooling the vegetables, she sliced them and added crisp apples, walnuts, and cherry tomatoes to a bowl of olive oil, lemon juice, and a touch of honey. The salad was tossed with the vinaigrette and served alongside a small pan of warm roasted root vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of roasted vegetables and herbs, while the golden vegetables and bright lemon scent filled the room. Jennifer arranged the colorful salad in a shallow glass dish, garnishing the edges with a sprig of rosemary and a few whole olives. The meal was a quiet, relaxed evening treat, prepared after a busy day of painting and sketching, with the warm aroma of dinner inviting her to enjoy the peaceful walk with Sam.
