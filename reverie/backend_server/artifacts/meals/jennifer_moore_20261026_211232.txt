Jennifer prepared a simple Garden-Style Vegetable Soup with Crispy Bread and Roasted Tomato for her work exhibition. The meal included carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, garlic, parsley, vegetable stock, and a generous portion of crusty bread. She sautéed the vegetables in olive oil and broth until crisp-tender, then added the potatoes and simmered them until the soup had a warm, inviting consistency. The bread was toasted until golden, and a few roasted tomatoes were stirred in before the soup was served.

The aroma of warm broth, caramelized vegetables, garlic, and toasted bread filled the kitchen. She arranged a small white bowl of browned crusty bread with a sprig of parsley and a few fresh tomatoes beside the soup. The presentation was modest and rustic, reflecting her calm, reflective style.

It was a comforting, self-sufficient lunch prepared for her exhibition and for the younger artists she mentors. The meal provided warmth and energy, matching the gentle rhythm of her studio and the peaceful walk she had shared with Sam at Johnson Park earlier that afternoon.
