Jennifer prepared a Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Butter Cream Sauce. The dish featured carrots, parsnips, red potatoes, and sweet potatoes, seasoned with olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a touch of honey. She roasted the vegetables until their edges turned golden, then finished them with a glossy, rich sauce made from softened butter, fresh lemon juice, and a splash of cream.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted carrots, garlic, herbs, and caramelized honey. Jennifer arranged the colorful vegetables in a shallow baking dish, drizzling the creamy sauce over them and garnishing the edges with a few sprigs of rosemary and edible flowers. She set the table for an early evening meal, serving herself a small portion of the dish along with fresh bread and a glass of red wine.

The occasion was an intimate family dinner before she returned to her studio. The meal reflected her thoughtful approach to cooking: simple, nourishing, and carefully prepared, with every element ready to bring her back to painting.
