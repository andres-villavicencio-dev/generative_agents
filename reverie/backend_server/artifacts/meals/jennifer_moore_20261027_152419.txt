Jennifer prepared Smoky Sage Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for lunch, a warm, garden-inspired dish she often makes for herself when she is alone.

She started with bone-in chicken thighs, seasoning them with dried sage, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. The vegetables—carrots, onions, parsnips, and potatoes—were tossed in olive oil, rosemary, garlic, and herbs before being roasted at 400°F until caramelized and tender. The chicken was seared first, then basted with a light pan sauce. She finished the dish with lemon slices and a drizzle of fresh thyme.

The aroma filling the kitchen was rich and comforting: savory chicken, fragrant herbs, roasted carrots, and the sharp brightness of lemon.

She presented the meal in an elegant white bowl, arranged the vegetables in a gentle mound beside the chicken, and placed a small lemon and sprig of rosemary on top. The dish was served to celebrate her upcoming watercolor exhibition and her continuing mentorship. It was a quiet, nourishing moment after a full day balancing painting, sketching, and her walk with Sam.
