Jennifer prepared a warm fruit and vegetable crisp with honey-glazed apples and lemon. The dish was simple and cheerful, designed as a quick breakfast or snack while she still had time for her work.

Key ingredients included firm Granny Smith apples, carrots, celery, red bell peppers, and fresh herbs. A tablespoon of honey, a squeeze of lemon, and a little olive oil added sweetness and brightness without overwhelming the fruit.

Preparation method involved slicing the apples and vegetables into thin, even pieces, tossing them lightly in the honey and oil, and spreading them on a baking sheet. She arranged them in a single, overlapping layer, letting the apples soften slightly at the edges. The crisp was baked until the vegetables became tender and slightly caramelized, while the apples retained their bright color and crisp edges.

The aroma rose strongly from the sweet, honeyed apple and citrus scent, with the earthy undertones of carrots and celery. She offered it as a warm, inviting breakfast or mid-morning snack, balancing the richness of the fruit with the freshness of the vegetables. It felt both nourishing and joyful—a practical choice for a busy day, perfect for sharing with Sam.
