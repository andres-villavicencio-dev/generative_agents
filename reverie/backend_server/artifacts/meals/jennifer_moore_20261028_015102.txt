Jennifer prepared Simple Tomato and Herb Breakfast Bowl, a warm, nourishing meal that reflected her wise, grounded style. The dish featured ripe tomatoes, a handful of toasted pine nuts, sunflower seeds, sliced cucumber, and a little lemon juice. She seasoned them with olive oil, garlic, fresh basil, and a pinch of salt, then gently tossed everything in a nonstick pan.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, lemon, basil, and toasted nuts. Jennifer placed the colorful bowl in the center of the table, arranging the crisp cucumber and bright tomato beside the golden pine nuts and seeds. The presentation was simple, inviting, and intimate—perfect for a quiet Thursday morning.

The meal was meant to be a gentle start to her day, balancing the energy of her watercolor preparations with a cozy breakfast. It was neither rushed nor celebratory, but a thoughtful, nourishing beginning to a peaceful morning.
