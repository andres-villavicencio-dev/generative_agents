Jennifer prepared a simple, nourishing Sunflower Seed and Herb Salad for her work exhibition. She used fresh spinach, baby carrots, crisp apple slices, a handful of walnuts, and the seeds and herbs of a small head of lettuce. She dressed the salad with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a few fresh basil leaves, letting the flavors meld gently in a small mixing bowl.

She arranged the colorful salad on a warm ceramic platter, drizzled extra olive oil over the top, and added the seeds of a few dried lavender flowers for a subtle aromatic finish. The scent was bright and herbaceous—citrus, garlic, and herbs combined with a hint of dried lavender.

The occasion was Jennifer’s upcoming watercolor exhibition, where she wanted a light, energizing lunch that would keep her focused on the details of her paintings. The meal reflected her calm, wise nature: simple, colorful, and full of warmth.
