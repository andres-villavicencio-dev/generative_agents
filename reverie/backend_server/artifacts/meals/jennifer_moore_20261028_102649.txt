Jennifer prepared Herb-Romance Potatoes, a rustic side dish for her February 15 exhibition and mentee-mentor dinner. The meal was a hearty combination of Yukon Gold potatoes, garlic, fresh thyme, rosemary, lemon zest, and a light buttery pan sauce. She sliced the potatoes, seasoned them generously, and roasted them in a preheated oven until the skin was crisp and the flesh was tender. For the pan sauce, she melted butter with the roasted garlic and herbs, then stirred in lemon juice and a splash of milk.

The kitchen filled with a warm, fragrant aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, lemon zest, and butter. Jennifer presented the dish in a simple white bowl, garnishing the edges with chopped thyme and a few rosemary sprigs. The meal was served on a quiet Wednesday evening, after a busy day of painting, sketching, and preparing for her exhibition. It was a simple, nourishing lunch meant to support her creative process and celebrate the warm, experienced artist she was becoming.
