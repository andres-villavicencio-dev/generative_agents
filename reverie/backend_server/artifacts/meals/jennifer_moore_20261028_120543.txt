Jennifer prepared a simple yet warming dinner of Sunday Gravy with Roast Chicken and Root Vegetables. The dish featured a golden roasted chicken, carrots, potatoes, onions, and mushrooms, all simmered in a rich brown gravy with a touch of mustard and a squeeze of lemon. She used a heavy-bottomed pot on the stove, browning the chicken first to release its fat, then adding the aromatics and vegetables, and cooking everything until the gravy thickened and the chicken was tender.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted onions, caramelized carrots, and the rich scent of the gravy. She plated the food in a shallow baking dish, arranging the vegetables beneath the chicken and garnishing it with parsley and a bright orange lemon wedge. The meal was simple but comforting, fitting the occasion of a family dinner before she and Sam explored Johnson Park and returned to her watercolor studio.
