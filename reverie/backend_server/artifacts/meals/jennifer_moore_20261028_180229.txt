Jennifer prepared a warm Herb-Roasted Root Vegetable and Smoked Salmon Salad, a simple yet elegant breakfast meant for the exhibition opening. She gathered baby carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes, then roasted them in the oven with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, and thyme until their edges softened and turned golden. The smoked salmon was sliced over the pan and seasoned with lemon zest, salt, and black pepper.

She boiled a small pot of water, added a handful of spinach, and quickly tossed it in. A few cold grapes and a lemon wedge were added for freshness. The salad was arranged in a shallow white bowl, garnished with a sprig of rosemary and a sprinkle of edible gold leaf. A small plate of warm sourdough bread and a glass of iced tea accompanied the meal.

The kitchen smelled of toasted garlic, dried herbs, and the savory scent of the smoked salmon. The presentation was understated and inviting—the vegetables and salmon looked colorful and inviting, while the warm bread offered a comforting contrast. It was the first breakfast for the family of four, followed by the long morning of watercolor preparation and her mentoring session.
