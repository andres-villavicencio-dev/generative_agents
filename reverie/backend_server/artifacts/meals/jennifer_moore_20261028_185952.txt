Jennifer prepared a slow-cooked Herb-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Butter Sauce for breakfast. She chose a whole chicken, its skin crisp and its breast tender, alongside carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and a few cloves of garlic. The vegetables were seasoned with oregano, thyme, black pepper, and salt, then roasted in a hot oven until golden and tender. She sautéed the chicken in a little olive oil, garlic, and butter, finished it with fresh rosemary, and prepared a bright lemon-butter sauce to serve over the vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the inviting aroma of roasted herbs, caramelized garlic, and warm butter. She presented the meal in a simple but elegant white platter, arranging the vegetables alongside the chicken and garnishing it with rosemary sprigs. The bright lemon and green herbs added a cheerful contrast to the earthy tones of the roasted vegetables.

This was breakfast for her early morning exhibition preparation. The warm, nourishing meal reflected her wise, experienced nature and the comfort she brought to the house. It also prepared her body and mind for a long day of mentoring younger artists and preparing her work for the show.
