Jennifer prepared Herb-Roasted Vegetable and Grain Salad, a comforting winter supper for her 40-year marriage. She washed a large pot of quinoa and carrots, then sautéed them in olive oil with rosemary, garlic, and a handful of fresh thyme. She added tomatoes, red onions, and a splash of lemon juice, then simmered everything until the vegetables were tender and the grains were cooked through.

For the main dish, she arranged a colorful salad of crisp vegetables and warm grains on a large platter, topped with a dressing of olive oil, lemon zest, and a pinch of salt. The meal was served at home on Tuesday, February 14, when Jennifer was preparing for her upcoming watercolor exhibition and mentoring the younger artists who would help inspire her. The aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and citrus filled the kitchen as she prepared the meal, while the bright colors of the vegetables and grains made it a warm, inviting centerpiece for the occasion.
