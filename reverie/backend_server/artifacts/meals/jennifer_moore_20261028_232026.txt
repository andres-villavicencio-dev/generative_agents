Jennifer prepared a simple light oatmeal-coconut breakfast, a comforting staple that matched the gentle rhythm of her day. She used rolled oats, a measured amount of coffee grounds, a splash of almond milk, and a spoonful of cinnamon and a pinch of nutmeg. The oatmeal was cooked gently in a pan until it softened, then topped with toasted coconut, fresh berries, and a drizzle of honey.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted coffee, warm oats, cinnamon, and coconut. She arranged the bowl neatly on the counter: the ceramic mug beside a small dish of leftover fruit and a plate with a couple of crisp berries, the cinnamon sticks arranged decoratively, and the coffee grounds neatly measured into a small container.

The meal was part of a relaxed Tuesday breakfast, balanced alongside errands, painting, and preparations for her exhibition. It offered Jennifer a warm, satisfying start to her busy day, with the familiar taste of the coffee grounds echoing the warmth that had defined her life over fifty years.
