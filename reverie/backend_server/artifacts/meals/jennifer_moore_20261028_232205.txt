Jennifer prepared Morning Tea with Fresh Fruit and Butter. Her breakfast began with a small cup of warm water, to which she carefully measured and added a splash of milk, a pinch of sugar, and a few drops of vanilla before bringing the kettle to a boil. She added sliced bananas, a pinch of cinnamon, and a handful of fresh strawberries, letting them soften in the warm milk.

The aroma was warm, inviting, and distinctly floral—creamy milk, cinnamon, and the sweet aroma of fruit filling the kitchen. She served the tea in a simple white cup, arranging the fruit around the rim in a neat, rustic presentation. She had set the mug beside a plate of crisp toast, a small bowl of honey, and a cup of tea, making it clear this was a quiet, mindful morning before her full day of creative work, mentoring, errands, and preparations for her exhibition.
