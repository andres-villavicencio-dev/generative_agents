Jennifer prepared a light herb and lemon chicken breakfast to complement her tea. The dish consisted of tender chicken breast marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, and a pinch of salt, then pan-seared until golden. She served it over crisp toast with sliced avocado and cherry tomatoes, garnished with fresh parsley.

As she prepared it, she carefully cut the chicken, seasoning it before the pan. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, roasted herbs, lemon zest, and toasted bread. The meal was presented simply but thoughtfully: the chicken rested on the plate, with the avocado and tomatoes arranged neatly beside it and a small sprig of rosemary added for visual balance.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday morning at home, intended as a relaxed start to her day. After breakfast, she prepared her watercolors, mentored young artists, and prepared a small breakfast for Sam, while sipping her tea. The meal reflected her disciplined, purposeful routine: nourishing, comforting, and easy to enjoy.
