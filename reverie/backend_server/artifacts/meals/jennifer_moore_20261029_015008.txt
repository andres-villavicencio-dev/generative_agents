Jennifer prepared a simple Warm Potato and Herb Soup for Tuesday, February 14. The dish featured roasted potatoes, carrots, green beans, and a creamy broth infused with thyme, rosemary, and a pinch of garlic. She simmered the vegetables in a pot over low heat, adding the potatoes at the beginning so they would soften fully by the time the soup finished. A splash of cream and a little lemon juice gave it a smooth, comforting finish.

The kitchen filled with the inviting aroma of roasted potatoes and herbs, while steam rose gently from the simmering vegetables. Jennifer presented the soup in a shallow ceramic bowl with a small sprig of rosemary and a wedge of lemon, leaving enough space for the creamy texture to be appreciated.

Dinner served at 5:00 PM, it was a quiet, nourishing meal on a day balancing errands, civic work, and her watercolor painting. It reflected her disciplined routine and the steady, warm comfort she found in preparing food for herself and others.
