Jennifer prepared Sunday Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice for an intimate family dinner. The main dish was a tender, buttery chicken breast seasoned with garlic, dried oregano, lemon zest, and a touch of dried thyme. She pan-seared it until golden, served it over fluffy rice, and added a crisp green vegetable and a small roasted tomato for balance.

The kitchen smelled of toasted garlic, citrus, savory herbs, and warm butter. She plated the chicken in a shallow bowl with the rice, lemon slices, and herbs arranged around it, creating a clean, inviting presentation. She accompanied it with a simple salad of crisp lettuce, cucumber, and orange vinaigrette.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday night dinner, a quiet moment before her exhibition. Jennifer’s steady, warm cooking and the comfortable family atmosphere made the meal feel both nourishing and meaningful.
