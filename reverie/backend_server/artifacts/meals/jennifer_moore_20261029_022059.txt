On a Tuesday morning, Jennifer prepared Heritage Vegetable Soup for a quiet breakfast before errands, civic tasks, and her upcoming exhibition.

She began with a small pot of simmered vegetable broth, then added chopped carrots, celery, onion, potato, and pearl onions. Her mother’s recipe called for a pinch of salt, freshly cracked black pepper, and a splash of vinegar to brighten the flavor, while a teaspoon of olive oil and a clove of garlic helped the vegetables soften. She added a handful of carrots, celery, potato, pearl onion, and a pinch of salt, then covered the pot and let it simmer until the vegetables were tender.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the broth bubbled—warm, herbaceous, and full of earthy vegetables. When the soup was ready, she ladled it into shallow bowls and sprinkled the remaining vegetables and herb garnish on top.

She served it at breakfast table for an early family brunch, the simple comfort food echoing the warmth and peace she hoped to carry into her day.
