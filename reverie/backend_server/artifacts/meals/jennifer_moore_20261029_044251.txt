Jennifer prepared Blueberry-Buttermilk Pancakes with Honey-Glazed Berries, a comforting breakfast made over a cold Sunday morning. She used whole-wheat flour, buttermilk, eggs, butter, and fresh blueberries. Her favorite trick was to cook the batter in a buttered pan over medium heat, flipping the golden pancakes just before they seemed done. She sliced fresh strawberries, raspberries, and blackberries, then glaze them lightly with honey and a tiny splash of vanilla.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of baking flour, milk, and sweet fruit. She arranged the pancakes on a white plate, arranging the berries in a colorful border and placing a small bowl of maple syrup and cinnamon sticks beside it. A glass of cold water and a slice of lemon went into the pan.

This was her first meal while pausing near the park in the familiar spring scenery. The quiet atmosphere made the familiar breakfast feel both ordinary and deeply personal—a simple moment of nourishment and reflection before she returned to her watercolor work.
