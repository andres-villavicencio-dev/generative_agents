Jennifer prepared Garden of Calm Blues, a colorful, nourishing dinner for Sam, completed around 5:00 PM.

She selected fresh seasonal vegetables—cubed zucchini, carrots, and green beans—grilled chicken breast, a handful of walnuts, and a portion of whole-grain bread. The meal was assembled in a wide shallow bowl, with the vegetables arranged around the chicken and garnished with parsley and a drizzle of olive oil. A small bowl of lemon-herb yogurt was placed beside it for dipping.

The cooking method combined gentle oven-grilling with a quick pan sear. The chicken was basted with butter and herbs, while the vegetables were briefly steamed before being finished over the pan. Warm whole-grain bread was placed on top, and the whole ensemble was finished under a broiler until the chicken was golden and crisp.

Her aroma was rich and inviting: the savory scent of grilled chicken, earthy sweetness of carrots and zucchini, and a warm finish of toasted nuts and herbs. The presentation was rustic and cheerful, with vivid blues, greens, and golden yellows contrasting against the neutral tones of the bread.

The meal was prepared to accompany Sam during his errands and to serve as a celebratory lunch before the exhibition.
