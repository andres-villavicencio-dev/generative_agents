Jennifer prepared Blue-Glaze Blueberry Pancakes with Lemon-Butter Slather, a warm breakfast fit for a rainy Wednesday morning. She arranged the pancakes on a white platter, topped them with fresh blueberries, and spread the golden lemon-butter glaze over the edges before serving.

The pancakes were made by mixing two cups of flour, two eggs, a splash of milk, and a pinch of salt, then cooking them until they turned golden and puffed. She gently folded in fresh blueberries and finished them with a little butter. For the glaze, she melted butter, added honey and lemon zest, and folded in a few blueberries before drizzling it over the top.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of baking berries, sweet honey, and toasted butter. The meal was simple and comforting, but elegant enough for a weekend breakfast. Jennifer placed it in the refrigerator before heading to the grocery store to organize her ingredients, balancing her morning routine with creative work and preparation for her exhibition.
