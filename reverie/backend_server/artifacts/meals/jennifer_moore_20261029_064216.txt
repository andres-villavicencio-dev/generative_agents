Jennifer prepared a comforting Smoked Salmon and Dill Butter Salad with Roasted Carrots, a warm, nourishing breakfast-and-dinner meal. She layered creamy smoked salmon, crisp dill, sliced radishes, and thinly sliced carrots with a tangy lemon-herb dressing. The carrots were lightly roasted until tender, while the salmon was cooked gently and seasoned with smoked paprika and garlic.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized onions, toasted herbs, and the subtle, rich scent of smoked fish. It was prepared for a quiet Wednesday morning, balancing her errands, creative work, and preparation for her upcoming exhibition. The meal felt like a peaceful first step into a full and purposeful day.
