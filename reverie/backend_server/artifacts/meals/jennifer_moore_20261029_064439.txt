Jennifer prepared Blueberry-Morning Glazed Chicken for her Wednesday dinner. The dish featured whole chicken thighs, baby potatoes, carrots, sliced onions, fresh blueberries, and a glaze of maple syrup, lemon juice, and a little soy. She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic, then browned the thighs in a skillet until golden. She roasted the vegetables with the chicken in the same pan, adding butter and a splash of water to finish the sauce.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onions, roasted chicken, and sweet blueberries. Jennifer served the meal in a shallow white bowl, arranging the chicken and vegetables around her plate and garnishing it with fresh herbs and a few bright blueberries. It was a simple, comforting family dinner, balanced by her busy schedule of errands, creative work, and mentoring younger artists.
