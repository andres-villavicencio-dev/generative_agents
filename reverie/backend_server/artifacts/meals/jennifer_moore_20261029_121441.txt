Jennifer prepared a hearty Heritage Breakfast Burrito for Sam, a simple yet comforting way to meet him for lunch. The dish included scrambled eggs, cheddar cheese, diced tomatoes, spinach, chorizo, and a little sour cream, all wrapped in a warm tortilla and topped with a generous layer of black beans and salsa.

She cooked it in a large skillet over medium heat, seasoning the eggs and chorizo with salt and pepper and cooking until the chorizo was crispy and the eggs were fully set. The skillet was then filled with the colorful mix of tomatoes, spinach, and beans, which cooked gently until everything was tender and the flavors melded together.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and inviting—rich chorizo, toasted tortilla, savory cheese, and the fresh scent of spinach. She presented the burrito on a white plate, garnishing it with a sprig of cilantro and a few black beans before serving it to Sam. The occasion was a relaxed breakfast lunch on Thursday, February 16, after Jennifer had spent several hours mentoring young artists and preparing for her upcoming exhibition.
