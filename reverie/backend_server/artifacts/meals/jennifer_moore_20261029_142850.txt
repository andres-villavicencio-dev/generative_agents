Jennifer prepared a Blueberry-Crusted Egg Breakfast Sandwich for her Friday morning. The dish features a golden-brown egg, crisp blueberries, toasted breadcrumbs, and savory bacon, all nestled between two slices of soft whole-grain bread.

The eggs were poached gently in a skillet, then flipped with fresh blueberries and a generous coating of breadcrumbs before being toasted lightly. The breakfast was served on a large white plate with a small ramekin of maple syrup and a sprig of mint beside it. The warm aroma of toasted nuts, caramelized blueberries, and savory bacon filled the kitchen.

Jennifer’s simple, comforting meal was prepared for a quiet family breakfast on Friday, February 17, while she celebrated her watercolor exhibition. She carried forward the full day of creative and civic responsibilities with the intention of nourishing herself before visiting her gallery, helping Sam, and supporting younger artists.
