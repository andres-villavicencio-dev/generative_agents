Jennifer prepared a warm lentil soup with lemon, herbs, and a small piece of roasted root vegetable. The lentils were slowly simmered with vegetable stock until tender, then finished with a splash of lemon juice and a few sprigs of fresh thyme. She added a few dried apricots and a pinch of cumin, finishing it with a squeeze of fresh lemon.

The aroma emerged as a comforting blend of earthy lentils, warm root vegetables, lemon, and the subtle warmth of cumin and thyme. The lentils were chopped and arranged in a shallow ceramic bowl, with the root vegetable garnish and lemon slices placed on top. A small sprig of thyme rested beside the dish.

The meal was a simple, nourishing lunch for a quiet Friday after a long day of painting and civic responsibilities. It reflected Jennifer’s steady, grounded style—simple, warm, and deeply satisfying.
