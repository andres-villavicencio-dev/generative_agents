Jennifer prepared Herbed Sage and Lemon Chicken with Creamy Potato Salad for breakfast, preparing it while the coffee finished steeping. She sliced a chicken breast into thin pieces, seasoned it with olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, dried herbs, and pepper, then pan-fried it gently until the skin was crisp and the meat was just cooked through. A side of creamy potato salad was tossed with diced potatoes, fresh herbs, lemon juice, and a little butter.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory chicken. She arranged the golden chicken, crisp potatoes, and colorful salad on a white platter, garnishing it with a drizzle of lemon juice and a few whole herbs before placing it on the table. It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast on a Saturday morning, a simple meal intended to ground her as she prepared for the day’s watercolor painting, gallery visits, and conversations with family and friends.
