Jennifer prepared a comforting Mushroom and Herb Breakfast Bowl, a simple, nourishing meal made with brown mushrooms, sliced carrots, chopped shallots, a few cloves of garlic, and fresh herbs such as thyme and rosemary. She gently sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until the carrots turned tender, then added the mushrooms and garlic. The mixture was finished with a splash of vegetable broth, a pinch of salt, and a drizzle of lemon juice, allowing the flavors to meld into a fragrant, earthy soup.

She poured the hot broth over the remaining coffee grounds in a mug, creating a warm second cup of tea. The meal was presented in a simple white ceramic bowl, surrounded by small bowls of fresh fruit and a cup of the steaming tea. The aroma of roasted mushrooms, savory herbs, and citrus filled the kitchen, while the golden-brown vegetables and bright garnish gave the breakfast a cheerful, inviting appearance.

The occasion was a quiet Saturday morning before a day of watercolor painting and gallery visits. It was an intimate, grounding routine that balanced her purposeful schedule with moments of warmth and connection for Sam and her younger artists.
