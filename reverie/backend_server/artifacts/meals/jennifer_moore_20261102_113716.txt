On Saturday, February 18, Jennifer prepared a warm breakfast of Savory Breakfast Hash with Sweet Potato and Blueberries, a simple, nourishing meal she made before heading to the grocery store. The dish consisted of diced potatoes, onions, carrots, celery, and eggs cooked with a little butter and olive oil, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. A cup of oatmeal and a bright blueberry muffin were also included.

The ingredients were measured carefully as Jennifer prepared them on the counter, her steady hands and experienced familiarity with basic cooking making the process practical and unhurried. She seasoned the eggs gently, letting the eggs set in the pan, then added the vegetables and cooked them until crisp but still tender. The sweet potatoes and blueberries added a subtle sweetness that balanced the savory flavors.

Aromatic, comforting, and unmistakably homey, the breakfast filled the kitchen with the scent of caramelized onions and warm oats. It was presented in a simple white bowl, with the eggs arranged in the center and the colorful fruit scattered around the vegetables. The meal was prepared for a quiet Saturday morning, a relaxed start to her day before the watercolor painting, gallery visits, and errands that followed.
