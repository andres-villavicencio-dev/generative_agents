Jennifer prepared Baked Scrambled Egg with Spinach and Toasted Whole Wheat Toast. The dish began with a small pan heated in the kitchen, followed by the addition of olive oil and butter. She cracked two eggs into the pan and stirred them gently until the white and yolks separated. A handful of fresh spinach was added, then seasoned with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. The eggs were cooked until they were softly set and golden, while the spinach wilted into a bright green.

She toasted slices of whole-wheat bread in the pan until the edges turned crisp, then folded the warm eggs and spinach together with the bread. The meal was arranged in a shallow bowl, with the golden toast, fluffy eggs, and crisp spinach arranged in a colorful, inviting pattern.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of butter, herbs, and toasted grains. The occasion was a quiet Saturday breakfast before her Saturday, February 18, watercolor exhibition preparations. Jennifer’s warm smile and deliberate presentation reflected her wise, experienced nature: practical, comforting, and full of life.
