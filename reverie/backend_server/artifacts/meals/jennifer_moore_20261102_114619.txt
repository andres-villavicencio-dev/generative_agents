At 5:30 AM, Jennifer prepared a simple Herb-Roasted Vegetable and Toasted Toast, a nourishing breakfast that balanced comfort with a little freshness. She toasted two slices of sourdough, seasoned them with olive oil, garlic, and a pinch of salt, then layered them with sliced carrots, zucchini, spinach, and roasted red pepper. The vegetables were lightly sautéed in butter until tender, and the toast was served warm beneath a crisp bed of greens.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of toasted grain, roasted garlic, and warm herbs. Jennifer had tasted the finished meal and decided to serve only enough for her and Sam—a generous portion of toast, a small bowl of spinach salad, and a cup of coffee. The presentation was practical and inviting: the colorful vegetables arranged neatly on a platter, the golden toast beside them, and a small glass of coffee set between them.

The occasion was a quiet Saturday morning, a reflection of her purposeful, grounded life. Jennifer had just finished her routine and was ready to begin her day with intention, warmth, and a little care for her familiar meals.
