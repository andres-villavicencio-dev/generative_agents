Jennifer prepared a Hearty Vegetable Soup with Crusty Bread for an early Saturday breakfast before heading to the grocery store. The soup was made with carrots, celery, onions, garlic, potatoes, and a generous amount of vegetable broth, finished with fresh thyme and a pinch of salt. Her son Sam helped chop the vegetables and stir the soup gently, while Jennifer seasoned it herself.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, onions, herbs, and simmering broth. She ladled the translucent, fragrant soup into a wide, oval-shaped ceramic bowl and served it with thick slices of crusty bread. The presentation was simple and inviting—warm colors and a rustic arrangement suggested comfort and a quiet meal.

It was an early morning meal designed to nourish her body and mind, giving her a steady start to the day while leaving room for errands, conversations, and her watercolor paintings.
