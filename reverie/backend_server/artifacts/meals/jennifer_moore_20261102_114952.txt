Jennifer prepared a Simple Lentil and Herb Breakfast Bowl, a warm, nourishing dish ideal for her Saturday morning. Key ingredients included cooked lentils, diced carrots, chopped celery, onions, tomatoes, garlic, thyme, oregano, lemon juice, olive oil, and fresh parsley. She sautéed the vegetables in a small pot until softened, then added the lentils and simmered them briefly until the grains were tender but not mushy.

The aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and warm carrots filled the kitchen, giving the meal a comforting, earthy scent. She arranged the colorful ingredients in a shallow white bowl, drizzling extra olive oil and lemon juice over the top. A wooden spoon rested beside the bowl, ready for serving.

This was a quiet, purposeful breakfast for her busy Saturday, balancing her watercolor work with simple, nourishing nourishment before heading to the grocery store.
