At 5:30 AM, Jennifer prepared a warm bowl of sourdough oatmeal with roasted apple and cinnamon, the simple breakfast that grounded her busy Saturday. She toasted the oats with a pinch of cinnamon and ground cloves, then added sliced apples and a handful of walnuts, cooking them gently in a little water until the bread came together.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted bread, caramelized sugar, and warm nuts. She served the oatmeal in a simple white bowl, topped it with a drizzle of honey and a generous sprinkle of chopped nuts, and arranged the apples beside it. The presentation was understated and inviting, reflecting her preference for warmth and simplicity.

It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast before heading to the grocery store. Jennifer’s intention remained clear: prepare for her exhibition while continuing to support Sam and nurture younger artists. The meal gave her a moment of peace, grounding her purposeful day.
