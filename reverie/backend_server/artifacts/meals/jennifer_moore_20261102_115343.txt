Jennifer prepared a slow-cooked beef and vegetable skillet breakfast. She used a rack of skirt steak, carrots, onions, green beans, and diced potatoes, seasoning the meat with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and a splash of Worcestershire sauce before sautéing everything in a skillet. The vegetables softened as the meat browned, and she finished the dish with a tablespoon of butter and fresh thyme. She served it in a shallow bowl with a sprig of rosemary, the vegetables arranged neatly around the meat, and a small dish of chopped parsley for garnish.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted beef, caramelized onions, carrots, garlic, and thyme. The presentation was simple and inviting—the warm beef and golden vegetables looked ready to be eaten with plenty of bread. Jennifer made it before heading to the grocery store, intending to eat it with Sam. It was a quiet breakfast meant for her and her companion, a moment of care and companionship before the day’s errands.
