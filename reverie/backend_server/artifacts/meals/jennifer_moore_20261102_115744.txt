Jennifer prepared a simple but warm breakfast of Oatmeal with Maple-Sugar Fruit and Fresh Berries. The dish was cooked in a small stainless steel pan with rolled oats, water, a pinch of salt, cinnamon, and a little vanilla extract, then covered and gently warmed until the oatmeal was soft and the berries were plump.

She added two ripe strawberries, blueberries, and a few sliced raspberries, drizzling a generous teaspoon of maple syrup and a small pinch of brown sugar over the top. The aroma was fragrant and inviting: warm oats, sweet cinnamon, toasted nuts, and the bright, crisp scent of berries mingling with the gentle sweetness of the syrup.

Jennifer arranged the oatmeal in a small white bowl, placing the colorful fruit around the edges and a small cinnamon stick beside it. She sprinkled a few chopped walnuts on top, creating an orderly, colorful presentation. The meal was prepared for a quiet Saturday breakfast before she headed to the grocery store, with the intention of nourishing herself and supporting Sam while preparing for her upcoming exhibition.
