At 5:30 AM, Jennifer prepared a simple, hearty breakfast for herself: Buttermilk Pancakes with Maple Syrup and Fresh Berries. She used two cups of all-purpose flour, one egg, half a cup of buttermilk, a pinch of salt, a sprinkle of cinnamon, and a generous spoonful of maple syrup. Fresh blueberries, strawberries, and raspberries were folded into the batter and cooked until the edges were golden and the pancakes were tender.

The kitchen smelled of warm oats, maple, caramel, and the gentle scent of fresh berries. Jennifer cooked the pancakes in a non-stick pan over medium heat, flipping them once until they were lightly browned. She served them on a wide plate, arranging the berries beside them in a neat circle, and drizzled the maple syrup over the top.

The meal was meant to be a quiet, purposeful start to the day: nourishing, familiar, and comforting. Jennifer sipped the syrup while taking a small cup of tea, remembering the day ahead. She had planned to paint, visit the gallery, and support Sam and younger artists, but this simple breakfast gave her a moment of warmth and grounding before her full day of creative work.
