Jennifer prepared a Slow-Cooked Chicken and Root Vegetable Breakfast for Sam. She began with two ears of corn, two carrots, a yellow onion, and a handful of green beans. After washing them, she sliced the carrots into sticks, peeled the onion, and cut the green beans into short pieces. She seasoned the vegetables with salt, pepper, and a little lemon juice, then added two bone-in chicken thighs to a large Dutch oven. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onions, roasting carrots, and chicken.

She covered the pot and cooked everything on the stove until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were tender-crisp, then drained any excess liquid before serving. She arranged the chicken in the center of a plate, with the bright green beans and orange carrots peeking around it, and placed a small bowl of warm corn with a dollop of Greek yogurt on the side. The dish looked rustic and inviting, with golden chicken, earthy vegetables, and a lightly salty-sweet glaze.

She prepared it early Sunday as a hearty, nourishing breakfast for Sam, leaving room for her watercolor exhibition and continued support of younger artists.
