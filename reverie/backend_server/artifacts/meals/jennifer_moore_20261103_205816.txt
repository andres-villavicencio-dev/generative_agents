Jennifer prepared a comforting Mushroom and Sage Roast with Warm Creamy Butter for breakfast. Key ingredients included fresh mushrooms, onion, garlic, thyme, parsley, butter, and a splash of cream. She sautéed the vegetables and mushrooms in a pan, then roasted them until golden. The dish was finished with buttered toast and a glass of warm tea.

The kitchen filled with a rich aroma of roasted garlic, earthy mushrooms, and fresh thyme, accompanied by the gentle scent of tea and butter. She arranged the mushrooms, toast, and tea on her desk, creating a warm, inviting setting. This meal was part of her quiet morning routine before the exhibition preparations for the next day, offering a peaceful moment of reflection and creativity.
