On Monday, February 13, Jennifer prepared Herbs, Sage, and Lemon Tea with Toasted Sourdough and Fig Jam as part of a quiet morning breakfast. She began by steeping fresh tea leaves in warm water, infusing them gently over several minutes so the delicate flavors remained balanced. To the warm tea, she added freshly chopped sage, a pinch of lemon zest, a splash of cream, and a few figs.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the dark amber tea was poured over a small plate of crisp toast, accompanied by the fragrant fig jam and a spoonful of creamy custard. The aroma was rich and comforting—a warm blend of sage, lemon, fig, and tea.

This meal was prepared for Jennifer and Sam to enjoy together on a quiet Monday morning, before she began preparing for her exhibition and mentoring other young artists. It reflected her calm, experienced nature: nourishing, practical, and full of the warmth of a forty-year marriage.
