Jennifer prepared a Warm Lemon-Orange Herb Tea with Toasted Fruit and Toasted Grains. The meal was inspired by her quiet mornings and the warmth of the exhibition she was preparing for.

She began by steeping loose-leaf tea with lemon, orange, and a pinch of dried rosemary. Toasted grains, such as millet or oat groats, were mixed with honey and a few dried berries. The fruits—perhaps apples, pears, or oranges—were lightly toasted, while the grains were toasted until their edges turned golden brown. A small bowl of honey, warm milk, and a splash of citrus juice served the meal.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of toasted bread, honey, and citrus. Jennifer plated the meal in simple white bowls, adding a few dried flowers to the sides for visual contrast. It was a quiet breakfast for her husband and themselves, a simple moment of nourishment before the day’s creative work.
