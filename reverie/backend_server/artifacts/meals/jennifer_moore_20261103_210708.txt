Jennifer prepared a simple, comforting breakfast of oatmeal, fruit, and fresh honey. The oatmeal was cooked until tender, with cinnamon, a pinch of nutmeg, and a few dried apricots and blueberries stirred in, while the fruit was sliced and the honey added at the end to preserve its brightness. She used warm water and a small amount of cream, then topped the bowl with a few sprigs of mint and a dusting of brown sugar.

She prepared the meal in the quiet kitchen near the desk, wiping the counter and washing her hands before beginning. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of oatmeal, cinnamon, and fruit. She arranged the bowl beside the sink, then returned to her watercolor painting, the scent of the breakfast mingling with the quiet studio.

The occasion was Jennifer’s preparation for an upcoming exhibition and a morning with her long-time husband, Sam. The meal was simple but thoughtful, meant to provide gentle energy before a busy day and a peaceful moment of reflection.
