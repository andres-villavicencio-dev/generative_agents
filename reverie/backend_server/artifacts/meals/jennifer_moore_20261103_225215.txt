Jennifer prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy, a comforting family-style meal prepared for the Hobbs Cafe’s lunch rush. The dish featured a whole chicken roasted with rosemary, garlic, lemon zest, and herbs until the skin was golden and crisp. Alongside it were carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and onions simmered in a rich brown gravy.

She cooked everything on the stove, then finished it with a few sprigs of rosemary and served it in a shallow ceramic serving platter. The chicken was arranged on the left side, while the root vegetables and gravy filled the remaining space. Her warm, inviting expression and the golden-brown crust made the meal look both hearty and welcoming.

For the lunch rush, she carefully portioned the food into customer trays and arranged it on the counter, ensuring everything was easily accessible. The aroma of rosemary, garlic, and roasted root vegetables filled the kitchen. It was a practical, nourishing lunch designed to sustain the restaurant team until the evening rush began.
