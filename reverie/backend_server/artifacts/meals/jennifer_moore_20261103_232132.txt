Jennifer prepared a Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and Grilled Chicken for her family. The dish featured tender chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, onions, and green beans, all slow-roasted with rosemary, garlic, thyme, and a splash of beef stock. The vegetables were gently sautéed before being combined with the cooked chicken, allowing the flavors to mingle and the juices to redistribute.

Aromatic rosemary, garlic, and thyme filled the kitchen as the chicken came out of the oven, their warm, savory scent blending with the gentle aroma of caramelized potatoes and tender carrots. Jennifer arranged the roast on a large platter, garnishing it with parsley and serving the vegetables and chicken around it in neat, rustic portions.

The meal was prepared for an intimate family gathering on Monday, February 13, when Jennifer was preparing for an exhibition of her watercolor paintings. She also wanted the children to see the family’s art and gather together for breakfast, lunch, and an evening walk around Johnson Park.
