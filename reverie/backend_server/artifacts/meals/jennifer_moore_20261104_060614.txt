Jennifer prepared Warm Country-Breakfast for her family, using ingredients she kept readily available in the kitchen. Her breakfast included oatmeal, warm milk, bananas, sliced apples, walnuts, and a little honey. She toasted the walnuts in a pan, seasoned them lightly with salt and pepper, and arranged them beside a large bowl of oatmeal. The milk and honey were warmed gently in a small saucepan, and the fruit was arranged around the oatmeal in a shallow dish.

The aroma was comforting and inviting: the earthy scent of toasted nuts, the gentle sweetness of bananas and honey, and the warm aroma of oatmeal. She served the breakfast on a simple white plate, garnished with a sprinkle of cinnamon and a few dried berries. The occasion was her family’s morning table while she prepared for her exhibition and mentored younger artists.

Jennifer’s patient, unhurried preparation made the meal feel familiar and nourishing, reflecting the wisdom and warmth she has carried for over fifty years. She watched her family eat with smiles, enjoying the simple pleasure of a home-cooked meal before heading to bed at 9 p.m.
