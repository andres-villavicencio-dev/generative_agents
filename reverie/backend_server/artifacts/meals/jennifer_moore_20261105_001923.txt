Jennifer prepared a warm Sunday Club Sandwich with roast chicken breast, sliced baguette, fresh spinach, tomato, red onion, and a yogurt-based dressing. She toasted the bread, sliced the chicken, and layered the ingredients on sturdy white-bread sandwiches, covering them with a light lemon-herb dressing. A warm apple-sugar crumble was served alongside the sandwiches.

The kitchen filled with the comforting scent of toasted bread, savory chicken, fresh herbs, and caramelized apples. The presentation was simple and inviting: the sandwiches were arranged neatly on a silver platter, with the crumble beside them, and each sandwich topped with a sprig of parsley.

The meal was prepared for Jennifer’s full-day shift at Hobbs Cafe, including lunch for her staff and a quick breakfast for her. It was a hearty, comforting lunch that reflected her warm, experienced nature—grounding, nourishing, and ready for the busy day ahead.
