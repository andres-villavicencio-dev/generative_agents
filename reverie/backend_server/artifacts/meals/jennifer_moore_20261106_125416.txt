Jennifer prepared Warm Toast with Herbs and Tea to restore her body and mind for the day ahead.

She toasted slices of whole-grain bread in a pan, then added chopped fresh dill, a sprinkle of toasted seeds, and a generous dash of honey. She poured the leftover tea into a small glass, adding a few drops of lemon and a splash of honey. The aroma was rich, earthy, and warmly comforting.

She arranged the bread beside the tea on a simple white plate, garnished with a sprig of dill and a small lemon wedge. The presentation was clean, minimalist, and inviting. This was a quiet breakfast for a busy Tuesday, intended to help Jennifer begin her work on the exhibition and community-garden project with a steady, refreshed start.
