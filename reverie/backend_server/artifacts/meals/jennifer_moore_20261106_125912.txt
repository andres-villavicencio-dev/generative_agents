For Tuesday morning, Jennifer prepared Golden Lemon Herb Oatmeal to serve as a simple, nourishing breakfast. She used leftover oats, a cup of almond milk, fresh lemon juice, dried cranberries, chopped nuts, and a pinch of cinnamon. The oats were toasted briefly in a pan, then simmered with the warm milk until soft and glossy. She stirred in the fruit and nuts toward the end, allowing the flavors to meld.

She prepared it for an early gathering at the community garden, offering a warm, welcoming start to her day. The aroma of cinnamon, toasted nuts, and citrus rose through the kitchen, evoking the calm, harvest-like atmosphere of the garden. Jennifer presented the oatmeal in a small white bowl, garnishing it with a bright lemon slice and a few scattered seeds.

As she ate, she reflected on her long career as a watercolor painter, her warm, experienced spirit, and the quiet purpose of supporting the new garden project. The meal was simple but deeply comforting—a reminder that nourishment can also nourish the mind.
