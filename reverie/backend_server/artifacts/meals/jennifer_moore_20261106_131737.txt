Jennifer prepared a simple, comforting cup of tea and breakfast for the exhibition-opening morning. The dish was a warm oat and berry breakfast bowl. Her oats were cooked with water and a touch of honey, while the berries—raspberries, blueberries, and sliced strawberries—were folded into the oat mixture. She added a small handful of chopped walnuts and toasted pine nuts, then drizzled the bowl with a little maple syrup and a splash of milk.

She prepared the tea at a small table in the garden, infusing loose-leaf black tea with honey until it was lightly amber. The aroma was rich and inviting: the earthy scent of the garden, the sweet aroma of the berries, and the comforting fragrance of the honeyed tea.

For the occasion, she set the breakfast on a simple white plate beside a small bowl of tea and a warm mug. The presentation was rustic and homey, with the colorful berries adding a cheerful contrast to the neutral oat base. It was a quiet, peaceful breakfast, intended to accompany her work on the exhibition and garden items as she prepared to open the show at the Hobbs Cafe.
