For Tuesday, February 14, Jennifer prepared Slow-Boiled Chicken with Root Vegetables and Golden Toasted Bread.

She sliced chicken breast into small cubes, brushed the pan with olive oil and salt, then gently cooked it over medium heat until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. Root vegetables—carrots, onions, and potatoes—were added last and simmered until soft. She tossed them with a little chicken stock, cracked the eggs into a small bowl, and scrambled them into the pan. The bread was toasted until crisp and browned on the edges.

The meal smelled warmly earthy, rich with the savory scent of chicken and caramelized onions, and fragrant with the earthiness of carrots and potatoes. It was served in a simple white bowl, with the golden bread beside the vegetables and a drizzle of lemon juice. Jennifer ate it slowly in her quiet room, using the session to organize the exhibition, Hobbs Cafe, and garden tasks. The warm breakfast helped her feel grounded, grateful, and ready for a productive day.
