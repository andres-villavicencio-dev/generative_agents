Jennifer prepared a simple, warm breakfast of scrambled eggs with spinach and whole-grain toast. The eggs were lightly beaten and cooked in butter until golden, while the spinach was wilted in a little oil and folded in just before serving.

She toasted the slices of whole-grain bread with a pinch of salt and a small knob of butter, then arranged the eggs and toast on a white plate. A small bowl of fresh fruit and a glass of warm water accompanied the meal. The aroma of buttery eggs, earthy spinach, and toasted bread filled the kitchen.

This was a quiet morning meal on Tuesday, February 14, after a long, fulfilling day of painting and preparing her exhibition. The simple, nutritious breakfast provided comfort and energy for the upcoming job search and continued mentoring work.
