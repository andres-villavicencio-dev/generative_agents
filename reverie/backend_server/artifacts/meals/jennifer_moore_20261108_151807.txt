Jennifer prepared Herb-Crusted Vegetable Soup for a quiet Monday dinner. The soup was made with carrots, onions, celery, potatoes, leeks, and a small handful of fresh herbs, including thyme and rosemary. She simmered the vegetables in a pot of vegetable stock until the potatoes were tender, then added a handful of fresh spinach and a handful of parsley. For the crust, she mixed breadcrumbs with chopped parsley, garlic, lemon zest, and a pinch of onion powder, then coated the soup in a thin layer before baking until the edges were golden.

The aroma of the soup filled the kitchen early in the evening: savory carrots, soft garlic, and the earthy warmth of herbs. It was served in a simple white bowl, garnished with a few fresh herbs and a sprinkle of Parmesan. The meal was prepared for Jennifer’s exhibition opening, when she would share her work with family and guests. Dinner was a peaceful, nourishing evening, fitting both her creative life and her long marriage to Sam.
