Jennifer prepared a comforting Heritage Vegetable Risotto with Lemon Herb Oil to serve as a hearty family lunch before the exhibition. The meal featured Arborio rice, fresh asparagus, carrots, potatoes, onions, parsley, and a light cream-based broth infused with lemon zest and chopped rosemary. She sautéed the vegetables until crisp-tender, then slowly cooked the rice with the broth, stirring constantly to create a creamy texture. She finished the dish by tossing it with a generous drizzle of lemon-herb oil and serving it with crusty bread and a small bowl of fresh fruit.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, nutty scent of aromatic rice, earthy asparagus, and warm rosemary, accompanied by the delicate tang of lemon. Jennifer presented the dish in a simple white bowl, with the colorful vegetables arranged around the rice, and served it at the quiet Tuesday dinner. Her warm, experienced demeanor and the familiar, nourishing meal reflected her lifelong passion for food and the community she nurtures for younger artists.
