Jennifer prepared a Warm Lentil & Herb Soup, a simple, nourishing lunch for the quiet morning before her exhibition. She combined dried red lentils, chopped carrots, celery, onions, and diced tomatoes with a creamy broth, finishing it with fresh thyme, rosemary, and a generous handful of toasted pine nuts. She simmered the soup over low heat, letting the flavors meld and the lentils soften into a smooth, velvety texture.

The aroma was warm and earthy, with hints of roasted root vegetables and dried herbs. At the table, she arranged the finished soup in a shallow ceramic bowl, accompanied by crusty bread and a small side of figs. The setting was calm and inviting, fitting a Monday lunch during her exhibition preparations. Jennifer had chosen this meal to balance her creative focus with a slow, mindful approach to the day’s demands.
