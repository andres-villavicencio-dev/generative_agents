On a snowy Monday morning, Jennifer prepared Oatmeal with Warm Berries and Cinnamon. She cooked the oats in a skillet over medium heat, adding unsweetened almond milk, a spoonful of brown sugar, and a generous pinch of ground cinnamon. She stirred in chopped dried cranberries, blueberries, and sliced almonds, then served the warm porridge in a simple white bowl.

The aroma filled the kitchen—a comforting scent of cinnamon, toasted nuts, and warm fruit. Jennifer served the oatmeal with a small bowl of honey and a piece of fresh orange, while Sam added a slice of banana and a drizzle of maple syrup. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the colorful berries, golden almonds, and amber syrup created a cheerful contrast against the clean white dish.

The meal was part of Jennifer’s pre-exhibition breakfast routine. It was a warm, nourishing start to the day, reflecting her steady, experienced approach to life. After eating, she turned her attention to mentoring younger artists, believing that the simple, heartfelt food she prepared had the same kind of warmth and encouragement her paintings had earned.
