Jennifer prepared Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and a Lemon-Butter Glaze. She washed the carrots, potatoes, and onions, then roasted them over a hot stove until tender and lightly caramelized. The vegetables were seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic, thyme, and a touch of orange zest, while the lemon- butter glaze was made by slowly reducing butter and lemon juice in a pan until it became glossy and fragrant.

She arranged the golden carrots, creamy potatoes, and tender greens in a shallow baking dish, then spooned the warm glaze over the top. The aroma rose through the kitchen: buttery, caramelized, savory, and gently aromatic. She served it at home for lunch on February 14, feeling satisfied and quietly proud of the meal’s balanced flavors.
