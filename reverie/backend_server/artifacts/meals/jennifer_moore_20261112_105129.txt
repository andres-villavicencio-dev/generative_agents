Jennifer Moore prepared a Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Butter Glaze, a comforting family-style dinner for Tuesday, February 14.

The dish featured ribs, carrots, potatoes, onions, and carrots, simmered in water, vegetable stock, rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a simple lemon-butter glaze. The sauce became fragrant and golden as it caramelized over the oven, releasing a warm aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, and citrus.

She arranged the golden-brown roast in a wide serving dish, surrounded it with crisp roasted vegetables and topped it with a generous drizzle of the glossy glaze. A small bowl of lemon-herb soup and a crisp green salad accompanied the meal.

It was a quiet evening meal, prepared in her home kitchen with the intention of nourishing her mind. After cooking, she spent her afternoon turning the neighborhood into watercolors, turning the quiet rhythm of the rain into vivid, expressive marks on paper. The meal provided a simple, grounded reminder that beauty could be found in everyday routines.
