Jenny Rivera prepared a hearty Salsa & Cumbia-Infused Fruit and Nut Oatmeal Bowl. The dish featured rolled oats, cinnamon, cardamom, flaky sea salt, a generous portion of fresh fruit (banana, berries, pineapple, kiwi), and crunchy roasted nuts including walnuts, almonds, and pistachios.

The oats were soaked and cooked with water or oat milk, then topped with the fruit and nuts. She finished the bowl with a drizzle of homemade salsa sauce, which she had hoarded from her late-night salsa cravings. Jenny toasted the nuts until they were golden, stirred in fresh fruit, and added a pinch of salt to balance the sweetness.

The aroma smelled warm and aromatic: caramelized oats, toasted nuts, citrus zest, cinnamon, and the bright, spicy hint of salsa. The presentation was vibrant and inviting, with colorful fruit arranged around the oats and the colorful salsa sauce creating a lively contrast.

The occasion was a busy Tuesday morning before heading to Johnson Park at 7:30 AM. Jenny wanted to start strong, give herself an energizing breakfast, and leave ready for her first class at 6 AM, while also keeping in mind her fitness goals, Wolfgang Schulz, Mei Lin, and upcoming salsa night with Francisco Lopez.
