Jenny Rivera prepared Salsa-Corn Breakfast Burritos, a practical, energizing meal that matched her busy Tuesday morning. The dish featured crispy corn tortillas, fluffy white rice, shredded chicken, diced tomatoes, onions, garlic, and a generous portion of homemade salsa.

She cooked the corn and chicken in a skillet until golden and tender, then warmed the rice and added the salsa to create a vibrant, zesty base. Her signature salsa jar, which she hoards for late-night cravings, became the meal’s defining flavor—bright red, tangy, and full of heat.

The presentation was warm and colorful: golden tortillas, bright orange corn, creamy rice, and pink salsa arranged on a small plate with a fork, spoon, and a tiny bowl of extra chicken. The aroma rose from the skillet—crispy corn, savory chicken, roasted onions, and a burst of fresh salsa.

It was a quick breakfast for her first morning class at Johnson Park, combining energy, comfort, and the familiar salsa twist that made her fitness routine both functional and fun.
