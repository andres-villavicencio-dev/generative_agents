Jenny Rivera prepared Cumbia-Style Salsa and Vegetable Stew for an early Wednesday dinner. She sliced fresh red bell peppers, tomatoes, onions, and carrots; added olive oil, cumin, chili flakes, salt, and pepper; and cooked everything on a large skillet until the vegetables were tender and the sauce was rich and glossy.

The dish had a warm, spicy aroma of roasted vegetables and warm spices that filled the kitchen. Jenny arranged colorful bell peppers, tomatoes, and crisp onions around a central mound of creamy stew, garnishing it with fresh cilantro and a bright red pepper rim. The presentation was vibrant and appetizing, resembling a festive plate perfect for a relaxed family dinner.

It was a simple yet satisfying meal designed to balance her busy fitness routine. Jenny had prepared a little extra salsa sauce for a potential late-night craving, giving the stew an extra kick of color and flavor. The occasion was informal, but her practical approach reflected her energetic, purposeful lifestyle: nutritious, flavorful, and well-prepared for a quiet evening.
