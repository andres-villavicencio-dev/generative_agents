Jenny Rivera prepared a Salsa and Cumbia Breakfast Bowl, an energizing, colorful dish that reflects her fitness and creative side. It included fluffy scrambled eggs, warm whole-grain toast, a bowl of fresh fruit, and a generous portion of homemade salsa—tomatoes, onions, cilantro, lime, and a hint of cumin.

The eggs were cooked in a nonstick pan until golden, while the toast was toasted to a crisp and served with a little butter. She arranged the eggs and toast around the fruit and salsa, adding a sprig of cilantro and a single orange slice for visual appeal. The bowl was presented in bright, contrasting colors, with the warm, savory toast and crisp fruit balancing the bright red salsa.

The aroma was earthy and vibrant, with the scent of toasted grains, caramelized onions, fresh herbs, and the sharp tang of lime. The occasion was Jenny’s first full morning before her class in Johnson Park. It felt full of purpose and anticipation, as she was checking her groceries, preparing her meal, and getting ready to lead an energetic class.
