Jenny Rivera prepared a Quick Salsa-Light Egg Lunch to use up leftover white bread and eggs. The dish used a small loaf of sourdough bread, a hard-boiled egg, a handful of baby spinach, a squeeze of lemon, a sprinkle of chili flakes, and a little mayonnaise for texture. She toasted the bread in a pan, cracked the egg into it, and added the spinach and seasonings before serving with a bowl of leftover salsa.

The aroma was warm and savory: toasted bread, egg, and fresh spinach combined with the sharp tang of lemon and the subtle heat of chili. In the kitchen, the smell filled the small apartment, making the simple breakfast feel like a hearty, familiar meal.

It was a spontaneous, self-served lunch for a relaxed Thursday. Jenny wanted something quick and energizing that wouldn’t require much cleanup, and she prepared it just in time to take a short walk afterward. The meal reflected her practical, energetic style: practical, flavorful, and ready to move on.
