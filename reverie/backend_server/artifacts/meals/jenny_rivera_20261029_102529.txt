For her Thursday morning class, Jenny prepared Salsa Verde and Cucumber-Cilantro Salad as a quick, colorful lunch and snack.

She chopped a small red onion, sliced a cucumber, torn fresh cilantro, and sliced tomatoes into a large bowl. She added a handful of crisp romaine, handful of cherry tomatoes, and a few crisp green onions. For the dressing, she combined the juice of one lime with a small amount of olive oil, vinegar, and a generous pinch of salt, whisking it until bright and zesty. The salad was served over a bed of cool, crisp greens.

The aroma was lively and citrusy—the sharp tang of lime, the sweet crunch of cucumber, and the warm aroma of sautéed red onion. Jenny topped each serving with a bright red salsa made of diced tomatoes, red onion, garlic, cilantro, and lime, finishing the dish with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of chili flakes.

It was a cheerful, energizing lunch to take to her first class at Johnson Park, packed with energy and the colorful rhythm of salsa and cumbia.
