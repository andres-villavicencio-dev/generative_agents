Jenny’s quick lunch was a Crispy Chicken with Black Beans, Corn, and Lime Salsa, a vibrant, budget-friendly bowl that perfectly matched her fitness-oriented, energetic style.

She used leftover roasted chicken, canned black beans, corn, diced tomatoes, shredded lettuce, and fresh lime juice, then tossed everything with a simple garlic-oil dressing. The pantry ingredients were simple, and she prepared the dish in about 15 minutes, reheating the chicken and heating the beans until they were warm.

The aroma of toasted chicken, roasted corn, garlic, and fresh lime filled the kitchen, while the bright colors of green lettuce, yellow corn, and red salsa made the presentation lively and inviting. She served it in a clear plastic bowl with a small plate of extra lime wedges, ready for her morning reflection at the table.

It was a relaxed Thursday lunch—simple, nourishing, and practical—designed to support her busy schedule after an early class, but still flavorful enough to satisfy a late-night craving.
