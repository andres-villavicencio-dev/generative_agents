Jenny Rivera prepared Spicy Salsa Breakfast Bowls, a quick, colorful breakfast that satisfied her late-night craving without needing extra preparation. She combined chopped red and green bell peppers, sliced avocado, cucumber, sliced tomatoes, and sautéed onions with chili peppers, cumin, and ground cumin. For the sauce, she blended red onion, cumin, lime, hot sauce, and olive oil into a bright red salsa. The ingredients were tossed in a pan with a little water until the vegetables softened, then served over fluffy whole-grain toast with fresh fruit and a drizzle of yogurt.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted peppers and toasted cumin, while the presentation was lively and colorful—bright red and green vegetables contrasted against creamy avocado and toasted bread. It was a relaxed, energizing brunch suitable for a busy morning, reflecting Jenny’s practical and spontaneous approach to cooking: simple, flavorful, and designed to satisfy both hunger and the lingering salsa craving.
