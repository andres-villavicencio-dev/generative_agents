Jenny prepared a Cumbia-Style Vegetable Fajita Lunch for a quick Friday lunch while testing fresh salsa. The meal combined ripe red and green tomatoes, zucchini, bell peppers, onions, corn, and black beans with a zesty salsa verde made from fresh cilantro, garlic, onion, and lime. It was seasoned with cumin, chili powder, salt, and a little olive oil, and finished with a drizzle of lime and a pinch of cumin for warmth.

She sautéed the vegetables in a pan until their edges turned slightly caramelized, then added the black beans and cooked them briefly until the beans were tender but still flavorful. The aroma was rich and warm, with the savory scent of chili, the bright notes of fresh herbs, and the subtle sweetness of the tomatoes.

Jenny presented the colorful, steaming bowl in a simple white dish and topped it with a generous portion of crisp corn, crunchy onions, and a slice of fresh lime. She placed a small jar of homemade fresh salsa next to the bowl, completing the meal in a way that reflected her energetic, hands-on approach. The occasion was a casual Friday lunch before testing the fresh salsa samples and heading to Johnson Park for her first class.
