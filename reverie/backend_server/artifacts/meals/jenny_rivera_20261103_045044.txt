Breakfast: Salsa-Pastry Toast with Fresh Salsa

Jenny made a simple but vibrant breakfast to energize the first class at Johnson Park. She used two slices of sourdough bread, sliced red onion, a handful of fresh spinach, and a generous amount of extra salsa. She toasted the bread in a pan until it was golden and slightly crisp, then added the spinach, red onion, and a drizzle of olive oil for a quick warm-up.

She served the toast in a small white bowl on the counter, arranging the salsa in a colorful spiral around the edges. The bright red and green colors made it look inviting and ready to eat. The aroma of toasted bread, onion, and fresh salsa filled the kitchen, with hints of garlic and olive oil adding depth.

The occasion was a casual Saturday morning meal before her fitness routine, but she treated it as a fun way to test the new salsa flavor. The extra jar was meant for a late-night craving, but the fresh samples proved that her energy could be as colorful and exciting as her cumbia workouts.
