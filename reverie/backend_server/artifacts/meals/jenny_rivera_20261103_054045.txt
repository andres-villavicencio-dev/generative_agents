Jenny prepared a Salsa Verde & Cumbia Bowl for her Saturday meal-prep session. The dish featured fresh corn, sweet potato, diced chicken, avocado, red bell peppers, onions, tomatoes, jalapeños, cilantro, lime, and a generous portion of homemade salsa verde.

She cooked the corn and chicken over medium heat with a splash of water, then sautéed the vegetables until tender. The salsa was made by finely chopping the colorful ingredients, mixing them with the chicken and warm salsa sauce, and finishing with a little lime juice and chili flakes. The finished bowl was arranged in a colorful, rustic presentation, with the bright salsa contrasting against the warm orange corn and creamy avocado.

The aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized potatoes, lime, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen. It was a lively, warm meal meant to energize the clients and complement the energetic first class at Johnson Park.
