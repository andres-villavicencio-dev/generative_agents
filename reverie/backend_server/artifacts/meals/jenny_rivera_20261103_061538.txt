On Saturday morning, Jenny prepared a Fresh Fruit and Yogurt Breakfast Bowl for herself and her clients. She used ripe mango, pineapple, strawberries, kiwi, and blueberries, with a creamy Greek yogurt base, chia seeds, honey, and a drizzle of warm cinnamon. The fruit was lightly mixed with the yogurt and topped with granola and fresh mint.

She prepared the breakfast in two steps: first, she washed and chopped the fruit, then whisked the yogurt with honey and seeds before adding the fruit. The aroma of warm cinnamon and sweet tropical fruit filled the kitchen, with the bright colors of the fruit contrasting against the creamy white yogurt. The presentation was colorful and inviting, arranged in a white bowl with a sprinkle of chia seeds and a sprig of mint.

The meal served as a refreshing breakfast before her Saturday class, but it also became an informal lunch for her clients. It was a simple, healthy, and energizing meal that fit her busy schedule, though Jenny felt it needed a little more thought before her first class at Johnson Park.
