Jenny prepared Cumbia-Style Vegetable Skewers for the second client. The dish features firm fingerling potatoes, red bell pepper, zucchini, onion, and cherry tomatoes, arranged on skewers and lightly toasted in a pan with garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of salt. A little lime juice and chili flakes were added for a bright, slightly spicy finish.

The kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of roasted potatoes, caramelized onions, and warm spices. On the serving tray, the colorful skewers were arranged in a neat, rustic pyramid with lemon wedges and a small bowl of extra salsa sauce beside them. It was a quick, energizing breakfast for a client whose first class was scheduled for 6:00 AM.
