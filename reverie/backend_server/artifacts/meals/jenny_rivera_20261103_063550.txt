Jenny Rivera prepared Salsa Cumbia with Warm Sweet Potato, a vibrant breakfast bowl designed for her first cumbia class. The dish features tender sweet potatoes simmered in a savory broth, topped with crisp corn, red cabbage, jalapeño, and fresh herbs. She mixed them with house-made salsa, a generous portion of lime juice, and a pinch of cumin, while a small bowl of warm tortillas and a cup of black coffee completed the meal.

The aroma was immediately aromatic and spicy, with the earthiness of the potatoes, the sharp heat of the jalapeños, and the bright citrus of the lime creating an inviting scent. The presentation was colorful and appetizing: the golden-orange sweet potatoes, crisp red cabbage, and bright green salsa made the bowl look as lively as the cumbia rhythms she was about to lead. It was prepared for her first class at Johnson Park, a practical, energetic morning meal meant to energize her clients and make the class feel welcoming and spontaneous.
