Jenny prepared a vibrant Garden Fresh Salsa Bowl as a quick weekday breakfast. The dish consists of diced red onion, tomatoes, jalapeños, cilantro, lime juice, garlic, and a generous amount of fresh salsa sauce. She also added crisp cucumber, avocado, and a handful of toasted corn kernels.

She blended the vegetables with the salsa sauce, garlic, and lime until the mixture was glossy and fragrant, then arranged the colorful ingredients over a bed of warm rice. The presentation was bright and cheerful: the reds, greens, and yellows stood out against the pale rice, with a small lime wedge and a spoonful of extra salsa sauce nearby.

The aroma was lively and inviting—the sharpness of the onion, the sweetness of the tomatoes, the heat of the jalapeños, and the earthy freshness of the cilantro filled the kitchen. The occasion was a refreshing Saturday morning start, a quick way to energize her body before heading to Johnson Park, and a small surprise that she could keep for later cravings.
