Jenny prepared Golden Oatmeal & Fruit Morning, a simple, energizing breakfast perfect for a Saturday class debut. She used rolled oats, cinnamon, a pinch of salt, fresh ginger, and a little honey. For the fruit, she sliced oranges, bananas, and strawberries, tossing them with a squeeze of lemon and a teaspoon of chia seeds.

She prepared the oatmeal in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat, slowly adding water and stirring until the oats absorbed the liquid and turned into a fluffy, golden porridge. The warm cinnamon and ginger added subtle warmth, while the sweet fruit brightened the flavor. She topped it with the colorful fruit before serving.

The aroma filled the kitchen with toasted oats, caramelized ginger, citrus peel, and the faint sweetness of honey. On the plate, the oatmeal was arranged in a neat circle around the fruit, with a small bowl of warm milk nearby. This wholesome, colorful breakfast was Jenny’s first step toward a strong start to the day, before she led her first SaborFit class at 6:00 a.m.
