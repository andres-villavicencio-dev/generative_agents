Breakfast and Lunch: “Cumbia-Style Breakfast Bowl”

Jenny assembled a hearty breakfast bowl with scrambled eggs, shredded cheddar, sliced tomatoes, red onion, and a generous portion of her usual salsa sauce. She added fluffy whole-grain toast and a handful of fresh spinach, then tossed everything together with a light lemon-olive oil dressing.

The aroma of toasted bread, savory eggs, and the sharp-sweet salsa filled the kitchen as she prepared the meal. The colorful presentation featured bright orange tomatoes, green spinach, creamy cheese, and golden toast arranged in a wide, rustic bowl.

She prepared this for her Saturday class debut in Johnson Park and her early morning training session. The combination of breakfast and lunch served as a quick, energizing start to her day, blending her fitness passion with the cumbia rhythms she loves.
