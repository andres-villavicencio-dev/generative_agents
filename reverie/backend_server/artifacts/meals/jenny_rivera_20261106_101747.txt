Jenny’s breakfast was a hearty SaborFit Breakfast Bowl, designed to energize her body and support her fitness classes. The dish included scrambled eggs, ripe red and yellow bell peppers, cherry tomatoes, avocados, shredded chicken, and a generous portion of salsa. She added a small lime wedge for brightness and a pinch of chili flakes for subtle heat.

To prepare it, she cracked the eggs into a bowl, seasoned them with salt and pepper, and cooked them over medium heat until the whites were set and the yolks remained soft. The colorful vegetables were tossed with a splash of olive oil, and the eggs were folded over them. She placed the bowl on a warm breakfast plate and topped it with a colorful fruit arrangement—bananas, oranges, and kiwi—and a small bowl of leftover salsa.

The meal filled the kitchen with the savory aroma of egg, tomato, and cumin, mingling with the bright, citrusy scent of the fruit. It was a practical, colorful breakfast for a busy Tuesday, balancing her cooking, community service, and preparation for the SaborFit signature routine at Johnson Park on Saturday, February 14.
