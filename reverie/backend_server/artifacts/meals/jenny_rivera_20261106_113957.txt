Jenny Rivera prepared Salsa-Infused Black Bean & Corn Tacos for her Friday dinner. The dish featured crisp corn tortillas, soft black beans, diced red and yellow bell peppers, shredded cabbage, avocado, fresh cilantro, lime juice, and a creamy salsa made with onions, garlic, cumin, chili, and a splash of cream.

She prepared it in about twenty minutes, toasting the tortillas, sautéing the vegetables, and cooking the beans in a skillet until tender. She added the salsa and finished it with a squeeze of lime and a drizzle of olive oil. The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted corn, caramelized onions, toasted cumin, and fresh herbs.

Each taco was arranged neatly on a white plate, with the colorful vegetables, creamy avocado, and vibrant salsa arranged alongside a folded tortilla and a sprinkle of chili flakes. The meal was simple, colorful, and balanced—perfect for a quick, satisfying night after her SaborFit classes.
