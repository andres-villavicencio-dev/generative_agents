Jenny Rivera prepared Salsa Fit Breakfast Bowl, a vibrant, energizing brunch designed to pair with her morning fitness classes. The dish combines frybread, carnitas, avocado, corn tortillas, black beans, and a rich chili-lime salsa. For the salsa, she sautéed red peppers, onions, and jalapeños until crisp, then blended them with lime, cilantro, cumin, and a splash of avocado cream.

The preparation is both practical and spontaneous: Jenny measured the ingredients precisely before cooking the bread and meat, then added the toppings to create a colorful, handheld meal. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized pork, roasted peppers, and toasted corn, while the bright lime and cilantro added a lively citrus finish.

Presented in an earthen bowl, the bowl stacked neatly with warm tortillas, crispy corn, and scattered chilies, the presentation was hearty and inviting. It was Jenny’s first public breakfast in the park, intended to energize her 6:00 AM class and the clients who were training toward their fitness goals. The meal reflected her practical, energetic approach to cooking: nutritious, flavorful, and ready to inspire movement.
