Dish Name: Salsa-Crusted Avocado Toast with Cumin-Olive Oil, Lemon, and Black Pepper

Jenny prepared a quick breakfast for her early morning class, using the extra jar of salsa she had stored away for late-night cravings. She toasted whole-grain sourdough in a skillet until it was golden and crisp, then topped it with ripe avocado, a generous drizzle of olive oil, cumin, and black pepper.

She added thinly sliced radishes and red onion for crunch, then stirred in a bright red salsa that gave the dish its signature kick. The final touches came with a lemon wedge and a few crushed pistachios. She toasted the toast until the cheese melted, then plated it in a simple, rustic arrangement with a small bowl of extra salsa nearby.

The aroma was warm, earthy, and lively—a savory combination of toasted bread, rich avocado, zesty citrus, and savory spices that matched the energy she carried into her morning class. The meal was practical, energizing, and perfectly suited to the occasion: a quick breakfast on Tuesday, February 14, before heading to the gym.
