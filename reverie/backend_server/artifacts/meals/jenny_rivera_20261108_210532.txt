Jenny Rivera prepared Salsa-Infused Grilled Avocado Toast with Smoked Salmon and Cucumber. The dish blends her fitness-inspired energy with a quick, colorful breakfast.

She used two slices of sourdough bread, a ripe avocado, a smoked salmon fillet, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and a generous amount of her signature salsa sauce. The salsa was prepared by mixing fresh tomatoes, red onion, cilantro, cilantro, and lime juice with a splash of olive oil and a pinch of salt. Jenny grilled the salmon until it was just cooked through, then sliced the avocado and cucumber before serving.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, garlicky scent of the salsa and the savory, smoky notes of the salmon. She arranged the avocado toast in a small bowl with the salmon, cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion arranged around the edges, topped with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil and a fresh lemon wedge. The bright colors and fresh presentation turned the simple ingredients into a festive, celebratory breakfast, perfect for sharing with a small companion before her first class.
