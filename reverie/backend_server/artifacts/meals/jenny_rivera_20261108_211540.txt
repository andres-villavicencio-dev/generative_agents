Jenny prepared Salsa-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa, a quick, colorful breakfast that turned her late-afternoon slump into a fun morning celebration.

The dish featured tender chicken breasts marinated in lemon juice, garlic, chili flakes, and a small amount of her usual salsa sauce, then tossed with a little olive oil and crushed corn. Roasted bell peppers, cherry tomatoes, onions, and potatoes added sweetness and texture, while a cup of quinoa and a glass of water completed the meal.

It was warm, fragrant, and filled with the scent of roasted garlic and citrus, making the kitchen feel bright and inviting. She arranged the chicken in a shallow bowl, surrounded it with colorful vegetables, and placed the quinoa and dressing beside it. The presentation was casual and energizing—perfect for an unstructured Tuesday morning.

The occasion was her own victory lap: after completing her morning routine, stretching, and preparing the meal, Jenny celebrated her renewed energy and creativity. She ate quickly and moved toward her shower, ready to begin her day with enthusiasm.
