Jenny Rivera prepared Cumbia-Chili Breakfast, a simple yet flavorful brunch dish designed to energize her morning classes. The recipe combined chicken thighs, yellow potatoes, corn, diced tomatoes, red bell pepper, onion, garlic, and cumin, with a salsa sauce made from fresh tomatoes, chili, cilantro, lime, onion, and oregano. She sautéed the vegetables, browned the chicken, and simmered everything with a little water until the potatoes were tender and the sauce reduced.

The aroma was warm and aromatic—rich cumin, roasted chicken, tomato, and fresh herbs mingling with the subtle sweetness of the corn and potatoes. She plated the meal in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the golden potatoes, crisp chicken, and colorful vegetables around a bright lime wedge, with the extra salsa sauce spooned over the top.

The dish was prepared for the Saturday SaborFit class at Johnson Park, where she was choreographing her signature routine. It offered a hearty, spicy breakfast that complemented the energetic, rhythmic cumbia music she planned to teach, while the extra salsa sauce satisfied her late-night craving.
