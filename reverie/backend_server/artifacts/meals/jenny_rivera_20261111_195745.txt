Breakfast: Berry & Oat Power Bowl

Jenny prepared a warm oatmeal breakfast for the early Tuesday morning. She combined rolled oats, a splash of almond milk, a pinch of cinnamon, and a tablespoon of honey with ripe bananas and fresh blueberries and strawberries. The berries were lightly toasted with cinnamon and sprinkled over the finished bowl.

She stirred it gently over a small pan, then let the oatmeal absorb the liquid and warm through. She added a handful of blueberries and sliced strawberries, stirring until everything was evenly coated. The warm, honeyed oatmeal had a comforting aroma of oats, cinnamon, and sweet fruit.

She presented it in a colorful, rustic bowl with a spoon resting beside it. Jenny smiled and took a small bite, enjoying the warm meal with the energy she needed to begin her day. This simple breakfast was more than nourishment—it was a quiet way to welcome the day ahead, balancing her fitness routine with a moment of peaceful preparation.
