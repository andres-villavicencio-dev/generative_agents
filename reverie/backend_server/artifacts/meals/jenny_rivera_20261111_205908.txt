Jenny prepared Salsa-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Rice for Tuesday’s dinner. She used chicken thighs, red bell peppers, carrots, onions, tomatoes, garlic, oregano, chicken broth, and a generous amount of fresh salsa. The chicken was pan-seared to a golden brown, then coated in a warm salsa sauce and returned to the pan until the sauce thickened and the chicken stayed moist. Roasted vegetables added crisp edges, while the rice provided a comforting base.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of caramelized chicken and aromatic tomato-herb sauce. Presented in a simple white bowl, the meal was colorful and inviting—golden chicken, bright red peppers, orange carrots, green onions, and white rice. It was served for an early Tuesday dinner, with Jenny’s focus on preparing a nourishing, energizing meal to support her fitness training.
