Jenny Rivera prepared a simple, energizing bowl of SaborFit Oatmeal Breakfast. The base was rolled oats cooked with water, topped with a cinnamon-sugar swirl, fresh berries, a handful of nuts, and a generous drizzle of warm cinnamon-sugar syrup. She stirred it with a wooden spoon and served it in a colorful, rustic bowl.

The meal was prepared in the early morning kitchen: oats were toasted briefly, then cooked and stirred until creamy and fragrant. Jenny added sliced apples, strawberries, blueberries, walnuts, and chia seeds, finishing with a pinch of salt and a little cinnamon. As the kitchen filled with the sweet, warm aroma of toasted oats and cinnamon, she served the bowl.

The presentation was bright and inviting: the blueberries, strawberries, and nuts added pops of color against the creamy oat base. It was meant to be a quick, nutritious start to her Saturday morning classes at Johnson Park, with her signature SaborFit routine about to begin.
