Jenny Rivera prepared Quick Salsa-Lemon-Herb Breakfast Sandwiches to fuel her morning training. The key ingredients were whole grain bread, eggs, red onion, fresh cilantro, tomato, and a generous portion of salsa verde for the lunch.

She prepared the sandwiches by cooking the eggs in a skillet with a splash of olive oil until firm, then slicing them with the red onion and cilantro. The warm bread was toasted lightly to create a crispy exterior, while the filling remained fresh and vibrant. She layered the eggs over the sandwich, adding a pinch of oregano and a squeeze of lemon juice to brighten the savory flavors.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of toasted bread, the sharp tang of lime, and the warm richness of the egg and vegetable filling. The sandwich was presented on a white plate with the remaining salsa verde, fresh cilantro, and a lemon wedge beside it.

The meal was a spontaneous lunch for an early morning training day, chosen because it was quick, energizing, and matched her signature SaborFit style. She had also brought the extra jar of salsa sauce from her usual late-night cravings, which she hoped would become her first salsa night at the pub with Francisco Lopez.
