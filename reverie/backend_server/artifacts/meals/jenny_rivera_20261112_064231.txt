Jenny Rivera prepared a Salsa-Infused Breakfast Bowl, a quick, energizing meal designed to fuel her Saturday class while avoiding eggs. The bowl featured crisp kale, shredded sweet potato, whole-grain toast, ripe bananas, and a generous portion of homemade salsa with smoked paprika, cumin, garlic, and chili. A drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of salt completed the flavor.

She toasted the toast until golden, sliced the sweet potato, and lightly warmed the kale in a pan. As she stirred, the kitchen filled with the savory scent of toasted grains, caramelized fruit, and aromatic spices. The colorful, rustic presentation included the toast, fruit, vegetables, and salsa arranged around a bowl of oatmeal.

The occasion was a preclass breakfast before Jenny led her first-ever SaborFit class in Johnson Park. Her practical, energetic approach ensured she was fully fueled without needing eggs, allowing her to focus on the salsa night and train with Wolfgang Schulz and Mei Lin afterward.
