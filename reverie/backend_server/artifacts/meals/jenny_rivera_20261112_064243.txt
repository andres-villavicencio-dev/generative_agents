Jenny Rivera prepared Sabor Fit Breakfast Bowl, a vibrant, energy-replenishing meal tailored for her Saturday SaborFit debut and late-night salsa cravings.

The dish consists of scrambled eggs with cheddar and spinach, black beans, toasted almonds, avocado, and a side of warm corn tortillas. Her signature salsa sauce—made with canned tomatoes, garlic, onion, cilantro, lime juice, and a pinch of cumin—was stirred in at the end.

She prepared it by gently cooking the eggs over low heat, then adding the beans, cheese, and fresh herbs to finish the flavors. The salsa sauce was served separately, allowing her to toss it with the eggs or toast the tortillas before eating.

The aroma was warm and savory, with toasted seeds, onion, fresh herbs, and the bright tang of lime. It smelled invitingly breakfastlike, with a little extra salsa scent suggesting energy and celebration.

Presented on a colorful ceramic breakfast plate, the bowl featured golden eggs, bright green vegetables, creamy avocado, crunchy nuts, and colorful tortillas. The presentation felt practical and lively—perfect for a morning class, a training session, or an early pub gathering.
