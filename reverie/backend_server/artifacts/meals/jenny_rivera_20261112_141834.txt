Jenny Rivera prepared a Sabor Fit Breakfast Bowl, a simple, energizing meal designed to support her morning fitness class. The dish featured fluffy whole-grain eggs, sautéed spinach and red bell peppers, crispy bacon, cherry tomatoes, and a tangy salsa sauce. A squeeze of lime and a sprinkle of chili flakes added a bright, spicy finish.

She cooked the eggs in a nonstick pan with a little oil, then added the vegetables and bacon in batches to keep the pan from overcrowding. After letting the eggs set, she served the colorful, aromatic bowl over a grain of choice with warm whole-grain toast. The aroma was earthy and savory, with hints of roasted vegetables and fresh herbs.

The presentation was cheerful and inviting: the eggs were cracked atop the vegetables, and the bowl was garnished with a lime wedge, chili flakes, and a small sprig of parsley. It was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday breakfast before her SaborFit class, balancing her morning routine with her energy for the day ahead.
