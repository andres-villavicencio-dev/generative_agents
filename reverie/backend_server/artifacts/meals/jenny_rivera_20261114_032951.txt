Jenny Rivera prepared Sabor Fit Breakfast Burrito with Salsa-Crusted Chicken, designed to complement her first class at Johnson Park. The meal combines hearty breakfast staples with a vibrant salsa-inspired twist. Key ingredients include shredded chicken, rice, black beans, shredded cheese, avocado, fresh cilantro, onion, bell pepper, and a generous portion of her signature salsa sauce.

She pan-seared the chicken with garlic and cumin, then wrapped it in a whole-wheat tortilla with the remaining salsa. The salsa was lightly toasted with chili flakes and served with fresh fruit and a drizzle of extra yogurt. A side of steamed broccoli added a crisp, healthy element.

The aroma was warm and aromatic—sautéed onions, cumin, garlic, toasted chili, and the rich, spicy notes of salsa filled the kitchen. Jenny presented the burrito in a colorful paper wrapper, garnished with a lime wedge and a sprinkle of red pepper flakes, creating a visually lively breakfast that matched her energetic style. It was the perfect pre-class fuel, combining enough protein and carbohydrates to keep energy up before training while satisfying cravings with her usual salsa habit.
