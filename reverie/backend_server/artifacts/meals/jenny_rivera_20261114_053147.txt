Jenny Rivera prepared SaborFit Breakfast Bowl, a hearty, energizing brunch designed to match her SaborFit morning workout. The dish features a colorful base of quinoa, corn, and roasted sweet potato, topped with cumin-laced kale, avocado, chickpeas, and a bright orange carrot. She mixed in black beans, lime juice, chili flakes, and a generous drizzle of house-made salsa, finishing the bowl with a drizzle of yogurt and a sprinkle of chili flakes.

Jenny tossed the quinoa and vegetables in a hot skillet until the edges were crisp, then simmered the chickpeas and beans until tender. She stirred in the fresh herbs and finished the bowl with lime zest and a final burst of salsa. The aroma was warm, earthy, and inviting—crisp spices, caramelized sweetness, and the scent of fresh herbs filling the kitchen.

Jenny presented the bowl in a vibrant, rustic ceramic dish and arranged the toppings in a pleasing, asymmetrical pattern. It was prepared in advance and reheated just before class, allowing her to keep her energy high while staying true to the meal’s purpose: fueling her first Johnson Park class and supporting her friends’ fitness goals.
