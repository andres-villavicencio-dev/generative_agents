Jenny Rivera prepared SaborFit Salsa Bowl, a colorful, energy-packed breakfast and lunch that satisfied her hunger while leaving room for her salsa-night plans.

The dish featured chopped tomato, red onion, jalapeño, diced avocado, cherry tomatoes, fresh cilantro, lime juice, and a few strips of grilled chicken. She added a small handful of cucumber and sun-dried tomatoes for texture, while the chili flakes and garlic contributed a warm, aromatic depth.

She chopped the vegetables and peppers into bite-sized pieces, seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and cumin, then sautéed everything in a pan with just a splash of olive oil. The warm chicken and fresh herbs released their flavors, creating a lively aroma of roasted garlic, chili, and citrus.

She plated the meal in a wide bowl, arranging the colorful ingredients around the chicken and garnishing it with chopped parsley and a squeeze of lime. The bright colors, crisp textures, and savory-sweet flavors made it feel both refreshing and celebratory, perfectly suited as a quick breakfast to fuel her early training before her first class in Johnson Park.
