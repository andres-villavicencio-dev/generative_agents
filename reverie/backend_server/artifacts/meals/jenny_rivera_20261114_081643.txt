Jenny Rivera prepared SaborFit Breakfast Bowl for Saturday’s class at Johnson Park. The dish combines scrambled eggs, black beans, corn, black beans, diced avocado, salsa, and a generous amount of sour cream. She topped it with toasted whole-grain toast, orange slices, and a drizzle of lime juice.

The breakfast was prepared quickly in a large pan: eggs were scrambled until just set, then the beans and corn were added and cooked until the beans were tender and the corn was golden. Avocado was scooped in last to keep it bright and creamy, while the salsa provided a bright, spicy finish. Lemon zest and a pinch of smoked paprika were stirred in for flavor. The meal was presented as an inviting, colorful stack in a large ceramic bowl, with the orange slices arranged around the edges for visual appeal.

The aroma of toasted grains, roasted corn, savory eggs, and fresh salsa filled the kitchen, with warm spices adding a comforting undertone. It was a practical, energetic breakfast suited to an all-day class schedule and Jenny’s love of blending salsa and cumbia rhythms into her fitness routine.
