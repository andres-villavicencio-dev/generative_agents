Job Amely prepared Smoked Chicken with Root Vegetables and Grilled Asparagus for a simple breakfast the next morning.

The dish features tender smoked chicken breast, baby potatoes, carrots, onions, and asparagus arranged in a shallow dish. He sliced the chicken against the grain, marinated it with smoked paprika, garlic, olive oil, and a splash of lemon juice, then grilled it until golden and juicy. While it cooked, he roasted the vegetables until crisp-tender and tossed them with a little olive oil and dried thyme. The final presentation was rustic and inviting: the chicken rested on a serving platter beside the vegetables, garnished with fresh parsley and a bright lemon wedge.

The aroma filled the kitchen as he turned on the broiler—smoky, savory, and warm. Job served the breakfast over a white cloth, making sure everyone could see the colorful arrangement. It was a straightforward, nourishing meal after his busy repair rounds, reflecting his practical nature and his habit of sharing home-cooked meals with others.
