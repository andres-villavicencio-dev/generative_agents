On Tuesday, February 14, Job Amely prepared a hearty Mornay Chicken and Egg Baked Potato for breakfast. He used a ripe russet potato cut into quarters, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg, then baked it until the skin was crisp and the flesh was tender. The potato was sliced to reveal the fluffy white yolk, while a small pan of chicken thighs cooked in butter with garlic and rosemary. An egg yolk rested atop the sliced potato, and a little fresh chives and chives added a bright pop of color.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted potatoes, garlic, and rising eggs. Job placed the dish in a shallow white bowl and garnished it with a drizzle of olive oil and a sprinkle of dried thyme. It was presented as a comforting, rustic breakfast, fitting for a weekday morning with a storm coming through. Job ate it with his family at home, the familiar smell of bread and eggs reminding him how much he loved preparing simple meals for others.
