Meal: Smoked Chicken, Mashed Potatoes, Green Beans, and a Lemon-Orange Pan Sauce

Job prepared a simple, hearty breakfast for the Moore family after finishing his Wednesday shift. He started with a bowl of fluffy, warm mashed potatoes, topped with sliced smoked chicken, and served the vegetables with a bright lemon-orange pan sauce made from butter, shallots, garlic, lemon juice, and a little sugar.

The ingredients came from the store: chicken breasts, potatoes, green beans, butter, garlic, fresh lemon, orange, and salt. Job lightly seared the chicken in a pan, added the vegetables, and finished the dish by pouring the pan sauce over everything. He garnished it with a sprinkle of parsley and a few chopped sun-dried tomatoes, then served it in a shallow white bowl.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of smoked chicken and the bright scent of citrus. The presentation was simple and inviting—the golden chicken, creamy potatoes, and colorful vegetables looked comforting and ready to share.

It was a quiet, family breakfast prepared to warm the house and support Job’s day.
