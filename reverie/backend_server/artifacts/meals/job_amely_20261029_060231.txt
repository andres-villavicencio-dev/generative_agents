The meal was Tom’s Roast Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Grilled Lemon-Basil Rice, prepared for a simple Wednesday lunch after his repair shift.

Amely chose a whole chicken, carrots, potatoes, and onions, then marinated them in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and pepper. He roasted everything on the grill, searing the chicken first to develop a golden crust while the vegetables softened. While the chicken finished, he cooked the rice with the remaining aromatics and sliced fresh basil for garnish.

The air filled with the savory scent of roasted poultry and caramelized onions, warm from the grill, and bright from the citrus. Amely arranged the chicken in the center of a serving platter, surrounded by golden vegetables and a small mound of fragrant rice, using a wooden spoon to create an inviting, rustic presentation.

It was a straightforward family lunch, but the careful preparation and warm aroma reminded Amely of the home-cooked meals he prepared for others.
