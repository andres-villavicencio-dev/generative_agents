The meal was Roasted Chicken with Herb-Crusted Potatoes and Root Vegetables, prepared as a comforting family-style dinner for a quiet Wednesday evening.

Job began by searing the chicken in a skillet, then tossing it with a mixture of rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, onion, and a pinch of black pepper. The vegetables—sautéed potatoes, carrots, and onions—were warmed in the same pan until golden, while the chicken roasted until the skin turned crisp and the meat reached an even, juicy red. The entire dish was plated in a shallow bowl, with the golden vegetables arranged beneath the chicken and a generous drizzle of pan juices over the top.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, and citrus. Job carried the meal to the counter, letting the kitchen cool before serving it. The simple, rustic presentation made the family dinner feel like a small celebration of his steady hand and caring spirit. By 7:00 pm, the food had been refrigerated while Job went to bed at 10:00 pm.
