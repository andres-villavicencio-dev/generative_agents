Job prepared a hearty Smoked Trout and Root Vegetable Casserole, a family-style dinner that reflected his hands-on approach to cooking and the care he took in every ingredient.

The dish featured fresh smoked trout fillets, carrots, parsnips, potatoes, onions, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a touch of lemon zest. For the sauce, Job used a buttermilk-based gravy, seasoned with smoked paprika and a little flour, and served it over the root vegetables. He roasted the potatoes first, then gently warmed the trout in the pan with herbs before serving everything in a shallow, golden casserole dish.

The aroma was warm and savory—smoked fish, roasted vegetables, and a nutty garlic undertone filled the kitchen. It presented as a rustic, golden-yellow casserole, with plump carrots and parsnips peeking out from beneath the trout. Job plated it for a Sunday family dinner, sharing the meal with his wife and two young children. His meticulous preparation and patient, encouraging demeanor made the occasion feel both comforting and celebratory.
