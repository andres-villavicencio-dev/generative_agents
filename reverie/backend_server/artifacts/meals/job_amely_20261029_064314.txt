The evening meal was Roasted Root Vegetable and Herb Pot Pie, prepared on Wednesday, February 15, after a full day of repairs at Tom Moreno’s house.

Job gathered carrots, onions, potatoes, carrots, and a small amount of peas, then sliced them thinly. In a large skillet, he sautéed the onions and carrots until they had softened, then added the potatoes, a splash of vegetable stock, and plenty of herbs, including thyme, oregano, and a touch of garlic. He covered the pan and simmered until the potatoes were tender and the vegetables released their natural sweetness.

Afterward, he transferred the filling into a golden, flaky pie crust, added a little milk and extra herbs for richness, and baked the pot pie until the crust was crisp and the filling bubbled gently. He served it in a shallow bowl, garnished with fresh chives and a lemon wedge, and set the table just before 7:00 p.m.

The kitchen was filled with the warm, savory scent of roasted onions, garlic, and herbs. The pot pie looked inviting and homely, with a golden crust and steam rising from the bubbling filling. Job served one large portion, a smaller side of green beans, and a glass of warm wine. It was a thoughtful, home-cooked meal, and he ate it with quiet satisfaction, having turned another day’s maintenance into a comforting evening.
