Meal: Roasted Tomato Basil Soup with Toasted Whole Wheat Bread

Job prepared a warm, hearty Roasted Tomato Basil Soup for his breakfast table. The soup featured ripe tomatoes, a handful of fresh basil, garlic, olive oil, vinegar, salt, pepper, and a pinch of cumin. He sautéed the garlic and tomatoes in a pan until soft, then simmered them with the broth and basil until the soup was thick and fragrant. Toasted whole-wheat bread was added for a sturdy companion to every bowl.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized tomatoes and basil. Job arranged the steaming soup on a white plate, topped it with a generous swirl of basil, and placed a slice of golden-brown toast beside it. The simple presentation felt inviting, practical, and celebratory—a quiet moment of warmth before continuing the day’s repair tasks.

It was a comforting breakfast, and Job had no doubt it would make a fine lunch or even a supper for someone waiting around.
