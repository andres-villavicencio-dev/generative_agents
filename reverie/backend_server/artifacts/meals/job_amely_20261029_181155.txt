On a quiet Friday, Job Amely prepared a Simple Chicken Panini with Roasted Vegetables and a Lemon-Lemon Herb Dressing. For the panini, he sliced a whole chicken breast into thin strips, toasted sliced baguette with a little olive oil and salt, and layered it with roasted carrots, zucchini, and onions. He brushed the chicken with the dressing and toasted it until the edges turned golden.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onions, fresh herbs, and toasted bread. Job arranged the panini on a warm platter and placed small bowls of crisp vegetables and the bright dressing beside it. The meal was simple and hearty, with the savory chicken and earthy vegetables balancing the lemony dressing.

It was meant as a quick, nourishing lunch after his routine repair rounds, but it also felt like a small celebration—warm, practical, and grounded in the care he takes for everyone he serves.
