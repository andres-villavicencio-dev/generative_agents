Dish Name: Simple Tomato-Garlic Egg Breakfast

Key Ingredients:
Large eggs
Whole milk
Bread
Chopped tomatoes
Garlic
Salt, pepper, and a touch of olive oil
Fresh parsley

Preparation Method:
Job cooked the eggs in a hot pan until they were fully set, then added the tomatoes, garlic, and seasonings. He stirred gently until the tomatoes heated through, then folded in the parsley. He served the eggs over slices of warm bread, with a little extra butter and fresh fruit for garnish.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the rich, savory scent of cooking eggs, garlic, and tomatoes, followed by the faint, crisp aroma of toasted bread.

Presentation: The plate was arranged simply with the bright yellow eggs, colorful tomato halves, and golden-brown bread slices. A small bowl of orange juice and a piece of crusty bread completed the breakfast spread.

Occasion: A quiet Saturday morning meal after work, prepared for Job’s own enjoyment before heading out to help the Moore family’s roof and Tom Moreno’s house.
