The meal was Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Pan-Roasted Potatoes, prepared for a relaxed Saturday lunch at Tom Moreno’s house. Amely sliced a whole chicken into tender pieces, seasoned it with garlic, lemon zest, thyme, and cracked black pepper, then seared it in hot oil until the skin turned golden brown. He arranged the chicken alongside carrots, potatoes, and onions, then roasted everything until the vegetables were caramelized and the chicken was cooked through.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, cinnamon, herbs, and savory poultry. Amely plated the meal in a rustic ceramic bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a generous handful of roasted nuts. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the golden chicken and deep orange vegetables creating a cheerful contrast.

It was a straightforward meal prepared for a family gathering. Amely had prepared it with care, balancing his hands-on cooking with the practical tasks he had been handling throughout the day. The scent and warm colors made the occasion feel cozy, like a quiet lunch shared with friends before heading home.
