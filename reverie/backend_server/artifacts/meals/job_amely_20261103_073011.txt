“Tom’s Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Pantry Staples”

Job Amely prepared a simple, hearty dinner for the Moore family after fixing the roof and addressing the burst pipes. He used a whole chicken, baby potatoes, carrots, onions, a yellow onion, garlic, olive oil, paprika, salt, and pepper, with a splash of chicken broth.

After preheating the oven, he seared the chicken in a hot pan until the skin turned golden, then transferred it to the oven. He roasted the vegetables until tender, tossing them halfway through with paprika to give them a warm, savory finish. The kitchen filled with the aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, garlic, and the earthy scent of olive oil.

He plated the chicken in a shallow pan, arranging the vegetables around it, and sprinkled fresh parsley over the top. The meal was simple and warm, with the pantry staples providing a comforting backdrop. It was prepared for the Moore family’s Saturday dinner, offering a peaceful, nourishing conclusion to a full day of repairs.
