Dish Name: Warm Potato and Herb Risotto with Roast Chicken

Key ingredients: Arborio rice, chicken breast, Parmesan cheese, thyme, garlic, fresh parsley, olive oil, and a splash of chicken stock.

Preparation: Amely heats the stock slowly while sautéing onions and garlic until soft, then adds the rice. He stirs in the chicken breast, covering the pan until the rice absorbs the liquid and turns creamy. Parmesan and chopped herbs are stirred in at the end, and the dish finishes by simmering until the grains are tender but still distinct. The chicken is seasoned simply, then rested briefly before being sliced and plated.

Aromatically rich, the restaurant smells of toasted garlic, dried thyme, warm olive oil, and the savory richness of Parmesan. Amely presents it in a shallow white bowl, arranged with the chicken and a crisp lemon wedge, garnished with a final pinch of salt and fresh herbs.

The occasion was a quiet family dinner on a rainy Tuesday, a chance to turn his favorite recipe into something comforting and shareable.
