Dish: “Morning’s Simple” Buttered Toast with Egg and Herb

Job prepared a warm, rustic breakfast using sourdough slices toasted to a golden brown, topped with softened butter, salt, and a handful of fresh herbs. He cracked a large egg into a small pan, seasoned it with pepper, and slid the toasted bread into the center before finishing the egg with a generous drizzle of butter and a sprinkle of chives.

The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of toasted bread and the rich aroma of frying egg. The dish was presented simply on the counter, with the buttery egg resting beside the crisp toast. Job ate it slowly, savoring the comforting simplicity and feeling grateful for a quiet morning before beginning his repair rounds.
