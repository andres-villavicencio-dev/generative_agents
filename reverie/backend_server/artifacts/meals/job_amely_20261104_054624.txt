For Monday, Job prepared Sunday Smoked-Salmon and Root Vegetable Breakfast for his morning breakfast and a simple Oatmeal with Berries for lunch.

He began by gently warming the smoked salmon in a pan until the skin softened, then sliced it and placed it on a clean plate. Around it, he arranged roasted carrots, parsnips, and a small orange, sprinkling them with sea salt and a dash of smoked paprika. For the oatmeal, he cooked rolled oats with warm water, honey, cinnamon, and sliced strawberries, finishing with a sprinkle of walnuts.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of smoked fish, caramelized root vegetables, and cinnamon. He arranged the breakfast in a rustic wooden serving bowl, placing the salmon in the center and the colorful vegetables in neat rows beside it. He carried a small glass of lemonade and set it down before taking a seat.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday morning: Job wanted to start his day with something nourishing, share a meal with his housemate, and begin the week with a relaxed, cheerful mood.
