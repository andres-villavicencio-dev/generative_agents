Breakfast: “Fishing-Trap” Omelet with Smashed Bread and Tomato

Job Amely made a warm, hearty omelet for his delivery driver, the town’s partner in the weekend fishing trip. He paired it with two slices of toasted sourdough, a handful of crispy tomato slices, and a drizzle of olive oil.

The omelet began as a loose mixture of egg, chopped chives, a pinch of garlic, and a splash of lemon juice. Job stirred the ingredients until the whites became opaque and the yolks began to soften, then folded the egg into a lightly floured batter. After heating a small pan, he poured the batter in a neat circle and let it cook until the edges turned golden. He finished it by sliding in the sourdough slices and adding the tomato pieces.

As the omelet set, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of butter and eggs, fresh chives, and the faint scent of toasted bread. Job presented the dish on a warm ceramic plate, arranging the orange slices around the golden omelet and placing a small wooden tag on the side.

The meal was simple and practical: a quick, filling breakfast designed to give the delivery driver the energy he needed for the long day ahead of the weekend fishing trip. Job took a quiet moment to enjoy it before heading out.
