Dish Name: Smoked Pork and Root Vegetable Hash

Job prepared this hearty, one-pot breakfast while cleaning the empty house. He began by sautéing onions and carrots in butter until tender, then added diced smoked pork, potatoes, and corn. After the vegetables softened, he tossed in chopped parsley and a splash of milk for a creamy consistency. The dish was finished with a pinch of salt, black pepper, and a drizzle of olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of smoked pork, caramelized onions, and earthy vegetables. He served the hash in a shallow dish, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a few scattered toasted nuts. It was a simple, rustic meal designed to provide comfort after a long day of repairs. Job prepared it for himself before sharing it with his delivery driver, the town’s housemate, as a quiet way to ease into a relaxed morning.
