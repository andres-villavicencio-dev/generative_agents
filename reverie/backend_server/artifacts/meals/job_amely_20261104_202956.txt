The meal was a simple, comforting dinner of Roast Root Vegetables with Lemon-Thyme Rice, prepared as a quiet family-style lunch after Job finished his repair rounds.

Job sliced carrots, parsnips, and potatoes into a small bowl, then arranged them with a handful of brown rice, fresh parsley, and lemon. He tossed the vegetables with olive oil, salt, and freshly cracked black pepper, spreading the mixture over the rice. After roasting the vegetables until the skin softened and turning golden, he added the rice to the pan. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm, earthy notes of caramelized root vegetables, roasted potatoes, and the bright perfume of lemon and thyme.

He plated the dish in a shallow bowl, arranging the vegetables in a colorful, rustic pattern and scattering the parsley and nuts over the top. He served a small cup of tea beside it. The occasion was a peaceful evening between Job, his housemate, and the delivery driver—no pressure, no social obligations, just a relaxed dinner in the warmth of an old neighborhood home.
