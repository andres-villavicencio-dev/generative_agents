Breakfast: “Tom’s Home-Style Breakfast”

Job prepared a hearty breakfast of scrambled eggs, toast with butter and a splash of milk, and a simple oatmeal toast with raisins and a slice of lemon. The eggs were cracked over a small pan of butter, stirred gently, and served alongside warm toast and a bowl of oatmeal. He prepared the oatmeal in the empty pantry, using the empty shelves as a makeshift kitchen station for flour, oats, and water.

As he worked, the kitchen filled with the familiar aroma of toasted bread, butter, and warm oats. He plated the breakfast neatly, arranging the eggs in a small mound and placing the toast beside a bowl of oatmeal. A small lemon wedge added a bright finish, while warm butter and a little cinnamon dusted the pan gave the meal an inviting, comforting appearance.

Job took his morning coffee while he ate, his meticulous and resourceful nature evident in every careful touch. The occasion was a quiet weekday breakfast—a small reward for a full, purposeful day of storm repairs and an extra reward for the upcoming fishing trip. It was warm, practical, and perfectly suited to his mood.
