Meal: Roasted Root Vegetables with Herbed Chicken and a Lemon-Roasted Potato  

Job prepared a simple, hearty family-style dinner for the empty kitchen. He sliced carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, then tossed them with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of lemon juice. The chicken was marinated in olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, and fresh herbs before being roasted alongside the vegetables. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, and citrus, creating a cozy, nostalgic atmosphere for his home-cooked meal.

He arranged the golden-brown vegetables and tender chicken around a large platter, garnishing it with a few fresh herbs and a lemon wedge. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the golden vegetables and bright yellow chicken contrasting against the earthy colors of the potatoes. The meal was designed to be warm, nourishing, and easy to enjoy while the kitchen was empty.

On February 14, Job prepared the dish for a quiet Sunday evening—perhaps a chance to share it with his housemate who was fishing. Its simplicity and warmth made it a fitting treat after a full day of storm repairs, balancing his practical work with a moment of comfort and connection.
