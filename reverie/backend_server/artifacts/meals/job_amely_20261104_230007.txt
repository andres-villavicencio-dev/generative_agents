For his lunch break, Job prepared Pork-Chopped Stew with Potatoes and Green Beans. The stew was seasoned with thyme, garlic, a splash of vinegar, and a pinch of smoked paprika, with the pork chopped into bite-sized cubes and potatoes cut into uniform rounds. The green beans were lightly sautéed, then added to the pot along with a handful of carrots and onions, simmering until the meat was tender and the vegetables were crisp-tender.

The kitchen smelled of caramelized garlic, warm herbs, and roasting pork. Job arranged the potatoes and carrots around the green beans on a wide, platter-style dinner plate, letting the bright green beans and golden potatoes frame the darker pork. He served it with crusty bread and a simple lemon-water dressing, making the meal feel generous and comforting.

It was a quiet, celebratory lunch for his Tuesday, balancing the storm repairs and a family appointment with the chance to relax with his housemate. Job took a satisfying pause over the finished stew, grateful that his craftsmanship had turned into something more than just a repair.
