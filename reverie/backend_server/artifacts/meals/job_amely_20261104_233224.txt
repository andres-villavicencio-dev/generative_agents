Job Amely prepared Stove-top Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Butter. For the roast, he used a whole chicken, 2 lbs of potatoes, 1 red bell pepper, 1 yellow squash, 2 carrots, 2 onions, garlic, and fresh rosemary. He seasoned the chicken with salt, black pepper, dried thyme, and lemon zest, then roasted it in a large skillet over medium heat until golden and juicy. The vegetables were roasted separately until tender, then returned to the pan with a generous drizzle of butter, lemon juice, and garlic for 5 minutes.

The aroma was warm and inviting—golden-brown skin, savory chicken, caramelized carrots, and the sharp scent of lemon and herbs filling the kitchen. It was presented on a rustic wooden platter with a simple green garnish and a small bowl of crisp lemon wedges. Job served it at home on Tuesday, February 14, after a full day of storm repairs and preparing a simple meal for himself.
