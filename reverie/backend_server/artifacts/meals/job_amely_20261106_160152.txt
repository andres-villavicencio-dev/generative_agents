Dish Name: Warm Oat and Fruit Breakfast Bowl

Job prepared a hearty, nourishing breakfast bowl for the early morning after his workday. He used locally farmed oats, cinnamon, a pinch of nutmeg, a handful of dried cranberries, sliced banana, apples, and a few almonds. A splash of vanilla-infused milk and a generous drizzle of honey warmed the oats, while fresh berries added a bright, tart finish.

The preparation was simple yet thoughtful: oatmeal was boiled until soft, then fluffed with a fork. Fresh fruit was sliced and arranged around the edges of a ceramic bowl, while Job added a small spoonful of honey and a generous pinch of cinnamon to the center. He covered the bowl with a warm cloth to let the flavors meld, then served it at room temperature.

The aroma was inviting—cinnamon, vanilla, caramel, and the gentle sweetness of fruit filled the kitchen. The presentation was warm and inviting, with the colorful fruit arranged around the creamy oats and a small piece of cinnamon stick for visual balance. Job served it with a small spoon, ready to share the meal with his housemate while they discussed the upcoming repairs. It was a quiet, home-cooked start to the day, a small reward for keeping Smallville’s buildings safe and a reminder that meticulous work often turns into moments of warmth.
