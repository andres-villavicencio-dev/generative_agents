Dish: Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Herb Crust

Amely prepared a whole chicken roasted with lemon, garlic, and rosemary, served with carrots, potatoes, and green beans. The chicken was seasoned with smoked paprika, dried thyme, and black pepper, then marinated for two hours. It was roasted in a single oven until the skin turned crisp and the meat fell off easily. Alongside the vegetables, Amely made a simple herb crust using chopped herbs, olive oil, and breadcrumbs, which he brushed around the chicken’s edges before roasting.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of caramelizing pork, warm garlic, and fresh rosemary. Amely arranged the golden chicken, bright vegetables, and lemon slices on a large platter, garnishing it with fresh rosemary sprigs and a lemon wedge. He placed the meal on the table before heading home to prepare a quiet, comforting dinner for a rainy evening.
