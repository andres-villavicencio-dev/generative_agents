Job prepared Roast Chicken with Garden Potatoes and Lemon Herb Sauce for his weekly lunch break. He sliced the chicken, seasoned it with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and black pepper, then roasted it until the skin was crisp and the meat was golden. He paired the chicken with crisp red potatoes and a small handful of mixed greens.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, rosemary, and lemon. Job arranged the potatoes and greens on a platter alongside the cooked chicken, garnishing it with a few fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon. He served it at a quiet family-style table in the cafe, the bright colors of the vegetables and the golden brown of the chicken creating a cheerful, inviting presentation.

Job had planned the meal to honor the upcoming storm, using the crisp potatoes and flavorful sauce as a practical lunch while keeping the mood relaxed and homey.
