On a quiet Tuesday morning, Job Amely prepared Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Roasted Potatoes for the Moore family. He roasted the chicken over the stove until it turned golden, then tossed the root vegetables and potatoes with lemon, herbs, and a little olive oil before serving them alongside the poultry.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of roasted chicken, lemon, and herbs. Job placed the vegetables in a shallow dish and arranged the golden chicken on the serving platter, using a sharp knife to create a simple, rustic presentation. He garnished the sides with fresh thyme and a sprinkle of salt, then brought the plate to the table.

For the Moore family, the meal was a heartwarming family dinner, prepared with care and the warmth of long years of neighborhood service. Job enjoyed his simple dinner, grateful that his time was finally rewarding.
