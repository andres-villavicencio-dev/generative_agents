Job Amely prepared Crispy Chicken with Garlic Herb Sauce and Roasted Vegetables for dinner on Tuesday, February 14. He sliced chicken thighs, marinated them in olive oil, lemon, garlic, and dried herbs, then pan-fried them until the skin turned golden and crisp. The vegetables—zucchini, carrots, and red bell peppers—were roasted until tender and caramelized, their edges lightly charred. A creamy garlic sauce was poured over the chicken, while the vegetables were arranged around a bed of jasmine rice.

The aroma of sautéed garlic, toasted chicken, and caramelized vegetables filled the small café kitchen. The meal had a warm, savory flavor with hints of lemon and citrus, balanced by the richness of the sauce and the freshness of the vegetables. Job presented it on a simple white plate, the golden chicken and vibrant green vegetables standing in contrasting colors, completing the rustic aesthetic of his home-cooked dinner.

It was a quiet, satisfying meal after a long day of repairs—most notably, the Johnson Park bulletin board and the follow-up with Isabella Rodriguez regarding the Moore family’s roof. Job enjoyed the comfort of the food and the satisfaction of having completed his scheduled tasks.
