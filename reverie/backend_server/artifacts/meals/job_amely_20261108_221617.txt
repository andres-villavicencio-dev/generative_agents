Meal: Smoky Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Browned Potatoes

Job Amely prepared a hearty family-style lunch for his home-cooked meal. He roasted a whole chicken with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of apple cider vinegar, then sliced carrots, parsnips, and potatoes into thick chunks, seasoned them with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and roasted them until they were tender and deeply caramelized.

The chicken was served over a bed of roasted root vegetables, with a simple pan sauce made from the pan drippings and a pinch of onion powder. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm smoky notes, herbaceous rosemary, and the savory richness of roasted root vegetables. Job arranged the chicken in the center, with the bright orange carrots and golden potatoes around it, and topped the dish with a handful of fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice.

The presentation was simple and inviting—the rustic platter looked prepared from the kitchen rather than the restaurant. Job took a quiet moment to enjoy it after two hours of work, grateful that his hands-on repairs had ended with a warm, nourishing meal.
