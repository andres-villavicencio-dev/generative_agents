On Tuesday, February 14, Job Amely prepared Morning Crisp & Herb Toast, a simple yet hearty breakfast for the house. He toasted sourdough over a hot pan, then added crispy bacon, thinly sliced red onion, fresh chives, and a generous handful of toasted pine nuts. For the egg, he cracked a whole hen’s egg into a pan, letting it run through a few drops of water before seasoning it with pepper, dried thyme, and a pinch of salt. The final touch was sliced avocado and lemon zest, with a dash of hot sauce added for a bright finish.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of toasted bread, bacon, and herbs, while the warm yellow light from the window made the meal feel inviting and homey. He plated the breakfast in a small wooden bowl, arranging the crisp toppings around a soft, golden egg and resting it beside the toasted bread. It was a quiet Sunday-morning breakfast—simple enough to prepare in about an hour, but substantial enough to make him feel warm, accomplished, and ready for the day ahead.
