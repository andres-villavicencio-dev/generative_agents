The occasion was a quiet Friday evening after a long weekend of repairs: Job Amely was preparing dinner for Tom Moreno and his family before helping with the burst pipe and patching the Moore family’s roof.

He prepared Tom’s Garden Bowl for a modest yet hearty family meal. The dish combined baby spinach, cherry tomatoes, red onion, feta cheese, a drizzle of olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of sea salt, with toasted pine nuts and a few sun-dried tomatoes. He also made a small pan of garlic butter chicken thighs, browned the chicken, and finished it with roasted asparagus.

The kitchen smelled of toasted nuts, warm garlic, and fresh herbs. A bright lemon-lime dressing balanced the richness of the chicken, while the colorful garden vegetables added visual energy and a refreshing crunch. Job presented the bowl in a simple white bowl, garnishing it with parsley and a few whole peppercorns, then set the plates down beside the sink where he was replacing the pipe and patching the roof.

The meal was simple, warm, and perfectly suited to his hands-on, homey lifestyle—just what he needed to finish the repairs and reconnect with Tom.
