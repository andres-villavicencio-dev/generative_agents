Dish: “River-Stone” Roast Chicken with Herb-Crusted Carrots and Green Beans

Job prepared a hearty family-style dinner on a Sunday night, treating the meal as a quiet pause after his long day. He roasted a whole chicken over a low flame, seasoning it generously with garlic, rosemary, thyme, dried oregano, and a splash of lemon juice. The carrots and green beans were cut into bite-sized pieces and tossed in olive oil, salt, pepper, and a pinch of smoked paprika before roasting alongside the bird.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized garlic and roasted vegetables, along with the savory scent of herbs and a hint of chicken. When the chicken was nearly done, Job turned the oven off and finished it in the pan, creating a golden, crispy skin. He arranged the pan on the table, garnishing it with fresh rosemary and a squeeze of lemon. The carrots and green beans were plated separately, their bright colors adding a cheerful contrast to the rich orange of the roast.

It was a simple, satisfying meal designed for a relaxed Sunday. Job ate slowly, savoring each bite as if sharing a secret with someone he trusted. As he placed a plate of cold crackers beside the vegetables, he felt a warm sense of fulfillment—not just from completing the repairs, but from creating something nourishing and homey.
