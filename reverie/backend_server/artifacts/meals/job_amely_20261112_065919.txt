Lunch: “Tom’s Garden” Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce

Job took a quiet lunch break in the small kitchen of his house, preparing a simple, warm meal for himself and his housemate. He started with a whole chicken, skin-on and seasoned with salt, black pepper, rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a touch of lemon zest. The bird was seared until the skin became crisp and golden, then slowly roasted until the flesh was tender and the juices ran clear.

Next, he sliced carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, tossing them with olive oil, salt, and pepper before roasting alongside the chicken. Fresh herbs and a splash of vinegar gave the sides bright color and a pleasant tang. He served the chicken and vegetables over a simple green salad with a lemon-dill vinaigrette.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, garlic, and herbs, while the golden-brown chicken and vibrant vegetables added a cheerful, inviting glow. Job arranged the plates in an orderly basket and added a small loaf of bread and a cup of herbal tea. It was a relaxed, home-cooked lunch—a simple meal that reminded him of the steady, caring work he had spent two decades doing for the town.
