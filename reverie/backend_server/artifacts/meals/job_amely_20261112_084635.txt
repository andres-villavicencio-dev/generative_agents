Meal: Tomato Basil Garlic Breakfast Bowl

Job prepared a simple, warming breakfast at home while finishing the burst-pipe repairs at Tom Moreno’s house. The meal consisted of whole-grain porridge, poached eggs, sliced tomatoes, chives, and a drizzle of olive oil and lemon juice, served with toasted bread.

He began by heating the porridge in a small pan, seasoning it lightly with salt and pepper. After cooking the eggs in a pot of simmering water, he sliced fresh tomatoes and chives and arranged everything in a wide, shallow bowl. A generous squeeze of lemon and a few fresh chives added a bright finish.

The aroma was rich and inviting—warm oats, creamy egg, and the scent of garlic. Job presented the dish with a small wooden spoon in one hand and a neatly folded napkin in the other, his hands visibly calm as he settled into the quiet morning. He had no time to celebrate, but he took the moment to prepare something nourishing while the storm was still gathering outside.
