Dinner: Simple Lemon-Butter Garlic Chicken with Roasted Vegetables

Job prepared a warm, comforting meal for a quiet Monday evening after his repair rounds. He cooked a whole chicken seasoned with olive oil, lemon, garlic, thyme, and a touch of paprika, while vegetables such as carrots, potatoes, and onions were roasted alongside him. He added a splash of white wine and a handful of fresh parsley to the pan, cooking the ingredients until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory poultry. After removing the pan from the heat, he turned off the oven, letting the chicken rest briefly so its juices would redistribute. He plated the chicken on a white plate, arranging the vegetables in a small bowl beside it, and finished the dish with a drizzle of lemon-herb butter.

Job set the table with a cup of tea, a glass of water, and a piece of bread. For him, it was a cozy, unhurried meal after a long day of fixing roofs, pipes, and equipment. He sat down with his housemate and enjoyed the simple satisfaction of a well-prepared dinner before heading to bed at 10:00 p.m.
