Job prepared Sunny-Side-Up Eggs with Smoked Ham and Buttery Toast, a simple, hearty breakfast for the upcoming fishing trip.  

He cracked two eggs into a small pan over medium heat, adding a splash of water and a pinch of salt until the whites set. He sliced a smoked ham into thin strips, toasted a slice of bread with butter, and arranged the eggs, ham, toast, and a few chopped chives beside it. The dish was garnished with a drizzle of olive oil and a dusting of cracked pepper.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of fried eggs, smoked meat, warm butter, and toasted bread. Job served it in a simple white bowl, with the bread cut into two portions. It was meant to be a quick, satisfying breakfast for his housemate and to fuel his preparation for the fishing trip.
