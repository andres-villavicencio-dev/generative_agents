Job Amely prepared a hearty Tomato and Egg Scramble with Whole Wheat Toast and Fresh Milk. He used two eggs, a handful of diced tomatoes, onions, garlic, spinach, cheddar cheese, and a few fresh herbs, with the whole wheat bread providing a sturdy base.

He cracked the eggs into a lightly oiled pan, sautéed the tomatoes and onions until the pan released its oils, then folded in the spinach and cheese. The finished scramble was glossy and fragrant, with the earthy tomatoes and rich cheddar adding a warm, savory aroma. He served it alongside a crisp slice of toasted bread and a glass of fresh milk.

The meal was served on a simple wooden platter, with the golden bread, bright green spinach, creamy cheese, and golden eggs creating a colorful, welcoming breakfast. It was prepared for a quiet morning at the town market, where Job wanted to nourish his body and prepare for the busy days ahead.
