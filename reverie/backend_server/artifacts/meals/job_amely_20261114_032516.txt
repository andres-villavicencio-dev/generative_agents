Job prepared Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy for a quiet Monday breakfast. The centerpiece was a whole chicken roasted with carrots, potatoes, onions, and garlic until golden and tender, then served with a savory gravy made from the pan juices and a pinch of smoked paprika. He added fresh parsley, lemon wedges, and a drizzle of olive oil for freshness.

He prepared it over a small, quiet table inside the Supply Store, letting the kitchen warm-up while he checked the work orders. The aroma rose from the roast and pan, warm and inviting—smoky, savory, and lightly herbaceous. The golden-brown chicken and bright orange vegetables contrasted beautifully with the creamy gravy, creating a balanced visual.

This was a relaxed family-style breakfast, but the careful presentation suggested a celebration of his hands-on work. Job had prepared it with care, anticipating that a quick meal would help him focus on his upcoming repairs while sharing the warmth of home with his housemate.
