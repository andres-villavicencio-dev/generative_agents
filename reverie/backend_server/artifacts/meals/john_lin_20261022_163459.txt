John Lin prepared a comforting bowl of ginger chicken rice for his lunch break at Willow Market and Pharmacy. The dish uses boneless chicken thighs, fresh ginger, garlic, scallions, soy sauce, sesame oil, and long-grain rice, with a handful of steamed broccoli and carrots for color and crunch.

He began by thoroughly washing his hands, then rinsed and sliced the vegetables. In a small skillet, he sautéed the ginger and garlic until fragrant, added the chicken, and browned it lightly before stirring in soy sauce, sesame oil, and a splash of water. Meanwhile, he cooked the rice and steamed the vegetables until tender but still crisp. Everything was combined in a bowl, topped with scallions, and served with a small side of chili sauce.

The meal smelled warm and savory, with the sweet fragrance of ginger and toasted sesame mingling with the rich aroma of soy-braised chicken. Its simple presentation—neatly arranged rice, golden chicken, and bright vegetables—suited John’s organized nature. It was an everyday pharmacy lunch, prepared during a brief break between helping customers, and intended to give him steady energy for the afternoon.
