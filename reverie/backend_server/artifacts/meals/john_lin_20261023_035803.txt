John prepared a comforting dinner of Braised Pork with Root Vegetables and Rice. The key ingredients were tender pork belly, carrots, potatoes, celery, onions, ginger, soy sauce, sugar, and a splash of rice wine.

He marinated the pork in soy sauce, honey, ginger, garlic, and a little oil until the skin became slightly crisp. He seared the pork quickly, then added the vegetables to the pan and let them simmer until they absorbed the savory sauce. The aromas filled the kitchen: the rich scent of soy, the warm smell of caramelizing pork, and the sharp, fresh note of ginger.

When it was ready, the vegetables were plated and the rice was portioned, creating an inviting centerpiece with colorful shapes and a golden, glossy appearance. He set the table with a simple dinner plate, a bowl of rice, and a side of steamed vegetables.

The occasion was a quiet evening after a busy day of running the Willow Market and Pharmacy, with John, Mei Lin, and Eddy Lin returning home to relax and eat together.
