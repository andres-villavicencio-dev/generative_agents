John Lin prepared Chicken Stir-Fry with Vegetables and Brown Rice, a simple, nourishing lunch suited for an early morning at the Willow Market and Pharmacy. The main dish features tender chicken breast marinated in soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a little oil, then stir-fried until golden and cooked through. He added crisp carrots, bell peppers, snap peas, and onions, stirring them until they softened and the chicken was evenly heated. The brown rice was rinsed and sautéed alongside the vegetables, adding a nutty texture and hearty flavor.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of ginger, garlic, and caramelized chicken as he tossed the ingredients together. The vibrant colors of the vegetables contrasted beautifully with the rich brown of the rice and the golden hue of the cooked chicken, making the meal visually appealing and comforting.

He plated the dish in a shallow white bowl, arranging the vegetables neatly around the rice and placing a small piece of chicken in the center. It was a casual, practical lunch taken for a routine morning at the pharmacy, balanced with the family dinner he had planned for later.
