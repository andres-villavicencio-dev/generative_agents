John prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry for dinner on Wednesday, February 15. He began by washing and chopping fresh chicken breast, carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, and ginger, then sautéed them in olive oil with a splash of soy sauce and a few drops of sesame oil. He stirred the vegetables until they turned slightly opaque, added the chicken, and cooked it until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were crisp-tender.

The aroma was warm and comforting, with the savory scent of chicken, garlic, ginger, and vegetables filling the kitchen. He served the stir-fry over steamed jasmine rice, garnishing it with chopped green onions and a tiny drizzle of sesame oil. The presentation was simple yet inviting: colorful vegetables arranged alongside the glossy chicken, with the white rice adding contrast.

It was a quiet Wednesday meal, prepared after a busy day of pharmacy work and online classes. John chose the dinner to keep his mind focused and his body nourished, using the relaxed evening to reflect on the people he helps and the community he loves at Willow Market and Pharmacy.
