On Wednesday, February 15, John Lin prepared a simple yet comforting Chicken and Vegetable Vegetable Soup with Toasted Nuts for lunch at Willow Market and Pharmacy. The small portion of protein included a tender chicken breast and a handful of nuts, while the side consisted of colorful vegetables such as carrots, broccoli, and bell peppers.

The chicken was sliced and marinated in a mild garlic-herb broth before being slowly simmered until it was fully cooked. It was served alongside a bright, crisp vegetable soup, with the nuts added at the end to preserve their texture. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, fresh herbs, and the savory scent of chicken.

John arranged the meal in a white bowl, placing the golden chicken pieces alongside the vegetables and nuts. The presentation was clean and inviting, with the warm broth reflecting the gentle light of the market entrance. He served the soup and small protein portion to a customer who was looking for a quick, nutritious lunch, making it feel both practical and welcoming. The occasion was simply a quiet lunch break during his busy day of shopping and helping customers, reflecting his patient, kind, and helpful nature.
