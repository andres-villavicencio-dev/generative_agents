John Lin prepared a comforting bowl of Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry. He sliced chicken breast and carrots, then tossed them in a small bowl with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a splash of sesame oil. He added frozen broccoli, snap peas, baby carrots, and bell peppers to the pan, stir-frying everything until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were crisp. He finished the dish with a handful of brown rice.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, sesame seeds, ginger, and savory poultry. He arranged the colorful vegetables and golden chicken over the rice, garnishing it with sesame seeds and a little fresh cilantro. The presentation was simple yet inviting—each ingredient was evenly distributed and the colors contrasted beautifully.

He made the meal for lunch on Monday, February 13, while preparing for his regular shift as a pharmacy shop keeper. He wanted a quick, balanced meal that would keep him energized during the long day of helping customers and keeping up with his online pharmacy training.
