John prepared a simple but comforting chicken and vegetable stir-fry for lunch. He used boneless chicken breasts marinated with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a little cornstarch. He sliced carrots and bell peppers, then stir-fried them in a pan with a splash of oil. The chicken was added last to keep it tender, while the vegetables were cooked until crisp-tender. He finished the dish with a generous drizzle of sesame oil, chopped green onions, and a small amount of soy-sugar glaze.

The kitchen smelled warm and savory—ginger and garlic mingled with the rich aroma of the chicken and slightly caramelized vegetables. He plated the stir-fry in a shallow white dish, arranging the chicken around the sides and scattering the vegetables neatly at the bottom. Each slice of chicken was garnished with a few green onions, and the dish was presented alongside a small bowl of steamed rice.

He prepared the meal on a Monday evening while gathering information for the local mayor election. The lunch served him a quiet moment of organization and preparation before heading home to prepare for the next day’s work at the Willow Market and Pharmacy.
