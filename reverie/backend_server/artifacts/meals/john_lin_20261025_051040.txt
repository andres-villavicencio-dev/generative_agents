John prepared Chicken and Broccoli with Rice for breakfast. He sliced a chicken breast, marinated it in soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a splash of oil, then pan-seared it until golden. He added fresh broccoli florets and cooked them with the same aromatics, then finished the dish with fluffy rice, a dollop of yogurt, and a sprinkle of sesame seeds.

The kitchen filled with the inviting aroma of garlic, ginger, roasted chicken, and steaming rice. John plated the breakfast neatly in a white bowl, arranging the chicken, vegetables, and rice in an orderly pattern and adding a small spoonful of soy sauce and a sprig of cilantro for a fresh finish.

It was a simple, comforting breakfast for a busy Tuesday, but John also made it a welcoming gesture for Mei Lin and Eddy. After cooking, he prepared a small side of fruit and toast, intending to serve them all and drive Eddy to school.
