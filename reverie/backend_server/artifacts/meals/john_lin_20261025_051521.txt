On Tuesday, February 14, John prepared Golden Eggplant and Herb Rice for breakfast. He used ripe eggplant, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, and fresh cilantro, along with a small portion of rice and a spoonful of sweet chili sauce.

The eggplant was sliced into thin rounds and lightly stir-fried, then added to a warm pan with the aromatics and cooked until soft and golden. The sauce was simmered briefly to coat the eggplant, and sesame seeds were sprinkled on top before a final stir.

The dish had a warm, savory aroma of garlic, ginger, and toasted sesame oil. John presented it on a small white plate, arranging the eggplant in a neat row and scattering the rice and garnish around it. The bright green herbs and glossy sauce made the meal look inviting, and he served it to himself with a glass of water.

It was a simple, comforting side dish designed to accompany John’s eggs and toast. He prepared it for his own breakfast, but the care with the ingredients and presentation also gave the meal a welcoming, almost ceremonial feel.
