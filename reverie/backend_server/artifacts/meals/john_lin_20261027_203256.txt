John Lin prepared a simple, comforting breakfast of oatmeal with banana slices for Wednesday, February 15. The dish was made by boiling oats in water with a little milk until the mixture thickened, then adding warm banana slices before serving. He also toasted a few slices of whole-grain bread and toasted a small pan of buttered nuts for a quick side.

The aroma was warm, nutty, and sweet—like toasted bread, ripe bananas, and the gentle scent of cinnamon. John plated it neatly: the oatmeal in a white bowl, banana slices arranged beside it, buttered bread on a small plate, and a small dish of the toasted nuts. It was an easy, home-cooked breakfast for a busy morning, suitable for his work at the Willow Market and Pharmacy.
