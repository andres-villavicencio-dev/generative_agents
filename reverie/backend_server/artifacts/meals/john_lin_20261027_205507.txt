John prepared a simple Oatmeal and Banana-Slice Breakfast for his wife, Mei. He chose gentle, naturally sweet oats and a ripe banana, using just a little salt and a pinch of cinnamon to balance the mild sweetness. He cooked the oats in a pan with a splash of water until they softened, then set aside a small portion of the warm oatmeal for serving.

The banana was split into soft slices and placed beside the bowl. John arranged them carefully on a white plate, adding a small spoonful of oatmeal, a dash of cinnamon, and a few berries for color. The meal smelled warm and comforting, with the scent of toasted oats and ripe bananas filling the small kitchen.

It was a quiet, practical breakfast intended to make the busy morning easy. Measuring the oatmeal, selecting the right banana, and preparing the presentation reflected John’s patient, organized nature. He intended to enjoy it alone, offering a peaceful start to his Wednesday at the Willow Market and Pharmacy.
