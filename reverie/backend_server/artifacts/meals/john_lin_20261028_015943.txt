John Lin prepared a simple, comforting bowl of oatmeal with milk and a few slices of banana. The key ingredients were rolled oats, water or milk, a banana, a pinch of salt, and optionally a small knob of butter or honey. He began by rinsing his hands and setting out a small bowl, then cooked the oats in a pan over medium heat until the liquid absorbed and the oats were soft. He added the sliced banana and a little honey for a subtle sweetness, stirring until the mixture thickened and appeared creamy.

As he prepared it, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of oats, milk, and ripe banana. He presented the dish in a shallow bowl, placing a bright orange slice on top. The presentation was clean and inviting, resembling a small, home-style breakfast with a hint of sweetness.

The meal was his morning breakfast at the Willow Market and Pharmacy, on Thursday, February 16. He prepared it calmly and deliberately, balancing the quality of the banana and oatmeal with the speed of service he expected to give his customers.
