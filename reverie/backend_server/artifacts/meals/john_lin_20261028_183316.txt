John prepared a Morning Vegetable Porridge with Steamed Fish and Brown Rice, a traditional breakfast for his family at the Willow Market and Pharmacy.

The porridge was made from rice, chicken stock, chopped carrots, green beans, mushrooms, and a touch of ginger, simmered until the grains were soft. He steamed a whole fish over the porridge and added brown rice and scallions for a balanced, nourishing meal. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of boiling broth, toasted rice, and gently sizzling fish.

The presentation was simple and inviting: the fish rested beside the porridge, brown rice was arranged neatly in the center, and fresh scallions, carrots, and mushrooms were placed around the plate. It was a quiet, family breakfast on Monday, February 13, as John shared the meal with his wife, Mei Lin, and son, Eddy, while taking an online class about local pharmacy innovations.
