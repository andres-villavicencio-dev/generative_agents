John prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Glazed Sesame Sauce for his evening dinner at home, while Carmen checked the seasoning and temperature. The dish included chicken breast, broccoli, bell peppers, carrots, onions, and snap peas, all gently stir-fried with soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and a little sugar. The sauce gave the food a warm, savory aroma, while the bright colors and tender vegetables added a cheerful visual appeal.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the colorful pieces were arranged on a white plate with the sauce drizzled over the top, and a small bowl of sesame seeds or chopped scallions served as a garnish. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday family evening—John, Carmen, and Carlos shared a meal to relax after balancing his pharmacy work with restocking and preparing food together.
