On Tuesday, February 14, John Lin prepared a comforting Pan-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy for dinner with his wife, Mei Lin, and son, Eddy. The meal was a wholesome family supper to celebrate his connection to his family and local community.

Key ingredients: chicken breast, carrots, potatoes, onion, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a savory herb-based gravy.

Preparation method: The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, and thyme, then pan-seared until golden. It was transferred to a roasting pan and roasted with the vegetables until tender and the skin crisped. The chicken was removed, and the pan was filled with browned fat and liquid to make a rich, glossy gravy. All ingredients were returned to the pan, and the mixture was gently simmered until thoroughly cooked.

Aroma: Warm, savory, and herbaceous, with notes of roasted poultry, caramelized onions, garlic, thyme, and a hint of rosemary.

Presentation: The dish was arranged in a shallow roasting pan, with the golden chicken, crisp vegetables, and creamy gravy visible beneath. A sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge were placed on the side, adding a fresh touch.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday dinner, balancing John’s pharmacy responsibilities with his care for his family. Its simple warmth reflected his patient, kind nature and his ongoing support of Mei Lin, Eddy Lin, and their family life.
