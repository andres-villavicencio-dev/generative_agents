On Tuesday, February 14, John Lin prepared a simple yet comforting dinner with Garlic Roast Chicken and Root Vegetables. He used chicken thighs, baby potatoes, carrots, and onions, seasoned with olive oil, smoked paprika, garlic, salt, and pepper. The chicken was roasted until golden and tender, while the vegetables became soft and slightly caramelized. John added a handful of fresh parsley and a few lemon slices for brightness.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and savory poultry. John arranged the colorful vegetables and golden chicken on a platter and garnished it with a drizzle of lemon juice and a few sprigs of parsley. The meal was served at home for dinner with his wife, Mei Lin, and son, Eddy Lin. John’s patient, organized nature made the process easy and enjoyable, while the gentle flavors reminded him of the peaceful home life he shared with his family.
