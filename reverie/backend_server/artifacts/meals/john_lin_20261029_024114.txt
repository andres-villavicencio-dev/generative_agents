John prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry for Carmen and Carlos, a simple, comforting dinner suitable for a Tuesday evening at the Willow Market and Pharmacy.

The dish features tender chicken breast, broccoli, bell peppers, snap peas, and carrots, all seasoned with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a hint of five-spice. John stir-fried the ingredients quickly in a wok over medium heat, adding the aromatics toward the end so the flavors remained bright and fresh. The vegetables softened while the chicken remained juicy and slightly browned, creating a colorful, balanced plate.

Aromatic notes of ginger, garlic, and warm spices filled the kitchen as John stirred the wok. The final presentation was casual but inviting: the vegetables were arranged around the plate with the chicken centered at the top, and the dish was finished with a small piece of fresh corn. It was prepared for Carmen and Carlos to celebrate their anniversary, offering a relaxed, homey meal after John’s pharmacy work and walk.
