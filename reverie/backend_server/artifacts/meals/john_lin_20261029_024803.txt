John Lin prepared Chicken and Rice with Green Beans for a simple Tuesday dinner with Mei Lin and Carlos. He sliced bone-in chicken, marinated it in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and a little honey, then roasted it until the meat was golden and tender. The rice was seasoned with soy sauce, garlic, and ginger, while green beans were stir-fried with a splash of oil and served alongside the meal.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of garlic, ginger, caramelized chicken, and savory soy. John arranged the food neatly on a small white plate, placing the chicken in the center, the steaming rice around it, and the bright green beans on the side. The presentation was simple, warm, and inviting, with the golden chicken creating a soft contrast against the pale rice and vivid green vegetables.

This was a quiet, comforting family dinner for the Tuesday evening, intended for Mei Lin, Carlos, and John and Eddy. It offered a relaxed opportunity to prepare together, share a meal, and enjoy the company of their family before heading to the library.
