John prepared a comforting Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast Bowl for a quiet morning with Carmen and Carlos. The dish featured tender roasted chicken, steamed broccoli, bell peppers, carrots, and brown rice, seasoned with garlic, lemon, soy sauce, and a little sesame oil. He sliced the chicken into bite-sized pieces and pan-seared it until golden, then added the vegetables to the pan just before serving for a bright, colorful presentation.

The aroma of roasted garlic, savory chicken, and citrus filled the kitchen as he tossed the vegetables with lemon and soy sauce. The warm, fragrant food was arranged on a white plate with a small bowl of warm soy-sauce rice beside it, creating a simple but inviting breakfast. He prepared the meal to share with Carmen and Carlos, offering each of them a portion while they enjoyed the hearty, nourishing meal together on a Wednesday morning.
