On Wednesday, February 15, John Lin prepared a Miso-Glazed Carrots and Brown Rice Bowl, a warm, comforting family meal. He used finely diced carrots, a generous handful of brown rice, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, garlic, and a little warm miso paste, along with a handful of sesame seeds and fresh green onions.

John began by washing and slicing the carrots, then sautéed them in oil until they turned slightly crisp. In a small pan, he seared the brown rice with a little butter and seasoning, then added the miso-glazed carrots. He cooked everything together over medium heat until the rice absorbed the sauce and the carrots softened. He finished it with a final dash of soy sauce, a sprinkle of sesame seeds, and a few green onions.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm sesame, savory soy, and sweet ginger. John presented the bowl in a simple white ceramic dish, adding a small bowl of steamed broccoli and a side of pickled radish. The crisp-rice-and-carrot contrast offered a bright, satisfying balance.

The occasion was a relaxed Wednesday dinner for John, Carmen, and Carlos, with the family gathering around the table. The meal was both nourishing and practical, reflecting John’s role as a pharmacy shopkeeper and his dedication to making everyday meals feel welcoming and purposeful.
