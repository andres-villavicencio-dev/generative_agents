On a Wednesday morning, John prepared a simple Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with garlic, ginger, and a touch of soy sauce for dinner with Carmen and Carlos. He sliced chicken breasts into bite-sized pieces, chopped carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, and onions, and tossed everything with the aromatics in a wok. The chicken was lightly marinated in soy sauce, oil, and garlic, then stir-fried until golden and cooked through. The vegetables were added at the end, just before the sauce reduced, so they remained crisp.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, ginger, and fresh vegetables. The final dish was plated on a white ceramic plate, with golden chicken pieces, colorful vegetables, and a glossy sauce. The presentation was clean, appetizing, and practical for sharing. It was a cozy family meal, a quiet contrast to John’s busy pharmacy work, and a thoughtful way to spend time with Carmen and Carlos.
