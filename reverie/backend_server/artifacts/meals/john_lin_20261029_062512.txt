John prepared a simple Garden Vegetable Stir-Fry with brown rice. The pan began with sliced carrots, bell peppers, snap peas, and onions, sautéed until slightly caramelized. He added a can of plum tomatoes, a handful of spinach, and a few cloves of garlic, then stir-fried everything briefly with a pinch of soy sauce and a splash of water. The rice was prepared separately, rinsed, and cooked until the grains were tender but still firm.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of carrots, sweet onion, garlic, and the earthy aroma of the tomatoes. When the pan was ready, the vegetables were tossed into the rice, completing the meal with a light, savory finish. John plated the dish in a shallow bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables neatly around the rice and adding a small side of fresh herbs.

He prepared it for dinner on March 16, 2025, for Carmen and Carlos. The easy, comforting meal felt like a quiet reminder that his busy work at the Willow Market was balanced by family time.
