On Wednesday, February 15, John Lin prepared Chinese Char Siu Chicken with Sweet Potato and Jasmine Rice for dinner with Carmen and Carlos. The dish featured tender strips of chicken, soft sweet potatoes, glossy charred carrots, and the aromatic herbs of garlic, ginger, and five-spice powder, all simmered in a savory soy and honey glaze.

The chicken was seared in a hot pan until golden, then transferred to a pot where it finished cooking in the sauce, infused with the warm, earthy aroma of caramelized garlic and ginger. John turned off the heat and carefully plated the chicken over the remaining sauce, arranging the bright orange vegetables beside it. He added a generous mound of fluffy jasmine rice and served the meal with a small bowl of pickled vegetables.

The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the glossy chicken, vibrant vegetables, and warm rice creating an appetizing contrast. John prepared the meal in about an hour, balancing his pharmacy duties with helping Carmen and Carlos create a comforting, family-style dinner. It was a quiet, grateful occasion—a reminder that purposeful evenings could be shared with loved ones.
