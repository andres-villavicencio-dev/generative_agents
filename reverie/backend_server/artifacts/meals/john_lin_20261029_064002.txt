On Wednesday, February 15, John prepared a family-style dinner to help Carmen and Carlos prepare for dinner. The meal was Slow-Cooked Beef Stew with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Basil Sauce.

Key ingredients included a 2-pound beef chuck, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, garlic, ginger, tomatoes, chicken stock, and fresh herbs. John gently sautéed the aromatics and vegetables, browned the beef, then added the stock, herbs, and a splash of lemon juice. The stew simmered on low heat for three hours, allowing the meat to become tender and the flavors to meld.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and comforting: rich beef, caramelized onions, garlic, ginger, and earthy tomatoes gave it a cozy, homey scent. John plated the stew in shallow bowls, garnishing each with a sprig of parsley and serving a small side of steamed vegetables and lemon slices.

The occasion was an informal family dinner after Carmen and Carlos had prepared the ingredients together. John helped with the seasoning and plating, and the group shared the meal, laughing and talking about their day. The relaxed atmosphere made the meal feel special, balancing work with family time.
