On Wednesday, February 15, John Lin prepared a simple but comforting family-style meal for Carmen and Carlos while helping them balance their dinner. The dish was Garlic Herb Chicken and Asparagus with Roasted Potatoes, a classic home-cooked family dinner.

It featured boneless skin-on chicken thighs, fresh asparagus spears, and crisp potatoes seasoned with garlic, olive oil, lemon, salt, and dried herbs. John sautéed the chicken until golden, tossed the asparagus with a little olive oil, salt, and pepper, and roasted the potatoes until their skin was crispy and the flesh was tender. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic and herbs.

The meal was presented on a large family platter with the chicken arranged on the center plate, vegetables arranged around it, and golden potatoes around the edges. It was ready for Carmen and Carlos to taste and help season, and it served as a relaxed meal to celebrate their Wednesday at the Willow Market and Pharmacy.
