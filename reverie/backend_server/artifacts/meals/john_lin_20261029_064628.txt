John Lin prepared a comforting Thai Coconut Chicken Curry, a warm, savory dish that reflected his patient, homey style. The meal featured tender chicken, coconut milk, carrots, bell peppers, onions, tomatoes, Thai basil, and a touch of ginger. John marinated the chicken overnight in coconut milk, garlic, and lime, then sautéed the vegetables before simmering the curry until the chicken was cooked through. He added fresh basil at the end to preserve its bright aroma.

The kitchen filled with the rich scent of coconut, garlic, roasted vegetables, and herbs. John arranged the colorful curry in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with sliced ginger, Thai basil, and a few fresh red chilies. A small plate of jasmine rice and a bowl of steamed vegetables accompanied the dish, while a simple glass of lemon-lime drink was placed nearby. The occasion was a relaxed Wednesday dinner for John, Carmen, and Carlos, celebrating the balance between his pharmacy work and caring for his family. The meal felt practical and inviting—warm, familiar, and perfectly suited to a day of shared meals.
