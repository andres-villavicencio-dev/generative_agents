On Wednesday, February 15, John Lin prepared a simple family dinner for Carmen and Carlos—a warm Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice. The dish was made with chicken thighs, broccoli, snap peas, carrots, bell peppers, and garlic in a savory soy-ginger-sesame broth. John marinated the chicken briefly, then seared it in a pan until it was golden and cooked through before adding the vegetables. The aromatics released their fragrance as the pan sizzled, filling the kitchen with a rich, savory aroma.

The stir-fry was tossed with soy sauce, sesame oil, and chopped green onions, while the leftover chicken was shredded and served over fluffy white rice. It was presented in a shallow white bowl, arranged neatly, with the vegetables bright green against the creamy chicken and golden rice. The meal was prepared for a casual family dinner, reflecting the relaxed, purposeful routine John was balancing with his pharmacy work and caring for his wife, Mei Lin, and son, Eddy Lin.
