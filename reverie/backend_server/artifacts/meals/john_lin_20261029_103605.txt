For a warm Thursday evening, John Lin prepared Golden Rice and Sesame Chicken Soup. The dish was made by sautéing thinly sliced chicken with ginger, garlic, and soy sauce, then simmering it with rice, carrots, and green beans in vegetable broth. A splash of sesame oil added the aroma at the end, giving the soup a nutty, savory scent.

The presentation was simple and inviting: a clear glass bowl filled with fluffy rice, golden chicken cubes, bright green vegetables, and a small mound of creamy sesame sauce. John plated the meal in the family’s warm white ceramic bowl and set the utensils beside it.

The occasion was a relaxed family meal for Carmen, Carlos, and Eddy. John wanted the food to feel comforting and encouraging, giving the children a chance to enjoy it with their parents. The meal felt practical, nourishing, and full of quiet affection.
