John prepared Garlic Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for dinner with Carmen and Carlos. For the chicken, he marinated breast pieces in olive oil, garlic, dried herbs, lemon juice, and a splash of soy sauce, then roasted them until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. He roasted carrots, potatoes, green beans, and a small portion of zucchini alongside the chicken, using a little butter and a pinch of salt to keep the vegetables bright.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted garlic and herbs. John plated the meal on a wooden board, arranging the golden chicken around a bed of colorful vegetables and serving him a small bowl of plain brown rice. He added a drizzle of fresh lemon juice and a few fresh herbs before serving.

The occasion was a quiet family dinner on Thursday, February 16, as John balanced his pharmacy work with caring for Carmen and Carlos. The meal was simple but comforting, reflecting his patient, organized nature and his desire to make cooking a shared family experience.
