John Lin prepared a simple, comforting breakfast of scrambled eggs and toast with fresh fruit. The day’s fresh eggs were whisked with a little salt and pepper, then gently cooked until the whites were set but the yolks remained soft. A warm slice of whole-grain bread was toasted, and a handful of sliced figs, banana, and a small orange added a bright, refreshing crunch.

The kitchen table was decorated with a small white plate, a cup of tea, and a glass of water. John arranged the eggs in a neat row, the toast beside them, and the fruit in colorful paper cups, creating an orderly, inviting breakfast. The aroma of toasted bread, ripe fruit, and egg milk filled the room, while the gentle clinking of plates and the soft clatter of a kettle made the morning feel warm and welcoming.

The occasion was a quiet family breakfast on Friday morning, with John helping Mei Lin and Carlos organize the kitchen and prepare the pantry. This simple meal reflected his patient, kind nature and his pride in creating nourishing food for his family, balancing his pharmacy work with the care he shares.
