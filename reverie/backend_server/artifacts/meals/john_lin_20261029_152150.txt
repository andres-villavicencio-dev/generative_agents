On a quiet Friday morning, John Lin prepared Mushroom and Creamy Chicken Frittata for breakfast. The simple yet warming dish featured chicken breast, sliced mushrooms, spinach, tomatoes, onions, garlic, and fresh herbs, all bound together with egg and a splash of cream.

He began by lightly sautéing the mushrooms and vegetables in olive oil until soft and fragrant. The chicken was then seared on a pan until golden, after which it was added to the pan along with the herbs and garlic. Spinach was wilted over the last minute of cooking. The mixture was transferred to a greased baking dish, topped with shredded cheese, and baked until the edges were crisp and the center was just set.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of butter, garlic, and toasted vegetables. When John Lin presented the frittata, it looked inviting—golden and evenly browned, with golden edges and a creamy, herb-rich center. He served it with a small side of toasted bread, offering it to his family and friends to help them rest after a long day.

The meal was meant to be peaceful and nourishing, a quiet way to prepare the kitchen before the day began. It was not an elaborate occasion, but a simple morning ritual honoring his patient, kind nature and the relationships he had built with Carmen, Carlos, Andrea, and the people who support him.
