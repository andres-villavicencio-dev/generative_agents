John prepared Sunny Sunday Supper, a comforting family-style chicken and vegetable stir-fry. The key ingredients included chicken thighs, baby spinach, carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, and garlic, with soy sauce, ginger, sesame oil, and a touch of honey for a balanced flavor.

He began by sautéing the garlic and ginger, then stir-fried the chicken until golden and cooked through. Afterward, he added the vegetables to the pan and stirred them until the broccoli was crisp-tender. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of savory chicken, caramelized vegetables, and toasted garlic.

The finished dish was presented in a shallow bowl with fresh parsley and sliced ginger on the side. It was simple, hearty, and ready for a quiet Friday evening with his family and friends.
