The meal John Lin prepared was Sunny Sunday Breakfast, a simple, comforting family brunch featuring eggs, toast, oatmeal, seasonal fruit, and a glass of warm milk.

For the breakfast, John stirred the eggs in a pan until they were set, sliced them, and served them alongside fluffy white toast. A bowl of oatmeal with cinnamon, a handful of fresh berries, and a small piece of fruit added a colorful, nourishing finish. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of fried eggs, toasted bread, and freshly baked fruit.

The meal was prepared in the early morning, with the eggs, oatmeal, and fruit ready by the time John Lin arrived at the Willow Market and Pharmacy. It was made for Mei Lin and the other customers, and John Lin prepared it with the same attentive care he gives to preparing prescriptions and organizing the pharmacy.

Served simply on a small white platter, the breakfast looked inviting and homey, with the golden toast and bright fruit contrasting against the warm tones of the pan. It was meant to be a quiet, comforting start to a Friday at home, giving John Lin and Mei Lin something nourishing to enjoy before he returned to his pharmacy work.
