John Lin prepared Sunny Sunday Supper using leftover bread, eggs, and a modest pantry of vegetables and chicken. He began by toasting the stale bread, then scrambled the eggs with finely chopped spinach, sliced onions, and a small amount of butter. The leftover bread was folded into the pan with the cooked chicken and vegetables, creating a warm, hearty dish.

Aromatically, the meal filled the kitchen with the savory scent of caramelized onions and toasted bread, a soft, golden aroma from the eggs, and the crisp fragrance of the spinach. He served the meal in a simple white bowl, presenting the colorful vegetables, golden chicken, and fluffy egg whites alongside the bread. The occasion was a quiet family Sunday dinner, a special evening for John’s wife and children.

The result was a comforting, practical supper that turned ordinary ingredients into something satisfying. As John sat down, his eyes lit up with pride, grateful that the meal reflected both his pharmacy work and his family’s love.
