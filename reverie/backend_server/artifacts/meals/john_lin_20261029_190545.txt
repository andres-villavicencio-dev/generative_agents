John Lin prepared Sunny Sunday Supper, a comforting family meal that blends the warmth of home with the practical help of his pharmacy background. The key ingredients included chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, broccoli, brown rice, olive oil, lemon, garlic, and fresh herbs.

He began by sautéing the carrots and broccoli in olive oil until they were tender, then adding the chicken and cooking it until the skin browned. The potatoes were roasted alongside the vegetables, and he simmered the rice with the chicken until fully cooked. Seasoned with salt, pepper, lemon juice, and chopped herbs, the meal smelled rich and aromatic of garlic, roasted carrots, and chicken.

The finished dish was plated generously in a shallow bowl, with the golden chicken and fluffy rice arranged around the edges. He served it with a side of warm salad, making it easy to share among family and friends. John was proud that the simple ingredients created a satisfying, nourishing meal that could be prepared while balancing his work and helping customers at the Willow Market and Pharmacy. It was the kind of warm, practical dinner he loves to create for those who support him.
