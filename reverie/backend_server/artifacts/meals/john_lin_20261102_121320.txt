Dish: “Sunny Sunday” Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast

John prepared a simple, comforting breakfast for family and friends, featuring boneless chicken thighs, sliced potatoes, carrots, broccoli, and steamed spinach. The chicken was marinated in olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, and dried herbs, then pan-seared until golden and tender. The vegetables were quickly stir-fried with garlic and butter, while the spinach and carrots absorbed the savory broth.

The aroma was warm and inviting: caramelized garlic, lemon zest, herbs, and a gentle richness from the chicken. He plated the dish in large serving bowls, arranging the colorful vegetables around the chicken and adding a small side of whole-grain toast.

The meal was prepared for a Saturday family gathering, where John wanted everyone to gather around the table with a warm, home-cooked start to the day. The practical preparation and generous portioning made the meal feel welcoming and inviting.
