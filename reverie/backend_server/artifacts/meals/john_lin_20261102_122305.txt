John Lin prepared Sunny Sunday Supper, a warm, comforting family breakfast using eggs, butter, sliced whole-wheat toast, and a small portion of fruit. The key ingredients were fresh eggs, butter, whole-grain bread, and seasonal fruit, which gave the meal a bright, wholesome appearance.

He cooked the eggs in a pan over medium heat, stirring them gently until they were fully set. Sliced toast was toasted until golden and crisp, while the fruit was lightly warmed to soften it. The dish was presented on a clean white plate, with the eggs arranged neatly in a row and a small bowl of fruit beside it. Warm butter was placed on the toast for added richness.

The aroma of the breakfast filled the kitchen—creamy, golden toast, rich eggs, and the sweet scent of fresh fruit made it feel inviting and cozy. The simple, practical breakfast was prepared for a Saturday family gathering, though John had no confirmed appointment that day. It remained a gentle way to end the day while preserving the energy and patience he brings to his pharmacy work.
