John Lin prepared Crispy Chicken and Veggie Omelet, a simple yet comforting breakfast for his family. The dish featured chicken breast, eggs, spinach, diced tomatoes, onions, and feta cheese, all seasoned with garlic, black pepper, and a little lemon. John sliced the chicken, lightly pan-fried it, then added the vegetables and eggs to a skillet. The pan was covered briefly so the eggs set without becoming rubbery, then the omelet was folded with the cheese and cooked until golden.

Aromatics filled the kitchen with the savory scent of garlic, herbs, and slightly sweet tomatoes. The omelet was presented on a white plate, garnished with fresh parsley and a few whole eggs for a rustic touch. It was ready for an early Saturday morning breakfast, a warm, family-style meal John had made after preparing prescriptions and organizing the kitchen.
