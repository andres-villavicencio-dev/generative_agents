On Saturday, February 18, John prepared Lin’s Market Family Breakfast & Evening Meal, a warm, wholesome breakfast followed by a simple Sunday supper. The breakfast included scrambled eggs, toast with honey, fruit, and fresh berries. He cooked the eggs in a pan with butter and a pinch of salt, then served the warm toast and fruit for a balanced start to the day.

For Sunny Sunday Supper, John made a comforting Golden Pantry Night featuring roasted chicken, creamy mashed potatoes, and steamed broccoli. The chicken was seasoned with garlic, herbs, and olive oil, then roasted until golden and tender. He finished the meal with the creamy potatoes and bright green broccoli, adding a drizzle of lemon-herb sauce for brightness.

Lin’s Market Dinner was a simple three-meat entrée: grilled chicken thighs, pan-seared beef strips, and roasted root vegetables, served with quinoa and a lemon-herb sauce. The flavors were savory, aromatic, and full of fresh herbs.

The meals were presented in tidy bowls and plates, with the golden chicken and golden potatoes glowing warmly against the dark background. They were prepared for a quiet Sunday evening—a simple, comforting meal for John and his family.
