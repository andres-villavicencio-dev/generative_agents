John Lin prepared Sunny Sunday Supper, a warm, comforting breakfast for Saturday. He gathered eggs, whole-grain toast, orange segments, sliced bananas, fresh berries, and a few nuts. He cracked the eggs into a small pan, seasoned them lightly with salt and pepper, and let them cook gently until the whites were set but the yolks remained soft. The toast was toasted until golden, and the fruit was scattered over it for a bright, colorful topping.

As he prepared it, the kitchen filled with the pleasant aroma of toasted bread, caramelized egg, warm fruit, and a hint of cinnamon. He arranged the eggs and toast neatly on a white plate, adding a small bowl of yogurt and a few extra berries for guests. The simple, balanced meal was presented in a clean family-style setting, with fresh fruit, nuts, and a small side of yogurt and tea ready to accompany it.

It was a quiet Saturday breakfast, designed to start the day gently and warmly. John Lin’s patient, kind nature made the meal feel inviting, while the familiar ingredients reminded him of the many people he had helped and the meals he had created for his family.
