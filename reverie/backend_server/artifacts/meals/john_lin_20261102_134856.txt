John prepared a simple, comforting Sunny Sunday Supper, a one-pan chicken and vegetable meal made for Saturday, February 18. He used bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, onions, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice, with a pinch of salt and pepper. The chicken was seared in a hot skillet until golden, then sautéed with the vegetables until tender, followed by a brief finish of chicken broth and lemon to keep the sauce bright.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, and garlic. John arranged the colorful vegetables beside the golden chicken, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a few lemon slices. He served the meal in a shallow bowl, with the broth carried in a small cup, making it easy to eat quickly while still enjoying the flavors.

This meal was prepared for a quiet Saturday morning after John checked his messages and took a few minutes to relax. It reflected his patient, kind nature and the thoughtful meals he has made for family and friends, including Sunny Sunday Supper, Golden Pantry Night, and Lin’s Market Dinner.
