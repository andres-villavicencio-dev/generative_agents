John prepared Sunny Sunday Supper, a simple, comforting lunch for his family. The centerpiece was a skillet of golden pan-fried chicken breast, steamed broccoli, sweet potato, and a spoonful of homemade garlic yogurt. He sliced the chicken, seasoned it with salt, pepper, and a little lemon juice, then pan-fried it until the skin was crisp and the meat was cooked through. The vegetables were quickly sautéed until tender, and the creamy yogurt was stirred in at the end to retain its tang.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of grilled chicken, roasted potatoes, and garlic. John arranged the skillet in a shallow dish with the vegetables and chicken, serving it in large plates with a side of crisp bread. He added fresh mint and a drizzle of lemon juice, giving the meal a bright, cheerful finish.

It was a relaxed family lunch, prepared after a long Saturday. John’s patient and kind approach to cooking reflected his pride in making nutritious, comforting meals for everyone he cared about, especially for Carmen and Carlos.
