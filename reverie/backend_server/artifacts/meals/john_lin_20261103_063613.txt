John Lin prepared Lin’s Market Dinner for Saturday, February 18, a comforting home-cooked meal to celebrate his restful Saturday while balancing his pharmacy work and family commitments.

The dish was a hearty Chicken and Vegetable Stew with Lemon-Herb Bread. Key ingredients included chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, celery, onions, garlic, bell peppers, and tomatoes, with fresh herbs and lemon juice adding brightness. John gently browned the chicken, then sautéed the vegetables and aromatics before simmering everything with broth and water until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were tender-crisp.

The aroma was warm, savory, and aromatic: the scent of caramelized chicken, earthy carrots, garlic, herbs, and a hint of lemon filled the kitchen. He plated the stew in a rustic ceramic dish and sliced the lemon-herb bread to serve alongside it, arranging the bread neatly at the side.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Saturday dinner with family and friends, offering a simple yet nourishing dinner. John’s patient, kind nature made the cooking process feel like a shared experience, and his pride in creating the meal reflected his dedication to caring for others.
