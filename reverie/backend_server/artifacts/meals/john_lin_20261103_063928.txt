On Saturday, February 18, John Lin prepared Lin’s Market Dinner, a family-style hearty dinner for the rest of the family and friends. The main dish was a creamy golden chicken and vegetable stir-fry, cooked in a pan with garlic, ginger, and a rich garlic-soy sauce, while the vegetables included carrots, bell peppers, broccoli, and snap peas. John added soy sauce, oyster sauce, a splash of chicken broth, and a little cornstarch to help the sauce cling to the ingredients. The chicken was marinated briefly in the same pan and cooked until golden, then served over the vegetables with a side of steamed rice.

The meal had a warm, savory aroma with hints of garlic, ginger, and caramelized chicken. It was presented simply on a white plate with the colorful vegetables arranged around the golden chicken, rice, and sauce, and garnished with chopped green onions and a drizzle of sesame oil. John prepared the meal himself at the family table, balancing his pharmacy responsibilities with the desire to create a comforting, home-cooked dish. The occasion was a relaxed Saturday evening, and he felt proud that the meal would bring people together.
