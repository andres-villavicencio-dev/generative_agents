On Saturday, February 18, John Lin prepared Golden Pantry Night, a simple, comforting home-cooked meal for himself and family. The dish features tender chicken thighs, carrots, onions, garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, and a touch of sesame seeds. John seared the chicken in a pan until golden brown, then added the vegetables and aromatics. He simmered everything together until the chicken was cooked through and the vegetables absorbed the savory broth.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, and soy sauce. He plated the meal in a wide ceramic bowl, arranging the golden chicken alongside the orange carrots and green onions. A small bowl of steamed rice and a squeeze of lemon completed the presentation.

John prepared it for a relaxed family dinner on Saturday evening. He had also restocked the pharmacy and labeled the prescription containers by medication and customer, helping to make the process easier and more welcoming for his customers.
