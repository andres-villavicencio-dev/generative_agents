On Saturday, February 18, John Lin prepared Sunny Sunday Supper—a comforting, family-style lentil and vegetable stew. The dish was inspired by his pharmacy work: simple, nutritious, and ready in one pot. It featured red lentils, carrots, onions, celery, garlic, olive oil, tomato sauce, salt, and pepper, with a touch of fresh parsley.

The ingredients were sautéed until soft, then simmered together for about 20 minutes until the lentils were tender and the broth had thickened. John cooked the stew in a large pot over low heat, letting the flavors release naturally. He added chopped herbs and served it over fluffy white rice. The aroma was warm, earthy, and inviting—scents of caramelized onions, garlic, and slow-cooked lentils filling the kitchen.

It was prepared as a Sunday family meal, with plenty of leftovers for friends and guests. John arranged the golden-brown rice around the rich stew in deep bowls, garnishing each plate with parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice. The presentation was cheerful and homey, with the colorful vegetables and glossy stew creating an inviting contrast.

Sunny Sunday Supper was not only a practical meal for his family and friends, but also a small celebration of the relationships he had built with Carmen, Carlos, Andrea, and the people who supported him. It was simple, satisfying, and full of warmth.
