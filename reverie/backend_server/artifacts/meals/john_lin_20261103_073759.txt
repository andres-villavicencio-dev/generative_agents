John Lin prepared Sunny Sunday Supper, a simple, heartwarming family dinner for Saturday, February 18. The meal featured golden-brown chicken thighs, creamy mashed potatoes, crisp green beans, carrots, onions, garlic, and a fresh lemon-herb dressing.

He started by cooking the chicken in oil with garlic and lemon juice until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. The potatoes were roasted with butter and herbs until they turned golden, while the vegetables were sautéed until crisp-tender. A simple garlic-lemon dressing was tossed with the chicken and potatoes to give the dish a bright, aromatic finish.

The smell of roasted chicken and garlic filled the kitchen, with hints of lemon and savory herbs drifting through the air. John plated the meal in a warm ceramic bowl, garnishing the chicken with fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon dressing. It was presented as a cozy, intimate Sunday dinner for the family, celebrating the relaxed weekend while honoring his love for sharing nourishing meals.
