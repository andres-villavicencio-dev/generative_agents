John Lin prepared a comforting Wonton Soup with Ginger-Green Onion Salad, a hearty and familiar meal for Eddy. The key ingredients included bone broth, wontons, ginger, green onions, soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, sesame seeds, and a fresh lemon wedge.

To make it, John simmered the broth until rich and fragrant, then carefully added the wontons. The soup was gently simmered until the wontons were tender, and a few final touches of soy sauce, sesame oil, and toasted sesame seeds gave it a savory finish. A crisp green onion salad was served alongside it, completing the meal’s balance of warmth and freshness.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of simmered broth, aromatic ginger, savory soy, and toasted sesame seeds. John arranged the dinner on a large white bowl, garnishing it with fresh greens, sesame seeds, and a lemon wedge. The presentation was simple, inviting, and thoughtfully balanced, suitable for a quiet Monday evening when John was preparing dinner for his family.
