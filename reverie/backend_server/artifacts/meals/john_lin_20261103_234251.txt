John Lin prepared German-Polish Potato Pancakes, inspired by his discussion with David Puskar. He used thinly sliced potatoes, onions, garlic, parsley, flour, salt, and a pinch of sugar, folded them into a crisp batter, and pan-fried them in oil until golden and fluffy. He added a little grated cheese and a dash of paprika toward the end, then served them with a bowl of warm broth.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized onions, garlic, and toasted flour. Each pancake was shaped like a soft boat and arranged neatly in the center of a platter, with a small spoonful of cheese and a drizzle of dressing beside them. It was his first dinner after a long day at the Willow Market and Pharmacy, and he shared it with Eddy, giving the meal a warm, inviting home-cooked feel.
