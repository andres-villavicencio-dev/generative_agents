For dinner, John Lin prepared a comforting Chili with Rice for his family. The meal featured a rich, red chili sauce made with tomatoes, onions, garlic, ginger, and smoked paprika, served with fluffy white rice and a handful of crisp vegetables.

He seasoned the sauce with crushed chili flakes and a pinch of cumin, letting it simmer until the flavors melded. The dish was served in simple ceramic bowls with a clear plastic cover, the vegetables arranged neatly around a generous portion of rice. John Lin arranged the food on the table with a small plate of crackers and a glass of water, making the meal feel warm and inviting.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of tomatoes, garlic, and toasted chili. This was a quiet, casual family dinner—John returned home after a long day at the Willow Market and Pharmacy, prepared the meal with patience and care, and shared it with his wife, Mei Lin, and son, Eddy.
