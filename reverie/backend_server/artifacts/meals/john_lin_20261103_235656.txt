John Lin prepared a comforting Roasted Potato and Herb Chicken for Eddy. He used a whole chicken, yellow potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, fresh rosemary, thyme, and oregano, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little lemon juice. He preheated the oven, arranged the ingredients in a baking dish, and placed the chicken in the center. After roasting until the chicken was golden and the potatoes were crisp, he sliced the vegetables and served them over the chicken with some of the pan juices.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and savory chicken. John presented the meal on a warm white plate, arranging the golden chicken, colorful vegetables, and lemon slices around it. Eddy sat at the table while John helped him eat. The occasion was a simple family dinner on a Tuesday evening, and John’s patient, kind preparation made the meal feel warm and welcoming.
