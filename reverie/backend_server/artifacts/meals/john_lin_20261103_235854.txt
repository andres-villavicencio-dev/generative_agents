John Lin prepared a comforting bowl of Chicken and Vegetable Pho with Lemon-Tahini Dressing, a warm, nourishing meal fitting his patient and home-cooking style. He used chicken thighs, bone-in beef brisket, carrots, celery, and bell peppers, then simmered them with a rich broth of chicken stock, water, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a touch of oyster sauce. He finished the dish by ladling the flavorful broth and garnishing it with fresh cilantro and scallions before topping it with the lemon-tahini dressing.

The kitchen smelled of roasted chicken, simmering broth, ginger, and warm herbs. The bowl presented as a generous, golden mound of tender meat and vegetables, with the aromatic dressing served alongside crisp pickled vegetables. The meal was prepared for a quiet evening at home, with John Lin and his wife, Mei Lin, taking a relaxed dinner break before returning to the Willow Market and Pharmacy.
