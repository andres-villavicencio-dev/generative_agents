On a Tuesday morning, John prepared a hearty Crispy Chicken and Rice Bowl for Mei Lin and their son, Eddy. The dish consisted of chicken thighs marinated in soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and lemon, then pan-fried until golden. It was served over fluffy white rice, with sliced scallions, carrots, and a small handful of steamed broccoli.

John stirred the pan over medium heat, browning the chicken until the skin was crisp and the juices fell in a golden brown layer. The sauce was thickened with a splash of cornstarch water, adding a glossy finish. Freshly chopped scallions and carrots were arranged around the plate, while the chicken and vegetables were served alongside the rice.

The aroma of toasted garlic, savory chicken, and warm soy sauce filled the kitchen, with the bright yellow of the carrots adding a cheerful touch to the meal. It was a simple, nourishing breakfast for his family, prepared after a busy morning of organizing the pharmacy and checking the local election listings before heading home.
