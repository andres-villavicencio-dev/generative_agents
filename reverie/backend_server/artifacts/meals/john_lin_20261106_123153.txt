The meal was a warm, comforting Roast Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze, prepared on Tuesday, February 14. John washed and prepared the vegetables—carrots, potatoes, and onions—peeling and cutting them carefully, then seasoned them with garlic, olive oil, rosemary, and lemon. The chicken was marinated overnight, roasted until golden and tender, and finished with a bright, citrusy glaze.

Aromatic rosemary, garlic, and caramelized carrots filled the kitchen, while the lemon glaze added a fresh zesty aroma. The vegetables were arranged around the golden chicken on a large serving platter, creating an inviting, rustic presentation. The occasion was a quiet Tuesday evening meal for John, his wife Mei Lin, and their young son, Eddy.

The meal felt both nourishing and celebratory, balancing the savory warmth of the roast with the crisp brightness of the vegetables and lemon. By 5:00 PM, the plates were set, the kitchen was tidy, and John was grateful that the day’s busy pharmacy work and family responsibilities had left enough time to prepare and serve dinner.
