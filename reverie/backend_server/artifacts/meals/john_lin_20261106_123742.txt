John prepared a fragrant Stuffed Chicken with Smoked Paprika and Roasted Vegetables for dinner. The main bird was seasoned with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and a pinch of smoked paprika, then stuffed with a mixture of rice, onions, carrots, and fresh herbs. He roasted the vegetables separately until their skins softened, then placed them around the chicken. The smoked paprika gave the dish a warm, earthy aroma, while the rosemary and lemon added a fresh brightness.

The meal was served in a shallow golden bowl, with the chicken arranged in the center and the colorful vegetables around it. John had set the plate for his wife, Mei Lin, and son, Eddy, so they could enjoy it together. On Tuesday, February 14, the occasion was a quiet family meal during his recovery from the busy pharmacy day. He had confirmed the food was ready and set out for his son, while he and Mei Lin looked forward to sharing the rare part of the village.
