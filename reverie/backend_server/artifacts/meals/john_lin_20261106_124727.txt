John Lin prepared Stew with Root Vegetables and Roast Chicken for his family. The dish included tender chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, baby potatoes, onions, celery, and a dash of garlic. John sliced the chicken and roasted it in a pan with oil, seasoning, and a little rosemary, while the vegetables cooked slowly in the same pot. A splash of chicken stock, thyme, and a pinch of black pepper gave the stew a rich, herbaceous flavor.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken and simmering vegetables. John plated the chicken alongside the vegetables and served it with crusty bread and a small bowl of salad. The presentation was simple and inviting: golden chicken pieces surrounded colorful root vegetables, with the restaurant’s logo on the platter.

On Tuesday, February 14, the dinner was for John and his wife Mei Lin, celebrating the rare part of the village where his son Eddy was visiting. The meal was a quiet, comforting occasion, and John was deeply satisfied that everything had been prepared thoroughly, especially after a full day of pharmacy work.
