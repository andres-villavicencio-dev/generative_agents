The meal was a simple, heartwarming breakfast of Chinese herbal breakfast tea with steamed tofu and boiled potatoes, served with a side of steamed buns and a bowl of hot water.

John prepared the tea first, pouring it over a small basket of dried herbs and adding a splash of water. The tofu was gently steamed until tender, then sliced and arranged neatly on a small plate. The potatoes were boiled until firm, then cut into bite-sized pieces and added to the tea. John carefully arranged the steamed buns on the table, topped them with a sprig of mint, and set the bowl of tea beside them.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of simmering herbs and toasted buns—a comforting, familiar fragrance that made Mei Lin and Eddy feel welcome. It was a quiet Tuesday breakfast, prepared for their parents’ special reunion while John prepared the rare opportunity to enjoy the city’s quiet morning.
