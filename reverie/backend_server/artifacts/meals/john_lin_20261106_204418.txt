John Lin prepared Chicken Teriyaki with Rice and Broccoli for dinner. The key ingredients were chicken thighs, broccoli, baby carrots, soy sauce, honey, ginger, garlic, and a small portion of brown rice. He marinated the chicken in the sauce mixture overnight, then sautéed the vegetables with fresh ginger and garlic until tender, followed by the chicken and a final glaze with honey and soy sauce.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of garlic, soy, and caramelized chicken. He arranged the golden-brown chicken and steaming broccoli around a platter of fluffy rice, garnishing the dish with green onions and a drizzle of sesame oil. The presentation was simple and inviting, balanced with bright colors and an attractive golden crust.

He made the meal on a Tuesday, February 14, to celebrate the upcoming local mayoral election. While he asked everyone he met who would be running for office, he also enjoyed sharing the comforting dinner with his wife, Mei Lin, and son, Eddy Lin, as a quiet moment together.
