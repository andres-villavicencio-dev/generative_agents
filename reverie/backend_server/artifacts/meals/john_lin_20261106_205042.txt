John prepared Crispy Vegetable Stir-Fry with Sesame Oil and Garlic. He sliced carrots, bell peppers, broccoli, and snap peas, then stir-fried them quickly in oil with sliced ginger, garlic, and a splash of soy sauce. At the end, he tossed the vegetables with a handful of sesame seeds.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, toasted sesame seeds, and savory vegetables. He plated the dish in a shallow white bowl, arranging the colorful ingredients neatly before the final garnish. The bright orange bell pepper and green broccoli stood out against the golden carrots and crisp green peas.

He prepared it for dinner with his wife, Mei Lin, and son, Eddy Lin, on a warm Monday evening. The simple, comforting meal was intended as a relaxing way to spend time with them before going to bed around 10 p.m.
