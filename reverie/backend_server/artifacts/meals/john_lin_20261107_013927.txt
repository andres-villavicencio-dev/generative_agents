John prepared Stir-Fried Vegetable Stir-Fry with Chicken, a simple, affordable meal he bought on Tuesday, February 14. The key ingredients were chicken breasts, broccoli, carrots, snap peas, garlic, ginger, and a few tablespoons of soy sauce and sesame oil.

He sliced the chicken, washed the vegetables, and lightly sautéed them in a wok with garlic and ginger. The chicken was then cooked quickly, with the vegetables added near the end so they remained crisp. He finished the dish with the soy sauce and sesame oil, letting the flavors meld slightly.

The aroma was savory and fresh—rich chicken, crisp vegetables, and a hint of garlic. It smelled comforting and ready to eat, especially after a busy day.

The presentation was warm and colorful: golden chicken, bright green broccoli and carrots, and neatly arranged vegetables with the sauce glistening on top. John served it with plain brown rice and a small bowl of sesame oil for dipping.

The occasion was a casual lunch on a Tuesday morning, while he was helping Mei Lin with grocery shopping. He intended to eat it quickly before heading home, and he would save the remaining ingredients for another simple meal later that day.
