John Lin prepared Chicken and Vegetable Broth with Rice, a simple, nourishing breakfast meant to energize him for work.

The dish features tender chicken thighs, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, and leafy greens simmered in a rich chicken and vegetable broth. It was seasoned with salt, pepper, and a touch of ginger, allowing the natural flavors of the vegetables to shine. The aromatics released a warm, savory aroma of carrots, celery, and ginger that filled the kitchen as he worked.

He arranged the chicken and vegetables neatly in a shallow bowl, with fluffy rice served alongside for easy eating. The presentation was warm, inviting, and comforting, with the golden chicken contrasting against the bright green vegetables and the brown of the rice.

The meal was prepared for his morning breakfast, offering him a balanced start to his day while he stood behind the Willow Market and Pharmacy counter preparing to open.
