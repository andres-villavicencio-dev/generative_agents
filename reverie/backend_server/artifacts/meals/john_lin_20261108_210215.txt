John prepared a simple Chicken and Veggie Stir-Fry for his Tuesday lunch. He used breast chicken, carrots, bell peppers, broccoli, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, chicken broth, and a little coconut milk. After washing the vegetables, he sliced the chicken, stir-fried it in a wok with the aromatics, and added the vegetables in batches, cooking until the chicken was golden and tender. He finished the dish with a splash of soy sauce, ginger, and a pinch of black pepper.

Aromatic, warm, and savory, the stir-fry released rich fragrance from the garlic and ginger as the vegetables softened. John plated it in a shallow white bowl, arranging the chicken in the center with colorful vegetables around it, garnished with green onions and a drizzle of sesame oil. It was presented neatly for a quick, healthy lunch before his busy afternoon work at Willow Market and Pharmacy and the Valentine’s Day party.
