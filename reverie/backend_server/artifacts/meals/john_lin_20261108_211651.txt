For his Tuesday afternoons at Willow Market and Pharmacy, John Lin prepared a simple, heartwarming Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry. He used chicken thighs, bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas, carrots, and a handful of mushrooms, all seasoned with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a little oil. The vegetables were stir-fried first to soften them, then the chicken was added and cooked until the meat was golden and the sauce had thickened into a glossy, savory glaze.

The kitchen filled with the appetizing aroma of toasted ginger and garlic, along with the scent of savory chicken and fresh herbs. John arranged the colorful vegetables and glazed chicken on a white rectangular platter and garnished it with sesame seeds and a few fresh mint leaves, creating an inviting presentation that looked hearty and comforting.

Occasion: The meal was prepared as a personal treat during his busy day, but its cheerful, homey style made it suitable for sharing with colleagues. He took a quick bite afterward and continued helping customers, comparing prices, and researching mayoral candidates, showing his patient, kind, and organized nature.
