John prepared a simple Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry for dinner on Monday, February 13. He cooked chicken breast with rice, broccoli, carrots, onions, and snap peas, then added a splash of soy sauce, a little sesame oil, and ginger for flavor. The vegetables were lightly sautéed until crisp, while the chicken was pan-seared to an even golden-brown.

As he plated the dish, a warm, savory aroma filled the kitchen—aromas of roasted chicken, vegetables, soy sauce, and ginger mingling with the scent of rice. He arranged the colorful vegetables neatly around a white plate, with the golden chicken sitting in the center and a small portion of steamed rice beside it.

The meal was simple and family-style, prepared for a quiet, restful evening after work. It was the right kind of comfort food for a day spent preparing medication, helping customers, and answering questions about the upcoming local mayor election.
