John prepared a family-style vegetable stir-fry with chicken for lunch on February 14. The main ingredients included cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, carrots, broccoli, snap peas, and a handful of mushrooms, along with chicken breasts, a tablespoon of soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a splash of oil.

He chopped the vegetables into small, uniform pieces and sliced the chicken into bite-sized pieces before mixing them in a wok. The vegetables were stir-fried quickly until just tender, then tossed with the chicken and seasoned with soy sauce, ginger, and garlic. John added the mushrooms at the end to keep them from overcooking.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, ginger, and vegetables cooking in the hot wok, while the bright colors of the vegetables and golden chicken created a warm, inviting appearance. John served the dish in a shallow white bowl with a small side of steamed rice.

This was a simple, nourishing lunch for John, Mei Lin, and Eddy Lin before his evening study routine, reflecting his patient and caring nature as he prepared a wholesome meal for the family.
