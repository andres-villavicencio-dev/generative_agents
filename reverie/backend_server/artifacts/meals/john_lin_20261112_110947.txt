John prepared Chicken and Vegetable Soup with Crispy Bread, a family-style lunch for Tuesday, February 14.

He began with chicken thighs, carrots, baby potatoes, onions, celery, and garlic. After scrubbing the vegetables, he washed the chicken and sliced the potatoes into uniform cubes. He pan-seared the chicken until golden, then transferred it to a large pot. The vegetables were sautéed in butter until translucent, and the chicken was returned to the pot. He added chicken broth, water, a bay leaf, and a pinch of salt, then simmered the soup for about 20 minutes. The potatoes were partially cooked through, and the soup was finished with a splash of cream.

He arranged the soup in a shallow bowl, serving each portion with a slice of crusty bread and a handful of fresh carrots. The aroma of savory chicken, roasted vegetables, and warm broth filled the kitchen, while the golden chicken and creamy soup gave it a comforting appearance.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday lunch after John had been busy with pharmacy work, online medication courses, and civic research. Sharing the meal with his wife, Mei Lin, and son, Eddy Lin, made the day feel warm, purposeful, and homey.
