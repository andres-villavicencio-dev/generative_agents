John Lin prepared Chicken and Vegetable Rice Bowl for lunch, a simple yet hearty family meal.

The dish used boneless chicken thighs, baby potatoes, carrots, snap peas, and onions. He marinated the chicken in olive oil, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and a splash of lemon juice, then seared it until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. The vegetables were lightly sautéed until their colors brightened, and a few sprouts were added to add freshness.

The aroma was warm and comforting: savory chicken, garlic, ginger, and caramelized onions mingled with the slightly sweet taste of the potatoes and carrots. He arranged the bowl in a neat row, placing the rice in the center and arranging the vegetables and chicken around it. A small plate of pickled ginger and a lemon wedge were placed beside the bowl.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday lunch, with John Lin serving it to himself and preparing it carefully to ensure every ingredient was properly arranged. It was a relaxed, family-style meal that reflected his patient and organized nature.
