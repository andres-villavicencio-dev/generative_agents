John prepared a slow-roasted vegetable stir-fry for his family dinner, including leftover carrots, zucchini, broccoli, bell peppers, and snap peas. The vegetables were sautéed in a generous amount of vegetable oil until lightly browned and tender, then stir-fried with finely chopped garlic, ginger, and a splash of soy sauce. A teaspoon of sesame oil and minced fresh ginger were added, followed by the remaining vegetables, which were finished with a little soy sauce and a pinch of sesame seeds.

The dish had a warm, earthy aroma of roasted carrots, garlic, and lightly steamed vegetables. He plated it in a shallow oval dish with a small bowl of steamed jasmine rice and a side of steamed egg and bok choy. The presentation was simple and appetizing—the golden vegetables glowed beneath the savory sauce, with the colorful egg adding a bright touch.

The meal was prepared for John and his family at home on Tuesday, February 14, while he was preparing dinner.
