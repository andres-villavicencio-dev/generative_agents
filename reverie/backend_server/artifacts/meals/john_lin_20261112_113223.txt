John Lin prepared a comforting Chinese rice bowl with beef and vegetables, which became his lunch on a Tuesday evening. The meal was easy to make, using beef tenderloin, sliced carrots, bell peppers, onions, cabbage, green onions, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, and rice.

He marinated the beef briefly, then pan-fried it until golden and the vegetables were tender, adding a generous portion of cooked rice to the bowl. He finished it with a small amount of sesame oil, chili oil, and sliced green onions, while adding a few fresh herbs for a subtle fragrance.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the scent of toasted beef and caramelized vegetables filling the kitchen. He plated the bowls neatly in a single row, arranging the rice in the center and the beef and vegetables around it. The bright green vegetables and orange peppers gave the meal a cheerful, inviting appearance.

This was John’s usual home-lunch routine, and he prepared it to tidy the kitchen afterward so the workspace remained clean and ready for the next meal.
