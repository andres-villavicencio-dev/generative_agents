On a Tuesday morning, John Lin prepared Lemon Herb Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice for breakfast. He sliced chicken breast, then marinated it in lemon juice, garlic, soy sauce, ginger, and a few chopped green onions. He pan-seared the chicken until golden and tender, then added rice and vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken and citrus. He arranged the rice in a shallow dish and topped it with the cooked chicken, garnishing it with green onions and a drizzle of sesame oil. The meal was simple, colorful, and balanced, with the bright lemon and herbs adding a cheerful, fresh flavor.

It was part of a relaxed Tuesday morning routine, with John eating alone at home. He took a few quiet minutes to enjoy the meal while considering the election, keeping his patient, kind nature and love of helping others in mind.
