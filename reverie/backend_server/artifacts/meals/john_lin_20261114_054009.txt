John prepared a comforting Garlic-Butter Chicken for lunch on his dining table. The dish was a hearty chicken-and-rice meal, prepared with aromatic, tender chicken thighs, creamy yogurt-based sauce, fresh cilantro, lime, and finely chopped red onion. He sliced the chicken into bite-sized pieces, pan-seared it until the skin turned golden, then simmered it in the creamy sauce. He added a generous portion of cooked basmati rice and finished the dish with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lime.

The kitchen filled with a rich aroma of roasted garlic, warm chicken, and toasted nuts. John arranged the chicken in a shallow bowl beside the rice and served it with a small side of steamed carrots and green beans. He placed a folded napkin with a neatly arranged fork and a glass of water on the table.

The occasion was a relaxed lunch break at the Willow Market and Pharmacy. John was washing the table and preparing it for another meal, but the warm, familiar flavors of the chicken and rice made the simple kitchen setting feel cozy and inviting.
