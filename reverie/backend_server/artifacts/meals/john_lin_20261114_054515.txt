John prepared a simple but filling Chicken and Vegetable Rice Bowl. The dish featured boneless chicken breast, cherry tomatoes, carrots, snap peas, bell pepper, and a spoonful of creamy chicken broth. He sautéed the chicken until golden, then sliced the vegetables and arranged them around a bed of steamed rice.

The aroma was warm and savory—rich with roasted chicken, caramelized carrots, and a hint of ginger. The bright orange vegetables and green peas added a cheerful visual balance, while the golden chicken gave the meal a comforting appetite-inducing warmth.

It was a quiet, comforting lunch at home on a rainy Monday. John had been preparing it for himself and his wife, Mei Lin, before returning to the Willow Market and Pharmacy to serve customers. The meal was prepared quickly, with every ingredient neatly arranged so that it looked inviting and easy to eat.
