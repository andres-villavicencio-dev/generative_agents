Klaus Mueller prepared Herb-Lime Breakfast Bowl for his Wednesday breakfast at Hobbs Café. He topped a fluffy omelet with toasted sourdough, crisp green onions, sliced cherry tomatoes, and fresh basil. A lemon-herb vinaigrette was drizzled over the bowl, and a small glass of chilled sparkling water completed the dish.

Klaus used a non-stick pan to cook the omelet, letting the eggs set before mixing in the herbs and cheese. As the pan sizzled, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of nutty eggs and herbs, with a faint citrus note from the lemon vinaigrette. He arranged the colorful ingredients in a warm, rustic ceramic bowl and added a sprig of basil for a final visual highlight.

The meal was a quiet, communal breakfast, designed to energize Klaus and the café’s early guests. It reflected his thoughtful approach to food and community: simple, balanced, and warm enough to make someone feel welcome.
