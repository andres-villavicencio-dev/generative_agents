Klaus Mueller prepared a Savory Lentil and Herb Soup with Roasted Vegetables, a simple, nourishing meal suited to his student life and research interests. The soup was made with red lentils, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, tomato, vegetable broth, olive oil, and a pinch of cumin. He roasted carrots and Brussels sprouts until slightly charred, then simmered the soup until the lentils were tender and the vegetables softened.

As he cooked, the kitchen filled with the aroma of warm lentils, roasted root vegetables, and savory herbs. He arranged the soup in a white ceramic bowl, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a bright slice of lemon. The presentation was clean and inviting, with the golden vegetables contrasting against the deep orange-red broth.

The meal was his quick lunch at Hobbs Cafe, following his breakfast. It served as a calm, practical pause before focusing on his sociology paper—reflecting his kind, inquisitive nature while giving him the energy he needed to continue his research on gentrification and community inclusion.
