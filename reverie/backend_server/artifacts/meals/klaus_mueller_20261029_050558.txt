Klaus Mueller prepared a hearty Savory Potato Pancake with Tomato and Herbs. The dish featured golden pancake-like pancakes made from potatoes, flour, eggs, milk, and a pinch of baking powder, topped with fresh tomatoes, chopped herbs, garlic, and a little cream.

He cooked the pancakes over medium heat, stirring until they puffed and turned golden, then added the tomato mixture and gently folded in the cream. The aroma was warm and savory, with the scent of garlic and herbs rising from the pan. He used a spatula to spread the pancakes evenly on a plate, arranging the tomatoes in a colorful, rustic circle around the edges.

The meal was his first lunch of the day at Hobbs Cafe. It was a simple, comforting meal designed to fuel his social work studies and support his research on gentrification.
