On Thursday, February 16, Klaus prepared Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured chicken thighs, sweet potatoes, carrots, parsnips, and red onions, seasoned with lemon, garlic, thyme, rosemary, oregano, and salt. Klaus marinated the chicken overnight, then grilled it over an open flame until the skin became crisp and the meat was tender. The vegetables were roasted until soft and caramelized, with the juices pooled around them for a glossy finish.

The aroma of saffron, roasted root notes, garlic, and citrus filled the kitchen and was particularly noticeable when Klaus opened the window. He presented the meal in a simple white bowl, arranged the vegetables in a colorful, rustic pattern, and placed the chicken in the center, garnished with fresh parsley.

He prepared the meal while working as a food station assistant, balancing meal-service organization with his research on gentrification in low-income communities. The occasion was a quiet Thursday workday, and the food reflected his kind, inquisitive nature and his desire to create nourishment that felt both comforting and socially inclusive.
