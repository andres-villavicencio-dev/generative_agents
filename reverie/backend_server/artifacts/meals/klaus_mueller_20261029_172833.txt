Klaus prepared a simple Mediterranean Vegetable Panini with Hummus and Grilled Chicken. He toasted whole-grain bread with tomato slices, spinach, cucumber, red onion, feta cheese, and a drizzle of olive oil, then added a thin slice of grilled chicken. The panini was closed, baked until the bread was golden, and served with a side of fresh salad and a bowl of hummus.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, savory chicken, and the earthy scent of herbs. Klaus arranged the ingredients on a white plate, adding feta and green onions for a colorful presentation. He planned it for a casual Friday dinner, intending to eat quickly before heading to the grocery store. The meal was relaxed and communal, reflecting his interest in sharing food and supporting the community.
