Klaus Mueller prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Herb Glaze and Quinoa Salad for dinner at Hobbs Cafe. He used carrots, sweet potatoes, Brussels sprouts, and red onions, topped them with a honey-lemon-oregano glaze, and served the dish alongside fluffy quinoa, crisp lettuce, cherry tomatoes, and fresh herbs.

The preparation began with scrubbing and thinly slicing the vegetables, then roasting them at 400°F until caramelized and tender. Meanwhile, he toasted the quinoa with olive oil, chopped garlic, and a touch of cumin, then tossed it with the salad components. He drizzled the glaze over the vegetables before serving, creating a warm, fragrant aroma of roasted root vegetables, citrus, herbs, and toasted grains.

The presentation was elegant and colorful, with the glazed roots arranged alongside the vibrant salad on a white ceramic plate, accompanied by a small glass of water. The occasion was a quiet evening of social dining at Hobbs Cafe, where Klaus shared the meal with Isabella and the other patrons, using the gathering as a chance to connect and reflect on the challenges and possibilities of gentrification.
