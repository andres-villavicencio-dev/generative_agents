For a quick, nourishing Tuesday lunch, Klaus made Quinoa and Black Bean Salad with Roasted Vegetables.

The key ingredients were cooked quinoa, canned black beans, red onion, cilantro, lime juice, olive oil, diced bell peppers and carrots, and fresh spinach. He rinsed the quinoa, rinsed the beans, and sautéed the vegetables until crisp-tender before tossing them with the beans. The lime and lemon brightened the dish, while the warm vegetables added a comforting aroma of roasted root vegetables and fresh herbs.

Klaus plated the salad in a small glass bowl and arranged the colorful vegetables around the quinoa base. He added a few sprigs of cilantro and a squeeze of lime, then served it with a simple side of grilled whole-grain bread.

The occasion was a late Tuesday lunch for his sociology class. Klaus had prepared it quickly so he could eat while working on his research paper about gentrification, reflecting on housing, displacement, and community resilience. The simple, balanced meal felt like a fitting, energetic pause before his afternoon studies.
