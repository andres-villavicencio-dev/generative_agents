Klaus Mueller prepared Savory Lentil and Vegetable Soup, a simple, nourishing meal inspired by his interest in food justice and community resilience.

The soup was made with canned or canned-and-dried lentils, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, tomato, vegetable broth, olive oil, salt, and pepper. A splash of lemon juice and a pinch of cumin added a warm, earthy finish. After sautéing the vegetables and cooking the lentils briefly, he simmered the broth until everything was tender.

The aroma was earthy, warm, and subtly herbal—like roasted root vegetables and slow-cooked lentils. He ladled the clear, fragrant broth over fluffy cooked rice, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a few crisp herbs. The presentation was simple and inviting: the colorful vegetables arranged around the rice in a shallow white bowl, with the clear broth poured gently beside it.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday dinner after his morning routine. He prepared the meal for himself before writing his research paper on gentrification, using the meal to reflect on his own needs and the possibilities of supporting a community that feels resilient. The meal was both a pause for reflection and a grounding reminder of the social fabric he was studying.
