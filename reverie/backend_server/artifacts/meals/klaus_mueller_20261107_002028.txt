The meal was a simple Kale and Chickpea Bowl with Lemon-Tahini Dressing, prepared by Klaus in his dorm kitchen to make room for his research and social work.

He washed a large head of kale, measured two cups of canned chickpeas, a cup of water, a handful of parsley, and a squeeze of lemon, then chopped them into a small bowl. He added a teaspoon of tahini, a pinch of cumin, salt, and pepper, and stirred everything together. The dressing was served over the warm kale and chickpeas, with a small bowl of whole-grain rice and a side of whole wheat toast nearby.

The aroma was fresh, earthy, and lightly nutty, with the bright lemon scent cutting through the warm aroma of the chickpeas. Klaus presented the bowls neatly on the counter, arranging the ingredients in an inviting, balanced pattern. The occasion was a quiet morning meal before beginning his day of research and reflection on gentrification and community resilience.
