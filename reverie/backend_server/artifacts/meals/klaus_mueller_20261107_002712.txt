For his Tuesday dinner, Klaus prepared Garden-Salad Bowl with Quinoa and Grilled Chicken, a nutritious, simple meal inspired by his interest in community resilience. He washed the quinoa, chicken, and vegetables, then sautéed the chicken with garlic and lemon until golden, while the quinoa was rinsed, drained, and lightly seasoned. He added chopped spinach, cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, and parsley, then topped the bowl with a lemon-herb vinaigrette. The aromatic scent of toasted garlic and fresh herbs filled the kitchen, creating a warm, hopeful atmosphere.

Klaus presented the meal in a white bowl with a colorful arrangement of the green salad, bright red tomatoes, and golden chicken, alongside a small side of brown rice and a glass of water. He served it around 5:00 PM, enjoying a quiet moment of reflection after completing his morning routine. The meal symbolized his personal care for himself and his desire to nourish his mind and community through thoughtful, accessible food.
