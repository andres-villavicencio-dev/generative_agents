On Tuesday, February 14, Klaus Mueller prepared Quinoa and Roasted Pepper Soup for lunch. He used organic quinoa, chickpeas, carrots, celery, onions, garlic, tomato, vegetable broth, olive oil, cumin, paprika, and a touch of lemon. The ingredients were chopped in a pot, then simmered until the liquid reduced and the flavors melded.

Klaus sautéed the onions and garlic first, adding the peppers, carrots, and celery, then poured in the vegetable broth and spices. He covered the pot and let it simmer until the soup was rich and the quinoa was tender. For serving, he ladled the soup into a wide bowl, topped it with fresh parsley, and arranged the quinoa in a small decorative dish beside it. A squeeze of lemon added a bright finish.

The aroma of warm broth, caramelized peppers, and toasted spices filled the kitchen. Klaus presented the meal on a simple white plate, completing the morning routine with kindness, curiosity, and care for his community. He would eat it alone, reflecting on the research paper he was writing about gentrification and resilience.
