On Tuesday, February 14, Klaus Mueller made a simple Grilled Chicken and Quinoa Bowl. He seasoned chicken with olive oil, lemon, garlic, and herbs, then cooked it quickly over the stove. Quinoa was rinsed and sautéed with onions, carrots, and a pinch of cumin before being mixed with the remaining pan juices.

The aroma was fresh and warm: the savory scent of the chicken mingled with the crisp herbs, caramelized onions, and earthy flavor of the vegetables. Klaus arranged the colorful bowl in the kitchen, placing a piece of chicken, steamed vegetables, and a bowl of cool dressing on top. He added a small sprig of parsley and a few grains of raw sea salt for a finished touch.

The meal was a quiet weekday lunch, prepared with care and a focus on nourishment and community. Klaus ate it with a fork and spoon, enjoying the balance of protein, whole grains, and vegetables while reflecting on his research paper.
