For his Monday morning, Klaus prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Herbs and Lemon, a simple, nourishing breakfast suited to his early studies and research. He began by rinsing and toasting whole-grain oats, then added water or milk. While the oats absorbed the liquid, he chopped fresh basil, chives, and a squeeze of lemon into a small bowl for the oatmeal.

He cooked the oats in a pan over medium heat, stirring occasionally until the mixture was warm and slightly thickened. Once ready, he folded in the herbs and lemon before serving. The aroma was warm and inviting—crisp oat, soft basil, and a hint of citrus made the kitchen feel cheerful.

Klaus presented the oatmeal in a clear bowl beside a small plate of whole-grain toast and a glass of water. The meal was simple yet balanced, reflecting his interest in thoughtful, everyday living. It was the first meal of his day, a quiet start to a productive Monday as he prepared for the library session and began researching gentrification in low-income communities.
