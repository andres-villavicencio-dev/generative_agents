Klaus Mueller prepared a Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Mashed Potatoes for dinner at Hobbs Cafe.

He used a whole chicken, chicken thighs, bone-in potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, onions, garlic, fresh thyme, rosemary, lemon, olive oil, and a little soy sauce. After washing and drying the chicken, he seasoned it with salt, pepper, and soy sauce, then brushed the skin with oil and rosemary before broiling it at 400°F for about 25–30 minutes until the juices ran clear and the meat was easily pulling away from the bone.

The vegetables were sliced and roasted in the same oven while the chicken finished cooking, giving the meal a warm, golden aroma of herbs, garlic, and caramelized vegetables. He plated the chicken in the center, surrounded the glazed vegetables, and topped it with fluffy mashed potatoes.

The meal was simple, filling, and comforting, fitting Klaus’s need for a relaxed, nourishing dinner while he worked on his sociology research paper. It was prepared for a quiet evening at Hobbs Cafe, where he and his friends enjoyed a meal, stories, and conversation.
