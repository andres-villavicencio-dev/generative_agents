Klaus Mueller prepared a simple Hobbs Cafe Breakfast Bowl for his early Monday morning. The dish featured fluffy eggs, scrambled with chives, toast, sourdough bread, banana slices, honey, and a splash of orange juice. He added a few fresh berries and a handful of crisp vegetables for balance.

Klaus cooked the eggs in a small pan over medium heat, then topped the warm bowl with toasted bread and fruit. He drizzled the honey over the eggs and placed the banana and berries on top. To finish, he sprinkled a pinch of cinnamon over the entire dish. The aroma was warm and inviting—a comforting blend of toasted bread, caramelized eggs, banana sweetness, and citrus.

The breakfast was presented as a colorful, neatly arranged bowl, ready for a quick lunch at Hobbs Cafe. It was his first breakfast after waking up early for his sociology research paper, making it both nourishing and a thoughtful start to his day.
