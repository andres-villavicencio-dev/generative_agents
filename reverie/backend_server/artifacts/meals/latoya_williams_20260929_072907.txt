Latoya’s dinner is a roasted vegetable quinoa bowl with lemon‑glazed chicken, served as the evening’s hearty finale before her quiet journaling session. Tender chicken breasts are brushed with a bright citrus glaze and pan‑seared until crisp, their aroma of fresh lemons mingling with caramelized onions. The bowl combines fluffy quinoa tossed with roasted mixed vegetables—sweet bell peppers, bitter broccoli, smoky zucchini—and a sprinkle of toasted almond slivers for crunch. It’s presented in a shallow ceramic dish, garnished with micro‑herbs and a lemon wedge that catches the low evening light. The meal marks both her structured routine (dinner at 17:30) and the Valentine’s Day anticipation unfolding around the city, blending nourishment with subtle celebration.
