Latoya’s quinoa‑bowl dinner is a vibrant harmony of roasted vegetables, lemon‑glazed chicken, and fluffy quinoa in a creamy cauliflower‑yogurt dressing. She first roasts cherry tomatoes, zucchini, and bell peppers until caramelized; the chicken breast is brushed with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and her signature glaze of honey and thyme. The bowl layers sautéed quinoa, the roasted medley, and a silky sauce that blends yogurt, cauliflower puree, and fresh herbs. Aromas of citrus, toasted nuts, and earthy smoke fill the kitchen. Served warm in shallow ceramic bowls, she garnishes with microgreens and a lemon wedge—light, balanced, and intimate. The meal is a quiet celebration of routine and reflection, echoing her even‑tempered Tuesday rhythm on February 21.
