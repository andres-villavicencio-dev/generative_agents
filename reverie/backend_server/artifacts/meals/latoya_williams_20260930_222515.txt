Latoya’s evening centerpiece is her “Midnight Quinoa Symphony,” a harmonious bowl of roasted vegetables, lemon‑glazed chicken breast, and fluffy quinoa, all drizzled with a bright herb vinaigrette. The dish was assembled by first tossing zucchini, bell pepper, and cherry tomatoes in the oven until caramelized, then brushing chicken thighs with lemon zest and garlic before roasting to achieve a crisp, fragrant exterior. Quinoa is steamed separately for a light base that soaks up the citrus‑herb glaze. Aromas of roasted earth and zesty lemon mingle as she plates golden quinoa at the center, artfully arranging vegetables around it, then crowns the chicken with microgreens and a final zest burst. Presented in a matte black bowl, the meal feels both intimate and celebratory—perfect for quiet reflection after her Tuesday’s rhythm of art, discussion, and photography.
