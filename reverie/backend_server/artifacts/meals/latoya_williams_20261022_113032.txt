For breakfast on Monday morning, Latoya Williams prepared warm apple-cinnamon oatmeal with blueberries and toasted almonds. She began by bringing a small pot of water to a simmer, then stirred in rolled oats, a splash of milk, diced fresh apple, ground cinnamon, and a drizzle of honey. While the mixture cooked, she toasted sliced almonds in a dry pan until golden and lightly fragrant. Once the oatmeal had thickened, she spooned it into a simple ceramic bowl and topped it with fresh blueberries, toasted almonds, and a small drizzle of honey.

The kitchen filled with the cozy aroma of cinnamon, warm oats, and toasted nuts. Its soft, creamy texture contrasted with the crisp almonds and juicy berries, making the bowl look bright and inviting despite the cool February morning. This nourishing breakfast was part of Latoya’s calm, orderly start to the day, giving her steady energy before she returned to the artists’ co-living space to work on her travel-inspired photography series.
