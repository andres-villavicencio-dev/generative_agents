Latoya prepared a golden lemon-herb chicken with roasted vegetables, a simple dinner suited to her organized, detail-oriented nature and her evening routine at the artists’ co-living space.

The key ingredients were boneless chicken breasts, baby Yukon Gold potatoes, carrots, red onion, lemon, garlic, fresh rosemary and thyme, olive oil, and black pepper. She preheated the oven, seasoned the chicken, and arranged it on a sheet pan with the chopped vegetables. Everything roasted together at about 200°C until the chicken was cooked through and the potatoes were tender, with caramelized edges.

As it finished, the kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, lemon zest, rosemary, and lightly browned chicken. Latoya plated the food neatly, placing the chicken beside the colorful vegetables and garnishing it with fresh herbs and a thin lemon slice. The golden potatoes and green rosemary made the presentation inviting and rustic.

The meal was intended as a quiet weekday dinner after her photography work. She ate around 5:30 p.m., then relaxed and discussed the upcoming mayoral candidates with friends in the communal space.
