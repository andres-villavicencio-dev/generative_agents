Latoya prepared Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa, a warm, nourishing lunch that reflected her organized, thoughtful approach to the day’s work. The savory chicken was served over a bed of fluffy quinoa, giving the meal a balanced texture and satisfying volume.

Her kitchen smelled of citrus, herbs, garlic, and roasted vegetables. The presentation was simple and inviting: the golden chicken was arranged next to vibrant vegetables, with the neatly prepared quinoa and lemon slices providing a colorful contrast. Latoya ate it in a relaxed setting with a glass of water, finishing the meal at 17:30.
