Latoya prepared a warm oatmeal breakfast with mixed berries, fresh fruit, cinnamon, and a drizzle of honey. The oats were cooked with water until tender, topped with sliced strawberries, blueberries, and a handful of raspberries, then folded into the warm grain. A small bowl of chia seeds added a subtle crunch, while a pinch of cardamom enhanced the aroma. The dish was served in a simple white bowl, with the colorful fruit arranged artfully around the oats and a cinnamon stick placed beside it.

The meal was prepared as a quiet, mindful breakfast for Wednesday, February 15. Its purposeful ingredients reflected Latoya’s analytical and organized nature, while the earthy spices carried forward the emotional connection she felt toward her community. It was neither a formal meal nor a celebratory occasion, but a gentle start to a productive day focused on photography, neighborhood engagement, and creative reflection.
