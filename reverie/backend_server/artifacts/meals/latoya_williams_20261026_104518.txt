Latoya prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and Cinnamon. She combined rolled oats, milk or plant milk, honey, cinnamon, a pinch of salt, and chopped walnuts, then stirred in sliced apples, banana, and a few dried berries. The oatmeal was cooked gently in a saucepan until the oats were soft and the mixture was creamy.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of oats, cinnamon, honey, and ripe fruit. Latoya arranged the oatmeal in a shallow bowl, placing the fruit around the edges and finishing the presentation with a small sprig of rosemary. She added a silver spoon and a decorative wooden spoon for a rustic, home-cooked feel.

The meal was prepared for her Wednesday breakfast and served at breakfast, a relaxed moment before her purposeful photo review. Its organized, nourishing nature reflected her structured approach to organizing her artwork, while the fresh ingredients and careful arrangement mirrored the neighborhood project’s emphasis on detail and community connection.
