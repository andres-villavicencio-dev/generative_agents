Latoya Williams prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and Cinnamon for breakfast on Wednesday, February 15. She stirred rolled oats, milk, and water in a small pan, then added sliced bananas, blueberries, and a pinch of cinnamon. The oats became warm and creamy as she stirred, while the fruit kept the dish bright and nutritious.

The meal was presented in a simple white bowl with a wooden spoon and a small plate of sliced apples for dipping. The warm aroma of oats, sweet fruit, and cinnamon filled the kitchen, giving the breakfast a comforting, inviting feel. This wholesome breakfast was a quiet moment of self-care before Latoya began her focused work on the corrected photo, the neighborhood-character clue, and the old oak and café display. It reflected her organized, attentive nature and her purposeful connection to the community project.
