Latoya Williams prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and Honey-Lemon Drizzle for breakfast on Wednesday, February 15. The presentation was simple yet inviting: the oatmeal was arranged on a clean white plate, with fruit and nuts scattered around it. The warm, golden oats contrasted beautifully with the bright colors of the fruit and the golden-brown walnuts.
