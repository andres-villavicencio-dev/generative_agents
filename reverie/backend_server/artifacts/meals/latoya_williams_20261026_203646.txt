Dish Name: Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and a Berry Compote

Latoya prepared a warm breakfast of oatmeal with rolled oats, warm milk, sliced bananas, blueberries, strawberries, and a drizzle of honey. She also prepared a small bowl of fruit compote using the same berries and a few pitted cherries.

The oats were cooked in a pan until the liquid reduced slightly, then blended with the warm milk and topped with fresh fruit. Latoya chopped the berries and mixed them into the compote, allowing the juices to combine with the honey. She arranged the oatmeal bowl with the fruit in alternating layers, finishing with a sprinkle of cinnamon and a few dried apricots.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the sweet scent of oats, honey, and ripe fruit. The visual presentation was simple yet inviting, with colorful fruit, creamy oatmeal, and golden caramelized compote creating a cheerful contrast. Latoya ate it at home on Wednesday, February 15, as a quiet morning reward after a purposeful day focused on photography, community, and creative detail.
