Latoya prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and Warm Nuts, a comforting breakfast for Wednesday, February 15. The dish was made in two parts: cooked rolled oats were simmered with water until thickened, then frozen bananas, sliced apples, walnuts, and cinnamon were stirred in for easy eating. Latoya topped the oatmeal with a sprinkle of crushed blueberries, honey, and a serving of freshly baked oat cookies.

She prepared it in a large bowl and a small glass, using a wooden spoon to fold the fruit and nuts over the oatmeal. The warm, cinnamon-savory aroma filled the kitchen, especially after the cooler February morning. She served it with a small glass of iced tea, creating a cozy, nourishing breakfast.

The meal was part of her early-morning routine on the Wednesday after her feedback on election materials, helping her begin the day focused, creative, and connected to her community project. It was simple, familiar, and designed to energize her morning before she began her focused work with the neighborhood photo.
