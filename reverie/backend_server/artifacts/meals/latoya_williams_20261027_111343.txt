Latoya prepared Warm Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and Walnuts, a comforting, nourishing breakfast fit for a quiet Wednesday. The dish featured rolled oats cooked in milk or almond milk, topped with sliced apples, pears, raspberries, and chopped walnuts. A sprinkle of cinnamon, a pinch of nutmeg, and a light drizzle of honey added subtle sweetness and warmth.

She prepared it in two parts: first, she cooked the oats on the stove until the liquid was absorbed, then stirred in the fruit and nuts. After letting it rest briefly to soften the fruit, she served the warm oatmeal, garnishing the dish with the colorful fruit and scattered nuts. The aroma was warm, slightly sweet, and fragrant with cinnamon and toasted nuts.

She presented the breakfast at her kitchen table, arranging the oatmeal in a simple bowl and placing the fruit and walnuts neatly beside it. The occasion was a personal morning routine: a quiet way to start her Wednesday, combining the structured attention she uses for photography with nourishment and creativity.
