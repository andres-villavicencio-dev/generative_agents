Latoya Williams prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and Honey, a comforting, nourishing breakfast that reflected her organized and attentive nature. She began with a small bowl of warm oats, adding rolled oats, milk or oat milk, cinnamon, and a drizzle of honey. Fresh berries—strawberries, blueberries, and red grapes—were arranged around the bowl, their bright colors contrasting with the creamy oatmeal. A slice of whole-grain toast and a few crunchy nuts were placed beside it.

She stirred gently, letting the oats soften while the cinnamon infused the warm milk with a fragrant aroma of caramelized grains, berries, and toasted nuts. The meal was presented on a simple white plate, with the colorful fruit arranged artfully around the oats and a small wooden spoon for easy eating. It was a quiet, purposeful breakfast prepared for a quiet Wednesday morning, combining warmth with the attention to detail Latoya brings to her photography and neighborhood project.
