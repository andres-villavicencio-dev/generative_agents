Latoya Williams prepared a warm oatmeal breakfast for herself on Wednesday, February 15. The dish was Warm Oatmeal with Wild Blueberries and Honeycomb.

She began by boiling water and simmering oats until tender. While the oatmeal cooked, she chopped fresh wild blueberries, sliced honeycomb, and added a few walnuts for texture. The warm, aromatic steam filled the kitchen as the oats absorbed the sweet honey and fruit.

She spooned the oatmeal into a small bowl, topped it with the glossy blueberries, and arranged a small piece of honeycomb beside them. The bright blue and amber colors contrasted beautifully with the golden oatmeal, making the presentation both inviting and visually balanced.

This was a simple, nourishing breakfast for a quiet Wednesday, combining the comfort of familiar ingredients with the organized care Latoya brings to her photography and daily life.
