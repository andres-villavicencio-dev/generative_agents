Latoya Williams prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Fruit and Honey, a warm breakfast that mirrored the neighborhood project she was working on. She combined old-fashioned oats with sliced banana, blueberries, apple, and a drizzle of honey, finishing with a sprinkle of cinnamon and a few crushed peanuts.

The oatmeal was simmered gently in a small pot, then set aside to cool slightly. She cooked the fruit in a separate pan until the bananas softened, then folded the cooled oatmeal into the warm fruit mixture. A little warm milk was stirred in to give it a creamy texture. The aroma of toasted oats, sweet fruit, honey, and cinnamon filled the kitchen as she prepared it.

She arranged the oatmeal in a white ceramic bowl, placing the colorful fruit around its edges. A small spoonful of honey rested beside it, while a piece of apple and a sprig of cinnamon served as a subtle garnish. The presentation was simple but inviting, reflecting her organized and attentive nature. It was the perfect morning breakfast to start her Wednesday focused on the corrected unseen-path photo, neighborhood-character clues, and the old oak and café display.
