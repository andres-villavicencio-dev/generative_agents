Latoya prepared Chicken and Vegetable Lemon-Herb Stew, a simple yet nourishing lunch for her co-living space. She combined bone-in chicken thighs with carrots, celery, onions, tomatoes, and a savory broth seasoned with lemon, garlic, thyme, salt, and black pepper. The stew was simmered gently for about 20 minutes until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were partially softened.

The aroma rose from the kitchen as warm chicken, caramelized carrots, and aromatic herbs filled the room. Latoya plated the stew in a shallow ceramic bowl, garnishing it with a sprig of thyme and a thin slice of lemon. The warm, golden chicken and colorful vegetables contrasted beautifully with the clear broth, giving the meal a bright, homey appearance. It was prepared for a quiet Wednesday lunch, balancing her photography, civic commitments, and time spent reflecting on the artists’ co-living community.
