Latoya Williams prepared Savory Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Mashed Potatoes for breakfast at Hobbs Cafe. She combined boneless, skin-on chicken breasts with fresh lemon, garlic, oregano, and a splash of soy sauce for a savory, balanced flavor. The chicken was seared to a golden brown before finishing in the oven, while the vegetables—broccoli, carrots, and zucchini—were roasted with olive oil, salt, and pepper until crisp-tender. Mashed potatoes were finished with butter, chives, and a pinch of sugar to soften their texture.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and freshly baked bread. Latoya arranged the meal in a simple white ceramic bowl, serving the chicken in the center with a colorful medley of vegetables and fluffy mashed potatoes around it. The bright lemon color and crisp edges stood out against the creamy yellow potatoes.

The occasion was a relaxed Saturday breakfast at the cafe, intended to be comforting and nourishing. Latoya’s attentive preparation reflected her practical, organized approach to everyday life, and the meal felt both welcoming and purposeful.
