Latoya prepared Chicken and Vegetable Risotto, a comforting one-pot dinner perfect for an early Saturday morning. She started with thickly sliced chicken thighs, baby carrots, green peas, corn, chopped onions, garlic, fresh parsley, and sautéed mushrooms.

She sautéed the chicken and vegetables first, then added white rice and gradually toasted it with white wine and stock. The rice absorbed the liquid while the chicken remained tender, and the aromatics added a savory, slightly sweet scent. The mixture was gently simmered until the rice became creamy and the flavors melded.

Latoya plated the dish in a shallow bowl, arranging the golden chicken, colorful vegetables, and a generous spoonful of rich risotto. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with parsley sprinkled over the top. The aroma of warm rice, roasted chicken, and garlic filled the kitchen.

It was the simple, satisfying meal Latoya had prepared for herself on Saturday, February 18—her first full Saturday morning without a confirmed appointment.
