Latoya prepared Crispy Chicken with Quinoa and Lemon-Herb Rice. She cooked boneless chicken thighs with a secret lemon-herb glaze, toasting the chicken until crisp and the rice until fluffy. The quinoa was rinsed and seasoned with garlic, lemon zest, parsley, and a pinch of salt, while the chicken was arranged alongside the grains. A small bowl of warm lemon-tahini dressing was kept on the side.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized chicken, toasted nuts, and warm spices. Her organized approach ensured every ingredient was labeled and the recipe followed a clear sequence, creating a clean, inviting meal.

The dish was plated on a white ceramic dinner plate with the golden chicken and pale rice arranged neatly beside a small glass of water. The bright lemon-gold contrast made it look fresh and balanced. Latoya prepared it as a nutritious, satisfying light lunch, intended for a productive morning before she began her photography work from the artists’ co-living space.
