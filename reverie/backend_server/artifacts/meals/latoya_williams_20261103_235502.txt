Latoya Williams prepared a Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken with Potatoes and Garlic Bread for dinner on a Monday evening. The dish featured a whole chicken seasoned with olive oil, lemon, rosemary, garlic, and dried thyme, alongside golden potatoes and warm garlic bread.

Latoya carefully measured the cooking times for both the oven and the pan, checking the chicken several times to ensure it was fully cooked. She roasted the chicken at 400°F until the meat reached an internal temperature of 165°F, then added the potatoes and finished them under the broiler. She prepared the garlic bread in batches, keeping it warm while she checked the cooking times.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, citrus, and butter. When Latoya plated the meal, the golden-brown chicken, fluffy potatoes, and crisp garlic bread were arranged around a small white plate, with a glass of water beside it. It was a tidy, comforting family dinner, reflecting her organized and attentive nature as she prepared the meal for family members.
