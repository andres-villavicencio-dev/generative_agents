On Monday, February 13, Latoya prepared Cajun Lemon Herb Chicken with Sautéed Vegetables and Corn, a hearty family dinner inspired by her travels. For the main dish, she marinated chicken thighs in olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and a touch of cayenne before cooking them until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The chicken was served alongside roasted potatoes, carrots, and corn, with each vegetable tossed in a savory lemon-herb sauce.

The aroma was warm and inviting: savory chicken, sweet corn, caramelized carrots, and the bright scent of fresh lemon and garlic filled the kitchen as Latoya stirred the pot. The presentation was simple yet elegant—the golden chicken, vibrant vegetables, and crisp corn were arranged on a white platter beside a bowl of warm polenta and a glass of lemonade.

The occasion was a relaxed family evening on the way to the main-dish cooking station. Latoya had prepared the sides first, then returned to prepare the chicken, creating a balanced, flavorful meal perfect for sharing with her family.
