Latoya prepared a Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for lunch. The dish featured a whole chicken seasoned with lemon, garlic, rosemary, olive oil, salt, and pepper, roasted until golden and tender. Alongside it, carrots and sweet potatoes were tossed with olive oil, lemon juice, and thyme, while bell peppers and onions were caramelized in the pan.

She prepared everything at home, cooking the chicken in a large skillet and roasting the vegetables in a separate oven. The kitchen filled with a bright, citrusy aroma of lemon, garlic, herbs, and roasted vegetable sweetness, especially noticeable as the chicken began to crisp.

Latoya served the meal in a simple white bowl with a light lemon dressing and a side of green salad. The presentation was clean, vibrant, and homey, with the golden chicken and colorful vegetables creating an inviting visual. It was a relaxed, nourishing lunch that balanced her photography work and family commitments without requiring an expensive outing.
