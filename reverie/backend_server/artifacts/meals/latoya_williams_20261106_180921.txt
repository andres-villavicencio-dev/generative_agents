On Monday, February 13, Latoya Williams prepared Hobbs Cafe’s “Morning Brew” breakfast drink, a refreshing batch of oatmeal, lemon-lime iced tea, and a small slice of warm banana. She chose a neutral ceramic mug and a stainless-steel pitcher to keep the drinks clean and hygienic.

The ingredients included rolled oats, milk, water, fresh lemon slices, lime wedges, banana, honey, and a pinch of cinnamon. Latoya soaked the oats briefly, then simmered them in the pitcher with the milk and water. After cooking, she drained the liquid and layered the warm banana with the chilled tea. The final drink was served in a colorful, insulated cup, topped with a drizzle of honey and a fresh mint sprig.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm oat, sweet banana, citrus, and a hint of cinnamon. She arranged the drinks neatly on a small wooden board, garnishing each with a lemon wedge. The occasion was her work shift at Hobbs Cafe, where she was preparing the next batch of breakfast drinks before serving customers.
