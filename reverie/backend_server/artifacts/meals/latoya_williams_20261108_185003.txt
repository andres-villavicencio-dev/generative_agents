Breakfast: Fruit Yogurt and Whole-Grain Toast

Key ingredients: whole grain toast, plain yogurt, sliced fresh fruit (apples, berries, and banana), honey, and a pinch of cinnamon.

Preparation method: Latoya spread a thin layer of honey on the warm, buttered toast. She arranged the fruit around the edges, adding a small piece of banana in the center. She stirred the yogurt and carefully drizzled it over the toast, then sprinkled cinnamon on top. The warm pan of fruit was placed nearby to keep the aroma alive.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the rich, earthy scent of honey and toasted grain, softened by the citrusy notes of the fruit. Cinnamon added a gentle warmth, while the bright scent of the fruit made the space feel inviting and fresh.

Presentation: The meal was arranged on a white plate with the toast and fruit arranged in a small, rounded cluster. The yogurt was placed in the center, creating a balanced, colorful composition. A small glass of water and a cinnamon stick completed the simple, elegant display.

Occasion: It was a quiet Tuesday breakfast before Latoya continued her travel and election photography series. The purpose was to nourish herself and prepare for a productive day of capturing the morning light and her creative vision.
