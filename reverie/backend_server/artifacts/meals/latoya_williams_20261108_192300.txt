Latoya Williams prepared a quiet, minimalist breakfast inspired by her photography’s attention to light and warm domestic scenes. The dish was a pan-seared egg with a lemon-and-chive crisp, served alongside a cup of freshly brewed coffee and a small glass of water.

She began by whisking the eggs in a small bowl, then searing them in a pan with a little olive oil, salt, and pepper. The crisp was prepared by slicing thin strips of onion, garlic, and apple, pan-frying them until golden and slightly caramelized. After flipping the eggs, she drizzled them with fresh lemon juice, cracked a few black pepper flakes over the pan for aroma, and added a final squeeze of lemon zest.

The meal was served in a simple white ceramic bowl, with the eggs arranged neatly beside the crisp and a tiny spoon of honey. The kitchen window caught the soft early-morning light, giving the scene a calm, inviting quality. It was a thoughtful, nourishing breakfast prepared for Latoya’s quiet Tuesday—the morning before the clinic post and the local mayor’s election, where she needed steady energy and a warm start.
