Latoya prepared a simple, nourishing breakfast lunch inspired by the quiet morning light in her kitchen. She used whole-grain sourdough bread, freshly roasted coffee beans, eggs, and milk. The eggs were browned in a nonstick pan until golden, then poached in the milk with a splash of cream and a pinch of sea salt. A drizzle of olive oil and fresh herbs finished the dish.

Aromatic, earthy roasted coffee filled the small kitchen, while the warm kitchen window created a cozy, inviting mood. Latoya arranged the bread, eggs, and coffee cups neatly on a white plate, adding a small glass of water and a sprig of mint. The presentation was clean and elegant, emphasizing the fresh ingredients and the gentle morning light.

This was a quiet, mindful lunch for a productive Tuesday. After finishing the clinic post, Latoya wanted to feel relaxed and connected, so she prepared something warm and simple to enjoy as she continued working on her photography series about the local mayor’s election.
