Latoya prepared a simple panini with roasted chickpeas and spinach, a hearty breakfast lunch that balanced her morning routine with an energizing breakfast-style meal. The key ingredients were whole wheat bread, roasted chickpeas, fresh spinach, feta cheese, a drizzle of olive oil, and a pinch of red pepper flakes.

She toasted the bread until golden, added the spinach and chickpeas to warm up, then folded in the feta. The panini was grilled until the edges were crisp and the bread was lightly toasted. The warm kitchen window added a soft amber glow, creating a cozy, inviting aroma of herbs, garlic, and toasted bread.

She presented the panini on a white plate with a small side of hummus and sliced tomato. It was simple, visually appealing, and ready to eat. The occasion was her Tuesday morning routine: waking up, completing the morning routine, and preparing a nutritious lunch at home.
