Latoya Williams prepared a Herb-Roasted Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice and Veggies for lunch on February 14. The dish began with a whole chicken seasoned with garlic, rosemary, thyme, paprika, and black pepper. It was roasted in the oven until the skin turned golden and the meat was tender, while the rice was gently simmered with onions, carrots, celery, and rice vinegar. Fresh herbs were added at the end to preserve their bright green color.

The kitchen smelled of roasted herbs, garlic, and citrus, with a warm aroma of toasted vegetables filling the space. The chicken was served on a white platter, surrounded by golden rice and a colorful bed of carrots, green beans, and sliced tomatoes. Lemon wedges and a small bowl of mint were placed beside the meal.

This simple lunch was a quiet, nourishing celebration of the quiet morning light. It was prepared before the main photography session, allowing Latoya to enjoy a balanced meal while continuing to capture the series inspired by her travels and local conversations.
