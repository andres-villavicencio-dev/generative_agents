Latoya prepared a simple Kale, Lemon, and Chickpea Power Bowl. The key ingredients were fresh kale, roasted chickpeas, cucumber, red onion, a lemon-herb vinegar dressing, and a handful of spinach. She toasted the chickpeas until golden, sliced the cucumber and onion, and chopped the kale with a little olive oil and lemon zest.

She whisked the dressing and tossed it over the warm vegetables, then arranged the colorful bowl on a wooden table with a small bowl of chickpea salad nearby. The aroma rose through the kitchen—rich from the chickpeas, fresh from the herbs and lemon, and crisp from the cool salad.

The meal was a nutritious, energizing breakfast, prepared for an early Tuesday morning. Its clean presentation reflected Latoya’s organized nature, while the bright colors and balanced ingredients made it a practical start to her day.
