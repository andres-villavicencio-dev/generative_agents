Lesbia Martinez prepared Simple Garden Breakfast for the Gazette’s Mayoral Election Coverage. She gathered eggs, spinach, tomatoes, onions, and a little lemon, then sautéed them gently in a pan with a pinch of salt and pepper. The eggs were cooked until firm, and the vegetables were wilting slightly but still bright. She arranged the eggs on a white plate and placed the colorful tomatoes and spinach beside them, adding a few sprigs of fresh rosemary for aroma.

The dish had a warm, savory scent with hints of garlic, lemon zest, and the earthy freshness of spinach. It presented as a neat, rustic breakfast platter: golden eggs, crisp greens, and a glossy red tomato contrasted with the pale yellow of the eggs.

The meal was prepared to serve as a quiet, community-focused backdrop for the election coverage. It reflected Lesbia’s disciplined and organized nature, while the fresh ingredients and bright colors suggested honesty and a clear focus on the story behind the story. The simple breakfast was designed to be comforting and unpretentious—perfect for breakfast before a morning editorial.
