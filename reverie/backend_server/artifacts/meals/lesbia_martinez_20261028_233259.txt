Lesbia prepared a simple Greek-Inspired Vegetable Omelette and Whole Grain Toast for her light Tuesday breakfast. The omelette used eggs, diced tomatoes, onions, spinach, feta cheese, and a pinch of red pepper flakes. She sautéed the vegetables in a skillet until crisp, folded them into fluffy omelettes, and served the whole wheat toast alongside a small bowl of lemon-herb yogurt.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of toasted bread, gently cooked eggs, and the fresh crunch of tomatoes and herbs. She arranged the omelettes on a white plate and placed the toast beside them, adding a sprig of parsley for color. The meal was a quiet, nourishing start to her Tuesday, designed to balance her evening writing work with the Gazette’s coverage of the mayoral election, including residents’ access to campaign information and responsible voting.
