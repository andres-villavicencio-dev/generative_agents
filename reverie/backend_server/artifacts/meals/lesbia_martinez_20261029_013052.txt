Lesbia prepared a Salsa and Cumbia Breakfast Bowl, designed to complement her early morning plans and provide a hearty, colorful start to her day.

The dish combines toasted corn tortillas, avocado, roasted sweet potatoes, black beans, diced tomatoes, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime, and a zesty salsa made with tomato, onion, cilantro, jalapeño, lime, and a touch of cumin. She warmed a little olive oil in a skillet, sautéed the vegetables, then layered everything over the tortillas.

Aromatic notes of roasted potatoes, cumin, fresh cilantro, and the bright acidity of lime filled the kitchen. She served the bowls on warm plates, garnished with a final squeeze of lime and chopped cilantro, using the vibrant reds, greens, and creamy whites to create an inviting, rustic presentation.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday evening meal, intended as a simple, nourishing break after supper. Though she had no confirmed appointments, the meal reflected her disciplined and curious nature: organized, focused, and thoughtful about both her daily routine and the stories shaping her local newspaper.
