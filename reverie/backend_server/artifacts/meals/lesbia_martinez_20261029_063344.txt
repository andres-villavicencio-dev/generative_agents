For Wednesday, February 15, Lesbia prepared Hearty Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Crusty Bread, a simple yet warming dinner for her family. The main dish features a whole chicken seasoned with garlic, dried rosemary, thyme, and a touch of paprika, roasted until the skin turned golden and the meat was tender. Alongside it were potatoes, carrots, and red bell peppers, all seasoned with salt, pepper, and a splash of olive oil. A few sprigs of rosemary were arranged on the platter, adding a fragrant aroma of herbs and roast garlic to the kitchen.

The presentation was rustic and inviting: the golden-brown chicken, colorful vegetables, and warm crusty bread were arranged in a shallow serving dish, creating a balanced, family-style meal. Lesbia served it over the bread while she read a local news article, using the quiet meal time to review the Gazette’s mayoral-election coverage. This relaxed dinner was neither a formal occasion nor a hurried supper, but a comforting way to transition from her busy day to the evening she was looking forward to.
