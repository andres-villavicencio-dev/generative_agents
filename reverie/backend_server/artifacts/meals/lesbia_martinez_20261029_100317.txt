Lesbia prepared Winter Garden Breakfast with Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple, hearty option that honored the cozy, seasonal mood of her February morning.

She began with a pan-infused russet, apple, and pear, caramelizing them in butter until the apples softened and released their sweetness. To keep the mixture warm, she layered it in a small oven-safe dish over a sheet of parchment, then brushed it with a little maple syrup. While the fruit warmed, she tossed creamy buttermilk eggs, chopped chives, and a pinch of smoked paprika in a skillet, letting them finish cooking until the yolks were slightly runny and the whites were set.

For the sides, she roasted carrots, parsnips, and red bell peppers until their edges turned golden and crunchy. The warm dish was arranged on a white ceramic plate, garnished with fresh chives and a sprinkle of sea salt. The aroma filled the kitchen with caramelized apples, nutty herbs, and the earthy scent of roasted roots, making it feel inviting yet uncomplicated.

It was a quiet, purposeful meal for an early Thursday morning—prepared for a relaxed breakfast, then stored to serve as a nutritious breakfast choice for her next grocery trip.
