Lesbia prepared Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for a quiet, home-cooked Friday dinner. She chose lean chicken breasts, baby potatoes, carrots, and zucchini, seasoning them with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, dried oregano, and smoked paprika. The chicken was seared until golden, then grilled until the juices ran clear, while the vegetables were tossed with a little more oil and roasted until tender.

Assemble time, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and lightly smoked spices. She plated the chicken on a rustic white plate, serving the vegetables in a colorful medley beside a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing. The meal was simple, nourishing, and intended to satisfy the family without demanding much attention.

On Friday, February 17, Lesbia made the meal after covering the Gazette’s mayoral-election coverage for the remaining hours. The home-cooked dinner offered a peaceful pause before a purposeful evening.
