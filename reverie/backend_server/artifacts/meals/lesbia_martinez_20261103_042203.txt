On Saturday, February 18, Lesbia Martinez prepared Chicken and Vegetable Quinoa Bowl, a simple yet nutritious lunch for the Gazette’s mayoral-election coverage. She sautéed diced chicken in olive oil with onions, carrots, and bell peppers until the chicken was golden, then simmered the cooked vegetables in vegetable broth. She added rinsed quinoa, lemon-herb dressing, and fresh spinach before serving the colorful bowl over a bed of cooked quinoa.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, earthy vegetable flavors, and the subtle scent of lemon zest. She presented the meal in a simple white ceramic bowl, garnishing it with parsley and a few lemon slices for a bright, inviting appearance.

The occasion was a relaxed Saturday lunch while Lesbia balanced her editorial work with the local community. She had no confirmed appointment or meal, so the meal felt both purposeful and spontaneous—a quiet reflection of the “story behind the story” she sought to reveal in her newspaper.
