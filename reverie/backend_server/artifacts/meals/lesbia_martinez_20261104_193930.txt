On a rainy Tuesday evening, Lesbia prepared a Hearty Winter Tomato-Cauliflower Soup with Roasted Root Vegetables. She combined creamy tomato broth, roasted carrots, parsnips, potatoes, cauliflower, and a touch of thyme and garlic. The aromas filled the café with the scent of earthy warmth, toasted garlic, and savory herbs, while the kitchen worked quietly around her as she prepared the food.

She began by roasting the root vegetables on the stove, then sautéed them until lightly caramelized. After simmering the tomatoes with the vegetables in a slow cooker, she strained the broth and folded in the cooked roots and cauliflower. The soup was served in a deep ceramic bowl, garnished with fresh parsley and a few crispy potato chips. It presented simply and invitingly, with the golden vegetables, rich broth, and creamy texture making it look comforting and substantial.

The meal was part of a quiet dinner before drafting the Mayoral Election coverage for the Smallville Gazette. Its purpose was not to impress anyone but to provide a calm, nourished moment for her mind while she asked Adam Smith and Hailey Johnson for their essays and interviewed Mei Lin and Yuriko Yamamoto about the town’s community fund.
