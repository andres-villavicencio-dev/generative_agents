Lesbia prepared a Vegetable Minestrone Soup for dinner on Monday, February 13. The dish used a generous base of vegetable broth, tomatoes, onions, garlic, and carrots, with carrots, zucchini, celery, green beans, and kidney beans added toward the end of cooking. A handful of spinach was folded in last, followed by a splash of olive oil and a pinch of salt and pepper.

She prepared it in a large soup pot over medium heat, sautéing the aromatics until soft, then simmering the vegetables until they were tender. The soup was gently stirred and seasoned at the end, ensuring the beans and greens remained vibrant without becoming mushy.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm, savory scent of roasted garlic, earthy carrots, and fresh tomatoes. The presentation was simple and inviting: the pot was set on a wooden table with a small plate of crusty bread, a handful of fresh herbs, and a spoonful of garlic butter. It served as a comforting, home-style meal that matched Lesbia’s disciplined and approachable style.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday evening before the Gazette’s coverage of the mayoral election. After dinner, she planned to interview Mei Lin and Yuriko Yamamoto about how the community fund should be spent.
