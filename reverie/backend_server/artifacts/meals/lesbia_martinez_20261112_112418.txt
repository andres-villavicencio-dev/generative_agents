Lesbia prepared Eggs Benedict with Toasted Toasted Bread and Fresh Milk, a simple but warmly comforting breakfast for her early Tuesday. The meal began with two large eggs in a skillet, seasoned with salt, black pepper, and a pinch of onion powder. She cracked the eggs into a small bowl, then added the butter and stirred the whites until they formed a golden, slightly runny custard. While the eggs cooked, she toasted slices of whole-grain bread and added a splash of milk before serving.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of buttered eggs, toasted bread, and fresh milk. She arranged the golden custard and bread on a platter, garnishing it with a sprinkle of fresh herbs and a lemon wedge. Beside the breakfast, she placed a glass of milk and a bowl of fresh fruit.

The occasion was her morning routine after a demanding day of Gazette editing, newspaper contributions, and interviews. She hoped the meal would give her a gentle start to her Tuesday, allowing her to concentrate on the mayoral election and the Garden Park proposal while remaining grounded in the community’s daily rhythms.
