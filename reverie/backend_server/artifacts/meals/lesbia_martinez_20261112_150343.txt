Lesbia Martinez prepared Smoked Chicken and Root Vegetable Breakfast Burritos for a hearty Tuesday morning. She used lean chicken thighs, diced potatoes, onions, bell peppers, cabbage, and fresh cilantro, then sliced the chicken and mixed it with the vegetables in a skillet. She toasted the tortillas, folded the ingredients in, and rolled the burritos in warm corn tortillas before grilling them in a panini press.

Aromatic and comforting, the meal smelled of roasted poultry, caramelized onions, and the earthy scent of spices. The golden tortillas and crisp vegetables were arranged alongside steaming chicken on a platter, with small bowls of salsa, sour cream, and fresh fruit nearby.

It was a simple, energizing breakfast for the following day, balancing the demands of the Gazette with a nutritious start to her productive morning.
