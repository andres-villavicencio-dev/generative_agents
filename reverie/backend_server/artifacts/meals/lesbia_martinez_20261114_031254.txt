Lesbia Martinez prepared SaborFit Morning Panini, a breakfast panini with grilled chicken, avocado, cheddar, tomato, and a tangy vinaigrette. She toasted the bread, grilled the chicken until golden, sliced the panini in half, and added the remaining ingredients just before serving. The dish was garnished with fresh parsley and served alongside a bowl of warm oatmeal.

The aroma of toasted bread, savory chicken, and ripe avocado filled the kitchen as Lesbia carefully arranged the panini on a wooden board, ensuring the crisp edges and soft, creamy interior were evenly distributed. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the colorful toppings arranged neatly and a small card attached to the top.

On a Monday, February 13, the meal was part of a quiet Monday morning routine before Lesbia began covering the mayoral election for the Smallville Gazette. She wanted the breakfast to feel both nourishing and celebratory, turning a simple meal into a moment of reflection on her work and life.
