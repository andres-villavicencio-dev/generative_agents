Lesbia prepared a savory Sunday Scramble with Eggs Benedict, a hearty breakfast for the Gazette’s early coverage. She cracked eggs into a bowl, whisked them with whole milk, garlic, fresh parsley, and a pinch of salt, then cooked the eggs in a skillet until golden and crisp. She added diced ham, cheddar, smoked salmon, crispy bacon, and caramelized onions to the pan, stirring until everything warmed through.

She served the dish with toasted sourdough, a side of steamed broccoli, and a small glass of sparkling water. The kitchen filled with a rich, savory aroma of butter, garlic, eggs, and caramelized onions. The presentation was simple but inviting: the eggs arranged neatly beside a plate, garnished with parsley, and the bread resting nearby to hold the sauce.

The meal was prepared for the Gazette’s morning breakfast coverage and served at 7:00 a.m. Lesbia ate it while checking the time, using the breakfast to focus on her election coverage and the essays and interviews she had planned to collect.
