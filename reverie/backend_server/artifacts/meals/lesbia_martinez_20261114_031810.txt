Lesbia Martinez prepared Golden-Egg Breakfast with Toasted Vegetables, a simple yet hearty Sunday brunch for the Gazette’s opening coverage of the mayoral election. She boiled eggs, then carefully washed the cooked eggs and wiped down the kitchen before serving, a practice reflecting her disciplined and meticulous nature.

The dish included soft boiled eggs, sliced tomatoes, onions, a drizzle of garlic-herb butter, and crisp corn, all lightly toasted. She seasoned the eggs with pepper and salt, letting them soften slightly while keeping the vegetables crisp. The aroma was warm and inviting, with the scent of butter, garlic, herbs, and lightly toasted corn filling the kitchen.

She arranged the food on a white ceramic platter, placing the warm eggs in the center and arranging the vegetables around them. A small glass of orange juice and a slice of fresh fruit completed the presentation, giving the meal a balanced, refreshing appearance. The occasion was a quiet Sunday brunch for the Gazette contributors, but the meal itself was neither hurried nor rushed—it was prepared with care, reflecting Lesbia’s organized and thoughtful approach to life.
