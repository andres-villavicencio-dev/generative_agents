Lesbia prepared Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice, a simple, nourishing lunch for a weekday. She used bone-in chicken thighs, baby potatoes, carrots, broccoli, and bell peppers, with soy sauce, garlic, ginger, rice, and sesame oil. The chicken was marinated briefly, then stir-fried until golden, while the vegetables were stir-fried separately and tossed with the cooked meat. The ingredients were arranged in a shallow baking dish, with the rice placed around them, and a small sprig of fresh cilantro and lemon wedges added for color and freshness.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, roasted chicken, and steaming vegetables, while the warm, golden rice completed the comforting scent. Lesbia plated the meal for the mayoral-election coverage, preparing it for her colleagues, Adam Smith and Hailey Johnson, whose essays would appear on the front page. The simple, balanced meal reflected her disciplined approach to food: practical, unpretentious, and designed to energize the team while leaving room for the day’s local news stories.
