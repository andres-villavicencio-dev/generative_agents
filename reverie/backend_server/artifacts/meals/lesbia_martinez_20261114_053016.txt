Lesbia prepared a simple Chicken and Vegetable Wrap with Rice, a quick lunch designed to avoid running out of bread and eggs while supporting her work on the Mayoral Election coverage.

She sliced chicken breast, cooked it with a little oil, and warmed sliced vegetables such as carrots, bell peppers, onions, and spinach. The chicken was sliced into bite-sized pieces and placed on a warm tortilla, with the vegetables and a handful of rice arranged around it. Sesame seeds, lemon zest, and fresh herbs were sprinkled over the wrap before she closed it.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of roasted chicken, warm vegetables, and toasted sesame seeds. She presented the wrap on a small white plate with a fork and a side of sliced cucumber, while writing her notes for the Gazette nearby. The meal was a quiet, nourishing pause during a busy day of editing and interviewing Mei Lin and Yuriko Yamamoto about the town’s community fund.
