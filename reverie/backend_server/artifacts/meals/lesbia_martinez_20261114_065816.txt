Lesbia prepared a Braised Beef Stew with Root Vegetables and Crusty Bread, a warm, nourishing meal inspired by the community’s need for steady, comforting food. She grilled a small chuck roast with onions, carrots, potatoes, and carrots, then simmered it with beef broth, garlic, and bay leaves until the meat fell apart. She served the stew over crusty sourdough, topped it with a crisp pan-seared egg, and added a small green vegetable for freshness.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted beef, caramelized onions, and garlic, while the steam rising from the warm stew gave the meal a cozy, inviting glow. Lesbia presented the meal on a simple wooden board, arranging the stew, bread, and vegetables in an orderly line—the same neatness she applied to the Gazette’s election coverage.

The occasion was her late-evening dinner before heading home around 10 p.m. The meal reflected her disciplined, organized nature and her interest in the town’s local community, though she had not yet decided how to share the funds Mei Lin and Yuriko Yamamoto would be discussing.
