Lesbia prepared a Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry, designed to be simple, filling, and nutritious for the town’s residents. The dish featured a seasoned chicken breast sliced into bite-sized pieces, broccoli florets, carrots, snap peas, bell peppers, and a small amount of brown rice. She used olive oil, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and a pinch of salt to season the ingredients.

She stir-fried everything over a medium flame for about ten minutes, allowing the chicken to turn golden, the vegetables to soften, and the aromatics to release their fragrance. As she cooked, the kitchen filled with the savory scent of roasted garlic, toasted ginger, and caramelized vegetables.

The final presentation was arranged in a shallow white bowl, with the chicken in the center and colorful vegetables arranged around it. She plated the rice neatly on the side and added a tiny bowl of sesame oil with rice vinegar for easy dipping. The meal was prepared for a quick dinner for the Gazette’s staff and residents, offering everyone a simple, home-style meal without needing to carry out any complicated cooking.
