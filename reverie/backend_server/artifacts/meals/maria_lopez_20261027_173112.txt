Maria prepared Lemon-Herb Quinoa Salad with Roasted Vegetables and Feta. The dish balanced fluffy white quinoa with crisp broccoli, carrots, bell pepper, and cherry tomatoes. Fresh parsley, red onion, and whole-grain mustard added a bright citrus kick, while crumbled feta and a drizzle of olive oil provided savory richness.

She toasted the vegetables until they were slightly caramelized, then gently cooked the quinoa until the grains were tender. Warm herbs, lemon zest, and plenty of olive oil gave the salad a fresh, herbaceous aroma, while the roasted vegetables added a warm, nutty scent.

The colorful, rustic salad was presented in a shallow white bowl, with the quinoa and vegetables arranged artfully around the feta and lemon dressing. It was served with crusty whole-grain bread for dipping.

On Wednesday, February 15, Maria prepared the meal for the Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe, an affordable, inclusive event designed to celebrate her community while offering unexpected delights.
