Maria prepared a Quick Garden Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Tomato-Herb Chicken, a colorful, energizing lunch to match her Wednesday morning. The dish featured quinoa cooked with lemon, garlic, and a splash of olive oil, topped with roasted cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and feta. A piece of chicken was roasted with rosemary, thyme, and honey until golden, then sliced and arranged over the quinoa. Fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon vinaigrette completed the presentation.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and caramelized tomatoes. Maria served the bowl in a simple white ceramic dish, adding a small card that read “For the Valentine’s Day party,” so the meal felt both nourishing and celebratory. As she ate, the bright colors and fresh flavors reminded her of her physics-class experiments and her time at Hobbs Cafe, blending the practical, evidence-based approach to meal planning with the enthusiastic spirit she brings to her community projects.
