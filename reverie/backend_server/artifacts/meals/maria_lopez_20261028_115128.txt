The meal was a colorful, fresh Quick Vegetable Stir-Fry with Sesame-Tofu and Roasted Vegetables, prepared for Maria’s Valentine’s Day lunch at Hobbs Cafe.

She stir-fried broccoli, bell peppers, mushrooms, snap peas, and carrots with sliced tofu in a small amount of vegetable oil. Adding savory soy sauce, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, and chili flakes gave the dish its aromatic, warm character. The bright green vegetables and golden-roasted tofu added visual contrast, while the golden-brown edges of the mushrooms suggested rich, savory flavors.

Mildly aromatic and fresh, the stir-fry smelled of garlic, ginger, and toasted sesame oil, with hints of sweetness from the tomatoes and sweetness from the carrots. It was presented in a vibrant ceramic bowl, piled with colorful ingredients and garnished with chopped green onions and fresh cilantro. The meal felt celebratory and warm, balancing Maria’s physics classes and Twitch work with a festive Valentine’s Day lunch.
