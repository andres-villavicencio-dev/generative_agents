Maria prepared a Vibrant Valentine’s Spring Quinoa Salad, designed to be affordable, inclusive, and filled with a bright, celebratory surprise.

Key ingredients included cooked quinoa, cherry tomatoes, cherry tomatoes, crisp cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, red onion, baby spinach, chickpeas, feta cheese, and a dressing made with lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, and a touch of honey. Maria arranged the salad in a wide serving bowl, garnished with parsley and lemon slices, and placed a small, colorful surprise card nearby.
