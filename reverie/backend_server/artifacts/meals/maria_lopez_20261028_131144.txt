Maria prepared a Quinoa & Roasted Vegetable Bowl, a quick, nutritious dinner designed for a weekday evening before streaming. The dish features cooked quinoa, cherry tomatoes, yellow peppers, zucchini, red onion, and garlic, seasoned with olive oil, lemon juice, sea salt, and black pepper. She roasted the vegetables in a skillet until slightly charred, then tossed them with the quinoa and lemon dressing.

The aroma was warm and inviting: golden-roasted vegetables, garlic, and citrus mingled with the savory, earthy scent of toasted quinoa. Maria presented the bowl in a simple white ceramic dish, topped with bright green spinach, a sprinkle of feta, and a fresh herb garnish.

It was part of her Valentine’s Day party preparations, though Maria kept the meal light and inclusive, incorporating affordable, practical ideas from her classmates while leaving a small surprise for a resident. It was a relaxed, celebratory dinner meant to energize her Wednesday between physics study and community work.
