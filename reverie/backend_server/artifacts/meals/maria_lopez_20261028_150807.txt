Maria prepared a Quinoa-Potato-Scramble with Fresh Herbs and Citrus Drizzle for breakfast. She used cooked quinoa, roasted red potatoes, scrambled eggs, spinach, diced tomatoes, chili flakes, black pepper, and a splash of lemon juice. The dish was seasoned with garlic, cumin, and oregano, then finished with a bright dressing of olive oil, lemon, and a pinch of salt.

Aromatic, earthy, and zesty, the meal smelled warm, savory, and lightly citrusy. The presentation was colorful and inviting: fluffy quinoa topped with golden potatoes, bright green spinach, and creamy scrambled eggs, all drizzled with a glossy herb-lime sauce. It served as a balanced and cheerful breakfast to complement her Wednesday studies and her community-focused work at Hobbs Cafe.
