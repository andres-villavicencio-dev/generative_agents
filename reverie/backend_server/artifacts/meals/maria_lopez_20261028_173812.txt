Maria prepared a Quinoa and Vegetable Grain Bowl for her Wednesday breakfast at Hobbs Cafe. The dish was a colorful, balanced meal using cooked quinoa, chopped kale, roasted sweet potato, red bell pepper, cucumber, and cherry tomatoes. She added a handful of chia seeds and a drizzle of lemon-herb dressing for texture and freshness.

The preparation method was simple yet thoughtfully prepared: she rinsed and cooked the quinoa with a little water or broth, then sautéed the vegetables until crisp-tender. The dressing was whisked together at the end, and the bowl was assembled with the grains, vegetables, and seeds. The aroma was warm, inviting, and fragrant—herbs and roasted sweet potato mingled with the nutty scent of the quinoa, while bright citrus suggested a fresh, lively flavor.

She presented the bowl on a small white plate at the counter, arranged the colorful ingredients clearly, and placed a sprig of parsley on the side. The occasion was her Wednesday morning routine at Hobbs Cafe, complementing her physics studies and work on the Valentine’s Day party. She wanted the meal to be affordable and inclusive, reflecting the ideas from Yuriko and Isabella, while also creating a warm, welcoming table for the celebration.
