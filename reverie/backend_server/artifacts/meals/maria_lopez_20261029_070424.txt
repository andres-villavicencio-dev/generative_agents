Dish Name: Creamy Egg Scramble with Roasted Tomato and Garlic

Maria prepared a quick, comforting breakfast-style scramble using four eggs and one cup of coffee grounds for flavor. She washed the eggs, cracked them into a bowl, and sautéed the coffee beans in a skillet until the coffee infused them with a warm aroma. The eggs were stirred into the pan, then seasoned with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. The coffee grounds added a subtle bitterness and savory depth, while the roasted tomatoes and fresh garlic brought a bright, aromatic finish.

The meal was presented on a white plate, with the golden eggs arranged around a small mound of roasted tomatoes and scattered pieces of garlic. The dish was colorful, rustic, and inviting, with a smooth, slightly glossy texture and a rich, earthy aroma that reminded Maria of coffee, cheese, and breakfast. It was prepared as a warm, home-cooked meal, likely for a quiet Wednesday morning dinner or a casual gathering with Giorgio, Isabella, Amilcar, Eddy, and Sharon, celebrating the neighborhood and her expanding circle of friends.
