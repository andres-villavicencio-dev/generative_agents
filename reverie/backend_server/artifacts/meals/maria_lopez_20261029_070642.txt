Maria prepared Egg Scramble with Coffee Crumble for dinner at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured two dozen eggs, coffee beans, garlic, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a touch of onion. She cracked the eggs into a heated skillet, then cooked them gently until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft. She finished the pan by adding a small amount of butter, garlic, and finely chopped onion, stirring them until fragrant. The coffee beans were toasted briefly in a separate small pan, then used to create a warm, earthy crumble over the eggs.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted coffee, garlic, and toasted nuts, while the eggs gave off a savory, slightly nutty scent. Maria plated the dish in a shallow ceramic bowl and garnished it with a generous dusting of cracked black pepper, finely chopped chives, and a drizzle of olive oil. The presentation was simple but inviting: the golden eggs stood against the dark brown crumble, with fresh herbs and the dark coffee beans adding visual texture.

The meal was prepared as a relaxed weekday dinner, combining her physics and cooking interests. She enjoyed it with a cup of coffee while reflecting on how creativity and community can turn everyday ingredients into something meaningful.
