Maria prepared Egg Coffee Crunch, a savory breakfast-idea inspired by her physics curiosity. She boiled eggs in a pot of coffee, then added crispy bacon, diced tomatoes, chopped chives, and a pinch of garlic. The eggs were cooked until fully set and lightly browned, creating a warm, nutty aroma of roasted coffee and savory herbs.

The dish was plated in a small white bowl, with the eggs arranged in the center, bacon crisped around the edges, and fresh chives sprinkled on top. It was served alongside a small glass of coffee and a plate of fresh fruit.

The occasion was Maria’s Wednesday lunch at Hobbs Cafe, where she balanced physics homework, Twitch streaming, and socializing with Giorgio, Isabella, Amilcar, and new friends Eddy and Sharon. The meal reflected her love for creativity and community, turning a simple home-cooked dish into a comforting centerpiece for her day.
