On a quiet Friday morning, Maria prepared Breakfast for Two with Herbs and Toast, a simple, nourishing meal designed to balance her physics studies and Twitch streaming. She used two slices of sourdough bread, a handful of fresh spinach, a small orange, and a few sprigs of rosemary for flavor. She toasted the bread until golden, then sautéed the spinach in a little olive oil, adding the orange juice and chopped rosemary toward the end to preserve its bright zest.

As it cooked, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted sourdough and the fresh, herbaceous scent of rosemary. Maria arranged the golden bread, juicy orange slices, and green spinach on a white plate, garnishing it with a tiny sprig of rosemary and a drizzle of honey. The inviting presentation made the simple breakfast feel celebratory.

The occasion was a relaxed morning around Hobbs Cafe, where Maria was strengthening her social life with Giorgio, Isabella, Amilcar, Eddy, and Sharon. The meal was practical and intimate—perfect for a quiet morning when she needed a little energy to focus on her physics class and connect with her friends.
