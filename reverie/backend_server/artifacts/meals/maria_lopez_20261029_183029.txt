Maria prepared a simple, comforting vegetable stir-fry for dinner at Hobbs Cafe on Friday, February 17, 2025. The dish was called Fresh Veggie Stir-Fry with Lemon-Herb Oil.

She sliced carrots, bell peppers, onions, and tomatoes, then sautéed them in a wok until the vegetables were crisp-tender. Maria added a handful of fresh cilantro, a few cloves of garlic, and a pinch of smoked paprika for depth. With a small amount of olive oil, she tossed everything together before finishing it with a lemon-herb oil.

The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant—aromas of caramelized onions, herbs, garlic, and a hint of lemon zest. It smelled inviting, like a cozy evening meal ready for family.

Maria plated the colorful vegetables in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with extra cilantro and a drizzle of the lemon-herb oil. The presentation was bright and cheerful, with the vivid red, orange, and green vegetables contrasting against the neutral beige of the plate.

It was a casual Friday dinner meant to bring people together, especially while Maria balanced physics studies and her Twitch stream with cooking and conversation.
