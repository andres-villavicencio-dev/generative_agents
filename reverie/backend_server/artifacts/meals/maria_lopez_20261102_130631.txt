Maria prepared a Hearty Vegetable Risotto for a casual Saturday dinner at Hobbs Cafe. She combined Arborio rice with a creamy broth made from milk, water, onion, garlic, and a splash of white wine. The vegetables included sautéed onions, carrots, celery, and spinach, with extra eggs and bread to create a complete, filling meal.

She sautéed the vegetables in a pan, then slowly heated the rice and broth while stirring until it turned creamy and absorbed the liquid. The eggs were cracked into the pan alongside the rice, then cooked until they formed soft, golden custard-like pools. She topped the dish with freshly torn parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of onions, garlic, and the rich, savory fragrance of the creamy rice. The rustic wooden bowl presented the colorful vegetables, creamy white rice, and golden egg centers, with golden-brown crusts of the bread adding a comforting contrast. Maria served it on a Saturday evening, balancing physics notes with dinner and reflection, creating a warm, communal meal.
