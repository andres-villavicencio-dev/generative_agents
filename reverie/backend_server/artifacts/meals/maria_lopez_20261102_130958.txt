Maria prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Sweet Potato at home for dinner. The dish featured a boneless chicken breast marinated in lemon, garlic, and olive oil, alongside roasted carrots, zucchini, and sweet potatoes, all seasoned with paprika, cumin, and a little salt.

She began by preheating the oven and preparing a pan with olive oil, garlic, and lemon. The vegetables were sliced and roasted until tender, then the chicken was grilled until golden and cooked through. A simple side salad of crisp lettuce and cucumber added a refreshing contrast.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm smell of garlic, citrus, and caramelized vegetables. Maria arranged the colorful chicken, golden vegetables, and crisp potatoes on a white plate, garnishing it with parsley and a squeeze of lemon. The presentation was bright and inviting, with each component clearly visible.

This meal was the centerpiece of Maria’s Saturday evening at Hobbs Cafe, a quiet celebration of her cooking, creativity, and social life with Giorgio, Isabella, Amilcar, Eddy, and Sharon.
