Maria prepared Egg Scramble with Toasted Eggs, Spinach, and a Lemon-Balanced Oatmeal Bowl for the family breakfast. She used eggs, milk, milk chocolate chips, oatmeal, fresh spinach, and a pinch of cinnamon. First, she whisked the eggs, milk, and cinnamon until they formed a smooth custard. She then cooked the eggs with the spinach in a pan, adding the oatmeal and chocolate chips at the end so they remained soft and melted. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of eggs and milk, the crisp scent of toasted oatmeal, and a faint lemony note.

She arranged the pan eggs in a neat pile, scattered the spinach and chocolate chips over them, and set the oatmeal bowl beside it. The meal was prepared for a warm Saturday family breakfast, with Maria’s early college studies and Twitch streaming taking center stage in the background.
