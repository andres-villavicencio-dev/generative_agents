Maria prepared Baked Salmon with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Butter for the family’s Saturday meal. She sourced fresh salmon, whole eggs, milk, bread, carrots, onions, bell peppers, and garlic from the grocery store before the refrigerator ran low.

She sliced the salmon and prepared the vegetables for roasting, then sautéed the carrots, onions, and peppers in olive oil until they softened. The lemon, garlic, and fresh herbs were whisked together with butter and drizzled over the salmon, while the eggs were cracked into a small pan for a simple omelet. She arranged the colorful vegetables, golden salmon, and fluffy omelet around the pan for a bright, balanced presentation.

The aroma of savory salmon, caramelized onions, and lemon filled the kitchen, while the rich buttery sauce and crisp vegetables made the meal feel comforting. It was prepared for a warm Saturday family dinner, combining Maria’s interest in cooking with the comfort of a home meal.
