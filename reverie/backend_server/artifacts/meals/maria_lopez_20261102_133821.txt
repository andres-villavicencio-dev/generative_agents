On Saturday, Maria prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Bread, a warm, family-style dinner for herself and Giorgio. She selected fresh eggs, milk, and bread, comparing prices and choosing eggs from the freshest carton, milk from the best store, and bread with the most generous crust.

The meal began with a simple pan fry: the chicken was seasoned with garlic, salt, pepper, and paprika, then grilled until golden and tender. Alongside it, Maria roasted carrots, onions, and bell peppers with olive oil, salt, and fresh herbs, letting them become slightly caramelized. She finished the dish with warm, crusty bread and drizzled a little lemon juice over the chicken and vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of garlic, herbs, toasted bread, and roasting vegetables. She plated the meal with the chicken on one side and the colorful vegetables on the other, serving the bread in a small basket for easy eating. It was a cozy, informal dinner for her evening at Hobbs Cafe—a quiet moment of comfort while she balanced physics studies, gaming, and creative work with her friends.
