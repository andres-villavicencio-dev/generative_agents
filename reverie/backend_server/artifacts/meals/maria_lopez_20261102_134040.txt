Maria prepared Garden-Style Chicken and Root Vegetable Stew, a warm, hearty lunch that turned her Saturday into a relaxed meal at Hobbs Cafe. She gathered fresh chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, garlic, and tomatoes from the store, selecting the cheapest options that still looked appetizing.

She began by sautéing the onions and celery in a pan until they became translucent, then added the garlic and cooked until fragrant. The carrots and potatoes were cubed and browned in a little oil, followed by the chicken, tomatoes, and broth. She covered the pan, let it simmer until the chicken was tender and the potatoes were fork-tender, then simmered briefly to keep everything moist.

The finished dish had a rich, savory aroma of garlic, tomatoes, and roasted vegetables. She plated it in a large serving bowl, arranging the colorful root vegetables around the chicken with fresh parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice. The bright presentation made the warm stew look inviting, and Maria served it with crusty bread and a glass of milk.

The meal was meant to be a cozy Saturday lunch, balancing her physics studies and creative work with a simple, nourishing meal. Its comforting flavors reminded her of the community and imagination that connect people and inspire new ideas.
