Maria prepared Egg Scramble with Toasted Whole Wheat Bread and Fresh Spinach. For the breakfast, she gathered eggs, milk, bread, spinach, and a pinch of salt from her shopping list, ensuring the purchased items matched what she needed.

She cracked the eggs into a small pan, added the milk, and stirred until the whites set and the yolks remained slightly runny. The spinach was wilted briefly, and the bread was toasted lightly in the same pan. The whole wheat bread provided a warm, nutty base, while the eggs added richness and the spinach added a fresh, earthy texture.

The aroma filled the kitchen with rich, buttery notes from the eggs and milk, earthy green freshness from the spinach, and the familiar scent of toasted bread. Maria presented the meal in a simple white bowl, with the golden toast arranged beside it and a small pile of spinach scattered on top.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed Saturday morning at home, balancing her studies and creative work with the familiar comfort of a hearty breakfast.
