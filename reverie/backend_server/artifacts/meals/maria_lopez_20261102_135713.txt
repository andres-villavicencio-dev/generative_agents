Maria prepared Chicken and Vegetable Scramble for dinner at Hobbs Cafe, preparing it for herself and Giorgio while the fridge ran low on eggs, milk, and bread.

The meal featured chicken breast, soft-boiled eggs, spinach, tomatoes, onions, garlic, and chives. Maria sautéed the vegetables in olive oil, then added the cooked chicken and scrambled the eggs with a splash of milk. The pan juices and finely chopped herbs gave the dish a warm, savory aroma, while fresh herbs and colorful vegetables added a bright, cheerful fragrance.

She plated the scramble on a large white plate, arranging the eggs in a small nest and garnishing the sides with fresh herbs and a drizzle of lemon juice. The presentation was rustic and inviting, reflecting her energetic, enthusiastic style.

The occasion was an informal community dinner, a chance to share food with Giorgio and friends while reconnecting over laughter and conversation.
