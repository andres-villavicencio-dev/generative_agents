Maria prepared Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Veggies for a Saturday family dinner at Hobbs Cafe. The meal featured tender chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, and zucchini, all seasoned with lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, paprika, and fresh thyme. She roasted everything in a preheated oven until the chicken reached an easy-to-eat tenderness, then finished the vegetables with a little butter.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized garlic, roasted potatoes, and lemon zest. She served the colorful, hearty dish in a shallow black platter, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with each vegetable arranged around the chicken so the meal looked balanced and appetizing.

The occasion was a relaxed family gathering on Saturday, February 18, 2025. Maria treated the meal as a warm reminder of home while reflecting on the day’s balance between physics studies, gaming, and creative work. The meal felt special because it was prepared with care, sharing a warm moment with Giorgio, Isabella, Amilcar, Eddy, and Sharon.
