For her Saturday at Hobbs Cafe, Maria prepared Egg Scramble with Toasted Bread and Fresh Herbs, a hearty breakfast meant to energize her studies and creative work. The dish came together quickly: eggs were whisked, then carefully scrambled in a pan until golden and fluffy, while buttery slices of bread were toasted before being folded in. Fresh chives, a pinch of salt, and a drizzle of olive oil added a bright, savory finish.

She checked that the eggs reached the proper temperature and carefully cooked the bread through while avoiding the pan. The kitchen filled with a warm, earthy aroma of toasted butter and herbs, with the golden eggs and crisp bread creating a comforting visual contrast.

The occasion was a quiet Saturday at home, balanced with physics study and creative projects. The meal was a personal reward: a warm, nourishing meal that felt both practical and creative, reflecting Maria’s energetic, inquisitive spirit and her pride in making delicious, well-planned food.
