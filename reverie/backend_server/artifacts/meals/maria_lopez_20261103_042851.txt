Maria prepared a Hearty Tomato-Mushroom Soup with Crusty Bread, serving it alongside two eggs and a slice of whole-grain bread. The soup began with sautéed mushrooms and carrots in a pot of vegetable broth, then welcomed in a can of diced tomatoes. She added garlic, onion, thyme, and a few sprigs of fresh parsley, simmering until the flavors melded. A splash of cream gave it richness, while the eggs were poached separately in butter and parsley before being arranged on top of the warm soup.

The aroma filling the kitchen was earthy and comforting—rich mushrooms, caramelized onions, garlic, and the gentle scent of fresh herbs. She served the meal in a large white serving bowl, filling it with the golden broth and carefully placing the eggs and bread on the rim. It was a relaxed Saturday evening meal shared with friends, including Giorgio, Isabella, Amilcar, Eddy, and Sharon, offering warmth and a cozy way to celebrate her creative life.
