Maria prepared Cinnamon-Oatmeal Breakfast with Extra Bread at Hobbs Cafe on a chilly Monday morning. The dish featured rolled oats cooked in a small pot, topped with warm milk, a spoonful of cinnamon, a sprinkle of nutmeg, and a generous portion of extra bread. Fresh banana slices and chopped nuts were added for texture and sweetness, while a drizzle of maple syrup completed the flavor.

She prepared it in a portable kitchen set: the oats were stirred until creamy, the banana was sliced, and the bread was toasted in the microwave until golden. The kitchen’s low stock was compensated for by adding a splash of water and extra butter. As the oatmeal warmed, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted nuts, cinnamon, and warm milk, accompanied by the cheerful sound of a nearby fan.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the oatmeal was arranged neatly in a ceramic bowl, the extra bread was torn into three pieces, and a small glass of orange juice was placed beside it. Maria served it with a bright smile, ready to make breakfast with friends while continuing her physics studies and Twitch streams. The occasion was a relaxed morning meal before a long day of studying and streaming.
