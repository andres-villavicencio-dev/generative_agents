Maria prepared a Braised Chicken with Root Vegetables to nourish herself and keep the cafe’s bread and milk supplies stocked. She marinated chicken thighs with garlic, onion, paprika, black pepper, and a splash of soy sauce, then seared them until golden. She sautéed carrots, potatoes, and parsnips until crisp, then simmered everything with broth, carrots, and a bit of flour until the chicken fell off the bone.

The dish had a warm, savory aroma of caramelized onions, roasted root vegetables, and toasted garlic. Maria served it in a wide white bowl, with the chicken arranged around colorful glazed vegetables and garnished with fresh parsley and a lemon wedge.

This meal was a simple, practical lunch to balance her physics studies and Twitch-streaming commitments, giving her enough energy to continue working without running out of basic groceries.
