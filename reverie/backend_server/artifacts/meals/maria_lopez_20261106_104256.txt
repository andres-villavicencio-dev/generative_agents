Meal: “Golden Morning Bowl”

Maria prepared a vibrant, hearty breakfast using oatmeal, fresh strawberries, blueberries, honey, walnuts, and a splash of vanilla bean syrup. She toasted the oats, stirred in the fruit, and finished the bowl with cracked walnuts and a drizzle of honey. The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of cinnamon, toasted oats, and sweet berries, while the cool blueberries added a refreshing contrast.

She arranged the bowl in the center of the Hobbs Cafe table, placing a small glowing lantern beside it and a notebook with her moral-questions artwork nearby. The presentation was simple yet inviting: golden oatmeal, colorful fruit, crunchy nuts, and a glossy honey glaze.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday morning gathering with friends Jennifer and Hailey. Maria had spent five minutes making and brewing the breakfast, then shared it with them as they took a quick lunch break. The meal felt intimate and productive, combining her academic routine with connection and her creative projects.
