For Tuesday’s dinner, Maria prepared Hobbs Café-Inspired Chicken and Vegetable Stew. She gathered chicken breast, carrots, celery, potatoes, onions, and a generous amount of diced tomatoes, along with olive oil, garlic, paprika, lemon juice, and a pinch of cumin.

She sautéed the vegetables in a hot pan until lightly browned, then cooked the chicken until it was fully heated through. The stew was finished with a splash of water, salt, and pepper. Maria served it over a bed of creamy mashed potatoes, garnishing the top with fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, and earthy vegetables. The presentation was simple yet inviting: colorful vegetables and golden chicken arranged in a large skillet, the potatoes neatly beside them.

This hearty meal was a cozy family-style lunch shared with Jennifer and Hailey. It balanced Maria’s physics coursework with social time and creative projects, offering comfort while she continued her studies and Twitch-streaming schedule.
