On Tuesday, February 14, Maria prepared a Simple Garlic Herb Roast Chicken for dinner, while her lunch consisted of lettuce, cucumber, and a small portion of cooked quinoa with lemon vinaigrette. The meal was simple enough to prepare while she was still reviewing Monday’s homework and following up with Michael Poirier about using the bathroom.

The chicken was seasoned with olive oil, garlic, dried herbs, salt, and pepper, then placed skin-side down in a roasting pan. It was roasted until golden and cooked through, with the skin crisp and the meat tender. The aromas of roasted garlic, herbs, and butter filled the kitchen, creating a warm, inviting scent. She arranged the chicken on a white platter and served the vegetables beside it, arranging the crisp lettuce and cucumber around it.

Maria made the meal for a casual dinner-and-lunch day, balancing her physics studies and Twitch streaming responsibilities. Her bright presentation and relaxed preparation made the meal feel both nourishing and cheerful, fitting into her energetic and inquisitive lifestyle.
