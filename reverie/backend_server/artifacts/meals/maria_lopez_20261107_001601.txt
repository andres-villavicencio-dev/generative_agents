Maria prepared a simple Roasted Vegetable and Chicken Pasta for dinner on Tuesday, February 14. The key ingredients were chicken breast, cherry tomatoes, red onion, garlic, oregano, olive oil, lemon juice, and a small amount of red pepper flakes. She marinated the chicken for 15 minutes, then roasted it alongside the vegetables in the oven until golden and tender. The pasta was tossed briefly with the cooking liquid before being added to the pan.

The meal was warm and fragrant throughout, with the savory chicken, sweet tomatoes, and earthy onion creating a harmonious aroma. Maria presented the dish in a shallow white bowl, arranging the pasta in the center and the colorful vegetables around it. A bright red pepper and fresh basil garnish added a pop of color.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday dinner while Maria worked on her physics degree and followed up with Michael Poirier about using the bathroom before heading home. The simple, home-cooked meal offered a warm, energetic conclusion to a productive day.
