Maria prepared a simple Bowl of Quinoa, Roasted Chickpeas, Kale, and Lemon-Herb Chicken for her lunch. For dinner, she made Roasted Vegetable Stir-Fry with Lemon-Tahini Sauce, featuring carrots, bell peppers, onions, broccoli, and chickpeas.

She cooked the chicken in a pan with olive oil, garlic, and fresh herbs, then tossed the vegetables with a quick stir-fry in sesame oil and soy sauce. The lemon-tahini sauce was made from tahini, lemon juice, yogurt, and a pinch of cumin. The meal was seasoned generously with salt, pepper, and a dash of chili flakes, giving it a warm, savory aroma.

The presentation was bright and colorful: the golden chickpeas, crisp kale, and orange vegetables contrasted with the creamy sauce, while the chicken rested beside the bowl. Maria arranged a large serving dish in the center, with smaller plates for each person. It was a casual, nutritious lunch for her physics class, fitting her energetic and inquisitive nature.
