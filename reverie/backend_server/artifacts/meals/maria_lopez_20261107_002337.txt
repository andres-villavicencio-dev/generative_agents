Maria prepared a fresh and colorful lentil and vegetable stir-fry for dinner and lunch, combining familiar comfort with a quick, interactive cooking session.

The dish includes red lentils, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, tomato, bell pepper, broccoli, and corn, along with a little coconut milk and soy sauce for a mild, savory-sweet flavor. It was sautéed in a stainless-steel skillet over medium heat until the vegetables softened and the lentils absorbed the liquid. The aroma filled the kitchen with warm garlic, earthy root vegetables, and toasted coconut.

She arranged the colorful stir-fry in a shallow dish, using a spoon to gently fold the ingredients and create small piles of contrasting colors. Served over brown rice and accompanied by a simple side salad, the meal had a warm, inviting presentation.

The occasion was Maria’s Tuesday lunch break, while she balanced her physics degree with her part-time Twitch streamer duties. The lively cooking process made the meal feel energetic and creative, fitting her enthusiastic and inquisitive personality.
