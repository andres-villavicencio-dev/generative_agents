For Tuesday, Maria made a simple Quinoa, Lemon, and Roasted Vegetable Bowl with a lemon-herb dressing. She chose quinoa as the base, adding diced tomatoes, bell peppers, onions, carrots, and red onion. She roasted these vegetables until their edges softened, then tossed them with a dressing of olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and fresh herbs. The quinoa was cooked until tender and rinsed, then combined with the warm vegetables for a bright, colorful meal.

The aroma of the dish was fresh and inviting—herbs, garlic, citrus, and vegetables created a clean, savory scent. Maria presented the bowl on a white plate with a small side of brown rice and a glass of water, arranging the colorful vegetables around the quinoa in an inviting spiral. She served it around 7:00 p.m., enjoying a quiet dinner while connecting with friends and continuing her physics studies.
