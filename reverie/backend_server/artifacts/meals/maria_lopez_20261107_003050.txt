On Tuesday, February 14, Maria prepared a simple but satisfying dinner called Creamy Tuscan Chicken with Zucchini Noodles. She gathered chicken thighs, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, broccoli, garlic, olive oil, fresh basil, and a splash of lemon juice. She first sliced the vegetables into thin strips and tossed them with olive oil, garlic, and salt. The chicken was seared in a skillet until the skin was crispy, then finished in the pan with the vegetables and a spoonful of cream.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, fresh basil, and the gentle sizzle of vegetables cooking. She plated the colorful dish in a shallow bowl with the crisp chicken and bright green vegetables on top, finishing it with a drizzle of lemon and a few fresh basil leaves.

This casual meal was Maria’s relaxed Tuesday dinner, a peaceful way to wind down after a busy day while preparing for her physics studies and Twitch streaming. The simple combination of flavors made her feel satisfied, energized, and ready to focus on learning.
