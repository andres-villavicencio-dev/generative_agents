Maria prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa, a simple dinner that balanced protein, colorful vegetables, and whole grains. She started by seasoning chicken breasts with garlic, lemon, black pepper, and herbs, then marinated them briefly. She roasted carrots, bell peppers, and red onions in a little oil until they were tender, then added sliced chicken and covered everything with foil to let the flavors meld. After removing the chicken, she toasted the quinoa with a sprinkle of salt and served it over the cooked vegetables.

The aroma was warm and fragrant: roasted garlic, sweet onions, and citrusy lemon filling the kitchen. Maria arranged the colorful vegetables in a neat circle around the grilled chicken and garnished each portion with fresh parsley and a lemon wedge. She plated the meal on a white dinner plate, making the vibrant colors pop and the fluffy quinoa look inviting.

The occasion was a relaxed Friday evening dinner at home. After an earlier day of physics coursework and Twitch streaming, Maria wanted a comforting, easy meal that would satisfy her hunger without requiring much effort.
