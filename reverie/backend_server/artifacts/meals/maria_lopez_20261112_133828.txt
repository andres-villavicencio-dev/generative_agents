Maria prepared Pork-Chickpea and Vegetable Stew for her Tuesday evening dinner. She sautéed finely chopped onions, carrots, and celery, then added the main ingredients: seasoned ground pork, canned chickpeas, diced tomatoes, garlic, and fresh parsley. A handful of spinach was added near the end, along with a splash of vegetable broth to keep the dish moist and flavorful.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of onions, garlic, and warm pork, while the chickpeas added a nutty, earthy undertone. Once the stew simmered for about 20 minutes, it was finished, served in a shallow baking dish, and garnished with chopped parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

The presentation was bright and inviting: colorful vegetables, golden pork, and vibrant yellow chickpeas stood out against the warm, rustic tones of the pan. Maria served it with a simple lemon-herb crusty bread, making it a hearty, family-style meal. It was a relaxed Tuesday dinner, neither a special occasion nor a rushed weekday meal, but a cozy, satisfying treat for her and her friends.
