Breakfast: Creamy Egg Scramble with Toast and Cold Coffee

Maria prepared a simple, comforting breakfast for the run-out bread. The dish was a fluffy egg scramble made with eggs, diced cheddar cheese, chopped spinach, and a few slices of bread, all cooked in a skillet until the eggs were set and the spinach was wilted. She added a splash of milk and butter for creaminess, then sprinkled the mixture with freshly grated cheddar before serving it over the warm toast. A cup of cold coffee was poured over the pan.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the eggs sizzled and the cheese melted, releasing a savory, buttery scent. She arranged the eggs in a warm bowl, placed the toasted slice of bread on the side, and set the coffee cup beside them. The presentation was simple and tidy: bright egg yolks, golden cheese, crisp toast, and a dark cup of coffee.

The occasion was a quiet, solitary morning before Maria began her day searching for steady work at the cafe and clinic. The meal was meant to be comforting and easy to eat, offering her the energy she needed to focus on her job and studies.
