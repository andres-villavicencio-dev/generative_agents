Maria prepared Mediterranean Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Quinoa and Roasted Vegetables for breakfast. The main dish is a tender piece of chicken seasoned with olive oil, garlic, dried oregano, lemon zest, salt, and pepper, grilled over a hot skillet until the skin is golden and the juices run clear. It is served alongside fluffy quinoa, crisp carrots, zucchini, and tomato slices.

She cooked the quinoa until it absorbed the broth, stirred in the warm chicken, and topped everything with a lemon-herb dressing. The aroma filled the kitchen: toasted garlic, savory chicken, bright citrus, earthy herbs, and a hint of roasted vegetable depth. Maria arranged the colorful vegetables and golden chicken in a small glass bowl, with a lemon wedge and a sprig of rosemary resting beside it.

The meal was a quiet, solitary breakfast intended to balance her busy week of job applications and study sessions. It was neither a formal meal nor a special occasion, but a simple, nourishing start to her Monday.
