Maria prepared a Mediterranean Breakfast Bowl for a quiet Monday morning. The dish combined whole-grain toast, creamy avocado, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, feta, and a lemon-herb dressing, with a small bowl of Greek yogurt and walnuts for a protein boost.

She toasted the bread until it was golden and slightly crisp, then gently warmed the vegetables and avocado in a skillet. Herfeta was added last so it would remain firm, and she tossed everything together with the dressing. The bowl was plated with the yogurt and nuts reserved in a small dish, allowing Maria to enjoy the meal without distraction.

The aroma was warm, earthy, and bright—smelling of toasted grains, citrus, herbs, and nutty cream. Maria arranged the ingredients in an inviting spiral, topped with a few bright tomatoes and a generous drizzle of lemon, giving the breakfast a colorful, balanced appearance.

The occasion was her first breakfast after arriving in Smallville, with the goal of building a simple, sustainable routine while finding steady work around town.
