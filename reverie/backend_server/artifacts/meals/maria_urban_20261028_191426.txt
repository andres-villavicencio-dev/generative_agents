Maria prepared Butterfield’s Classic Coffee, a creamy, comforting breakfast drink made with hot water, instant coffee, milk, and a small amount of sugar. She boiled the coffee, stirred in the milk, and topped it with a light dusting of sugar. The aroma of roasted coffee and warm milk filled the kitchen, with a hint of vanilla from the sugar.

She presented the coffee in a clear glass mug, topped with a thin layer of foam, and set it beside a plate of sliced fruit and whole-grain toast. The presentation was simple and elegant, matching her need for order and balance.

This meal was her morning breakfast on Monday, February 13, a quiet moment before she began her busy week searching for work in Smallville.
