Dish Name: Fruit & Herbs Breakfast Toast

Key Ingredients: Whole-grain toast, sliced banana, apple, berries, avocado, lemon-lime juice, and a small handful of fresh herbs.

Preparation Method: Maria brushed the toasted bread with olive oil and lemon juice, then arranged the fruit and herbs on top. She drizzled the lemon-lime juice over the pan, letting the fruit soak briefly before cutting the toast into neat squares and serving it on a small white plate.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the warm scent of toasted bread, ripe fruit, avocado, citrus, and the subtle freshness of herbs.

Presentation: The breakfast was arranged in a simple, inviting bowl or plate with the fruit distributed evenly across the toast. Herbs were scattered artistically around the edges, and the lemon-scented juice was kept for a final drizzle.

Occasion: It was Maria’s first breakfast in Smallville, a quiet morning before she applied for a job at the new clinic. The meal was prepared in the solitude of her home, reflecting her organized, methodical approach to her new life.
