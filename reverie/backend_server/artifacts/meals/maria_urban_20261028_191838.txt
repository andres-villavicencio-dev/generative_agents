Maria prepared a Berry-Crusted Quinoa Breakfast Bowl for breakfast. The meal combined fluffy quinoa, fresh strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, and orange segments, with walnuts, chia seeds, and a touch of cinnamon for texture and warmth. She toasted the quinoa in a skillet, then sautéed the berries in a little honey until they began to soften, before tossing everything with a light yogurt–honey dressing.

Aromatic rosemary, cinnamon, citrus zest, and the tartness of the berries mingled with the nutty warmth of the walnuts and quinoa. Maria arranged the bowl on a wooden cutting board, scattering the berries across the top and placing the quinoa in the center, creating an inviting, colorful presentation.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday morning in town, a simple way for Maria to start her day while balancing her job search, budgeting, and creative pursuits. It was a nourishing, solitary breakfast that felt both comforting and mindful.
