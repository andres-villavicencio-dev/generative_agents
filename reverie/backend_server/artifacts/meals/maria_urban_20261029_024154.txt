Maria prepared a Simple Grain-Root Breakfast Bowl, combining a warm grain, fresh vegetables, and a savory protein. Her key ingredients were quinoa, roasted sweet potato, avocado, spinach, tomatoes, and an egg or two.

She toasted the quinoa in olive oil and water until the grains were fluffy, then roasted the sweet potato until golden. She sautéed the spinach and tomatoes briefly, added the egg, and mixed everything together. The dish was served in a white ceramic bowl, with the warm quinoa at the bottom, colorful vegetables around the edges, and a drizzle of lemon-olive oil on top.

Her aroma was earthy, warm, and slightly sweet—quinoa, roasted sweet potato, and fresh herbs. The presentation was neat and cheerful: the bowl looked inviting with its contrasting textures and vibrant colors, suitable for a quiet morning breakfast. She prepared it for a relaxed Sunday morning breakfast, using the meal as a peaceful way to begin her day before applying for her new job at the clinic and studying for her medical assistant certification.
