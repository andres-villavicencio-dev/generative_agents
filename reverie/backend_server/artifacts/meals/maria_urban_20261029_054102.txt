On Wednesday, February 15, Maria prepared a simple Mediterranean-Style Quinoa and Vegetable Bowl for her lunch. She combined cooked quinoa with roasted sweet potato, bell peppers, broccoli, chickpeas, cucumber, and a lemon-tahini dressing. For the dressing, she whisked olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a touch of cumin.

Her preparation method was straightforward: the vegetables were roasted in the oven until crisp and slightly caramelized, then rinsed and tossed with the dressing. The quinoa was cooked with water until al dente, drained, and combined with the other ingredients. The meal was presented in a colorful, rustic ceramic bowl, with the golden squash and crisp broccoli adding visual contrast.

The aroma was warm and savory, dominated by roasted squash, garlic, and the subtle warmth of cumin, while the lemon and tahini brought a bright, citrusy finish. Maria ate it alone in her home kitchen, taking the meal to work afterward. The lunch was neither rushed nor overly elaborate—it felt nourishing and thoughtful, reflecting her organized, budget-conscious approach to cooking while she searched for steady work.
