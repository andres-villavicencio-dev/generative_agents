Meal: “Breadless” Vegetable Breakfast Bowl with Scrambled Eggs and Fresh Herbs  
Key Ingredients: Avocado, spinach, cherry tomatoes, red onion, whole-grain oatmeal, eggs, olive oil, lemon juice, chili flakes, and a pinch of smoked paprika.  

Maria prepared the meal in a small skillet. She sautéed the red onion and tomatoes in olive oil until translucent, then added the spinach and chopped garlic to wilt quickly. After seasoning with lemon juice, chili flakes, and paprika, she scrambled two eggs and folded them in. Finally, she added the whole-grain oatmeal and topped it with sliced avocado and cherry tomatoes.  

The aroma was earthy, smoky, and bright—a warm blend of garlic, herbs, toasted grains, and the sharpness of lemon. It smelled like a cozy Sunday breakfast but with enough spice to feel purposeful.  

She presented the bowls in a simple, unadorned way: one bowl served immediately, the other arranged neatly beside it. The small, balanced meal replaced the usual bread and coffee-bean breakfast, offering Maria a comforting, budget-conscious option for a quiet Wednesday morning.
