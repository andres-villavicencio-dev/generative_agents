Maria prepared a Budget Veggie Breakfast Burrito for her Wednesday morning. She used whole-grain tortillas, shredded spinach, diced tomatoes, onions, canned black beans, shredded cheddar, and a splash of lemon juice. She wrapped the ingredients in a warm tortilla and rolled it into a compact, colorful bundle. The burrito was wrapped tightly and placed on a white plate with a small side of fruit.

The kitchen smelled of toasted nuts, warm cheddar, fresh herbs, and the rich aroma of spices. Maria arranged the burrito in the center of the plate, garnishing it with extra spinach and lemon slices. The presentation was simple and cheerful, with bright green and orange accents balanced by the golden cheddar.

The occasion was a quiet, solitary breakfast after a long day. Maria had no confirmed appointment for that day, so the meal was a small, affordable treat. It was neither a special celebration nor a formal meal, but an organized, satisfying way to start the day.
