Dinner: Smoked Chicken with Lemon-Herb Roasted Potatoes

Maria prepared a simple, nourishing meal for her Wednesday night before heading out for a park hike. She sliced chicken breasts with the skin on and roasted them with garlic, lemon, rosemary, thyme, and a little olive oil until the skin was crisp and the meat was golden. The potatoes were peeled, sliced, and tossed with olive oil, salt, pepper, and the same aromatics.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasting chicken and the bright scent of lemon and herbs. She arranged the chicken on a platter, arranged the potatoes around it, and drizzled a final drizzle of lemon juice over the dish. The meal looked balanced, elegant, and ready to be enjoyed as a quiet family dinner.

She prepared it without much time pressure, choosing ingredients that kept her budget in check while satisfying her hunger. The meal was simple, comforting, and a small but satisfying reward for a productive day.
