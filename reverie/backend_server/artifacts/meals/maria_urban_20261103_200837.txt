Maria prepared Oat and Walnut Breakfast Bowl, a simple, warming meal that reflected her mindful approach to food. She gathered oats, rolled oats, cooked quinoa, dried berries, sliced banana, walnuts, chia seeds, and a few slices of whole-grain toast. She cooked the oatmeal with water and a teaspoon of coconut milk, then topped it with the nut toppings and banana before serving it in a small ceramic bowl with a piece of toast.

The meal had a clean, comforting aroma of oats, warm milk, toasted nuts, and sweet berries. It was presented in a bright white bowl, arranged neatly with the oatmeal and toppings, and accompanied by a small glass of water. Maria made it on Sunday, February 19, before beginning her job applications and fundraising work. She did not expect to eat it again until Monday, treating breakfast as a quiet way to begin the new week.
