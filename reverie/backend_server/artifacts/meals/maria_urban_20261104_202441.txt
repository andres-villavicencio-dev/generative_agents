Maria prepared a Warm Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast Sandwich for a quiet Monday morning. The dish featured a grilled chicken breast, sautéed spinach, tomatoes, red onion, and crispy bacon or sausage, all served on a toasted English muffin with a thin layer of melted cheese.

Maria cooked the chicken in a skillet with a little olive oil, seasoning it with salt and pepper before baking it until the meat was firm and the juices were clear. The vegetables were quickly stir-fried until crisp, then layered with the chicken. A slice of cheddar was added just before baking so the cheese would melt.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the scent of toasted chicken, savory herbs, caramelized onions, and the warm smell of melted cheese. She arranged the sandwich on a white plate and placed a small side of sliced apple and a cup of black coffee nearby, letting the ingredients’ textures and flavors suggest a simple, satisfying start to her week.
