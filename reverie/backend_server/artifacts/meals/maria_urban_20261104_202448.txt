Maria prepared Herb-Lemon Caprese Quinoa Bowl for a relaxed weekday breakfast. The key ingredients were quinoa, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, fresh basil, cherry tomatoes, a lemon dressing, and feta cheese. She rinsed the quinoa and cooked it with a little water until tender but still firm, then added the tomatoes, cucumber, and basil before seasoning with salt, pepper, and lemon juice.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of toasted quinoa, the bright citrus of the lemon, and the fresh herb notes of basil. She arranged the colorful bowl on a wooden breakfast table, placing the crisp vegetables, golden quinoa, and creamy feta in a neat, balanced pattern while adding a small lemon wedge for garnish.

The occasion was a quiet, solitary morning of settling into town. It was not a celebratory meal, but a thoughtful, nourishing breakfast designed to help her feel settled and focused as she continued her job hunt and college studies.
