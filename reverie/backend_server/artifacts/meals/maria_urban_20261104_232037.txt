Maria prepared a simple Hearty Lentil & Vegetable Soup, a comforting one-pot meal that reflected her organized, budget-conscious lifestyle.

She simmered a vegetable and lentil base with carrots, celery, onions, bell peppers, garlic, and a pinch of cumin, then added tomatoes and a few dried herbs. The soup was gently simmered for twenty minutes until the lentils were tender and the vegetables were softened. She served it in a clear glass bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.

The aroma was warm and earthy—the gentle scent of sautéed garlic, carrots, and herbs mingled with the savory, slightly nutty aroma of the lentils. She had served the bowl in her kitchen for dinner, but it also felt like the first real step of her new routine. The meal was modest in appearance and taste, but it balanced her morning walk and evening craft time, offering the quiet satisfaction of a well-prepared evening.
