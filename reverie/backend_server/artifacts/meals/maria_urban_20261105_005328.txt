Breakfast: “Sunrise” Oat Porridge with Fig and Blueberry

Maria prepared a warm, nourishing porridge in her small kitchen. The dish consisted of rolled oats, a splash of almond milk, cinnamon, a pinch of nutmeg, chopped figs, and fresh blueberries. She toasted the oats in a pan, then gently stirred in the warm milk and berries. The aroma rose from the kitchen—creamy, nutty, sweet, and slightly earthy, like a quiet morning awakening.

She arranged the porridge in a simple white bowl and scattered the figs and blueberries over the top, letting the vibrant colors stand out against the golden oats. The breakfast was a modest, solitary treat designed to provide steady energy before she began her 8 a.m. job search and park hike.
