Dish: Chicken and Vegetable Rice Bowl with Roasted Tomatoes

Key ingredients: boneless chicken breast, rice, cherry tomatoes, bell pepper, onions, garlic, olive oil, lemon, and fresh herbs.

Preparation method: Maria roasted the chicken and vegetables in the oven, then stirred the cooked rice and warm chicken into a bowl with chopped tomatoes, garlic, and lemon. She added a splash of water or broth and a few herbs to finish the rice.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the savory scent of toasted chicken, caramelized onions, and garlic, accompanied by the sharp brightness of lemon.

Presentation: She arranged the colorful vegetables and golden chicken around the bowl of fluffy rice, garnishing with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon.

Occasion: It was her first solo meal in the kitchen after opening the café. She prepared it alone for comfort and to begin her day focused, organized, and ready to face the challenge of finding steady work.
