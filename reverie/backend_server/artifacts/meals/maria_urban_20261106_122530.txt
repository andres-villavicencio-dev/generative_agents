Maria prepared a hearty Mediterranean Oatmeal with Herbs and Fruit, the breakfast that made her feel both grounded and relaxed. She mixed rolled oats with steamed milk, cinnamon, vanilla, a pinch of sea salt, and dried figs, then stirred in fresh mint and a generous handful of walnuts. For the presentation, she arranged the oatmeal in a shallow bowl and placed the walnuts, cinnamon sticks, and figs beside it, with a small bowl of honey nearby. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of oats, cinnamon, mint, and fresh fruit.

The occasion was a quiet moment of self-care after a full and purposeful day. By eating slowly, Maria let the meal nourish her body and mind, using the oatmeal as a peaceful reminder of the stability and balance she had worked toward.
