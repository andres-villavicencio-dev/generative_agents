For Monday, Maria made Grilled Lemon-Garlic Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables. She used chicken breasts, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, and a pinch of salt, then roasted carrots, sweet potatoes, and red peppers in the oven until the skin was lightly crispy. She grilled the chicken until it reached an internal temperature of 165°F and served it over a bed of the roasted vegetables.

The aroma was bright and comforting: the sharp scent of lemon mingled with garlic and warm roasted root flavors, while the savory chicken added a savory, smoky note. Maria plated the meal in a simple white bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a drizzle of olive oil. It was prepared for a quiet Sunday evening dinner, a relaxed occasion where she could focus on her food without distractions.
