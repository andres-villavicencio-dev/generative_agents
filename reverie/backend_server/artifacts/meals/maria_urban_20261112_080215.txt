Maria prepared Crispy Chickpea Salad with Lemon-Herb Dressing for a simple lunch on a Monday morning. The key ingredients were canned chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, green bell pepper, apple, celery, and a dressing made from olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, and a splash of vinegar. She toasted the chickpeas in a skillet until golden and crisp, then chopped the vegetables and tossed them with a light dressing. The warm chickpeas added a pleasant nuttiness, while the crisp vegetables kept the salad refreshing.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the earthy smell of toasted chickpeas, the sharp citrus of lemon, and the savory hint of garlic. She arranged the salad on a white plate and topped it with a few bright green parsley leaves, creating a clean, colorful presentation. It was a quiet, solitary lunch before her day of applying for café and clinic positions, with no particular occasion to celebrate.
