Maria prepared Eggplant and Tomato Breakfast Salad, a simple, filling brunch that turned a Tuesday morning into a calm moment.

She sliced a firm eggplant into thin rounds, tossed it with a few chopped tomatoes, and whisked eggs until the whites were fully set. The salad was dressed with olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt, while fresh basil, feta, and whole-grain toast completed the plate. She arranged the salad on a white plate and topped it with the golden toast, creating a bright, warm presentation.

As she ate, the aroma of roasted garlic, sweet tomatoes, fresh basil, and the rich creaminess of the eggs filled the kitchen. It was a quiet, purposeful meal—her only breakfast this morning. She had prepared it without rushing, keeping the ingredients simple and the mood peaceful, though she still had a busy day ahead.
