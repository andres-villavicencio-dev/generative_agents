Maria prepared Saffron-Basted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Grains for her Tuesday lunch. She basted a whole chicken with butter, lemon zest, garlic, and a little saffron, while roasting carrots, onions, and sweet potatoes with olive oil, rosemary, and thyme. She finished the meal with a small plate of quinoa, whole-grain bread, and a few fresh herbs.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized chicken, rosemary, and toasted grains. Maria arranged the golden chicken and vegetables on a white ceramic plate, garnished with parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice, and placed a small bowl of grains beside her. The presentation was simple, rustic, and inviting—the perfect balance of color and texture for a nourishing lunch.

It was a quiet, solitary lunch while Maria walked through the park. The meal was meant to be simple, satisfying, and reflective of her organized, cautious approach to daily life. Afterward, she prepared a warm loaf of leftover bread and drank water to replenish her energy.
