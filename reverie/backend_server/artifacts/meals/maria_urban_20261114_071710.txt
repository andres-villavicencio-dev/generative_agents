Maria prepared Grain Bowl with Roasted Vegetables and Hard-Boiled Eggs. The key ingredients were whole-wheat bread, eggs, plain milk, chopped broccoli, carrots, red onion, and a handful of spinach. She toasted the bread, boiled the eggs, and roasted the vegetables until crisp and lightly golden. For the sauce, she combined the milk, lemon juice, garlic powder, and olive oil, tossing everything until the eggs were cooked through and the vegetables were tender.

The aroma was warm and comforting: the toasted bread, smoky vegetables, earthy garlic, and creamy milk filling the kitchen. She presented the meal in a simple white bowl, with the broccoli, carrots, and spinach arranged neatly around the eggs and bread.

The occasion was a simple lunch at home on Monday, February 13, as Maria settled into town while continuing her job applications, park hikes, and crafts. The meal was inexpensive and easy to prepare, reflecting her organized, budget-conscious lifestyle.
