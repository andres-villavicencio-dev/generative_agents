Marisol Vega prepared Spicy Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a warm, comforting main dish for dinner with Hua Lai after working through the day.

The recipe called for boneless chicken thighs, onion, garlic, bell peppers, carrots, cauliflower, and a savory tomato-herb sauce. She sautéed the chicken until golden, roasted the vegetables with their skin on, and simmered everything together in a rich sauce seasoned with cumin, chili powder, garlic, and a splash of soy sauce.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm herbs, roasted garlic, and savory spices. The chicken was tender and lightly glazed, while the vegetables retained a bright, crisp texture. She plated the dish with the chicken arranged on the center plate and the colorful vegetables around it, garnishing the sides with lemon wedges and fresh herbs.

The meal was a simple, practical family dinner, and Marisol served it with a quiet sense of satisfaction.
