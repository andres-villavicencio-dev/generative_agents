On Saturday, February 18, Marisol prepared a simple but comforting breakfast of Sourdough Toast with Avocado and Eggs. She sliced a crusty sourdough, toasted it lightly in a pan, and topped it with sliced avocado, a cracked egg, and a generous sprinkling of fresh chives. The warm bread and crisp egg made the meal feel nourishing without being overly heavy.

She cooked the eggs over medium heat, adding a little olive oil and a pinch of sea salt, then rested them briefly before slicing them into perfect halves. She arranged the toast on a white plate, placing a small green herb garnish beside each egg and a small bowl of lemon juice nearby. The aroma of toasted bread, rich egg, and creamy avocado filled the kitchen, while the bright colors and clean presentation made the meal feel inviting and balanced.

The occasion was a quiet Saturday morning before her morning routine and job search. Marisol prepared the meal to give herself a sense of calm and purpose, then used the remaining ingredients to make her coffee.
