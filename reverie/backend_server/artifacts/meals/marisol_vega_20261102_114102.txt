The aroma was inviting: the richness of the eggs, nutty toast, sweet potato, and citrus finish combined into a hearty, savory scent that reminded her of home. She prepared it herself in the kitchen, using fresh ingredients and simple equipment, and she served it with a quiet sense of pride.
