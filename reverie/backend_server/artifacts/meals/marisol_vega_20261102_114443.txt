The meal was a simple Lemon Herb Chicken and Vegetable Bowl, prepared as Marisol would for a quiet Saturday lunch. She sautéed chicken breast with garlic and lemon zest, then added cherry tomatoes, cucumber, zucchini, and bell peppers. Herbs, a splash of olive oil, and a little soy sauce were stirred in before the bowl was ready.

The kitchen filled with the bright aroma of roasted garlic, fresh herbs, and savory chicken. The vegetables added a crisp, refreshing contrast, while the lemon gave the dish a warm, inviting brightness. Marisol arranged the colorful ingredients in a shallow white bowl and placed a paper plate on top, finishing with a sprig of parsley.

She prepared it for a relaxed lunch during her commute home from work. The meal was comforting and practical—easy enough to eat quickly while commuting, yet satisfying enough to feel nourished. It reflected her organized, determined nature: simple, warm, and ready to bring her to work.
