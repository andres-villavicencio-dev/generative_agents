Marisol prepared Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast Bowl for a Saturday family breakfast. The dish included shredded chicken, cooked spinach, diced tomatoes, onions, mushrooms, and whole-grain rice, with a little yogurt and fresh herbs for extra flavor. She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and a pinch of garlic powder, then gently cooked it in a skillet until the edges were crisp. The vegetables were sautéed briefly before being combined with the rice and chicken in a large bowl.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of toasted bread, caramelized onions, garlic, and herbs—a comforting aroma that reminded her of home. Marisol served the bowl in a simple white ceramic dish, garnished with chopped parsley and a dollop of yogurt. It was prepared for a relaxed Saturday morning gathering, with everyone bringing fruit and tea for a slow, nourishing breakfast together.
