Marisol Vega prepared Roasted Garlic Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice for a simple Saturday lunch.

She roasted bone-in chicken thighs with garlic, lemon, rosemary, and oregano until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. While the chicken roasted, she sautéed rice with olive oil, chopped onion, celery, and garlic, finishing the rice with fresh parsley and a splash of lemon juice. The chicken was sliced and served alongside fluffy, savory rice, lemon wedges, and a small bowl of crisp green vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herb-rich chicken, and toasted rice. The meal was presented on a white ceramic plate, arranged around a large piece of parchment paper, with a bright lemon wedge, parsley garnish, and a small piece of crusty bread.

It was a relaxed, nourishing lunch for Marisol after a busy Saturday. She made it with her own hands, combining the practicality of her former warehouse life with the comfort of a home-cooked meal.
