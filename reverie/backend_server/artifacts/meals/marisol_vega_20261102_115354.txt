Marisol Vega prepared Simple Pan-Seared Chicken with Herb Sauce and Roasted Vegetables, a warm, practical lunch for a Saturday Hobbs Cafe shift.

The meal included boneless chicken thighs, potatoes, carrots, and bell peppers, seasoned with garlic, oregano, lemon zest, and a little soy sauce. After washing and drying the vegetables, Marisol seared the chicken in a skillet over medium heat until golden brown, then sliced it into bite-sized pieces. She poured a little olive oil and vinegar into the pan, added sliced garlic and herbs, and cooked the vegetables until crisp. A simple lemon-herb sauce was made by blending the pan juices with lemon juice, a squeeze of extra virgin olive oil, salt, and pepper, before tossing everything together.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized garlic, and warm vegetables. The golden chicken, bright orange carrots, and crisp potatoes were arranged on a white plate, with the glossy sauce drizzled over the top. It was ready for a quick Hobbs Cafe lunch, with the possibility of adding extra sauce and seasoning if she had time to make it fresh.
