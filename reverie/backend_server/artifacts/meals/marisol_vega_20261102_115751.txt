Dish Name: Warm Vegetable Rice Bowl with Chicken and Green Beans

Key Ingredients: cooked rice, shredded chicken, cherry tomatoes, bell pepper, onions, broccoli, green beans, avocado, salsa, and a drizzle of lemon-herb dressing.

Preparation Method: Marisol washed and chopped the vegetables, stir-fried the chicken in a skillet with garlic and soy sauce, then tossed everything together with the rice, vegetables, and dressing. She added avocado and sliced tomatoes just before serving.

Aroma: Warm, savory, and lightly aromatic—richly seasoned chicken, caramelized onions, and the fresh crunch of vegetables carried a comforting scent of cooking.

Presentation: The meal was served in a shallow white bowl, with the colorful vegetables arranged around the rice and chicken. A small side of crisp green beans and avocado slices added a bright pop of color, while the glossy lemon-herb dressing was drizzled over the top.

Occasion: This was a simple lunch prepared for a quiet Saturday. After a full day of organizing groceries, cleaning the kitchen, and preparing for a potential Hobbs Cafe shift, Marisol had prepared enough time for herself to eat something comforting and satisfying before heading out.
