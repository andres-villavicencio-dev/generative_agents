On Saturday, February 18, Marisol Vega prepared a hearty breakfast for the Hobbs Cafe: Mediterranean-Style Egg and Spinach Sandwiches. The meal came together in a warm, practical kitchen filled with the scent of toasted bread, toasted nuts, and fresh herbs.

Marisol used two slices of sourdough bread, a generous handful of spinach, a dozen eggs, a little olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, red pepper flakes, and a pinch of salt. She boiled the spinach until crisp, scrambled the eggs into soft, golden curds, and layered the filling between the bread slices before toasting the top for a crispy crust. She added a sprinkle of feta, chives, and a dash of lemon zest for brightness.

The aroma was comforting and aromatic—fresh herbs, toasted nuts, garlic, and the savory richness of eggs filling the kitchen. The sandwiches were arranged neatly on a white platter, with a small bowl of lemon-tahini dressing beside them. Marisol served them to a small group of cafe staff, treating them to a familiar, nourishing breakfast before the day’s shift began.
