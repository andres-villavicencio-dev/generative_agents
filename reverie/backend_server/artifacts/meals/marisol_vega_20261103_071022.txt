Marisol Vega’s dinner was a Clover Honey Apple-Orange Stew, a warm, colorful family-style meal she prepared in her kitchen after a full day of organizing and job applications. The stew combined tender apples, oranges, and clover honey, with carrots, celery, diced potatoes, and a splash of vegetable stock. A few fresh green onions and a pinch of salt rounded out the flavors.

She began by sautéing chopped onions and celery in a large skillet until softened, then added the apples and carrots and simmered them for about ten minutes. Marisol stirred in the oranges, clover honey, and vegetable stock, letting the fruit soften and the stew simmer uncovered until it thickened slightly. She added the diced potatoes and cooked until the potatoes were tender, stirring occasionally so the apple-clover mix would not burn.

The aroma filling the kitchen was unmistakably citrusy and honeyed, with the earthy sweetness of clover honey and the fresh brightness of the oranges blending with the gentle caramelization of the apples. She served the stew in a white ceramic bowl, spooning it over a bed of sliced carrots and green onions, and drizzed a little extra honey over the top. It was a cozy Saturday dinner, prepared for an evening of quiet conversation and relaxation with friends, though she also carried the lingering concern of still searching for a confirmed shift at the Hobbs Cafe.
