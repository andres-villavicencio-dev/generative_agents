Marisol Vega prepared a simple Hearty Garlic Chicken and Potatoes for a quiet Saturday dinner. The dish used boneless chicken thighs, potatoes, onions, garlic, olive oil, paprika, thyme, salt, and pepper. She sautéed the chicken in a pan until golden, then cooked the potatoes on the same stove until crisp. A little olive oil, lemon juice, and fresh herbs were added at the end to keep the vegetables bright.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic and herbs, with savory chicken and the crisp scent of potatoes filling the small kitchen. Marisol plated the chicken in a white bowl, topped with browned potatoes and a drizzle of lemon-herb oil, then served it with a small plate of bread. The meal was prepared for an informal family-style dinner around 6:30 PM, a relaxed occasion after she spent the morning organizing, applying for a Hobbs Cafe shift, and following up with Carmen Ortiz.
