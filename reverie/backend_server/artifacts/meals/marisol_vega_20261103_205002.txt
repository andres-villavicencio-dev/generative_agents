On a quiet Monday morning, Marisol prepared a warm fruit and oatmeal breakfast for herself and her roommate, Hua. She stirred a bowl of rolled oats with cinnamon, a tablespoon of honey, and a generous handful of fresh berries. A small bowl of yogurt and sliced banana made a simple, comforting side. She topped the oats with sliced apples and a pinch of nutmeg, then added a few raisins for sweetness.

The kitchen filled with the familiar aroma of oats, cinnamon, and fruit. Marisol arranged the colorful breakfast on a simple white ceramic plate, using a small spoon to place each ingredient neatly beside the oatmeal. The presentation was modest but welcoming, with the bright berries and golden oats creating a cheerful contrast.

The occasion was a quiet, unhurried morning before the day’s work began. Marisol’s warm, determined smile suggested she was eager to start a new chapter in Smallville.
