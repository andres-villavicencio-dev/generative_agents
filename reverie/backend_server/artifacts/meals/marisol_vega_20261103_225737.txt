Marisol Vega prepared Hearty Vegetable Soup with Roast Chicken and Rice, a warm, comforting meal prepared for dinner on Monday, February 13.  

Key ingredients included chicken thighs, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, tomato, vegetable broth, soy sauce, ginger, and rice. She began by searing the chicken until golden, then added the vegetables and cooked everything slowly in the pot. The aroma of roasted chicken, aromatic herbs, and a rich, savory broth filled the kitchen as she finished simmering the soup.  

She finished it with a generous portion of fluffy white rice, serving the soup and rice in a simple, round white bowl. The meal looked inviting and practical, with the yellow carrots and orange vegetables adding a cheerful pop of color. Marisol had prepared it for herself and her new home, Hua Lai, offering a warm, nourishing meal after a busy day.
