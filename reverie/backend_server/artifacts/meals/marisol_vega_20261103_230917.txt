Marisol prepared a Thai Basil Chicken Salad with Lemon-Herb Rice, a fresh, warming lunch she brought to the Harvey Oak Supply Store.

The meal consisted of thinly sliced chicken breast marinated in lime juice, garlic, and chili, topped with fresh basil, cilantro, and sliced red onion. It was served over crisp lettuce, cucumber, and tomato, with the rice prepared by toasting the grains and adding warm vegetable broth. Marisol tossed the ingredients together and served them in a simple white bowl, with a small lemon wedge and a pinch of red pepper flakes on the side.

The kitchen smelled of toasted rice, basil, garlic, citrus, and warm chili. The presentation was bright and inviting: the pale chicken and vivid greens stood out against the creamy white rice. Marisol served it at lunchtime in February, after preparing and serving it herself. It was a practical, home-style lunch meant to keep her energized while she looked for work opportunities at Johnson Park and Harvey Oak.
