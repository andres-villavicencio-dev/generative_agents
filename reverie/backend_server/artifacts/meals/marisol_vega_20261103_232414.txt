Marisol Vega prepared a Fresh Harvest Bowl with Roasted Vegetables and Warm Rice, a simple but nourishing lunch that reflected her practical, warm style. The meal included cooked brown rice, roasted carrots, bell pepper, zucchini, and corn, along with a lemon-tahini dressing and a small handful of fresh herbs. It was seasoned lightly with sea salt, black pepper, and a touch of cumin, giving it a bright, earthy aroma and a crisp, warmly golden appearance.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the vegetables were arranged neatly beside the rice, garnished with chopped parsley and a sprinkle of smoked paprika. Marisol served it at the Harvey Oak Supply Store, where she had lunch with Hua Lai during a slow afternoon break. It was a quiet, community-oriented meal—one of the few opportunities she had to pause and connect, showing her practical side and genuine warmth in a small-town setting.
