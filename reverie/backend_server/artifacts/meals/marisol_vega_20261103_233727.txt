Marisol Vega prepared Crispy Chicken with Herb‑Broiled Vegetables and Roasted Potatoes, a hearty, family-style dinner for her and Hua. The dish used boneless chicken breasts, carrots, bell peppers, onions, mushrooms, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, rosemary, thyme, and salt, with the potatoes cut into large wedges and seasoned with salt and pepper.

Marisol cubed the chicken and coated it with flour, then pan‑fried it in olive oil until golden and crispy. She sautéed the vegetables until crisp-tender, then roasted the potatoes in the oven until the skin was blistered and the inside was fluffy. She finished the meal with a lemon-herb pan sauce, tossing the vegetables and potatoes together with the pan juices.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, and caramelized chicken. The presentation was simple but inviting: the golden chicken rested beside the bright vegetables and golden potatoes, with a small white bowl of lemon sauce nearby. It was a warm, nourishing dinner shared for dinner at 7:00 p.m., before Marisol went to bed at 10:00 p.m.
