Marisol prepared a simple fruit and oatmeal breakfast, a comforting, practical meal made for herself before starting her shift at Hobbs Cafe.

Dish name: Fruit and Oatmeal Bowl  
Key ingredients: rolled oats, water or milk, cinnamon, a spoonful of honey, sliced bananas, strawberries, blueberries, and a few raisins.  
Preparation method: Marisol washed and chopped the fruit, then cooked the oats in a pot with water or milk. She stirred the mixture, added the honey and cinnamon, and topped it with the fresh fruit before letting it cool.  
Aroma: The kitchen filled with the warm scent of oats, sweet honey, cinnamon, and ripe fruit.  
Presentation: She arranged the oatmeal in a shallow white bowl, scattering the colorful fruit over the top. A small wooden spoon and a folded napkin were placed beside it for easy eating.  
Occasion: This was a quiet morning meal on Tuesday, February 14, as Marisol prepared for her first 8:30 a.m. shift at Hobbs Cafe. The breakfast reflected her practical, organized nature—simple, nourishing, and ready to fuel her day.
