Marisol prepared a simple Garlic Butter Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for dinner. She marinated chicken breasts with minced garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt, then pan-seared them until golden. She roasted carrots, bell peppers, and zucchini with olive oil, salt, and a little thyme until crisp, then combined everything in a skillet.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of garlic, roasted vegetables, and savory chicken. Marisol served it in a shallow white bowl, plating the chicken on one side and the colorful vegetables beside it. She added a small glass of water and a lemon wedge for brightness, making the meal look clean and inviting.

This was a relaxed weekday dinner shared with Hua Lai. After a long day of looking for work and exploring the community, the meal felt comforting and practical—a simple, nourishing way to celebrate the night they were together.
