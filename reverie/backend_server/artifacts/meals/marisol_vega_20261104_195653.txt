Marisol Vega prepared a Chicken and Vegetable One-Pot Dinner for Monday evening. The dish consists of boneless chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, onions, garlic, and rice, seasoned with soy sauce, ginger, turmeric, and a little oil.

She began by sautéing the vegetables in a large pot until they were tender, then added the chicken and browned it until the skin was crisp. She stirred in the sauce and rice, covering the pot to let everything absorb the flavor. The aroma rose warmly from the pot: savory chicken, roasted carrots, gentle garlic, and a subtle hint of turmeric.

The final presentation was simple and inviting. The colorful vegetables were arranged around the platter, with the golden chicken on top and the fluffy rice mixed in. Marisol topped it with a final drizzle of soy sauce and served it with a small side of steamed green beans.

The occasion was a quiet dinner for her new home with Hua Lai. The meal was practical and comforting—designed to be easy to prepare while still nourishing her body. It was meant to reflect her grounded nature and the warmth of her new life in Smallville.
