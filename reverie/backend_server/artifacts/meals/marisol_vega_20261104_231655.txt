On a Tuesday morning, Marisol prepared a simple Sautéed Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Rice for lunch. She used a small oven-safe pan with olive oil, minced garlic, fresh parsley, and a pinch of cumin. Chicken breast was seasoned with salt, pepper, lemon juice, and a little soy sauce, then seared until golden. Meanwhile, yellow potatoes, carrots, and bell peppers were roasted with paprika and a splash of water until tender. The finished dish was served over fluffy white rice, with extra herbs and a squeeze of lemon.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasting vegetables, toasted garlic, and savory poultry. Marisol arranged the colorful vegetables around the chicken and placed the golden rice neatly beside it. The presentation was practical and inviting—each ingredient clearly visible, with plenty of white space around the plate.

The meal was meant for a quiet lunch at home on February 14, the day when she continued checking the community bulletin board and visiting Harvey Oak Supply Store and Willows Market for steady work. Afterward, she hoped to relax with Sharon, Carl, and Jenny, taking time to enjoy the simple comfort of a home-cooked meal.
