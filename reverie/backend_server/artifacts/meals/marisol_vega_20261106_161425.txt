Marisol Vega prepared a simple but comforting Scrambled Eggs with Toast and Herbed Sourdough. She used three eggs, a small handful of garlic, salt, pepper, and dried herbs, then gently scrambled them in a pan and let them cool slightly before spreading the warm slices of sourdough toast over them. A little olive oil and a sprinkle of fresh herbs added a bright finish.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of butter, toasted bread, and savory eggs. To serve, she arranged the golden toast and plump, glossy eggs on a white plate, adding a small bowl of chopped parsley for a fresh accent. The meal was a simple weekday breakfast—perfect for starting her day after moving into her new home and before her morning routine at Johnson Park.
