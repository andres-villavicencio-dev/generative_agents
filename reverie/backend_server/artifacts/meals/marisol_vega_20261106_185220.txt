For her first day at Hobbs Cafe, Marisol prepared a hearty Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Soup. The soup featured tender chicken pieces, carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, and a mild tomato base, all simmered with plenty of chicken broth. She sliced the vegetables carefully and added them to the pan, then simmered everything until the chicken was cooked through and the vegetables were tender. She stirred in a handful of spinach at the end to add a bright green color and extra freshness.

The kitchen smelled warm, aromatic, and inviting—richly of roasted chicken, garlic, carrots, and the gentle scent of broth. Marisol presented the soup in a long, communal serving bowl, garnishing it with a generous drizzle of olive oil and a sprinkling of fresh herbs for the table. It was a comforting, practical dish prepared for the cafe’s Monday lunch, reflecting her practical, organized, and warm approach to her new work.
