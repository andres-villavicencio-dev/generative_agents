Marisol Vega prepared Smoked Chicken with Root Vegetables and Red Beans for breakfast. She seasoned sliced chicken breast with cumin, chili powder, garlic, and smoked paprika, then browned it in olive oil. Root vegetables—carrots, celery, onions, and potatoes—were sautéed until crisp, while red beans were slow-cooked until tender and served on the side. She made the dish fresh for her Tuesday routine, combining the savory chicken with the comforting warmth of the beans.

The meal had a rich, smoky aroma of cumin, roasted vegetables, and caramelized onions, with hints of chili and warm spices. It was presented simply but invitingly: the golden-brown chicken resting beside the crisp root vegetables and glossy red beans, arranged on a white plate with a small bowl of lime juice and a pinch of chili flakes nearby.

She made it on a quiet Tuesday morning at The Willows Market, after washing up and checking her shift. It was a simple, practical meal intended to energize her for the day’s work and help her feel grounded in the community around Smallville.
