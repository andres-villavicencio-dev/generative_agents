Marisol prepared a Warm Vegetable Breakfast Bowl, a simple but comforting dish to begin her Tuesday. The bowl featured sliced bagel, scrambled eggs, cheddar and feta cheese, cherry tomatoes, diced avocado, a handful of fresh herbs, croutons, and a sprinkle of black pepper. She added a splash of milk for creaminess and stirred in some spinach before serving.

The kitchen smelled of toasted bread, herbs, and warm dairy—the inviting aroma of a freshly made breakfast. She arranged the breakfast in a large white bowl, using a wooden spoon to gently fold the ingredients together. Her presentation was casual yet orderly, with the colorful vegetables and golden cheese adding visual interest.

The occasion was her first reliable breakfast after returning to Smallville. Though anxious about finding a steady job, Marisol wanted a dependable start to her day. The simple meal offered comfort without fuss, giving her time to settle in before her shift at The Willows Market.
