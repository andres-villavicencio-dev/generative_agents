Marisol prepared a Hearty Roast Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Herb Butter, a comforting meal for her Tuesday at The Willows Market. She chose it for its satisfying comfort and practical preparation.

The centerpiece was a whole chicken, roasted with garlic, rosemary, thyme, black pepper, and a splash of lemon juice. She also roasted carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and red onions, seasoning them with salt and a little paprika. Her herb butter—butter softened and infused with garlic, rosemary, and thyme—became the sauce, while fresh parsley and lemon wedges finished the plate.

She sautéed the chicken in olive oil until it turned golden, then basted it with the buttered vegetables. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, rosemary, and roasted potatoes.

The meal was served simply on a white plate, with the golden chicken and colorful vegetables arranged around it. It was prepared for a quiet Tuesday lunch—a practical, warm dinner suitable for working at the grocery store or police station.
