Marisol prepared Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables. She cooked chicken breasts with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and lemon zest, while carrots, sweet potatoes, and parsnips were roasted with olive oil, paprika, and a little butter. The vegetables were sliced or cut into bite-sized pieces and arranged around the pan. A small bowl of lemon-herb chicken sauce was served alongside the hearty, golden vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, and lemon. Marisol presented the meal neatly on a ceramic serving platter, garnishing it with a few fresh rosemary sprigs and serving the chicken on a crisp platter. It was a simple, comforting dinner for Tuesday, February 14—the day she had just arrived at The Willows Market and was beginning a busy shift. The meal reflected the practical, grounded side of her life: easy to prepare, filling, and fitting with the steady rhythm of grocery work and neighborhood life.
