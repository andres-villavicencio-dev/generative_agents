Meal: Marisol’s Warm Ham Sandwich and Berry-Crusted Pastries Batch  
Key ingredients: cooked ham, sliced turkey, cheddar, lettuce, tomato, whole-grain bread, ripe berries, cinnamon, maple syrup, and a light egg wash.  
Preparation method: Marisol toasted the bread in a dry pan, spread the ham, turkey, cheese, and vegetables on each slice, and baked it in a 350°F (175°C) oven. In the meantime, she prepared the pastries by coating the dough in the egg wash and baking them until golden. She made a small batch of fruit salad with the berries and drizzled them with maple syrup.  
Aroma: Warm, savory, slightly sweet, with hints of toasted bread, caramelized cheese, and fresh fruit.  
Presentation: The sandwiches were arranged neatly on a large serving platter, while the pastries were piled beside them. A small bowl of fruit salad, a drizzle of syrup, and a napkin for each guest completed the spread.  
Occasion: A modest lunch prepared for a small cafe gathering. Marisol wanted the meal to feel hearty and inviting, suitable for friends and fellow workers meeting in the community.
