On Tuesday, February 14, Marisol prepared Breakfast of the Day, a warm, nourishing brunch for her first morning at Hobbs Cafe. She started with pancakes made from flour, milk, eggs, a pinch of salt, and vanilla extract, pan-fried until golden and fluffy. A generous syrup of brown sugar, maple syrup, and cinnamon was drizzled over the pancakes, while blueberries, sliced bananas, and a few fresh strawberries were arranged on the plate. She topped it with a scoop of Greek yogurt and a drizzle of honey, finishing with a sprig of mint.

The kitchen smelled of toasted oats, warm milk, and fruit. She presented the breakfast in a simple white bowl, arranging the berries and banana slices neatly around the pancakes. It was a thoughtful, intimate breakfast meant for herself and her first morning colleague. The meal felt practical and comforting, reflecting Marisol’s steady approach to life—simple, prepared with care, and ready to make her work at Oak Hill College feel welcoming.
