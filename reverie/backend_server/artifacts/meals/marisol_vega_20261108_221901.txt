Marisol prepared a Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Soup for Tuesday’s lunch, using the canned vegetables, chicken broth, and pantry staples she had available. She marinated the chicken in olive oil and garlic powder, then simmered it with potatoes, carrots, onions, and celery. The soup was finished with a splash of lemon juice and served in a wide, shallow glass bowl.

The aroma was warm and comforting, filled with the rich scent of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, garlic, and the earthy fragrance of carrots. Marisol arranged the golden chicken and bright orange vegetables around a mound of creamy broth, garnishing the bowl with chopped parsley. The presentation was simple and inviting, suitable for a quiet Tuesday lunch.

The meal was prepared quickly at home, using mostly leftovers and pantry items rather than fresh produce. Its practical, wholesome nature made it a satisfying choice for a short lunch before heading toward the Hobbs Cafe shift.
