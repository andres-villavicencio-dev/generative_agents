Marisol prepared Hearty Lentil Stir-Fry with Vegetables and Tofu, a balanced, home-cooked meal for the evening before teaching. The dish used brown lentils, firm tofu, broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, onion, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, brown rice, and fresh cilantro. She marinated the tofu and vegetables, stir-fried them with the aromatics until lightly browned, and finished the lentils with sesame seeds and lime.

The aroma was earthy, warmly spiced, and lightly savory, carrying the familiar scent of dried herbs and toasted sesame. She plated it in a simple white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the rice and placing a portion of tofu on the side. The presentation was clean and inviting, suitable for a weekday dinner or an informal family meal.

She prepared it for quick dinner, using a small amount of time and ingredients so she could focus on her Monday plans and follow-up with the February 14 appointment. The practical, wholesome meal reflected her organized, warm nature and her desire to make everyday cooking enjoyable.
