Marisol prepared a hearty sandwich and pastry breakfast batch for the cafe. Her sandwiches used whole-grain bread, fresh tomato slices, crisp lettuce, creamy avocado, sliced radish, and a tangy mustard-honey dressing. She paired them with warm rolls and a batch of fluffy cinnamon-sugar pastries.

She began by toasting the rolls and heating the avocado, then layered the vegetables and filling carefully. The cinnamon-sugar dough was rolled out, filled with jam, and baked until golden. The kitchen grew warm with the scent of toasted bread, caramelized onions, and cinnamon.

When served, the pastries looked inviting in rows, while the sandwiches were arranged neatly on the serving platter. Marisol presented them with a warm smile, ready to greet her customers. The occasion was a cozy breakfast for the cafe, combining her organized, practical approach with a warm, welcoming atmosphere.
