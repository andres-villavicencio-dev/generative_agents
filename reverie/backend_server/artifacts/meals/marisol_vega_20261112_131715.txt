Marisol prepared a simple but hearty Hearty Vegetable Stir-Fry with Grilled Chicken and Quinoa.

She washed and dried the vegetables in batches—carrots, broccoli, snap peas, bell peppers, onions, and zucchini—then sliced them into bite-sized pieces. The chicken was marinated with garlic, lemon juice, cumin, and pepper before being grilled over medium heat. While the chicken cooked, she sautéed the prepared vegetables in a skillet until crisp-tender, then added the quinoa and a splash of broth to complete the dish. She finished it with a drizzle of soy sauce and a little fresh ginger.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, onions, and the earthy sweetness of the vegetables. She plated the meal in a shallow white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the golden chicken and neatly stacked quinoa grains, garnished with a few crisp herbs and a squeeze of lemon.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday meal at home, but Marisol treated the stir-fry as a practical way to use the groceries efficiently.
