Marisol prepared Slow-Simmered Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Basil Rice, a warm, home-cooked meal for herself on a Tuesday morning.

She sautéed carrots, onions, and celery in olive oil until golden, then added a whole chicken, garlic, and a splash of chicken broth. She covered the pan and simmered it gently until the chicken became tender and the vegetables softened. The rice was cooked separately and fluffed with lemon juice and fresh basil, adding a bright citrus aroma that balanced the savory, rich flavors of the chicken and vegetables.

The dish was plated with the chicken arranged prominently in the center, surrounded by the glazed vegetables. A bright lemon wedge and a small sprig of fresh basil rested beside it, while the golden rice and crisp onions completed the presentation. The meal was prepared for an early lunch, but its comforting aroma made her feel grounded and ready to start her day.
