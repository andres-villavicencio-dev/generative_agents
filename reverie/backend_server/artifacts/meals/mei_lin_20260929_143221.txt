Mei Lin’s Friday‑night dinner is Miso‑Glazed Salmon with Steamed Jasmine Rice—a comforting, nourishing dish that matches her nurturing nature. The salmon fillets are brushed with a sweet‑savory miso paste, baked until the skin crisps, and served atop fluffy jasmine rice. Key ingredients: fresh salmon, white miso, honey, ginger, scallions, and a side of pickled radish for brightness. The preparation is gentle—baking in a preheated tray—so minimal cleanup aligns with her “no‑interruptions” rule. A warm, earthy aroma of miso and toasted sesame fills the kitchen as she plates it on the center of the table, garnished with chopped scallions and a drizzle of sesame oil. The meal is simple yet special, honoring her love for helping others—perfect for a quiet evening after teaching at Oak Hill, celebrating family time and the calm routine that feeds both body and mind.
