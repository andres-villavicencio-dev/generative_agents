Mei Lin prepared a steaming bowl of miso-glazed salmon with roasted asparagus and quinoa, served on a simple white ceramic plate at her kitchen table. The dish combined tender salmon fillet glazed in rich, umami-rich miso sauce, crispy edges from broiling, alongside charred green asparagus spears and fluffy quinoa. Aromatic notes of garlic, soy, and warm earthiness filled the air during preparation. Presented neatly with a sprig of dill for color, it symbolized her nurturing nature—feeding both body and mind. She made this meal to honor her students’ progress and her family’s quiet Sunday ritual; a simple act of love on a busy day.
