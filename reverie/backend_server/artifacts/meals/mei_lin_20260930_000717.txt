Mei Lin prepared a simple yet comforting bowl of miso-glazed sweet potato with steamed broccoli and sesame seeds. Key ingredients include sweet potatoes, white miso paste, brown sugar, olive oil, broccoli florets, toasted sesame seeds, and sea salt. She roasted the sweet potatoes at 400°F until tender, then brushed them with a warm miso-sweet glaze before baking again for a caramelized finish. The steam from the broccoli released an earthy aroma, while sesame seeds added a nutty whisper. Served in a shallow white ceramic bowl on the kitchen counter, the meal felt like a quiet celebration of balance—perfect for morning calm and shared focus with John before his teaching day began.
