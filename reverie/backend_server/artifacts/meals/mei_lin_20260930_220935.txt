Mei Lin prepared baked salmon with steamed broccoli for a quiet weekday dinner after her morning teaching session. The salmon, seasoned simply with salt, pepper, and lemon zest, was roasted to golden perfection, its rich aroma filling the home as it cooled from the oven. Broccoli florets were blanched briefly and steamed until tender-crisp, their earthy scent balanced by the citrus brightness of lemon. Plated on a white ceramic dish in her living room corner, the meal looked light and intentional—two halves of salmon nestled atop crisp green broccoli. Served with a small bowl of lime-infused rice on the side, it was minimalist yet nourishing. The occasion? A peaceful, self-nourished break from the week’s responsibilities—a daily ritual of care, quiet and steady as her morning routine.
