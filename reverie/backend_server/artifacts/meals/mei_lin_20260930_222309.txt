Mei Lin’s dinner is baked salmon with steamed broccoli—simple yet nourishing, a quiet celebration of balance. The salmon, seasoned lightly with sea salt and dill, bakes in her oven until flaky and golden, its rich, smoky-sweet aroma mingling with the crisp scent of steaming broccoli. Prepped ahead, the meal is arranged on a white ceramic plate: the salmon at the center, vibrant green florets fanned around it like a natural crown. Served warm in the morning light, it feels like an act of care—meant to nourish her body after teaching and to honor the rhythm she’s built for John. This quiet meal is more than food; it’s a daily ritual of peace, shared without words, a small comfort before dinner tea begins at 6:00 PM.
