Mei Lin prepared baked salmon with steamed broccoli for a quiet, nourishing evening meal—an act of gentle care after her teaching day. The dish features seasoned salmon fillets, lightly coated in garlic and lemon, roasted until golden and flaky; vibrant green broccoli florets steamed to preserve their crispness. The aroma was subtly savory, with a whisper of citrus cutting through the richness. Presented simply on a white plate beside a small glass pitcher of chilled tea, it felt unhurried and intentional. This meal marked her return home from Oak Hill College, a daily ritual that honored both nourishment and peace—an intimate pause in her steady rhythm, shared with John as they settled into the evening’s calm.
