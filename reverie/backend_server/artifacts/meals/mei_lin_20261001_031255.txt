Mei Lin prepared baked salmon with steamed broccoli for a cozy Friday evening with her husband John. The salmon fillet is seasoned simply with lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper before being placed under the oven’s gentle heat until perfectly flaky. The broccoli florets are blanched briefly, then covered, preserving their bright green color and tender‑crisp texture. As it bakes, a faint aroma of citrus mingles with the nutty scent of roasted vegetables, filling the kitchen with warmth. Presented on a simple white plate, the salmon is nestled beside the steamed broccoli, garnished lightly with lemon zest for a pop of color. The dish reflects Mei Lin’s nurturing nature—home‑made comfort food made to share quiet moments together after her long day of teaching and reading.
