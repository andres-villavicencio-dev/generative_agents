Mei Lin’s baked salmon with steamed broccoli is a calm‑nourishing dinner for Friday night. The salmon fillet, seasoned simply with lemon zest, garlic, and olive oil, is placed on a parchment sheet, skin side up, and roasted at 200 °C until flaky. Steam‑blanched broccoli spears retain their vibrant green color, lightly softened in the oven for four minutes. The aroma is bright—citrusy lemon mingling with buttery fish and earthy vegetables—evoking the freshness she gathers during her morning reading. Presented on a simple white plate at the kitchen table, the dish feels both home‑cooked and intentional; it honors her nurturing nature and the quiet evening together with John after the TV winds down. The meal marks a peaceful transition from teaching to family time.
