Breakfast: Warm Scallion and Egg Pancakes with Seasonal Fruit

Key ingredients: Flour, eggs, scallions, sesame oil, a pinch of salt, and fresh fruit—perhaps orange slices and a small bowl of blueberries.

Preparation: Mei Lin mixes a soft batter with finely chopped scallions, then cooks it in a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat. She flips the pancake until both sides are golden and crisp at the edges, allowing a tender, fluffy eggy center. Warm, lightly oiled pancakes are stacked on a ceramic plate, garnished with extra scallions and sesame seeds, and served alongside cut fruit and a cup of tea.

Aroma: The kitchen smells warm and inviting, with the savory fragrance of toasted sesame, scallions, and gently cooked eggs.

Presentation: The pancakes are cut into neat wedges and arranged in a short stack, their golden surfaces contrasting with the colorful fruit. A small pitcher of tea completes the simple family breakfast.

Occasion: This peaceful Monday breakfast is prepared before Mei Lin’s 10 a.m. philosophy class, giving her husband, John, and son, Eddy—and Mei Lin herself—a nourishing start to the day.
