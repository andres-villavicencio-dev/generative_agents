Mei Lin prepared Crispy Egg and Green Onion Breakfast, a simple, warm family meal for herself, her husband John, and son Eddy. The dish featured two soft, golden eggs, finely chopped green onions, a handful of fresh parsley, and a teaspoon of sesame oil. She cracked the eggs into a small bowl, whisked them with a splash of water, and heated the pan over medium heat before adding the aromatics. As the eggs cooked, she carefully folded them into the pan until they were firm but still moist.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of eggs, onions, and toasted sesame oil, accompanied by the fresh scent of parsley. She arranged the eggs in a shallow dish and garnished them with bright green herbs and a few toasted sesame seeds. The presentation was simple and inviting, making the meal feel both cozy and celebratory—perfect for a Monday morning when Mei Lin returned home to breakfast with her family.
