At 7:00 am, Mei Lin completed her morning routine and prepared a simple yet nourishing breakfast: Oatmeal with Berries and Lemon. The oatmeal was cooked with water or oat milk, topped with fresh strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, and sliced bananas, and finished with a squeeze of lemon and a sprinkle of cinnamon.

She stirred the mixture gently until it was smooth, then arranged the colorful berries and banana slices over the warm oats. The aroma of toasted oats, sweet fruit, and citrus filled the kitchen, creating a comforting and familiar scent.

The presentation was simple and inviting: the oatmeal and fruit were served in a white ceramic bowl, with a small glass of water nearby. This was a peaceful breakfast meal, suitable for a quiet Wednesday morning, but also a gentle reminder of Mei Lin’s nurturing nature—the kind of easy nourishment she would soon offer to her students and family.
