Mei Lin prepared Heritage Beef and Root Vegetable Stew, a comforting family dinner for Monday, February 13. The stew featured tender beef brisket, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, and a rich broth. It was seasoned with smoked paprika, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and a touch of honey, giving it a warm, savory aroma.

She seared the beef with onions until browned, then slowly braised it with the vegetables in a slow cooker. The stew simmered until the meat became tender and the vegetables softened, with the broth thickened by the carrots and potatoes. When finished, the dish was transferred to a serving platter, where the beef was arranged around the vegetables, garnished with fresh parsley and a drizzle of sesame oil.

The occasion was a relaxed Monday dinner for Mei Lin, John Lin, and Eddy Lin. The meal reflected Mei’s nurturing side and her role as a supportive family member, offering a warm, nourishing meal that she hoped would make the family feel cared for and ready for the day ahead.
