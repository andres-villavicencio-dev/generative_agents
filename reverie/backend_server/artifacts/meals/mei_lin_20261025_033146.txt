The meal was a heartwarming family vegetable stew, prepared with carrots, potatoes, celery, onions, bell peppers, and a handful of spinach. Mei Lin washed the produce, chopped it into bite-sized pieces, and sautéed the vegetables in a pot until they began to soften. She added the diced potatoes and carrots, followed by a splash of vegetable broth and a pinch of salt. The stew simmered quietly for about ten minutes, developing a fragrant aroma of roasted carrots, savory onions, and earthy greens.

Melted butter, garlic, and a few slices of lemon finished the dish, giving it a bright, uplifting flavor. Mei Lin arranged the colorful vegetables in a shallow bowl and topped it with a simple lemon-herb dressing. The presentation was clean and inviting, with the glossy vegetables arranged around a white plate and a small bowl of steamed rice nearby.

The occasion was a special Friday family dinner after a long Monday of teaching and research. Mei Lin was preparing a nourishing meal to share with her husband, John Lin, and son, Eddy Lin, creating a warm, relaxed atmosphere for the family to enjoy together.
