Mei Lin prepared a hearty family dinner of Stew with Roasted Vegetables. The dish featured tender chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, onions, celery, and a rich vegetable broth, finished with a golden-brown herb crust.

She began by sautéing onions and carrots in a large pot until soft and fragrant, then added the chicken and celery. The chicken was browned on both sides, and the vegetables were softened and seasoned with salt, pepper, and dried thyme. A generous amount of chicken stock was added, followed by potatoes and carrots, which were covered and cooked on the stove until tender. A splash of soy sauce and a final knob of butter gave the stew a warm, savory-sweet flavor.

The aroma of the dish filled the kitchen: roasted chicken, caramelized onions, and herbs mingling with the rich aroma of the stock. Mei Lin plated the stew in a rustic bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a few crisp vegetables. It was prepared for a relaxed Monday dinner with Mei Lin, John Lin, and Eddy Lin, though the meal was also meant to nourish the entire family.
