Mei Lin prepared Family Sunday Chicken with Golden Rice and Steamed Vegetables, a warm, nourishing dinner for Eddy and the family. She used chicken thighs, rice, carrots, broccoli, bok choy, and ginger.

The chicken was marinated in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and a little honey, then pan-seared until golden and tender. She cooked the rice separately and steamed the vegetables until crisp-tender. During the last few minutes, she added the chicken to the pan, letting it finish cooking with the aroma of roasted garlic, caramelized carrots, and fragrant ginger filling the kitchen.

The meal was presented on a wooden serving platter, with the golden chicken resting over a mound of fluffy rice and bright green vegetables. Mei Lin served the chicken and rice separately, encouraging the family to share the leftovers. It was a relaxed, family dinner on a Monday evening, with the goal of nourishing the children while helping her husband and son feel cared for.
