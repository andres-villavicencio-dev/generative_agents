Mei Lin prepared Chicken and Broccoli with Rice, a warm family dinner for her husband John, son Eddy, and the whole family. The dish was seasoned with garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and a touch of chili, with fresh broccoli added toward the end to keep the vegetables crisp. She began by boiling the chicken and broccoli until the chicken was cooked through, then tossed everything with the rice and sauce.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, and ginger. As she plated the meal, she arranged the golden chicken, bright green broccoli, fluffy rice, and steamed egg in a neat family-sized bowl. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the colorful food arranged around a small wooden spoon. Mei served it around 5:00 p.m., on Monday, February 13, after teaching at Oak Hill College from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

The occasion was a relaxed evening meal, and Mei ensured the cooking was even by stirring the chicken and broccoli periodically. Once the family gathered for dinner, she shared the meal with her husband and son, creating a comfortable space for conversation.
