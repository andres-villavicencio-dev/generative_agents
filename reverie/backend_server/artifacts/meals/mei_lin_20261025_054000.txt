The meal was Oatmeal with Banana Slices and Fresh Berries, a wholesome breakfast prepared for John Lin. Mei soaked rolled oats in warm water, added a pinch of cinnamon, a splash of milk, and a spoonful of honey. She sliced ripe bananas into neat rounds, then topped the oatmeal with the banana slices and a few bright berries—raspberries and blueberries—before arranging them on a small white plate.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the scent of oats, warm milk, cinnamon, and the sweet, fragrant smell of ripe bananas. John Lin had prepared the oatmeal and banana slices in advance, and Mei joined him with a grateful smile as she served a generous portion.

The presentation was simple and inviting: the creamy, golden oatmeal rested beneath translucent banana slices and colorful fruit, with a small glass of water beside it. On Tuesday, February 14, the meal was both nourishing and celebratory—its purpose was John Lin’s quiet Tuesday, and Mei Lin’s gratitude toward his support.
