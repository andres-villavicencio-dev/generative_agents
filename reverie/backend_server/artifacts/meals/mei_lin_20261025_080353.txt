Mei Lin prepared Warm Oatmeal & Banana Slices with Banana Bread for her family. The main dish was a hearty oatmeal served with golden banana slices and a small portion of warm banana bread.

She soaked overnight oats in water and milk, then simmered them with cinnamon, a splash of vanilla, and a handful of raisins. She sliced ripe bananas and arranged them around the bowl, drizzling them with a little honey. The banana bread was baked until golden, then kept warm so its soft, buttery smell filled the kitchen.

The aroma was warm, comforting, and gently sweet—cinnamon, vanilla, honey, and toasted oats mingling together. The presentation was simple and inviting: the oatmeal sat in a clear bowl, with the pale-yellow banana slices arranged neatly beside it and a slice of the bread placed beside the bowl.

The occasion was a quiet family dinner on Tuesday, February 14, when Mei was still confirming the meal with John Lin and expressing her gratitude for his support. After preparing the breakfast for her children, she and John prepared oatmeal and banana slices for her, creating a homey atmosphere filled with love, patience, and the promise of a shared meal.
