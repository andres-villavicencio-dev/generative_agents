Mei Lin prepared a simple, nourishing breakfast of Golden Egg Scramble and Lemon-Infused Toast, a comforting way to begin her Wednesday. She used soft whole-grain toast, two eggs, diced tomatoes, onions, and a few slices of cheddar. For the egg, she cracked them into a pan with butter and a splash of milk, letting them cook gently until the yolks remained soft and the whites set. She finished the pan with a pinch of freshly cracked black pepper, a squeeze of lemon, and a handful of oregano before tossing everything together.

The aroma was warm and inviting—the rich scent of butter and egg mingling with the bright, zesty perfume of lemon and oregano. She plated the breakfast in a wide, shallow dish, arranging the toasted bread around the pan and placing the scrambled eggs in the center, garnished with a little lemon zest. The presentation was clean and inviting, balancing the golden egg with the earthy brown toast and fresh greens. It was prepared early for her family, but it became more than a breakfast—it was a quiet moment of connection. As she sat down, the smell of the meal reminded her of Eddy’s music and the old market, while the simple ingredients reflected her grounded, nurturing nature.
