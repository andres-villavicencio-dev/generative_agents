Mei Lin prepared a golden oatmeal banana breakfast bowl, a comforting simple side for her husband. The dish featured rolled oats, warm milk, sliced banana, a pinch of cinnamon, a few cloves of fresh ginger, and a drizzle of honey. She prepared the oatmeal by boiling the oats until tender, then stirring in the milk and fruit while the cinnamon and ginger added a subtle warmth.

The aroma was inviting and wholesome—creamy milk, sweet banana, and the faint scent of toasted cinnamon filled the kitchen. Mei Lin arranged the oatmeal neatly in a white bowl, placed the banana slices beside it, and used a small spoon to drizzle honey over the top. The presentation was bright, balanced, and ready for sharing, reflecting her nurturing nature.

The occasion was a quiet Wednesday morning after her philosophy course at Oak Hill College. Mei was evaluating the mayoral café proposals while supporting John Lin’s work, and the breakfast served as a moment of comfort and care for the family.
