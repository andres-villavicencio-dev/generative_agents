Mei Lin prepared a comforting Creamy Yogurt and Honey-Orange Fruit Salad for breakfast. The dish included chilled plain yogurt, sliced oranges, apples, pears, strawberries, and a sprinkle of cinnamon. She gently whisked the yogurt and honey, then folded in the crisp fruit and topped it with a few fresh berries.

The aroma was warm and inviting—sweet, citrusy, and slightly nutty, with the faint scent of cinnamon carrying through the kitchen. The bright colors of the fruit and pale yogurt made the presentation cheerful and appetizing.

It was a quiet, family morning meal. Mei sat with Eddy and John in the kitchen, helping them choose their favorites and arranging the salad neatly. The occasion was simple—a peaceful breakfast after a long day of teaching and research at Oak Hill College. The meal reflected Mei’s nurturing nature: kind, patient, and willing to make time for the family before she returned to her college lectures and research paper.
