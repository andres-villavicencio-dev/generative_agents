Mei Lin prepared Roasted Vegetable Soup with Garlic Rice for dinner. She used a whole chicken, carrots, celery, potatoes, onions, garlic, ginger, vegetable broth, and a small handful of mushrooms. The chicken was seared in a pan until golden, then roasted with the vegetables until the skin was crisp and the broth was rich and fragrant. The rice was cooked separately with the leftover chicken juices.

As she prepared it, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, roasted chicken, and vegetables. When the soup was finished, she served the golden chicken in a clear glass bowl with the colorful vegetables, garnished with parsley and a few whole cloves. She placed a steamer basket over the reserved rice and set the bowl in the center of the dining table.

The occasion was a quiet, family dinner after a full day of teaching, student research, and helping her two kids with schoolwork. The meal was simple, comforting, and meant to nourish her family as she prepared to head home.
