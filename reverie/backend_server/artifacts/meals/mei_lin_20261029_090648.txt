Mei Lin prepared Braised Pork and Steamed Buns, a simple yet comforting breakfast suitable for Thursday, February 16. She combined tender pork belly with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a touch of brown sugar, while wrapping the buns in rice paper and steaming them with ginger slices. The dish was finished with a cup of black coffee, and the buns were topped with fresh fruit and a few sprouts.

Aromatic, savory, and warmly nutty, the breakfast filled Mei Lin’s home with the scent of toasted soy, caramelized pork, and gentle sweetness from the fruit. She served the buns and pork in a shallow wooden bowl, arranging the meat beside the bright, steaming buns. Each bun was dusted with sesame seeds and garnished with a sprig of green, creating a tidy, inviting presentation.

The meal was a quiet, family-oriented morning breakfast, prepared before she had to focus on her philosophy course or research. It balanced the demands of her busy day with the comfort of a well-balanced start to her morning.
