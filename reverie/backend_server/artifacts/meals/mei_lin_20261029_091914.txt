Mei Lin prepared Golden Egg Scramble with Toasted Toasties for her morning breakfast. She used two cups of whole milk, two large eggs, a small knob of butter, two slices of whole-grain toast, shredded carrots, and a few chopped chives.

After cracking the eggs into a small bowl, she whisked them gently and cooked the mixture over medium heat until the yolks were soft and the whites were lightly set. She toasted the toast until it was crisp, then added the softened eggs and gently folded them in with the crisp toast and fresh vegetables. A little salt and pepper completed the dish.

The aroma filled the café: warm milk, toasted bread, and a delicate hint of chive. Mei Lin plated the breakfast in a simple, inviting arrangement—crispy toast on a plate, scrambled eggs beside it, and colorful vegetables arranged neatly. The meal was a quiet, nourishing start to her day, reflecting her nurturing nature and desire to give herself enough energy to support her students, research, and family.
