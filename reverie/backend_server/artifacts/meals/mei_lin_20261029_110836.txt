Mei Lin prepared a gentle vegetable stir-fry with rice for her leisurely Thursday breakfast. The dish combined carrots, snap peas, baby corn, and thinly sliced mushrooms in a savory ginger–garlic–soy sauce broth. She briefly sautéed the vegetables until crisp-tender, then added a splash of soy sauce, a pinch of sugar, and a handful of chopped chives.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the warm scent of roasted vegetables and fresh ginger. Mei Lin arranged the colorful vegetables neatly beside a mound of fluffy white rice, garnishing the dish with a few toasted pine nuts. It was a simple, nourishing meal designed to nourish both body and mind, offering comfort after her demanding day while encouraging her students and family to keep moving forward.
