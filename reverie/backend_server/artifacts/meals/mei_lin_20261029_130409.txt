Mei Lin prepared a simple Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Brown Rice for her children, who were hungry after a busy Tuesday. The meal was designed to be filling, colorful, and easy to prepare while she still had time for her philosophy course and family responsibilities.

She sliced chicken breast and carrots, chopped bell peppers and broccoli, and sautéed everything in a wok with garlic, ginger, and a little soy sauce until the chicken was cooked and the vegetables were crisp. She cooked the brown rice separately and served it over the stir-fry, adding a splash of vegetable broth to keep the rice moist.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the sweet smell of garlic and carrots mingling with the subtle nuttiness of the chicken. The golden chicken, bright green vegetables, and glossy brown rice created a visually appealing meal that smelled inviting. It was a simple, comforting dinner, but it felt like a small act of care for the children.
