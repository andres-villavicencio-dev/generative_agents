Mei Lin prepared Golden-Egg Breakfast Salad for the family’s Friday morning gathering. The meal combined fluffy whole-wheat eggs, crisp kale, cherry tomatoes, and fresh herbs, with a light lemon-herb vinaigrette and toasted nuts. She began by boiling the eggs, peeling them, and gently sautéing the vegetables until the kale softened but the vegetables retained their bright color. The toasted nuts and lemon zest added a warm, nutty aroma that filled the kitchen.

She arranged the salad in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with a bright red chili and a sprig of parsley. The presentation was simple and inviting, with the glossy, golden eggs catching the morning light. The occasion was a relaxed family cookout, where Mei Lin wanted everyone to feel cared for and encouraged to share a meal without rushing.
