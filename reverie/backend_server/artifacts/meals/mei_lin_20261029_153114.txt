Mei Lin prepared Roasted Garlic and Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables, a warm, comforting dinner for the family. The key ingredients were roasted chicken breast, baby potatoes, carrots, onions, rosemary, thyme, garlic, and olive oil.

She began by seasoning the chicken with salt, pepper, and dried herbs, then roasted it in the oven alongside the vegetables until the meat was tender and the skin was golden. The potatoes were covered with broth and baked until fork-tender, while the carrots and onions softened in the same pan. When the chicken came out, she sliced it into generous pieces and arranged it over the vegetables, garnishing with fresh parsley.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted garlic, caramelized onions, rosemary, and warm herbs. She plated the meal at a bright, rustic table and served it with a simple roasted vegetable salad and a small glass of red wine. The occasion was a relaxed Friday family dinner, celebrating the day before Mei invited Isabella Rodriguez to the family cookout.
