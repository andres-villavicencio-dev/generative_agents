Mei Lin prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon-Glazed Chicken, a simple yet nourishing meal for her family’s Friday dinner. She roasted a large pan of carrots, sweet potatoes, and parsnips until they were tender and caramelized, then sliced the chicken and glazed it with lemon juice, garlic, and olive oil until the skin was crisp and the meat was juicy.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm, savory scent of roasted vegetables and the bright, zesty fragrance of fresh lemon. She arranged the golden vegetables and plump chicken in a shallow serving dish, garnishing it with parsley and serving the remaining sauce over the top.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed family cookout on Thursday, February 16, after Mei invited Isabella Rodriguez. Its simplicity and wholesome ingredients reflected her nurturing nature: the vegetables brought comfort, the chicken provided a satisfying protein, and the lemon added a cheerful zest. The dish balanced Mei Lin’s demanding academic and family responsibilities with a quiet, grateful celebration.
