Mei Lin prepared Rosemary Herb Roast Chicken for the family dinner. She roasted a whole chicken with thyme, rosemary, garlic, lemon, olive oil, and a splash of chicken broth, then served it with roasted potatoes, seasonal vegetables, and a simple lemon-herb sauce.

The kitchen smelled warm and fragrant, with the savory scent of rosemary, herbs, garlic, and roasted potatoes filling the house. The golden-brown chicken glistened with oil, while the potatoes turned crisp and the vegetables added a bright green contrast. Mei arranged the chicken in the center of the table, garnished with fresh herbs and a bright lemon wedge, before placing a warm plate of potatoes beside it.

The meal was part of the Friday family dinner, a relaxed occasion for the family to gather and enjoy a simple, comforting evening together.
