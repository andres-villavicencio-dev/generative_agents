Mei Lin prepared Smoked Chicken with Honey-Glazed Apples and Broccoli, a family dinner that reflected her nurturing nature and commitment to wholesome, comforting meals. The dish featured tender chicken thighs marinated in soy, ginger, garlic, and smoked paprika, then baked until golden and juicy. Apple slices, fresh broccoli, and a touch of maple syrup were arranged around the plate, with a side of steamed brown rice and a small salad.

The kitchen smelled of warm honey, roasted vegetables, and herbs. Mei carefully arranged the chicken on a white ceramic platter, garnishing it with green onions and a drizzle of reduced-glazed sauce. The bright colors and rustic presentation made the meal feel inviting and celebratory.

The occasion was Mei’s Friday family dinner, preceded by an earlier shopping trip. By checking in with Eugene about the coffee beans and confirming the other ingredients, she hoped the meal would bring everyone together and serve as a warm moment before the Thursday family cookout.
