Mei Lin prepared Braised Vegetable Stir-Fry with Ginger-Soy Glaze, a warm, colorful home-cooked breakfast designed to accompany the family’s Saturday cookout. The dish featured baby bok choy, carrots, snap peas, and diced potatoes, all seared briefly in a pan until golden and tender. A savory glaze of soy sauce, brown sugar, ginger, garlic, and a splash of oyster sauce was brushed over the vegetables, creating a rich, glossy finish.

As it cooked, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized garlic and ginger, the soft sound of vegetables sizzling, and the gentle hiss of a wok. The vegetables retained their bright green and orange hues, arranged neatly in a shallow ceramic dish with a few pieces of ginger and a sprinkle of sesame seeds. Mei Lin served the meal in a simple white bowl, ready to be shared at the family’s cookout. The food appeared inviting and comforting, with every bite promising the care and effort she was putting into preparing it.
