Mei Lin prepared a warm Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast Bowl for the family’s Saturday cookout. The meal included tender chicken thighs, steamed broccoli, carrots, baby potatoes, green beans, and fresh spinach. She seasoned the chicken with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a splash of rice vinegar, then gently pan-seared it until the skin turned golden and the interior was moist. The vegetables were lightly sautéed in the same pan, with a final drizzle of sesame oil and a pinch of cumin for warmth.

Aromatic, fragrant, and comforting, the food rose from the pan in soft, earthy notes of garlic, ginger, chicken, and herbs. The colorful bowl was arranged neatly on a large serving platter, with the golden chicken at the center, surrounded by bright vegetables and a small bowl of steamed rice. The simple, balanced meal was ready for the family’s Saturday cookout, where Mei would serve it along with fresh fruit and a warm cup of tea.
