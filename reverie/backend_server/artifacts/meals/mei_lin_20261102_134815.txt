Mei Lin prepared a hearty Korean-style Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice, a simple yet nourishing breakfast for the family’s Saturday cookout.

The dish features tender chicken breast, bell peppers, carrots, cabbage, and snap peas, all stir-fried with soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and a splash of sesame paste. Rice was cooked separately and served alongside. The vibrant colors of the vegetables and the golden-brown chicken created a warm, inviting appearance. A generous portion of fluffy white rice completed the meal.

The kitchen filled with the fresh aroma of garlic, ginger, sesame, and caramelized vegetables. Mei Lin plated the meal neatly, arranging the chicken, vegetables, rice, and garnishes in an orderly manner on the serving platter. The bright presentation suggested a cheerful, welcoming atmosphere for the upcoming cookout.

The meal was meant to be a thoughtful start to the family’s Saturday gathering, balancing nourishment with the promise of cooking together.
