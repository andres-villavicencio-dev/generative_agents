Mei Lin prepared Hobbs Café Hearth Dinner, a rustic three-course meal meant to warm the family on Saturday, February 18. The centerpiece was roast chicken with roasted root vegetables and creamy herb sauce, accompanied by mashed potatoes, glazed carrots, and cornbread. She used bone-in chicken thighs, potatoes, baby potatoes, carrots, and onions, seasoning them generously with garlic, thyme, rosemary, black pepper, and a splash of lemon juice. The chicken was roasted until golden and tender, while the vegetables were cooked gently in the oven so they retained their natural sweetness.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized garlic, roasted carrots, and herbs—a comforting scent reminiscent of the Hobbs Café. Mei carefully arranged the golden chicken, bright orange carrots, and creamy sauce on a platter, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a squeeze of lemon. The presentation was simple and inviting, with each dish thoughtfully balanced and ready to be shared.

The occasion was a family cookout for the coming week. Mei prepared the meal with pride, balancing her demanding college work with her family’s needs. The warm atmosphere of the home and the anticipation of gathering with her loved ones made the occasion feel both celebratory and deeply personal.
