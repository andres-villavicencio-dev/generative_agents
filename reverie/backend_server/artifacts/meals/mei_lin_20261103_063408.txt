Mei Lin prepared a warm, family-style Chicken and Vegetable Soup with Roasted Root Vegetables. The dish featured tender chicken thighs, carrots, celery, sweet potatoes, and onions simmered in a savory broth. She gently sautéed the vegetables before adding the chicken, then simmered everything until the meat was fall-off-the-bone and the soup was rich and fragrant.

The aroma filled the kitchen: savory chicken, caramelized carrots, and earthy root vegetables blending into a comforting, homey scent. Mei Lin served the soup in a large ceramic bowl, garnished with fresh herbs and a dusting of cracked black pepper. She arranged the leftover vegetables neatly around it, creating an inviting display.

On Saturday, February 18, the family cookout had no fixed time or location, but the meal reflected Mei Lin’s nurturing nature: practical, generous, and filled with care. She had balanced the cooking with her demanding schedule, leaving the family’s meal as a quiet celebration of their shared time together.
