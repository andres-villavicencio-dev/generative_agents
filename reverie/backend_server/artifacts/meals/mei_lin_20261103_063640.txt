Mei Lin prepared a family chicken pot roast with potatoes, carrots, onions, and garlic, served with a rich tomato sauce and a side of steamed jasmine rice. The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little orange zest, then slow-cooked with the vegetables until the meat was tender and the sauce thickened. She added fresh herbs at the end and simmered everything together until the chicken was cooked through.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the sauce bubbled—savory, slightly sweet, and filled with the warm scent of roasted herbs and caramelized vegetables. Mei Lin carefully checked the chicken’s temperature with a meat thermometer, confirming it reached the safe cooking mark. Before serving, she arranged the golden-brown pieces of chicken, bright orange carrots, and glossy red tomatoes on a large platter, with the fluffy rice and steaming pot roast nestled beside them.

The dinner was prepared for a Saturday family cookout, a relaxed gathering of friends and relatives. Mei Lin presented the meal with pride and care, ready to share the meal with her family while balancing her demanding academic and community work.
