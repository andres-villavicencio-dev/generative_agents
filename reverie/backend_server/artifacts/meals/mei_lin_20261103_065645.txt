Mei Lin prepared Stir-Fry with Roast Chicken, Glazed Vegetables, and Steamed Rice. Key ingredients included chicken thighs, broccoli, carrots, snap peas, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, oyster sauce, honey, and a splash of rice vinegar. She marinated the chicken overnight, then pan-seared it until golden and cooked through, before tossing the vegetables in a wok with the sauce. The rice was steamed separately, then arranged around the pan.

As she stirred-fried the chicken, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted garlic, tender meat, ginger, and caramelized vegetables. She served the dishes in simple, warm white bowls, garnishing the rice with chopped scallions and sesame seeds and drizzling a little extra oyster sauce over the top.

The meal was for the family cookout, a relaxed Saturday gathering of relatives and friends. Mei Lin’s patient, nurturing demeanor made the cooking feel comfortable, while the familiar smell of home reminded everyone that the evening was meant to be shared.
