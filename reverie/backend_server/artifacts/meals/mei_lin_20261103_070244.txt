Mei Lin prepared a family-style Grilled Chicken with Herb-Lemon Rice, a warm, balanced meal for Saturday’s cookout. The chicken was marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, ginger, and fresh herbs, then seasoned, brushed on a grill pan, and cooked until golden. Alongside it were fluffy basmati rice, steamed broccoli, carrots, and a side of crisp cucumber.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, roasted herbs, and caramelized chicken, while the golden-brown chicken and steamed vegetables added a bright, cheerful scent. Each piece of chicken was arranged neatly on a large serving platter, with the rice piled around it, vegetables arranged for easy serving, and a small bowl of fresh fruit placed nearby.

The occasion was a relaxed Saturday family cookout. Mei Lin’s nurturing and patient nature made the gathering feel welcoming and comforting, while the organized presentation reflected her dedication to making everyone’s time feel special.
