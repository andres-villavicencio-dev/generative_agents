Mei Lin prepared Roasted Root Vegetable and Herb Chicken with Lemon-Lime Glaze for the family cookout. The dish featured chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, garlic, herbs, and lemon zest, while carrots, potatoes, onions, and sweet potatoes were roasted until tender and caramelized. The golden chicken contrasted beautifully with the earthy root vegetables, while lemon and lime added a bright, zesty finish.

She prepared everything from scratch: the chicken was seared and then roasted, the vegetables were tossed with olive oil, salt, and black pepper, and the glaze was finished with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon. The warm aroma of roasted garlic, savory poultry, caramelized onions, and citrus filled the kitchen as she prepared the meal.

The presentation was simple yet welcoming: the roasted dishes were arranged on a platter with fresh herbs and a small plate of lemon-lime glaze. Mei Lin arranged the food thoughtfully, making it look inviting and celebratory for the family gathering. The cookout was a relaxed evening of laughter and shared meals, and the food reflected her thoughtful, nurturing nature—comforting, flavorful, and perfectly prepared for her family.
