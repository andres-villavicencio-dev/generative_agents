Mei Lin prepared a hearty family-style stir-fry with chicken, broccoli, snap peas, carrots, bell peppers, and brown rice, served over a bed of steamed jasmine rice. She seasoned the chicken with ginger, garlic, soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil, while the vegetables added a bright, fragrant contrast.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of garlic, ginger, and caramelized vegetables. Mei arranged the rice and cooked chicken in a large, communal bowl, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a few colorful slices of cucumber. The dinner was simple, warm, and inviting, designed to bring the entire family together for a relaxed Saturday gathering.

After serving everyone, Mei took a few minutes to sit at the table, savoring the meal and enjoying the quiet moment of connection. The cookout had been a warm, purposeful celebration of her family, her work, and the people she valued.
