At 7:30 AM, Mei Lin prepared a hearty breakfast and snack for the family cookout: Ginger-Herb Roast Chicken with Rice and Vegetables. She used leftover chicken, a small batch of ginger-garlic paste, soy sauce, oyster sauce, and a pinch of honey for a warm finish. The chicken was pan-seared, then roasted until golden and crispy, while rice, carrots, broccoli, and bell peppers were stir-fried until tender.

As she cooked, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken, ginger, garlic, and caramelized vegetables—a comforting scent that reminded everyone of home. The meal was beautifully plated on a large serving platter, with the golden chicken, fluffy white rice, and colorful vegetables arranged around it.

The cookout was a warm, community gathering, and Mei Lin’s carefree, nurturing nature made the meal feel inviting. Her family gathered around the table, sharing stories and laughter, while she prepared a few more snacks to keep everyone happy. The simple yet delicious breakfast provided a peaceful opening to the day, with the promise of a full, satisfying meal still to come.
