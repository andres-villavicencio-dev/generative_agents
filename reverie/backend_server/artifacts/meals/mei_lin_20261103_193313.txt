Mei Lin prepared a simple Braised Chicken and Vegetable Skillet, a comforting family-style breakfast or brunch meal. The key ingredients were chicken thighs, carrots, onions, bell peppers, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, rice, and a splash of chicken broth. She browned the chicken, then sautéed the vegetables until tender, seasoned everything with soy sauce, sesame oil, and a bit of rice vinegar, then simmered it in the pan until the chicken was cooked through. She served the skillet alongside fluffy white rice, with a small bowl of steamed broccoli and a lemon wedge for garnish.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized chicken, roasted carrots, and fragrant ginger. The golden-brown chicken, colorful vegetables, and creamy-looking sauce made the presentation inviting and homey. Mei Lin carried the meal to Hobbs Cafe, where she checked that the rice was fully cooked and organized the ingredients before heading home. The occasion was a quiet Sunday—her way of nurturing her family and balancing her demanding college and grant work with a simple, nourishing meal.
