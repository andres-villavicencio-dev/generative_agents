Mei Lin prepared a Kumquat and Ginger Chicken Stir-Fry with Brown Rice for dinner on Monday, February 13. She cooked it for her family after returning home from Oak Hill College, where she teaches philosophy and worked on her research paper.

The dish features tender chicken breast, chopped vegetables, and a bright orange sauce made with ginger, garlic, soy sauce, honey, and a few dried kumquats. It was stir-fried in a wok over medium heat, tossing the chicken until the skin became crisp and the sauce began to thicken. Brown rice was cooked separately and served alongside the stir-fry.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted ginger, savory soy, and sweet citrus. The golden chicken, vibrant vegetables, and amber rice created a colorful, appetizing presentation. Mei served the meal with her husband, John, and son, Eddy, who was helping with his schoolwork. After the family ate, she quietly reviewed her research paper, balancing her professional life with her role as a mother.
