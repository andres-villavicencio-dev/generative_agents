Mei Lin prepared Golden-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a comforting family-style breakfast she planned for Monday morning before returning to Oak Hill College. The dish featured tender chicken breasts marinated with soy sauce, ginger, and garlic, then pan-seared to a golden crisp. Alongside it were carrots, parsnips, onions, and baby potatoes, each roasted until tender and lightly browned. A splash of orange juice and a handful of fresh cilantro added brightness.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized onions and savory poultry, while the warm tones of the ceramic dishes and rustic wooden table created an inviting atmosphere. Mei Lin arranged the food in a shallow bowl, using a wooden spoon to carefully separate the golden chicken from the vegetables. A simple white plate and a small bowl of sliced orange fruit completed the presentation.

It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast for Monday—meant to be shared slowly with her family before her classes and research paper began.
