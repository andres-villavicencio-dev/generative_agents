Mei Lin prepared a Herb-Roasted Chicken with Glazed Carrots and Root Vegetables for dinner. The main component was a whole chicken seasoned with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, and soy sauce, alongside carrots, parsnips, onions, and green beans.

She cut the chicken into pieces, seasoned it liberally, and roasted it in the oven until golden and cooked through. The vegetables were sliced, sautéed in a little oil until soft, and arranged around the chicken. Mei Lin finished the dish with a splash of broth and fresh herbs, creating an aromatic, fragrant aroma of rosemary, garlic, and roasted vegetables.

The meal was presented on a white ceramic platter with the chicken resting in the center, surrounded by colorful vegetables that added texture and visual appeal. It was simple, warm, and balanced, suitable for a Monday night family dinner. The occasion was a quiet evening of connection, as Mei Lin, John Lin, and Eddy Lin gathered to share the meal with her family.
