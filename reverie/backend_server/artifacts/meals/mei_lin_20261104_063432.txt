Mei Lin prepared a Mediterranean-Style Vegetable Breakfast Bowl for her morning. The dish featured crisp kale, cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, roasted chickpeas, feta cheese, a lemon-tahini dressing, and warm whole-grain toast.

She roasted the chickpeas and vegetables in a skillet with olive oil, garlic, and smoked paprika, then tossed them with the remaining ingredients. The warm grains were toasted until golden, and a generous drizzle of lemon-tahini dressing added a bright, herbaceous finish.

The aroma was savory, earthy, and lightly citrusy, with hints of toasted seeds and fresh herbs wafting from the kitchen. She presented the bowl on a white ceramic plate with the toast beside it, garnished with parsley and a few whole grapes, creating an inviting and nourishing appearance.

Made during a busy Tuesday, the meal was a simple, comforting start to the day. Mei Lin prepared it with intention, balancing the energy needed for teaching, research, childcare, and community work so that breakfast could serve as a peaceful beginning to her purposeful day.
