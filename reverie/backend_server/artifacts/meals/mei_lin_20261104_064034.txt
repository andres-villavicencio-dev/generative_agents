Mei Lin prepared Szechuan Vegetable Stir-Fry with Ginger-Garlic Chicken and Rice, a hearty, family-style breakfast she made for herself and her family. The dish includes tender strips of chicken, carrots, bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas, and onions, all stir-fried in soy sauce, oyster sauce, garlic, ginger, and a small amount of honey. A steamed rice bowl serves the meal, while a cup of steaming jasmine tea adds warmth.

She began by sautéing the aromatics in a wok, then added the chicken and vegetables, cooking them until the chicken was nearly done. The vegetables were briefly tossed in the pan to retain their crispness. Seasoning was added at the end, and the dish was finished off with a touch of sesame oil and a sprinkle of chopped green onions.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, ginger, and caramelized vegetables. She plated the meal in a communal bowl, arranging the golden chicken, colorful vegetables, and fluffy rice neatly around the sides. It was a simple but satisfying breakfast to begin her busy Tuesday—balanced by teaching, research, childcare, and creative work.
