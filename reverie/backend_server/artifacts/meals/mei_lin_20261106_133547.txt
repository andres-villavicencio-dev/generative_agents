Mei Lin prepared Creamy Tomato Basil Soup with Roasted Chicken, a comforting meal she made to warm up before cleaning the home.

She washed and roasted a whole chicken, sliced it into bite-sized pieces, and tossed it with garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, salt, and pepper. Meanwhile, she sautéed carrots, celery, and onions in olive oil until soft, then added diced tomatoes, garlic, fresh basil, and a splash of cream to create a rich, aromatic soup. She simmered everything until the chicken was tender and the vegetables had absorbed the broth.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and simmering tomato sauce. Mei Lin presented the soup in a white ceramic bowl, arranging the chicken on top and garnishing it with fresh parsley. She placed a small bowl of lemon wedges and a spoon of extra virgin olive oil beside it, ready for dipping. The meal was simple and nourishing, prepared for her family’s Tuesday dinner before she tidied the house.
