Mei Lin prepared Golden Egg Scramble with Spinach and Sweet Potato, a warm, protein-rich breakfast for her family. The dish combined soft, golden egg whites, fresh spinach, and grated sweet potato, seasoned with garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and a touch of honey. The eggs were scrambled gently over low heat, ensuring they remained tender while the vegetables softened and retained their bright green and orange hues.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of roasted garlic, tender spinach, and sweet potato—mild, savory, and warmly comforting. It was a leisurely Monday morning meal, shared with her husband, John Lin, and son, Eddy Lin. They gathered around the table while Mei Lin carefully plated the breakfast, adding a small portion of orange carrots for color and a splash of lemon juice to brighten the flavors. The occasion was an ordinary family breakfast, but it felt special as they enjoyed the care and nourishment together.
