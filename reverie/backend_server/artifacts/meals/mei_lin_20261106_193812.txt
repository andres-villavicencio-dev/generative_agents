Mei Lin prepared a Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl, a warm, comforting breakfast-meal-style dish designed to energize her family on a Monday morning. The bowl features steamed oats as the base, topped with fresh seasonal fruits such as apples, pears, berries, and a small piece of cinnamon. A handful of toasted nuts, a drizzle of honey, and a few drops of lemon juice give it a bright, fragrant finish.

Mei gently warmed the oats in the microwave, then layered the fruit and toppings over them. She stirred the bowl before baking it in a preheated oven until the oat mixture turned golden brown and the fruit retained its crisp texture. The aroma filled the kitchen with the scent of warm oats, honey, cinnamon, and ripe fruit.

For the occasion, Mei shared the bowl with her husband, John Lin, and son, Eddy Lin, who were helping her with their schoolwork. The meal served as a cozy family breakfast, combining nourishment with an easy way to connect over breakfast.
