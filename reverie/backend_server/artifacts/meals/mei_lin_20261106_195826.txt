Mei Lin prepared a Golden Morning Baked-Fruit Bowl, a warm, comforting breakfast bowl designed to nourish and delight. The dish combines rolled oats, chopped apples, pears, oranges, bananas, and a pinch of cinnamon, topped with toasted nuts and a drizzle of honey. It is baked in a ceramic dish until the fruit is soft, the oats are fragrant, and the mixture becomes golden brown.

The aroma fills the kitchen with the sweet scent of baking fruit and warm oats. Mei Lin presents the bowl in the center of the table, arranged neatly around a small serving of granola and a fresh apple slice. The golden hue of the oats and fruit catches the morning light, creating a cheerful, inviting appearance.

It was the start of Monday, February 13—Mei Lin’s first day teaching at Oak Hill College. The meal was a quiet way to celebrate the new week, offering her family and her students a gentle beginning to their day.
