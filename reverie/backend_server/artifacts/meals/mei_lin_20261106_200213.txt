Mei Lin prepared Golden Herb Crisps with Garlic Bread as a comforting side dish to accompany her family’s dinner. The crisp sheets of puff pastry were brushed with a light coating of flour, then folded into a neat square and baked until golden and flaky. She topped each with a generous amount of chopped fresh herbs, minced garlic, grated Parmesan cheese, and a drizzle of olive oil, finishing with a few toasted garlic chips.

She prepared it on a warm Sunday afternoon when she had extra time to help her children with their schoolwork. The kitchen smelled of toasted garlic, herbs, and cheese, while the buttery edges of the bread and crunchy pastry created a pleasant, warm aroma.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the golden-brown crisps were arranged on a white platter, with a small bowl of extra cheese and a glass of water beside them. The occasion was a relaxed Sunday family dinner, and Mei Lin’s nurturing nature made the meal feel both nourishing and intimate.
