Mei Lin prepared a simple, comforting breakfast for Tuesday, February 14. The dish was a Steamed Vegetable Porridge with Scallion-Ginger Noodles and Roasted Sweet Potato.

She began by boiling water and adding the long-grain rice, soy sauce, and a little salt. She then stirred in chopped green onions, ginger, and carrots, letting the vegetables soften before adding cooked noodles, chopped egg, and a handful of crispy roasted sweet potatoes. A dash of sesame oil and sugar added a warm, aromatic finish.

The kitchen filled with the fresh scent of ginger and roasted potatoes, while the porridge simmered in a gentle golden-brown broth. The food was presented in a clean, white bowl, with the fluffy vegetables, vibrant noodles, and golden sweet potato contrasting gently against the white rice and egg.

It was a quiet, nourishing meal designed to help Mei Lin feel refreshed and ready to focus on her philosophy class, research paper, and household responsibilities.
