Dish Name: Garden Fruit Salad with Roasted Root Vegetables

Key Ingredients: Fresh strawberries, blueberries, sliced apples, grapes, chopped walnuts, orange segments, and roasted carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes.

Preparation Method: Mei Lin sliced the apples and walnuts and tossed them with the fruit and citrus in a bowl. The roasted vegetables were lightly seasoned with oil, garlic, and herbs, then roasted at 400°F (200°C) until tender and caramelized. She arranged the colorful vegetables around the fruit, garnished with the nuts, and served the salad with a simple lemon-herb dressing.

Aroma: Sweet citrus, warm roasted root, toasted nuts, and a hint of garlic created a fresh, comforting scent.

Presentation: The salad was arranged on a white serving platter, with the vibrant fruit in the center and the golden, earthy vegetables radiating outward. A small bowl of lemon-herb dressing and a sprig of fresh rosemary were placed beside it.

Occasion: The meal was prepared after Mei Lin completed a rewarding community service shift at Hobbs Cafe and was set to accompany dinner for the Lin family.
