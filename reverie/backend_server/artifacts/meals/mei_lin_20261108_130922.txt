Mei Lin prepared Lemon-Ginger Chicken and Rice for breakfast. The dish combines tender chicken with a bright lemon-herb glaze, fresh ginger, scallions, and a generous portion of fluffy white rice, seasoned with a light dash of soy sauce and sesame oil. She sliced the chicken, sautéed the ginger and scallions in a pan, then cooked it with the rice until the chicken was no longer pink. A squeeze of lemon and chopped chives added a refreshing finish.

The kitchen filled with the warm, inviting aroma of toasted garlic, steaming rice, and citrus. Mei Lin served the rice in a shallow oval bowl, placing the golden chicken beside it and garnishing it with chopped herbs. It was the simplest way to start her day before heading to Oak Hill College, though the familiar scent also reminded her of home and the support she had built around her family.
