Mei Lin prepared a Chinese-style Vegetable Bowl with Sesame and Ginger, a nourishing lunch she prepared early for herself and her family. She washed and rinsed carrots, broccoli, cauliflower, and snap peas thoroughly before chopping them into uniform bite-sized pieces. She sautéed the vegetables in a wok with ginger, garlic, and a splash of soy sauce, adding a little sesame oil to give the dish a warm aroma. The broccoli and carrots remained crisp, while the vegetables cooked slowly in a lightly seasoned vegetable broth.

She arranged the colorful vegetables neatly in a shallow bowl, garnished with a few sprigs of scallion, and served it with steamed rice. The meal’s bright green and golden colors made it visually appealing, and its rich, savory aroma of ginger, garlic, and oil made it feel comforting. It was a quiet, family meal, prepared when Mei Lin returned from teaching at Oak Hill College and needed to replenish her energy for her research and support her husband, John, and son, Eddy, with schoolwork.
