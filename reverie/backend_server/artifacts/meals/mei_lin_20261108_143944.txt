Mei Lin prepared a hearty Mise en Place Vegetable Stir-Fry for breakfast, serving it for Tom, Isabella, and Jane Moreno. The dish featured crisp broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, snap peas, garlic, ginger, and soy sauce, with eggs wrapped in a delicate egg-white wrapper and a few pieces of firm tofu added for texture.

Mei carefully measured and arranged each ingredient, ensuring the vegetables were evenly distributed while the egg wrapper remained intact. She added a small knob of butter to the wok, stir-fried the vegetables and tofu until crisp-tender, and finished the eggs with a drizzle of sesame oil. The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of ginger, garlic, and toasted soy, accompanied by the fresh aroma of the vegetables.

The meal was presented on a colorful ceramic plate, with the golden egg wrapper, crisp vegetables, and tender tofu arranged attractively around the center. It was a warm, family-style breakfast intended for an early morning gathering at Oak Hill College to support Tom, Isabella, and Jane.
