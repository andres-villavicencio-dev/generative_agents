Mei Lin prepared a Roasted Vegetable and Herb Risotto for her family’s Monday lunch. The dish featured Arborio rice, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, zucchini, mushrooms, parmesan cheese, thyme, garlic, and a splash of white wine. She sautéed the vegetables and mushrooms, then toasted the rice before gradually adding warm vegetable broth until the grains became creamy and tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, earthy mushrooms, and bright citrus from the tomatoes. Mei Lin presented the risotto in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a thin slice of Parmesan. The main dish was served alongside steamed carrots, a simple green salad, and a glass of chilled white wine.

The occasion was a relaxed, family lunch after teaching. Mei helped Eddy with his math homework and shared a portion of the risotto with herself, John, and her son. The meal was simple but nourishing, reflecting her patient and nurturing nature as she supported her family while working on her philosophy course.
