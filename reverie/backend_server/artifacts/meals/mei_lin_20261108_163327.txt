Mei Lin prepared a simple Mediterranean Breakfast Bowl. The dish featured fluffy whole-grain tortillas, sliced avocado, cherry tomatoes, crisp cucumber, feta cheese, and a lemon-herb olive oil dressing. Her son Eddy helped spread the tortillas, while Mei carefully arranged the colorful ingredients in a shallow bowl.

She toasted the avocado, then gently folded the toppings over the warm tortilla. The dish was served at breakfast with a side of warm fruit and a glass of fresh orange juice. As it warmed in the morning kitchen, it smelled rich and aromatic—herbs, citrus, toasted nuts, and the subtle scent of olive oil mingling together.

The occasion was a quiet Saturday breakfast, an early start for the week, and a chance to spend time with her family. Mei Lin’s nurturing nature made the meal feel thoughtful rather than rushed, and she took time to ensure everyone was comfortable and enjoying it.
