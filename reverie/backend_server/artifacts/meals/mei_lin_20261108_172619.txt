Mei Lin prepared Eggplant and Tomato Egg with Stir-Fried Green Beans for a quiet, nourishing breakfast before Eddy’s morning studies.

The dish included two perfectly fried eggs, sliced eggplant, halved tomatoes, green beans, shredded carrots, a tablespoon of soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, and a pinch of red pepper. Mei stir-fried the vegetables until the beans were crisp and the eggplant softened, then added the eggs and gently folded them in. She served it on a white ceramic plate, garnishing it with sesame seeds and a little fresh cilantro.

The kitchen smelled of toasted sesame, garlic, and the warm aroma of simmering eggs. The presentation was simple and inviting—soft, golden eggs, vibrant green vegetables, and a bright red tomato created a cheerful, balanced meal. It was a quiet, family breakfast prepared for Eddy, with Mei Lin keeping the workspace tidy and ready to support him in his studies.
