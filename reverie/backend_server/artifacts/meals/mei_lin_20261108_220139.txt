Mei Lin prepared a Braised Pork and Ginger Chicken with Brown Rice for a family dinner on Tuesday, February 14. The dish was made by searing bone-in chicken breasts, garlic, ginger, and a splash of soy sauce, then simmering them with soy sauce, oyster sauce, and a small amount of brown sugar for 30–35 minutes until the chicken was tender and the sauce had thickened. She added fresh broccoli, carrots, and snap peas, then finished the rice with a dash of sesame oil.

The aroma was warm and comforting: roasted garlic, ginger, soy, and caramelized pork mingled with the savory scent of the rice. She served the chicken in a shallow bowl, arranged the vegetables neatly beside it, and placed a portion of fluffy brown rice on the side. The bright green vegetables and golden chicken contrasted beautifully with the rich brown rice.

Mei Lin enjoyed the meal while the kitchen was still warm, making it a cozy family dinner for the evening before the Johnson Park fundraiser. It was a simple, nourishing meal designed to bring everyone together and support the event’s spirit of community.
