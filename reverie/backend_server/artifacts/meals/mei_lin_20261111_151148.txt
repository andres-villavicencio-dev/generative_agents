Mei Lin prepared a hearty Korean-Style Beef and Vegetable Stir-Fry, a comforting family dinner inspired by her husband’s favorite. The main dish featured tender beef strips marinated in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil, sliced into even bite-sized pieces. With the beef cooked until caramelized, she added chopped onions, carrots, bell peppers, snap peas, and a handful of edamame.

The preparation method was quick and efficient: all ingredients were stir-fried in a large skillet over medium heat, with the beef cooked first before the vegetables were added for a few minutes. She stirred frequently to ensure the beef remained tender and the vegetables stayed bright and crisp.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and savory—rich from the soy sauce, garlic, and ginger, with the fresh scent of vegetables adding a cheerful brightness.

She plated the dish in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the beef and adding a small bowl of rice for easy eating. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the golden-brown meat as the centerpiece and the vivid vegetables completing the colorful dish.

The occasion was a relaxed Monday evening dinner, with Mei Lin’s family gathered around the table. It was a quiet, nourishing moment of connection, while she also prepared a note for her next research paper.
