Mei Lin prepared Golden Rice with Roasted Chicken and Vegetable Stew, a comforting family dinner for her husband, John, and son, Eddy. The dish featured cooked golden rice, tender roasted chicken, carrots, potatoes, garlic, ginger, and a savory tomato-onion gravy.

She began by toasting the rice and sautéing the vegetables in a large wok over medium heat. The chicken was seared until golden, then added to the wok with the vegetables and spices, cooking until the chicken was tender and the rice was fluffy. She finished the stew with a little soy sauce, sesame oil, and a pinch of sugar to balance the flavors.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted garlic, ginger, and savory broth. Mei served the dish in a wide ceramic bowl, garnishing it with chopped green onions and scattering the remaining golden rice around the edges. It was simple yet hearty, fitting the quiet evening after her college teaching and research, and ready for the family to enjoy together.
