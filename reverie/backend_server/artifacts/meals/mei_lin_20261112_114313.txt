Mei Lin prepared Roasted Vegetable Soup with Crispy Chicken, a comforting family meal that reflected her nurturing nature and love for simple, nourishing food. She used a yellow onion, carrots, parsnips, garlic, thyme, rosemary, vegetable broth, chicken breasts, and a touch of soy sauce for a savory, aromatic flavor. The chicken was seared in a skillet until golden, then simmered in the broth with the root vegetables, which softened and released their natural sweetness.

She added fresh herbs, chopped parsley, and a splash of lemon juice before serving, while the vegetables retained a vibrant green and the chicken remained tender and slightly crispy on the outside. The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted garlic, thyme, and simmered broth.

The meal was served in a white ceramic bowl, with the soup filling the bowl and the chicken arranged beside it. Topped with a small sprig of parsley and a sprinkle of cracked pepper, the presentation was simple yet elegant, making the meal feel welcoming and balanced.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday lunch on February 14, the day after Valentine’s Day. Mei prepared it while balancing her teaching and research duties with her children’s schoolwork, creating a calm, nurturing meal for herself and her family.
