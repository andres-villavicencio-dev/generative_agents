Michael prepared a Golden-Browned Breakfast Egg and Veggie Platter for breakfast. His favorite ingredient was the sunny‑side‑up egg, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a sprinkle of paprika. He also added diced tomatoes, cucumber slices, shredded cheddar, and a small handful of spinach.

He began by gently cooking the eggs in a pan, letting each yolk set slowly. While the eggs cooked, he sautéed the vegetables in butter until the pan released a warm, savory aroma. When the eggs were perfectly set, he folded the colorful vegetables into a warm, golden‑brown mixture. He arranged the eggs and vegetables on a white ceramic plate and topped the dish with toasted breadcrumbs and fresh parsley, adding a final drizzle of lemon juice.

The presentation was cheerful and inviting, with the bright colors of the vegetables contrasting against the creamy egg. The occasion was Michael’s regular morning breakfast, and the food smelled fresh, comforting, and full of breakfast energy.
