Dish: Luminous River Oatmeal  
Key Ingredients: rolled oats, milk or plant-based milk, cinnamon, a pinch of salt, a few drops of vanilla, and chopped fresh fruit or berries for color.  
Preparation Method: Michael stirred the oats with the milk until it thickened, then added the warm spices and fruit to infuse the oatmeal with a gentle, warm aroma. He let the mixture simmer briefly, then topped it with the fruit, drizzled a little honey, and served it in a small, ceramic bowl.  
Presentation: The oatmeal was arranged attractively on a simple wooden tray, with the colorful fruit providing a bright contrast against the creamy base. A small spoon rested beside the bowl, and the warm cinnamon scent seemed to linger in the air.  
Occasion: It was a quiet Tuesday breakfast for Michael at Oak Hill College. After a busy day of classes, sketching, and photographing the river, he treated the oatmeal as a peaceful way to begin his day.
