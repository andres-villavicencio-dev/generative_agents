Michael prepared a Luminous River Oatmeal Breakfast for himself on Thursday, February 16. The dish combined rolled oats, steamed milk, cinnamon, warm honey, and a splash of vanilla extract, with toasted almonds and a few chopped figs for texture and sweetness. He stirred the oatmeal gently over medium heat, letting the oats absorb the liquid and soften, then topped the bowl with golden-brown toast and a small fresh lemon wedge.

The aroma was warm, comforting, and subtly earthy—cinnamon, honey, toasted nuts, and the faint scent of steamed milk. The presentation was bright and inviting: the oatmeal was served in a white ceramic bowl, with the golden toast and honey drizzle adding visual contrast. It resembled a cozy riverside breakfast, with the golden oats flowing like a gentle stream and the warm cinnamon creating a luminous, dreamy effect.

The occasion was a quiet morning routine: Michael woke up, made coffee, prepared the oatmeal, and reviewed his homework while continuing to fill his sketchbook with new fantasy character ideas. It was a peaceful start to a busy day, blending the nourishment of breakfast with his creative and academic responsibilities.
