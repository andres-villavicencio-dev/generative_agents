Michael prepared a Luminous River Rice Bowl for lunch. The dish featured short-grain rice, warm chicken thighs, cherry tomatoes, red bell peppers, baby spinach, cucumber, and a light sesame oil dressing with a splash of lemon juice. He sautéed the chicken until golden, then arranged it with the colorful vegetables and rice in a wide, shallow bowl. A small bowl of sesame seeds and a sprig of cilantro were placed nearby, while the finished meal was photographed with the river in the background.

The aroma of sautéed chicken and fresh herbs filled the kitchen, while the bright reds, greens, and golden rice created a cheerful, inviting presentation. He prepared it to share with his classmates during the lunch break, turning a simple weekday meal into a creative lunchtime experience. The relaxed setting made the bright colors and simple ingredients feel purposeful and joyful.
