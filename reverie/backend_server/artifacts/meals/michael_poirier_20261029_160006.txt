Michael prepared a simple but warm Lemon-Ginger Butter Chicken with Rice and Green Beans, a classic comfort meal that he adapted for Friday’s relaxed lunch. He sautéed chicken in olive oil, added chopped ginger, garlic, and fresh parsley, then simmered it with chicken broth, a pinch of salt, and a few drops of lemon juice until the meat reached a gentle pink. He served the chicken over fluffy rice and green beans, garnished with fresh basil and a drizzle of toasted pine nuts.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of roasted ginger and garlic, the savory scent of the chicken, and the fresh scent of lemon. He presented the meal in a simple white bowl, with the green beans and pine nuts adding bright color against the golden chicken. He ate it while washing his hands and sketching a new fantasy character, his sketchbook and Procreate canvas ready for the day’s creative work.
