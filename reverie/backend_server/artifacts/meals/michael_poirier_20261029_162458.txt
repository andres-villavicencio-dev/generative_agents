On Friday, February 17, Michael prepared Luminous Rice and Mushroom Risotto for lunch while visiting the nearby market. He washed, drained, and sliced mushrooms, then sautéed them in a pan with garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of salt until their edges turned slightly golden. He added Arborio rice, stirring it until it absorbed the liquid and began to soften. After about ten minutes, he poured in a small amount of warm vegetable broth, stirring continuously until the rice was creamy and tender. He finished the dish by simmering it briefly and serving it with a side of steamed broccoli and a sprinkle of fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with the rich, earthy aroma of toasted garlic, mushrooms, and rice. The risotto appeared as a creamy mound of golden grains, with translucent mushroom bits and finely chopped herbs arranged carefully in a shallow bowl. It was served for a quiet lunch before Michael returned to sketch his fantasy characters and photograph the river, giving him time to rest and replenish his energy before continuing his creative work.
