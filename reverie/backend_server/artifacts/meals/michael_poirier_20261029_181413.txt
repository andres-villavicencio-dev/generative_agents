Michael prepared a simple River of Possibilities Vegetable Stir-Fry. He sliced carrots, peas, baby carrots, snap peas, and bell peppers, and added a small handful of mushrooms for extra earthiness.

He stir-fried the vegetables in a pan with a little oil until they warmed through, then tossed in a pinch of toasted sesame oil and a splash of soy sauce. A little ginger and garlic added a fresh aroma, and he finished the dish with a squeeze of lime. The colorful vegetables retained their bright colors and crisp edges.

He plated the stir-fry in a shallow bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around a small plate of plain rice with a spoonful of chili oil on the side. The presentation was cheerful and appetizing, matching the imaginative, misty river landscapes he was developing.

The meal was meant to be a quiet dinner after checking his Procreate canvases and sketchbook, perhaps to accompany a walk with Hua Lai or simply to enjoy a relaxed Friday evening.
