Michael prepared a River-Roasted Vegetable Sandwich with Honey-Lime Crostini and Fresh Fruit Tart. The dish featured whole-wheat sandwich bread, honey-roasted carrots, celery, radishes, and cucumbers, with a bright dressing of lemon juice, olive oil, and herbs. He layered the vegetables on the sandwiches, topped them with crisp lettuce and a creamy goat cheese dip, and placed lemon slices, toasted nuts, and fresh fruit on the crostini. A small tart with vanilla custard and berries served as a sweet finish.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, caramelized carrots, garlic, and citrus. Michael presented the meal in a simple, rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the sandwiches on a platter and setting the tart beside them. The occasion was a quiet family lunch for the café opening, and Michael wanted everyone to enjoy a colorful, comforting meal while he finished his homework.
