On a quiet Monday evening, Michael prepared River-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze for dinner with his friend Maria. The meal reflected his creative side: golden roasted chicken, carrots, parsnips, and red potatoes arranged on a warm ceramic platter, with fresh herbs, lemon slices, and a sprinkle of sea salt for garnish. Michael marinated the chicken in a mixture of olive oil, garlic, rosemary, lemon juice, and a little soy sauce, then roasted it over the oven until the skin became crisp and the meat was tender. He arranged the vegetables beside it, then drizzled a glossy lemon-herb glaze over the chicken before serving.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, citrus, and savory chicken. Michael carried the plate to his bedroom table, where Maria sat beside him.
