Michael prepared a Warm Oatmeal Breakfast with Fruit and Lemon, a simple yet nourishing start to his Tuesday. The dish consisted of cooked oatmeal topped with fresh berries, sliced banana, and a small bowl of whole almonds. He used water or milk to cook the oats until they were soft and slightly creamy, then arranged the fruit on the counter beside a plate. A lemon wedge and a pinch of cinnamon sugar were placed nearby, adding a warm, aromatic touch.

The preparation method was straightforward: the oats were simmered with water and a little milk, then gently heated with the fruit and almonds. The kitchen filled with the comforting scent of oats, honey, citrus, and cinnamon. Afterward, Michael plated the breakfast in a casual, inviting way—oats in the center, fruit arranged around it, and the lemon and cinnamon sugar as a finishing accent.

The meal was meant to serve as a quiet, creative breakfast before his confirmed dinner at 7:00 PM. It reflected his relaxed, purposeful routine and the care he puts into his everyday life, with the warm aroma of breakfast giving him a gentle reminder to stay focused on his creative work.
