Michael Poirier prepared a Riverlight Fantasy Side: Smoked Salmon with Roasted Red Onion and Herbed Potato. The dish is a simple but flavorful side that complements his Riverlight Fantasy project.

The smoked salmon was thinly sliced, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little lemon zest, then arranged around the plate. Roasted red onions were sliced thinly and tossed with a small amount of olive oil, salt, and fresh thyme. Herbed potatoes were lightly roasted until crisp, then served alongside the other ingredients.

Aromatic smoke drifted from the salmon and roasted potatoes, while the bright orange-red color of the onions and golden potatoes gave the dish a warm, inviting appearance. The presentation was casual and rustic—served on a wooden serving board with a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing and a tiny pile of chopped nuts.

The meal was prepared as a relaxed dinner for his lunchtime break. Although Michael was carrying over important school and creative reminders, the side dish gave him a moment to pause, reflect, and enjoy the simple pleasure of a well-prepared meal before continuing his busy day.
