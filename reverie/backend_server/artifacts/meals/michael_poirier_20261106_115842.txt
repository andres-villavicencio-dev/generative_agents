Michael prepared a Warm Cinnamon-Sugar Toast with Lemon Zest and Toasted Almonds. The dish began with toasted almonds and fresh lemon zest finely ground over a small pan, creating an aromatic, fragrant base of warm honey and citrus. He layered this with a lightly buttered slice of sourdough and sprinkled it with cinnamon sugar, then baked it until the golden crust was crisp and the interior was warm and tender.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: a small ceramic plate with the golden toast, a few extra almonds, a lemon wedge, and a sprig of fresh mint. Michael served it at 7:00 PM on Tuesday, February 14, during a confirmed dinner at The Rose. He followed the meal with a quiet evening, sketching a new fantasy character while jotting down his ideas in his notebook, balancing the creative energy of his day with the warmth of a carefully prepared treat.
