Dish: River-Side Vegetable Soup with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Butter Croutons

Michael prepared a hearty, colorful soup for lunch on a rainy Monday. He began by sautéing carrots, onions, and celery in olive oil until soft, then added a can of diced tomatoes, a few garlic cloves, and a pinch of thyme. He poured in vegetable broth, covered the pot, and let it simmer until the vegetables were tender.

For the presentation, he roasted a red potato, a yellow potato, and a small red pepper with lemon and black pepper, then sliced them into uniform rounds. He added fresh herbs and a drizzle of butter before serving the soup over warm crusty bread. He finished the meal with a simple lemon-herb crouton and a glass of water, keeping a napkin nearby for wiping his hands.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of carrots, garlic, broth, and lemon. The presentation was bright and rustic, with the warm, earthy tones of the vegetables contrasting against the creamy soup. It was a quiet, nourishing meal, prepared before Michael had to tackle homework and sketchbook ideas for his digital fantasy character portfolio.
