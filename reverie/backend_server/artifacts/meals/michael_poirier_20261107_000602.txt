Michael prepared River Leo’s Forest Feast, a simple yet whimsical lunch for his college day. The dish featured fresh carrots, sweet potatoes, and roasted root vegetables, with chicken thighs simmered in a herb- and lemon-infused broth and topped with crispy golden-brown potatoes. The vegetables were roasted until tender and caramelized, while the chicken was cooked until the meat was juicy and the skin was crisp.

The kitchen smelled of warm roasted herbs, citrus, and the savory aroma of simmering broth, filling the small dining table. Michael arranged the colorful vegetables and meat on a platter, adding a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing and sprinkling the finished dish with a few edible green onions. The presentation was bright and playful, with the vegetables arranged in a loose, imaginative line that reflected the whimsical River Leo character Michael was refining.

The occasion was an informal Tuesday lunch at Oak Hill College, after Michael had spent the morning developing his fantasy project and sketching the character. The meal was practical and comforting, chosen so Michael could focus on his homework and listen to the music with his friend Eddy Lin.
