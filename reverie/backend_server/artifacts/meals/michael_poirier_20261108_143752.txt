Michael prepared Roasted Tomato, Herb, and Garlic Chicken Salad with Sweet Potato as his main lunch. He sliced whole chicken pieces, tossed them with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and oregano, then roasted them at 400°F until the skin turned golden and the meat was tender. He sliced the chicken, chopped the sweet potatoes, and added crisp tomatoes, cucumber, arugula, and a lemon-herb vinaigrette. The salad was plated over a bed of roasted sweet potato, garnished with fresh herbs and a few whole olives.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, and herbs, while the bright reds, greens, and creamy vinaigrette made the presentation vibrant and inviting. It was a hearty, colorful lunch for his Friday afternoons at Oak Hill College, designed to keep him energized and ready to sketch his fantasy characters and prepare for his theme-song collaboration with Eddy Lin.
