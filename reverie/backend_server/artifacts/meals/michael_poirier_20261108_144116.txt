Michael prepared Garden Herb Roasted Salmon with Lemon-Parmesan Crust as a refreshing side to balance his main course. He arranged the salmon fillets on a large platter and topped them with roasted carrots, green beans, and a small mound of mixed seasonal vegetables. The fish was seasoned with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, salt, and pepper, then brushed with a simple crust made from flour, egg, and a pinch of paprika. After roasting until the skin turned golden and the meat was tender, Michael finished the dish with a bright lemon wedge and a sprinkle of fennel seeds.

The kitchen filled with the savory, earthy aroma of roasted vegetables and the warm scent of garlic, rosemary, and sea salt. The presentation was colorful and inviting: the golden salmon stood out among the vibrant green beans and orange carrots, while the creamy lemon-parmesan crust added a touch of texture. The meal was prepared for a casual family dinner on a Monday night, with the warm, comforting side intended to complement Michael’s favorite main course.
