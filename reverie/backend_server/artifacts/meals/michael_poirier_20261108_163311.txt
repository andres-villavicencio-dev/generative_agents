Michael prepared a simple River Trout Salad with Lemon-Lime Vinaigrette, inspired by the river he photographs. The meal was made for dinner around 6 p.m., after he had completed his homework and sketchbook.

Key ingredients: fresh river trout, crisp cabbage, carrots, apple, celery, lemon-lime vinaigrette, and a sprig of rosemary.

Preparation method: Michael washed the trout and sliced it into thin strips. He sautéed the vegetables in a pan with a little olive oil until the carrots were tender-crisp, then added the trout to finish cooking. He dressed the salad just before serving and arranged the ingredients in a colorful bowl.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the rich scent of searing fish, caramelized carrots, and fresh herbs.

Presentation: The salad was arranged on a white plate with a small bowl of lemon-lime dressing nearby, a tiny glass of water beside it, and a sprig of rosemary added for visual appeal.

Occasion: It was a relaxed family dinner on a Monday, prepared for Michael’s evening homework and creative projects. He wanted a simple, nourishing meal that would let him focus on sketching and writing.
