Michael prepared Crispy Egg and Herb Toast for breakfast. He used two slices of whole-grain bread, two soft-boiled eggs, chopped chives, parsley, minced garlic, and a little lemon juice. He spread the egg mixture over the toast and let it crisp in a hot pan, then topped it with the fresh herbs.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, gently caramelized egg, and savory herbs. The presentation was bright and inviting: golden brown toast served alongside two contrasting eggs, with the herbs sprinkled generously over everything.

This was a simple, nourishing breakfast for an early Tuesday morning, giving Michael enough time to follow up with Tamara Taylor, check the town-service shift details, and begin his creative work at Oak Hill College.
