Michael prepared Rustic Vegetable Risotto with Lemon and Garlic for lunch. The key ingredients were Arborio rice, chicken broth, chopped onions, carrots, celery, parmesan cheese, and a pinch of red pepper flakes. He sautéed the vegetables in a pan until they softened, then added the rice and broth, stirring constantly until the grains absorbed the liquid and became creamy. He finished the dish by sprinkling Parmesan and adding a final squeeze of lemon.

The aroma was warm and earthy, with the bright scent of lemon and herbs carrying through the kitchen. He plated the risotto in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with thin strips of carrot and a small lemon wheel. The simple, colorful presentation made the meal feel inviting and balanced.

He prepared this as a relaxed lunch on a Tuesday morning, using the extra time to cook instead of eating immediately. It was a quiet, nourishing meal, and the cheerful smell of cooking made him feel ready to face the day ahead.
