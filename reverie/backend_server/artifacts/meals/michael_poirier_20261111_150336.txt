Michael prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Marinade, a simple but colorful lunch for dinner. He marinated chicken thighs with lemon, garlic, olive oil, and dried herbs, then brushed them with the mixture before grilling them over a medium flame. He also roasted red bell peppers, carrots, and onions until their skins softened and their edges caramelized.

The meal smelled warm and aromatic as the grill released its scent of garlic, herbs, and slightly charred vegetables. It was bright and hearty, with golden chicken, crisp vegetables, and a glossy lemon-infused glaze.

He plated the chicken on a white plate, arranging the vegetables around it in neat, contrasting shapes. A small bowl of lemon-herb sauce and a few fresh herbs served as a finishing touch. The occasion was an early lunch before he returned to Oak Hill College, where he wanted to refuel his energy before cooking dinner.
