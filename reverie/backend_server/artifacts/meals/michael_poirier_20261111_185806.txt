Michael prepared a Simple Fruit-Root Vegetable Salad with Warm Vegetable Soup for himself and Eddy. The meal combined crisp apple slices, pears, and celery with carrots, beets, and zucchini, seasoned with honey, lemon, and a dash of cumin. For the soup, he simmered the leftover vegetables with a little vegetable broth, garlic, and a pinch of salt until the carrots and celery softened.

Aromatic, the soup had a warm, savory aroma of caramelized carrots and fresh herbs, while the salad offered a bright, tangy finish. He arranged the colorful fruit and vegetables in a shallow bowl, ladled the soup over them, and placed a small plate of fresh berries beside it. The meal was presented as a cheerful, family-style lunch on a Tuesday afternoon, with Eddy joining him to eat.
