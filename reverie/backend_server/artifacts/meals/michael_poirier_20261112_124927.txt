Michael prepared Misty’s Riverroot Soup, a comforting, herbal broth he made in the kitchen before heading to the grocery store. He began by washing root vegetables—carrots, parsnips, and celery—then sliced them into thin rounds. He minced garlic, chopped fresh dill, added a handful of onions, and poured in vegetable broth, herbs, and a splash of vinegar. He gently simmered the soup for about twenty minutes until the flavors melded and the vegetables were tender.

The finished dish had the aroma of warm root vegetables, garlic, and fresh herbs. He served it in a shallow bowl, garnishing the top with chopped parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice. The presentation was simple and rustic, with translucent carrots and bright green dill contrasting against the rich broth. It was a wholesome, family-style meal for his Tuesday dinner, prepared with care and the same attention Michael gave to his fantasy character sketches.
