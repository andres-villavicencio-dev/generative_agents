Michael prepared a Misty Valley Vegetable Stir-Fry, a colorful, hearty dinner to accompany his dinner preparation. He chopped carrots, bell peppers, broccoli, and onions, then added olive oil, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and a splash of lemon juice to a wok. He stir-fried the vegetables until they were bright and crisp, then tossed them in a small bowl with rice, soy sauce, and a pinch of red pepper flakes.

The dish was seasoned generously, releasing a warm aroma of garlic, ginger, and toasted vegetables. He arranged the rice and vegetables in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with sliced green onions and a few whole peppercorns. The meal was simple but nourishing, fitting into his busy Tuesday without overpowering his focus on sketchbooks and homework. It was prepared as a practical family-style dinner, ready for sharing after he had finished cooking.
