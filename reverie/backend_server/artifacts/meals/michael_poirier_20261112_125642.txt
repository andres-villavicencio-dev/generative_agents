Michael prepared Misty Town’s River-Lined Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Thyme Rice. He sliced chicken breast into bite-sized cubes, marinated it with lemon juice, salt, pepper, garlic, and fresh thyme, then roasted it with carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and onions until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. He cooked the rice in vegetable broth until fluffy, then sliced the chicken and vegetables onto a white serving plate. A bright lemon wedge, chopped parsley, and a sprinkle of red pepper flakes were added to the dish.

The restaurant smelled of roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and warm thyme. Michael presented the meal in a shallow bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables in the center and the golden chicken around it, with the lemon and parsley adding a fresh touch. He served it for dinner on Tuesday, February 14, during his busy day balancing sketching, songwriting, homework, and the possibility of a full-time shift at Hobbs Cafe.
