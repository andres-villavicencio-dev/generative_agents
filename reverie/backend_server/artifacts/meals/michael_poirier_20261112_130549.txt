Michael prepared Riverlight, a hearty two-egg, bread, and milk breakfast for his family. The dish featured two golden, fluffy eggs served with a soft, golden-brown crust on a rustic sourdough bread and a warm, creamy milk. A pinch of sea salt and a tiny sprig of fresh thyme added a subtle hint of earthiness.

He broke the eggs into a lightly buttered pan, letting them sizzle and turn into a rich, yellow custard. He sliced the bread to a generous thickness and warmed the milk until it bubbled gently, then ladled it over the eggs and bread.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the comforting scent of toasted bread, rich butter, fresh milk, and a hint of savory herbs. He presented the dish on a small wooden platter, arranging the warm eggs around the bread and pouring the milk over the top before serving.

The occasion was an early Tuesday morning meal for his family, and the simple, rustic presentation made Riverlight feel inviting and comforting. It was more than breakfast—it was a quiet gathering around the kitchen, a moment of shared warmth before the day’s adventures.
