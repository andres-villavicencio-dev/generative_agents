Michael prepared Riverroot & Herb Pot-Pourie, a warm, earthy vegetable stew inspired by the misty town and hidden paths he was developing. The dish included baby carrots, leeks, celery, carrots, onions, mushrooms, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a splash of lemon juice.

He sliced the vegetables, washed them, and sautéed the garlic and herbs in a pan until fragrant. He then added the vegetables, mushrooms, and carrots to the pan, stirring them over medium heat until the carrots softened and the vegetables began to soften. As the dish cooked, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbaceous leeks, carrots, and earthy mushrooms.

Michael presented the pot-pourie in a shallow white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables in an appealing spiral. A small sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge were placed on the side. The presentation was simple and rustic, emphasizing the seasonal, garden-inspired flavors and the mysterious, cozy atmosphere of his fantasy world.

It was the perfect late-afternoon meal for his Tuesday dinner, balancing his busy schedule with creativity and a comforting, nourishing dish.
