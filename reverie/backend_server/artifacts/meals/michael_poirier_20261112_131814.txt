Michael prepared a simple Misty Town Vegetable Risotto for his dinner on Tuesday, February 14. He began by washing and chopping fresh vegetables—carrots, celery, onion, garlic, and bell peppers—while he stirred the rice in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat. He added the broth gradually and stirred until the rice absorbed the liquid, then finished it with a little butter and olive oil.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of toasted onions, caramelized carrots, and savory broth. When he plated the dish, he arranged the vegetables in a small bowl and served it with a side of crusty bread and a small glass of red wine. The bright colors and rustic presentation felt informal, as if it were an early dinner after a full workday.

He ate it quietly, perhaps thinking about the mysterious local vendor, misty town, and hidden paths he had been sketching. The meal was comforting, and his calm reflection suggested that dinner had made him feel more settled.
