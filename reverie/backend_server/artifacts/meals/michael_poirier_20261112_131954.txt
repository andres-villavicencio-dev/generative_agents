Michael Poirier prepared Roasted Root Vegetable Stew with Herb Croutons, a warm, hearty dinner perfect for a Tuesday night.

The main dish consists of carrots, parsnips, red bell peppers, onions, and potatoes, all tossed with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, and sea salt. The vegetables are roasted until tender and caramelized, then simmered with a splash of vegetable broth and a little cream to create a rich, comforting stew. He finished the dish with a generous serving of herb-crouton bread, adding a little extra garlic and a sprinkle of paprika for brightness.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted root vegetables, garlic, rosemary, and a little butter. The bright orange carrots, deep red peppers, and golden-brown croutons made the colorful meal visually appealing. He presented it in a simple white bowl, arranging the vegetables in a neat mound and placing the croutons on the side.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday dinner after school. Michael had prepared the meal to enjoy a satisfying, nourishing evening while he still considered whether his Hobbs Cafe shift would be permanent.
