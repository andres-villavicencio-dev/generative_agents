Michael prepared a Misty Town Mushroom Risotto with Lemon Herb Oil to serve as a cozy dinner for his evening. The dish was made with Arborio rice, wild mushrooms, garlic, shallots, and a splash of dry white wine, cooked in a slow pan until the rice became creamy and the mushrooms were tender. He added fresh parsley, lemon zest, and a final drizzle of the warm herb oil, giving it a bright citrus finish.

The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of roasted mushroom and garlic, followed by the fresh aroma of lemon and herbs. He arranged the rice in a shallow dish, sprinkled the mushrooms over the top, and placed a lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley beside it. The presentation was simple and rustic, with the warm amber rice contrasting against the green parsley and colorful mushroom caps.

It was a quiet, intimate dinner for his Tuesday evening, a gentle balance of warmth and freshness that complemented the mysterious fantasy ideas he was developing.
