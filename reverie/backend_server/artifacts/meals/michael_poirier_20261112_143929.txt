Michael prepared a Morning Garden Breakfast with Honey-Lemon Oatmeal, a simple, nourishing meal that matched his relaxed Tuesday. He used rolled oats, warm milk, honey, lemon slices, blueberries, and a sprinkle of cinnamon. He toasted the oats briefly, then stirred them into the milk until the oats softened, adding the fruit and honey for a sweet finish. The warm broth and fresh fruit gave the dish a gentle aroma of citrus, cinnamon, and earthy berries.

On a warm plate, Michael arranged the oatmeal in a small mound and scattered the blueberries around it, with lemon slices and a few toasted oats visible on the side. The presentation was simple and inviting, with the colorful fruit providing a pop of color against the creamy oatmeal. It was the perfect quick breakfast for finishing his homework, followed by a quiet moment of reflection before heading to Hobbs Cafe.
