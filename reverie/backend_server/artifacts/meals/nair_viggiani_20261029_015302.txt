Nair prepared Salsa Verde with Roasted Corn and Quinoa, a vibrant Mexican-inspired dinner that balanced savory, citrusy, and floral flavors. The key ingredients were fresh green chilies, red onion, cilantro, garlic, lime juice, cumin, oregano, salt, pepper, and roasted sweet corn. He marinated the vegetables in a warm lime-cumin sauce, then roasted them until the chilies softened and the corn burst with golden sweetness. The quinoa was cooked separately and topped with sliced avocado, a dollop of crema, and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of roasted corn, warm chilies, fresh cilantro, and the sharp, citrusy perfume of lime. Nair presented the dish on a rustic wooden platter, arranging the colorful vegetables in a loose pyramid with the quinoa and avocado as the finishing touches. A fresh lime wedge and a piece of crusty bread accompanied the meal.

The occasion was Nair’s Tuesday dinner at home, a relaxed evening before he began work on his debut album and student Eddy Lin’s piano recital. The simple, communal preparation reflected his laid-back, community-minded nature, while the colorful presentation added a joyful touch to an otherwise ordinary Tuesday.
