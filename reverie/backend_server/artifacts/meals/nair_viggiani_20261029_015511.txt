Dish: Cecina Soup with Crispy Chicken and Lentils

Key ingredients: bone-in chicken, smoked chorizo, tomatoes, onion, garlic, cumin, oregano, vegetable broth, diced potatoes, carrots, and green beans.

Preparation method: Nair first seared the chicken and chorizo in a pan, then deglazed the pan with broth and simmered the soup until the chicken fell off the bone. He added the vegetables, potatoes, and beans, stirring until the liquid reduced and the flavors melded. The stew finished on the stove, and he finished it in the oven for even heating.

Aroma: Warm, smoky, earthy, and subtly spicy—cumin, smoked paprika, and roasted chicken filling the kitchen.

Presentation: He served a steaming bowl of soup with the chicken resting on the side, colorful vegetables, and the crispy chorizo crisped in the pan. A simple lemon wedge and a sprig of cilantro added a bright finish.

Occasion: It was a quiet Tuesday night for Nair preparing for his debut album and student Eddy Lin’s piano recital. The meal reflected his community-minded, laid-back side, while his focus remained on music and preparation.
