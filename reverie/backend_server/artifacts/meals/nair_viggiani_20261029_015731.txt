Nair prepared Crispy Roast Chicken with Garlic Roasted Vegetables for dinner, making it a hearty, communal meal suitable for his rehearsal and student recital.

The dish features a whole chicken roasted with paprika, cumin, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a splash of lemon juice, while the vegetables—including carrots, potatoes, onions, and green beans—were caramelized in the same pan. Nair finished the chicken by basting it with pan juices and finished it with a squeeze of lemon and fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and savory poultry. When Nair plated the meal, he arranged the golden chicken in the center and surrounded it with colorful vegetables, garnishing it with a simple citrus drizzle. The dish was presented on a platter with a handwritten note, “Good evening, everyone,” giving the dinner a warm, welcoming feel.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday evening, balancing his music and piano-teaching routines. Nair hoped the hearty meal would encourage students and friends to gather around, share stories, and enjoy a quiet evening together.
