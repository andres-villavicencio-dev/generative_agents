Nair prepared Fajitas with Roasted Corn, Black Bean Salad, and Warm Tortillas for an early Tuesday dinner. The main dish featured thinly sliced onions and bell peppers marinated in garlic, cumin, chili powder, and a little lime juice, then seared until charred and tender. He roasted sweet corn in olive oil and topped it with a black bean salad of diced tomatoes, onions, cilantro, and a light vinaigrette. The warm tortillas served alongside the meal were seasoned with the same Mexican-inspired spices.

The aroma filled the kitchen with toasted corn, warm peppers, chili, and the earthy scent of garlic, creating a comforting, earthy scent. He arranged the colorful plates in a casual cluster, garnished with fresh cilantro and a drizzle of lime, with the bright orange corn standing out against the dark beans and crisp peppers.

The occasion was a relaxed family dinner, not the debut album or Eddy’s recital, so the meal was simple, communal, and filled with the laid-back energy Nair is known for.
