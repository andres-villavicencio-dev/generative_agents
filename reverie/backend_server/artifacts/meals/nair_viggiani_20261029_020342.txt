Nair prepared Chicken Fajitas with Chimichurri-Lime Rice, a simple yet flavorful dinner for Tuesday, February 14. The main dish featured chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, garlic, cumin, paprika, and a splash of lime juice, then sautéed until tender. Brightly colored bell peppers, onions, and jalapeños were sliced and briefly roasted until crisp. The chicken was shredded, mixed with the vegetable juices, and served over fluffy rice flavored with black beans, chili powder, cumin, and a generous amount of fresh chilimarron sauce.

Chimichurri—made from parsley, garlic, olive oil, red pepper, and fresh lime—was poured over the rice and peppers, adding a spicy, herbaceous finish. The aroma was warm and savory, with the scent of roasted vegetables, citrus, garlic, and chili filling the kitchen.

Nair presented the meal on a large white ceramic platter, with the golden chicken, colorful vegetables, and bright rice arranged around a small bowl of white rice garnished with sesame seeds. The setting was casual and inviting, fitting the relaxed Tuesday evening before his piano recital and upcoming debut album. It was a quiet, communal meal—part of his preparation for teaching, but also a peaceful way to unwind after a long day.
