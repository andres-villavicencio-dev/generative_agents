Nair prepared Cajun-Roasted Chicken with Herb Crusted Potatoes for his evening meal. He sliced a whole chicken, seasoned it with smoked paprika, garlic, thyme, oregano, and a pinch of cayenne, then roasted it in the oven until the skin was crisp and golden. The potatoes were buttered with garlic, thyme, and a splash of lime juice before being baked alongside the chicken.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted poultry, garlic, and herbs, while the sharp citrus of the lemon added a bright finish. Nair plated the chicken in a simple white bowl and arranged the golden potatoes around it, garnishing each with a sprig of parsley and a few dried chili flakes. He set the dishes down for lunch on February 14, the day before his debut album and Eddy Lin’s recital, using the meal as a quiet moment of rest before preparing for rehearsals and creative work.
