Nair prepared Lentejo con Jamón Ibérico and Chorizo, a hearty, colorful Spanish breakfast-style lunch. The dish featured slow-cooked lentils, diced potatoes, onions, carrots, celery, garlic, cumin, oregano, and paprika, topped with crispy ham and spicy chorizo.

Nair sautéed the vegetables in olive oil, then added the beans, potatoes, and spices. He simmered everything until the lentils were tender and the potatoes were fully cooked, serving the dish over warm crusty bread. The combination of savory chorizo and sweet ham gave it a rich, aromatic depth, while the bright reds and golden yellows made the meal visually festive.

Nair presented the lunch at home on a simple wooden table, surrounded by his favorite bowls and plates. The setting reflected his laid-back, community-minded nature—the relaxed meal prepared with the remaining ingredients from Monday’s demanding launch, with music and garden exploration providing a gentle backdrop. It was a warm, purposeful lunch meant to nourish both his body and his creative life.
