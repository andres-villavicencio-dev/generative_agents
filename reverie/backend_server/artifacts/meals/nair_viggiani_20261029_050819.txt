Nair prepared Roasted Red Pepper, Garlic, and Herb Rice with Roasted Chicken, a simple yet warm Monday lunch that balanced his demanding schedule. He washed vegetables and chopped garlic, herbs, and chicken, then tossed everything in olive oil, salt, pepper, and a touch of smoked paprika. The chicken was seared, then slow-roasted until golden and tender, while the rice absorbed the savory juices and became fragrant with roasted peppers and aromatic herbs.

The kitchen filled with the rich scent of caramelized garlic, sweet roasted vegetables, and toasted herbs. Nair presented the rice in a shallow white bowl, placing a whole roasted chicken and a colorful arrangement of peppers and herbs beside it, garnished with a sprig of basil. It was a casual, nourishing meal designed to nourish both his body and his mind as he returned home, cooking lunch while exploring the garden.

The occasion was a quiet transition from Monday’s busy launch to a relaxed Wednesday. Nair ate with Eddy Lin, reflecting on his music, the upcoming recital, and the garden’s healing energy. The meal felt grounding, creative, and celebratory—not just of the food, but of what his community and creative pursuits meant to him.
