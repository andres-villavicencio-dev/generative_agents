Nair Viggiani prepared Garden-Rooted Stew with Crispy Tortillas, a warm, communal lunch inspired by the healing garden he explored. The stew featured heirloom tomatoes, garlic, onions, carrots, celery, cumin, chili powder, and a splash of warm vinegar, with a pinch of salt added to balance the flavors as he remembered. He stirred the pot over low heat until the vegetables softened, then finished it with a little olive oil and a generous handful of fresh cilantro.

He served the stew in a shallow bowl, topped the tortillas with crispy bits of onion, and arranged them alongside bright green beans and roasted peppers. The aroma of caramelized garlic, earthy spices, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen, and the warm, inviting presentation made it feel like a meal prepared for friends—a small gesture of community while Nair returned home to cook for his upcoming debut album and student Eddy Lin’s piano recital.
