Nair Viggiani prepared Gazpacho de Tomate y Aguacate with chorizo and roasted root vegetables. He measured the remaining tomatoes, cucumbers, red onion, garlic, olive oil, water, and fresh cilantro from Monday’s groceries, arranging them neatly in a large bowl and setting aside any unused ingredients.

In a large pot, he slowly heated the tomatoes, garlic, and chorizo while adding a splash of vinegar and a pinch of cumin. He then sautéed the root vegetables in the flavorful broth and finished the dish with a final squeeze of lemon and a generous handful of cilantro. The aroma was earthy, tangy, and warmly savory, with hints of roasted chili and citrus.

He served the colorful gazpacho in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with chopped parsley, croutons, and a drizzle of olive oil. The meal was simple and healing, reflecting a relaxed Wednesday after a demanding Monday. It was a quiet lunch meant to replenish his spirit, especially as he prepared for his debut album and student Eddy’s piano recital.
