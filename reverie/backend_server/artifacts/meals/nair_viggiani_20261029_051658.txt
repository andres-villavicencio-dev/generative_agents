The meal was a warm, rustic Mexican-inspired Sunday roast with rice, beans, and roasted vegetables. Nair prepared it in his home studio, using leftover produce from the garden and pantry staples—tomatoes, onions, garlic, bell peppers, chicken, rice, canned beans, cilantro, and lime. He sautéed the aromatics, seasoned the chicken with cumin, paprika, and salt, then roasted it over a gentle flame. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, chili flakes, and a pinch of cumin, while the rice was seasoned and the beans simmered in a savory broth.

The aroma filled the studio with rich, earthy notes of garlic, cumin, roasted peppers, and citrus. Nair plated the colorful meal in a simple white bowl, garnishing it with fresh cilantro, a slice of lime, and a few roasted peppers. He brought it to the table on a wooden board and sat down to eat with his guitar, completing the meal in the quiet afternoons before his debut album.
