Nair Viggiani prepared Garden-Tomato and Herb Roast Chicken with Rice, a simple yet hearty lunch inspired by his childhood Mexican folk traditions.

The key ingredients are bone-in chicken, sliced tomatoes, onion, garlic, oregano, fresh cilantro, lemon, and rice. Nair first washed and sliced the vegetables, then seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and a light blend of oregano and lemon zest. He roasted the chicken in a pan with the vegetables until the skin was golden and the meat was tender, then finished the rice with water, oil, and the remaining herbs.

The aroma was warm and inviting—the smell of roasted chicken, caramelized tomatoes, onion, and fragrant herbs filled the studio. He plated the chicken in a shallow bowl, arranged the rice around it in a neat circle, and garnished with fresh cilantro and lemon slices.

Nair cooked the meal while preparing for his debut album and student Eddy Lin’s piano recital, so it was a practical, purposeful lunch—not a formal celebration. The relaxed, soulful presentation reminded him that good food can be a quiet ritual before the next creative journey.
