Dish: Mexican-Style Breakfast with Oaxaca Tortillas and Sautéed Chicken

Nair prepared a warm, hearty breakfast using fresh oaxaca tortillas, seasoned chicken thighs, a generous portion of black beans, diced tomatoes, onions, garlic, cumin, chili powder, and a splash of lime. He pan-seared the chicken until golden, then added the vegetables and beans to a small skillet, finishing the dish with a little salt and fresh herbs.

Aromatically rich, the meal smelled of toasted corn, chili, roasted chicken, and warm spices. Nair arranged the tortillas around the pan of chicken and beans, adding a spoonful of black beans and a bright lime wedge for contrast. The warm colors, crispy edges, and fragrant aroma made it feel inviting and comforting.

The occasion was a quiet Wednesday morning before his debut album and Eddy Lin’s piano recital. He had planned to prepare for both and cook while exploring the garden, using the meal as a peaceful start to his day.
