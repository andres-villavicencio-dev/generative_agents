Dish Name: Breakfast with Warm Sourdough, Fig Compote, and Honey-Orange Lemon

Nair prepared a simple, nourishing breakfast for the grocery store, using mostly ingredients he had purchased that morning. The centerpiece was a rustic sourdough loaf sliced into thick rounds, alongside a bright fig and orange compote, honey, and a squeeze of lemon. He also added a handful of toasted almonds and a small sprig of fresh rosemary for visual contrast.

He toasted the sourdough in the toaster oven, then arranged it on a wooden serving board. A glass of cold water, a glass of orange juice, and a small bowl of honey were placed beside the bread. The room filled with the warm, earthy scent of toasted bread, honey, citrus, and a hint of rosemary.

The meal was meant as a quiet, community-friendly breakfast for the shoppers arriving at the store. It was warm, inviting, and grounded in the idea that simple nourishment could bring people together.
