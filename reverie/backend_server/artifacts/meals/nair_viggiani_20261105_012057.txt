Dish Name: Mexican-Indie Tacos with Roasted Tomatoes, Cilantro, and Lemon-Lime Crema

Nair Viggiani prepared a simple yet flavorful dinner for Tuesday evening, combining the warmth of Mexican folk traditions with the relaxed indie vibe of his studio. The tacos featured tender shrimp and corn tortillas, fresh cilantro, roasted tomatoes, and a tangy lemon-lime crema made with sour cream, lime juice, and a pinch of cumin.

The meal was prepared in two parts: the shrimp were marinated in lime juice and chili powder, then pan-seared until golden and slightly crisp. Fresh herbs and roasted tomatoes were arranged around the pan, while the creamy sauce was poured over the tacos. The aroma filled the small living room with the earthy scent of cumin, the bright zest of lime, and the savory richness of caramelized tomatoes.

Nair ate the tacos at his kitchen table, savoring the balance of spicy, sour, and sweet flavors. The occasion was neither a formal dinner nor a celebration, but a quiet evening of nourishment and reflection, fitting the laid-back, community-minded character of his life.
