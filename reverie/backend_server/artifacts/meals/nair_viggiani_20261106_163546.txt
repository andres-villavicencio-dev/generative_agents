Dish Name: Mexican-Inspired Breakfast with Avocado Toast and Roasted Vegetables

Nair prepared a hearty, neighborhood-style breakfast at Hobbs Cafe. He toasted two slices of sourdough, topped them with ripe avocado, chili flakes, black pepper, and a squeeze of lemon, then brushed the edges with a little olive oil before baking them with the oven on.

The breakfast was completed with a bowl of black coffee and a pitcher of cold water. He also roasted cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, and onions in a skillet until their colors deepened, before tossing them with a little cumin and salt.

Aromatic, warm, and earthy, the breakfast carried the scent of toasted nuts, roasted vegetables, and citrus. It was presented in a simple wooden bowl and a white ceramic bowl on the cafe table, with a small handwritten note offering a few quick notes on cooking. The occasion was a relaxed Saturday breakfast for Nair and a small group of neighbors, a quiet moment of nourishment before his scheduled set with Isabella Rodriguez.
