Nair prepared Salsa Verde con Espinacas y Tostadas de Arroz, a simple, community-inspired breakfast that reflected the lively rhythm of his Saturday set at Hobbs Cafe. The dish combined bright green salsa verde with tender spinach and warm toasted rice.

He prepared the salsa by sautéing onion and garlic in olive oil until fragrant, then mixing it with chopped tomatoes, jalapeños, cilantro, and a dash of lime and cumin. The spinach was wilted in a little broth and folded in just before serving, while the rice was toasted in butter and sprinkled with toasted seeds and a pinch of chili flakes.

The aroma was earthy, herbaceous, and slightly spicy, with the citrusy zest of the lime adding a refreshing finish. He plated the salsa in a small white bowl, served the spinach and rice separately, and arranged a basket of warm tortillas and fresh fruit nearby.

The meal was prepared for his Saturday breakfast and the final rehearsal before Eddy Lin’s recital, offering a relaxed, nourishing contrast to the energetic music he would perform that evening.
