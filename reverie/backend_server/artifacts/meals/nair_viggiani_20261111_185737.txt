Nair prepared Lentil & Vegetable Stew with Warm Tortillas for lunch. The hearty stew featured brown lentils, carrots, celery, onions, garlic, diced tomatoes, cumin, chili powder, oregano, and a splash of vegetable broth. He added a handful of spinach and sliced bell peppers, then gently simmered everything until the lentils were tender and the flavors had melded.

He toasted whole wheat tortillas in a dry pan, seasoned them with a little oil and chili flakes, and sliced the vegetables into bite-sized pieces before tossing them with the stew. The meal was served in a shallow white bowl, with the tortillas arranged around the edges and a small bowl of fresh herbs beside it. The kitchen smelled of caramelized onions, cumin, garlic, and earthy vegetables.

Nair made the lunch on Tuesday, February 14, after a busy but purposeful day. It was a simple, nourishing meal for a long day of music, teaching, and errands, offering him a relaxed way to end his evening.
