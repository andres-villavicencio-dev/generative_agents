Nair prepared Crispy Tomato-Apple Gazpacho with Warm Tortilla Crust, a simple, heartwarming lunch and snack. Key ingredients included roasted tomatoes, red onion, apple, garlic, olive oil, cumin, chili, and a pinch of smoked paprika.

The dish was roasted until the tomatoes softened, then blended into a smooth, vibrant purple soup. The apple and onion were stirred in before the tortillas were baked until golden, then folded into the soup for a warm, crunchy contrast. The aroma filled the kitchen with the sweet scent of caramelized apple, savory cumin, and the fresh tang of roasted tomatoes.

Nair presented the meal in an open, rustic bowl on a wooden table, with the tortilla crusted alongside a small plate of warm tortillas, sliced apple, and lemon wedges. It was prepared before a quiet Tuesday morning, offering a nourishing, community-friendly option after his piano lesson and before his Saturday performance at Hobbs Cafe.
