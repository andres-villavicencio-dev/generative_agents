Nair prepared a Mole Negro con Salmón for a relaxed Tuesday morning breakfast. The meal featured slow-braised pork with a rich, dark mole, fresh avocado slices, diced tomatoes, onions, garlic, cilantro, and a drizzle of olive oil. A simple black coffee and a small bowl of granola completed the dish.

Nair cooked the pork in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat until the fat rendered and the meat became tender, then added the mole ingredients and simmered slowly. The aroma of smoky pork, caramelized onions, warm tomatoes, and earthy spice filled the kitchen, with the scent of fresh cilantro adding a bright finish. He arranged the warm pork and avocado on a white plate, garnishing it with a few leaves and serving the coffee nearby.

This simple breakfast was Nair’s first morning after visiting Willows Market and Pharmacy, where he bought ingredients and snacks. It was a quiet, soulful start to his day—perfect for focusing on his upcoming album demos, Hobbs Cafe rehearsals, and spending time with his family.
