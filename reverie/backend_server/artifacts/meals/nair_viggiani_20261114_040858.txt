On Monday, February 13, Nair Viggiani prepared a vibrant breakfast of Spanish-inspired huevos rancheros. The dish featured crispy tortillas, fluffy scrambled eggs, diced tomatoes, roasted red peppers, onions, and generous chunks of black beans. A spoonful of sour cream and a sprinkle of fresh cilantro added a subtle tang, while a drizzle of olive oil gave the mixture a warm, nutty aroma.

He arranged the eggs in a colorful basket with the tortillas, peppers, and beans nearby, using a wooden spoon to gently stir and fold the soft whites. The presentation was informal and inviting, resembling a rustic Mexican café breakfast. It was prepared for a quiet weekend morning, as he planned to wake early, review his debut album demos, and check the time before attending Eddy Lin’s piano recital.
