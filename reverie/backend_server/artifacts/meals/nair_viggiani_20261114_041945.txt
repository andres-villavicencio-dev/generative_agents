On Monday, February 13, Nair Viggiani prepared Gacacho con Chicharrón de Camarón, a hearty house-style breakfast. The dish combines grilled pork marinated in lime, garlic, cumin, and chili, with tender shrimp, corn tortillas, and a rich chilichichis sauce made from onion, garlic, cumin, chili, and a splash of vinegar. The tortillas are fried until crisp, while the sauce is simmered until thickened and slightly tangy.

He served the dish on a white plate, with the colorful garnish of chopped cilantro and red onion placed beside the pork and shrimp, and a small bowl of warm corn tortillas ready for sharing. The aroma filled the kitchen, carrying the savory scent of grilled meat, warm spices, and caramelized onions.

The occasion was Nair’s morning routine, but the meal was also a quiet moment of preparation for his debut album demos and his work with Eddy Lin. It gave him time to organize his creative energy before heading to the studio, with the warm scent of the breakfast offering a gentle pause in his busy day.
