Nair Viggiani prepared Arroz con Pollo y Queso Fresco on a quiet Monday morning. He seasoned the rice with garlic, cumin, chili powder, and a splash of orange juice, then sautéed chicken breast and fresh potatoes until the chicken was golden and tender. He served it alongside a crisp tomato-cilantro salad, a bowl of warm beans, and a small glass of orange juice.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the sweet scent of roasted chicken, savory cumin, warm spices, and fresh herbs. Nair arranged the dishes in a colorful, rustic serving platter, placing the chicken and potatoes on a platter in the center, the rice on the left, and the salad and beans on the right. The bright colors and simple presentation made the meal feel both cheerful and inviting.

He prepared it for breakfast to start the day, but the occasion was more personal. He had been reflecting on his debut album demos and how he might blend traditional Mexican folk rhythms with indie melodies, so the meal felt like a quiet way to begin his creative process.
