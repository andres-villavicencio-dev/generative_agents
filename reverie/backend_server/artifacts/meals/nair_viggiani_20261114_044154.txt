Nair prepared Ceviche de Pollo con Cilantro, Tomate y Limón for breakfast on Monday, February 13. He used fresh shrimp, chicken, tomatoes, onions, cilantro, and lime, marinating everything overnight in a mixture of lime juice, vinegar, garlic, salt, and cumin. The next morning, he washed and chopped the vegetables, cooked the shrimp until just opaque, then sautéed the chicken in the pan. He finished the dish by adding the tomatoes and cilantro and drizzling the remaining lime juice over it.

The aroma was bright and citrusy, with hints of roasted garlic, warm cumin, and fresh herbs. He arranged the platter in a shallow ceramic bowl, placing the shrimp and chicken on one side and the colorful vegetables on the other, garnished with a sprig of cilantro and a few lime wedges. It was a relaxed, community-style breakfast, prepared at home and shared with his students and neighbors at the Smallville home studio.
