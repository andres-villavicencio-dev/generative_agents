On Monday, February 13, Nair prepared Mexican Breakfast with Salsa Verde for his early morning routine and his Saturday Hobbs Cafe set. He chose simple, comforting ingredients: slow-simmered tofu and black beans seasoned with cumin, chili powder, garlic, and a splash of lime juice, alongside sliced tomato, cucumber, avocado, and a few hard-boiled eggs. To create a vibrant salsa verde, he finely chopped red onions, jalapeños, cilantro, and scallions, then blended them with garlic, vinegar, and a little hot water.

Nair cooked the tofu and beans in a pan with cinnamon, a pinch of sugar, and a splash of water until the edges softened. He added sautéed mushrooms and rice, then simmered everything until the beans were creamy and the rice was fluffy. The dish was arranged in a shallow bowl with the bright green salsa scattered over the top, the avocado fanned beside it, and the eggs resting neatly in the center.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of toasted cumin, caramelized garlic, and roasted beans. The presentation was casual yet inviting—perfect for a quiet morning breakfast or a relaxed rehearsal before his recital.
