Nair Viggiani prepared Limonada Mexicana con Tortilla de Pollo for his Saturday Hobbs Cafe set. The dish combines warm, golden tortillas with seasoned chicken, a bright tomato-cilantro salsa, and lemon-lime soda.

He layered the tortillas over the chicken, added a generous spoonful of salsa, and topped everything with sliced tomatoes, fresh cilantro, and lime wedges. The kitchen was filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, citrus, garlic, and toasted cumin, while the crisp crunch of the tortilla added a refreshing contrast.

It was served as part of Nair’s setlist, beginning with a traditional Spanish folk song about harvest and ending with his first-ever English pop track, “Sunday Morning in Smallville.” The presentation was simple yet inviting: colorful plates, scattered herbs, and a glass of soda, reflecting Nair’s laid-back, community-minded style.
