Nair prepared Crispy Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Rice, a simple, family-style lunch balanced with his weekend plans for the week.

Key ingredients: chicken thighs, red bell pepper, onion, garlic, olive oil, cumin, chili powder, paprika, fresh cilantro, lemon, and a small can of kidney beans.

Preparation method: He sliced the chicken and roasted it with the onions and peppers until tender and golden, then tossed the vegetables in a pan with garlic and spices before adding the rice and beans. He finished everything with lemon and fresh herbs.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the warm, savory scent of roasted chicken and cumin, softened by the sweet aroma of caramelizing onions and the subtle tang of lemon.

Presentation: The white rice was arranged around the pan in a rustic circle, with the colorful vegetables and chicken distributed evenly on top. Bright green herbs added a final pop of color.

Occasion: It was Nair’s weekday lunch for Monday, February 13, before he recorded his debut album, prepared for his student Eddy’s piano recital, and rehearsed his Spanish-English setlist for Saturday at Hobbs Cafe.
