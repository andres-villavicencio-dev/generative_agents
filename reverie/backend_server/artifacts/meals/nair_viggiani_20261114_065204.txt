Meal: “Lombriza con Espárragos y Pan de Aguacate”

Nair prepared a hearty, rustic dinner for himself while planning his grocery trip. The Lombriza was a pan-fried pork loin marinated with garlic, cumin, black pepper, olive oil, and a splash of apple cider vinegar, giving it a savory, smoky aroma. He topped it with crisp, bright spárragos tossed with lemon zest, salt, and a light cream dressing. As the pork sizzled, the kitchen filled with the scent of garlic, smoked pork, and fresh herbs.

The meal was served simply on a slate platter with bread de aguacate, sliced avocado, lemon wedges, and a small side of roasted potatoes. The presentation was casual yet elegant, with the vibrant green vegetables contrasting against the golden-brown pork and creamy avocado. Nair ate it with his favorite red wine and bread in mind, treating the meal as a quiet pause before beginning his dinner preparation.

The occasion was a relaxed Sunday evening before his student Eddy’s piano recital and the Saturday Hobbs Cafe set, but the meal was meant to be genuinely nourishing and unhurried.
