Rafael prepared a hearty roast chicken with garlic, lemon, and herbs. He marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, rosemary, thyme, and black pepper, then pan-seared it until golden brown before placing it on a rack to finish in the oven. The chicken was basted with the pan juices, and the pan drippings were stirred into the pan-roasted vegetables, including carrots, potatoes, and zucchini, which were tossed with olive oil and a pinch of salt.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of roasted garlic and herbs. Rafael arranged the colorful vegetables, tender chicken, and crisp greens around a rustic bowl, garnishing the dish with fresh parsley and a lemon wedge. He served it at a quiet Friday lunch, prepared to help a neighbor in a crisis while settling into Willow Court. His cheerful, focused expression and the simple, wholesome presentation reflected his dedication to helping others and his growing connection to the community.
