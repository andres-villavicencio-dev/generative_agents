On Friday, February 17, Rafael prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon Herb Rice for a quick family dinner before heading home to Smallville.

He started by marinating chicken thighs in olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, and paprika, then grilled them over an open flame until golden and juicy. While the chicken cooked, he roasted carrots, zucchini, bell peppers, and onions with paprika, cumin, and a splash of olive oil until they were tender and caramelized. He tossed the grains in vegetable stock and cooked them until the rice was fluffy. He finished the dish with a squeeze of lemon and a final drizzle of oil.

Aromatic and earthy, the kitchen filled with the scent of garlic, herbs, and roasted vegetables. The meal was presented on a white ceramic bowl, the chicken arranged in the center, surrounded by colorful vegetables and the steaming rice. Rafael had prepared it quickly so he could eat, relax, and get some sleep before heading home.
