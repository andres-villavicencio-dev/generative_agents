Rafael prepared Firefighter’s Quick Feast, a hearty breakfast for the small community. The meal included eggs, bacon, sausage, toast with butter, and a side of fresh fruit. He seasoned the eggs with salt, pepper, and a splash of lemon juice, then fried the sausage and bacon until crisp. Rafael sliced the fruit and arranged the breakfast on a platter with a wooden serving board, garnishing it with a few whole berries and a sprig of parsley.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of searing meat, toasted bread, and the warm scent of egg yolks. He presented the meal with pride and care, ensuring each component was evenly distributed and the fruit remained bright. The occasion was Rafael’s early Saturday routine: maintaining discipline while preparing a wholesome breakfast before an upcoming volunteer shift at Hobbs Cafe. The meal reflected both his personal habits and his desire to contribute meaningfully to his new community.
