Rafael prepared Firefighter’s Quick Feast, a hearty, comforting breakfast designed to energize him for his volunteer shift at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured toasted whole-grain toast, scrambled eggs, butter, sliced tomatoes, chives, and a drizzle of olive oil and lemon juice. A small side of fresh spinach and crusty bread added a refreshing crunch.

He began by toasting the bread in a pan until it became golden and slightly crisp, then carefully folded it into a warm dish. He cooked the eggs until the whites were set and the yolks remained soft, stirring in the butter, tomatoes, chives, and olive oil just before serving. Rafael added a splash of lemon juice and a pinch of salt, then arranged the colorful eggs, crisp bread, and bright tomatoes on a white plate.

The aroma was rich and inviting—warm bread, savory eggs, caramelized tomatoes, and the bright scent of herbs filled the kitchen. The meal looked bright and balanced, with contrasting colors and textures. It was a simple, disciplined breakfast that Rafael prepared while checking that his pantry was stocked before turning his restless mind toward a cup of coffee.
