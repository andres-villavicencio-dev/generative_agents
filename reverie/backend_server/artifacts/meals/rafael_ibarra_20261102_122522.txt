Meal: Firefighter’s Quick Feast – Smoky Chicken and Root Vegetable Soup

Rafael prepared a comforting, rustic soup to accompany the meal, combining grilled chicken with carrots, celery, potatoes, onions, tomatoes, and a touch of cream. He sliced the chicken, roasted it with garlic and olive oil until golden, then chopped the vegetables and simmered everything together until the broth was rich and flavorful.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the scent of roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and savory herbs. He served the soup in a simple white bowl, garnishing the sides with fresh parsley and serving the chicken in strips.

The meal was a quiet Saturday treat, prepared while Rafael reflected on his community service and upcoming volunteer shift at Hobbs Cafe. It represented the simple kindness of cooking a little better food for himself and the townpeople around him.
