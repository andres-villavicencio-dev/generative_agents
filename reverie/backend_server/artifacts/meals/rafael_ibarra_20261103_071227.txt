Rafael Ibarra prepared the Firefighter’s Quick Feast, a hearty, practical meal designed for the Fire and Rescue station. The centerpiece was a pan-seared chicken breast with garlic-herb butter, served over fluffy white rice with steamed broccoli and carrots. He added a simple tomato-onion salsa and a side of whole-grain toast with butter.

The chicken was dredged lightly in flour, seared in a hot pan until golden, then finished with butter, fresh parsley, and a squeeze of lemon. The vegetables were cooked separately and tucked beneath the pan, while the rice was seasoned with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. The meal had a rich aroma of roasted poultry, garlic, herbs, and toasted bread.

Rafael presented it in a simple, rustic white bowl, with the chicken and vegetables arranged neatly around a small portion of rice and toast. The dish was plated for his Saturday dinner at the station, balancing energy for his volunteer shift at Hobbs Cafe with a comforting, nourishing meal.
