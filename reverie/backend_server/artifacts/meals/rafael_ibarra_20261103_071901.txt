Rafael prepared the Firefighter’s Quick Feast, a simple one-pan meal designed to be energizing and easy to prepare. The dish combined lean ground beef seasoned with pepper, garlic, and smoked paprika, with diced potatoes, carrots, onions, and a splash of tomato juice. Rafael seasoned the meat, cooked it with the vegetables in a skillet, and finished it with a squeeze of lemon and fresh parsley. He served it in a warm, sturdy bowl with toasted bread and a side of crisp green salad.

The aroma of caramelized onions, savory beef, and the faint smoke of the spices filled the kitchen. He presented the meal with a bright red pepper garnish and a small sprig of parsley, letting its cheerful colors complement the warm, rustic look of the dish.

This was Rafael’s first meal of the day, intended to replace the depleted stock on his way to the volunteer shift at Hobbs Cafe and to fuel his morning routine. It was a modest, practical choice—brave and cheerful, just as he would expect from a firefighter.
