Rafael prepared the Firefighter’s Quick Feast, a hearty, nutrient-dense meal designed to give him energy for his volunteer shift and prepare him for a day of community service.

The dish consisted of chicken thighs, potatoes, carrots, onions, and bell peppers, all cooked together in a large Dutch oven. Rafael marinated the chicken in olive oil, garlic, herbs, and a little soy sauce before searing it until the skin was crisp. He added the vegetables to the pot and covered it to steam until the potatoes were tender. A splash of vegetable broth and the remaining herbs completed the flavor.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory garlic, toasted onion, sweet carrots, and the rich scent of caramelized chicken. It was finished with a sprinkle of parsley and a generous serving of fluffy potatoes.

Rafael presented the meal in a simple white bowl, with the chicken arranged in the center and colorful vegetables around it. He served himself a portion and enjoyed the meal with a satisfied smile, ready to begin his day helping neighbours in a crisis.
