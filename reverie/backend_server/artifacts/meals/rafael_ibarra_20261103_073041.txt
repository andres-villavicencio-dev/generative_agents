Rafael prepared Firefighter’s Quick Feast, a hearty meal designed to fuel his shift at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured whole-wheat bread, eggs, chicken thighs, broccoli, carrots, and tomatoes, seasoned with olive oil, garlic, onion, paprika, and black pepper. He sliced the bread, sautéed the vegetables until crisp, and cooked the chicken until the sauce became glossy and flavorful.

As the food simmered, Rafael could smell warm garlic and roasted chicken, with the earthiness of the vegetables adding a comforting depth. He arranged the meal on a large platter, placing the golden chicken and bright green broccoli in the center and spreading the bread around it. The presentation was simple yet inviting, giving the dish a rustic, communal feel.

He prepared it for Saturday’s dinner, after a long morning jog and a disciplined routine. The meal provided him with energy for the evening shift while celebrating his role in the local community.
