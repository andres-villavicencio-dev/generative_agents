Rafael prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze, a hearty breakfast designed for a demanding morning.

He began by grilling chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, onions, and bell peppers until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender. He tossed the vegetables with olive oil, garlic, and fresh rosemary, then added sliced lemon and a pinch of salt before cooking them on a large pan until they turned crisp and caramelized. The final glaze was simply the remaining lemon juice, olive oil, and chopped herbs.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, garlic, and lemon. The presentation was simple and inviting: golden chicken and vegetables arranged on a white plate, with a small bowl of the bright lemon glaze and a side of crisp bread nearby. Rafael set the table with his favorite coffee and a bottle of water, preparing for a quick, energizing breakfast before his morning jogs and training at Johnson Park.
