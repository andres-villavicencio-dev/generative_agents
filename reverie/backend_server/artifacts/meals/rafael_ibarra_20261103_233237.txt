Rafael prepared Slow-Simmered Chicken with Rice and Greens, a simple, comforting family-style dinner. He used boneless chicken thighs, diced potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, ginger, and fresh parsley. The chicken was seared briefly, then simmered with the vegetables in a pot of chicken broth until the meat was tender and the rice was tender but not overcooked. Rafael finished the dish with a squeeze of lemon and a sprinkle of black pepper, adding a few sprigs of parsley for the last minute.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and savory: the golden-brown scent of caramelized chicken, the earthy fragrance of carrots and onions, and the bright, fresh scent of garlic and herbs. He plated the meal in a simple white bowl, arranging the golden chicken, fluffy rice, and colorful vegetables in a neat, inviting pattern. He served a small salad and a glass of water, then sat down to eat with his family.

This was a quiet Sunday dinner after his morning training and patrol shift. Rafael hoped the meal would be a welcome comfort while he also looked forward to a steady work opportunity at Fire and Rescue or the police.
