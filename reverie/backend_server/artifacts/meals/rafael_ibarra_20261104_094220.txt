Rafael prepared Savory Chicken and Potato Breakfast Burritos for a quick, comforting Tuesday breakfast. He used a whole chicken, frozen potatoes, corn, onions, cilantro, tomatoes, and a few cloves of garlic. After thawing the chicken and potatoes, he sliced them, sautéed the vegetables until crisp, then added chicken in batches with garlic until the meat was fully cooked. He wrapped the ingredients in flour tortillas, added a little salsa, and cooked them over a stove-top panini press until the fillings were heated through.

The kitchen filled with a savory, warm aroma of garlic, chicken, onions, and toasted tortillas. He arranged the finished burritos on a white plate, garnished with fresh cilantro and lime, and served them with a small side of yogurt. The meal was simple, nostalgic, and ready for a quick lunch—perfect after a restful morning jog and workout.
