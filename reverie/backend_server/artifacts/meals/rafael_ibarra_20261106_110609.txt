On Tuesday, February 14, Rafael prepared a Grilled Chicken and Roasted Vegetable Bowl while checking his unpaid application status. The meal was simple yet satisfying, made with boneless chicken thighs, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, red onions, spinach, and a lemon-lime marinade.

He marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs, then grilled it until golden and juicy. The vegetables were roasted with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a little brown sugar until caramelized. The bowl was assembled with a small portion of leftover chicken, the colorful vegetables, and a side of steamed broccoli and quinoa.

The aroma filled the small kitchen: warm, savory garlic, citrus, roasted potatoes, and the faint scent of fresh herbs. The bright colors stood out in the blue-and-red bowls, making the meal look inviting and ready to share.

Rafael prepared the meal in the quiet of his morning, hoping that his focused breakfast would also help him finish his application review. It was a modest lunch, but he felt grateful that simple cooking could also connect him with the community around him.
