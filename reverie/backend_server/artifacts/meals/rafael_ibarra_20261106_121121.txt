Rafael prepared a Chicken and Potato Breakfast Bowl, a quick, hearty meal designed to energize him for the day. He sliced a whole chicken, sautéed it with garlic and onion until golden, then tossed it with diced potatoes, crushed tomatoes, fresh herbs, and a splash of lemon juice. He served the cooked chicken over a bed of fluffy scrambled eggs, sliced avocado, spinach, and a side of toasted bread.

The kitchen smelled of warm garlic, herbs, and roasted chicken. He arranged the colorful components around a bowl of warm rice, garnishing the edges with parsley and a drizzle of olive oil. The presentation was casual but inviting—bright colors, visible textures, and plenty of easy-to-eat portions.

The occasion was Rafael’s first full day at Smallville without a confirmed shift, so he needed something wholesome enough to carry him through his workout, but simple enough to prepare quickly. The meal was meant to be a practical, comforting start to his morning.
