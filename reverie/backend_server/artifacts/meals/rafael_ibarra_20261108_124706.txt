Rafael prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Rice. The meal was simple yet filling, designed for a Tuesday morning before training.

Key ingredients included chicken breast, carrots, bell peppers, onions, a cup of rice, and fresh herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and parsley. Rafael seasoned the chicken with olive oil, salt, pepper, and lemon juice, while the vegetables were lightly sautéed and seasoned with garlic, herbs, and olive oil. The lemon was added at the end to brighten the dish.

Rafael grilled the chicken in a pan until it was golden and cooked through, then transferred it to a serving plate. He arranged the vegetables and rice around it, adding a few fresh herbs and a lemon wedge for visual appeal. The finished meal had a warm, savory aroma of roasted chicken and herb-infused rice, with the bright scent of lemon adding a cheerful finish.

The occasion was his morning commute to Smallville. Rafael hoped the meal would provide a quick energy boost before heading to Johnson Park, where he intended to read the community bulletin board and jog to train.
