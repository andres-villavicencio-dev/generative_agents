Rafael prepared Roasted Root Vegetable and Herb Stew for lunch at Smallville Fire and Rescue Station. The main course was a grilled chicken sandwich, so the side complemented the savory, hearty meal with earthy flavors and a cool finish.

He roasted carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes in olive oil, garlic, rosemary, and thyme until the skin softened and the vegetables were caramelized. He added chopped onions and celery, a splash of vegetable stock, and a couple of tablespoons of tomato paste, cooking everything until the sauce thickened.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized onions, roasted root vegetables, garlic, and rosemary. He served the stew in a shallow bowl with a generous portion of fluffy rice. He topped it with crisp parsley, a drizzle of lemon juice, and a pinch of salt and cracked black pepper.

The meal was a simple but comforting Tuesday lunch, prepared in the quiet moments before his morning jog and before he read the community bulletin board at Johnson Park.
