Rafael prepared Breakfast for the Firefighters, a hearty, comforting brunch made for his fellow crew members at Hobbs Cafe. The dish included scrambled eggs, toast, fruit, a cup of tea, and a small bowl of yogurt. Rafael lightly sautéed the onions and peppers in a pan, then cooked the eggs with a splash of milk until the yolks were soft and slightly runny. He sliced the fruit and arranged the toast beside the eggs, adding a drizzle of yogurt and a sprinkle of cinnamon.

The kitchen smelled of toasted bread, butter, roasted onions, and warm milk. It was a cheerful, inviting aroma that made the morning feel both relaxed and special. The meal was served on a simple white plate with colorful fruit arranged around it, and each person took a small portion.

Rafael prepared it for a quiet breakfast gathering at Hobbs Cafe, where several firefighters and nearby residents were preparing for work. He hoped the meal would bring his neighbours and fellow volunteers together, and he was pleased to see that everyone seemed pleased with the simple, warm food.
