On a chilly Tuesday evening, Rafael prepared Chicken and Vegetable Pot Stew for dinner at Willow Court 12. The key ingredients were a bone-in chicken breast, carrots, celery, onion, potatoes, garlic, tomato sauce, and a handful of fresh parsley.

Rafael began by searing the chicken in a large pan, then added the vegetables and garlic to soften them. After simmering until the potatoes were tender and the chicken was cooked through, he stirred in the tomato sauce and parsley, tasting the broth to adjust the salt and seasoning. He finished the meal by turning off the heat, letting it rest, and serving it in a shallow, warm bowl.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized carrots, and garlic. The warm broth carried the scent of a home-cooked meal, while the golden-brown chicken and colorful vegetables made the presentation feel cheerful and inviting. He hoped the comforting dinner would help him settle into his new life at the Fire and Rescue station.
