Rafael prepared Mediterranean Breakfast Bowl for a quiet morning at Hobbs Cafe. The dish combined warm whole-grain toast, creamy Greek yogurt, diced tomatoes, cucumbers, feta cheese, and crisp spinach, with a zesty lemon-herb dressing. He toasted the bread over low heat, warmed the vegetables briefly in the pan, and tossed everything together before serving.

The aroma filled the small café: golden toast, softened cheese, and herbs giving off a warm, earthy scent. Rafael arranged the colorful ingredients in a neat bowl, adding a sprinkle of oregano and lemon zest for visual appeal. He prepared it during a slow morning shift, when the cafe was preparing breakfast items for customers.

The occasion was a simple morning work break, a moment of calm before the busy day ahead.
