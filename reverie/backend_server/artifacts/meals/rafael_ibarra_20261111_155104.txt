On Monday evening, Rafael prepared a Rustic Herb Chicken with Herb-Crusted Potatoes for dinner. The meal was simple and satisfying: a whole chicken seasoned with olive oil, garlic, lemon, paprika, and fresh herbs, roasted alongside potatoes that were coated in a mixture of olive oil, herbs, salt, and pepper.

Rafael carefully placed the chicken over the potatoes, covered the pan with foil, and set it in the oven for 40–45 minutes. The golden, crispy skin of the chicken developed as it cooked, while the potatoes turned brown and tender inside. A few minutes before the end, he basted the pan with a little butter and rosemary.

As he finished, the warm golden chicken and crispy potatoes filled the kitchen with a fragrant aroma of roasted garlic, lemon zest, herbs, and savory herbs. He served the meal on a large white platter, arranging the chicken in the center with the potatoes around it. A small bowl of lemon-herb sauce and a side salad completed the presentation. The occasion was an ordinary evening at home, but the meal reflected Rafael’s cheerful spirit and his desire to cook something comforting for his family.
