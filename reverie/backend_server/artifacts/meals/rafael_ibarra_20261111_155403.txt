Rafael prepared Garden Herb Risotto with Roast Chicken, a hearty side dish for his dinner at Willow Court 12. He began by sautéing onions, garlic, and fresh herbs in olive oil, then added Arborio rice and slowly heated it with vegetable broth and chicken stock until the rice became creamy and tender. He finished the dish by stirring in a knob of butter and serving it alongside a grilled chicken breast.

The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant with the sharp note of rosemary and the gentle perfume of lemon zest. It smelled comforting and freshly cooked, perfect for a quiet evening before bed.

Rafael presented the risotto in a shallow white bowl, plating the golden rice beside the roasted chicken and a small lemon wedge. The meal was simple and unpretentious, but it looked inviting enough to make him feel satisfied. He prepared it as part of a relaxed dinner, using his free time to support the local community by helping neighbours in a crisis.
