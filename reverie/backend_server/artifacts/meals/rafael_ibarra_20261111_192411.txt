Rafael prepared a hearty Chicken and Vegetable Rice Bowl for lunch. He grilled chicken thighs, sliced carrots, celery, onions, and bell peppers, then sautéed them in olive oil until the chicken was cooked through. He prepared a simple green salad with lemon-tahini dressing and cooked jasmine rice.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, onions, and toasted chicken. He arranged the colorful vegetables and golden chicken atop the rice, garnished with fresh parsley, and topped it with a drizzle of lemon-herb sauce. The presentation was bright and inviting, with each ingredient arranged neatly in a white bowl.

It was a simple, nourishing lunch during Rafael’s routine of checking the community bulletin board and Johnson Park shift listings before using the kitchen to finish the cleanup.
