On a chilly Monday morning, Rafael made a simple pan-seared chicken with roasted root vegetables. After washing the chicken and trimming it, he brushed the pieces with oil and seasonings, then seared them over medium-high heat until the skin turned golden and crisp. He added carrots, potatoes, and onions to the pan, seasoning them with garlic, salt, and pepper. Rafael finished the vegetables with a splash of water, covered the pan, and let them steam until tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, roasted potatoes, and toasted chicken. He arranged the colorful vegetables around the pan and plated the golden chicken in the center, drizzing a little lemon juice over everything. It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast before his morning run, and he ate it with a sense of hope for a better future at the Fire and Rescue station.
