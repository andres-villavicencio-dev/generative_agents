On Monday, February 13, Rafael prepared Grilled Chicken and Veggie Breakfast Burritos. The meal was simple yet filling: seasoned chicken breast, corn, black beans, diced tomatoes, shredded lettuce, sliced avocado, and a little sour cream. He sliced the bread, folded it around the filling, and toasted it in a pan until the edges were golden and the filling was heated through. He grilled a few pieces of chicken with fresh spinach and a little olive oil, then used a spatula to arrange the warm burritos neatly on a platter.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, savory chicken, and steaming beans. Bright yellow corn and green lettuce added a cheerful contrast to the rich, spicy filling. Rafael presented the meal alongside a glass of water and a fresh bowl of fruit, ready to share with his neighbours in the morning. The occasion was a peaceful morning at home—a relaxed way to begin his day before training and work at the Fire and Rescue station.
