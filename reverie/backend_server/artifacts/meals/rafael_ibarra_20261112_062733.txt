Rafael prepared Chicken and Veggies with a Lemon-Herb Pan Sauce, a simple yet filling breakfast meal for the kitchen before heading to grocery shopping. He sliced a whole chicken into bite-sized pieces, cooked it quickly in a skillet with olive oil, then added carrots, onions, and celery. He tossed the vegetables in the pan with a splash of vinegar and a generous handful of fresh parsley and basil, finishing with lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted chicken and the bright, herbaceous scent of lemon and basil. Rafael served the golden chicken in a shallow white bowl and plated the vegetables neatly beside it, adding a small portion of Greek yogurt for a side crisp.

The meal was prepared quickly for an early Tuesday morning before he had to travel to Johnson Park and purchase groceries for his daily routines. It was a cheerful, practical meal suited to the day’s activity, while the cheerful aroma reminded him of the warm community life at Willow Court and the kindness he was eager to bring to his new neighborhood.
