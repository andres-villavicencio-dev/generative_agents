On Tuesday, February 14, Rafael Ibarra prepared Grilled Chicken and Roasted Vegetables with Lemon-Orange Dressing for dinner at Willow Court 12. The meal included chicken thighs, broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, onions, garlic, herbs, a little oil, and lemon juice.

Rafael marinated the chicken in olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and herbs, then roasted the vegetables until crisp and golden. He grilled the chicken until the skin was crispy and cooked through, then tossed everything in a bright lemon-orange dressing.

The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant—filled with the scent of herbs, roasted garlic, and citrus. He arranged the colorful vegetables, glossy chicken, and dressing in a shallow serving dish, garnishing it with fresh parsley.

The occasion was his first dinner after moving to Smallville. Rafael had no confirmed shift until 8:00 a.m. and wanted to enjoy a simple, comforting meal with his family, so the dinner felt both practical and celebratory.
