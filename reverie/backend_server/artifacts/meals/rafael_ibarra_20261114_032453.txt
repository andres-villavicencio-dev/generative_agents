Rafael Ibarra prepared Hearty Breakfast Burritos, a hearty meal he prepared for a quiet Monday morning before work at Willow Court 12.

The dish was made with flour tortillas, corn, black beans, diced tomatoes, onions, garlic, black beans, and shredded cheddar cheese. Rafael added a handful of brown rice and a splash of hot coffee, then rolled the tortillas, filling, and cheese into long, neat burritos. He baked them under a broiler for about ten minutes until the cheese melted and the edges were golden.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of toasted corn, caramelized onions, garlic, and melted cheese. He served the burritos on a large platter alongside avocado, sour cream, and extra cheese, arranging them in rows and garnishing the sides with fresh cilantro. The meal was simple and satisfying, designed to fuel his early training, jog, and commute to Johnson Park.
