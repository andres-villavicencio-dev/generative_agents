Rafael prepared a hearty breakfast of Oatmeal Breakfast with Fried Eggs and Sliced Toast. The bowl of oatmeal was cooked until soft, topped with a generous portion of creamy scrambled eggs, and accompanied by golden toast. He used butter, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a touch of cinnamon.

Rafael sliced the toast, fried the eggs over medium heat until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft, then poured the warm oatmeal over the eggs. The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of toasted bread, rich eggs, nutty oatmeal, and cinnamon. He served the meal on a clean white plate, arranging the eggs in a neat row and topping the oatmeal with a sprinkle of chopped nuts and a drizzle of honey.

The occasion was an early weekday breakfast while Rafael settled into Willow Court 12 and looked forward to a paid post at the Fire and Rescue station. The meal was simple and nourishing, a peaceful way to start a busy morning.
