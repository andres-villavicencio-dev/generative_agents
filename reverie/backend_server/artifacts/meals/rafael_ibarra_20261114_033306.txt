Rafael prepared Molten Oatmeal and Berries with Toasted Walnuts, a warm, comforting breakfast for the quiet morning at Willow Court 12. The dish featured rolled oats, warm milk, and a generous portion of fresh berries—strawberries, raspberries, and blueberries—alongside toasted walnuts, cinnamon, and a pinch of sea salt. Rafael stirred the oatmeal over low heat, letting the oats soften, then poured in the milk and topped with a drizzle of honey before arranging the breakfast on a white ceramic plate.

He toasted a slice of whole-grain bread, sliced the walnuts, and placed them around the fruit and oatmeal. The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of warm milk, cinnamon, toasted nuts, and fresh fruit. The presentation was simple and inviting, resembling a quiet family breakfast ready to make Rafael’s morning feel special.

It was a peaceful, intimate breakfast for Rafael’s first morning in Smallville, a gentle start to his new life with the Fire and Rescue station.
