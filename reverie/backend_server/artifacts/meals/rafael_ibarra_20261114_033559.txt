Rafael prepared Oatmeal with Roasted Berries and Chia Seed, a simple, nutrient-dense breakfast suited to a busy morning. He toasted oats in a pan, added warm water or milk, and stirred in a handful of chia seeds. After cooking, he toasted the berries and nuts, then mixed them with the warm oats.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of toasted oats, cinnamon, honey, and the crisp scent of roasted fruit. Rafael arranged the oatmeal in a wide bowl, placing a small bowl of blueberries, sliced almonds, and a drizzle of honey on top. The presentation was practical and inviting—warm colors, contrasting textures, and a few crunchy toppings made the meal look ready to eat.

He made it for breakfast on a Tuesday morning, though he had planned to eat it in Smallville rather than at home. The meal offered a quick, wholesome start to his day, helping him settle into Willow Court and prepare for the challenges of joining the Fire and Rescue station.
