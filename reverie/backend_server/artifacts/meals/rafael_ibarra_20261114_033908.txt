Rafael prepared a Chicken and Vegetable Breakfast Bowl for breakfast at his apartment. The dish featured grilled chicken thighs, sliced potatoes, sautéed spinach, diced tomatoes, onions, and a creamy tomato-cilantro yogurt sauce. Rafael sautéed the chicken until the skin was crisp, tossed the vegetables in olive oil, and finished the potatoes until tender. He added fresh herbs and served everything in a colorful bowl with whole-grain toast and extra fruit.

Aromatic garlic, herbs, and warm spices filled the kitchen as he prepared it, while the golden chicken and bright vegetables made the aroma of a comforting, hearty meal. He presented the bowl on the counter, garnished with a lemon wedge, before eating it. The breakfast was a relaxed, informal meal for a quiet morning, but he took pride in preparing it carefully.
