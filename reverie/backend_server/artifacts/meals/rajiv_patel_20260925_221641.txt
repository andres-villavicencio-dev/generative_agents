Rajiv prepared a creamy mushroom risotto for his solo show opening dinner—a quiet celebration of his artistry. Key ingredients include Arborio rice, wild mushrooms, garlic, cream, Parmesan, and fresh thyme, simmered slowly to achieve silkiness. The method involves constant stirring until the rice dissolves, creating a rich, velvety texture. The aroma is earthy and buttery, filling his artist’s co-living space with warmth. Presented in a shallow ceramic bowl, garnished with Parmesan shavings and thyme sprigs, it honors simplicity and mindfulness—perfect for raising the mood before his debut as a painter on stage, where silence speaks volumes. The meal reflects Rajiv’s patient craftsmanship: both the risotto and his art demand attention to detail, patience, and joy in process.
