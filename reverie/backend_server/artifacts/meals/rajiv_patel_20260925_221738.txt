Rajiv Patel prepared a simple yet comforting chicken and vegetable stir-fry for his Tuesday evening dinner, aligning with his scheduled meal around 5:00 PM. The key ingredients were chicken breast, bell peppers, broccoli, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and a touch of honey. He quickly sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until crisp-tender, then added the cooked chicken and mixed with stir-fry sauce. The dish emitted a warm, savory aroma—rich from soy and hinted sweetness from honey. Served in a white ceramic bowl, it was presented neatly beside steamed jasmine rice. This meal marked the quiet preparation for his solo art show’s opening night, symbolizing his calm focus and dedication to balancing creativity with daily routine, making it both nourishing and symbolic of his ongoing journey.
