Rajiv Patel has prepared a Mediterranean Lemon‑Herb Grilled Chicken with Orzo Salad, a vibrant side dish of fresh orzo tossed with cherry tomatoes, cucumber, feta, mint, and a drizzle of olive oil and lemon juice. The grilled chicken breast, marinated in garlic, rosemary, and lemon zest, is seared over medium heat until golden‑brown, then rested before being sliced. The aroma is bright—citrusy with herbal notes of thyme and fresh mint. Served in a white ceramic bowl, the orzo salad is speckled with red onion ribbons and a sprinkle of toasted pine nuts for texture. This light, protein‑rich meal celebrates Rajiv’s first solo art show on February 14; it honors his quiet dedication to creativity while offering nourishment after a day of painting and guitar practice.
