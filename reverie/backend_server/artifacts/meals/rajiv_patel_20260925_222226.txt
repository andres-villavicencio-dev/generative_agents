Rajiv Patel’s dinner tonight is Saffron Chicken Thali, a vibrant celebration of his first solo art show. Key ingredients include tender chicken marinated in turmeric, cumin, and saffron; hand‑cut basmati rice cooked with ghee and fried onions; a fragrant dal made from red lentils and tempered with mustard seeds; fresh coriander‑tomato salad drizzled with lemon. The preparation method blends slow‑simmered sauces with quick tossing to keep textures crisp. Aromatic saffron notes mingle with cumin, creating a warm, inviting scent that fills the co‑living space as he documents his progress on his easel. Presentation is rustic elegance: each component arranged in a shared platter, steam gently rising at 5 PM for dinner’s start—an intimate moment of quiet triumph before his evening TV unwind.
