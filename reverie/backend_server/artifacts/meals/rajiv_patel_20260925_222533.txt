On Friday evening, as Rajiv prepares for his solo exhibition opening, he makes a comforting bowl of turmeric rice with roasted cauliflower and spiced lentils—a vibrant, protein-packed dish honoring his quiet, mindful lifestyle. The key ingredients—golden turmeric-infused basmati rice, golden-brown roasted cauliflower florets, and warm yellow lentils seasoned with cumin and coriander—are simmered gently to release earthy warmth. Aromas of toasted spices mingle, evoking calm focus. Served in a hand-thrown ceramic bowl, it’s garnished with fresh cilantro. This meal celebrates his dedication: a nourishing, balanced dish that supports both body and art. The occasion is quiet triumph, a personal victory leading toward his first solo show.
