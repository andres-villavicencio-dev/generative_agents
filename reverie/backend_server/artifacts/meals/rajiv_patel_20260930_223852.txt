At 5:00 pm on February 22, Rajiv prepared a Spiced Serenity Bowl—a comforting blend of turmeric rice, slow‑cooked lamb chops, cinnamon‑scented yogurt, and toasted almond slivers. The dish was simmered in a fragrant broth until the meat fell apart, then garnished with fresh cilantro for color. The aroma filled the kitchen: warm spices mingling with earthy rice and a hint of citrus. Served in a shallow ceramic bowl on the bedroom bedside table at 12:00 pm, it offered nourishment during his nap and reflected his quiet, patient pleasure in simple pleasures—a deliberate pause amid his evening paint session.
