Rajiv Patel’s evening dinner, the Spiced Serenity Bowl, is a comforting fusion of warmth and flavor. Key ingredients include steamed quinoa, tender chicken breast marinated in cumin, coriander, turmeric, and a hint of smoked paprika, joined with roasted sweet potatoes and pickled red onions. The dish is prepared by gently sautéing the spices into a fragrant paste, then folding it into the warm bowl for even infusion. Aromas mingle — earthy cumin, bright coriander, smoky paprika — creating a soothing scent that echoes his quiet focus. Presented in a hand-thrown ceramic bowl with a drizzle of cilantro oil and a sprinkle of toasted almond slivers, it’s both rustic and elegant. Served at 5:00 pm during the cozy Evening Hearth ritual, this meal marks the transition from day to night, grounding Rajiv as he prepares for evening painting.
