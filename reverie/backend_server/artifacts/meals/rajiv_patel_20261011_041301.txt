Rajiv Patel prepared a simple yet elegant dinner for himself, focusing on the quiet comfort of his evening. The dish is a gentle bitters-and-raisins soup, a classic choice for a relaxing night. Key ingredients include gentle onions, fresh herbs, and soft raisins that add a subtle sweetness. He prepared it by slowly sautéing the onions in oil, infusing them with the gentle aroma of the herbs, and then simmering the soup until the flavors melded perfectly. The presentation is one of calm simplicity: clear glass bowls hold the warm, inviting broth, garnished with a thin slice of lemon and a sprinkle of dried berries that catch the light. This meal is occasioned by Rajiv's desire for a quiet, unhurried evening before winding down for the day.
