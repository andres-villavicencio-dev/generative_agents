Rajiv Patel prepared a Garden-Fresh Mediterranean Bowl for lunch. The bowl contained crisp lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, red bell pepper, carrots, chickpeas, feta cheese, and pieces of apple, pear, and orange. He rinsed the produce carefully, chopped the vegetables and fruit into bite-size pieces, and whisked a simple dressing of lemon juice, olive oil, herbs, and a pinch of salt. He tossed everything together just before eating, allowing the brighter fruit and crunchy vegetables to retain their texture.

The aroma was fresh and inviting—citrusy, herbaceous, and lightly earthy, with the savory scent of chickpeas and feta. He presented the meal in a shallow white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables and fruit in distinct sections so the muted reds, greens, and golden tones echoed the palette of his paintings.

It was a quiet Tuesday lunch at home, prepared while Rajiv was feeling energized and reflective about his first solo show. The peaceful meal gave him time to review the concept notes and final prints for “Coexisting in Blue” before continuing his work.
