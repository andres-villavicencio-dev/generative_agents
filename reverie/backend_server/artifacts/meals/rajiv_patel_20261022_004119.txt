Rajiv Patel prepared a comforting bowl of chana masala with jeera rice, a simple vegetarian lunch chosen to keep him grounded before the excitement surrounding his first solo show. The dish featured chickpeas, tomatoes, onions, ginger, garlic, cumin, coriander, turmeric, and a gentle chili warmth. He simmered the spiced tomato gravy until the chickpeas became tender, then folded it with a few spoonfuls of cooked basmati rice. Fresh coriander was scattered over the top, while the rice had been cooked separately with cumin and whole spices so each grain remained distinct.

The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of toasted cumin, warm ginger, and slow-cooked tomatoes. He served the food on a plain ceramic plate, arranging the golden rice beside the rich orange-brown curry and adding a small spoonful of plain yogurt. A glass of water—or perhaps hot tea—completed the understated setting. It was an informal, reflective lunch on Tuesday, February 14, intended as a quiet pause before he continued preparing for his show.
