The main dish Rajiv Patel prepared was Garlic Butter Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a comforting main course designed for his first solo show. He marinated chicken thighs in olive oil, garlic, turmeric, cumin, and a touch of smoked paprika before cooking them skin-side down in a hot pan until golden. The chicken was finished in a garlic-butter sauce with fresh parsley, lemon zest, and a splash of cream. Alongside it, he roasted carrots, onions, and green beans with olive oil, salt, and a pinch of smoked paprika until crisp and caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted garlic, savory herbs, and buttery chicken. Rajiv plated the dish on a white ceramic plate, arranging the golden chicken on one side and the colorful vegetables on the other, garnished with a few mint leaves. The presentation was simple yet elegant, fitting the quiet, contemplative mood of his solo show.

He prepared the meal while working from the artists’ co-living space, balancing his cooking with the preparations for his exhibition. The comforting dish provided a peaceful pause during a busy week, while his guitar playing added a cheerful touch to the evening.
