Rajiv prepared Gourmet Egg with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Cream, a simple yet warmly comforting weekday breakfast.

The dish featured three eggs poached in a light lemon-herb cream, topped with roasted carrots, parsnips, and crisp bell peppers. He lightly toasted the vegetables with olive oil, garlic, and a pinch of salt before mixing them with the eggs and serving them over whole-grain toast. The pan smelled of roasted roots, garlic, and fresh lemon—delicate notes of earthiness, richness, and citrus.

He presented the meal in a small white ceramic bowl, arranged the vegetables in neat rows, and placed the eggs beside a small plate of warm toast. The bright colors and clean presentation felt inviting and cheerful, matching his relaxed, purposeful morning. It was the beginning of a productive day, setting the tone for his painting work, guitar practice, and quiet anticipation of the neighborhood.
