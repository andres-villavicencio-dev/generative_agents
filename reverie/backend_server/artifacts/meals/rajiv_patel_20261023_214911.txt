Rajiv Patel prepared a Mushroom & Herb Pan-Roasted Lamb Shank, a warm, comforting main dish designed to match the quiet rhythm of his first solo show.

The lamb shanks were seasoned with smoked paprika, garlic, cumin, black pepper, and a little lemon zest, then seared in butter until the skin turned crisp. They were seared separately in a pan, then added to a pot with sliced cremini mushrooms, shallots, thyme, rosemary, and a splash of red wine. The lamb was slow-braised until the meat was tender, the wine reduced, and the dish finished under a gentle oven rack.

Aromatically, the kitchen filled with the rich scent of garlic, roasted lamb, thyme, and mushrooms. Rajiv presented the dish in a rustic ceramic bowl, garnishing it with fresh rosemary, a few lemon wedges, and a crisp herb sprig. He served it alongside crusty bread and a simple green salad.

The meal was prepared for a quiet lunch during his first solo show, when he wanted a warm, satisfying break while his creative day continued.
