Rajiv Patel prepared a quiet, home-style lentil stew, Mukhi Dal, to celebrate his first solo show. The dish combines brown lentils, chickpeas, carrots, potatoes, onions, garlic, cumin, turmeric, and a handful of fresh coriander. He sautéed the spices and aromatic vegetables in a small pan until the oil shimmered, then cooked the lentils in water until tender. A little coconut milk and warm broth added depth, while the remaining lentils and vegetables were simmered together until the flavors melded.

The aroma filled the small studio: cumin, turmeric, garlic, and the earthy scent of cooked lentils mingled with a hint of onion and a subtle sweetness from the carrots. He finished the dish with a drizzle of ghee and garnished it with fresh cilantro and a pinch of chili powder. Rajiv presented it in a simple white bowl, arranged carefully so the colorful vegetables stood out against the dark brown lentils.

Served at 5:00 p.m., the meal was intended as a peaceful pause between his painting and guitar practice before a quiet dinner. Its warm colors and comforting flavors made it feel both ordinary and special—reflecting his desire to enjoy his everyday life while remaining focused on his creative work.
