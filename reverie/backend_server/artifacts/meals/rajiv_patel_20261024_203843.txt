Rajiv Patel prepared Rajiv’s Creamy Tomato Rice with Spicy Chicken for lunch. The dish used basmati rice, canned tomatoes, chopped onions, garlic, ginger, chili flakes, cumin, coriander, ginger-garlic paste, chicken pieces, spinach, and a splash of coconut milk. He sautéed the vegetables and spices, then cooked the rice while the chicken simmered beneath it. He added the spinach just before serving and finished the dish with a little cream.

The aroma was warm, earthy, and fragrant—herbs, toasted garlic, and caramelized tomatoes filling the kitchen. He plated the rice in a white bowl, arranged the golden chicken and green spinach around the edges, and sprinkled a few crushed red chilies and dried coriander over the top. The colorful presentation balanced the creamy sauce with the fluffy rice, making it a hearty, inviting meal.

He prepared it at home for a quiet family lunch, enjoying the food and the peaceful atmosphere of his artists’ co-living space.
