Rajiv Patel prepared Garden-Style Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Rice for lunch on Wednesday, February 15. The meal was a simple, nourishing family meal, prepared in a large skillet. He sliced the chicken and marinated it with olive oil, garlic, lemon, and herbs. The vegetables—zucchini, carrots, and bell peppers—were roasted with rosemary and thyme until tender. He then stirred in cooked rice and served it on a white plate, arranging the colorful vegetables around the golden chicken.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic and herbs. Rajiv carefully plated the dish, letting the bright green vegetables contrast with the rich brown rice and creamy chicken. It was prepared for a quiet Wednesday lunch, with family members gathering nearby to enjoy the meal. The relaxed, nourishing presentation reflected his patient and cheerful nature, making the occasion feel both ordinary and thoughtful.
