On Wednesday, February 15, Rajiv Patel prepared Garam Masala Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for lunch. The dish was simple yet flavorful, featuring tender chicken thighs marinated in yogurt, turmeric, cumin, and a hint of garlic, alongside carrots, potatoes, bell peppers, and spinach.

Rajiv marinated the chicken at home overnight, then stir-fried it gently with whole spices and aromatic herbs until it became moist and evenly cooked. The vegetables were roasted until crisp and caramelized, then tossed in the pan with the sauce. He finished the dish with a splash of lemon juice and chopped cilantro.

The aroma was warm and inviting, filling the small kitchen with the scent of turmeric, cumin, garlic, and roasted vegetables. He plated the colorful meal in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the vegetables around the chicken and garnishing it with fresh herbs and a drizzle of yogurt. It was a relaxed, family-oriented lunch, balancing his painting and guitar practice with a quiet moment of nourishment and conversation.
