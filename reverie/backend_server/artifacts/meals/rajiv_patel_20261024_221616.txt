Rajiv Patel prepared a simple yet comforting family lunch—a Chicken and Vegetable Curry with Naan served at his artists’ co-living space.

The dish featured tender chicken cooked in a rich curry made with tomatoes, garlic, ginger, curry powder, cumin, coriander, garam masala, and a splash of coconut milk. It was served with steamed zucchini, carrots, bell pepper, and spinach, with a drizzle of olive oil and lemon. Naan bread, lightly toasted and brushed with ghee, was placed alongside the curry, while yogurt and sliced cucumber provided a refreshing contrast.

Rajiv’s kitchen smelled of warm spices, roasted garlic, and gently sizzling chicken. He sautéed the vegetables first, then cooked the chicken until golden and tender, letting the fragrant curry simmer to balance the flavors. The meal was presented on a white ceramic plate, arranged neatly with the bread, vegetables, and yogurt in the center.

It was a relaxed family dinner, prepared with patience and care to share with his wife and children after a long day of painting and guitar practice.
