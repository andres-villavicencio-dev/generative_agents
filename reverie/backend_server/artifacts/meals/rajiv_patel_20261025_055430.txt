Dish Name: Rajiv’s Quiet Breakfast Bowl  
Key Ingredients: oatmeal, maple syrup, fresh berries, sliced banana, chia seeds, and a small portion of whole-grain toast.  
Preparation Method: Rajiv boiled the oats in water with a little milk until fluffy, then added the berries, banana, and chia seeds. The warm oatmeal was served with the toast, and a small bowl of yogurt was prepared for a quick energy boost.  
Aroma: The meal lifted the quiet co-living space with the earthy scent of oats, sweet vanilla of the maple syrup, and the fresh scent of citrus from the sliced banana.  
Presentation: The dishes were arranged on a simple white plate: the oatmeal bowl beside the toast, with the yogurt and extra fruit arranged neatly nearby. Rajiv added a warm cinnamon stick to the oatmeal for visual appeal.  
Occasion: It was his first solo-show preparation, so the breakfast was both a practical routine and a moment of calm. He wanted to arrive at the studio ready and relaxed, using the quiet morning to organize his paints, brushes, and other supplies without rushing.
