Dish: Simple Tomato Basil Pasta with Peppers and Zucchini  

Key Ingredients:  
Pasta (spaghetti or penne)  
Tomato sauce, olive oil, garlic, red pepper flakes, oregano, basil  
Zucchini, red bell pepper, onions, and fresh tomatoes  
Parmesan cheese and black pepper  

Preparation Method:  
Rajiv sliced the vegetables and sautéed them until soft, then added the garlic and red pepper flakes. He simmered the tomatoes with olive oil and seasonings until the sauce thickened, then tossed in the pasta and topped it with melted Parmesan. The dish was finished with a drizzle of olive oil and freshly chopped basil.

Aroma: Warm, savory, and gently spicy, with hints of garlic, tomatoes, and dried herbs.

Presentation: The pasta was plated with the colorful vegetables still retaining their bright colors, Parmesan sprinkled over the top, and a small sprig of basil placed beside it.

Occasion: The meal was prepared for Rajiv’s first solo painting show, a quiet personal occasion to nourish his body and mind while preparing for a creative milestone.
