Rajiv Patel prepared Gajar Ka Halwa with Roasted Dates, a warm, comforting dessert prepared for his Wednesday family dinner. The dish combines finely grated carrots, ripe dates, yogurt, cumin, and a little cardamom, layered gently in a shallow pan. He roasted the dates until soft, then simmered the remaining ingredients until the carrots softened and the dates released their sweetness.

The aroma was warm and earthy: cumin, dried fruit, and yogurt mingled with the faint sweetness of caramelized carrots. He presented the halwa in a white clay bowl, garnished with a few whole roasted dates and a drizzle of yogurt, arranged beside a simple plate of steamed bread and a cup of warm chai. It was prepared for a quiet family meal on a Wednesday evening, balancing the creative energy of his painting with a sense of home and comfort.
