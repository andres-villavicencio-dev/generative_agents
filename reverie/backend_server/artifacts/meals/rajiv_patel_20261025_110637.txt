Rajiv Patel prepared a Family Sunday Roast for lunch on Wednesday, February 15, while balancing his painting with family responsibilities. The meal featured a golden-brown chicken stuffed with tender carrots, onions, and garlic, seasoned with turmeric, cumin, paprika, and a hint of lemon. Alongside it was roasted root vegetables—carrots, potatoes, and sweet potatoes—tossed with olive oil, garlic, thyme, and salt.

Rajiv seasoned the chicken and vegetables in a large skillet, then arranged them in a shallow baking pan to roast at 180°C for 40–45 minutes. The golden crust formed around the edges while the center remained moist and flavorful. He finished the dish with a simple lemon-herb sauce and served it on a white ceramic platter alongside warm crusty bread.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and inviting, with the rich fragrance of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, and savory spices creating a cozy family feel. The presentation was simple and practical: the colorful vegetables and plump chicken stood out against the bright kitchen background. Rajiv prepared the meal with patience and care, reflecting the quiet rhythm he wanted to maintain in his life while supporting his family.
