Rajiv Patel prepared Lemon Herb Roasted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a simple yet comforting vegetarian meal designed for his Wednesday evening.

The chicken was marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, dried oregano, cumin, and a pinch of smoked paprika, then roasted until golden and tender. Alongside it, carrots, potatoes, and parsnips were glazed in the same herb-infused oil and roasted until caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, cinnamon, citrus zest, and the subtle smoke of the paprika. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the chicken rested on a wooden board, surrounded by roasted vegetables, with a small bowl of lemon-tahini dressing and a green herb sprig beside it.

It was a quiet, nourishing meal for his first solo show preparation, offering a peaceful contrast to the creative energy of painting and guitar practice.
