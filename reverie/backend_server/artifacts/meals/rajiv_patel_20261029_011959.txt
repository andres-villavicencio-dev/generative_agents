On Tuesday, February 14, Rajiv Patel prepared Chai with Warm Lentil Biryani for a quiet neighborhood lunch. The dish is a comforting blend of whole wheat rice, gently simmered with soaked lentils, fresh coriander, cumin, cardamom, a handful of almonds, and ginger-garlic paste. A fragrant cup of black tea was poured over the rice, and a warm lemon wedge was served alongside it.

Rajiv stirred the rice and lentils together, allowing the spices to release their gentle aroma as the bowl settled. The dish had a warm, earthy fragrance with hints of cardamom, cumin, and toasted nuts, balanced by the clean scent of tea. It was presented in a simple white bowl, with the rice garnished with green herbs and a bright lemon slice, inviting a sense of calm and satisfaction.

He ate it with a quiet sense of contemplation, the warm meal complementing his peaceful Tuesday. The occasion was simply a moment of rest—a chance to nourish himself after a full day while continuing to paint with interest and a sense of creative fulfillment.
