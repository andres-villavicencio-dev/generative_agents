Rajiv prepared Ginger-Turmeric Lentil Soup with Roasted Potatoes, a simple, comforting dinner for Tuesday, February 14. The recipe included dried red lentils, chopped carrots, celery, onions, garlic, fresh ginger, turmeric, cumin, and a touch of tomato paste. He sautéed the vegetables in a large pot, then added water and lentils and simmered them until the grains were tender. Roasted potatoes were tossed with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then arranged around the soup for a bright contrast.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of simmering spices, roasted garlic, and earthy vegetables. At the table, Rajiv plated the soup in a shallow bowl, garnished with chopped parsley and a few whole peppercorns, with the golden roasted potatoes arranged beside it. The occasion was a quiet, informal evening after an active day—no work was scheduled, and he was preparing the meal himself before placing it on the table.
