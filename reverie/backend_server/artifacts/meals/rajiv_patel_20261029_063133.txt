On Wednesday, February 15, Rajiv Patel prepared Garnished Herb Rice with Grilled Chicken and Roasted Vegetables to complement his evening of dinner, television, and painting.

The meal consisted of basmati rice cooked with ginger, garlic, turmeric, and a small amount of coconut milk, while the chicken was grilled with lemon, olive oil, and a pinch of salt. Alongside it were carrots, sweet potato, and green beans, each roasted until tender and lightly glazed. He tossed them together with fresh herbs, cilantro, and a drizzle of olive oil for a bright finish.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasting vegetables and the savory scent of the chicken, while the rice absorbed the golden herbs and rendered oil, creating a fragrant, earthy aroma. He plated the dish in a simple white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables and tender chicken with a few sprigs of parsley and a small lemon wedge beside it. The occasion was a quiet, self-rewarding evening: after a full day of work and conversation with Eugene and Carmen, Rajiv sat down to enjoy his meal while calmly watching television and preparing to continue painting.
