Rajiv Patel prepared a simple Saffron Chicken Curry with Basmati Rice for dinner. The dish consists of chicken pieces marinated in yogurt, ginger, garlic, turmeric, cumin, and a pinch of saffron, along with basmati rice, onions, tomatoes, and green chilies.

He sautéed the onions and tomatoes in a pan, then added the chicken and spices. The curry simmered for about twenty minutes until the chicken was tender and the sauce thickened. A spoonful of saffron-infused oil was stirred in near the end to give the dish its warm golden hue.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the comforting scent of cumin, turmeric, ginger, and roasted chicken. Rajiv plated the curry in a shallow bowl, placing the rice beside it and garnishing it with fresh coriander. The meal was simple and home-style, prepared for a quiet evening of relaxation before watching TV and returning to his canvas.
