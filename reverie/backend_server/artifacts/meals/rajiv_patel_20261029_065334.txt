At 5:00 p.m., Rajiv Patel prepared Garden Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for dinner. The dish featured tender chicken thighs marinated in lemon, garlic, and fresh herbs, served alongside carrots, parsnips, potatoes, and bell peppers roasted until tender and caramelized. He roasted everything in a large skillet with olive oil, sea salt, and a pinch of cumin, allowing the vegetables to turn vibrant orange, golden, and slightly crisp.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory chicken. Rajiv plated the meal carefully, arranging the colorful vegetables around the golden chicken and scattering a few sprigs of mint and parsley over the top. He served it with a simple lemon-herb rice and a side of cool cucumber salad, creating a balanced, rustic presentation that felt both nourishing and cheerful.

The dinner was prepared for a quiet evening of television and painting. After eating, Rajiv settled into his evening routine, continuing his work on a new landscape scene—a quiet field at dusk with glowing trees and a calm river reflecting the sky.
