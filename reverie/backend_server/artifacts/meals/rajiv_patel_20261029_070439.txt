On Wednesday, February 15, Rajiv Patel prepared Rajiv’s Creamy Vegetable Risotto for an early evening dinner. The dish combined Arborio rice, gently sautéed baby carrots, button mushrooms, peas, cherry tomatoes, and a touch of Parmesan cheese. The base consisted of toasted pine nuts, garlic, and a savory broth enriched with white wine, while a small knob of butter and lemon zest added subtle richness.

Rajiv simmered the rice over low heat, stirring frequently to allow the grains to absorb the broth without becoming mushy. After the final minutes, he finished the dish by stirring in the Parmesan and tossing in a final drizzle of olive oil. The aroma filled the kitchen with toasted grains, garlic, earthy mushrooms, and the warm scent of lemon and herbs.

He plated the risotto in a shallow white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables and golden Parmesan in an inviting pattern. The meal was simple and comforting, prepared for a quiet Wednesday night. It was a familiar, home-style dinner, balanced by the rhythm of the evening and the peaceful continuation of Rajiv’s creative process: painting while shaping the next part of the landscape.
