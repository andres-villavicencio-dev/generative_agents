Dish Name: Rajiv’s Quiet Breakfast—Savory Egg and Bread with Fresh Herbs

Rajiv prepared a simple, nourishing breakfast at 9:30 AM. He gathered white bread, eggs, a pinch of salt, freshly chopped coriander and chives, and a drizzle of olive oil. He boiled two eggs, seasoned them lightly, and toasted the bread with a little butter. The whole meal came together in a small ceramic bowl and a lightly glazed plate, with the bread beside two golden eggs.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, butter, and the gentle scent of herbs. He arranged the ingredients neatly, adding a pinch of salt and squeezing the coriander from a small bunch before plating. The colors were subdued—creamy bread, sunny yellow eggs, green herbs, and golden butter—creating a calm, harmonious composition.

This was a quiet weekday breakfast, intended to energize Rajiv before heading back to work. He treated it as a peaceful moment of self-care, balancing his painting, responsibilities, and the rhythm of his day with intention and care.
