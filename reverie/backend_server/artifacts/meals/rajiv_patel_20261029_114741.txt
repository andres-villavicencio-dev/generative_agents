Rajiv Patel prepared a Mediterranean Rice and Vegetable Bowl for dinner. The dish featured basmati rice, cherry tomatoes, roasted red peppers, onions, garlic, and a lemon‑herb yogurt dressing. He sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until crisp, added the rice, and then mixed in a creamy yogurt sauce with chopped herbs.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and citrus. Rajiv plated the meal carefully, arranging the colorful vegetables around the rice and drizzling the dressing over the top. It was a quiet, comforting dinner intended to accompany his evening work, with little special occasion and no special guests.
