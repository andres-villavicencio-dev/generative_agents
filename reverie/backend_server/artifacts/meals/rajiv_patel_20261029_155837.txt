Rajiv Patel prepared a warm lentil and vegetable bowl for lunch, a simple yet comforting meal that reflected his patient, cheerful nature. The dish combined cooked red lentils, finely chopped carrots, onions, and a handful of fresh spinach, with a small portion of cooked rice and a spoonful of yogurt to balance its savory richness. He seasoned it gently with cumin, turmeric, and a pinch of salt, then simmered everything in a little water until the lentils were tender and the vegetables had softened.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of the lentils, the bright color of the carrots, and the fresh scent of the spinach. He presented the bowl in a clean, white ceramic dish, arranging the colorful vegetables around a mound of rice so the meal looked inviting and orderly.

The occasion was a quiet Friday lunch after a productive day of painting and community planning. Rajiv ate it while washing his hands and preparing his lunch, feeling energized, content, and thoughtful. He looked forward to visiting new studios, talking with friends and neighbors, and helping at Ethel’s February 14 party, hoping the meal would bring him comfort and encouragement.
