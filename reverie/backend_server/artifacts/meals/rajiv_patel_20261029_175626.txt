Rajiv Patel prepared Garnished Lentil and Vegetable Chana Curry, a comforting, nutrient-rich main dish that reflected his patient, cheerful nature. The curry combined red and green lentils, carrots, cauliflower, chickpeas, onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and cumin. He sautéed the vegetables in oil until golden, then added the lentils and spices and simmered everything until the lentils were tender and the sauce thickened.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and earthy—creamy, slightly smoky cumin, tender vegetables, and the subtle sweetness of caramelized tomatoes. Rajiv finished it with fresh cilantro, chili flakes, and a generous drizzle of lemon-lime yogurt.

He plated the curry in a shallow white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables and chickpeas around a mound of creamy lentils. Garnishes of parsley and a sprinkle of dried apricot completed the presentation. The meal was prepared for a quiet Friday dinner, but Rajiv also felt grateful for the chance to connect with new community members and look forward to visiting friends and neighbors at Ethel’s February 14 party. The simple yet hearty dish made him feel satisfied, orderly, and ready to enjoy a relaxed evening.
