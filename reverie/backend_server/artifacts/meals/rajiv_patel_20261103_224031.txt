Rajiv Patel prepared a Garnished Lentil and Vegetable Stir-Fry for a simple, filling lunch. The dish was built on a base of brown lentils and finely chopped carrots, with onions, ginger, garlic, and fresh cilantro added for warmth. He stir-fried everything quickly in a pan with vegetable oil until the lentils were tender and the vegetables were lightly caramelized.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of cumin, the sharp hint of garlic, and the fresh fragrance of herbs. He finished the meal with a dollop of coconut yogurt and sliced fresh tomatoes before serving it in a simple white bowl over brown rice. Rajiv ate it quietly at his kitchen table on a cold Monday morning, enjoying the comforting aroma as he prepared to go grocery shopping.

Afterward, he went to Johnson Park to relax in a quiet spot, checking the community bulletin board for openings to apply for.
