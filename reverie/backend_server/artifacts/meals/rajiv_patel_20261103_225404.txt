Rajiv Patel prepared Buttermilk Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Rice, a simple yet comforting lunch that matched his relaxed approach to cooking. He marinated chicken thighs in buttermilk, garlic, and a touch of cumin, then pan-seared them until the skin was golden and crispy. He added carrots, bell pepper, and onion to a roasting pan, sprinkled them with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and roasted them at 200°C (400°F) until tender and caramelized. The rice was rinsed and gently simmered with vegetable broth, garlic, and lemon until fragrant and fluffy.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of caramelized garlic, roasted vegetables, and rich buttermilk. Rajiv plated the dish in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the chicken in a neat, golden-brown line while the bright yellow lemon-herb rice and colorful vegetables surrounded it. He served it with a small side of cucumber and mint salad and a glass of chilled lemonade.

The meal was prepared for a quiet lunch break at Johnson Park, where Rajiv wanted to enjoy his food in peace before heading home. It was a calm, nourishing meal, fitting for a first solo-show preparation day.
