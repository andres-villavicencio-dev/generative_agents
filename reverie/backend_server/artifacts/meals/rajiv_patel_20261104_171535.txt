Rajiv prepared Panchkari, a classic Indian lentil stew made from chickpeas, kidney beans, carrots, potatoes, onions, tomatoes, and fresh ginger. He sautéed the onions and ginger until fragrant, then added the spices and simmered the lentils with water until the vegetables softened and the gravy became creamy and slightly tangy. Rajiv added fresh coriander and served it with naan and a small bowl of mint chutney.

The aroma of warm, earthy spices mixed with the savory, creamy taste of the lentils filled the co-living studio, creating a gentle, comforting atmosphere. Rajiv presented the dish in a simple white bowl, arranging the naan beside it so it looked inviting. He served it for lunch on a quiet Monday afternoon, balancing the meal with time for his first solo exhibition and his new guitar.
