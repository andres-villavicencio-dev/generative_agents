Rajiv Patel prepared Garlic Butter Chicken with Lemon-Tahini Salad for his Tuesday lunch. The dish featured chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, turmeric, cumin, garlic, and a pinch of chili, then pan-seared until golden and juicy. The sauce was made by melting butter in a skillet, sautéing the minced garlic until fragrant, then stirring in cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper before simmering the chicken until the sauce thickened.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the chicken was arranged on a white ceramic plate alongside a crisp salad of lettuce, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and lemon-tahini dressing. Sliced cucumber and parsley were garnished on top, with a small bowl of lemon wedges and a cup of water nearby.

As he ate, he savored the warm aroma of garlic and butter mingling with the bright lemon scent and earthy spices. The meal balanced comforting comfort food with a refreshing, colorful salad, perfect for a quiet lunch while preparing for his first solo show and discussing his application details with Latoya Williams.
