Dish Name: Rajiv’s Quiet Tuesday Lunch

Rajiv prepared a mild tomato and herb chicken salad, a warm, comforting meal suited to his first solo show. The main ingredient was chicken thighs, sliced into bite-sized pieces and marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a little honey. He added grilled chicken breasts, crisp carrot sticks, and cucumber slices. To finish the salad, he tossed in cherry tomatoes, fennel, red onion, and parsley, along with a generous handful of baby spinach. He stirred everything in a wide, shallow pan with a fork, letting the chicken release its juices while keeping the vegetables crisp.

The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of roasted garlic and caramelized herbs, with a bright, citrusy aroma from the lemon and tomato. He arranged the salad in a simple white ceramic bowl, garnishing it with a few fresh parsley leaves and a light dusting of white pepper.

The meal was prepared for February 14, Rajiv’s first solo show, when he also met with Latoya Williams to discuss her application details. It was a relaxed Tuesday lunch, but he took the time to complete the salad and eat a small fruit snack, turning a simple meal into a quiet moment of preparation and reflection.
