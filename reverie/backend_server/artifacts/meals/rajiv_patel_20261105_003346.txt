Rajiv prepared a simple Lentil Soup with Roasted Vegetables and Crispy Avocado for lunch. The dish used lentils, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, tomato, olive oil, salt, and pepper. He roasted the vegetables until their edges were slightly caramelized, then sautéed the lentils with warm water until soft, stirring in the roasted vegetables and fresh avocado. He topped it with a generous lemon-herb dressing and served it in a small ceramic bowl.

The warm aroma of roasted garlic and lemon filled the kitchen as he prepared it. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the colorful vegetables, creamy lentil soup, and golden avocado were arranged around a small loaf of sourdough bread, with lemon wedges and herbs placed beside it.

It was Rajiv’s first lunch on February 14, a quiet day focused on preparing for his first solo show and discussing his application with Latoya Williams. The meal was informal and unhurried, giving him a peaceful place to pause, review his plans, and take a few moments of breathwork before his afternoon activities.
