Rajiv Patel prepared Chicken and Asparagus with Lemon-Herb Gravy, a simple yet comforting dinner for his Tuesday evening. He sliced a chicken breast into thin strips, tossed them with asparagus spears, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, and a touch of olive oil, then pan-seared the chicken before finishing it in the pan. The asparagus was quickly blanched and added to the pan, while the chicken and vegetables were covered to cook gently until the liquid reduced into an amber gravy.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, thyme, and lemon. Rajiv carefully monitored the pan so the chicken would cook evenly without drying out. When the gravy had thickened, he finished the dish by garnishing it with fresh parsley and a few sprigs of rosemary.

Plated on a white ceramic plate with a small lemon wedge and a sprig of rosemary, the meal looked inviting and balanced. He ate it quietly, pausing occasionally to check that the chicken and asparagus were cooking evenly, while the cheerful atmosphere of the house made the evening feel special. This was a relaxed Tuesday, part of his preparation for his first solo show and his ongoing life of quiet creativity.
