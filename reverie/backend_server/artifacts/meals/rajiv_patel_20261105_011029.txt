Rajiv prepared a simple yet elegant lentil and vegetable curry for lunch, with warm rice as the side. The dish is named Lentil Veg Curry with Rice.

Key ingredients include cooked brown lentils, carrots, celery, potatoes, onions, tomato paste, cumin, coriander, garam masala, turmeric, and coconut milk. The curry was simmered until the lentils were tender, with the vegetables softened and the rice cooked separately. He finished it with a drizzle of coconut oil and a sprinkle of fresh coriander.

The aroma filled the kitchen: earthy cumin, sweet tomato, warm coconut, and the gentle scent of simmering spices. Rajiv arranged the colorful curry on a white plate, with the fluffy rice neatly folded beside it, using small serving spoons and a small yellow plate for the garnish.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday lunch, while Rajiv continued preparing for his first solo show and discussing the application with Latoya Williams. The relaxed atmosphere made the meal feel both nourishing and personally meaningful.
