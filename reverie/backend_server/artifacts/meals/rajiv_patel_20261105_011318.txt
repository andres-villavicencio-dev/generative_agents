On Tuesday, February 14, Rajiv Patel prepared a simple yet elegant Indian-style lentil stew called Aloo Matar Dal. He used a small pot of red lentils, cabbage, cucumber, onion, tomato, cumin, coriander, garam masala, and salt, along with a generous handful of frozen peas. He sautéed the vegetables in oil until the onions turned translucent, then added the lentils and simmered everything gently until the dal absorbed the flavors.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and aromatic—rich spices, caramelized onions, and the earthy sweetness of the cabbage. Rajiv arranged the colorful lentils and vegetables in a shallow bowl, placing a sprig of fresh coriander beside them. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with a small plate of roasted peanuts and chopped green chilies nearby.

He served the meal for himself and his dinner, enjoying it quietly in the late afternoon. The occasion was informal, not his first solo show, but a relaxed Tuesday meal before he prepared for his exhibition with Latoya Williams. The lunch meeting had been scheduled for that day, although no specific time or location had been confirmed.
