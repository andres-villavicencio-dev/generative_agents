Rajiv Patel prepared a simple Rice and Vegetable Lunch for his Tuesday lunch. The dish was made in a shallow pan: rice was rinsed, then cooked with a generous portion of chopped tomatoes, garlic, and a little olive oil until the grains became tender and the sauce had a mild sweetness. A small handful of seasonal vegetables—cucumber, onion, and carrots—was added at the end, giving the dish a bright green texture and a pleasant crunch.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with the gentle scent of roasted garlic, caramelized tomatoes, and the subtle aroma of rice cooking in the pan. Rajiv presented the meal in a small white bowl, garnishing it with a few crisp vegetables and a sprig of fresh coriander. It was a straightforward, comforting lunch suitable for preparing his first solo show—simple, nourishing, and easy to enjoy while watching television.
