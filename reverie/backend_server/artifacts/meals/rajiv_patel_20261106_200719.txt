Rajiv Patel prepared Garam Masala Chicken with Rice and Green Peas, a simple yet comforting home-cooked meal prepared for his first solo show. The recipe used chicken thighs, onion, garlic, ginger, green peas, basmati rice, tomato sauce, yogurt, cumin, coriander, garam masala, and a splash of lemon juice. He marinated the chicken overnight, sliced the onions and garlic, then sautéed them until golden. The chicken was pan-fried until cooked through, while the vegetables were simmered in the tomato and yogurt sauce. Seasonal herbs and fresh green peas were stirred in for color and freshness.

The aroma of warm chicken, cumin, and roasted garlic filled the kitchen, giving the meal a rich, inviting scent. Rajiv presented the dish in a simple white bowl, surrounded by neatly arranged rice and colorful peas. A bright yellow lemon wedge and a sprig of coriander added a cheerful touch, while a small side of naan complemented the warmth. The meal was prepared quietly in his artists’ co-living space, reflecting his desire for a relaxed evening before performing alone.
