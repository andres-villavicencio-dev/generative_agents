Rajiv Patel prepared Gajar-Kaakkav for his first solo art exhibition dinner. Aromatic and earthy, the dish features spiced carrots, onions, garlic, ginger, turmeric, cumin, coriander, red chili powder, and a splash of lemon juice, with a little butter and cream added for richness.

He sautéed the onions and ginger in a pan, then added the carrots, cumin, and spices to soften and coat them with the warm, savory aroma. The curry finished gently on medium heat, creating a fragrant, lightly caramelized flavor. He poured the cooked masala over the vegetables and simmered it briefly until the carrots were tender and the sauce thickened.

Rajiv served the curry in a shallow bowl, garnished with chopped coriander and a pinch of salt. The meal was simple yet comforting, balanced by a side of naan and a glass of cold lemonade. After dinner, Rajiv sat quietly in his apartment, turning on his guitar and reflecting on his first solo exhibition.
