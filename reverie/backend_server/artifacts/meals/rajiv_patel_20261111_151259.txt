Rajiv Patel prepared Tandoori Chicken with Basmati Rice for a quiet dinner before his first solo show. The main dish was marinated chicken thighs with yogurt, chili, garlic, ginger, turmeric, cumin, and a little garam masala, then roasted in a tandoor until golden and tender. He finished it with basmati rice, cucumber, and a simple tomato-onion curd.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted spices, garlic, and grilled chicken. Rajiv worked slowly, checking the chicken’s color and temperature before serving. He arranged the golden chicken and fluffy rice in a simple white plate, garnishing it with fresh herbs and a lemon slice.

The meal was prepared for an intimate family dinner, a quiet evening before his first solo exhibition. It was a relaxed, nourishing meal that balanced the creative energy of his paintings with a peaceful, familiar rhythm.
