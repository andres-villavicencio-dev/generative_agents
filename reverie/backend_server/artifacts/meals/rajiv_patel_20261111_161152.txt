Rajiv Patel prepared Rajiv’s Spicy Paneer Butter Masala, a warm, comforting main dish for his evening dinner. The recipe featured cubed paneer, tomatoes, onions, garlic, ginger, chili powder, garam masala, yogurt, and fresh cilantro. He sautéed the vegetables until crisp, then simmered them with the paneer until the milk thickened into a rich, glossy sauce. The dish was finished with a sprinkle of fresh coriander and a dash of lemon juice for brightness.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized onions, toasted garlic, and gently spiced milk. Rajiv plated the curry in a shallow, rustic bowl, arranging the golden paneer cubes around the rich sauce and garnishing it with a few fresh herbs. He served it with rice and a side of yogurt, creating an inviting, warm presentation that suited the occasion: a quiet first solo art exhibition. The meal reflected his patient, cheerful nature—calm and nourishing enough to accompany his creative process, while the guitar he was playing in the background added a cheerful, lively touch.
