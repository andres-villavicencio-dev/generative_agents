Rajiv Patel prepared Lemon Herb Butter Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a comforting side dish paired with his main curry. The dish included chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, onions, bell peppers, and a rich sauce made from yogurt, butter, lemon juice, ginger, garlic, and turmeric. He marinated the chicken, pan-seared it gently, then simmered the vegetables until tender. The sauce reduced into a glossy, fragrant finish, while the vegetables retained their bright, earthy colors.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized onions, garlic, and lemongrass, accompanied by the gentle sound of the guitar resting nearby. Rajiv arranged the colorful vegetables and golden chicken on a white plate, with a sprig of coriander and the main dish beside it. He served it with warm rice and yogurt at home around 5:00 PM on Monday, February 13, as part of his relaxed first solo-show preparation.
