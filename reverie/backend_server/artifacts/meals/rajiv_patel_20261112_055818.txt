Rajiv Patel prepared Kadhai Biryani, a warm, fragrant rice dish made with basmati rice, onion, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and a generous amount of fresh curry leaves. The onions and tomatoes were sautéed until soft and translucent, then cooked briefly with ginger and garlic before being added to the rice. He stirred in a little oil and salt, covered the pan, and let the rice simmer until the grains became tender while retaining their individual texture.

The aroma was unmistakably spicy and earthy: the fragrance of the ginger-garlic mix, the subtle heat of the curry leaves, and the savory scent of caramelized onions filled the small kitchen. Rajiv arranged the finished biryani in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with chopped cilantro and a few whole leaves for visual balance.

It was prepared for his first solo show, where he hopes to share his art and music with fellow artists. The quiet, patient kitchen scene gave his usual cheerful energy a calm, homey finish, and the comforting meal reinforced his desire to live quietly, paint, and enjoy everyday life.
