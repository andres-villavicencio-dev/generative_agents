Rajiv Patel prepared Chicken Tikka Masala with Roasted Vegetables and Brown Rice.

For the chicken, he marinated pieces of chicken breast in yogurt, garlic, cumin, coriander, and lemon juice, then cooked them gently until the skin was golden and the interior was tender. The vegetables—including carrots, potatoes, onions, and bell peppers—were roasted with oil, turmeric, and chili to retain their color and crunch. A creamy tomato-onion gravy was finished with fresh cilantro, garam masala, and a splash of cream.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted spices, warm garlic, and savory chicken. He plated the dish with the vegetables arranged around the base and served the fluffy brown rice on the side. Rajiv chose the meal for an easy weekday dinner while preparing for his first solo show, balancing his quiet creative life with the warmth of a home-cooked feast.
