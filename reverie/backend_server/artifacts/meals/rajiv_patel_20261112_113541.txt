On Tuesday, February 14, Rajiv Patel prepared Garden-Root Lentil Curry with Grilled Chicken and Roasted Vegetables. He used fresh lentils, diced chicken breast, carrots, potatoes, onions, garlic, ginger, and a handful of seasonal greens. He sautéed the aromatics and vegetables in oil, added the chicken and spices, covered the pot, and simmered until the lentils were tender and the chicken was cooked through.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of whole spices, caramelized onions, and gently cooking chicken. Rajiv plated the curry in a shallow bowl, arranging the golden chicken pieces and colorful vegetables around a creamy, herb-infused rice base. He served it with a small side of lemon-topped naan and a glass of water.

The meal was a relaxed, nourishing lunch for his quiet afternoons at home. It reflected the calm, purposeful rhythm of his life—simple, wholesome, and carefully balanced, much like the mood-driven concepts shaping The Quiet Canvas.
