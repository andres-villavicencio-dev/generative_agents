Rajiv prepared a simple Glowing Morning Omelet with fresh herbs and whole-grain toast, a quiet breakfast suited to his contemplative lifestyle. The omelet used eggs, diced tomatoes, spinach, feta cheese, and a pinch of dried thyme. He scrambled the eggs, folded in the vegetables, added the cheese, and gently folded the omelet in half before serving. He toasted sliced sourdough bread and arranged it alongside the omelet, topped with a sprig of parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of cooking eggs and savory herbs. The presentation was neat and inviting: the golden omelet rested beside the golden-brown toast, while the colorful vegetables added a lively touch of green and red. Rajiv prepared the meal on Tuesday, February 14, carrying a quiet sense of accomplishment from his solo show and new mood-driven music concepts. It was an unhurried, nourishing breakfast that helped him transition into the day.
