On Tuesday, February 14, Rajiv Patel prepared Vegetable Pan-Fry with Lemon-Butter Sauce.

He sliced red onion, bell pepper, and zucchini into thin strips, then sautéed them in butter and olive oil until the edges were crisp and the vegetables softened. A few tablespoons of lemon juice and a pinch of salt finished the dish. He tossed the vegetables with a warm pan of butter and garlic and drizzled a little extra lemon over the top.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, onion, and toasted butter. In a shallow, warm pan, he arranged the vegetables in overlapping rows, garnishing them with fresh parsley. The dish was plated simply, with a small bowl of lemon dressing beside it.

It was a quiet Tuesday dinner, shared with friends after his solo show and job search. Rajiv’s cheerful mood and patient cooking made the meal feel warm and inviting, the gentle citrus finish balancing the earthy vegetables. He enjoyed it quietly, as he always does, while his paintings and music continued to unfold.
