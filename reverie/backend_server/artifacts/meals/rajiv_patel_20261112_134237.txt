On Tuesday, February 14, Rajiv Patel prepared a simple yet comforting Herb-Roasted Chicken with Lemon Butter, paired with roasted potatoes and a small bowl of seasonal salad.

The chicken was seasoned with salt, cracked black pepper, and dried rosemary and thyme, then marinated for a few hours. It was roasted in a baking pan with olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, and a splash of butter until the skin turned golden brown and the meat was tender. The potatoes were seasoned with salt, pepper, and a pinch of cinnamon, roasted until their skins crisped. A crisp lettuce and tomato salad was tossed with a little olive oil and lemon juice before being served.

As he plated the meal, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, and lemon. The dish presented in a shallow white bowl, with the creamy golden chicken, bright orange potatoes, and green salad creating an inviting, rustic look. Rajiv served himself at home, savoring the dinner as a quiet reflection on his productive Tuesday and his continuing journey as a painter.
