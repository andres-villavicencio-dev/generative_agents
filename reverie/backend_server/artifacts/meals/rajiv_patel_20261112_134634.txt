Rajiv Patel prepared a Garden-Style Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetable Quinoa. For the meal, he chose chicken thighs, quinoa, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, zucchini, and garlic, seasoning them with lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, herbs, and salt. He sautéed the vegetables first, then browned the chicken, adding a splash of water to finish cooking it. The quinoa was rinsed and cooked in a separate pan until tender.

The meal had a warm, earthy aroma with hints of garlic, lemon, herbs, and roasted vegetables. Rajiv plated the colorful ingredients around a white plate, placing the golden chicken and fluffy quinoa in the center with a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing and fresh parsley beside it. He also added a small glass of chilled water.

The simple Tuesday dinner was a quiet moment of completion—after his solo-art show, job search, and community gathering, Rajiv let the meal rest in the refrigerator, turning off the heat and enjoying dinner without distraction.
