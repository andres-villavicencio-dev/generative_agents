Dish Name: Simple Fruit & Toast Breakfast  
Key Ingredients: Whole-grain toast, ripe bananas, oranges, apples, berries, yogurt, honey, and a sprinkle of cinnamon.  
Preparation Method: Rajiv prepared the toast by toasting the slices until golden and crispy, then cut them into uniform squares. He arranged them in a shallow bowl and piled the fresh fruit on top, alternating slices with yogurt for a creamy, colorful contrast. A small bowl of honey and cinnamon was set beside it.  
Aroma: The kitchen filled with the warm scent of toasted bread, sweet citrus, ripe fruit, and the faint, calming aroma of cinnamon.  
Presentation: The breakfast was arranged on a rustic white plate, with the bright fruit arranged in a loose spiral around the toast. The yogurt and honey were placed neatly beside it, and Rajiv added a small sprig of mint for a fresh touch.  
Occasion: The meal was prepared after Rajiv left the artists’ community gathering for the morning. It was a quiet, cheerful breakfast intended to give him energy while he prepared for his solo show, The Quiet Canvas.
