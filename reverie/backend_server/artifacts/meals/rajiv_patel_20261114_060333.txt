Rajiv Patel prepared Chana Masala with Whole Grains and Roasted Vegetables, a warm, comforting Indian-style lunch. The meal combined canned chickpeas, chopped onions, bell peppers, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, cumin, coriander, and yogurt. He sautéed the vegetables and spices, then added the chickpeas and simmered until the sauce thickened, finally tossing in cooked basmati rice and whole-grain naan.

The aroma rose from the kitchen with the rich scent of cumin, toasted onions, warm yogurt, and caramelized vegetables. In the artists’ co-living space, the simple, rustic presentation made the meal feel inviting and practical. Rajiv ate it while checking the grocery list for ingredients needed for his first solo exhibition, balancing nourishment with creative preparation.
