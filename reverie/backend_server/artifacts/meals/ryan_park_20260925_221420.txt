Ryan Park’s Tuesday dinner is a savory miso-glazed sweet potato bowl, served at home on February 14th as a quiet act of self-care after two days of routine. The key ingredients—sweet potatoes, white miso paste, sesame oil, and scallions—are roasted to caramelized perfection in the oven. The aroma is earthy and warm, with a hint of umami that lingers like memory. He plates it on a simple ceramic bowl, garnished with crisp green chives and a drizzle of toasted sesame seeds, creating a contrast of texture and color. The dish feels balanced—nourishing yet unhurried—meant not for guests but for reflection. On this Tuesday, where time loops and routines blur, it’s a small ritual to anchor himself: food as pause, preparation as proof he still shows up, even when clarity is missing.
