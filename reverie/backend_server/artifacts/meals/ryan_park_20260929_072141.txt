Ryan Park’s 5 pm dinner was a deconstructed miso‑glazed salmon with quinoa pilaf and pickled daikon—an analytical fusion of protein and grain. He seared the salmon in a hot pan, basting it with a blend of white miso, maple syrup, and grated ginger until the crust caramelized. The aromas were earthy, sweet, and faintly smoky. His plate presented three equal sections: the glazed salmon atop fluffy quinoa studded with toasted sesame seeds, the daikon slices in a tangy vinegar glaze beside sesame‑oil sprouts. Served on a minimalist white platter, it reflected his pragmatic love for clean systems and quiet efficiency—perfect for a solitary evening of reflection after his reading marathon.
