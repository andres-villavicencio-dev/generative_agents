Ryan Park prepared a Midnight Logic Stirrer—a deconstructed ramen bowl served in the living room at dusk. The broth swirled with miso, shoyu, and a hint of kelp, infused with turmeric for golden clarity. Key ingredients: rice noodles, seared egg yolk, soft-boiled egg, pickled daikon, and scallions. He stirred the broth over low heat until fragrant, letting it simmer like a quiet algorithm. The aroma—earthy, slightly smoky—filled the room as he plated it in a black ceramic bowl, garnished with crisp green onions. No occasion beyond ritual: a solitary act of mental reset. Served untouched at 17:00, the dish waited while TV hummed, a paradox of stillness and motion.
