Ryan prepared a Mediterranean Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Yogurt Dressing for lunch. He gathered quinoa, chickpeas, roasted bell pepper, zucchini, carrots, and cucumber, then cooked the vegetables until their bright orange-red colors softened. The cooked grains were mixed with a crisp salad and topped with creamy lemon-herb yogurt, offering a refreshing balance of protein, fiber, and freshness.

He toasted the vegetables until lightly caramelized, then tossed them with plenty of olive oil, salt, black pepper, and dried oregano. The dish was plated on a white ceramic bowl, with the colorful vegetables arranged neatly over the quinoa and yogurt. Garnished with a few sprigs of parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil, the meal had a warm aroma of roasted peppers, lemon zest, and lightly toasted grains.

It was a simple, satisfying lunch for Ryan as he cleaned the counter and set out his phone for the next development task.
