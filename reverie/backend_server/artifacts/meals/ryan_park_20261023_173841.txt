Dinner: Savory Chicken Risotto with Roasted Root Vegetables

Ryan prepared a hearty Italian-inspired risotto using chicken thighs, Arborio rice, Parmesan, garlic, white wine, and a touch of lemon. He roasted carrots, potatoes, and parsnips with olive oil, herbs, and a pinch of smoked paprika before finishing them just before cooking the rice.

The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little garlic, then seared in a skillet until golden. As the rice cooked, he added the warm ingredients gradually, stirring constantly until the grains became creamy and the chicken was tender. The finished dish smelled rich and aromatic—the scent of toasted garlic, roasted root vegetables, and savory Parmesan filled his kitchen.

Ryan plated the risotto in a shallow bowl, arranging the golden chicken and colorful vegetables around it. He finished the dish with a generous sprinkle of grated Parmesan and a drizzle of lemon oil. It was a practical, satisfying meal suited for a quiet Monday evening, helping him focus on his upcoming mobile app development.
