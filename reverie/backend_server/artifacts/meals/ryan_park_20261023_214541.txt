Ryan prepared Smoked Salmon and Herb-Crusted Asparagus as a simple yet elegant side for dinner. The dish featured fresh asparagus, thinly sliced salmon, lemon wedges, capers, and a bright herb dressing of parsley, lemon zest, garlic, olive oil, and red wine vinegar.

Ryan cooked the asparagus in a pan with just salt and a little oil until crisp-tender, then pan-seared the salmon until just cooked through. He finished the dish with a quick drizzle of the herb lemon dressing and chopped parsley. The aroma was fresh, savory, and lightly smoky, with the lemon adding a bright citrus finish.

The presentation was clean and modern: the asparagus arranged in a neat fan, the salmon placed beside it, and the herbs and capers garnished over the top. Ryan served it alongside his main dinner on a white plate, making it ready for a relaxed, family-style evening.
