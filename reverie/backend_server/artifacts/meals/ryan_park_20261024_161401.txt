On Tuesday, February 14, Ryan prepared a simple yet satisfying lunch of Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Herb Butter and Quinoa. The salmon was pan-seared until golden and flaky, with the pan juices reduced into a glossy, aromatic sauce. Quinoa was cooked with a touch of lemon, garlic, and parsley, while the pan drippings were reduced to create a rich, buttery sauce.

The dish was arranged in a shallow baking dish, with the salmon and quinoa side by side. A small bowl of lemon-herb butter and a side salad were placed beside it for a fresh, colorful contrast. Warm rosemary and garlic infused the kitchen, while the golden-brown crust of the salmon and fluffy quinoa gave the meal an inviting, comforting aroma.

Ryan prepared it for a relaxed Tuesday lunch, balancing work around the new mobile app with the need to nourish himself before heading to bed around 1:00 AM. The practical approach of cooking a straightforward meal, rather than an elaborate one, aligned with his pragmatic, problem-solving nature.
