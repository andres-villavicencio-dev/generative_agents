Ryan Park prepared a simple quinoa-and-cherry tomato salad with lemon-herb dressing as a quick lunch. The dish included cooked quinoa, baby spinach, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and feta, all tossed with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, dried oregano, and a pinch of salt.

He prepared it in a large skillet with a little water and covered it to steam the vegetables and quinoa together, then drained them and added the remaining lemon juice and dressing before tossing everything. The kitchen filled with the sharp scent of garlic and lemon, followed by the earthy aroma of feta and the bright citrus note.

He plated the salad in a colorful bowl and served it at home for a relaxed Tuesday lunch, balancing the practical cooking routine with a few moments of reflection on the work he had been doing.
