Ryan Park prepared a hearty Korean-style beef and vegetable stir-fry, a balanced lunch meant to fuel his long workday. The dish features tender strips of marinated beef, crisp broccoli, carrots, snap peas, and onions, seasoned with soy sauce, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, and a touch of sugar for sweetness. It was cooked quickly in a wok over medium-high heat, with the beef sliced into bite-sized pieces and stir-fried until caramelized, then gently folded in with the vegetables.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm garlic, savory soy, and the sharp, fresh scent of sesame oil. Ryan presented the meal in a shallow, wide bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around a plump beef portion. He added a small side of steamed rice and a side of crisp lettuce to balance the richness. The lunch was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday lunch, where he could focus on testing and documentation while keeping an eye on realistic deadlines and unexpected issues.
