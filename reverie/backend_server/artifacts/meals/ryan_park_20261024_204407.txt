Ryan prepared Buddha Bowl with Warm Roasted Vegetables for lunch. The dish combines fresh kale, spinach, roasted carrots, red bell peppers, chickpeas, and a handful of quinoa. He prepared the vegetables first, roasting them with olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, and smoked paprika until they were tender and slightly caramelized. The quinoa was cooked separately with water and a little lemon juice, then cooled and gently tossed with the vegetables.

The aroma was bright and savory: warm roasted carrots, citrusy lemon, toasted garlic, and a hint of smoked paprika. He presented the bowl on a wooden serving platter, arranging the colorful vegetables in a circular pattern around the white quinoa, with a small lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley as garnish.

This was a relaxed lunch during his Wednesday, February 15 routine, while he continued reviewing requirements and planning the new mobile app. It was neither a celebratory meal nor a heavy dinner, but a focused, nourishing pause before his evening work.
