Ryan prepared a fresh Thai Peanut Chicken Salad as a balanced lunch, using chicken breast, romaine lettuce, cucumber, carrots, cherry tomatoes, red onion, cucumber, and Thai chili. He marinated the chicken in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and roasted garlic, then pan-seared it briefly before slicing it across the bowl. The dressing combined roasted peanut butter, lime juice, honey, fish sauce, and crushed peanuts to create a rich, savory-sweet flavor.

Aromatic, warm, and slightly spicy, the salad filled the kitchen with the scent of roasted garlic, toasted nuts, and fresh herbs. It was presented in a white bowl with colorful vegetables arranged around the chicken and a bright lime wedge on the side, finished with a drizzle of sesame oil.

The meal was a practical choice for a Monday lunch: it was easy to prepare, filling, and suited his focus on improving mobile applications through nutritious, time-efficient eating.
