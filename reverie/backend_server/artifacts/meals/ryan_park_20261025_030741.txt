Ryan prepared a Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Herb Butter and Asparagus for a late Monday dinner. The key ingredients were salmon fillets, asparagus spears, lemon, garlic, olive oil, fresh herbs, and a simple herb butter.

He sliced the salmon thinly, then pan-seared it over medium-high heat until the skin turned golden and the fish was just cooked through. Meanwhile, he roasted the asparagus until crisp-tender. To finish, he pan-fried the garlic and lemon, then melted the butter and added the herbs for a bright, citrusy finish.

The aroma was warm and herbaceous, with notes of garlic, lemon, and savory seafood. The pan-seared salmon was arranged on a white plate, surrounded by golden asparagus and topped with a glossy herb butter sauce. The dish looked clean, balanced, and ready for a relaxed Monday evening meal.
