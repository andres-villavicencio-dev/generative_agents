The meal was a Lemon-Crusted Chicken with Grains and Roasted Vegetables.

Ryan used chicken breasts marinated in a mixture of olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, herbs, and a splash of vinegar. He tossed the chicken in the pan until the skin turned golden and crispy, then arranged the grains and vegetables around it. The vegetables—roasted carrots, zucchini, and bell peppers—were seasoned with dried herbs and spices until they softened and turned glossy.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of lemon zest, roasted herbs, garlic, and butter. The presentation was simple and elegant: the golden chicken served on a white plate, garnished with fresh parsley and a lemon wedge, with the colorful grains and vegetables providing a textured, natural contrast.

This was a relaxed Tuesday lunch, prepared after a demanding day of building the mobile app. Ryan chose the meal for its balanced composition, practical flavor, and ability to provide a satisfying, energizing pause before heading home at 1:00 AM.
