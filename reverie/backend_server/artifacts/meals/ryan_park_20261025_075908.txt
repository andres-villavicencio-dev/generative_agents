Ryan Park prepared a lemon-cajun herb salad, a refreshing side dish for lunch. The dish combined crisp red cabbage, thinly sliced radishes, crisp carrots, and crunchy celery with a bright lemon-lime vinaigrette, a pinch of black pepper, and a few finely chopped chilies for a hint of heat. He added a handful of toasted pumpkin seeds and a few crushed pistachios for texture, while a small bowl of creamy blue-celery cream with a splash of lime juice was served as a crunchy alternative to the crisp vegetables.

The preparation was methodical and straightforward: the vegetables were washed, chopped, and tossed lightly with the dressing, while the pumpkin seeds and pistachios were toasted in a dry pan until golden. The dressing was whisked to create a light, fragrant emulsion that balanced the freshness of the lemon and herbs with the savory notes of the vegetables.

Assembled on a white ceramic plate, the salad appeared vibrant and balanced, with the cool greens and pale vegetables contrasting against the warm golden seeds and pistachios. The citrus aroma lingered lightly in the air, and the bright colors of the vegetables and seeds made the dish inviting and visually pleasing. Ryan prepared it while reviewing requirements and planning the mobile app, using lunch as a moment to recharge and focus on solving technical problems. It was a practical, nourishing lunch suited to a productive Tuesday.
