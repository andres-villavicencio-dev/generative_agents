Ryan prepared a lemon-cured chicken with roasted root vegetables and a citrus-herb glaze. Key ingredients included a whole chicken, lemon, garlic, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, onion, carrots, potatoes, and red pepper.

He seared the chicken in a pan until the skin was golden, then marinated it in lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, herbs, and a pinch of smoked paprika. The vegetables were tossed in the same oil and roasted until tender. The finished dish was plated with a bright lemon wedge, rosemary sprig, thyme, and a small glass of rosé.

The aroma was warm and fragrant, dominated by roasted skin, lemon zest, garlic, and herb notes. The golden chicken contrasted beautifully with the orange vegetables, while the bright lemon glaze added a fresh, zesty finish. It was an informal, nourishing lunch—perfect for a relaxed Wednesday when Ryan needed to focus on finishing his mobile app without rushing.
