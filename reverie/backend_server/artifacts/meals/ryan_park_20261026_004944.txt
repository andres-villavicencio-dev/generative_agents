The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus zest, and savory chicken. Ryan presented the plate in two distinct sections: the golden chicken resting on a bed of colorful vegetables, topped with a bright lemon-scented sauce. The dish looked fresh, balanced, and ready to be enjoyed.
