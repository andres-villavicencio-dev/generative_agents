Ryan prepared Lemon-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables as a quick, protein-rich side to complement his lunch. He combined chicken breast with lemon zest and a little olive oil, then tossed it with roasted carrots, parsnips, and red potatoes.

The chicken was pan-seared until golden and juicy, then briefly broiled to crisp the skin. The vegetables were arranged on a shallow baking sheet, drizzled with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and roasted until tender and slightly caramelized. Ryan added the lemon-herb chicken on top before serving.

The dish had a bright, zesty aroma of lemons, garlic, and savory herbs. It was presented on a rustic white platter with the vegetables arranged in a circular pattern around the chicken, giving the meal an informal yet inviting appearance. Ryan made it while preparing lunch, so it felt like a practical, easy lunch for him to carry and eat on the go.
