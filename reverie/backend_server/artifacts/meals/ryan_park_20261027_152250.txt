On Wednesday, February 15, Ryan Park prepared Lemon Curaçao with Grilled Chicken and Roasted Vegetables for a relaxed Wednesday lunch while testing his mobile app.

He used 1–2 chicken breasts, a handful of cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, onions, garlic, and lemon zest, along with a splash of white wine, dry white wine, fresh lemon juice, and a little sugar. He marinated the chicken in the lemon mixture for 20 minutes, then grilled it over medium heat until golden and cooked through. The vegetables were tossed in oil, garlic, and lemon before roasting until crisp and slightly caramelized.

The warm kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of lemon, garlic, toasted chicken, and citrus, while the bright, colorful presentation of the golden chicken, glowing vegetables, and bright yellow lemon zest created an inviting lunch. It was a casual, energizing meal that balanced his focused development work with a chance to recharge before bed.
