Ryan prepared a Lemon-Curaçao Chicken Bowl for a quick Wednesday lunch, balancing his need for a light, energizing meal with the afternoon research for his mobile app.

The dish features chicken breasts, cucumber slices, cherry tomatoes, avocado, corn, and a lemon-curaçao dressing made with olive oil, vinegar, garlic, lemon juice, and a touch of honey. The dressing was whisked until glossy and slightly tangy, then drizzled over the warm chicken.

Ryan sliced the chicken, seasoned it with salt and pepper, and pan-seared it over medium heat until the skin turned golden. He arranged the ingredients in a shallow bowl, adding the dressing just before serving so the flavors would release. The aroma was bright and citrusy, with notes of caramelized chicken, fresh cucumber, and warm spices.

The presentation was simple and colorful: the bright yellow avocado and green cucumber complemented the deep orange of the chicken, while the cherry tomatoes and corn added pops of color. It was a practical, nourishing lunch suited to his workday—light enough to eat after reviewing app requirements and challenging enough to help him feel focused on his priorities.
