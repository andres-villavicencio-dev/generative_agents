Ryan prepared a Lemon-Crusted Chicken with Lemon-Curry and Coconut Rice side dish as part of his lunch. The main component was a pan-seared chicken thigh, lightly dusted with lemon zest and rubbed with a flour-based crust, then roasted until the skin turned golden and the meat was tender. He added the chicken to a pan with coconut milk, curry powder, ginger, garlic, and fresh herbs, then covered it to simmer. The rice was prepared separately, seasoned with coconut milk, curry, and a squeeze of lemon juice, with the pan’s juices folded into the grains for a rich, savory texture.

The aroma was bright and fragrant: warm coconut milk, toasted garlic, citrus zest, and the savory aroma of roasted chicken. It had a clean, slightly sweet finish without becoming cloyingly rich.

Ryan served the dish with a small portion of lemon-herb chicken, a handful of brown rice, and a side of steamed asparagus. The presentation was simple and elegant: a white dinner plate with the chicken arranged in the center, the rice piled beside it, and the vibrant green asparagus adding a pop of color. It was prepared for a relaxed lunch on a warm Wednesday, though Ryan intended to revisit Arthur Burton’s place later that day.
