Ryan prepared a simple Lemon Herb Garlic Chicken with Asparagus and Quinoa for a nutritious Tuesday dinner. The key ingredients were chicken breast, fresh asparagus, quinoa, lemon, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of herbs.

He seared the chicken over medium heat until it was golden and cooked through, then added the asparagus and lemon halves to the pan. Asparagus was quickly wilted, followed by the cooked quinoa. The mixture was finished with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a few chopped herbs.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of garlic, citrus, and roasted chicken. He plated the dish in a shallow white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the chicken and garnishing with a little parsley. It was a calm, practical meal—perfect for a relaxed Tuesday when Ryan had no confirmed appointments, using the flexibility to explore new technologies while nourishing himself.
