On Thursday, February 16, Ryan prepared a Hearty Vegetable Stir-Fry with Brown Rice and Sesame Ginger Soup for dinner. He began by heating the oil in a wide pan over medium heat, adding sliced carrots, bell peppers, broccoli, and snap peas. After a few minutes of sautéing, he stirred in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil, then combined the vegetables with brown rice, tofu, and a splash of vegetable broth. A few minutes later, he added the remaining vegetables and a handful of spinach.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of garlic and toasted sesame oil, along with the fresh, herbaceous scent of the vegetables. The presentation was simple yet satisfying: the bright, evenly cooked vegetables rested atop the fluffy brown rice, while the clear soup added a refreshing contrast.

Ryan prepared this as a relaxed yet nourishing dinner, complementing it with the organic breakfast he was helping plan with Eugene. It was a quiet Thursday evening—a practical meal without an occasion—and reflected his balanced routine of working, eating, and reading.
