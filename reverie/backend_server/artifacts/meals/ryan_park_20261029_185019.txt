Ryan prepared Organic Farm-Fresh Breakfast Bowl, a warm, nourishing meal made with organic eggs, non-dairy milk, avocado, spinach, cherry tomatoes, and a drizzle of olive oil. The eggs were poached to a soft yolk, while the tomatoes and avocado were lightly steamed or sautéed to preserve their bright colors and crisp texture. Fresh herbs were sprinkled over the top, and a small bowl of yogurt was added for a refreshing dip.

The aroma of the kitchen filled the room as the eggs released their gentle, savory scent, while the avocado and spinach added a fresh, herbaceous freshness. Ryan arranged the ingredients in a wide ceramic bowl, letting the vibrant greens and creamy yellow eggs stand out against the pale orange yogurt. The meal was presented as a balanced, visually inviting breakfast, ready to be enjoyed as a quiet Friday start before work.

It was a relaxed, wholesome occasion—Ryan was proud of his cooking and wanted to share it with Eugene. The meal also marked his connection to The Willows Market, where he might buy coffee beans for later.
