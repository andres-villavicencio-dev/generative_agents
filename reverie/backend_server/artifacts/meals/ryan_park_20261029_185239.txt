The meal was a warm, garden-inspired organic breakfast: Grain & Herb Pancakes served with smoked salmon, avocado, and seasonal vegetables.

Ryan used oat flour, whole-wheat flour, eggs, milk, and a pinch of salt for the pancakes. The pan was lightly buttered and heated, then filled with batter and cooked until golden and fluffy. He topped the pancakes with thinly sliced smoked salmon, creamy avocado, crisp kale, and a handful of red onions. A drizzle of honey and a sprinkle of toasted sesame seeds added a final touch of sweetness.

As the kitchen filled with the aroma of toasted oats, roasted onions, and rich smoked fish, the meal felt both nourishing and celebratory. It was an early Friday breakfast, prepared for the relaxed atmosphere of Eugene’s home, where Ryan shared his cooking and plans while sitting down with Eugene.
