Dish Name: Organic Quinoa & Herb Breakfast Bowl with Roasted Vegetables

Key Ingredients: organic quinoa, kale, cherry tomatoes, red onion, carrots, cucumbers, avocado, lemon, olive oil, cumin, smoked paprika, and fresh herbs.

Preparation: Ryan began by washing and toasting the quinoa in a dry pan, then rinsed and chopped the vegetables. In a large skillet, he sautéed the onions and carrots until softened, added the carrots and cumin, and cooked them until they softened. He roasted the tomatoes and peppers in the pan, then added the avocado and lemon to finish the dish. Everything was mixed with the cooked quinoa, kale, and fresh herbs.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of toasted quinoa, the sharp smell of roasted vegetables, cumin, and the bright citrus aroma of lemon and avocado.

Presentation: The bowl was arranged neatly on a wooden table, with the quinoa forming the base, vegetables scattered over the top, avocado sliced into a creamy layer, and lemon wedges for a final touch.

Occasion: A relaxed Friday brunch with Eugene, a quiet meal to balance work and rest after Ryan’s long day.
