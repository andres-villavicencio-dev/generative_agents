Ryan Park prepared Organic Berry-Infused Oat-Toast Bowl, a warm, nourishing breakfast for the couple.

The dish combines rolled oats, organic berries (strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries), chia seeds, honey, lemon zest, and a splash of oat milk. Toasted almonds and a handful of spinach were added for texture and brightness. Ryan made the berries with a lemon-sugar syrup and cooked the oats in a pan with a little butter and oat milk until the mixture became creamy and fragrant.

The aroma was warm, earthy, and subtly sweet—spices from the oats, honey, and lemons mingling with the fresh fruit. He presented the bowl in a simple white ceramic dish, arranging the colorful berries in a decorative circle around the oats and sprinkling the seeds over the top.

It was the start of their Friday breakfast, a quiet moment of companionship before Ryan continued his work on the new mobile app and technology research.
