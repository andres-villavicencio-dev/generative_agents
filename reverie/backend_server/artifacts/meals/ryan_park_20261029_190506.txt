On Friday, February 17, Ryan Park prepared a hearty organic breakfast with Hearty Vegetable Egg Scramble with Whole Grain Toast and Fresh Citrus, served alongside a smooth Berry-Apple Smoothie. The dish used a mix of organic eggs, spinach, tomatoes, onions, carrots, mushrooms, and seasonal seasonal vegetables. Ryan lightly sautéed the eggs with olive oil, herbs, and a pinch of sea salt, then folded them into a warm, fluffy scramble. He toasted whole-grain bread with a little butter and sliced the fruit for the smoothie, pairing the bright citrus and berries with the savory, earthy flavors of the eggs.

The kitchen smelled of butter, roasted vegetables, herbs, and the sweet aroma of ripe fruit. The meal was presented on a wooden table with the eggs in the center, surrounded by the colorful vegetables, crisp toast, and bright fruit pieces. Ryan made the breakfast for Eugene, who arrived to join them. After cooking and serving the eggs, Ryan sat down with Eugene, sipping the berry smoothie and sharing a few thoughts about the day’s work, including his curiosity about whether the mobile app would address real community concerns.
