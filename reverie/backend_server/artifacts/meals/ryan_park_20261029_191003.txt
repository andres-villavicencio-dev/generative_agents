Ryan prepared Sunflower Seed & Herb Lemon Herb Gravy Pancakes for breakfast. He started with a bowl of organic eggs, plain yogurt, honey, and fresh herbs, then cooked fluffy pancakes with a savory lemon-herb gravy, topped them with avocado and a side of warm fruit salad. The aroma filled the kitchen with butter, caramelized onions, toasted seeds, and the bright scent of lemon and herbs.

He arranged the pancakes on a slate platter, drizzling them with the glossy golden gravy and adding crisp lettuce, cherry tomatoes, and a scattering of nuts. The bright yellow pancakes contrasted with the green salad and creamy yogurt, making the meal look inviting and cheerful.

The occasion was a relaxed family breakfast on Friday, February 17. Ryan and Eugene worked together to plan the meal, while Ryan followed up with Victor about the coffee beans and considered whether The Willows Market would still be open for purchase.
