Ryan prepared a hearty Farmhouse Egg, Roasted Vegetables, and Whole-Wheat Oatmeal Breakfast for Sunday breakfast, later adding a Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Herb Glaze for dinner.

The meal centered on locally sourced ingredients. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted oats, caramelized honey, savory fish, and fresh herbs. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the eggs rested on a warm plate, the salmon shimmered in a small bowl, and the vegetables and oatmeal were arranged alongside a simple bread bowl.
