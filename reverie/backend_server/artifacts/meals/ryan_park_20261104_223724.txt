Ryan prepared a simple but satisfying breakfast of pan-fried eggs with a lemon-herb pan sauce, served with whole-grain toast and fresh fruit.

The key ingredients were eggs, butter, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, parsley, salt, pepper, and whole-grain bread. He cracked the eggs into a hot pan, fried them until the whites were firm and the yolks were just set, then poured the pan sauce over them. The aroma of warm eggs, butter, garlic, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen as he turned off the heat and carefully arranged the eggs and toast on a wooden board.

The presentation was clean and inviting: golden-browned eggs arranged beside bright yellow toast, with a small colorful fruit piece and a sprig of parsley adding a pop of color. The meal was a relaxed Tuesday morning breakfast, designed to energize him before continuing his work on the mobile app and considering his flexible shift at The Rose and Crown Pub.
