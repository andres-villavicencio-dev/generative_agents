On Tuesday, February 14, Ryan prepared Korean-inspired Biryani for lunch. The main dish consisted of basmati rice, sliced red onions, green chilies, ginger, garlic, fresh cilantro, yogurt, and a rich spice blend of cumin, coriander, turmeric, cardamom, cinnamon, and garam masala. For flavor, he added chopped chicken and a couple of eggs, while a small pot of coconut milk and a handful of peas provided moisture and color.

The preparation began with sautéing the onions and spices in oil until fragrant, then cooking the rice until it reached the desired texture. The chicken was marinated briefly, sautéed, and added to the rice along with the eggs. The yogurt and peas were stirred in at the end, giving the dish a creamy yet vibrant finish.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with warm cumin, cardamom, toasted nuts, and savory spices. Ryan presented the biryani on a lightly oiled plate, garnishing it with sliced green onion and a few toasted pistachios. It was a simple, balanced lunch that reflected his pragmatic approach to daily life—efficient, nourishing, and ready to enjoy.
