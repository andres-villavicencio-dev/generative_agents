On Tuesday, February 14, Ryan prepared Roasted Chicken with Roasted Garlic and Herb Broth for lunch, treating the meal to the celebratory occasion of Valentine’s Day.

The key ingredients were a whole chicken, garlic, fresh rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, olive oil, chicken broth, and red pepper flakes. He roasted the chicken until the skin was golden and the meat was tender, then made a rich savory broth by simmering the bones, garlic, and herbs. The chicken was finished in the reserved broth, creating a bright, aromatic dish with the scent of roasted garlic, herbs, and the savory depth of chicken.

The chicken was presented on a white platter, garnished with lemon wedges, parsley, and a small sprig of rosemary, with a small bowl of lemon-herb broth and a red pepper-flake garnish nearby. It was a simple, elegant lunch intended to be both comforting and festive.
