On February 14, Ryan Park prepared a Roasted Vegetable & Herb Quinoa Bowl after returning from work. The meal featured cooked quinoa, roasted bell pepper, zucchini, red onion, cherry tomatoes, and feta cheese, with a drizzle of lemon-herb olive oil and a sprinkle of black pepper.

He toasted the vegetables in a pan with olive oil until they were caramelized, then mixed them with the quinoa, herbs, and feta. The warm, savory aroma of roasted vegetables and toasted herbs filled the kitchen, while the bright lemon and crisp feta added a refreshing contrast.

The presentation was simple but elegant: the colorful vegetables and creamy quinoa were arranged on a white plate, with the feta and lemon oil creating a warm, appetizing finish. Ryan planned it as a relaxed, home-style lunch to balance his work and enjoy a satisfying, practical meal with his family on a special occasion.
