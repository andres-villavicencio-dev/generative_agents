Ryan prepared Korean-Style Beef with Kimchi-Garlic Rice, a simple yet flavorful dinner that matched his pragmatic cooking style. The centerpiece was a thinly sliced beef steak marinated in soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a dash of sesame oil, then seared until the crust was crisp and the interior remained tender. Kimchi was added for its bright tang, and a small portion of freshly cooked rice was served alongside.

The kitchen smelled strongly of savory meat, fermented vegetables, and toasted sesame seeds. The beef was plated on a warm white plate, with the kimchi arranged alongside it and a few sprigs of green onions on top. The contrast between the rich, dark beef and the vivid green kimchi created an appetizing visual that made the meal feel both familiar and exciting.

It was his first truly complete dinner after his workday, and Ryan was glad he had prepared it ahead of Monday’s planned grocery shopping. The meal was satisfying, though he would have preferred a little more room in the refrigerator.
