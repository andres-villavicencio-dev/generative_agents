On Tuesday, February 14, Ryan prepared Ginger-Sesame Chicken and Rice, a warm, home-style meal he planned for Monday. The dish combines tender strips of chicken with rice, carrots, snap peas, and a savory ginger-sesame sauce. He sliced the chicken, marinated it with soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil, then pan-fried it until the skin was crisp and the meat was cooked through. The vegetables were stir-fried alongside the chicken before the sauce was added, allowing the flavors to meld.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the sharp scent of ginger, warm sesame, savory soy, and toasted garlic. Ryan presented the meal on a white rice plate, arranging the chicken and vegetables neatly around the center of the rice. He topped it with a few fresh green onions before serving.

The meal was a relaxed family dinner intended for a Tuesday lunchtime. Ryan prepared it quickly so he could continue his work on the mobile app, while also considering the flexible shift at The Rose and Crown Pub.
