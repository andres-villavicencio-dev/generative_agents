On Tuesday, February 14, Ryan prepared Garlic Butter Shrimp and Asparagus for lunch. He used a box of fresh shrimp, asparagus, cherry tomatoes, lemon, olive oil, garlic, and a pinch of paprika. The shrimp were peeled and cooked in a pan until translucent, while the asparagus was quickly blanched. The tomatoes were stirred in near the end to preserve their bright color.

The dish was finished by tossing everything with a warm garlic butter sauce and a squeeze of lemon. The aroma filled the kitchen with the sharp scent of garlic, the crisp smell of asparagus, and the subtle sweetness of the tomatoes.

Ryan presented the meal in a shallow white bowl, arranging the shrimp and vegetables in a neat spiral with extra lemon slices and a small herb garnish. It was a simple, practical lunch meant to give him energy for his mobile app and system work. The occasion was not celebratory, but it felt rewarding—a tidy, nourishing meal that mirrored the efficiency and attention to detail Ryan brought to every task.
