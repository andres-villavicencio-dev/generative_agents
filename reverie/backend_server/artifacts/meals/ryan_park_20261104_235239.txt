On February 14, Ryan prepared Miso Glazed Salmon with Rice and Veggies for a weekday dinner. He sourced the fish from The Rose and Crown Pub, checking both its online availability and delivery time, which arrived Tuesday evening. The meal featured salmon fillets, brown rice, steamed broccoli, snap peas, carrots, and a creamy miso-orange sauce, along with soy sauce and sesame seeds for balance.

Ryan marinated the salmon with ginger, garlic, soy sauce, and a dash of sesame oil before searing it over medium-high heat. He pan-seared the fish until it was golden and flaky, then added the rice to absorb the remaining sauce. The aromas of roasted ginger, garlic, and warm rice filled the kitchen, while the bright orange and green vegetables added an inviting visual contrast.

The dish was plated simply, with the salmon arranged on the side and a small portion of extra rice and chopped vegetables nearby. Ryan served it for supper, balancing his ongoing work on the mobile app with a practical, nourishing meal. It was a quiet, unobtrusive dinner—perfect for a Tuesday when he had both work and household responsibilities.
