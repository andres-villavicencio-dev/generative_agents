Ryan prepared a simple Greek-inspired yogurt breakfast for Tuesday, February 14. The dish was made with full-fat Greek yogurt, frozen berries, sliced banana, handful of almonds, chia seeds, and a drizzle of honey and lemon juice.

He prepared it in two parts. First, the yogurt was stirred with the frozen fruit and seeds, then refrigerated for about 30 minutes until the berries softened. The banana and honey were mixed in at the last minute to keep the yogurt cold and creamy. He topped it with sliced almonds and a few whole walnuts.

The kitchen smelled fresh and warm, with the sweet aroma of honey and berries mingling with the tangy scent of yogurt and nutty seeds. He presented the bowl in a simple white dish, arranging the colorful fruit, golden nuts, and dark seeds so the bright berries and creamy yellow yogurt stood out.

It was a practical breakfast that balanced energy with relaxation, fitting Ryan’s focus on improving systems while he reviewed technology and considered the flexible shift at The Rose and Crown Pub.
