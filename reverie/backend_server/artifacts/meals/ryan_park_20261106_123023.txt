Ryan Park prepared a Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Herb Butter for dinner on Tuesday, February 14. The main protein was a salmon fillet marinated in lemon, garlic, and rosemary, then pan-seared to a golden-rustic crisp. It was served over jasmine rice, garnished with fresh parsley, microgreens, and a drizzle of butter.

The preparation began with washing and drying the fish, followed by seasoning and brief marinating. The pan was heated and salmon seared until the skin turned crisp, then the rice was cooked separately and plated. Ryan finished the dish with the remaining herb butter and served it on a clean white plate, emphasizing the vibrant orange of the salmon against the pale rice and bright green garnish.

The aroma was fresh and aromatic, with the sharp scent of lemon, savory garlic, warm rosemary, and toasted butter. The meal was practical and satisfying, fitting Ryan’s analytical, pragmatic approach to cooking while supporting the work-life balance he had carefully structured for the day. It was a relaxed post-work dinner, allowing him to wind down after the demanding day of mobile app development and job applications.
