Ryan prepared Miso-Glazed Salmon with Quinoa Rice and Ginger-Soy Glazed Carrots. The dish was a balanced, lightly spicy side for lunch.

The quinoa rice was cooked with water until tender, then seasoned with salt, soy sauce, and a little sesame oil. Sliced salmon was pan-seared until the skin was crisp, then rested briefly to keep it moist. The carrots were sliced and roasted with ginger, garlic, and salt until slightly caramelized. The warm rice and glazed carrots were plated with the salmon, which was arranged beside them.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the sharpness of miso, the fragrance of ginger, and the subtle sweetness of caramelized carrots. The presentation was clean and modern: the salmon’s golden skin and bright orange carrot pieces stood out against the pale quinoa and dark rice.

The meal was a relaxed, nourishing lunch after a long day focused on the new mobile app project. It provided steady energy and a satisfying balance of protein, grains, and vegetables.
