For a Monday morning, Ryan Park prepared a simple but satisfying Korean-style Vegetable Gochujang Stir-Fry for breakfast. The dish featured crisp tofu, sliced radish, garlic, scallions, carrots, snap peas, and mushrooms, along with a vibrant red gochujang-based sauce and a splash of fish sauce.

The preparation was quick and methodical: he stir-fried the vegetables until they were crisp-tender, added the tofu and seasonings, and tossed everything together in the pan. The aromatics of garlic and ginger released their fragrance, while the gochujang imparted a savory, slightly spicy, earthy flavor.

The dish was presented in a small, clean white bowl, with the tofu and colorful vegetables clearly arranged around the glossy sauce. A small side of steamed rice or toast completed the meal. It was a casual, practical breakfast—designed to energize Ryan’s focus before a long day of software development.
