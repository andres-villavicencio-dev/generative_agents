Meal: Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken with Quinoa and Roasted Vegetables

Key Ingredients:
Boneless, skinless chicken breasts
Quinoa
Carrots, bell peppers, and onions
Lemon, garlic, olive oil, salt, and pepper

Preparation Method:
Ryan washed and sliced the chicken, then seared it in a skillet until golden and cooked through. He roasted the vegetables with onions and peppers in the same pan, adding lemon juice and garlic for brightness. He prepared the quinoa by cooking it with water, then fluffed it with a fork.

Aroma and Presentation:
The kitchen filled with the warm, savory scent of garlic, herbs, and roasted vegetables. The meal was plated in a shallow bowl with the chicken arranged in the center, surrounded by the colorful vegetables and fluffy quinoa. Lemon zest was sprinkled over the top for a final burst of freshness.

Occasion:
This was a simple, filling lunch before Ryan’s evening dinner and the meeting with Arthur Burton. Ryan’s analytical approach translated into a straightforward approach to the meal: using the available ingredients efficiently and preparing the dish quickly so he could focus on the app and his conversation with Arthur.
