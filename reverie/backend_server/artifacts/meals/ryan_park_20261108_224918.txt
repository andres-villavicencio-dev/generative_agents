Ryan prepared a simple Hearty Chicken Soup with Roasted Root Vegetables, a warming, nourishing dinner perfect for sharing with Arthur.

The meal consisted of chicken thighs, carrots, potatoes, onion, garlic, and a splash of chicken broth, finished with fresh parsley and lemon. Ryan seared the chicken until golden, then braised it with the vegetables and aromatics in the pan, letting the flavors meld during the final simmer. The soup was ladled into a shallow bowl, garnished with parsley, and topped with a few crisp roasted potatoes.

Aromatic notes of roasted root vegetables, caramelized garlic, and savory chicken filled the kitchen. The presentation was rustic and inviting: warm golden-brown chicken, bright orange vegetables, and a lightly lemony broth created a visually pleasing contrast.

It was an informal evening-meeting meal—informal, practical, and thoughtfully prepared—designed to make Ryan and Arthur comfortable for a drink and conversation about the clinic’s story and the neighborhood’s character.
