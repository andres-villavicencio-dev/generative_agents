Ryan prepared Pan-Seared Salmon with Asparagus and Lemon-Garlic Butter. He chose a lean salmon fillet, seasoned it with salt, pepper, and lemon juice, then seared it in a hot pan until the skin was crisp and the flesh was opaque. He added asparagus spears, roasted until tender, and finished the dish with a pan of garlic-infused butter to give it a golden sheen.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted garlic and ocean fish, while the bright citrus notes balanced the rich seafood. Ryan arranged the salmon and asparagus in a shallow dish, drizzling melted butter over the top and garnishing with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed dinner on Monday, February 13, after a long day of work. It was a simple yet satisfying evening meal, chosen to provide energy without feeling overly indulgent.
