Ryan Park prepared Braised Pork with Kimchi and Rice, a hearty Korean-inspired meal suitable for a weekday dinner. The dish features lean pork belly marinated in soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a dash of oyster sauce, then simmered until tender. It is served over rice with kimchi, scallions, and a side of steamed vegetables.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized pork and warm spices, while the bright yellow kimchi and green herbs added a lively, refreshing contrast. The golden-brown pork glistened with sauce, surrounded by fluffy rice and crisp vegetables.

The meal was the occasion of a relaxed, practical evening: Ryan had finished a project, wanted to nourish himself before bed, and spent the remaining time quietly reading about new mobile technologies while cooking. The simplicity and balance of the dish reflected his analytical, pragmatic approach to both technology and daily life.
