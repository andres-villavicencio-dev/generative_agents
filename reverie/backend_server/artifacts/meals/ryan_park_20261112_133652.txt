Ryan prepared Pan-Seared Scallops with Lemon-Herb Butter, a simple yet elegant dinner for Tuesday, February 14. He chose fresh sea scallops, garlic, shallots, lemon zest, capers, thyme, and a pinch of salt. He sliced the scallops and seared them in a hot pan with olive oil until golden, then added the shallots and cooked them briefly. The garlic and herbs were sautéed until fragrant, followed by the capers and a spoonful of butter that had been prepared earlier.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of garlic, sea salt, butter, and the delicate scent of lemon. He plated the scallops in a shallow pan with their juices, serving them alongside roasted potatoes and a small portion of brown rice. The dish was simple, visually appealing, and balanced—bright yellow scallops, soft potatoes, and creamy brown rice creating a comforting, family-style meal.

It was a relaxed Tuesday lunch, not a formal occasion. Ryan was focused on the new mobile app, his IT certification, and the math problem at Hobbs Café, but the meal provided a quiet break from his busy day. The warm kitchen atmosphere and the satisfying aroma of garlic and butter reminded him that even in a productive week, a simple meal can bring comfort.
