Ryan Park prepared a hearty Pan-Sear Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for breakfast. The dish featured chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, onions, garlic, and a splash of orange juice, all seasoned with salt, pepper, paprika, and a little lemon zest.

He sautéed the chicken in a pan until it browned and cooked through, then added the root vegetables and garlic to roast until the carrots became tender and the potatoes softened. A final splash of juice and a pinch of sugar helped the vegetables caramelize without burning.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and the rich scent of chicken. Ryan plated the meal in a shallow dish, arranging the vegetables in a colorful arc and placing the chicken in the center. A small lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley were added as a finishing touch.

It was an early-morning breakfast for work, balanced by the need for energy during the day.
