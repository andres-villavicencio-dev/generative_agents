Sam’s Saturday dinner is a simple yet comforting Chicken‑and‑Potato Pot Pie, served in a cast‑iron skillet. The crust is made from 2 cups of buttery flour, milk, and cold butter, rolled out and topped with golden edges; inside, tender chicken thigh pieces are simmered together with diced potatoes, peas, and a splash of cream, creating a creamy, savory filling. When the pan hits the stove, the aroma is warm and buttery, drifting through his living room like a cozy hug. The dish arrives steaming, its bubbling surface glistening under soft lamplight; steam curls around his reading‑laced glasses of tea. It’s a quiet celebration of home—comfort food for a calm evening after the day’s gentle rhythm.
