Sam prepared a Smoked Salmon and Herb Crostini, a simple, elegant side dish perfect for a quiet Friday dinner. Fresh salmon fillets were lightly seasoned with lemon juice, garlic, and dried dill, then arranged neatly on a platter alongside crisp vegetables. Toasting bread, sliced tomatoes, and cucumber in a skillet created golden, fragrant edges. The dish was drizzled with olive oil and finished with a pinch of salt and pepper.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the earthy scent of smoked salmon, bright lemon, garlic, and toasted vegetables. Sam presented the platter with a silver spoon and a few small garnish garnishes, making it look inviting and balanced. After preparing the side, he sat down with Jennifer and checked that the salmon was still warm while discussing the upcoming mayoral campaign with his neighbors. Dinner was a peaceful evening of shared meals and thoughtful conversation.
