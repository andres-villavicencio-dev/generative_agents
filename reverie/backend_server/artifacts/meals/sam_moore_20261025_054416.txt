The meal was Garlic-Lemon Pasta with Fresh Herbs and Lemon Cream. It was prepared for Tuesday, February 14, a special evening to celebrate Jennifer’s exhibition and the occasion’s romantic tone.

Sam chopped fresh garlic into small pieces and sautéed it with shallots and a pinch of salt until fragrant. He added olive oil, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice, then sautéed the vegetables and pasta until the sauce had a silky texture. He finished it with a little flour to thicken the sauce, serving it with a small bowl of lemon cream and chopped parsley.

The aroma was unmistakable: warm garlic, bright lemon, and the sharp scent of herbs. Each strand of pasta was coated in the glossy sauce, while the herbs provided a cheerful, festive finish.

It was a simple, elegant dinner meant to create a warm, intimate atmosphere for the evening. Sam’s cheerful preparation reflected his relaxed, resourceful side, while the garlic and lemon celebrated the occasion’s romantic mood.
