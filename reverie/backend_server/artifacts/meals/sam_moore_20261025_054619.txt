Dish: Garlic-Lemon Pasta with Fresh Herbs

Key ingredients: long strands of spaghetti or penne, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, parsley, basil, and a pinch of salt.

Preparation method: Sam salted a large pot of water and set it on the stove. While it heated, he peeled garlic cloves, chopped the lemon, and washed the vegetables. Once the pasta was al dente, he drained it and added it to the simmering water. He tossed the pasta with the garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, and fresh herbs, then served it immediately.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, citrus zest, and herbs. The bright lemon added a lively, fresh flavor, while the gentle heat of the pasta made every bite feel comforting.

For Tuesday, February 14, he prepared the meal while helping Jennifer with the exhibition and exploring Johnson Park with her, discussing Giorgio’s findings. The occasion was special: a quiet, personal celebration of his life and the people he loved.
