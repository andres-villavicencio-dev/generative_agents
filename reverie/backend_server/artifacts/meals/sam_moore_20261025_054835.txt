Dish: Garlic-Lemon Pasta with Fresh Lemon Butter  
Key Ingredients: spaghetti, olive oil, garlic, fresh lemon, Parmesan cheese, parsley, black pepper, and a splash of lemon juice.

Preparation Method: Sam heated a pot of water, adding the pasta until it was nearly done. In the meantime, he sautéed minced garlic in olive oil until fragrant, then poured in the lemon juice and chopped lemon. Parmesan and parsley were stirred in, followed by the cooked pasta. The pan was covered briefly, then opened, and the mixture was tossed with a little butter until the pasta was al dente and the sauce had thickened into a bright, zesty dressing.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the sharp scent of lemons, toasted garlic, and warm butter.

Presentation: Sam arranged the pasta in a shallow bowl, drizzling the garlicky lemon sauce over it and sprinkling generous flakes of Parmesan and a few fresh herbs.

Occasion: It was a special Tuesday for dinner, while Sam prepared ingredients for Jennifer’s exhibition and planned a brief visit to Johnson Park. The meal was a quiet, cheerful tribute to his wife’s birthday, balancing his creative work with his military background and the gentle curiosity he shared with Giorgio.
