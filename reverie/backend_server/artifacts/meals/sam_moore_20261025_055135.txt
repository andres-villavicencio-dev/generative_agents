Dish: Garlic-Lemon Pasta with Parmesan Crust

Sam prepared garlic-lemon pasta for Tuesday’s family dinner, a simple but flavorful dish celebrating February 14. He used penne pasta, plenty of olive oil, minced garlic, fresh lemon, Parmesan cheese, and a pinch of salt. He sautéed the garlic in olive oil until fragrant, added the pasta, and tossed it with lemon juice, Parmesan, and parsley before serving.

The aroma was earthy and bright, with the sharp zest of lemon cutting through the rich garlic and warm Parmesan. He carefully adjusted the salt and lemon, tasting the sauce and ensuring the garlic remained tender while the Parmesan stayed firm. The pasta was plated in a shallow dish, generously topped with grated Parmesan and fresh herbs, giving the dish an elegant, rustic appearance.

The meal was prepared for a quiet family dinner on Tuesday, February 14. Sam had set out the ingredients in advance so he could focus on tasting the sauce and making small adjustments, reflecting his resourceful, patient nature.
