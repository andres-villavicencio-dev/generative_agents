Sam prepared garlic-lemon pasta for Tuesday, February 14, as part of his preparations for an exhibition and a celebration with Jennifer. The dish featured long strands of spaghetti, a rich garlic-based sauce, finely chopped lemons, olive oil, fresh parsley, and a light pinch of Parmesan cheese.

He sautéed the garlic in oil until fragrant, then added the pasta and lemon. The sauce became creamy and aromatic, with the garlic releasing its warm scent as the pasta absorbed the flavors. He finished it with a little extra lemon zest and a generous drizzle of olive oil.

On the table, the pasta was arranged in neat, parallel strands, with a small side dish of roasted vegetables beside it. The bright yellow of the lemons, the golden brown garlic, and the creamy sauce created an inviting, warm presentation. The occasion was an intimate Sunday lunch, a quiet celebration of a shared meal while Sam helped organize the exhibition and discussed his time in the military with Jennifer.
