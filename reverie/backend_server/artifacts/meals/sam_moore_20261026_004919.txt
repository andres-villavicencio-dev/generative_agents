Sam prepared a simple but warmly comforting Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a light, nostalgic lunch meant to match the peaceful afternoon he was exploring Johnson Park.

The chicken breast was seasoned with garlic, dried oregano, lemon juice, salt, and pepper, then marinated and grilled over a medium heat until the skin became golden and crisp. He roasted carrots, zucchini, and potatoes nearby, tossing them with olive oil, lemon zest, and a pinch of cumin. The vegetables softened gently while retaining a bright, herbaceous flavor.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of warm garlic, lemon, toasted bread, and savory herbs. Sam plated the chicken in a wide white bowl, arranging the roasted vegetables around it and garnishing with fresh parsley. He added a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing and toasted whole-grain crackers beside the meal.

It was a relaxed lunch for Sam, prepared after a full day of purposeful activity and quiet exploration. The dish was simple and practical, yet the comforting smell and warm presentation made it feel inviting—perfect for a leisurely afternoon.
