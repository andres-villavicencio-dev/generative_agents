Sam prepared Sunday Pot Roast with Root Vegetables and Gravy, a comforting dinner for his wife and two grandchildren. He began by washing his hands and preparing the kitchen, then sliced a whole lamb roast into thin, even pieces. He seared the meat first to develop a golden crust, then browned the root vegetables and carrots before adding a generous splash of beef broth. The roast simmered covered in the flavorful gravy, while potatoes, carrots, and onions absorbed the rich sauce.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized lamb and simmering vegetables. When Sam served the meal, the pot roast nestled in a shallow bowl, surrounded by bright orange carrots and golden potatoes, with the glossy gravy pooling around it. He placed a small white plate in front of each guest, and each received a serving of the hearty stew.

The occasion was a warm family dinner on February 15, following Sam’s purposeful Wednesday in Johnson Park. The meal was simple yet satisfying, reflecting his steady routine and the grateful connections he was building—especially with Jennifer and Giorgio.
