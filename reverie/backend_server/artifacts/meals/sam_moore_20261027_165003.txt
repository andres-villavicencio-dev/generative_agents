Sam prepared a simple, comforting dinner of Herb-Crusted Salmon with Lemon Garlic Butter for an early Wednesday dinner at home. He gathered fresh salmon fillets, baby spinach, lemon wedges, garlic, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg from the kitchen counter.

He gently seared the salmon over medium-high heat, allowing the skin to crisp before finishing it in the pan with butter and lemon. The spinach was wilted quickly in the pan, with the garlic released its aroma as the fish cooked. Finally, he spooned the golden, herb-filled pan sauce over the salmon and piled the bright greens on top.

The meal smelled warm, fresh, and inviting—the sharp scent of lemon, garlic, sea salt, and butter filling the kitchen. It was a relaxed, informal dinner, likely meant for a quiet evening after work, with Sam’s wife and children nearby.
