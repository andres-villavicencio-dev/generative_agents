Sam prepared a warm Old-Fashioned Blackberry and Blueberry Breakfast for himself at the Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured ripe blueberries, blackberries, and sliced strawberries tossed with a little honey, topped with toasted whole-grain toast and a generous drizzle of maple syrup. For a quick, comforting breakfast, he added a small handful of blueberries, a few raspberries, and a few plump blackberries.

He began by lightly heating butter and a splash of milk in a small pan, then carefully incorporated the berries. The mixture was gently stirred, then poured over the toast. Sam toasted the toast until it was golden but not burned, then added the berries and a pinch of cinnamon.

The aroma filled the café with the sweet, tart fragrance of berries and toasted grain. He plated the breakfast in a simple white bowl, with the toast and berries arranged neatly beside a small cup of tea. It was a relaxed, nostalgic treat—the kind of simple breakfast a retired officer might prepare on a quiet Wednesday morning, grateful for the connection he was building with Jennifer and Giorgio.
