Sam prepared a hearty Grandma’s Chicken and Potato Breakfast Sandwich at Hobbs Cafe. He sliced a whole chicken, shredded it with diced potatoes, onions, carrots, tomato, and a generous amount of buttered breadcrumbs, then arranged the filling on two sturdy slices of toasted bread. He added a sunny-side-down egg, fresh lettuce, and a warm slice of tomato on top.

The kitchen smelled warmly of buttered bread, caramelized onions, and savory chicken. Sam made the sandwich on the counter, adding a little extra sauce and a final drizzle of lemon juice. When he served it, he arranged the golden-brown bread and colorful toppings in neat rows, with the egg and lettuce peeking from beneath.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday at Hobbs Cafe, where Sam shared the meal with neighbors and enjoyed his favorite reading spot before heading to the park. It was a simple, satisfying breakfast that reflected Sam’s relaxed, resourceful nature.
