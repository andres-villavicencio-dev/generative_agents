Sam prepared a Citrus and Herb Breakfast Bowl, a simple yet hearty morning meal. The dish featured steamed eggs, crisp orange and grapefruit segments, roasted carrots, kale, a handful of almonds, and a splash of lemon juice. He seasoned the eggs with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a pinch of garlic powder, then topped the bowl with a small drizzle of honey.

The ingredients were prepared quickly: the eggs were fried in a hot skillet until firm, while the carrots and kale were sautéed briefly in the same pan. A few lemon wedges were squeezed over the eggs, and the bowl was garnished with the colorful fruit and toasted almonds.

Aromatic, fresh, and bright, the meal filled the kitchen with the scent of citrus, toasted nuts, and the savory aroma of egg. Sam arranged the bowl on a bright white plate, adding a small bowl of yogurt and a sprig of mint for extra color. He ate it at home on Tuesday, February 14, as part of his calm Tuesday routine while preparing for his upcoming mayoral campaign.
