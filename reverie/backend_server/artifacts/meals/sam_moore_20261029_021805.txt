Sam prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables as the centerpiece of his Tuesday evening meal. The dish featured a whole chicken seasoned with garlic, lemon, dried rosemary, and paprika, alongside carrots, potatoes, and green beans cooked with onion and a small amount of butter. Sam stirred the chicken frequently until the meat was evenly browned and the sauce thickened, while adjusting the heat to keep the vegetables crisp-tender.

The kitchen warmed with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and caramelized onions. When the chicken finished, it was plucked and rested before being carved and served alongside the vegetables, which were garnished with parsley and a drizzle of lemon juice.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday dinner, but Sam treated it as a special occasion. He carried the tray to the kitchen table and served it with a cup of coffee, while Jennifer Moore and Jane joined him for a conversation. Sitting quietly with the family, Sam enjoyed the warm food and the sense of connection, using the meal as a small reminder that his civic responsibilities and personal life are both worth honoring.
