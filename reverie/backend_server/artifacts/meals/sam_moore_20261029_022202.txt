Sam prepared a simple Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and Potatoes, the centerpiece of his Tuesday evening meal. The key ingredients were fresh carrots, parsnips, red potatoes, garlic, rosemary, thyme, olive oil, butter, and a splash of red wine.

He washed the root vegetables, peeled the potatoes, and sliced the carrots and parsnips. He placed them in a large roasting pan, added the garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper, and covered everything with butter and the wine. The pan was roasted in the oven until the potatoes were tender and the vegetables turned golden. A generous knob of butter and the reserved juices were poured over the finished dish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory, earthy notes of roasted root vegetables, garlic, herbs, and a hint of wine. Sam plated the roast in a shallow baking dish, arranging the golden vegetables around the center and serving a small portion of pan juices over the potatoes. He added a warm crusty bread and a crisp green salad for a side.

The occasion was Sam’s Sunday dinner—a quiet, relaxed evening of cooking, conversation, and reflection. It was also part of his daily routine when preparing for the upcoming mayoral election.
