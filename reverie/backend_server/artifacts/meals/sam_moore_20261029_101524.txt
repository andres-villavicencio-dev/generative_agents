On Thursday, February 16, Sam prepared a hearty, family-style breakfast of smashed egg scramble with whole-grain toast, oatmeal, and fresh fruit. He gathered his ingredients from the pantry, fridge, and freezer, carefully choosing egg whites, spinach, tomatoes, cheese, and a couple of whole-grain slices.

The eggs were cracked into a skillet and scrambled gently with the spinach and cheese until golden, then folded into the oatmeal and fruit. A spoonful of toast was brushed with butter before the scramble, and a bowl of warm milk with honey was ready for the children.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of eggs, toasted bread, and sweet fruit. He arranged the breakfast on a simple white tablecloth, adding a small bowl of orange juice and a cup of tea beside the food. The meal was a relaxed, cheerful gathering—perhaps his first chance to prepare breakfast while preparing for his upcoming term as local mayor.
