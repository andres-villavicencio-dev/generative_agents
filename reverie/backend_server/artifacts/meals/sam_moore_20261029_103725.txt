Sam prepared Morning Pot and Bread, a simple but warmly nourishing breakfast for himself and Jennifer. He layered sliced eggs, crisp bacon, shredded cheddar, and a creamy cheese sauce over freshly toasted sourdough, with a bowl of warm oatmeal and a small bowl of fresh fruit nearby.

The ingredients came from a small pantry: eggs, bacon, cheddar, butter, sourdough, fruit, and a little milk. He fried the eggs in a pan until golden, sliced the bacon, and melted the cheese over the bread. Sam stirred the eggs carefully to avoid breaking them, then added the cheddar and butter for a rich, golden finish.

As he ate, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of bacon and toasted bread, along with the gentle scent of milk and fruit. He arranged the plate neatly beside a glass of water, presenting it as a quiet, nourishing meal—a relaxed way to start the day. The occasion was simple: a peaceful breakfast for two on a quiet Thursday morning.
