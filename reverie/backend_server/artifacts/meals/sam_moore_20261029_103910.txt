On a quiet Thursday morning, Sam prepared a hearty Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Fresh Herbs. He used a whole chicken, garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon, olive oil, honey, carrots, potatoes, onions, and baby spinach. After washing and drying the chicken, he seasoned it generously, placed it skin-side down in a roasting pan, and set it over a medium heat. While it basted with its own juices, he tossed the vegetables in a pan with a little oil until they were tender.

When the chicken began to brown, he opened the oven and finished it until the skin was golden and the meat was cooked through. He served the chicken on a large platter, arranging the golden vegetables and bright green spinach around it. A lemon wedge and fresh herbs were placed beside the platter for guests to enjoy.

The aroma of roasted garlic, rosemary, and citrus filled the kitchen as Sam turned the oven. The meal was simple but comforting, fitting Sam’s relaxed, humorous style. It was a quiet Sunday lunch, prepared before he headed out to the local supermarket to load the car and go shopping.
