Sam prepared a hearty Sunday Gravy Potato and Vegetable Bake. The dish came together quickly on a weekday when he was stretching between civic duties, Jennifer’s exhibition, and neighborhood conversations.

He started with a pot of brown gravy, using a can of cream of mushroom soup as a quick base. The vegetables—onions, carrots, celery, and baby potatoes—were diced and sautéed briefly in a skillet. The canned goods added a savory, golden depth. Fresh parsley and a pinch of thyme were stirred in for color and aroma.

The mixture was transferred to a baking dish and topped with the remaining potatoes, cheese, and fresh chives. It was covered with foil, baked at 400°F until golden and bubbling, then removed and broiled for a few minutes to crisp the edges.

The smell was warm and comforting, with savory onions, earthy carrots, and a hint of mushroom. He arranged the potatoes and vegetables in an inviting, rustic loaf, garnishing the center with the bright parsley and adding a small sprig of rosemary for visual appeal.

He plated it on a wooden board for dinner, making it look like a comforting Sunday meal for himself or his friends. The occasion was simple: a relaxed weekday dinner, but with pride in the effort he put into the dish while balancing his civic and family responsibilities.
