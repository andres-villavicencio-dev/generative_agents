On a quiet Thursday morning, Sam prepared Roasted Vegetable Risotto with Lemon-Herb Cream. He chose a simple, nourishing combination of baby spinach, carrots, zucchini, garlic, thyme, and parmesan for his 65-year-old mother-in-law, while his own favorite were the creamy rice and crisp carrots.

He washed the vegetables, sautéed them briefly, then added Arborio rice and slowly toasted it in olive oil over low heat, stirring until it absorbed the liquid. He added vegetable broth and parmesan, allowing the grains to absorb the broth while slowly releasing their flavors. For the finish, he reduced the pan for a golden crust, then finished it with a lemon-herb cream and fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted rice, garlic, thyme, and citrus. Sam plated the dish in a shallow bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the creamy rice and garnishing it with a small sprig of parsley. He served it with crusty bread, a simple vinaigrette, and a glass of wine, enjoying the satisfying meal at the park. It was a peaceful, family-style dinner, balanced with civic duties and a visit to the local grocery store.
