Sam prepared Grilled Lemon-Herb Chicken with Sweet Potato and Roasted Vegetables.

He used a whole chicken, skin-on, with bone-in sweet potatoes, carrots, onions, bell peppers, and a bouquet garni. The chicken was seasoned with olive oil, garlic, dried oregano, lemon zest, and salt, then seared until the golden skin became crisp. While it cooked, he roasted the vegetables with olive oil, red pepper flakes, and a splash of lemon juice. A simple garlic-herb butter was made to finish the chicken.

The kitchen filled with the savory smell of caramelized chicken, roasted potatoes, garlic, and citrus. The chicken sat golden and juicy, while the vegetables were soft and colorful. They were arranged on a platter with the sweet potatoes arranged around it, garnished with parsley.

He prepared the meal for his Friday family dinner—a relaxed evening to share with Jennifer, Jane, and the neighborhood friends after a busy week. Sam also wanted to test his new mayoral campaign ideas, so he chose a familiar, comforting dish that could satisfy everyone at home.
