Sam prepared a Hearty Garden Harvest Bowl, a warm, colorful meal for Thursday’s family breakfast. The dish combined smashed potatoes, roasted sweet potatoes, seasonal greens, kale, diced ham, cheddar, and a creamy garlic‑herb sauce, with crispy bacon and fresh berries for a balanced, satisfying brunch.

He sliced the potatoes until they softened, tossed the vegetables with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and cooked the potatoes with a little butter over low heat. After finishing the sauce, he added the bacon and cheese, then arranged the bowl on a wooden table with the colorful fruits and greens arranged around the potatoes.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm bacon, golden roasted potatoes, and savory herbs, while the bright colors reminded Sam of the garden he loved. This was a simple, comforting meal for Thursday’s quiet morning—a relaxed way to spend time with his family before heading toward the park cleanup, conversations with his neighbors, and preparing for his upcoming term as mayor.
