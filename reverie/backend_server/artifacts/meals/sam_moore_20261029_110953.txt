Sam prepared Smoked Salmon with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Lemon Butter for a quiet Thursday evening. He used smoked salmon, roasted carrots, broccoli, red cabbage, and potatoes, garnishing them with capers and fresh dill. He marinated the vegetables in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a little butter, then roasted them until crisp and lightly browned before mixing them with the warm salmon and a simple lemon dressing.

Aromatic, earthy, and bright, the scent rose from the warm pan as Sam stirred the vegetables and added the salmon. The golden vegetables, crisp as crisp celery, contrasted with the tender, slightly oily salmon, while the lemon gave the dish a zesty brightness.

He presented it in a shallow ceramic bowl, with the vegetables arranged in an arc around the salmon and garnish scattered artistically. He served it with two slices of crusty bread, a small bowl of lemon juice, and a glass of sparkling water. The occasion was a peaceful family dinner—simple, nourishing, and full of the warmth of connection between Sam and Jennifer.
