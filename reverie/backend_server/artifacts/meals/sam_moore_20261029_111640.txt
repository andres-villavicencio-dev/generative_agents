Sam prepared Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for Thursday, February 16. The dish was simple yet elegant: lean chicken thighs, garlic, rosemary, lemon zest, olive oil, and a touch of Dijon, alongside carrots, onions, bell peppers, and baby spinach.

He marinated the chicken in the morning, then grilled it over medium heat until the skin was crisp and the meat reached a perfect medium. While it cooked, he roasted the vegetables in the oven until tender and slightly caramelized. He served the chicken and vegetables on a wide plate, arranging the carrots and peppers in a rustic row and placing the chicken on the center. A bright lemon wedge rested beside the chicken, and a small bowl of lemon-herb yogurt dressing was placed nearby.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of garlic, rosemary, and toasted vegetables. Sam’s wise, humorous personality made the meal feel comforting and celebratory—appropriate for the occasion: a quiet family dinner before an important civic day, including his upcoming term as local mayor. The meal was simple, nourishing, and full of the energy he had carried all day.
