Sam prepared Roasted Vegetable Soup for a quiet Thursday morning. He used carrots, celery, onions, garlic, potatoes, and a few cups of fresh spinach, adding a splash of vegetable broth and a pinch of salt before heating it gently.

He measured and chopped the vegetables carefully, washing them thoroughly and setting aside the tougher greens. While the soup simmered, he toasted slices of sourdough and toasted some buttered almonds for a small breakfast with his coffee. The finished soup had a warm, savory aroma of roasted root vegetables, garlic, and herbs.

Sam plated it in a simple white bowl, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a few whole almonds. He served it with the bread, enjoying a slow, comforting meal while reflecting on his recent civic work and conversation with Jennifer. The meal felt neither rushed nor overly formal—it was a simple, satisfying breakfast for a busy day.
