Sam prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and a Garden Salad for an early Sunday breakfast.

The dish featured a whole chicken seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic, thyme, rosemary, and a touch of lemon, with the skin browning in a shallow pan. He added potatoes, carrots, and onions to the pan, then tossed the vegetables with olive oil, salt, and pepper. A crisp garden salad of lettuce, carrots, and a lemon-herb dressing was served alongside the chicken.

The kitchen smelled richly of roasted garlic, herbs, and caramelized onions. Sam placed the golden-brown chicken on a platter, surrounded it with bright green vegetables, and arranged the salad beside it. He served a small bowl of warm lemon-herb potatoes and a side of crisp greens.

The meal was a simple, hearty breakfast meant to energize him for the busy days ahead. It was neither a grand feast nor an elaborate lunch—it was a modest Sunday morning meal that reflected Sam’s practical, homey style.
