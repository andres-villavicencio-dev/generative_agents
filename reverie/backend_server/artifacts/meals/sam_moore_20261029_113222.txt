Sam prepared Mediterranean Grains and Legume Bowl for Thursday’s civic gathering. The dish featured quinoa, brown rice, lentils, chickpeas, spinach, cucumbers, tomatoes, feta, and olive oil, with a lemon-herb dressing.

He measured the grains and legumes by cup, then rinsed them and simmered the lentils separately until tender. The grains were toasted in a pan with olive oil and chopped herbs, while the legumes were cooked in a pot with water and a pinch of salt. Fresh parsley and oregano were stirred in at the end. A small pot of broth was used to create a flavorful dipping sauce, and chopped nuts were sprinkled over the finished bowl.

The aroma filled the kitchen with toasted grains, earthy lentils, fresh herbs, and the warm scent of lemon. Sam presented the bowl in a simple white dish, arranging the colorful ingredients in neat rows and placing a lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley beside it.

This was a simple, nourishing meal shared with friends and neighbors at the community gathering. The communal atmosphere made Sam’s wise, humorous stories about his time in the navy feel more meaningful, as he had promised to help Jennifer with her exhibition while maintaining his civic commitments.
