Sam prepared Chicken and Root Vegetable Stew for dinner on Thursday, February 16. Key ingredients included chicken breasts, carrots, potatoes, celery, onions, tomato sauce, garlic, paprika, salt, pepper, and fresh herbs.

After washing the vegetables, Sam chopped the carrots and celery into bite-sized pieces, then sliced the chicken into even strips. He seared the chicken in a pan until golden, then added the vegetables and sauce to the pot. The chicken was basted with the juices, and everything was simmered until the carrots were tender and the potatoes were soft. A pinch of paprika and fresh thyme finished the dish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm, savory notes of garlic, caramelized onions, and herbaceous smoke. The stew was served over a bed of white rice, with the chicken resting on the side and the vegetables garnished with parsley. It was a simple, hearty meal suitable for an evening after a demanding day, balanced by civic work and conversations with his neighbors.
