Sam prepared a simple, hearty Bread and Egg Toast with Tomato and Cheese, the perfect quick breakfast to solve his running out of bread and eggs situation.

He toasted two slices of whole-grain bread, then cracked two eggs into a pan with a pinch of salt and a splash of milk. The golden-yellow egg mixture was stirred until the whites were set and the yolks were soft, then slid onto the warm bread. A generous slice of melted cheddar was added, followed by a handful of fresh tomatoes and a drizzle of olive oil.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of toasted bread, steaming egg, and melted cheese. Sam served it on a white plate, garnished with a slice of lemon and a sprig of parsley, and toasted a small bagel beside it. The meal was bright, comforting, and ready for a quick mid-morning snack.

It was the kind of simple breakfast Sam loved preparing for Jennifer—easy, nourishing, and cheerful enough to lift her spirits for her exhibition. He ate it with a satisfied smile, knowing he’d returned home with enough energy to help her, then enjoy a quiet moment with his neighbor.
