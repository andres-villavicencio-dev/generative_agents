Sam prepared Grain & Vegetable Soup with Smoked Salmon. The dish was cooked over a small stove, using water to heat grains, carrots, peas, potatoes, onions, celery, and canned fish. He added smoked salmon, a splash of lemon juice, and a pinch of salt, then simmered everything gently until the grains were tender and the soup was warm.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of sautéed carrots, celery, and onions, mingling with the smoky, oceanic scent of the salmon. Sam served the soup in a simple white bowl, garnished with chopped parsley and a bright lemon wedge.

The occasion was Sam’s Friday lunch for Jennifer Moore. He prepared it with care, balancing his weekday civic duties with family and community responsibilities while offering it as a thoughtful, comforting meal.
