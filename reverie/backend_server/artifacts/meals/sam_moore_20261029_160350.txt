Sam prepared a simple, nourishing lunch of Chicken and Vegetable Pan-Sear with Lemon Herb Marinade.

He began by sautéing diced carrots and onions in oil until they softened, then added chicken thighs cut into bite-sized pieces. The chicken was cooked until the flesh turned brown and tender, with the vegetables retaining their bright colors. A small amount of lemon juice, chopped garlic, and fresh herbs were stirred in at the end to finish the dish.

The aroma rose strongly from the pan as the chicken glistened and sizzled. He plated it in a shallow skillet, arranging the chicken and colorful vegetables around a small piece of bread, and drizzed extra lemon juice over the top.

The meal was a relaxed family lunch, prepared early in the week so Sam could share a hearty, easy-to-cook meal with his wife, Jennifer. It reflected his calm, resourceful side and the kind of simple, satisfying comfort he shares with his family.
