Dish Name: Smoky Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Crispy Potato Wedges

Sam prepared a simple, warming lunch for himself after checking that the oven had reached the right temperature and adjusting the heat to brown the remaining potatoes evenly. He sliced a whole chicken into thirds, seasoned it with smoked paprika, thyme, garlic, and a squeeze of lemon, then arranged it alongside carrots, potatoes, and onions. He seared the chicken first, then roasted everything in the oven until the meat was tender and the vegetables were golden.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, thyme, and caramelized potatoes. On the counter, the golden chicken, crisp vegetables, and slightly crisp potato wedges were arranged in a shallow dish, with Sam’s handwritten notes beside them. This was a quiet Friday lunch—simple, practical, and fitting for a day of civic responsibilities, family visits, and exploring the neighborhood parks.
