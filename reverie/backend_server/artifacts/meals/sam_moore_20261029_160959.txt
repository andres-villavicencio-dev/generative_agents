Sam prepared a Buddha Bowl with Roast Chicken, Quinoa, and Lemon-Tahini Salad for a simple, family-friendly lunch. The bowl held warm quinoa, chickpeas, crisp broccoli, roasted chicken, and crisp leafy greens, with a tangy lemon-tahini dressing.

He cooked the chicken in a skillet with olive oil, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice until golden and tender, then sliced it and served it on a bed of greens. The quinoa was rinsed, lightly toasted, and mixed with chopped vegetables and the dressing. Sam stirred everything together at the end, letting the flavors meld and the broth thicken naturally.

Aromatic rosemary and garlic filled the kitchen as he stirred, while the golden chicken and bright, crunchy vegetables added a cheerful aroma to the warm room. He plated the bowl in a shallow white dish, garnishing it with a few lemon slices and a sprig of parsley.

The meal was prepared for an easy weekday lunch, but Sam wanted it to feel special. He added a final stir and served it alongside a crisp apple and a small piece of honeycomb, turning a simple dinner into a comforting, nourishing treat.
