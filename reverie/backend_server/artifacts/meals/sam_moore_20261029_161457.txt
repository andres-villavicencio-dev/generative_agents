On Friday, February 17, Sam prepared Chicken and Kale Hash with Toasted Potatoes, a simple, hearty lunch to serve as an afterthought for the week’s leftovers.

He sliced a whole chicken, shredded the chicken, and sautéed it with a generous handful of kale until the greens turned crisp. He added diced potatoes, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a splash of lemon juice, then let everything simmer for five minutes. He finished the pan with a few slices of lemon and served the dish over a bed of fresh spinach. He topped each portion with a slice of toast and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of sautéed chicken, caramelized potatoes, and fresh herbs. Sam presented the lunch on a simple white plate, arranging the golden potatoes and tender chicken in alternating rows beside a crisp green salad and a side of crisp bread. The meal felt like a cozy reminder that even small acts of cooking can make a day feel purposeful.
