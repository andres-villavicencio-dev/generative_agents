Sam prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Brown Rice for dinner. The main dish was a whole chicken that had been rubbed with garlic, dried herbs, and sea salt, then roasted until the skin was golden brown and the meat was tender. He added carrots, onions, potatoes, and cauliflower to the pan for color and flavor, while the brown rice was cooked separately with a little oil and lemon juice.

As the oven turned on, he could smell the rich aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and savory poultry. When the food was nearly ready, he plated it on a warm white platter. The chicken was arranged at the center, surrounded by bright orange carrots, crisp onions, and fluffy white rice, with a small green garnish of parsley.

The occasion was Sam’s final evening before running for local mayor. His wife, Jennifer, had been his partner for forty years, and he wanted the meal to feel both familiar and celebratory. The warm, comforting dinner was meant to ground him before the long walk around Johnson Park and a quiet evening at Hobbs Cafe.
