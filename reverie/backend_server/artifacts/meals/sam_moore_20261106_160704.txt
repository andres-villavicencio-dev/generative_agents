Sam prepared Hobbs Café-style Oatmeal with Fresh Berries and Lemon, a simple breakfast suited to his relaxed morning routine. He toasted oats in a pan, added cinnamon and a splash of warm milk, then stirred in chopped blueberries, strawberries, and a few raspberries. He finished the bowl with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice and a sprinkle of toasted nuts.

The kitchen filled with the warm, nutty aroma of toasted oats and the bright citrus scent of the lemon. The oatmeal was neatly arranged in a large white bowl, with the colorful fruit and nuts arranged around it. The presentation was rustic yet inviting, making it an ideal breakfast for a quiet morning walk around Johnson Park.

He prepared the meal for himself as a moment of calm before continuing his day, though he had also prepared it to share with his wife, Jennifer. The breakfast also served as a small ritual before his upcoming run and his plans for running for local mayor.
