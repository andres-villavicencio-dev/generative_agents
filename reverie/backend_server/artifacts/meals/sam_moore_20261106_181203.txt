Sam prepared Herb-Crusted Salmon with Garlic Butter and Asparagus, a classic comfort meal for his evening with Jennifer. He marinated the salmon in olive oil, lemon zest, paprika, garlic, and a touch of dill, then seared it in a hot pan until golden and tender. Afterward, he sautéed fresh asparagus with a little butter, seasoning it with rosemary and pepper. The pan was finished with a generous drizzle of garlic butter, creating a rich, savory aroma that filled the kitchen.

The dish was plated on a white ceramic plate, with the salmon arranged in a neat arc, lemon slices garnished nearby, and the asparagus tucked underneath. A small bowl of steamed rice, roasted potatoes, and fresh salad greens accompanied the meal. It was a relaxed Tuesday dinner, prepared after Sam’s walk around Johnson Park and read at Hobbs Café, but with enough warmth and pride to make it feel like a celebration of his life and upcoming mayoral campaign.
