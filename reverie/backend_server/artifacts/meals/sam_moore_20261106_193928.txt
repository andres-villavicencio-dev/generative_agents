Sam prepared Sunday Gravy Chicken for dinner on a quiet evening in February. The meal was simple, filling, and perfect for a busy week: a whole chicken, a large onion, carrots, potatoes, celery, and garlic, all simmered in a rich stock with paprika, thyme, and a touch of lemon. He roasted the chicken until the skin was golden and crispy, then sliced the vegetables and arranged them around the bird in a shallow baking dish. He topped the whole dish with a generous spoonful of the golden gravy, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a lemon wedge.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized onions, roasted garlic, and savory poultry. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the plump chicken standing proudly at the center and the colorful vegetables arranged symmetrically beneath it. Sam ate it with his family around 5:00 p.m., treating the meal as a comfortable reminder of how simple nourishment can bring people together. The occasion was a relaxed family dinner before Sam’s planned visit to the park, where he would continue talking with his wife, Jennifer, and the neighborhood.
