Sam prepared Jumbo Lemon-Herb Chicken with Grilled Asparagus and Roasted Potatoes for dinner. He had the chicken breast marinated in lemon juice, garlic, dried oregano, and a splash of water, then pan-seared it until golden and tender. The asparagus was lightly tossed with olive oil, salt, and pepper, while potatoes were roasted with rosemary until crisp.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory scent of roasted garlic and fresh herbs, while a bright lemon aroma filled the room. For the occasion, Sam made it a special family meal for his wife, Jennifer, and two neighbors before his local-mayor campaign. He arranged the food on a large platter, garnishing the chicken with thyme and lemon zest. After dinner, he planned a walk around Johnson Park, then sat at Hobbs Cafe to read and discuss the upcoming mayoral election with his neighbors.
