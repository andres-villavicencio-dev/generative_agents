For his Monday dinner, Sam prepared Jumbo Shrimp with Lemon Butter and Garlic. He bought frozen shrimp, red onion, garlic, chicken broth, lemon juice, butter, and fresh parsley.

He thawed the shrimp and sliced the red onions thinly, then sautéed them in a large skillet. After adding the broth, he turned the heat down and simmered it until the liquid reduced to about one-third of its volume. He added the butter and lemon juice, then cooked the shrimp until they turned pink. Finally, he stirred in the chopped parsley.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and lemon, and the steam made the scent feel inviting. Sam plated the shrimp in a shallow dish, arranging them around a small mound of cooked rice with a lemon wedge and a sprig of parsley beside them.

The meal was simple, cheerful, and perfectly suited for a rainy Monday evening. It was the kind of humble dinner Sam would recommend to neighbors while he talked about his local mayoral campaign, his wife, and his life in the navy.
