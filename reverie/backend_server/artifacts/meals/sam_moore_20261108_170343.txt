Sam Moore prepared a hearty Johnson Park Sandwich and Pastry Batch for the cafe. The sandwiches were made with crusty white bread, a generous filling of tomato, lettuce, cucumber, smoked salmon, and a creamy avocado spread, all wrapped snugly with fresh thyme and rosemary. The pastries were flaky croissants, buttery muffins, and a batch of caramelized apples.

He toasted the bread, layered the ingredients, and folded the sandwiches before placing them on a platter. The pastries were arranged neatly beside them, with warm cinnamon sticks and a drizzle of honey. As the aroma filled the cafe, the rich scent of toasted bread and savory salmon mingled with the buttery fragrance of the croissants.

The occasion was Sam’s community gathering at Hobbs Cafe, where he was sharing his neighborhood stories and discussing his mayoral campaign. The simple, warm meals reflected his resourceful, homey approach to hosting, bringing neighbors together over food and conversation in a relaxed, inviting atmosphere.
