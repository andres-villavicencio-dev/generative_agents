For Tuesday, February 14, Sam prepared a Spicy Lemon-Honey-Thyme Coffee & Lunch Box for his Hobbs Cafe lunch appointment. The dish combines a rich coffee with a simple, flavorful lunch.

The main ingredients include black coffee, spicy lemon zest, honey, thyme, a bit of garlic, and a sprinkle of smoked paprika. The coffee was brewed to a medium-brown strength, while the lunch was assembled on a warm ceramic plate using toasted bread, crisp lettuce, and a small pan of roasted nuts.

To make it, Sam brewed the coffee first, letting the honey simmer with the lemon and thyme for a minute to release their warm aroma. He then layered the coffee, bread, and vegetables neatly over it, garnishing the plate with fresh thyme and a sprig of rosemary.

The presentation was bright, inviting, and thoughtfully arranged: the dark coffee created a warm base, while the golden honey, citrus zest, and green herbs added color. The occasion was Sam’s Valentine’s Day party, and he intended to send the invitations early so he could confirm his attendance and coordinate with Jennifer Moore, who had planned a coffee outing that afternoon.
