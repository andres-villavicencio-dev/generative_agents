Sam prepared a fragrant spicy lemon-honey-thyme coffee and lunch at Hobbs Cafe. The dish was a warm lemon-honey-thyme stew with sautéed spinach, roasted chicken, and a spicy lemon-honey glaze.

The key ingredients included ripe lemons, honey, thyme, chicken, spinach, and a pinch of cayenne pepper. Sam began by sautéing the chicken until golden, then added the lemons and honey to create a bright, aromatic sauce. He stirred in the thyme and spinach for a few minutes, letting the herbs release their flavors.

The aroma was inviting—rich lemon, honey, savory chicken, and the earthy hint of thyme. Sam plated the chicken and greens in a warm white bowl, garnishing it with a sprig of thyme and a small lemon wedge. The presentation was cheerful and rustic, with a simple plate and a matching cup of coffee beside it.

It was prepared for Sam’s February 14 Valentine’s Day party. The meal balanced a hearty lunch with a refreshing coffee, while the warm, festive presentation suggested a cheerful celebration.
