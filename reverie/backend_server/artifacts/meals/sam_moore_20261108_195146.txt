Sam prepared a Spicy Lemon-Honey-Thyme Coffee and Lunch at Hobbs Cafe, combining a rich coffee with a flavorful sandwich. The dish was designed as a Valentine’s Day treat, reflecting his retirement from the Navy and his love of sharing memorable experiences.

The sandwich featured a toasted sourdough base, grilled chicken, crisp lettuce, ripe tomatoes, cucumbers, and a generous spread of spicy honey-lemon dip. A side of crisp fries, cucumber salad, and fresh fruit completed the meal. Sam added thyme, lemon zest, and honey to the coffee, enhancing its aroma with warm spices, citrus, and a hint of sweetness.

The presentation was bright and appetizing: the golden-brown bread and colorful vegetables stood out against the dark coffee, while the spicy dip added a vivid orange-red contrast. Sam served the lunch in a simple ceramic bowl, with the coffee resting nearby so he could savor both the rich brew and the savory sandwich.

The occasion was an early Valentine’s Day gathering in Hobbs, where Sam wanted to confirm attendance for the party and remember that Jennifer Moore was coming for coffee in the afternoon. The meal balanced his relaxed, retired lifestyle with an affection for food and thoughtful preparation.
