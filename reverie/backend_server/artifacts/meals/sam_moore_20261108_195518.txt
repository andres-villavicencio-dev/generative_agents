Sam prepared a lemon-honey-thyme pot roast, a warm, comforting meal that complemented his afternoon coffee and lunch at Hobbs Cafe. He used a whole chicken, sliced red onions, carrots, celery, and potatoes, with a generous amount of beef stock to make the roast tender and flavorful. The chicken was seared in a pan, then slowly roasted in the oven until golden and juicy, while the vegetables were simmered alongside it.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and inviting—rich browned beef, sweet caramelized onions, savory chicken, and bright citrus notes blending with the herbaceous fragrance of thyme. He plated the meal in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the golden chicken around a bed of colorful vegetables and serving a small side of crusty bread.

The meal was part of Sam’s preparations for the Valentine’s Day party at Hobbs Cafe, where he had confirmed the theme and added the spicy lemon, honey, and thyme to the drinks. He intended to send the invitations early so he could confirm attendance, while his reminder to meet Jennifer Moore later in the afternoon would help him track her progress.
