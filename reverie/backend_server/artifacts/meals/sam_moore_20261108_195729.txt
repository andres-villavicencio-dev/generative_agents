Sam prepared Spicy Lemon-Honey-Thyme Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for Tuesday, February 14. He sautéed chicken breasts with minced garlic, chopped red pepper, and a pinch of chili flakes, then finished them with a splash of lemon juice and a generous drizzle of honey. He added chopped onions, carrots, and bell peppers to the pan, seasoning them with dried thyme and a touch of salt before serving.

The meal had a warm, fragrant aroma of caramelized garlic, honey, citrus, and thyme. It was presented simply but attractively: the chicken arranged beside a colorful platter of roasted vegetables, with a small bowl of lemon-honey sauce and crisp bread nearby.

Sam shared the dish at the Hobbs Cafe, where Jennifer Moore arrived for coffee in the afternoon. He poured his cup, checked the temperature, and added a little more honey and thyme to his drink, preparing it as a thoughtful Valentine’s Day treat. The meal was designed for a relaxed, celebratory lunch, balancing spicy warmth with sweet, savory, and herbal flavors.
