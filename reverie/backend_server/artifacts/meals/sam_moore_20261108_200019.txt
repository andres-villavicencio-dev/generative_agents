Sam prepared Spicy Lemon-Honey-Thyme Soup, a warm, comforting dish inspired by the flavors he used in his tea. He cooked boneless chicken thighs with lemon, honey, thyme, and a touch of garlic and onion, then simmered everything in vegetable broth until the chicken was tender and the soup was fragrant and rich.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the scent of caramelized garlic, lemon zest, thyme, and honey. Sam ladled the soup into a clear glass, arranging a sprig of thyme and a lemon wedge beside it. He added a small wedge of lemon to each cup and placed a single piece of pepper on the table.

The meal was served at the Hobbs Cafe on Valentine’s Day, celebrating Sam’s retirement and his warm connection with Jennifer Moore. The spicy, savory soup offered a hearty, inviting lunch, while the lemon and honey brought a festive, cheerful touch to the occasion.
