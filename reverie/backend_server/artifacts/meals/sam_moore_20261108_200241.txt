Sam prepared a Spicy Lemon-Honey-Thyme Chicken Bowl for the February 14 Valentine’s Day party. The dish featured grilled chicken breast marinated in lemon juice, honey, garlic, and thyme, served over steamed white rice with roasted carrots, green beans, and a crisp lemon vinaigrette.

He toasted the chicken in a skillet until the skin was golden, added the warm honey and thyme, and finished it with a splash of broth and fresh herbs. The aroma rose from the kitchen: warm, savory, and subtly sweet.

The bowl was presented on a white plate with a small sprig of rosemary, a lemon wedge, and a silver fork, giving it a refined yet festive appearance. It was designed as a casual, crowd-friendly meal for the Valentine’s Day gathering at Hobbs Cafe, combining Sam’s military-cooked tradition with a bright, comforting presentation.
