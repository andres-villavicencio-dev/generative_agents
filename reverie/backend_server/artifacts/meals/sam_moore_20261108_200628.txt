Sam prepared a Spicy Lemon-Honey-Buttermilk Chicken Salad with Thyme, a hearty, bright lunch meant to celebrate Valentine’s Day.

The dish featured tender chicken breast, crisp lettuce, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and crunchy walnuts, all tossed in buttermilk, lemon juice, honey, and a savory thyme-oregano dressing. The spicy lemon-honey flavor balanced the richness of the chicken, while the crisp salad provided refreshing contrast. He arranged the salad on a white ceramic plate, garnished with fresh herbs and a small lemon wedge, and served it with a side of grilled bread and a glass of iced tea.

The aroma was inviting: warm lemon zest, savory thyme, gentle honey sweetness, and the subtle richness of buttermilk. The presentation was clean and cheerful, reflecting the romantic occasion without being overly dramatic. Sam prepared the meal after confirming the theme and adding the spicy lemon, honey, and thyme to the drinks, then invited Nair Viggiani to join him for lunch and tea afterward.
