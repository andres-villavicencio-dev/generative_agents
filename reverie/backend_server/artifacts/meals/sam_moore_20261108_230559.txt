Sam prepared Lemon-Honey-Thyme Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a simple yet elegant dinner inspired by the spicy lemon, honey, and thyme flavor he was refining for the Valentine’s Day party.

He seasoned the chicken with olive oil, garlic, lemon, honey, thyme, dried oregano, and a small pinch of red pepper flakes, then seared it until the skin was golden and the juices were released. While the chicken cooked, he roasted carrots, potatoes, zucchini, and bell peppers in the oven until they were tender and caramelized. He served them around the chicken, garnished with parsley and a squeeze of fresh lemon.

The aroma was rich and warm: roasted garlic, honey, thyme, and the bright, citrus scent of lemon filled the kitchen. The meal was presented in a white ceramic bowl, with the golden chicken and colorful vegetables arranged around it and a small bowl of lemon-tarrine sauce beside it.

It was a comforting, family-style dinner for Sam’s Tuesday, February 14, Valentine’s Day party. He added the spicy lemon-honey-thyme flavor to his drinks and sent the invitations early so he could confirm attendance.
