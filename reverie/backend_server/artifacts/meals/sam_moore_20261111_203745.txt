Sam prepared a hearty Sunday Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy, a comforting family-style dinner fitting the Valentine’s Eve celebration. The centerpiece was a whole chicken, golden-brown and generously seasoned with rosemary, thyme, garlic, paprika, and a hint of cayenne. He added carrots, potatoes, onions, and baby potatoes to the oven, allowing them to soften and absorb the savory juices. For a quick side, he roasted a small pan of kale with olive oil, lemon juice, and salt, then served it alongside the chicken.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of roasted poultry, fresh herbs, caramelized onions, and a rich, creamy gravy. He plated the meal in a simple white bowl, arranging the golden chicken and colorful vegetables with a silver spoon and serving plate, then set the table for dinner. It was a cozy, purposeful evening: Sam had completed the morning routine, walked through Johnson Park, helped Jennifer and Isabella prepare Valentine’s specials, and was considering community opportunities, while enjoying a satisfying meal before bed.
