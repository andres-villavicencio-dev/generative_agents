On Tuesday, February 14, Sam prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Gravy for a quiet Valentine’s dinner at home. He chose a bone-in chicken breast, roasted it with rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a splash of olive oil, then gently pan-seared the vegetables and potatoes before tossing everything together in a rich garlic-gravy.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of rosemary, roasted garlic, potatoes, and savory herbs. Sam served the meal in simple white bowls, arranging the golden chicken and colorful root vegetables around the center of the table. A small glass of red wine and a plate of crisp vegetables completed the simple, intimate dinner.

It was not a grand celebration, but Sam chose it for a peaceful Tuesday evening. The meal reflected his relaxed, purposeful life, while the opportunity to spend time with Jennifer and Isabella added a special warmth to the occasion.
