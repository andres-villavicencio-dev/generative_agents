Sam prepared a hearty family-style chicken pot pie for Tuesday’s dinner. The filling included tender chicken, sliced potatoes, carrots, onions, and celery, all simmered with a thick, savory broth and a few drops of mustard. A flaky, buttery crust was cut into strips and placed over the filling, while bright green peas and a scattering of herbs added a cheerful pop of color.

The kitchen smelled of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, and the warm aroma of butter and herbs. Sam arranged the golden pie in a large baking dish, garnished with fresh parsley and a sprig of thyme, then covered it with foil before placing it in the oven.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday evening with Jennifer and Isabella, who had helped prepare Valentine’s specials. Sam ate the pie sitting at the table, sharing stories of his time in the navy and offering encouraging advice about his local campaign. The warm, comforting meal became a small celebration of the day, while he settled down to prepare for bed.
