Sam prepared Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze for an early dinner with his wife, Jennifer. The meal was rich, savory, and comforting, fitting a relaxed Monday evening after a long day at the park.

The chicken thighs were seasoned with garlic, paprika, oregano, rosemary, and black pepper, then placed in a large roasting pan with a mix of carrots, potatoes, and onions. They were roasted at 400°F (200°C) until the chicken was golden, juicy, and easily pulled from the bone. The root vegetables were softened by simmering in vegetable broth and a splash of stock before the chicken was added back in, allowing their flavors to meld.

A lemon-herb glaze of butter, lemon juice, honey, and chopped herbs provided a bright, citrusy finish. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized garlic, savory herbs, roasted potatoes, and golden chicken.

The dish was plated in a shallow white bowl, with a small plate of crusty bread nearby for dipping. The vibrant colors of the roasted vegetables and tender chicken created an inviting, rustic presentation. It was a simple, home-style dinner—simple enough for a retired officer, yet special enough to honor the quiet life Sam enjoyed with Jennifer.
