Sam prepared Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables for a quiet dinner with Jennifer. He sliced chicken breasts with the skin on, tossed them in a dry rub of dried herbs, garlic, salt, and pepper, then grilled them over a hot skillet until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. Alongside the chicken, he roasted carrots, potatoes, and red bell pepper with olive oil, honey, and lemon slices until they browned. A small pot of lemon chicken broth was reserved for a simple zesty lemon rice.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of garlic, herbs, and citrus. Sam plated the chicken in a shallow dish with the golden root vegetables, drizzled the reserved lemon broth over the rice, and garnished everything with fresh herbs. The dinner was simple, comforting, and a celebration of his retirement life.

He cooked it around 5 p.m., after a long day of tending the park and reading in Hobbs Cafe. The meal was meant for his wife and a few trusted neighbors, and Sam had even prepared a few remarks about running for mayor, though he kept them low-key. It was a quiet, wholesome evening with no grand occasion, just the comfort of home and friends.
