Sharon prepared Mushroom & Honey Croissant with Creamy Lemon-Citrus Salad, a warm café meal designed for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

The croissants were baked to golden-brown perfection, filled with a savory mushroom mixture of sautéed mushrooms, shallots, thyme, and a touch of lemon zest. They were brushed with softened butter and sliced to serve alongside the salad. The salad combined crisp arugula, honey-lemon vinaigrette, crisp radishes, cherry tomatoes, and feta, creating a bright, herbaceous contrast to the buttery dough.

Aromatically, the meal filled the kitchen with toasted nuts, caramelized mushrooms, and the subtle scent of warm pastry and citrus. Sharon arranged the croissants in a rustic bread basket, garnishing them with a sprig of rosemary and tiny edible flowers. The meal was served for the weekend baking workshop, where Isabella and Maria joined Sharon to taste-test the new croissant recipes and discuss the café’s expanding sustainable pastries.
