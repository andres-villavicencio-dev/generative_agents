On Wednesday, February 15, Sharon Antle prepared Mediterranean Herb Croissants for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe as part of an exclusive weekend baking workshop. The croissants were made from flour, butter, eggs, milk, salt, and dried rosemary and thyme, with the dough cut into evenly sized rounds and the filling prepared in advance.

Sharon rolled the dough, scored it, and baked the croissants in the café’s warm oven. She brushed the pastry with a little olive oil and finished them with a drizzle of lemon-herb butter. The aroma was unmistakable: buttery, toasted, and bright with rosemary and thyme.

The pastries were presented on silver platters with a small bowl of warm honey-lemon dressing, a sprig of rosemary, and a decorative arrangement of berries and nuts. The occasion was a special workshop event honoring local baking traditions, while Isabella Rodriguez’s input guided the seasonal ingredients and sustainable packaging of the final line.
