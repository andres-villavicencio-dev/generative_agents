Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant & Fig Compote, a refined treat designed for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

The dish consists of three layers: buttery, golden croissants filled with a creamy vanilla cream cheese, topped with crisp caramelized figs, toasted almonds, and a drizzle of honey. The fig compote features sliced ripe figs, a touch of cinnamon, lemon zest, and a little maple syrup.

She prepared it in two stages. First, she baked the croissants on a parchment-lined baking sheet, adjusting the oven temperature to 375°F and setting a precise timer. The crisp, flaky pastry released steam as it finished, while the warm figs added a fragrant sweetness. Once the oven door opened, she immediately began preparing the compote by caramelizing the figs in a pan until soft and golden.

The aroma was rich and inviting: butter, toasted almond, fig, cinnamon, and honey mingling with the subtle aroma of freshly baked pastry.

The presentation was delicate and elegant: the croissants were arranged in a neat row, dusted with powdered sugar, and the warm compote was poured over them in a shallow ceramic bowl. It would serve as a thoughtful dessert at a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe or as a special treat for Isabella Rodriguez.
