Sharon Antle prepared a Mediterranean Herb Croissant with Warm Lemon-Honey Glaze, a refined breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The croissant was made from butter, flour, eggs, rosemary, thyme, and a pinch of sea salt, then baked at a moderate temperature until golden and flaky. After the croissants had cooled slightly, Sharon brushed them with a warm lemon-honey glaze, adding the fragrant zest and honey for a bright finish.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with the buttery scent of the croissant, the sharpness of the rosemary, and the citrusy sweetness of the glaze. She arranged the croissants on a wooden board, placing a small glass of water, a sprig of rosemary, and a lemon wedge beside each one.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon was demonstrating how classic New England recipes could be transformed through seasonal inspiration and careful presentation. The meal was prepared to inspire and delight the participants, leaving everyone with a satisfying breakfast and a sense of the cafe’s creative spirit.
