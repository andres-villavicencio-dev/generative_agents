Sharon prepared a Midnight Biscuit Croissant with Lemon-Maple Compote, a refined adaptation of classic New England baking.

Key ingredients included softened butter, flour, eggs, sugar, lemon zest, maple syrup, and a pinch of cinnamon. She layered the croissants with a delicate lemon-maple filling, then baked them with the pastry crisp while preserving the signature flaky layers.

The preparation was meticulous and slow: she folded the dough, brushed it with melted butter, and placed it in the oven until the edges turned golden and the filling set. She finished the meal by spooning the warm compote over the croissants, allowing the buttery crisp and sweet lemon to complement one another.

The aroma was inviting—warm butter, toasted nuts, and lemon with a subtle cinnamon undertone. The croissants were arranged in a small glass bowl on a white platter, their golden peaks catching the cafe’s soft morning light, with the compote nestled beside them.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe. Sharon and Maria Lopez had tasted the new croissant recipes together, and the successful outcome became the inspiration for Sharon’s sustainable pastry line and her continuing partnership with Isabella Rodriguez.
