Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant-Infused Tartlets for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish combines classic New England pastry with a surprise finish: golden-brown croissants filled with a warm hazelnut-caramel sauce, served with a simple lemon-chive crisp and a chilled lavender tea.

The tartlets were baked in batches early on a Wednesday, with the croissants rested overnight and then reheated to keep their delicate layers crisp. Sharon layered the tart shells, filling, and garnish before carefully arranging them on a slate stand alongside a glass of tea.

As she prepared the display, the kitchen filled with the rich, buttery aroma of toasted dough, caramelized hazelnuts, and a hint of lavender. The presentation was neat and inviting, with the pastries arranged in a balanced row, the crisp and tea resting beside them. The occasion was the weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Isabella Rodriguez had invited Sharon to showcase her creative pastries.
