Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant with Blueberry Compote and Honey Glaze, designed for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish combines a golden, flaky New England-style croissant with sweet blueberries, a crisp honey glaze, and a glossy fruit compote.

She began by gently separating the dough layers with a pastry brush and a small knife, then rolled the croissant into a long log. While the croissant baked in a preheated oven at 400°F for about 12 minutes, she toasted the blueberries and folded them into the filling. After removing the pastry from the oven, she brushed it with the honey glaze and spooned the warm fruit compote over the warm croissant.

The aroma was unmistakable: buttery, slightly nutty, and fragrant with the bright, floral notes of blueberries and honey. Sharon arranged the pastries on a wooden board, decorating each with a dusting of cinnamon and a small sprig of mint for the final presentation. It was a quiet, celebratory dinner meant to honor Isabella’s Hobbs Cafe and celebrate Sharon’s creative baking.
