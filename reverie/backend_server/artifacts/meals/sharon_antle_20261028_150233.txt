Sharon prepared a Midnight Croissant for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, a treat she baked around midnight before her usual 2:00 am wake-up. The pastry used laminated dough made with butter, whole wheat flour, eggs, milk, and a pinch of sea salt. The croissant was rolled into a square and baked until golden, then chilled before being sliced.

She flavored it with cinnamon, a touch of lemon zest, and a drizzle of honey, while the butter provided a rich, nutty aroma. The presentation was elegant and inviting: the crisp, layered croissant was arranged on a white ceramic platter, garnished with a dusting of powdered sugar and a small sprig of mint, with two warm cookies beside it.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon was testing the new croissant line with Maria Lopez’s feedback. Her meticulous preparation, thoughtful presentation, and creative experimentation created an indulgent, inspiring meal that reflected both classic craftsmanship and her desire to explore new flavors.
