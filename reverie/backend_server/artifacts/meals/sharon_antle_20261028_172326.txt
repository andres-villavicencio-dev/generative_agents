Meal: Midnight Croissant & Honey-Glazed Fig Tart

Sharon prepared a classic French-style croissant, glazed with honey and served with fig compote and a warm lemon drizzle. The croissant was made with buttery flour, softened butter, eggs, salt, and a touch of cinnamon, folded and baked until golden and flaky. A small fig tart, glazed with honey and topped with toasted nuts, completed the meal.

The aroma was rich, buttery, and sweet—warm cinnamon, toasted nuts, honey, and the faint scent of caramelized fruit filling the kitchen. Sharon presented the croissant in a white ceramic dish, arranged alongside the tart and garnished with fresh figs and a lemon wedge. It was served to Isabella Rodriguez as part of the weekend baking workshop, with Maria Lopez’s feedback helping Sharon refine the croissant’s texture and flavor.
