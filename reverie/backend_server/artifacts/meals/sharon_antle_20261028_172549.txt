Sharon Antle prepared a Mediterranean-Light Pastries Breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The meal featured golden-brown croissants, a crisp lemon-tahini muffin, and a warm side of olive oil and lemon yogurt.

She began by separating the croissant dough into two discs, rolling them out, and cutting them into uniform shapes before baking them at 375°F for about 12 minutes. She followed this with a lemon-tahini muffin in a muffin tin, baking it at 350°F for 18 minutes. The side was simple: chopped mint, lemon zest, and olive oil drizzled over a bed of quinoa.

As the pastries were ready, Sharon arranged them on a white ceramic platter with small bowls of yogurt and mint. Her meticulous, artistic approach created a bright, balanced presentation, while the warm aroma of butter, lemon, herbs, and toasted dough filled the café. This was a special breakfast for Isabella’s early-week workshop gathering.
