Sharon prepared Midnight Croissant with Lemon-Balanced Butter, a refined yet indulgent breakfast that balanced her midnight baking inspiration with Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe’s classic recipes. The dish featured two golden-brown croissants filled with a bright lemon curd and a richly flavored butter mixture, finished with toasted hazelnuts.

She toasted the croissants on a clean baking sheet until the pastry was crisp and the butter softened, then sliced them to reveal the creamy filling. The lemon curd provided a bright citrus note, while the butter-bread combination added warmth and richness. A generous dusting of toasted hazelnuts and a final sprinkle of sea salt completed the profile.

The aroma was unmistakably buttery, lemongrass, and subtly savory. Sharon plated the croissants in a simple wooden basket beside a small bowl of lemon curd, with a sprig of thyme and a few whole hazelnuts for texture.

The meal was prepared for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe’s weekend breakfast workshop, where Sharon would demonstrate the techniques and share her creative variations with the participants.
