Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant-Infused Lemon-Ginger Tartlet, a refined treat designed for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe’s weekend baking workshop.

The tartlets used flaky pastry, buttery lemon curd, fresh ginger, sweetened almond cream, honey, and a pinch of sea salt. She began by folding the pastry into a warm pan, then layered the lemon curd and ginger into the dough before baking until the edges were golden and the center remained crisp. Maria Lopez’s feedback helped her test the ginger-spice balance, while Isabella’s input guided the final tartlet shape and garnish.

The finished tartlets emerged steamy and fragrant with the warm aroma of butter, lemon zest, ginger, and toasted pastry. She arranged them on a white ceramic platter, topped each with a dusting of powdered sugar, a sprig of mint, and a tiny decorative lemon slice. The presentation was understated, elegant, and perfectly suited to the workshop’s intimate, creative atmosphere.
