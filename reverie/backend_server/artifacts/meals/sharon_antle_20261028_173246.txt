Sharon prepared a Midnight Croissant with Fresh Lemon Butter. Her favorite croissant, baked before dawn, was brushed with a warm, golden lemon-butter glaze and served with a crisp baguette for the cafe’s weekend gathering.

She made the croissant by folding softened butter through layers of flour and water, letting it rise until the dough doubled in size, then baking it until crisp and golden. The lemon glaze was infused with fresh lemon zest, warmed butter, and a touch of honey to create a balanced, aromatic finish.

The scent was unmistakably croissant-like—rich, buttery, and bright lemon—rising from the oven as Sharon carefully turned the first croissants and smoothed their even edges. She arranged them on a white serving platter, garnished with a sprig of rosemary and a small lemon wedge, with the baguette beside them.

The occasion was Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe’s weekend baking workshop, where Sharon’s meticulous approach and collaborative experimentation with Maria Lopez’s feedback helped make the pastries feel both comforting and creatively inspired.
