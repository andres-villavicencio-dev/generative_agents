Sharon prepared Midnight Croissant with Lemon-Herb Cream, a refined breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish features a golden, flaky croissant made with butter, flour, egg, and unsalted butter, layered with a crisp, lemon-scented pastry crust. It was brushed with a sage and thyme cream, then baked until the pastry turned deep golden brown and the filling was warm, buttery, and crisp.

The aroma was unmistakably croissant-like—rich, nutty, and fragrant with the bright, citrusy note of lemon and the subtle warmth of sage. It was served on a rustic wooden board with a side of whole-grain toast and a small bowl of cold yogurt, all arranged in a neat, inviting presentation.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon was testing the cafe’s new pastry line with Isabella’s input and Maria Lopez’s feedback. The meal was both a quiet reward for her late-night baking and a demonstration of the sustainable, artisanal approach Sharon was developing for the cafe.
