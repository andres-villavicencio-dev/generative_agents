Sharon Antle prepared a Savory Herb Croissant with Fresh Artichoke Salad for the cafe’s weekend breakfast batch. The croissant was laminated with butter, shortening, and egg yolks, then filled with herb-infused cream cheese and toasted goat cheese before being brushed with a lemon-herb glaze. The artichoke salad used crisp leaves, arugula, halved artichoke hearts, toasted almonds, and a lemony vinaigrette.

Aromatic rosemary, thyme, garlic, butter, and lemon filled the kitchen, while the golden pastry and bright green leaves created a warm, fragrant contrast. It was presented on slate platters with a small bowl of dressing and a few fresh herbs, ready to be served at the cafe’s opening.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop and a collaborative lunch at Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. Her meticulous attention to texture, artistic experimentation, and thoughtful presentation made the meal feel both comforting and inventive.
