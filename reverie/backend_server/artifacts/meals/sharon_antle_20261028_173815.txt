Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Mocha Croissants for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, the third batch of pastries baking in the oven Wednesday, February 15.

The croissants were made with butter, flour, sugar, eggs, whole milk, and strong black coffee. A pinch of cinnamon and a dash of vanilla enhanced their classic New England flavor, while a small amount of sea salt added a subtle finish. The dough was rolled out, folded into thirds, and brushed with butter before baking at 375°F until golden and crisp.

As they baked, the kitchen filled with a warm, comforting aroma of toasted butter, caramelized sugar, roasted coffee, and lightly sweetened pastry. After baking, Sharon sliced them, drizzled them with dark chocolate, and served them in elegant white paper circles with a scoop of homemade mousse and a dollop of whipped cream.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop hosted at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon, Maria Lopez, and the cafe’s team were sharing creative pastries. The setting was bright, with warm lighting and a cheerful gathering of friends, making the meal both nourishing and celebratory.
