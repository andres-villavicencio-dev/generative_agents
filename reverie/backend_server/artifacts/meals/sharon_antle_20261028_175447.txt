Sharon prepared Molasses & Maple Biscotti for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, a quiet morning breakfast meant to welcome guests and inspire the weekend baking workshops she was organizing.

She began by baking whole-wheat biscotti for the afternoon, then layered them with sage cream cheese, toasted almonds, dried cranberries, and a light maple glaze. For the biscuits, she used whole-wheat flour, unsalted butter, molasses, baking soda, salt, and warm water, baking them until they turned golden and firm. She toasted the almond nibs and dried cranberries separately before folding them into the dough.

The finished pastries were cooled on a wire rack and arranged in neat rows on a warm wooden table, each biscotti brushed with a glossy glaze and garnished with a sprig of fresh sage. Their aroma filled the kitchen: notes of toasted nuts, warm cinnamon, molasses, and buttery cream cheese.

The occasion was a gentle Sunday morning for Hobbs Cafe, blending Sharon’s classic baking with her artistic, sustainable experiments. The crisp, sweet-bitter biscuits would serve as a comforting breakfast while encouraging the weekend workshops to feature both familiar flavors and creative twists.
