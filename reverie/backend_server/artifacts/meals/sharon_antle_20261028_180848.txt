On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant Breakfast with Fresh Herbs and Honey.

She began by heating the ingredients over low heat: warm butter, finely chopped chives, a few drops of lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and a teaspoon of honey. Small pastry shells and soft croissants were added and gently warmed so the butter melted evenly without burning. The aroma was warm, buttery, and softly sweet, with the subtle scent of chives adding freshness.

She arranged the croissants on a white platter and garnished them with the chive mixture, a drizzle of honey, and edible gold flakes. The presentation was simple and elegant—a quiet, inviting breakfast that balanced the rustic croissants with the bright herb notes.

The occasion was Sharon’s midweek baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she was tasting the croissants and developing a sustainable pastry line with Isabella Rodriguez’s input. The meal was both a creative collaboration and a moment of inspiration, reflecting her meticulous approach and compassionate attention to the details of everyday cooking.
