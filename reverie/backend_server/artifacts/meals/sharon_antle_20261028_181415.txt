Sharon Antle prepared a Molten-Crusted Croissant with Fig and Lemon for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish combines a flaky, golden-brown croissant with a fragrant lemon-fig dressing, reflecting her blend of classic New England baking with her spontaneous, artistic experiments.

For the croissant, Sharon rolled out the dough and used a simple, butter-heavy technique: she brushed the surface with lemon juice, folded the pastry gently, cut a small slit, and brushed it with melted butter before baking. The warm, buttery aroma filled the kitchen as the dough puffed and the golden layers separated.

Served on a white ceramic plate, the croissant was nestled alongside a small bowl of lemon-fig dressing and a drizzle of honey. The bright, citrusy aroma contrasted with the sweet tang of the figs and the warm buttery notes of the pastry. It was the perfect companion to the weekend baking workshops Sharon was organizing at Hobbs Cafe, reflecting her compassionate, detail-oriented nature and her desire to blend tradition with creativity.
