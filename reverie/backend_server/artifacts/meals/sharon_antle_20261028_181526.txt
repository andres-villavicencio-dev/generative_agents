Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Lavender-Honey Glaze, a refined breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe opening. The dish featured golden-crust croissants filled with buttery pastry, toasted hazelnuts, and a creamy vanilla custard, crowned with a golden honey-lavender glaze.

She began by warming the croissant dough in a pan, spreading the butter evenly before placing it on the pan. She folded the pastry to create thin layers, adding a pinch of salt and a few chopped pistachios to the filling. Once it was golden and puffed, she brushed the top with a warm honey-lavender glaze, letting the herbs and honey mingle for a few minutes. After baking, the croissants were brushed with a simple syrup and garnished with fresh lavender and a dusting of sea salt.

The aroma was warm and inviting—a harmonious blend of butter, toasted nuts, vanilla, lavender, and honey. She arranged the croissants on a small white platter, arranging them in a gentle arc so the golden crusts caught the morning light. The presentation was understated yet elegant, with a small ceramic bowl of warm cream cheese and a sprig of lavender nearby.

Sharon prepared the meal to serve as part of her weekend baking workshop, offering Isabella’s creative input while maintaining the classic New England baking style she loved.
