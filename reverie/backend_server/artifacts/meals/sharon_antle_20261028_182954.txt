Sharon Antle prepared Mornstar’s Midnight Croissant for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe’s weekend tasting event. The croissant was rolled from butter, warm milk, granulated sugar, cinnamon, and a touch of sea salt, then baked on parchment until the golden surface was crisp and the center remained softly doughy.

The aroma was unmistakable: buttery, warm, and gently spiced with cinnamon. After baking, Sharon sliced the croissant into thin, irregular slices and arranged them on a white ceramic platter, garnishing each with a tiny sprig of fresh rosemary.

The occasion was a collaborative tasting session where Maria Lopez’s feedback guided the recipe, and the sustainably packaged ingredients were incorporated into the final batch. Sharon presented the croissant alongside Maria’s tasting notes and Isabella’s feedback, using it to test how the eco-friendly packaging affected the pastry’s texture, flavor, and visual appeal.
