Sharon prepared Savory Biscuit Breakfast Platter for dinner on Monday, February 13. The dish combined her favorite breakfast pastries with a savory, comforting main component.

Key ingredients: rustic whole-grain biscuit dough, cream cheese, diced ham and cheddar, sautéed spinach, caramelized onions, and toasted butter.

Preparation method: She preheated the oven for the croissants and filled the baguettes with the biscuit dough mixture, baking them until golden and crisp. The main dish was assembled in a large skillet: the spinach and onions were sautéed, then combined with the biscuit dough, cream cheese, ham, cheddar, and butter. Everything was baked until the edges were crisp and the filling bubbled.

Aroma: Warm, buttery, slightly nutty, with hints of caramelized onion and savory cheese.

Presentation: The croissants were arranged in the center of a white platter, while the baguettes and skillet dish were set beside them. Toasted almonds, a lemon wedge, and fresh berries completed the colorful display.

Occasion: Sharon was hosting a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, combining the cafe’s classic New England recipes with creative, sustainably packaged pastries. Dinner was part of the evening’s relaxed celebration.
