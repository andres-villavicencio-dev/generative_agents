Sharon Antle prepared Elegant Weekend Pastries, a harmonious morning breakfast featuring a buttery croissant, delicate oatmeal cookies, and a warm cinnamon-sugar waffle. She used whole-wheat flour, unsalted butter, brown sugar, eggs, milk, oats, and vanilla for the waffles; cookies were made with butter, flour, sugar, oats, and cinnamon, while the croissant was baked with a crisp, golden crust.

The croissant was kneaded until the dough became smooth, elastic, and easy to handle, then rolled into rounds, brushed with butter, and folded into a classic crescent shape. It was placed on a baking sheet, brushed with egg wash, and baked until golden and tender. The waffles were lightly toasted, and the cookies were arranged beside the croissant.

Aromatic and inviting, the meal smelled of butter, cinnamon, toasted oats, and warm vanilla. The pastries were presented in a simple white serving bowl on a wooden table, with the croissant placed prominently in the center.

This was a special weekend brunch for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, honoring her with a refined, artistically inspired breakfast.
