On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant Breakfast Pastries for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish combined her meticulous croissant baking with a nourishing breakfast, making it a wholesome, artistic meal.

The key ingredients were flour, butter, eggs, milk, baking powder, a pinch of salt, and optional lemon zest or maple syrup. Sharon divided the dough into small, rounded portions, shaping each into a croissant with a simple but elegant twist. She baked them in a preheated oven until golden and flaky, letting them cool before pairing them with warm oatmeal, fresh fruit, and a glass of milk.

The aroma rose from the kitchen: butter, vanilla, cinnamon, and a subtle citrus note, with a crisp, savory finish. She presented them on a simple white platter, arranged with loose nuts and a sprig of mint, so the warm pastries stood out against the clean background.

This was part of her weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she was tasting her new recipes with Maria Lopez and refining the pastries through Isabella Rodriguez’s creative input.
