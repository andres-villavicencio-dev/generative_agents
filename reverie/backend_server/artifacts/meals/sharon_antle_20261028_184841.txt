Sharon Antle prepared a Bacon, Egg, and Herb Croissant Breakfast Plate, served as a wholesome brunch meal. The centerpiece was a golden, flaky croissant filled with a tangy cheese mixture and topped with crispy bacon, fresh herb-cured eggs, and crisp caramelized onions.

She prepared the croissant by rolling out the dough from a refrigerator, folding it into a tight cylinder, and baking it at 400°F until the edges turned golden brown and the crust became crisp. The filling was spooned inside the pastry, then the edges were brushed with a little egg wash before the whole assembly was pan-seared for two minutes.

The aroma was warm and comforting: butter, toasted garlic, savory cheese, and a hint of herb. The presentation was bright and rustic, with the golden croissant arranged beside the eggs, bacon, and caramelized onions on a white ceramic platter, garnished with fresh thyme and a small sprig of parsley.

She prepared it for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe as part of the weekend baking workshop, serving it as a creative, nourishing example of blending classic New England baking with spontaneous artistic experimentation.
