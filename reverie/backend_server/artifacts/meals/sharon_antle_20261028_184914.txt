Meal: The Midnight Croissant and Breakfast Pastry Dinner

Sharon Antle prepared a warm, rustic dinner centered around freshly baked croissants and a comforting breakfast pastry. She sliced the golden, flaky croissants into individual portions, letting the remaining croissants cool on the counter before serving. Her breakfast included a warm oatmeal bowl topped with cinnamon sugar and sliced fresh fruit.

The dinner relied on buttery croissants as its centerpiece, paired with a simple, savory breakfast pastry—likely a lemon-herb crepe or similar panini—served with a drizzle of balsamic glaze and a small glass of red wine.

The room filled with the familiar aroma of toasted butter, cinnamon, and warm pastry. The presentation was inviting: croissants arranged on a warm platter with crisp fruit arranged beside them, while the breakfast pastry received a bright garnish.

This was a relaxing, intimate occasion, honoring the creative rhythm of Sharon’s baking life. After a long day, she enjoyed the comforting dinner in the quiet kitchen, reflecting on her workshops, her collaboration with Maria Lopez, and her continued inspiration from Isabella Rodriguez.
