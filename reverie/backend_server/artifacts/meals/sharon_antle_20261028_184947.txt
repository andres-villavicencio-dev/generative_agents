Midnight Croissant Breakfast with Chocolate-Fruit Muffins

Sharon Antle prepared a warm breakfast of golden croissants and chocolate-fruit muffins for dinner at 5:00 pm. The croissants were made with the classic New England recipe, filled with a buttery, chocolate-filled puff pastry that was brushed with fruit compote. The muffins were filled with softened roll dough, chocolate, and fresh fruit.

Aromatic notes of warm butter, toasted brown sugar, fruit, and molasses filled the kitchen. The croissants were golden, flaky, and fragrant, while the muffins had a rich, moist texture with bursts of chocolate and tart fruit. They were arranged on a white ceramic platter with a sprig of rosemary, a lemon wedge, and a small bowl of warm cinnamon-sugar.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she was collaborating with Isabella Rodriguez on a sustainable line of pastries. Her meticulous, compassionate approach turned the morning kitchen into a place of creation, and the meal completed her late-afternoon inspiration.
