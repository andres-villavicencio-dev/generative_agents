The meal Sharon Antle prepared was a simple, nourishing breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, featuring eggs, toast, and fruit. She prepared the eggs by cooking them in a small pan with a splash of milk, allowing them to set gently while keeping the yolks intact. The toast was made with lightly buttered sourdough, warmed until crisp, and topped with fresh berries, a drizzle of honey, and a dusting of cinnamon.

The aroma of the morning filled the kitchen as the eggs sizzled, butter melted, and cinnamon warmed the air. She arranged the toast in a neat row on a white plate, placing the eggs beside it and scattering the colorful fruit around. A small glass of water and a cup of herbal tea joined the arrangement, creating a balanced and inviting breakfast.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon was teaching Isabella how to combine classic New England recipes with spontaneous, artistic experimentation. The meal was both practical and creative, reflecting Sharon’s meticulous nature, compassionate approach to food, and love for sharing her craft.
