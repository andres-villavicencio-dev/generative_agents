The meal Sharon Antle prepared was a New England-style croissant breakfast and dinner, served as one shared, elegant dinner. She made two batches: the morning batch included butter, flour, salt, cold water, and sugar, while the evening batch incorporated softened butter, egg, cream cheese, and a splash of milk. The croissants were brushed with the egg mixture, baked until golden, then sliced into perfect wedges and arranged on a platter with fresh berries, lemon slices, and a drizzle of honey.

The kitchen filled with a warm, buttery aroma that rose from the golden pastries, accompanied by the faint scent of cinnamon and toasted nutmeg. Presenting the warm croissants beside a simple bowl of warm milk or a small glass of tea, Sharon’s meticulous arrangement and thoughtful pairing turned the meal into an intimate, quiet evening. It was prepared for dinner with Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, combining classic recipes with the spontaneous, artistic flair she brings to every batch.
