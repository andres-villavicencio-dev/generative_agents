Sharon Antle prepared a New England Breakfast Croissant with Fresh Fruit and Maple Syrup, accompanied by a warm sourdough toast with roasted apples.

She began by rolling out her favorite sourdough dough, shaping it into a rectangular croissant, and baking it at 375°F until the golden, flaky layers turned crisp. The finished croissant was brushed with a little maple syrup, dotted with sliced apples, and sprinkled with cinnamon and nutmeg. The sourdough toast was toasted to a crisp, then sliced and topped with the same fresh fruit.

The kitchen smelled of buttery dough, caramelized apples, toasted nuts, and a sweet, comforting aroma that filled the room. She plated the breakfast in a simple white bowl, with the croissant arranged beside the toast and a small bowl of maple syrup nearby.

The meal was prepared for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, where she served the pastries to customers. The relaxed, inviting presentation made the breakfast feel both familiar and artistically special, echoing Sharon’s meticulous yet creative approach to baking.
