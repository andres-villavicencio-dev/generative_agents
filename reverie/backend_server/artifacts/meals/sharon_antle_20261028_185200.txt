Sharon Antle prepared Classic Midnight Croissant with Warm Buttermilk Pancakes for dinner at 5:00 p.m. The meal was a cozy, homemade breakfast-and-dinner combination designed to feel both comforting and creative.

The croissant was made with a simple mixture of flour, sugar, butter, salt, and a touch of cinnamon. After folding and trimming the dough, she baked it until it turned golden and fragrant. She also prepared warm buttermilk pancakes with a little maple syrup and fresh berries.

The kitchen was filled with buttery, savory aromas from the croissant and the rich, sweet scent of the pancakes. Sharon plated the croissant on a warm white plate, garnishing it with a few raspberry leaves and a sprig of thyme. Beside it, the golden pancakes were stacked with glossy syrup and scattered with blueberries and toasted almonds.

The occasion was an evening of relaxation at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon was testing her latest croissant recipes with Maria Lopez. The meal was intended to be a quiet, artistic dinner—a simple but satisfying way to end her weekend baking while sharing her latest creations with Maria.
