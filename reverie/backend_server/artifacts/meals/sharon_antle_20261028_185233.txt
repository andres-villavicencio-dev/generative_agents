Dish Name: Midnight Croissant & Breakfast Pastries

Key Ingredients: All-purpose flour, cold butter, egg yolks, vanilla extract, granulated sugar, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt. The pastries are finished with a brushed egg wash and topped with toasted pecans.

Preparation Method: Sharon made two types of pastries. For the croissants, she rolled out the dough and layered butter with flour, folding and cutting the layers until the pastry resembled a stack of coins. She baked them in a 425°F oven until golden. For the breakfast pastries, she folded the dough and baked them until crisp. The remaining ingredients were organized into the refrigerator before breakfast.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the rich, buttery scent of warm croissants and the delicate, caramelized fragrance of the breakfast pastries.

Presentation: The breakfast pastries were arranged in a clear glass bowl, with the croissants placed neatly beside them on a small serving platter. She added a small glass of water, a lemon wedge, and a cinnamon stick.

Occasion: The meal was prepared in preparation for Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she intends to demonstrate her sustainable pastries and share feedback with Maria Lopez.
