Dish Name: Midnight Croissant & Breakfast Pastries

Key Ingredients:  
Butter, flour, sugar, cinnamon, eggs, milk, baking powder, and whole-grain flour. Maria Lopez contributed a subtle rosemary-infused butter for the croissants.

Preparation Method:  
Sharon began by washing the kitchen sink and wiping down the surrounding surfaces, then preheated the oven to 400°F (200°C). She baked croissants with golden, flaky layers and buttery, savory fillings. Breakfast pastries were rolled and topped with cinnamon, honey, and a light dusting of sea salt.

Aroma:  
Warm, buttery, and fragrant with hints of cinnamon, vanilla, and toasted almond.

Presentation:  
The croissants were arranged on white ceramic plates with a small ramekin of cream cheese and honey beside them. The pastries were placed in a rustic paper bag, their golden crusts catching the morning light.

Occasion:  
A quiet weekend breakfast gathering at Hobbs Cafe, organized for Sharon’s weekend baking workshops. The meal honored her meticulous, compassionate baking style and Maria’s creative influences.
