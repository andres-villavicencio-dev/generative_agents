Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant Biscuits with Honey-Glazed Lemon Butter, a quick, indulgent breakfast for her weekend baking workshop.

The dish combines butter, flour, salt, sugar, milk, eggs, and vanilla, with a hint of lemon zest for balance. She began by making a creamy lemon-butter sauce, then folded the batter into the biscuits while gently shaping them with her fingers. After baking for about ten minutes, she brushed each one with the warm honey-glazed glaze and topped them with a dusting of cinnamon and a few fresh mint leaves.

The aroma filled the small cafe kitchen with the rich scent of butter, toasted dough, lemon, and cinnamon. She plated the croissants on a simple wooden board, arranging them in neat rows with a small bowl of warm honey and a lemon wedge beside them. The meal was served for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe’s weekend workshop, where participants would try the croissants and discuss the creative possibilities of blending classic baking with spontaneous experimentation.
