Sharon Antle prepared Molten Lemon-Butter Croissants with Fresh Basil, a refined breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The recipe combined tender, buttery croissants with bright lemon zest, creamy vanilla bean butter, and a sprinkle of basil.

She carefully folded the croissant into quarters, placed them on a parchment-lined baking sheet, and brushed the tops with a little olive oil and sea salt. The kitchen filled with a warm, buttery aroma of toasted dough, lemon, and vanilla.

The presentation was elegant and inviting: golden, flaky pastries arranged in a small basket alongside crisp fruit, a lemon wedge, and a small dish of minty dressing. Sharon served it at the weekend brunch of the Cafe, where Isabella’s input helped the recipes feel both familiar and artistically fresh.
