Sharon prepared Eggs Benedict with Croissant Toast. The dish featured two English muffin halves, eggs poached in a savory broth, sliced ham, and melted Gruyère cheese, all served with toasted baguette slices that had been baked alongside the croissants.

She began with a light breakfast of fresh fruit and oatmeal before heading to the Hobbs Cafe to demonstrate batch-croissant baking. Using a two-batch method, she carefully folded and scored the dough, filled the croissants with buttery cream cheese, and baked them in batches of eight. After the first batch, she tested the filling, adjusted the oven, and baked the second batch until the golden crusts were crisp.

The aroma drifted from the kitchen—richly buttery, cheesy, and warm. She arranged the croissants in rows on a wooden board, garnished them with fresh herbs, and placed a small Benedict with golden toast beside them. The meal was prepared for the Hobbs Cafe’s weekend baking workshop, celebrating the community’s enthusiasm for the collaborative class and the potential results of the new croissant recipe.
