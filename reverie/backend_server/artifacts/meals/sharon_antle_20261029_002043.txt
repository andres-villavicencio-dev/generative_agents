Sharon Antle prepared a warm Spiced Cinnamon-Orange Croissant for her Hobbs Cafe weekend baking workshop. She used a refined Dutch oven pan with a small amount of melted butter, rolled the dough to a thin circle, cut a slits across the top, and scored the pastry. The filling consisted of softened butter, sugar, golden raisins, toasted pecans, and cinnamon, which she pressed into the pastry before baking.

She baked the croissant in the oven at 400°F for about 12 minutes until it turned golden and firm, then rested it on a cooling rack while she prepared the remaining batches. The warm, buttery aroma filled the kitchen, with hints of orange zest and toasted nuts mingling with the cinnamon.

The croissants were presented on a white ceramic platter in a neat row, dusted lightly with powdered sugar and garnished with a sprig of mint. Sharon served them to her colleagues and guests during the Monday 11:30 a.m.–12:30 p.m. testing session, celebrating her collaborative efforts and the workshop’s growing momentum.
