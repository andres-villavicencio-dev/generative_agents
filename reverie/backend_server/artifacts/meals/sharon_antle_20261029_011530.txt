For Tuesday, February 14, Sharon Antle prepared Smoky Maple Crusted Chicken with Herb-Glazed Potatoes, a hearty, warm comfort meal she prepared while cooking her lunch on the stove. The dish featured bone-in chicken thighs, potatoes, onions, carrots, garlic, rosemary, thyme, smoked paprika, and a sweet maple-butter glaze. She marinated the chicken with lemon, garlic, and herbs, seared it until the skin caramelized, then transferred it to a baking sheet with the potatoes. The oven was set to 425°F for about 20 minutes, allowing the skin to crisp and the flavors to meld.

As she turned off the oven, Sharon plated the golden chicken and crisp potatoes, drizzing the pan with more maple syrup and garnishing the dish with a bright green herb sprig. The aroma filling the kitchen was rich and inviting: smoked paprika, caramelized onions, roasted potatoes, rosemary, and warm maple syrup. She served it with crusty French bread, and the meal was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday lunch after balancing the Hobbs Cafe baking workshops with Eugene and Maria. Although the schedule made her feel stretched, Sharon remained satisfied with the comforting result and carried forward the momentum of the weekend.
