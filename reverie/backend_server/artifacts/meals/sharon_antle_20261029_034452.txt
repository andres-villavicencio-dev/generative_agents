Sharon Antle prepared Braised Root Vegetable and Herb Crouton for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured roasted carrots, parsnips, and root vegetables with a savory mustard–herb glaze, served with a warm crouton topping and a side of crisp potato salad.

She began by sautéing onions and garlic in olive oil until fragrant, then added the root vegetables and seasoned with smoked paprika, garlic powder, dried thyme, and a splash of lemon juice. After roasting until tender, she tossed the vegetables with the croutons, letting the buttery bread soften in the warm glaze. The meal was arranged on a slate platter with the vegetables arranged in a neat row, the crouton topping dusted with coarse salt and pepper, and the potatoes arranged beside them.

The aroma was warm, earthy, and subtly smoky, with notes of garlic, onion, lemon, and toasted bread. It was a simple, comforting dinner intended for the weekend baking workshops, reflecting Sharon’s compassionate, artistic side as she thoughtfully shared her culinary creations with the cafe community.
