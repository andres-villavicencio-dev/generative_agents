Sharon prepared Morning Baked Herb Croissants, a new batch of her experimental croissant line. The dough included softened butter, warm eggs, finely chopped fresh herbs, a touch of lemon zest, and a pinch of sea salt. She began by creaming the butter into the flour, then folded in the eggs and seasonings. The mixture was rolled into a smooth sheet, cut into squares, and brushed with melted butter before being baked at 425°F for about 15 minutes.

As she turned the oven off to let the pastry cool, she closed her eyes and imagined the warm, buttery aroma filling the Hobbs Cafe kitchen. The croissants were golden on the edges, with a buttery sheen and a faint scent of lemon, herbs, and toasted dough. They were arranged in a neat, rustic stack on a platter, with a small glass of cold water and a sprig of parsley beside them.

The presentation was simple and inviting, fitting the café’s warm aesthetic. Sharon had made the croissants for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, and they were ready to be tested and shared during the weekend baking workshops.
