Sharon prepared Midnight Honey-Mint Croissant with Fig Compote, a warm dessert designed for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The croissants were made with laminated butter, whole wheat flour, almond flour, honey, mint, and a touch of lemon zest. After chilling the first batch overnight, the final batch was cooled on a wire rack and then measured in a kitchen scale to confirm the intended weight for the weekend’s batch.

The aroma rose from the oven: butter, toasted nuts, warm honey, and the sharp, fresh scent of mint. The golden-brown croissants were arranged on a white platter, each with a generous bite of fig compote and a drizzle of honey. The presentation was simple yet elegant, with cinnamon sticks and a small mint sprig adding a touch of color.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe. She had baked the croissants early on Wednesday to test new recipes while Isabella provided feedback on the sustainably packaged line. The warm, inviting dessert was meant to complement the workshop’s morning gathering, offering a cozy, artistic treat that balanced her busy schedule with her creative ambitions.
