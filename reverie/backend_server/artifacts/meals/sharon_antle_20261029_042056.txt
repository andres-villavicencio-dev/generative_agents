Sharon Antle prepared New England-Style Croissants with Creamy Butter and Dark Chocolate Compote. The recipe combined warm, buttery croissants with a rich chocolate sauce made from dark chocolate, heavy cream, vanilla, and a touch of lemon zest.

She baked the second batch of croissants before dawn, using a reliable yeast-leavened dough that she had developed alongside Isabella Rodriguez. The first batch was checked for even rise and golden color, while the second batch was kept in the oven to test the new spice blend. As the pastries baked, the room filled with a warm, buttery aroma, while the chocolate compote released a rich, caramel-like scent.

Each golden, flaky croissant was arranged on a slate platter and served with the glossy chocolate sauce. The presentation was simple and elegant, with a small spoon of sauce placed beside each pastry and a sprig of rosemary for visual appeal. The meal was prepared for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe weekend baking workshop, where Sharon shared her creative ideas and helped participants understand the principles of baking.
