On Wednesday, February 15, Sharon Antle prepared Sourdough Croissant with Warm Maple-Orange Glaze.

The key ingredients were a long, warm sourdough starter, rich buttermilk, cold unsalted butter, all-purpose flour, a pinch of salt, and dried fruit. She folded the butter into the dough, scored the croissants with a fluted pastry wheel, and brushed them with a warm glaze made from maple syrup, orange zest, and a little honey. Baked at 375°F for about 12 minutes until golden and crisp, the croissants were served warm with a spoon of custard.

The aroma filled the small kitchen as the sourdough rose and butter melted, releasing notes of toasted bread, citrus, and caramel. She arranged the croissants in a small, rustic tin and drizzled each with the glossy glaze, finishing with a small orange and a sprig of rosemary.

The occasion was a quiet weekend breakfast for Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon was testing the first croissant of her new sustainable line. She wanted to evaluate whether the buttery flavor and fragrant aroma would appeal to customers, while the warm glaze provided a comforting, inviting presentation.
