Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Fresh Lemon-Butter, a dessert designed to delight her guests while keeping her early-morning schedule in balance. The croissants were made with flour, cold butter, sugar, egg, and a pinch of salt, folded and baked to a golden, flaky texture. Freshly sliced lemons, butter, and a touch of honey created a bright, citrusy aroma that filled the kitchen.

She arranged the croissants on a white platter, garnishing each with a lemon wedge and a small sprig of rosemary. The warm, buttery pastries were presented alongside a glass of chilled lemon-lime soda, ready to be shared at Hobbs Cafe’s weekend baking workshops.

The meal was simple yet thoughtful, balancing the familiar appeal of a croissant with fresh lemon and the comforting richness of butter. Sharon’s meticulous attention to the recipe’s texture and presentation made each piece feel special, while the aroma and colorful arrangement gave the gathering a festive, welcoming atmosphere.
