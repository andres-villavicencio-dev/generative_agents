Sharon Antle prepared Morning-First Croissant with Lemon-Balanced Butter, a simple yet fresh breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. She used three unsalted butter slices, two eggs, two cups of whole-milk flour, 1/2 cup of sugar, a pinch of salt, and a few fresh lemon slices. After melting the butter, she whisked the eggs and flour, then folded in the lemon zest and slices. The dough was stretched and folded into a classic croissant shape, brushed with the remaining butter, and baked until golden.

The croissant rose slowly, filling the kitchen with a warm, buttery aroma and a delicate citrus note that seemed to linger in every crease. It was presented on a white ceramic platter, surrounded by a small bowl of jam and a glass of warm milk, creating an inviting, artistic breakfast.

The occasion was a quiet weekday morning at the cafe, where Sharon was testing her new croissant recipe and balancing the demands of baking, shopping, and creative work. The meal was meant to be wholesome, spontaneous, and inspiring—perfect for a moment of solitude before the weekend baking workshops began.
