Sharon Antle prepared a Winter Berry Croissant with Fig Compote, a refined yet intimate breakfast favored during Hobbs Cafe’s weekend baking workshops. The dish featured golden-brown croissants filled with creamy vanilla custard, topped with a crisp layer of honeyed figs and wild strawberries. Key ingredients included butter, egg yolks, sugar, vanilla extract, sour cream, and a medley of fresh berries, with the croissants baked before dawn to ensure a tender, airy texture.

She began by folding softened butter into the custard, then filling the flaky, buttery dough with the warm mixture. The croissants were brushed with honey and sprinkled with dried figs and berries before being arranged on a slate board. A small bowl of dark fig jam and a silver spoon of whipped cream completed the presentation.

The aroma was unmistakably fragrant—rich vanilla, buttery pastry, and tart berries filling the kitchen. At the workshop gathering, Sharon served the croissants alongside the warm fig compote, sharing her passion for combining classic New England recipes with spontaneous artistic experimentation. Her meticulous preparation, combined with the inviting presentation, made the meal feel both comforting and celebratory—a small but meaningful part of the collaborative weekend baking experience.
