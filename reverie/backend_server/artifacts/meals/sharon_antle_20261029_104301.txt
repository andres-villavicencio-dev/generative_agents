Meal: Spiced Sourdough Toast with Lemon-Balmy Butter

Sharon prepared a simple yet satisfying breakfast using naturally fermented sourdough bread, fresh lemon juice, olive oil, sea salt, and a little honey. She gently warmed a small pan, added the butter, and toasted the sourdough until the crust was crisp. After that, she sliced the bread, drizzled it with the lemon mixture, and toasted it until the golden edges softened.

The aroma was warm and earthy, with hints of citrus, yeast, and toasted grain. She presented the dish in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the toasted slices in a neat arc and letting them rest briefly to let the lemon scent settle. She served it before the weekend Hobbs Cafe workshop, intending to enjoy it alone. The meal was both nourishing and thoughtful—a quiet way to begin the day while respecting the creative work that surrounded her.
