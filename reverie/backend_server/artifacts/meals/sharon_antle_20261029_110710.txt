Sharon Antle prepared Morning Croissant Rounds, a warm, buttery, almond-infused breakfast treat designed to inspire her creative kitchen. She placed the croissants in the oven at 120°F (49°C) for the first few minutes to allow the pastry to soften without overbaking.

The croissants were made with flour, unsalted butter, almond extract, brown sugar, cinnamon, a small amount of yeast, and a dash of salt. The recipe was inspired by classic New England comfort baking, but Sharon added a light maple-sugar glaze and a hint of sea salt to give each pastry a subtle, aromatic complexity.

As they baked, the kitchen filled with a rich, creamy aroma of melted butter, toasted almond, warm cinnamon, and a delicate sweetness. Once golden and puffed, the croissants were arranged in a ceramic dish and brushed with the glaze before being covered with a sheet of parchment paper.

The meal was prepared to accompany a quiet lunch, while Sharon prepared a small side of warm oatmeal and a glass of milk. It was a simple, intimate breakfast intended to energize her work at Hobbs Cafe and inspire her to keep experimenting with her baking.
