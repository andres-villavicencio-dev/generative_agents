Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant Breakfast for her late-night creative session. The dish featured three golden-crust croissants layered with a delicate mixture of cream cheese, lemon zest, brown sugar, and a hint of cinnamon. A drizzle of warm honey and a dusting of flaky sea salt completed the flavor profile.

The croissants were baked until crisp on the outside and tender inside, with the remaining dough folded together and placed in the oven to finish. Sharon arranged them neatly on a cooling rack, letting the soft, buttery aroma fill the kitchen.

She served the pastries as a warm breakfast after eating lunch, combining her usual New England comfort with an artistic, improvisational twist. The occasion was her quiet Thursday baking time—no official event, but a moment of purpose and anticipation for the weekend workshops. The meal reflected her balanced approach: nutritious, comforting, and full of creative energy.
