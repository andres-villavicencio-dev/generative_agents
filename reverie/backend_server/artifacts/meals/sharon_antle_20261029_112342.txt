Sharon Antle prepared a Mediterranean Herb Croissant with Lemon-Lime Cream. The croissant’s key ingredients were butter, flour, salt, an egg wash, and softened goat cheese and fresh herbs. She layered the pastry, brushed it with a rich lemon-lime cream, and topped it with crumbled goat cheese, chopped parsley, and a drizzle of olive oil.

The croissants were baked in a hot oven until they puffed and turned golden, with the filling melting into a creamy, savory pocket. As it baked, the kitchen filled with the earthy scent of butter, the bright aroma of lemon and herbs, and the warm smell of freshly baked pastry.

She presented them on a clean white platter, arranging the golden-brown pastries in a neat row and garnishing them with a few sprigs of parsley. The setting was a quiet lunch break at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon had paused from her morning routine to prepare the pastries for the weekend baking workshops.
