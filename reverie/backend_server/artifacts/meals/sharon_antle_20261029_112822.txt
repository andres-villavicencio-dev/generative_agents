Sharon prepared Midnight Croissant Panini for lunch, a creative twist on Isabella Rodriguez’s classic New England breakfast croissant. The dish consists of two golden-brown croissants filled with a warm cheese, cream cheese, and honey-garlic mixture, served with toasted baguette slices.

She spread the savory filling between the croissants, wrapped them tightly, then pan-seared them in a skillet until the cheese melted and the edges crispened. The panini was finished with a drizzle of melted butter, a sprinkle of cracked black pepper, and fresh parsley.

The aroma filled the kitchen with buttery warmth, toasted bread, savory cheese, and the subtle sweetness of honey. Her presentation was simple yet inviting: the golden croissants arranged on a clean white platter, accompanied by a small bowl of warm cream cheese and fresh herbs.

The occasion was an early lunch break at Hobbs Cafe, where she prepared the finished batch for the weekend baking workshops. The meal was neither too simple nor too elaborate, embodying Sharon’s meticulous, artistic approach to baking while balancing her busy day with genuine enjoyment.
