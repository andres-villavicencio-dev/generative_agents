Sharon prepared Mushroom and Herb Crisp with Warm Toast, a comforting New England-inspired side for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The crisp was tossed with sautéed cremini mushrooms, shallots, thyme, rosemary, garlic, and a touch of lemon juice, then baked until golden and crisp, with a little butter to enhance the flavor.

The aroma filled the kitchen before dawn: earthy mushroom, savory shallots, and a warm, herbaceous scent that seemed to carry the café’s signature warmth. After letting the crisp cool slightly, Sharon arranged the golden pieces alongside slices of toasted sourdough, a dollop of yogurt, and a drizzle of olive oil.

The meal was prepared for Isabella’s weekend baking workshop, where the crisp would serve as a flavorful accompaniment to fresh croissants. Its presentation was simple yet inviting: the crisp arranged on a wooden board with the toast and yogurt placed beside it, creating a balanced, rustic table setting. Sharon’s meticulous attention to the crisp’s crisp edges and fragrant finish reflected her artistic side, while the warm, nourishing nature of the meal made the occasion feel both purposeful and delightfully comforting.
