Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Crumb Croissants, a refined yet whimsical breakfast treat inspired by Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe collaboration. The croissants were made with flour, unsalted butter, egg yolks, a touch of vanilla, and a hint of cinnamon, folded and baked until they were buttery golden and flaky.

Sharon prepared the dough by hand, dividing the butter into small pockets and pressing them into the pastry before folding and baking. She added a little maple syrup and a few toasted walnuts to the pan. As soon as the croissants emerged, she draped them with a crisp pastry sheet and a glossy glaze.

The aroma was rich and comforting: butter, pastry, cinnamon, and a faint sweetness. The presentation was elegant but warm—individual croissants arranged in a neat row on a dark plate, with the glaze catching the morning light and the walnuts adding texture.

The occasion was an unexpected early breakfast gathering at the Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon gathered inspiration and ideas for a new croissant recipe. The meal felt celebratory and intimate, a quiet moment of creativity amid a busy Thursday.
