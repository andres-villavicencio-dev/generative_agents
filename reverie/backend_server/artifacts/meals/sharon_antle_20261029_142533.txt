On Friday evening, Sharon prepared Midnight Croissant with Honey-Crusted Baguette and Fig Jam. Her kitchen smelled of warm butter, cinnamon, toasted nuts, and the rich aroma of caramelized figs.

She made the croissants from scratch using flour, butter, egg yolks, sugar, salt, and a little water. After folding the dough, she baked them in a preheated oven until golden and puffed. For a healthier alternative, she used whole-grain flour and a small amount of olive oil, then added a honey-glazed baguette and a generous spread of homemade fig jam.

The presentation was elegant yet inviting: the golden croissants arranged on white ceramic with a contrasting baguette in front, the jam spread in a small glass bowl, and fresh figs and nuts scattered around. It was the centerpiece of a cozy Friday dinner prepared for a quiet weekend treat, reflecting Sharon’s love of wholesome comfort food, artistic experimentation, and thoughtful preparation.
