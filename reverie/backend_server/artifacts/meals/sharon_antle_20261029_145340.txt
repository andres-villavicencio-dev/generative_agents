Sharon prepared Smoked Baked Potato with Roasted Veggie Salad for an intimate Saturday brunch at Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured a golden potato baked in the oven until its edges were crisp, topped with a vibrant mix of roasted carrots, red onions, cherry tomatoes, and crisp kale, all dressed with a lemon-herb vinaigrette.

She used smoked paprika and smoked paprika-infused olive oil, fresh dill, lemon zest, and a touch of garlic for warmth and complexity. The potato was first brushed with oil and roasted to the golden brown, while the vegetables were tossed in their dressing just before serving.

The aroma rose in warm, earthy notes of smoked cheese, caramelized onions, and bright citrus. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the potato was placed on a large platter, with the salad arranged in a colorful pile around it. Garnishes of parsley and a drizzle of olive oil made the dish look freshly prepared and ready to share.

The occasion was a relaxing, communal brunch at Hobbs Cafe, a place Sharon had been helping maintain while developing her line of sustainably packaged pastries. The meal reflected her balance of creative experimentation and comforting, nourishing tradition.
