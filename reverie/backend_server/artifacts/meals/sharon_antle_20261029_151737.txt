Sharon prepared a Midnight Croissant with Fresh Berry Compote as a cozy Friday treat for Isabella Rodriguez, who visited Hobbs Cafe to help with her morning baking and test new croissant recipes.

The dish consists of two golden, flaky croissants and a vibrant compote made with pomegranate molasses, honey, and sliced fresh strawberries. Sharon gently folded the warm pastries into the simmering fruit mixture, then served them with a drizzle of toasted almond butter.

The aroma filled the cafe like a familiar winter morning: toasted butter, caramelized pomegranate, and the earthy sweetness of honey. The presentation was simple yet inviting—serving the croissants on warm white plates alongside bright berries, a dollop of whipped cream, and a few cinnamon sticks for visual texture.

This meal marked Sharon’s creative evening before heading home. It was an act of care and inspiration, combining the comforting reliability of classic New England baking with her artistic experiments in spontaneous croissant creation.
