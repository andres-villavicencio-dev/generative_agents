Sharon Antle prepared Mediterranean Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a balanced, nourishing meal that reflected her meticulous approach to cooking while adding a splash of warmth.

She chose chicken thighs, zucchini, yellow bell pepper, red onion, garlic, lemon, olive oil, thyme, rosemary, and a small amount of feta. The chicken was marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, herbs, and a pinch of paprika, then roasted until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The vegetables were roasted together until their edges caramelized, creating a fragrant aroma of lemon, garlic, herbs, and roasted vegetables.

She plated the dish in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the chicken in the center and arranging the colorful vegetables around it. A drizzle of extra virgin olive oil, lemon wedges, and a few sprigs of rosemary completed the presentation. The meal was prepared for a quiet Friday dinner, offering Sharon and her family a comforting, wholesome meal to share before heading to bed at 8:00 p.m.
