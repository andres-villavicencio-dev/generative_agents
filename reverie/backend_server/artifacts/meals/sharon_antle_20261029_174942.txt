Sharon prepared Sunday Roast with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Crouton, a warm, comforting New England-style dinner.

The main dish used a whole roast chicken, red potatoes, carrots, parsnips, and onions. She rubbed the chicken generously with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and thyme, then wrapped it in foil so it could braise gently in its own juices. Meanwhile, she roasted the root vegetables until tender, finished the chicken with a pan sauce, and topped it with the herb-crouton mix.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, caramelized onions, and the rich scent of poultry. The golden, crisp croutons added a delightful contrast, while the vegetables provided earthy sweetness.

Served on a platter with lemon wedges and a small salad of spinach and feta, the meal was presented as a family dinner: warm, colorful, and inviting. It was likely prepared to accompany Isabella’s Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon was helping with baking and planning. The occasion was a relaxed Friday evening at home, designed to bring comfort and creativity together.
