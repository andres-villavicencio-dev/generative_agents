Sharon prepared Baked Blueberry Croissants with Fresh Lemon-Basil Cream, a sweet breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez at Hobbs Cafe. The dish features golden, flaky croissants folded with whole-blueberries, a crisp lemon slice, and a drizzle of honey. A rich lemon-basil cream is served alongside the croissants, while toasted almond slivers add a subtle crunch.

She began by preheating the oven and baking the croissants until they turned golden and puffed. While the pastry rested, she prepared the cream using fresh basil, lemon zest, cream, and a pinch of sea salt. The blueberries were gently toasted in a little butter, then folded into the warm croissants and finished with a light dusting of cinnamon.

The aroma was unmistakable: the nutty scent of the pastry, the sharp tang of the lemon, the herbal warmth of basil, and the sweet undertones of blueberries filled the small café. The presentation was simple yet elegant—each croissant arranged on a white plate, garnished with a lemon wedge and a single blueberry, with the cream resting beside it.

She made the breakfast around 3:30 a.m. as part of her early morning routine for Isabella, but the experience became a quiet inspiration. The comforting, colorful dish reminded her why she bakes before dawn: the warmth of home, the joy of creating something fresh, and the satisfaction of sharing a beautifully made meal.
