Sharon Antle prepared Buttermilk Biscuit Pancakes with Honey-Mustard Butter, a warm, comforting meal designed for Isabella’s Hobbs Cafe. She rolled two thick pancakes, filling each with fluffy biscuit batter, a generous spoonful of honey, and fresh fruit. To balance the sweetness, she added a drizzle of warm honey-mustard butter, which she brushed over the edges and scattered with toasted seeds.

She prepared the pancakes by heating a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat, lightly buttering it, and spreading the biscuit batter. After a few minutes, she flipped the pancakes gently, letting them cook slowly until they turned golden and tender. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized butter and the rich scent of toasted seeds.

The presentation was simple yet inviting: the pancakes were arranged neatly on a shallow white platter, with a small side of warm fruit and honey-mustard butter nearby. Sharon served it to Isabella after breakfast, her eyes lighting up at the warmth and generosity of the meal. It was a quiet evening of creative comfort, blending classic New England baking with the spontaneous artistic spirit that had been guiding her baking for Isabella since early morning.
