The meal Sharon Antle prepared was a Midnight Muffin Tart, a warm, comforting dessert inspired by her midnight croissant experiments. The tart used a sturdy, slightly crumbly shortcrust pastry baked to a golden hue, topped with a creamy custard and a crumbly streusel layer of buttery flour and toasted pecans.

She began by heating the butter, flour, and sugar until the mixture came together, then folded in the eggs and vanilla. The custard was cooked gently until it thickened, and the pecans were toasted to a deep golden brown. The tart was filled with the custard and chilled before the crust was baked to crisp edges and a rich, honeyed crumb.

Its aroma was warm and inviting: toasted nuts, caramelized butter, and vanilla rose through the kitchen as the custard set. The tart presented in a simple glass dish, with the golden crust contrasting the rich custard and shimmering chocolate chips.

This comforting dish was prepared for Saturday’s Hobbs Cafe baking workshop, reflecting Sharon’s passion for blending creative baking with sustainable, accessible ingredients.
