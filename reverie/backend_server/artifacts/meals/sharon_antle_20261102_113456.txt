Sharon prepared a Sustainable Herb-Crusted Croissant and Roasted Vegetable Skillet for the Hobbs Cafe baking workshops.

The dish features laminated croissants with a crisp, herb-infused crust, enriched with lemon zest, garlic, and a little olive oil. The warm croissants were paired with roasted carrots, zucchini, and red bell peppers seasoned with rosemary, thyme, and smoked paprika, all baked until their edges caramelized.

She prepared everything in a large skillet: the vegetables were tossed with olive oil and herbs, then roasted with the croissants on top until golden and slightly crisp. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized potatoes, roasted garlic, and toasted nuts.

She presented the dish in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the golden croissants in a small pyramid and scattering rosemary and thyme over the roasted vegetables. The meal was prepared as a warm, comforting family-style breakfast for the weekend baking workshops, offering guests a chance to recreate the croissants and vegetable skillet at home.
