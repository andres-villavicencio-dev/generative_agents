Sharon Antle prepared Smoky Herb-Crusted Herb Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Lemon-Garlic Cream Sauce for her Hobbs Cafe weekend baking workshops.

She began by washing and drying the chicken, then seasoned it with garlic, smoked paprika, black pepper, rosemary, thyme, and lemon zest. The vegetables—carrots, parsnips, celery, and onions—were tossed with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a little rosemary. After preheating the oven to 400°F (200°C), she placed the chicken on a rack over the vegetables and roasted it until the skin was golden and the juices ran clear. She finished the dish by spooning the golden, fragrant sauce over the vegetables and letting it simmer for a few minutes.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm spices, caramelized onions, roasted root vegetables, and the sharp bite of lemon. She served it in a rustic ceramic bowl with the vegetables arranged around the chicken and served it at her Friday evening workshop dinner. The meal was meant to be comforting, nourishing, and inspiring—perfect for sharing with guests preparing for the weekend baking events.
