Meal: Midnight Croissant and Berry Tartlet

Key ingredients: almond flour, butter, whole-grain flour, brown sugar, egg, vanilla, almond extract, fresh strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, lemon zest, and a pinch of sea salt.

Sharon prepared the tartlets by assembling a crisp, golden-crisped croissant, then filling it with a tart, bright filling of lemon zest, honey, and berries. She folded the tartlets, brushed them with egg wash, and baked them until the pastry turned golden and the filling bubbled. She then paired them with warm honey-lemon yogurt, serving them at the Hobbs Cafe weekend baking workshops.

The aroma rose from the warm kitchen as cinnamon, nuttiness, and a hint of honey filled the room. The tartlets were arranged on white ceramic plates, with colorful berries and a delicate lemon twist beside each one. Their golden crusts glistened against the crisp pastry, while the fresh berries added a burst of color.

The occasion was the Hobbs Cafe weekend baking workshops, where Sharon’s creative pastries and cozy, comforting dishes were being prepared for guests.
