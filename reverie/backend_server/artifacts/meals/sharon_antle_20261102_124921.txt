Sharon Antle prepared a simple but warm breakfast of scrambled eggs with smoked salmon, avocado, and a lemon-herb vinaigrette for Isabella Rodriguez. The dish was plated on a white ceramic plate, with the creamy, golden eggs in the center and the bright orange avocado and dark smoked salmon adding vibrant color. Lemon zest and fresh thyme were sprinkled over the top, completing the visual balance.

She cooked the eggs with a little butter, then sliced the smoked salmon and avocado into thin rounds. The vinaigrette was whisked with lemon juice, olive oil, and a pinch of salt, then drizzled over the breakfast. The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, savory scent of salmon, the buttery richness of the eggs, and the bright citrus fragrance of lemon.

The occasion was Isabella’s weekend breakfast at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon was helping prepare the café’s menu while testing a new croissant recipe. The meal reflected her compassionate, artistic side—comforting, nourishing, and thoughtfully crafted—before she headed home to begin her evening baking and reflecting on her sense of accomplishment.
