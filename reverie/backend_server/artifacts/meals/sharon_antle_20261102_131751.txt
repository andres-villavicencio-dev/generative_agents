Sharon Antle prepared a Sustainable New England Breakfast, combining fresh sourdough bread with a homemade egg dish and seasonal vegetables. The key ingredients included whole-grain sourdough, soft white bread, two eggs, spinach, carrots, diced tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. She used a nonstick skillet to sauté the vegetables and garlic, then scrambled the eggs, finishing the dish with fresh herbs.

The meal was warm, aromatic, and colorful, with the savory eggs, sharp lemon zest, earthy garlic, and bright orange carrots creating a fresh yet comforting aroma. Sharon plated it on a rustic wooden board, arranging the bread slices, eggs, vegetables, and a small plate of fresh fruit around a bowl of warm yogurt.

The occasion was a quiet Saturday morning breakfast at Hobbs Cafe, where she enjoyed the meal alongside Isabella Rodriguez and other cafe staff while reflecting on her creative work. The setting was simple and inviting, emphasizing the warmth of the food and the satisfaction of making it.
