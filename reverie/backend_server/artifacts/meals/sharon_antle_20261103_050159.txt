At 3:30 a.m., Sharon prepared Spiced Sourdough Breakfast Pockets for her weekend breakfast. The dish consists of whole-grain sourdough slices, creamy avocado, roasted sweet potatoes, and a warm dressing of lemon, garlic, and smoked paprika. She sliced the bread, brushed it with the dressing, and carefully placed a portion of avocado, sweet potato, and a sprinkle of cinnamon on each piece.

The croissants were baked until golden and flaky, then arranged alongside the savory pockets on a ceramic platter. Sharon arranged them with a small bowl of warm oat milk and fresh berries, creating a colorful, rustic breakfast. The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich smell of baking sourdough, toasted nuts, caramelized sweet potatoes, and the subtle fragrance of warm cinnamon.

The occasion was Sharon’s quiet Saturday morning at Hobbs Cafe, where she was preparing for the weekend baking workshops. After a long night of midnight croissant experiments, the satisfying breakfast gave her a sense of accomplishment and inspired her to keep experimenting with sustainable, artistic pastries.
