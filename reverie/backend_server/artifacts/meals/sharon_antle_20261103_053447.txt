Sharon prepared a New England-inspired croissant and fruit tart, designed to celebrate the weekend baking workshops while showcasing her sustainability-focused croissant line.

The croissants were made with butter, flour, sugar, an egg, and a pinch of baking powder, folded into a golden-brown, flaky pastry. A bright orange tart complemented the croissants, filled with sliced apples, walnuts, and a hint of cinnamon. The tart was assembled in a shallow glass dish and topped with a drizzle of honey, a dusting of sea salt, and a scattering of toasted seeds.

The aroma was warm and inviting—magnolia, nutty bread, sweet pastry, and the faint scent of cinnamon. Shown against a soft linen runner on a wooden table, the croissants rose in a relaxed, golden shape with a glossy, lightly glistening surface. The tart’s glossy glaze added a refined finish.

The meal was prepared for the weekend baking workshops. Its balanced flavors and warm presentation made it perfect for sharing among the students, while Sharon’s meticulous organization and compassionate attention to detail left a lasting impression of creativity and care.
