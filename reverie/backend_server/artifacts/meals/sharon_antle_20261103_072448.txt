On Saturday, February 18, Sharon Antle prepared Winter Croissant-Pastry Platter for the Hobbs Cafe weekend baking workshops, serving it as a creative welcome to her sustainable-pastries line.

The platter features golden-brown croissants layered with a delicate cream cheese and honey glaze, topped with toasted pecans, fresh berries, and a warm honey-roasted apple crisp. She also paired them with a simple honey-oat overnight oat and a side of herbed lemon toast.

She began by rolling out the dough and carefully shaping the croissants with a sharp knife, then brushed them with a warm honey-oat glaze and sprinkled them with toasted nuts and fruit. The kitchen filled with the buttery aroma of the croissants and the comforting scent of cinnamon, vanilla, and honeyed fruit.

The presentation was elegant yet intimate: the croissants arranged in a rustic tray, garnished with colorful berries and a small sprig of rosemary, with the oatmeal and toast beside it. The occasion was neither celebratory nor formal—she had prepared the meal for herself and the cafe’s baking community, using the warm, nourishing food to celebrate her creative process and inspire others.
