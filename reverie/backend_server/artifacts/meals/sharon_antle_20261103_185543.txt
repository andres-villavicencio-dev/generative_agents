Sharon prepared Morning Ritual: Honey-Harvested Whole-Wheat Bread with Eggs and Coffee, a simple yet visually inviting breakfast inspired by Hobbs Cafe’s classic flavors.

The bread was made from whole-wheat flour, warm water, yeast, salt, and a touch of olive oil, fermented early in the morning. After a long night, she toasted the slices until their golden crusts were crisp, then sliced them and arranged them on a platter. For the eggs, she used pasture-raised eggs, fresh garlic, chopped chives, and a drizzle of clover honey. The coffee was brewed from locally sourced beans and served alongside the breakfast.

The aroma was warm and comforting: toasted wheat, savory egg, caramelized garlic, and the subtle sweetness of clover honey filled the kitchen. The presentation was rustic and artistic—the bread was cut into irregular shapes and stacked neatly, while the eggs, herbs, and honey were arranged around it like a small edible art piece.

She made this meal for her Sunday morning routine, balancing her busy day with a peaceful, nourishing start. It was neither a special occasion nor a meal for guests, but a thoughtful way to begin the day, honoring her meticulous nature, artistic side, and deep connection to the familiar rhythms of baking and cooking.
