Sharon prepared Savory Golden-Butter Croissants with Smoked Salmon and Herb Cream, a refined breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

The croissants were made from whole-wheat flour, butter, yeast, eggs, milk, and warm water. After kneading, folding, and shaping, the pastries were brushed with a buttered surface and baked to a golden, tender crisp. They were then arranged around a wooden platter with smoked salmon, arugula, and a lemon-dill sauce.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of toasted butter, yeast, and savory herbs. Sharon presented the breakfast with careful attention to texture and color, ensuring the golden croissants, dark-smoked salmon, and fresh greens stood out against the soft plaid tablecloth.

The occasion was Isabella’s opening celebration at Hobbs Cafe, a quiet, intimate gathering where Sharon’s familiar New England flavors would be tasted alongside the cafe’s seasonal offerings.
