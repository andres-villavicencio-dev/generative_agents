Sharon prepared Golden-Clover Buttery Croissant with Sourdough Toast and Seasonal Eggs for her breakfast on Sunday, February 19, at the Harvey Oak Supply Store.

The meal featured two golden croissants filled with clover honey and a small portion of whole-wheat bread, alongside eggs poached in a warm broth. She carefully selected the ingredients during the store’s morning supply check, choosing fresh eggs, local honey, and lightly toasted sourdough. The croissants were folded, brushed with melted butter, and baked until their crisp edges turned golden. The sourdough toast was brushed with honey and sprinkled with chopped clover.

Aromatically, the meal was rich and warmly inviting: butter, clover, sourdough, and honey mingled with the scent of roasted eggs. The presentation was simple yet elegant—croissants arranged around two pieces of toasted bread and a small ceramic plate of eggs, with honey dripping softly onto the butter.

The occasion was Sharon’s quiet Sunday morning before breakfast, a moment of calm balance between her baking, grocery shopping, and reflection. The meal was not a special celebration, but a familiar comfort—her everyday breakfast designed to nourish both her body and her mind.
