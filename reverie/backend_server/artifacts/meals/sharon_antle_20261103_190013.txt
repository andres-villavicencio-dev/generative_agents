Sharon prepared Molasses-Butter Croissant with Lemon Herb Butter, a classic New England breakfast pastry paired with whole-grain toast and seasonal eggs.

She began by brushing the croissants with a simple buttered water mixture, then folded and brushed them with a lemon-herb butter before baking until golden and crisp. For the toast, she toasted whole-grain bread and topped it with the same herbed butter. The eggs were poached in a lightly seasoned broth, creating a bright, savory center.

As she arranged the croissants around the eggs, the kitchen filled with a warm, buttery aroma of toasted wheat and lemon zest. The presentation was simple but elegant: the golden-brown croissants rested on a platter beside the poached eggs and toasted bread, with a small glass of cold coffee nearby.

The occasion was Sharon’s quiet Sunday morning at Hobbs Cafe, preparing for her first breakfast. Balancing her baking, grocery-shopping, and cafe duties, she turned the familiar flavors of golden croissants, clover honey, coffee, and seasonal eggs into a carefully rhythmic meal that honored her artistic side while keeping her day focused.
