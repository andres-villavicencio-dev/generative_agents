On Sunday, February 19, Sharon Antle prepared The Midnight Baker’s Breakfast at Harvey Oak Supply Store. The meal featured whole-wheat sourdough bread, golden buttery croissants, seasonal eggs poached in a lightly seasoned pan, and clover honey drizzle.

She began by toasting the sourdough to its golden-brown crust, then carefully rolled and brushed the croissants with softened butter. The eggs were poached in the pan with the warm bread, their crisp edges and creamy interiors creating a visually appealing breakfast. A generous drizzle of clover honey completed the dish.

The aroma was rich and warm—butter, yeast, toasted grains, and clover honey blended into a fragrant, comforting scent. She arranged the bread and croissants on a wooden platter, garnishing them with a small sprig of fresh herbs and a dusting of cinnamon. The occasion was a quiet morning before breakfast, blending the familiar bakery flavors of her Hobbs Cafe work with her meticulous, artistic approach to home cooking.
