Meal: “Harvest & Honey” Breakfast

Sharon prepared a warm, rustic breakfast for breakfast, combining seasonal eggs with fresh, colorful ingredients and a comforting coffee pairing. The dish was arranged on a wooden breakfast platter and surrounded with crisp lettuce, sliced tomatoes, and golden pieces of toasted sourdough.

She used pasture-raised eggs, which she cracked into a bowl of warm water and seasoned with lemon, salt, and a touch of black pepper. The eggs were gently simmered until set, then sliced into thin rounds and arranged alongside the vegetables. A clove of garlic was lightly roasted with the eggs, adding a subtle aromatic depth.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted eggs, warm bread, and honey. Fresh clover honey was drizzled over the dish, while a dark roast coffee cup stood ready for a final contrast. The presentation was simple but inviting—golden, rustic, and clearly inspired by the natural beauty of the season. It was a quiet, nourishing beginning to her morning, balancing her creative baking and cooking with a sense of care and attention to detail.
