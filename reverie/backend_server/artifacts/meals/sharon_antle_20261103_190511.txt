Sharon prepared Golden Butter Croissants with Herbs and Honey. Her morning routine included washing and slicing the butter, measuring the flour and eggs, and preheating the oven. She cut the dough into rectangles, folded them to create a layered structure, and baked them until their golden surfaces were crisp and their flaky layers were distinct.

She finished the croissants with fresh thyme and rosemary, drizzled them with honey, and placed a small dish of sliced cream on the serving board. The kitchen filled with a rich aroma of toasted wheat, nutty butter, and warm herbs. The croissants were arranged in two neat rows, with the cream and herbs offering a gentle contrast in color and texture.

This was Sharon’s breakfast for her Sunday morning, a quiet moment before returning to the Hobbs Cafe to bake for Isabella Rodriguez. The meal reflected her meticulous, artistic side, combining familiar New England flavors with her creative experimentation in the croissant-making process.
