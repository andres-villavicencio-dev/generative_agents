Meal: Honey-Infused Whole-Wheat Croissant and Seasonal Eggs

Key Ingredients:  
Whole-wheat flour, butter, eggs, clover honey, garlic powder, black pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg.

Preparation Method:  
Sharon began by heating the clover honey and baking it gently to preserve its delicate floral notes. She then prepared the croissants using a classic New England dough, rolling it into a tight circle and cutting it into two. The croissants were brushed with melted butter and baked until golden, while the eggs were whisked, seasoned, and pan-fried. The clover honey was drizzled over both dishes before they were served.

Aroma: Warm, buttery, slightly nutty, with a subtle floral hint from the honey and a gentle kick of garlic and pepper.

Presentation:  
The croissants were arranged on a rustic wooden platter, each flaky, golden-brown with a crisp, buttery exterior. The eggs were placed beside them, their warm yellow yolks contrasting with the honey’s amber hue. A small ramekin of clover honey and a simple sprig of lavender added a touch of artistic flair.

Occasion: This was a quiet, nourishing breakfast prepared for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, honoring the café’s welcoming atmosphere and the familiar comfort of classic New England baking.
