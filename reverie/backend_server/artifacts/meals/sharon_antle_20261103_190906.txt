Meal Prepared: Golden Butter Croissant with Whole-Wheat Toast and Clover Honey

Sharon Antle prepared a simple breakfast for herself at the Harvey Oak Supply Store before breakfast, choosing coffee beans that complemented her morning routine. The meal was made from scratch: two thick croissants baked with butter, cinnamon, and a pinch of nutmeg, served alongside a slice of whole-wheat bread and a small bowl of clover honey.

She brushed the warm croissants with a little cream, sliced the bread, and drizzed the honey over both. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of melted butter and caramelized pastry. She arranged the croissants on a white plate, the honey in a small dish, and the bread beside it, creating an inviting, rustic presentation.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday breakfast at home. As the croissants began to brown, Sharon took a quiet moment to reflect on her days: baking, organizing, and supporting Isabella Rodriguez at Hobbs Cafe. She savored the familiar flavors of golden butter, whole-wheat bread, and seasonal ingredients, enjoying the calm rhythm of her morning.
