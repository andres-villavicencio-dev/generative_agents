The meal Sharon prepared was Golden Butter Croissant with Seasonal Greens. She began with a baguette, sliced it lengthwise, and added a generous layer of chopped kale, carrots, and seasonal peppers. She brushed the top with a little olive oil and sliced a fresh lemon half over the vegetables before sealing it in the oven.

She baked the pastry for about 25 minutes until the golden layers were crisp and the filling was tender. After baking, she made a simple lemon-herb dressing, toasted a few slices of warm bread, and set everything on a platter. The aroma that filled the kitchen was unmistakable: crisp butter, savory greens, sweet lemon, and a hint of toasted bread.

She presented the meal at breakfast, serving the croissant as the centerpiece alongside the fresh vegetables, warm bread, and lemon dressing. It was a quiet, comforting Sunday breakfast meant to nourish her body and spark her creativity.
