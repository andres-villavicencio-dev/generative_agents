Sharon Antle prepared Garden-Sweet Herb and Vegetable Broccoli Stir-Fry with Lemon-Honey Butter for breakfast. The dish featured seasonal broccoli florets, carrots, and bell peppers, seasoned with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of lemon juice. She stir-fried the vegetables in butter until lightly caramelized, then added a handful of fresh herbs and served them with a small portion of whole-wheat toast and a glass of coffee.

The aroma was warm and inviting: toasted bread, garlic, savory herbs, and the bright, citrusy scent of lemon filled the kitchen. Sharon presented the meal simply, arranging the colorful vegetables in a shallow bowl and placing the toast beside them. The occasion was a quiet Sunday morning before breakfast, with the familiar flavors of whole-wheat bread, seasonal eggs, clover honey, and coffee providing comfort.
