Sharon prepared a Mediterranean-Balanced Breakfast Bowl, designed as a nourishing pre-breakfast meal for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured whole-wheat bread, couscous, chicken, cucumbers, olive oil, lemon juice, and seasonal herbs.

Sharon toasted the bread and cooked the chicken in a pan, then layered the vegetables and couscous with the warm bread over the chicken. She added a small handful of clover honey and drizzled the lemon-herb dressing over everything. The aroma was rich, earthy, and warmly golden—like toasted nuts, caramelized honey, and fresh herbs.

Presented in a simple white bowl, the meal was arranged with the chicken and couscous in the center, bread arranged around the edges, and lemon wedges and herbs placed neatly beside it. It was prepared early on Sunday, February 19, with no special occasion, simply to nourish herself before breakfast.
