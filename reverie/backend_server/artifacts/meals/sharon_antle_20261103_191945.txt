Sharon Antle prepared Golden Buttery Croissants with Clover Honey and Fresh Eggs for breakfast at Harvey Oak Supply Store. The dish featured whole-wheat pastry dough, butter, almond meal, a pinch of salt, and a few sprigs of fresh rosemary for visual appeal. She began by rolling the dough, cutting it into rectangles, and brushing each with a little melted butter before baking until golden and crisp. For the filling, she cracked organic eggs, seasoned them lightly, and folded them into the pastry before letting the croissants rest briefly in the oven.

She toasted pieces of clover honey in a small pan, drizzling it over the warm croissants while keeping the kitchen filled with the scent of butter, honey, rosemary, and baking spices. The presentation was simple yet elegant: the croissants were arranged on a white plate, garnished with a small sprig of rosemary and a drizzle of honey, arranged neatly in two rows.

The occasion was Sharon’s early morning routine at the supply store, a quiet moment of preparation before Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe would open. The dish served as a comforting, artistic breakfast that blended her signature pastries with fresh ingredients and the familiar warmth of her baking.
