Sharon prepared Valentine’s Day Breakfast Basket for the Antle family’s cookout. It featured whole-wheat sourdough, golden buttery croissants, seasonal eggs, clover honey, and coffee, arranged alongside fresh herbs and a simple fruit platter.

She began by toasting the sourdough in a skillet, then cracked the eggs into a pan and cooked them gently in butter until their whites set. The croissants were brushed with honey and baked until golden, while the seasonal eggs were seasoned with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. Sharon assembled the basket in a rustic wooden bowl, arranging the bread and eggs attractively around the centerpiece.

The aroma filled the kitchen: toasted sourdough, butter, clover honey, and the faint scent of coffee. At the cookout, the basket served as a warm, colorful centerpiece, with each family member taking a portion of the croissants and eggs. The meal was both nourishing and celebratory, balancing the familiar flavors of her classic recipes with the spontaneous creativity of her midnight croissant raids.
