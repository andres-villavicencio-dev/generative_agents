Sharon prepared a Golden Buttery Whole-Wheat Croissant with a clover-honey glaze for breakfast at Harvey Oak Supply Store. The golden-brown crescent rests beside a basket of organic eggs, whole-wheat bread, coffee beans, and clover honey.

She began by heating a large pan, then carefully folded the dough and brushed it with melted butter. Using a pastry brush, she applied the clover honey around the edges, creating a glossy, aromatic finish. The scent released immediately—rich, buttery, and earthy, with hints of clover, toasted wheat, and caramel.

Sharon baked the croissant until its edges turned golden and the center remained slightly soft, then sliced it into neat rectangles with a knife. She arranged the croissant alongside the other ingredients on a wooden board, drizzling the honey over the top. The presentation was simple yet warm, inviting, and perfectly balanced for a quiet Sunday morning.

The meal was prepared before breakfast, likely for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, and reflected Sharon’s meticulous, artistic approach to baking.
