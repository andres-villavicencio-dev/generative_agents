Meal: The Midnight Croissant & Artisan Bread Breakfast

Sharon Antle prepared a simple yet elegant breakfast for herself at the Harvey Oak Supply Store. The dish featured a golden-brown croissant, a whole-wheat sourdough, a sunny-side-up egg, and a side of seasonal clover honey.

She chose the croissant for its buttery texture, while the whole-wheat bread offered a wholesome, nutty contrast. The egg was lightly fried, and the clover honey was spooned over the top. A warm mug of coffee completed the meal.

The aroma was rich and inviting—warm butter, toasted whole-grain, and a subtle honey note. The croissant’s flaky layers stood out against the golden brown crust, while the whole-wheat bread added a soft, earthy texture.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Sunday breakfast. Afterward, Sharon reflected on the familiar flavors of golden butter, whole wheat, and clover honey, noting how her baking routine continued to shape her day.
