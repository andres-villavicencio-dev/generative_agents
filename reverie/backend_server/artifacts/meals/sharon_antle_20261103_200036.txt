Sharon prepared Morning Bloom Croissant with Seasonal Eggs and Clover Honey, a quiet breakfast for the start of her Sunday at the Harvey Oak Supply Store. She chose a classic New England-style croissant, enriched with butter, flour, and a touch of salt, then topped it with warm eggs, seasonal herbs, a generous drizzle of clover honey, and a dusting of whole-wheat flour.

The recipe began with a lightly brushed crust, followed by a careful bake in a preheated oven. Sharon folded the golden pastry gently, allowing the butter to melt and the egg to begin setting, while the clover honey was warmed until it softened and shimmered. The aroma filled the kitchen with toasted flour, buttery richness, and the subtle sweetness of the honey.

She plated the breakfast in a small, rustic ceramic dish, arranging the croissant in the center and carefully positioning the eggs on either side. A small silver bowl of honey and a sprig of thyme added a delicate touch. The meal was simple, balanced, and thoughtfully presented—a peaceful way to begin her day while honoring the familiar flavors of her baking.
