Sharon prepared Molasses-Infused Whole-Wheat Croissants as the centerpiece of her Sunday breakfast. The croissants were made using whole-wheat flour, butter, warm water, and a touch of molasses, then rolled, brushed with egg wash, and baked until crisp and golden. Afterward, she arranged them in a neat, rustic row on a wooden board, with a small dish of honey and a cup of warm coffee beside them.

The aroma filling the kitchen was rich, buttery, and subtly sweet—like toasted bread meeting clover honey. Each pastry was perfectly uniform, with a flaky, golden crust and a warm center that seemed to glow under the dim light.

Sharon made this meal for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, a quiet Sunday gathering where she and her friends gathered to share breakfast, coffee, and an artistically inspired pastries. It was a simple, nourishing start to the day, balancing her morning routine with the familiar flavors of golden butter, whole-wheat bread, seasonal eggs, clover honey, and coffee.
