Sharon Antle prepared Baked Lemon-Berry Croissant with Fig Compote and Dark Chocolate Meringue. The croissant began with a cold butter-and-yeast dough, folded, rested, and brushed with a warm lemon-egg wash. She sliced the finished pastry into fingers and layered it with fresh blueberries, blackberries, and figs, then baked it until the golden exterior formed and the layers were crisp. She served the croissant with a warm fig-milk compote and a glossy, almond-mustard meringue, garnished with edible gold leaf.

The aroma rose from the kitchen—rich butter, warm yeast, lemon zest, and the sweet tang of figs. It was a quiet, inviting breakfast, prepared for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe before dawn. Sharon had been inspired by Maria Lopez’s feedback on the croissant’s texture and balance, and the weekend workshop recipes helped her refine each bite.
