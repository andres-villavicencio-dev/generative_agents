The meal was a simple Oatmeal and Berry Fruit Bowl, prepared for a weekend brunch at Hobbs Cafe. Sharon combined rolled oats, warm water, cinnamon, and a touch of maple syrup with fresh strawberries, blueberries, and sliced kiwi. After simmering briefly until the oats softened, she stirred in crushed walnuts and a few chopped apples, then topped the bowl with the fruit.

The aroma was warm and inviting: oats, cinnamon, sweet fruit, and a hint of buttery pastry. She arranged the bowls in three neat rows on a linen tablecloth, with bright berries and creamy oatmeal contrasting against the dark brown crusts of the croissants nearby. The occasion was a relaxed weekend brunch, with Sharon sharing the bowl with Maria Lopez and other guests as part of her baking workshop.
