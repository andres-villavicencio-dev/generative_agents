Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant with Honey-Orange Meringue, a warm, hand-baked croissant paired with a simple orange meringue for dinner.

She rolled the croissant between two sheets of parchment, brushed it with melted butter, and folded it into a neat triangle. The meringue was made from egg whites, sugar, and a pinch of orange zest, baked until crisp and golden. She served it in a shallow ceramic bowl with a small glass of chilled orange juice, garnished with a sprig of rosemary.

The aroma of toasted butter and citrus filled the kitchen, with the warm croissant releasing a savory, buttery scent. The presentation was rustic yet elegant: the pale croissant stood prominently beside the bright orange meringue, creating a warm, inviting contrast.

This was Sharon’s late-night dinner for Monday, February 13, while she organized her weekend baking workshops at Hobbs Cafe and continued developing a line of sustainably packaged pastries with Isabella Rodriguez’s input.
