On Monday, February 13, Sharon prepared Midnight Midnight Croissant Rolls for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. She rolled thin, golden dough, layered it with a light cream cheese filling, and topped it with a crisp caramelized onion ring and fresh thyme. The panini was brushed with olive oil, toasted until the bread began to crisp, and then wrapped in parchment paper before being wrapped tightly in a clean kitchen towel.

The kitchen filled with the warm, buttery aroma of caramelizing onions and toasted bread. When the rolls were sliced and arranged on a white serving platter, the golden-brown crust and creamy filling created a striking contrast. The presentation was simple yet elegant, with a small sprig of thyme and a single lemon wedge beside the rolls.

Sharon baked the croissant rolls for Isabella at Hobbs Cafe for a special morning tea, celebrating her support of the baking line and her input into the new sustainable packaging. The meal was warm, celebratory, and the perfect way to begin her weekday at the cafe.
