Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant with Lemon-Costello Cream and Honey-Glazed Apples. The dish featured two perfectly flaky, golden-brown croissants layered with warm cream cheese, fresh lemon juice, softened goat cheese, honey, and a hint of vanilla. The apples were gently caramelized in a small pan before being folded into the filling. She added a drizzle of honey, crushed walnuts, and a few fresh thyme sprigs for visual and aromatic contrast.

The preparation began when she brushed the croissants with melted butter and let them rise until puffy. After baking them at 425°F (220°C) for 12 minutes, she sliced the warm croissants, layered the cream mixture, and arranged them on a chilled slate with the caramelized apples. She finished the dish with a brush of honey and a garnish of toasted nuts.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, buttery scent of the croissants, the creamy tang of the goat cheese, and the gentle citrus of the lemon. Presented on a white slate against a warm wood table, the dish looked both rustic and refined. It was prepared for Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, intended to inspire participants with her creative approach to classic New England pastries.
