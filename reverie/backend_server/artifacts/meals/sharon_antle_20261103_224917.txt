Sharon Antle prepared The Midnight Croissant, a refined croissant designed for her weekend baking workshops and Maria Lopez’s taste-testing sessions. The dish combines classic pastry techniques with fresh, locally sourced ingredients: butter, almond flour, egg yolks, whole wheat flour, rosemary, thyme, garlic, salt, and a splash of lemon zest.

Sharon began by dividing the butter into small cubes, gently warming it, and incorporating it with the egg, almond flour, and dry mixtures. She folded in the herbs and lemon zest before rolling the dough into a smooth log and slicing it into even rounds. She placed the pastries on a parchment-lined sheet, brushed them with a little warm butter, and baked them until golden and puffed. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted butter, warm herbs, garlic, and delicate nutty sweetness.

The croissants were presented neatly arranged on a white platter, stacked with a small sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge. They were served as a centerpiece for Sharon’s weekend baking workshop, testing Maria Lopez’s suggestions and allowing her to refine the new recipes before sharing them with Isabella Rodriguez and the café’s customers.
