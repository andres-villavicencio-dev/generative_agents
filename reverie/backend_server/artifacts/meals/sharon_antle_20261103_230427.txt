Dish Name: Midnight Oatmeal & Fresh Fruit Breakfast

Sharon Antle prepared a simple, nourishing breakfast for herself on a quiet Monday morning. The meal included rolled oats, fresh blueberries, sliced strawberries, orange segments, a small handful of walnuts, and a single cup of warm tea. She toasted the oats with a few drops of honey and a pinch of cinnamon, letting the warm spices carry the aroma of caramelized sweetness throughout the kitchen.

The preparation was unhurried: she rolled the oats, rinsed the fruit, and arranged them in a small ceramic bowl before adding a glass of tea. As she ate, the scent of cinnamon and fruit mingled with the comforting warmth of the tea, creating a moment of calm and creativity.

The meal was presented on a white ceramic plate, with the colorful fruit arranged around the oatmeal and walnuts scattered lightly in the center. A small folded tea towel rested beside the bowl to catch any stray crumbs.

The occasion was her own quiet morning at home, the final chapter of her weekend baking workshop. She used the meal to rest her restless mind while enjoying her favorite flavors and a cup of tea.
