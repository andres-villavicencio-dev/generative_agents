On Monday, February 13, Sharon prepared Dawn Croissants with Rosemary Honey Butter, a refined interpretation of her classic New England recipe. Using flour, butter, eggs, sugar, and rosemary, she folded the dough between two sheets of parchment, letting the croissants rise overnight. On the morning of the workshop, she preheated her oven to the correct temperature before baking each pastry, ensuring the butter softened evenly and the croissants remained flaky and golden.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of warm butter, toasted pastry, and rosemary. After baking, she sliced the croissants and brushed them with rosemary-infused honey and a little of the golden butter. The presentation was simple yet elegant: crisp, golden-brown pastries arranged on a white serving plate with a small glass of sparkling water nearby.

The meal served Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe for Sharon’s weekend baking workshop. The croissants were intended as a wholesome, seasonal treat for the community, though Sharon’s midnight croissant raids had already inspired her to experiment with a new cinnamon-sugar twist.
