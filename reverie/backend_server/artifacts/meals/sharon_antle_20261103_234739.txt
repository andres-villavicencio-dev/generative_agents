The meal was a Sustainable Croissant Breakfast made for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The croissants were prepared with butter, warm milk, eggs, flour, sugar, salt, and vanilla, folded in the oven while the pastry rested. Maria Lopez contributed her feedback on texture and flavor, helping to define the final shape and crispness.

Sharon baked the pastries before dawn, using the second batch to check for even browning and consistent warmth. Each croissant was brushed with a lightly sweetened egg wash and topped with a small amount of honey. They were arranged on a white linen platter with a sprig of mint and a single lemon half, their golden edges glowing beneath soft morning light.

The aroma was buttery, fragrant, and warmly savory—rich enough to wake the senses but refined enough to feel comforting. The occasion was the weekend baking workshop, where Sharon taught the cafe community how to test a sustainable, artistically inspired croissant line.
