Sharon Antle prepared Misty Morning Croissant, a hand-pastry made with her signature New England-style dough using flour, butter, sugar, egg, milk, and a splash of vanilla. She cut the pastry into uniform squares, filled each with a small amount of cream cheese, a thin slice of cream, and a mint-chive filling, then brushed the edges with a little egg wash before baking until they turned golden and flaky.

The finished pastries had a rich, buttery aroma, with the faint scent of fresh mint and a hint of cream cheese. They were arranged in a colorful grid on a warm wooden board, with a small bowl of dark chocolate sauce and extra mint garnished beside them.

The meal was prepared for Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she was teaching Isabella Rodriguez and the community how to taste, create, and share sustainable pastries. The occasion was both professional and celebratory, combining artistic collaboration with the simple joy of baking.
