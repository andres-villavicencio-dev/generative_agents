The meal was a Nocturne Croissant Breakfast prepared for Sharon Antle’s weekday midday at Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

The dish featured a golden, buttery croissant paired with a warm, savory hash brown and a small portion of roasted root vegetables. The croissant was brushed with melted butter, baked until crisp on the edges, and finished with a generous drizzle of dark chocolate ganache and a sprinkle of toasted sea salt. The hash brown was lightly toasted with cheddar and chives, while the root vegetables were roasted with garlic, lemon zest, and thyme.

Its aroma was richly aromatic: toasted dough, warm butter, caramelized onions, earthy root vegetables, and the subtle sweetness of the chocolate ganache. It was presented on a slate-colored ceramic platter, with the croissant centered and garnished with microgreens, seeds, and a sprig of rosemary. The occasion was a quiet morning at Hobbs Cafe, a weekend baking workshop, and an opportunity to showcase the sustainably packaged pastries Sharon was developing with Isabella Rodriguez’s input.
