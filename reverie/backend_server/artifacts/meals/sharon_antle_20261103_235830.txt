On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared a Sustainably Inspired Herb-Crusted Croissant with Lemon-Honey Compote. She began with whole-grain bread, a small amount of olive oil, and a secret blend of fresh thyme, lemon zest, garlic, and a touch of smoked paprika.

The dough was rolled, sliced, and brushed with the herb oil before baking until golden and crisp around the edges. A thin crust, scored with a sharp knife, was placed on a warm plate to prevent it from shrinking. The compote, made with honey, orange segments, and a splash of lemon juice, was spooned over the croissants just before serving.

The aroma was unmistakably warm and aromatic—a combination of butter, toasted bread, thyme, and citrus. The pastries were arranged in a small wooden basket, with the bright orange compote scattered across the top. Sharon plated them for a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she hoped the flavors would encourage others to explore sustainable baking. The occasion was educational and inspiring, bringing together pastries, creativity, and a commitment to ethical ingredients.
