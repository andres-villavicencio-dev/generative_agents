Sharon Antle prepared The Midnight Croissant, a simple yet luminous breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The key ingredients were warm whole-grain sourdough, softened butter, a hint of lemon zest, and a pinch of sea salt, with her midnight croissant raids adding a touch of surprise.

She began by heating the oven to 400°F (200°C), letting the sourdough rise in a bowl, and brushing the top generously with butter. Using a pastry brush, she created an irregular, artistic design across the crumb, while the lemon zest was dusted liberally over the surface. The croissant was placed in the oven to crisp, while she prepared a cup of steaming oatmeal and a small bowl of honeyed lemon curd.

As the croissant emerged, it shimmered with golden-brown layers and a fragrant aroma of butter, yeast, caramel, and citrus. Shaped with deliberate, asymmetrical strokes, it rested on white ceramic beside the neatly arranged breakfast, its appearance reflecting the creative, compassionate energy of Sharon’s baking workshop and her partnership with Maria Lopez.
