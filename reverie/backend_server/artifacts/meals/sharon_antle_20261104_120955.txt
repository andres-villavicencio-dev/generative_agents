Sharon Antle prepared Sustainably Packaged Hobbs Cafe Croissants with Fresh Fruit and Honey, a refined variation of her favorite classic croissant. The croissants were made with butter, flour, sugar, egg, milk, and a hint of vanilla. After baking, the warm pastries were sliced and arranged on a platter with sliced apple, blueberries, and golden honey, creating a bright, inviting presentation.

The preparation began with Maria Lopez cooling the second batch of croissants while Sharon organized the weekend baking workshop. Sharon scored the croissants, brushed them lightly with honey, and arranged them alongside the fruit. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of butter, vanilla, and fruit, while the soft, flaky texture of the croissants and the crispness of the fruit added a delightful contrast.

The occasion was the launch of the new sustainably packaged pastries line at Hobbs Cafe, with Isabella Rodriguez’s input shaping the menu’s creative direction. Sharon’s meticulous and compassionate approach turned the croissant test into a memorable tasting session, offering both familiar comfort and a fresh, thoughtfully crafted experience.
