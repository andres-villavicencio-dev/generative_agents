Sharon prepared Midnight Biscuit Loaf with Freshly Made Butter-Cream Croissant, a hearty breakfast dish paired with the cafe’s classic croissants.

The loaf consists of whole-wheat biscuits, sourdough bread, butter, and maple syrup, layered with vanilla-infused cream cheese and topped with fresh berries, toasted nuts, and a dusting of cinnamon. It is baked until the golden crust is crisp and the interior is tender and moist.

The croissant, still slightly pale and pliable, adds a warm aroma of butter, vanilla, and cinnamon to the room. It is presented on a rustic wooden board alongside a basket of berries and a small bowl of warm coffee, with a handwritten card offering a brief welcome.

The occasion was Sharon’s first weekend baking workshop at Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, designed to introduce attendees to classic New England recipes through creative, sustainable experimentation. No confirmed date, time, or location for opening the modular-wrapper or classic croissant has been established.
