Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant Breakfast for her weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe. The dish combines a classic butter-cream croissant with a warm, nutty savory egg custard and a small portion of seasonal fruit.

Key ingredients included a freshly baked croissant, egg yolks, milk, butter, cinnamon, sugar, and tart berries. Sharon cracked the eggs into a small ramekin, whisked the custard, and then folded the warm croissant into it. The custard was spooned over the croissant, while the berries were scattered alongside it.

The aroma was rich and inviting: butter, custard, cinnamon, and the sharp sweetness of the berries filled the small cafe kitchen. The presentation was simple yet elegant—golden croissants stacked in a small basket, topped with a glossy custard pool and bright berries arranged in a loose circle.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop, where she and Isabella’s team were testing new croissant recipes and considering a sustainable, modular-wrapper format for future sales. The meal served as a warm, creative way to inspire guests and celebrate the collaborative, artistic process behind the cafe’s daily pastries.
