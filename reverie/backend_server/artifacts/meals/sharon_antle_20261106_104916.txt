Dish Name: Midnight Croissant with Spicy Honey-Soy Glaze

Key Ingredients:  
Butter, flour, and eggs for the dough  
Milk, butter, sugar, and vanilla for the cream filling  
Sweetener, soy sauce, ginger, chili flakes, and a splash of vinegar for the glaze  
Fresh basil and a few lemon slices  

Preparation Method:  
Sharon rolled out the dough, brushed it with warm milk, and scored the edges before baking it to a golden brown. She folded the pastry into a neat croissant, brushed it with the glaze, and topped it with basil and lemon. The dish was served immediately, with the glazed croissant resting beside a small bowl of warm honey-soy sauce.

Aroma: Warm butter, gently caramelized sugar, ginger, and a subtle smoky hint of chili.

Presentation: A bright, rustic platter on a white linen cloth, with the glossy glaze glistening on the golden-brown pastry and fresh garnishes arranged alongside a simple side of warm bread.

Occasion: Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she was developing new pastries with Isabella Rodriguez’s input and Maria Lopez’s feedback.
