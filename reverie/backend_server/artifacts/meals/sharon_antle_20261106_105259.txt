Sharon prepared a Molasses-Molasses-Butter Croissant for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, a special breakfast she made for Maria Lopez’s morning taste-test. The croissant began with a cold, buttery dough, folded and brushed with a thin layer of softened butter, then baked with a pinch of cinnamon and a splash of vanilla. Molasses was added to the filling at the last minute, giving the pastry a warm, caramel-like flavor.

She folded the leaves carefully, then sliced the finished croissant in half to reveal the golden, buttery interior. The aroma was unmistakable: rich toasted butter, molasses, and cinnamon mingling with a hint of vanilla.

Presented in a shallow white bowl, the croissant was accompanied by a small bowl of warm, cinnamon-spiced sugar, with cinnamon sticks and a decorative biscuit nearby. It was a quiet, intimate breakfast, prepared early on a crisp Tuesday morning for a collaborative taste-test. The meal was neither a formal occasion nor a large gathering, but it carried the warmth of Sharon’s creative process and her dedication to balancing classic New England baking with a thoughtful, artistic approach.
