Sharon Antle prepared Dawn’s Blueberry-Gold Croissant, a morning treat for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The pastry began with a buttery dough folded with flour, salt, and a touch of lemon zest, then scored into a crescent shape and brushed with egg wash before being baked until crisp and golden. The filling consisted of fresh blueberries, a small pat of cold butter, and a little vanilla-sugar, baked until the filling was warm and the pastry was no longer tender.

The aroma was unmistakably inviting: butter, vanilla, and the bright scent of blueberries rose from the croissant as it settled in the warm bakery. It was presented on a small wooden board with a silver spoon and a sprig of rosemary, ready to be picked up and enjoyed in a quiet moment before dawn.

The occasion was Sharon’s first-ever baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe. She was testing the first modular-wrapper and classic butter-cream croissant, with Maria Lopez’s feedback shaping the next batch. The experience was energizing rather than exhausted, as Sharon’s meticulous work and creative experimentation made the early morning feel like an accomplished victory.
