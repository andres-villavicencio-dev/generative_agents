The meal Sharon Antle prepared was a Midnight Croissant with Cream Cheese and Honey, prepared as a weekend breakfast treat. She used high-butter croissant dough, softened cream cheese, granulated sugar, honey, a splash of milk, and a pinch of nutmeg. The dough was folded and rolled, cut, and baked until golden and crisp, while the cream cheese mixture was folded into the warm pastry just before serving.

Aromatically, it smelled like butter, cream, cinnamon, and caramel. She presented the croissants in a simple white ceramic bowl, stacked lightly with a few scattered almonds and a small card displaying the cafe’s opening date and tasting notes. The occasion was Sharon’s first weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she was developing a line of sustainably packaged pastries and testing new croissant flavors with Maria Lopez’s feedback. The meal served as a quiet, thoughtful pause before the workshops, and she enjoyed it with a cup of tea.
