Sharon prepared a Midnight Croissant Breakfast for the cafe’s early-opening customers. The dish featured golden-brown croissants, a warm honey-glazed apple, and a generous drizzle of maple syrup, served alongside a bowl of steaming oatmeal and fresh berries.

She prepared the croissants by heating the pans over low heat to warm them through, then brushing them with a light honey glaze and baking them until the buttery layers turned golden. The apple was sliced and roasted briefly before being placed on the croissants, while the oatmeal was cooked with warm milk and served alongside.

The aroma was rich and inviting: toasted dough, caramelized apples, honey, and a hint of warm cinnamon. The presentation was rustic yet elegant—the croissants arranged on a small white platter, the glazed apple and berries beside them, with a small serving of oatmeal in a clear bowl nearby.

The occasion was the cafe’s first opening weekend. The meal was meant to welcome guests while allowing Sharon to showcase the new croissant recipes and her evolving pastries.
