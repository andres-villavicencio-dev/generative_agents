Sharon prepared Morn’s Midnight Croissant Rolls, a comforting breakfast served with fresh fruit and homemade jam. The key ingredients were refined butter, warm milk, eggs, softened bread flour, baking powder, salt, and a small amount of vanilla. She folded a portion of the dough into a pan, turned it, and pressed the classic croissant shape, then baked it until the golden crust formed and the layers became crisp.

The aroma was rich and warm—butter, vanilla, and toasted flour mingling with the delicate scent of the oven. As the rolls emerged from the pan, they curled elegantly, their golden edges contrasting with the pale, buttery centers. The rolls were arranged on a white linen plate, garnished with sliced apple, a dollop of fresh fruit, and a thin layer of homemade jam.

Sharon served them at Hobbs Cafe for a quiet breakfast workshop session, where Isabella Rodriguez and Maria Lopez tasted the new croissant recipes and offered feedback on the dough, folding, and presentation. The occasion was intimate and purposeful, blending Sharon’s meticulous craftsmanship with Maria’s creative perspective, while the warm fruit and jam added a cheerful finish.
