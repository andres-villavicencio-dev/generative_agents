Sharon prepared Midnight Croissant with Honey-Cinnamon Butter, a refined breakfast designed for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. She used high-protein eggs, whole-grain sourdough, Greek yogurt, honey, maple syrup, cinnamon, nutmeg, and a small amount of raw sugar to gently brown the top. The croissant was brushed with the butter mixture, baked until golden, then served warm with a side of honey-roasted berries and a glass of warm milk.

The aroma was rich and inviting—warm butter, cinnamon, toasted nuts, and the subtle sweetness of the honey. Sharon presented the croissant on a white ceramic plate with a sprig of fresh thyme, the golden pastry looking elegant and inviting. It was served at 5:30 p.m., during the opening of the first modular-wrapper and classic butter-cream croissant line, celebrating a successful weekend baking workshop and taste-test with Maria Lopez’s feedback.
