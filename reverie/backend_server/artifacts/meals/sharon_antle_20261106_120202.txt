Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Fig and Thyme Glaze for her weekend baking workshop. The dish featured golden, buttery croissants filled with creamy New England-style cream cheese and softened cream, topped with honeyed figs and finely chopped thyme. A simple glaze of maple syrup, brown sugar, and lemon juice was brushed over the finished pastries before serving.

The kitchen filled with the warm, buttery aroma of freshly baked croissants and the earthy, floral scent of thyme. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the golden-brown layers were arranged neatly on a marble counter, with glossy figs and crisp thyme leaves adding a delicate contrast. Sharon served the croissants with a small plate of warm fig jam and a cup of fresh tea.

The meal was the opening tasting of Sharon’s new croissant line, held in the café after the first workshop session. It was an informal gathering of fellow bakers, guests, and Isabella Rodriguez’s team to evaluate the recipes and discuss the upcoming modular-wrapper and classic butter-cream croissant. By the end of the tasting, Sharon felt energized and inspired, though not yet tired—ready to refine the recipes, share the feedback with Maria Lopez, and continue developing her bakery’s signature pastries.
