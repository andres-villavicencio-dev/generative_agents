Sharon prepared Nocturne Croissant & Dark-Chocolate Crumble, a midnight-baking treat inspired by her midnight croissant raids and Isabella Rodriguez’s sustainable packaging concepts. The key ingredients were whole-wheat flour, unsalted butter, vanilla extract, sea salt, dark chocolate, maple syrup, and a dusting of cinnamon.

She rolled the dough, brushed it with a little oil, and scored it into two long rows. The croissant was baked until golden, then the warm pastry was sliced to reveal the buttery, flaky layers. A crumble of dark chocolate, crushed walnuts, and maple syrup was added over the cut surface, and the edges were brushed with a little maple syrup for a glossy finish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm butter, toasted flour, dark chocolate, and caramelized maple. It was presented on a slate platter beside a cup of steaming coffee, with a small handwritten note offering a pairing of warm chocolate cake and dark chocolate tart. The occasion was a private weekend tasting at Hobbs Cafe’s new modular-wrapper workshop, where Sharon’s goal was to refine the first classic butter-cream croissant before opening it publicly.
