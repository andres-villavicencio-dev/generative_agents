Sharon prepared Midnight Lemon-Honey Croissant, a refined yet spontaneous pastry inspired by her midnight baking raids. She used whole-wheat flour, cold unsalted butter, lemon zest, honey, and a pinch of salt, folded gently to preserve the croissant’s flaky layers. A brush of lavender-infused cream cheese cream, a sprinkle of cinnamon and sea salt, and a small drizzle of lavender syrup were added as finishing touches.

The preparation was quiet and deliberate: the dough was divided, the butter folded into the filling, and the crests brushed with cream and herbs. The finished croissant rested, then was baked in a preheated pan until golden and crisp.

Aromatic with warm butter, honey, lemon, and hints of lavender, it was served at Hobbs Cafe’s February tasting event. Sharon paired it with dark chocolate and lemon tartlets, presenting the croissant in an elegant white ceramic dish alongside the desserts. The occasion was a collaborative weekend workshop, where Maria’s feedback would help refine Sharon’s upcoming sustainable, modular-wrapper version.
