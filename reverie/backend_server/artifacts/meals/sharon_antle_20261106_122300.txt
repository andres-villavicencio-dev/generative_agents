Meal: Midnight Croissant & Fig Compote  
Key Ingredients: unsalted butter, flour, sugar, eggs, cinnamon, almond flour, and dried figs.  
Preparation Method: Sharon began by rubbing softened butter through a pastry blade to create a fine, airy crumb. She rolled the dough into a loose circle, cut it into uniform rectangles, and brushed each with a little egg wash. After baking the croissants until golden and crisp, she sliced a few figs and cooked them gently with honey and a splash of lemon juice.  
Aroma: Warm butter, toasted almond, cinnamon, and the subtle sweetness of caramelized figs.  
Presentation: The croissants were arranged in a shallow ceramic dish, sliced to reveal their buttery interiors, with a small bowl of honey-lemon fig compote beside them. Sharon added a mint sprig and a single dried fig for visual contrast.  
Occasion: A quiet Sunday breakfast at Hobbs Cafe, honoring Isabella Rodriguez’s feedback while balancing the creative energy of the weekend baking workshop.
