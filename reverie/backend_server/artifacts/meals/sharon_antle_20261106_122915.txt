Sharon prepared a Midnight Hobbs Cafe Croissant for the weekend workshop tasting. The croissant used butter, sugar, flour, salt, egg, and baking powder, with a generous amount of sugar to balance the butter. It was brushed with a little egg wash, then baked until golden and crisp.

The aroma of freshly baked butter, vanilla, and cinnamon filled the kitchen, carrying the scent of a warm, buttery croissant. Sharon presented the croissant in a small white ceramic dish, surrounded by a tiny glass of warm milk and a dusting of sugar. The occasion was the final taste-test of the new croissant recipes developed during the weekend workshop, with Maria’s feedback helping Sharon refine the morning-baking formula.
