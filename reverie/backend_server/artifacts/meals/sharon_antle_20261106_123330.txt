Meal: Midnight Croissant with Butter-cream and Fresh Strawberries

Sharon prepared a two-layer croissant, brushed with softened unsalted butter, and topped it with a sweet-butter cream and halved fresh strawberries. The cinnamon-sugar crust gave the pastry a rich, golden hue, while the glossy white cream added a delicate, fragrant sheen.

She used the classic butter-crea technique, slicing the pastry, filling it with the cream, and folding it around the strawberries. She brushed the top with a little pastry butter and pinched it to seal the edges, then baked it for about 12 minutes until the edges turned crisp. The warm aroma of caramelized butter, rosemary, and berries filled the kitchen.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: the golden croissant stood upright in a white ceramic dish, with the cream-frosted strawberries arranged beside it. It was prepared for a weekend baking workshop tasting and the first modular-wrapper and classic croissant opening, though the exact date, time, and location were still undecided. Sharon felt energized rather than exhausted as she reviewed her notes and materials with Maria, eager to test the recipes.
