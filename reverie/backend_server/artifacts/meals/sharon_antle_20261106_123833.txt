Sharon Antle prepared Dawn Croissant with Fresh Lemon-Butter Cream, a simple yet refined breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. She used laminated dough, softened butter, milk, eggs, whole-wheat flour, sugar, and salt, incorporating a pinch of cinnamon for warmth. The dough was folded and turned until it rose, then sliced into two even halves. She brushed each half with a mixture of butter and lemon juice, baked them at 400°F for 12–15 minutes, and served them with a generous drizzle of lemon-cream sauce.

The aroma was richly buttery, citrusy, and slightly nutty, with the warm lemon balancing the creamy richness. She presented the golden-brown croissants on a white ceramic platter, accompanied by a small bowl of lemon-cream sauce and a sprig of fresh mint. The meal was an early-morning treat for Isabella and the cafe’s early customers, creating a welcoming, unhurried atmosphere.
