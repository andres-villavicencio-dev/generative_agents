Sharon Antle prepared a Molten Butter-Butter Croissant with Vanilla Bean Compote.

She used milk, unsalted butter, flour, sugar, salt, eggs, baking powder, and vanilla, along with a few whole vanilla beans for the cream. The pastry was folded into a lightly floured circle, brushed with softened butter, and baked until golden. The butter-cream filling was poured into the oven along with the filling, then returned to the pan to create a warm, golden shell.

The aroma was rich and comforting—creamy, buttery, and subtly sweet. She arranged the croissant in a shallow ceramic dish, topped it with a glossy vanilla-sugar glaze, and surrounded it with a crisp fruit compote of tart apple and raspberry pieces.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: the warm croissant stood as the centerpiece, with the colorful compote providing a bright contrast. It was served at Hobbs Cafe’s Wednesday tasting night, where Sharon paired the pastry with Maria Lopez’s new recipe and shared the experience with other guests.
