Sharon prepared a Lemon-Tahini Green Salad for a quick Wednesday lunch while testing a new recipe and reviewing her grocery plan. The dish used crisp cucumbers, sliced carrots, and finely chopped kale, dressed with lemon-tahini and a small amount of olive oil. She lightly tossed the vegetables, letting the lemon brighten the earthy, nutty flavors before serving.

The salad was arranged on a white platter, with the colorful vegetables arranged artfully and the lemon-tahini dressing drizzled over the top. Its aroma was fresh and aromatic—green, nutty, and slightly tangy—evoking the crisp croissants and pastries Sharon bakes before dawn for Isabella Rodriguez.

It was a simple, nourishing lunch, prepared before her evening social visits and intended to keep her productive while she continued developing her sustainably packaged pastries at Hobbs Cafe.
