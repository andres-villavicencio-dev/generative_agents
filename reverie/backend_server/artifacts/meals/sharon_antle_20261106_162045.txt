Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant Breakfast Spread, a whimsical New England-inspired brunch featuring three golden croissants, cold butter, honey, maple syrup, toasted nuts, blueberries, and a light cream cheese tart.

She began by baking the croissants before dawn, then arranged them on a serving platter surrounded by the remaining ingredients. The platter was presented with a small croissant, a glass of cold milk, and a cup of freshly brewed coffee, creating a rustic, inviting breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

The aroma was rich and inviting: warm croissant butter, toasted nuts, honey, and the subtle sweetness of maple. Sharon’s meticulous presentation—croissants arranged in a gentle arc with neatly garnished fruit—turned her ordinary kitchen into a creative canvas. She intended the spread to inspire both the cafe’s weekend baking workshops and Maria Lopez’s signature croissant recipes, reflecting her blend of reliable tradition with artistic experimentation.
