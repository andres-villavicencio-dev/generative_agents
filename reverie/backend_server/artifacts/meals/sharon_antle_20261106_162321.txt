Sharon Antle prepared Molten Croissant with Savory Butter. The dish featured a golden, flaky pastry filled with butter, cheddar, onions, and garlic, accompanied by crisp baguette slices and a honey-mustard glaze.

She began by carefully clearing the kitchen, wiping the counter, and arranging the ingredients in neat rows. After baking the croissants until their edges turned deep gold, she sliced them into strips and melted the butter over the filling. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted dough, savory cheese, garlic, and warm honey.

She presented the croissant strips on a wooden board, arranging them alongside the baguettes in alternating patterns. The warm pastries looked inviting and rustic, with the glossy glaze catching the soft morning light.

Occasion: A Saturday weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon and Maria were taste-testing new croissant recipes. The meal was prepared before the workshop began, allowing Sharon to prepare ingredients and demonstrate the final dishes while Maria and the attendees tested the flavors.
