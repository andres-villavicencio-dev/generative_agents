On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant with Lavender-Cinnamon Cream Cheese for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

The dish features butter, egg yolk, flour, sugar, salt, lavender, cinnamon, and a pinch of nutmeg, topped with a warm cream cheese glaze. Sharon began by dividing the dough into three even portions, letting one rest while she prepared the cream cheese and glaze. She folded the dough gently, brushed the edges with warm butter, and baked the first croissant until its golden crust began to set. The second portion was filled with the warm cream cheese mixture, while the third became the finished croissant.

The aroma filled the kitchen as the croissant baked: buttery, sweet, and fragrant with hints of lavender and cinnamon. She served the warm croissant with the soft, glossy glaze, presenting it on a small, rustic white plate. The occasion was the cafe’s 8:00–10:30 shift application review, and Sharon used the session as an opportunity to showcase her baking to Isabella, who offered practical feedback on the recipe.
