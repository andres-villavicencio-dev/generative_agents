Sharon Antle prepared Brioche Croissants with Fig and Honey, a refined breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe on Monday, February 13. The croissants were made with high-gluten flour, butter, eggs, sugar, yeast, salt, and lemon zest, while the filling combined fresh figs, honey, cinnamon, and a hint of vanilla.

Sharon preheated the oven to 425°F and used a buttered pastry pan to score and roll the dough. She folded the dough, brushed it with egg wash, and baked the croissants until golden and crisp. The figs were caramelized briefly, then sliced into thin rounds before the warm croissants were assembled on a small platter.

The aroma was rich and buttery, with the subtle sweetness of honey, fig, and cinnamon mingling with the savory richness of the brioche. The presentation was elegant: the golden-brown croissants lined the platter, topped with glossy caramelized figs, dusted with cinnamon, and accompanied by a small bowl of honey and a glass of cool water.

This was Isabella Rodriguez’s cafe breakfast, designed to inspire guests to linger over a warm, indulgent meal. Sharon’s meticulous approach and creative use of ingredients reflected her compassionate, artistic nature, while Maria Lopez’s feedback ensured the croissants tasted reliably delicious.
