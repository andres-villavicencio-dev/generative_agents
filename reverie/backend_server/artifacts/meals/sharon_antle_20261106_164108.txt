Sharon Antle prepared Morning-Baked Croissants for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The key ingredients were refined all-purpose flour, butter, egg yolks, a little water, and a hint of salt. She brushed the dough with a thin egg wash before baking until the pastry turned golden and flaky, then covered the pan with parchment paper while the croissants rested.

The warm aroma filled the small kitchen: buttery, golden, and subtly nutty, with a faint, silky scent from the egg wash. The finished croissants were arranged in neat rows on a wooden serving board, dusted lightly with powdered sugar, and presented with a small bowl of warm cream cheese and a lemon wedge.

The occasion was Isabella’s weekend baking workshop, where Sharon was teaching customers how to make their own croissants. The simple, comforting meal also honored the cafe’s tradition of baking before dawn, with the croissants’ golden color and delicate layers symbolizing the warmth of Hobbs Cafe’s morning life.
