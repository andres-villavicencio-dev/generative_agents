Midnight Croissant with Blueberry Compote and Honey

Sharon prepared a batch of golden, buttery croissants for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The key ingredients were high-quality puff pastry, unsalted butter, whole-wheat flour, blueberries, brown sugar, vanilla extract, and a touch of lemon zest. She began by rolling out the pastry, filling it with the berries, and folding it into a traditional croissant shape before baking until the edges turned crisp and the interior was flaky and golden.

After baking, Sharon let the pastries cool completely on a wire rack, carefully checking their crispness and color with a toothpick and the side of the pan. She sliced them and served them with a simple blueberry compote and a drizzle of honey, arranged neatly on a white ceramic platter. The room smelled of warm butter, vanilla, and fresh fruit, creating a cozy atmosphere perfect for Isabella’s cafe.

This meal was prepared for the weekend baking workshop, where Sharon and other instructors would test the croissants and share the recipes. It was also a thoughtful addition to Isabella’s new line of sustainably packaged pastries, blending her classic techniques with Maria Lopez’s creative input.
