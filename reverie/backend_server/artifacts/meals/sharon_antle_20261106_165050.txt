Sharon Antle prepared The Midnight Croissant, a classic New England-inspired breakfast treat with a soft, flaky, golden-topped pastry and a pocket of creamy vanilla custard. The croissants were made before dawn using high-quality flour, butter, eggs, and milk, folded until crisp, and brushed with a simple egg wash. Her custard was lightly seasoned with vanilla, a pinch of salt, and a dash of lemon zest, giving it a rich aroma of toasted butter, warm cream, and sweet pastry.

Sharon arranged the finished pastries on a wooden board, placing each with its golden crust facing outward and the custard pocket open. She carefully labeled them with the workshop’s name, “Hobbs Cafe Weekend Baking Workshop,” and the serving time, adding a small handwritten note for each customer. The occasion was a weekend community baking event at Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon and other participants would taste, share, and enjoy the freshly prepared pastries together.
