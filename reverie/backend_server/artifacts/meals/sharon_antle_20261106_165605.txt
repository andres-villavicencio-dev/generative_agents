Sharon prepared a Baked Cream Cheese Croissant with Rosemary Compote for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured laminated croissants filled with rich cream cheese and rosemary, accompanied by a bright, herbaceous compote of caramelized apples, pears, and a hint of honey.

She began by washing and sanitizing the pastry pans, then carefully storing any remaining dough, cream cheese, and fruit ingredients. She preheated the oven, brushed the croissants with melted butter, and baked them until golden. After slicing them, she spooned the warm compote over the pastries, drizzled a little honey, and arranged them on a platter.

The aroma was unmistakably warm: butter, cream cheese, rosemary, caramel, and the gentle scent of baked pastry filled the kitchen. The presentation was elegant and inviting—the golden croissants stood ready for Isabella to serve as part of a weekend baking workshop. The occasion was a lively, communal session where guests could taste Sharon’s refined pastries and help shape the cafe’s expanding line of sustainably packaged goods.
