On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Savory Cream-Chicken Biscuits with Root Vegetable Soup, a cozy breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

The biscuits used tender buttermilk biscuits, cream cheese, shredded cheddar, chopped chives, and a generous amount of melted butter. She pressed the dough into a small round, cut a pocket, filled it with the filling, and rolled it into a crisp square. The root vegetable soup, simmered with carrots, turnips, onions, and celery, was served with a lemon-herb crust.

The aroma filled the kitchen with buttery dough, savory cheese, warm vegetables, and lemon zest. Sharon arranged the biscuits in neat rows on platters, garnished them with chives, and placed the soup in a warm serving bowl beside a small dish of lemon confit.

The meal was prepared for the café’s breakfast and brunch workshops, offering guests a satisfying, artistically balanced start to their day.
