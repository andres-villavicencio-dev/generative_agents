On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared a Savory Croissant with Fresh Herb Butter and Roasted Peppers for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The key ingredients were high-quality dough, softened unsalted butter, fresh chives and thyme, roasted red peppers, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice.

Sharon began by preheating the oven to 425°F (220°C) and preparing a well-oiled baking sheet. She rolled out a thin, evenly scored croissant, placed it on the pan, and brushed the surface with the herb butter before folding and baking. She arranged the golden, crisp croissants around a serving platter and topped them with a drizzle of lemony butter, crumbled parsley, and a small pile of roasted peppers.

The aroma of butter, thyme, garlic, and roasted vegetables filled the kitchen as the croissants baked. The warm, golden pastry contrasted with the bright colors of the garnish. She served the dish at Hobbs Cafe during the weekend baking workshop, inviting fellow bakers to taste the recipe and share their own creations.
