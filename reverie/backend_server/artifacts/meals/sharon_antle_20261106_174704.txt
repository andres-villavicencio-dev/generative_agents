Sharon prepared a Midnight Croissant with Fig Jam and Warm Cinnamon Toast, a comforting breakfast for her weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe.

She layered a buttery, golden croissant with fig jam, cinnamon, and a drizzle of honey, then topped it with fluffy warm toast and a few fresh figs. The preparation began with room-temperature croissants brushed with butter, baked until crisp and golden, then stacked with the fig mixture, toasted, and a final drizzle.

Warm cinnamon, butter, fig, and honey filled the kitchen with the scent of vanilla and baked pastry. The presentation was simple yet inviting: the croissant’s golden crust, glossy jam, and bright figs arranged beside a plate of warm toast, with a small glass of milk nearby.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop, where she wanted to share a balanced, warm treat that could satisfy both her artistic experimentation and the practical needs of her workshop participants.
