Sharon Antle prepared Molasses-Sweetened Roasted Root Vegetables with a Lemon-Balanced Crème Sauce as a side to accompany the steak dinner. She began by slicing carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes and tossing them with olive oil, smoked paprika, sea salt, and a pinch of black pepper. The vegetables were roasted at 425°F for about 25 minutes until caramelized and tender.

She made the sauce by blending a little cream, lemon juice, fresh thyme, garlic, and a touch of honey until it achieved a light, glossy consistency. She drizzled the sauce over the finished vegetables and finished them with a little fresh herbs.

The aroma was warm and inviting—rich caramel, roasted root, lemon zest, garlic, and earthy spices filling the kitchen. She arranged the colorful vegetables in a small serving dish and topped them with a delicate dusting of flaky sea salt and a few fresh thyme sprigs.

The meal was prepared for Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she was introducing the workshop’s sustainable pastries to the students.
