Sharon Antle prepared Savory Herb-Crusted Chicken with Lemon-Cilantro Rice and Roasted Root Vegetables.

She seasoned a whole chicken with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and lemon zest, then wrapped it in a crispy herb crust. The rice was seasoned with cumin, paprika, and chopped parsley, while carrots, parsnips, and potatoes were roasted with olive oil, salt, and pepper before being arranged around the pan.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted vegetables, toasted bread, and herbs. It smelled like a Sunday lunch under a winter sky—rich, aromatic, and inviting.

For her weekend baking workshop, Sharon presented the meal as a cozy New England-style dinner, suggesting it was perfect after a long day of organizing workshops. She plated the food in a simple ceramic bowl, with the chicken, rice, and vegetables arranged in a balanced, warm, and rustic composition.
