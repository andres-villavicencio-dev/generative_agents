Sharon Antle prepared Braised Lemon-Basil Chicken with Creamy Root Vegetable Soup, a comforting New England-inspired dinner to accompany her weekday baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe.

The meal featured a golden-brown chicken simmered in tomato, onion, garlic, and fresh basil, finished with a bright lemon reduction. Herbs, carrots, parsnips, celery, and potatoes were simmered in vegetable broth, then finished with cream, thyme, and a drizzle of honey.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, simmering root vegetables, and the crisp scent of basil. Sharon plated the chicken in a shallow, rustic bowl, arranging the colorful root vegetables around it and garnishing each side with lemon slices and microgreens.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop, where she was testing a new croissant recipe while Maria Lopez’s feedback helped refine the bread’s flavor and texture. The meal felt both practical and cozy, balancing her work with the creative energy of her morning baking.
