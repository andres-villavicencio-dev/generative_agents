Sharon Antle prepared a Mediterranean-Inspired Herb-Lemon Chicken and Rice Bowl. The dish balanced tender chicken, aromatic herbs, crisp rice, and a bright lemon dressing, representing the café’s lunch-focused culinary identity.

The main ingredients included boneless chicken thighs, jasmine rice, cherry tomatoes, red onion, cucumber, kalamata olives, and a dressing of olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, dried oregano, and a hint of lemon zest. Sharon began by sautéing the chicken in olive oil until it was cooked through, then added the vegetables and cooked rice. She finished the dish with the flavorful dressing and topped it with feta cheese and fresh herbs before serving.

The aroma was warm and inviting: roasted chicken, citrus, garlic, and earthy Mediterranean herbs mingled with the soft scent of rice. The presentation arranged the colorful vegetables and golden rice around the chicken, with the feta cheese and bright lemon garnish adding a cheerful pop of color.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where she taught attendees how to prepare the croissant and rice dish while encouraging creative variations.
