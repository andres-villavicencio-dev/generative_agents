Sharon Antle prepared Heritage Lemon-Herb Toast, a simple New England-inspired lunch for the weekend baking workshop.

She toasted thick-sliced sourdough, then layered it with lemon slices, roasted sweet potato cubes, and fresh herbs. The pan was toasted with extra butter until the bread became crisp, while the potatoes were lightly caramelized before being folded into the toast.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, lemon zest, and savory herbs. The final presentation was rustic and inviting: the bread was arranged on a linen-covered platter, with the golden potatoes and herbs arranged around it. A small glass of chilled lemonade and a sprig of rosemary completed the look.

It was a quiet, intimate lunch for the workshop, offering a fresh, comforting meal after a busy day of planning.
