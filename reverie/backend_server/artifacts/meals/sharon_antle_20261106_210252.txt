Sharon prepared a Weekend Baking Workshop Croissant-Infused Pastry Platter for the Hobbs Cafe weekend event. The dish featured golden, flaky croissants, cream cheese, caramelized onions, fresh thyme, lemon zest, and a small bowl of toasted pine nuts.

She began by preheating the oven and baking the croissants until they were puffed and crisp. As they cooled, she layered them with softened cream cheese, finely chopped caramelized onions, and aromatic herbs. The lemon zest brightened the savory notes, while the toasted pine nuts added texture and a subtle nutty richness.

The finished platter was arranged in a rustic ceramic dish, with the golden croissants leaning against a small serving of caramelized onions and fresh herbs. A sprig of thyme and a lemon wedge completed the presentation. The aroma of warm butter, toasted nuts, and caramelized onions filled the kitchen, while the slightly crisp edges of the croissants added a satisfying crunch.

The platter was prepared for the weekend baking workshop, designed to inspire participants to try spontaneous pastry experiments inspired by classic New England recipes.
