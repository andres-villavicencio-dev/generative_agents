On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared The Midnight Croissant, a warm croissant filled with buttery cream cheese and dark chocolate ganache. The recipe began with flour, unsalted butter, sugar, salt, eggs, and sour cream in a large bowl, worked into a smooth dough, and then rolled and cut into rounds. She baked the croissants until they turned golden-brown and flaky before filling them with the rich ganache and letting them cool.

The aroma was inviting and sophisticated: toasted butter, sweet cream, dark chocolate, and a hint of vanilla rose through the kitchen. Each croissant was arranged on a white ceramic plate, dusted with powdered sugar, and garnished with a small sprig of mint, creating a balanced, elegant presentation.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at The Willows Market and Pharmacy. She had purchased the new croissant recipes with Maria Lopez and tasted them together before heading home. Her meticulous approach and Maria’s encouraging feedback combined with her love for spontaneous experimentation, making the meal a joyful, nourishing reflection of her creative process.
