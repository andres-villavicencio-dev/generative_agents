Sharon prepared Crisp-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a comforting New England-inspired dinner for herself. She used tender chicken breast cutlets, carrots, red potatoes, and onions, finishing with a creamy garlic sauce and a drizzle of olive oil. The dish was pan-seared until the chicken was golden and crisp, then finished in a warm oven until the vegetables were tender.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized carrots, garlic, and savory chicken. Sharon arranged the golden chicken pieces alongside the bright orange roots, garnishing them with herbs and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt. The clean, rustic presentation made her feel relaxed and satisfied.

It was a quiet evening after a busy day of baking for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe and meeting David Puskar over lunch. Sharon wiped down the counter and put away the remaining ingredients, enjoying a simple, nourishing meal that brought her comfort and peace.
