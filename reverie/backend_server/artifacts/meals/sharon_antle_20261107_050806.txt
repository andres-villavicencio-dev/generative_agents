Sharon prepared Afternoon Croissant with Honey-Roasted Root Vegetables, a quiet evening treat for herself and Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The key ingredients were flour, butter, sugar, salt, eggs, milk, whole-wheat flour, apples, carrots, parsnips, and honey.

She began by rolling dough in a large bowl, letting it rest until the eggs and milk were absorbed. Then she folded in softened butter, sprinkled sugar, and folded it once to create the signature flaky layers. The dough was rolled into a long log, sliced, and baked in a 400°F oven until golden and puffed. Afterward, she roasted the root vegetables with honey, oil, and garlic until their edges browned and their interiors were tender.

Aromatic, warm, and fragrant, the dish smelled like butter, caramel, cinnamon, and roasted earth. It was presented on a white ceramic platter with a few caramelized apples and a lemon wedge, arranged simply so the golden croissants stood out.

This was a quiet midday ritual—Sharon was checking the bulletin board around 4:00 p.m., meeting David Puskar over lunch, and attending the Willow Clinic on Monday, February 13, at 2:00 a.m., for the fluid drip. The meal was both nourishing and relaxing, a brief pause in her busy, productive days before she could finally prepare for bed.
