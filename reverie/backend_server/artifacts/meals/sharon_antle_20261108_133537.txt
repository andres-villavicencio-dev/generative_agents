On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Honey-Balanced Butter for a quiet breakfast at Hobbs Cafe. Using Maria Lopez’s feedback on a neutral, buttery croissant, she combined softened unsalted butter, finely chopped toasted walnuts, a touch of maple syrup, and a pinch of nutmeg. The recipe was simple yet refined: the pastry was rolled and brushed with the butter mixture, then baked until golden, while the filling was finished with a warm honey glaze.

The aroma filled the cafe: butter, toasted nuts, and cinnamon rose gently from the oven, creating a comforting, aromatic atmosphere. Sharon arranged the croissants on a white ceramic plate, garnishing each with a small mint leaf and drizzling the honey glaze over them. The presentation was delicate and inviting, with the warm pastries standing out against the soft café background.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop, where Sharon shared her passion for classic New England recipes while encouraging customers to create their own croissant variations. The meal offered a cozy, artistic treat that mirrored Sharon’s meticulous yet spontaneous approach to baking.
