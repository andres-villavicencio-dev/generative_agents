The meal was a Midnight Croissant Breakfast Plate, prepared around 2:00 a.m. for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

Sharon assembled three golden croissants, a creamy lemon curd, a drizzle of honey, and a small bowl of dark chocolate curls. She brushed each slice with warm honey, cracked a few warm walnuts over them, and placed them on a lightly oiled baking sheet. The warm croissants were arranged on a white platter with a small glass of cold lemonade beside it.

The aroma was rich and inviting: butter, pastry, lemon, toasted nuts, and a hint of honey. It smelled comforting and festive, especially after a long day of baking.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe. Sharon wanted the croissants to look freshly made and inspiring, while Maria Lopez’s feedback ensured the flavors remained balanced and the presentation elegant.
