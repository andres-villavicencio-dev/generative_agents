Sharon prepared a Midnight Croissant Breakfast Bowl, combining her midnight-baked croissants with fresh berries, warm yogurt, and toasted almonds. The croissants were baked at dawn, their golden, buttery layers soft enough to stretch into elegant crescents. She layered them with a creamy vanilla yogurt, sliced seasonal berries, and crunchy almonds, then drizzled them with a little honey and cinnamon.

The kitchen filled with the warm, buttery aroma of baked pastry and the bright citrusy scent of the berries. She arranged the bowls on a wooden serving platter, garnished with a sprig of thyme and a dusting of sea salt, then served them at Hobbs Cafe for the weekend baking workshops. The occasion was an intimate, communal tasting session, where participants could sample the croissants and yogurt in quiet conversation.
