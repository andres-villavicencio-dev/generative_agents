On a quiet Monday evening, Sharon Antle prepared Molasses-Macadamia Croissant with Maple-Lemon Glaze, inspired by the recipes she developed for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish combines the golden, buttery layers of a classic French croissant with a warm glaze made from maple syrup, fresh lemon, and a pinch of sea salt.

Sharon began by preheating her oven to 425°F (220°C) and carefully checking that the oven temperature was accurate before starting the second croissant. She brushed the tops of both croissants with the glaze, then baked them for 12 to 14 minutes until they were golden, puffed, and crisp. The smell was rich and inviting—creamy butter, toasted brown sugar, warm lemon zest, and caramelized maple.

She served the croissants on white ceramic plates with a small bowl of maple-lemon glaze, a dusting of sea salt, and a sprig of rosemary. The meal was prepared for a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon’s meticulous and artistic approach to pastry-making offered a creative inspiration for attendees.
