Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant with Berry Compote, a late-evening treat inspired by her midnight baking raids. She layered two golden croissants in a small glass dish and topped them with a warm, glossy mixture of fresh strawberries, blackberries, and golden raisins, then drizzled a hint of honey over the top.

The croissants were sliced open with a serrated knife, revealing their airy, flaky layers, while the berries were arranged artistically in the surrounding spaces. The aroma filled the small café table: buttery, rich, and slightly sweet, with the scent of caramelized fruit mingling beneath it.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe. Sharon wanted Maria Lopez’s feedback to shape the new croissant, so she carefully tested the second croissant’s doneness and recorded the result, while her meticulous presentation added a touch of artistic flair to the tasting session.
