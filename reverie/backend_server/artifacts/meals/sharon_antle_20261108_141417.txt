On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Crispy Croissant with Honey-Glazed Raspberries, a weekend treat inspired by classic New England baking.

She began by preheating the oven to 400°F and rolling out two sheets of flour-dusted phyllo dough. A thick, buttery croissant was brushed with softened butter, then folded, rolled, and baked until golden. While it rested, she arranged bright red raspberries over the warm pastry. For a finishing touch, she brushed the croissant with a warm honey glaze and sprinkled crushed toasted almonds.

The aroma was rich and buttery, mingling with the tart sweetness of the berries and the crisp almond crunch. The presentation was elegant and inviting: the golden-brown croissant stood on a white platter, surrounded by the colorful raspberries.

She prepared the meal for a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where customers would taste-test the croissant and learn how to make their own. The combination of familiar ingredients with Sharon’s spontaneous artistic experiments made the workshop both rewarding and memorable.
