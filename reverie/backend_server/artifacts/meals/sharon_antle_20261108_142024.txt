On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Honey-Lemon Butter, a refined breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. She used two brioche-style croissants, softened butter, honey, fresh lemon juice, and a pinch of cinnamon. The butter was brushed over the pastry while it rested, then folded in; the honey and lemon were folded in last before baking. She baked the croissants at a low, slow temperature until the butter melted and the pastry turned golden and flaky.

The aroma was warm, buttery, and subtly sweet, with hints of lemon and cinnamon filling the kitchen as she turned the oven. Her presentation was simple and elegant: two golden croissants placed side by side on a white ceramic platter, with a small bowl of honey-lemon butter and a cinnamon-sugar drizzle beside them.

She prepared the meal as part of the weekend baking workshops, offering both experienced and novice bakers a chance to try the classic New England croissant technique.
