Sharon prepared Midnight Croissant with Fig Compote for her weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, combining classic New England tradition with spontaneous artistic experimentation.

The dish featured two golden-brown croissants, brushed with softened butter and dusted with cinnamon and a touch of sea salt. Alongside them was a simple fig compote made from fresh figs, honey, lemon zest, and a little vanilla, balanced with a whisper of smoked sea salt.

She rolled the croissants tightly, sliced them lengthwise, and arranged them around the compote on a large white platter. The warm aroma of nutty pastry and honeyed figs drifted through the kitchen, while cinnamon, butter, and lemon zest created a comforting aroma in the air.

The presentation was elegant and intimate: two contrasting croissants stood beside a glossy, translucent compote, with a small spoon reserved for tasting. It was prepared for the workshop’s tasting session, where participants could appreciate the balance of texture, flavor, and the unexpected twist of figs.
