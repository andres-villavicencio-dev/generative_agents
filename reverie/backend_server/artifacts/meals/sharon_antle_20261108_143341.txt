Meal: Midnight Croissant & Berry Crostini

Sharon Antle prepared a simple yet visually striking breakfast for a weekend brunch at Hobbs Cafe, using a midnight-time croissant and fresh fruit. The croissant was brushed with melted butter, baked until crisp and golden, then cooled to reveal its flaky layers. She paired it with toasted whole-grain bread, sliced ripe strawberries, blueberries, and a drizzle of honey.

The meal began with Sharon warming her hands over the stove, then placing a small pan of butter on the counter to let it rise. As the butter melted, a warm, buttery aroma filled the kitchen, mingling with the sweet scent of berries and toasted bread. She sliced the croissant into two halves, arranged them on a platter, and scattered the fruit and honey over them. The presentation was elegant and inviting, with the golden pastry contrasting against the bright red berries.

She served it with a glass of cold water, a perfect way to start the weekend. The occasion was a special brunch gathering for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, where the croissants were also part of the weekend baking workshop. Sharon’s meticulous approach and attention to detail made the meal feel both comforting and artistically polished.
