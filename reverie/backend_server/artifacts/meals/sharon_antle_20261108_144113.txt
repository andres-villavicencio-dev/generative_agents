On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Hobbs Cafe Midnight Croissant Rolls, a refined croissant-inspired breakfast offering that blends classic New England baking with her midnight-raiding experiments. The rolls featured a crisp buttery pastry, golden-brown cinnamon-sugar glaze, and pockets of warm chocolate ganache and fresh blueberry compote.

She rolled the croissant dough into a neat rectangle, sprinkled it with coarse granulated sugar, folded it, and brushed the edges with a little warm milk. After baking until the golden crust was firm and the interior was tender, she sliced them into individual rolls and arranged them in a glass bowl. The aroma was rich, warm, and inviting—combining buttery, cinnamon, vanilla, and a hint of blueberry.

The presentation was elegant and seasonal, with the rolls garnished with a mint sprig and a small cluster of berries. It was served as a special holiday breakfast at Hobbs Cafe, honoring Isabella Rodriguez’s influence while celebrating Sharon’s passion for creating comforting, artistic dishes.
