Sharon Antle prepared Mushroom & Berry Croissant, a breakfast-optimized version of the croissants she bakes for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish combines fresh brioche dough with mushrooms, wild berries, toasted hazelnuts, and a warm lemon-herb glaze.

She began by folding the softened dough into small squares, pressing them lightly, and baking them until golden and crisp. Before serving, she folded the pastry, drizzled the glaze, and sliced it into neat rounds. The final croissant was filled with caramelized mushrooms, chopped blueberries, toasted almonds, and a fragrant lemon-herb mixture, with a dusting of sea salt and a sprig of fresh thyme.

The aroma was rich and earthy, with notes of toasted nuts, dark fruit, and a hint of savory mushroom. It presented as a colorful, rustic breakfast platter on a wooden board, illuminated by soft morning light. Sharon served it for Isabella’s weekend breakfast workshop, where she wanted to celebrate the creative community’s shared love of baking.
