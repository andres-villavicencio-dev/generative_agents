Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant with Maple-Caramelized Apples and Brown Butter for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured golden-brown croissants, caramelized apples, a drizzle of maple syrup, and a rich brown butter sauce with toasted nuts.

She began by measuring flour, butter, sugar, salt, and apples by hand before rolling out the croissants. She carefully placed them in the pans, brushed the tops with melted butter, and scored the dough before baking. The oven was preheated, and the croissants were baked until their golden crusts turned crisp and their airy interiors became flaky. The caramelized apples were simmered in a pot with brown butter until their edges deepened and the syrup reduced to a glossy glaze.

The finished croissants were plated on a rustic wooden board, arranged in a neat row with a spoon of caramel sauce and a dusting of sea salt on each. Their aroma reminded her of warm cinnamon, roasted nuts, and the comforting scent of baked bread rising in the morning. She served the meal during the weekend baking workshops, helping participants learn the classic New England croissant method while she demonstrated a spontaneous artistic twist.
