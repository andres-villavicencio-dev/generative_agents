Sharon Antle prepared Morn’s Midnight Croissants and Lemon-Butter Scones for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The meal was a quiet, intimate brunch meant to inspire the cafe’s weekend baking workshops.

The preparation began early on a cool Monday morning. She divided the dough into even portions, rolled the scone dough into small rectangles, and baked the croissants until their golden, flaky edges revealed themselves. The scone dough was brushed with softened butter and dusted with finely chopped lemon zest before baking. The croissants were arranged in a warm basket, dusted with sea salt, and topped with a generous smear of lemon butter.

The kitchen filled with a rich, buttery aroma of toasted dough, warm lemon, and butter. The warm croissants looked elegant and inviting, while the bright lemon-scone garnish added a cheerful pop of color. Sharon served the meal at Hobbs Cafe for the workshop opening, where Isabella, Maria, and other bakers tasted the creations and shared their ideas for the new line of sustainably packaged pastries.
