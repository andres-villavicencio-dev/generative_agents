Sharon Antle prepared Misty-Seasoned Baked Puff Pastry with Lemon-Puree Compote, a comforting breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

The dish consists of golden, flaky puff pastry filled with lemon curd and a rich custard, topped with toasted almonds, a drizzle of honey-lemon glaze, and a dusting of cinnamon. Key ingredients include puff pastry, eggs, milk, heavy cream, lemon zest, fresh lemon, butter, sugar, vanilla, and toasted almonds.

Sharon began by preheating the oven to 375°F and brushing the pastry with melted butter. She folded the custard gently into the pastry, then baked them until crisp and golden. The finished croissants were arranged on a white ceramic platter, with the lemon compote served in a small ramekin beside them, accompanied by fresh berries and a small glass of cold lemonade.

Her kitchen filled with the warm aroma of butter, pastry, and lemon. It was a quiet Sunday-morning breakfast, intended to inspire Isabella and encourage the weekend baking workshops she was developing at Hobbs Cafe.
