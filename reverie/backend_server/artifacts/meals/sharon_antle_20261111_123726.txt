Sharon Antle prepared Molasses-Marbled Croissants with Honey-Thyme Compote, a treat for the weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe. The croissants began with a rich dough of whole wheat flour, butter, egg, milk, and a hint of molasses for depth. After folding and trimming, she brushed the tops with a thin glaze of honey and sea salt before baking them at 390°F (200°C) until they turned golden and crisp. The compote consisted of caramelized apples, honey, thyme, and a splash of lemon juice, arranged on small plates for easy sharing.

Aromatic, the warm croissants filled the kitchen with buttery notes, caramelized sugar, and herb freshness. The honey-thyme glaze added a subtle citrus brightness, while the honey-apple compote provided a sweet, earthy contrast. The croissants were arranged in overlapping rows on a wooden board, with the golden pastries facing inward. The presentation was simple yet elegant: rustic linen napkins, a small ceramic croissant dish, and a glass of chilled lemon water served alongside.

This meal was part of the weekend baking workshops and the new sustainably packaged pastry line developed with Isabella Rodriguez and Maria Lopez. Sharon’s meticulous taste-testing ensured the recipes balanced classic New England baking traditions with spontaneous artistic creativity.
