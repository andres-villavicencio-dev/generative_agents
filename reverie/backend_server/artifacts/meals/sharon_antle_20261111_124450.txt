On a quiet Monday morning, Sharon prepared Midnight Croissant with Honey and Lemon, tasting it with Maria Lopez to refine the recipe. She placed two crisp, golden croissants on a serving platter and arranged thin slices of honey, fresh lemon, and a few chopped walnuts beside them. Her warm, buttery aroma filled the small café, inviting guests and passersby to linger.

The croissants were baked until their edges turned golden brown, then brushed with a glossy glaze and arranged with the lemon and honey. The platter was presented for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe’s weekend baking workshop, where Sharon hoped to demonstrate how classic New England recipes could be elevated through spontaneous artistic experiments.
