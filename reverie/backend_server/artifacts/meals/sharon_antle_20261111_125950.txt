The meal Sharon Antle prepared was Morning Brioche & Blueberry Crumble, a breakfast bowl designed for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. She made it before dawn, using flour, water, sugar, butter, and salt, with fresh blueberries, warm honey, cinnamon, nutmeg, and a pinch of sea salt. The batter was gently folded, then baked until the golden-brown bread became soft and the blueberries caramelized beneath. For the crumble, she used a mix of toasted nuts, sugar, and cinnamon, which were baked until crisp and golden.

As it cooled, the aroma filled the kitchen: buttery brioche, sweet blueberries, cinnamon, and toasted nuts mingled with the quiet scent of cream and honey. She plated it in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the golden-brown bread around the center and scattering the crunchy crumble over the top. A drizzle of honey and a dusting of cinnamon completed the presentation.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, and the meal was part of the tasting session for the first batch of pastries. Its simple, comforting ingredients and relaxed presentation reflected her compassionate, artistic approach to baking—grounding the cafe’s culinary identity in warmth, creativity, and thoughtful experimentation.
