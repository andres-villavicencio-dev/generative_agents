Meal: Midnight Croissant with Honey-Glazed Fig Jam and Toasted Almonds

Sharon Antle prepared this treat for an early Sunday baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, using her midnight-inspired croissant raids and Maria Lopez’s feedback to create a fresh, seasonal flavor profile.

The dish combines two warm New England classics: a buttery, flaky croissant and a honey-glazed fig jam, balanced with toasted almonds for texture. The croissant is rolled out, cut into quarters, and filled with a generous amount of fig jam before the buttery layers are brushed with honey and sprinkled with almonds. It is then baked until the edges are golden and the center remains tender.

Aromatically, the kitchen fills with the scent of warm butter, honey, caramelized fruit, and toasted nuts. The presentation features a glossy, amber glaze, neatly arranged fig pieces, visible golden almond crunch, and a rustic wooden platter.

The meal is part of Sharon’s weekend baking workshops, where she teaches guests the art of pastry preparation. The warm, nourishing dish serves as both a practical lesson in baking and an opportunity to share her passion for classic recipes with fresh, spontaneous experimentation.
