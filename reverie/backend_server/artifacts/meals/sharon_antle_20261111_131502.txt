On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Lemon-Balanced Berry Compote for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

She used unbleached all-purpose flour, unsalted butter, granulated sugar, whole-wheat flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon zest, folded together with whole milk, egg, and a pinch of salt. The dough was worked at room temperature, rolled out to 1/2-inch thickness, and brushed with softened butter before being baked at 425°F for about 12 minutes until golden and crisp.

The finished croissants smelled of warm butter, cinnamon, and bright lemon, with delicate hints of toasted wheat and vanilla. They were arranged in a warm croissant stand alongside the glossy, amber-colored berry compote, which had been simmered with whole strawberries, raspberries, blackberries, and blueberries.

The presentation was elegant and inviting, with each croissant resting on a small white plate and garnished with a sprig of mint and a single lemon wheel. The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop, where the pastries were meant to inspire creativity and reinforce the café’s commitment to sustainable, hands-on baking.
