Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Honey-Butter Glaze for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The croissants were made with butter, flour, egg, salt, and a pinch of cinnamon, then baked to a golden golden-brown. After removing them from the oven, she cooled them completely on wire racks before glazing with a honey-soy mixture and a dusting of chopped nuts.

The aroma was warm and inviting: butter, cinnamon, and toasted nuts mingled with the subtle sweetness of honey and a hint of vanilla. She arranged the cooled croissants in a neat row on a rustic wooden board, topped each with a decorative dusting of sea salt and a few candied nuts, and placed them beside a small glass of chilled lemonade.

The meal was prepared for a Sunday brunch at Hobbs Cafe, with Isabella serving the croissants as part of the weekend baking workshop. Sharon’s meticulous, compassionate approach ensured every pastry was perfectly baked and beautifully presented, reflecting both her classic New England influences and her spontaneous creative experimentation.
