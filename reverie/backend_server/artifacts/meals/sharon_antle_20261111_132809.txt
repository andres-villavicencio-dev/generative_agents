Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Fig Compote, a simple yet deeply artistic breakfast inspired by her midnight raids and the cafe’s elegant style.

The dish featured two golden-brown croissants, a crisp fig compote of sliced golden apples, figs, and a little honey, and a side of lightly toasted oat bran. She brushed the croissants with a mixture of softened butter and a touch of cinnamon, then baked them until the pastry was crisp around the edges and the centers remained tender. The warm, buttery aroma of cinnamon, caramel, and toasted nuts filled the kitchen.

She served the croissants on a white ceramic plate, arranging them in a gentle arc with the colorful compote beside them. A small glass of chilled apple juice and a piece of dark chocolate garnish completed the presentation. The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, where she was introducing her signature croissants and encouraging attendees to personalize their own pastries.
