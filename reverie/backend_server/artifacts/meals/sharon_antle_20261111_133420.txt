Sharon Antle prepared Dawn Croissant with Fig and Honey, a simple yet elegant breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

The key ingredients were high-protein almond flour, whole-wheat pastry dough, ripe figs, honey, and a touch of vanilla. She began by rolling the dough into a thin, rectangular sheet and cutting it into four squares. The figs were sliced thinly across the dough, then arranged on top while the edges were folded over. The croissant was brushed with honey, baked at 375°F for about 10 minutes, and allowed to crisp slightly for a golden, buttery finish.

The aroma was warm and inviting—a blend of toasted nuts, honey, ripe figs, and the gentle scent of vanilla. It smelled comforting and celebratory, as if the kitchen had been celebrating another successful weekend.

The croissant was presented on a white ceramic plate with a small bowl of honey and a fresh fig beside it. It was served on a quiet Monday morning, offering a moment of indulgence before the week began.
