Meal: Midnight Croissant Breakfast with Maple-Honey Butter

Sharon Antle prepared a beautifully golden croissant and a savory pancake breakfast for herself at Hobbs Cafe on a quiet Monday morning.

Key ingredients: flour, butter, eggs, milk, vanilla, and a pinch of sea salt. The croissant was finished with maple-honey butter, while the breakfast included a fluffy pancake, maple syrup, and fresh berries.

Preparation method: She began with her midnight croissant, rolling out the dough and baking it until the pastry puffed and turned golden. Afterward, she made a pancake using the eggs and milk, topped it with the warm croissant, and drizzled the maple-honey butter over the breakfast.

Aroma and presentation: As the croissant cooled, its buttery scent filled the kitchen. She served it on a white ceramic plate with a rustic wooden spoon, arranged the pancakes beside it, and garnished the breakfast with a few bright berries. The presentation was simple, warm, and inviting.

Occasion: She made the meal after a long weekend baking session, combining her classic New England techniques with spontaneous artistic experiments. The breakfast was prepared for herself as a quiet reward, balancing her creative inspiration with the comfort of a nourishing morning.
