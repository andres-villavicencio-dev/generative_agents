Sharon Antle prepared Molten Lemon-Butter Croissants with Fresh Fruit and Dark Chocolate as a weekend treat at Hobbs Cafe. The croissants were made with laminated pastry, softened butter, shredded Parmesan, lemon zest, and a simple sugar-and-yeast dough. Maria Lopez’s feedback ensured the recipe balanced a classic New England style with a spontaneous, slightly airy finish.

She filled the golden, flaky croissants with caramelized fresh fruit, a drizzle of dark chocolate, and a whisper of lemon. The warm oven produced a fragrant aroma of toasted butter, zest, vanilla, and sweet fruit. The pastries were arranged on a white platter with a single sprig of rosemary, their glossy glaze catching the cafe’s soft light. The meal was part of a gentle weekend baking workshop, offering attendees a chance to taste the new croissant recipe and share their feedback.
