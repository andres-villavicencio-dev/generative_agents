Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant with Fig and Blueberry Compote. The dish featured golden-brown croissants, a crisp fig glaze, sweet blueberries, and a drizzle of warm honey.

She rolled the pastry sheets into thin rounds and brushed them with melted butter, then folded them in half and baked them until crisp, bubbly, and fragrant. The compote simmered figs, cinnamon, brown sugar, and blueberries until the fruit softened and the sauce glazed them.

As she plated the croissants, she arranged each one alongside a dollop of blueberry cream and a side of warm fig tart. The aroma of butter, honey, and caramelized fruit filled the room, with a gentle cinnamon note lingering in the air.

The meal was served as part of Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, encouraging participants to try their own creations and share feedback with Maria Lopez and Isabella Rodriguez.
