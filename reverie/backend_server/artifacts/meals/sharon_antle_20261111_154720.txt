On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Noon Croissant with Fig Jam and Honey Glaze for Maria Lopez’s neighborhood café.

Key ingredients: dough from her classic New England croissant recipe, fresh figs, honey, almond paste, and a touch of lemon zest.

Preparation method: She rolled the croissant by hand in her apron, letting the layers separate before baking it in a preheated oven. After baking, she sliced it diagonally and spread the fig jam and honey glaze over the warm pastry.

The aroma filled the café: a crisp, buttery scent mingling with the sweet, earthy notes of fig and honey.

Sharon arranged the croissants on a white ceramic platter, garnishing them with a bright lime wedge and a sprig of rosemary. The simple presentation emphasized the golden, flaky texture of the pastry and the colorful contrast of the jam.

The occasion was a quiet breakfast table at Maria’s café, where Sharon shared her creations with Maria and several café patrons as part of a small, friendly gathering.
