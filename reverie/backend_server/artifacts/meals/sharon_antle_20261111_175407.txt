The meal Sharon Antle prepared was a classic New England breakfast of toasted whole-grain toast and a cup of coffee. She used a thin slice of whole-grain bread, butter and maple syrup, a few slices of avocado, and a generous drizzle of honey. The toast was toasted until crisp, while the coffee was brewed to a rich, lightly bitter depth.

She prepared the coffee by pouring fresh water over the grounds in a small glass carafe and letting it steep before pressing the coffee. The avocado was sliced and added just before the toast was finished to retain its creamy texture. A small bowl of honey was kept nearby for the avocado and toast, while the coffee was served alongside fresh berries.

The aroma was warm and inviting: toasted nuts, caramelized bread, and a subtle hint of roasted coffee. The presentation was simple yet intimate—the buttered toast was arranged neatly on a white plate, topped with the sliced avocado and honey, with the coffee cup beside it. It was prepared for a quiet morning breakfast at Hobbs Cafe, a relaxing way to start the day after the weekend workshop and tasting sessions.
