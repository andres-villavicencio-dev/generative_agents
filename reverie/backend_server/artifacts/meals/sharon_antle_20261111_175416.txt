Breakfast: Midnight Croissant Stack with Honey-Butter Lemon Meringue and Fresh Apricot

Sharon Antle prepared a simple yet elegant breakfast for herself on February 14, treating her morning routine to the croissants that had inspired her at Hobbs Cafe. The meal was carefully balanced and visually presented: a warm, golden croissant stack was folded with a small cup of steaming tea and a few fresh pastries.

The croissants were baked until their layers softened, then stacked with honey-buttered lemon meringue and fresh apricot. The meringue was baked to a crisp, golden edge, while the apricots brought a bright, tart sweetness. A delicate drizzle of honey and a touch of cinnamon added a subtle warmth.

The aroma was unmistakably comforting—butter, citrus, honey, and toasted pastry filling the kitchen. The arrangement was understated and artistic: the croissant stack stood as the centerpiece, with the tea cup, pastries, and apricot slices arranged around it. It was neither a formal breakfast nor a celebratory meal, but a quiet moment of nourishment—a peaceful way to begin her busy Tuesday.
