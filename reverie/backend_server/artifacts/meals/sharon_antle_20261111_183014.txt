Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant Pâtisserie, a warm, rustic breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish used three brioche-like croissants, a small batch of buttery baguettes, whole-grain toast, and a generous serving of homemade cinnamon-spiced oat muffins. She toasted the baguettes, filled the croissants with butter and cinnamon, and arranged them alongside the muffins in a simple wooden serving tray. A small bowl of maple syrup and a cup of tea completed the table.

The kitchen filled with the scent of toasted flour, vanilla, warm butter, and cinnamon. The croissants had a golden, slightly chewy exterior and a soft, flaky interior, while the muffins offered a balanced blend of grainy texture and smooth sweetness.

The presentation was understated and elegant: the croissants stood in a row, the muffins arranged neatly beside them, and the tea cup was placed within easy reach. The occasion was an early Tuesday breakfast after the weekend workshop, intended to help Isabella and the team start the new week refreshed and encouraged.
