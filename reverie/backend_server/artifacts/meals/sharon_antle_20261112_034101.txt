On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant Breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. She began with two buttery croissants, softened by the gentle heat of a panini press, then sliced them into quarters. To the warm croissant pieces, she added two egg yolks, a small handful of finely chopped chives, a dash of lemon zest, and a teaspoon of maple syrup. The egg mixture was whisked until glossy, then gently folded into the croissants so the delicate pastry absorbed the richness without losing its airy texture.

The finished dish had a warm, buttery aroma with hints of citrus, maple, and savory chives. It was arranged on a white ceramic platter, with the croissant slices stacked in a small mound and garnished with a few chives and a dusting of powdered sugar. The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop, celebrating the cafe’s mission to blend classic New England baking with sustainable, artistic experimentation.
