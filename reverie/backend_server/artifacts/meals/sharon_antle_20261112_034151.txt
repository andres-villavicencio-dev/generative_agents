Sharon Antle prepared Amuse-Bouche Croissant with Maple-Lemon Butter, a refined treat made for her morning opening at Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The pastry was filled with sweetened cream cheese and finished with a warm maple-lemon butter sauce, while lemon wedges, honey, and a dusting of sea salt provided a bright finish.

She began by gathering her ingredients: fresh baguette, cream cheese, powdered sugar, maple syrup, butter, flour, and vanilla. After washing and patting the baguette dry, she sliced it lengthwise, filling each half with the cream cheese and topping it with sugar and lemon. The warm butter was brushed over the pastry before being folded and baked at 375°F for 15 minutes.

The aroma was rich, golden, and slightly citrusy—combining the toasted bread, savory cream cheese, caramelized maple, and fresh lemon. She arranged the pastries on a white ceramic platter and served one alongside a glass of chilled lemonade, the colors and textures echoing her meticulous approach to baking. The occasion was a quiet morning at Hobbs Cafe, where the pastries were intended to warm guests before the day began.
