Sharon Antle prepared Molten Oatmeal with Fig and Almond Crumble, a simple yet warming breakfast made for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured creamy oatmeal cooked with warm water, a little honey, and cinnamon, topped with toasted almonds and chopped dried figs. Her homemade almond crumble—made with flour, butter, honey, and a pinch of nutmeg—was baked until golden and melted beneath the fruit topping.

She prepared it in the early hours of the morning, while still in bed at two a.m., using the quiet time to experiment with classic New England flavors. Maria Lopez’s feedback helped refine the crumble’s texture and flavor, while Isabella’s input guided the presentation. Sharon arranged the oatmeal in a small ceramic bowl, decorating it with a sprig of rosemary and a small decorative cookie. The aroma of toasted nuts, honey, fig, and cinnamon filled the kitchen, giving her a peaceful, inviting start to the day.

It was a quiet weekday breakfast prepared for the cafe, but the careful attention to detail reminded Sharon of her artistic nature. The meal felt both familiar and personally creative, balancing her routine with the spontaneous creativity that keeps her inspired.
