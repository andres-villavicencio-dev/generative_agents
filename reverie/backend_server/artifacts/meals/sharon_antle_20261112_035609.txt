Sharon prepared Sourdough Sear & Herb Croissant for the weekend baking workshop. The dish combines a rustic sourdough loaf with flaky croissants, fresh herbs, and a savory herb-herb sauce.

The croissants were baked with butter, flour, and sea salt until golden, then brushed with a warm cream and garnished with chives and dried thyme. The sourdough was toasted beneath a broiler and served alongside the croissants. She also added a simple herb butter and a small bowl of warm cranberry sauce.

The aroma filled the kitchen: a rich, nutty scent from the sourdough, buttery pastry, and the bright, fresh scent of rosemary and thyme. The presentation was simple yet elegant—the dark crust of the bread and pale, buttery croissants were arranged neatly on a wooden platter, with the colorful cranberry sauce adding a pop of visual contrast.

The meal was prepared for the weekend baking workshop, where Sharon wanted to share her blend of classic New England baking and spontaneous experimentation with the other participants.
