Dish Name: Midnight Croissant with Honey-Lemon Curd and Fresh Herbs

Sharon Antle prepared a light, aromatic breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, adding the cooked croissants to the plate before the café opened. The meal included two golden, flaky croissants, honey-lemon curd, fresh thyme and parsley, and warm toast.

She began by rolling out the dough, cutting it into uniform shapes, and baking the croissants in a preheated oven until their golden crusts were crisp. After cooling, she folded the warm croissants into the warm honey-lemon curd, drizzled the honey over the tops, and finished them with finely chopped herbs. A small piece of warm toast and a glass of chilled tea completed the breakfast.

The aroma was warm and inviting—crisp pastry, honey, lemon, and thyme filled the kitchen. Sharon presented the croissants on a white plate, arranging them in a neat row and garnishing them with visible herbs. The occasion was a weekend baking workshop: she was organizing the event, tasting new recipes, and developing a line of sustainably packaged pastries inspired by Isabella’s input and Maria Lopez’s feedback.
