On a chilly Monday, Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Lemon-Cream Butter. The key ingredients were fresh brioche dough, unsalted butter, lemon zest, fresh thyme, toasted almonds, and a crisp apple topping. She began by rolling the dough and brushing it with melted butter, then scored it into delicate shapes. Baked until golden and slightly crisp, the croissants were folded and sliced, with a thin layer of almond cream and apple filling.

The kitchen smelled of warm butter, toasted almonds, lemon zest, and thyme. Each golden pastry was arranged on a dark serving platter, garnished with almonds and a drizzle of honey. Sharon served it at Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe as the opening for her weekend baking workshop, designed to inspire attendees to create their own creative pastries.
