Sharon Antle prepared a Savory New England-Style Lamb and Herb Croissant tasting menu at Hobbs Cafe. The dish was designed for a weekend baking workshop, blending classic recipes with her spontaneous artistic experiments.

The croissant was filled with a savory herb-crusted lamb, finely diced root vegetables, pearl onions, thyme, rosemary, garlic, and a small knob of butter. It was baked until golden, then finished with a crisp crust and a delicate lemon-pepper glaze. The lamb was served warm with a bright, herbaceous jus and a small portion of crisp root vegetable salad.

Herbs, warm lamb, and butter gave the meal an inviting aroma of toasted nuts, green leaves, and toasted bread. She arranged the croissants on a slate board, garnished each with a sprig of rosemary, a lemon wedge, and a few edible flowers. The presentation was artistic yet simple, reflecting Sharon’s meticulous approach to baking.

The tasting menu was prepared for a Saturday workshop at Hobbs Cafe, allowing participants to experience her evolving blend of traditional flavors and creative experimentation.
