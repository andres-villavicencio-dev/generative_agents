Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Midnight Croissant for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish consists of golden, laminated croissants filled with creamy buttery cream cheese and a hint of lemon zest, topped with fresh strawberries and toasted almonds.

She rolled the croissants in a sheet of parchment, cut them, and brushed the edges with a little butter before baking them until they turned golden brown and puffed. She baked them early in the morning, after several midnight raids. The kitchen filled with the rich scent of butter, lemons, and freshly baked pastry, followed by the bright aroma of fruit.

She presented the pastries on a white ceramic platter, arranging the croissants in a circular pattern and placing the garnishes neatly beside them. Each was accompanied by a small plate of sliced strawberries and almonds. The setting was elegant and inviting, prepared for Isabella’s weekend baking workshop, where customers would taste the warm, artisanal pastries before the workshop began.
