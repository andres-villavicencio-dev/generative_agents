Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Dark Chocolate Fondant for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, serving it as the opening piece to her weekend tasting workshop.

The dish featured two golden-brown croissants brushed with a warm cinnamon-lavender glaze, accompanied by a rich dark chocolate fondant and a drizzle of dark chocolate ganache. Key ingredients included butter, flour, powdered sugar, vanilla, baking powder, and a pinch of cinnamon. The croissants were rolled in flour, brushed with the glaze, baked until crisp, and then placed on a bed of dark chocolate ganache with the fondant folded atop them.

As the oven finished baking, the kitchen filled with the buttery aroma of warm croissants, sweet vanilla, and the deep, velvety scent of chocolate. Sharon sliced the fondant to reveal its glossy interior, drizzled the ganache, and plated the arrangement on a warm white ceramic plate.

The tasting session was an intimate, community-focused gathering, designed to encourage guests to try the new croissant recipes and share their favorite flavors. Sharon’s meticulous attention to texture and aroma transformed the meal into a sensory celebration of classic baking with an artistic touch.
