Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant Collection with Buttermilk Pancakes and Coffee.

The breakfast began with the slow heating of fresh coffee and steamed milk. She arranged the hot coffee in a ceramic pitcher, placed two golden croissants on a serving platter, and set the buttermilk pancakes beside them. The aroma was warm, rich, and unmistakably bakery-like—creamy vanilla, toasted butter, caramelized sugar, and the faint scent of buttered pastry rising from the coffee.

Sharon sliced the croissants into neat halves, lightly brushed them with a little more coffee, and arranged them in a gentle arc around the pancakes. The presentation was simple yet intimate: the golden-brown pastry, the soft white pancake, and the steaming milk creating a visually balanced, rustic table.

The meal was prepared in the early morning hours for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, with the Midnight Croissant Collection and her sustainable-pastries line in mind. Sharon had not yet decided whether to work the weekend, so this quiet breakfast served as a peaceful way to reflect on her week and prepare for the upcoming Tuesday.
