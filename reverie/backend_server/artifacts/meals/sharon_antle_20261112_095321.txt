Sharon Antle prepared Misty-Seasoned Overnight Oats, a warm, comforting breakfast that reflected her meticulous, artistic approach to everyday routines. The dish was made using oats, chia seeds, almond milk, maple syrup, cinnamon, nutmeg, and a generous handful of dried cranberries. She toasted the oats briefly in a dry pan to add a golden, nutty aroma before combining them with the warm milk and allowing the mixture to cool into a glossy, inviting oatmeal. The dried cranberries added a subtle tartness and visual contrast, while the cinnamon and nutmeg gave the dish a cozy, aromatic finish.

She presented the oatmeal in a simple ceramic bowl, topped with a drizzle of homemade honey and a few fresh raspberries. The meal was served alongside a small side of crusty bread and a glass of iced tea. Prepared at 2:30 a.m., the breakfast was meant to be nourishing and reflective, honoring her quiet moment of gratitude after a full week of baking and cafe responsibilities. On Tuesday, February 14, it was the first of several quiet mornings, before her plans for the Midnight Croissant Collection and the sustainably packaged-pastries line with Isabella Rodriguez could take over.
