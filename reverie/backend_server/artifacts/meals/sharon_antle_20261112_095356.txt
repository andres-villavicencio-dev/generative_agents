Dish Name: Midnight Croissant with Honey-Butter Cream Cheese

Sharon Antle prepared a classic New England breakfast for Tuesday, February 14. The centerpiece was a golden-brown croissant, layered with honey-glazed cheese, warm honey, and a spoonful of cream.

Key Ingredients:
Croissant dough made from flour, butter, sugar, eggs, and milk
Honey and cream cheese for the filling
Honey for glazing and drizzling
Sliced cream cheese and fresh berries for presentation

Preparation Method:
Sharon brushed the pastry with honey, layered the filling in the croissant, and baked it at 375°F (190°C) until the edges were crisp and the interior was golden. She then folded it into a warm bowl, drizzled it with honey, and topped it with a generous amount of cream cheese and a few fresh berries.

Aroma and Presentation:
The kitchen filled with the rich, buttery scent of honeyed dough and warm cheese. It was presented on a white linen plate with the croissant resting atop the cream and berries, surrounded by a small dish of honey, fresh berries, and a cup of steaming coffee.

Occasion:
This was a quiet Tuesday breakfast prepared for Sharon’s reflection on the past week. She had run the Hobbs Cafe and the Midnight Croissant Collection, including Maria Lopez’s morning feedback, while developing the sustainably packaged-pastries line with Isabella Rodriguez. The meal offered a gentle pause before her usual 8 p.m. bedtime.
