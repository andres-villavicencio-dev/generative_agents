Sharon prepared Mistyfield Croissant with Honeyed Apricot Jam, a refined breakfast treat for Tuesday, February 14. The dish combines a flaky, golden-brown croissant with a warm, honeyed apricot jam.

She began by rolling out the dough, adding a few drops of egg wash, and shaping it into a perfect, symmetrical croissant. After baking until crisp and golden, she sliced the pastry and filled it with the jam. For the jam, she combined apricot puree, a pinch of lemon zest, a splash of warm milk, and a little honey, simmering it gently until it thickened and bubbled.

The room filled with the rich, caramel-like aroma of toasted dough, honey, and dried apricot. Sharon presented it on a small white ceramic plate, arranging the croissant in the center and the jam in a neat, rounded bowl beside it. The muted pink-and-gold palette, softened by the delicate lemon, reflected her meticulous, artistic approach.

Occasion: A quiet Tuesday morning breakfast honoring her week’s baking, including the Midnight Croissant Collection and the sustainably packaged-pastries line. The meal served her a cup of coffee, offering the warmth of a simple, well-made treat.
