Sharon prepared Hobbs Midnight Croissant for her Tuesday, February 14 morning ritual. The dish is a golden-brown, flaky croissant filled with a creamy lemon curd and topped with a dusting of sea-salted granola, toasted pistachios, and a drizzle of melted vanilla butter.

She began by gently folding the dough into a loose spiral, letting it rise until it doubled in size. The warm kitchen filled with the buttery scent of rising yeast and the subtle aroma of nutty caramel. Before baking, she brushed the surface with lemon zest and sprinkled a pinch of cinnamon and a few crushed pistachios, adding a quiet, creative surprise.

For presentation, the croissant rested on a linen-covered board beside a small bowl of warm lemon curd and a handful of garnishes. It was simple yet thoughtfully arranged, evoking the balance between her disciplined baking craft and spontaneous artistic experimentation.

The meal was meant to be a peaceful transition into a new day. After reflecting on her week’s work with Isabella Rodriguez and Maria Lopez’s feedback, she prepared the croissant for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, ready to begin her baking duties and creative responsibilities with intention and warmth.
