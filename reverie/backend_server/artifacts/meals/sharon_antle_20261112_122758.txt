Sharon Antle prepared Morning Croissant with Maple-Butter Cream, a classic New England pastry served at Hobbs Cafe. The dish features golden, flaky croissants brushed with melted butter and topped with a rich, sweet cream of maple and butter. She folded in a few fresh berries and chopped pistachios before baking them until the pastry was crisp and the filling was warm and creamy.

The kitchen filled with the familiar scent of butter, vanilla, cinnamon, and toasted nuts. Sharon arranged the croissants on a white ceramic platter, letting their golden colors and delicate shapes create an inviting visual. She served them with a side of warm sour cream and a simple seasonal fruit compote.

The meal was prepared for Tuesday, February 14, when Sharon reflected on her week and celebrated the success of her Midnight Croissant Collection and sustainably packaged-pastries line for Isabella Rodriguez.
