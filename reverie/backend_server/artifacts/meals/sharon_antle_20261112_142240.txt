Sharon prepared a Midnight Rose Croissant with Local Honey and Smoked Brie, the centerpiece of her Tuesday brunch. She used a warm, buttery shortcrust pastry, filling it with creamy white brie, honey, and a hint of smoked paprika. The butter warmed the pastry, while the honey softened the brie and the smoked paprika added a gentle warmth.

The aroma was rich and inviting: warm butter, sweet honey, creamy brie, and faintly smoky notes filled the kitchen as she brushed the finished pastries onto a parchment-lined platter. The croissants were golden and flaky, arranged on a white ceramic plate with a sprig of rosemary and a small glass of chilled lemonade beside them.

The occasion was Sharon’s Tuesday morning brunch at the Hobbs Cafe, designed to welcome guests and celebrate the local ingredients she had tested during the week.
