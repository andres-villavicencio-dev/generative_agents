On Tuesday, February 14, Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Croissant with Creamy Lemon-Blueberry Compote for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish features two golden, flaky croissants brushed with butter, filled with warm cream cheese and toasted pecans, and served alongside a bright lemon-blueberry compote made from ripe blueberries, lemon zest, and a touch of warm cream.

She prepared the meal with meticulous care: the croissants were rolled, brushed with softened butter, and baked until the pastry turned crisp, while the fillings were gently warmed to preserve their richness. The compote simmered until the berries softened and the fruit released its vivid colors, with the lemon brightening the warm, buttery aroma.

The presentation was elegant yet inviting: the croissants were arranged in a small wooden tray, dusted with powdered sugar and drizzled with a little more butter, while the compote was poured in a shallow dish beside them. A small card reading “A quiet celebration of morning and midnight baking” completed the thoughtful, romantic atmosphere.

The occasion was Sharon’s pre-opening stocking routine for Tuesday, February 14. She arranged the shelves before Carmen opened the cafe, carefully placing her signature pastries, homemade goods, and sustainably packaged options among the fresh stock. The meal served as a quiet reflection of her balanced week—her baking, recipe development, and cafe responsibilities—and a personal celebration of the Hobbs Cafe’s evolving identity.
