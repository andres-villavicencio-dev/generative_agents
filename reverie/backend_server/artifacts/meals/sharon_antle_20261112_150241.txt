Sharon prepared Midnight Croissant Collection, a signature pastry inspired by Maria Lopez’s feedback that the morning croissants lacked. The dish features two golden, flaky croissants filled with a creamy vanilla custard, dark chocolate ganache, and a delicate meringue topping.

She worked the pastries in the Hobbs Cafe kitchen before dawn, rolling the dough, filling the pastries, and baking them until they turned a rich golden brown. The warm aroma of melted butter, vanilla, and chocolate drifted through the bakery, while the cooling pastries filled the cool morning with a comforting scent.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: two perfectly parallel croissants stood side by side on a wooden board, each topped with a glossy meringue and a small edible flower. A handwritten note beside them read, “Made for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.”

The occasion was Sharon’s quiet reflection on a full week of cafe responsibilities and baking. It was a personal celebration of her craft, with the Midnight Croissant Collection serving as a tribute to the recipes that had inspired her.
