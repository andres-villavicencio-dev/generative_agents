Sharon Antle prepared Morning Harvest Croissant Buns, a simple, nourishing breakfast for her baking workshop. The buns were made with whole-grain flour, softened unsalted butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and a hint of lemon zest, folded into a warm oven-baked dough before being filled with buttered cream cheese and topped with crushed pecans and bright yellow lemon slices.

She prepared them by rolling the dough into a long log, cutting it into small rounds, and baking them in a preheated oven until the tops were golden and the filling puffed. After cooling briefly, she arranged them in a shallow wooden tray, garnishing each with a lemon wedge and a dusting of sea salt.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of buttery dough, cinnamon, and toasted nuts. The presentation was rustic and inviting—warm, earthy pastries stacked neatly for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, ready to inspire the weekend workshops. It was the perfect energizing break before her focus on developing the line of sustainably packaged pastries.
