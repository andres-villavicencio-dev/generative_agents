On Monday, February 13, Sharon prepared a simple, warm breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez and Maria Lopez at Hobbs Cafe. The meal was Mornish Croissant and Honey-Glazed Apple Slices.

The preparation began with buttered, flaky croissants that Sharon had baked before dawn. She sliced ripe apples, brushed them lightly with honey, and arranged them alongside the croissants. A drizzle of warm honey, a sprinkle of cinnamon, and a few whole mint leaves gave the dish a delicate, inviting aroma.

Sharon presented the breakfast on a small white platter with a rustic ceramic plate underneath, surrounded by a cup of coffee and a small glass of water. The pastel croissants, golden brown and flaky, were arranged in two neat rows, their glossy apple slices creating a colorful contrast. The meal was a quiet, comforting breakfast for the weekend baking workshop, honoring Isabella’s input on sustainable packaging and Maria’s feedback on the croissant recipes.
