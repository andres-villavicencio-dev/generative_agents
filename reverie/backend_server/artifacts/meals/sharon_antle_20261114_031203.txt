Sharon Antle prepared Midnight Midnight Croissant with Fig and Blueberry Compote for her early-morning workshop at Hobbs Cafe. She used a high-protein sourdough crust, buttery laminated dough, fresh figs, blueberries, and a hint of cinnamon, with lemon zest and vanilla extract to enhance the aroma.

The croissants were brushed with softened butter and baked until golden and crisp, then stacked to retain their shape. Each piece was brushed with a light glaze before being served with a warm, fragrant compote of figs, blueberries, and lemon juice, topped with a drizzle of honey and a dusting of flaky sea salt.

The meal was presented on a simple white platter and accompanied by a chilled glass of sparkling water. It was part of Sharon’s weekend baking workshop, where she gathered other pastry makers to discuss her morning croissant ideas and how Maria Lopez’s feedback would shape the next batch. The workshop’s creative, communal atmosphere made the simple breakfast feel both nourishing and inspiring.
