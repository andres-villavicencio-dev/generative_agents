Sharon Antle prepared Savory Herb-Crusted Lamb with Roasted Root Vegetables for a private family dinner at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured tender lamb loin, seasonal root vegetables such as carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, along with thyme, rosemary, garlic, lemon, olive oil, and a pinch of smoked paprika.

She seared the lamb first to develop a golden crust, then added the vegetables to the oven with rosemary and lemon zest. The herbs were gently infused into the vegetable juices, while the lamb remained moist and flavorful through the final sear.

The aroma was warm, savory, and fragrant, with notes of rosemary, garlic, lemon, and the subtle smoke of the paprika. It was presented on a rustic platter in a dark wooden bowl, arranged with the colorful vegetables around the lamb and garnished with a sprig of rosemary. The occasion was a relaxed Sunday dinner with friends, celebrating the weekend baking workshops and the cafe’s upcoming sustainable pastries.
