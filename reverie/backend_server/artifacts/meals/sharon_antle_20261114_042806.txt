On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared Noir Croissant Crisp, a small-batch croissant paired with roasted chickpeas and a sharp lemon-herb dressing. She used a flourless dough of warm bread, melted butter, and a pinch of salt, then rolled it between two sheets of parchment paper to achieve a crisp, golden exterior. The croissants were baked in a 425°F (220°C) oven until their crust turned deep amber before being served warm.

The meal was prepared for a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon and Maria Lopez tasted the croissants side by side. Maria’s feedback encouraged Sharon to incorporate a touch of toasted pumpkin seeds, a hint of warm caramel, and a slightly stronger rosemary finish, transforming the croissant from a simple test into a more comforting holiday-inspired treat.

Aromatic, the pastry emitted a buttery, nutty scent as it baked, while the toasted seeds added a savory warmth. Sharon arranged the warm croissants on a slate platter with the crisp chickpeas and a bright lemon-herb dressing, creating a visually elegant and inviting presentation.
