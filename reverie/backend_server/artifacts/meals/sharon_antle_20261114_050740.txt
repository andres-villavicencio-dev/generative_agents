Midnight Croissant with Fig and Brown Butter

Sharon prepared a simple yet refined croissant with fig, brown butter, and toasted almonds for a quiet weekend brunch. She used a warm Dutch oven, buttered the bottom, and filled a flaky pastry with the figs, brown butter, and almonds. After baking until the golden crust was crisp and the center melted, she finished the dish with fresh mint, a squeeze of lemon, and a drizzle of honey.

The aroma filled the cafe kitchen: rich, buttery, sweet, and subtly earthy. The warm croissant offered a crisp outer layer that cracked against the warm brown butter, while the fig added a soft, caramelized sweetness.

The plate was arranged on a linen placemat with the croissant resting beside a small bowl of honey and a sprig of mint. It was simple enough to be comforting, yet visually inviting—perfect for her weekend baking workshop.
