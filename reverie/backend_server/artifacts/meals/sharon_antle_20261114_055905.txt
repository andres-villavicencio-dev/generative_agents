Dish Name: Midnight Croissant with Warm Butter and Honey

Sharon Antle prepared a large batch of croissants for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe, using the same fresh butter, flour, yeast, salt, and sugar she had measured before dawn. The croissants were folded, brushed with warm butter, and baked at 425°F (220°C) until golden and puffed. They were then arranged on a slate platter, accompanied by a small bowl of honey, toasted almonds, and a sprig of rosemary.

As Sharon assembled the croissants, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of yeast, butter, and warm spices. She carried the pan to the pastry station, where Maria Lopez’s feedback had helped refine the recipe, while Isabella Rodriguez’s input shaped the presentation. The croissants were arranged in neat rows, each dusted lightly with powdered sugar and garnished with a sprig of mint.

The occasion was Sharon’s weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe. Though she was tired, the completed croissants offered a moment of quiet satisfaction. She placed the platter on the counter, took a small bite, and smiled, ready to begin her evening at 8 p.m.
