Sharon Antle prepared a Braised Beef and Lemon Herb Sandwich with a Creamy Herb Croissant for the Hobbs Cafe’s open house on Monday, February 13.

She sliced a tender ribeye into the sandwich and topped it with thinly sliced yellow onions, thyme, rosemary, lemon zest, and a generous amount of fresh parsley. A secret ingredient was the slow-cooked beef itself, infused with a little apple, garlic, and a splash of red wine. She layered the warm bread with the savory filling before finishing it with a drizzle of balsamic glaze.

The accompanying croissant was baked with butter, a touch of cream cheese, and a pinch of cinnamon, then dusted with coarse sea salt. Its golden, flaky exterior gave the meal a warm, comforting aroma of toasted dough, cream, and herbs.

Presented on a slate platter with the croissant resting beside a small bowl of lemon-lime yogurt and a sprig of rosemary, the meal was arranged with intention and care. It was an intimate, creative tasting menu intended to celebrate the cafe’s creative collaboration and the community’s growing appreciation of handmade, locally sourced food.
