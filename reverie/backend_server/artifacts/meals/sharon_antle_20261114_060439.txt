On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared a Molten Chocolate Crème Brûlée for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured a rich custard made with whole milk, egg yolks, vanilla extract, and dark chocolate, topped with a fragrant caramelized sugar layer.

Sharon followed a classic New England technique, first creaming the butter and sugar, then gently folding in the eggs. After a long, careful bake, she flipped the pan and let the custard set before returning it to the oven for a minute or two to melt the center. She brushed the top with a thin layer of maple syrup and basted the caramel with a little butter, creating a glossy, golden crust that crackled when heated.

Aromatic notes of vanilla, chocolate, caramel, and warm butter filled the kitchen. The pastries were arranged on a wooden board, garnished with toasted almonds, a mint leaf, and a sprig of rosemary, with each piece dusted lightly with cocoa.

The occasion was the weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon and Isabella prepared the café’s signature croissants alongside the crème brûlée for guests.
