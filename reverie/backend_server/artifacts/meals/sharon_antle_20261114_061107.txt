Sharon Antle prepared a Midnight Croissant & Lemon-Thyme Tart for Isabella Rodriguez's Hobbs Cafe. The tart featured golden, flaky croissants, a buttery lemon-tart base, fresh thyme, honey, and a hint of cinnamon.

She arranged the warm croissants on a parchment-lined baking sheet, carefully balancing them so each had room to rise without touching. The oven was set to 425°F, and as it preheated, she added a little flour and the tart filling. After the croissants puffed and turned golden, she brushed the lemony glaze over the filling and finished it with a few crumbly, golden crumbs.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm, buttery, and fragrant with citrus, thyme, and cinnamon. To present the dish, Sharon placed the tart in a shallow ceramic dish, garnished with a few thyme sprigs and a dusting of sea salt, then arranged the warm croissants beside it. It was prepared for Isabella’s weekend baking workshop, a gathering of friends who loved experimenting with classic New England pastries.
