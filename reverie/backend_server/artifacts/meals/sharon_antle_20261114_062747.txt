Sharon Antle prepared a Molasses-Crusted Croissant with Brioche Filling for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish combines golden-brown croissants with a rich brioche filling, while the crusts are dusted with a hint of cinnamon and a few flakes of sea salt. Key ingredients include butter, flour, sugar, molasses, eggs, milk, vanilla, brioche dough, and a simple cinnamon-sugar topping.

Sharon began by gently folding cold butter into the dough, then folding, rolling, and cutting the croissants until they were evenly golden and fully cooked. She baked them early in the morning, letting their crisp outer shells develop before filling them with warm, buttery brioche. The aroma was unmistakable: butter, vanilla, molasses, cinnamon, and toasted dough.

She arranged the croissants in a rustic wooden tray, garnishing each one with a dusting of cinnamon sugar and a sprig of fresh thyme. The warm croissants were displayed on a small platter for Isabella’s weekend brunch, where they would serve as a comforting, aromatic centerpiece. The occasion was a relaxed Hobbs Cafe gathering for the weekend’s baking workshops, celebrating the blend of classic New England recipes and spontaneous artistic experimentation.
