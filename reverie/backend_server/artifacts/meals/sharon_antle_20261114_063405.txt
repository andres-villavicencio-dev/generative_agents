The meal Sharon Antle prepared was a Mediterranean Herb Croissant Brunch, designed as a light breakfast for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe.

She sliced two perfectly golden croissants into bite-sized portions and topped them with feta, roasted red peppers, Kalamata olives, caper brine, and fresh herbs. The savory feta and caramelized peppers contrasted beautifully with the buttery croissant, while the briny olives and lemony dressing added a Mediterranean finish.

She baked them in a preheated 400°F (200°C) oven for 10–12 minutes, allowing the pastry to crisp around the edges without burning. The warm, buttery aroma filled the cafe, with notes of roasted garlic, fresh herbs, and a hint of lemon.

She presented the croissants on a slate platter with a small bowl of caper brine and a sprig of rosemary, creating an elegant, rustic brunch. The dish was prepared for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe’s weekend breakfast workshop, offering guests a chance to taste and recreate Sharon’s creative approach to classic croissants.
