Meal: Midnight Croissant Sandwiches with Lemon-Herb Butter and Honey-Glazed Berries

Key ingredients: flour, butter, eggs, milk, flour (for croissants), sourdough bread, butter, fresh lemon, honey, dried cranberries, blueberries, and organic berries.

Preparation method: Sharon begins by baking croissants from scratch before dawn. She arranges them on paper liners and then makes lemon-herb butter by melting butter with lemon zest and honey. After toasting the sourdough, she fills the warm croissants with a generous layer of butter, topping with honey-glazed berries and arranging the slices. The sandwiches are cooled, stacked, and refrigerated before being served.

Aroma: The kitchen fills with the rich, buttery scent of baking croissants, a sharp lemon fragrance, and the subtle sweetness of honey and berries.

Presentation: The croissants are stacked with a crisp paper liner underneath, creating a neat, layered display. A small glass of cold lemonade accompanies the sandwiches, with additional berries and herbs arranged beside them.

Occasion: Sharon prepares the meal for a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe. The crisp, warm presentation is meant to inspire guests with the creativity behind blending classic New England recipes with spontaneous artistic experimentation.
