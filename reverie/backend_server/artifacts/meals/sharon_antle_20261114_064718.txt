On Monday, February 13, Sharon prepared a Mediterranean-Light Croissant Sandwich for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The key ingredients were buttery croissants, fresh spinach, crisp cucumber, feta, capers, olive oil, lemon juice, and a creamy white sauce.

She sliced the buttery croissants lengthwise, layered the vegetables and cheese, and brushed them generously with the warm white sauce. Her meticulous knife work created neat, parallel cuts, while the lemon zest and capers added a subtle burst of acidity.

The warm sandwich rose with a rich, buttery aroma, balanced by the crispness of the vegetables and the delicate fragrance of feta. Sharon presented it on a small wooden board beside a glass of chilled lemonade. The occasion was a weekend baking workshop, where Isabella and her guests would taste-test the flavors and share the croissant-inspired lunch.
