On Monday, February 13, Sharon Antle prepared a Molten-Crepe Breakfast Bowl for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish combines delicate crepes with warm eggs, avocado, diced tomatoes, chives, and a little honey.

Sharon prepared the crepes on a clean baking station, using a flour-and-water batter, a pinch of salt, and a generous amount of butter. After cooking the crepes, she arranged them on a platter and poached two sunny-side-up eggs, adding the tomatoes, avocado, chives, and honey just before serving. She garnished the dish with a sprig of parsley and a drizzle of honey, presenting it in a simple white ceramic bowl with a small wooden spoon and a folded napkin.

Her kitchen smelled of butter, toasted wheat, roasted egg, and herbs. The occasion was a weekend baking workshop, and the meal reflected Sharon’s meticulous, artistic approach to classic New England baking while incorporating the cafe’s fresh ingredients.
