Sharon Antle prepared Amber Crostini with Lavender-Honey Tartlets for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured golden-brown crostini topped with honeyed lavender, toasted almonds, and a drizzle of warm honey, while the tartlets contained a buttery base, fresh blueberries, and a delicate lavender-scented filling.

Sharon began by warming the crostini in a skillet over low heat, then folded them into a warm, fragrant pastry dough. She folded the dough, rolled it out, and cut it into tartlets, filling each with blueberries and a spoonful of honey-infused lavender mixture. The kitchen filled with the scent of toasted butter, vanilla, and crushed lavender buds.

Each crostini was arranged in a shallow bowl, garnished with chopped pistachios and a lemon twist, while the tartlets were nestled alongside them. The pastries were presented for the weekend baking workshop, offering Isabella an opportunity to experience both classic New England baking and Sharon’s spontaneous, artistic experiments.
