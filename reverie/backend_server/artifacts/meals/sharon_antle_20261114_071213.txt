Sharon Antle prepared Golden Hour Croissants, a fresh, buttery croissant line designed for Isabella Rodriguez’s Hobbs Cafe. She used high-protein whole-wheat flour, unsalted butter, eggs, milk, vanilla extract, and a pinch of sea salt. The croissants were rolled, brushed with butter, and baked until crisp on the outside while remaining soft and flaky inside.

The kitchen filled with the warm, buttery aroma of toasted dough, warm milk, and vanilla. Each golden, rectangular pastry was arranged in a shallow oval platter, garnished with a dusting of cinnamon and a small sprig of fresh thyme, with a small glass of milk beside them.

The occasion was a weekend baking workshop at Hobbs Cafe, where Sharon was helping Isabella Rodriguez prepare pastries for the cafe and developing a sustainably packaged croissant line.
