Soo-jin Park prepared Sesame-Glazed Roast Chicken with Vegetables and Grilled Noodles, a simple one-pan dinner suitable for a Friday family meal.

She sliced chicken breasts, garlic, ginger, and onion, then sautéed them in olive oil until the chicken was golden and cooked through. Next, she added carrots, potatoes, broccoli, and bell peppers to the pan, seasoning them with salt, pepper, and a splash of soy sauce. The vegetables softened as the chicken released its juices. A tablespoon of sesame oil and toasted sesame seeds were stirred in at the end for a nutty aroma.

She arranged the chicken on a wide serving platter, topped it with a generous portion of noodles and vegetables, and drizzled the remaining sauce over everything. The meal smelled warm and savory, with the scent of roasted chicken, garlic, and sesame filling the kitchen.

The occasion was Soo-jin’s Friday dinner at home. The tight budget and irregular income made the dish appealing, and it was prepared quickly enough to keep the family together.
