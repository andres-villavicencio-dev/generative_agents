Soo-jin Park prepared Bread & Egg Breakfast, a simple meal made with freshly baked sourdough bread, eggs, leftover vegetables, and a modest amount of butter and olive oil. She toasted the bread, fried two eggs, and cooked a small portion of zucchini and carrots, checking the pantry before gathering everything together.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of sourdough, toasted bread, and butter. The golden-brown crust contrasted with the fluffy, yellow yolks and the bright green of the vegetables, arranged in a neat oval on a white plate. She added a drizzle of olive oil and a sprinkle of herbs for a touch of color.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Saturday breakfast at Hobbs Cafe, where Soo-jin was checking in and sketching the café’s bustling atmosphere. It was a practical, unpretentious start to her day, combining the familiar comfort of a homemade breakfast with the creative inspiration she sought in her freelance work.
