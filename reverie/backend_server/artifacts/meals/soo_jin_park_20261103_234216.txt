Soo-jin prepared Sesame Scrambled Eggs with Kimchi and Grilled Veggies for a quick, filling lunch. She used two eggs, a bowl of mixed kimchi, thinly sliced radish, carrots, onions, and a sprinkle of sesame oil. She scrambled the eggs in a pan, seasoned the kimchi with garlic, ginger, and a little soy sauce, then added the vegetables just before serving.

The dish had a warm, savory aroma of toasted sesame, caramelized onions, and pungent kimchi. Soo-jin arranged the golden eggs, bright green vegetables, and colorful kimchi on a small white plate, garnished with sesame seeds and a drizzle of soy sauce. It was simple but satisfying, perfect for a rushed Wednesday lunch while she checked the community bulletin board for her picture-book commission.
