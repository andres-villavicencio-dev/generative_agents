Soo-jin prepared Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for the café run at Hobbs Cafe and Johnson Park.  

The dish featured chicken thighs, cherry tomatoes, onions, bell peppers, carrots, and baby potatoes. She seared the chicken in oil until golden, then added the vegetables and a splash of lemon juice and fresh herbs. The sauce reduced gently until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were caramelized.  

The kitchen filled with a warm smoky aroma of garlic, herbs, and roasted vegetables, mingling with the bright citrus scent of the lemon. She arranged the meal on a white platter, placing the golden-brown chicken in the center with colorful vegetables arranged around it.  

The occasion was a casual café run, and she wanted the food to feel inviting and familiar. The presentation was simple and rustic, with the meal ready to be shared with friends or customers.
