Soo-jin prepared Pork and Vegetable Pan-Seared Rice for her Tuesday dinner. She sliced pork belly into bite-sized strips, browned it lightly, and stir-fried it with chopped carrots, bell pepper, onions, mushrooms, and a splash of soy sauce. The rice was prepared separately, rinsed, and cooked until fluffy before being combined with the savory pork.

The kitchen filled with the scent of caramelized pork, toasted rice, and warm vegetables. The final presentation was simple yet inviting: the rice arranged neatly in a shallow bowl, topped with the colorful stir-fry and a small garnish of fresh cilantro. She served it with steamed white rice and a side of pickles, making it a practical, family-style meal.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday dinner, balancing her freelance work and planned café run. She prepared the meal independently, checking the fridge, pantry, and refrigerator for any leftover staples before shopping, while also keeping in mind Rafael’s schedule for helping organize the kitchen and sharing a drink afterward.
