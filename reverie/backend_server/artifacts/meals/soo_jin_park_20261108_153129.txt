Soo-jin prepared Kimchi-Ginger Beef Banchan, a hearty Korean side dish she made to share with Rafael at Willow Court 12. The meal featured crisp lettuce, thinly sliced daikon, and radish, with the kimchi adding a bright, tangy flavor. A small bowl of ginger-ginger sauce, sesame oil, garlic, and soy sauce was used to warm the vegetables and give the dish a warm, aromatic finish.

The preparation was simple but careful: she sliced the daikon and radish, lightly sautéed the vegetables until they became slightly translucent, and added the warm kimchi. The ginger-ginger sauce was brushed over everything before the beef was cooked. Soak the beef in water first, then pan-fry it in oil until it was browned, and serve it alongside the vegetables.

The aroma rose from the pan—savory beef, fermented kimchi, ginger, garlic, and warm sesame oil mingling with the bright crunch of the vegetables. She presented the dish on a shared platter, arranging the vegetables and meat in an inviting cluster with the kimchi bowl in the center.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Monday dinner at Willow Court 12, when Soo-jin and Rafael shared the food while checking the community bulletin board for odd jobs. The occasion was informal and relaxed, with the neighborhood community board serving as the backdrop for her sketching and small moments of connection.
