On a rainy Monday evening, Soo-jin Park prepared Stir-Fried Chicken with Brown Rice and Green Peas, a simple but comforting dinner for dinner at home. The main dish featured sliced thighs marinated in soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and chili paste, seared until golden, then simmered with vegetables and a splash of water. The brown rice was cooked in the same pan, adding a fragrant aroma of toasted grain and savory broth. Green peas and carrots were stir-fried briefly at the end to retain their bright color.

She served the chicken in a shallow dish, arranged the rice around it, and placed the vegetables and extra peas on top, garnished with a few crisp green onions. The meal smelled warm and inviting, with the sharp aroma of garlic, the earthy scent of roasted chicken, and the fresh, earthy fragrance of the vegetables.

The occasion was a quiet, solitary dinner with no guests or special event. It reflected Soo-jin’s grounded, everyday life: affordable, practical food made on a small budget, accompanied by her sketchbooks and the steady rhythm of her freelance life.
