On Tuesday, February 14, Soo-jin Park prepared a vibrant Korean-Style Stir-Fried Chicken with Kimchi and Gochujang for dinner. The dish featured tender chicken thighs marinated in ginger, garlic, soy sauce, and oyster sauce, then stir-fried with crisp carrots, snap peas, and bok choy. A generous amount of spicy kimchi and a spoonful of rich gochujang added depth and warmth.

She cooked the chicken over a high flame, letting it develop a golden-brown crust, then tossed the vegetables in the pan until the chicken was cooked through and the vegetables had a bright, slightly bitter-tangy texture. The aroma filled the kitchen with the sharp fragrance of garlic, ginger, and the earthy heat of chili peppers and fermented kimchi.

Soo-jin plated the dish on a white ceramic plate and arranged the colorful vegetables and dark kimchi alongside the glossy chicken. She added a small bowl of kimchi juice and a few sprigs of green onion for garnish. The presentation was simple but appetizing, with the red and yellow hues of the kimchi contrasting against the deep orange of the chicken.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday evening, with no confirmed appointments, gigs, or time slots. After cooking, Soo-jin sat down to enjoy the meal, balancing her creative work with an evening of comfort food and conversation.
