Soo-jin prepared Stir-Fried Pork with Sesame and Kimchi, a simple but warming Korean-inspired dinner for a quiet Monday evening. The key ingredients were thinly sliced pork belly, napa cabbage, carrots, snap peas, garlic, ginger, and a generous amount of fresh kimchi. She marinated the pork briefly in sesame oil, soy sauce, garlic, and ginger, then stir-fried it until the meat was tender and the vegetables were crisp. The kimchi was added at the last minute for its bright peppery flavor.

As the kitchen warmed, the aroma filled the small apartment: savory pork, toasted sesame, ginger, garlic, and the sharp tang of kimchi mingled with the soft scent of napa cabbage and carrots. Soo-jin arranged the colorful vegetables and golden pork in a shallow dish, garnishing the center with a few crisp green onions and a drizzle of sesame oil. The simple, rustic presentation evoked a cozy evening meal while she made the most of the few dollars available.

Dinner was prepared on a Monday evening, when Soo-jin was returning home from her freelance illustration work. The occasion was intimate and practical—a quiet meal in Willow Court 12 to end the day without feeling rushed.
