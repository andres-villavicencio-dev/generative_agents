On a Tuesday morning, Soo-jin Park prepared a simple but nourishing Miso-Glazed Tofu Bowl for breakfast at Hobbs Cafe. The dish featured firm tofu marinated in warm miso broth, chopped scallions, ginger, and sesame oil, then gently pan-seared until golden. A side of steamed rice and sliced radish added freshness, while a small bowl of sesame seeds and pickled radish provided a subtle tang.

The kitchen smelled warmly of roasted soy, ginger, garlic, and sesame. Soo-jin arranged the bowl in a neat, asymmetrical manner: the tofu took center stage, the rice was arranged around its base, and the colorful toppings were spaced deliberately. She served it with a clear, mildly sweet miso sauce on the side.

The meal was prepared quickly to make room for sketching townspeople and helping patrons clean up afterward. For Soo-jin, it was more than breakfast—it was a quiet moment of focus and community connection.
