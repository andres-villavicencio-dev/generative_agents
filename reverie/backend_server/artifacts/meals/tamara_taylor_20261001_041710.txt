Tamara’s comforting dinner is a Homemade Chicken & Herb Soup served with warm, crusty sourdough bread. The soup simmers gently in chicken broth enriched with thyme, rosemary, and a touch of lemon zest—her key ingredients. She prepares it by sautéing diced carrots and celery, then blending them into the broth for silky texture. Sliced chicken breasts are folded in at the end to keep them tender. The aroma is earthy yet clean, rising like morning mist from her kitchen window. Spooned over a thick slice of honey-slathered sourdough, it’s served on mismatched vintage plates, reflecting her creative spirit. This nourishing meal, paired with tea and quiet reflection, marks the calm end to her writing day—a gentle celebration of patience and care. The occasion is simple: a homemade embrace for herself after a long, inspiring journey through Whispers of Gentle Courage.
