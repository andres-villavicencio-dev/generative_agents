On Monday, February 13, Tamara Taylor completed her morning routine and then prepared Sunrise Apple-Cinnamon Oat Pancakes for herself and her housemate, Carmen Ortiz. She combined rolled oats, grated apple, eggs, milk, baking powder, cinnamon, chopped pecans, and a touch of vanilla in a large bowl. After letting the batter rest, she spooned portions onto a buttered skillet and cooked them over medium heat until golden, flipping each pancake once before adding warm maple syrup.

The kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of toasted oats, cinnamon, and caramelized apples. Tamara stacked the pancakes on a simple white plate, drizzling them with maple syrup and scattering fresh blueberries over the top. A small glass of orange juice and a mug of tea completed the presentation.

The meal was a relaxed Monday breakfast, shared before Tamara began working on her children’s book series and comic book project. It felt especially cheerful and creative, with Carmen joining her for the quiet start to the day.
