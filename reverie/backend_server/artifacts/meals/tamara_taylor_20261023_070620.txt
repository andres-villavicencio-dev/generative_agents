Tamara prepared a Morning of Golden Eggs for breakfast, a colorful, egg-based dish inspired by her children’s book series. She began by heating a small pot of water and sautéing chopped onions and minced garlic until the onions softened and the garlic turned lightly golden. Then, she cracked three eggs into a pan and cooked them gently, separating the whites from the yolks before adding a splash of milk and a pinch of salt.

The aroma was warm, rich, and unmistakably comforting—fragrant with garlic, onion, and the subtle sweetness of the milk. Tamara arranged the finished eggs on a white ceramic plate, garnishing each with a bright orange carrot stick, a green herb, and a drizzle of warm mustard. She served the dish in a small glass bowl, placing it beside a mug of steaming tea.

The occasion was her first full morning as a 30-year-old author, after an early start and a long day of creative work. Breakfast provided a simple, nurturing pause, while the bright colors and cheerful presentation reflected Tamara’s imaginative side.
