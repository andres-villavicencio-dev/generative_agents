Tamara prepared a comforting Smoked Chicken with Herb Crust for lunch. The dish features tender chicken thighs marinated in a savory mix of lemon juice, garlic, thyme, black pepper, and smoked paprika, with the skin rendered crisp and the meat fully cooked. The chicken is topped with a fragrant herb crust made with flour, chopped parsley, rosemary, lemon zest, and a pinch of salt.

She seared the chicken in a hot pan until the skin turned golden brown, then added the herbs and finished the dish in the oven. The aroma of toasted garlic, citrus, smoked spice, and savory herbs filled the kitchen before the meal was served.

Presented on a simple white plate, the golden-brown chicken sits alongside creamy roasted potatoes and a bright green salad, with a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing. It was a quick, nourishing lunch for Tamara during her busy work routine—inspired by the playful, imaginative world she creates for young readers.
