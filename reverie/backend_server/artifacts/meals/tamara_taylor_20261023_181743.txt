Tamara prepared a warm Herb-Roasted Tomato and Chickpea Salad with Warm Croutons. She washed the tomatoes, chickpeas, and green onions, then sautéed the onions in olive oil until golden. She tossed the tomatoes and chickpeas in the pan, seasoning them with garlic, cumin, oregano, lemon zest, and a touch of balsamic vinegar. The salad was finished with toasted whole-grain croutons and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, roasted tomatoes, and toasted bread. Tamara presented the salad on a white ceramic platter, arranging the colorful vegetables in neat rows and garnishing it with fresh herbs. She served it at 6:00 p.m., just before dinner, making the meal feel special and comforting.
