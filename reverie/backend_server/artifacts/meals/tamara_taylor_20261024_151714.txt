Tamara prepared Herb-Glazed Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Orange Glaze, a comforting dinner meant to balance her demanding creative schedule.

She sliced chicken breast, rosemary, garlic, lemon, orange, and carrots into a small oven-safe dish. She brushed the chicken with a mixture of olive oil, honey, garlic, rosemary, and lemon, then roasted it alongside carrots, onions, and potatoes. The vegetables were glazed with honey, orange zest, and a little olive oil before baking until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were tender-crisp.

The kitchen filled with the warm, fragrant aroma of roasted herbs, caramelized garlic, citrus, and olive oil. Tamara arranged the golden chicken and bright vegetables on a white plate, sprinkling with fresh thyme and a final dusting of sea salt. She served it with a simple lemon-herb salad and a side of crusty bread.

The meal was prepared on a Tuesday, February 14, when she was balancing chapters and research on her children’s books and comic projects with her adult comic work. It was a relaxed, nourishing lunch intended to give her creative energy a moment before heading home at 10 p.m.
