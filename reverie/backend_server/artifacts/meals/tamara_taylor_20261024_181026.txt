Tamara prepared Blueberry Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice, a light, colorful family lunch. She sliced boneless chicken breasts, arranged them around a bed of fluffy rice, and topped them with fresh blueberries, lemon slices, and finely chopped herbs. A drizzle of olive oil, lemon juice, and a touch of garlic created a gentle fragrance that filled the kitchen.

She grilled the chicken until the skin was golden and the meat was fully cooked, then gently arranged the grains and berries in a shallow bowl. The dish presented in a simple white dish with a small lemon garnish, offering a balanced mix of savory, sweet, and bright flavors. It was prepared for a relaxed Sunday lunch—she made it to enjoy with friends and family, enjoying a quiet, nourishing meal without the pressure of her creative work.
