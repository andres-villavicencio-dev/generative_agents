Tamara Taylor prepared a simple, nourishing Hearty Vegetable Soup with Whole-Wheat Bread and Toast, a balanced lunch for her Wednesday workday.

The soup was made by sautéing carrots, celery, onions, and garlic in olive oil, then simmering them with chicken, tomatoes, and a splash of vegetable broth until the vegetables softened and the broth had thickened. She added dried herbs and a pinch of salt, then let it simmer briefly before removing it from heat. The bread was soaked in the same hot broth for a few minutes, warmed through, and served alongside slices of toasted whole-wheat bread and a small handful of chopped parsley.

The aroma was warm and earthy, with the savory depth of carrots and onion mingling with the fragrant herbs and the slightly sweet undertone of the tomatoes. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the translucent soup was ladled into a shallow bowl, while the golden-brown bread and crisp parsley added a cheerful pop of color.

This was a relaxed, uneventful lunch, prepared when no appointments had been scheduled. It served as a gentle pause in her busy day, balancing the creative energy of her children’s book work with the calm routine of her daily life.
