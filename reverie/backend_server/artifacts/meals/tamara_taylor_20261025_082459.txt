Tamara prepared Sunset Broccoli Soup with Roasted Tomato Compote, a warm, comforting dinner for her rest-day evening.

The soup was made by sautéing chopped broccoli and carrots in olive oil until softened, then adding vegetable broth, a splash of lemon juice, and a pinch of salt and pepper. A handful of frozen peas and a small handful of torn parsley finished the dish. The roasted tomato compote was prepared by roasting cherry tomatoes with garlic, olive oil, and a dash of lemon until charred and tender.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of broccoli, garlic, and roasted tomatoes, which carried through the quiet evening. Tamara presented the soup in a shallow white ceramic bowl, garnished with fresh parsley, while the compote was served in a small bowl beside it. She placed a small candle on the table to soften the room’s light, making the meal feel like a cozy bridge between her creative work and her rest.
