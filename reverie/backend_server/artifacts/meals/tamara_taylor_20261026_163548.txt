Tamara prepared Rustic Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables. The meal was a comforting family-style dinner for her Wednesday rest day.

She used a whole chicken, bone-in thighs, carrots, sweet potatoes, and onions. A few tablespoons of olive oil, a generous pinch of sea salt, fresh thyme, lemon zest, and a splash of lemon juice created the key flavors. The chicken was seared in a hot skillet until golden, then pan-seared with the vegetables until their edges turned brown. Afterward, the chicken was transferred to a baking dish and roasted until the skin was crisp and the vegetables were tender.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, and the bright, cheerful scent of lemon. The final presentation arranged the chicken in the center with colorful root vegetables around it, garnished with fresh parsley and lemon slices. The meal was simple, warm, and perfectly suited to a quiet Wednesday, leaving room for her creative writing and manuscript organization.
