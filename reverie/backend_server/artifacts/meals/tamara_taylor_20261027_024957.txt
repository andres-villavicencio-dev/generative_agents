Tamara Taylor prepared a Garden Herb Roast Chicken with roasted root vegetables, lemon, garlic, and a simple herb glaze.

The key ingredients included a whole chicken, root vegetables such as carrots, parsnips, and potatoes, fresh rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, olive oil, and a little butter. She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and herbs, placed the vegetables on the same roasting tray, and arranged them around the bird for even browning. The lemon and garlic were sliced on their edges before cooking, while the rosemary and thyme were loosely added.

Tamara roasted everything in a covered oven at 400°F for about 45 minutes, basting the chicken with pan juices halfway through. When it was done, she finished the vegetables with a little butter, lemon zest, and fresh herbs, then sliced the chicken into portions and arranged the roasted vegetables and lemon slices on a white dinner plate.

The meal had a warm, aromatic scent of rosemary, garlic, and roasted root vegetables. It was served on a quiet Wednesday rest day, after she had reviewed her outstanding children’s book chapter and planned to continue organizing the manuscript and preparing for a creative-writing session.
