Tamara prepared Roasted Root Vegetable Soup with Herb Crust, a warm, nourishing meal fitting her relaxed Wednesday. The stew was made with carrots, potatoes, parsnips, celery, onions, carrots, and fresh herbs, all simmered in a broth with a touch of vegetable stock and garlic. It was finished with a creamy sauce and a generous garnish of parsley and thyme.

Tamara roasted the root vegetables first, then transferred them to a pot with the aromatic vegetables and broth. The soup simmered gently until the vegetables softened and the flavors melded, with the herbs contributing a bright, earthy aroma. The dish was finished in the oven, creating a rich, golden hue.

It was served at 6:00 PM during dinner, accompanied by a simple green salad and a bowl of warm milk. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with colorful roasted vegetables arranged alongside the pale, creamy soup. Tamara sat down for her meal on her rest day, enjoying the comforting aroma and the quiet moment of nourishment.
