Tamara Taylor prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Lemon Herb Butter, a simple, earthy side for dinner. She sliced carrots, parsnips, and baby potatoes, tossed them with olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper, then roasted them at 400°F for about twenty minutes until the skins softened and the flesh turned golden.

She finished the dish with a rich garlic-lemon butter sauce. After turning the vegetables off the heat, she stirred the butter into the pan, letting the garlic warm through, and finished with a final drizzle of zest and a sprinkle of fresh parsley. The aroma was warm and aromatic: toasted garlic, caramelized carrots, and the subtle richness of the butter filled the kitchen.

She served the vegetables alongside a small portion of quinoa or a fresh salad. The presentation was bright and inviting—the colorful root vegetables contrasted with a crisp green salad and a creamy white side. It was a relaxed Wednesday evening dinner, balanced by her creative work and the promise of a quiet, restful night.
