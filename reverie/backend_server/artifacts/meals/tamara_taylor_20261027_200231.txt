Tamara Taylor prepared a Warm Oatmeal Breakfast with Fresh Strawberries.

She used rolled oats, milk, a pinch of salt, cinnamon, and a little vanilla, then cooked the oats with the milk until the grains became soft. She sliced fresh strawberries, letting their bright red color peek over the creamy oatmeal. A squeeze of lemon added a pleasant tang.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of oats, cinnamon, milk, and ripe strawberries. She arranged the oatmeal in a small ceramic bowl, garnishing it with the colorful fruit and a cinnamon stick. The presentation was simple, inviting, and balanced—oatmeal and strawberries together.

It was a quiet Wednesday morning, the day before her creative-writing session, and the meal reflected the calm, patient care she brings to her work.
