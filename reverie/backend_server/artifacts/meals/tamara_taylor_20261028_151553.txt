Tamara prepared Sunrise Vegetable Soup, a warm, comforting bowl of vegetables designed to nourish her body and help her begin the creative day.

She washed fresh carrots, celery, zucchini, and green onions, then chopped them into small, neat pieces. In a large pot, she sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until the carrots softened, then added vegetable broth, garlic, thyme, and a splash of lemon juice. The pot was covered and simmered until the vegetables cooked through, with the broth becoming rich and fragrant.

Aromatic notes of roasted carrots, celery, garlic, thyme, and lemon filled the kitchen as she stirred the pot. Tamara presented the soup in a clear glass bowl, garnishing it with bright green parsley and a few whole rosemary sprigs. The colors contrasted beautifully against the golden broth.

The meal was prepared on a quiet Wednesday afternoon while she rested, and she intended to eat it with a cup of tea. The gentle aroma, warm broth, and colorful presentation made the simple lunch feel special and inviting.
