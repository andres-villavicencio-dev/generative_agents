Tamara Taylor prepared a Hearty Berry and Oatmeal Breakfast Bowl, a comforting morning meal designed to encourage creativity and calm. She combined rolled oats, fresh berries, chia seeds, a splash of almond milk, and a pinch of cinnamon. For extra color, she folded in a small piece of honeycomb and topped it with a spoonful of yogurt.

She began by heating the oats and simmering them until the milk was gently absorbed, then stirred in the fresh ingredients while the breakfast warmed. The kitchen filled with the warm, inviting aroma of oats, honey, berries, and cinnamon. Tamara arranged the colorful bowl in the center of the table, adding a small plate of fruit and a cup of tea. The breakfast was presented as a gentle, nourishing start to her Monday morning, balanced with the energy needed for her children’s book series.
