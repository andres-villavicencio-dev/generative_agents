Tamara prepared Golden Sage Breakfast Bowl, a nutritious, colorful morning meal designed to inspire young readers. The dish combined soft scrambled eggs, oatmeal, whole-wheat toast, and a fresh fruit-topped yogurt parfait, all arranged in a warm ceramic bowl.

She prepared the eggs gently over a stove, cooking them just until the whites were set and the yolks remained rich and creamy. The oatmeal was simmered with vanilla and cinnamon until the grains were tender, while the toast was toasted to a golden brown. Toppings included sliced strawberries, blueberries, shredded bananas, and a light drizzle of honey and maple syrup.

The meal filled the kitchen with the warm aroma of toasted bread, sweet fruit, cinnamon, and the comforting scent of freshly cooked eggs. Presenting the bowl on a bright wooden table, Tamara arranged the colorful ingredients in a cheerful, balanced way, echoing the playful designs of her children’s books.

On Monday, February 13, the occasion was a quiet morning after a long week—Tamara’s usual breakfast before heading to work and her home with Carmen Ortiz. It was a simple, nourishing routine meant to nourish both her body and her imagination.
