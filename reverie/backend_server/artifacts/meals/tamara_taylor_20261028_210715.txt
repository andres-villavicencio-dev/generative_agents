Tamara Taylor prepared Savory Chicken and Herb Sandwiches with Fresh Pastries for her café breakfast.

The sandwiches used whole-grain bread, grilled chicken breast, crisp lettuce, sliced tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, mustard, mayonnaise, and a sprinkle of fresh parsley. She grilled the chicken until golden and seasoned it with garlic, oregano, and lemon juice. Each sandwich was carefully sliced and arranged on warm toast, then accompanied by a dozen soft pastries, including croissants, cinnamon rolls, and a buttery bagel.

Warm rosemary and thyme filled the kitchen, while the bread smelled richly toasted and the pastries had a buttery, slightly cinnamon aroma. She plated the sandwiches in neat rows with the pastries beside them, giving the café breakfast a cheerful, inviting appearance.

The meal was prepared for a busy Monday morning at the café, where Tamara wanted a quick, nourishing start before beginning her work on her new children’s book series.
