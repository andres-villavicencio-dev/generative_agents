Tamara prepared a hearty Lemony Chicken Stir-Fry with Quinoa and Vegetables for a simple, nourishing lunch before cooking dinner. She used breast chicken thighs, broccoli, bell peppers, carrots, onions, and leafy greens, along with a cup of quinoa and a little olive oil.

For the stir-fry, she sliced the chicken into bite-sized pieces, sautéed them until golden, and cooked the vegetables until crisp-tender. She tossed the quinoa with a splash of water and a pinch of salt before stirring it with the cooked proteins and vegetables. The dish was finished with a squeeze of lemon juice and chopped cilantro, giving it a bright, fragrant aroma.

She plated the colorful stir-fry in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with parsley and a drizzle of lemon-vinegar glaze. It was presented simply and invitingly, with the vibrant vegetables and fluffy quinoa contrasting against the golden chicken. It was a balanced, energizing meal for her busy Wednesday, before she continued her creative work and civic projects.
