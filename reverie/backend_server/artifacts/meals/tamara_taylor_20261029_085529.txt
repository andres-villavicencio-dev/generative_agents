Tamara prepared Creamy Tomato Basil Soup for a relaxed Thursday morning breakfast. She simmered a can of crushed tomatoes with a handful of fresh basil leaves, onion, garlic, carrots, and celery until the soup was rich and creamy, then added a little cream for warmth. The dish had a bright, aromatic scent of basil, garlic, and warm tomatoes.

She presented the soup in a shallow white bowl, garnishing it with a few extra basil leaves and a thin slice of lemon. The colorful, velvety soup looked inviting and balanced, with soft orange carrots and bright green herbs adding a cheerful contrast.

The meal was part of her peaceful Thursday routine, complementing her plans to attend the Valentine’s Day Party and continue her creative and civic work at Hobbs Cafe.
