On Thursday, February 16, Tamara prepared a simple yet comforting Lemon Garlic Chicken Salad, a healthy lunch that balanced her creative and civic work.

Key ingredients: chicken breast, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, lemon, garlic, olive oil, and a light dressing of lemon juice, vinegar, and herbs.

Preparation method: She seasoned the chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic, then marinated it briefly. While it cooked, she chopped the vegetables and whisked the dressing. She arranged the ingredients in a colorful salad bowl, topped the chicken with a generous lemon drizzle, and arranged it neatly for presentation.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of roasted garlic, lemon zest, and savory chicken, followed by the fresh burst of cucumber and tomatoes. Her presentation was bright and cheerful, with the chicken sitting atop the vegetables and a small lemon wedge added for visual appeal.

The meal was Valentine’s Day-themed, reflecting Tamara’s kind, patient, and imaginative nature. It was a relaxed, nourishing lunch that she prepared to balance her day and prepare for the upcoming Valentine’s Day Party.
