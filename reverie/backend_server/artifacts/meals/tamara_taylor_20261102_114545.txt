Tamara prepared a Warm Roast Chicken and Golden Roast Potatoes with Herbs and Lemon Dressing for breakfast, followed by a simple Lemon-Orange Toast with Fresh Fruit for lunch.

For breakfast, she sliced the chicken breast and roasted it with garlic, rosemary, and thyme. She sliced the potatoes, tossed them with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and placed them in a baking pan. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and golden potatoes. She served the roasted chicken, potatoes, and lemon-herb dressing alongside the breakfast bread.

For lunch, she toasted whole-grain bread and served it with sliced fruit and the leftover dressing. The meal was simple yet nourishing, and the bright lemon and orange added cheerful colors that matched the relaxed Valentine’s Day mood. It was a quiet, comforting lunch after her morning routine, making the busy Saturday feel purposeful and full.
