Tamara prepared Simple Garden Vegetable Scramble, a colorful, wholesome breakfast that became the basis for her simple lunch. The dish featured egg, diced tomatoes, spinach, cucumber, shredded carrots, and a touch of feta cheese, all cooked with a little oil and a pinch of salt. She stirred the eggs gently, folded in the vegetables, and served it over toast.

The kitchen filled with the warm, earthy aroma of roasted eggs, fresh tomatoes, and spinach. She arranged the eggs in a small round dish and placed a small bowl of yogurt, fruit, and a few whole berries beside it, creating a neat, inviting presentation.

It was a quiet, nourishing lunch on Saturday, February 18, during a particularly full, purposeful, and increasingly joyful week. Tamara’s careful preparation reflected her patient, kind nature and the balance she sought to maintain while balancing work, reading, and planning her children’s books.
