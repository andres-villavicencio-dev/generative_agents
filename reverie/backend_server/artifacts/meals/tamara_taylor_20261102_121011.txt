Tamara prepared Chicken Parmesan with Garlic Butter for her Saturday lunch. She sliced boneless chicken breast into bite-sized pieces, marinated them in olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, and dried oregano, then pan-seared them until the skin turned golden brown. She topped the chicken with sliced red bell peppers, onions, marinara sauce, mozzarella cheese, and fresh herbs before broiling it until the cheese melted and bubbled.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of caramelized garlic, roasted peppers, and cheesy tomato sauce. Tamara arranged the colorful portions on a white dinner plate with a small side of roasted asparagus and a lemon wedge. The presentation was bright, cheerful, and inviting, making her lunch feel like part of a larger celebration.

This simple, comforting meal was a peaceful lunch on a busy Saturday, balancing her schedule while she continued her work at Hobbs Café, read, and planned her children’s books. It also left her feeling refreshed, ready to enjoy the Valentine’s Day Party on February 14.
