Tamara prepared Savory Herb-Crusted Chicken with Roasted Potatoes and a Lemon-Butter Glaze. She began by slicing bone-in chicken thighs, seasoning them with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and black pepper, then pan-searing them to a golden brown. Afterward, she roasted potatoes in the same pan until the skin was crisp, then finished them with butter, flour, and a splash of lemon juice. She let the glaze cool slightly, drizzled it over the chicken, and topped everything with a crisp layer of parsley and a generous pinch of flaky sea salt.

The kitchen filled with the warm scent of roasted garlic, herbs, and butter. The meal was presented on a simple white platter, with the golden chicken and shiny potatoes arranged around a bowl of warm, steaming grains and a small side of fresh fruit. Tamara ate it slowly, savoring each bite as she reflected on her new children’s book series, her patient hands moving deliberately between fork and knife. The relaxed occasion was her quiet morning at home, combining nourishment with calm companionship with Carmen Ortiz.
