Tamara prepared Egg Poached with Herb Butter and Sautéed Vegetables for an early Monday dinner. The meal included two large eggs, fresh spinach, onions, cherry tomatoes, and a small piece of whole-grain bread. She also purchased a carton of milk and eggs for the fridge, but the meal featured the eggs and vegetables as the main components.

To cook the eggs, she cracked them into a lightly oiled pan and poached them over medium heat, letting the whites turn pale yellow while the yolks remained soft and runny. The vegetables were sautéed in butter until crisp, with the spinach adding a bright green color and the tomatoes adding a subtle sweetness. A generous drizzle of herb-infused butter completed the dish.

The aroma of warm garlic, herbs, and caramelized onions filled the kitchen, while the golden-brown vegetables and glossy poached eggs created a visually appealing presentation. Tamara served the meal with bread and milk and accompanied it with a warm glass of water. It was a simple, nourishing breakfast-style dinner, perfect for a busy Monday morning.
