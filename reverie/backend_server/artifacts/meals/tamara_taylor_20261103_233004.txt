Tamara prepared Cilantro-Lime Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Rice, a hearty family dinner for Monday night. The dish features thick chicken thighs, yellow corn, sweet potatoes, red bell peppers, onions, garlic, lime, cumin, oregano, and rice, all seasoned with fresh herbs and a little olive oil.

Tamara began by washing and chopping the vegetables, then sautéed onions, garlic, and peppers until fragrant. She grilled the chicken until the skin was golden and the meat was cooked through, while the corn, potatoes, and peppers roasted together until tender. The rice was cooked alongside, and the finished meal was tossed with fresh lime juice and a pinch of salt.

The aroma was warm and earthy—rich with garlic, onion, cumin, and the fresh scent of lime and herbs. The meal was presented on a white plate with the vegetables arranged around the chicken and rice placed neatly beside it. The bright colors and inviting arrangement made it perfect for sharing during a relaxed Friday night gathering with Carmen Ortiz and friends.
