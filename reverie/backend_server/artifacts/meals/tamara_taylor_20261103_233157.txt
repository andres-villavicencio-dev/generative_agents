Tamara prepared Roasted Vegetable & Herb Stuffed Chicken, a hearty dinner for The Willows Market. She mixed seasoned chicken with finely chopped carrots, baby potatoes, zucchini, onions, and fresh herbs, then stuffed the chicken with the mixture and placed it in an oven-proof dish. She arranged it on a platter alongside a side of steamed broccoli, brown rice, and a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing.

To prepare it, Tamara roasted the vegetables until they were tender, then basted the chicken with a mixture of garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, and sage. She basted the chicken until the skin was golden and the stuffing was bubbling. The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and caramelized potatoes.

The meal was served at The Willows Market, where Tamara and Carmen bought groceries and prepared dinner together. It was a simple, nourishing meal designed to fill the fridge and energize Tamara for a productive day of writing and creative work.
