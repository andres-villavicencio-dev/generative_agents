Tamara prepared a Garden-Patch Herb Breakfast Bowl, a gentle, colorful meal designed to encourage children’s imaginations while providing a wholesome start to her busy day.

The bowl included fluffy oat cereal, warm whole-grain toast, sliced bananas, walnuts, blueberries, and a drizzle of honey. Fresh herbs—mint, chives, and parsley—were sprinkled over the top, along with a few crisp apple slices. She prepared the breakfast in a non-stick skillet, sautéing the herbs in olive oil until they released their aroma, then mixing everything together in a large bowl.

The warm, earthy scent of toasted nuts and herbs mingled with the sweetness of the fruit, creating a comforting and inviting aroma. The presentation was bright and cheerful: the oatmeal and toast were arranged around a central bowl of fruit, topped with colorful herbs and a small decorative lemon slice.

It was a simple yet meaningful breakfast for Tamara’s Tuesday morning, giving her a calm and nourishing start to her demanding schedule.
