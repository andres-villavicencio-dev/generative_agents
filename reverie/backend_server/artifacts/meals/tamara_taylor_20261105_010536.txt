Tamara prepared Smoky Herb Roast Chicken, a comforting main dish for her Tuesday evening family dinner. She started with a whole chicken, seasoning it generously with rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon, olive oil, salt, and black pepper. After drying the skin, she roasted it in a hot oven until the chicken was golden, the skin crisp, and the meat tender. She finished it with a simple pan sauce, adding carrots and potatoes for a savory, earthy flavor.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, and caramelizing poultry. Tamara presented the chicken on a rustic wooden platter, garnished with fresh parsley and a bright lemon wedge, with the vegetables arranged beside it. The meal was served for an intimate family gathering on Tuesday, February 14, when the café was closed for the day, allowing Tamara to celebrate her professional momentum while enjoying her homemade dinner with Sam, Carmen, and Latoya.
