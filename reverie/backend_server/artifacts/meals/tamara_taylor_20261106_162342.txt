On Monday, February 13, Tamara prepared a Chai-Infused Lemon-Apple Breakfast Bowl, a warm, nurturing breakfast for herself and her housemate, Carmen Ortiz. The dish featured fresh apples, lemon slices, cinnamon, honey, warm chai tea, and a few crispy whole-grain crackers for a satisfying snack.

Tamara arranged the ingredients in a shallow ceramic bowl, arranging the bright apples and lemon slices on top and placing the golden-hued chai tea and cinnamon around them. She used a small spoon to stir the apples gently, releasing their fragrance, then poured the warm tea over the fruit. The aroma was inviting and cozy—a sweet, citrusy scent of cinnamon, honey, and steaming tea.

The presentation was simple and cheerful: the bowls were arranged with a colorful napkin, and the crackers were placed neatly beside the bowl. It was prepared as a quiet morning breakfast for a special Saturday family breakfast, but Tamara added an extra side of fruit to keep it balanced.

She then tidied the kitchen, washed the fruit, and stored the remaining ingredients in labeled containers. This was a peaceful, thoughtful meal made for comfort and creativity, reflecting Tamara’s patient, kind nature while she continued her work as a children’s book author.
