On Monday, February 13, Tamara prepared Golden Morning Coffee Muffins for her breakfast at Hobbs Cafe. The batch featured whole-grain oat flour, honey, cinnamon, brown sugar, cocoa powder, shredded coconut, toasted walnuts, and a splash of almond milk. She mixed the dry ingredients, folded in the eggs, then folded the batter into the oven.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of toasted nuts, cinnamon, and steaming coffee. Tamara shaped the muffins with gentle hands, baking them until they were golden and slightly crisp. She arranged them on a white plate with small bowls of cinnamon sugar and fresh berries nearby, creating a cheerful, inviting presentation.

The occasion was a quiet morning at Hobbs Cafe, where Tamara was preparing a batch of breakfast coffee drinks for her customers. She worked patiently to ensure each muffin was cooked evenly and the cafe smelled welcoming.
