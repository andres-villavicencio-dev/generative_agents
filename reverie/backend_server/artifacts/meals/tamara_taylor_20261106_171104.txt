Meal: “Morning Bloom” Breakfast Bowl

Tamara prepared a colorful, nutritious breakfast bowl for herself at Hobbs Cafe, balancing the next batch of children’s breakfast drinks with a satisfying morning meal. The dish was inspired by her children’s books, featuring a bright blueberry oat pudding, crispy fried egg, honey-roasted carrots, and a lemon-topped avocado toast.

She cooked the oat pudding in a pan with warm milk, oats, and a splash of vanilla until it set, then served it over the crisp egg. The carrots were lightly caramelized with honey and sprinkled with cinnamon, while the avocado added a creamy green finish. Fresh fruit and a drizzle of honey were added for visual appeal.

The aroma of warm oats, caramelized carrots, honey, and lemon filled the small kitchen. Tamara arranged the bowl on a white plate, carefully arranging the fruit and toppings to create a cheerful, layered presentation. The occasion was a quiet moment at Hobbs Cafe, where she enjoyed her breakfast before beginning work on her new children’s book series.
