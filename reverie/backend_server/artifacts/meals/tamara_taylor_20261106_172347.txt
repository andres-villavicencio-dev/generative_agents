Tamara prepared Cinnamon-Roasted Vegetable Soup with Crispy Chicken and Cornbread, the signature dish for Hobbs Cafe’s early breakfast. The soup was made by sautéing carrots, celery, onions, and potatoes in butter until golden, then adding chicken, vegetable broth, and a little cinnamon. The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, and lemon zest, while the vegetables were roasted until tender. Cornbread was baked to a crisp golden brown, providing a sturdy base for the warm, comforting soup.

The kitchen filled with a rich aroma of toasted nuts, caramelized onions, cinnamon, and savory chicken. A small bowl of warm broth was served alongside the soup and a generous portion of golden cornbread. Tamara arranged the dishes attractively in large ceramic serving bowls, garnishing the chicken with fresh parsley.

This was the beginning of a busy Monday morning shift at Hobbs Cafe, where Tamara filled bowls and prepared fresh breakfasts for customers. The meal was simple but comforting, designed to make even a rushed morning feel welcoming.
