Tamara prepared Hobbs Cafe’s Classic Breakfast Coffee Batch, a comforting batch of coffee drinks with fresh fruit and warm whole-grain toast.

The batch included three cups of black coffee, a glass of orange juice, and two slices of toasted sourdough. She added fresh strawberries, blueberries, and a drizzle of honey to each cup, along with a warm cinnamon-sugar topping. Tamara toasted the bread in a skillet until it was golden and crisp, then arranged the coffee cups around a small plate with the fruit and toast.

The aroma filled the kitchen: rich roasted coffee, sweet honey, toasted grain, and a hint of cinnamon. The presentation was colorful and inviting—the warm amber coffee contrasted with the bright red and purple berries, while the golden toast added a rustic, inviting glow.

The meal was prepared for Tamara’s Tuesday workday, when she planned to spend time with Carmen Ortiz at Café 4. It was a quiet, purposeful moment of creativity and connection, balancing her adult comic-book projects with a relaxed, familiar breakfast.
