Tamara prepared a colorful breakfast bowl for Carmen Ortiz, the morning treat that would warm her as she headed to Café 4. The dish was a Chia-Pumpkin Seed Breakfast Bowl. Key ingredients included cooked quinoa, shredded pumpkin, toasted walnuts, blueberries, chia seeds, a drizzle of maple syrup, and a spoonful of Greek yogurt.

Tamara cooked the pumpkin until it was soft but still bright orange, then chopped it into small pieces and stirred it into the warm quinoa. She mixed in the chia seeds for texture and added the fruit for a burst of color. The yogurt was chilled briefly so it remained creamy, while the walnuts were toasted to give the bowl a crunchy finish. A pinch of cinnamon was sprinkled over the top, and a few fresh mint leaves were tucked into the side for visual appeal.

The aroma filled the kitchen with toasted nuts, warm pumpkin, berries, and the gentle sweetness of the cinnamon and maple syrup. In a small, white bowl, the ingredients were arranged in an overlapping, colorful circle, each layer catching the morning light. Tamara served the bowl at 6:30 AM, the first ingredient of the day and a quiet celebration of creativity, patience, and the joy of sharing a warm breakfast with Carmen.
