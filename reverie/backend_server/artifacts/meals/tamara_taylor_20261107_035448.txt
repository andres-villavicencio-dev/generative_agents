Tamara prepared Roasted Root Vegetable Soup with Lemon-Herb Crust, a warm, nourishing side designed to accompany her lunch. The soup was made by simmering carrots, sweet potatoes, parsnips, and onions with broth until the vegetables became tender and the broth turned rich. Afterward, she roasted the finished sides until their edges turned crisp, then sliced them into colorful, bite-sized chunks.

She seasoned the vegetable chunks with salt, pepper, and a pinch of lemon, then spread the warm, golden crust over them. The aroma of caramelized onions, roasted carrots, and lemon filled the kitchen, creating a cheerful, inviting scent.

The presentation was simple and cheerful: the vibrant, unevenly sliced vegetables were arranged in a warm bowl, with the crisp crust adding a final pop of color. It was a comforting, family-style side prepared for her Tuesday lunch—a quiet moment of nourishment before she met Carmen at Café 4.
