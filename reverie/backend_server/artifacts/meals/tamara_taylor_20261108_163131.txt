Tamara Taylor prepared Root Vegetable Soup with Lemon and Thyme, a warm, comforting bowl of home-style soup for dinner on a Monday evening.

The soup featured carrots, onions, celery, potatoes, and a small amount of root vegetable scraps. A splash of vegetable broth, a pinch of salt, and fresh thyme were added, with the lemon juice finishing the dish. The vegetables softened until the broth was rich and fragrant, while the potatoes remained plump and tender.

Tamara simmered everything gently over medium heat, letting the aromatics release their flavors and the broth reduce into a glossy, savory broth. The soup was served in a simple white bowl, with lemon slices and chopped parsley added before serving. A small wedge of lemon and a sprig of thyme were placed beside the bowl.

The meal was a quiet evening reward: Tamara sat down at the kitchen table with Carmen Ortiz, sharing the fragrant soup and enjoying the peaceful moment before bed. It was neither a formal occasion nor a special celebration, but a simple, nurturing dinner that reflected Tamara’s patient, kind nature.
