Tamara prepared a warm, comforting Chicken and Vegetable Risotto, a simple yet nutritious main dish for her Tuesday evening. She slowly sautéed onions and garlic in olive oil, then added chicken breasts, rice, and a small handful of vegetables. After rinsing the rice and adding a little water, she cooked it gently over low heat until the grains were creamy and tender. A little cream and Parmesan cheese were stirred in toward the end, creating a rich, aromatic broth.

The kitchen filled with the golden scent of toasted rice and savory herbs. Tamara plated the risotto in a shallow bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a few sliced cherry tomatoes. The meal was simple, thoughtfully prepared, and especially meaningful because it was part of her preparation for the 10:30 AM job interview at the community-board meeting, along with her plans to meet Carmen and Francisco about clinic-shift opportunities.
