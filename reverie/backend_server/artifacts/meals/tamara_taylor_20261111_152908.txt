Tamara prepared Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for a simple family dinner. The meal featured tender chicken thighs seasoned with garlic, lemon, rosemary, thyme, and oregano, along with roasted carrots, baby potatoes, and green beans. Her housemate, Carmen Ortiz, helped season the chicken, while Tamara arranged the vegetables neatly on a serving platter.

The chicken was seasoned generously, then grilled until golden and juicy. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, salt, pepper, and lemon zest before being roasted until crisp and caramelized. As they finished, the kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of herbs, garlic, and toasted vegetables.

Tamara presented the meal in a shallow white bowl with a colorful garnish of parsley and lemon slices. A small plate of crackers and a glass of sparkling water accompanied the dinner. It was a cozy Monday evening meant for her housemates, a quiet moment of family bonding, and the beginning of the next chapter in her children’s book series.
