Tamara Taylor prepared a simple breakfast for herself and Carmen Ortiz: pan-fried egg with bread and coffee. She used two slices of sourdough, a soft-boiled egg, butter, salt, pepper, and a little milk. She lightly buttered the pan, placed the bread and egg in it, and cooked them gently over low heat until the egg was just set and the bread was golden. She finished the meal with a cup of hot coffee.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of toasted bread, savory egg, and warm coffee. Tamara arranged the pan on the table in front of her, placing the bread beside the egg and coffee cup, with a small glass of water for Carmen. The meal felt cozy, purposeful, and intimate—the simple breakfast she prepared for herself between the early morning routine and her workday, celebrating the beginning of her Tuesday.
