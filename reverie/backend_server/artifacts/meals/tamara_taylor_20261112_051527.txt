Tamara prepared a Morning Garden Breakfast for her customers at the café. The meal combined fresh scrambled eggs with cheddar, whole-grain toast, smashed avocado, crispy bacon, and a handful of fresh herbs. She added a splash of lemon juice and a small bowl of crisp salad for the diners.

The dish was prepared in two parts. Tamara cooked the eggs in a hot skillet until the yolks were soft and the whites were set, then arranged the bacon and avocado on a white plate. Toast and fresh herbs were served with the eggs, while the salad was kept crisp and chilled. The ingredients were arranged in a warm, rustic bowl, creating a colorful, inviting presentation.

Amidst the busy café morning, Tamara’s kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of toasted bread, savory bacon, and fragrant herbs. The bright, cheerful colors of the presentation made the simple breakfast feel especially welcoming. The meal was prepared for a busy weekday shift, with customers arriving to enjoy a warm, nourishing breakfast at the café.
