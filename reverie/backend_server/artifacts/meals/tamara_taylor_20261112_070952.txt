Tamara prepared Roasted Root Vegetable Soup with Lemon-Lime Crust, a comforting, colorful meal designed to inspire young readers through her children’s book series. The dish featured carrots, parsnips, sweet potatoes, zucchini, and onions, seasoned with garlic, rosemary, cumin, and a splash of lemon-lime zest.

Tamara began by washing and chopping the vegetables, then sautéed them in a pot until softened. She added vegetable broth, onions, and the remaining spices, letting the pot simmer until the carrots and root vegetables turned tender. Afterward, she transferred the soup to a baking dish and topped it with the citrusy crust. The oven was preheated to 375°F (190°C) for about 45 minutes. As it baked, the crust turned golden brown and bubbly, while the vegetables released a rich, savory aroma with hints of garlic, herbs, and lemon.

The finished soup was served in a clear glass bowl, garnished with a sprig of rosemary and a lemon wedge. It was ready for Monday’s family dinner, a quiet evening where Tamara shared the meal with Carmen and created a relaxed opportunity to discuss her new children’s book series with her imagination.
