Tamara prepared Roasted Garlic Herb Chicken with Lemon-Basil Rice for a Sunday family dinner. She purchased chicken breasts, garlic, onions, bell peppers, carrots, chicken broth, rice, and fresh herbs. She washed and dried the chicken, then seasoned it with salt, pepper, and dried oregano. In a large skillet, she sautéed the onions and peppers until softened, then added the chicken and broth to braise until the meat was tender. Separately, she toasted the rice in butter and stirred in the remaining vegetables until the grains were fluffy and the chicken absorbed the flavorful broth.

She finished the dish with a squeeze of lemon and sprinkled chopped parsley over the top. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, warm broth, and sweet herbs. The colorful presentation featured golden chicken, vibrant vegetables, glossy rice, and a clean white plate. It was a cozy meal shared with her housemate Carmen and their children on a relaxing Sunday evening.
