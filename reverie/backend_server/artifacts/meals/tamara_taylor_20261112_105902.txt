Tamara Taylor prepared Bread & Egg Breakfast for Hobbs Cafe, a warm breakfast to serve with her Tuesday meeting with Amilcar Rosales.

The dish consisted of golden-brown sourdough bread, creamy scrambled eggs, a dollop of sour cream, fresh chives, and a small bowl of warm cinnamon apple sauce. She toasted the bread in a pan, cracked the eggs into the pan, and cooked them gently over medium heat until they were fluffy and golden. She added the sour cream and chives for a crisp finish, then sliced the bread and arranged it on a white plate with the eggs and sauce.

The kitchen smelled of toasted wheat, warm egg, and sweet cinnamon. The presentation was simple and inviting—the rustic bread, bright yellow eggs, creamy toppings, and colorful chives created a cheerful contrast. The occasion was a welcoming Tuesday café gathering, where Tamara was proud to offer a comforting, homemade breakfast to Amilcar and his friends.
