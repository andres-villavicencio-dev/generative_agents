Tamara prepared Amilcar’s Sunday Brunch Pastries, a warm breakfast treat for the café’s Tuesday dinner gathering. She used whole-wheat pastry dough, yogurt, fresh cream cheese, brown sugar, almond flour, cinnamon, and caramelized walnuts. After kneading the dough, she rolled it into small, evenly spaced rounds, dipped them in a light cinnamon-sugar glaze, and baked them until golden and crisp.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of caramel, toasted nuts, and vanilla. She arranged the pastries on a white ceramic platter, adding a small bowl of warm honey and a sprig of fresh basil. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the golden pastries arranged in a neat arc and a few fresh cookies nearby.

Tamara served the pastries to Amilcar for the café’s Tuesday dinner, making them the centerpiece of her cooking and sharing experience.
