Tamara prepared Lemon-Herb Chocolate-Pastry Cookies for her Tuesday dinner with Amilcar Rosales. The cookies were made from finely chopped dark chocolate, butter, sugar, flour, and lemon zest, with vanilla extract and a pinch of cinnamon adding warmth. After creaming the butter and sugar, she folded in the chocolate and zest, then rolled the dough into small rounds and baked them until golden.

The aroma was rich, warm, and fragrant—rich cocoa, butter, citrus, and a hint of cinnamon filled the kitchen. Tamara sliced the cookies and served them with lemon wedges and a small bowl of honey, arranging them neatly on a white plate. The occasion was a quiet family dinner: a thoughtful surprise for Amilcar, who had been a patient and kind listener during Tamara’s writing.

Tamara’s imaginative, patient approach made the dessert feel special and comforting, while her relaxed demeanor made the gathering feel welcoming and joyful.
