Tamara prepared Savory Breakfast with Roasted Berries and Fig Jam, a simple yet comforting early-morning meal for herself and Carmen. The key ingredients were eggs, spinach, whole-grain toast, sliced apples, walnuts, and a small bowl of honeyed fig jam. For the eggs, she cracked them into a nonstick pan, added a few leaves of spinach, and cracked the remaining eggs over them, cooking them gently until the whites were set. Toasted walnuts and sliced apples were scattered on top, and she drizzled a little warm honey and jam over the finished dish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted nuts, earthy greens, and sweet honey. She served the breakfast on a warm plate, arranging the eggs, toast, berries, and jam in a small family-style bowl. It was a quiet breakfast for a Monday morning, designed for calm concentration and nourishment while she worked on her children’s book series and comic book project.
