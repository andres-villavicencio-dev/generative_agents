Tamara prepared Morning Sunflower Breakfast, a heartwarming breakfast meant for her housemate Carmen. The meal consisted of fluffy whole-grain pancakes, a glass of warm honeyed apple juice, toasted almonds, and fresh berries.

She began by toasting the seeds and frying them until golden, then sautéed the apples with cinnamon and a splash of honey. For the pancakes, she used whole-wheat flour, eggs, milk, and a pinch of baking powder, flipping them gently until they were bubbly and golden. She topped the pancakes with the warm fruit, nuts, and fresh berries, arranging them neatly around a small plate.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of toasted seeds, caramelized apples, and warm milk. The presentation was simple and inviting, with the pancakes arranged in a cheerful circle and colorful fruit sprinkled around the edges. It was the perfect early-morning breakfast for breakfast, when the sun was just beginning to rise.
