Tamara Taylor prepared Morning Garden Breakfast, a warm, comforting meal of oatmeal topped with fresh fruit and a drizzle of honey. The dish featured rolled oats cooked with water, a handful of strawberries, a small banana, chopped blueberries, and cinnamon. She used a stainless-steel pot, adding the water and oats first, then stirring in the fruit before letting it simmer until the oats were tender. She finished the meal with a splash of milk and a pinch of cinnamon, then sliced the fruit for presentation.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of cinnamon, roasted oats, ripe fruit, and gently steaming milk. Tamara arranged the oatmeal in a small glass bowl, garnishing it with a colorful fruit wheel and a few cinnamon sticks for decoration. It was prepared for a quiet Monday morning breakfast, intended to inspire her creative work and give her a peaceful start to the day.
