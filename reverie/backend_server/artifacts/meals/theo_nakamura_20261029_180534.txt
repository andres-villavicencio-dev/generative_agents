On Friday, February 17, Theo prepared a simple, nutritious dinner of Japanese-style tofu with bok choy and ginger. The dish consisted of firm tofu marinated in soy sauce, mirin, and a little sesame oil, then pan-fried until the surface was lightly crisp. Thinly sliced bok choy, chopped green onions, and a drizzle of sesame oil completed the meal.

He prepared it in a small, lidded wok over medium heat, stirring the tofu and bok choy together until the aromatics softened and the tofu turned golden. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted ginger and the savory, slightly sweet scent of the soy-based marinade.

Theo presented the meal on a small white plate, garnishing it with a sprig of green onion and a few sesame seeds. This was an informal family-style dinner, shared with Carl Demissie and his friends for a relaxed Friday night.
