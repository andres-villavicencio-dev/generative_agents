Dish Name: Lemon Herb Crispy Vegetable with Garlic Parmesan

Theo prepared this simple Mediterranean-inspired side as a quick, comforting dinner for himself and Carl. The dish features broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, and baby potatoes tossed with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, and a pinch of chili flakes. He finished the vegetables with a generous layer of grated Parmesan before baking them in the oven until the skin was crisp and the interior was tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, thyme, and caramelized vegetables. Theo arranged the colorful vegetables on a white platter, garnished with fresh herbs and a small lemon wedge, and sprinkled the remaining Parmesan over the top.

He prepared it for a relaxed Friday evening dinner. The meal was simple, flavorful, and made just enough to satisfy his hunger without feeling too heavy.
