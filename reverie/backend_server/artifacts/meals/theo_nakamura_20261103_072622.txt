Theo prepared Sunday Roast Chicken with Herb-Glazed Potatoes and Lemon-Basil Sauce for a Saturday family dinner. The key ingredients were whole chicken, potatoes, carrots, onions, garlic, fresh herbs, olive oil, lemon juice, and a splash of broth. He roasted the chicken until the skin was golden and the meat was tender, then glazed the potatoes and vegetables with a rich, aromatic herb sauce.

Aromatic rosemary, thyme, garlic, and lemon filled the kitchen as he stirred the sauce and turned the chicken. The dish was presented in a shallow white bowl, surrounded by the caramelized potatoes and carrots, with a bright lemon wedge nearby. Theo checked the food’s color and texture before serving, then added salt and pepper to balance the flavors.

The occasion was a simple Saturday dinner for his family, reflecting his methodical approach to cooking and his desire to serve the community with care.
