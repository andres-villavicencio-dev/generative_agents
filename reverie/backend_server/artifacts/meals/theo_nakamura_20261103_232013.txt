Theo prepared Roasted Tomato and Herb Chicken for an early Monday dinner with Carl Demissie and his mother. The chicken was cut into bite-sized pieces and pan-seared until golden, then rested before being returned to the oven. It was roasted with roasted tomatoes, garlic, fresh parsley, and a blend of rosemary and thyme. A splash of lemon juice and a drizzle of olive oil added brightness, while a simple lemon-lime dressing served the dish with bright, herbaceous notes.

Theo arranged the chicken in a shallow baking dish, garnishing it with fresh herbs and serving the accompanying pan sauce beside it. The aroma of warm roasted chicken, garlic, herbs, and lemons filled the kitchen, with citrus and savory notes lingering through the open window. It was a quiet, home-cooked meal, not a formal celebration, but a thoughtful effort to prepare something comforting for a rainy Monday evening.
