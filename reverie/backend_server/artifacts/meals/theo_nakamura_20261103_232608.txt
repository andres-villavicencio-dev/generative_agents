Theo prepared a Sunny Garden Baked Potato for dinner, celebrating his first real job interview. He boiled a russet potato until tender, then sliced it and topped it with butter, shredded cheddar, fresh parsley, diced onions, and a sprinkle of black pepper. The potato was baked until golden and bubbly, with a few carrots and a splash of garlic butter adding warmth and aroma.

As he cooked it, Theo’s kitchen filled with the savory scent of caramelized onions and toasted butter. He arranged the potato on a white platter, garnishing it with a small sprig of parsley and a single red pepper. The simple, rustic presentation made the dish look inviting and perfect for a quiet Sunday dinner.

The occasion was a personal milestone—not just a meal, but a chance to reflect on his new beginning. Sharing it with Carl and the family offered him comfort and encouragement as he prepared for his first day at Johnson Park.
