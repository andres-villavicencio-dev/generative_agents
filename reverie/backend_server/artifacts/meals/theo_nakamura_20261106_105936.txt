Theo prepared a simple chicken and vegetable stir-fry for lunch on February 14. He used a whole chicken, bone-in breast, carrots, snap peas, corn, ginger, garlic, and a splash of soy sauce with a little sesame oil. The chicken was lightly seared, then sautéed with the vegetables until tender, followed by a quick glaze of soy sauce, sesame oil, and a pinch of sugar.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, ginger, savory chicken, and steaming vegetables. He arranged the finished dish on a shallow white platter and served it with a small bowl of brown rice. The presentation was neat and practical: the chicken rested in the center, with colorful vegetables arranged beside it, and the rice placed at the bottom.

The meal was a quiet work lunch before his evening routine. It reflected his methodical approach to organizing his life—simple, nourishing, and purposeful—before he finished preparing for dinner with Tom on Monday and continued his job search.
