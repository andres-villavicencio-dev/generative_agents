Theo prepared Tomato, Herb, and Sage Panini Sandwiches alongside a small batch of warm lemon-cinnamon croissants for the cafe.

The sandwiches used toasted ciabatta, thinly sliced tomato, fresh basil, chives, and toasted sage, with a light mayonnaise-based dressing. Theo used a flat-top panini press, pressing the fillings between two slices until the bread was golden and the filling warmed through. For the croissants, he brushed the dough with melted butter and cinnamon sugar, then scored them and baked them in a 350°F (175°C) oven until crisp on the outside and fluffy on the inside.

The aroma in the kitchen was warm and inviting: toasted bread, savory herbs, fresh basil, and a subtle warmth from the cinnamon. He arranged the sandwiches on a clean white plate, placing two in the center and two on each side, with the croissants arranged neatly beside them. A small glass of water was placed next to each sandwich.

The occasion was Theo’s first professional meal before heading to the cafe for work. He wanted the food to look inviting and feel prepared with care, giving him energy to follow up with Francisco and Yuriko about the Town Hall and records-office positions.
