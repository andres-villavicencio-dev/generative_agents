Theo prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry for dinner, a simple yet warming meal that reflected his methodical approach to cooking. He marinated chicken breasts with ginger, garlic, soy sauce, and a splash of sesame oil, then sliced carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, and snap peas. After searing the chicken until golden, he tossed the vegetables in a wok over medium-high heat, adding a little vegetable oil and cooking until the vegetables became crisp-tender. He finished the dish with a handful of fresh cilantro and served it in a shallow white bowl with the chicken resting on top.

The aroma of roasted chicken, garlic, and fresh herbs filled the kitchen, while the bright colors of the vegetables added a cheerful visual appeal. Theo’s neat, organized preparation mirrored his earnest desire to work methodically and serve the community. The meal was a quiet, satisfying dinner shared with Carl and the other housemates before bedtime.
