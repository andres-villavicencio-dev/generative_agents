Theo prepared a Thai Green Curry for dinner on Monday, February 13. The dish used aromatic lemongrass, galangal, shallots, garlic, chili, coconut milk, coconut yogurt, palm sugar, and a handful of fresh Thai chilies. He sautéed the aromatics and vegetables in a little oil, then added the curry paste and coconut milk, simmering it for about 15 minutes until the chilies softened and the sauce thickened. He finished it with a spoonful of fresh basil and a sprinkle of chili flakes.

The curry was presented in a shallow white bowl, garnished with chopped basil, sliced chilies, and a few sprigs of fresh herbs. Theo arranged the ingredients so the colorful green curry stood out against the pale bowl, making the dish look inviting for sharing.

It was a quiet, informal dinner while he cooked in Johnson Park, checking the community bulletin board for town-service posts. The warm aroma of lemongrass, garlic, and coconut filled the kitchen, and he hoped the meal would help him relax before checking for a first real job.
