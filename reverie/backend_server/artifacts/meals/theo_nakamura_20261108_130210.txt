Theo prepared Bran Baked Oatmeal for breakfast. The dish used unbleached old-fashioned oats, a cinnamon-apple slice, a tablespoon of honey, a pinch of salt, and a small knob of butter. He toasted the oats in a nonstick pan until lightly golden, then added the warm honey and cinnamon. He folded in the butter and sliced apple, stirring gently to create a soft, warm oatmeal.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the comforting scent of toasted oats, cinnamon, and caramelized apples. Theo plated the oatmeal in a simple white bowl and topped it with a dusting of cinnamon. He served a small bowl of warm milk alongside it, choosing the classic, no-fuss breakfast suitable for a first-job day. The meal was prepared for an ordinary morning: Theo sharing a simple breakfast with Carl Demissie, ready to begin his day as a town employee.
