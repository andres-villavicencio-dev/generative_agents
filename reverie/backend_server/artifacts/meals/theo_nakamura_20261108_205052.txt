Theo prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Brown Rice, a practical lunch for a busy Tuesday. He sliced chicken breasts, sautéed them with carrots, onions, and bell peppers, then added a small amount of soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger. The aromas of the golden-sweet vegetables and savory chicken filled the kitchen, though he kept the cooking relatively simple to avoid overdoing it.

He used a stainless-steel wok over medium heat, tossing the ingredients until the chicken turned opaque and the vegetables were crisp. Once cooked, he served the stir-fry with fluffy brown rice, garnishing it with a sprinkle of sesame seeds and a few fresh mint leaves. The meal was plated in a simple white bowl, with the colorful vegetables contrasting against the golden rice.

Theo ate it while helping Amilcar with the town-service application. It was a modest, unpretentious lunch meant to provide energy and a sense of accomplishment after a long day.
