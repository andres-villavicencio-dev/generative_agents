The meal was a quick breakfast of Scrambled Eggs with Toast and Orange-Apple Jam. Theo scrambled two eggs with a little butter, chive oil, and salt, then toasted a slice of whole-grain bread and sliced a tart apple. He topped the toast with a thin layer of the warm jam and served the eggs in a small white bowl beside it.

The kitchen was filled with the aroma of butter, roasted nuts, fresh herbs, and the sweet scent of fruit. It was a simple, low-cost breakfast for Theo’s Tuesday morning—the same routine he follows when he checks the community bulletin board or reviews the work plans at Johnson Park. The meal reflected his practical, methodical approach: practical, satisfying, and ready to start helping people.
