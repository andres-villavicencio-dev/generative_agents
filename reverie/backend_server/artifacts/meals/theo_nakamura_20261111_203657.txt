At 7:30 a.m., Theo prepared a simple oatmeal breakfast. He used rolled oats, a splash of almond milk, a spoonful of cinnamon, and a few slices of blueberries. He toasted a small bagel, buttered it, and arranged the oatmeal and berries on it.

The kitchen smelled warm and comforting, with the aroma of toasted oats, cinnamon, and fruit filling the small room. He poured a glass of water and placed the breakfast on the table beside a notebook.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday morning at Hobbs Cafe, a quiet first day of work as a public administrator. He was not rushing; he wanted the meal to feel like a small reward for the methodical preparation he had practiced.
