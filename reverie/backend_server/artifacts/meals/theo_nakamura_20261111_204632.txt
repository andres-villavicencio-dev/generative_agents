Theo prepared Simple Herb Roast Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for a quiet Tuesday dinner. He washed a whole chicken and trimmed its skin, then seasoned it with garlic, rosemary, lemon, salt, and black pepper before placing it on a roasting rack. He arranged carrots, potatoes, onions, and bell peppers around it in a single pan, adding a splash of olive oil, a pinch of paprika, and a small amount of vegetable broth. The oven was set to 400°F for one hour and twenty minutes.

As the chicken cooked, Theo carefully measured the quantities of each ingredient, noting the exact weight of the chicken and the amount of oil used. He turned the oven off before removing the tray, leaving the vegetables to finish cooking. As soon as the chicken was done, he transferred it to a warm platter, garnished it with the roasted vegetables, and served it with a small portion of pan juices.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted skin, garlic, and herbs. He plated it simply—no fancy cutlery or elaborate presentation—only enough room to see the golden chicken and bright vegetables. The meal was prepared not for a special occasion, but for a simple, nourishing supper after a long day, reflecting his methodical nature and desire to serve the town with care.
