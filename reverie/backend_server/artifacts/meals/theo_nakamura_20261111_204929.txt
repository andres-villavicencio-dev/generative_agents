On Tuesday, February 14, Theo Nakamura prepared a simple Hearty Chicken and Broccoli Soup for dinner. The main ingredient was chicken thighs simmered with carrots, celery, onion, and garlic in a pot of vegetable broth. He added finely chopped broccoli and leafy greens near the end to retain their crisp texture, seasoning the soup with salt, pepper, and a touch of lemon juice.

The preparation method was straightforward and methodical: he washed the vegetables, sliced the chicken, and added everything to a clean pot, then covered it and let it simmer until the broth was rich and the chicken was tender. As it cooked, the aroma became warm and comforting—a mixture of garlic, carrots, and savory broth.

The soup was presented in a clear glass bowl, with the translucent broth filling most of the vessel and the golden chicken and bright green broccoli visible at the surface. A small spoonful of toasted sesame seeds served as a garnish. It was a humble, home-style meal intended to nourish Theo and provide a comforting end to a productive Tuesday, reflecting his earnest desire to serve his community through simple, practical cooking.
