On Tuesday, February 14, Theo prepared a simple Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice to serve with friends and colleagues. The dish featured bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, broccoli, green onions, and a small portion of brown rice, all coated in a savory soy-ginger glaze. Theo sliced the chicken, stir-fried the vegetables until crisp-tender, then combined everything in a pan with the rice and rice sauce, adding a splash of water and a pinch of salt to finish the cooking.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken, ginger, garlic, and caramelized vegetables. Theo carefully checked the heat on the stove, adding a little extra water if the sauce seemed too thick and adjusting the seasoning to ensure the rice remained fluffy. He plated the food in a shallow bowl, arranging the chicken, colorful vegetables, and rice in a neat, inviting stack before adding a final garnish of sliced green onions.

The meal was prepared for a Casual Tuesday gathering at Hobbs Cafe, where Theo hoped to share a relaxed meal with friends and support his continued work at the cafe and other community projects.
