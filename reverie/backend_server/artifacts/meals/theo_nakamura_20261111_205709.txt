On Tuesday, February 14, Theo Nakamura prepared a simple Miso-Potato Bowl with roasted chicken and a glass of cold lemon-lime water.

He sliced the potatoes, parsnips, and carrots into thin strips, then tossed them in a pan with olive oil, sea salt, and a few slices of ginger. The chicken was grilled until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. In a small pan, he browned the chicken in the same oil, then added a spoonful of miso paste and a splash of water. He finished the broth with fresh parsley and served it over the warm vegetables.

The aroma was warm and savory, blending the rich scent of roasted garlic, ginger, and miso with the subtle sweetness of the potatoes. He presented the bowl with a small plate of rice and a side of crisp lettuce, arranging everything neatly as if it were part of an official form.

The meal was prepared for the health-education project at Hobbs Cafe, intended as a comforting, nutritious starter for the town’s upcoming gathering. The careful presentation reinforced Theo’s methodical nature, while the simple flavors reflected his earnest desire to serve his community.
