On a Tuesday morning, Theo Nakamura prepared a simple weekday dinner for himself. The meal was Chicken and Rice with Steamed Green Beans, a comforting, economical dish suited to a first real job.

The chicken breast was marinated in salt, lemon juice, garlic, and ginger, then pan-seared until golden and cooked through. A small pile of fluffy basmati rice was served alongside the chicken, while green beans were steamed briefly to retain their crisp texture. A side of roasted carrots added warmth and color.

The kitchen smelled gently of toasted garlic, citrus, and savory poultry. At 7:00 a.m., Theo finished preparing breakfast at 7:30, then returned at 7:00 p.m. to finish the main course and clear the table.

The dinner was a quiet, ordinary meal, but he felt proud of the methodical preparation. It was not meant to impress anyone, but to give himself something simple and satisfying to start the day.
