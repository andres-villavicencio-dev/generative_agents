Dish Name: Hearty Lentil and Vegetable Soup with Roasted Tomato Crust

Key Ingredients: cooked red lentils, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, vegetable broth, canned tomatoes, parsley, olive oil, salt, and pepper.

Preparation Method: Theo washed the vegetables and lentils in warm water and sautéed the aromatics in a pot until the onion and carrots softened. He added the tomato paste and broth, letting the soup simmer for about 20 minutes. The lentils were added last, covered, and cooked until tender. The roasted tomato crust was made by mixing canned tomatoes with butter, garlic, and herbs, then baking them in the oven until golden and slightly crisp.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of simmered vegetables, warm broth, toasted garlic, and caramelized tomatoes.

Presentation: A clear glass bowl held the colorful soup, while a golden, slightly crispy tomato crust was placed beside it. Fresh parsley and a sprinkle of black pepper added a final pop of color.

Occasion: It was a simple Tuesday meal, prepared for Theo’s first real job at Hobbs Cafe. He made it quietly, hoping the food would feel warm and inviting enough to give him the confidence he needed to serve others.
