Theo prepared a simple Pan-Fried Egg and Toast breakfast, a classic, hands-on meal suited to his early start. The dish consisted of thick slices of white bread toasted in butter, topped with a poached egg and a drizzle of olive oil. He chopped a few green onions, seasoned the eggs with salt and pepper, and let the bread warm in the pan while he prepared the eggs.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of toasted bread, butter, and the delicate scent of eggs cooking. He served the food in a shallow white bowl, arranging the golden toast neatly beside the bright yellow egg and green garnish.

He made the breakfast for a quiet morning routine, using it to transition from sleep to his day of checking the community bulletin board at Johnson Park and searching for a first job.
