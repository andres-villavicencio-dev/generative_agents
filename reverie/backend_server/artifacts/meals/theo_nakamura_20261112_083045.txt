On a cold February evening, Theo prepared Chicken and Root Vegetable Risotto. He began by sautéing onions and carrots in olive oil, then added chicken breast and a handful of cherry tomatoes. After briefly simmering the chicken, he incorporated Arborio rice, slowly adding vegetable broth and stirring with a wooden spoon until the grains absorbed the liquid. He finished the dish with Parmesan cheese and a sprinkle of fresh parsley.

As he stirred the pot, the kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted nuts, garlic, and savory chicken. The creamy rice coated the golden chicken in an inviting amber, while the bright orange carrots and crisp greens added a cheerful contrast.

He plated the dish in a simple white bowl, arranging the chicken around the rice with a small spoonful of Parmesan on top. It was the first meal of his evening, serving as a quiet pause before checking the community bulletin board at Johnson Park for town-service jobs.
