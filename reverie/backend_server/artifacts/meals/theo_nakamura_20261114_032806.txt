Theo prepared Miso Ginger Chicken with Brown Rice and Broccoli for breakfast. He marinated chicken thighs with ginger, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and a splash of sesame oil, then pan-fried them until golden and slightly crisp. He cooked brown rice and steamed broccoli in a small pot, then combined everything with a few drops of sesame oil and a pinch of salt.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted sesame, ginger, savory chicken, and steaming vegetables. Theo presented the meal on a simple white plate, arranging the golden chicken over the rice and broccoli, and adding a small paper towel for absorption. It was a wholesome, comforting first breakfast, shared with Carl Demissie in their house, as they prepared for the day ahead.
