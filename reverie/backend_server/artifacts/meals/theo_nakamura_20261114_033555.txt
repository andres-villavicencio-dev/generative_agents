Theo prepared Pork and Asparagus Risotto for a quick weekday lunch. He began by sautéing carrots and celery with a knob of butter in a large pan, then added a diced pork shoulder and cooked it until the fat rendered and the meat was tender. He stirred in a vegetable broth, rice, and sliced asparagus, adding the remaining broth gradually while stirring until the grains were tender and the sauce had thickened.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of pork, caramelized carrots, and aromatic asparagus, while the gentle sizzle of the pan added a cheerful warmth. Theo served the dish in a shallow bowl, arranging the pork in the center and sprinkling the asparagus on top. He added a small garnish of fresh parsley and a drizzle of olive oil before eating.

The meal was simple, satisfying, and made for a quiet Monday lunch. It represented his methodical approach to cooking as he sought his first job: practical, nourishing, and ready to serve the town.
