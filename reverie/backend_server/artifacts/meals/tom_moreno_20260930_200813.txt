Tom Moreno prepared a vibrant Cilantro-Lime Grilled Salmon with Smashed Avocado and Roasted Sweet Potatoes at 8:00 pm for a relaxed dinner with Harmony in a Bowl. Key ingredients included fresh salmon, lime zest, cilantro, avocado, sweet potatoes, and olive oil; the dish was seasoned and grilled to golden perfection. The aroma of smoky herbs mingled with citrus brightness as steam rose from the plate. Tomatoes were sliced just before serving, adding color. Presented on a rustic ceramic platter, the meal balanced protein, healthy fats, and carbs. Tom, though usually gruff, smiled at Harmony in a Bowl over this nourishing dish—it was his calm moment, a simple celebration of home-cooked comfort after a day’s rush.
