Tom prepared a hearty Herb-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Crispy Potatoes. The meal was designed for a relaxed Tuesday dinner at The Willow Market, after he had finished helping customers, handling Jane’s pharmacy refill, and managing the store’s day-to-day operations.

The chicken was seasoned with garlic, herbs, lemon zest, smoked paprika, and black pepper. It was seasoned thoroughly, then roasted until golden, juicy, and tender. Root vegetables—carrots, parsnips, and potatoes—were roasted in the same oven, with the chicken placed on top to roast evenly. The skin turned crisp while the flesh remained moist.

The kitchen smelled rich and aromatic, filled with the scent of roasted garlic, thyme, lemon, and savory herbs. Tom plated the meal on a large serving platter with a rustic wooden board, arranging the chicken and colorful vegetables around it. A simple lemon-herb sauce was drizzled over everything, and he served a side of crusty bread.

It was a warm, family-style meal, prepared with care and enthusiasm. Tom enjoyed the rich aromas and the satisfying contrast of crispy edges and tender meat, taking a moment to reflect on a fulfilling evening before heading home at 11:00 PM.
