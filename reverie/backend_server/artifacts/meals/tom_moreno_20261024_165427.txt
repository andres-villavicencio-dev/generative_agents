Tom Moreno prepared a hearty Hearty Chicken and Vegetable Soup for a quiet Tuesday lunch with Hailey. The soup had chicken breast, carrots, celery, onion, potatoes, tomato, garlic, and a splash of broth, with a pinch of salt and pepper. Tom sliced the vegetables into uniform pieces, washed them thoroughly, and added them to a pot with the remaining ingredients. The soup simmered gently for about twenty minutes until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were softened.

Aromatic, earthy, and comforting, the soup smelled warm and savory of roasted chicken, carrots, and garlic. Tom poured it into a large bowl, garnished it with a few fresh chives and lemon slices, and served it with crusty bread and a small plate of sliced cheese. The simple, satisfying meal was prepared as a casual family lunch—quiet, practical, and full of care—without any confirmed occasion.
