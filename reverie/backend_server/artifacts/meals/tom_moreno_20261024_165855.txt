Tom prepared a Grilled Chicken with Lemon Herb Butter and Roasted Vegetables for Hailey. The main protein was a breast cut from a whole chicken, seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic, lemon zest, and dried oregano, then grilled until golden and tender. The vegetables—potatoes, carrots, onions, and bell peppers—were tossed with olive oil, garlic, oregano, and salt before baking until crisp and caramelized.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, caramelized onions, and the smoky scent of the grill. Tom arranged the golden chicken on a platter and surrounded it with the bright orange carrots, crisp potatoes, and colorful bell peppers. A simple white plate, a small bowl of lemon dressing, and a side of whole-grain crackers completed the presentation.

The occasion was a casual Tuesday lunch at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, while Tom was helping out, though he did not confirm a specific meal with Hailey. The meal was simple, cheerful, and meant for everyday comfort rather than a celebration.
