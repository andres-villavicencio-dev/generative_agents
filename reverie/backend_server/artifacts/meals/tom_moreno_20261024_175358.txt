Dish: Tomato Basil Chicken with Roasted Vegetables

Key ingredients: chicken breast, cherry tomatoes, red bell pepper, onions, garlic, basil, olive oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt and pepper.

Preparation method: Tom began by washing the vegetables and cutting the tomatoes into bite-sized pieces. He sliced the onion and pepper, then minced the garlic. While the chicken browned in a pan, he added the vegetables and cooked them until tender. He sautéed the garlic and basil, then combined the cooked chicken with the vegetables and tossed in the lemon-olive oil mixture. The dish was finished with a little more seasoning and served with warm whole-grain bread.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the sharp scent of garlic, roasted vegetables, lemon zest, and fresh basil.

Presentation: Tom arranged the colorful vegetables neatly around the chicken and topped each portion with a thin slice of sourdough. He served the bread in two crisp slices, with a small bowl of lemon-herb dressing beside it.

Occasion: It was Tom’s simple Tuesday lunch, prepared for Hailey before their outing at The Willow Market and Pharmacy. The cheerful atmosphere made the meal feel informal and welcoming, though Tom’s usual blunt, energetic manner softened the occasion just enough to keep the day moving.
