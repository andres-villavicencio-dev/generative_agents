Tom Moreno prepared a simple Garlic Herb Chicken Sandwich with Avocado Toast, a quick lunch to share with Hailey. The main protein was a thick-roasted chicken breast, seasoned with fresh garlic, lemon, pepper, salt, and crushed red pepper flakes. He sliced the chicken, seared it in a pan until golden, then let it rest while seasoning with more salt and pepper. The sandwich was assembled with warm bread, avocado, tomato, and lettuce, and served with a glass of water.

The kitchen smelled of garlic, roasted chicken, lemon, and warm herbs. Tom plated the sandwich on a white plate, arranging the chicken and toppings in an attractive, casual arrangement before handing it to Hailey. He ate it with his own hands, praising the meal and joking about its lack of sides.

It was a quiet lunch outing for the two friends, perhaps a small treat to celebrate Tom’s Tuesday without a confirmed plan. The meal was simple but satisfying, and the relaxed atmosphere made Tom’s normally sharp personality feel less tense.
