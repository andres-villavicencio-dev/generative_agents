Tom prepared a warm Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with rice for Hailey’s simple Tuesday lunch. The dish used chicken thighs, carrots, bell peppers, snap peas, and a handful of broccoli florets. He tossed the vegetables in a small amount of oil, then added cubed chicken and a tablespoon of soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a splash of chicken broth. He stir-fried everything quickly until the chicken was cooked through and the vegetables were tender.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic, ginger, and toasted vegetables. Tom arranged the bright, colorful stir-fry over a bed of fluffy white rice, plating it so the chicken, vegetables, and rice created an inviting rainbow. He set a simple wooden serving plate on the counter and took a few bites himself.

It was an ordinary, comforting lunch—a practical choice for a busy Tuesday—but the warm colors and balanced flavors made it feel like a special little meal. He prepared it for Hailey, who needed something quick and filling to start her day.
