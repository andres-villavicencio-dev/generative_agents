Tom Moreno prepared a simple Garlic Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Pan-Roasted Potatoes for Hailey. The dish included a whole chicken, skin-on, carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, red onion, celery, and potatoes, all seasoned with olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

Tom washed the vegetables and peeled the garlic before slicing the chicken. He placed everything in a large roasting pan, added enough water to cover the ingredients, and roasted it in the oven at 400°F (200°C) until the chicken was golden brown and the vegetables were tender. He finished the potatoes under the broiler until crispy.

As the oven worked, the kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, garlic, and slightly sweet potatoes. Tom plated the meal on a large white platter, arranging the chicken in the center and the colorful vegetables in neat rows around it. The potatoes added a crunchy contrast to the soft meat.

The lunch was meant for their Tuesday lunch break, but Tom’s eager, energetic personality made it feel like a cheerful, informal gathering. By the time they finished eating, Tom’s smile suggested the day had finally felt rewarding, even if his mood was still full of hope and lingering dislike for Sam Moore.
