Dish Name: Tuna and Veggie Panini

Tom prepared a simple lunch for Hailey using a large skillet. He seasoned a handful of fresh vegetables with salt, pepper, a little lemon juice, and garlic, then stirred them in with canned tuna and a splash of soy sauce. He pressed the mixture into a toasted sandwich baguette, sliced it in half, and stacked it with the filling before broiling it until the bread was crisp and golden.

The meal had a warm, savory aroma of toasted bread, garlic, soy sauce, and the rich, oceanic scent of the tuna. Tom plated it on a white plate with a bright green vegetable garnish, arranged the tuna in the center, and added a slice of lemon for a fresh finish.

He made it for a quiet Tuesday lunch with Hailey, who had asked him to prepare the meal while they shared a meal at home. The occasion was casual and comforting, with no special event or celebration.
