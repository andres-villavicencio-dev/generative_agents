Dish Name: Simple Market Lunch with Hailey

Tom prepared a warm, rustic bowl of oatmeal with berries and a little honey. The key ingredients were rolled oats, blueberries, sliced apples, a pinch of salt and pepper, and a few drops of olive oil. He turned off the stove, stirred in the salt, pepper, and oil, and added the remaining fruit before serving.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the familiar scent of warm oats, sweet berries, and lightly toasted grains. Tom presented the meal in a simple white bowl, arranged the fruit neatly around the oatmeal, and added a small spoonful of honey for extra warmth.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday lunch with Hailey at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, after Tom had been helping Maria with her morning routine. It was a relaxed, informal meal—not a celebratory occasion—but the combination of warm food and familiar companionship made it feel special.
