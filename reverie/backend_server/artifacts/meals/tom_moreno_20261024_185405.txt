Tom prepared a simple Grilled Chicken and Herb Roast for a casual lunch with Hailey. The dish consisted of a whole chicken seasoned with olive oil, salt, pepper, dried oregano, thyme, garlic, and lemon slices, arranged on a baking sheet with a small side of roasted potatoes.

He brushed the chicken with oil, arranged it over the potatoes, and placed the tray in the oven. He roasted it until the skin was crisp and the meat was cooked through, then sliced it and served it with the potatoes.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken, herbs, garlic, and lemon. He arranged the platter on a white plate, adding a small lemon wedge and a sprig of fresh thyme. The bright colors and balanced presentation made the meal feel ready for a relaxed gathering.

The lunch was simple and satisfying, prepared for an easy Tuesday lunch with Hailey. Tom did not announce the occasion, but his careful attention to the dish suggested he was eager to share a warm meal before heading home.
