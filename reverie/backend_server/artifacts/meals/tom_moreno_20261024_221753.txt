Tom prepared a hearty Braised Chicken and Root Vegetable Lunch for a Wednesday morning at The Willow Market. The dish included a whole chicken, carrots, potatoes, onions, bell peppers, and a rich tomato-onion sauce, with a side of warm rice and crusty bread.

He roasted the chicken until the skin was golden and crispy, then braised it in a savory, caramelized broth with carrots and potatoes. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of savory chicken, caramelized onions, and simmering tomatoes. Tom finished it by seasoning the vegetables with paprika, garlic, and oregano, then arranged them attractively on a serving platter with the golden chicken resting atop.

The lunch was prepared for a busy Wednesday, but its comforting appearance and satisfying flavors made it feel like a thoughtful family meal. Tom served it with the bread to accompany the warm, filling dish.
