Tom prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze on Tuesday, February 14, for a family breakfast on the way to work. He sliced a whole chicken, marinated it in olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and fresh herbs, then grilled it until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. Alongside the chicken, he roasted carrots, potatoes, and Brussels sprouts with a little oil and spices, finishing the dish with a golden lemon glaze.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and citrus, mingling with the warm scent of roasted vegetables. Tom arranged the colorful vegetables around the platter and placed the chicken in the center, garnishing it with fresh parsley. The bright colors and golden glaze gave the meal an inviting, cheerful presentation, while the lemon and herbs added a bright freshness.

He prepared it for a quick, nourishing family breakfast on the way to work, with a glass of water and a plate of fresh fruit for dessert.
