Tom prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa Salad, a simple yet satisfying lunch for the store’s lunch break. He marinated chicken breasts with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and dried herbs, then grilled them until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. He roasted carrots, bell peppers, and onions with cumin and paprika, then cooled them before mixing them with fluffy quinoa, chopped kale, lemon-tahini dressing, and fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with the sharp scent of garlic, the bright aroma of citrus, and the earthy warmth of roasted vegetables. Tom plated the meal in a white ceramic bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables around the chicken and garnishing it with a few whole parsley leaves. The presentation was clean, inviting, and balanced, making it an ideal quick meal for a busy Tuesday. He served it with a side of warm whole-grain bread and a small glass of water before heading into the store.
