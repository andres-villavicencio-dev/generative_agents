Tom prepared a hearty Baked Chicken with Asparagus and Lemon-Herb Roast for the family dinner on Tuesday, February 14. The dish featured boneless thighs, asparagus spears, carrots, onions, garlic, chicken broth, thyme, rosemary, and butter. It was seasoned with salt, pepper, lemon juice, and freshly ground black pepper, then roasted at 400°F (200°C) until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, herbs, citrus, and roasted vegetables. The asparagus was plucked from the heat and served alongside the chicken and carrots, which were lightly garnished with chopped parsley and a drizzle of lemon-tartar sauce. Tom plated the meal in a large ceramic baking dish, arranging the chicken in the center and the colorful vegetables neatly around it.

The occasion was a relaxed family dinner before the day’s grocery and meal coordination. Tom served his wife Jane and the children, creating a warm, homey atmosphere with the comforting aroma of roasted herbs and the cheerful presentation of fresh, bright vegetables.
