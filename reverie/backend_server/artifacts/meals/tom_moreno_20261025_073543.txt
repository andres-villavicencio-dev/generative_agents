Tom prepared a hearty Herb-Roasted Chicken with Potatoes and Green Beans. The recipe called for a whole chicken, potatoes, green beans, garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon, olive oil, and black pepper. He roasted the whole chicken first, then sliced the potatoes and green beans, seasoning them with salt and pepper.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of rosemary, garlic, citrus, and savory roasted vegetables. Tom arranged the chicken on a white platter, surrounded it with golden potatoes and crisp green beans, and garnished it with lemon wedges and fresh herbs. The meal was served for a quiet Tuesday lunch at The Willow Market, where he had prepared it to share with customers.
