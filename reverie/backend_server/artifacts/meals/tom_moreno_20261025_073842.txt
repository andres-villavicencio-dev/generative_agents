Tom prepared a hearty Roasted Vegetable and Chicken Salad, a simple yet refreshing lunch for the Wednesday morning shift. He roasted chicken breasts with garlic, lemon, rosemary, and a splash of olive oil, then roasted carrots, zucchini, bell peppers, and red onions until the vegetables were tender and the skin was caramelized. He arranged the salad in a white bowl, layering the warm chicken over crisp greens, crisp carrots, and a light yogurt-cucumber dressing.

The dish had a bright, savory aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and lemon. Tom presented it in two neat portions, carefully arranging the colorful vegetables and golden chicken so the salad looked inviting and balanced. He served it with a small side of toasted bread and fresh fruit, giving it a cheerful, practical presentation suited to a lunch break. The meal was meant to be a quick, satisfying lunch for Tom, Jane, and their customers after a busy day at The Willow Market and Pharmacy.
