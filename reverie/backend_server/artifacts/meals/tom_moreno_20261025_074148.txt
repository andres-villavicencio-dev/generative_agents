Tom prepared Baked Herb-Roasted Chicken with Gravy and Sweet Potatoes for a Tuesday family dinner. He began by seasoning chicken breasts with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and salt, then seared them in a skillet until the skin was crisp. He added bone-in chicken and carrots to the pan, pouring in chicken broth, vegetable stock, and a splash of soy sauce. The mixture was simmered until the chicken was tender and the gravy had thickened. Sweet potatoes were roasted alongside the chicken until golden and caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of rosemary, garlic, caramelized sugar, and savory chicken. Tom plated the dish in a shallow baking dish, arranging the golden chicken and potatoes around a small pile of gravy. He served a white rice pilaf with fresh parsley. The meal was hearty and comforting, perfect for a Tuesday evening when Tom, Jane, and their family gathered around the table.
