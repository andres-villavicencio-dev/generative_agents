Tom prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Glaze for a Friday family dinner at The Willow Market. The dish features a tender chicken breast marinated in lemon, garlic, olive oil, and dried herbs, grilled to a golden crisp, then glazed with a bright lemon-herb mixture. It was paired with roasted carrots, Brussels sprouts, and asparagus seasoned with rosemary, salt, and pepper.

Tom marinated the chicken in the refrigerator the night before, flipping it halfway through to ensure even cooking. The vegetables were tossed with oil and rosemary and roasted in the oven until the skins were crisp and the meat was tender. He cooked everything in a large skillet with a little butter, allowing the herbs to release their fragrance.

When finished, the golden chicken glowed with savory, lemony aroma, while the vegetables added a sweet, earthy undertone. Tom plated the dish in a rustic white bowl, arranging the vegetables around the chicken and drizzling extra lemon juice over the top. The presentation was simple and hearty, with the bright lemon zest and green herbs making the meal feel inviting and celebratory. It was prepared for a Friday family dinner, with Tom serving it alongside his wife Jane, John Lin, and other market colleagues.
