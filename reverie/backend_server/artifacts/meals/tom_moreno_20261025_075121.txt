Tom prepared a hearty Garlic Herb Chicken Stir-Fry with Brown Rice. He sliced a whole chicken, sautéed it with garlic, ginger, onions, and red pepper flakes, then added vegetable broth, soy sauce, rice, carrots, broccoli, and snap peas. The dish was finished with a splash of sesame oil and chopped green onions.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, toasted rice, and caramelized onions. Tom plated the chicken in a bright, rustic bowl and arranged the colorful vegetables around it. The presentation was simple but visually inviting, with the golden chicken, amber rice, and green garnish creating a cheerful contrast.

The meal was prepared for an early weekday lunch at The Willow Market. Tom and Jane chose it as a practical, nourishing option, while Jane added a small side of fruit. Tom had no occasion to serve it; he simply wanted a quick, satisfying lunch to keep up with the store’s busy schedule.
