Tom prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce for a Tuesday family breakfast. He marinated chicken breasts, carrots, onions, and bell peppers in olive oil, garlic, lemon, herbs, and salt, then grilled them until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender. A simple lemon-herb sauce was prepared separately, and the chicken was arranged on a platter, garnished with fresh herbs, and served with crusty bread.

The kitchen smelled of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and warm grill smoke. The presentation was bright and inviting: the bright yellow vegetables, golden chicken, and white sauce created a cheerful family meal. He prepared the dish for a cozy Tuesday breakfast, bringing everyone together before the morning workday.
