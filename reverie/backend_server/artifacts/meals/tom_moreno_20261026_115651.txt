Tom prepared a hearty Hearty Vegetable and Herb Bowl for lunch. The dish featured crisp carrots, tender cabbage, broccoli florets, cherry tomatoes, and red onion, all tossed in a savory herb-lime vinaigrette made with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, fresh parsley, and a pinch of black pepper.

He washed and chopped the vegetables, then sautéed them in a large skillet until the carrots began to soften. Tom added the tomatoes and red onion, followed by a splash of lemon juice and a generous handful of chopped fresh herbs before serving the warm mixture over a bed of lightly steamed cabbage. The bowl was garnished with a few whole cherry tomatoes and a sprig of parsley.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized carrots, roasted garlic, and bright citrus. It was a warm, satisfying lunch on a Wednesday morning at The Willow Market, prepared with care to complement the busy grocery and prescription work Tom was balancing.
