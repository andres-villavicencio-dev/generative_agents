Breakfast: Tomato, Egg, and Fruit Toast

Tom prepared a quick, hearty breakfast using eggs, whole-grain toast, sliced tomatoes, a handful of sliced apples, and a cup of tea. The eggs were lightly fried until the yolk remained soft, then sliced and arranged on the top slice of toast alongside the fruit. He topped the dish with a few crisp tomato slices and scattered them with the apples, giving it a colorful, rustic appearance.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, sweet fruit, and savory egg yolks. Tom arranged the meal on a white plate, adding a sprig of parsley for a finished touch. It was a simple, comforting breakfast intended for a quiet Thursday morning, with the promise of a calm start to his day at the Willow Market.
