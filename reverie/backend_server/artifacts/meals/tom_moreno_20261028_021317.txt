Tom prepared a simple grilled chicken breakfast for himself and his breakfast companions at The Willow Market. He marinated the chicken in olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and a pinch of salt, then grilled it over a hot skillet until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. The breakfast included fluffy scrambled eggs, sliced bagel, a handful of sliced tomatoes, and a side of yogurt with fresh fruit.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of garlic and lemon, and the golden-brown chicken released a savory fragrance as it cooked. Tom arranged the eggs in a neat row, topped the bagel with a drizzle of yogurt, and placed the chicken and fruit beside it, creating an inviting, colorful presentation.

The occasion was a relaxed quick breakfast gathering. Tom wanted to make the meal affordable and easy to prepare, while still ensuring everyone was well-fed. The warm, fresh food and cheerful arrangement reflected the community-oriented atmosphere at the market, and Tom enjoyed the simple pleasure of starting his day with a satisfying, comforting meal.
