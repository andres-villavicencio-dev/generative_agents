At The Willow Market, Tom prepared Tom’s Maple Bacon Breakfast Sandwich for Jane. He grilled two thick slices of bacon until crisp and golden, then sliced them into strips and pan-fried two slices of bacon. He scrambled two eggs over a pan of diced potatoes and chopped green onions, adding a splash of milk and a pinch of salt. The eggs were folded into the potatoes, while the bacon was torn over the top.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of toasted bacon, caramelized onions, warm eggs, and a hint of maple syrup. Tom arranged the sandwiches on sturdy white plates, placing the eggs and bacon-side down so the golden-brown bacon remained visible. Jane picked up a sandwich, smiling as Tom offered her another choice while making sure she enjoyed her meal.

The occasion was a peaceful Monday morning at home, with Tom ready to start the day and Jane happy to be cared for.
