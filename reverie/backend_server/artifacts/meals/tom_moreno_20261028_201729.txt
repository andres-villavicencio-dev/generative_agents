Tom prepared Roasted Chicken with Herb-Roasted Vegetables as a side dish for Tom Moreno’s dinner with Jane. The meal featured a whole chicken seasoned with dried rosemary, thyme, garlic, lemon zest, and smoked paprika, then roasted until the skin turned golden and crispy. Root vegetables—including carrots, potatoes, and green beans—were tossed with olive oil, rosemary, salt, and pepper before roasting alongside the chicken.

The aroma of the kitchen filled with the warm scent of roasted garlic, citrus, and herb-rich vegetables, creating a cozy, inviting atmosphere. Tom arranged the food on a platter with the chicken resting atop a bed of colorful vegetables, garnished with a lemon wedge and parsley. It was presented for a relaxed family dinner, with Jane sitting beside him as he plated the dish.
