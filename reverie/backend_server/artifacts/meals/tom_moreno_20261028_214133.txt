Tom prepared a hearty Tom’s Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Cranberry Sauce for dinner. The main dish included a whole chicken, carrots, parsnips, potatoes, onions, garlic, and fresh herbs, along with half a loaf of sourdough bread.

He seasoned the chicken generously with salt, pepper, paprika, and dried thyme, then roasted it in a large oven-safe pan until the skin was golden and the breast was cooked through. The vegetables were roasted together until tender, and a simple cranberry sauce was made by simmering cranberries, orange juice, vinegar, and a touch of honey. The sourdough served as a rustic side.

As he plated the meal, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted chicken, caramelized carrots, garlic, and dried herbs. Tom arranged the chicken in the center of a white serving platter, with bright orange vegetables and a glossy cranberry sauce around it. He added a small wedge of lemon and a sprig of rosemary before serving.

The occasion was a relaxed family dinner at home, and Tom’s energetic, eager demeanor added a lively, cheerful touch to a simple, nourishing meal.
