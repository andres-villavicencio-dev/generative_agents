Tom prepared Crispy Roasted Chicken with Garlic Mashed Potatoes and Green Beans for dinner on Monday, February 13.

The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic, and a splash of lemon juice, then roasted at 400°F until golden and tender. It was served with creamy garlic mashed potatoes, crisp green beans, and a simple lemon-herb sauce. The potatoes were mashed with butter, parsley, and a little garlic, while the green beans were tossed in olive oil and roasted until bright and crunchy.

The kitchen smelled rich and warm—savory chicken, roasted garlic, butter, herbs, and a hint of lemon. Tom presented the meal with a wooden serving platter, a colorful salad bowl, and a small dish of lemon-herb sauce, arranging the chicken prominently in the center.

He served it at the family’s dinner table after work, ready to share a comforting meal with his wife, Jane. The occasion was an ordinary weekday evening, but it felt special because Tom always made sure everyone was well taken care of.
