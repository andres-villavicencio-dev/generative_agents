Tom prepared a hearty Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables and Cranberry Glaze for a quick family dinner. He used a whole chicken with thighs, carrots, potatoes, onions, and celery, seasoned them with garlic, rosemary, salt, pepper, and a touch of dried thyme. For the glaze, he combined the chicken with chopped cranberries, honey, mustard, and a splash of apple cider vinegar, then roasted everything at 400°F for about 25–30 minutes until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender.

The kitchen smelled warm and savory—golden brown chicken, roasted carrots, caramelized onions, and a hint of citrus. Tom arranged the golden-brown chicken on a large platter and placed the colorful vegetables beside it, garnishing with a scattering of fresh herbs and a bright lemon slice. The meal was simple, comforting, and ready in under an hour, fitting into Tom’s busy schedule before he went home to eat with Jane.
