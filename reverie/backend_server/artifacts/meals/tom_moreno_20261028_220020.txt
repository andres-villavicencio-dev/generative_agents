Tom prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables for dinner on Monday, February 13. He sliced a whole chicken, seasoned it with garlic, rosemary, thyme, black pepper, and a splash of lemon juice, then roasted it with potatoes, carrots, onions, and celery until the skin was golden and the chicken cooked through.

The kitchen smelled rich and aromatic as the vegetables softened and the herbs released their fragrance. Tom arranged the chicken in the center of a baking sheet, surrounded by bright orange carrots, crispy potatoes, caramelized onions, and a small portion of lemon-herb chicken sauce. He plated the meal at the table, drizzling the sauce over the chicken and garnishing it with fresh parsley and a lemon wedge.

The occasion was a simple, relaxed family dinner after Tom had been busy at The Willow Market and Pharmacy. He chose the chicken as the main meal because it was hearty enough for everyone and looked comforting enough to satisfy both his wife and his appetite.
