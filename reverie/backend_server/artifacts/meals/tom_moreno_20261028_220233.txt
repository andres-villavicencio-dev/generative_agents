Tom prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Basil Sauce for Jane’s Monday dinner. He marinated chicken thighs with garlic, olive oil, lemon zest, and oregano, then grilled them until golden and tender. He roasted seasonal vegetables—including zucchini, carrots, and bell peppers—with rosemary and salt, then tossed them with the sauce.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, roasted garlic, lemon zest, and fresh herbs. Tom arranged the chicken on a white platter, surrounded it with colorful vegetables, and drizzled the glossy lemon-basil sauce over the top. A small red pepper and a sprig of parsley completed the presentation.

The occasion was a simple home dinner for Tom and Jane. After opening The Willow Market and Pharmacy and helping customers, he decided to spend some time at home, preparing the meal while Jane cleared the counter and cleaned the kitchen. The meal was meant to be nourishing, cheerful, and celebratory, but Tom’s eager, slightly aggressive demeanor made the occasion feel more like an energetic family gathering than a quiet evening.
