Tom prepared a simple Hearty Vegetable Stir-Fry for dinner. The dish featured a colorful mix of carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, onions, snap peas, and mushrooms, with a savory soy-ginger sauce and a touch of brown sugar. He chopped the vegetables, sautéed them in a wok until crisp-tender, and tossed them with the sauce.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich fragrance of garlic, ginger, and caramelized onions. Tom arranged the vegetables in a shallow pan and poured the sauce over them, garnishing the dish with fresh herbs. It was served on a warm dinner plate as he and Jane sat down to eat together.

This was a casual family dinner after Tom finished his shift at the market. It was relaxed and informal, with Jane helping him prepare and store the vegetables afterward.
