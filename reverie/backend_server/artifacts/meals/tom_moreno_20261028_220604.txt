Tom prepared Crispy Chicken with Lemon Herb Pan-Roast, a hearty main dish for home dinner. He seasoned the chicken with olive oil, garlic, dried herbs, salt, pepper, and a pinch of smoked paprika, then tossed it with sliced carrots, onions, mushrooms, and thyme. While the chicken roasted, he sliced potatoes and roasted them until the skin was golden and crispy.

The aroma filling the kitchen was rich and savory—garlic, herbs, roasted vegetables, and the savory scent of caramelized chicken. He plated the dish in a shallow pan, arranging the golden chicken, browned vegetables, and crisp potatoes around the edges. Tom presented it simply, letting the warm colors and golden crust suggest flavor without needing extra decoration.

The meal was prepared quickly at home, and Tom ate it with Jane around 5 p.m. It was an ordinary dinner, but the cheerful, energetic atmosphere made it feel special.
