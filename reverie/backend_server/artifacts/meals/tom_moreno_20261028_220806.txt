That evening, Tom prepared Tomato Basil Chicken with Lemon-Herb Crust for dinner with Jane. The key ingredients were chicken breasts, fresh tomatoes, garlic, Italian herbs, lemon, olive oil, and a crisp crust of panko.

He brined the chicken overnight, then seared it until golden before browning it in a pan. The tomatoes were gently sautéed until soft, then combined with the chicken and aromatics. Tom sliced garlic, chopped parsley, and grated lemon over the dish, seasoning it with salt, pepper, and a touch of oregano. He drizzed the pan with a little extra oil and toasted the crusty bread before serving.

The aroma rose from the kitchen—warm, savory, and bright with the scent of garlic, lemon, herbs, and caramelized tomatoes. The chicken was served in a shallow white bowl, surrounded by crisp crusty bread and a small side of roasted vegetables, with the rich tomato sauce pooling beneath.

The occasion was a quiet Sunday evening at home, where Tom and Jane enjoyed a relaxed, informal dinner. He had also remembered to wash up afterward, clearing the dishes and putting everything away as he went back to bed around 11 p.m.
