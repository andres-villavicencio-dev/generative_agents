Tom prepared a Simple Chicken and Vegetable Soup with Garlic Bread.

The soup was made by boiling chicken thighs in water with carrots, celery, onion, and a few cloves of garlic, then blending the broth and vegetables. A rich, golden broth with tender chicken and hearty vegetables gave it a comforting aroma of roasted root, savory chicken, and warm spices. The garlic added a subtle bite, while carrots and celery added sweetness and texture.

For the presentation, the soup was ladled into a white bowl with a sprig of parsley and a lemon wedge resting beside it. Tom placed a golden-brown garlic bread on the side, making the meal look neat and inviting.

The occasion was a quiet Tuesday lunch at The Willow Market, where Tom was preparing to manage the market’s daily operations. He wanted his meal to be both nourishing and simple, fitting his tired but proud mood. He prepared it calmly and proudly, enjoying the satisfaction of having a well-made meal while continuing his work.
