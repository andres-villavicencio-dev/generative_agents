Tom prepared a hearty Tomato Basil Chicken with Garlic and Herb Rice for an early Friday dinner with his wife. He marinated chicken thighs in olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, dried oregano, and a splash of red wine, then seared them until golden. While the chicken cooked, he stir-fried onions, carrots, and bell peppers in a pan with fresh thyme and a pinch of sugar. The rice was rinsed and sautéed with butter, garlic, and basil until tender.

The kitchen smelled warmly of roasted garlic, savory herbs, and citrus. He served the chicken in a shallow dish, surrounded the rice in a round bowl, and topped it with a glossy lemon-olive oil glaze. The presentation was simple but inviting—a practical, rustic family meal.

Tom cooked it in the middle of a long, busy evening after managing the market and pharmacy. It was neither a special occasion nor a celebratory dinner, but a quiet moment of care and affection for his wife. The meal reflected his busy, demanding schedule and his steady, proud sense of responsibility.
