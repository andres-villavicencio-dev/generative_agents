Tom prepared Crispy Chicken and Roasted Vegetables with Rice and Green Beans. For the meal, he marinated chicken thighs with lemon juice, garlic, oregano, and a splash of soy sauce, then pan-seared them until golden and crispy. He roasted red bell peppers, zucchini, carrots, and onions with olive oil and salt until tender, then tossed them with the chicken before serving over fluffy rice and green beans.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, savory chicken, and caramelized vegetables. Tom plated the meal in a simple ceramic bowl, using crisp white rice, neatly arranged green beans, and generous portions of chicken and peppers. A bright green tomato basil sauce was served on the side, while a small bowl of warm salad greens and crackers completed the table.

The occasion was a family meal on Wednesday, February 15, after a long day of managing grocery and pharmacy duties. Tom’s energetic, informal approach made the meal feel lively and homey, and the simple preparation reflected the practical, hands-on nature of his work.
