Tom prepared Grilled Chicken Panini with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce for the Wednesday afternoons at The Willow Market. He marinated chicken breasts with oregano, garlic, salt, and pepper, then grilled them until golden and juicy. He topped the panini with crisp carrot slices, tomato wedges, and red pepper strips, drizzled it with a bright lemon-herb sauce, and sliced it into thin rounds.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, herbs, toasted bread, and savory meat. He arranged the colorful vegetables and juicy panini on a platter, adding a crisp lettuce leaf on top. The presentation was vibrant and inviting, with the golden chicken contrasting against the warm reds, oranges, and greens.

The meal was prepared for early Wednesday lunch, when Tom and Sharon Antle discussed their plans for breakfast and bakery work at The Willow Market. Tom served a generous portion with a crisp salad and orange juice, enjoying the rewarding reminder that his busy role at The Willow Market and Pharmacy had created the time to connect with Sharon and build a stronger local community.
