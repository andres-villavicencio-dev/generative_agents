Tom prepared Rice and Vegetable Curry, a warm, hearty Sunday dinner. The dish used basmati rice, diced tomatoes, chopped onions, garlic, ginger, carrots, potatoes, and canned chicken. He stir-fried the vegetables and curry in olive oil until the onions softened, then added the remaining ingredients and simmered until the rice was tender and the chicken was cooked through.

The aroma filled the kitchen with warm spices, savory tomatoes, aromatic ginger, and a rich golden sauce. He plated the rice in a shallow bowl, arranging the vegetables and chicken around it, then garnished the dish with fresh coriander and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

He prepared the meal for a quiet Sunday family dinner, serving everyone a balanced portion of rice, flavorful vegetables, and tender chicken. The warm, comforting presentation turned a simple home-cooked dinner into a welcoming meal that felt both practical and celebratory.
