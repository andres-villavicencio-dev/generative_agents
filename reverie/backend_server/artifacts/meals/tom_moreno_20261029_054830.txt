Tom prepared a simple, hearty breakfast for Jane and Amilcar: pan-seared eggs with smoked salmon, sourdough toast, and a glass of orange juice.

He began by heating a skillet over medium heat and frying two eggs until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft. He added a few drops of lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and a tiny knob of butter to create a gentle sizzle. After seasoning lightly, he slid the eggs onto warm toast and sprinkled the pan with the smoked salmon for a subtle smoky finish. Tom arranged the breakfast on a white placemat, keeping the eggs bright, the toast glossy, and the orange juice inviting.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of butter, roasted salmon, lemon zest, and toasted bread. He presented the meal with a cheerful smile, making sure everyone was included while keeping the atmosphere lively and welcoming. It was an ordinary weekday breakfast, but Tom treated it as a sincere gesture of care toward Jane and Amilcar.
