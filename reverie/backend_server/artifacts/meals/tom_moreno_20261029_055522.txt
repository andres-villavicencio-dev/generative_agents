Tom prepared a rustic Crispy Vegetable Stir-Fry with Garlic-Lemon Oil for lunch on Wednesday, February 15. He began with a bowl of mixed green vegetables—carrots, zucchini, bell peppers, snap peas, and spinach—seasoned with olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, and a little soy sauce. After washing and drying them, he sliced the carrots and bell peppers into thin strips and tossed them in a hot skillet until golden and crispy. The vegetables were then stir-fried with the remaining garlic, fresh herbs, and a splash of soy sauce before being plated over steamed rice.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of garlic and citrus, while the vegetables emitted a savory, herbaceous scent. He arranged them in a colorful, casual bowl with the rice neatly piled beside it, adding a small lime wedge and a sprig of fresh cilantro.

The meal was a cheerful, hearty lunch for Tom and his colleagues, serving as a relaxed break after his busy day.
