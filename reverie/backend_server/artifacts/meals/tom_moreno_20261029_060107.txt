Tom prepared Chicken and Vegetable Casserole with Roasted Vegetables, a hearty comfort dish for his Wednesday lunch. He browned chicken thighs in a skillet, then added chopped onions, carrots, celery, bell pepper, garlic, and a splash of broth. He simmered the chicken until it was nearly tender, then transferred it to a baking dish with cooked rice and crushed tomatoes.

The casserole was baked at 375°F for 45 minutes until the chicken was golden and the vegetables were tender. Tom added shredded cheddar and fresh parsley near the end, then covered the dish with foil and returned it to the oven.

The kitchen smelled warm and savory, with caramelized onions, garlic, and chicken filling the air. Tom presented the golden, bubbling casserole in a shallow white dish, garnishing it with extra cheese and green herbs. He served it at The Willow Market’s Wednesday lunch, where customers gathered for a warm, communal meal. The occasion was a quiet, everyday moment of shared comfort, with the local mayor’s election campaign running in the background.
