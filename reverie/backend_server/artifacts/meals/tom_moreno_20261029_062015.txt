Tom prepared a hearty Smoked Chicken Pot Pie for dinner. He sliced smoked chicken breast, sautéed onions and carrots in olive oil, then added chopped spinach, frozen peas, and a small amount of cream to thicken the pan. He topped the filling with biscuit crust, filled it, and baked it until golden and the filling bubbled gently.

The aroma was warm and savory: golden-brown crust, caramelized onions, smoky chicken, fresh spinach, and a hint of savory cream. Tom plated it in a rustic ceramic dish, garnishing it with sliced green onions and a drizzle of lemon juice.

Dinner was a quiet Wednesday celebration. After managing his duties at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, Tom used his shopping list to prepare the meal with Jane and Amilcar. It was a relaxed, family-style dinner meant to connect them and make time for the local mayor’s election.
