Tom prepared a hearty Chicken and Vegetable Soup for lunch, perfect for preparing a meal without running out of supplies. The soup combined tender chicken breast, carrots, celery, diced potatoes, onions, garlic, and a splash of chicken broth. It was slowly simmered until the vegetables softened, then gently stirred in.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm, savory scent of roasted chicken and caramelized vegetables. He sliced the chicken and arranged it over a bed of colorful vegetables in a large serving bowl, garnishing it with fresh parsley. The presentation was rustic and welcoming, with the golden chicken contrasting against the pale yellow carrots and green celery.

He served the soup with crusty bread and a side salad for lunch. While Tom was busy at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, he enjoyed the comforting food and the quiet rhythm of preparing it. The meal reflected the day’s work—busy, connected, and grounded in the satisfaction of nourishing others.
