For lunch, Tom prepared Baked Beans with Cornbread. He used leftover roast chicken bones, canned diced tomatoes, canned corn, and a few carrots, adding a splash of soy sauce and a pinch of garlic powder for the last few minutes of cooking.

The kitchen smelled rich and savory. The golden cornbread was warm and slightly crisp around the edges, while the beans were plump and glossy with a savory aroma. Tom arranged the cornbread around a white plate, with the beans arranged neatly in the center and a small piece of apple beside them.

He served it for lunch before the local mayor’s election campaign, hoping it would keep everyone feeling refreshed and hungry.
