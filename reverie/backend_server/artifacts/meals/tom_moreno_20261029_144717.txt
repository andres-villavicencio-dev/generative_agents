Tom prepared a hearty Buttermilk Scrambled Egg and Spinach Breakfast Bowl for his morning routine. He used fresh spinach, eggs, diced tomatoes, shredded cheddar, onion, garlic, and a splash of buttermilk. He gently cooked the eggs and cooked the spinach until crisp, then combined everything in a small skillet. A little hot buttermilk was stirred in to loosen the yolks and keep the dish bright and glossy.

The aroma filled the kitchen with earthy greens, savory bacon, toasted garlic, and the warm, rich scent of cooked eggs. Tom served the colorful bowl on a white ceramic plate, garnishing it with fresh parsley and a pinch of red pepper flakes.

The occasion was a quiet Friday morning at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, where Tom was preparing for the day’s customer service while also managing the local mayor’s election. The simple, satisfying meal was his way of beginning the day focused, energized, and ready to help others.
