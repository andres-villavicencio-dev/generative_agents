Tom prepared Sunday Gravy with Roast Chicken, a warm Sunday dinner he wanted to share with friends. The meal featured a whole chicken roasted until golden and tender, served with crisp potatoes, carrots, peas, and a rich, dark brown gravy.

He used a large Dutch oven, browning the chicken first to develop a flavorful crust, then adding onions, garlic, carrots, and potatoes. The gravy was thickened with a little flour and water, seasoned with salt, pepper, and a touch of dried thyme. A squeeze of lemon and fresh parsley finished the dish. The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted poultry, caramelized onions, and warm spices.

The meal was presented on a platter, with the chicken prominently displayed and vegetables arranged neatly around it. Tom served a small portion of gravy on the side, making it easy to share with guests. It was a relaxed, family-style dinner prepared for a pleasant Friday evening at home.
