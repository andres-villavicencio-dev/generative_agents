Tom prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Herb Sauce, a hearty Friday dinner for The Willow Market and Pharmacy. He marinated chicken breasts in olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, and dried oregano, then seared them on a hot skillet until golden and tender. While the chicken cooked, he roasted carrots, onions, bell peppers, and potatoes in the oven until crisp, finishing the vegetables with a little more butter and salt.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of garlic, roasted garlic, herbs, and caramelizing chicken skin. He arranged the grilled chicken in the center, surrounded it with colorful vegetables, and drizzled the bright orange lemon-herb sauce over everything. Each plate was garnished with fresh parsley and served with a side of brown rice.

The meal was prepared for an informal family dinner on Friday, February 17, as Tom relaxed after a busy day managing the market and pharmacy. The simple, comforting presentation and generous portions made the occasion feel welcoming, while his relaxed state suggested a quiet, satisfied evening.
