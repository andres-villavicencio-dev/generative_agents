Tom prepared a simple pan-seared chicken breast with lemon garlic herb sauce for a quiet Friday morning breakfast at home. He used a whole chicken, skin-on breast, olive oil, garlic, fresh rosemary, lemon zest, salt, pepper, and a splash of chicken stock. After searing the skin until crisp, he sliced the breast into portions and finished it in the pan with the flavorful sauce.

The kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of garlic, herbs, and roasted chicken, while the golden-brown skin and bright yellow lemon created a warm, inviting aroma. Tom plated each piece on a white plate, drizzing extra lemon juice and sprinkling a few rosemary leaves over the top. The bright colors and fresh herbs gave the meal an elegant, cheerful presentation, making it feel like a relaxed Sunday brunch.

This was a quiet, simple breakfast for a busy day of managing The Willow Market and Pharmacy. Tom’s relaxed mood reflected his satisfaction with the day’s work, and the meal provided a peaceful moment to unwind before continuing his work.
