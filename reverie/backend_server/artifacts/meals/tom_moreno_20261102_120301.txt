The meal was Cinnamon Oat Breakfast with Milk and Water, prepared by Tom Moreno during his Saturday morning routine at The Willow Market. He boiled water in a kettle, added rolled oats and milk to it, and then sprinkled a generous pinch of cinnamon over the mixture.

The preparation took about ten minutes. After the oats absorbed the warm liquid, Tom stirred them gently to combine the ingredients evenly. He let the mixture rest for a few minutes, which allowed the oats to soften and the cinnamon to infuse. The aroma filled the kitchen with a warm, comforting scent—sweet oats, rich milk, and warm cinnamon.

To serve, Tom arranged the breakfast in a simple white bowl, using a small spoon to place a generous portion of the oat mixture beside a glass of milk. He added a few fresh cinnamon sticks to the side as decoration. The presentation was clean and inviting, with the rich, amber-colored oatmeal contrasting against the creamy milk.

He prepared the breakfast as an early-meal treat before managing the day’s operations at the market. The meal was neither rushed nor overly elaborate, but it reflected his practical, home-style style. It served as a quiet pause after a busy Saturday and helped him begin his day refreshed and ready for customer service.
