At The Willow Market and Pharmacy, Tom Moreno prepared a hearty bowl of oatmeal with cinnamon, a sliced apple, a dash of honey, and a sprinkle of walnuts. He used a large serving bowl and a small whisk to gently fold the oats into the warm water, then added the fruit and nuts to complete the meal.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of oats, cinnamon, and the gentle sweetness of honey. Tom arranged the oatmeal in the center of the bowl, with the apple and walnuts resting beside it, while a small cinnamon stick added a subtle visual hint.

The occasion was a relaxed Saturday breakfast. Tom took a few seconds to check the serving bowl and tasted the oatmeal before serving it, then helped a customer who needed a small cup of coffee. The meal felt simple and comforting, a familiar part of his workday at The Willow Market.
