Tom prepared a hearty Oatmeal Breakfast Bowl for breakfast. He combined rolled oats, milk or a plant-based milk, honey, chopped walnuts, and a pinch of cinnamon in a ceramic bowl. He stirred the mixture until the oats were soft and the nuts were evenly coated, then served it with sliced apple and a small side of whole-grain toast.

The preparation was quick and comforting: Tom measured the oats, heated the liquid, and layered the toppings while the mixture was still warm. He added one or two cinnamon sticks for aroma, then stirred carefully to avoid burning the oats. The dish had a warm, nutty fragrance with hints of honey, cinnamon, and toasted nuts.

It was presented in a bright white bowl, with the golden oatmeal contrasting against the red apple, green apple slices, and scattered dark walnuts. The meal was simple, satisfying, and meant for a quiet Saturday morning. No special occasion had been planned, but Tom was proud of the balanced, nourishing breakfast.
