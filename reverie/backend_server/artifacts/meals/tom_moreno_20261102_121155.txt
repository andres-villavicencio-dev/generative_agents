Tom prepared Honey-Butter Crostini with Grapes, a simple, hearty breakfast for his Saturday morning. He sliced sturdy white bread into thin rounds, brushed each piece with a generous coating of softened butter, and sprinkled them with brown sugar. He arranged the toasted bread in a shallow bowl, added a small handful of plump red grapes, and drizzled the remaining honey over the top.

The kitchen filled with the warm, buttery aroma of toasted bread mingling with the sweet, floral scent of honey. Tom arranged the crostini in a neat row in the center of the bowl, with the grapes spaced between them for a colorful, inviting presentation. His occasion was a relaxed Saturday breakfast at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, where he was checking prices, helping customers, and preparing to manage the day’s operations.

He ate it calmly and with satisfaction, using a wooden fork and finishing the last crumbs without making a fuss. His rude but polite demeanor remained unchanged—he ate the remaining toast with a piece of bread in his hand—but the meal itself was gentle, satisfying, and perfectly suited to the quiet Saturday morning.
