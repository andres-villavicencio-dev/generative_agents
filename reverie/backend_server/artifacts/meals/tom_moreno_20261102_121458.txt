Tom prepared a hearty breakfast of Scrambled Eggs and Toast with Tomato Basil. The eggs were cracked into a bowl, seasoned with salt and pepper, and scrambled until fluffy. Sliced bread was toasted on a pan, then topped with fresh tomato slices, chopped basil, and a drizzle of olive oil. A small glass of water was set beside the plate as Tom sipped it, finishing the last of his meal.

The aroma of toasted bread, savory egg, basil, and tomatoes filled the kitchen, while the warm eggs and crisp toast created a comforting, rustic breakfast. Tom presented it in a simple, uncluttered dish with a small plate of nuts and a slice of orange beside the toast.

It was a quiet Saturday breakfast, the kind he prepared while managing the day-to-day operations at The Willow Market and Pharmacy. He had no confirmed appointments, so the meal was simply a pause in his busy routine—a familiar, satisfying start to his day.
