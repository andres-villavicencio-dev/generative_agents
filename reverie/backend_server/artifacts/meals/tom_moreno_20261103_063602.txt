Tom prepared Spaghetti Carbonara for dinner, a quick, comforting pasta dish suited to his busy Saturday. He used fresh spaghetti, high-quality Italian eggs, Pecorino Romano cheese, guanciale or pancetta, black pepper, garlic, and a touch of olive oil.

In a large pan, he browned the meat until crisp, then added the garlic and a little oil. He sautéed the noodles briefly, then added the eggs and cheese, tossing everything quickly so the pasta absorbed the creamy sauce without becoming mushy. He finished it with a few black pepper flakes and a drizzle of good-quality olive oil.

The kitchen filled with the rich, savory aroma of cured pork, toasted cheese, and garlic. Tom plated the spaghetti generously over warm fettuccine, topped with the rich sauce, and served it in a simple, rustic dish. He ate it with a fork, taking small bites as he enjoyed the warmth and satisfaction of the meal.

The occasion was a relaxed, home-cooked evening at The Willow Market and Pharmacy, where he balanced his grocery shop duties with a satisfying dinner. The meal felt informal yet rewarding, giving him a moment of comfort after a long day.
