Tom prepared a hearty Garlic Butter Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for dinner. He began by sautéing chopped carrots, celery, onions, and bell peppers in olive oil, then added a tablespoon of olive oil for extra richness. After searing a chicken breast until golden, he transferred it to a pan with the vegetables and simmered everything with garlic, rosemary, salt, and pepper until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were caramelized.

He topped the dish with a rich garlic butter sauce and served it over white rice. The meal had a warm, savory aroma of garlic, herbs, and roasted vegetables, with the golden-brown chicken adding a hint of nuttiness. It was presented in a simple white ceramic bowl with the rice neatly arranged around it, garnished with chopped parsley and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

The occasion was a quiet, informal evening after a long day of managing the store and dealing with thirst and discomfort. Tom ate it without much ceremony, content to enjoy a simple, comforting meal with a glass of water.
