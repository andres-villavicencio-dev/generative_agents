Tom prepared Rustic Roast Chicken with Root Vegetables. The main dish was a whole chicken seasoned with rosemary, garlic, lemon, salt, and pepper, roasted until the skin was golden and the meat was tender. It was served with roasted carrots, parsnips, and a small side of creamy mashed potatoes.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of garlic and herb-roasted skin, followed by the warm, savory scent of the potatoes. Tom arranged the chicken in the center of the table, with the root vegetables and potatoes arranged around it. He topped the dish with a simple lemon-herb jus and garnished it with a few fresh rosemary sprigs.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday dinner, likely for Tom and Jane. He cooked it himself after reviewing the shopping list and checking the bulletin board, carefully applying the recipe to satisfy his hunger. The meal reflected his calm, methodical side—simple, comforting, and ready to be shared.
