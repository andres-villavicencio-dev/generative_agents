On Tuesday, February 14, Tom Moreno prepared Tomato Basil Chicken with Herbs and Garlic Bread for Jane and Theo. The meal featured tender chicken thighs marinated in olive oil, garlic, parsley, and dried oregano, cooked until golden and slightly crispy, served over a bed of roasted potatoes and carrots. Fresh basil and a generous drizzle of balsamic glaze finished the dish.

The kitchen smelled of savory garlic, caramelized onions, and warm herbs. Tom arranged the food in a large platter, arranging the chicken in the center, potatoes and carrots around it, and garnishing the sides with herbs. A plate of buttery garlic bread sat beside it, with a small bowl of lemonade nearby. The presentation was cheerful and rustic, fitting a relaxed Tuesday dinner.

The occasion was a family meal at home, with Tom helping his children cook. The meal was warm, hearty, and full of shared laughter—especially when Tom shared his recipe with Jane and Theo. It was a simple but satisfying dinner, reflecting the relaxed, supportive atmosphere of his family life.
