Tom prepared Chicken and Root Vegetable Stir-Fry, a hearty family dinner for his wife Jane. The key ingredients were bone-in chicken thighs, carrots, baby potatoes, bell peppers, onions, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, and a handful of fresh cilantro.

Tom marinated the chicken in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and a splash of water, then seared it until the skin was crispy and the chicken was just cooked through. He stir-fried the vegetables with the chicken, adding the sauce toward the end so everything retained its crunch. The aroma filled the kitchen with savory garlic, caramelized onion, toasted sesame, and the savory scent of the chicken.

The plated dish was arranged in a colorful bowl: the bright orange and red vegetables stood around the glossy, golden chicken, with the cilantro adding a fresh green touch. It was presented for an early Monday dinner after his shift, with Jane watching from across the table.
