For their Monday dinner, Tom prepared Grilled Chicken with Lemon Butter Sauce and Roasted Root Vegetables. The meal was simple, comforting, and made to satisfy his wife Jane without making her feel like he was doing it for her.

Tom chose a whole chicken, skin-on and generously rubbed with olive oil, garlic, salt, black pepper, and dried oregano. While the chicken roasting pan settled in the oven, he sliced carrots, parsnips, and onions into a baking dish. He roasted everything until the vegetables were tender and slightly caramelized, then set the chicken aside to finish. He brushed the chicken with the lemon-butter sauce—melted butter, lemon juice, garlic, and a pinch of paprika—and grilled it until the skin was crisp and the juices ran clear.

The aroma was warm and savory: roasted garlic, herbs, citrus zest, and the rich smell of butter coating every surface. Tom plated the chicken on a white platter, arranged the vegetables in a rustic bowl beside it, and added a small bowl of lemon-herb sauce to garnish. He served it with plain brown bread, a few crisp apples, and a glass of red wine.

It was a relaxed, home-style dinner for Tom and Jane, made with care and enough care not to make her feel like she was being served.
