Tom prepared a hearty Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables and Herb Glaze for dinner. The meal consisted of a whole chicken seasoned with garlic, rosemary, thyme, lemon, and olive oil, alongside carrots, potatoes, and Brussels sprouts glazed in the same aromatic sauce. Tom roasted everything on a large skillet, then finished it in the oven until the chicken was golden, juicy, and falling apart at the bone.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and caramelized onions. The vegetables glistened with oil and herbs, while the chicken glowed with golden skin. He plated the dish in a rustic ceramic bowl, arranging the vegetables around the chicken and drizzling the glaze over the top.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday dinner, though Tom had no specific occasion in mind. As he ate at home, he reflected on a full, purposeful day spent helping customers, supporting Jane, and preparing for his early walk on the neighborhood election. He was tired but satisfied, grateful for the simple comfort of the meal.
