Tom prepared Tom’s Classic Morning Gravy for breakfast. The dish used pan-dried beef, chicken breast, onions, carrots, potatoes, celery, bell pepper, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, butter, garlic, and water, with fresh herbs added for brightness.

He sliced the meat, chopped the vegetables, and sautéed them in a pan with the onions and carrots until crisp and slightly browned. He then added the remaining vegetables, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, butter, garlic, and water, stirring until everything melted into a rich, golden gravy.

The aroma was warm, savory, and slightly sweet, with hints of garlic, beef, caramelized onions, and herbs. He plated the meal in a shallow ceramic dish, arranging the golden gravy over the cooked meat and vegetables, garnishing it with a sprinkle of herbs and a lemon wedge.

He made it for a leisurely Monday breakfast at home, sharing the meal with his wife Jane. The simple, hearty breakfast felt comforting and familiar, and he was pleased to see how well the flavors complemented one another.
