Tom prepared a hearty breakfast of oatmeal with cinnamon, a bowl of warm milk, and fresh berries for his wife, Jane. The oatmeal was cooked with rolled oats, cinnamon, a pinch of nutmeg, a few dried fruits, and a splash of milk until the grains softened. Jane added sliced bananas and a handful of fresh strawberries before the final warm milk.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of oats, cinnamon, and roasted nuts. Tom presented the meal in a simple white bowl, arranging the berries in a neat circle around the oatmeal and garnishing it with a few fresh leaves. It was a warm, inviting breakfast, prepared for a quiet morning meal before Tom headed to work.
