The meal Tom prepared was a hearty oatmeal breakfast with cinnamon and a glass of whole milk. He stirred oats into a pot of water, added a pinch of cinnamon, and let the mixture cook until soft and slightly creamy. He stirred in a splash of milk, then topped it with a little sugar and a cinnamon stick for aroma.

The kitchen smelled like warm oats, brown sugar, and cinnamon. Tom presented the steaming bowl in a simple white mug, with a spoon resting beside it and a small cinnamon stick on the table. It was a quiet, comforting breakfast for a busy Monday morning.

The occasion was a calm Tuesday at The Willow Market, where Tom was preparing for his wife Jane’s day. After the store opened at 8:00 AM, he stood behind the grocery counter, making coffee, preparing oatmeal, and assisting customers. By 11:00 AM, he was heading home to eat dinner with Jane, winding down after a long day.
