Tom prepared a hearty Tomato, Avocado, and Feta Oatmeal Bowl, a familiar breakfast for his wife, Jane. The bowl contained rolled oats, cooked banana, chopped strawberries, crumbled feta cheese, toasted almonds, and a drizzle of honey. He added a few slices of whole-grain toast and a small cup of cold coffee, then set the dish on the kitchen counter with a clear glass.

He mixed the oats and fruit in a large pan, then cooked them over low heat with a splash of milk and a pinch of cinnamon. The warm mixture was transferred to a serving bowl, where the crisp feta and sweet fruit contrasted with the creamy oat texture. A bright red tomato and green avocado added a pop of color, while the honey finished the dish with a subtle golden sheen.

The aroma of cinnamon, honey, warm oats, ripe fruit, and toasted nuts filled the kitchen. Tom presented the bowl as if it were the centerpiece of a relaxed Sunday gathering, with his wife Jane and a few other family members nearby. The meal was meant to be comforting and cheerful, reflecting the calm routine of his day: starting the market, helping customers, and ending with breakfast and dinner together.
