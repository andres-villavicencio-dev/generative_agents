Tom prepared a simple, inexpensive breakfast called Tom’s Quick Pancakes for Jane. He used all-purpose flour, eggs, milk, a little oil, and a pinch of salt, making the pancakes fluffy and filling. He cooked them on a skillet over medium heat, flipping them once until they were golden brown and slightly crisp around the edges. The kitchen filled with the aroma of butter, egg, toasted flour, and warm pancakes.

He served the pancakes with a glass of milk, a slice of buttered toast, and a small bowl of fruit. The breakfast was presented in a simple white dish, with the pancakes arranged neatly and the toppings arranged attractively in the center. It was a quiet Tuesday morning meal, prepared without much fanfare but shared with Jane.
