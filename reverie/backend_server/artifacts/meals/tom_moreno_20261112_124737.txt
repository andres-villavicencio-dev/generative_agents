Tom prepared a hearty Pan-Seared Chicken with Rosemary and Lemon-Herb Roast Vegetables for an early Tuesday dinner. The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic, and rosemary, then pan-seared until golden and crisp on the outside while staying juicy inside. It was served alongside carrots, potatoes, green beans, and a simple lemon-herb sauce.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, herbs, and toasted bread. Tom arranged the vegetables and chicken in a shallow baking dish, adding a drizzle of the pan sauce and sprinkling fresh parsley over the top. The food looked inviting and family-friendly, with the golden chicken contrasting against the bright green vegetables.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday dinner, with no special occasion or guests. Its simple, comforting presentation made it a practical choice for a weekday family meal.
