Tom prepared Garden-Style Chicken and Root Vegetable Soup for a simple, hearty Tuesday dinner. The key ingredients were bone-in chicken breasts, carrots, celery, onions, potatoes, and a handful of spinach. He sautéed the vegetables until softened, then simmered them with chicken broth and salt until the potatoes were tender. He added the spinach at the end to preserve its color, then sliced the cooked chicken into bite-sized pieces and stirred the soup.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of roasted chicken, caramelized onions, and earthy carrots. He served the soup in a large bowl, garnishing it with chopped fresh parsley and a drizzle of olive oil. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the colorful vegetables and golden chicken contrasting against the creamy broth.

The occasion was a relaxed Tuesday evening meal, prepared without haste but with care. It was a practical, comforting way to end the day after managing the store’s daily operations.
