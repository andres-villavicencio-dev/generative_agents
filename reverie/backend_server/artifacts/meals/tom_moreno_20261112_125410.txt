Tom prepared a simple Bread-and-Egg Breakfast Platter for himself and Jane.

Key ingredients: sourdough bread, whole-wheat eggs, cheese, butter, milk, and a few seasonal fruit slices.

Preparation method: Tom toasted the sourdough in a skillet, cracked the eggs, and scrambled them gently with the cheese and butter. He sliced the fruit and arranged everything on a white platter.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with warm bread, nutty egg, melted cheese, and the faint scent of butter.

Presentation: The dish was set on a clean white board with the bread, golden eggs, and fruit arranged in neat rows. A small glass of milk and a folded napkin added a tidy finish.

Occasion: It was a quiet, practical meal for a Tuesday morning—Tom and Jane taking care of themselves before beginning their day.
