Tom prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon Butter Sauce for an early Tuesday dinner with Jane. The meal featured a whole chicken grilled to a golden crisp, served alongside carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, and onions roasted with garlic, olive oil, and rosemary. He finished the dish with a bright lemon-butter sauce and served it over fluffy white rice.

To prepare it, he sliced the chicken into thick pieces, marinated it briefly with olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper, then grilled it over hot coals until the skin was crispy and the meat was cooked through. The vegetables were tossed with olive oil, herbs, and garlic before roasting until tender and caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted vegetables and searing chicken, balanced by the sharp scent of lemon and rosemary. He plated the meal in a large white bowl, surrounded the chicken with crisp vegetables, and drizzled the golden sauce over the top. The presentation was simple, colorful, and inviting, suggesting a warm, family-style dinner rather than a special occasion.
