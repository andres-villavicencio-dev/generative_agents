Tom prepared Grilled Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Garlic Herb Sauce for the family dinner. The centerpiece was a whole chicken with skin-on breast, thighs, and drumsticks, served alongside a colorful mix of bell peppers, zucchini, carrots, and mushrooms.

He marinated the chicken in olive oil, paprika, garlic, lemon zest, and oregano, then grilled it until the skin was crisp and the juices ran clear. The vegetables were roasted in the oven until tender, then arranged around the chicken in a shallow pan. A simple garlic-herb sauce was made by frying garlic and parsley in the pan juices before pouring it over the food.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of garlic, roasted vegetables, and the smoky fragrance of the chicken. Tom plated the meal in a large serving dish, adding a lemon wedge and fresh parsley for a bright final touch. He served it at home for Tuesday’s family dinner, combining warmth with the energetic, friendly atmosphere of their Tuesday.
