Tom prepared Roast Chicken with Herb-Roasted Vegetables on Tuesday, February 14. The meal was simple yet hearty, with a whole chicken seasoned with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a splash of lemon juice. Alongside it were carrots, parsnips, onions, and green beans tossed in olive oil and topped with crispy bread.

Tom preheated the oven, then roasted the chicken and vegetables on a baking sheet until the chicken reached an internal temperature of 165°F. The herbs released their fragrance, filling the kitchen with a warm, savory aroma. He sliced the chicken into generous pieces and arranged the vegetables in a colorful, rustic platter with lemon wedges and a small bowl of olive oil for dipping.

The presentation was informal and family-style, with the chicken prominently displayed and the vegetables arranged around it. Tom served it with whole-grain bread and a glass of wine, making it a casual meal suitable for a Tuesday dinner.
