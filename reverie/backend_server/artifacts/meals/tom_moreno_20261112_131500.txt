The meal began with the chicken marinated in soy sauce and garlic, then pan-fried until golden. He tossed the vegetables in a hot pan, adding the aromatics before stirring everything together. The kitchen filled with the savory smell of garlic and ginger, the crispness of vegetables, and the rich aroma of soy sauce.

Tom presented the dish on a large white plate, arranging the colorful vegetables in a neat circle with the golden chicken on top. A small bowl of steamed rice and a splash of extra soy sauce were included. The meal was prepared for a relaxed Tuesday dinner with Jane, while Tom’s notes left the specific occasion open to the day’s activities.
