Tony prepared a simple Savory Oatmeal Bowl with Fruit and Toasted Pineapple. The dish featured rolled oats cooked with water or milk, topped with sliced bananas, blueberries, chopped walnuts, and a sprinkle of cinnamon. A small piece of toasted whole-grain toast and a few slices of pineapple added a bright pop of color.

Tony cooked the oatmeal in a small non-stick pan over medium heat, stirring until the oats were warm and the pan was dry. He then arranged the ingredients in a clear, low bowl, letting the warm oats and glossy fruit contrast with the golden toast. The aroma of toasted oats, cinnamon, and sweet pineapple filled the small kitchen as he plated it.

The meal was a quiet, comforting breakfast for a Monday morning. He prepared it before checking in with his regular calls and Sam, Jennifer, and his community emergency-preparedness workshop, using the simple ingredients to create a calm, visually appealing meal that balanced his busy schedule with an appetite for comfort.
