Dish Name: Simple Oatmeal Breakfast with Fruit and Toast

Key Ingredients: rolled oats, water or milk, cinnamon, a few berries (blueberries, raspberries, or strawberries), honey or maple syrup, and a small piece of toast.

Preparation: Tony prepared the oatmeal by cooking the oats with water or milk until tender, then stirring in the fruit and a little honey. He toasted a slice of whole-grain toast in a pan, let it cool slightly, and served it alongside the warm bowl. The berries were added at the end to preserve their bright color and juiciness.

Aroma: Sweet, warm, and comforting, with notes of cinnamon, oats, honey, and lightly toasted bread.

Presentation: The breakfast was served in a simple white bowl. The oatmeal was spooned around the fruit, with a small piece of toast placed beside it. A splash of milk was offered separately for those who wanted to adjust the sweetness.

Occasion: This was a quiet Tuesday breakfast, part of Tony’s weekday routine as he prepares for his paramedic certification. The simple meal reflected his calm, unflappable nature and his practical approach to daily life, though the small, cheerful setting added a touch of color to the otherwise purposeful schedule.
