At the Willow Clinic, Tony prepared a simple Creamy Garlic Butter Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a quick, comforting meal to replace the expired bread, eggs, and milk. He used whole chicken breasts, butter, olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, dried thyme, and diced carrots, onions, and bell peppers. After searing the chicken, he sautéed the vegetables briefly, then added butter, garlic, and lemon for a bright finish. The chicken was finished in the oven until the skin was crisp, while the vegetables became tender and caramelized.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, roasted vegetables, and softened butter. Tony plated the meal in a simple white dish, arranging the golden chicken around a mound of colorful vegetables and serving it with a small side of whole-grain toast and orange juice. On a quiet Wednesday afternoon, the meal felt both ordinary and satisfying—proof that a little imagination and careful preparation can make a simple grocery haul feel purposeful.
