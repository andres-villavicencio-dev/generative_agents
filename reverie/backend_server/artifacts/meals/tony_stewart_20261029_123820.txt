Tony prepared Quick Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice Noodles, a simple, colorful lunch inspired by his paramedic background. He sautéed chicken breast, snap peas, carrots, and bell peppers in a little oil, then tossed in pre-cooked rice noodles and a splash of soy sauce and sesame oil. He added a handful of cilantro and chopped green onions before serving over steamed brown rice.

The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of seared chicken, roasted vegetables, and toasted noodles. The presentation was bright and balanced: golden chicken, crisp green vegetables, orange carrots, and yellow peppers arranged around the white rice.

On a Thursday, this quick meal was more than just food—it was a moment of stillness. Tony made it while consciously checking out local ingredients, buying affordable snacks, and choosing something practical for himself. The dish had no pretensions of luxury, and its cheerful colors helped redirect his thoughts from the luxury dream he had carried into his day.
