Tony prepared a quick skillet breakfast of pan-fried eggs, toast, and orange slices, seasoned lightly with olive oil, lemon zest, and a pinch of garlic. In a small nonstick pan, he cooked two eggs, then toasted the toast while slicing the citrus over it. The orange’s bright aroma filled the kitchen, mixing with the savory warmth of the eggs and the clean scent of toasted bread.

The meal was simple, elegant, and satisfying—a practical morning routine. He arranged the eggs and toast neatly on a white plate, garnishing it with sliced orange and a few whole lemon leaves. It was prepared in less than twenty minutes, offering a crisp start to the day without requiring much cleanup.
