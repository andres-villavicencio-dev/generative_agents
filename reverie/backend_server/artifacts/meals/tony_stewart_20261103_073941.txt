Tony prepared Garlic Butter Lemon Chicken with Asparagus and Roasted Potatoes for a simple Friday dinner. He used bone-in chicken breasts marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a little paprika, then seared them until golden. The asparagus was tossed in olive oil, salt, and pepper before roasting alongside the potatoes.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken and garlic, while the bright citrus scent of lemon and the earthy smell of potatoes created a cheerful, homey atmosphere. Tony arranged the golden chicken, crisp vegetables, and fluffy potatoes in a shallow pan with a drizzle of melted butter and fresh herbs. He plated it before a glass of water and a small piece of fruit.

The meal was relaxed and nourishing, suitable for a Saturday night before his final paramedic certification review. It reflected his spontaneous, unflappable nature—he created something comforting quickly, without needing much preparation.
