Tony prepared Grilled Lemon-Herb Chicken with Lemon-Herb Rice and Asparagus. The meal was simple, bright, and suited to a Saturday lunch.

The chicken was breast-shaped, sliced, and marinated with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, dried oregano, and a pinch of salt. It was seasoned on both sides and grilled until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. The rice was cooked with a generous amount of lemon-herb broth, garlic, and fresh herbs, then fluffed into fluffy grains. Asparagus spears were lightly buttered and tossed with the same herb mix before being grilled with the chicken.

As the food came together, the kitchen filled with the savory aroma of garlic and herb-infused oil, the bright scent of lemon zest, and the earthy fragrance of grilled vegetables. The presentation was simple and appealing: bright green asparagus and lemon slices arranged around two golden pieces of chicken and a mound of fluffy lemon-rice. The dish was served at home on Saturday, February 18, in the calm after a busy day of paramedic shifts.
