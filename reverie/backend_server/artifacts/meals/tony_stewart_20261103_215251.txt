Tony prepared Neon Herb Crisps and Lemon-Garlic Toast, a simple, colorful lunch meant to energize the day while fitting his busy schedule. He used two slices of sourdough, crumbled feta, crisp kale, sliced cucumber, red onion, and plenty of lemon slices. The herbs were lightly toasted in the pan, then tossed with lemon juice, salt, pepper, and a pinch of garlic before being served on crisp toast.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of lemon, garlic, and toasted nuts. Tony arranged the crisp salad on a small white plate, topped it with a bright yellow lemon wedge, and placed the toast next to it, creating a cheerful, balanced presentation. It was a quiet, practical meal for an exam day, giving him time to sketch and study before heading into his community emergency-preparedness workshop with Dr. Kibler and Hua Lai.
