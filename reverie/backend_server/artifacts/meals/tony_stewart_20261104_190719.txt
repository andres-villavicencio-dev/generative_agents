Tony prepared a simple but colorful breakfast of Neon Citrus Avocado Toast with a Lemon-Herb Crisp. The dish used a slice of sourdough bread, ripe avocado, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and feta. He topped it with a bright orange salsa, fresh basil, chili flakes, and a drizzle of lemon-tahini dressing. A small crisp of crispy potatoes and corn finished the plate.

He toasted the bread until it was golden and slightly crisp, then sliced the avocado into thin rounds and added the colorful toppings. The warm citrus, creamy avocado, and savory herbs created a refreshing balance. The aroma was bright and inviting—sweet citrus, tangy herbs, a hint of butter, and a faint savory finish.

It was an informal, relaxed breakfast for a quiet morning at home, before Tony continued studying for his paramedic certification and preparing for his community emergency-preparedness workshop with Dr. Chad Kibler and Hua Lai.
