On a Tuesday morning, Tony prepared Smoky Lemon Garlic Potatoes, a quick, comforting side meant to complement the crispy chicken he was reheating. He chopped a yellow onion, sliced a garlic clove, and seasoned them with olive oil, smoked paprika, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. He tossed the vegetables with potatoes and garlic, then roasted them at 400°F (200°C) until the skins were crisp and the potatoes were tender.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic and lemon zest, while the kitchen counter glowed with the vivid orange, red, and green of his fresh herbs and neon paintbrushes nearby. He presented the potatoes on a small white platter, garnished with a sprig of parsley and a bright wedge of lemon.

The meal was part of his community emergency-preparedness workshop, where he was teaching flatmate Dr. Chad Kibler and Hua Lai how to safely prepare portable meals. It was a practical, informal gathering aimed at building practical skills and encouraging collaboration among participants.
