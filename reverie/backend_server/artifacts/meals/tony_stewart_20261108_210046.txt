Tony prepared Neon-Spaghetti with Cherry and Basil Risotto for a quiet Tuesday evening at the Johnson Park supply store. The dish served as a festive, community-friendly meal, echoing the bright colors of the neon mural behind the Willow Clinic.

The key ingredients were Arborio rice, cherry tomatoes, garlic, parmesan cheese, fresh basil, and a splash of lemon juice. Tony cooked the risotto slowly over low heat, stirring the rice until it absorbed the liquid and became tender while the tomatoes softened and released their juices. The aroma was warm, earthy, and bright—a fragrant blend of toasted rice, citrus, garlic, and herbs.

Tony plated the risotto in a simple white bowl, topped with a generous drizzle of cheese, a few whole cherry tomatoes, and a sprig of basil. He added a little lemon juice for brightness and garnished it with a few fresh basil leaves. It was a relaxed, cheerful meal designed to bring people together and encourage conversation during the supply-store gathering.
