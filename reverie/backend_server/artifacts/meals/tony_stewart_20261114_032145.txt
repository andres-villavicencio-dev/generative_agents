Dish Name: Lemon-Herb Scramble with Toasted Toasties and Fresh Fruit

Tony prepared a simple, colorful breakfast on the kitchen counter for his early morning study session. The dish featured eggs, bacon, sliced tomatoes, chives, and a splash of lemon juice, along with toasted whole-grain toast and a few fresh berries.

Tony whisked the eggs, cooked them briefly in a pan, and then sliced the bacon until crisp. He seasoned the scrambled eggs with salt, pepper, and fresh chives, then arranged the eggs over the toast and fruit. The bright red tomatoes and green chives added a vivid contrast to the pale yellow eggs and golden toast.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of butter, bacon, herbs, and lemon. Tony’s unflappable demeanor was evident as he prepared the meal quietly while checking his notes on paramedic certification. The presentation was casual yet inviting, giving the small breakfast a cheerful, almost artistic flair that reflected his creative side. It was a simple, practical breakfast for an early morning, but it felt like the start of a creative day.
