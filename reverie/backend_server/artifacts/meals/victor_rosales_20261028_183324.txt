Victor prepared Carne con Espárragos y Zanahoria (Chopped Chicken with Spinach and Carrots), a slow-cooked Mexican-style breakfast that honored the family’s traditional recipes while adding a refined, polished touch.

The meal was made over an open flame. Victor sliced chicken, carrots, onions, and bell peppers, then added them to a pot filled with chicken broth. He stirred in chopped spinach and a handful of fresh oregano, letting the flavors meld as the chicken cooked slowly for about two hours. The final dish was garnished with fresh parsley and served with a crisp tortilla or cornbread.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized chicken and earthy vegetables, while the gentle scent of oregano and toasted spices carried through the house. Victor presented the colorful meal in a simple white bowl, arranging the carrots and spinach neatly beside the golden chicken. He ate it slowly at his table, pausing to appreciate the flavors and the quiet comfort of the home-cooked breakfast.

The occasion was a warm family gathering, though it was prepared as a peaceful morning breakfast rather than a festive meal. Victor made the dish with great care, reflecting his meticulous nature and devotion to passing down Mexican traditions for the younger generation.
