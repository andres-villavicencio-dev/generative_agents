Victor prepared Carne Asada con Pollo a la Mexicana, a hearty breakfast dish featuring grilled flank steak, chicken, corn tortillas, and fresh vegetables. The meat was seared with oil until it turned golden and slightly charred, then finished with chili oil, cumin, and a hint of garlic. The chicken was marinated in lime juice, onion, and cumin, then pan-seared until the skin became crisp.

The dish was served on a large wooden board with tortillas, sliced avocado, pico de gallo, lettuce, and a side of corn tortillas. A simple bowl of warm corn or beans provided a refreshing contrast. The kitchen smelled of toasted nuts, citrus, chili, and the rich aroma of caramelized meat.

The meal was prepared for Victor’s family’s Día de los Muertos celebration, a tradition honoring deceased loved ones through music, food, and communal gatherings. Victor carefully arranged the colorful presentation, ensuring each component reflected the warmth and care he had spent decades developing. He also prepared the hand-carved trellis for his neighbor Amilcar, continuing his meticulous, disciplined routine of creating something beautiful for others.
