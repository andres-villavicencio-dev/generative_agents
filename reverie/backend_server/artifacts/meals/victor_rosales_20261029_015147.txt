Honey-Cured Eggs and Golden Trellis with Warm Rice

Victor prepared a simple, comforting meal for his neighbour Amilcar’s garden and for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering. The dish features golden, honey-cured eggs poached in the same warm broth used for the trellis, alongside a pan of fluffy, warm rice.

The eggs are poached in a savory tomato and onion broth, while the golden trellis is made by slowly caramelizing honey and cinnamon in a little butter until the edges turn deep amber. The honey-cured eggs are then gently fried to crisp the whites while remaining tender inside. Warm rice is served on the side, and a glass of water accompanies the meal.

The aroma is rich and inviting: honey, caramelized nuts, garlic, onion, and cinnamon mingle with the earthy scent of the trellis. Victor presents the meal with a wooden spoon, plating the eggs, trellis, and rice in neat, rustic layers before filling a glass with water. It is a simple, nourishing celebration of heritage, friendship, and the quiet joy of a well-prepared family meal.
