Dish Name: Honey-Cured Egg Tortilla with Vegetable Panini and Golden Trellis

Victor prepared a hearty, rustic breakfast dinner for the family, combining his woodworking and culinary traditions into one simple yet satisfying meal. The main dish was a honey-cured egg tortilla made with eggs, flour, garlic, and a generous amount of honey, then toasted until the edges turned golden. Victor added chopped red onion, parsley, and a drizzle of olive oil before serving it alongside a panini of bread and fresh vegetables.

The vegetable panini was quick and satisfying, featuring crisp lettuce, diced tomatoes, cucumbers, and a side of roasted peppers. He also prepared a small golden trellis for Amilcar’s garden—carved from reclaimed wood with a lattice design inspired by his work as a retired engineer—but the actual meal was the centerpiece of the day.

The aroma was warm and comforting: toasted eggs, caramelized honey, savory garlic, and the fresh scent of vegetables. The golden trellis added a cheerful, artisanal touch, while the meal’s colors—golden eggs, red tomatoes, green herbs, and earthy bread—made the table look inviting. It was a peaceful Tuesday dinner, chosen for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering, with its balanced ingredients and clear, practical presentation.
