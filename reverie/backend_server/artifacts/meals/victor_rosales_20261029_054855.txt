Victor prepared Honey-Cured Egg Tortillas with Roasted Vegetables, serving the gathering as a warm, celebratory family cookout on Wednesday, February 15. He sourced organic eggs and clover honey, comparing quantities with Tamara Taylor’s version before finalizing the arrangement.

The tortillas were made by boiling the eggs with the honey and clover, then carefully shaping the mixture into thin, crispy layers. The tortillas were toasted in a skillet until golden and slightly crisp, while the roasted vegetables—including carrots, zucchini, bell peppers, and red onions—were seasoned with garlic, cumin, chili, and olive oil. The honey caramelized slightly during the cooking, giving the meal a rich, nutty aroma.

The presentation was simple yet elegant: the golden tortillas were arranged in a colorful mound, surrounded by the bright vegetables and topped with a generous slice of crusty bread. Victor plated the dish before the cookout, considering whether the honey-cured egg tortilla should be the centerpiece. The meal felt intimate and purposeful, combining his meticulous cooking with the familiar Mexican traditions he cherished.
