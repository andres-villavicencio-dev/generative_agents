Victor Rosales prepared Honey-Cured Egg Tortillas with Roasted Vegetables and Crusty Bread for the family cookout. He sourced organic eggs, clover honey, and fresh vegetables, then prepared the tortillas by curing the eggs in a honey-based mixture, shaping them into flat pancakes, and baking them until golden and firm. The roasted vegetables—zucchini, carrots, bell peppers, and tomatoes—were seasoned with chili powder and salt, then arranged alongside the warm tortillas on a platter. A simple crusty bread served as the final accompaniment.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, honeyed scent of curing eggs, caramelized vegetables, and toasted bread. The final presentation featured the golden tortillas arranged in neat rows, the colorful vegetables, and the rustic bread, creating an inviting centerpiece. Victor compared the arrangement with Tamara Taylor’s version, deciding to center the gathering on the honey-cured egg tortilla and fresh produce rather than other ideas.

This meal was part of the Wednesday February 15 family cookout, a day dedicated to sharing food, music, and family traditions while Victor balanced his responsibilities with purposeful, meaningful moments.
