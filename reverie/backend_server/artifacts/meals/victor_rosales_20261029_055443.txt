Victor Rosales prepared Honey-Cured Egg Tortilla with Roasted Vegetables and Crusty Bread for the family’s Wednesday cookout.

The tortilla was made with organic eggs, natural honey, flour, and a pinch of salt, then baked until golden and crisp. He roasted red peppers, tomatoes, onions, and zucchini until tender, seasoning them with garlic, cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika. The vegetables were tossed briefly in the honey-cured egg mixture, then arranged on a large platter alongside crusty bread.

The aroma was warm and savory: sweet honey, toasted garlic, savory smoked pepper, and earthy roasted vegetables mingled with the rich taste of the egg tortilla. Victor presented the dish in a simple wooden bowl with the bread beside it, letting the colorful vegetables and golden tortilla create an inviting centerpiece.

The meal was prepared for the family’s Wednesday cookout, with a focus on fresh ingredients and a comforting, rustic Mexican-inspired table.
