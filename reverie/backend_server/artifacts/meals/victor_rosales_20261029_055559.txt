Victor prepared a honey-cured egg tortilla with roasted vegetables, a warm family breakfast-meal-style dinner for the February 15 cookout. The tortilla is topped with fresh corn, carrots, and green beans, while the sides include crisp roasted potatoes and a colorful medley of roasted vegetables. He used two organic eggs, organic eggs, and clover honey, with the honey infused into the tortillas and added to the vegetable pan before serving.

The preparation was meticulous: the honey was warmed with a pinch of cumin and a splash of water to loosen it, while the vegetables were roasted until charred and tender. Victor arranged the food on a wide platter, surrounded it with a rustic wooden board, and placed a large slice of crusty bread beside the central dish. The aroma was rich and inviting—smoky caramel from the honey, earthy sweetness of the corn, and the savory depth of the roasted vegetables.

The presentation was elegant yet warmly rustic. The golden tortilla stood as the centerpiece, with the vegetables arranged around it and the crusty bread providing a sturdy, textured backdrop. The occasion was a lively family cookout, and Victor’s careful attention to the final arrangement helped him choose the most appealing option for the gathering.
