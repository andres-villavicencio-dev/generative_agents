The meal is a honey-cured egg tortilla and roasted vegetable dish, served with crusty bread. Victor prepared the tortillas by frying them in hot oil until the egg layer set, then brushing them with warm honey and letting them cool. For the vegetable dish, he sautéed carrots, bell peppers, onions, and zucchini in olive oil until tender, then finished them with a little lemon juice and fresh herbs.

The aroma was rich and earthy—honey, garlic, roasted vegetables, and the slightly sweet scent of cured eggs filling the kitchen as Victor arranged the plates. He presented the tortillas on a wide white platter, arranging the colorful vegetables alongside them, with the crusty bread set neatly beside the plates. The gathering was a family cookout, a celebration of Mexican traditions and Victor’s desire to honor Mei’s recipes while balancing the demands of work, mentoring, and family life.
