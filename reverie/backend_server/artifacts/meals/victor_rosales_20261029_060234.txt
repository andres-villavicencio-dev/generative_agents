Victor prepared a hearty family cookout dinner centered on honey-cured egg tortillas, accompanied by roasted vegetables and crusty bread. The tortillas were made from clover honey and eggs, giving them a golden, glossy finish and a fragrant scent of caramelized honey and warm egg. He softened the tortillas, sliced them into rounds, and seasoned them lightly with cumin, salt, and a splash of lime juice.

Roasting vegetables—including carrots, bell peppers, and onions—brought a bright, smoky aroma to the table, while the crusty bread served as a sturdy base. Victor arranged the warm tortillas on a large platter alongside the vegetables and bread, arranging them in an inviting circular pattern.

The meal was prepared for the February 15 family cookout. Though Victor considered centering the gathering around the honey-cured egg tortilla and fresh produce, he chose the more balanced arrangement of vegetables and bread. His meticulous attention to detail, combined with Mei’s supplier plans, gave the cookout a sense of order and care, making it both a celebration and a comforting reminder of his Mexican traditions.
