Victor Rosales prepared a Honey-Cured Egg Tortilla and Roasted Vegetable Dinner, a comforting, family-style meal for the February 15 cookout. The tortilla was made from organic eggs and clover honey, then pan-fried until crisp and golden. Roasted vegetables—including carrots, zucchini, tomatoes, and onions—were seasoned with garlic, cumin, and a pinch of salt, then tossed with the honey-cured eggs. Crusty bread served alongside added fresh produce and the family’s favorite side of black beans completed the spread.

The meal had a warm, savory aroma of caramelized honey, roasted garlic, cumin, and earthy vegetables. Victor arranged the dishes on a large white platter, arranging the honey-cured eggs and fresh produce prominently in the center and grouping the bread, beans, and vegetables around the edges.

This dinner was part of a family cookout celebrating the season’s harvest. Victor’s meticulous preparation and thoughtful arrangement reflected his disciplined nature, while the shared meal brought the family together.
