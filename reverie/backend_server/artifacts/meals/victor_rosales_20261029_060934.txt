Victor prepared Honey-Cured Egg Tortillas and Roasted Vegetables for the family cookout, served with crusty bread. The meal featured organic eggs, clover honey, garlic, onion, bell peppers, tomatoes, and fresh herbs. He first prepared the honey-cured tortillas, then roasted the vegetables until crisp, and warmed the bread.

The process began with a warm bowl of honey, garlic, and onion, where the eggs were gently cooked to the firm-yolk stage. He spread the golden tortillas over the pan, then added the colorful vegetables and drizzled everything with honey. The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of caramelized eggs, roasted peppers, and sweet honey.

Victor presented the dish on a large platter, arranging the tortillas in the center and the vegetables around the edges, with the crusty bread resting beside it. The meal was simple, rustic, and deeply comforting. The cookout’s gathering reflected Victor’s disciplined, meticulous nature, with each ingredient carefully chosen and prepared.
