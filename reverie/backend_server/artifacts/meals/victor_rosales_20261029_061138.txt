Victor prepared a honey-cured egg tortilla with roasted vegetables and crusty bread for the family cookout. The tortilla was made using organic eggs, honey, clover honey, garlic, onion, and a small amount of olive oil. He rolled the eggs into a thin tortilla, seasoned it lightly, and cooked it until crisp around the edges. The roasted vegetables included carrots, sweet potatoes, onions, and bell peppers, arranged in a colorful circle on the dinner plate.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of roasted garlic, sweet honey, and savory vegetable oils. Victor served the warm tortilla alongside the vegetables and a generous portion of the crusty bread, adding a little olive oil and lemon juice. The presentation was simple and rustic: the golden tortilla surrounded by the vibrant vegetables, with the dark honey-cured eggs adding visual contrast.

This meal was part of a lively family cookout and gathering with Mei, Tamara Taylor, and other relatives. Victor had chosen the honey-cured egg tortilla as the centerpiece, inspired by Tamara’s version, and prepared the dish with the same careful attention to proportions and balance he applied to engineering designs.
