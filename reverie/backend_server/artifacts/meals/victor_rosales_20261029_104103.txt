Victor prepared a hearty Mexican family cookout dish called Honey-Cured Egg Tortillas with Roasted Vegetables and Crusty Bread. The tortillas were made with flour, water, and a pinch of salt, baked until firm and golden, then brushed generously with honey and cured egg while still warm. He arranged the tortillas on a platter alongside colorful roasted vegetables—potatoes, carrots, corn, and bell peppers—basted with olive oil, garlic, and cumin. A few crisp crusty bread slices were placed on the side to share.

The meal smelled deeply aromatic: honey, roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and warm spices carried strong, comforting flavors. The golden-brown tortillas glowed under the kitchen light, their edges crisp and the interior soft.

On Thursday, February 16, the dishes were ready for the family cookout. After dinner, Victor planned to compare his final arrangement with Tamara Taylor’s version, decide whether to highlight the honey-cured egg tortilla and fresh produce, and bring the prepared food to guests. The cookout was a cherished occasion where the meticulous preparation of family food, music, and conversation blended with the discipline and creativity of his retired engineering life.
