Victor Rosales prepared a hearty honey-cured egg tortilla with roasted vegetables and crusty bread. The tortilla is made from warm corn dough, seasoned with chili powder, garlic, salt, and a touch of honey, then fried until golden and slightly crisp. He added a colorful medley of roasted sweet potatoes, carrots, zucchini, and corn, seasoned with smoked paprika, cumin, and chili oil. The bread was toasted alongside the vegetables, adding crunch and a savory-sweet contrast.

The kitchen filled with the warm, caramelized aroma of toasted grains, garlic, smoked paprika, and honey. The presentation was rustic and inviting: the golden tortilla rested among the roasted vegetables and toasted bread, arranged on a platter with fresh herbs and a few sprigs of cilantro.

The meal was part of a family cookout on Thursday, February 16, with Victor’s meticulous preparation serving as a joyful tribute to Mexican traditions. He carefully arranged the ingredients to compare with Tamara Taylor’s version, considering whether the honey-cured egg tortilla and fresh produce should be the gathering’s highlight. The meal was comforting, celebratory, and filled with the shared anticipation of the family gathering.
