Victor prepared a honey-cured egg tortilla with roasted vegetables and crusty bread for his family’s Friday cookout. The tortilla is made of flour, eggs, salt, garlic, and chili, then covered in honey and cured overnight. For the main dish, Victor roasted potatoes, bell peppers, onions, and corn until tender, then folded the warm tortillas over them. The crusty bread adds a hearty, buttery finish.

The aroma fills the kitchen with the rich, spicy smoke of the cured egg, honey, caramelized vegetables, and toasted bread. Victor presents the meal in a rustic wooden bowl, surrounded by the colorful produce and arranged neatly on a platter, giving it a warm, inviting appearance. The cookout is a beloved family tradition, and Victor’s meticulous preparation reflects his devotion to preserving Mexican culinary customs while sharing the meal with loved ones.
