Victor prepared a Honey-Cured Egg Tortilla and Fresh Produce Salad, a warm, flavorful side for the Saturday cookout.

The tortilla was made from corn tortillas, warm over a low flame until soft, then topped with a generous amount of honey-cured egg. The eggs were poached and simmered in honey until set, their golden shells and bright yolks giving the dish a rich aroma of toasted nuts, honey, and garlic. He added crisp lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, and finely chopped red onion to create a refreshing crunch, with cilantro and lime juice for brightness.

The presentation was simple and inviting: the tortilla rested on a small white plate, surrounded by neatly arranged fresh produce. Warm honey-golden eggs stood prominently beside the salad, while the bright colors of the vegetables contrasted with the tortilla’s earthy hue.

The occasion was a relaxed family cookout, where Victor was considering whether to emphasize this simple yet elegant side dish. He hoped its warm, fragrant flavors would complement the savory main and provide a familiar comfort for the gathering.
