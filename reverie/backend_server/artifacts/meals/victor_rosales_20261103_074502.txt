On Saturday, February 18, Victor prepared a Honey-Cured Egg Tortilla with Fresh Produce and Garden Veggie Salad for his family’s cookout. The tortillas were made by boiling a whole egg, letting it harden, then scoring the surface and frying it until golden, with the yolk preserved inside. He mixed it with honey, garlic, cumin, coriander, and a little chili for a warm, savory flavor. The salad included crisp tomatoes, cucumbers, lettuce, radishes, and a dressing of lime juice, vinegar, olive oil, and a pinch of salt.

The kitchen filled with the aroma of caramelized garlic, toasted corn, and honey. Victor arranged the tortillas on a platter alongside the colorful salad, garnished with chopped cilantro and a sprig of parsley, and offered them as an inviting centerpiece for the Saturday family gathering. He had prepared the meal to honor his Mexican traditions while considering whether to emphasize the honey-cured egg and fresh produce, balancing his meticulous approach with a family-friendly presentation.
