Victor Rosales prepared Egg Tortillas with Honey-Cured Egg and Fresh Produce for the Family Cookout.

The tortillas were handmade from scratch using warm tortilla dough, which he shaped into large, soft round pieces. They were filled with fresh eggs, honey, onions, and a generous amount of chopped fresh vegetables. He then lightly toasted them until the exterior became crisp, while the interior remained tender and slightly warm.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted dough, caramelized onions, honey, and fresh herbs. He arranged the tortillas on a serving platter, placing the honey-cured egg on top and arranging colorful vegetables around it. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the vibrant produce adding a cheerful touch to the warm, golden-colored dish.

This was part of the family cookout, a cherished Saturday gathering where Victor wanted to honor both his Mexican traditions and his wife’s careful arrangement. By carefully comparing his version with Tamara Taylor’s, he hoped to find the most balanced and delicious choice.
