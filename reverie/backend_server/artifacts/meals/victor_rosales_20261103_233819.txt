Victor prepared Día de los Muertos Tacos de Pollo con Tortilla de Ajo, a warm, family-style dinner for the upcoming Día de los Muertos gathering in his neighborhood. The tacos were made with corn tortillas, seasoned chicken, black beans, diced tomatoes, onions, cilantro, and a little lime juice, topped with crumbled cheese and a warm, creamy tomato sauce. He stirred the sauce gently and served it alongside the tacos, garnishing it with a few sprigs of parsley.

The aroma filled the small kitchen with the rich scent of fried chicken, toasted corn, garlic, and warm spices. Victor plated the meal on a simple white plate, arranging the tacos in neat rows and placing a small bowl of salsa in the center, followed by a folded tortilla and a cup of tea.

The occasion was a quiet, communal celebration honoring the community and the departed. The familiar Mexican flavors brought comfort to Victor, while the familiar rhythm of the songs prepared for the gathering gave the dinner a festive, nostalgic character.
