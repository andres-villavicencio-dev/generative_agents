Victor prepared Tortas de Frutos del Mar, a simple, colorful Mexican breakfast of fruit-based tarts filled with soft custard, topped with toasted almonds and cinnamon. The key ingredients were fresh figs, apricots, pears, and oranges, layered with warm vanilla custard and a drizzle of honey.

He sliced the figs and apricots, mixed the custard with cinnamon and a splash of milk, and placed each tart in a shallow wooden bowl before baking until the custard set and the fruit turned golden. The aroma filled the kitchen with sweet figs, caramelized citrus, warm spices, and lightly toasted almonds.

Victor served the tarts in a ceramic platter, arranging them in a neat spiral with a small bowl of honey and a sprig of rosemary beside them. The meal was meant to be a quiet, nourishing beginning to his Monday, offering a familiar Mexican morning tradition while he continued his work on the hand-carved trellis for Amilcar Rosales and rehearsing traditional songs for his upcoming Día de los Muertos gathering.
