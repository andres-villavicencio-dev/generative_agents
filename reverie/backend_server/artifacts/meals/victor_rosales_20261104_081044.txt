Victor prepared Carne Frita con Salsa Verde, a hearty Mexican breakfast of fried pork with crispy tortillas, black beans, shredded lettuce, tomatoes, avocado, and fresh cilantro. His key ingredients were pork belly, flour tortillas, black beans, corn tortillas, a green salsa made from tomatoes, onions, garlic, cilantro, and lime, and a little sour cream.

He cut the pork into small cubes, pan-fried it until the skin was golden, and then arranged the warm tortillas alongside the beans and vegetables. A generous portion of green salsa was served over the dish, while the remaining tortillas were stacked neatly beside it. The aroma of sizzling pork and warm spices filled the kitchen, with a hint of lime and fresh herbs echoing through the room.

Victor placed the breakfast on a white plate and set it beside his favorite coffee, creating a simple yet inviting meal. It was prepared to celebrate Día de los Muertos, honoring his late wife and family members through familiar flavors, music, and the tradition of sharing food with loved ones.
