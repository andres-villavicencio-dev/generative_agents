Victor prepared a simple, nourishing Mexican Breakfast Bowl, a hearty family meal inspired by traditional breakfasts and used during the upcoming Día de los Muertos gathering.

The bowl included a cup of warm oatmeal, a generous portion of brown rice, a small side of black beans, crisp lettuce, tomato, and a dollop of cream cheese, with fresh orange slices and a pinch of cumin. He cooked the rice and oats in a small pot until the rice was fluffy and the oatmeal was creamy, then simmered the black beans with a little water until tender. The dish was presented in a shallow, white ceramic bowl, with the bright orange fruit and green herbs providing a cheerful contrast to the warm brown tones of the rice and beans.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of toasted oats, earthy brown rice, and warm spices. The meal was simple yet comforting, reflecting Victor’s discipline in combining familiar family traditions with practical, everyday cooking. It was prepared for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering, a time when Victor would share stories, music, and a warm meal with friends and relatives.
