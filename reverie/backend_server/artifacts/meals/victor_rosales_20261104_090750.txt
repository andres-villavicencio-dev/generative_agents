Victor prepared Arroz con Pollo, a hearty Mexican rice-and-chicken breakfast with eggs. The main ingredients were brown rice, chicken thighs, diced onions, carrots, tomatoes, garlic, cumin, paprika, cilantro, and a splash of tomato sauce.

He washed and rinsed the rice, then sautéed the onions, carrots, and garlic in oil until softened. He added the tomatoes and sauce, then simmered everything until the liquid reduced and the rice absorbed the broth. For the eggs, he cracked them into a pan, seasoning them with salt and pepper before frying them until the whites were firm and the yolks remained soft.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of toasted rice, garlic, cumin, and savory chicken. He served the dish in a simple white bowl, arranging the bright orange carrots, green cilantro, and golden chicken alongside the white rice and golden egg yolks. He served it for Día de los Muertos, a family gathering honoring his late mother and grandmother, where the meal shared affection, tradition, and the comfort of home.
