Dish Name: Tostadas con Jamón de Res y Aguacate

Victor prepared two golden slices of toasted corn tortillas with crisp chicken breast, crisp lettuce, and a generous spread of softened butter, garnished with fresh cilantro, sliced tomatoes, and a drizzle of hot honey.

The butter was softened in a small pan until it melted into a rich, golden liquid. Victor warmed the tortillas on the stove, spreading the butter evenly across each side while flipping them to keep them crisp. The chicken was pan-seared until the skin was golden, then shredded and added to the pan with the remaining butter. The lettuce was added last, and the tortillas were arranged in a neat, warm stack.

The aroma filled the kitchen: toasted corn, savory chicken, warm butter, and the subtle sweetness of hot honey. Presenting the breakfast on a simple white plate, Victor added a pinch of cracked pepper and a small sprig of cilantro.

The meal was prepared for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering, offering a warm, comforting lunch that honored his Mexican traditions while encouraging his young mentees to create their own.
