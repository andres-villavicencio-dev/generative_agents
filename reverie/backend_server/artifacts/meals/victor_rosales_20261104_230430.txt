Victor prepared a hearty Día de los Muertos “Pasta con Zanahoria”—a warm Mexican-inspired pasta dish with roasted sweet potato, red onions, garlic, and chili. For the occasion, he used leftover family ingredients and added a pinch of cumin and a hint of cinnamon to honor the gathering without overwhelming the meal.

He chopped the sweet potato into small cubes, sliced the onions, and sautéed them in olive oil until soft. Roasted garlic and chili paste were stirred in, followed by chicken breast and a generous portion of white pasta. The mixture simmered gently until the sauce thickened and the flavors released into each bite.

The aroma rose from the kitchen: roasted sweet potato, warm garlic, earthy chili, and a savory undertone of cumin. Victor presented it in a shallow ceramic bowl, garnishing it with fresh cilantro, a sprinkle of toasted cumin, and a drizzle of olive oil. The meal reflected his meticulous, disciplined style—simple, balanced, and deeply personal—while honoring the family traditions he continues to preserve.
