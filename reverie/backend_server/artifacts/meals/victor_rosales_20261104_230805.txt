Día de los Muertos “Tres Frutos,” a festive Mexican family brunch, prepared on Tuesday, February 14. The meal featured a savory black bean and vegetable salad, grilled chicken with roasted red peppers, corn tortillas, fresh avocado, radishes, cilantro, and lime.

Victor washed the vegetables and prepared the ingredients on the kitchen counter. He sliced the chicken, grilled it briefly, and seasoned the beans and vegetables with cumin, paprika, garlic, and a hint of oregano. The warm tortillas were toasted until crisp, and the toppings were arranged in colorful paper plates, with tortillas, beans, fruit, and a small bowl of salsa.

The kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of roasting peppers, warm corn, toasted tortillas, and savory herbs. Victor served the meal with a cup of hot coffee, and the lively arrangement reflected the cheerful family gathering. The dish was a tribute to Día de los Muertos, blending his disciplined cooking with the warmth of his Mexican heritage and the college’s community spirit.
