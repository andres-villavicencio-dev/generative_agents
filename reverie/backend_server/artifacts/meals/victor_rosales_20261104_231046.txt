Victor prepared Carnitas with Roasted Vegetables, a comforting family Sunday brunch. The dish featured tender, smoky pork chunks, a fragrant salsa verde of finely diced onion, jalapeño, tomato, and cilantro, and a colorful medley of roasted peppers, zucchini, and bell peppers.

He marinated the pork in cumin, garlic, orange juice, and a little vinegar, then browned it in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat. As it cooked, the kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of roasted garlic and warm spices. He added the vegetables to the pan, stirring them until they turned bright orange and crisp, then sliced the pork into bite-sized portions and arranged them on a platter with the vegetables.

The meal was presented in simple, rustic dishes on a warm wooden table, with a small bowl of fresh corn and lime wedges beside it. It was a relaxed, celebratory brunch for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering, reflecting Victor’s devotion to his roots.
