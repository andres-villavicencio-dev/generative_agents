Victor prepared salsón de pollo con vegetales for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering. The dish featured chicken thighs, bell peppers, onions, tomatoes, cilantro, garlic, cumin, oregano, and a splash of lime. He sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until crisp, then browned the chicken and finished it in the pan with the aromatics. The warm, savory scent of roasted chicken mingling with fresh herbs and the sharp tang of lime filled the kitchen.

It was served in a rustic white bowl, garnished with a few roasted peppers, fresh cilantro, and a sprinkling of lime zest. A small paper plate and a traditional sugar-leaf dip completed the festive presentation. Victor had prepared it to share with his family after the workday, honoring the tradition of gathering around the meal to celebrate the departed.
