For Tuesday, February 14, Victor prepared a hearty family breakfast of Mexican-style scrambled eggs and grilled tomatoes with a warm tortilla, accompanied by a cup of black coffee. He gathered the ingredients in the kitchen before washing his hands, using a precise knife to slice the tomatoes and carefully arranging the eggs in a neat, even pattern. The warm tortilla was brushed with olive oil and cooked until crisp, while the eggs were gently folded before being served.

The kitchen filled with the savory scent of frying eggs, caramelized tomatoes, and toasted flour. Victor presented the meal on a simple white plate, placing the golden eggs and bright red tomatoes beside the warm bread and coffee. The occasion was a quiet family gathering, with the simple meal echoing his disciplined, devoted nature and preserving the traditions of Mexican cooking. It was a humble but satisfying breakfast intended to bring the family together in the early hours of the morning.
