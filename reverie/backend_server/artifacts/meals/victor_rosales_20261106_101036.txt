Victor prepared Tostadas de Ajo con Huevos, a simple yet warming breakfast for the family on Tuesday, February 14. The dish featured crisp corn tortillas, generous yellow eggs, a pinch of cumin, a splash of hot water, salt, and a little chili for contrast.

He cracked the eggs into a dry pan, stirred them gently with a wooden spoon, and cooked them in one piece until the whites were crisp and the yolks remained soft. He toasted the tortillas in a second pan until crisp, then served them alongside the scrambled eggs, garnished with fresh cilantro and a drizzle of hot sauce. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted corn, egg, cumin, and chili.

It was an informal family breakfast, prepared for a relaxed Tuesday morning before Victor took the family for a park walk. The meal reflected his disciplined approach to cooking, his devotion to preserving Mexican family traditions, and the thoughtful routine Victor had developed for his retirement.
