Dish: Tamales de Calabaza con Chiles de Poblano

Key ingredients: dried pumpkin, chiles poblano, onion, garlic, cumin, oregano, tomato sauce, corn tortillas, and a dash of chocolate for the filling.

Preparation: Victor arranged the small pumpkin halves in a baking dish and covered them with a warm, savory tomato mixture. He wrapped each one tightly in a corn tortilla, then stacked them in a roasting pan. The pan was placed in the oven at 375°F (190°C) until the tortillas were browned and the pumpkin was tender, about 45 minutes.

Aroma: The kitchen filled with the earthy scent of cinnamon and warm pumpkin, the sharp bite of smoked chiles, and the comforting aroma of toasted corn.

Presentation: Victor placed the golden-topped tamales on a large rectangular platter, arranging them in neat rows. A small plate of sliced chiles, lime wedges, and a cup of cinnamon tea was served beside it.

Occasion: The meal was prepared for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering, a reflective and joyful celebration honoring loved ones with homemade food, songs, and colorful decorations. Victor’s careful preparation and devotion to family traditions make the meal feel both celebratory and deeply meaningful.
