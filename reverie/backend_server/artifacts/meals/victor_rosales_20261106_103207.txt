On Tuesday, February 14, Victor prepared Tostadas con Jamón Ibérico and Aguacate, a traditional Mexican breakfast for his family. He used toasted corn tortillas, sliced fresh jalapeño, diced tomato, feta cheese, ripe avocado, and a generous spread of black olives. He sliced thin strips of black pepper, smoked paprika, and honey over the dish, then arranged the colorful ingredients in a neat, rustic bowl with a small plate of warm tortillas.

The preparation was methodical and precise: the vegetables were washed and sliced to uniform sizes, the tortillas were heated until crisp, and the ingredients were layered carefully without spilling. His meticulous approach created a harmonious scent of toasted nuts, savory peppers, feta, and caramelized spices, while the golden-brown tortillas added a warm aroma of toasted corn and herbs.

The meal was presented on an unadorned wooden table, with the ingredients arranged in a simple yet inviting pattern and the warm tortillas beside it. It was an informal family breakfast meant to encourage conversation and the exchange of memories, fitting into Victor’s disciplined routine before his evening walk and Wednesday’s mentoring sessions.
