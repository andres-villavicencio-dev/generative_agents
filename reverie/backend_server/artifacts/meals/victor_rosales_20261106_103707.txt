Victor prepared Día de los Muertos Butter-Cream Croissan, a family-inspired twist on the classic croissan. The dish featured warm Mexican cheese as the base, butter, sour cream, fresh cilantro, onion, corn, and mildly spicy salsa, topped with sliced tomatoes and a touch of dried oregano.

He began by lightly sautéing the onions and tomato in the pan, then added the butter and cheese to create a fragrant, creamy filling. After simmering briefly until the cheese melted and the mixture thickened, he folded in the corn, sour cream, and fresh herbs. The croissan was assembled on a warm tortilla, garnished with the remaining vegetables, and served with a side of Mexican rice and salsa verde.

Aromatic steam rose from the pan, carrying notes of toasted butter, caramelized onions, and fresh herbs. The dish was presented in a colorful bowl with a rustic paper napkin and a small wooden spoon, reflecting the family’s cheerful, communal style. The meal was prepared for Día de los Muertos, honoring the family tradition of celebrating the departed through music and shared food.
