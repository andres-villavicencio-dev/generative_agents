Victor prepared Tortilla de Pollo con Arándanos y Salsa de Tomate, a simple but heartfelt Mexican breakfast-dinner hybrid. The dish consists of two thin tortillas, seasoned chicken, red bell peppers, onions, tomatoes, garlic, cumin, and dried chili. He sautéed the vegetables and chicken until the meat was golden and the peppers softened, then folded one tortilla over them and warmed the remaining tortilla together with the filling.

The kitchen smelled of toasted garlic, cumin, caramelized peppers, and warm chicken. The presentation was rustic and balanced: the bright red peppers and crisp onions stood out against the soft orange tortilla, with the chicken evenly distributed across its surface.

The meal was prepared for Día de los Muertos and shared with his neighbors, offering a quiet, nourishing tribute to loved ones. Victor’s meticulous preparation and warm hospitality reflected his disciplined nature and devotion to his family’s traditions.
