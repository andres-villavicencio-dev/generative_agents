Victor prepared Carnitas con Queso Fresco and Green Beans, a warm, family-style Mexican dinner for Tuesday, February 14. The dish featured tender pork shoulder marinated in cumin, garlic, oregano, lime juice, and a little vinegar, then slow-roasted until the meat fell off the bone. Fresh green beans, onions, tomatoes, and a generous portion of soft Mexican cheese were added just before serving.

The kitchen filled with a fragrant aroma of toasted garlic, savory pork, citrus, and warm spices. The wooden cutting board held neatly arranged ingredients, while the pan shimmered with golden, caramelized meat and bright green beans. The meal was served in a white ceramic bowl, garnished with chopped cilantro, a squeeze of lime, and a spoonful of grated cheese.

It was a cozy  Día de los Muertos gathering, honoring the family’s heritage and celebrating the warmth of home. Victor had completed the dinner while preparing for the gathering, using the same disciplined, patient approach he applied to woodworking and mentoring.
