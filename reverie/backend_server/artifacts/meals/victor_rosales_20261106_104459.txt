Victor prepared Carnitas con Papas con Queso, a hearty Mexican dinner with slow-cooked pork, potatoes, corn, and melted cheese. He seasoned the meat with cumin, chili powder, garlic, and black pepper, then roasted it in an oven until the exterior was deeply browned and the interior was tender. The leftover pork, potatoes, and corn were simmered with a little water until the vegetables softened, then served with a side of salsa verde and warm tortillas.

The kitchen smelled rich and aromatic—warm cumin, roasted garlic, citrus zest, and the subtle sweetness of the meat mingling with the savory aroma of caramelized onions. On the table, the golden-brown pork rested beside bright yellow potatoes, orange corn, and a creamy white cheese sauce. Victor plated the meal in a simple oval dish, garnishing it with fresh cilantro and a few whole orange slices.

The occasion was a quiet Día de los Muertos gathering in the family home. After sharing traditional songs and stories, the family gathered to eat and celebrate the memory of loved ones, with Victor’s meticulous attention to the meal’s balance of flavors and presentation adding a personal, comforting touch to the celebration.
