Victor prepared Carnitas with Roasted Vegetables and Rice for his family’s Día de los Muertos dinner. The centerpiece was a seasoned, slow-cooked pork cutlet, while the vegetables were roasted with tomatoes, onions, and peppers. He used canned tomatoes, garlic, cumin, paprika, chili powder, and a little olive oil for the meat, adding fresh cilantro and lime at the end. The rice was cooked with vegetable broth and served with tortillas and a small bowl of salsas.

The kitchen filled with the warm, smoky aroma of roasting pork, caramelized onions, and toasted spices. Victor arranged the colorful vegetables and golden meat in a shallow wooden platter, garnishing it with orange and purple flowers. The presentation was both rustic and celebratory, fitting the festive occasion and honoring the family’s traditions.
