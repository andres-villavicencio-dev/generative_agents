Victor prepared Tacos de Guacamole con Pollo Asado, a hearty family dinner for the Tuesday, February 14. His key ingredients included grilled chicken, ripe avocados, fresh cilantro, red onion, lime, and a warm tomato-salsa base. He marinated the chicken with garlic, cumin, oregano, and a little olive oil, then grilled it over a charcoal stove until the skin was crisp and the meat was tender. The chicken was sliced and served alongside the grilled vegetables, guacamole, and salsa.

The kitchen smelled of roasted meat, fresh herbs, and warm spices. Victor arranged the tacos on a white linen tablecloth, garnished them with cilantro, and placed a small plate of fresh fruit beside them. The meal was simple yet unmistakably Mexican, reflecting his devotion to family traditions and his love of good food. It was prepared for an intimate Tuesday dinner, a quiet celebration of home before his evening walk and volunteering.
