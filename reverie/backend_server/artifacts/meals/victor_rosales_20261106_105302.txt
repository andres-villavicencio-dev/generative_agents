Victor prepared tacos al pastor, a traditional Cuban-Mexican dish that brought his family’s culinary traditions to life during Tuesday’s relaxed evening. The meal featured tender pork marinated in onion, garlic, cumin, oregano, and lime, sliced and grilled until the meat warmed through. Thin slices of lettuce, diced tomatoes, onions, and a creamy sauce were arranged over the grilled pork, finished with fresh cilantro and a drizzle of lime juice.

The kitchen filled with the scent of caramelized onions, smoky grilled pork, warm spices, and the bright aroma of fresh herbs. Victor presented the dish on a rustic wooden platter, with the colorful ingredients arranged neatly before him and a few simple garnishes for symmetry. Accompanied by warm tortillas and a glass of red wine, the meal felt both celebratory and intimate—a quiet dinner shared with his family before preparing for the Día de los Muertos gathering.
