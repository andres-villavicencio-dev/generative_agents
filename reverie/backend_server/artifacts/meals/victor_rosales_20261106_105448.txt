Victor prepared Día de los Muertos Tortilla de Salmón, a family-style Mexican breakfast for the upcoming gathering. The dish featured fresh salmon, tomatoes, onions, and cilantro wrapped in tortillas, with a warm salsa and generous portion of roasted plantains as a side.

Victor washed the vegetables and sliced the salmon and plantains carefully, arranging the ingredients on a large wooden cutting board. He cooked the salmon over a gas stove until the skin was crisp, then added the tomatoes, onions, and cilantro to the pan. The kitchen filled with the earthy aroma of roasted fish and the aromatic scent of sautéed onions and herbs.

He served the warm tortillas on a platter, placing the salmon, colorful vegetables, and plantains on top. The presentation was rustic and inviting, echoing the hand-carved trellis Victor had completed for his neighbour. The meal was an intimate family breakfast for the Día de los Muertos gathering, celebrating the beloved spirits with warmth, tradition, and the shared rhythm of home cooking.
