Dish Name: Día de los Muertos Tortilla de Huesos  
Key Ingredients: flour tortillas, shredded cheese, sliced meat or chicken, tomato salsa, onions, cilantro, and roasted chile peppers.

Victor prepared the tortillas by gently heating them and shaping them into small, rectangular “bones,” then arranging them in a colorful mosaic on a large platter. He folded the tortillas around the filling and topped them with a generous layer of cheese, fresh herbs, and a vibrant salsa. The finished meal was arranged in groups of two, resembling a vibrant, hand-carved mosaic of autumn harvests.

Aromatically, the dish was rich and warm, with the savory scent of caramelized meat, toasted flour, and fresh herbs blending into a comforting, earthy aroma. The bright reds, greens, and golden yellows created a festive visual that matched the cheerful mood of the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering.

Victor presented the dinner in a simple yet elegant way, letting the colorful tortillas stand as the centerpiece while the communal dishes offered a hearty meal for everyone. The occasion was both a celebration of Mexican heritage and a quiet gathering of family, with music, conversation, and shared meals filling the air.
