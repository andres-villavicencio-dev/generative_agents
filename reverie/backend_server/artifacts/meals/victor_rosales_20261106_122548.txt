Victor prepared Día de los Muertos Tortillas with Calamari and Salsa Verde, a traditional Mexican breakfast for the family’s gathering. He used two large tortillas, shredded chicken, sliced peppers, onions, tomatoes, black beans, and fresh cilantro, topped with creamy sour cream and a bright green salsa. The tortillas were pan-fried until crisp, the chicken was sautéed quickly, and the vegetables were lightly stir-fried with cumin and paprika.

Aromatic rosemary, garlic, and the tangy salsa filled the kitchen, while the warm, earthy aroma of toasted tortillas and savory chicken mingled with the sharp citrus of the salsa. Victor arranged the food on a wooden platter, placing the tortillas, shredded chicken, colorful vegetables, beans, and garnishes in neat rows. He added a final drizzle of olive oil and a sprinkle of dried oregano before serving.

The meal was prepared for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering, a solemn yet joyful celebration honoring the dead. Victor served it with pride, his meticulous presentation reflecting the devotion and discipline he has brought to every aspect of life.
