Victor prepared Tamales de Zanahoria con Huevos de Olla, a simple yet hearty breakfast for the café reservation. The dish combines corn dough wrapped in banana leaves and filled with roasted carrots, onions, and spices, topped with a rich oiled egg.

He boiled eggs in a copper pot, adding a splash of orange juice and a pinch of cumin for brightness. While the eggs cooked, he filled the dough with the filling, wrapped it around the eggs, and sealed the edges with a little water. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted corn, caramelized onions, and savory spices.

He arranged the tamarind-topped eggs in a shallow dish, garnishing them with a bright yellow lime wedge and a final cumin sprinkle. The presentation was elegant and communal, fitting the Día de los Muertos occasion he had prepared earlier that day.
