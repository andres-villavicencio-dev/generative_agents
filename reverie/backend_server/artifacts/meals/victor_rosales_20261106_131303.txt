Victor prepared Bread and Eggs with Salsa Verde, a simple, hearty breakfast for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering. He cracked six eggs into a lightly oiled bowl and heated a pan of butter until the butter began to brown. He placed the eggs in the pan and cooked them until the whites were set and the yolk remained soft and golden. After removing the eggs, he toasted slices of sourdough bread in the remaining butter. A small bowl of fresh salsa verde, seasoned with garlic, oregano, and chili pepper, was placed beside the pan.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted bread, butter, garlic, and chili. Victor arranged the bread in a wide platter, placed the eggs atop it, and added the salsa with a generous spoon. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the bright green contrast between the eggs and salsa adding a festive touch. He served it at the café reservation on Monday, February 13, and later at the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering, honoring the tradition while enjoying a comforting breakfast.
