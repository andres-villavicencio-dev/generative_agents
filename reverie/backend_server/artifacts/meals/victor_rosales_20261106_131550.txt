On Tuesday, February 14, Victor prepared a simple Mexican-inspired breakfast burrito, a fitting tribute to his family’s traditions. The dish consisted of sliced avocado topped with shredded corn, diced tomatoes, diced onions, diced tomatoes, a few jalapeños, and fresh cilantro, wrapped in warm brown tortillas. He added sliced egg, sliced chorizo, and a generous amount of Mexican cheese, then folded the burrito around a small serving of fresh rice and beans.

He cooked the chorizo in a cast-iron pan over medium heat, letting it crisp before adding the vegetables and cheese. The egg was browned in the same pan, and the rice and beans were warmed together. He placed the burrito on a plate and topped it with the egg and cheese before arranging a small bowl of beans and rice beside it.

The kitchen filled with the aromata of toasted corn, savory chorizo, smoky cheese, fresh herbs, and warm rice. Victor presented the meal with care, using his familiar, disciplined hands to fold and arrange the ingredients. It was prepared for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering, honoring the memories of those who had passed while celebrating their enduring love of food and music.
