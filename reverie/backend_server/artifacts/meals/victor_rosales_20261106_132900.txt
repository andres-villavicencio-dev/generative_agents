Victor prepared Carnitas with Roasted Potatoes and Warm Tortillas, a simple yet hearty Mexican-inspired dinner. He chopped pork shoulder into small cubes, marinated it with cumin, garlic, oregano, lime juice, and a splash of vinegar, then seared it until the edges turned crisp and the meat fell off the bone. He sliced the potatoes in half, roasted them with olive oil, garlic, cumin, and chili powder until browned and tender, and served them alongside warm tortillas.

The kitchen smelled of roasted pork, toasted cumin, garlic, and the earthy aroma of caramelized potatoes. Victor arranged the golden potatoes, tender meat, and soft tortillas on a white plate, placing a small bowl of fresh lime wedges and a spoonful of chopped cilantro beside them. The meal was prepared for a quiet Tuesday evening, honoring his family’s traditions while providing a satisfying, home-cooked meal for his retirement life.
