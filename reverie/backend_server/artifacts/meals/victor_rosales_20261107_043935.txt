Victor prepared Hearty Mexican-Style Oatmeal with Berries and Honey, a comforting breakfast designed to accompany the family’s Tuesday dinner.

The dish began with a pot of warm oatmeal, milk, and cinnamon, while mixed berries—strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, and sliced mango—were added for color and freshness. A drizzle of honey and a few chopped almonds completed the flavor. He stirred it gently over medium heat, allowing the oats to soften without becoming mushy, then covered the pot and let it simmer for a few minutes until the berries were plump.

As it cooked, the kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of toasted nuts, cinnamon, and honey. Victor poured the warm oatmeal into a shallow bowl, arranging the colorful berries in the center and scattered almonds around the edges. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the bright fruit creating an appetizing contrast against the warm oatmeal.

The meal was prepared as part of Theo’s Hearty Breakfast Heist, a family tradition meant to be enjoyed together at the Tuesday dinner. It offered nourishment and a familiar Mexican warmth, fitting Victor’s role as a devoted father, mentor, and keeper of his family’s culinary traditions.
