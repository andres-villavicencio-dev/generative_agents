The meal is Tortilla Hei with Warm Eggs and Roasted Tomato-Apple Compote, prepared for Theo and his family’s Tuesday breakfast afternoons.

Victor uses two large flour tortillas, two eggs, a cup of hot water, a handful of fresh cilantro, green onions, and a small amount of olive oil. The eggs are lightly fried in a pan, then folded into the warm tortillas, which are folded into a neat square. The apple and tomato mixture is cooked until soft, with the apples slightly caramelized, and is served alongside the eggs in a shallow bowl.

The scent fills the kitchen as the tortillas are folded: toasted flour, warm eggs, roasted tomatoes, and the savory aroma of cilantro mingle with the sweet, fruity touch of the apples. The presentation is rustic and inviting—tortillas arranged on a warm wooden board, eggs in a small bowl nearby, the colorful fruit compote beside them, and a small dish of warm salsa.

Theo’s Hearty Breakfast Heist is meant to be a shared, comforting moment before the family dinner. Victor’s meticulous attention to the orderly arrangement reflects his disciplined approach to life, while the simple, nourishing breakfast makes the occasion feel warm, familiar, and celebratory.
