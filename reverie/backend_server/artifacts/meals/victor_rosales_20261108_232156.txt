Dish Name: “Tres Frutos” Fruit Salad with Lemon-Lime Dressing

Victor prepared a colorful, refreshing fruit salad for dinner, combining ripe mango, pineapple, honeydew melon, diced apples, and sliced pears. He added fresh mint, a pinch of cinnamon, and a generous amount of lime juice and honey to balance the sweetness. The ingredients were tossed gently in a bowl, allowing the flavors to mingle without losing their crisp texture.

The dressing was made by whisking lime juice, a little olive oil, honey, and a splash of water until the mixture became light and fragrant. Each piece of fruit was arranged in a shallow bowl, with the dressing spooned over in a generous, glossy layer. The bright green of the mint and pineapple provided a vivid contrast to the warm amber of the honey and golden fruit.

Aromatically, the dish smelled of honey, citrus, pine, and sweet fruit. Victor served it at a quiet Tuesday evening with his family, preparing for Día de los Muertos. The meal was simple, nourishing, and reflective of the disciplined, joyful traditions he cherished, making it the perfect comfort food to finish the day.
