Victor prepared a Roasted Vegetable Breakfast Salad with Warm Tortilla and Avocado Toast. For the meal, he roasted red onions, tomatoes, bell peppers, corn, and red cabbage until their colors brightened, then sliced them for the salad. He toasted whole-grain tortillas with a little olive oil and topped them with avocado, a dollop of sour cream, and a drizzle of hot honey. A simple boiled egg completed the breakfast.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of roasted vegetables and toasted bread. The bright reds, golden yellows, and creamy greens contrasted beautifully in the shallow white bowl. Victor arranged the eggs and tortillas neatly beside the salad, adding a small sprig of parsley for color.

He prepared the meal for Día de los Muertos—the Day of the Dead—gathering the ingredients before the gathering and testing the carvings on the trellis. The careful, orderly presentation honored his Mexican heritage while leaving room for the family’s celebration.
