Victor prepared Roasted Vegetable and Cinnamon Tostada Salad, a rustic side dish to accompany the main family meal. He toasted whole-grain tortillas, then arranged red and yellow bell peppers, carrots, onions, and tomatoes in a shallow pan. He added cumin, chili flakes, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt, then tossed them with olive oil and a splash of vinegar.

As the vegetables roasted, the kitchen filled with the rich aroma of savory caramelized peppers, warm spices, toasted corn, and toasted nuts. Victor served the salad in colorful paper plates, garnishing it with a dusting of chili powder and a final drizzle of lemon-vinegar oil.

The meal was prepared for Día de los Muertos, a celebration honoring his late father and family members. Victor served it with a glass of red wine and a simple corn tortilla, creating a warm, comforting dish that reflected the family’s Mexican heritage. The vibrant colors of the vegetables, the golden toasted tortillas, and the warm cinnamon-spice aroma made the table feel both festive and homey.
