Victor prepared Tortillas de Pollo con Enchiladas de Guacamola. For the breakfast, he used long-grain Mexican rice, cooked with brown sugar, cumin, and a splash of water until the grains were tender and slightly caramelized, then let them cool before assembling the meal. The tortillas were warm, folded around the rice, and topped with shredded chicken, fresh cilantro, chili powder, lime, and a generous amount of guacamole.

Virtually finished, the dish filled the kitchen with the rich aroma of toasted chicken, toasted cumin, and the earthy fragrance of garlic and lime. Victor arranged the colorful plates in the family dining room, placing the warm rice and crispy chicken on the left, while the bright green avocado and warm tortillas stood together in the center. The meal was prepared for a Día de los Muertos gathering, a traditional celebration honoring loved ones with vibrant colors, sweet and savory flavors, and music.
