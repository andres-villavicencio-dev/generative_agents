Victor prepared Tostada con Fríjolero, a hearty Mexican breakfast with grilled chicken, roasted vegetables, and a spicy bean relish. The dish featured chicken cut into bite-sized pieces, corn tortillas, cabbage, carrots, and peppers, along with small green and red chilies, garlic, cumin, oregano, onion, and tomato.

Victor sautéed the vegetables in olive oil until crisp-tender, then added the chicken to the pan and cooked it over medium heat until the skin turned golden brown. He finished the dish with a generous portion of black beans, tomatoes, and a dash of lime. The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of caramelized chicken and toasted spices, while the warm, earthy vegetables offered a balanced crunch.

Victor served the meal on a simple wooden platter surrounded by colorful garnishes and warm tortillas. It was presented for Día de los Muertos, a traditional family gathering celebrating the memory of loved ones. The comforting meal reflected Victor’s disciplined approach to cooking: precise, traditional, and deeply connected to his Mexican heritage.
