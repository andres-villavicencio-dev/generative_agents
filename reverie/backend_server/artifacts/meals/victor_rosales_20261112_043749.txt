Victor Rosales prepared Carne Fritta con Pollo a la Mexicana, a hearty Mexican-style breakfast for his family.

The meal consisted of crispy, caramelized ground beef and chicken with red onion, cilantro, garlic, and a mild tomato sauce. He added fluffy white rice, diced tomatoes, and fresh corn, then topped the dish with a crisp tortilla and a generous drizzle of creamy cheese.

Victor cooked the meat in a hot skillet, letting it brown until the edges were crisp, then stirred in the tomatoes and sauce for about five minutes. He chopped the vegetables, sliced the rice, and gently warmed everything together, ensuring the flavors stayed harmonious without becoming overly salty.

The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of garlic, toasted meat, and caramelized onions. He arranged the rice in a warm bowl, placed the colorful vegetables and cheese on top, and topped it with a golden tortilla. The meal was ready for breakfast on a cold Monday morning, a simple but comforting way to prepare lunch and honor the family’s Mexican traditions.
