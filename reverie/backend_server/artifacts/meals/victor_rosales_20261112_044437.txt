Dish Name: Tortillas de Salsa y Lechuga (Sliced Tortillas with Tomatillo Salsa and Leafy Salad)

Victor prepared two thin tortillas, buttering one lightly and slicing it with a thin, knife-shaped knife. He used the remaining tortilla to scoop a bright red salsa made of tomatoes, tomatillos, chili peppers, lime, and a touch of cumin. The crisp green lettuce, radish, and a small portion of cooked rice were arranged beside the tortilla.

The meal was prepared over a small gas stove. The tortilla was warmed in the pan until it became soft and pliable, then Victor added the salsa and folded the lettuce over, letting the flavors meld. He served it on a large white plate, adding a sprig of cilantro and a dusting of chili flakes for contrast.

The aroma of warm tortillas, ripe tomatoes, and warm chili filled the small kitchen. The simple, earthy colors contrasted with the vivid red salsa, while the crisp green lettuce added texture. Victor presented the dish with a calm, proud expression, as if serving a family tradition.

The meal was prepared for his neighbor’s garden trellis project while he gathered for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering. It was a humble, comforting lunch meant to bring comfort, shared memory, and the familiar flavors of his Mexican heritage.
