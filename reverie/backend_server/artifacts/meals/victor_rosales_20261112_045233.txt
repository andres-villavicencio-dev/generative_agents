Dish Name: Lentejas con Pollo y Arroz de Campo (Lentils with Chicken and Farm Rice)

Victor prepared a hearty, rustic Mexican lunch for the family’s Día de los Muertos gathering. The dish features slow-cooked chicken seasoned with salt, black pepper, cumin, paprika, oregano, chili powder, and garlic, alongside red lentils, chopped tomatoes, onions, and fresh herbs. The rice was prepared using a traditional arroz a la mexicana method: rice was rinsed, seasoned with salt and oil, then cooked until it remained slightly tender.

The cooking process began with searing the chicken until the skin turned golden, then adding the remaining ingredients and simmering everything together for about an hour until the lentils were soft and the chicken was tender. As it finished, the aroma of warm spices, roasted chicken, and earthy lentils filled the kitchen.

For presentation, the chicken was sliced and arranged over the rice, with a small bowl of extra virgin olive oil, a sprig of fresh parsley, and a few crushed dried chili peppers placed beside the bowl. The simple, colorful arrangement reflected Victor’s disciplined attention to detail: every ingredient was measured, every ingredient was evenly distributed, and the final plate remained clean and inviting.

The meal served a warm, communal meal for the family, celebrating the rich cultural traditions of Mexico while honoring Victor’s decades of engineering and woodworking.
