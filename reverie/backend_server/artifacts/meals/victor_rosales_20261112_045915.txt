Victor prepared Fajitas de Pollo y Calabacitas with a warm Zesty Lime Vinaigrette. The meal featured tender chicken thighs marinated in lime and garlic, crisp red and yellow bell peppers, baby bok choy, cucumber, and onions, all arranged on a wooden serving platter. Fresh cilantro and shredded cabbage were added at the end to keep the salad crisp.

The dressing was made by whisking olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, vinegar, and a pinch of cumin until the oil emulsified. As Victor mixed the salad with the dressing, the kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of garlic, citrus, and warm spices. He arranged the colorful vegetables in neat rows, garnished them with whole cloves and a few fresh basil leaves, then added the golden chicken and a sprinkle of chili flakes.

The meal was prepared for Día de los Muertos, a family gathering honoring the deceased with traditional food, music, and storytelling. Victor’s meticulous approach reflected his disciplined nature, while the warm colors and communal presentation created a joyful, nostalgic atmosphere.
