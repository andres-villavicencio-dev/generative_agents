Victor prepared Tres Leches con Fruta de la Ciudad de México, a traditional Mexican dessert of rich milk, sweetened condensed milk, and fresh fruit topped with a caramelized sugar crust. Key ingredients included shredded chicken, pineapple, mango, peach, orange, and a small amount of cinnamon, while the caramel was made from browned butter and sugar.

The preparation began with carefully slicing and arranging the fruit, followed by folding the shredded chicken into a warm tortilla. The tortilla was layered over the fruit and soaked in the mixture of milk, condensed milk, and cinnamon. After baking until lightly golden, Victor brushed the top with a thick caramel sauce. He finished the dessert by dusting the caramel with a little cinnamon and a dusting of powdered sugar.

Aromatic, warm, and sweet, the aroma of caramel, cinnamon, pineapple, and fresh fruit filled the kitchen. The dessert was presented in a white ceramic bowl, surrounded by a colorful fruit arrangement and a few decorative marigolds. It was served for Día de los Muertos, a day honoring the departed, inviting the family to gather and celebrate in honor of Victor and his loved ones.
