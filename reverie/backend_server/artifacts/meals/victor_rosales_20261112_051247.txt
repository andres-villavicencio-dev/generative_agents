Dish Name: Cocido de Pollo con Arroz y Tortillas de Ajarro

Victor prepared a hearty Mexican lunch featuring chicken cocido—a slow-cooked stew of chicken, potatoes, carrots, and cabbage—served over long-grain rice with a side of fresh lettuce, tomatoes, onions, and a drizzle of lime-herb sauce. He finished the meal with warm ajarro, a Mexican egg-and-onion breakfast sandwich.

To prepare the dish, Victor cooked the chicken and vegetables in a large pot with water and salt until the meat was tender and the sauce reduced to a glossy brown. The rice was seasoned with cumin, chili powder, and garlic, then simmered alongside the stew. He arranged the rice and chicken in a shallow bowl, sprinkled the fresh salad over the top, and placed several tortillas on the side.

The aroma was unmistakably Mexican: warm cumin, roasted garlic, savory potatoes, and the earthy scent of cooking chicken. The meal was simple and comforting, presented with the generous, communal style of home cooking. It was served for dinner on Monday, February 13, while Victor prepared for Día de los Muertos, a family gathering honoring the departed and celebrating life.
