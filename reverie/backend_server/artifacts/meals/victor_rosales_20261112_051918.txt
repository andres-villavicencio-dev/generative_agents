Victor prepared Tortilla de Pechuga con Verduras y Queso, a hearty Mexican breakfast served with fresh tomatoes, onions, cilantro, and a warm tortilla. His wife, Maria, has been helping him prepare the meat, which he carefully slices into thin strips and places in the pan. The pan is covered to allow the meat to cook gently, releasing its rich, savory aroma. A little oil and salt are stirred in, and the tortilla is warmed until it is crisp.

The dish is presented on a simple white ceramic plate, with the golden-brown meat arranged neatly beside the vegetables and cheese. Fresh green herbs and a few bright red tomato wedges add a vivid color contrast. The warm tortilla serves as the foundation, holding everything together with a comforting rustic appearance.

The meal was prepared for Día de los Muertos, celebrating the day of the deceased. Its warm, nostalgic aroma and cheerful presentation honor the family’s tradition while leaving room for reflection.
