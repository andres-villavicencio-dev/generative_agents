Victor prepared Tortillería de la Abuela, a warm Mexican breakfast with corn tortillas, cheese, and savory scrambled eggs, served with a glass of fresh orange juice and a side of roasted sweet potatoes.

The ingredients came from his grandmother’s kitchen: masa tortillas, cotija cheese, eggs, shredded cheese, and diced vegetables. Victor first warmed the tortillas on the stove, then cooked the eggs in a skillet until set, adding a small pan of vegetable stock to create a rich, savory scramble. The roasted sweet potatoes were seasoned with cumin, paprika, and a pinch of salt before being garnished with grated cheese and fresh chili.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of toasted tortillas, caramelized onions, citrus, and warm spices. Victor arranged the plates in an orderly row, arranging the tortillas in the center, the golden eggs around them, and the sweet potatoes beside them. The simple, homey presentation reflected his meticulous approach to both cooking and woodworking, creating a comforting meal for his family’s Día de los Muertos gathering.
