Victor prepared Día de los Muertos “Café con Cítricos”—a warm, festive Mexican lunch meant to accompany the family’s gathering. The main dish was a bowl of chocolate concha flavored with orange and lime zest, served over rice, cinnamon sticks, sugar, and chocolate chips. A tamales with savory ham and cheese, plus a small mole for the guests, were arranged on platters.

He first wiped down the counter, checking each knife and bowl before organizing the remaining ingredients. He toasted the cinnamon sticks, then prepared the chocolate concha in the microwave, carefully arranging the citrus zest and seeds among the chips. The aroma rose with warm cinnamon, bright orange, and sweet sugar. The presentation was simple yet inviting: the platters were placed side by side, with the tamales arranged neatly beside the dessert and drinks.

Victor made the meal for Día de los Muertos, celebrating his friend Sam Moore’s birthday and honoring the family’s traditions through food, song, and music.
