Victor prepared Carne con Zanahorias y Calabacitas with a warm chocolate-caramel sauce. The meal began with a pot of chicken thighs, onions, and bell peppers sautéed until the chicken was tender and the vegetables were softened. He added the chicken to a pot of water and simmered it until cooked through, then transferred the stew to a serving dish.

In the same pan, he roasted sweet potatoes, carrots, and green beans until they were caramelized. He finished the dish with a rich chocolate sauce, garnishing it with fresh cilantro, lime, and a pinch of chili flakes. The aroma was warm, savory, and slightly sweet—rich chicken, caramelized vegetables, and earthy spices filling the kitchen.

Victor presented the meal on a deep, ornate platter, arranging the colorful vegetables beneath the golden chicken and sauce in a rustic, family-style manner. He paired it with crusty Mexican bread and a glass of red wine for his neighbor Amilcar’s garden trellis and the upcoming Día de los Muertos gathering.
