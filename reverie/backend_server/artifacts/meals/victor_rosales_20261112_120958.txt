Victor prepared Café con Lechón and Tortillas for breakfast. His breakfast table was set with a bowl of warm chardonnay, fresh tortillas, sliced avocado, roasted sweet potato, and a small plate of cinnamon sugar. The chicken was marinated in olive oil, garlic, onion, and smoked paprika, then roasted until golden and juicy. It was torn into thin strips and simmered briefly in the broth, then served alongside the crisp tortillas.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of roasted chicken, warm herbs, toasted nuts, and the rich scent of coffee. Victor arranged the food neatly on a white plate, adding a sprig of cilantro and a drizzle of honey for the final presentation. He ate it at home on Tuesday, February 14, preparing to meet Eddy Lin after his café shift and complete the Morning Garage Breakfast application. The quiet morning meal was part of his balanced routine, combining his meticulous woodworking with the comforting, traditional Mexican traditions he has kept alive through his family’s celebrations.
