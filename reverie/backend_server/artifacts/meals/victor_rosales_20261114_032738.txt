Dish Name: Crispy Vegetable-Spinach Pan-Fry with Toasted Garlic and Coffee

Victor prepared a simple yet satisfying breakfast using the remaining vegetable scraps from his cooking. He sliced them into thin strips and gently pan-fried them in a little olive oil until they turned golden and slightly crisp. He added a handful of fresh spinach, toasted garlic, and a pinch of red pepper flakes, then folded everything over before serving with a cup of black coffee.

The pan-fry released a warm aroma of roasted garlic, earthy greens, and savory spices that made the kitchen feel inviting. The toasted garlic added a fragrant, slightly nutty note, while the crisp vegetable strips provided a satisfying crunch. The spinach added a bright, mild freshness that balanced the dish.

Victor served the vegetables in a small white bowl beside a slice of rustic toast, a glass of coffee, and a spoon for extra flavor. The meal was prepared for a quiet Monday morning—perhaps the final breakfast before his volunteer work at Smallville College, where he continues teaching engineering students the same disciplined, structural rigor he learned as an engineer.
