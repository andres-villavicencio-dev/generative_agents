On Monday morning, Wolfgang Schulz prepared a high-protein berry oatmeal bowl to fuel his early run, chemistry studies, and afternoon training.

The dish began with rolled oats simmered in low-fat milk with a spoonful of chia seeds, a sliced banana, and a pinch of cinnamon. Once the oats had thickened into a creamy porridge, Wolfgang stirred in a spoonful of peanut butter and topped the bowl with fresh blueberries and strawberries. He prepared it by gently stirring the ingredients to preserve the fruit’s shape and kept the heat low so the milk would not scorch.

The finished bowl smelled warm and comforting, with the sweet aroma of berries and cinnamon, the earthy fragrance of chia seeds, and the rich roasted scent of peanut butter. It was presented in a dark ceramic bowl, the pale oats and colorful fruit arranged in neat sections for an energizing morning photograph.

The occasion was an ordinary but important weekday breakfast before Wolfgang’s run and a full day of college coursework and athletic training.
