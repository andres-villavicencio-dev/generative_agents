Wolfgang Schulz prepared one-pan lemon herb chicken with roasted vegetables, a fitting dinner before his evening workout and study session. He combined chicken thighs with baby potatoes, carrots, broccoli, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, dried oregano, thyme, and a pinch of paprika. Using his skillet and a sheet pan, he seasoned the chicken, browned it skin-side down, then transferred it to the pan with the chopped vegetables. Everything roasted together until the chicken was cooked through and the potatoes were tender and lightly crisp.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, oregano, lemon, and sizzling chicken, complemented by the earthy scent of broccoli and carrots. He plated the meal attractively, placing the golden chicken beside colorful vegetables and garnishing it with fresh parsley and a lemon wedge, with extra olive sauce on the side. It was a nourishing pre-competition dinner, prepared on Monday evening for Wolfgang to refuel after his morning run, support his training, and keep him focused while studying for his chemistry exams.
