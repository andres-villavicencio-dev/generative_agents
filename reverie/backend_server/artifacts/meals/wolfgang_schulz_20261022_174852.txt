On Monday evening, Wolfgang Schulz prepared a quick roasted vegetable and chickpea couscous bowl for dinner. The dish featured sweet potato, broccoli, red bell pepper, red onion, and chickpeas, tossed with couscous and dressed with lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and a pinch of salt and pepper.

He chopped the vegetables, boiled the chickpeas, and roasted everything on a baking sheet at high heat until the sweet potatoes caramelized and the broccoli developed lightly charred edges. Meanwhile, he cooked the couscous and folded it through the roasted vegetables with fresh parsley. The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, earthy cumin, and smoky paprika.

The meal was served in a simple white bowl, with the colorful vegetables arranged over the couscous and a lemon wedge on the side. It was an appropriate dinner after a morning run and a full day of studying: hearty, nutritious, and easy to prepare before his evening workout.
