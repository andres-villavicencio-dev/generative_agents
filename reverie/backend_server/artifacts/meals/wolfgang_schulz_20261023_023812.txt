Wolfgang prepared Oven-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-herb Sauce for breakfast. The dish features a whole chicken roasted with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and a dash of smoked paprika, served alongside carrots, potatoes, and onions, along with a bright lemon-herb sauce.

He toasted the chicken and vegetables in a pan, then transferred everything to a 400°F oven until the skin was golden and the chicken reached an internal temperature of 165°F. The pan juices were basted, and Wolfgang finished the dish by whisking the sauce to a glossy consistency and tossing the vegetables lightly.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the rich fragrance of roasted skin and herbs mingling with the sharp scent of lemon and garlic. He plated the chicken in the center, arranging the vegetables around it in a rustic, colorful presentation. This meal was his first part of a hearty breakfast, intended to give him energy for his upcoming competition and chemistry exams.
