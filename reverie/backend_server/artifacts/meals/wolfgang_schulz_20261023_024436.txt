Wolfgang Schulz prepared a Greek-style Greek Salad with Feta and Lemon Dressing, a colorful, refreshing side designed to complement his upcoming running competition. The dish used tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, Kalamata olives, cherry tomatoes, green peppers, and feta cheese. It was dressed with olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic, and a generous amount of crumbled feta for a bright, tangy flavor.

The salad was tossed in a large bowl, allowing the ingredients to cool slightly before Wolfgang added a final drizzle of dressing. He arranged it in a shallow white bowl, garnished with fresh parsley and a few extra olives, creating an inviting presentation. The crisp, slightly tangy aroma of the vegetables and the savory richness of the feta filled the kitchen as he prepared it for breakfast.

The meal was part of his productive early Monday morning routine before his run, study, and evening exercise. It was a simple, balanced breakfast meant to energize him for the day ahead.
