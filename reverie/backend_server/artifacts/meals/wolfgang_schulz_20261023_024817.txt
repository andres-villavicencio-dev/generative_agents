Wolfgang prepared a hearty breakfast of pan-fried eggs with roasted tomatoes and crispy bacon, served over sourdough toast with avocado and whole-grain toast. The eggs were lightly fried with a little salt and pepper, their yolk golden and creamy, while the bacon was crisp on the outside and tender inside. Roasted red tomatoes added a bright red color and a juicy, slightly sweet flavor. Avocado was sliced on top for texture and a burst of healthy fat.

The breakfast was arranged neatly on a white plate: two golden-brown eggs, the bacon, tomatoes, avocado, and toast spread across the surface. A small glass of cold water and a cup of tea were placed beside the plate. The aroma filled the kitchen with the savory scent of bacon, the tangy freshness of tomatoes, and the rich, buttery aroma of eggs.

The occasion was a morning routine before Wolfgang’s competition and exams. He wanted to fuel his run efficiently while keeping his studies focused, so he prepared a balanced, filling breakfast that supported his energy levels.
