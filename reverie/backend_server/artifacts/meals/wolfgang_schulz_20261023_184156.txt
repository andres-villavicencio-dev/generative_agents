Wolfgang prepared a Sautéed Vegetable Stir-Fry, a quick, protein-light side dish perfect for sharing with friends or teammates. The main ingredients included fresh spinach, bell peppers, broccoli florets, carrots, onions, and a handful of cherry tomatoes. He used olive oil, ginger, garlic, a touch of soy sauce, and a small portion of lean chicken for the main protein.

He sliced the vegetables and chicken into bite-sized pieces, then sautéed them in a wok or skillet until the chicken became golden and the vegetables became bright green and slightly caramelized. He finished it with a splash of soy sauce and a squeeze of lemon juice, adding the spinach at the end to preserve its color. The dish was served in a shallow bowl, with the protein arranged on one side and colorful vegetables around it.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and fragrant: sweet garlic, earthy ginger, and savory soy notes balanced by the fresh taste of the vegetables. The presentation was simple yet inviting, with the golden chicken standing out against the vivid green and red vegetables.

The meal was prepared for a dinner gathering, likely Wolfgang’s teammates or college friends preparing to train together. It reflected his practical, resourceful approach to cooking while supporting his academic and athletic goals.
