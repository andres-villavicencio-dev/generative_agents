Wolfgang prepared a Pan-Seared Chicken with Herbs and Lemon for the Tuesday, February 14 dinner. The main ingredient was a whole chicken breast, seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic, dried thyme, rosemary, and a splash of lemon juice. He seared the chicken in a skillet over medium-high heat, then transferred it to a baking sheet to bake until the skin was crisp and the meat reached an internal temperature of 165°F. A few sprigs of rosemary and a squeeze of lemon completed the dish.

The aroma of the meal was warm, savory, and bright—strong chicken, garlic, herbs, and a hint of citrus. It was presented simply on a white plate with the chicken resting on a bed of roasted vegetables, garnished with fresh thyme and lemon zest. The occasion was a quiet, focused dinner to prepare for his upcoming competition and exams. Cooking it in his own kitchen allowed him to have a satisfying meal without the distractions of a restaurant, while the easy preparation gave him enough time to review his study sheet and organize his notes for the next competition.
