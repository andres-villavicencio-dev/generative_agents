Wolfgang Schulz prepared Pan-Seared Chicken with Herb-Roasted Vegetables and Lemon-Corn Salad. For the chicken, he marinated it in olive oil, garlic, rosemary, and lemon juice, then seared it in a pan until the skin was golden and the interior cooked through. The vegetables—zucchini, bell pepper, and carrots—were roasted with olive oil, onions, thyme, and garlic until tender, while corn kernels added a bright pop of yellow.

He finished the meal with a simple lemon-corn salad, tossing the crisp corn and chopped parsley in the pan drippings. The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted garlic, earthy herbs, and fresh citrus. He plated the colorful chicken and vegetables in a shallow dish, arranging them alongside the salad before finishing the presentation with a drizzle of lemon-olive oil and a final sprinkle of parsley.

The meal was prepared for a relaxed weekday lunch, combining Wolfgang’s chemistry focus on practical cooking with his training. It was simple, nourishing, and carefully balanced, though the demanding schedule of chemistry, exams, and competition preparation meant he was still recovering his energy.
