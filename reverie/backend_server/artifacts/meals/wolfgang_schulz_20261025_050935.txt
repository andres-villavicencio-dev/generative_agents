On Tuesday, February 14, Wolfgang prepared Rustic Sage Herb Chicken with Grilled Potatoes and Lemon Garlic Butter. The dish came together in a small kitchen table while he gathered chicken breasts, garlic, fresh thyme, rosemary, lemon, and olive oil. First, he sliced the potatoes and marinated the chicken with a mixture of lemon juice, garlic, and herbs. While the chicken rested, he tossed the potatoes with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then roasted them until golden. He basted the chicken with the pan juices and finished the potatoes under the oven. The aroma filled the room: warm, savory, and fragrant with the sharp zest of lemon, earthy notes from the herbs, and the rich scent of garlic.

Wolfgang presented the meal on a simple white plate, arranging the chicken in the center, seasoned potatoes around it, and a small lemon wedge beside the plate. It was a quiet, reflective lunch for a quiet Tuesday—inspired by sketches at Johnson Park and his balance of chemistry and running. The meal felt nourishing and purposeful, reflecting his dedication to both his studies and his athletic life.
