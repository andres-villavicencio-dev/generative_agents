Wolfgang Schulz prepared Oatmeal with Fresh Berries and Lemon-Thyme for breakfast. The dish included rolled oats, blueberries, strawberries, raspberries, sliced bananas, a lemon-lime zest, and a few sprigs of thyme. He toasted the oats briefly in a dry pan, then added cold milk and stirred in the fruit. A small knob of butter and a few drops of vanilla gave it a warm, rich aroma.

The presentation was simple and inviting: the warm oatmeal was served in a clear bowl, topped with colorful fruit and a drizzle of honey. Wolfgang prepared it while continuing his morning routine, balancing study with his training and reflecting on how to make chemistry and athletics more efficient. It was his everyday breakfast, but the preparation felt more purposeful—the fresh ingredients and careful cooking reflected his dedication to both his academics and his sport.
