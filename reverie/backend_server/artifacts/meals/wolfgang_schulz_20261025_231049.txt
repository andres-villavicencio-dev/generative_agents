Wolfgang prepared a Hearty Vegetable & Herb Rice Bowl, a nutritious lunch for his study and competition routine. He used basmati rice, diced carrots, onions, bell peppers, baby spinach, cherry tomatoes, chickpeas, and a splash of lemon-tahini dressing. He sautéed the vegetables in a little olive oil and garlic, added the rice and chickpeas, then simmered everything gently until the rice was tender and the vegetables were evenly cooked. He topped the bowl with fresh parsley and a drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil before serving.

The dish had a warm, earthy aroma of garlic, carrots, and toasted grains, with a slightly tangy lemon note. It was presented in a simple white bowl, colorful and inviting, with the crisp vegetables arranged around the soft grains. He prepared it for lunch during his Wednesday afternoon break, a quiet moment to recharge before revisiting the garden, comparing his route with Sam’s quiet spots, and checking the watercolor sketches of Jennifer’s garden.
