Wolfgang Schulz prepared a Pan-Seared Chicken with Lemon-Herb Butter and Roasted Vegetables. He began by searing a whole chicken in a skillet with olive oil, garlic, and fresh thyme until the skin became golden and crispy. He then rested the bird briefly, sliced it into pieces, and placed it in a baking dish to finish cooking.

To the chicken, he added fancy rosemary and fresh thyme, along with a generous drizzle of lemon juice, a pat of butter, and a pinch of salt. He arranged the pieces in a shallow pan, then roasted them in the oven until the meat was cooked through and the vegetables were tender and caramelized.

The aroma was rich and savory—herbs and garlic mingled with the gentle scent of butter, citrus, and roasted vegetables. He presented the dish in a simple white bowl, arranging the chicken in a neat arc and sprinkling the remaining herbs and lemon zest over it. The warm, golden chicken contrasted beautifully with the dark vegetables, making the meal feel both comforting and celebratory.

He prepared it for breakfast or lunch, choosing the lighter option because he was still training for a competition and had no confirmed appointments. The meal was part of a purposeful day of study, sport, and quiet exploration at Oak Hill College.
