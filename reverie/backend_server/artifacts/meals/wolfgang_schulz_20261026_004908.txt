Wolfgang prepared a Hearty Garden-Style Chicken Salad, balancing protein, vegetables, and a touch of acidity. The chicken was marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and a pinch of smoked paprika, then grilled until golden and tender. It was sliced into bite-sized pieces and arranged on a bed of crisp romaine lettuce, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and diced carrots. A few lemon-chili vinaigrette drizzles added brightness without overwhelming the flavors.

The kitchen smelled warm and aromatic—garlic, roasted chicken, and citrus mingling with the fresh scent of vegetables. Wolfgang presented the meal in a simple ceramic bowl with a white cloth napkin, using a small spoon to serve the salad so that each piece remained lightly moist. The occasion was a quiet Wednesday lunch on the college campus, a simple act of nourishment after a busy day of training, studying, and preparing for his next competition.
