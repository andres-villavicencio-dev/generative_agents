Wolfgang prepared Garden Vegetable Soup with Roasted Chicken, a simple yet nourishing lunch made in his dorm kitchen. He used a small pot, chicken thighs, red onion, carrots, celery, pearl onions, and a small handful of fresh herbs. He sautéed the onion, carrot, and celery until golden, then added the chicken and broth, simmering it uncovered until the chicken was tender. Afterward, he added the vegetables and let the soup reduce gently.

The aroma filling the kitchen was warm and savory—herbs, roasted chicken, and slightly caramelized vegetables blended into a comforting broth. He served the soup in a white ceramic bowl, garnished with chopped parsley and a few whole garlic cloves, and arranged the chicken beside it. It was a practical, unpretentious meal suitable for a quiet Wednesday morning, but the careful presentation and careful portioning suggested he was treating it as a special occasion. It complemented his late-night training and academic preparation, giving him a moment to pause and enjoy something simple before heading toward the garden.
