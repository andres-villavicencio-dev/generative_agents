Wolfgang prepared a Rustic Herb Crusted Salmon with Asparagus and Lemon Butter, a simple yet elegant main dish for dinner. He used fresh salmon fillets, asparagus spears, lemon zest, garlic, olive oil, rosemary, thyme, and a touch of Dijon mustard. The salmon was pan-seared to a golden brown, while the asparagus was roasted until tender-crisp. The lemon-butter glaze was prepared by melting butter with lemon juice, garlic, and herbs and spooning it over the fish and vegetables.

The aroma was warm and savory—rich with roasted garlic, lemon zest, rosemary, and the buttery richness of the sauce. Wolfgang presented the dish on a white serving platter, arranging the salmon in a gentle curve and placing the asparagus in a small bowl beside it. A bright lemon wedge and a sprig of rosemary completed the presentation.

The occasion was a quiet Wednesday evening at Oak Hill College, after a long day of studying chemistry, training for a competition, and helping Sam find a quiet garden route. The meal reflected his focused, purposeful lifestyle—simple ingredients, careful attention to detail, and a satisfying, comforting ending to a busy day.
