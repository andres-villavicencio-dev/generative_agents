Wolfgang Schulz prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Potatoes and Garlic Bread for dinner on Wednesday, February 15. The main dish featured bone-in chicken thighs, baby potatoes, carrots, onions, fresh rosemary, thyme, garlic, and a simple herb-and-lemon glaze. He tossed the ingredients in olive oil, seasoned them with salt and pepper, and roasted them at 425°F (220°C) until the chicken was golden and the potatoes were crisp. The garlic bread was made by toasting slices of sourdough while baking them with butter and chopped parsley.

As the oven began, Wolfgang could smell the warm, savory aroma of roasted chicken mingling with the earthy scent of rosemary and garlic. He served the meal in a large white ceramic bowl, with the golden chicken resting beside the pan of potatoes and a stack of warm garlic bread. The presentation was rustic and inviting, with the vibrant orange of the carrots and carrots, the dark brown chicken, and the pale golden bread creating a cheerful contrast.

It was a hearty family-style dinner, prepared in a relaxed evening when Wolfgang had no confirmed appointments. He had made the meal to enjoy after his day of training, studying, and exploring the garden at Oak Hill College.
