Wolfgang prepared a simple but satisfying breakfast of scrambled eggs and toasted bread with fresh strawberries, orange zest, and a drizzle of honey. The eggs were seasoned lightly with salt and pepper, then cooked over medium heat until they were fully set and lightly golden. Toasted slices of sourdough were warmed in the pan, while the strawberries were briefly steamed and sliced.

The aroma came through the kitchen: the rich, savory scent of eggs, the sharp citrus of the orange zest, and the sweet, floral fragrance of the strawberries mingled with the warm aroma of toasted bread. He arranged the eggs in a small white ceramic dish, placed the golden toast beside them, and scattered the bright red berries over the top. A small glass of water and a piece of fresh fruit complemented the meal, making it look tidy and inviting.

The occasion was a quiet first breakfast before he began exploring the garden at Oak Hill College. It was not a celebratory meal, but a purposeful start to a busy day—one of discovery, academic work, and athletic preparation.
