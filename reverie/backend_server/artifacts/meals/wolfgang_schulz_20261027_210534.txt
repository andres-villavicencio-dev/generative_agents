Wolfgang prepared Savory Potato and Herb Pancake with Apple Sauce, a simple breakfast for his Wednesday morning. He soaked two medium potatoes, sliced them, and grated the skin on one side while keeping the other soft for the pancake. He sautéed a handful of onions with a pinch of salt, then added chopped herbs—basil, thyme, and a little garlic—and a splash of oil until the vegetables softened. The potatoes were gently fried until crisp, and he topped the pancake with the fruit.

The aroma was warm and savory, with hints of onion, herbs, and sweet apple. The pancakes were golden and slightly crispy, while the apple sauce ran lightly over the pan. The presentation was casual and inviting: a few golden pancakes arranged neatly beside a small bowl of warm sauce and a spoonful of crisp potatoes.

The meal was prepared to start Wolfgang’s productive Wednesday—after waking at 5 a.m., he would use the breakfast to refocus his chemistry and sports routine before heading toward the garden. It was a humble, purposeful breakfast, not a formal celebration.
