Wolfgang Schulz prepared a simple Hearty Breakfast Sandwich for himself at Oak Hill College.

He used a toasted whole-grain bread, soft boiled eggs with a little mustard, sliced tomatoes, and fresh chives. The eggs were cooked in a pan with a pinch of salt and pepper, while the bread was brushed with butter and toasted until golden. He arranged the ingredients on two slices of bread, added a small amount of melted cheese, and placed a fresh strawberry on top. The final presentation was simple and inviting, with the golden bread, bright green chives, and colorful fruit creating a cheerful breakfast aesthetic.

The aroma of toasted bread, rich egg, and slightly sweet strawberry filled the small kitchen. It was prepared quickly during his Wednesday morning routine, but he treated it with care because he still needed to make room for a visit to the garden and compare his route with Sam’s and Jennifer’s watercolor sketches. It was a purposeful, self-made meal that balanced his academic and athletic life.
