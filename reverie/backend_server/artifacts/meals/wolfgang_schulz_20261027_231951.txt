Wolfgang Schulz prepared Mediterranean Egg Breakfast with Fresh Herbs, a hearty morning meal designed to fuel his studies, training, and daily discovery.

The dish consists of two whole eggs, a bowl of spinach, a handful of tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and feta cheese, along with chili oil, lemon juice, and olive oil. Wolfgang poached the eggs in a pan, then added the vegetables to the pan and simmered them until the tomatoes softened. The eggs were seasoned with chili oil, and the whole bowl was finished with a squeeze of lemon and chopped fresh herbs.

Aromatic, earthy rosemary and thyme blended with the savory eggs and slightly sweet tomatoes created a warm, inviting aroma. The eggs remained creamy and golden, while the crisp vegetables and creamy feta provided a pleasing contrast.

Wolfgang presented the meal in a simple white ceramic bowl, with the vegetables arranged around a few golden eggs. A sprig of fresh rosemary and a small lemon half rested beside it, giving the dish an elegant, rustic appearance. It was the perfect breakfast for a busy Wednesday—a wholesome, nourishing start to his day before he began his planned garden visit and comparison with Sam’s quiet spots and Jennifer’s watercolor sketches.
