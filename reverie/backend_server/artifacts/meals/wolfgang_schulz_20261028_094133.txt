Wolfgang Schulz prepared a scrambled-egg-and-oatmeal breakfast. The dish combined fluffy scrambled eggs with oatmeal, a splash of milk, a pinch of salt, and a drizzle of honey for sweetness. It was seasoned lightly with black pepper and served warm.

He cooked the eggs in a nonstick pan over medium heat, stirring them until the edges became slightly crisp but the center remained creamy. The oatmeal was cooked gently in a separate pan until the grains softened and turned golden. He divided the eggs and oatmeal between two small bowls, garnishing the oatmeal with a little fresh honey.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich scent of butter, egg, oats, and honey. The presentation was simple and inviting: the eggs rested in a shallow bowl beside the oatmeal, with a wooden spoon resting nearby. Wolfgang intended to eat it at Oak Hill College on Wednesday, using the meal as a quiet moment of comfort after a busy, purposeful day.
