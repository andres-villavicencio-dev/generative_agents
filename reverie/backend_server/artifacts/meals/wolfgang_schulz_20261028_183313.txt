The meal Wolfgang prepared is Savory Chicken and Veggie Scramble with Toasted Whole Grain Toast.

Key ingredients include chicken breast, spinach, mushrooms, onions, tomatoes, bell peppers, eggs, whole-grain bread, and a little olive oil and garlic. He began by thoroughly washing his hands, then carefully preparing a clean plate and a small bowl of hot water to keep the eggs fresh.

After cooking the chicken, he sautéed the vegetables and mushrooms until the aromatics softened, added the eggs, and scrambled them until fluffy. The mixture was finished with a pinch of salt, pepper, and fresh herbs. He toasted the whole-grain bread before serving it alongside the warm scramble.

The dish had a savory, slightly smoky aroma from the garlic and chicken, with the fresh scent of herbs and ripe tomatoes. It was presented on a bright, white plate, with golden toast, colorful vegetables, and a glossy pan of eggs creating an inviting, balanced look.

He prepared the meal for breakfast, a relaxed morning ritual before heading out to run and study at Oak Hill College.
