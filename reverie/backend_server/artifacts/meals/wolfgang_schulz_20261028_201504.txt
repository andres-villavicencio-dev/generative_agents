Wolfgang prepared a Chicken and Vegetable Pan-Sear with Herbs and Lemon. He began by boiling water for the meal, then sautéed chicken thighs until golden, followed by onions, carrots, and bell peppers in the same pan. The vegetables softened, while the chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic, oregano, and a pinch of lemon zest. A splash of chicken broth and a little butter helped the dish release its rich aroma, creating a fragrant, savory scent that filled the small kitchen.

The vegetables were finished by tossing them briefly with olive oil, lemon juice, and fresh thyme. The chicken was returned to the pan just until it was no longer pink, then arranged on a foil-lined baking sheet. A pat of butter was drizzled over the top, and the whole dish was brushed with a little olive oil and rosemary.

The meal was presented in a clear glass bowl, with the golden chicken, colorful vegetables, and aromatic herbs arranged around it. The bright lemon slices and parsley additions gave it an appealing, fresh appearance. The occasion was an efficient lunch during a busy Monday at Oak Hill College, balancing his chemistry studies and competition training while trying to make the most of his limited time.
