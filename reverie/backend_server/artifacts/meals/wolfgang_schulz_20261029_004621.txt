Wolfgang prepared a Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables, a classic side-dish for a college competition meal. The dish consists of chicken thighs, red onion, carrots, parsnips, and a handful of cherry tomatoes. Wolfgang marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, dried herbs, and salt, then seared it over medium heat until the skin turned golden and the meat came away easily from the pan. The vegetables were roasted with a little water until tender, and Wolfgang finished everything with a final squeeze of lemon.

The kitchen filled with the aromatic scent of garlic, citrus, and roasted vegetables. He presented the meal on a white ceramic platter, arranging the chicken in the center and the colorful root vegetables around it. A small bowl of lemon-herb dressing and a side of crusty bread completed the presentation.

The occasion was an early lunch while he prepared for his next competition and exams. After a long day, Wolfgang felt tired but satisfied, eager to test himself in the kitchen and leave a fresh, flavorful meal behind.
