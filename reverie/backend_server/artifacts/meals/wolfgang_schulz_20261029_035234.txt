Wolfgang prepared a simple vegetable omelet with whole-grain toast and a side of roasted potatoes, a balanced breakfast that reflected his dedication to both chemistry and his sport. He began with two eggs, one egg white, and a small handful of spinach, then gently folded them into a lightly cooked omelet using a non-stick pan. After removing the omelet and spreading the pan juices over the eggs, he added the spinach and toasted the whole-grain bread. The potatoes were roasted with oil, salt, pepper, and dried herbs until they were golden and slightly crisp.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the rich, nutty scent of the eggs and butter, the sharp freshness of the spinach, and the earthy warmth of the roasted potatoes. He plated the omelet in a small ceramic dish with two slices of toast and a neat mound of potatoes, garnishing it with a sprinkle of parsley. The presentation was clean and practical, suited to a Wednesday breakfast before his competition and study schedule.

Occasion: A quiet, purposeful start to his day, balancing relaxation with the preparation needed for the next competition and his upcoming chemistry exams.
