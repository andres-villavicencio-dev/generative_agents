Wolfgang Schulz prepared a Pan-Sear Chicken with Herb-Roasted Vegetables for lunch. The main ingredient is a whole chicken, seasoned with garlic, olive oil, lemon, rosemary, thyme, black pepper, and a pinch of smoked paprika. He seared the chicken in a large skillet until a golden crust formed, then added the vegetables—peppers, onions, carrots, and zucchini—which were briefly sautéed until tender. A splash of chicken broth and a few sprigs of parsley were stirred in to finish the dish.

The kitchen smelled warm and savory: roasted garlic, herbs, chicken, and caramelized vegetables released their aromas as Wolfgang stirred the pan. He finished with a generous drizzle of pan sauce and fluffed the vegetables with a fork. The meal was presented in a simple white bowl, with the chicken resting atop the vegetables and a small lemon wedge beside it.

Wolfgang had prepared it for an early Wednesday lunch before preparing for his chemistry exam and upcoming competition. The meal felt balanced and purposeful, reflecting his dedication to cooking and studying. Despite being tired, the meal made him feel satisfied, and he looked forward to the next day with renewed energy.
