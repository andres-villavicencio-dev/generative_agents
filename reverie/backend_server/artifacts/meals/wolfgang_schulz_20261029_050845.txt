Wolfgang Schulz prepared Savory Chicken and Vegetarian Stir-Fry, a quick, nutritious lunch designed to fuel his study and athletic routine. The dish combines tender chicken breast, carrots, bell peppers, snap peas, and onions in a savory soy-ginger sauce with sesame oil and garlic.

The food was prepared in a large wok: chicken was marinated briefly, then stir-fried until golden and fully cooked, while the vegetables were cooked until crisp-tender. Wolfgang stirred the mixture continuously to ensure even cooking and prevent the sauce from burning. The kitchen filled with the sharp aroma of garlic, soy, ginger, caramelized onions, and sesame oil.

He plated the dish in a wide white bowl, arranging the colorful vegetables and translucent chicken in a neat layer. The warm, golden color of the chicken and the bright green vegetables gave the presentation a cheerful, energetic look. It was a simple, wholesome meal prepared for a relaxed Wednesday—sufficient for studying chemistry and preparing for his upcoming competition.
