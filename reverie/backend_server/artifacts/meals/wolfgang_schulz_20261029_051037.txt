Wolfgang prepared a Garlic Butter Salmon with Lemon-Herb Rice and Roasted Asparagus. The key ingredients were salmon fillets, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, fresh rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, butter, and short-grain rice, along with asparagus spears and a small handful of cherry tomatoes.

He began by salting and drying the salmon, then searing it in a hot skillet until the skin turned crisp. In the same pan, he melted butter and sautéed minced garlic until fragrant, before adding sliced tomatoes and finishing the pan with lemon juice and rosemary. The rice was cooked in a separate pot with a little olive oil, then gently folded in with the warm salmon. Asparagus was roasted with the asparagus oil mixture until tender-crisp.

The aroma filled the kitchen with rich, savory garlic, buttery notes, and the bright, fresh scent of lemon and herbs. He plated the salmon and rice on a white ceramic platter, adding the colorful asparagus on top for a striking visual contrast, and sprinkled fresh thyme and a few lemon wedges over the top. The presentation was simple yet elegant, reflecting his balanced approach to both chemistry and his athletic commitments. The meal was prepared for a quiet Wednesday lunch, after a long day of studying, training, and preparing for his upcoming competition.
