Wolfgang prepared Ratatouille with Lemon-Herb Oil, a colorful Mediterranean-style vegetable medallions prepared in a slow-slow pan with tomatoes, zucchini, eggplant, red peppers, onions, spinach, and carrots. He added a small handful of fresh herbs, garlic, and a pinch of salt, then finished the dish with a generous drizzle of lemon juice and warm herb oil before serving it over a plain crusty bread.

The aromas rose strongly from the caramelizing onions, softening tomatoes, and earthy vegetables, with the warm lemon oil adding a bright, citrusy finish. The dish was arranged in a shallow baking dish for easy serving, with the colorful vegetables stacked neatly around the bread and a small garnish of parsley on top.

The meal was prepared in his dorm kitchen during a quiet Wednesday morning, before his next competition and chemistry exams. It was a simple yet hearty lunch meant to energize him, and Wolfgang savoring its warm flavors gave him a sense of pride and satisfaction.
