Wolfgang prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Ginger-Soy Sauce for lunch on Wednesday, February 15.

He sliced boneless chicken breast, sliced carrots, bell peppers, and onions, then stir-fried them in a wok with garlic, ginger, and a small amount of oil. He tossed the vegetables with the chicken, added a splash of water, and covered the pan to let the flavors develop. The dish finished in the pan, with the vegetables slightly softened and the chicken golden and tender. A spoonful of ginger-soy sauce was added, and the remaining ingredients were carefully eaten.

The aroma was warm and savory—ginger, garlic, and savory chicken mingling with the sweetness of carrots and vegetables. He served the stir-fry in a shallow, lightly oiled bowl and topped it with a few green onions. The presentation was simple and practical, with the colorful vegetables arranged beside the chicken and the sauce lightly poured over the top.

The meal was a casual lunch during his study block before a competition. It was hearty enough to sustain him through the day, but not so heavy as to leave him exhausted. Wolfgang’s focus remained sharp: he had finished the remaining ingredients with care and was ready to test himself.
