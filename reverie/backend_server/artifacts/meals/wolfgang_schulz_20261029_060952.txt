Wolfgang prepared pan-fried egg with spinach and whole-grain toast, a simple, hearty breakfast that balanced a nutritious morning with a quick source of energy for his competition and studies.

The dish began with a lightly buttered pan, to which Wolfgang added chopped scallions, a handful of fresh spinach, and a spoonful of olive oil. He fried the spinach until it wilted, then cracked two eggs into the pan. The eggs were cooked until the whites set and the yolks remained soft, finished by sprinkling them with chopped chives and a pinch of black pepper. Wolfgang toasted slices of whole-grain bread with a little butter and the pan juices before placing them beside the eggs.

The kitchen filled with a warm aroma of roasted garlic, tender greens, toasted grains, and savory egg. The presentation was simple and inviting: golden brown bread, creamy white eggs, vibrant green spinach, and a small bowl of lemon-herb yogurt for dipping. It was a quiet, satisfying meal meant to support Wolfgang’s busy routine—fueling his preparations for the next competition while giving him time to study and test himself.
