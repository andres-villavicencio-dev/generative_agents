Wolfgang Schulz prepared Chicken Caesar Salad with Lemon-Herb Dressing for lunch on Thursday, February 16. The meal was a light, refreshing lunch to accompany his chemistry flashcard preparation and a quick rest before sitting down to eat.

The chicken was marinated in olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, dried herbs, and a pinch of salt, then pan-seared to a golden brown. Sautéed spinach, romaine lettuce, and croutons were added, along with a creamy Caesar dressing made from Parmesan cheese, Parmesan, anchovy fillets, lemon juice, and Dijon mustard.

As the heat rose, the kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of roasted garlic, lemon, herbs, and nutty cheese. Wolfgang plated the salad in a simple white bowl, arranging the crisp greens, juicy chicken, and crunchy toppings in an appealing, asymmetrical way. The bright lemon and fresh herbs created a lively contrast against the rich colors of the chicken and Parmesan.

The occasion was a relaxed lunch on a quiet Thursday, combining study and sport with a simple, satisfying meal.
