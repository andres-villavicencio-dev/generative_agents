On Thursday, February 16, Wolfgang prepared a simple but satisfying breakfast of egg and whole-grain toast with a side of fresh fruit. The dish was balanced and easy to prepare, fitting his routine of eating dinner around 6:00 p.m.

For the eggs, Wolfgang used two large eggs, a pinch of salt, pepper, and a little butter. He cracked them into a hot pan and let them sit until the whites were fully set while the yolks remained soft. For the toast, he sliced a slice of whole-grain bread and toasted it briefly with the butter. He added sliced apples and a small handful of blueberries, letting them soften slightly as the toast warmed.

The aroma was warm and inviting, with the nutty scent of whole-grain bread, a subtle richness from the butter, and bright notes of ripe apples and blueberries. His presentation was simple and appealing: the golden toast and fluffy eggs were arranged neatly beside the colorful fruit, with a glass of water nearby. It was a quiet, nourishing breakfast before a productive session reviewing chemistry flashcards and organizing his study materials.
