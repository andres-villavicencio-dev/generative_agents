Wolfgang Schulz prepared Grilled Chicken with Herb-Lemon Broth, a simple yet flavorful home-cooked dinner for the Friday cookout. He marinated chicken breasts with lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, dried thyme, rosemary, and a pinch of salt, then grilled them over medium heat until the skin was crisp and the meat was cooked through. The savory broth was simmered with carrots, onions, and celery until tender, then finished with fresh parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

The aroma was warm, herbaceous, and slightly smoky, with notes of garlic, citrus, and toasted vegetables. The chicken was platter-style, seasoned with cracked black pepper, served alongside glazed carrots and roasted potatoes, with the herb-lemon broth ladled over the top for a bright, refreshing finish.

This hearty main dish was prepared for an early Friday cookout, where Wolfgang and his friends would gather to cook together. His structured schedule and preparation allowed him to focus on the meal while still having time to compare ingredients and place the order.
