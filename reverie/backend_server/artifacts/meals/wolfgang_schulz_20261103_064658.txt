Dish Name: Grain, Vegetable, and Protein Power Bowl

Key Ingredients: Quinoa, roasted sweet potato, kale, chickpeas, chickpea salad, feta, and mild clover honey.

Preparation: Wolfgang washed the grains and prepared the vegetables. He roasted the sweet potato until it was tender, chopped the kale, and sautéed the chickpeas with a little olive oil. He cooked the quinoa with a splash of water, then combined it with the other ingredients in a large bowl. He stirred in the feta and honey before serving.

The aroma is warm and inviting: roasted potatoes, earthy kale, and the savory scent of toasted grains. The presentation is colorful and satisfying—golden grains and bright vegetables arranged around a bed of creamy chickpea salad.

The dish was prepared for Wolfgang’s Friday cookout on Saturday, February 18, after his Saturday schedule had become packed. He wanted a nutritious, quick meal that would keep him energetic while matching his chemistry and running interests. The honey added a subtle sweetness, and the combination of grains, protein, and vegetables made it a satisfying, easy dinner.
