Wolfgang prepared a simple, hearty breakfast of scrambled eggs with whole-grain toast, a glass of milk, and a small bowl of fresh fruit. He used the organic eggs he had carefully compared with other suppliers, choosing fresh eggs from his preferred source while noting the price and ingredient list.

To prepare the eggs, he cracked them into a bowl, added a pinch of salt, and whisked them before cooking over medium heat. The pan was lightly greased, and the eggs were stirred gently until they formed smooth, uniformly golden yolks and firm whites. He toasted the whole-grain toast in a little butter before adding the eggs, letting them cook until the edges were just set while keeping the soft centers.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of butter, toasted grains, and the rich smell of cooked eggs. He plated the breakfast in a clean bowl, arranging the eggs neatly beside the toast and fruit. For the occasion, the meal was prepared as a relaxed Sunday breakfast, a quiet way to unwind after a busy week while he continued preparing for his chemistry studies and upcoming competition work.
