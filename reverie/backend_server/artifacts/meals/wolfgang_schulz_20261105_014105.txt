Wolfgang Schulz prepared a simple grilled chicken with roasted vegetables and a lemon-herb dressing for an early Tuesday breakfast.

The chicken was seasoned with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and a pinch of paprika, then grilled until golden and juicy. He roasted carrots, potatoes, and bell peppers with olive oil, salt, and lemon zest. For the dressing, he whisked olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, and fresh parsley until the flavors blended.

The kitchen filled with the scent of roasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory spices. Wolfgang arranged the warm chicken and vegetables around two small plates, drizzling the bright dressing over the chicken. The meal was casual and hearty, designed to energize him for the day’s chemistry study and athletic routine.
