Wolfgang prepared Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice. The meal was a simple, filling dinner for his Tuesday night after a full day of training and studying. He used chicken breast, carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, and onions. For the wok, he sautéed the vegetables first until crisp, then added the chicken and cooked it until the meat was lightly browned and the chicken was fully cooked. He finished the dish with a splash of soy sauce, a touch of sesame oil, and a teaspoon of cooking sugar for a little sweetness.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the subtle fragrance of ginger, garlic, and the earthy sweetness of the vegetables filling the kitchen. He presented the meal on a small white plate, arranging the fluffy white rice neatly beside the colorful stir-fry. He added a small green sprig of scallions for a fresh visual contrast. The dish was warm, balanced, and ready in about twenty minutes, giving him plenty of time to relax and prepare for the evening competition.
