Wolfgang Schulz prepared Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Orange Glazed Carrots. He used two chicken breasts marinated with olive oil, lemon zest, garlic, thyme, and herbs, then grilled them until the skin was crisp and the meat was fully cooked. The roasted carrots, potatoes, and root vegetables were seasoned with salt, pepper, and a little olive oil, then tossed with a few sprigs of thyme and roasted alongside the chicken until tender and caramelized. The glaze was made by reducing lemon juice, honey, and orange juice with a pinch of sugar until it glistened.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of toasted garlic, citrus, herbs, and savory meat. Wolfgang plated the dish on a white platter, arranging the golden chicken in the center and the colorful vegetables around it, garnished with fresh parsley. The meal was a relaxed family dinner on Tuesday, February 14, as Wolfgang balanced training, chemistry study, and the promise of his upcoming competition.
