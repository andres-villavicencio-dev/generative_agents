Wolfgang prepared Honey-Glazed Apple Chicken with Roasted Vegetables. The dish combines tender chicken breasts marinated in lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, and herbs, with caramelized apples and carrots glazed with honey and brown sugar. He roasted the vegetables in the oven first to soften them, then pan-seared the chicken until golden and the sauce thickened.

The kitchen counter smelled of roasted garlic, caramelized apples, lemon zest, and the savory richness of the chicken. He arranged the chicken on a white plate surrounded by crisp vegetables and topped it with a glossy honey glaze, using a small spoon to drizzle extra glaze over the top. The meal was a warm, hearty breakfast designed to keep his energy up for his 7:00–11:00 AM study block and chemistry exam preparation.
