Wolfgang prepared a Greek-inspired breakfast bowl for his Monday morning. The dish featured crispy chickpeas, honey-ginger yogurt, fresh cucumber, tomatoes, feta cheese, red onion, cumin, and a few lemon slices. He toasted the chickpeas in olive oil until golden, then whisked the yogurt with honey and chopped herbs. Cucumber, tomatoes, feta, and red onion were sprinkled over the mixture, and Wolfgang added a pinch of cumin for a warm finish.

Aromatic herbs and lemon filled the kitchen as he tossed the ingredients, while golden chickpeas, caramelized onions, and fresh herbs rose from the bowl. The final presentation was colorful and inviting: a bright yellow chickpea base, crisp cucumber rings, creamy yogurt, and feta cheese arranged on a white plate with a small bowl of lemon juice and a sprig of rosemary beside it.

The occasion was his morning breakfast before heading out—a quick, energizing meal that balanced his studies and physical training. It was a simple, healthy option designed to keep him focused while still feeling rewarding and satisfying.
