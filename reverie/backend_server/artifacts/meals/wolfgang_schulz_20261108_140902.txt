For Monday, Wolfgang prepared Oatmeal Breakfast Bowl in his dorm kitchen. The dish featured rolled oats, ripe bananas, walnuts, blueberries, chia seeds, and a splash of almond milk, with a sprinkle of cinnamon and a pinch of salt. He cooked the oats in a small pan with a little water until they were fluffy, then arranged them in a warm bowl.

He added the fresh fruit and nuts for texture, then let the oats soften in the remaining liquid. The warm aroma of cinnamon, sweet fruit, toasted nuts, and oatmeal filled the small kitchen. For presentation, he arranged the fruit and nuts on top, sprinkling cinnamon generously and adding a small lemon wedge for a fresh citrus scent.

The occasion was a quiet, productive breakfast to begin his busy day. It was neither a celebratory meal nor a hurried lunch, but a mindful way to replenish his energy before heading to the dorm for quiet study and a few moments of reflection.
