Wolfgang Schulz prepared a hearty Hearty Breakfast Bowl with Lemon-Butter Chicken and Quinoa. The dish combines roasted chicken, golden egg‑yolk rice, fluffy quinoa, fresh spinach, sliced carrots, and crisp apple slices in a small bowl.

He heated the chicken in a skillet with olive oil, then browned it before adding lemon juice, minced garlic, dried parsley, and a splash of yogurt to keep it moist. The quinoa was rinsed, cooked briefly, and mixed in with the cooked chicken and vegetables, while the spinach wilted during the final minute.

The aroma was warm and fragrant—savory chicken, toasted nuts, caramelized carrots, and the bright scent of lemon. He arranged the colorful ingredients in the bowl, adding a sprig of parsley and a drizzle of lemon dressing for a fresh finish.

The occasion was his morning routine before heading into college and training for his upcoming competition. He wanted something quick, nutritious, and energizing to start his day.
