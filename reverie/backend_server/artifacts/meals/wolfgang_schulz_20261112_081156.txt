Wolfgang Schulz prepared Zucchini Parmesan Pasta with Roasted Chicken, a hearty, Italian-inspired dinner suited to his balanced life of studying, training, and competition.

He began by washing and chopping the zucchini and chicken, then sautéed the chicken in olive oil with garlic and dried thyme until it turned golden. While it cooked, he sautéed the zucchini in butter, adding salt and pepper to bring out its natural sweetness. The pasta was cooked separately, then tossed with the sauce and finished in the pan until the chicken was tender and the zucchini was tender-crisp.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the rich notes of garlic, herbs, and tomato balancing the savory richness of the chicken. Wolfgang plated the pasta in a shallow bowl, arranging the chicken in the center and scattering golden croutons and a generous dusting of Parmesan cheese over it. The presentation was simple, rustic, and inviting, with the bright color of the zucchini contrasting against the creamy sauce.

The meal was intended for a quiet evening before a competition, allowing Wolfgang to nourish his body and mind while preparing for the next training session.
