Meal: Simple Peanut-Lemon Chicken Salad

Wolfgang prepared a quick, nutritious lunch featuring grilled chicken breast, roasted carrots, and a lemon-honey dressing. The main ingredients were chicken, carrots, onions, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, honey, soy sauce, and a small handful of peanuts. He marinated the chicken, roasted it with the carrots, and tossed the salad with a bright lemon-honey dressing before serving.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted chicken and toasted nuts. Wolfgang arranged the colorful ingredients in a clear glass bowl, garnished with chopped parsley, and set the plate in the living room for lunch. The occasion was a focused chemistry study session at Oak Hill College, during which he was preparing for upcoming exams and a competition while organizing his notes and practicing substitution-reaction questions with his peers.
