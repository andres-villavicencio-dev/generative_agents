Wolfgang prepared Herb-Roasted Chicken with Roasted Vegetables for breakfast. He marinated chicken breast with lemon, garlic, and thyme, then roasted it until golden and juicy. He sliced carrots, onions, and bell peppers, added a splash of broth, and roasted them alongside the chicken until the vegetables became tender and slightly caramelized.

The kitchen counter smelled of toasted garlic, warm herbs, and savory poultry. Wolfgang arranged the food on a large white plate, garnishing it with chopped parsley and a few whole cracked black peppercorns. A small glass of cold lemon water rested beside the dishes.

The occasion was a quiet morning before his academic and athletic commitments—the simple, hearty breakfast that helped him regain focus while supporting his energy needs for the upcoming competition and exams.
