Wolfgang prepared Creamy Garlic Chicken Thighs with Potatoes and Brown Rice. He marinated the chicken in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, and salt before cooking the thighs until the meat was golden and tender. He added roasted potatoes, a handful of fresh spinach, and the cooked brown rice to the pan, stirring everything together with a splash of cream and a final drizzle of lemon.

The aroma was warm and savory: roasted potatoes, garlic, thyme, and the rich scent of lemon-infused cream filled the kitchen. The dish was presented in a white ceramic bowl, with the golden chicken arranged beside the bright green spinach and the earthy brown rice, garnished with a few fresh thyme leaves.

This was a simple dinner for an important competition training session. Wolfgang had prepared it efficiently while balancing his chemistry studies, exercise routine, and running practice, ensuring he could eat something nourishing without delaying his training.
