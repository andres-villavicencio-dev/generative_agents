Dish: Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Butter Sauce and Roasted Asparagus

Key Ingredients: Fresh salmon fillet, unsalted butter, fresh lemon juice, capers, garlic, thick asparagus spears, olive oil, salt, and black pepper.

Preparation Method: Yuriko begins by patting the salmon dry and seasoning it generously with salt and pepper. In a cold skillet, she places the salmon skin-side down and slowly raises the heat to achieve a crisp, golden-brown crust without overcooking the flesh. Meanwhile, she prepares the asparagus by snapping off the woody ends, tossing them in olive oil, salt, and pepper, then roasting them in the oven until tender-crisp. For the sauce, she sautés minced garlic in butter, adds a splash of fresh lemon juice and a handful of capers, reducing the mixture until it emulsifies into a glossy, rich glaze. She finishes the salmon with a final basting of the sauce before plating.

Aroma: The kitchen is filled with the bright, zesty scent of lemon and the savory, nutty aroma of searing salmon, complemented by the earthy fragrance of roasting green vegetables.

Presentation: The dish is arranged elegantly on a white plate. The salmon sits prominently, its pink flesh contrasting with the crispy skin. A vibrant green bed of roasted asparagus fans out beside it, and a generous drizzle of the golden lemon-butter sauce glistens over the fish, dotted with tiny green capers for a pop of color.

Occasion: This is a well-earned dinner after a long day of tax work. Yuriko prepares this specific meal on Mondays as a ritual to transition from her professional life to her personal time, enjoying a nutritious, home-cooked meal before her 11 pm bedtime.
