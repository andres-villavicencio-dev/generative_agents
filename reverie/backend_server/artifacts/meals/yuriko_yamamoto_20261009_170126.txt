Dish Name: Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Butter Sauce and Roasted Asparagus

Key Ingredients: Fresh Atlantic salmon fillet, unsalted butter, fresh lemon juice, capers, garlic cloves, thick asparagus spears, olive oil, salt, and black pepper.

Preparation Method: Yuriko begins by patting the salmon dry and seasoning it generously with salt and pepper. She heats a skillet over medium-high heat with a drizzle of olive oil, searing the salmon skin-side down until the flesh is opaque and crispy, then flips it to finish cooking. Meanwhile, she blanches the asparagus in boiling water for three minutes before tossing them in olive oil and roasting them in the oven until tender-crisp. For the sauce, she deglazes the salmon pan with fresh lemon juice and white wine, whisking in cold cubes of butter to create a glossy emulsion, finishing with a handful of capers and minced garlic.

Aroma: The kitchen is filled with the bright, citrusy scent of lemon cutting through the rich, nutty aroma of searing salmon and toasted garlic.

Presentation: She plates the salmon atop a nest of vibrant green asparagus, drizzling the golden butter sauce generously over the fish. A sprinkle of bright green capers and a thin lemon wheel garnish add pops of color against the pale pink salmon.

Occasion: This is a quiet weeknight dinner, prepared as Yuriko unwinds after a long day of tax work. It serves as a nourishing, relaxing meal before she settles in to review new tax laws for her evening studies.
