Yuriko prepared Crispy Tofu with Sesame Drizzle and Steamed Broccoli. The dish featured firm tofu pan-fried until golden and crisp, seasoned with soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, garlic, and a little honey for sweetness. Steamed broccoli was arranged alongside the tofu, with a final drizzle of sesame sauce over the entire dish.

The aroma of toasted sesame, savory soy, warm ginger, and caramelized honey filled the kitchen as she cooked. Her organized, detail-oriented approach ensured every ingredient was measured precisely, and the steamed broccoli was carefully arranged in neat rows.

The meal was a relaxed Tuesday dinner for her. Yuriko, who works in tax compliance and is curious about the upcoming mayoral race, prepared the dish to enjoy a simple, comforting meal after her workday. The crisp tofu and bright green broccoli offered a clean, satisfying contrast to her focused day.
