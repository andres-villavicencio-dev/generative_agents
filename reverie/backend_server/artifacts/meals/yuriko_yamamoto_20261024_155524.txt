Yuriko prepared a comforting Tofu and Vegetable Tacos with Avocado Toast, a quick, balanced lunch suited to her busy Tuesday. Key ingredients included firm tofu marinated in soy sauce and garlic, cabbage, carrots, green onions, corn, and avocado, along with flour tortillas and whole-grain toast.

She began by slicing the tofu and cooking it until golden, then shredded the vegetables and mixed them with the tofu. For the tortillas, she lightly toasted the flour tortillas, warmed the toast, and added a sprinkle of cumin and chili flakes before folding in the fillings. The warm avocado was sliced over the top, and a drizzle of lime juice and a pinch of smoked paprika added a bright citrus finish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with savory soy, garlic, toasted flour, and fresh herbs. The presentation was simple and inviting: the colorful tacos stacked neatly beside a plate of crisp avocado toast, garnished with fresh herbs. It was Yuriko’s lunch on a Tuesday, balanced with work on a tax compliance project and a tax law class, while the gathering and photographing of the water path and old pier provided a welcome break.
