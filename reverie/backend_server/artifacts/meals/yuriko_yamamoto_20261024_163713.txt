Yuriko prepared a simple Miso-Glazed Chicken Salad with Rice Noodles, a warm, protein-rich lunch that reflected her organized, detail-oriented style. The dish used chicken breast, rice noodles, fresh spinach, carrots, cabbage, sesame seeds, and a savory miso-soy glaze.

She washed the noodles and sliced the chicken, then stir-fried the chicken in a wok until it was cooked through. While it cooked, she sautéed the vegetables in a little oil, adding a spoonful of miso and a splash of water to create a glossy, aromatic glaze. She tossed the noodles and chicken together, tossing them gently to ensure everything was evenly coated.

The meal smelled rich and earthy from the warm wok, with the subtle aroma of sesame and toasted miso filling the kitchen. She plated the salad in a small white bowl, arranging the vegetables neatly around the chicken and rice noodles, and garnished it with sesame seeds and a few green leafy bits. The presentation was clean and balanced, suggesting a meal prepared for a focused lunch while she worked on her tax compliance project. It was a quiet, nourishing break before the day’s responsibilities.
