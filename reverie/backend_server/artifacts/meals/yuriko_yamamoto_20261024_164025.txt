Yuriko prepared a simple Oven-Fried Salmon with Lemon-Herb Pan Sauce, a light and nutritious lunch she shared with Carmen for their walk along the water path.

She used salmon fillets, lemons, fresh thyme, garlic, olive oil, salt, and black pepper. The salmon was lightly seared until golden, then transferred to a hot oven to finish cooking through. A bright lemon-herb pan sauce was made with the pan juices and simmered briefly before being served. The fish was arranged on a white plate with the sauce, garnished with chopped parsley and a lemon wedge.

The meal was warm, aromatic, and fragrant with the bright scent of lemon, garlic, and herbs. Yuriko’s careful knife work and neat, balanced presentation gave it a tidy, professional appearance. The lunch was prepared for an informal Tuesday gathering, offering a simple, satisfying meal while she continued her busy work and planned the walk.
