Yuriko prepared Pan-Seared Salmon with Lemon-Herb Rice and Asparagus.  

The meal combined three fresh ingredients: salmon fillets, a bed of cooked rice, asparagus, and a fragrant lemon-herb sauce. She marinated the salmon in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, dried oregano, and a dash of soy sauce before searing it over medium-high heat until it was golden and cooked through. Asparagus was lightly tossed with butter, salt, and pepper, while the rice was simmered with vegetable broth and topped with fresh parsley.  

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of garlic, citrus, and roasted seafood. Yuriko presented the dishes in a simple white bowl and a ceramic dish, using a spatula to arrange the salmon and asparagus before turning them off. She served everything at lunch, likely while taking a brief pause from her tax-compliance project and preparing for the gathering with Carmen.
