Yuriko prepared a Japanese-style chicken-and-broccoli with ginger-garlic sauce. For the main dish, she marinated chicken thighs with soy sauce, mirin, sake, sesame oil, minced ginger, and garlic, then pan-seared the meat until the skin was crisp and the juices collected in the pan. She finished the dish with steamed broccoli, a splash of water, and a few whole black peppercorns. The aroma was warm and savory, with faint notes of ginger, garlic, sesame, and caramelized chicken.

She arranged the chicken over the broccoli on a large white plate, garnishing it with sliced green onions and a few whole black peppercorns. The presentation was simple yet elegant, with the golden chicken contrasting against the bright green vegetables and the clean white background. Yuriko made this as part of a Tuesday workday that included a tax compliance project, studying for tax-law classes, researching mayoral candidates, and preparing for a gathering with Carmen. The meal was a practical, nourishing lunch—a reminder that while her professional responsibilities were demanding, she still made time for comfort food and the quiet reflection that keeps her purposeful and fulfilled.
