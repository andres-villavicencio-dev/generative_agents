Yuriko prepared a simple but comforting Tofu Scrambled Egg with Steamed Broccoli, a quick lunch meant to fuel her tax-compliance work. She used firm tofu, a small handful of green onions, a ripe tomato, a few cloves of garlic, a squeeze of lemon, and a generous amount of low-fat soy sauce. The eggs were lightly beaten and scrambled with the tofu, while the broccoli was steamed until crisp-tender.

She seasoned the pan with a little sesame oil and chopped scallions, cooking gently until the eggs were set and the tofu had softened. The warm aroma of caramelized garlic, savory soy sauce, and steamed broccoli filled the kitchen. She arranged the colorful ingredients on a white ceramic plate, garnishing it with sesame seeds and a sprinkle of chili flakes.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Monday lunch after a long day on the tax compliance project. Yuriko wanted something nourishing without rushing, a simple lunch that would help her focus on her work while reminding her to take time for herself.
