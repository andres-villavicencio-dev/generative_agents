Yuriko prepared Roasted Root Vegetables with Garlic Herb Chicken and Quinoa, a hearty, budget-friendly main dish designed for the café project. She sliced carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes, then roasted them with olive oil, salt, and pepper until the skin turned golden and the flesh was tender. The chicken was marinated in garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, and herbs, then pan-seared to crisp the skin. Quinoa was cooked with vegetable broth and served alongside the vegetables and chicken.

The kitchen filled with the rich aroma of roasted garlic, caramelized onions, and savory herbs. She plated the meal with colorful vegetables around the chicken and a generous portion of fluffy, golden quinoa, garnished with fresh parsley. The presentation was simple yet inviting, emphasizing affordability and balanced nutrition.

The occasion was a practical meal during Yuriko’s tax-compliance workday. It was neither a celebration nor a formal dinner, but a wholesome lunch prepared in her spare time while she researched mayoral candidates.
