Yuriko prepared a simple roast chicken with seasonal vegetables and roasted potatoes, a balanced side dish for dinner. The chicken was seasoned with olive oil, garlic, lemon, and herbs, then roasted until golden and tender. The kitchen filled with a warm, savory aroma of roasted herbs, garlic, and lemon zest. She arranged the platter on a white ceramic platter, garnishing it with fresh mint and a small lemon wedge. The presentation was clean, rustic, and inviting, with the golden chicken and colorful vegetables arranged to create an appetizing contrast.
