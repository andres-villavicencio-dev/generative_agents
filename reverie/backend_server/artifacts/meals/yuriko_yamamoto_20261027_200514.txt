Yuriko prepared a simple lemon-herb chicken wrap with whole-grain toast and a small bowl of warm oatmeal. She used chicken breast, sliced lemon, fresh chives, garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of salt, wrapping the cooked chicken in the herbs and toast. The oatmeal was prepared with oats, milk, cinnamon, and a splash of lemon juice.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm, savory scent of chicken, garlic, and fresh herbs. Yuriko sliced the wrap and arranged it beside the oatmeal and toast, garnishing both with a bright lemon wedge. The meal was light, colorful, and practical for a quick breakfast.

She prepared it in the morning while setting the breakfast items on the table, intending it as a nourishing start to her Wednesday work. The clean, inviting presentation matched her organized approach, and the meal felt appropriate for a quiet morning before her tax-compliance project.
