Yuriko prepared Japanese-style Omelet with Seasonal Greens and Toasted Toast, a nourishing breakfast for her Wednesday morning. She used two eggs, a handful of spinach and chives, a slice of lemon, a few sprigs of parsley, and a generous piece of sourdough bread.

The eggs were cracked into a nonstick pan and cooked gently over medium heat until the whites set while the yolks remained slightly soft. She added the spinach and chives toward the end to keep them bright and tender, then folded the omelet over the bread. The lemon and herbs were drizzled over the finished dish before it was served.

Aromatic, earthy, and lightly citrusy, the meal filled the kitchen with the crisp smell of eggs, herbs, and warm bread. It was presented simply on a white plate, with the golden omelet and golden-brown toast arranged in an appealing, balanced way.

The occasion was Yuriko’s routine Wednesday breakfast, after a long day of tax research and mayoral-candidate analysis. She ate it early enough to finish before heading into work, then shared the updated café sales figures with Maria Lopez and considered how best to share her findings about the rarely visited part of the city with Jennifer Moore.
