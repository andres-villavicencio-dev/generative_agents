Yuriko prepared a simple Japanese-style breakfast called Oyakon no Kaiseki, a savory rice dish with grilled egg, shredded chicken, and a bright garnish of scallions and pickled ginger. The meal was prepared on a small, warm kitchen stove while Yuriko’s coffee cooled beside it.

The rice was prepared by soaking the grains until soft, then stirring them with a dash of soy sauce, a pinch of salt, and a little sesame oil until the broth absorbed the grains. The egg was lightly fried to golden perfection, while the chicken was pan-seared to a crisp exterior with a tender, juicy interior. Fresh scallions and thinly sliced ginger were arranged around the plate, adding a vivid contrast of green, white, and pale gold.

The aroma was warm and fragrant: toasted rice, savory chicken, a hint of garlic, and the subtle scent of toasted sesame oil. Yuriko arranged the dish in a shallow wooden bowl, using a spoon to gently divide the egg into a small serving for herself, leaving the main portion for guests.

The occasion was a quiet Wednesday breakfast. After completing her tax compliance project and researching mayoral candidates, Yuriko served the food to Maria Lopez and shared the updated café sales and savings figures before recommending the most affordable, balanced option to residents.
