The meal was a simple, comforting breakfast of pan-fried egg with toast, avocado, and fresh fruit. Yuriko prepared two thin slices of sourdough bread, sliced an avocado, and arranged the fruit alongside them in a small ceramic bowl. She cracked the eggs into a small pan, added a little water and a pinch of salt, and cooked them until the yolks were set and the whites were lightly browned. The pan was covered with a lid to steam the eggs gently, then she flipped one half over for a golden finish.

The kitchen smelled of toasted wheat, ripe avocado, warm milk, and the faint savory aroma of the egg. She served the eggs and toast in a neat bowl, garnishing it with a few lemon wedges and a sprig of mint. The presentation was clean, rustic, and inviting, with the bright yellow of the eggs contrasting against the green and golden tones of the breakfast.

It was a quiet Tuesday morning meal prepared for herself after a busy day of tax compliance, classes, and local-mayor election research. The food felt like a peaceful pause—a simple reminder that even on a full schedule, nourishment could bring calm and focus.
