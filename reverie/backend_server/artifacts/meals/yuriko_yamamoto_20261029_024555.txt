On Tuesday, February 14, Yuriko prepared Roasted Vegetable Pasta with Garlic Parmesan, a simple yet comforting pasta dish that made the busy day feel less overwhelming. She chose red, yellow, and orange carrots, bell peppers, zucchini, and onions, then sautéed them in olive oil until soft. After rinsing the pasta, she tossed it with a light garlic sauce and topped it with parmesan cheese and fresh parsley.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of roasted vegetables, garlic, and freshly baked cheese. The pasta was presented in a white bowl, arranged neatly in a spiral shape, and garnished with a bright drizzle of olive oil. Yuriko served a side of lemon wedges and served herself a portion on a small plate, choosing a quiet moment to eat before heading home.

The occasion was neither a formal meal nor a celebration, but a relaxed lunch to restock and recharge. Her organized, focused nature made the dinner feel like a peaceful pause between her tax-compliance work and her tax-law classes. As she sat down, Yuriko felt content and satisfied, though she could still hear the distant sounds of the city outside.
