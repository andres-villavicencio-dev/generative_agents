Yuriko prepared Katsu Curry with Egg and Veggie Rice. The dish featured thinly sliced bread, chicken breast, carrots, bell peppers, green onions, and a rich curry sauce, served over fluffy rice.

She began by toasting the bread, then pan-fried the chicken until golden, followed by stir-frying the vegetables until crisp. A savory curry sauce was prepared by combining curry powder, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and vegetable stock with simmered carrots and peppers. The finished curry was poured over the prepared rice, and the egg and remaining vegetables were folded in before the dish was completed.

The aroma was warm and comforting, with toasted bread, savory chicken, and the fragrant blend of curry, ginger, and garlic filling the kitchen. She served the meal at her breakfast table for a quiet Wednesday morning. Yuriko’s meticulous attention to the ingredients and cooking method made the simple lunch feel both nourishing and satisfying.
