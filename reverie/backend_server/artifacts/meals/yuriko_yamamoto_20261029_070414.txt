Yuriko prepared Miso-Glazed Salmon with Asparagus and Brown Rice, a warm, elegant dinner suitable for a quiet Wednesday evening. The dish featured salmon fillets marinated in miso, lemon, ginger, and a splash of soy sauce, then pan-seared until the skin turned crisp and tender. Asparagus was lightly sautéed, brown rice was cooked separately, and a small portion of the pan juices was poured over the rice to create a glossy glaze.

The kitchen filled with the savory aroma of toasted garlic, the earthy scent of miso, and the fresh scent of lemon and ginger. Yuriko arranged the salmon on a white plate, topped it with asparagus and a drizzle of glaze, and placed a small mound of brown rice beside it. The presentation was simple and inviting, with the warm colors of the salmon contrasting against the pale rice and dark greens from the asparagus.

It was the final meal of her Wednesday schedule, not a celebratory occasion, but a quiet moment of comfort after a demanding day of tax compliance and civic research. She sipped tea while looking toward the window, grateful that the meal had brought a small sense of calm to her busy days.
