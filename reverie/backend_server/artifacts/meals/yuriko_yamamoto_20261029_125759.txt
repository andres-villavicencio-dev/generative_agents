Yuriko prepared a simple, comforting breakfast of pan-seared eggs with lemon-tarrine bread for a quiet Thursday morning.

She used two large eggs, which she cracked into a hot pan and cooked until the whites were set and the yolks were soft. She added a splash of lemon juice and a few sprigs of fresh parsley, then finished the pan with a little butter and a pinch of black pepper. The pan seared the eggs, releasing a warm, savory aroma of butter and egg whites.

The bread was toasted until golden, then sliced and spread with the lemon-tarrine mixture. Yuriko arranged the eggs and bread on a clean white plate, garnishing the top with a small sprig of parsley. The bright yellow of the eggs and lemon contrasted with the golden-brown bread, creating a simple yet visually appealing presentation.

Yuriko prepared the meal for herself, making it a peaceful way to begin her productive day. It was neither rushed nor overly elaborate, reflecting her organized and calm approach to her busy schedule.
