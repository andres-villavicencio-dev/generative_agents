Yuriko prepared Miso-Glazed Salmon with Asparagus for a simple Monday breakfast. The dish features salmon fillets marinated in miso, ginger, and soy sauce, glazed with the same sauce before being topped with roasted asparagus and a scattering of sesame seeds. The meal is served in a shallow, warm ceramic bowl, with the salmon glazed to a glossy finish.

Yuriko cooked the salmon over medium-high heat, seasoning the pan with oil and the ginger-ginger-sesame mixture. Asparagus was tossed with olive oil, salt, and garlic at the end to retain its crisp texture. The warm broth released its aroma, while the sesame seeds added a subtle nutty fragrance. The scent filled the kitchen with the rich aroma of ginger, miso, and caramelized vegetables.

Yuriko arranged the salmon in the center of the bowl and placed the asparagus and seeds evenly around it. A small bowl of warm miso sauce and lemon wedges were set beside the plate. This quiet breakfast was part of her daily routine: preparing, eating slowly, and beginning the day with a calm, nourishing meal.
