On Tuesday, February 14, Yuriko prepared a comforting Oatmeal Bowl with Fresh Fruit and Nuts for breakfast. She began by cooking oats in water or milk with a little cinnamon and a pinch of salt, letting them soften until they reached the desired creamy consistency. She added a handful of frozen blueberries, raspberries, sliced bananas, and chopped walnuts, stirring them gently to combine the flavors before serving.

The meal was warm and fragrant, with the earthy scent of oats, sweet berries, and toasted nuts filling the kitchen. Yuriko plated the oatmeal in a small white bowl, arranging the colorful fruit and nuts around the edges. A small piece of toast and a glass of water completed the presentation. It was a simple, nourishing breakfast suited to her purposeful Tuesday, balancing her tax-compliance work with civic-shift records and the Town Hall role.
