Yuriko prepared Golden Morning Loaf, a comforting family-style breakfast bread. The key ingredients were whole-grain flour, eggs, water, baking powder, salt, and a hint of cinnamon. She folded the eggs into the flour, mixed the batter, and folded in chopped spinach and a few slices of lemon. The bread was baked until golden and crisp on the edges.

The aroma was warm and inviting, carrying the scent of toasted bread, lemon zest, and savory eggs. Yuriko presented the loaf on a white serving plate, arranging it with a small side of butter and lemon slices. It was a simple, practical meal prepared for a Tuesday morning when she had to drop off customer-service interview requirements at Hobbs Cafe, follow up with Michael about their arrival, and continue her tax-compliance work.
