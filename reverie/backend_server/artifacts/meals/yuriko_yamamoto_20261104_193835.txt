Yuriko prepared Japanese-style grilled chicken with lemon, garlic, and steamed jasmine rice. The main meat was seasoned with soy sauce, mirin, sugar, and finely minced garlic, then seared on a large cast-iron skillet until golden and cooked through. The rice was washed and steamed with a little vegetable oil, water, and a pinch of salt until it stayed fluffy.

Aromatically, the kitchen filled with the savory scent of garlic and searing meat, the bright sharpness of lemon, and the subtle fragrance of jasmine rice. She plated the dish with a few green onions scattered over the top, and the bright yellow rice contrasted beautifully with the golden chicken and pale green vegetables.

The meal was prepared for a quiet Monday dinner, perhaps for herself or a close friend, after Yuriko’s tax-compliance project and local-mayor election research. It was a simple, comforting evening meal, reflecting her practical, organized nature and the relaxed atmosphere of her everyday life.
