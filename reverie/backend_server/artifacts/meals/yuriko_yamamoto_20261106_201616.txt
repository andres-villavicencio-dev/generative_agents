On Monday, February 13, Yuriko prepared Chicken Teriyaki with Rice and Green Onions—a simple, comforting dinner suitable for a busy Monday. She bought a chicken breast, a block of firm tofu, fresh spinach, green onions, and rice, along with soy sauce, honey, ginger, and garlic.

First, she sliced the chicken and marinated it in soy sauce, honey, ginger, and garlic. She pan-fried it until golden, then cooked the tofu until crisp and the spinach until tender. The rice was rinsed and seasoned with the same sauce before being topped with the chicken and green onions.

The kitchen filled with the warm aroma of garlic, ginger, and slightly sweet soy sauce as she prepared it. The final presentation was elegant yet casual: the rice arranged around a bed of cooked vegetables, the golden chicken resting on top, and a few vibrant green onions garnishing the dish.

Yuriko chose the meal for a relaxed dinner after work, intending to enjoy it with her family. It was a simple yet thoughtful evening meal that balanced comfort with the organized, detail-oriented approach she brings to her tax career.
