Dish: Katsu Curry with Brown Rice and Broccoli

Yuriko prepared a comforting, family-style curry for her Monday dinner. She stirred brown rice into a slow-simmered pot of curry base, adding carrots, potatoes, and an abundance of chopped broccoli. For the protein, she pan-seared thin strips of pork belly until golden, then tossed the katsu in a creamy sauce made with soy sauce, tamari, ginger, garlic, and a hint of miso. A sprinkle of sesame seeds and chopped green onions completed the dish.

The aroma filled the kitchen with rich savory notes, warm curry, roasted garlic, and the savory scent of caramelized pork. Yuriko arranged the rice in the center, the curry around it, and the pan-fried katsu beside it, with the steamed broccoli adding a vibrant green contrast.

The meal was prepared for her Monday tax-compliance project—a relaxed, nourishing dinner after a busy workday. Her organized approach to cooking reflected her attention to detail: every ingredient measured, the seasoning balanced, and the presentation neat and inviting.
