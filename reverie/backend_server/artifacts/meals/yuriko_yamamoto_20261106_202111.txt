Yuriko prepared Kikunehi with Shiso and Ginger, a warm, savory side dish designed to complement her dinner of grilled salmon and rice. The dish features thick slices of pork belly, tender fish, and a generous portion of rice, all lightly browned in a pan. Shiso leaves and ginger are sliced thin and added to the pan during the final minutes, infusing the dish with bright green color and subtle freshness. A splash of soy sauce and a little sugar help balance the savory, lightly smoked flavors.

As she finished cooking, the kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of seared pork, toasted ginger, and the fragrant scent of citrus peel. The finished dish was arranged in a shallow oval bowl, with the golden-brown pork and rice layered on the side and the colorful shiso leaves placed nearby. Yuriko served it simply, offering it beside her salmon and rice. It was part of a quiet Monday evening, as she prepared a balanced meal while reflecting on her tax compliance project and thinking about who would run for the local mayor’s next election.
