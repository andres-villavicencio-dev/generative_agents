Yuriko prepared Miso-Tofu Salsa with Roasted Vegetables and Rice, a warm, colorful lunch for Monday, February 13. She began by sautéing carrots, onions, and bell peppers until lightly caramelized, then added a few cloves of garlic and a splash of soy sauce for savory depth. She mixed this base with a creamy miso sauce, fresh cilantro, a pinch of chili flakes, and a squeeze of lime.

For the rice, she cooked long-grain rice with a little vegetable broth and a teaspoon of sesame oil, then plated it alongside the colorful vegetable medley. She arranged the sliced tofu and roasted vegetables on the plate, garnishing the center with a small lime wedge and a fresh sprig of cilantro. The dish was served with a small side of pickled mustard beans.

Aromatic, slightly spicy, and warmly comforting, the salsa had the savory scent of toasted garlic and miso, with bright notes of cilantro and citrus. The presentation was simple but visually appealing, with the vibrant vegetables contrasting against the creamy, golden rice and tofu. It was an informal, home-style lunch for Yuriko, who wanted something nourishing and satisfying after her tax-compliance work.
