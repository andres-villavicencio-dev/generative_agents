Yuriko prepared Nigiri-style Salmon with Lemon-Tahini Sauce and Steamed Asparagus. The main component was fresh salmon fillets, which she lightly seared over medium-high heat before adding a small amount of rice wine and a splash of soy sauce. She topped the salmon with a crisp lemon-lime tahini sauce and served it beside steamed asparagus, which she added with a squeeze of lemon and a drizzle of sesame oil.

The kitchen filled with the warm, savory aroma of seared salmon, toasted sesame seeds, citrus, and a hint of garlic. The vibrant colors of the salmon, bright green asparagus, and golden lemon-tahini sauce created an appetizing presentation. The meal was prepared for Yuriko’s evening dinner, a quiet moment of relaxation after an intense Monday. It was not a festive occasion, but a simple, comforting dinner intended to nourish her body and ease her mind.
