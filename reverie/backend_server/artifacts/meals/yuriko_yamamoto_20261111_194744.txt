Yuriko prepared Lemon Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables, a simple yet comforting weekday meal. She marinated chicken thighs with lemon juice, garlic, herbs, and olive oil, then cooked them until golden and juicy. Alongside the chicken, she roasted carrots, zucchini, bell peppers, and onions until crisp-tender. She added a few cloves of garlic and a sprig of parsley before serving.

The aroma filled the kitchen with the warm scent of garlic, citrus, herbs, and roasted vegetables. The meal was presented on a white ceramic plate, with the golden chicken resting beside colorful vegetables that had turned bright orange and green. It was a relaxed lunch meant to balance her busy schedule.

Yuriko ate it on a quiet Tuesday while balancing work at the Willow Clinic, tax compliance research, and the Rose and Crown job search. The meal was not festive, but it was familiar, nourishing, and a small moment of calm in her demanding week.
