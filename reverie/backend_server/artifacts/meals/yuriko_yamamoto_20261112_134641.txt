Yuriko prepared a quick Japanese-style rice bowl with chicken, vegetables, and a light soy–ginger sauce. The dish included steamed rice, carrots, peas, sliced chicken breast, and a small handful of mushrooms. She seasoned the ingredients with a few drops of soy sauce, a little ginger, and a splash of sesame oil, cooking the chicken until it was tender and the vegetables crisp-tender.

The aroma was warm and savory, with the subtle tang of ginger, fresh garlic, and toasted sesame oil filling the kitchen as the chicken came out of the pan. Yuriko arranged the colorful ingredients in a neat circular pattern on the white ceramic bowl, drizzle of sauce over the top, and a few sprigs of green onion beside it. She presented it on a small plate for breakfast, choosing it for its comforting simplicity and easy preparation. The meal was a quiet contrast to the busy tax project and neighborhood election, offering her a moment to nourish herself before continuing her work.
