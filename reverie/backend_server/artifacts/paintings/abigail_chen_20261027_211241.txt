A contemporary digital-animated mural scene on Abigail Chen’s tablet, showing her hand tracing a glowing, translucent character with a glowing digital brush in the foreground, an ambiguous third mural with hidden narrative lines and a mysterious figure in the distance. The artwork should blend painterly realism with clean vector design, using a warm palette of ochre, terracotta, muted teal, indigo, cream, and soft blue to create an intimate, contemplative mood. A layered composition places the tablet’s interface and the character’s expressive gestures in the center, with balanced negative space, soft diffused window light, and subtle shadows giving the scene a luminous, emotionally resonant atmosphere.
