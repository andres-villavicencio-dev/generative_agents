An abstract, contemporary digital-art piece depicting Abigail Chen as a three-dimensional virtual artist surrounded by glowing screens, brush-like digital tools, and glowing circuit traces, with small animated particles drifting around her. The style is a surreal blend of fluid digital painting, isometric illustration, and clean tech-minimalism, rendered in a luminous palette of electric blue, indigo, rose, teal, warm amber, and soft cream. The composition places Abigail in the foreground, with layered screens and sketches arranged across the frame, creating a sense of depth and creative motion, while diffused, ethereal light and subtle reflections evoke a calm, focused, and hopeful mood.
