A warm, lyrical watercolor and oil painting of 69-year-old Amber Williamson gathering painting supplies at Johnson Park, carefully checking her finished silk-scarf backdrop while noting a final plan for her next story. Soft golden, sage green, misty blue, and dusty rose washes create a gentle, imaginative atmosphere, with her rounded figure and colorful garden elements arranged in a layered, intimate composition. Diffused morning light filters through the trees, casting long shadows and a peaceful, purposeful mood of quiet creativity, generosity, and the comfort of a busy but meaningful Tuesday morning.
