A warm, finely detailed watercolor-and-ink illustration of Amber Williamson, a retired storyteller in her sixties, carefully sketching a quiet corner of Oak Hill College’s entrance path and the surrounding garden, with her silk scarf draped over her shoulder and a notebook nearby. Soft washes of sage green, muted rose, dusty blue, and warm ochre create a nostalgic, intimate palette, while delicate line work and faint floral motifs suggest the garden’s hidden paths. The composition places Amber and the entrance hall in the foreground, with the park and college buildings receding into a gently illuminated, misty background, evoking a calm, generous, and hopeful mood.
