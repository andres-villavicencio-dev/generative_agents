A warmly inviting library room for the story hour, rendered in a lyrical, semi-illustrative style with softly textured brushwork. The composition features wooden tables, layered shelves, paper lanterns, and gentle curves of books and paper, with an elderly woman seated beside a child as the central subject. A soft palette of ochre, terracotta, muted sage, creamy white, and warm amber creates a cozy, welcoming atmosphere, while soft window light and diffused lantern glow establish a calm, intimate mood.
