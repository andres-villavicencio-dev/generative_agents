A warm, painterly scene of Amilcar Rosales, a 62-year-old market gardener in a weathered apron and sturdy work boots, carefully sketching a new market-garden layout onto a rough notebook with a fine reed pen. Surrounding him are rows of fresh produce, hummingbird feeders, and handwritten market notes, rendered in vivid terracotta, sage green, ochre, indigo, and muted ochre. Soft morning light filters through the garden, creating a peaceful, meticulous, and community-focused mood as Amilcar’s sociable, hardworking life blends modern precision agriculture with his grandparents’ traditional farming wisdom.
