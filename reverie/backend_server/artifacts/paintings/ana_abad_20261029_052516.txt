A warm, semi-illustrative watercolor and ink study of 28-year-old Ana Abad sketching at an easel in the Rose and Crown Pub, with detailed outlines of a café entrance, seating areas, and public space around her. Soft blue, sage green, ochre, terracotta, cream, and muted rose tones create a cozy, nostalgic atmosphere, while Ana’s focused expression and neatly arranged sketch convey creativity, resilience, and quiet purpose. The composition places her and the easel in the foreground, with the pub’s entrance and public space receding into the background, illuminated by gentle early-morning window light that adds depth and an inviting mood.
