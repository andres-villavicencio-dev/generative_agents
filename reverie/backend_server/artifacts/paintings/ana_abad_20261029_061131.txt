Ana Abad, a 28-year-old Dominican artist, stands at a wooden café table with a finished café illustration and a hand-filled sketchbook, her easel behind her holding a final sketch in progress. Rendered in a warm, semi-realistic watercolor and ink style, the artwork features a cozy Dominican café with a wooden counter, hanging lanterns, a rustic counter, and welcoming patrons, surrounded by coffee cups, brushes, and loose charcoal sketches. Use a muted palette of terracotta, sage green, cream, ochre, and deep indigo, with soft morning light filtering through the window and creating a serene, curious, and resilient mood. Her relaxed, purposeful composition emphasizes the finished illustration, the open sketchbook, and her focused expression, while delicate brushwork and fine paper textures add depth and visual interest.
