Diane Bosse, a 38-year-old meticulous hairdresser with a delicate expression, sits at a small wooden table in her Smallville salon, carefully sketching and arranging organic beauty products while comparing color-work sketches for Isabella Rodriguez’s appointment. Rendered in a warm, contemporary watercolor style, the scene uses muted sage greens, terracotta, dusty rose, cream, and soft ochres, with delicate botanical textures and finely detailed paper textures. The intimate composition places Diane at the center, surrounded by jars of organic ingredients, brushes, and layered color studies, illuminated by soft morning window light and gentle amber lamp glows that create a calm, quietly anxious mood.
