A finely detailed editorial illustration of Diane Bosse, a 38-year-old hairdresser and owner, quietly sketching salon expansion plans on a worn wooden table in Smallville, carefully arranging artwork, testing a neon glow, and preparing appointments for Isabella Rodriguez. Rendered in a contemporary mixed-media style with soft brushwork and layered textures, the composition emphasizes her focused expression, meticulous hands, and the orderly arrangement of art, circulation routes, and display areas. The palette blends warm terracotta, dusty rose, sage green, muted teal, and touches of neon blue and amber, while diffused morning light filters through the window, creating a grounded, purposeful, slightly anxious but ultimately energized mood.
