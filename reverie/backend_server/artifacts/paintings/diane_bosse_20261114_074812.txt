A contemporary editorial-style illustration of Diane Bosse in her Smallville salon studio, carefully sketching a balanced art-gallery layout while comparing organic product suppliers’ prices and product quality for foil applications. Soft, warm brushstrokes and muted sage green, terracotta, cream, and dusty rose create a calm, meticulous atmosphere, with subtle anxiety expressed through a tight, contemplative expression. The composition places Diane and the sketch at the center, surrounded by neatly arranged foil supplies, pigment palettes, small canvases, and the gallery’s proposed wall arrangement, illuminated by soft window light and a warm lamplight.
