A contemporary watercolor and ink-and-gouache illustration depicts an elderly chaplain, 64, quietly seated beside a riverside temple courtyard, his hand gently placed on a flowing vessel while an ethereal figure emerges from the water. Soft washes of mist, ripples, and reflected light create a contemplative, spiritual atmosphere, with muted sage, misty blue, warm ochre, and touches of crimson. The composition balances the figure in the foreground with the open, luminous landscape, while delicate brushwork and translucent paper textures evoke faith, continuity, and inner peace.
