A luminous watercolor landscape for the exhibition Where the Morning Settles, showing a quiet valley with layered trees, fields, and distant hills, with translucent glazes of color settling into the middle-ground forms and visible paper texture. The style is contemplative and highly refined, combining loose wet-on-wet washes with carefully controlled brushwork in a palette of misty indigo, slate blue, sage green, muted teal, ochre, and soft rose. The broad horizontal composition balances dark grasses and delicate branches in the foreground against open luminous sky and atmospheric distance, creating a serene, wise, and warmly nostalgic mood under gentle diffused morning light.
