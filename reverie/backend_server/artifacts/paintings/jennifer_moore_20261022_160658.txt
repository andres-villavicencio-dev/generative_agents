A watercolor portrait of Jennifer Moore, age 68, refining the delicate transitions between freshly applied washes on paper at her easel, her silver hair softly illuminated and her expression calm, wise, and warmly focused. Rendered in a traditional watercolor style with soft-edged forms, visible paper texture, subtle pigment blooms, and fluid brushwork, using a muted palette of indigo, cerulean, sage green, ochre, dusty rose, and warm cream. Compose the scene in a quiet, intimate studio with brushes, water containers, and stacked artwork softly receding into the background. Gentle morning light from the side creates a serene, contemplative mood and emphasizes the luminous layers of color.
