A warm, realistic watercolor portrait of Jennifer Moore, age 68, refining a selected painting at her easel while comparing it with exhibition-planning notes, her silver hair softly lit and her expression wise, experienced, and gently focused. The composition includes layered watercolor studies, brushes, pigment palettes, and loosely arranged sketchbook pages in an intimate studio setting, rendered with delicate washes, textured paper, soft edges, and visible brushwork. A muted palette of sage green, dusty rose, ochre, warm cream, and slate blue creates a nostalgic, contemplative mood, with diffused afternoon light and subtle golden highlights suggesting both creative rigor and the warmth of her long career.
