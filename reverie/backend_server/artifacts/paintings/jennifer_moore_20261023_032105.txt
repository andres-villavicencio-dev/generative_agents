A finely rendered winter watercolor study of a quiet snowy landscape, showing gently lifted and rearranged washes of mist, distant trees, and a warmly glowing house beside a frozen lake. Soft washes of pale blue, cool grey, muted teal, ochre, and cream create a serene, intimate composition with a layered foreground, middle scene, and receding horizon. Warm golden lamplight filters through the branches, casting gentle shadows and lending the scene a quiet, wise, contemplative mood.
