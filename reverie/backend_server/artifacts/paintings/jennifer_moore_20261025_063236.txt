A warm, softly rendered watercolor study of Jennifer Moore, a mature 68-year-old artist with silver hair, carefully preparing a small group of watercolors for her exhibition while sketching their composition. The scene is composed with her hands, brushes, and palette arranged in the foreground, with the exhibition space, garden, and subtle reflections behind her, unified by her contemplative gaze. Use muted sage greens, ochres, dusty rose, and warm umber, with delicate washes of blue and cream, creating a peaceful, wise, and intimate mood illuminated by gentle early-morning light.
