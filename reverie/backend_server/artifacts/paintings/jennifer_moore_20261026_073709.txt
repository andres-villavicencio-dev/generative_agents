A realistic watercolor painting of 68-year-old Jennifer Moore in a warm studio, carefully inspecting a sketchbook for remaining marks and areas needing attention. Use soft washes, delicate washes, and finely rendered brushwork to create a serene, intimate scene. The palette of muted ochres, sage green, dusty blue, warm terracotta, cream, and soft ochre evokes her fifty years of watercolor art. Arrange Jennifer beside her easel and unfinished sketches, with a quiet, purposeful mood and diffused morning light casting gentle shadows across the studio.
