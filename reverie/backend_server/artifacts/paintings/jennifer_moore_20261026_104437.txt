A luminous, painterly watercolor study of 68-year-old Jennifer Moore in her studio, carefully blocking in warm geometric shapes and establishing a soft palette of ochres, muted teal, dusty rose, cream, and faded blue, with visible washes and delicate paper texture. An intimate three-quarter composition places her seated at a wooden worktable, surrounded by brushes, palette, sketches, and watercolor papers, while a nearby easel holds a rough thumbnail of a landscape. Gentle morning light enters from the left, casting long warm shadows and creating a quiet, wise, purposeful atmosphere of experienced creativity.
