A warm, softly painterly watercolor of 68-year-old Jennifer Moore in her studio, carefully blocking the main shapes of a landscape and establishing a muted palette of ochre, sage, blue-gray, dusty rose, and cream. A loose pencil sketch sits on the worktable beside her, while brushes, pigment pots, and a watercolor wash stand nearby. Soft morning window light creates gentle shadows and subtle highlights across the forms, with a calm, wise, and deeply warm mood.
