A lyrical watercolor scene of 68-year-old Jennifer Moore walking through Johnson Park with her elderly companion Sam, sketching along the quiet route and pausing to check a faded park sign near Hobbs Cafe, with her watercolor palette nearby and notebook open in her hand. Rendered in a gentle, textured watercolor style with soft washes of sage green, moss green, muted terracotta, warm ochre, and delicate touches of blue, rendered in a warm, nostalgic palette. The intimate three-quarter composition captures Jennifer’s thoughtful expression, Sam’s relaxed hand, and the winding park path receding into the distance, illuminated by late-afternoon golden light filtering through the trees.
