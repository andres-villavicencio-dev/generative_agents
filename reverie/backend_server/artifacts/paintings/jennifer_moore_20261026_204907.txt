A warmly rendered watercolor portrait of 68-year-old Jennifer Moore in her studio, carefully inspecting the finished pieces of her artwork while sketching notes beside her, with brushes and pigment jars arranged around her. The soft, textured edges and delicate washes evoke an intimate watercolor style, using muted sage green, terracotta, ochre, dusty rose, cream, and faded blue-gray tones. An asymmetrical composition places Jennifer in the foreground with her sketchbook and palette, while the blurred studio window and warm window light create a calm, wise, and purposeful atmosphere.
