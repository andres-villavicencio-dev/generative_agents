A warm, watercolor-and-pastel-style illustration of 68-year-old Jennifer Moore in her studio, carefully blocking in the main shapes of her canvas and refining a rough color scheme for an upcoming exhibition. Soft washes of ochre, sage, muted teal, warm terracotta, and dusty rose surround her with brushes, palettes, and an easel, while an old faded Johnson Park sign suggests her recent walk with Sam. The composition places Jennifer and the sketching process in the foreground with the studio’s quiet, cozy atmosphere, illuminated by gentle morning light that creates a wise, patient, and deeply purposeful mood.
