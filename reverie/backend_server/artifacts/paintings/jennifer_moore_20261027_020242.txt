A warm watercolor illustration of 68-year-old artist Jennifer Moore sketching along a quiet path through Johnson Park with her companion Sam, while an elderly Adam Smith stands nearby offering a wise, side-by-side comparison of recent park proposals. Rendered in a lyrical, semi-illustrative style with soft brushwork, muted earth tones, sage green, faded teal, ochre, and dusty rose, the scene feels intimate and contemplative. The layered foreground emphasizes the path and faded signage, while Jennifer’s easel, watercolors, and scattered sketches create a balanced, nostalgic composition under gentle late-afternoon light.
