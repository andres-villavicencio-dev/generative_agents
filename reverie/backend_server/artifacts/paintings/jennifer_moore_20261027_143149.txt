A realistic watercolor painting of Jennifer Moore, a warm, wise 68-year-old artist, arranging brushes, paints, and a watercolor set in her sunlit studio while preparing for an upcoming exhibition. Soft washes of sage green, ochre, muted teal, dusty rose, and cream create a calm, nostalgic palette, with visible paper texture and delicate brushstrokes. An intimate three-quarter composition places Jennifer and her organized supplies at the center, with an easel, sketchbook, and jar of pigments creating a cozy, contemplative mood in the warm morning light.
