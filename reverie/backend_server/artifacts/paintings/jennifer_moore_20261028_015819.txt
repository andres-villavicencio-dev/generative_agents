A serene watercolor-and-gouache painting of 68-year-old Jennifer Moore carefully sketching a nearby tree and path in Johnson Park while checking notes for her Wednesday, February 15 watercolor exhibition. Soft washes of sage green, misty blue, warm ochre, and muted rose create a harmonious palette, with layered paper texture and delicate visible brushwork enhancing the intimate, quietly purposeful mood. The asymmetric composition places her figure and the nearby trees and paths in gentle foreground balance, illuminated by early morning mist and soft golden morning light casting long, peaceful shadows.
