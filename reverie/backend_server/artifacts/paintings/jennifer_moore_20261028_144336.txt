A warm, realistic watercolor study of Jennifer Moore, a 68-year-old artist in her studio, carefully sharpening and defining the edges of a landscape with a fine brush. The composition features her seated at a worn wooden desk surrounded by watercolor papers, a fine brush, and a palette, with a sketchbook, pencil, and jar of pigment nearby. Softly saturated washes of sage green, muted blue, dusty rose, ochre, and cream create a luminous, intimate palette, while gentle morning window light highlights the paper’s texture and renders the scene with a calm, wise, and purposeful mood.
