A luminous watercolor painting of Jennifer Moore, a 68-year-old woman in her studio, carefully blending wet pigment edges to unify the composition of a landscape scene. Use a muted palette of ochre, sage, muted terracotta, dusty rose, and soft blue to convey her warm, experienced, wise character and the gentle warmth of the artwork. Arrange her focused hands, brushes, and partially finished paintings in the foreground with the finished landscape receding into the background, illuminated by soft morning light filtering through a large window.
