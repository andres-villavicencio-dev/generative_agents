A warm, finely detailed watercolor painting of Jennifer Moore, a 68-year-old artist, carefully rinsing a brush and checking a clean, textured paper for a fresh surface before beginning her next session. Soft, translucent washes in muted blue, teal, sage, dusty rose, and warm ochre create a serene, contemplative mood, while delicate brushstrokes and visible paper grain give the scene an intimate, painterly realism. The composition places her hands, brushes, and paper in the foreground, with a tranquil garden scene softly blurred behind her, illuminated by gentle morning light filtering through a window.
