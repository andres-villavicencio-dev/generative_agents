A warm, watercolor-and-pencil exhibition portrait of 68-year-old Jennifer Moore at her home studio, carefully comparing a recent watercolor with her own hand-drawn sketch for an upcoming show, surrounded by brushes, paper, and canvases. Soft washes of sage green, muted ochre, terracotta, cream, and indigo create a painterly, intimate scene with visible brush textures and delicate ink lines. The balanced three-quarter composition captures her focused, wise expression and the quiet anticipation of preparing the exhibition, illuminated by gentle morning light filtering through a window.
