A luminous watercolor painting of a serene garden at Johnson Park, featuring a weathered wooden chair and a small wooden table surrounded by colorful flowers, with a softly focused background of rose and crown trees. Use an impressionistic watercolor style with translucent washes, delicate brushwork, and visible paper texture. The palette consists of muted sage green, dusty rose, warm ochre, soft blue-gray, and touches of terracotta. Arrange the composition so the chair and table occupy the lower third, with layered foliage and distant tree branches above, creating a balanced, intimate sense of place. Warm late-afternoon light filters through the branches, casting long gentle shadows, a peaceful nostalgic mood, and a subtle feeling of quiet companionship.
