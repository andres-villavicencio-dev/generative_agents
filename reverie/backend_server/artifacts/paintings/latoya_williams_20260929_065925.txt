Latoya Williams’ piece captures a lone figure standing before a vibrant mural on Hobbs Café’s side wall, where urban graffiti meets soft pastel hues of sunrise; rendered in a minimalist yet expressive digital painting style with a muted turquoise and coral palette that evokes quiet anticipation. The composition centers the figure in silhouette against a golden backlight, creating a serene mood that contrasts the city’s energetic buzz while emphasizing stillness and reflection.
