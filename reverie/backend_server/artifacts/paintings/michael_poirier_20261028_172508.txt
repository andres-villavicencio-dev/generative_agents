A whimsical fantasy character sketchbook by Michael Poirier, filled with dynamic comic-style illustrations of creatures, enchanted landscapes, and his school setting, with one large page showing a protagonist and his sketchbook resting beside a guitar, river, and digital canvas. The artwork blends expressive line work, textured paper textures, and soft watercolor washes in a dreamy palette of teal, moss green, ochre, burnt orange, and muted rose. Warm window light and gentle evening glow create a curious, energetic, and imaginative mood, while the balanced composition draws the viewer’s eye from the character to the hidden artwork in the sketchbook.
