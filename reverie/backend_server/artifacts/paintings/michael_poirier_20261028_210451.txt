A vivid, painterly storybook scene shows 14-year-old Michael Poirier sketching a fantastical river in his notebook while photographing the calm current, submerged roots, moss-covered stones, and towering trees with glowing ripples. Warm amber, teal, emerald green, and dusty rose paint the water and mist, while soft golden afternoon light and gentle shadows create a curious, imaginative mood. The composition places Michael and the river across the three-quarters frame, with detailed natural elements layered in the background to draw the eye into the tranquil, dreamlike world.
