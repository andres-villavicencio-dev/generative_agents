A luminous, semi-realistic editorial painting of Michael Poirier, a 14-year-old student at Oak Hill College, standing beside the winding River of Possibilities while sketching a fantasy character with a pencil and photographing the water with a camera. The composition places him in the lower third, with the river meandering behind him, a fantasy river creature emerging from the water, and an open sketchbook, Procreate canvas, and Procreate app floating nearby. Soft golden morning light, cool blues, teal, violet, and warm amber tones create a dreamy, curious, and imaginative mood, with diffused window light, gentle ripples, and subtle mist enhancing the tranquil, purposeful atmosphere.
