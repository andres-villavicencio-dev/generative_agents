A luminous, ethereal fantasy river scene rendered in a loose digital painterly style, with misty reeds, flowing water, and three mysterious characters emerging from the mist among towering trees. The composition uses layered foreground, middle ground, and background to guide the eye from the riverbank toward the glowing figures, while a vibrant palette of turquoise, teal, moss green, indigo, and warm amber creates a dreamlike atmosphere. Soft, golden morning light filters through the canopy, casting gentle highlights and long shadows, while the quiet, curious expressions of the characters evoke wonder, imagination, and purposeful discovery.
