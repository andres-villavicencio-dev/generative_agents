A vibrant, contemporary fantasy-illustration scene depicts 14-year-old Michael Poirier at The Willows Market, sketching a whimsical river deity with a pencil and camera while conversing with Francisco and Mei L over a pub night. The composition places Michael and the lively street scene in the foreground, with a large, glowing mural of a river deity and the bustling market filling the background. Warm golden lamp light and soft evening window glow create a nostalgic, dreamy mood, while a cool teal-blue and ochre palette adds energy without losing a sense of calm.
