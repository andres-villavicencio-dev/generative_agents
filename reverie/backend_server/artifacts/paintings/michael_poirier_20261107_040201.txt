A vibrant, semi-realistic watercolor-and-ink illustration of young Michael Poirier sitting at a wooden table in Oak Hill College, sketching a fantasy river with a pencil and a camera, while the flowing water’s shifting blue, teal, and gold hues are carefully noted in a journal. The composition places him in the foreground with the river’s luminous reflections behind him, rendered in flowing, lyrical lines and soft, translucent washes. Soft early-morning window light creates a gentle, imaginative mood, with a balanced foreground, midground figure, and background that gives the scene a serene, creative energy.
