A serene portrait of Rajiv Patel seated in quiet contemplation at his artists' co-living space, painted in a soft, impressionistic style with delicate brushstrokes reminiscent of Monet’s light and atmosphere; the color palette blends muted earth tones with gentle hues of dawn—pale golds, cool blues, and warm ochres—to reflect tranquility and focus. The composition centers on his stillness, framed by an open window revealing a hush of early morning light, evoking a mood of peaceful introspection and creative balance.
