A detailed still life of a weathered oil paint palette and three brushes resting on a wooden workbench, bathed in the warm, golden light of late afternoon streaming through a nearby window. The artistic style is a blend of impressionistic realism and contemporary realism, featuring a vibrant, saturated color palette of deep Prussian blue, cadmium red, and earthy ochre, contrasted against the warm amber glow.
