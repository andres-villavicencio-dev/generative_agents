A contemplative painter, Rajiv Patel, works at an easel in a cozy artists’ co-living space, brushing muted abstract forms onto a large canvas while canvases, pigments, and a guitar rest nearby. The artwork has a warm, earthy palette of terracotta, ochre, sage green, dusty blue, and cream, with soft, layered textures evoking quiet memories and everyday domestic life. A balanced composition places Rajiv in gentle profile beside the luminous canvas, surrounded by overlapping shapes and subtle architectural lines. Soft natural window light creates a peaceful, intimate mood, with delicate shadows enhancing the serene, unhurried atmosphere of his first solo exhibition.
