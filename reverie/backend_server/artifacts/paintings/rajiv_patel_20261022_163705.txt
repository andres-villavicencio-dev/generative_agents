A textured contemporary abstract painting by 27-year-old artist Rajiv Patel, featuring layered, controlled brushstrokes that build tactile ridges, soft scraped passages, and expressive impasto across the canvas. The artwork uses a warm, earthy palette of burnt sienna, ochre, muted teal, deep indigo, and creamy ivory, with occasional flashes of rust-red, creating a quiet yet energetic rhythm. Balanced asymmetrical composition with dense, layered passages on the right dissolving into more open, lightly brushed negative space on the left. Soft directional studio lighting casts delicate shadows over the raised paint, emphasizing the handmade surface and producing a contemplative, patient, and quietly cheerful mood.
