A contemporary mixed-media landscape painting titled “Quiet Studio,” depicting Rajiv Patel’s 27-year-old artist co-living space, with a large, textured canvas resting on a wooden table beside a guitar, a brush, and a freshly dried painting. The composition uses warm earthy terracotta, muted ochre, sage green, and soft indigo tones, with visible brushstrokes and layered paint creating a cozy, creative atmosphere. Soft morning window light casts gentle shadows across the workspace, giving the quiet, patient mood a sense of calm anticipation for Rajiv’s first solo show.
