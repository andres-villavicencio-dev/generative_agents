A warm, impressionistic realism painting of Rajiv Patel seated at a wooden studio table, applying the first layer of paint with a brush while organizing brushes, pigments, and canvases in an artists’ co-living space. Soft, muted colors—slate blue, terracotta, sage green, cream, and pale gold—create a balanced palette, with the canvas glowing beneath the brushstrokes in the foreground. The intimate composition combines Rajiv’s focused gesture with neatly arranged studio supplies and the quiet garden beyond, illuminated by gentle morning light, evoking patience, hope, and the peaceful rhythm of creative living.
