A serene contemporary landscape painting of Rajiv Patel’s first solo show, composed with an intimate, painterly style and layered brushwork. Warm gold, ochre, burnt sienna, muted teal, and soft cream tones create a luminous palette centered on the gallery space, intimate paintings, and a quiet outdoor courtyard. Diffused golden-hour light filters through the windows, casting gentle shadows and adding a peaceful, hopeful mood, while the balanced composition suggests calm, creativity, and anticipation.
