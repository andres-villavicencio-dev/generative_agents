A contemporary realistic watercolor-and-gouache painting of Rajiv Patel at an easel, carefully refining the edges of a human-form figure and its surrounding garden objects, with a quiet, contemplative mood. Soft washes of sage green, muted sage, dusty blue, warm ochre, and terracotta create a harmonious palette, while the composition places the painter and easel prominently in the foreground with delicate details. Gentle early-morning window light casts diffused highlights across the canvas and brushes, evoking a peaceful, focused atmosphere amid a quiet studio.
