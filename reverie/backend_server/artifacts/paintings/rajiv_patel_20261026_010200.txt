A contemporary realist painting of Rajiv Patel, a 27-year-old painter, carefully returning a completed canvas to the easel and marking “Work Done,” with an easel, brushes, and a guitar leaning nearby in an artists’ co-living studio. Use a soft, painterly style with muted sage green, terracotta, ochre, and charcoal tones, accented by the warm gold of his guitar and the deep blue of a nearby painting. Arrange the scene in a calm, three-quarter composition with the easel as the focal point, illuminated by gentle morning light filtering through a window to create a quiet, patient, and cheerful mood.
