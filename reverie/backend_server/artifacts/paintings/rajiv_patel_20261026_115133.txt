A finely detailed painting in progress, titled Garden Stillness – The Quiet Canvas, shows Rajiv Patel at a wooden easel in a softly lit artists’ co-living studio, carefully recording the completed section of his landscape while preparing the next canvas. The artwork features gentle garden paths, misty trees, and delicate brushstrokes, rendered in a lyrical realism style with soft, textured edges. A warm palette of sage green, terracotta, muted ochre, dusty blue, and cream creates a serene, contemplative atmosphere, while natural window light casts calm shadows and highlights the evolving layers of paint.
