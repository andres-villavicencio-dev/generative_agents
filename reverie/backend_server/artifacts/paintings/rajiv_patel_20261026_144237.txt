A contemporary painter, Rajiv Patel, sits calmly in the artist’s co-living space, carefully clearing his easel and wiping brushes before beginning a new painting. The scene is rendered in a luminous, semi-realistic style with soft brushwork and quiet naturalism, using muted sage, terracotta, ochre, dusty blue, cream, and warm amber tones. A partially completed landscape and several brushes create a balanced composition around him, while gentle early-morning window light casts a peaceful, contemplative glow across the room.
