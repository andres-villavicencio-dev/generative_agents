A textured contemporary landscape painting on a rustic wooden easel, featuring layered brushstrokes, soft hills, distant trees, and a tranquil garden path leading toward a quiet cabin. Use a muted palette of ochre, sage, terracotta, dusty blue, warm umber, and soft cream, with subtle visible paper grain and delicate, imperfect brushwork. The composition should balance the rising canvas in the foreground with an open, peaceful scene beyond, creating a sense of calm and creative anticipation. Diffused morning window light casts gentle shadows across the room, while a warm amber lamp adds a cozy, intimate glow.
