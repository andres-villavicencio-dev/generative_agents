A contemporary realist painting of Rajiv Patel, a calm Indian painter at the easel, applying his first brushstroke to create a textured layered background for an upcoming solo exhibition; the canvas fills the composition with organic earthy patterns, visible brushwork, and subtle paper texture. Use a muted palette of terracotta, ochre, burnt sienna, sage green, and dusty blue, with muted blue jeans and a simple wooden easel visible in the foreground. Arrange the scene with the easel and brushes in the lower third and an empty gallery space receding behind him, illuminated by soft early-morning window light that creates gentle shadows and a quiet, patient, contemplative mood.
