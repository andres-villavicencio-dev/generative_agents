A contemporary realist painting of 27-year-old artist Rajiv Patel continuing his first solo show, carefully adding a new color to the central part of an expansive canvas in the artists’ co-living space, surrounded by brushes, pigments, and an unused easel. Rendered in a lyrical semi-abstract style with visible brushwork and gentle texture, the composition features the artist’s hands and the central canvas as the focal point, with layered natural forms and carefully balanced surrounding elements. Use a muted palette of terracotta, sage green, ochre, soft blue, and cream, illuminated by warm, diffused morning light that creates a patient, quietly cheerful mood.
