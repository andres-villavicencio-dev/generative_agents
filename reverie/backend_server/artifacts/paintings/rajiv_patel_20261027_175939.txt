A warm, contemporary realist painting of Rajiv Patel, a patient 27-year-old painter, carefully arranging brushes, palettes, canvas, and supplies at the artists’ co-living space before storing his finished work and preparing for a first solo show. Use a soft, textured brushstroke style with muted terracotta, sage, olive green, dusty rose, cream, and charcoal accents, creating a quiet, orderly atmosphere. Compose the scene in three-quarter view, showing Rajiv seated at a small worktable with brushes and canvases in the foreground and neatly stacked supplies behind him, illuminated by gentle morning light from a nearby window.
