A realistic, semi-illustrative painting of Rajiv Patel at the artists’ co-living space, carefully arranging brushes and pigments by function in labeled containers while holding a fresh canvas, with his guitar practice nearby. The composition uses an intimate three-quarter view with brushes, pigments, labeled storage jars, a palette, and his studio supplies arranged in a clean, organized foreground. A muted palette of warm ochres, terracotta, sage green, cream, and soft blue creates a quietly contemplative mood, balanced by soft morning light and gentle shadows.
