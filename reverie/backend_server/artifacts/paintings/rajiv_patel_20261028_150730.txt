A contemporary abstract painting of a geometric, fragmented canvas resting on an easel, with visible brushstrokes, layered forms, and subtle geometric patterns suggesting rhythm and structure. Warm ochres, terracotta, muted ochre, sage green, dusty blue, and soft cream create a warm, harmonious palette, while a gentle misty horizon gives the scene depth. The balanced composition features the artist’s hands, brushes, and canvas in the foreground, with abstract forms receding into the background, illuminated by soft golden-lim light that creates a peaceful, contemplative mood.
