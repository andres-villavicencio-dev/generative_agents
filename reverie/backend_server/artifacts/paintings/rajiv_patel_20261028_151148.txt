A contemporary, expressive watercolor-and-gouache painting of Rajiv Patel at his easel in an artists’ co-living space, with a confident, flowing brushstroke emerging from the foreground and a partially finished landscape canvas visible behind him. Soft washes of sage green, muted teal, ochre, dusty rose, and warm cream create a calm, painterly palette in a loose, textured style with visible brushwork and subtle translucent layers. The warm window light casts a gentle glow over his easel and guitar, evoking a quiet, patient, and cheerful mood of creative anticipation for his first solo show.
