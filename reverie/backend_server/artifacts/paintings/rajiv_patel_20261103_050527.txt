A contemporary oil-painting-style landscape study in which Rajiv Patel, a focused painter, stands at an easel adding a geometric shape to a carefully layered composition of hills, trees, and a distant cottage. Soft muted blues, sage greens, ochres, and warm cream tones create a harmonious palette, while delicate brushwork and subtle atmospheric details suggest a quiet, contemplative mood. Natural window light enters from the left, casting gentle shadows across the easel and landscape, with the artist’s steady posture and the new geometric addition emphasizing purpose, patience, and creative growth.
