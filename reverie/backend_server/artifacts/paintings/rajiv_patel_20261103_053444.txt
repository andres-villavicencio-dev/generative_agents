A serene contemporary landscape painting of Rajiv Patel at his easel, carefully reapplying paint over visible brushwork with a fine brush, while two small easels and canvases stand nearby. Use a luminous palette of warm ochre, muted terracotta, sage green, terracotta, and soft cream, with subtle blue-gray shadows in the background. Arrange Rajiv’s focused expression, brushes, and landscape elements in a balanced, intimate three-quarter composition, illuminated by warm morning light streaming through a window, creating a patient, cheerful, quietly purposeful mood.
