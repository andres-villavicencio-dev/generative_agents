A contemporary mixed-media landscape painting on an easel, with Rajiv Patel adding a third visible layer of paint to a textured composition of mountains, fields, and a quiet village scene. Use a warm earthy palette of ochre, terracotta, sage green, muted blue, and soft gold, with visible brushstrokes, layered glazes, and subtle pigment texture. Arrange the subject in a balanced three-quarter composition, with the easel and emerging paint leading the eye toward a softly glowing horizon. Soft morning window light creates gentle shadows and luminous highlights, conveying patience, hope, and a peaceful, purposeful mood.
