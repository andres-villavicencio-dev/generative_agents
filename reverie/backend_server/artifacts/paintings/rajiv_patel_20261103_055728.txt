A contemporary landscape painting in soft realist realism, featuring a quiet easel, a canvas with a carefully adjusted focal point on a distant hill, and a calm, textured brushwork style. Use a palette of muted sage green, warm ochre, dusty blue, and creamy white, with gentle washes and visible brush textures.
