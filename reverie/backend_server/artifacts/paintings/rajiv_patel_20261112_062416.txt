A contemporary mixed-media solo exhibition on a wooden gallery wall, featuring a large landscape sketch, an oil painting, a textured brush, and a guitar resting beside an artist’s sketchbook, with Rajiv Patel standing in the quiet studio between three layered canvases. Render in a warm, softly painterly realism with muted terracotta, sage green, ochre, indigo, cream, and warm amber tones. Use an intimate three-quarter composition with balanced negative space, gentle natural light from a window, and subtle shadows to create a peaceful, patient, and quietly cheerful mood.
