Rajiv Patel, a young Indian artist and musician, works carefully at a cluttered wooden worktable in his artists’ co-living studio, checking a framed exhibition label, brushes, paint tubes, and canvases for any remaining issues. Render him as a realistic yet softly painterly figure with a paintbrush in one hand, guitar in the other, and a small notebook beside him, with an easel, brushes, pigments, and a partially completed canvas arranged in a warm, cluttered composition. Use a palette of ochre, terracotta, muted teal, sage green, and warm cream, accented with deep reds and gold highlights, creating a patient, cheerful, quietly focused mood. Soft golden window light enters from the left, casting gentle shadows, while an intimate close-up composition emphasizes his attentive expression, the tactile details of the artwork, and the cozy, domestic atmosphere of his creative studio.
