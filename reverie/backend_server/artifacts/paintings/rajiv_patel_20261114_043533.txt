A contemporary mixed-media abstract painting of a small home studio, with a visible artist’s easel, brushes, canvases, and a guitar resting nearby, rendered in loose, expressive brushwork and textured layers. Use a warm palette of terracotta, ochre, muted blue, cream, and deep indigo, with the guitar’s acoustic curves subtly echoing the abstract forms. Arrange the composition in a balanced three-quarter view, with layered foreground details, a softly glowing window, and a luminous, intimate atmosphere. Diffused late-afternoon light creates gentle shadows and warm highlights, conveying patience, quiet contemplation, and the cheerful anticipation of Rajiv Patel’s first solo show.
