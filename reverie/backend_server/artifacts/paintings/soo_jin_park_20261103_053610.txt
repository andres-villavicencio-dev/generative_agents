A serene, painterly illustration of 27-year-old freelance illustrator Soo-jin Park walking through Johnson Park on a Saturday afternoon, sketching the winding paths, benches, and bustling street life while she reflects on her restless mood and dreams of saving for a luxury lifestyle. Soft, warm hues of sage green, dusty rose, cream, and muted terracotta create a dreamy palette, with her relaxed posture and contemplative expression emphasized in the foreground. A balanced three-quarter composition places the park in the background and Soo-jin’s notebook in the foreground, illuminated by gentle golden sunlight filtering through the trees and casting a calm, hopeful glow across the neighborhood.
