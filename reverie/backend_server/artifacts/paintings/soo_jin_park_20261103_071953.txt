A warm, editorial-style illustration of Soo-jin Park, a 27-year-old freelance illustrator and community artist, standing in the quiet garden of Johnson Park with her easel, brushes, and a sketchbook filled with townpeople in various poses. Rendered in a refined, semi-realistic watercolor mixed with delicate linework, using muted sage green, dusty blue, cream, ochre, and touches of terracotta to evoke nostalgia and gentle creativity. The composition places her centered in the frame, with Johnson Park’s trees, pathways, and distant buildings creating a layered urban backdrop, while soft early-morning light filters through the branches and creates a dreamy, contemplative mood.
