A serene watercolor painting of an ethereal night sky filled with scattered luminous stars that seem to float in infinite depth and gentle movement. The artwork features soft, blended hues of deep indigo, velvet purple, and warm golden amber where distant galaxies merge with the cosmos, creating a dreamy atmosphere without distinct boundaries between reality and imagination. Soft diffused light filters through subtle cloud formations above, casting an overall hazy glow that enhances the tranquil mood while maintaining high-definition clarity on every single star.
