A contemporary street-art mural on the wall behind the Willow Clinic, featuring a large, glowing neon heart intertwined with a stylized paramedic badge and a community-emergency symbol. Bold, electric blue, magenta, and amber neon light contrasts with warm amber and teal graffiti texture, using sharp geometric lines and layered brushwork. The composition places the heart and badge at the center, with a quiet neighborhood sidewalk and soft evening glow creating a calm, hopeful mood under balanced, cinematic street-art lighting.
