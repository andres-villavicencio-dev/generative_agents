A vivid street-art-style mural depicts Tony Stewart, a 37-year-old paramedic, transforming an Oak Hill College library canvas with energetic brushstrokes, while a local mural artist works nearby. The mood is optimistic, creative, and unflappable, capturing Tony’s blend of calm paramedic focus and spontaneous, community-engaging spirit.
