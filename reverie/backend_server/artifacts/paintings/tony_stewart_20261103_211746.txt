A dynamic, semi-realistic neon mural depicts a cluster of paramedics in dark coats and emergency uniforms positioned around an illuminated clinic entrance at the Willow Clinic, with Tony Stewart sketching a glowing, abstract version of their arrangement in the foreground. The artwork combines sharp vector-style line work with layered, painterly textures, using deep indigo, electric blue, magenta, coral, and warm amber against a muted hospital-gray wall. Dramatic neon light spills from above and from the scene, casting long reflections and creating a tense, hopeful mood of calm focus amid the rush of emergency calls.
