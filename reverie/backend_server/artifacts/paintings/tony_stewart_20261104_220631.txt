A vivid, contemporary street-art-style mural depicting a calm Willow Clinic skyline, with glowing neon emergency lights, colorful paramedic symbols, abstract medical icons, and twisting vines blending into a futuristic urban corridor. The composition uses tall vertical windows, emergency vehicles, and layered geometric shapes to create a dynamic, three-dimensional space. Warm amber, electric blue, teal, coral, and muted gold glow from hidden street lamps, with soft flashlight beams and subtle brush textures adding depth and a hand-drawn, slightly dreamlike mood.
