A hyper-detailed street-art mural inspired by emergency care, with stylized paramedics, glowing emergency lights, a neon cross, and a Willow Clinic sign subtly integrated into the scene. The artwork is rendered in a vibrant neon-soaked street-art style, using electric blues, hot pinks, magentas, and luminous yellow-green highlights against a dark wall. The composition places the mural in the background, with Tony Stewart carefully tracing it and checking its alignment beside the clinic entrance, while soft window light and warm street lamps create a calm, unflappable mood of quiet concentration and creative urgency.
