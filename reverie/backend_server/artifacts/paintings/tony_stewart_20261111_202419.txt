A contemporary street-art scene depicts Tony Stewart, a 37-year-old paramedic, sketching a glowing neon mural behind the Willow Clinic with the community-path route from the clinic to the nearby park emerging through him. The mural features luminous orange, teal, and magenta lights along a winding path between a small-town clinic and a nearby park, while Tony’s focused, confident expression and careful hand capture his creative energy. Warm street-lamp illumination mixes with cool neon hues, creating a calm, hopeful mood with an energetic composition that balances the clinical setting, the park, and the artist’s creative process.
