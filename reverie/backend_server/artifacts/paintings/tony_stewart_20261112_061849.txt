A dynamic, painterly street-art sketch depicts Tony Stewart, a focused 37-year-old paramedic at the Willow Clinic, sketching a glowing neon mural on the clinic’s exterior wall. The artwork features angular, electric-cyan and magenta geometric shapes, a glowing red-and-blue emergency symbol, and stylized lightning bolts, rendered in bold brushwork, layered textures, and vivid color with strong chiaroscuro contrast. Warm lamplight and cool neon glow create a tense, hopeful mood, while the curved clinic entrance and nearby emergency vehicles form a balanced, dynamic composition.
