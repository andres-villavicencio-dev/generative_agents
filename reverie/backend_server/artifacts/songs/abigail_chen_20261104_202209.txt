Verse 1  
I stood before the easel tonight,  
The background waiting, soft and plain.  
I wanted more—more light, more room,  
To make the image sing instead of hum.  
I tweaked the edges, smoothed the corners,  
Let every pixel breathe.  
The composition shifted, slowly,  
Like thoughts waking up inside.

Chorus  
Oh, technology has its own voice,  
A new way to tell a story.  
I refine the background, refine the line,  
Turn static shapes into living mind.  
With every minor change,  
The whole becomes a scene.  
I’m making art, not just a screen,  
A digital dream, a canvas of the unseen.

Verse 2  
I tested the animation, checked the timing,  
Found rhythm in the subtle shifting.  
The audience was curious, bright, and ready,  
To follow the path where I led.  
It wasn’t perfect at first glance,  
But it was mine.  
I learned that beauty often hides  
In the details, in the little, quiet chance.
