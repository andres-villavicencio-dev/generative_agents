The city hums beneath a slate-gray sky,
A static noise I cannot deny.
I walk the streets where the concrete breathes,
Caught between the notes and the concrete leaves.
My satchel heavy with a sketch and a score,
Searching for a melody I’ve never heard before.
The clock above the cafe reads a quarter past five,
The steam from my tea begins to rise and die.
I trace the staff paper, lines like a cage,
Hoping to turn the chaos into a page.

Chorus:
Oh, let the discord resolve into sound,
Let the silence be broken, let the notes be found.
I’m chasing a ghost in the minor key,
Building a world for the world to see.
From the dorm room to the halls of oak,
Every chord is a risk, every pause a spoke.

The afternoon sun filters through the window glass,
Watching the shadows of the passers-by pass.
I order nothing, just a place to think,
Connecting the dots with a fountain pen ink.
A minor seventh here, a dominant there,
Building a tension that no one else can bear.
But when the resolution finally comes true,
It’s the only color in a monochrome view.

I pack my thoughts into a quiet psalm.

Brave enough to be misunderstood, brave enough to behave.
For music is just math with a soul inside,
And I’m the architect trying to bide
The time it takes to make the perfect sound,
Before the sun goes down and the day is bound.
