The morning light cuts through the blinds,
A quiet hour before the grind.
I grab my notebook, head to class,
Watching the minutes slowly pass.
Ten o'clock the doors unlock,
New lectures, waiting to be unlocked.

But the real work waits at two p.m.,
Where the steam and the pastries loom.
Hobbs Cafe, the wooden tables,
Where the creative thoughts unravel.
A latte bitter, a croissant sweet,
The rhythm of the street below the seat.
I sketch out motifs on the napkin,
Trying to find the sound I’m chasing.

Sometimes the silence is too loud,
Searching for a promised cloud.
But I keep humming, keeping time,
Building up a fragile rhyme.
With every chord and every line,
I’m turning the lead into gold, I’m fine.
