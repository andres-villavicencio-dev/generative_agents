Title: The Melody of Now

(Verse 1)
The classroom lights dim, the day's work done,
I step out into the evening's gloom.
My guitar strings hum a low, gentle tune,
Reflecting on the lessons I have known.
Curiosity drives my step, I seek new sounds,
To expand what I already have.

(Chorus)
Oh, the melody of now, the rhythm of life,
A blend of theory and personal strife.
From Oak Hill to the cafe, I find my way,
Composing every moment I can lay.

(Verse 2)
Dinner sits on the table, warm and slow,
I review the chords I've been so slow to hold.
The afternoon sun casts a soft, golden glow,
As I study the past and look to the future.
Hobbs Cafe waits, I'm ready to begin,
To explore the music that I never seen.

(Chorus)
Oh, the melody of now, the rhythm of life,
A blend of theory and personal strife.
From Oak Hill to the cafe, I find my way,
Composing every moment I can lay.

(Outro)
So let the chords ring, let the keys click,
This is my world, this is my life.
The final draft is done, I'll leave it here,
With a song that speaks to everyone I care.
