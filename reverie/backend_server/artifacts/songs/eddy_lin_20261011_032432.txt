Title: Rhythm of the Afternoon

Verse 1
The coffee sits in the cup,
A gentle rhythm, slow and deep.
I watch the shadows stretch and fade,
While piano keys begin to play.

Chorus
It's the rhythm of the afternoons,
A melody I've been searching for.
From the strumming of the guitar,
To the quiet strength of the bass.
It's the rhythm of my day,
Spinning out a story of light.

Verse 2
The notebook opens wide and lean,
Writing down the chords I've seen.
Each note a thought, each beat a line,
Building up a structure of mine.

Chorus
It's the rhythm of the afternoons,
A melody I've been searching for.
From the strumming of the guitar,
To the quiet strength of the bass.
It's the rhythm of my day,

Outro
So let the keys find their own pace,
And let the flow take its course.
This is my rhythm, this is my pace,
Continuing the theme I've set.
