“Counterpoint at Five”

[Verse 1]  
At ten I cross the campus with a melody in mind,  
A counterpoint still searching for a line I hadn’t planned.  
I map the passing rhythms, let the harmony unwind,  
Then carry unfinished phrases where the afternoon stands.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Every silence holds a possibility,  
Every chord can open up a door.  
I’m still learning how the notes can be  
A little more than what they were before.  

[Chorus]  
Write it down, let the melody climb,  
Find the pulse and give it space and time.  
One note leads, another answers,  
Two lines become a song of mine.  
At Hobbs Cafe, the evening settles down,  
While I turn a thought into a sound.  
I don’t need the whole world to know—  
I’m building something from a single note.  

[Verse 2]  
The bass line moves like footsteps on a quiet street,  
The chords reflect the colors of the fading light.  
I test a phrase, revise it, let the tempo change,  
And hear the missing part in every choice.  

[Bridge]  
Maybe this is how a person learns to grow—  
By listening past the obvious refrain,  
By making space for what the notes still know  
And giving them a voice through every strain.
