Title: Static in the Room

Verse 1  
The metronome ticks, a steady heartbeat  
I count through twelve and then one  
Every chord changes the room’s color  
A minor key, a sudden fear.  
I listen for the space between the notes  
Not just what they say, but what they mean.  
The guitar’s a tangled string of stories  
Carrying every mistake and every dream.

Chorus  
Static in the room, a quiet voice  
Beneath the noise that keeps us alive  
I trace the harmony like a map  
Where all the tangled roots lead  
We build our song from broken pieces  
One imperfect, honest melody.

Verse 2  
I study scales and listen to the past  
From classical rooms to jazz and street-side light  
A bluesman’s bending note makes the key  
A minor chord, a sharp leap, a whole note.  
I want to make music feel real  
Not perfect, not polished, not afraid.

Bridge  
Maybe a minor key is not a cage  
Maybe it’s where the music begins  
We can follow the tension and release  
Turn every mistake into a release.  
Every pause is a space for breath  
Even in a crowded, noisy street.
