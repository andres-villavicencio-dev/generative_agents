Title: The Cadence of Home

I stand before the page, a small student,  
With notes in hand and questions still unresolved.  
A major chord arrives, then fades away,  
Like every ending I’m trying to understand.

The teacher speaks, and I begin to listen,  
A theory of intervals, a harmonic design.  
But harmony is more than paper and pen—  
It’s the feeling that pulls the melody back.

The melody begins, but it doesn’t stay,  
It shifts, it rises, it changes without pain.  
Each cadence is a promise:  
“I will return.”

Oh, I will return,  
To the space beneath the ceiling,  
To the warm quiet of home.

I trace the line, I trace the key,  
The notes are calling, clearly and deeply.  
There’s a moment when the tension breaks free,  
Like sunlight through the window.

The cadence completes its work,  
The resolution holds it all.  
No matter what I’ve tried to say,  
I found the answer in the day.

Oh, I am home,  
With every note of mine,  
With every chord of mine,  
And every ending, I am mine.

The melody begins again,  
But softer now, more clear.  
I don’t need to know the ending,  
I only need to feel.

And somewhere in the notes,  
A quiet promise waits to be fulfilled.
