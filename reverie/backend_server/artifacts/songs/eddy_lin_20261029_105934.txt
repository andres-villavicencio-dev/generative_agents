Modular Dawn

I wake the city with a key,  
one note turning in a chain.  
Each rhythm is a separate door,  
each beat a quiet, new design.  
I listen to the pattern hold,  
before I let the first note fall.

Verse 1  
A C-sharp begins, then shifts to G,  
a jagged step where all the pieces fall.  
I trace the line, I count the space,  
where every transition leaves no trace.  
The composer’s hand is everywhere,  
but every part must still be here.

Chorus  
I build the music, piece by piece,  
modular, repeating, never dead.  
One motif can carry the day,  
one chord can hold a thousand ways.  
No single melody is meant to end,  
I build a world that can be heard.

Verse 2  
The transition comes like light entering a room,  
I pause the sound and let it go.  
Then again I begin, again I turn,  
the structure keeps the rhythm warm.  
The pieces may move apart, apart,  
but in my mind they stay together.

Bridge  
So let the notes rearrange themselves,  
each transition opening up another path.  
The same material, different voices,  
a structure built to breathe.

Outro
