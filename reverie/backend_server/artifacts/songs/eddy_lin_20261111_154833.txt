Title: “Open the Door”

Verse 1  
I opened the notebook at seven,  
curious about every note.  
The first chord felt like a question,  
a little door unlatched.  
I traced the rhythm on the page,  
watching time become a map.  
Every interval whispered,  
“Where is the song?”

Chorus  
So I’ll begin with a clear tune,  
a melody that takes the space.  
Open the door and step inside,  
listen to the rhythm of the place.  
I’ll weave the theory into the air,  
turn notes into a path.  
One note at a time,  
I’ll find where the song begins.

Verse 2  
Hobbs Cafe lights the afternoon,  
a rhythm beneath my feet.  
Coffee tastes like an idea,  
study feels like a new theme.  
I change the tempo on the piano,  
let the structure shift.  
Sometimes a bar can become a bridge,  
sometimes a pause can become a sigh.

Bridge  
Let the bass take the floor,  
let the harmony feel bold.  
No rule can hold the melody back,  
and the rhythm will lead the way.  
I’m not following a fixed key,  
I’m building a new way.  
Open the door and let the sound  
become everything I’ve learned.
