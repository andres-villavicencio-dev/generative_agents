Song: “Found Notes”

Verse 1  
The chalk dust settled on the board  
A rhythm I didn’t know  
I traced the chord, then drew the line  
Found notes I’d never seen  
Oak Hill’s quiet, steady wind  
Told me how to listen deep  
The melody arrived without a sign  
I wrote it all down, felt the whole thing sleep.

Chorus  
Oh, the old melody is still there

And make the silence make a sound.
