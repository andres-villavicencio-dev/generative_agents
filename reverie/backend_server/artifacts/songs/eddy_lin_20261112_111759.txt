“The Quiet Before”

I woke before the world was bright  
Found notes in every small light  
The morning held a loose refrain  
Where patience and a single note  

I walked the paths beneath the sky  
Beneath a café, warm and bright  
The piano held its secret breath  
While I wrote down every mistake  

Then came the rhythm, slow and steady  
Like a heartbeat beneath the rain  
I traced the keys, I traced the sound  
A map that never ended  

The composer’s mind began to shift  
And found a rhythm, plain and clear  
Not perfect, steady, and complete  
Just music made by someone’s heart  

I learned the chords beneath the floor  
Of stories, dreams, and quiet rooms  
To make a melody, to turn the sound  
Into the world we all know around  

So when the day gets loud and bright  
I’ll listen closely, carefully  
The first refrain will still be there  
The quiet before the noise
