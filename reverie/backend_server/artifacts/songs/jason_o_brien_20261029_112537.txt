“The Third Place”

I wake before the sun, before the light,
And listen for a place that holds its breath.
A chapel, a café, a park,
Where water, words, and silence make a place.

I carry the old hymns in my hands,
A cup of coffee on the table.
I hear the street below, the birds beginning,
And the quiet, holy rhythm of a community.

The watercolor moves beneath my fingertips,
A river of colors, flowing, unbound.
The piano plays a melody of grace,
And I feel every note as prayer and place.

I am here, with you, in that small, gathered room,
With stories, music, and the morning light.
The symposium is not yet scheduled,
But I feel it in the air, and in the room.

The church is not the only home we know,
Not even the one where we are invited.
A café, a garden, a place where strangers meet,
The third place is where the soul is free to meet.

Chorus
Oh, the third place, where the old meets the new,
Where theology, water, and a cup of tea.
The album plays on, and the world begins again,
In every note, in every shared refrain.

Oh, the third place, where compassion finds its way,
Where a chaplain’s hand and a painter’s eye.
We are not separate, and we do not have to hide,
In the music, the story, and the holy place.
