Title: “River Current”

Verse 1  
The river woke before the sun  
I traced the stones with every finger  
A map of moss and silver light  
Where every story begins to sing  
The current pushes past the bank  
With promises I can’t quite unpack  

Chorus  
River current, flowing free  
I carry every dream that I’ve ever seen  
From sketchbook pages to the camera lens  
You’re the water, I’m the river, one and one  
We’ll meet beneath the bridge tonight  
Where fantasy and real life start to blend
