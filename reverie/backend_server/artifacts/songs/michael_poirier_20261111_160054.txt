Title: River Current

(Verse 1)  
I woke before the sun and drew the river’s silver line,  
Across the fields where mist still clings to every stone.  
The creek began beneath my sketchbook, quiet and green,  
With tiny fish swimming beneath the painted moon.  
My fingers moved across the Procreate canvas,  
Making every branch feel like a dream I could have done.

(Chorus)  
Oh, the current carries stories through the woods so dark,  
Where every branch holds a secret that the night has learned.  
I sketch the river, I sketch the path that leads me home,  
And wonder how the river knows the way.  
It’s not a road that makes a song,  
It’s the current carrying me along.

(Verse 2)  
I filled the book with characters who never sleep,  
With dragons hiding behind stones and foxes with too many eyes.  
Tom and Jane said I should be studying instead,  
But I had too much imagination to sit still.  
Every corner of my mind became a character,  
And every line of pencil turned into a living letter.

(Chorus)
