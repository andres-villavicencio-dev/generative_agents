“Stories in the River”

There’s a river in the quiet town,  
carrying stories down the stream.  
I carry sketches in my head,  
and a melody beneath my feet.

At home, the kitchen is a home,  
with pans where I make bread for two.  
The oven hums a little tune,  
so I can relax and let the sun.

But I don’t want to be the one  
to stop the journey, to keep it close.  
I want the world to keep its pace,  
and make room for every strange face.

The sketchbook is full, the Procreate canvas gleams,  
and Eddy Lin is calling out from the screen.  
He wants to record every image,  
and make a song to make the images seem.

We’ll start with a gentle hum,  
a river’s path along the shore,  
where the forest wakes beneath the rain,  
and every story has a place to be.

Let the river carry on,  
let the melody keep the night.  
Even when the homework is late,  
the song will keep the story alive.
