“The River’s Path”

I woke at six, before the world could wake,  
Filled my Procreate canvas with glowing, new faces.  
Each sketch held a question, a secret, a spark—  
A fantasy world where the river’s path is a spark.

The morning light was pale, but the river was bright,  
Its path made of gold through the mossy, dark night.  
Every bend held a story, a hidden heart,  
A place beneath the surface where the old trees part.

I saw a figure stepping across the current,  
With a lantern that warmed the river’s long, dark breast.  
The world was soft and quiet, full of strange, glowing light,  
And I wanted to draw him into every single night.

So I asked the river what the path had taught me,  
And the water whispered back with a sound of blue.  
It spoke of the world before the first house was built,  
Where every stone held a story in its quiet soul.

I’m not just making a song, I’m making a place,  
With a character who found his way home through the place.  
The river’s path is a song the world has never heard—  
I’m giving it a melody that is mine to share.
