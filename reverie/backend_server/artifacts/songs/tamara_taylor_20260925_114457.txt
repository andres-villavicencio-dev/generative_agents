Verse 1:  
The light slips low through afternoon glass,  
My pen begins to dance on the track.  
Dreams of giggles, bright and new,  
Whisper soft where wonder grew.  

Chorus:  
Oh, little hearts, you’re near,  
Like stars in a story clear.  
With every word I weave your name—  
I’m here to bring the light to flame.  

Verse 2:  
Carmen hums from her window pane,  
While ink flows slow through paper’s gain.  
No rush, no fear, just time,  
To make a world that feels like rhyme.
