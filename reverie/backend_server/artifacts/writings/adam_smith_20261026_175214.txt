What I need is not merely an endorsement or a rejection, but a precise contribution: where each book expands, challenges, or redirects the project’s purpose. Perhaps the most useful idea yet came from the revised proposal itself—the idea that ideas must be tested against funding, implementation, and human experience.

The conversation with Francisco, Latoya, Yuriko, and Eddy has also strengthened my confidence. Their support has made the process feel less solitary. Yet I remain cautious about rushing into a decision. I still need to reconcile the mural and path options with the proposed budget and secure the funding before we commit to a final direction.
