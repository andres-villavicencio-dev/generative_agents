It is Wednesday, February 15, and I have spent the morning with a purpose I seldom have time to keep clear. I corrected my proposal, reviewed the three mural and path options, and compared their budgets and funding sources side by side. The conversations with Francisco, Latoya, Yuriko, and Eddy have made this process feel less solitary and more grounded. I am grateful for their encouragement, yet I must remain decisive.

The comparison is not merely administrative. Each candidate offers a different way to connect the community with its shared space. Francisco’s proposal emphasizes accessibility, with clear funding sources that appear sustainable. The mural and path options present strong possibilities, though I cannot yet confirm that they will match the broader goals of the project. I have identified recurring patterns among the candidates: a shared emphasis on public accessibility, practical sustainability, and the need for clear communication about funding and design. Yet the underlying assumptions differ. Francisco’s model relies on community participation, while the others may depend on external support.

The creative-writing work is another matter of pattern and theme. I have noticed that repeated motifs of memory, belonging, and transformation appear in the books I am reviewing. Characters often carry unresolved questions that the story does not immediately answer. This suggests that a story can be powerful not because it resolves every doubt, but because it makes the reader feel the weight of what remains uncertain. I am tempted to connect this with my own reflections about the proposed project, but I should not force a connection that may obscure the substance.

I am also beginning to wonder whether the mural and path options will support the project beyond their initial visual appeal. A proposal that can stand on its own merits, while the others depend on continued external support, may be more resilient. I have not yet determined which option carries the strongest case, but the evidence must support a choice that is both beautiful and practical.

My follow-up is scheduled for Tuesday, February 14, at 6:58 a.m., and I still need to compare the three proposals fully. I will not rush the decision, but neither will I let incomplete comparison prevent it.
