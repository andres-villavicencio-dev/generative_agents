To the town-service team,

My name is Amber Williamson, and I am applying for a job at the community bulletin board at Johnson Park. I am not asking for an easy task; I am asking for a place where every neighbour’s story can be heard, and where every neighbour’s story can change the day.

I am sixty-nine years old, and I have spent my life learning that kindness is not always loud. It is often a quiet gift—a shared cup of coffee, a patient explanation of a sign, or a little encouragement for someone who has walked home late. I do not believe in turning every conversation into a speech. I believe in listening, then listening again, until the truth is heard.

I am also a gardener. In Johnson Park, I grow flowers and try to make the place feel alive. Sometimes I tell the same story to two different children, and I am not surprised by the difference in the responses. A story has no one right way to be told.

I am available for any reasonable service on this Tuesday, though I have no confirmed appointment. I may be free around 8:00 a.m., perhaps at the bulletin board, though I cannot promise the exact time. My schedule is not a rigid contract, but it is not entirely open either. I would appreciate the opportunity to explain what I can bring to your community.

I am prepared to work honestly and quietly, and to listen as well as I work. I am not here to make things perfect. I am here to help neighbours remember that they are never truly alone.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be grateful to hear from you, and I look forward to the possibility of contributing to Johnson Park.

Sincerely,  
Amber Williamson
