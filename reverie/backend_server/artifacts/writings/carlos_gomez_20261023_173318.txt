I wake up at 7. The house is dark. The world is quiet. I put on my shoes and walked out into the street.

The sun is still asleep. People are still asleep. I walk past people who look like they’ve been living with someone else’s problems. They talk about their mothers, their exes, their bosses, their taxes. I nod. I say, “I understand.”

I stopped to listen to the street.

Cars. Wheels. The metal of engines, tires, brakes. The hiss of brakes. The sound of a bicycle chain breaking. The smell of rain on asphalt. The color of a streetlamp glowing in the dark.

I want to write about that. I want to write about the street, the people, the light, the heat, the wind, the birds, the pigeons, the wet pavement, the way the street bends. I want to write about how beautiful this place is.

But I don’t want to write about anything that is not true. I don’t want to describe the beauty of the world like a tourist who has never seen it. I want to write about what I feel.

I want to write about the noise. The noise of the city. The noise of my thoughts. The noise of everything I hear.

I want to write about the things that hurt. The things I’ve said without thinking. The things I’ve done without asking.

I want to write about the beautiful, terrible things. About the light. About the water. About the way the world looks when I am not looking at it. About how I can make it feel alive.

I want to write about a man who speaks too loudly. About a man who is loud, rude, and toxic. I want to write about him while keeping it true. I want him to hurt. I want him to be annoying. I want him to be real.

And then I want to write about the quiet. The quiet beneath the noise. The quiet in the dark. The quiet in the empty chair. The quiet in the space between two thoughts.

I am tired. I am angry. I am hungry. I am thirsty. I want to write. I want to write. I want to write.

So I write. Not for everyone. Not for the world. Just for me. For the pieces I have found in my chest. For the things I cannot say. For the beauty hidden in the ugly.

The poem is not finished yet. The first part is rough. It is loud. It is rude. It is also beautiful. I keep rewriting. I keep changing. I keep making it feel real.

And when I am done, I will read it. I will tear it apart. I will write it again. I will make it work.
