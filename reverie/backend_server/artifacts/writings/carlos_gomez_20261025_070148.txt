The workshop opened with a speaker who made us all feel like we were being tested. I asked for a real example from my revision process.

He said, “Tell me how you change a line.”

I told him: “I keep rewriting the same sentence until the meaning feels too simple, then I cut it until it feels too heavy. I’m not afraid of the line being honest.”
