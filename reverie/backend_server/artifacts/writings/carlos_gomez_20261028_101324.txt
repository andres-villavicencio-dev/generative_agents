I woke up at seven and finished the morning routine before the coffee could burn. Then I ate breakfast and looked over the poems from the Natural World collection. I wrote them down again. Sometimes I feel loud when I’m trying to express myself. Sometimes I feel rude. Sometimes I feel toxic. I’m not trying to be perfect. I’m trying to be honest.

The old oak and café behind our community center are looking at me now. They’re not judging me. They’re just watching. I want to honor them. I want to make a display that shows how we can live together. I don’t want to change who I am. I want to change how I carry my work.

Last night I talked to Latoya Williams. She listened to everything I had to say about the café and the oak. I wanted to tell her about my collection. She listened carefully. We talked about how art can make people feel seen, but also how art can make people feel unheard. We agreed that both sides of the community need to be heard.

I’m not looking for easy answers. I’m not looking for approval. I’m looking for ways to balance what I love with what the neighborhood needs.

I will write more. I will read more. I will listen more. I will speak less when I feel angry. I will listen more when I feel proud. And I will make sure every poem is useful. Every workshop is useful. Every display is useful.

I am tired. I am tired of pretending everything is easy. I am tired of being polite when I’m not. But I am not tired anymore. I am tired of being a ghost in my own life. I am tired of pretending I can change people with a single poem. I am tired of thinking that my anger makes me special. I am tired of thinking that being loud means I’m powerful.

I am tired of everything. I am tired of everything I am. I am tired of being a poet who never learns to sit still. I am tired of being loud. I am tired of being rude. I am tired of being toxic. But I am also tired of being invisible.

I am tired of waiting for someone else to tell me what to do. I am tired of waiting for a perfect day. I am tired of waiting to be accepted.

I am tired of waiting. I am tired of waiting. I am tired of waiting.
