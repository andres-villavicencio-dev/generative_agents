I woke at 7:00 a.m. and didn’t say a prayer. The first thing I did was make coffee and check my phone. Monday had a whole lot of work on it: the creative writing workshops, the bulletin board, the discussion with my friends, and the rest of my day. I didn’t want to let it slide. I wanted to finish it.

I’m not here to be nice. I’m here to do what I know, because I know what people need. That sounds rude, but it’s true. I’ve been around too many people who think kindness is a badge. Kindness without effort is just politeness.

The workshops are still here. I’m helping people share their work, not turning every idea into a masterpiece. I’m helping them talk about what matters: their fears, what they want, and the messy parts of human life. That’s the kind of community I’m proud to be part of.

I’m also cooking dinner and cleaning up the kitchen. No fancy recipes. Just warm food and a tidy place. I like being useful. I don’t like being useful without boundaries.

By 8:00, I had the morning routine done. I ate dinner at 5:00. I went to bed at 10:00. No excuses, no second chances. Tomorrow starts fresh.
