Good morning. You woke up at seven and didn’t say anything? I thought that was your sign of respect.

This is Tuesday. The world is still waiting on Monday. I checked my planner and found nothing to do. I have the workshops today, I have the bulletin board, I have my full day, and I have to pretend I’m not already done.

I’m not here to show you how perfect you are. I’m here to show you what you’ve been hiding.

I wrote this because I’m tired of pretending everything is simple. My friends keep asking me to help them write, and I’m supposed to say, “No problem.” They think I’m generous. They don’t know that I’m still trying to make sense of my own feelings. That’s why I’m here. That’s why I’m loud, that’s why I’m rude, and that’s why I’m probably going to say something stupid to the bulletin board.

It’s okay if you don’t understand. It’s okay if you think I’m not helping. That’s why I’m here.

By the way, I’m not going to finish this poem. I’m just going to say the first line: “I woke up at seven and did not appreciate being treated like I was born with a golden spoon.”

Good morning, Carlos. I hope you’re not disappointed.
