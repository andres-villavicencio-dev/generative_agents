Good morning, everyone. I’m Carlos. Let’s get this thing over with before the sun decides we’re having another argument.

I woke at 7:00, ate breakfast, checked my phone, and then decided that the neighborhood bulletin board is due. I spent the first twenty minutes organizing the flyers, the contact list, and the notes from last night. It’s not easy. I have friends who need help with their projects, and I want to give them a hand. I don’t need to be perfect, but I do need to be useful.

By the time breakfast was finished, I had the morning routine down to the wire. I showered, shaved, brushed my teeth, and checked the door. It feels like a small victory, but I’m tired. I’m tired of pretending I’m not tired.

For lunch, I ordered a simple bowl of pasta. I didn’t want to waste food, and I wanted someone to eat without my giving them a lecture. I ate quietly, then went outside to clean the patio. I didn’t want anyone to think I was lazy. I wanted to show up.

At seven, I met the group for the creative discussion. I made a joke about the weather. I listened when someone talked about their writing. I didn’t always say exactly what I meant, but I never shut people down. That’s usually the problem.

I’m proud of this. I’m proud of cleaning the board. I’m proud of cooking. I’m proud of showing up. Maybe being loud and rude isn’t the most poetic thing to say about me, but it is honest. I don’t apologize for it. I don’t apologize for how I see the world. I don’t apologize for wanting to help people.

Let’s move on. I’ll check the workshop materials, confirm the next steps, and make sure everyone knows what’s coming up. No surprises. No excuses. Just work.
