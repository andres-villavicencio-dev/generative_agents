Wednesday, February 15.

After John Lin shared the mayoral candidates’ proposals, I started comparing them with the café’s sales records. I noticed how the price range changed from $4.50 to $6.50, and how menu prices were sometimes inconsistent. I also checked the oatmeal and banana-slice quality. The oatmeal looked a little dry, and the banana slices were uneven.

One proposal suggested raising prices to cover higher ingredient costs. Another proposed a simpler menu to save time. I thought about what the café really needs: value, quality, and a good experience without making the proposals too complicated.

I also listened to a few pieces of music while comparing the proposals. The rhythm helped me notice how the candidates changed the presentation of the ideas. I didn’t want to get lost in the details; I wanted to keep the main points clear and useful.

I’ll make a short list of the strongest proposals, their strengths and weaknesses, and the changes needed. I’ll also write down what the menu and ingredients should actually look like. At the end, I’ll put together a brief café report: the price range, the quality issues, and the improvements needed.

By 11:00 I’ll be ready to sleep, but I want to make sure I’ve captured the important comparison. I’m curious, analytical, and still looking for ways to expand my knowledge. That’s the kind of thinking I should keep doing.
