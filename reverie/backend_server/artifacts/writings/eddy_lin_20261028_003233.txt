Wednesday, February 15

I woke at 7:00 a.m., ate breakfast at 7:30, and spent the first hour reviewing my notes. After John Lin finished sharing the mayoral candidates’ proposals, I compared them with the café’s sales records, menu prices, timing differences, oatmeal and banana-slice quality, and the proposed price range of $4.50–$6.50. The goal was simple: improve value and quality without making the proposals too complicated.

John’s proposals were ambitious, but they had a clear framework. The café should focus on consistent quality, better pricing, and a clearer service timeline. Oatmeal and banana-slice quality should be verified before they enter production, and staff should be trained to recognize inconsistencies so customers do not repeat complaints. The proposed price range needs a realistic floor and ceiling, with a transparent way to justify each change rather than relying on vague assurances.

The menu must remain practical. If the café wants to offer oatmeal and banana-slice at a reasonable price, the cost of ingredients and labor must remain affordable. A higher price range should not simply mean “more expensive”; it should mean better ingredients and clearer value. The $4.50–$6.50 band should not be left to the market. The café should consider whether the price supports a consistent quality standard and whether customers perceive that value.

The biggest gap in the current plan is clarity. Candidates should explain what they will do differently, not just what they will claim. A customer should be able to compare proposals, verify quality, and understand the cost before choosing. The café should also communicate changes clearly rather than assuming customers understand every detail.

I will write down my findings and ask John to summarize the key proposals in plain language. No proposal needs to become complicated to be useful. The café should improve value through better quality, reasonable pricing, and transparent communication. I will also prepare a short comparison of the proposals and a simple action plan for next week.

My goal is not to change everything overnight. I need to improve the café’s offerings, keep the proposals practical, and make sure the community understands the benefits. I will keep my goals in mind and write the finished analysis before bed.
