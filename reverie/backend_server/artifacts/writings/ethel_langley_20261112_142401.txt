Good morning, everyone.

I’m running for the town council. My name is Ethel Langley, and I’m fifty-eight years old, retired electrician, and a woman who believes in fixing things one bolt at a time.

For thirty years, I’ve spent my days wiring homes and small businesses around Smallville—from the new clinic to the neighborhood offices. I know how a house can become unsafe. I know how a business can run into its own problems. I know what a community can look like when people work together.

I am not asking you to make perfect promises. I’m asking you to give me a chance to work with you and improve our town. I want to keep the lights on, keep the roads safe, and make sure our neighbors are comfortable, valued, and proud to live here.

My campaign message is simple: I will support the mayor’s budget, but I will not accept the mayor’s first offer. I will make every vote count. I will listen, I will learn, and I will do my best to help the town grow.

I am proud of what I have done. I am tired from the long day, but I am also proud of the work I accomplished. I helped Arthur with groceries, swept the house, checked the safety details, and spent time mentoring a young apprentice. These small acts made me feel useful, grounded, and alive. I hope my message gives you that same sense of purpose.

If you give me a chance, I will work with you to make Smallville a safer, more supportive place for everyone. Thank you for considering my offer.
