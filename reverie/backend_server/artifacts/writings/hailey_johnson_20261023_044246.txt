The Sound of Color: A Podcast Concept

My idea is to turn the creative energy that runs through my day into something audible. I’m turning my novel, The Studio Collective, into a podcast. It follows a group of artists living and working together in a co-living space. Each episode will explore the people, places, and stories behind them. I want the sound to feel like a living room conversation, with music, laughter, and stories that make you want to reach out and hear them.

I’m looking for a second interview question that feels natural but still gives me a fresh direction. The first question I have is:

> “Your novel is set among a group of artists in a co-living space. What is the first thing you noticed about how the room felt when you arrived?”

The follow-up question I want to prepare is:

> “What part of that space’s atmosphere surprised you most?”
