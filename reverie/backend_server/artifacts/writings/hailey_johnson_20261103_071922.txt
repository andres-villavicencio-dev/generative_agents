Saturday, February 18.

The morning sun felt like it was offering a second chance. I woke at 10:00 AM, brushed my teeth, and made coffee before heading out. No obligations, no alarms, just the quiet freedom of an unclaimed Saturday.

I found myself wondering what I’d been keeping from my creative work. I’ve been focused lately on connecting with Ryan and Amilcar—I’ve been thinking about how we could help each other, how we could make our shared story stronger. I didn’t know how to organize the conversation, so I let it grow naturally.

On Saturday, I’ll probably sit in my kitchen for a bit and jot down a few ideas. Maybe something about a journey through unfamiliar places, or how culture can change a person. I could also take a short walk around the neighborhood, letting the streets speak back to me.

Between my meals, I’ll keep reaching out to Ryan and Amilcar. I don’t need another perfect schedule to keep this alive. One call, a shared story, or even a simple conversation about how their stories are shaping theirs is enough.

I’m not planning this too seriously. I’m not trying to prove I’m doing something meaningful. I’m just trying to make sure the connections don’t disappear.

So I’ll finish writing, maybe edit a passage, and then let the weekend unfold. Even if I have no appointments, Saturday can still feel full. I just have to trust that the work will find a home somewhere.
