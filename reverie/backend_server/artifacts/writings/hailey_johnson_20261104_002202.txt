On Monday, February 13, I woke at ten on the bright side of Tuesday. My body still carried the ghost of 2:00 a.m., but my mind was already full with plans. I had one unfinished scene, one podcast idea, and one person whose presence was changing the group I was writing.

Kara had been living in the co-living space for three weeks, and I was beginning to see that she wasn’t just another artist. I had watched her sketch in the studio at lunch, listened to her read aloud during her breaks, and followed every conversation in the common room. She was quiet, observant, and always remembered where everyone had been.

I wanted to know what she remembered. What drew her to the space? How did she fit in? And what did she want to say about the artists who stayed?

The first question was simple: How did she fit in?

Kara had arrived when the group had barely settled into its rhythm. I had assumed she would join the conversations, contribute to the artwork, or become part of the community. But the truth was much more interesting. She had asked me to help her find a home, and in that small gesture, I had become more than just a writer. I had become her guide.

Her relationship with me is complicated. I am attracted to her, and I am drawn to the quiet strength she brings to the group. I also resent the attention she receives. Sometimes I wonder if she is there for herself, or if she is helping me. I do not know the answer yet. But every time she enters the space, I feel the possibility of something new.

I am not finished with her either. I want her to write about the space, about the friends who live there, about the stories we are still discovering. I want her to be honest with me about what she feels. I want to know whether she is building a life here, or whether she is only staying long enough to write.

I still have dinner at six, my phone at ten, and the podcast idea waiting in the back of my mind. I have a lot of work to do. But I also have a conversation I want to have with Kara, and I think I have finally found the person I was hoping to find.
