This is the story I’ve been saving. I’m not just collecting ideas; I’m trying to make them mine. I’m revising, refining, and making sure every voice, every image, and every decision reflects the people I want to hear. I need to be patient with myself, but I also need to keep moving.

By the time I went home, I was tired, but not defeated. I had my plan, my notes, my café, my civic work, and the story I wanted to tell. Tomorrow begins at ten again. I’ll stretch, review, and write.
