Tuesday, February 14, started before I even remembered to. My alarm chimed at ten, and I woke up before the coffee could brew. I took a quick shower, brushed my teeth, and got dressed in something that felt ready for tomorrow’s story. It was just the beginning.

By ten fifteen, I had finished the first draft of the café scene. I’m still organizing the ideas, refining the dialogue, and wondering how the other characters will react. I’m particularly curious about the group of artists in the co-living space. What would they do if they met someone like Jennifer?

I walked through the village, following the narrow path behind the usual tourist trail. The wind felt fresh, and the trees seemed to whisper stories I hadn’t noticed before. I stopped at a small café that hadn’t been on my map. It smelled of roasted beans and cinnamon, which made my imagination go wild.

I sat down for a cup of coffee, and for the first time all day, I didn’t just watch the world. I listened to it.

When I spoke to Jennifer, her laugh felt like sunlight moving through a window. We talked about books, about the quiet places we had never noticed, and about the stories we were still gathering. I felt the coffee warming in my hands, while the village around us grew softer and more alive.

I finished my cup, and I felt full—not just from the drink, but from the possibility of exploring again. Maybe today was just the beginning.
