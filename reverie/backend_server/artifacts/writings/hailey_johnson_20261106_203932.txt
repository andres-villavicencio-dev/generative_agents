I woke up at 10:00 a.m. on Monday, February 13, and immediately thought about the first episode of my new podcast: The Painted Room.

The idea comes from a small place: a co-living space where ten artists share rooms, meals, ideas, and sometimes the same bathroom. I imagine their conversations—sharp, quiet, sometimes loud—cutting through the noise of city life. Maybe one day we’ll hear the same artists again.

My main purpose for the podcast is to make the co-living experience feel alive. I want listeners to hear the people behind the art, not just the art itself. I want to explore how artists find comfort, create, and sometimes make mistakes in the same space. I also want to connect stories from different cultures and show how creativity can cross borders.

I’m not making this a travelogue. I’m making it a conversation about how we live together.

I’m not here to write a book first. I’m here to listen, interview, gather stories, and help people imagine a world where creativity and community can thrive.

I’ll talk about the artists in my novel, the shared spaces that make us feel less alone, and the stories that keep us curious. I’ll ask for your opinions, your stories, and your ideas. Maybe I’ll even invite you to co-create an episode.

I go to bed around 2 a.m., wake up around 10 a.m., and eat dinner at 6. I’m tired, but I’m full of questions.

Welcome to The Painted Room. Let’s begin.
