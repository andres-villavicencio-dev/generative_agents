This is Hailey Johnson, the writer behind a new novel and the host of my first podcast.

I’m writing a story about a group of artists living in a co-living space. The main setting is a shared studio apartment in Portland, where five people each have their own space but share the same kitchen, lounge, and creative corner. I want the room to feel alive: lamps glowing, coffee steaming, laughter echoing through the walls, and ideas bouncing between every conversation.

For the podcast, I’ll meet the artists face to face. We’ll talk about the stories they’ve been working on, the places they’ve been drawn to, and the differences between how they see the world. I’ll be asking the questions no writer wants to answer: what keeps you awake at night? What made you choose this neighborhood? What does your art say about you?

My first episode will be called The Room That Thinks It’s a Home. It will feature the artists, an original sketch, and a short conversation about the idea of belonging. I’m also thinking of a second episode called The Co-Working Circle, where I’ll interview other writers and artists about how they keep their creativity fed.

I’m curious to see what happens when five strangers come together with nothing in common but a desire to create. I want the story to feel real, the conversations to feel genuine, and my voice to feel like mine.

I’ll wake up at 10:00 a.m., eat dinner at 6:00 p.m., and go to bed by 2:00 a.m. That’s all there is to it. Now let’s go make some noise.
