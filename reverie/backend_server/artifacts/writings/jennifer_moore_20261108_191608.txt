Dear Community Board,

I have been preparing for my exhibition, mentoring younger painters, and enjoying a Johnson Park walk with Sam and my family. My day begins at 5:00 a.m., and I typically go to bed around 9:00 p.m. I would be grateful to meet you and contribute in whatever way I can.

Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
