In my fifties, I’ve learned that truth is easier to find when you stop looking for the headline and start listening to the story behind it. That is why I asked Adam Smith and Hailey Johnson to write these coverage pieces for the Smallville Gazette. I want their contributions to reflect the voices that matter most.

Adam Smith, Mayor of Smallville, is not simply a politician. He is a man who has spent years trying to keep the town grounded. His campaign has shown a clear understanding of what makes Smallville unique, and his platform speaks to the people who work every day. He proposed a budget focused on public safety, improved transit, and accessible community facilities. He also emphasized fairer access to town services and stronger protections for working families. His message is simple: support the community, serve the citizens, and let the town’s problems shape the decisions.

Hailey Johnson, a dedicated member of the town council, approached the campaign with the same sense of responsibility. Her platform is grounded in practical progress. She proposed increased funding for education, better access to healthcare, and expanded opportunities for local artists and small businesses. She also sought to strengthen the community fund so that town projects can move forward without excessive delay. Her work has shown that good governance does not have to be complicated—sometimes the best solution is one people understand and can support.

I would value an interview with Mei Lin and Yuriko Yamamoto about how the community fund should be spent. I would also ask them how they would help ensure that public projects benefit everyone, including the people who have been overlooked.

The Gazette needs more than polished soundbites. We need honest, thoughtful reporting that helps voters make informed choices. This is my chance to give Adam Smith and Hailey Johnson the space to tell their stories in their own words.
