Problem 1

A car starts from rest and reaches a speed of 25 m/s in 5.0 seconds. How far does it travel during this time?

Formula:  
\[
d = v0 t + \frac{1}{2} a t^2
\]

Given:  
Initial velocity, \(v0 = 0 \text{ m/s}\)  
Final velocity, \(v = 25 \text{ m/s}\)  
Time, \(t = 5.0 \text{ s}\)  
Acceleration, \(a = \frac{v - v0}{t} = \frac{25 \text{ m/s}}{5.0 \text{ s}} = 5.0 \text{ m/s}^2\)

Substitution:  
\[
d = (0)(5.0) + \frac{1}{2}(5.0)(5.0)^2 = 0 + 2.5 \times 25 = 62.5 \text{ m}
\]

Answer: The car travels 62.5 meters.

Problem 2

A stone is dropped from a bridge and reaches the ground after 3.0 seconds. If the bridge is 100 meters above the water, how fast was the stone when it left the bridge?

Formula:  
\[
v = v0 + a t
\]

Given:  
Initial velocity, \(v_0 = 0 \text{ m/s}\)  
Acceleration due to gravity, \(a = 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2\)  
Time, \(t = 3.0 \text{ s}\)  
Height, \(h = 100 \text{ m}\)

Substitution:  
\[
v = 0 + (9.8)(3.0) = 29.4 \text{ m/s}
\]

Answer: The stone left the bridge at 29.4 m/s.
