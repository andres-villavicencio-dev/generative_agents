Dear Smallville Fundraising Team,

I am writing to ask for your help in supporting Maria Urban’s efforts to secure a stable livelihood and keep her life moving forward. Maria is thirty-three years old, newly arrived in Smallville, and without a confirmed job, clinic position, or steady routine, she is balancing job applications, clinic interviews, fundraising, journalism, and community projects.

She is not a burden. She is organized, purposeful, and fiercely dedicated. Her routine is clear: six a.m. for waking, the morning routine, and reviewing appointments; the first hour of her day focused on job applications and clinic positions; the rest of the day filled with solitary hikes, creative crafts, and balanced budgeting. She does not need help with money, and she does not ask for favors. She asks that her name be considered for a reliable position, and that any support she needs be matched with the care and stability Smallville has shown her.

The fund is not asking Maria to become someone else. It is asking for help to find the steady work she deserves. A confirmed job could mean regular income, a routine, and the chance to contribute meaningfully to the community. It could also mean the time and financial resources to support her local journalism, community projects, and creative pursuits.

The proposed fund would provide a modest living stipend, help cover living expenses, and support Maria’s continued participation in the city’s projects. This is not about taking money from her. It is about helping her secure the stable foundation she deserves.

I have been working hard, but I do not want to be the first to ask for help. I want to apply, interview, and earn a steady position. I am not asking for more than I can give. I am asking for a chance to prove I am useful, reliable, and ready to contribute.

Thank you for considering my request. I am ready to take the next step, and I hope we can move forward together.
