Good morning, everyone.

My name is Maria Urban. I’m thirty-three years old, and this Saturday, February 18, I’m without a job. For the first time in a long time, I’m also without a confirmed appointment, meeting, meal, or activity scheduled. But I’m not giving up. I’m still applying, still taking a clinic position, still writing for the papers, and still helping the community in ways I can control.

I started Smallville last year when I first found my way onto a trail. I didn’t know what I was doing, but the woods showed me that I was capable. I’ve learned to value quiet solitude, to appreciate a well-made piece of craft, and to keep my life balanced. I’m organized, I can be competitive, and sometimes I get anxious. That anxiety doesn’t make me weak—it just makes me careful. I don’t take every opportunity lightly, and I don’t settle for anything less than what I can achieve.

Now I’m looking for steady work. I’m open to full-time, part-time, or community roles that match my schedule. I’m a newcomer to Smallville, but I’m already committed to making a difference. I want to build something reliable, not just chase every opportunity. That’s why I’m doing this fundraising letter: to give every applicant a clear chance, to show myself as someone who is serious about helping others, and to keep the momentum going even when things feel uncertain.

I don’t want to change my routine. I want to protect my mornings, my hikes, my budget, and my purpose. I’m also willing to listen, give help, and keep talking.

Thank you for giving me the opportunity to be here. I’m ready to work, and I’m ready to keep moving forward.
