the wind is whispering through the trees like a secret only the softest hearts can hear  
a little girl with braided hair sits on her windowsill, watching dust float like tiny stars  
she does not know it yet, but she is brave—not because she risks everything, but because she listens to the quiet voice inside that says try  

this story began in a dream I couldn’t wake from. A child finds a door made of honeycomb behind her mirror—no handle, no key, just light and laughter. When she steps through, the world softens: skies turn pastel, rivers hum lullabies, and every worry turns into a butterfly that dissolves in the breeze  

she doesn’t need to prove anything. Her courage is gentle. It sits beside her like a cat, warm and calm. She carries it not as armor, but as a lantern—small enough to fit in her pocket, steady enough to guide her home  

i wrote this for anyone who feels too small to be heard. For the ones who love in quiet ways, who heal by listening, who find strength in stillness. courage is not always loud. sometimes it’s just choosing to keep going—even when your heart whispers maybe tomorrow  

so if you’re reading this, breathe deep. You are enough.
