I’m Tamara Taylor, a children’s book author who believes every young reader deserves a story that feels like a lantern waiting to be lit. I’m working on a new illustrated children’s series about a girl named Mara Bell, who lives in an old apartment building where every midnight, the elevator travels one floor farther than the building actually has.

Mara carries a tiny brass key that she inherited from her grandmother. She has never found the door it opens, but each night, mysterious sounds guide her through the hallway while shadows behave like curious animals. In the first story, Mara discovers that the building is collecting things that have been lost: a glove from a snowstorm, a red balloon from a birthday party, a paper boat that traveled down a river, and, most importantly, the laughter from a room where two friends once made promises. To save what has been collected, Mara must help the forgotten things find their way home.

The series will follow Mara as she travels through the building’s changing rooms, each one connected to a different kind of loss. Some adventures will be gentle and funny, like a staircase that insists children hop upside down. Others will ask deeper questions about courage, forgiveness, and remembering those we love. I want the stories to be magical without becoming confusing and moving without becoming frightening. Children should see their own feelings in Mara—the need to belong, the wish to be brave, and the comfort of knowing someone is listening.

I’m designing the books as a welcoming path rather than a single narrow road. Young readers can meet the characters, solve puzzles, laugh at talking mailboxes, and return whenever they want. There will be comforting repetition alongside new discoveries, just as a favorite bedtime story should feel both surprising and familiar.

Above all, I hope these books encourage children to look closely at ordinary places. A forgotten key, a closed drawer, or a light beneath a bedroom door might be the beginning of an entire adventure. That is the heart of the series: courage begins with curiosity, and kindness has a way of leading us toward exactly where we need to be.
