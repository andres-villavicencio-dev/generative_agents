Children’s Book Series

Today’s goal is to finish revising the second book in the series. I want to keep the voice warm and clear, especially in the scenes where Mara first learns that courage does not mean being fearless. I will strengthen that chapter, make sure the descriptions are vivid enough for young readers without becoming crowded, and preserve the quiet humor that makes the story feel like home. Target: 1,200 polished words by bedtime.

I also want to sketch the emotional arc for the third book. Beginning with three small notes on index cards feels right. One for the problem Mara must solve, one for the friendship that helps her, and one for the surprise she discovers at the end. The outline does not need to be perfect today. I only need to listen for where the story wants to go. I will write a one-page outline tomorrow and leave space for surprises.

Weekly targets: revise 8,000 words, outline the third book, update each character’s little habits and favorite things, and read one chapter aloud to Carmen if she has time. I want to read it aloud because stories should sound kind when spoken.

Adult Comic Series

For the adult comic, I need to create a clean, achievable script for pages 1–8 of the next issue. I will define each panel’s action, dialogue, and emotional beat before adding detailed art notes. The opening scene should establish the city’s mood immediately: rain on neon signs, a crowded train, and enough small details that readers feel they could live there. I will keep the dialogue sharp and give the supporting characters distinct voices rather than making everyone sound like me.

Target: a complete script and rough panel breakdown for pages 1–8, plus notes for the color palette and background details. By Friday, I want the first eight pages ready for the artist. I will send questions as they arise instead of letting uncertainty turn into delay.

I am also setting aside one evening this week to review the previous issue and note what worked, what felt rushed, and where the pacing can improve. Good stories are built patiently, one honest choice at a time.
