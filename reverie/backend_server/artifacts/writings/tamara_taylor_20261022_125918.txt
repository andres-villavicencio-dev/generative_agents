The Secret Map in Grandpa’s Garden

Mia stood beside her grandfather’s raised garden bed and traced the tiny lines on the blue piece of paper. They looked like rivers, mountains, and twisty roads.

“This is the map to the moon garden,” Grandpa said. “But the moon flowers only bloom at night.”

Mia looked up at the bright afternoon sun. “But I have to go to school tomorrow. I can’t stay up all night.”

Grandpa smiled. “Then we’ll need a plan.”

They searched the garden shed and found a silver flashlight, a jar of glowworms, and an old bicycle helmet. Mia also found her mother’s largest mixing bowl.

At sunset, they carried the supplies to the garden. When the moon appeared, Grandpa opened the gate behind the oak tree. The map led past the tomatoes, beneath the apple branches, and down a narrow path Mia had never noticed before.

At the very end stood a circle of pale flowers. Their petals were closed.

Mia checked the map. There was one more instruction: “Wake the sleeping flowers with water from the silver lake.”

But there was no lake anywhere in the garden.

Grandpa frowned. “Perhaps the map has changed.”

Mia studied the blue paper more closely. The silver lines sparkled when she tilted it toward the moonlight. Suddenly, she realized they matched the old birdbath near the fence.

“It’s not a lake,” she said. “It’s the birdbath!”

They hurried over, but when Mia reached for the bowl, she found a crack across the bottom. The bowl was empty.

“Now the problem is harder,” Grandpa said.

Mia’s first thought was to give up. Instead, she looked at the glowing jar of glowworms. She placed them inside the birdbath and added water from the rainwater barrel. Their tiny lights shimmered through the crack, turning the bowl into a silver lake.

Mia carried it carefully to the flowers and poured.

At first, nothing happened. Then one petal opened, followed by another. Soon, every flower in the circle was glowing like little moons.

Mia laughed with delight. Then she noticed a second message written on the back of the map.

“Tomorrow night, find the star that fell into the pond.”

Mia looked at the dark water. For a moment, she thought she saw a bright point moving beneath the surface.

“Grandpa,” she whispered, “I think the next adventure has already begun.”
