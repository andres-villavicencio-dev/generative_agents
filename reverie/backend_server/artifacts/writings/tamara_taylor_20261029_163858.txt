Notes from February 17

It feels like my life has been filled with purposeful motion lately. From Hobbs Cafe to Valentine’s Day, the schedule has been busy, but I do not feel rushed. I feel full, focused, and encouraged.

I woke up at 6:00 AM, completed the morning routine, and began writing. The first pages came easily, but I was careful to keep the sentences warm and the images vivid. I marked the passage about the garden, the bees, and the little bird with a pencil. I wanted to show how a small creature can change the day around it. I also marked the passage about the party, because I want to celebrate the sweetness of connection without saying everything in one sentence.

I wrote about how food reminds us what we value. I wrote about how a shared table can bring people together. I wrote about how patience is not always easy, but it can be beautiful when we give ourselves and others the space to grow.

The morning reading gave me a gentle pause. I read a passage about a girl who learned that kindness begins with noticing what others need. I read it aloud while I ate dinner, and I felt my own heart soften. I wanted to carry that feeling into the pages I am writing.

At home today, I am reading and writing for my children’s books. I am also enjoying the conversation with Carmen Ortiz over bread, eggs, honey, seasonal fruit, and vegetables. She is patient, creative, and kind, and her ideas have inspired me to keep making stories that feel alive.

I am still thinking about my Valentine’s Day plan. I do not want to turn the party into a performance. I want to make time to laugh, to talk, and to share food with the people who matter. I have marked the passage about the party because I want to show that love can be simple and joyful.

I also want to remember that I am not alone in this. I have a supportive family, kind friends, and a community that encourages me to create. I want to give that energy to the stories I write, to the children who listen, and to the work I carry forward.

I will write again tonight. I will take a few minutes to read, mark, and reflect. And I will begin with the passage I found most important.
