A Little House for a Little Rain

The rain tapped gently against my window, tapping like little fingers. I sat on my bed, wrapped in my cozy sweater, and opened my notebook. On the first page, I wrote: What kind of little house would you like to live in?

I imagined a house with a door that opened to clouds. Inside, there were no walls, only soft cushions, warm blankets, and a cup of tea. It was a house for a little girl named Clara. Clara had a teddy bear named Barnaby and a backpack full of stories.

“Is it raining?” Clara asked.

I smiled and wrote: Yes, and I’m glad it is.

I drew Barnaby sitting on the porch, shaking his wet fur. I drew the sky turning into a soft blue. I even drew a tiny puddle that became a pond.

Clara’s house had a garden. I wrote: The garden had flowers that sang when the wind blew. I wrote: The moon gave them a silver blanket.

By the time I finished, the page felt full of color. I closed my notebook and looked out at the rain. It was gentle, patient, and full of surprises.

I opened my phone. There were messages from Carmen. She asked about the bread we were making together. I smiled and wrote: It’s almost golden, Carmen. Maybe we can use some honey.

I put my notebook down, opened the window, and let the rain wash over me. I was tired, but I was also happy.

I had a party tomorrow, yes. But first, I was going to cook. I was going to make something that smelled like home. And I was going to write a story about a little girl who found a little house in the rain.

I smiled, took a sip of tea, and went to bed.
