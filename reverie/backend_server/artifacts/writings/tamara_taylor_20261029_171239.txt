Today felt busy, but purposeful. After a long week, my mind was full of questions, ideas, and stories. I woke at 6:00 AM, did my usual routine, and made my way to Hobbs Cafe. The café was full on Friday, and the smell of freshly baked bread filled the room.

At the counter, I found Carmen Ortiz. She was working with me on a breakfast project, and we talked about bread, eggs, honey, seasonal fruit, and vegetables. Carmen’s enthusiasm made every ingredient feel like a possibility. I learned that bread can be simple and delicious, and that eggs can be used in many kinds of ways. Their conversation made me feel supported and inspired. I left with a few new ideas and a feeling that I was making something new.

After breakfast, I went to the library. I read several children’s books and took notes for a new story. I loved how each book felt like a little world waiting to be discovered. The characters were imaginative, the emotions felt real, and the ending often surprised me. I wanted to give young readers a story with the same warmth.

At home, I read the story aloud to the children. Their reactions made it feel special. I made notes about which parts felt magical, which characters stood out, and where the story could grow. I also thought about what I’d like to include next: maybe a character who loves to surprise others, or a place where the smallest actions can change things.

I also remembered that I had a lot of creative energy to channel. I made a cup of tea, wrote down my ideas, and prepared a small breakfast. Tomorrow, I still have some appointments, but I don’t feel pressured. I simply want to create something meaningful.

Valentine’s Day is coming up, and I am excited to attend the party. I hope the story I’m writing will make the day feel warm and joyful. For now, I’m grateful that I’m here, that I’m learning, and that I can inspire the children with my imagination.
