The premise is simple, but I think it has the potential to become something special. I want the characters to have moments of warmth, humor, and even a little recklessness. I want readers to laugh with them, feel their hearts swell, and then feel comforted by the kindness they encounter.

I’m still figuring out how to make the comic work with the books, but I don’t need perfection today. I need to keep writing, keep listening, and keep believing that every small idea can grow into something beautiful.

I’ve been thinking about the characters again. One of them is named Leo, a young architect who dreams of building bridges between separated places. His neighbor is Maya, a quiet cartographer who maps the world in secret. They discover that sometimes the most important paths are the ones we haven’t noticed yet.

I’m excited to see how they grow together. I believe in patience, yes, but I also believe in possibility. A child’s book doesn’t need to be simple, but it shouldn’t be complicated either. It should make the reader feel seen and encouraged to imagine what’s possible.
