It’s Tuesday, February 14, and the sun is still gently warming our home. I woke up at six, made a fresh cup of tea, and ate breakfast before reviewing the children’s book series. Every story feels like a small world with a beginning, a middle, and a place to go. Today was full, but not busy.

I applied for the opening at 12:00. I’d hoped to speak with someone, but no specific appointment or location had been confirmed yet. I’m hopeful that someone will notice me by Tuesday. For now, I’m proud of the series and grateful for the community that helps me keep going.

I sat down at my computer and checked the manuscript. Pacing has been my favorite part of the work. I wanted the adventures to move forward without losing the little characters. Sometimes I slowed down, and sometimes I let the story take a little breath. I made sure the transitions felt natural, that the changes in setting and mood didn’t feel rushed.

At lunch, I ate and then read through the manuscript again. I noticed a scene where the characters could move together more easily. I changed the transition so that the change felt like part of the story rather than a sudden jump. I also added a few lines of dialogue that made the characters feel more real.

At six, I had dinner and reviewed the manuscript one more time. I wanted to check the ending, but I didn’t want to rush it. The story is still growing. I’m grateful for the patience of my readers, the encouragement of my friends, and the quiet kindness of writing with imagination.

I read and reflected until ten, then went to bed. I want to keep going, page by page, story by story.
