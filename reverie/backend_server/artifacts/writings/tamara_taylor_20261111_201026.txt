Good morning, children. I am Tamara Taylor, and today we are beginning a little journey.

I woke at six, made tea, and ate breakfast with the little ones. They loved the book so much that it feels like the whole world is waiting for us to begin.

At twelve, I went to work. For two hours, my mind was full of new scenes: a bear who forgot his home, a mouse who learned to stand tall, and a rabbit who wanted to be brave. I wrote them with care, because I believe every child carries a story inside them.

I also applied for an opening at nine o’clock, though no one had confirmed the appointment or location yet. I hope I hear from someone.

At six, I had dinner and reviewed the manuscript again. Some lines felt warm, while others seemed a little hesitant. I kept the ones that made me smile, and I made room for the ones that still felt unsure.

After dinner, I read and reflected until ten, then went to bed. Tomorrow, I will wake up again and make another cup of tea. I am proud of this series, grateful for the community, and grateful for the quiet work that keeps us all curious.

We are ready to begin again.
