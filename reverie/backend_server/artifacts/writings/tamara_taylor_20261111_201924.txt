Today was a full, purposeful Tuesday, and I’m proud to say I made every minute count. I woke at six, made a fresh cup of tea, and spent the first hour reviewing the manuscript and my series. The words came easily to me, but I was especially careful about consistency. Every character’s voice, age, and way of showing emotion had to feel the same across every page.

By noon, I had lunch and applied for the opening. I didn’t expect it yet, so I didn’t feel rushed. I was grateful for my community and the opportunity to help young readers find their way.

In the afternoon, I focused on the children’s book. I revised the manuscript again, paying close attention to tone and character development. The story was moving, but I wanted each scene to carry its own rhythm. I changed a few sentences and tightened the dialogue, making sure the emotional beats landed without being forced. A character I thought was too sharp was made more thoughtful, and a quieter moment gained more energy.

At six, I had dinner and once again reviewed the manuscript. The revision had felt like a small victory, though I still had a long way to go. I read through the series aloud, listening for the places where the words felt natural and where they needed a little more breathing room.

By ten, I had finished the day. I went to bed around ten, and I was tired, but I was also satisfied. I had spent the day working on something I loved, and I’m grateful for the time I had to nurture my book. I’m still looking for the next opening, but I’m also learning to be patient with the work. For now, I’ll rest, drink tea, and keep writing.
