Wednesday, February 15

Today has felt full without being filled. I woke at 5:00 a.m., made the morning routine, and walked to the east entrance of Oak Hill College. The path was faint at first, but the shade and the initial sign gave me a little direction. I marked the route, noted the gaps between the trees, and marked a few overlooked spots on my notes.

Afterward, I compared the route with the quiet spots I had not noticed before. Sam checked in with me, and we agreed that the shaded gaps hold interesting possibilities—perhaps a good place to study without disturbing the others. I also compared the path with Jennifer’s watercolor sketches. Her work suggested a more winding route than the one I followed, but she also seemed to notice things I had missed. I’m beginning to think that a careful observation can reveal a lot.

I still have no confirmed appointments or meetings today. That gives me some time to finish my third concept review. I plan to write down the main ideas, compare them with my notes, and see what I can improve. At the same time, I’m training for the next competition, so I need to balance my studies with training, exams, and trying to make my routine more efficient.

I do not have much to celebrate, but this day has felt purposeful. The garden visit, the quiet moments, and the comparisons have made me feel more curious. I hope I remember this: discovery does not always come from easy answers. It comes from paying attention, checking where we’ve been, and being willing to change our path.
